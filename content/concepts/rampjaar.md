**Rampjaar** (Dutch for “Disaster Year”) refers to **[[1672]]**, when the **[[United Provinces (Dutch Republic)]]** was simultaneously invaded by **[[England|England]]**, **[[France|France]]**, and **[[Munster (Germany)|Munster]]**, following the secret **[[Treaty of Dover (1670)]]**. The crisis weakened Dutch naval power, reducing protection for **[[VOC]]** shipping routes to the **[[Sultanate of Aceh]]** and other Asian colonies.

## Historical Interpretation
Historians such as **[[Gilbert Burnet]]** have linked the **Rampjaar** to the financial strains of the **[[Restoration]]** in England and the aggressive policies of **[[Louis XIV]]**.
