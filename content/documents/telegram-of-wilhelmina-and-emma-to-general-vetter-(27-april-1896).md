**The telegram of Wilhelmina and Emma to General Vetter** was a message received by the army commander General [[J. A. Vetter|Vetter]] from [[Amsterdam]] on [[27 April]] [[1896]], the day the bombardment of [[Pakan Badak]], [[Pasar Oleh Gli]] and [[Lampisang]] opened, conveying the appreciation of the Queen and the Queen Regent for the forces in [[Acheh|Atjeh]].

## Text

[[P. H. R. Beuming]] reproduces it: *Diner gisteren brachten hulde krijgsmacht Atjeh; geestdriftig toegejuicht. Met beste wenschen waardeering en volle vertrouwen volgen troepen en operaties. WILHELMINA EN EMMA.*

Rendered: at yesterday's dinner homage was paid to the armed forces in Atjeh; enthusiastically cheered. With best wishes, appreciation and full confidence we follow the troops and operations.

## Effect

Beuming records that all who then formed part of the land and sea forces in Atjeh were deeply sensible of *dit hooge bewijs van waardeering onzer geliefde vorstinnen* — this high proof of appreciation from their beloved sovereigns — and that the telegram *bracht weer nieuwe geestdrift in de gelederen* — brought new enthusiasm into the ranks — as the men looked forward to the reckoning with [[Teuku Umar|Toekoe Oemar]], the man who had brought them into all their misery and had cost the Government, besides treasure, a considerable number of human lives.

The telegram was received on the day [[Teuku Umar|Toekoe Oemar]] was formally dismissed as *[[Panglima Prang Besar|Panglima Prang-Besaar]]* of the Government and as *[[Hoeloebalang|hoeloebalang]]* of [[Lepong]], and immediately before the [[Three-day battle in the Beradin pass (23-25 May 1896)|three-day battle in the Beradin pass]].
