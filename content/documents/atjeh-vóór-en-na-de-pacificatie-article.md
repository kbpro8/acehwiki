**Atjeh vóór en na de pacificatie (article)** (Haagsch Maandblad, May 1928) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Article defending the civil administration against over-pessimism about the 1926 Bakongan uprising.

## Date

[[Haagsch Maandblad]], May [[1928]]

## Subject

Article defending the [[Binnenlandsch Bestuur (B.B.)|civil administration]] against over-pessimism about the [[1926]] [[Bakóngan uprising (1925–1927)|Bakongan uprising]].

## Significance

Atjeh vóór en na de pacificatie (article) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
