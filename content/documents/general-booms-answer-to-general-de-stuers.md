**General Booms Answer to General de Stuers** was a foundational military monograph published in The Hague in [[1875]] by General [[G. G. Booms]] under the title *Over de eerste expeditie tegen Atjeh: Een antwoord aan Generaal de Stuers*. The work provided a comprehensive analysis of the causes behind the defeat of the [[First Aceh Expedition]] in [[1873]].

## Genesis of the Debate

In April [[1875]], General [[F. V. A. de Stuers]] published an article in *De Gids* criticizing an earlier essay by [[G. G. Booms]]. De Stuers defended the [[Dutch East Indies Government]] and attributed the failure of the [[First Aceh Expedition]] to tactical errors by commanders General [[J. H. R. Köhler]] and General [[E. C. van Daalen]].

In his [[1875]] response, [[G. G. Booms]] systematically refuted de Stuers, arguing that the commanders were unfairly made "scapegoats" (*zondebokken*) for systemic governmental shortcomings.

## Primary Arguments and Evidence

[[G. G. Booms]] established five primary causes for the failure of the [[1873]] expedition:
1. Flawed political and diplomatic preparation by the [[Dutch East Indies Government]], which declared war overhastily.
2. Profound ignorance of Acehnese geography and society (*zeer geringe kennis van land en volk*).
3. The complete absence of a permanent [[General Staff of the KNIL]] to coordinate intelligence.
4. Unfavorable seasonal timing, arriving just before the [[Southwest Monsoon in Aceh Operations]].
5. Inadequate naval support to maintain communications and enforce a blockade.
