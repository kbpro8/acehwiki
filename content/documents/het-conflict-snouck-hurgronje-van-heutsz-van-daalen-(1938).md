**The Conflict Snouck Hurgronje, Van Heutsz, Van Daalen (1938)** was the academic dissertation by [[J. W. Naarding]], published as part XI of the *Utrechtsche Bijdragen* in [[1938]], which [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]] called "as a study of sources so complete".

Du Croo drew from it the summary of [[Christiaan Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]]'s three causes of the persistence of the resistance and the statement of the *new* Atjeh problem, and from its *Bijlagen* the documentary core of his eighth and ninth chapters: [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|van Heutsz]]'s assessment of [[Gotfried Coenraad Ernst van Daalen|Van Daalen]] (pp. 62 ff., 65–66), Snouck Hurgronje's warning letter to Governor-General [[Rooseboom]] of [[2 October]] [[1903]] (pp. 57 ff., 69), and the [[1908]] correspondence between van Heutsz and minister [[Alexander Willem Frederik Idenburg|Idenburg]] (pp. 151, 168 ff.). Du Croo distanced himself from Naarding's judgements of van Heutsz's character, which "were fiercely, and in our opinion not unjustly, contested by many; these are left out of consideration here."
