**Het Gajoland en zijn bewoners** is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Scholarly work on Gayoland and its inhabitants; cited (pag.

## Parties

by [[Christiaan Snouck Hurgronje|Prof. Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje]]

## Subject

Scholarly work on [[Gajo-landen|Gayoland]] and its inhabitants; cited (pag. 74) on the difficulty of determining the origin of any people in this archipelago: 'one can only establish, mostly by guesswork, a former dwelling-place, whereupon the question repeats itself'.

## Significance

Het Gajoland en zijn bewoners is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
