**Staatsblad 1899 no. 259** (1899) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Reorganization of the gewest Atjeh into two afdeelingen: Groot-Atjeh under an Ass.

## Date

[[1899]]

## Parties

Dutch colonial government

## Subject

Reorganization of the [[gewest]] Atjeh into two afdeelingen: [[Groot-Atjeh]] under an Ass. Res. at [[Koeta-Radja|Koeta Radja]] (with nine onderafdeelingen) and [[Onderhoorigheden]] under an Ass. Res. at [[Koeta-Radja|Koeta Radja]] (with seven onderafdeelingen).

## Significance

Staatsblad [[1899]] no. 259 is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
