**Regeeringsreglement** was the regulation on the conduct of government in the Netherlands Indies, of which article 67 supplied the legal foundation of indirect rule and article 43 the power under which [[H. N. A. Swart]] was charged with the government of [[Celebes]].

Article 67 ran: "so far as circumstances permit, the native population is left under the immediate direction of its own chiefs, appointed or recognised by the Government." Out of that article, [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]] wrote, arose the *[[Korte Verklaring]]*. Under article 43 of the *Reglement op het beleid der Regeering in Nederlandsch-Indië* Swart was on [[9 July]] [[1906]] temporarily charged with the civil and military authority in the [[Gouvernement Celebes en Onderhoorigheden]]. The colonial opposition in the Chambers had pressed for such provisions at the framing of the *Regeeringsreglement* of [[1854]].
