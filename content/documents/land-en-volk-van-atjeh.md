**Land en Volk van Atjeh** was the work on the land and people of [[Atjeh]] by [[J. Jongejans]], written by [[1938]] and cited by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]] for the later development of estate agriculture and *[[Volksonderwijs in Atjeh|volksonderwijs]]*.

Jongejans supplied the account of the creation of the estate area on the [[Oostkust van Atjeh|east coast]] "at a feverish tempo", the concession figures to the end of [[1937]], and the survey of village education in [[1938]]: 328 schools, over 600 teachers, more than 36,000 pupils, and a literacy rate of 1.1 per cent at the census of [[1930]]. He also stressed the dependence of schools, particularly girls' schools, on the support of the *[[oelèëbalang]]*.
