**Londensche tractaat (London Treaty)** (17 March 1824) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. England withdrew completely from Sumatra (in exchange for Malakka); Raffles-tractaat lapsed.

## Date

[[17 March]] [[1824]]

## Parties

[[Leyden (Leiden)|Netherlands]] and [[Engeland (England)|England]]

## Subject

[[Engeland (England)|England]] withdrew completely from Sumatra (in exchange for [[Malakka]]); [[Raffles]]-tractaat lapsed. Acehnese kingdom remained independent.

## Significance

Londensche tractaat (London Treaty) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
