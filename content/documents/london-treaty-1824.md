**London Treaty (1824)** was a bilateral agreement concluded on [[27 August]] [[1824]] between the [[United Kingdom|British]] and the [[Netherlands|Dutch]] that obliged the Dutch to ensure the security of the waters surrounding [[Sumatra]] and to respect the sovereignty of the [[Aceh Sultanate|Atjeh]] in northern Sumatra. The treaty is repeatedly cited in Dutch colonial correspondence as the legal basis for later military actions in Aceh, notably the declaration of war in [[26 March]] [[1873]] (see [[Declaration of War on Aceh (1873)]]).  

The treaty’s maritime security clause gave the Dutch the right to patrol the Strait of Malacca and the surrounding seas, while the sovereignty clause recognised the independent status of the Aceh Sultanate, a point that later Dutch authorities found “difficult to reconcile” with their expansionist aims. The text of the treaty was published in the Dutch colonial archives and remains a key primary source for scholars of early‑nineteenth‑century Dutch‑British relations in the East Indies.

## Context
The treaty followed the end of the Napoleonic Wars and the re‑establishment of Dutch authority in the former Dutch East Indies. Britain, having secured its own trading interests in the region, sought a clear division of spheres of influence to avoid conflict with the Netherlands. The agreement therefore delineated zones of naval responsibility and affirmed the political autonomy of local polities such as Aceh.

## Provisions relevant to Aceh
- **Maritime security**: The Dutch were required to patrol the waters of Sumatra to protect British shipping from piracy.
- **Sovereignty of Aceh**: The Dutch recognised the “onathankolijk‑heid” (sovereignty) of the Aceh Sultanate, obliging them not to interfere in its internal affairs without British consent.

## Significance
The treaty’s dual obligations created a diplomatic paradox that Dutch officials later cited to justify military intervention when they deemed Aceh’s “zeerovers” (pirates) a threat to regional stability. It set the legal backdrop for the Sumatra Treaty of [[2 November]] [[1871]] and the eventual outbreak of the Aceh War.
