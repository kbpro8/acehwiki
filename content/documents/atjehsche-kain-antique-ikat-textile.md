**Atjehsche kain (antique ikat textile)** (historical) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Antique Acehnese ikat-woven silk textile with characteristic lambajöng (dark purple) and silver thread.

## Date

historical

## Parties

Acehnese home industry

## Subject

Antique Acehnese [[Ikatten|ikat]]-woven silk textile with characteristic [[Lambajöng|lambajöng]] (dark purple) and silver thread. Documented as benchmark of vanished weaving tradition.

## Significance

Atjehsche kain (antique ikat textile) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
