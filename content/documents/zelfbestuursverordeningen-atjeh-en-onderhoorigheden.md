**Zelfbestuursverordeningen Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden** — the third bundle of Aceh self-government regulations, published in [[1930]] as Serie A No. 9 of the *Mededeelingen van de Afdeeling Bestuurszaken der Buitengewesten van het Departement van Binnenlandsch Bestuur* — is the printed compilation of twenty-three numbered regulations (*Zelfbestuursverordeningen* and *Zelfbestuursbesluiten*) issued between [[1912]] and [[1928]] by the [[Zelfbestuur|Zelfbestuurders]] — the *Oeleebalang* and *Kepala negeri* of the various *zelfbesturende landschappen* of the [[Atjeh|Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] — and printed in parallel Dutch and Malay columns by the Landsdrukkerij at Weltevreden. The compilation was the third in a series begun under earlier numbers of the *Mededeelingen* — its Voorwoord declares that "Het verschijnen van dezen derden bundel zelfbestuursverordeningen beteekent een nieuwe stap in de richting, aangegeven in het voorwoord van nummer 5 dezer Serie van de Mededeelingen" — and it is the most complete single printed record of the operational legal architecture of Dutch indirect rule in [[Atjeh]] after the [[Atjeh-oorlog (1873-1904)|pacification]].

## Context

The book belongs to the constitutional framework of Dutch indirect rule in [[Atjeh]] after the pacification. From [[1898]] onward, the successive *oeleebalang* of the coastal and highland *landschappen* had been bound to the colonial state by the so-called *Korte Verklaring* — the short political pact by which each signatory acknowledged Dutch sovereignty and promised to follow the Government's inzichten — and from [[1912]] onward the metropolitan statute *Staatsblad 1919 No. 822* (the *Zelfbestuursregelen 1919*) supplied the formal framework within which the *Zelfbestuurders* could issue their own *verordeningen*. The *Zelfbestuursregelen 1927* updated the framework, but the Voorwoord of the present compilation notes that "artikel 26 der zelfbestuursregelen 1927 in dit gewest nog geen toepassing heeft gevonden" — meaning that, in Atjeh, the collective-vaststelling practice (under which all *Zelfbestuurders* of an onderafdeeling issued their regulations jointly) still prevailed over the single-landschap system the 1927 reform envisaged.

The result was a corpus of regulations issued, throughout the [[1910s]] and [[1920s]], by the *oeleebalang* of the *onderafdeelingen* — [[Lhoksoekon]], [[Meulaboh]], [[Pidie|Sigli]], [[Takingeun]], [[Alaslanden]], Bireuën, [[Meureudoe]], Lammeulo, Padangtidji, [[Lhokseumawe]], Idi, Tjalang, Langsa, Tamiang, Tapaktoean, the Zuidelijke Atjehsche landschappen and Simeuloe — each regulation subject to the *goedkeuring* of the [[Civiel en Militair Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] and, in the most important fiscal cases, to the prior *vergunning* of the Gouverneur-Generaal van Nederlandsch-Indië. The compilation of [[1930]] is the third bundle of these regulations to appear in the *Mededeelingen* series; its predecessors (Serie A No. 5 and the unnumbered earlier bundles) had covered the earliest *adat*-confirming verordeningen of the 1910s.

## Structure

The compilation is organised, per its *Inhoud* (table of contents on pages V–VII), under four headings — Justice, Finances, Administration, and Agriculture/Industry/Trade — covering 23 numbered regulations.

**Justitie** comprises five regulations: Reg. No. 1 (anti-woeker regeling for the landschappen of the onderafdeeling [[Lhoksoekon]], dated [[9 December]] [[1926]], based on *Staatsblad 1916 No. 643*); Reg. No. 10 (Mohammedaan marriage and *talak* regeling for the onderafdeeling [[Meureudoe]], inwerking [[1 January]] [[1918]]); Reg. No. 11 (the *gevaarlijke lectuur en propaganda* regeling for the onderafdeeling Idi of [[15 November]] [[1924]], based on *Staatsblad 1919 No. 822*); Reg. No. 12 (supervision of Mohammedaan religious instruction for the landschappen of the onderafdeeling Lhoksoekon, [[30 November]] [[1925]]); and Reg. No. 13 (supervision of blacksmithing and manufacture of *blanke wapenen* in the onderafdeeling Tjalang, [[8 January]] [[1927]]).

**Financiën** comprises eight regulations: Reg. No. 2 (the originating *Pantjang Alas* regeling for the landschap [[Meulaboh]] of [[5 April]] [[1912]]); Reg. No. 3 (the *Gouverneursbesluit* of [[12 April]] [[1912]] No. 247/18 establishing heffing-rules for the *Pantjang Alas* across the entire Gouvernement); Reg. Nos. 4a–4c (the revised *Pantjang Alas* regeling for the afdeeling [[Pidie]] of [[27 July]] [[1925]], with its uitvoeringsbesluiten for Sigli and Takingeun); Reg. No. 5 (the cattle-slaughter tax regeling for the onderafdeeling Alaslanden of [[1 March]] [[1914]], modified [[28 April]] [[1924]]); Reg. No. 6 (the vee-uitvoerheffing for the gebied van Rädjö Boekét in Takingeun of [[1 December]] [[1917]]); Reg. No. 7 (the vee-uitvoerheffing for Meureudoe, inwerking [[1 July]] [[1918]]); Reg. No. 8 (the verkoop-heffing on onroerende goederen for Lhokseumawe of [[1 January]] [[1918]]); and Reg. Nos. 9a–9b (the inkomstenbelasting regeling for the landschap Peureula' of [[20 December]] [[1920]], with its *Nota van Toelichting*).

**Bestuur** comprises Reg. No. 14, the heerendienst (corvée) regeling of [[18 August]] [[1928]] for the onderafdeeling Bireuën, which became the model for parallel regelingen in all eighteen other onderafdeelingen, all entering force on [[1 January]] [[1929]].

**Landbouw, Nijverheid en Handel** comprises Reg. Nos. 15–23, ranging from the schoolgeld-regeling for the landschap Teunom-Panga ([[4 August]] [[1917]]) and the cattle-transport regeling for Alaslanden ([[16 September]] [[1914]]), through the Klapperverordening ([[25 August]] [[1916]]) and the tjemaraboomen-regeling of [[2 July]] [[1917]] for Meulaboh, to the alang-alang regelingen for Takingeun ([[20 May]] [[1924]]) and Pidië ([[21 January]] [[1928]]), with Reg. No. 21 — the *natuurmonument* designation for the Rafflesia sites of the landschap Serbeudjadi ([[1 January]] [[1921]]) — the most botanically distinctive entry.

## Bilingual form

Every regulation in the compilation is printed in parallel Dutch and Malay (or rather the colonial *Maleisch* of the 1910s–1920s) columns, the Dutch article headed *Artikel* and its Malay counterpart headed *Fatsal* (from Arabic *faṣl*, "chapter"). The vaststelling-formula itself is bilingual: each regulation opens with a *Menimbang* / *Overwegende* recital in parallel Malay and Dutch, then the signatory *oeleebalang* (in Malay *Kami Oeleebalang dari …*) state their authority under *Staatsblad 1919 No. 822* — *Gelet op Staatsblad 1919 No. 822* / *Diperhatikan boenjinja Staatsblad 1919 No. 822* — to issue the regulation. The closing formula is likewise bilingual: "Peratoeran ini didjalankan dari tanggal …" / "Deze verordening treedt in werking op …".

Each regulation carries the formal approval of the *Civiele en Militaire Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden* — the printed note "(goedgekeurd door den Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden op …)" — and, in the most important fiscal cases (notably the *Pantjang Alas* of [[1912]] and the inkomstenbelasting of [[1920]]), the prior *vergunning* of the Gouverneur-Generaal van Nederlandsch-Indië, obtained by telegram. The compilation also includes the formal *Gouverneursbesluiten* issued by the Civiel en Militair Gouverneur himself — Reg. No. 3 (the Pantjang Alas heffing-besluit of [[12 April]] [[1912]] No. 247/18) and Reg. No. 15b (the schoolgeld-administratie besluit of [[4 August]] [[1917]]) — which stand alongside the *Zelfbestuursverordeningen* as part of the same operational corpus.

## Significance

The compilation is the operational legal foundation of the colonial-*zelfbestuur* hybrid in [[Atjeh]] from [[1912]] to [[1928]]. It records, in twenty-three bilingual regulations and their comparative tables of *Gelijke Verordeningen*, the systematic extension of a single fiscal-and-administrative template — the *Pantjang Alas*, the slachtpadjak, the vee-uitvoerheffing, the inkomstenbelasting, the heerendienst, the schoolgeld — across all eighteen *onderafdeelingen* of the Gouvernement, each *onderafdeeling* issuing its parallel regulation in a slightly different year, but with the text and tariff structure remaining broadly identical. The compilation thus reveals, in a way no narrative colonial report could, how the Dutch colonial state actually governed Atjeh after the pacification: by inducing its *Zelfbestuurders* to legislate, in their own name and in their own Malay, the very fiscal and police measures the metropolis desired, with the *Civiel en Militair Gouverneur* supplying only the *goedkeuring*. Reg. No. 9b — the *Nota van Toelichting* on the inkomstenbelasting — even makes the constitutional logic explicit, citing the *Korte Verklaring* (in Malay *soerat Ikrar*) and the *Nota over politick beleid en bestuurszorg in de Buitenbezittingen* to explain why "de onderworpenheid van het Atjehsche Zelfbestuur aan het Europeesch gezag in het algemeen reeds uit de Korte Verklaringen voortvloeit". The book is therefore not only a legal reference work but a primary source for the constitutional theory of Dutch indirect rule in Atjeh under the [[Zelfbestuur|Zelfbestuursregelen]] of 1919 and 1927.
