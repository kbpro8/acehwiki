**Rubber-restrictie regulations** (1934) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Quota restriction on rubber production to balance supply and demand and maintain profitable prices.

## Date

[[1934]]

## Parties

International rubber restriction scheme

## Subject

Quota restriction on rubber production to balance supply and demand and maintain profitable prices. Brought relief after the [[Malaise|malaise]] years.

## Significance

Rubber-restrictie regulations is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
