**Sindoro** was a Dutch steamship (*Hr.Ms.*) that formed part of the **Royal Netherlands Navy** flotilla operating on the **Tamiang** river during the **February [[1893]]** expedition. It worked in concert with **[[Flores (ship)]]** and **[[Soembawa (ship)]]** under **[[W. Allirol]]**.

## Function

The ship’s armed boats helped clear rotan, rope and bamboo blockades that impeded the Dutch advance.
