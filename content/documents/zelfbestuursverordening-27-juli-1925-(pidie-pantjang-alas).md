**The Zelfbestuursverordening of [[27 July]] [[1925]] (Pidie Pantjang Alas)** — printed in the [[1930]] compilation of *Zelfbestuursverordeningen Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden* as Regulation No. 4a — is the revised *Pantjang Alas* verordening (ordinance) of the *afdeeling* (district) [[Pidie]], issued jointly by the *Zelfbestuurders* (self-governors) of twenty-four *landschappen* (territories) of the *afdeeling* in *overleg* (consultation) with the *Assistent-Resident van de Afdeeling Pidië* (Assistant-Resident of the District Pidië), and approved by the *Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden* (Governor of Atjeh and Dependencies) on [[3 July]] [[1926]]. Repealing the earlier *regeling* (regulation) of [[28 April]] [[1912]], it is the most detailed and comprehensive of all the *Pantjang Alas* verordeningen of the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Gouvernement]] and is the model on which the parallel *uitvoeringsbesluiten* (implementing decisions) (Regs. 4b and 4c) for the *onderafdeelingen* (subdivisions) of Pidië, Takingeun, Serbeudjadi, Gajo LoeBus, and the Alaslanden were drawn.

## The twenty-four signatory Zelfbestuurders

The regulation opens with the longest signatory-clause in the compilation: the *Zelfbestuurders* of twenty-four *landschappen* of the *afdeeling* Pidië, collectively named in the Malay column as "*Zelfbestuurders dari landsehap?, Pidië, Keumangan, Ndjong, Gloempang Pajoeng, Pineueng, Ië Leubeue, Gigieng, Bambi en Oenoë, Aron, Kraeung Seumideuen, Sama Indra, Aree, Iboih, Meureudoe, Trieng Gading, Pante Radja, Reubee, Keumala, Titene, Tjoembo, Troeseb, Andeue en Lala, Me Tareuem, Ilot, Tangse en Geumpang, jang masock dalam Afdeeling Pidië*" — "the *Zelfbestuurders* of the *landschappen* Pidië, Keumangan, Ndjong, Gloempang Pajoeng, Pineueng, Ië Leubeue, Gigieng, Bambi en Oenoë, Aron, Kraeung Seumideuen, Sama Indra, Aree, Iboih, Meureudoe, Trieng Gading, Pante Radja, Reubee, Keumala, Titene, Tjoembo, Troeseb, Andeue en Lala, Me Tareuem, Ilot, [and] Tangse en Geumpang, which fall within the Afdeeling Pidië." The four *onderafdeelingen* of the *afdeeling* Pidië — Sigli, Meureudoe, Lammeulo, and Padangtidji — together contain these twenty-four *landschappen*, of which three (Meureudoe, Trieng Gading, and Pante Radja) had earlier, in [[1918]], jointly issued the [[Zelfbestuurders van de landschappen Meureudoe, Trieng Gading en Panté Radja|Mohammedaan marriage regulation]] (Reg. No. 10).

## Repeal of the 1912 regeling

The substantive preamble recites the repeal of the earlier Pidië *Pantjang Alas* regeling of [[28 April]] [[1912]] — itself one of the cluster of April-1912 *landschap*-level regelingen that followed immediately upon the originating [[Zelfbestuursverordening 5 April 1912 (Meulaboh boschproducten)|Meulaboh verordening of 5 April 1912]] and the [[Gouverneursbesluit 12 April 1912 No. 247/18|authorising *besluit* (decree) of 12 April 1912 No. 247/18]]:

> "Overwegende dat het wensehelijk is de regeling ddo. 28 April 1912 betreffende de heffing en inning van de z.g.n. pantjang alas te wij- zigen"

— "Whereas it is desirable to amend the regulation of [[28 April]] [[1912]] concerning the *heffing* (levy) and *inning* (collection) of the so-called *pantjang alas*." The twenty-four *Zelfbestuurders*, *in overleg* with the *Plaatselijk Besturende Ambtenaren* (Local Administering Officials) and the *Assistent-Resident van de Afdeeling Pidië*, and *na verkregen instemming van den Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden* (with the obtained assent of the Governor of Atjeh and Dependencies), thus repealed the 1912 regeling and established the new one. The new regeling took effect — *inwerkingtreding* (entry into force) — on [[1 December]] [[1925]], almost eight months before the formal *goedkeuring* (approval) of the Gouverneur on [[3 July]] [[1926]].

## Citation of Staatsblad 1919 No. 822 and 823

The recital cites the legal basis of the regulation in the [[Staatsblad 1919 No. 822|Zelfbestuursregelen 1919]] (Self-Government Regulations 1919) (Staatsblad No. 822 and its companion No. 823) — the colonial statute governing *zelfbestuur* (self-government) in the Outer Possessions that is invoked as the constitutional basis of every regulation in the compilation:

> "Mengingat boenjinja Stbld. 1919 No. 822 dan 823. Gelet op Sthl. 1919 Nos, 822 en 823."

— "Having regard to Staatsblad 1919 No. 822 and 823." The 1919 *Zelfbestuursregelen* were the immediate constitutional warrant for the *Zelfbestuur*'s power to issue verordeningen in respect of the *anak negeri* (subjects) of its *landschap*; the *goedkeuring* of the *Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden* — itself required by the same *Zelfbestuursregelen* — was given on [[3 July]] [[1926]] (a parallel *goedkeuring* of the same date covers Reg. No. 4b, the *uitvoeringsbesluit* for the *onderafdeeling* Sigli).

## Content and provisions

The substantive articles of the verordening are set out in the standard bilingual Malay-Dutch columnar form, with the Malay *Fatsal* (Article) paired with the Dutch *Artikel*. *Fatsal 1 / Artikel 1* enumerates the taxable products: "*Pantjang alas wordt gehe- ven van atap, benzoë, boomschors, damar, getah*" — "*Pantjang Alas* is levied on *atap* (thatch), *benzoë*, *boomschors* (tree-bark), *damar*, *getah* (rubber)…" and continuing through the full catalogue of *boschproducten* (forest products): *ivoor* (ivory), *neushoorn* (rhinoceros[-horn]), *was* (wax), *timmerhout* (timber), *rotan* (rattan), *staartpeper* (long-pepper), *kamfer* (camphor), *kaneel* (cinnamon), *kapoer baroes* (camphor-barus), *reukhout* (scented-wood), *houtskool* (charcoal), *zand* (sand), *zwavel* (sulphur), *steen* (stone), and so forth. The rate, as in the originating Meulaboh verordening, is fixed at ten per cent of the quarterly valuation set by the *Hoofd van gewestelijk bestuur* (Head of Regional Administration) in *overleg* with the *Zelfbestuur*: "*De 'Pantjang alas' bedraagt 10% van de waarde, welke drie manndelijks door het Hoofd van Gewest bij besluit wordt vast- gesteld.*" *Artikel 5* makes the [[Atjehtram (place)|Atjeh Tram]] a controlled transport point: "*Het is verboden boschprodneten, die niet door een quitantie ziju ge- dekt ten vervoer aan te bieden aan den dienst der Atjeh Tram*" — "It is forbidden to offer for transport to the service of the Atjeh Tram any *boschproducten* that are not covered by a *quitantie* (receipt)." The full text of the regulation — *Fatsal* 1–7 — occupies pages 25–29 of the compilation.

## Significance

The *Zelfbestuursverordening van [[27 July]] [[1925]]* is the most detailed and most broadly-scoped of all the *Pantjang Alas* verordeningen of the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Gouvernement]]. Its signatory-clause naming twenty-four *landschappen* of the *afdeeling* [[Pidie]] makes it the broadest territorial *Pantjang Alas* regeling in the compilation — none of the other *landschap*-level regelingen approaches this territorial scope. The Pidië regulation was extended immediately by parallel *uitvoeringsbesluiten* (Reg. No. 4b for the *onderafdeeling* Sigli of [[27 July]] [[1925]], with parallel *besluiten* (decisions) for the *onderafdeelingen* Meureudoe, Lammeulo, and Padangtidji, all in *werking* (operation) from [[1 December]] [[1925]]), and by the more elaborate Takingeun *uitvoeringsbesluit* (Reg. No. 4c) of [[1 October]] [[1926]], which prescribed the *vergunning*- (permit-), *vervoerpas*- (transport-pass-), *kwitantie*- (receipt-), and *kasboekje*- (cashbook-)procedure in unusual detail. The comparative table extending parallel regelingen to Takingeun (1-10-1926), Serbeudjadi (1-10-1926), Gajo LoeBus (1-9-1926), and Alaslanden (1-11-1925), all in *werking* by 31-12-1926, makes the Pidië verordening the de facto model of the late-1920s *Pantjang Alas* regime. Its repeal of the earlier *regeling* of [[28 April]] [[1912]] — itself one of the immediate successors of the originating [[Zelfbestuursverordening 5 April 1912 (Meulaboh boschproducten)|Meulaboh verordening of 5 April 1912]] — marks the second generation of *Pantjang Alas* verordeningen, more detailed and more systematically administered than the original April-1912 cluster.
