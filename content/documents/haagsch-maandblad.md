**Haagsch Maandblad** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Maurice Henri du Croo]]

He emphasised that Swart had preserved no letters and no notes, possessed no archive and no diaries, so that recovering even the private correspondence with Schmidt required effort. He borrowed extended narrative passages from [[H. C. Zentgraaff]]'s *[[Atjeh (book by H. C. Zentgraaff)|Atjeh]]* — notably the deaths of [[Pang Nanggròë]], [[Tjoet Meutia]] and [[Tgk. di Barat]] — and from *[[Military Constabulary in Atjeh (1943)|Marechaussee in Atjeh]]*, and from [[J. C. Lamster]]'s Atjeh article in the *[[Haagsch Maandblad]]* for the survey of the pacifying administrative work.

### From [[J. C. Lamster]]

- *Atjeh voor en na de pacificatie*, in the *[[Haagsch Maandblad]]*, [[1928]].
- *Atjeh*, in the *Haagsch Maandblad*, May [[1938]], pp. 495–496.
- *Het daghet in den Oosten*, in the *Indische Gids*, [[1938]].
- *J. B. van Heutsz*.

### From [[Het Pacificeerende Bestuurswerk 1908-1918]]

The **pacifying administrative work of [[1908]]–[[1918]]** was the civil side of [[H. N. A. Swart]]'s governorship of [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]]: the reconstruction of a province ruined by thirty-five years of war, undertaken simultaneously with the last military operations against the bands. [[Maurice Henri du Croo|Du Croo]] built his account on the survey given by [[J. C. Lamster]] in his Atjeh article in the *[[Haagsch Maandblad]]* of May [[1938]], supplemented by Swart's own recollections, the *[[Koloniale Verslagen]]* and the *[[Monthly Reports of the Government of Atjeh and Dependencies|maandverslagen]]*.
