**Ons Program (Kuyper, 1878)** — *Our Program* — is the Anti-Revolutionary party manifesto published by [[Abraham Kuyper|Abraham Kuyper]] in [[1878]], in which the relation of the [[Netherlands]] to its colonies is for the first time framed in political form as that of "de voogd tot zijn pupil" — the guardian to his ward. The doctrine of the moral vocation of the [[Netherlands]] towards its colonial populations set out in the manifesto is the political seed-bed of the [[Ethische Politiek|ethical policy]] officially proclaimed in the [[Troonrede of 1901|troonrede of 1901]].

## The guardian-ward relation

In [[Ons Program (Kuyper, 1878)|Ons Program]] of [[1878]] [[Abraham Kuyper|Kuyper]] saw the relation of the [[Netherlands]] to its colonies as that of a guardian to his ward (*voogd-pupil*). The guardian's first duty was to bring to the Indies the blessings of security and order that the [[Netherlands]] had itself received with the Gospel. A second duty — cited by [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] with the reference: "A. Kuyper, *Ons Program*, 1892, blz. 332" — was to enable the Indies in the future to assume a more autonomous position. Of these two duties the second proved more acceptable to the non-confessional parties than the first, which carried a Christian-*zending* subtext.

## The two duties

For the Christian parties, especially the anti-revolutionaries, [[Abraham Kuyper|Kuyper]] said, it was clear that the [[Netherlands|Dutch]] first duty was to bring to the Indies "die zegeningen van veiligheid en orde, die Nederland, sinds hier het licht der evangeliën doordrong, door Gods goede gunst ontving en, God zij lof, nog steeds bezitten mag" — those blessings of security and order that the [[Netherlands]], since the light of the Gospels penetrated here, has received by God's good grace and, praise God, still possesses. The history had, [[Abraham Kuyper|Kuyper]] held, "ontegensprekelijk" — incontestably — shown that all peoples who remained outside the Christian sphere could not, from their own strength, rise to "een hoger standpunt" — a higher plane.

The second duty — preparing the Indies for greater autonomy — was more acceptable to the non-confessional parties. [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] — who in many respects was the spiritual leader of the *vrijzinnig* (liberal) ethici — gave it its classic liberal formulation in his *[[Nederland en de Islam ([[1911]])|Nederland en de Islam]]* of [[1911]].

## The troonrede of 1901

The [[Troonrede of 1901|troonrede of 1901]] — drafted under the [[Abraham Kuyper|Kuyper]] ministry — gave an *ereplaats* — place of honour — to the "staatkunde van zedelijke verplichting" — policy of moral obligation — that [[Ons Program (Kuyper, 1878)|Ons Program]] of [[1878]] had been the first to formulate in political form. The ethical policy as a program point was soon adopted by the other political parties; "het zou trouwens niet denkbaar zijn, dat ze was afgewezen" — it would not have been conceivable, after all, that it should have been rejected.

## Significance for Atjeh

[[Ons Program (Kuyper, 1878)|Ons Program]] is the document in which the moral frame within which the conquest and [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacificatie]] of Atjeh would be conducted was first politically articulated. The doctrine of the moral vocation supplied the official justification for the [[Van Daalen's Gajo-Alas-Batak expedition (1904)|Van Daalen expedition]] of [[1904]], for the [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacificatie]] that followed, and for the broader [[Ethische Politiek|ethical-policy]] program of irrigation, education and regional councils.
