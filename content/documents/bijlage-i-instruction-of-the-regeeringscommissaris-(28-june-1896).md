**Bijlage I — Instruction of the Regeeringscommissaris (28 June [[1896]])** — *Instructie door den Regeeringscommissaris te Atjeh achtergelaten bij zijn vertrek op 28 Juni [[1896]]* — is the instruction left by [[J. A. Vetter|Luitenant-Generaal J. A. Vetter]] as [[Regeeringscommissaris]] at his departure from Atjeh on [[28 June]] [[1896]], preserved as the first annex (*Bijlage I*) of [[A. Kruisheer|Kruisheer]]'s *[[ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel]]* on pp. 162–165. The instruction — signed by Vetter as Regeeringscommissaris and counter-signed by [[J. F. Breijer|Luitenant-Kolonel Breijer]] as adjudant — was the policy framework within which the acting [[Civiel en Militair Gouverneur]] (initially [[J. W. Stemfoort|Kolonel Stemfoort]]) was to govern Atjeh until the arrival of a permanent governor. The instruction was, however, *ingetrokken* (withdrawn) by the *Instruction for Civiel en Militair Gouverneur Van Vliet* (Bijlage III) of November [[1896]], which explicitly replaced it.

## The Departure of Vetter and the Instruction

The instruction was left by Vetter at his departure from Atjeh on [[28 June]] [[1896]] — the [[Departure of Vetter (28 June 1896)|departure of Lt.-Gen. Vetter as Regeeringscommissaris]] which closed the first semester of [[1896]]. The instruction was communicated in Vetter's letter of [[27 June]] [[1896]], No. 212, *zeer geheim* (very secret), and was formally handed to the *Generaal-Majoor J. J. K. de Moulin* on [[30 June]] [[1896]] at [[Padang]] — *Reeds op 30 Juni [[1896]] te Padang afschrift verleend aan den Generaal-Majoor DE MOULIN. De Luitenant-Kolonel, w. g. BREYER.* The instruction thus passed from Vetter to De Moulin at Padang, and was presumably the document which governed De Moulin's brief tenure as governor — though De Moulin's death on [[7 July]] [[1896]] prevented the instruction from being fully implemented.

## The Withdrawal by Bijlage III

The instruction was *ingetrokken* (withdrawn) by the *Instruction for Civiel en Militair Gouverneur Van Vliet* (Bijlage III) of November [[1896]], which explicitly stated: *De Instructie door den Regeeringscommissaris voor Atjeh- en Onderhoorigheden bij zijn vertrek uit genoemd gewest achtergelaten in zijnen brief van 27 Juni [[1896]], No. 212, zeer geheim, wordt ingetrokken, terwijl de tegenwoordige Instructie tevens in de plaats treedt van het afgesprokene in de conferentie van den Landvoogd met den Generaal-Majoor J. J. K. de Moulin op 18 Juni [[1896]] en het opgedragene in het regeerings-telegram van 17 Juli [[1896]], No. 273.* The Van Vliet instruction thus replaced all three prior policy documents — Vetter's instruction of [[28 June]] [[1896]], the conference of [[18 June]] [[1896]], and the telegram of [[17 July]] [[1896]] — and consolidated the policy framework within which the Van Vliet governorship would be conducted.

## Significance

Bijlage I is significant in the documentary record of *ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel* as the policy framework within which the acting [[Civiel en Militair Gouverneur]] — initially Kolonel Stemfoort — was to govern Atjeh in the period between Vetter's departure on [[28 June]] [[1896]] and the arrival of a permanent governor. The instruction was the document which governed (or was intended to govern) the brief tenure of Generaal-Majoor De Moulin, and was the policy framework within which the [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|surprise of Ana' Galoeng]] of [[29 June]] [[1896]] — the opening operation of the second semester — was undertaken. The instruction's withdrawal by Bijlage III in November [[1896]] marked the formal closure of the Vetter / De Moulin / Stemfoort transitional period and the consolidation of the policy framework within which the Van Vliet governorship would be conducted.
