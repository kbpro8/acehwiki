**Avifaunistische Studien in de Gajo-landen** — *Avifaunal Studies in the Gayo Lands* — is a Dutch-language ornithological paper by [[R. C. E. G. J. Snouckaert van Schauburg|Mr. Dr. Baron R. C. E. G. J. Snouckaert van Schauburg]] describing the first of two avifaunal collections made by [[F. C. van Heurn|Ir. Jhr. F. C. van Heurn]] in [[Acheh|Atjeh]] in [[1920]], the collection made in the region of [[Takengon|Lake Takengon]] (1200 metres above sea level) in [[Gajo-land|Gajo-land]]. The paper was published in issue I (*afl. I*) of the previous *Jaarbericht* of the [[Koninklijke Vereeniging Koloniaal Instituut|Koloniaal Instituut]] in [[Amsterdam|Amsterdam]], and is the predecessor of the *[[Over de tweede Atjeh-collectie van Jhr. F. C. van Heurn|Over de tweede Atjeh-collectie van Jhr. F. C. van Heurn]]*, which described the second collection made at [[Alas Peurba|Alas Peurba]] and [[Aloer Djamboe|Aloer Djamboe]] in [[September]]–[[November]] [[1920]].

## The collection

The first Atjeh collection of [[F. C. van Heurn|Van Heurn]] was made in the region of [[Takengon|Lake Takengon]] (1200 metres above sea level) in [[Gajo-land|Gajo-land]] (Atjeh). The highland, montane character of the collecting locality distinguished this collection from the second, which was made at the lowland estates [[Alas Peurba|Alas Peurba]] (200 metres) and [[Aloer Djamboe|Aloer Djamboe]] (50 metres) on the northeast coast of Atjeh. The two collections together — the one montane, the other lowland — gave a sample of the avifauna of Atjeh from the interior highlands to the coastal plain, and were worked up by Snouckaert in two successive papers in the *Jaarberichten* of the [[Koninklijke Vereeniging Koloniaal Instituut|Koloniaal Instituut]].

## Relationship to the other Van Heurn–Snouckaert papers

The *Avifaunistische Studien in de Gajo-landen* is the first of three papers that record the avifaunal collecting of [[F. C. van Heurn|Van Heurn]] in Atjeh. The second — the *[[Over de tweede Atjeh-collectie van Jhr. F. C. van Heurn|Over de tweede Atjeh-collectie van Jhr. F. C. van Heurn]]* — described the collection made at [[Alas Peurba|Alas Peurba]] and [[Aloer Djamboe|Aloer Djamboe]] in [[September]]–[[November]] [[1920]], and referred the reader back to the first paper for its map. The third — the *[[Over eene collectie vogelhuiden van Oost-Atjeh|Over eene collectie vogelhuiden van Oost-Atjeh]]* — was co-authored by Van Heurn and Snouckaert, and described material collected on the same East-Coast estates in a later, more comprehensive treatment.

## Significance

The *Avifaunistische Studien in de Gajo-landen* is significant within the AcehWiki corpus as the source document for the first of the two avifaunal collections made by [[F. C. van Heurn|Van Heurn]] in Atjeh in [[1920]], and as the document that established the highland, Gayo-land baseline against which the later lowland, East-Coast collection was measured. The paper is a document of the natural-historical work that was carried on in the interior of Atjeh in the early civilian period — after the [[Aceh War|Atjeh War]] and the [[pacification of Atjeh|pacification]] — by Dutch scientific collectors working through the research institutions of the colony.
