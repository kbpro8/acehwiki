**The Zelfbestuursverordening of [[21 January]] [[1928]] (Pidie alang-alang)** — printed in the [[1930]] compilation of *Zelfbestuursverordeningen Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden* as Regulation No. 23 — is the verordening (ordinance) of the *afdeeling* (district) [[Pidie]] establishing rules on the burning of *alang-alang* (cogon grass, *Imperata cylindrica*) and other *begroeiingen* (vegetation) on *woeste gronden* (waste lands) in the *landschappen* (territories) of the *afdeeling*. Issued jointly by the *Zelfbestuurders* (self-governors) of twenty-six *landschappen* of the *afdeeling* Pidië *in overleg met de Plaatselijk Besturende Ambtenaren in en den Assistent-Resident van de Afdeeling Pidie* (in consultation with the Local Administering Officials in and the Assistant-Resident of the District Pidie), and approved by the *Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden* (Governor of Atjeh and Dependencies) on [[30 April]] [[1928]], it is the final regulation in the compilation — closing the volume with the most broadly-scoped territorial regeling (regulation) and the only one that explicitly invokes *reboisatie* (reforestation) as a motive.

## The regulation as final entry of the compilation

Reg. No. 23 occupies the last pages of the compilation (pp. 133–134 of the printed book; the OCR's reading of "138" on page 145 is a misreading of the printed "133"). The chunk of OCR text from which it is extracted ends at the end of *Fatsal 4*, which is the *inwerkingtreding*-clause (entry-into-force clause) and is therefore typically the last article of a regulation — Reg. No. 23 thus appears to be the regulation on which the compilation closes. The recital cites the *Staatsblad 1927 No. 190* — the only regulation in the compilation to invoke this *Staatsblad* — and the OCR's misreading "19G" is corrected by the Malay column, which reads "190". Given Reg. No. 23's explicit invocation of *reboisatie*, the *Staatsblad 1927 No. 190* is most likely a forest / *reboisatie-Staatsblad* (reforestation statute) of the late colonial state.

## Prohibition on uncontrolled alang-alang burning

*Fatsal 1 / Artikel 1* (Article 1) prohibits on *woeste gronden* (waste lands) (a) the burning of *alang-alang*, other grass, *struikgewas* (shrubbery), or *begroeiingen* without the *schriftelijke vergunning* (written permission) of the *Zelfbestuur*, and (b) the laying of fires in such a way as to endanger such *begroeiingen*. *Fatsal 2 / Artikel 2* requires that permitted burns — and burns on *bebouwde terreinen* (cultivated terrains) — be watched to prevent spread. *Fatsal 3 / Artikel 3* sets the standard penalty: a maximum of one hundred guilders or three months' *hechtenis* (detention). *Fatsal 4 / Artikel 4* sets the *inwerkingtreding* on the thirtieth day after the *goedkeuring* (approval) by the *Gouverneur*.

The recital states the motive explicitly:

> "Overwegende dat het gewenscht is, bepalingen in het leven te roepen ter bescherming van begroeinneen op woeste gronden en ter bevorde- ring van en om hydrologische rede- nen gunstig geachte reboisatic."

— "Whereas it is desirable to institute provisions for the protection of vegetation on waste lands, and for the promotion of *reboisatie* (reforestation) favourable on hydrological grounds." The *reboisatie* (reforestation) motive — the only explicit reforestation reference in the compilation — and the *hydrologische redenen* (hydrological reasons) for the regulation together articulate the late-colonial conservation policy that had developed in the Netherlands-Indies since the early 1920s. The invocation of *reboisatie* distinguishes Reg. No. 23 from the [[Zelfbestuursverordening 20 Mei 1924 (Takingeun alang-alang)|Takingeun alang-alang regulation of 20 May 1924]] (Reg. No. 22), which had been framed primarily in terms of fire-prevention and the protection of the *dennenbosschen* (pine-forests).

## Penalty structure

The penalty-structure of Reg. No. 23 follows the standard pattern of the compilation: the maximum penalty is *f 100 of 3 maanden hechtenis* — one hundred guilders or three months' detention. There is no *aanbrenger-premie* (informer's reward) and no provision for *arbeidstelling* (public-works labour); the regulation thus takes the simplest penal form of any fire-prevention regulation in the compilation. The two earlier fire-prevention regulations — Reg. No. 22 (Takingeun) and Reg. No. 17 (Kloeet-Troemon's female-cattle-slaughter ban) — both employ more elaborate penalty-arrangements, including the substitution-rule of art. 30 of the *Wetboek van Strafrecht* (Penal Code) in Reg. No. 22 and the *aanbrenger-premie* in Reg. No. 17. Reg. No. 23's reliance on the standard f 100 / 3-months maximum reflects the more straightforward administrative character of the Pidië regulation, which is concerned solely with the *vergunning*-system (permit-system) and the *bebouwde terreinen* supervision.

## Significance

The *Zelfbestuursverordening van [[21 January]] [[1928]]* closes the [[Zelfbestuursverordeningen Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Zelfbestuursverordeningen compilation]] with the broadest territorial *regeling* in the volume — covering all twenty-six *landschappen* of the *afdeeling* [[Pidie]] — and the only one that explicitly invokes *reboisatie* (reforestation) as a motive. Its signatory-clause naming twenty-six *landschappen* — "*Pidië, Keumangan, Ndjong, Gloempang Pajoeng, Ië Leubenë, Pinenëng, Gigièng, Bambi en Oenoë, Aron, Kroečng Seumidenën, Sama Indra, Arêi, Iboih, Meureudoe, Trieng Gading, Panté Radja, Reubet, Keumala, Titenë, Tjoembo, Troeseb, Andeuë en Lala, Me Tareuem, Ilot, Tangsé en Geumpang*" — is the longest signatory-clause in the compilation. It extends the fire-prevention regime first established for [[Takengeun|Takingeun]] by [[Zelfbestuursverordening 20 Mei 1924 (Takingeun alang-alang)|Reg. No. 22 of 20 May 1924]] to the densely-populated north-coast *afdeeling* of Pidië, and signals the late-colonial state's growing interest in *reboisatie* as a hydrological-protective measure in the late 1920s. As the final regulation of the compilation, it also marks the chronological endpoint of the corpus of *zelfbestuursverordeningen* that the *Afdeeling Bestuurszaken der Buitengewesten* (Department of Administrative Affairs of the Outer Regions) assembled for publication in [[1930]].
