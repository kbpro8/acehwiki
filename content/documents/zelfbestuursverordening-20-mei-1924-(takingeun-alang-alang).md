**The Zelfbestuursverordening of [[20 May]] [[1924]] (Takingeun alang-alang)** — printed in the [[1930]] compilation of *Zelfbestuursverordeningen Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden* as Regulation No. 22 — is the verordening (ordinance) of the *onderafdeeling* (subdivision) [[Takengeun|Takingeun]] in the [[Gajo- en Alaslanden|afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden]] (district Gajo- en Alaslanden), issued collectively by the *Zelfbesturen* (self-governments) of the *landschappen* (territories) of Takingeun *in overleg met het Hoofd der onderafdeeling Takingeun* (in consultation with the Head of the subdivision Takingeun), regulating the burning of *alang-alang* (cogon grass, *Imperata cylindrica*) on *woeste* and *bebouwde gronden* (waste and cultivated lands) and the cutting and damaging of *dennenbosschen* (pine forests) in the *landschappen* of the *onderafdeeling*. Approved by the *Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden* (Governor of Atjeh and Dependencies) on the very day of issue, it took effect — with the unusual retroactivity-clause that is unique in the compilation — on [[1 May]] [[1925]], with the *strafbepalingen* (penal provisions) running back to [[20 May]] [[1924]].

## Issuers and recital

The regulation is issued by the collective *Zelfbesturen* of the *landschappen* of the *onderafdeeling* Takingeun — "*Zelibestuur dari negeri-uegeri di- dalam onderafdoeling Takingeuu. De Zeltbesturen van de tot het ressort der onderafieelinsskas van Takingeun behoorende landschap- pen*" — "The *Zelfbesturen* of the *landschappen* belonging to the *ressort* (jurisdiction) of the *onderafdeelingskas* (subdivision-treasury) of Takingeun." No individual *oeleebalang* (territorial chiefs) are named; the signatory is the corporate-collective *Zelfbestuur* of the *onderafdeeling*, acting in *overleg* (consultation) with the *Hoofd der onderafdeeling Takingeun* (Head of the subdivision Takingeun).

The recital cites the [[Staatsblad 1919 No. 822|Zelfbestuursregelen 1919 (Staatsblad No. 822 en 823)]] (Self-Government Regulations 1919) as the constitutional basis — "*Diperhatikan boenji Staatsblad 1919 No. 822 dan 823. Gelet op Stautsblad 1919 No. 822 en 823*" — the standard statutory warrant for the *Zelfbestuur*'s power to issue verordeningen, common to nearly every regulation in the compilation.

## Prohibition on uncontrolled alang-alang burning

*Fatsal 1 / Artikel 1* (Article 1) prohibits the burning of *alang-alang* — or any other grass or *struikgewas* (shrubbery) — on *woeste gronden* (uncultivated lands) without the *schriftelijke vergunning* (written permission) of the *Zelfbestuur*. *Fatsal 2 / Artikel 2* allows burning on *bebouwde gronden* (cultivated lands) only if the grass is heaped within the field, with proper watch kept to prevent spread. *Fatsal 3 / Artikel 3* imposes a duty of mutual aid (*hulp, dienst of bijstand* — help, service, or assistance) on the *omwonenden* (surrounding inhabitants) when fire breaks out in the *belendende terreinen* (adjacent terrains): "*Bij het uithreken van brand in de belendende terreinen zal ieder om- wonende, met nitzondering van hen, wier eigen woning of aamplant door den brand wordt bedreigd, verplicht zijn hulp, dienst of bijstand te ver- leenen*" — "On the outbreak of fire in the adjacent terrains, every surrounding inhabitant, with the exception of those whose own dwelling or planting is threatened by the fire, shall be obliged to render help, service, or assistance." The mutual-aid duty is signalled by the *tong-tong* (the village slit-drum) — the standard Acehnese alarm-signal for fire, flood, and other communal emergencies.

## Permitting system for dennenbosschen cutting

*Fatsal 4 / Artikel 4* prohibits the felling, damaging, or destruction of *dennen* (pine-trees — the *Pinus merkusii* forests of the Gayo highlands) without the *schriftelijke vergunning* of the *Zelfbestuur*. The Gajo/Alas highlands contain substantial *Pinus merkusii* forests — the only tropical pine native to the Southern Hemisphere — and the regulation's protective regime for the *dennenbosschen* (pine-forests) is the earliest in the compilation to address the sustained management of a defined forest-type. *Fatsal 5 / Artikel 5* sets the penalty for any violation of the regulation at a maximum of one hundred guilders or three months' *hechtenis* (detention) — the standard maximum in the compilation — and adds, in *Artikel 5(2)*, the substitution-rule of *art. 30 wetboek van strafrecht* (art. 30 of the Penal Code): unpaid *boete* (fine) is converted to *hechtenis* within two months. This is the only regulation in the compilation to invoke the substitution-rule of art. 30 of the *Wetboek van Strafrecht*.

## Fire-prevention rationale

The fire-prevention rationale is implicit throughout the regulation. The burning of *alang-alang* on *woeste gronden* is the principal cause of uncontrolled grass-fires in the highlands: the dead grass, when dry, carries fire rapidly across the landscape, threatening the adjacent *bebouwde gronden*, the *dennenbosschen*, and the *oer-woud* (primeval forest) of the *afdeeling*. The mutual-aid duty of *Fatsal 3 / Artikel 3* and the *tong-tong* alarm-signaal are the practical instruments of fire-suppression at the village level. The permitting-system for *dennenbosschen* cutting addresses the complementary danger: indiscriminate felling of the highland pines opens the forest canopy, exposes the soil to desiccation, and accelerates the spread of *alang-alang* into the degraded forest. The regulation's combination of (a) a *vergunning*-system (permit-system) for *alang-alang* burning on *woeste gronden*, (b) a *vergunning*-system for *dennenbosschen* cutting, and (c) a mutual-aid fire-fighting duty on the *omwonenden* is the most integrated fire-prevention regime in the compilation.

## Significance

The *Zelfbestuursverordening van [[20 May]] [[1924]]* is the most elaborate of the fire-prevention verordeningen of the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Gouvernement]]. Its comparative table extends parallel regelingen (regulations) to [[Serbeudjadi]] (29-5-1924, *inwerking* 1-5-1925), Gajo LoeBus (28-5-1924, *inwerking* 6-6-1924), and [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]] (6-6-1924) — the smallest comparative table in the compilation, confined to the three other *onderafdeelingen* of the [[Gajo- en Alaslanden|afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden]]. The regulation's retroactivity-clause — *inwerking* (entry into force) on [[1 May]] [[1925]] but with the *strafbepalingen* running back to [[20 May]] [[1924]] — is the only such retroactivity-clause in the compilation, and reflects the practical necessity of an immediate sanction-regime during the eleven-month lag between the *vaststelling* (establishment) and the *inwerkingtreding*. The regulation was followed, four years later, by the [[Zelfbestuursverordening 21 Januari 1928 (Pidie alang-alang)|Pidie alang-alang regulation of 21 January 1928]] (Reg. No. 23), which extended the same protective regime to the twenty-six *landschappen* of the *afdeeling* [[Pidie]] and added the explicit *reboisatie* (reforestation) motive that is absent from the Takingeun regulation.
