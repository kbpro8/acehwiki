**De Buitenbezittingen 1904–1914** was a statistical and descriptive compilation of the *Buitengewesten* (Outer Territories) of the [[Netherlands|Netherlands-Indies]] issued by the former *Encyclopaedisch Bureau* in the years before its reorganisation. It is cited in Broersma's *[[Verkenningen in Atjeh (1923)|Verkenningen in Atjeh]]* as the source of the export figure for pepper from [[Atjeh]] in [[1913]] — *'ruim 3 millioen kilogram'* — which Broersma contrasts with the [[Lampoeng]] export of the same year, *'12½ millioen K.G. (200 duizend picol)'*, to demonstrate the collapse of Atjehnese pepper-growing since the [[1873]] level of 5–6 thousand *kojang* a year reported by [[J. A. Kruyt|J. A. Kruyt]].

## Use by Broersma
Broersma also draws on the same compilation for the Atjeh rice-import statistics of [[1904]]–[[1915]], when the gewest imported *'ruim 6 millioen kilogram rijst minimum tot meer dan 13 millioen kilogram maximum'* per year, evidence he uses to refute the easy assumption that Atjeh was naturally a great rice-land. The volume's framing of Atjeh as one *Buitengewest* among others supplies the comparative perspective from which Broersma's economic judgements proceed.

## Significance
The compilation is one of the standard reference works for the early civilian period of the Buitengewesten, and is regularly cited alongside [[Dr. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]]'s *Atjèh* ([[1922]]–[[1923]]) and the year-reports of the *Afdeeling Landbouw* for the reconstruction of pre-[[1914]] conditions in the Outer Islands.
