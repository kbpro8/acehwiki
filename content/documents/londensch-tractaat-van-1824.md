**Londensch tractaat van 1824** (1824) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Re-regulation of Dutch-English relations in the East.

## Date

[[1824]]

## Parties

[[Engeland (England)|England]] and [[Netherlands|the Netherlands]]

## Subject

Re-regulation of Dutch-English relations in the East. [[Leyden (Leiden)|Holland]] got supremacy over the whole island of Sumatra except Atjeh. Dutch had to: (1) ensure no foreign nuisance from Atjeh (piracy, molest); (2) not exercise political power over Atjeh. Could not organize a police service in Atjeh because it would undermine Atjeh's political independence. Created an untenable relationship.

## Significance

[[London|Londensch]] tractaat van [[1824]] is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
