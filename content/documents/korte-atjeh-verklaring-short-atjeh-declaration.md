**Korte Atjeh-verklaring (Short Atjeh Declaration)** (1898) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Three-article political contract: (1) recognition that the territory is part of Nederlandsch-Indië with loyalty to the Queen of the Netherlands, from whose hands the rule is accepted; (2) promise not to enter political relations with foreign powers, treating Netherlands' enemies as enemies and friends as friends; (3) promise to comply with and uphold all regulations issued by the Governor of Atjeh and Onderhoorigheden regarding the landschap.

## Subject

Three-article political contract: (1) recognition that the territory is part of [[Nederlandsch-Indië]] with loyalty to the Queen of [[Netherlands|the Netherlands]], from whose hands the rule is accepted; (2) promise not to enter political relations with foreign powers, treating [[Leyden (Leiden)|Netherlands]]' enemies as enemies and friends as friends; (3) promise to comply with and uphold all regulations issued by the Governor of Atjeh and [[Onderhoorigheden]] regarding the [[landschap]]. Later adopted with minor local modifications as the '[[Uniform model korte verklaring|uniform model korte verklaring]]' for most of [[Nederlandsch-Indië]].

## Significance

Korte Atjeh-verklaring (Short Atjeh Declaration) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
