**Bijdragen tot de kennis van het inlandsch bedrijf (1904)** — *Contributions to the Knowledge of the Native Enterprise* — is the more modest, but at least as useful, alternative to the 33-volume report of the [[Commissie tot onderzoek van de mindere welvaart (1902)|commissie tot onderzoek van de mindere welvaart]] that the [[Ministerie van Koloniën]] published in [[1904]] under the editorship of [[C. Th. van Deventer|Van Deventer]], [[D. Fock|Fock]] and [[Kielstra]]. The publication is, in [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]]'s retrospective, the type-specimen of the [[Ethische Politiek|ethical-policy]] *bescheiden* — modest — documentation that, by avoiding the over-reaching of the 33-volume report, succeeded in furnishing the colonial administration with usable economic-historical knowledge.

## The Van Deventer-Fock-Kielstra editorship

The *bijdragen* were edited by [[C. Th. van Deventer|Van Deventer]] (v.d., liberal-democrat MP and *ereschuld* advocate), [[D. Fock|Fock]] (liberal, minister of Colonies [[1908]]-[[1909]], later governor-general [[1921]]-[[1926]]) and [[Kielstra]]. The editorship is one of the cross-party collaborative acts of the [[Ethische Politiek|ethical-policy]] period: [[C. Th. van Deventer|Van Deventer]] the liberal-democrat, [[D. Fock|Fock]] the liberal, [[Kielstra]] the conservative.

## The contrast with the Mindere Welvaart commissie

The contrast drawn by [[D. Fock|Fock]] — and preserved by [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] — is between the 33-volume report of the [[Commissie tot onderzoek van de mindere welvaart (1902)|commissie tot onderzoek van de mindere welvaart]] ("naar het meer dan volmaakte gestreefd" — having aspired to more than perfection) and the more modest *bijdragen* of [[1904]] ("maar minstens zo nuttig" — but at least as useful). The contrast is, in the [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] retrospective, the type-specimen of the [[Ethische Politiek|ethical-policy]] preference for workable over perfect knowledge.

## Significance for Atjeh

The *bijdragen* of [[1904]] bear on Atjeh because the [[Atjeh-oorlog ([[1873]]-[[1904]])|Atjeh-oorlog]] was, in the [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] retrospective, one of the two principal causes of the Indies poverty that the *bijdragen* and the [[Commissie tot onderzoek van de mindere welvaart (1902)|mindere welvaart commissie]] were meant to investigate. The *bijdragen* accordingly furnished the [[Ethische Politiek|ethical-policy]] state — and the post-[[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacificatie]] administration of Atjeh — with the economic-historical frame within which the costs of the war and the subsequent *pacificatie* were to be understood.
