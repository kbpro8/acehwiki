**Het Atjeh-vraagstuk** (*The Atjeh Question*) was the series of articles published by [[W. Cool]] and [[G. B. Hooijer]] in the *Militaire Spectator* of [[1886]], in which the two veterans of the [[1879]] campaigns discussed at length the strategic and political management of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. Three years before their book *[[Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis (Cool & Hooijer, 1889)|Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis]]* appeared, the articles carried the argument that the book's final chapter invokes as established: that the removal of Generaal [[General van der Heijden|K. van der Heijden]] from his high position at Atjeh, and the subsequent splitting of military and civil authority in the barely subdued country, dismantled piece by piece the structure raised with so much toil, blood and treasure.

## Citations in the book of 1889

The authors refer to the series twice. In the celebrated panorama passage on the berg-plateau of [[14 August]] [[1879]], at the moment the column first overlooked the valley of [[Groot-Atjeh]] and the road to [[Selimoen]], the footnote directs the reader: "Zie onze artikelen in de Militaire Spectator 1886, Het Atjeh vraagstuk." And in the closing chapter, recording the loss of the fruits of years of struggle, streams of blood and millions of treasure after Van der Heijden's dismissal, they write: "we vermelden 't o. a. reeds uitvoerig in ons meer genoemd werk: 'het Atjeh-vraagstuk.'" In their bibliography the series thus functioned as the fuller statement of the polemic of which the book's epilogue — the lament over the "kunstmatig kale, vlakke terreinstrook van een geweerschotsbreedte, in de lage ongezonde delta te midden der lagunes" within which the troops were thereafter confined — is the condensed resume. The argument anticipates by seven years the pamphlet literature of [[1893]] — [[W. A. Coblijn]]'s *Hoe Atjeh te onderwerpen* and [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]'s *[[De onderwerping van Atjeh (Van Heutsz, 1893)|De onderwerping van Atjeh]]* — in which the same indictment was renewed.
