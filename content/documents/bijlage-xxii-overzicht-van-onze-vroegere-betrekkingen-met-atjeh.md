**Bijlage XXII — Overzicht van onze vroegere betrekkingen met Atjeh** — *Overzicht van onze vroegere betrekkingen met Atjeh en van den huidigen toestand in dat gewest* (Survey of our former relations with Atjeh and of the current state in that territory) — is the extensive survey of the history, geography, population, administration, and economy of the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Gouvernement van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]], preserved as the twenty-second annex (*Bijlage XXII*) of [[A. Kruisheer|Kruisheer]]'s *[[ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel]]* on pp. 234–304, with an accompanying *overzichtskaart* (overview map) at 1:2,000,000 scale. The survey — covering twenty-six subsections from the *Historie* of Dutch-Atjeh relations to the *Verschillende gegevens betreffende de jaren [[1893]]–[[1912]] voor Atjeh* (various data concerning the years [[1893]]–[[1912]] for Atjeh) — extends the volume's temporal horizon beyond the events of [[1896]] and embeds them in the longer trajectory of Dutch colonial administration in Atjeh down to the eve of the First World War.

## The Twenty-Six Subsections

The survey is organised in twenty-six subsections, covering: (1) *Historie* — the history of Dutch-Atjeh relations from the earliest contacts to [[1913]]; (2) *Het Atjehsche volkskarakter* — the Atjehsche national character; (3) *Grootte en grenzen* — the size and boundaries of the gewest; (4) *Bevolking* — the population; (5) *Kusten* — the coasts; (6) [interior geography]; (7) *Mijnbouw* — mining; (8) *Wegen* — roads; (9) *Tramweg* — the tramway; (10) *Handel* — trade; (11) *Kunst en nijverheid* — art and industry; (12) *Landbouw, Europeesche en inlandsche cultures* — agriculture, European and indigenous cultures; (13) *Veeëelt* — cattle-breeding; (14) *Het onderwijs* — education; (15) *De hoofden* — the chiefs; (16) *De rechtspleging* — the administration of justice; (17) *Belastingen* — taxation; (18) *Landschapskassen* — the landscape treasuries; (19) *Staatkundige en administrative indeeling* — the political and administrative division; (20) *De gewapende politie* — the armed police; (21) *Het leger* — the army; (22)–(25) *Grafische voorstellingen den handel betreffend* — graphical representations concerning trade; and (26) *Verschillende gegevens betreffende de jaren [[1893]]–[[1912]] voor Atjeh* — various data concerning the years [[1893]]–[[1912]] for Atjeh.

## Significance

Bijlage XXII is significant in the documentary record of *ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel* as the extensive survey of the *huidigen toestand* (current state) of the Gouvernement van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden in [[1913]] — the year of the volume's publication. The survey extends the volume's temporal horizon beyond the events of [[1896]] and embeds them in the longer trajectory of Dutch colonial administration in Atjeh down to the eve of the First World War. The twenty-six subsections of the survey — covering history, geography, population, administration, and economy — provide the reader with the broader context within which the operations of the second semester of [[1896]] were undertaken, and the *Verschillende gegevens betreffende de jaren [[1893]]–[[1912]]* provides the statistical basis for the assessment of the Dutch position in Atjeh over the two decades bracketing the events of [[1896]]. The survey's *overzichtskaart* (overview map) at 1:2,000,000 scale — the third of the three maps bound into the volume — provides the geographical framework within which the operations of the second semester may be located.
