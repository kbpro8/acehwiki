**Export statistics 1937** (1937) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Rubber 11,800,000 K.G.; palm oil 8,838,500 K.G.; palm kernels 1,636,500 K.G.; coffee 589,500 K.G.; fiber 4,050,000 K.G.; rice imports 5,900 tons; rice exports 1,400 tons.

## Date

[[1937]]

## Parties

Colonial government of Aceh

## Subject

Rubber 11,800,000 K.G.; [[Palm Oil (Aceh)|palm oil]] 8,838,500 K.G.; palm kernels 1,636,500 K.G.; coffee 589,500 K.G.; fiber 4,050,000 K.G.; rice imports 5,900 tons; rice exports 1,400 tons.

## Significance

Export statistics [[1937]] is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
