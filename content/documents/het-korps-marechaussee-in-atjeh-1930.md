**Het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh 1890 - 2 April 1930** was a special commemorative extra jubilee edition of the colonial newspaper *Het Nieuws van den Dag voor Nederlandsch-Indië*, published in Batavia on [[2 April]] [[1930]] to mark the 40th anniversary of the founding of the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]].

## Content and Structure

The 16-page publication comprises a comprehensive history of the Marechaussee in Aceh, including:
- An introduction detailing Queen Wilhelmina's royal decree granting a military banner (*vaandel*) to the corps.
- A chronological summary of campaigns from [[1890]] to [[1930]], written by editorial staff and military veterans.
- Personal interview profiles of high-ranking officers, notably Colonel [[Louis Adriaan Snell|L. A. Snell]] ("Old Hand").
- A detailed narrative memoir of jungle patrolling written by Reserve Lieutenant [[W. J. Mosselman]].
- Historical reflections on commanders such as [[Jonkheer G. J. W. C. Graafland]], [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz]], [[Gotfried Coenraad Ernst van Daalen]], and [[Hendrik Christoffel]].
- An official list of all decorations (Military William Order 3rd and 4th Class, Honorary Sabres, Oranje-Nassau) awarded to officers and NCOs between [[1897]] and [[1928]].

## Historical Value

This publication serves as one of the primary consolidated historical sources regarding tactical organization, operational rosters, casualties, personal anecdotes, and internal jargon of the Marechaussee during the Aceh War.
