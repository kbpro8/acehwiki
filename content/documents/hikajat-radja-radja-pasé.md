**Hikajat radja-radja Pasé** is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Acehnese historical text containing a legend that suggests the Gayo already bore the name 'Gayo' before their conversion to Islam — i.e., they did not adopt a new name with the new religion.

## Parties

edited by [[E. Dulaurier|Dulaurier]]

## Subject

Acehnese historical text containing a legend that suggests the [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gayo]] already bore the name '[[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gayo]]' before their conversion to [[Islam]] — i.e., they did not adopt a new name with the new religion.

## Significance

Hikajat radja-radja Pasé is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
