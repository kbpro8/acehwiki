**Commander's Journal of the Second Aceh Expedition** was the official military daily log and command journal kept by General [[J. van Swieten]] and his staff during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] against the [[Aceh Sultanate]] in [[1873]] and [[1874]], excerpts of which were officially published in the *Javasche Courant*.

## Publication and Documentation

Excerpts from the journal were published in official government organs, beginning with *Javasche Courant* No. 3 on [[9 January]] [[1874]]. The journal documented troop movements, casualty statistics, logistical arrangements, and summaries of intelligence received from indigenous informants such as [[Teuku Nek of Meuraxa]] and [[Teuku Lampasei]].

## Role in Historical Debates

In the [[1876]] study *Atjeh gedurende de tweede expeditie en later*, the author used the published text of the [[Commander's Journal of the Second Aceh Expedition]] to defend General [[J. van Swieten]] against the criticisms of "Brutus" ([[Open Letter by Brutus to General Knoop]]). 

By cross-referencing the journal's detailed daily entries with the abbreviated [[General van Swieten War Telegrams]], the author demonstrated that the command staff had acted upon solid, verified intelligence rather than unwarranted optimism after the capture of [[Kraton of Aceh]].
