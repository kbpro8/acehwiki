**Dutch declaration of war on Atjeh** (26 March 1873) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Declaration of war issued immediately after the failure of the early 1873 Dutch mission to Atjeh.

## Date

[[26 March]] [[1873]]

## Parties

[[Leyden (Leiden)|Netherlands]] vs. [[sultanate|Sultanate]] of Atjeh

## Subject

Declaration of war issued immediately after the failure of the early [[1873]] Dutch mission to Atjeh. Began the ~40-year Aceh War.

## Significance

Dutch declaration of war on Atjeh is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
