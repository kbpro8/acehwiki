**The Indische Comptabiliteitswet amendment (26 June 1903)** — the amendment of the [[Indische Comptabiliteitswet]] enacted on [[26 June]] [[1903]] as *Wet* S. 172 — is the law by which the *bijdrage* (annual contribution) of [[Nederlandsch-Indië]] to the finances of the [[Netherlands|Dutch]] realm was formally abolished. The amendment is one of the foundation acts of the [[Ethische Politiek|ethical-policy]] state and the legislative closure of the [[Batig Slot|batig-slot]] era.

## The proposal

The *wetsontwerp* amending the [[Indische Comptabiliteitswet]] so as to abolish the *bijdrage* of [[Nederlandsch-Indië]] to the finances of the realm was submitted by [[J. T. Cremer|Cremer]] — minister of Colonies in the [[kabinet Pierson]] — with the explanatory formula: "ten einde te doen vervallen de bepalingen omtrent de bijdrage van Nederlands-Indië aan de middelen van het Rijk" — in order to cause to lapse the provisions concerning the contribution of the Netherlands Indies to the means of the Realm.

## The legislative trajectory

The bill came into force only in the sittings-year [[1902]]/3, under [[J. T. Cremer|Cremer]]'s successor [[Jhr T. A. J. van Asch van Wijck|Van Asch van Wijck]] (a.r.). The amendment was finally enacted on [[26 June]] [[1903]] (S. 172). In the same year, the *bijdrage* of the West-Indies to the Realm was likewise abolished.

## The "ereschuld" refusal

Neither [[J. T. Cremer|Cremer]] nor [[Jhr T. A. J. van Asch van Wijck|Van Asch van Wijck]] would hear of an [[Ereschuld|ereschuld]] — debt of honour: "geen terugtrede in hetgeen door de daartoe volkomen bevoegde macht vroeger in wettige vorm is geschied" — no retreat from what was done in lawful form by the authority fully competent to do so. The existing state of affairs was to be the starting-point for financial policy; the past could only strengthen the conviction that, when the Indies were in need, the motherland must support them according to its ability. The ground of the new policy was, accordingly, not the recognition of an obligation to restitution but the recognition of a moral vocation (*zedelijke roeping*) of the motherland towards the colonies and of the significance of the colonial condition for the entire Realm.

## Significance for Atjeh

The [[Indische Comptabiliteitswet amendment (26 June 1903)|amendment of 26 June 1903]] bears directly on Atjeh because the [[Atjeh-oorlog ([[1873]]-[[1904]])|Atjeh-oorlog]] was one of the principal charges on the [[Nederlandsch-Indië|Nederlandsch-Indische]] budget that the *batig slot* had been used to defray. The abolition of the *bijdrage* — alongside the [[Ethische Politiek|ethical-policy]] proclamation of [[Troonrede of 1901|1901]] — re-framed the Atjehnese war as a charge to be borne by the Indies itself, with motherland subvention in case of need.
