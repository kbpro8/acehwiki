**Meeting of the Governor with the administrative officials at Koetaradja (1913)** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Koeta Radja]]

The [[Gouverneurswoning te Koetaradja|Governor's residence]] is illustrated in [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]]'s book, as are the [[Meeting of the Governor with the administrative officials at Koetaradja (1913)|1913 meeting of the Governor with his civil and military administrative officials]] and the *openbaar gehoor* (public audience) of [[31 August]] [[1912]].

### From [[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)]]

The appendices comprise the [[Letter of the Acehnese Chiefs to General Swart (26 September 1933)|letter of the Acehnese chiefs]] in Malay with Dutch translation, the [[Extract from the service record of H. N. A. Swart|extract from Swart's service record]], and the [[List of works consulted (General Swart)|list of works consulted]]. A [[Map of Atjeh (General Swart)|map of Atjeh]] is bound at the front, showing the Straat Malaka, the Malacca Passage, the north, east and west coasts of Atjeh, the Indian Ocean, and the islands of [[Simeuloeë|P. Simeuloeë (P. Simaloer)]] and the [[Banjak-eilanden]]. The volume is illustrated with portraits of Swart's principal collaborators and with photographs of the [[Gouverneurswoning te Koetaradja|Governor's residence at Koetaradja]], the [[Tjakra Donja|historic bell Tjakra Donja]], the laying of the first stone of the [[Javasche Bank te Koetaradja]] in [[1915]], the [[Meeting of the Governor with the administrative officials at Koetaradja (1913)|1913 meeting of the Governor with his civil and military administrative officials]], and Swart's journeys along the [[Gajòweg]].
