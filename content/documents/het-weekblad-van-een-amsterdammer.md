**The Weekly of an Amsterdammer** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[H. N. A. Swart]]

As a private citizen Swart returned to the Netherlands, settling at [[Den Haag]], but Atjeh retained his warm and continuous interest. When resistance broke out again on the [[Westkust van Atjeh|west coast]] of Atjeh in [[1925]], his opinion was sought in Holland and reported by the journalist [[J. Schaap]], formerly editor-in-chief of the *Java-Bode*, in *[[The Weekly of an Amsterdammer]]* of [[14 September|14]] and [[21 September]] [[1927]] — a statement du Croo called "Swart's Atjehsch testament".

### From [[1925]]

In **[[1925]]** resistance broke out again on the [[Westkust van Atjeh|west coast]] of Atjeh, seven years after Swart's retirement. Swart's opinion was sought in Holland and recorded by [[J. Schaap]] in *[[The Weekly of an Amsterdammer]]* — "Swart's Atjehsch testament". In [[1925]] [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]] was acting *controleur* of [[Meulaboh]], where he examined the *onderstand* entries and held his own keutelige inspections; he found roads, bridges and the *keudé* neglected. [[R. Broersma|Dr. R. Broersma]]'s *Atjeh als land voor handel en bedrijf* appeared in [[1925]].

### From [[1927]]

In **[[1927]]** the journalist [[J. Schaap]] published [[H. N. A. Swart]]'s judgement on the renewed resistance in Atjeh in *[[The Weekly of an Amsterdammer]]* of [[14 September|14]] and [[21 September]] [[1927]] — "it is, as it were, Swart's Acehnese testament". In 1927/[[1928]] Swart revisited Atjeh and spoke again with [[T. Tjhi' Moehammad Djohan Alamsjah|T. Tjhi' Peusangan]]; chiefs continued to send him telegrams and letters at New Year.
