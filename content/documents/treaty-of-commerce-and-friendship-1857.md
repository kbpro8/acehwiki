The **Treaty of Commerce and Friendship (1857)** was a bilateral agreement concluded in [[1857]] between Major-General [[Jan van Swieten]] (Governor of Sumatra's West Coast) and [[Sultan Alauddin Mahmud Syah IX|Sultan of Aceh]].

## Provisions

The treaty established peaceful commercial relations and mutual recognition, with both parties renouncing all prior territorial claims made before [[1857]]. Relations deteriorated in [[1858]] when the Netherlands concluded a treaty with the Sultan of Siak, prompting protests from the Sultan of Aceh, who claimed Siak as a vassal state.
