**Bijlage XVI — Verlieslijst Atjeh-[[1896]]** — the casualty list for the Dutch forces in Atjeh during [[1896]] — is preserved as the sixteenth annex (*Bijlage XVI*) of [[A. Kruisheer|Kruisheer]]'s *[[ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel]]* on pp. 212–218. The list records the names, ranks, and regimental numbers (*algemeen stamboeknummer*) of the Dutch officers, NCOs, and men killed, wounded, or missing in action during the operations of [[1896]], and is the principal primary source for the assessment of Dutch casualties in the second semester of [[1896]]. The list covers the operations of both the first semester (covered in Volume 1) and the second semester (covered in Volume 3) of [[1896]], and includes the casualties of the [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|surprise of Ana' Galoeng]] of [[29 June]] [[1896]] — the opening operation of the second semester.

## Significance

Bijlage XVI is significant in the documentary record of *ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel* as the principal primary source for the assessment of Dutch casualties in [[1896]]. The list — covering the operations of both the first and second semesters — records the human cost of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] in [[1896]] at the level of the individual soldier, and provides the basis for the assessment of the relative cost of the various operations of the year. The list includes the casualties of the [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|surprise of Ana' Galoeng]] of [[29 June]] [[1896]] — including the death of *Eur. serg. Verhey, No. 24109* and the wounding of Luit. Rijnen, Luit. Wagener, Luit. Dijkstra, and Kapitein Graafland — and the casualties of the [[Excursie to the IV Moekims (Kroeng-Raba) (7 July 1896)|excursie of 7 July 1896]] — including the wounding of *luit.-adj. Hoekstein en luit. Meerburg, beiden licht — voorts 3 Eur. fus. en 3 Eur. cavaleristen*. The list is the documentary record of the human cost of the Dutch *pacificatie* (pacification) of [[1896]] — the cost which Kruisheer's *Besluit* (Conclusion) would seek to justify with the claim that *men in [[1896]] den rechten weg tot pacificatie insloeg*.
