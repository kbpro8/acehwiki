**Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu (the book itself)** (1938-1939) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Dutch colonial-era ethnography/administrative description of Aceh.

## Date

[[1938]]-[[1939]]

## Parties

by [[J. Jongejans]], foreword by [[Dr. H. Colijn]], published by [[Hollandia Drukkerij N.V. Baarn]]

## Subject

Dutch colonial-era ethnography/administrative description of Aceh. Foreword by [[Dr. H. Colijn]]. Published by [[Hollandia Drukkerij N.V. Baarn]] (c. 1930s, latest year referenced is [[1938]]).

## Significance

Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu (the book itself) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
