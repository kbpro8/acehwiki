**Military Constabulary in Atjeh (1943)** was the military history of the *[[Corps of Military Constabulary of Atjeh and Dependencies|Korps Marechaussee]]* in [[Atjeh]] by [[H. J. Schmidt]] and [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], published in [[1943]] and repeatedly cited in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]* for episodes not narrated at length there.

Du Croo refers to it for the destruction of the [[Tirò Teungkoes]] by Schmidt with his brigades under [[Molier]] and [[Von Gilsa]] — "that struggle has already been described in another book as a classic example of the colonial "small war""; for the conversation of [[1902]] in a bivouac in the [[Meulaboh|Meulabohsche]] between [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|van Heutsz]], [[Christiaan Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] and a captain, in which van Heutsz named [[Gotfried Coenraad Ernst van Daalen|Van Daalen]] as his intended successor; for the suppression of the [[Resistance in Daja and Lhong (1914)|resistance in Daja and Lhong]] of [[1914]] (pp. 118 ff.); and for the [[Coconut and Sugar-Cane Excursion (April 1918)|coconut and sugar-cane excursion]] of April [[1918]] (pp. 124 ff.). Schmidt's recollections in that book also supplied du Croo with many of the "ontmoetingen" that characterise Swart.
