**Map: Residentie Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden** (not specified) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Map of the Residency of Atjeh and Dependencies, scale 1:1,000,000.

## Date

not specified

## Parties

publisher

## Subject

Map of the Residency of Atjeh and Dependencies, scale 1:1,000,000. Shows Strait of [[Malakka]], Atjeh residency, parts of Sumatra ([[Tapanoeli]]), etc.

## Significance

Map: Residentie Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
