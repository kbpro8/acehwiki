**[[Pel's plan for the Atjeh line|Pel's plan for the Atjeh line]]** was the secret strategic plan devised by [[Generaal Pel|generaal Pel]] to isolate [[Groot-Atjeh]] from the sea by a defensive line running from the mouth of the [[Kroeng Raba]] on the west coast to the mouth of the [[Kroeng Raya]] on the north coast, reducing Atjeh to a binnenstaat and finally ending the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. Outlined by Pel at the [[Pel's Buitenzorg planning session (1875)|Buitenzorg planning session]] of July [[1875]] and approved by [[Gouverneur-Generaal van Lansberge|gouverneur-generaal van Lansberge]] in the second half of [[1875]], the plan was never communicated officially — not even to Pel's chef van den staf — and nothing about it was found in his archive after his [[Death of Generaal Pel (24 February 1876)|sudden death]] on [[24 February]] [[1876]]. With Pel's death the plan was lost; the stelling from Kroeng Raba to [[Kwala Gigieng]] remained "nagenoeg onveranderd" under [[Generaal Wiggers van Kerchem|Wiggers van Kerchem]], and the closure of Atjeh from the sea — the strategic goal for which Pel had given his life — was never achieved.

## The Buitenzorg Planning Session

Pel outlined the plan to Borel himself and to the newly appointed gouverneur-generaal van Lansberge and the commandant van het leger at [[Buitenzorg]] in July [[1875]], while he was recovering his shattered health on three months' leave from Atjeh. The plan had several stages. First, as soon as the good moesson returned, resume offensives from the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]] around [[Kota Radja|K. Radja]]. Push the post at [[Soerian]] forward. Invade [[Toekoe Nanta|Toekoe Nanta]]'s [[VI Moekims]]. Occupy [[Sinangri]] and sweep the western bank of the [[Kwala Tjangkoel]]. Break the strong enemy line at Doewa. Seize kampongs and rijstvelden. Then, in the longer term, extend the linie from the mouth of the [[Kroeng Raba]] on the west coast to the mouth of the [[Kroeng Raya]] on the north coast, isolating Atjeh from the sea and reducing it to a binnenstaat. The gouverneur-generaal agreed and decided to "den oorlog met Atjeh op de meest doortastende wijze te voeren" (to wage the war with Atjeh in the most decisive manner), and promised ruime versterkingen. The plan was, Borel notes, approved in the second half of [[1875]] by the government — but Pel kept the details to himself.

## The Execution: December 1875 – February 1876

Pel returned to Atjeh on [[9 November]] [[1875]] and, with the reinforcements that arrived on [[10 December]] [[1875]] ([[Bataljon Barissans van Madura|Barissans]]) and [[21 December]] [[1875]] (9de bataljon), launched the great [[Pel's offensive against the VI, IV and IX Moekims (1875–1876)|offensive of December 1875 – February 1876]]. Three colonnes in verbund moved against the [[VI Moekims|VI]], [[IV Moekims|IV]] and [[IX Moekims|IX Moekims]] on [[26 December]] [[1875]]. [[Ketapang Doewa]] fell "andermaal en nu voorgoed" on [[27 December]] [[1875]]; [[Djempit]] and [[Pakan Badak]] on [[28 December]] [[1875]]. [[Lamtengah]] fell on [[8 January]] [[1876]]; the hoofd of [[Lampagger]] submitted on [[10 January]] [[1876]]. A colonne marched through the kloof of [[Blang Kalla]] on [[15 January]] [[1876]] southward into the [[IV Moekims]] and reached [[Loenga]] on the right bank of the Kroeng Raba mouth — the projected westelijke grens of the new afsluitingslinie. [[Boekit Daroe]] and the [[Glitaroem]] bergpas were taken on [[28 January]] [[1876]]. [[Lampermej]] and [[Oeleh Soesoe]] on [[31 January]] [[1876]]; [[Atoeh]] on [[3 February]] [[1876]]; [[Lamrong]] and [[Lamsajoen]] on [[4 February]] [[1876]]. On [[7 February]] [[1876]] the troepen returned to K. Radja after a ten-day tocht, having placed the entire [[XXV Moekims|Sagie of XXV Moekims]] in Dutch hands. The first part of Pel's plan was now executed.

## The Death of Pel and the Loss of the Plan

On [[13 February]] [[1876]] Pel advanced in oostelijke richting with three colonnes against the [[III Moekims|III Moekims]]; [[Pango]] was conquered, but the [[Atoeh catastrophe (13 February 1876)|Atoeh catastrophe]] the same day marred the success. On [[14 February|14]]–[[15 February]] [[1876]] [[Meroe]], [[Lampermej]] and [[Tjap Octoe]] were bemachtigd. On [[22 February]] [[1876]] Pel marched from Pango northward via [[Lamkapang]], [[Loeëng Iek]], [[Poeklat]] and [[Oeleh Karang]] to kampong [[Pinang (kampong)|Pinang]], which was found abandoned ("in den rug genomen"). On [[23 February]] [[1876]] [[Langgoegoeb]] was doorgetrokken and [[Tongah]] on the linker-oever of the [[Kroeng Tjoet]] was bereikt. At the bivouak at [[Lamjoeng]], on the far side of the river between Tongah and [[Silang]], Pel in the night of [[24 February]] [[1876]] suddenly overleed at the gevolgen of a slagaderbreuk (ruptured artery). "Het vaderland verloor in hem een zijner trouwste, ijverigste en meest bekwame dienaren; dat verlies was in de gegeven omstandigheden bijna onherstelbaar en kon eene ware nationale ramp genoemd worden." Pel's plan remained geheim — he had not communicated it even to his chef van den staf — and nothing about it was found in his archive. [[Luitenant-kolonel Engel|lt.-kol. Engel]] continued the march via [[Tjade]] to [[Kwala Gigieng]], bereikt on [[26 February]] [[1876]], and posts were opgericht at Kwala Gigieng and on the eiland [[Kota Pohama]] in the lagune, to seal the gemeenschap between sea and the mouth of the Kroeng Tjoet — but the western end of the line, from the Kroeng Raba, was never completed. The sea-expedition to the mouth of the Kroeng Raya, later undertaken under [[Generaal Diemont|generaal Diemont]]'s administration by [[Karel van der Heijden|kolonel van der Heijden]], led to "geene bevredigende uitkomst" and did not link up with the Tongah–Kwala Gigieng line. Pel's plan was therefore not fully executed, and the closure of Atjeh from the sea was never achieved.
