The **Besluit van den Civielen en Militairen Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden of [[26 April]] [[1911]] No. 2199** was the gubernatorial decision by which *[[Verjaring in Atjehsch adatrecht|verjaring]]* (prescription, limitation of actions) — an institution unknown to Atjehnese *[[Adatrecht|adatrecht]]* — was introduced by administrative act in [[Aceh|Atjeh]], fixing periods of fifteen and ten years for inheritance claims according to whether they were written or unwritten.

## Occasion

The decree was issued on the reports of two district officials, cited in its recitals: the missives of the *[[Assistent-Resident van Groot-Atjeh|Assistent Resident van Groot-Atjeh]]* of [[7 March]] [[1911]] No. 593/9, and of the *[[Assistent-Resident van de Noordkust|Assistent Resident van de Noordkust]]* of [[29 March]] of the same year No. 606/9.

## Recitals

The decision recited four grounds, which [[D. W. N. de Boer]] reproduced in full:

- "Overwegende dat de behandeling van oude schuldvorderingen door de Moesapat's in dit gewest zeer verschillend plaats vindt en geheel willekeurig dan eens wel, dan weer geen verjaring wordt toegepast" — that the treatment of old claims of debt by the *[[Moesapat|moesapat]]*'s in this *gewest* (region) took place very differently, and that prescription was applied quite arbitrarily, now yes, now no;
- that in the interest of a good administration of justice it was desirable to bring unity into the matter;
- "Overwegende dat ook de Islam onder zeker voorbehoud verjaring erkent" — that Islam too, subject to certain reservations, recognised prescription;
- that even before the Netherlands establishment, in many districts under the *[[Adat|adat]]* no right was done upon claims which the plaintiff had without valid reason omitted to bring for fifteen years;
- "Overwegende dat zoowel de Atjehsche hoofden als de mohammedaansche schriftgeleerden de wenschelijkheid en de noodzakelijkheid tot regeling dezer materie inzien" — that both the Atjehnese chiefs and the Mohammedan scholars perceived the desirability and the necessity of regulating this matter.

## Operative provision

"Heeft besloten: Te bepalen dat zullen verjaren: Alle erfenisvorderingen na een onafgebroken tijdsverloop van 15 of 10 jaren, al naar gelang deze beschreven of onbeschreven zijn" — has resolved: to determine that there shall be prescribed all inheritance claims after an uninterrupted lapse of fifteen or ten years, according as these are written or unwritten. The decision closed with the customary direction that copies be sent for information and guidance.

## Significance

The decree is an unusually explicit instance of the introduction of a legal institution into Atjehnese customary law by colonial administrative act. Its recitals are notable for grounding the innovation simultaneously in three authorities — the practical inconsistency of the *moesapat* courts, the recognition of prescription in Islam, and an asserted pre-colonial *adat* practice of refusing relief on claims neglected for fifteen years — and for citing the concurrence of both the Atjehnese chiefs and the Mohammedan *schriftgeleerden* (scholars of the written law). De Boer, himself a former chairman of Atjehnese *moesapats* and *landschapsgerechten*, placed the decree at the very end of his study as the last of the innovations affecting the Atjehnese law of property.
