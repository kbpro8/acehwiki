**Koloniaal Verslag 1908 (Colonial Report 1908)** is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Contains Gouverneur-Generaal Van Heutsz's requirements for the future administrator of Aceh (long quote about open eye/ear/heart for the population's interests, just rule, etc.).

## Subject

Contains [[Gouverneur-Generaal van Heutsz|Gouverneur-Generaal Van Heutsz]]'s requirements for the future administrator of Aceh (long quote about open eye/ear/heart for the population's interests, just rule, etc.).

## Significance

[[Koloniaal Verslag]] [[1908]] (Colonial Report [[1908]]) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
