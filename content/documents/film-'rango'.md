**Film 'Rango'** (early 1930s) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Film shot in the Sumatra jungle featuring Datock, the famous tiger hunter of Blang Rakal on the Gajoweg.

## Date

early 1930s

## Parties

film crew

## Subject

Film shot in the Sumatra jungle featuring [[Datock]], the famous tiger hunter of [[Blang Rakal]] on the [[Gajoweg (Gayo Road)|Gajoweg]]. A scene of a tiger fighting a [[karbouw]] (buffalo) was filmed at a spot by a bridge on the [[Gajoweg (Gayo Road)|Gajoweg]].

## Significance

Film 'Rango' is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
