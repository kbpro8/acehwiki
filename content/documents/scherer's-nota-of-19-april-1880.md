**Scherer's Nota of [[19 April]] [[1880]]** — the *Nota, gedagteekend: Kota-Radja, 19 April 1880* (memorandum, dated: Kota-Radja, 19 April 1880) submitted by [[Scherer]] — at the time *wd. assistent-resident* (acting assistant-resident) on the [[Noordkust]] of [[Atjeh]] — to the Dutch colonial government from [[Kota Radja|Kota-Radja]] on [[19 April]] [[1880]] — is, in [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]'s [[1893]] tract *[[De onderwerping van Atjeh (Van Heutsz, 1893)|De onderwerping van Atjeh]]*, the foundational document of the [[Scheepvaartregeling|scheepvaartregeling]] (shipping regulation) concept that Van Heutsz would adopt, twelve years later, as the central prescription of his policy-tract. The Nota was submitted at the high-water mark of the [[General van der Heijden|Van der Heijden]] governorship — seven months after the [[Day-order of 5 September 1879|day-order of 5 September 1879]] had declared "*geheel Groot-Atjeh ten onder gebragt*" (all Groot-Atjeh brought under), and in the same year that the General's own [[Van der Heijden's day-order of 5 September 1879|day-order]] had marked the close of the [[XXVI Moekims campaign (1879)|XXVI Moekims campaign]] — and articulated, for the first time in the Atjeh record, the principles of a controlled routing of all Atjehnese maritime trade through Dutch-administered ports, with duties levied on the in- and out-flow of goods, as the alternative to both the [[Blokkade|blokkade]] (blockade) and the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentratiestelsel]] (concentration system). Van Heutsz, who served as Van der Heijden's *chef van den staf* (chief of staff) in Atjeh and would later reproduce the Nota's principles verbatim in the [[1893]] tract, calls it a "*zeer belangrijke Nota*" — a very important memorandum — and treats it as the "*gewichtige antwoord op de vraag van Pel in zijn brief van 30 April 1875*" (weighty answer to the question of Pel in his letter of 30 April 1875): the weighty answer to the question [[Pel (governor)|Pel]] himself had posed in his [[Pel's letter of 30 April 1875|letter of 30 April 1875]] — what, after the completion of his plan, was to be done?

## The dateline: Kota-Radja, 19 April 1880

The Nota's dateline — *Kota-Radja, 19 April 1880* — fixes its provenance in the seat of the Atjeh government at the moment of [[General van der Heijden|Van der Heijden]]'s high-water mark. Van Heutsz's reference:

> "Hem was het echter eene flinke marinemacht over den toen noodigen vorm van bestuur, zoo helder en overtuigend uitgebracht in zijne, daarover aan de Regeering ingediende zeer belangrijke Nota, gedagteekend: Kota-Radja, 19 April 1880".

The Nota was submitted by Scherer — at the time *wd. assistent-resident* on the [[Noordkust]] of [[Atjeh]] — to the Regeering (Government) at [[Batavia]] as his formal exposition of the *scheepvaartregeling* concept. The choice of [[Kota Radja|Kota-Radja]] as the dateline — rather than Scherer's own *standplaats* (post) on the Noordkust — marks the Nota as a *Kota-Radja* document, submitted through the seat of the Atjeh government and addressed, by the *Gouverneur tevens militair bevelhebber* (Governor and at the same time military commander), to the Regeering at Batavia. The date — [[19 April]] [[1880]] — places the Nota seven months after the [[Day-order of 5 September 1879|day-order of 5 September 1879]] and two months after the *verslag* (report) of the Commandant van het Indische leger (Commandant of the Indian army)'s September [[1879]] inspection, and is the moment at which, in Van Heutsz's reading, the *scheepvaartregeling* concept was, for the first time, articulated as a formal colonial-policy proposal.

## The Nota's content: the scheepvaartregeling concept

The Nota's central proposal was the controlled routing of all Atjehnese maritime trade through Dutch-administered ports, with the in- and out-flow of goods placed under Dutch *contrôle* (supervision) and duties levied on the trade as the instrument of coercion over the *hoofden van de verschillende staatjes* (chiefs of the various statelets). Van Heutsz's formulation of the principle:

> "Geen in- noch uitvoer toelatende, dan op de plaatsen waar wij gevestigd waren en daar alles geplaatst onder onze contrôle, zouden wij het heft in handen krijgen, omdat wij dan, zooals thans reeds in meerdere brochures is gezegd, op de geldkist zouden zitten. Wij zouden daardoor in staat zijn zoowel tot beloonen als straffen, wij konden daardoor dwingen tot gehoorzaamheid, tot volgen van bevelen".

The *op de geldkist zitten* (sitting on the cash-box) formula — was Scherer's own phrase for the political-economic mechanism the *scheepvaartregeling* would establish: by routing all trade through Dutch-administered ports and levying duties on its in- and out-flow, the colony would acquire the *middel tot beloonen als straffen* (instrument of reward and punishment) and the *middel om de hoofden van de verschillende staatjes in het rechte spoor te houden* (instrument to keep the chiefs of the various statelets on the right track). The Nota thus proposed a *stelsel* (system) that would, in Van Heutsz's reading, complete what the military conquest of [[Groot-Atjeh]] had begun: "*ware het den Generaal van der Heijden gegeven geworden, dit op een juisten grondslag berustende stelsel uit te voeren, hij, die reeds over het brandpunt van het verzet winnaar was van Groot-Atjeh* … zou in korten tijd het geheele rijk den voet op den nek gezet, het eerst tot volkomen rust en vrede, daarna tot welvaart hebben gebracht" (had it been given to General van der Heijden to carry out this system, which rested on a just foundation, he, who was already the conqueror of Groot-Atjeh over the focus-point of the resistance … would in a short time have set his foot on the neck of the entire realm, would first have brought it to complete rest and peace, and then to prosperity).

## The Gighen–Samalanga kruisboot-bangoen method

The Nota's empirical foundation was the [[Gighen]]–[[Samalanga]] *kruisboot-bangoen* (cruiser blood-price) test of [[1880]] — the operation in which Scherer, having obtained Van der Heijden's *vergunning* (permission), laid a *kruisboot* (a marine patrol vessel) before the harbours of [[Gighen]] and [[Samalanga]] and collected the *bangoen* (the blood-price) owed for the murders of [[Said Abdellah]] and [[Soetan Maharadja]] by levying duties on the in- and out-going goods. Van Heutsz records the empirical test:

> "door het gebeurde te Gighen en te Samalanga, waar onze zendelingen Said Abdellah en Soetan Maharadja vermoord waren, en waar men telkens uitvluchten zocht in het betalen van den opgelegden bangoen (bloedprijs). Hij verzocht en verkreeg toen van den Generaal van der Heijden vergunning om vóór die havens een kruisboot te leggen en den bangoen te innen door rechten te heffen op de in- en uit te voeren goederen. Dit hielp; te Gighen betaalde men nog vóór dat de kruisboot er lag en te Samalanga, nadat de kruisboot er slechts drie dagen gelegen had".

The *kruisboot-bangoen* method — the laying of a *kruisboot* before a harbour and the levying of duties on its trade to coerce a chief into paying the *bangoen* — was, in Van Heutsz's reading, the empirical discovery that gave Scherer the *fingerwijzing* (pointer) on which the entire *scheepvaartregeling* rested:

> "Dat was voor Scherer eene vingerwijzing en bracht hem op het denkbeeld, dat als slechts alle in- en uitvoer langs onze vestigingen plaats had onder onze contrôle, wij ook het middel in de hand hadden om de hoofden van de verschillende staatjes in het rechte spoor te houden".

The Nota of [[19 April]] [[1880]] was the formal articulation of this *denkbeeld* (idea) — the *kruisboot-bangoen* test generalised into a colony-wide *stelsel* of controlled trade. The Nota's principle — that the *contrôle op handel en scheepvaart* (supervision over trade and shipping) would put the Dutch "*op de geldkist van Atjeh*" (on the cash-box of Atjeh) — became, in the [[1893]] tract, the central prescription of the policy-prescription half, and was the principle Van Heutsz would himself implement, in modified form, as [[Civiel en Militair Gouverneur|Gouverneur van Atjeh]] from [[1898]] onward.

## The alternative to both blokkade and concentratiestelsel

The Nota's *stelsel* was, in Scherer's reading and in Van Heutsz's, the alternative to both the [[Blokkade|blokkade]] — the *rigoureuse sluiting* (rigorous closure) of the entire coast — and the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentratiestelsel]] — the *geconcentreerde stelling* (concentrated defensive line) around [[Kota Radja]] that would be imposed by the [[Concentratiebesluit (19 Augustus 1884)|Concentratiebesluit of 19 Augustus 1884]]. The *blokkade* — "*een oorlogsmiddel, dat slechts tijdelijk kan worden toegepast*" (a means of war, that can only be applied temporarily) — was, in Scherer's argument, a measure of war that could not be sustained; the *concentratiestelsel* was, in Van Heutsz's later reading, a measure of weakness that invited Atjehnese contempt. The *scheepvaartregeling* — "*een maatregel des vredes van duurzamen aard*" (a measure of peace of a durable nature) — was the durable peace-measure that combined the coercion of the blokkade with the trade-routing of a fiscal regime:

> "Bovendien is de scheepvaartregeling een maatregel des vredes van duurzamen aard, terwijl blokkade een oorlogsmiddel is, dat slechts tijdelijk kan worden toegepast. Bij verkrijging van Atjeh's onderwerping door blokkade zou men dus om het heft, daardoor in handen gekregen, niet wederom te verliezen en dan wellicht van voren af aan te moeten beginnen, die blokkade ten slotte toch in eene scheepvaartregeling hebben moeten doen overgaan".

The argument — that even a successful blokkade would, eventually, have to be converted into a *scheepvaartregeling* — was the Nota's structural warrant for the *stelsel* it proposed. The Nota's principle would be applied, partially and imperfectly, by [[Laging Tobias]] in May [[1883]] and by [[Demmeni]] in October [[1884]]; the full application would await Van Heutsz's own governorship from [[1898]] onward.

## Van Heutsz's praise of the Nota

Van Heutsz's praise of the Nota is the highest he accords any policy document in the entire [[1893]] tract. The Nota is, in his reading, the long-deferred answer to [[Pel (governor)|Pel]]'s question of [[30 April]] [[1875]] — *het gewichtige antwoord op de vraag van Pel in zijn brief van 30 April 1875 wat, na voltooiing van zijn plan, moest gedaan worden in ons politiek en handelsbelang* (the weighty answer to the question of Pel in his letter of 30 April 1875, what, after the completion of his plan, had to be done in our political and commercial interest) — and the long-deferred revision of [[Van Swieten's Instructie (22 April 1874)|article 5 of Van Swieten's Instructie]] — *dit geeft ook de wijziging aan, welke art. 5 van de Instructie van Generaal van Swieten moest ondergaan* (this also gives the modification which art. 5 of the Instruction of General van Swieten had to undergo). The Nota is "*zeer belangrijk*" — very important — and its author is the man "*uit wiens helder brein het eerst het denkbeeld van eene rationeele regeling voor handel en scheepvaart in verband met onze vestigingen op de kusten, is gesproten*" — from whose clear mind the idea of a rational regulation for trade and shipping in connection with our establishments on the coasts first sprang. The Nota's *stelsel* was, however, never applied in its original form. [[General van der Heijden|Generaal van der Heijden]] — the only governor Van Heutsz credits with the *krachtige individualiteit* (vigorous individuality) to carry the *stelsel* through — was dismissed "*in het begin van 1881 op onverantwoordelijke wijze*" (in the beginning of 1881 in an irresponsible manner), and the Nota's principle was, for the next decade, deferred. The first partial application came in May [[1883]] under [[Laging Tobias]] — "*de scheepvaartregeling door Scherer voorgesteld en door Laging Tobias ingevoerd gaf alles wat men kon verlangen*" (the scheepvaartregeling proposed by Scherer and introduced by Laging Tobias gave everything one could desire) — and was then abolished, with [[Demmeni]]'s hybrid *besluit* (decree) of [[13 October]] [[1884]] and the [[Concentratiebesluit (19 Augustus 1884)|Concentratiebesluit of 19 Augustus 1884]], in the colony's two great acts of self-betrayal.

## Significance

Scherer's Nota of [[19 April]] [[1880]] is, in Van Heutsz's narrative, the foundational document of the [[Scheepvaartregeling|scheepvaartregeling]] concept — the controlled routing of all Atjehnese maritime trade through Dutch-administered ports with duties levied on the in- and out-flow of goods — that Van Heutsz would adopt, twelve years later, as the central prescription of his [[1893]] tract. Its significance in the Aceh Wiki History record lies in four things. First, it was the first formal articulation of the *scheepvaartregeling* concept — the *stelsel* that, in Van Heutsz's reading, was the long-deferred answer to [[Pel (governor)|Pel]]'s question of [[30 April]] [[1875]] and the long-deferred revision of [[Van Swieten's Instructie (22 April 1874)|article 5 of Van Swieten's Instructie]]. Second, its empirical foundation was the [[Gighen]]–[[Samalanga]] *kruisboot-bangoen* test of [[1880]] — the operation in which Scherer, having obtained Van der Heijden's *vergunning*, laid a *kruisboot* before the harbours and collected the *bangoen* by levying duties on the trade — that gave Scherer the *fingerwijzing* on which the entire *scheepvaartregeling* rested. Third, its author was the man Van Heutsz identifies as the only colonial official who could have advised [[General van der Heijden|Generaal van der Heijden]] how to construct the *scheepvaartregeling* — and whose resignation in early October [[1884]], when [[Demmeni]] only half-applied his scheme, was the moment Van Heutsz marks as the colony's loss of the only man who could have advised the General. Fourth, its principle — that the *contrôle op handel en scheepvaart* would put the Dutch "*op de geldkist van Atjeh*" — was the principle Van Heutsz would himself implement, in modified form, as [[Civiel en Militair Gouverneur|Gouverneur van Atjeh]] from [[1898]] onward, and the principle on which the *pacification* of Atjeh would finally be accomplished.
