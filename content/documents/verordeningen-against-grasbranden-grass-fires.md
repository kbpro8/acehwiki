**Verordeningen against grasbranden (grass fires)** (undated (early 20th c.)) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Ordinances making the causing of grass fires on blang-terreinen punishable, to protect Pinus-complexen and prevent further deforestation.

## Date

undated (early 20th c.)

## Parties

Colonial government

## Subject

Ordinances making the causing of grass fires on [[Blang|blang]]-terreinen punishable, to protect Pinus-complexen and prevent further deforestation.

## Significance

Verordeningen against grasbranden (grass fires) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
