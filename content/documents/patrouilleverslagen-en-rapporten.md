**Patrouilleverslagen en Rapporten** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Maurice Henri du Croo]]

Du Croo built the biography on interviews with Swart himself, then seventy-nine years old, whose spoken recollections are quoted at length throughout; on the collaboration and memories of [[H. J. Schmidt]]; on official documents including the *[[Koloniale Verslagen]]*, the *[[Monthly Reports of the Government of Atjeh and Dependencies|Maandverslagen]]*, *[[Patrouilleverslagen en Rapporten]]* and the [[Extract from the service record of H. N. A. Swart|extract from Swart's service record]]; and on a published literature listed in the appendix *[[List of works consulted (General Swart)|Geraadpleegde boeken, brochures, artikelen, enz.]]*.
