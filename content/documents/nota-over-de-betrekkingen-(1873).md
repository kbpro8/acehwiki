**The Nota over de betrekkingen** — full title *Nota over de betrekkingen tusschen Nederland en Atjèh van 1824 tot 1873* — is the official Dutch government memorandum on the relations between the Netherlands and [[Acheh|Atjèh]] from [[1824]] to [[1873]], prepared for the States-General in [[1873]] and published in the *Officieele bescheiden betreffende het ontstaan van den oorlog tegen Atjèh in 1873* (Regeeringsuitgave, [[1881]]). It is, in [[J. Kreemer|Kreemer]]'s history, the principal official source for the causes of the [[Aceh War|Atjèh-oorlog]], and is cited throughout his account of the [[1824]]–[[1873]] period.

## Kreemer's use of the Nota

Kreemer refers to the Nota repeatedly in his account of the pre-war period, citing it by page for the principal incidents of the half-century of Dutch–Atjèhnese relations: the [[1831]] plundering of an American ship at [[Kuala Bathee|Koeala Batèë]] (Nota p. 10); the [[Schooner Dolphijn incident (1836)|1836 Dolphijn affair]] (Nota p. 13); the [[1844]] plundering of British vessels (Nota p. 14); the [[1851]]–[[1852]] plunderings (Nota p. 14); the [[De Haai flag visit (1855)|1855 De Haai visit]] (Nota p. 16); the [[Treaty of 1857 with the Sultan of Atjèh|1857 treaty]] (Nota p. 19); the [[1860]] seizure of Dutch ships (Nota p. 21); the [[1868]] affairs (Nota p. 27–28); and the [[1872]]–[[1873]] pre-war diplomacy (Nota p. 33, 36).

## The companion volume

The Nota was published as part of the *Officieele bescheiden betreffende het ontstaan van den oorlog tegen Atjèh in 1873* — a Regeeringsuitgave (government publication) of [[1881]] — which collected the official correspondence and documents relating to the outbreak of the war. Kreemer cites this volume, alongside [[E. S. de Klerck|De Klerck]]'s *De Atjèh-oorlog* dl. I ([[1912]]) and [[E. B. Kielstra|Kielstra]]'s *Beschrijving van den Atjèh-oorlog* dl. I ([[1883]]), as the principal authority for the pre-war period.

## Significance

The Nota is, in Kreemer's narrative, the official Dutch account of the breakdown of the [[Anglo-Dutch Treaty of 1824|1824 treaty]] framework — the documentary record on which the Dutch government's decision to declare war on [[26 March]] [[1873]] was based. Its publication in [[1881]] — eight years after the war's outbreak — was itself a political act, intended to establish before the States-General and the public that the war had been forced upon the Netherlands by the progressive failure of the treaty framework and by the Sultan's approaches to foreign powers.
