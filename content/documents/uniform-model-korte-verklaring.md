**Uniform model korte verklaring** (after 1898) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Uniform version of the korte Atjeh-verklaring applied to most of the rest of Nederlandsch-Indië, with minor local modifications.

## Date

after [[1898]]

## Parties

Dutch government ↔ zelfbestuurders throughout [[Nederlandsch-Indië]]

## Subject

Uniform version of the [[korte Atjeh-verklaring]] applied to most of the rest of [[Nederlandsch-Indië]], with minor local modifications.

## Significance

Uniform model korte verklaring is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
