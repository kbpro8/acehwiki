**Nederlanders over de Zeeën: 350 Jaar geschiedenis van Nederland buitengaats** — *Dutch Across the Seas: 350 Years of Netherlands Maritime History* — is the [[1955]] second wholly revised edition of the collaborative historical work edited by [[H. J. de Graaf|Dr. H. J. de Graaf]], with the cooperation of [[G. Gonggrijp|Prof. G. Gonggrijp]], [[W. R. Menkman|W. R. Menkman]] and [[P. J. van Winter|Prof. Jhr Dr P. J. van Winter]], published in Utrecht by W. de Haan N.V. (MCMLV). The [[1955]] edition is the source of the present Aceh Wiki History articles derived from the book.

## The seven chapters

The [[1955]] edition of *Nederlanders over de Zeeën* comprises seven chapters covering the 350-year history of Dutch maritime enterprise from the late sixteenth century to the Indonesian revolution:

- **Chapter I**: [[W. R. Menkman|W. R. Menkman]], *Nederland in Amerika en West-Afrika* (The Netherlands in America and West Africa), p. 9.
- **Chapter II**: [[H. J. de Graaf|Dr. H. J. de Graaf]], *De Nederlanders in Oost-Azië* (The Dutch in East Asia), pp. 82-142 — comprising:
  - II.1 *Azië voor de komst der Nederlanders* (Asia before the coming of the Dutch), p. 82
  - II.2 *De Geschiedenis der Nederlandse Oost-Indische Compagnie: Algemeen overzicht* (History of the VOC: General survey), p. 84
  - II.3 *De Compagnie in het Oosten van de Archipel* (The Company in the East of the Archipelago), p. 98
  - II.4 *De Compagnie op Java* (The Company on Java), p. 105
  - II.5 *De Compagnie in het Westen van de Archipel* (The Company in the West of the Archipelago), p. 128
  - II.6 *De Compagnie in Azië buiten de Archipel* (The Company in Asia outside the Archipelago), p. 132
- **Chapter III**: [[H. J. de Graaf|Dr. H. J. de Graaf]], *De overgang van Compagnie tot Gouvernement* (The transition from Company to Government), p. 143.
- **Chapter IV**: [[G. Gonggrijp|Prof. G. Gonggrijp]], *Ned.-Indië onder de Staat en als deel van het Koninkrijk* (Netherlands India under the State and as part of the Kingdom), p. 156.
- **Chapter V**: [[H. J. de Graaf|Dr. H. J. de Graaf]], *De oorlog met Japan en de Japanse bezetting van Nederlands-Indië* (The war with Japan and the Japanese occupation of the Netherlands Indies), p. 208.
- **Chapter VI**: [[H. J. de Graaf|Dr. H. J. de Graaf]], *Nederlands-Indië na de Japanse overgave* (The Netherlands Indies after the Japanese surrender), p. 217.
- **Chapter VII**: [[P. J. van Winter|Prof. Jhr Dr P. J. van Winter]], *Zuid-Afrika in de Hollandse tijd* (South Africa in the Dutch period), p. 227.

The volume closes with a *Literatuurlijst* (bibliography, p. 252) and a *Register* (index, p. 256).

## The Atjeh-relevant content

The Atjeh-relevant content of the [[1955]] edition is concentrated in:

- The introductory survey of pre-Company Asia (Chapter II.1, pp. 99-100), which situates sixteenth-century [[Acheh|Atjeh]] in the wider context of Muslim-state formation in the archipelago.
- The chapter on the VOC in the western part of the archipelago (Chapter II.5, pp. 154-159), which covers Atjeh's trade with [[Gujerat|Gujerat]] and [[Suratte|Suratte]], the elephant-fight illustration of [[1613]], and the VOC's conflict with Atjeh on Sumatra's Westkust.
- The chapter on the Indies under the State (Chapter IV, by [[G. Gonggrijp|Gonggrijp]], pp. 197, 205, 209, 216-219), which covers the [[Anglo-Dutch Treaty of 1824|1824 London Treaty]], the [[1857]] contract, the [[Sumatra-tractaat (1871)|1871 Sumatra-tractaat]], the [[Atjehoorlog|Atjehoorlog]] of [[1873]]-[[1904]], and the post-[[1904]] consolidation.
- The chapter on the ethical-policy period (Chapter IV, p. 238), which covers the post-Atjeh establishment of effective Dutch authority.
- The chapter on the Indonesian revolution (Chapter VI, p. 267), which records the [[1948]] Atjeh exemption.

## Citation

The standard bibliographic citation of the [[1955]] second edition is:

> *Nederlanders over de Zeeën: 350 Jaar geschiedenis van Nederland buitengaats*, onder redactie van Dr. H. J. de Graaf, met medewerking van Prof. G. Gonggrijp, W. R. Menkman en Prof. Jhr Dr P. J. van Winter, 2e geheel herziene druk (Utrecht: W. de Haan N.V., MCMLV).

## Significance

The [[Nederlanders over de Zeeën (De Graaf et al., 1955)|1955 Nederlanders over de Zeeën]] is the source of the present Aceh Wiki History articles on the pre-Company Indies, the VOC period, the colonial state, the Japanese occupation, and the Indonesian revolution. Every article in the present collection that cites *Nederlanders over de Zeeën* refers to the [[1955]] second wholly revised edition.
