**Contract with Siak Sri Indrapoera and onderhoorigheden** (21 February 1858) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Reference contract on which the political goal of the second Aceh expedition's agreement with the Sultan was to be based.

## Date

[[21 February]] [[1858]]

## Parties

[[Leyden (Leiden)|Netherlands]] and [[Siak]]

## Subject

Reference contract on which the political goal of the [[Second Aceh Expedition|second Aceh expedition]]'s agreement with [[Sultan of Aceh|the Sultan]] was to be based.

## Significance

Contract with Siak Sri Indrapoera and onderhoorigheden is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
