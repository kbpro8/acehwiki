**Register van Bendehoofden** was the thick folio register of band-heads kept by the Civil and Military Governor of [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]].

At the first interview between [[H. N. A. Swart]] and [[H. J. Schmidt]] in June [[1908]], Swart "seized a thick folio — and on that folio was written *Register of Band-Leaders* — and he searched and found [[Keudjroeën Oebit]], and asked: "do you mean that one?"" When Schmidt found Keudjroeën Oebit in the [[Tangsé]] valley in February [[1909]], within the three months he had promised, he was able to strike him from the register — "the first piece of work which this long-serving collaborator of Swart's, his "first tracker," delivered to the new Governor."
