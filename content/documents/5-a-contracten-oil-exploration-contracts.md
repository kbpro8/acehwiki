**5 A contracten (oil exploration contracts)** (from 1928) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Replacement of new oil concessions.

## Date

from [[1928]]

## Parties

[[Indische Regeering]] vs. [[N.V. de Bataafsche Petroleum Mij. (BPM)]] and [[N.V. Nederlandsche Koloniale Mij. (NKPM)]]

## Subject

Replacement of new oil concessions. BPM received 5 areas: Oost-Atjeh-blok, [[Tamiang]]-blok, Noord-Atjeh-blok, [[Djambo Ajé]]-blok, Atjeh-complex. [[N.K.P.M.|NKPM]] received 3 areas: [[Pasai|Pasé]], [[Rajeu]], [[Langsar|Langsa]].

## Significance

5 A contracten (oil exploration contracts) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
