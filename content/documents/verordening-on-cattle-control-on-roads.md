**Verordening on cattle control on roads** (recent (before 1938)) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Recently issued ordonnance engaging zelfbestuurders more than before in controlling free-roaming cattle on Atjeh's roads - appears to have some effect.

## Date

recent (before [[1938]])

## Parties

colonial government

## Subject

Recently issued ordonnance engaging zelfbestuurders more than before in controlling free-roaming cattle on Atjeh's roads - appears to have some effect. Various other measures under successive heads had no significant success.

## Significance

Verordening on cattle control on roads is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
