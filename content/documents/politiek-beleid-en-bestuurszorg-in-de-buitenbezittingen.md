**Political Policy and Administrative Care in the Outer Possessions** was the memorandum by [[Hendrikus Colijn]] on political policy and administrative care in the *buitenbezittingen*, named by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]] with the *[[Korte Verklaring]]* as Colijn's chief contribution.

Colijn was "the father of the Short Declaration and of the memorandum Political Policy and Administrative Care in the Outer Possessions, the right hand of Van Heutsz." The memorandum is listed among the works consulted for *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart]]*.
