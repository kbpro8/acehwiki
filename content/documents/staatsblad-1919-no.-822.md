**Staatsblad 1919 No. 822** — the *zelfbestuursordonnantie* (self-government ordinance) or *Zelfbestuursregelen 1919* — was the Netherlands-Indies statute whose article 15 sub I abolished the sovereign land levies of the Atjehnese *[[Oeleebalang|oeléëbalangs]]* and substituted for them a fixed *[[Civiele lijst|civiele lijst]]*.

## Abolition of the sovereign levies

[[D. W. N. de Boer]] recorded that the older taxes levied by the *oeléëbalangs* as sovereigns — the *[[Boeët oemòng|boeët oemòng]]* within the *[[Gampong|gampong]]* boundaries, and outside them the *[[Adat glé|adat glé]]* with its three heads (the like levy on wet fields, the *[[Padi lintjah|padi lintjah]]* on dry fields, and the *[[Thiploeëh-tha|thiploeëh-tha]]* tithe on gardens) — all of which left much room for arbitrariness, were brought to an end by article 15 sub I of the ordinance, "den oeléëbalang als recompensatie 'n vaste, voor elk hunner afzonderlijk berekende, *civiele lijst* toebedeeld" — the *oeléëbalang* being allotted by way of compensation a fixed civil list calculated separately for each of them.

## Restriction on alienation to non-indigenous persons

De Boer further cited article 15 sub I in conjunction with article 29 sub 3 of the *Zelfbestuursregelen* for the proposition that alienation of land to "niet tot de Inheemsche bevolking behoorende personen" — persons not belonging to the indigenous population — had since been taken in hand by the Gouvernement, referring also to *[[Politiek Beleid en Bestuurszorg in de Buitenbezittingen|Politiek Beleid]]*, pp. 111 ff. and 233 ff.

## Significance

The ordinance marks the fiscal turning point in the Atjehnese law of land: the territorial chief ceased to be a taxing sovereign over cultivation and became a salaried self-governing ruler, while retaining his share of concession revenues under [[Staatsblad 1919 No. 61 jo No. 281]] and his export duties on produce.
