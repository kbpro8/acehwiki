**Raffles-tractaat (contract)** (22 April 1819) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Contract intended to bind Aceh entirely to England and prevent settlement by third parties; automatically abrogated by the Londensche tractaat of 17 March 1824.

## Date

[[22 April]] [[1819]]

## Parties

[[Raffles]] ([[Engeland (England)|England]]) and Aceh

## Subject

Contract intended to bind Aceh entirely to [[Engeland (England)|England]] and prevent settlement by third parties; automatically abrogated by the [[London|Londensche]] tractaat of [[17 March]] [[1824]].

## Significance

Raffles-tractaat (contract) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
