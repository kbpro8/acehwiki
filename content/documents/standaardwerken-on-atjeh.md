**Standaardwerken on Atjeh** (various) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Standard scholarly works on Atjeh, consulted by Jongejans (with personal practice as test).

## Date

various

## Parties

[[Christiaan Snouck Hurgronje|Dr. Snouck Hurgronje]], [[Mr. van Vollenhoven]], [[Jacobs]], [[van Langen]], [[J. Kreemer|Kreemer]]

## Subject

Standard scholarly works on Atjeh, consulted by [[Resident Jongejans|Jongejans]] (with personal practice as test). Too scientific and too expensive for the general public.

## Significance

Standaardwerken on Atjeh is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
