**Kleinere werkjes on Atjeh** (various) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Smaller works on Atjeh consulted by Jongejans.

## Date

various

## Parties

[[Broersma]], [[J. Langhout|Langhout]]

## Subject

Smaller works on Atjeh consulted by [[Resident Jongejans|Jongejans]].

## Significance

Kleinere werkjes on Atjeh is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
