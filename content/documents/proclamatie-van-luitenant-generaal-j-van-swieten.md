**Proclamatie van Luitenant-Generaal J. van Swieten** (31 March 1874) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Proclamation establishing Dutch authority in the conquered territory of Groot-Atjeh.

## Date

[[31 March]] [[1874]]

## Parties

Dutch colonial government

## Subject

Proclamation establishing Dutch authority in the conquered territory of [[Groot-Atjeh]]. Followed by [[Teukoe Bintara Keumangan]]'s conditional submission (demanding rule remain in his family).

## Significance

[[Van Swieten's proclamation|Proclamatie van Luitenant-Generaal J. van Swieten]] is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
