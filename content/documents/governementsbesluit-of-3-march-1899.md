**Governementsbesluit of 3 March 1899** was a executive decree enacted by the Netherlands East Indies colonial government reorganizing and substantially expanding the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]].

## Key Provisions

- Expanded the total force strength to 20 officers and 1,212 enlisted men.
- Divided the force into 5 tactical divisions, each comprising 3 sections (*afdeelingen*), subdivided into 4 mobile brigades.
- Placed overall military management under the local military commander at [[Kuta Raja]].
- Assigned division commanders dual civil administrative powers as civil administrators (*civiel-gezaghebber*).
- Allocated f 10,000 annually at the personal disposal of the Governor of Aceh to reward individual initiative and extraordinary brave acts by Marechaussee soldiers.

## Strategic Impact

This decree transformed the Marechaussee into a permanent regional pacification army stationed across major posts in [[Seulimeum]], [[Indrapuri]], [[Cot Mentjang]], [[Cot Guè]], [[Lhoknga]], and [[Lambaro]].
