**Plan tot bestuursregeling voor de verschillende streken (incl. Laut Tawar)** is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Plan for administrative arrangement of various Gajo regions including Laut Tawar, drawn up after the 163-day Gajo-tocht.

## Subject

Plan for administrative arrangement of various [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]] regions including [[Laut Tawar]], drawn up after the 163-day [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]]-tocht.

## Significance

Plan tot bestuursregeling voor de verschillende streken (incl. Laut Tawar) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
