**maleisch naam-wichelboekje** is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Malay name-divination booklet used to choose a newborn's name, typically relating to the weekday and month of birth.

## Subject

Malay name-divination booklet used to choose a newborn's name, typically relating to the weekday and month of birth.

## Significance

maleisch naam-wichelboekje is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
