**Telegraphic price reports from Penang Consulate-General** (circa mid-1930s onward) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Telegraphic reports on product prices in Penang, created to break Chinese middlemen's excessive profits (which were due to population's ignorance of Penang wholesale prices).

## Date

circa mid-1930s onward

## Parties

[[Consulaat-Generaal (Penang)]] → Gewestelijk Bestuurshoofd of Aceh

## Subject

Telegraphic reports on product prices in [[Penang]], created to break Chinese middlemen's excessive profits (which were due to population's ignorance of [[Penang]] wholesale prices). Enabled Hoofden and [[Atjehers]] to ship products directly to [[Penang]] when Chinese tried to depress prices.

## Significance

Telegraphic price reports from Penang Consulate-General is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
