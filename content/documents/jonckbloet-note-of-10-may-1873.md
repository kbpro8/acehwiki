**Jonckbloet Note of 10 May 1873** was an influential colonial policy memorandum authored by Dutch politician and commentator [[W. J. A. Jonckbloet]] on [[10 May]] [[1873]], published as Annex A to the Parliamentary Report on the East Indies Budget extension for [[1873]].

## Content and Critique of the First Expedition

Written immediately following the retreat of the [[First Aceh Expedition]] from [[Kraton of Aceh]], the [[Jonckbloet Note of 10 May 1873]] provided a scathing critique of the assumptions underlying the spring [[1873]] campaign. The note famously observed that the [[Dutch East Indies Government]] had attempted to conquer the [[Aceh Sultanate]] "with a coup de théâtre," expecting immediate capitulation without preparing for serious Acehnese resistance.

## Historiographical Significance

General [[G. G. Booms]] prominently cited the [[Jonckbloet Note of 10 May 1873]] in his [[1875]] defense of army commanders General [[J. H. R. Köhler]] and General [[E. C. van Daalen]]. By quoting [[W. J. A. Jonckbloet]], Booms proved that contemporary observers in May [[1873]] recognized that the primary causes of the expedition's failure lay in defective governmental planning, poor intelligence, and overhasty timing rather than tactical mistakes by the military leadership.
