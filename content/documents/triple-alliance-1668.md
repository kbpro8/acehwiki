**Triple Alliance (1668)** was the diplomatic pact signed in **[[1668]]** between the **[[United Provinces (Dutch Republic)]]**, **[[England|England]]**, and **[[Sweden|Sweden]]**, negotiated by **[[Johan de Witt]]**, **[[William Temple]]**, and **[[Graf von Dohna]]**. The alliance aimed to check the expansionist policies of **[[Louis XIV]]** and to preserve the balance of power in Europe, indirectly safeguarding Dutch maritime trade with the **[[Sultanate of Aceh]]**.

## Provisions and Significance
The treaty stipulated mutual defence against French aggression and coordinated naval patrols, which helped maintain Dutch control over sea lanes used by **VOC** ships en route to Aceh.
