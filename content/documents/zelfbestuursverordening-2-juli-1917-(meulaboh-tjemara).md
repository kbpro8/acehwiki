**The Zelfbestuursverordening of [[2 July]] [[1917]] (Meulaboh tjemara)** — printed in the [[1930]] compilation of *Zelfbestuursverordeningen Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden* as Regulation No. 19 — is the *tjemaraboomen*-verordening (casuarina-tree ordinance) of the *onderafdeeling* (subdivision) [[Meulaboh]] in the [[Westkust van Atjeh|afdeeling Westkust van Atjeh]] (district West Coast of Atjeh), issued by the ten individually-named *oeleebalang* (territorial chiefs) of the *onderafdeeling*, regulating the cutting (*kappen*) of *tjemaraboomen* (casuarina trees, *Casuarina equisetifolia*) along the *strandwegen* (coastal roads) of the *afdeeling* (district). Approved by the *Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden* (Governor of Atjeh and Dependencies) on the same date, it took effect immediately — without the typical *inwerkingtreding*-delay (entry-into-force delay) of one or more months — and is the only regulation in the compilation whose signatory-clause names individual *oeleebalang* in sufficient number to form a ten-person collective.

## The ten named oelebalang signatories

The signatory-clause of Reg. No. 19 opens "*Kami kepala Pemerintah dalam negeri jang terseboet jaitoe*" — "We, the heads of government in the named *landschappen* (territories), namely" — and proceeds to list, in both the Malay and the Dutch columns, the ten individually-named *oeleebalang* of the *onderafdeeling* Meulaboh:

- **T. Mohamad Oesensjah** of Wojla / Kendjroeen Rajen' — "*T. Mohamad Oesensjah dari Wojla*" / "*T. Mohamad Oesensjah van Wojla Kendjrocen Rajen'*" (the *T.* is the [[Teuku|Acehnese title Teuku]]).
- **Nja' Radja** of Boebon — "*Keudjroeen Rajen' Nja' Radja van Boebon*" / "*T. Mahmood dari LhoBoebon*" (the *Nja'* is the [[Nya'|Acehnese honorific for an elder]]).
- **T. Mahmoed** of Lho' Boebon — "*T. Mahmoed van Lho' Boebon*" / "*T. Mahmood dari LhoBoebon*".
- **T. Tjhi' Mohamad Ali Aknhar** of Kaway XVI — "*T. Tjhi' Mohamad Ali Aknhar van Kaway XVI*" (the *Tjhi'* is the [[Teungku Chik|learned religious rank Teungku Chik]]).
- **Keumangan** of Seunagan — "*Keumangan dari Seunagan*" / "*T. Keumangan van Seunagan*" (named by his dynasty-title alone).
- **Potjoet Baren** of Toengkoh — "*Potjoct Baren dari Toengkoh*" / "*Potjoet Baren van Toengkob*" (the *Potjoet* is the [[Cut|Acehnese royal title Cut]]).
- **Mohamad Ali** of Tripa — "*Mohamad Ali dari Tripa*" / "*T. Mohamad Ali van Tripa*".
- **Peutoea Benngga** of Seuneu'am — "*Peutoea Benngga dari Seuneu'am*" / "*Peutoen Beungga van Seuneuam*" (the *Peutoea* is the [[Peutua|elder title Peutua]]).
- **Radja Ila** of Pamen — "*Radja Ila dari Pamen*" / "*Radja Ila van Pamen*".
- **T. Tjoet Achmad** of Beutong — "*Tjoet Achmad dari Beutong*" / "*T. Tjoet Achmad van Beutong*".

These ten *oeleebalang* — each named personally, in the title-and-place format characteristic of the [[Meulaboh]] *onderafdeeling* — form the largest individually-named signatory-collective in the compilation. Reg. No. 19's recital is issued *in overleg met den Besturenden Ambtenaar der onderafdeeling Meulaboh* (in consultation with the Administering Official of the subdivision Meulaboh) — the standard consultative requirement that gives the local Dutch district officer a veto-right over the *Zelfbestuur*'s regulations.

## Protection of casuarina trees on coastal roads

The substantive provision is set out in *Fatsal 1 / Artikel 1* (Article 1), which prohibits the felling or burning of *tjemaraboomen* along the shore and in the *strandmoerassen* (coastal marshes). The regulation's title gives the protective motive in full — "*tot verbod van het kappen van tje- maraboomen wier behoud noodig is voor natuurschoon en voor beschaduwing der strandwegen*" — "for the prohibition of the felling of casuarina trees whose preservation is necessary for *natuurschoon* (natural beauty) and for the *beschaduwing* (shading) of the *strandwegen*." The *tjemara* — *Casuarina equisetifolia*, the ironwood-pine-like shore tree — is the characteristic tree of the Acehnese coast, where it forms a wind-break and shade-canopy along the *strandwegen* and stabilises the *strandmoerassen* against erosion. Reg. No. 19 is the only regulation in the compilation that names *natuurschoon* — landscape aesthetics — as an explicit motive; the only other regulation in the compilation to address the protection of a defined tree-species on aesthetic or hydrological grounds is the [[Zelfbestuursverordening 1 Januari 1921 (Serbeudjadi Rafflesia)|Serbeudjadi Rafflesia natural-monument regulation]] of [[1 January]] [[1921]] (Reg. No. 21).

## Penalty structure

*Fatsal 2 / Artikel 2* sets the penalty for any violation at a maximum of twenty-five guilders or one month's *arbeidstelling* (public-works labour) — among the lowest maxima in the compilation. The standard maximum is *f 100* or three months' *hechtenis* (detention), and the lowest — *f 10* or three days' *hechtenis* — is that of Reg. No. 14 (the *heerendienst*-regeling of Bireuën). The *f 25* / one-month maximum of Reg. No. 19 reflects the regulation's preventive rather than punitive character: the *tjemara*-protection is a matter of *natuurschoon* and *beschaduwing*, not of revenue or public safety, and the penalty is calibrated accordingly. *Fatsal 3 / Artikel 3* sets the *inwerkingtreding* immediately — without the typical *inwerkingtreding*-delay of one or more months. No comparative table is given — Reg. No. 19 is unique to the *onderafdeeling* Meulaboh, and is the only regulation in the compilation that is not extended, in parallel *GELIJKE VERORDENINGEN*, to other *onderafdeelingen* of the *Gouvernement*.

## Significance

The *Zelfbestuursverordening van [[2 July]] [[1917]]* is the only regulation in the compilation that addresses the protection of a single tree-species on a defined infrastructure-corridor — the *strandwegen* of the [[Westkust van Atjeh|afdeeling Westkust van Atjeh]]. The choice of *natuurschoon* and *beschaduwing* as the explicit protective motives — rather than the revenue-protective, hydrological, or fire-preventive motives that dominate the other agricultural verordeningen of the compilation — marks Reg. No. 19 as an early instance of the colonial state's recognition of the aesthetic and infrastructural value of the Acehnese coastal landscape. The regulation's ten individually-named *oeleebalang* signatories — the largest individually-named signatory-collective in the compilation — also make Reg. No. 19 a uniquely well-documented regulation in respect of the indigenous ruling class of the Meulaboh *onderafdeeling* in the late 1910s; the names, titles, and *landschap*-affiliations of these ten *oeleebalang* are otherwise almost absent from the documentary record of the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Gouvernement]] outside this verordening. The immediate *inwerkingtreding* — and the absence of any parallel-regelingen comparative table — further mark Reg. No. 19 as a one-off intervention, prompted by a particular local threat to the *tjemara*-canopy of the Meulaboh *strandwegen* in the summer of [[1917]].
