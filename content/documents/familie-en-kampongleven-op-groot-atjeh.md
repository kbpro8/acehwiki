**Familie- en kampongleven op Groot-Atjeh** is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Work on family and village life in Groot-Atjeh; cited for a striking example of gratitude in an Acehnese boy.

## Parties

by [[Jacobs]]

## Subject

Work on family and village life in [[Groot-Atjeh]]; cited for a striking example of gratitude in an Acehnese boy.

## Significance

Familie- en kampongleven op Groot-Atjeh is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
