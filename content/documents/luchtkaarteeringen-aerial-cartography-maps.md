**Luchtkaarteeringen (aerial cartography maps)** (1934 onward) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Aerial mapping surveys of the BPM oil fields at Peureula', conducted using the 1934 landing field at Idi/Peudawa.

## Date

[[1934]] onward

## Parties

[[Bataafsche Petroleum Maatschappij]]

## Subject

Aerial mapping surveys of the BPM oil fields at [[Peureula]]', conducted using the [[1934]] landing field at Idi/[[Peudawa]].

## Significance

Luchtkaarteeringen (aerial cartography maps) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
