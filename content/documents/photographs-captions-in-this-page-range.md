**Photographs / captions in this page range** is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. 

## Significance

Photographs / captions in this page range is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
