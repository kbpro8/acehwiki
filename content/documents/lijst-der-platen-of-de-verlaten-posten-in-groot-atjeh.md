The **Lijst der Platen** ("List of Plates") of *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]* was the index of the twenty-four photographic plates around which [[G. B. Hooijer]] built his narrative of the Dutch outposts of [[Groot-Atjeh]] in the period [[1893]]–[[1896]]. Because Hooijer described his text as a *toelichting der platen* — an explanation of the plates — the list functions as the structural table of contents of the whole work.

## The plates

The list, printed with the page number of each plate, enumerated in order:

- *Heuvels van Tjot Goe* — the bare hills of [[Tjot Goeë]], p. 9
- *Tjot Goe* — the *benteng* of [[Tjot Goeë]], p. 11
- *Biloel* — the post of [[Biloel]], p. 13
- *Lam Koenjit* — the post of [[Lam Koenjit]], p. 19
- *Binnen Lam Koenjit* — the interior of [[Lam Koenjit]], p. 21
- *Toengkoeb* — the post of [[Toengkoeb]], p. 23
- *Woning van Toekoe Tjoet Toengkoeb* — the dwelling of [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb]], p. 29
- *Kroeng Kali* — the post of [[Kroeng Kali]], p. 31
- *Masdjid Siem* — the mosque of [[Masdjid Siem]], p. 33
- *In de benteng van Toekoe Tjoet Toengkoeb* — inside the *benteng* of Toekoe Tjoet at [[Kroeng Kali]], p. 35
- *Terrein bij Masdjid Siem* — the terrain by [[Masdjid Siem]], p. 37
- *Tjot Rang* — the post of [[Tjot Rang]], p. 35
- *Het terrein om Tjot Rang* — the terrain around [[Tjot Rang]], p. 37
- *Lam Barih* — the post of [[Lam Barih]], p. 39
- *Terrein bij Anagaloeng* — the terrain at [[Anagaloeng]], p. 41
- *Brug bij Anagaloeng* — the [[Bridge at Anagaloeng|iron bridge at Anagaloeng]], p. 43
- *Lam Soet* — the post of [[Lam Soet]], p. 49
- *Kroeng Gloempang* — the post of [[Kroeng Gloempang]], p. 51
- *Binnen Kroeng Gloempang* — the interior of [[Kroeng Gloempang]], p. 53
- *Senelop* — the post of [[Senelop]], p. 55

The optical character recognition of the list is defective in several places, and a number of entries — including views of [[Anagaloeng]] itself and of the interior of [[Anagaloeng]] — appear in the narrative without recoverable list entries.

## Character of the images

The plates were documentary photographs rather than battle art. They showed *bentengs* seen from outside and inside; the *ijzerdraadversperring* (wire entanglement) surrounding the parapets; barrack roofs, kitchens, bath-places and flagstaffs; officers and men grouped before their quarters in undress; the fourteen-day *vivres* transport with its ox-carts, coolies and rearguard; the terrain of rice-fields, coconut groves and *kampong* (village) hedges through which the columns had to fight; and stretches of the [[Acheh River|Atjeh-rivier]] valley with the mountains beyond. Hooijer used them to identify individual officers by name — among them [[Kapitein Van der Wedden|Van der Wedden]], [[Kapitein Schadée|Schadée]], [[Luitenant Zijdveld|Zijdveld]], [[Luitenant Zon|Zon]], [[Officier Dijkstra|Dijkstra]], [[Hendrikus Colijn|Colijn]], [[Kapitein Engelbert van Bevervoorde|Engelbert van Bevervoorde]], [[Luitenant Van Delden|Van Delden]], [[Luitenant Kastens|Kastens]] and the medical officers [[Officier van gezondheid Nanta|Nanta]] and [[Officier van gezondheid Tromp|Tromp]] — and to read off the ethnic composition, dress and mood of the garrisons.

## Significance

The list preserves the intended sequence and titling of one of the few systematic photographic surveys of the Dutch *buitenposten* (outer posts) in [[Groot-Atjeh]] during the alliance period, and it fixes the identity of places — [[Tjot Goeë]], [[Biloel]], [[Lam Koenjit]], [[Toengkoeb]], [[Kroeng Kali]], [[Masdjid Siem]], [[Tjot Rang]], [[Lam Barih]], [[Anagaloeng]], [[Lam Soet]], [[Kroeng Gloempang]] and [[Senelop]] — that were burnt or slighted within weeks of being photographed.
