**De onderwerping van Atjeh** ("The Subjugation of Atjeh") was the Dutch-language brochure published by [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] in [[1898]] — two years after the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (March 1896)|defection of Teuku Umar]] of [[March 1896|March]] [[1896]] and one year after the publication of [[J. A. Kruijt|Kruijt]]'s [[De Atjeh-Oorlog (Kruijt, 1896)|brochure of 1896]] — that confirmed, in its fullest form, the doctrine of the [[Scheepvaartregeling|scheepvaartregeling]] as the principal strategic instrument of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] and the doctrine that the centre of gravity of the war lay on the coasts rather than in [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]]. The brochure is described by Kruijt as "excellent and clear" and as confirming "in full" the opinions of [[Scherer|Scherer]] and [[General van Teijn|Van Teijn]].

## The Doctrine

[[Van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]'s brochure confirmed the doctrine first publicly articulated by [[Scherer|Scherer]] in his [[Scherer's lecture of 20 January 1891|lecture of 20 January 1891]] before the [[Indisch Genootschap|Indisch Genootschap]] — that the resistance came not from [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] but from the *onderhoorigheden* (dependencies) on the coasts — and the doctrine of [[General van Teijn|Van Teijn]]'s [[August 1889|August 1889]] memorandum — that the *Scheepvaartregeling* was the principal means by which the Dutch could exercise political influence on the coasts and thereby in Groot-Atjeh. The brochure is described by Kruijt as "*van Heutz bevestigde in zijn uitstekende en heldere brochure 'De onderwerping van Atjeh,' in 1898 verschenen, al deze meeningen ten volle.*" (Van Heutsz confirmed all these opinions in full in his excellent and clear brochure 'De onderwerping van Atjeh,' published in 1898.)

## The Context

[[Van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]'s brochure appeared in [[1898]] — two years after the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (March 1896)|defection of Teuku Umar]] of [[29 March]] [[29 March]] [[1896]], in the immediate aftermath of the Dutch counter-offensive under [[Vetter|Lt.-Gen. Vetter]], and at the moment of Van Heutsz's own appointment as Governor of [[Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] (he would be appointed Governor in [[1898]] and *Civiel en Militair Gouvernur* in [[1899]]). The brochure is, in effect, the strategic manifesto of the final Dutch pacification of Atjeh that Van Heutsz would carry through in the years [[1898]]–[[1904]].

## Significance

*De onderwerping van Atjeh* is the principal strategic document of the final phase of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. Its confirmation of the doctrines of [[Scherer|Scherer]] and [[General van Teijn|Van Teijn]] gave the authority of the future governor of Atjeh to the strategic analysis that [[J. A. Kruijt|Kruijt]]'s brochure of [[1896]] had also endorsed. The brochure's publication in [[1898]] — at the moment of Van Heutsz's own appointment as Governor — marked the institutional adoption of the *Scheepvaartregeling* doctrine by the Dutch colonial administration, and the beginning of the final Dutch pacification that would carry the war to its conclusion. The eventual Dutch victory under Van Heutsz — which rested on the systematic application of the *Scheepvaartregeling* and the marechaussée campaigns — was the vindication of the brochure's doctrine.
