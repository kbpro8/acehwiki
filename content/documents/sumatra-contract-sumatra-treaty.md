**Sumatra-contract (Sumatra Treaty)** (2 November 1871) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Freed Dutch hands; the proviso of Acehnese independence lapsed.

## Date

[[2 November]] [[1871]]

## Parties

[[Leyden (Leiden)|Netherlands]] and [[Engeland (England)|England]]

## Subject

Freed Dutch hands; the proviso of Acehnese independence lapsed. Led to the Aceh war.

## Significance

Sumatra-contract (Sumatra Treaty) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
