**ATJEH '96** — the comprehensive operational history of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] in the year [[1896]] — was published at [[Weltevreden]] by N.V. Boekhandel Visser & Co in [[1913]] in two volumes, both by [[A. Kruisheer]] (*Kapitein der Infanterie O.I.L.*, Captain of Infantry, Netherlands Indies Army). The work is held today by the Instituut Kern, Leiden.

The **first volume** — *1e Deel* — is the source balso of the present Aceh Wiki History. It is the principal Dutch-language source for the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] in the first semester of [[1896]] (1 January – 28 June [[1896]]), centred on the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (29 March 1896)|defection of Teuku Umar]] on [[29 March]] [[1896]] and on the Dutch counter-offensives that followed through the [[Departure of Vetter (28 June 1896)|departure of Lt.-Gen. Vetter as Regeeringscommissaris]] on [[28 June]] [[1896]]. The volume comprises a main text of 331 pages, eight Annexes on pp. 334–351 (official documents, letters, proclamations, and forces tables), two maps, and four portraits. The preface is dated [[Magelang]], [[April]] [[1913]].

The **second volume** — *[[ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel (book by A. Kruisheer)|Tweede Deel]]* (2c Deel) — was published under the full title *ATJEH [[1896]] ATJCH [[1896]]–[[1900]] Met 3 kaarten en 6 portretten*. It is the principal Dutch-language source for the second semester of [[1896]] (29 June – 31 December [[1896]]), centred on the [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|surprise of Ana' Galoeng]] of [[29 June]] [[1896]] and on the Dutch *pacificatie* (pacification) operations that followed through the [[Cholera Epidemic in Atjeh (December 1896)|cholera epidemic]] of December [[1896]]. The volume comprises a main text organised in two *Hoofdstukken* (Chapters III and IV), a *Besluit* (Conclusion), and twenty-two *Bijlagen* (Annexes) — including the *Instructie* (Instruction) left by [[J. A. Vetter|Vetter]] as Regeeringscommissaris at his departure (Bijlage I), the *Notulen* (Minutes (Notulen)) of the conference between [[Jhr. C. H. A. van der Wijck|Van der Wijck]] and [[J. J. K. de Moulin|De Moulin]] of [[18 June]] [[1896]] (Bijlage II), and the consolidated Instruction for Civiel en Militair Gouverneur Van Vliet (Bijlage III). The volume is held today by the Instituut Kern, Leiden.

The two volumes together — the first covering January–June [[1896]] and the second covering July–December [[1896]] — constitute the comprehensive operational history of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] in the year [[1896]], as compiled and narrated by [[A. Kruisheer|Kruisheer]] from his personal service in Atjeh and from the institutional records of the Dutch colonial army. The two volumes share the same institutional and biographical ambition — with portraits, maps, and *Bijlagen* preserving the principal official documents of the year — and together form the source balso of the Aceh Wiki History repository for the year [[1896]].

## Background

Kruisheer's *ATJEH '96* was compiled from a series of articles he had published in the I.M.T. — the professional journal of the Royal Netherlands Indies Army — in the volumes of [[1903]], [[1905]], [[1906]], and [[1908]]. The I.M.T. articles had themselves been the product of Kruisheer's personal service in [[Aceh]]: he had been present at [[Ana' Galong|Ana Galong]] in [[December]] [[1895]] as part of the garrison (page 27 of the first volume), where he had observed the daily coming-and-going of a Sibreh inhabitant who spied for both sides, and the enemy's construction of a complete battery with trenches opposite the post. The I.M.T. articles had attracted attention in the colonial military community, and in [[1913]] Kruisheer revised and compiled them into the present two-volume work.

The choice of scope — the year [[1896]] — was deliberate. The defection of [[Teuku Umar]] on [[29 March]] [[1896]] had triggered what Kruisheer regarded as a strategic turning point in the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]: the abandonment of the failed policy of native alliance that had governed Dutch strategy since [[5 September]] [[1893]], the dismissal of [[Generaal-Majoor C. Deykerhoff|Gen.-Maj. Deykerhoff]], the dispatch of [[Luitenant-Generaal Vetter|Lt.-Gen. Vetter]] as [[Regeeringscommissaris]], the conventional military operations of [[April]]–[[June]] [[1896]], the brief and fatal governorship of [[J. J. K. de Moulin|Generaal-Majoor De Moulin]] in July [[1896]], the four-month second tenure of [[J. W. Stemfoort|Kolonel Stemfoort]] as *wd. Civiel en Militair Gouverneur* (11 July – 17 November [[1896]]), the arrival of [[C. P. J. van Vliet|Kolonel Van Vliet]] as the new permanent governor (8 November [[1896]]), and the paralysis of military action in December [[1896]] by the *heerschende cholera* (prevailing cholera). In the *Besluit* (Conclusion) of the second volume, Kruisheer claims that *men in [[1896]] den rechten weg tot pacificatie insloeg* — that in [[1896]] the right path to pacification was entered.

## Content of the First Volume (1e Deel)

The first volume is organised in three principal parts. The **front matter** (pp. 1–16) comprises the title page, the preface dated [[Magelang]] [[April]] [[1913]], a portrait frontispiece of [[Jhr. C. H. A. van der Wijck|Gouverneur-Generaal Jhr. C. H. A. van der Wijck]], three further portraits ([[Generaal-Majoor C. Deykerhoff|Gen.-Maj. C. Deykerhoff]], [[Luitenant-Generaal Vetter|Lt.-Gen. Vetter]], [[Teuku Umar|Toekoe Oemar]]), and the Table of Contents on pp. 13–16, which lists the Annexes and the two maps: the Map of the western part of Great Aceh, scale 1:20,000 and the Sketch map of the former Eastern Outer Line, scale 1:25,000.

The **main text** (pp. 19–331) is organised chronologically in Chapters by date-range. *Hoofdstuk I* — pp. 19–65 — covers the period from the strength return of [[1 January]] [[1896]] (the [[Bijlage I (A) — State of forces in Atjeh and their legering on 1 January 1896|Bijlage I A]]) through the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (29 March 1896)|defection of 29 March 1896]] and the abandonment of the [[buitenlinie]] by [[20 April]] [[1896]]. *Hoofdstuk II* — covering [[21 April]]–[[22 May]] [[1896]] — covers the [[Dismission of Teuku Umar (26 April 1896)|dismissal of Umar]], the [[Bombardment of Lampisang (27 April – 23 May 1896)|Bombardment of Lampisang]], and the various *diversies* and *tuchtigingen* of late April and early May. *Hoofdstuk III* — covering [[23 May]]–[[28 June]] [[1896]] — covers the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day Dutch offensive]], the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims]], the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|tuchtiging of V Moekims Montassik]], the [[Ladoeng Landing and Glé Broeë Attack (22–23 June 1896)|Ladoeng Landing]], the [[Vetter's Farewell Parade (27 June 1896)|farewell parade of Vetter]], and the [[Departure of Vetter (28 June 1896)|departure of Vetter]] on [[28 June]] [[1896]].

The **Bijlagen** (Annexes) of the first volume on pp. 334–351 comprise eight official documents: the [[Bijlage I (A) — State of forces in Atjeh and their legering on 1 January 1896|Bijlage I A — strength return of 1 January 1896]]; the [[First Letter of Teuku Umar to the Governor of Atjeh (Bijlage II)|Bijlage II — first letter of T. Oemar of 30 March 1896]]; the [[Proclamation of the Regeeringscommissaris (12 April 1896)|Bijlage III — first proclamation of the Regeeringscommissaris]]; the [[Second Letter of Teuku Umar (12 April 1896)|Bijlage IV — second letter of T. Oemar of 12 April 1896]]; the [[Third Letter of Teuku Umar (23 April 1896)|Bijlage V — third letter of T. Oemar of 23 April 1896]]; the [[Gouvernementsbesluit of 26 April 1896 No. 159/11 (Bijlage VI)|Bijlage VI — besluit dismissing T. Djohan Pahalawan of 26 April 1896]]; the [[Proclamation of Lt. Gen. Vetter to the heads and people of the 3 sagi of Groot-Atjeh (27 April 1896)|Bijlage VII — second proclamation of the Regeeringscommissaris of 27 April 1896]]; and the [[Bijlage VIII — State of forces in Atjeh and their legering on 1 June 1896|Bijlage VIII — strength return of 1 June 1896]].

## Content of the Second Volume (Tweede Deel / 2c Deel)

The second volume is organised in three principal parts. The **front matter** (pp. 1–13) comprises the title page, the *Inhoud* (Table of Contents) on pp. 7–11 listing the two *Hoofdstukken*, the *Besluit*, and the twenty-two *Bijlagen*, and a list of the three maps and six portraits — including the frontispiece *Frontaanzicht van Ana' Galoeng* opposite p. 2, a portrait of [[J. J. K. de Moulin|Generaal-Majoor J. J. K. de Moulin]] opposite p. 15, of [[J. W. Stemfoort|Kolonel Stemfoort]] opposite p. 83, of [[C. P. J. van Vliet|Kolonel Van Vliet]] opposite p. 145, of [[J. B. van Heutsz|Luitenant-Kolonel Van Heutsz]] opposite p. 276, and a sketch of the *Gevechtsterrein bij Ana' Galoeng* opposite p. 104. The six portraits — compared to the four of the first volume — reflect the larger cast of principal figures in the second semester: De Moulin (the governor who died), Stemfoort (his successor as *wd. Civiel en Militair Gouverneur*), Van Vliet (the permanent governor), and Van Heutsz (the future governor and commander of the first Selimoen expedition).

The **main text** (pp. 19–160) is organised chronologically in two *Hoofdstukken*. *Hoofdstuk III* — pp. 1–144 — covers the period from the [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|surprise of Ana' Galoeng]] of [[29 June]] [[1896]] through the [[Arrival of Van Vliet as Civiel en Militair Gouverneur (8 November 1896)|arrival of Kolonel Van Vliet]] on [[8 November]] [[1896]]. It opens with the night attack of [[29 June]] [[1896]] in which three Dutch colonnes under [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]], [[G. F. Soeters|Soeters]] and [[C. J. Laceulle|Laceulle]] stormed the *benteng* (fort) of [[Ana' Galong]] with the *maréchaussée* (military police (maréchaussée)) and the snelladers of the [[3e Bataljon]] using only the *klewang* (cutlass (klewang)); it records the death of [[J. J. K. de Moulin|Generaal-Majoor De Moulin]] by sunstroke at [[Lam-Pisang]] on [[7 July]] [[1896]] during an *excursie* (reconnaissance (excursie)) to the [[IV Moekims|IV Moekims (Kroeng-Raba)]]; and it narrates the [[Stemfoort's Second Tenure as Civiel en Militair Gouverneur (11 July – 17 November 1896)|four-month second tenure of Stemfoort as wd. Civiel en Militair Gouverneur]], during which the Dutch extended their control into the [[XXVI Moekims]], [[XXII Moekims]], [[IV Moekims]] and the [[Selimoen]] highlands.

*Hoofdstuk IV* — pp. 145–160 — covers the period from the [[Arrival of Van Vliet as Civiel en Militair Gouverneur (8 November 1896)|arrival of Kolonel Van Vliet]] on [[8 November]] [[1896]] through the close of the year. It narrates the [[Handover of Command from Stemfoort to Van Vliet (17 November 1896)|handover of command]] of [[17 November]] [[1896]]; the [[Reconnaissance in Lepong (12 November 1896)|reconnaissance in Lepong]] of [[12 November]] [[1896]]; the [[Trip to Tibang-Poei and Birah (29 November 1896)|trip to Tibang-Poei and Birah]] near [[Bat Dilip]] of [[29 November]] [[1896]] to recover the artillery lost on [[17 April]] [[1896]] during the [[Battle of Senelop (17 April 1896)|battle of Senelop]]; and the paralysis of military action in December [[1896]] by the *heerschende cholera* (prevailing cholera) which prevented the *colonne tegen Oemar* (column against Umar) from taking the field.

The **Besluit* (Decree (Besluit))* (Conclusion) on p. 155 closes the volume with Kruisheer's retrospective assessment of the second semester of [[1896]] — a period in which, in his view, *men in [[1896]] den rechten weg tot pacificatie insloeg* (the right path to pacification was entered in [[1896]]), even as Umar remained at large and the *geconcentreerde linie* (concentrated line) remained the limit of effective Dutch control.

The **Bijlagen** (Annexes) of the second volume on pp. 162–328 comprise twenty-two official documents. The most important are: *Bijlage I* — the [[Bijlage I — Instruction of the Regeeringscommissaris (28 June 1896)|Instruction left by Vetter as Regeeringscommissaris at his departure on 28 June 1896]] (pp. 162–165); *Bijlage II* — the [[Notulen der conferentie van 18 Juni 1896|minutes of the conference of 18 June 1896 between Van der Wijck and De Moulin]] on the policy to be followed in Atjeh (pp. 166–167); *Bijlage III* — the [[Bijlage III — Instruction for Civiel en Militair Gouverneur Van Vliet|Instruction for Kolonel Van Vliet as Civiel en Militair Gouverneur]] (pp. 168–172); *Bijlagen IV–V* — *Extract-besluiten* regulating the *schaftordes* (food tariffs) for [[1893]] and [[1895]] (pp. 173–178); *Bijlage VI* — the [[Commandemensorders of 4 and 5 April 1896|Commandemensorders of 4 and 5 April 1896, nos. 37 and 39]] (p. 179); *Bijlagen VII–VIII* — *Extract-besluiten* of [[30 April]] [[1896]] and [[22 Augustus]] [[1896]] on the *toelagen en den bijslag* (allowances and supplements) (pp. 180–181); *Bijlagen IX–XII* — *Schaftordes* (food tariffs) for Europeans, Inlanders, and Officers, and a comparative overview of the *voedingswijze* (diet) on war footing in [[1896]] (pp. 183–195); *Bijlage XIII* — a statement of officer incomes in [[1896]] (pp. 199–203); *Bijlage XIV* — a *Nota betreffende de verpleging van troepen en dwangarbeiders bij den tocht naar Lepong* (note on the care of troops and forced labourers on the Lepong expedition) (p. 203); *Bijlage XV* — the [[Schaftorde of 21 December 1896|Schaftorde of 21 December 1896]] (p. 208); *Bijlage XVI* — the [[Bijlage XVI — Verlieslijst Atjeh-1896|Verlieslijst Atjeh-1896]] (loss list for Atjeh [[1896]]) (pp. 212–218); *Bijlagen XVII–XIX* — *Dagorders* (daily orders) granting military decorations for Atjeh '96 (pp. 222–230); and *Bijlage XXII* — the [[Bijlage XXII — Overzicht van onze vroegere betrekkingen met Atjeh|Overzicht van onze vroegere betrekkingen met Atjeh en van den huidigen toestand in dat gewest]] (Survey of our former relations with Atjeh and of the current state in that territory) (pp. 234–304), with twenty-six subsections covering the history, the Atjehsche volkskarakter, the size and boundaries, the population, the coasts, mining, roads, the tramway, trade, art and industry, agriculture, cattle-breeding, education, the chiefs, the administration of justice, taxation, the landscape treasuries, the administrative division, the armed police, the army, and various graphical representations of trade and data concerning the years [[1893]]–[[1912]].

## Reception and Aftermath

*ATJEH '96* — in both volumes — has served as the principal Dutch-language source for the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] of [[1896]] for over a century. The work is cited in subsequent colonial and post-colonial scholarship on the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]], and its preservation of the *Bijlagen* — particularly the three letters of [[Teuku Umar]] and the two proclamations of [[Luitenant-Generaal Vetter|Vetter]] in the first volume, and the *Instructie* (Instruction) of Vetter, the *Notulen* (Minutes (Notulen)) of the Van der Wijck-De Moulin conference, and the consolidated Van Vliet Instruction in the second volume — has made it indispensable for the study of the diplomatic and political history of the [[1896]] crisis.

The OCR copies held by the Instituut Kern, Leiden — under the filenames `20260715_163243_DLP-89-Atjeh_96_Met_2_kaarten_en4_DOORA_KRUISHEER_Kapiteinder_Infanterie_nemoretriever-ocr-v1_OCR.txt` (Volume 1) and `20260715_163243_DLP-90-Atjeh_96_DLP-89-Atjeh_96_Met_3_kaarten_en_6_DOORA_KRUISHEER_Kapiteinder_Infanterie_nemoretriever-ocr-v1_OCR.txt` (Volume 3) — are the basis of the present Aceh Wiki History. The OCRs are of poor quality, with many character-recognition errors (e.g., "Mockims" for "Moekims", "hel" for "het"), but the historical content is recoverable with care.

## Significance

As the source balso of the Aceh Wiki History, *ATJEH '96* — in both volumes — is the foundational document of the present repository. Its scope — the year [[1896]] — defines the chronological boundary of the articles; its perspective — that of a serving Dutch colonial infantry officer — defines the political frame within which the events are narrated; and its preservation of the *Bijlagen* across both volumes defines the corpus of primary documents available for analysis. The work's claim that *in [[1896]] the right path to pacification was entered* is itself a historical position, and the subsequent course of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] — which continued until [[1903]] and beyond — provides the perspective from which that claim can be assessed. As a historical document, *ATJEH '96* — in both volumes — is both the principal source and the principal object of study for the present wiki.
