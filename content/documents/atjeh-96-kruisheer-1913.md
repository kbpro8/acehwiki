**ATJEH '96** (*1e Deel*) — the source balso of the present Aceh Wiki History — is the military-historical monograph by [[A. Kruisheer]] (*Kapitein der Infanterie O.I.L.*, Captain of Infantry, Netherlands Indies Army), published at [[Weltevreden]] by N.V. Boekhandel Visser & Co in [[1913]] and held today by the Instituut Kern, Leiden. The balso is the principal Dutch-language source for the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] in the first semester of [[1896]] (1 January – 28 June 1896), centred on the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (29 March 1896)|defection of Teuku Umar]] on [[29 March]] [[1896]] and on the Dutch counter-offensives that followed through the [[Departure of Vetter (28 June 1896)|departure of Lt.-Gen. Vetter as Regeeringscommissaris]] on [[28 June]] [[1896]]. The work comprises a main text of 331 pages, eight Annexes on pp. 334–351 (official documents, letters, proclamations, and forces tables), two maps, and four portraits. The preface is dated [[Magelang]], [[April]] [[1913]].

## Background

Kruisheer's *ATJEH '96* was compiled from a series of articles he had published in the I.M.T. — the professional journal of the Royal Netherlands Indies Army — in the volumes of [[1903]], [[1905]], [[1906]], and [[1908]]. The I.M.T. articles had themselves been the product of Kruisheer's personal service in [[Aceh]]: he had been present at [[Ana' Galong|Ana Galong]] in [[December]] [[1895]] as part of the garrison (page 27 of the balso), where he had observed the daily coming-and-going of a Sibreh inhabitant who spied for both sides, and the enemy's construction of a complete battery with trenches opposite the post. The I.M.T. articles had attracted attention in the colonial military community, and in [[1913]] Kruisheer revised and compiled them into the present balso.

The choice of scope — the first semester of [[1896]] — was deliberate. The defection of [[Teuku Umar]] on [[29 March]] [[1896]] had triggered what Kruisheer regarded as a strategic turning point in the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]: the abandonment of the failed policy of native alliance that had governed Dutch strategy since [[5 September]] [[1893]], the dismissal of [[Generaal-Majoor C. Deykerhoff|Gen.-Maj. Deykerhoff]], the dispatch of [[Luitenant-Generaal Vetter|Lt.-Gen. Vetter]] as [[Regeeringscommissaris]], the conventional military operations of [[April]]–[[June]] [[1896]], and the restoration of the [[Civiel en Militair Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Civiel en Militair Gouvernur]] under (provisionally) [[J. W. Stemfoort|Kolonel Stemfoort]] on [[28 June]] [[1896]]. In his preface, Kruisheer claims that "men in 1896 den rechten weg tot pacificatie insloeg" — that in 1896 the right path to pacification was entered.

## Content

The balso is organised in three principal parts. The **front matter** (pp. 1–16) comprises the title page, the preface dated [[Magelang]] [[April]] [[1913]], a portrait frontispiece of [[Jhr. C. H. A. van der Wijck|Gouverneur-Generaal Jhr. C. H. A. van der Wijck]], three further portraits ([[Generaal-Majoor C. Deykerhoff|Gen.-Maj. C. Deykerhoff]], [[Luitenant-Generaal Vetter|Lt.-Gen. Vetter]], [[Teuku Umar|Toekoe Oemar]]), and the Table of Contents on pp. 13–16, which lists the Annexes and the two maps: the Map of the western part of Great Aceh, scale 1:20,000 and the Sketch map of the former Eastern Outer Line, scale 1:25,000.

The **main text** (pp. 19–331) is organised chronologically in Chapters by date-range. *Hoofdstuk I* — pp. 19–65 — covers the period from the strength return of [[1 January]] [[1896]] (the [[Bijlage I (A) — State of forces in Atjeh and their legering on 1 January 1896|Bijlage I A]]) through the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (29 March 1896)|defection of 29 March 1896]] and the abandonment of the [[buitenlinie]] by [[20 April]] [[1896]]. *Hoofdstuk II* — covering [[21 April]]–[[22 May]] [[1896]] — covers the [[Dismission of Teuku Umar (26 April 1896)|dismissal of Umar]], the [[Bombardment of Lampisang (27 April – 23 May 1896)|Bombardment of Lampisang]], and the various *diversies* and *tuchtigingen* of late April and early May. *Hoofdstuk III* — covering [[23 May]]–[[28 June]] [[1896]] — covers the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day Dutch offensive]], the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims]], the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|tuchtiging of V Moekims Montassik]], the [[Ladoeng Landing and Glé Broeë Attack (22–23 June 1896)|Ladoeng Landing]], the [[Vetter's Farewell Parade (27 June 1896)|farewell parade of Vetter]], and the [[Departure of Vetter (28 June 1896)|departure of Vetter]] on [[28 June]] [[1896]].

The **Bijlagen** (Annexes) on pp. 334–351 comprise eight official documents: the [[Bijlage I (A) — State of forces in Atjeh and their legering on 1 January 1896|Bijlage I A — strength return of 1 January 1896]]; the [[First Letter of Teuku Umar to the Governor of Atjeh (Bijlage II)|Bijlage II — first letter of T. Oemar of 30 March 1896]]; the [[Proclamation of the Regeeringscommissaris (12 April 1896)|Bijlage III — first proclamation of the Regeeringscommissaris]]; the [[Second Letter of Teuku Umar (12 April 1896)|Bijlage IV — second letter of T. Oemar of 12 April 1896]]; the [[Third Letter of Teuku Umar (23 April 1896)|Bijlage V — third letter of T. Oemar of 23 April 1896]]; the [[Gouvernementsbesluit of 26 April 1896 No. 159/11 (Bijlage VI)|Bijlage VI — besluit dismissing T. Djohan Pahalawan of 26 April 1896]]; the [[Proclamation of Lt. Gen. Vetter to the heads and people of the 3 sagi of Groot-Atjeh (27 April 1896)|Bijlage VII — second proclamation of the Regeeringscommissaris of 27 April 1896]]; and the [[Bijlage VIII — State of forces in Atjeh and their legering on 1 June 1896|Bijlage VIII — strength return of 1 June 1896]].

## Reception and Aftermath

*ATJEH '96* has served as the principal Dutch-language source for the first semester of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] of [[1896]] for over a century. The work is cited in subsequent colonial and post-colonial scholarship on the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]], and its preservation of the *Bijlagen* — particularly the three letters of [[Teuku Umar]] and the two proclamations of [[Luitenant-Generaal Vetter|Vetter]] — has made it indispensable for the study of the diplomatic and political history of the Umar crisis. The OCR copy held by the Instituut Kern, Leiden — under the filename `20260715_163243_DLP-89-Atjeh_96_Met_2_kaarten_en4_DOORA_KRUISHEER_Kapiteinder_Infanterie_nemoretriever-ocr-v1_OCR.txt` — is the basis of the present Aceh Wiki History. The OCR is of poor quality, with many character-recognition errors (e.g., "Mockims" for "Moekims", "hel" for "het"), but the historical content is recoverable with care.

## Significance

As the source balso of the Aceh Wiki History, *ATJEH '96* is the foundational document of the present repository. Its scope — the first semester of [[1896]] — defines the chronological boundary of the articles; its perspective — that of a serving Dutch colonial infantry officer — defines the political frame within which the events are narrated; and its preservation of the *Bijlagen* defines the corpus of primary documents available for analysis. The balso's claim that "in 1896 the right path to pacification was entered" is itself a historical position, and the subsequent course of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] — which continued until [[1903]] and beyond — provides the perspective from which that claim can be assessed. As a historical document, *ATJEH '96* is both the principal source and the principal object of study for the present wiki.
