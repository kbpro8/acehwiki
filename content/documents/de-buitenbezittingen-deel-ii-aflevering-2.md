**De Buitenbezittingen: Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden, Deel II, aflevering 2** was the volume of the *Buitenbezittingen* series on [[Aceh|Atjeh]] and its dependencies to which [[D. W. N. de Boer]] referred, at page 148 and following, for the background of the Atjehnese pepper economy in his description of the *[[Contract of the Atjehnese pepper planter|contract of the Atjehnese pepper planter]]*.

## Citation

The reference was given alongside *[[De Pepercultuur in de Buitenbezittingen|De Pepercultuur in de Buitenbezittingen]]*, instalment V of the *Mededeelingen* of the [[Encyclopaedisch Bureau]], as the two works to be compared on the pepper cultivation of the *gewest* (region).
