**The *Jawāhir al-ʿulūm*** ("The Jewels of the Sciences") is a Malay religious and mystical compendium by [[Raniri|Nuruddin ar-Raniri]], composed in [[Acheh]] during the period of his activity at the court of [[Iskandar Thani]] and his successor [[Sultan Tajul-alam|Taj al-Ālam]] ([[1637]]–[[1644]]). It survives in a London manuscript and is named by [[P. Voorhoeve|Voorhoeve]] in the [[1955]] edition of Raniri's two Malay treatises as one of the principal writings in which Raniri developed his argument against the [[Wujudiyyah]] and his exposition of the orthodox Sufi position.

## Content and character

The *Jawāhir al-ʿulūm* is a work of encyclopaedic scope — its very title, "the jewels of the sciences," indicating the breadth of its subject-matter. Like the [[Hall al-zill|*Ḥall aẓ-ẓill*]] and the [[Hudjdjat as-siddiq li-daf az-zindiq|*Ḥujjat aṣ-ṣiddīq*]], it belongs to that part of Raniri's oeuvre which Voorhoeve describes as "directed against the Wujūdiyyah." It cites repeatedly the [[al-Insan al-Kamil|*al-Insān al-kāmil*]] of [[Abd al-Karim al-Jili|ʿAbd al-Karīm al-Jīlī]] — the great Sufi cosmology — as an authoritative source. It also cites the *Ithbād an-naṣīḥa* and the *Irāʾat ad-daqāʾiq* of the Indian mystic [[Ahmad al-Mahaimi|ʿAlī b. Aḥmad al-Mahāʾimī]] (pp. 37, 39, 40, 58 of the London manuscript), as well as the [[Lata'if al-asrar|*Laṭāʾif al-iʿlām*]] (without naming its author). It cites the [[Shaikh Shamsuddin of Sumatra|Shamsuddin of Pasai]] by name, noting that the latter had appealed to the [[al-Insan al-Kamil|*al-Insān al-kāmil*]] but that he had — in Raniri's view — interpreted the text incorrectly. The *Jawāhir al-ʿulūm* thus bears direct witness to the textual controversy that lay behind Raniri's [[Raniri's public debate against the Wujudiyyah in Acheh|public disputation]] against the Wujūdiyyah at the court of [[Iskandar Thani]].

## Citations of other authorities

In the course of the work Raniri cites a number of authorities whose names are otherwise known from his other writings and from the apparatus of the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān*]]. Among the Sufis he cites [[Shaikh Shibli]] (p. 141), [[Shaikh Junayd|Shaikh Djunaid]] (pp. 45, 145), [[Ibn ʿAṭāʾ Allāh|Shaikh Ibn ʿAṭāʾ Allāh]] (pp. 141, 157), and [[Ibn Arabi|Ibn ʿArabī]] (repeatedly). The citations of al-Mahāʾimī and of the [[al-Insan al-Kamil|*al-Insān al-kāmil*]] point to the Indian and Ḥadramī channels through which Raniri's Malay Achehnese polemic was supplied with the materials of Arabic and Persian Sufism.

## Significance

The *Jawāhir al-ʿulūm* is one of the most extensive of Raniri's Malay religious writings and is unique among them in the explicitness with which it documents the textual controversy between Raniri and [[Shaikh Shamsuddin of Sumatra|Shamsuddin of Pasai]] over the interpretation of the [[al-Insan al-Kamil|*al-Insān al-kāmil*]]. It belongs, with the [[Hall al-zill|*Ḥall aẓ-ẓill*]], the [[Hudjdjat as-siddiq li-daf az-zindiq|*Ḥujjat aṣ-ṣiddīq*]], the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān*]], the [[Sirat al-mustaqim|*Ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm*]], the [[Lata'if al-asrar|*Laṭāʾif al-asrār*]] (lost), and the [[Bustan al-Salatin|*Bustān as-Salāṭīn*]], to the body of Malay religious prose composed in Acheh during the years of the orthodox reaction against the Wujūdiyyah.
