**De Wekker-artikelen ([[1907]])** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[1907]]

At the end of **[[1907]]** the [[Tirò Teungkoes|Tirò band]] fled from [[Geumpang]] to [[Pidië]] before the patrols of [[H. J. Schmidt]]. In November [[1907]] the half-brother [[T. Tjhi' Béntara]] was restored as *[[oelèëbalang]]* of [[Keureutòë]], whereupon [[Pang Nanggròë]] proclaimed the six-year-old [[T. Radja Sabi]] *oelèëbalang* in his stead. The *[[De Wekker-artikelen (1907)|Wekker]]* articles of [[1907]] belong among the sources of [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]]'s biography.
