**The News-sheet for the Government of Atjeh and Dependencies** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]]

The Government published the *[[Monthly Reports of the Government of Atjeh and Dependencies|Maandverslagen]]*, quoted extensively throughout du Croo's book, and the *[[The News-sheet for the Government of Atjeh and Dependencies|Nieuwsblad voor het Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]]*, known as *De Atjehcourant*, which reported Swart's departure in September [[1918]].

### From [[List of works consulted (General Swart)]]

Besides the works listed, du Croo drew on interviews with [[H. N. A. Swart]] himself; on the recollections and collaboration of [[H. J. Schmidt]]; on Swart's private letters to Schmidt of [[1909]], [[1911]] and [[1913]]; on the [[Extract from the service record of H. N. A. Swart|extract from Swart's service record]]; on the correspondence between [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|van Heutsz]] and minister [[Alexander Willem Frederik Idenburg|Idenburg]] and the *Bijlagen* to Naarding's dissertation; and on press articles in the *[[Nieuwe Courant]]*, the *[[Sumatra Post]]*, the *[[Java-Bode]]*, *[[De Locomotief]]*, the *[[Nieuwe Rotterdamsche Courant]]*, the *[[Deli Courant]]*, the *[[The News-sheet for the Government of Atjeh and Dependencies|Atjehcourant]]* and *[[The Weekly of an Amsterdammer]]*, as well as [[H. C. Zentgraaff]]'s *Vaderlandsche Kout* and the speeches of [[J. W. Meijer Ranneft]] and Governor [[Van Aken]].
