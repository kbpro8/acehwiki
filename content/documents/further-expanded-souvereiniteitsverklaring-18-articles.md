**Further expanded souvereiniteitsverklaring (18 articles)** (later (pre-1898)) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Further expansion of the souvereiniteitsverklaring to 18 articles.

## Date

later (pre-[[1898]])

## Parties

Dutch government ↔ oelèëbalangs

## Subject

Further expansion of the [[souvereiniteitsverklaring]] to 18 articles.

## Significance

Further expanded souvereiniteitsverklaring (18 articles) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
