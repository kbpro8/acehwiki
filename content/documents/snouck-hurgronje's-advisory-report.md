**Snouck Hurgronje's advisory report** (1892-1893) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Advice on the political situation of Atjeh: never negotiate with the enemy, only strike them, never give them the chance to recover - then show peaceful intentions.

## Date

[[1892]]-[[1893]]

## Parties

[[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] to Dutch colonial government

## Subject

Advice on the political situation of Atjeh: never negotiate with the enemy, only strike them, never give them the chance to recover - then show peaceful intentions. Implemented by [[Generaal J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] from [[1898]].

## Significance

Snouck Hurgronje's advisory report is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
