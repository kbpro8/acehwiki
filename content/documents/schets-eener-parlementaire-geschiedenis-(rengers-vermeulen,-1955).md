**Schets eener parlementaire geschiedenis van Nederland van 1849 tot 1901** — *Sketch of a Parliamentary History of the Netherlands from 1849 to 1901* — is the four-volume historical work by Mr W. J. van Welderen baron Rengers (extended in its [[1955]] fourth revised edition by Prof. Mr C. W. de Vries and Dr W. H. Vermeulen) that is the principal narrative source of the present Aceh Wiki History articles on the Atjeh debates of the [[Tweede Kamer der Staten-Generaal (colonial business)|Tweede Kamer]] in the [[1891]]-[[1918]] period. The [[1955]] fourth edition was published in 's-Gravenhage by Martinus Nijhoff.

## The four volumes

The [[1955]] fourth revised edition comprises four volumes. The first three cover the parliamentary history of the [[Netherlands]] itself; the fourth volume — the source of the present Aceh Wiki articles — comprises three sections: *Nederland 1914-1918* by Prof. Mr C. W. de Vries; *Oost-Indië 1891-1918* by Dr W. H. Vermeulen; and *West-Indië 1849-1918* by Dr W. H. Vermeulen. The colonial section by [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] is the spine of the Atjehnese-war parliamentary record as it appears in the present articles.

## The chapters on Atjeh

The Atjeh-related content of the [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] section of the fourth edition is concentrated in:

- Chapter I: *De vestiging van het gezag van Atjeh tot Nieuw Guinea* (pp. 267-275)
- Chapter II: *De rechtvaardiging van het gezag* (pp. 275-283)
- Chapter III: *Van batig slot en ereschuld* (pp. 283-294)
- Chapter IV: *Overheidszorg en particulier initiatief bij de economische politiek* (pp. 294-302)
- Chapter V: *De politiek met betrekking tot de bodemschatten* (pp. 302-311)
- Chapter VI: *Onderwijs en zending* (pp. 311-324)
- Chapter VII: *Naar juridische gelijkstelling. De nationalistische beweging* (pp. 324-343)
- Chapter VIII: *De verdediging van Indië. De Volksraad* (pp. 343-351)
- Chapter IX: *Conclusie* (pp. 351-355)

The appendices include an *Overzicht van de ministers van Koloniën en de gouverneurs-generaal van Nederlandsch-Indië 1848-1918* (p. 375) and an *Overzicht van de ministeries van 1848 tot 1918* (p. 368).

## Significance

The [[Schets eener parlementaire geschiedenis van Nederland van 1849 tot 1901 (Rengers-Vermeulen, 1955)|Rengers-Vermeulen *Schets*]] of [[1955]] is the principal narrative source of the present Aceh Wiki History articles on the Atjeh debates of the [[Tweede Kamer der Staten-Generaal (colonial business)|Tweede Kamer]] in the [[1891]]-[[1918]] period. Every article in the present collection that cites *Schets* refers to the [[1955]] fourth revised edition.
