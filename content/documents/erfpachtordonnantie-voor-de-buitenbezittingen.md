**Erfpachtordonnantie voor de Buitenbezittingen** (1919, effective 1 February 1920) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. New leasehold ordinance for the Outer Territories; delegated issuance of erfpachtperceelen to the Zelfbestuur (native self-government) with sanction of the Head of Regional Administration.

## Date

[[1919]], effective [[1 February]] [[1920]]

## Parties

Dutch colonial government / [[Zelfbestuur]]

## Subject

New leasehold ordinance for the Outer Territories; delegated issuance of erfpachtperceelen to the [[Zelfbestuur]] (native self-government) with sanction of the Head of Regional Administration. No new landbouw-concessies issued after this date.

## Significance

Erfpachtordonnantie voor de Buitenbezittingen is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
