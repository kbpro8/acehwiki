**Raja of Gighen Letter of Submission** was a formal diplomatic letter sent by the [[Raja of Gighen]] to Commander-in-Chief General [[J. van Swieten]] on [[30 December]] [[1873]] (11 Dzoelkangidah 1290 AH), during the opening phase of the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] against the [[Aceh Sultanate]].

## Content and Context

Following the capture of coastal strongholds around [[Meuraxa]] and [[Pekan Badak]] by the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]], the [[Raja of Gighen]] wrote to General [[J. van Swieten]] formally offering the submission of the coastal dependency of [[Gighen]] to the [[Dutch East Indies Government]].

## Strategic Impact on Dutch Command

The receipt of the [[Raja of Gighen Letter of Submission]], alongside similar overtures from [[Pedir]] and the assistance of [[Teuku Nek of Meuraxa]], reinforced General [[J. van Swieten]]'s assessment that coastal Acehnese dependencies were abandoning the royal court at [[Kraton of Aceh]]. 

This document formed part of the factual basis for the [[General van Swieten War Telegrams]] transmitted to [[Batavia]] in January [[1874]], which predicted the general pacification of northern Sumatra.
