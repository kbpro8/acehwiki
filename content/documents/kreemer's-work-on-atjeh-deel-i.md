**Kreemer's work on Atjeh (deel I)** is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Multi-volume scholarly work on Aceh; deel I cited for word-play jokes from the actie-tijd, for description of the Gajo Loeös divorce ceremony (rotan-cutting), for the description of men's three ideals, and for wedding blessings shouted at the groom.

## Parties

by [[J. Kreemer|Kreemer]]

## Subject

Multi-volume scholarly work on Aceh; deel I cited for word-play jokes from the [[actie-tijd]], for description of the [[Gajo Loeös]] divorce ceremony ([[Rattan (Aceh)|rotan]]-cutting), for the description of men's three ideals, and for wedding blessings shouted at the groom.

## Significance

Kreemer's work on Atjeh (deel I) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
