**Expanded souvereiniteitsverklaring (15 articles)** (later 1870s/early 1880s) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Expansion of the original 6-article model to 15 articles.

## Date

later 1870s/early 1880s

## Parties

Dutch government ↔ oelèëbalangs

## Subject

Expansion of the original 6-article model to 15 articles.

## Significance

Expanded souvereiniteitsverklaring (15 articles) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
