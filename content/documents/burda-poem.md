The **Burda** (Arabic: البردة, *al-Burda*, “the mantle”) is a qaṣīda composed by the 13th‑century Arab poet **Al-Busiri** in praise of the Prophet Muhammad. The poem is renowned for its eloquent descriptions of the Prophet’s virtues, miracles, and intercessory power. The OCR text provides a complete **Malay translation** of the *Burda*, accompanied by linguistic and exegetical footnotes that clarify archaic Malay terms and Arabic roots. The *Burda* has been widely recited, copied, and commented upon throughout the Muslim world, forming a central text in popular devotional practice.
