The ***Uittreksel uit het *stamboek* ("service record") van Henri Nicolaas Alfred Swart*** was the extract from the official service record of [[H. N. A. Swart]], printed as an appendix to [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]]'s *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]*. It gives the precise dates of his appointments, promotions, wounds and decorations from [[1880]] to [[1922]].

## Personal particulars

Born [[12 October]] [[1863]] at [[Tjibitoeng]] in the East Indies. Father: [[Klaas Swart|Klaas]]. Mother: [[Cornelia Maria Johanna Maria Gouay]]. Afflicted with myopic astigmatism of 3/4 D. in both eyes. Married on [[22 May]] [[1901]] to Miss [[Victorina Maria Geertruida Stadlmair]], then twenty-three years old. These notes on the *Staat van Dienst* were signed on [[30 September]] [[1918]] by Lieutenant-General [[Van Rietschoten]] as army commander.

## Netherlands service

- [[16 October]] [[1880]]: voluntarily engaged as a soldier for six years with the 8th Regiment of Infantry, with a bounty of eighty guilders.
- [[16 March]]: corporal.
- [[30 October]]: sergeant.
- [[16 October]] [[1886]]: re-engaged for a year.
- [[21 November]] [[1886]]: appointed *2de luitenant* in the Infantry of the Army in the Netherlands Indies, by His Majesty's decree no. 18 of [[24 November]] [[1886]], and *à la suite* of the [[Koloniaal Werfdepôt]].

## Indies service

- [[7 May]] [[1887]]: departed for the East Indies with the *s.s. Zuid-Holland*.
- [[17 June]] [[1887]]: arrived at [[Batavia]].
- [[22 June]] [[1887]]: posted to the 6th Battalion.
- [[20 August]] [[1891]]: transferred to the [[Korps Pupillen te Gombong]], and in connection therewith carried *à la suite* of his arm.
- [[7 April]] [[1892]]: *1ste Luitenant*, with the stipulation that he remain posted to the Korps Pupillen and carried *à la suite*.
- [[27 October]] [[1894]]: transferred to the *Subsistentenkader* at [[Semarang]], and accordingly brought back from *à la suite*.
- [[29 March]] [[1895]]: appointed adjutant to the 11th Battalion.
- [[11 December]] [[1895]]: honourably relieved of his adjutancy and transferred to the *Subsistentenkader* at Batavia.
- [[23 January]]: transferred to the IInd Division of the [[Departement van Oorlog]] (Head Bureau of Infantry).
- [[24 July]] [[1898]]: *Kapitein*.
- [[22 November]] [[1898]]: transferred to the 7th Battalion.
- [[30 May]] [[1899]]: transferred to the 12th Battalion.
- [[15 May]] [[1900]]: transferred to the 18th Battalion.
- [[6 July]] [[1900]]: granted a year's leave to Europe for long service, with retention of claim to promotion.
- [[18 July]] [[1900]]: departed thither with the *s.s. Lawoe*.
- [[21 August]] [[1901]]: returned from leave with the *s.s. Koning Willem III*.
- [[17 September]] [[1901]]: posted to the 12th Battalion.
- [[8 February]] [[1902]]: transferred to the 1st Garrison Battalion of [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] and in connection therewith *à la suite* of his arm.
- [[27 March]] [[1902]]: charged with the government of the division of the [[XXV Moekims]] with the southern settlements (Afdeeling [[Groot Atjeh]]).
- [[17 July]] [[1902]]: relieved of the government of the sub-division *[[sagi]]* of the XXV Moekims and southern settlements and charged with the government of the sub-division [[Poelò Raja]] with the title of *Civiel Gezaghebber*.
- [[12 October]] [[1903]]: honourably relieved of the government of the sub-division Poelò Raja with thanks for the good services rendered by him to the Land; and charged with the government of the sub-division [[Lho' Seumawè]], Afdeeling Onderhoorigheden van Atjeh, the commission to be regarded as temporary.
- [[24 October]] [[1903]]: extraordinarily promoted *Majoor*, for outstanding deeds and for extraordinary military talents demonstrated in action.
- [[4 June]] [[1904]]: his transfer to the 12th Battalion approved.
- [[3 March]] [[1905]]: temporarily charged with the Civil and Military Authority in the residency of the [[South and East Division of Borneo]], and in connection therewith carried *à la suite* of his arm, at a salary of 1,250 guilders a month and the further lawful advantages attached to the post of resident of that province, with the stipulation that he exercise those functions under the title of *Civiel en Militair Resident der South and East Division of Borneo*, and that for the duration of the commission his military income stand still, free medical treatment for himself and his family remaining assured.
- [[3 April]] [[1905]]: honourably relieved of the government of the sub-division Lho' Seumawè with thanks.
- [[24 October]] [[1905]]: *Luitenant-kolonel*, with the stipulation that he remain temporarily charged with the Civil and Military Authority in Borneo and carried *à la suite*.
- [[9 July]] [[1906]]: by virtue of the power referred to in article 43 of the *[[Regeeringsreglement|Reglement op het beleid der Regeering in Nederlandsch-Indië]]*, temporarily charged with effect from [[3 September]] [[1906]] with the Civil and Military Authority in the [[Gouvernement Celebes en Onderhoorigheden]], at a salary of 1,500 guilders a month, with the title of *Civiel en Militair Gouverneur van Celebes en Onderhoorigheden* and simultaneously Military Commander of [[Menado]] and [[Timor]] en Onderhoorigheden.
- [[4 May]] [[1908]]: with thanks for the many and weighty services rendered by him in Celebes, appointed Military Commander of Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden and simultaneously Governor of that province, at 1,500 guilders a month, with the title of *Civielen en Militairen Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden*.
- [[10 June]] [[1908]]: assumed the government of Atjeh.
- [[31 March]] [[1909]]: extraordinarily promoted *Kolonel*, for outstanding military deeds and extraordinary military talents demonstrated in action.
- [[18 November]] [[1911]]: *Generaal-Majoor*.
- [[6 September]] [[1912]]: *Luitenant-Generaal*.
- [[12 August]] [[1918]]: appointed with effect from September [[1918]] Vice-President of the [[Raad van Nederlandsch-Indië]], and honourably discharged from Her Majesty's military service with thanks, with a pension of 9,000 guilders a year, suspended for the duration of the Vice-Presidency.
- [[4 January]] [[1922]]: honourably discharged at his own request from the service of the State, with renewed thanks.

## Campaigns and wounds

Campaigns recorded: operations against Atjeh in [[1890]], [[1898]], [[1899]], [[1900]], [[1901]], [[1902]], [[1903]], [[1904]] and [[1905]]; operations in the Doesoen and Dajak lands in [[1905]] and [[1906]]; operations in Celebes in [[1906]], [[1907]] and [[1908]]; operations in the Gouvernement of Celebes en Onderhoorigheden in [[1908]]; operations in Atjeh in [[1908]], [[1909]], [[1910]], [[1912]], [[1913]], [[1914]], [[1915]] and [[1916]].

Wounds:
- [[11 June]] [[1890]]: on the [[Attack on the Boekit Roembia (11 June 1890)|march to the Boekit Roembiah]] ([[Idi]], Atjeh), a gunshot wound in the left upper arm.
- [[8 May]] [[1899]]: at the storming of the *kampong* [[Bambi]] ([[Pidië]], Atjeh), a grazing shot above the right temple.
- [[21 November]] [[1899]]: at the taking of the fortifications on the [[Glé Risé]] (Atjeh), a blood-blister on the belly from a thrown stone.

## Decorations and rewards

- Knight 4th Class of the [[Militaire Willemsorde]], for his conduct at various encounters with the enemy in Atjeh during [[1890]], royal decree of [[30 August]] [[1891]] no. 2.
- *Eereteeken voor belangrijke krijgsbedrijven*: Atjeh [[1873]]–[[1890]]; Atjeh [[1906]]–[[1910]]; South Celebes [[1905]]–[[1908]]; Lesser Sunda Islands [[1905]]–[[1909]].
- A sum of 825 guilders as reward for translating into Malay the *Uittreksel uit de Recrutenschool* with the principal duties and rules of conduct of the soldier and of the group commander in action, decree no. 13 of [[15 September]] [[1898]].
- Knight 3rd Class of the *Militaire Willemsorde* ("Military William Order"), for distinction in the operations in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden from [[1 June]] [[1899]] to [[31 December]] of that year, royal decree of [[13 July]] [[1900]] no. 29.
- The distinguishing mark for long Netherlands service as an officer, awarded [[6 December]] [[1901]].
- Commander of the Militaire Willemsorde, for distinction in the operations on the island of [[Soembawa]] between [[16 February]] and [[9 May]] [[1908]], royal decree of [[2 September]] [[1909]] no. 41.
- Knight in the [[Order of the Netherlands Lion]], royal decree of May [[1909]] no. 7.
- Adjutant in Extraordinary Service to Her Majesty the Queen, royal decree no. 65 of [[7 April]] [[1916]].
- Grand Officer in the [[Orde van Oranje-Nassau]], royal decree of [[27 August]] [[1921]] no. 49.

## The three citations

**First distinction, as second lieutenant.** Attached as *2e luitenant* to the expeditionary 6th Battalion Infantry at [[Idi]]. "On 11 June 1890, during the attack on the enemy positions on the left bank of the [[Idi-rivier|Idi river]], he was the first to force his way into the fortification situated on the summit of the Roembia and defended almost to the last moment; a moment later he was wounded — a shot through the muscles of the left upper arm — had himself bandaged and immediately thereafter, at the head of his section, again took part in the further operations." Appointed Knight of the M.W.O. 4th Class.

**Second distinction, as captain.** The capture of the enemy positions behind the [[Pasè-rivier|Pasei river]], as commander of a company of the 12th Battalion Infantry. "On [[21 June]] [[1899]], during the operations at [[Geudong]] in support of the *[[Corps of Military Constabulary of Atjeh and Dependencies|marechaussee]]*, being sent with his company to the foremost line and coming up against the heavily swollen Pasè river, he at once took energetic measures for the river crossing despite the violent enemy fire delivered at short range. He then crossed the river and, having gathered some seventeen soldiers about him on the right bank, drove the superior enemy with great skill and daring out of his more southerly position by a flank attack, inflicting heavy loss, and afterwards maintained himself in the captured position while awaiting the arrival of further troops." And the capture of the enemy position on the [[Glé Risé]]: "On 21 November following, leading his company with judgement and with great intrepidity in the attack on the very strong position stubbornly defended by the enemy." Appointed Knight of the M.W.O. 3rd Class.

**Third distinction, as colonel.** "For the manner in which, in the years 1905, 1906, 1907 and the beginning of 1908, he acquitted himself of the heavy task of Civil and Military Resident in the South and East Division of Borneo and of Civil and Military Governor of Celebes and Dependencies, and for the outstanding deeds of courage, judgement and loyalty shown by him as commander and general leader of various operations and engagements in those provinces." Appointed Commander of the M.W.O.

## Significance

The *stamboek* extract is the documentary skeleton of du Croo's biography and the source of all its precise dates. It also fixes the discrepancy over Swart's initials: it gives the name as **Henri Nicolaas Alfred Swart**, though other sources render the initials differently.
