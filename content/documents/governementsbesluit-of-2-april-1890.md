**Governementsbesluit of 2 April 1890** was an official decree issued by the Governor-General of the Netherlands East Indies establishing the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]].

## Key Provisions

- Established a specialized foot corps of light counter-insurgency troops to operate within the [[Concentratie-Stelsel|geconcentreerde stelling]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]].
- Set an initial strength of 1 division consisting of 1 captain-commander, 1 first lieutenant, 1 European sergeant-major, 1 European quartermaster, 12 European sergeants, 12 non-European sergeants, 12 non-European corporals, and 180 non-European privates.
- Mandated police duties, monitoring pepper and food smuggling, and preparing conditions for eventual civil governance under the [[Binnenlandsch Bestuur in Atjeh|Binnenlandsch Bestuur]].

## Significance

This decree laid the legal foundation for forty years of Marechaussee military operations in Aceh ([[1890]]–[[1930]]), creating what became the primary Dutch counter-insurgency instrument during the Aceh War.
