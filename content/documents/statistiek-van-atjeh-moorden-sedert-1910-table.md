**Statistiek van Atjeh-moorden sedert 1910 (table)** is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Year-by-year table of atjeh-moorden 1910-1938 (1st half year): total averaging ~4 per year (75% of listed cases after deducting 25% amok).

## Subject

Year-by-year table of [[Atjeh-moorden|atjeh-moorden]] [[1910]]-[[1938]] (1st half year): total averaging ~4 per year (75% of listed cases after deducting 25% amok). Highest years [[1913]], [[1917]] (11 each), [[1910]], [[1925]], [[1926]], [[1928]] (8-10). Lowest years [[1920]], [[1936]], [[1938]] (1 each).

## Significance

Statistiek van [[Atjeh-moorden]] sedert [[1910]] (table) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
