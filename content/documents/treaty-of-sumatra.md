The **Treaty of Sumatra** (*Sumatra-tractaat*) was an international convention signed on [[2 November]] [[1871]] between the United Kingdom and the Kingdom of the Netherlands that altered colonial spheres of influence in Sumatra.

## Key Provisions

Under Article I of the treaty, Great Britain withdrew all formal reservations against the expansion of Dutch sovereignty across Sumatra, effectively nullifying the independence guarantee contained in the [[Treaty of London]]. In exchange, the Netherlands ceded its colonial possessions on the Gold Coast (Guinea, West Africa) to Britain and granted equal commercial rights to British traders in Sumatra.

The treaty granted the Netherlands a free hand in Aceh, leading directly to the [[First Aceh Expedition]] in [[1873]].
