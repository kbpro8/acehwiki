**De Pepercultuur in de Buitenbezittingen** ("Pepper cultivation in the Outer Possessions") was instalment V of the *Mededeelingen van het [[Encyclopaedisch Bureau]]*, cited by [[D. W. N. de Boer]] as the reference work for the pepper economy of [[Aceh|Atjeh]] in his account of the *[[Contract of the Atjehnese pepper planter|contract of the Atjehnese pepper planter]]*.

## Citation

Introducing the pepper contract, De Boer directed the reader: "Vergelijk: *De Pepercultuur in de Buitenbezittingen*, afl. V van de Mededeelingen van het Encyclopaedisch Bureau; vgl. ook *De Buitenbezittingen: Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden*, Deel II, afl. 2, blz. 148 vlg." The two works together supplied the general economic background against which he described the particular legal forms of the Atjehnese pepper contract — the *[[Aneu' seuneubô|aneu' seuneubô]]*, the *[[Peutoeha pangkaj|peutoeha pangkaj]]*, the *[[Wathé pangkaj|wathé pangkaj]]* and the *[[Boh è|boh è]]*.
