**Voorloopige uitkomsten (volkstelling 1930)** (1930) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Preliminary results of the 1930 census of the Dutch East Indies, showing a 36% increase in Aceh's population since 1920 — but for Groot-Atjeh and Pidië no 1920 data exist, so this is only an estimate.

## Date

[[1930]]

## Parties

Dutch colonial government

## Subject

Preliminary results of the [[1930]] census of the [[Dutch East Indies]], showing a 36% increase in Aceh's population since [[1920]] — but for [[Groot-Atjeh]] and [[Pidië]] no [[1920]] data exist, so this is only an estimate.

## Significance

Voorloopige uitkomsten (volkstelling [[1930]]) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
