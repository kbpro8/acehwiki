**Londensche Conventie van 1814** (1814) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. England returns most Dutch colonies after Napoleon's fall, but keeps some parts (e.g., some regions on the Westkust of Sumatra).

## Date

[[1814]]

## Parties

[[Engeland (England)|England]] and [[Netherlands|the Netherlands]]

## Subject

[[Engeland (England)|England]] returns most Dutch colonies after Napoleon's fall, but keeps some parts (e.g., some regions on the [[Westkust]] of Sumatra). Atjeh remained an independent native state.

## Significance

[[London|Londensche]] Conventie van [[1814]] is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
