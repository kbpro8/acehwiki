The **Proclamation of Annexation of Aceh** was an official decree issued on [[31 January]] [[1874]] by Civil Government Commissioner [[Jan van Swieten]] following the [[Encirclement and Fall of the Kraton]] and the death of [[Sultan Alauddin Mahmud Syah IX]].

## Provisions and Impact

The proclamation formally declared that the [[Sultanate of Aceh]] was abolished by right of conquest and its territory incorporated into the Netherlands East Indies. The decree was approved by the Dutch Minister of Colonies on [[2 February]] [[1874]]. However, Acehnese leaders rejected the decree, continuing anti-colonial resistance for decades under [[Tuanku Hashim]] and [[Panglima Polim]].

## Account from *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*

By right of conquest, the realm of Aceh declared property of the Dutch-Indian Government; Dutch government stepped into [[Sultan of Aceh|the Sultan]]'s place; for the time being only [[Groot-Atjeh]] to be directly managed.

*Source: J. Jongejans, Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu (Baarn: Hollandia Drukkerij N.V., c. 1938), pp. 396.*
