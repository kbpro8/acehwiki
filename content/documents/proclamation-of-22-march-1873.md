**Proclamation of 22 March 1873** was the official manifesto issued by Commander-in-Chief General [[J. H. R. Köhler]] on [[22 March]] [[1873]], formally stating the war aims of the [[Dutch East Indies Government]] and precipitating the invasion of the [[Aceh Sultanate]] by the [[First Aceh Expedition]].

## Text and War Aims

Issued on board the flagship before landing at [[Meuraxa]], the [[Proclamation of 22 March 1873]] declared that the purpose of the military expedition was "to compel the rulers of the State of Aceh by force of arms to satisfy the demands set by the Government."

In his [[1875]] historical assessment, General [[G. G. Booms]] emphasized that the objectives defined in the [[Proclamation of 22 March 1873]] were broad and political, rather than restricted to the mere tactical capture of [[Kraton of Aceh]]. 

## Debate Over Strategic Objectives

General [[G. G. Booms]] cited the [[Proclamation of 22 March 1873]] to refute critics like General [[F. V. A. de Stuers]] who claimed that the capture of the kraton was the sole required success of the [[First Aceh Expedition]]. Because the proclamation demanded the complete submission of the Acehnese leadership, Booms argued that the [[Dutch East Indies Government]] should have planned for a long-term occupation and adequately provisioned the army before launching the [[1873]] campaign.
