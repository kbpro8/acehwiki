**Van Loon's investigation report** (1920) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Investigation of insanity in the gewest Aceh and onderhoorigheden; estimated serious insane at >1000.

## Date

[[1920]]

## Subject

Investigation of insanity in the [[gewest]] Aceh and [[Onderhoorigheden|onderhoorigheden]]; estimated serious insane at >[[1000]]. Recommended building a good asylum in Aceh itself.

## Significance

Van Loon's investigation report is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
