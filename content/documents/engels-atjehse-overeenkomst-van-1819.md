**Engels-Atjehse overeenkomst van 1819** (1819) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. England made an agreement with the Sultan of Atjeh by which control of Atjeh's foreign relations came entirely to England.

## Date

[[1819]]

## Parties

[[Engeland (England)|England]] and [[Sultan of Aceh|the Sultan]] of Atjeh

## Subject

[[Engeland (England)|England]] made an agreement with [[Sultan of Aceh|the Sultan]] of Atjeh by which control of Atjeh's foreign relations came entirely to [[Engeland (England)|England]].

## Significance

Engels-Atjehse overeenkomst van [[1819]] is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
