**Decree 112/K of 1906** (*Besluit 112/K*) was an administrative regulation issued on [[22 December]] [[1906]] by Governor [[Gotfried Coenraad Ernst van Daalen]] to reform pepper export levies in Aceh.

## Provisions

1. Mandated pepper weighing in metric units (pikuls of 61.75 kg) and calculation of duties in Netherlands Indies Guilders.
2. Replaced fixed customary levies with an ad valorem tax equal to 10% of export market value (*Wathe Oeleebalang*).
3. Standardized the supervisor fee (*Adat Peutoeha*) at 0.35 guilders per pikul.
4. Doubled export tax rates for white pepper.

The decree was subsequently modified by Governor [[H.E. Swart]] in [[1910]] to establish a fixed tax of 1.50 guilders per pikul.
