***Vierhonderd jaren missie in Nederlandsch-Indië, 1593–1934*** ("Four Hundred Years of Mission in the Netherlands Indies, 1593–1934") was the commemorative jubilee volume published at [[Batavia|Batavia]] in [[1934]] by the *Algemeen Comité van Voorbereiding tot de Feestelijke Herdenking van 400 jaar Missie in Ned.-Indië* (General Committee of Preparation for the Festive Commemoration of 400 Years of Mission in the Netherlands Indies), on the occasion of the four-hundredth anniversary of the establishment of the first permanent Catholic mission station in the archipelago at [[Ternate|Ternate]] in [[1593]]. The volume — a substantial compilation of the institutional history of the Dutch-colonial Catholic mission, organised by vicariate and by mission station — is the principal documentary source for the [[Catholic Mission of Koeta Radja-Atjeh|Catholic mission of Koeta Radja-Atjeh]] and for the biography of [[Pastoor H. C. Verbraak|Pastoor H. C. Verbraak]], the *soldatenpastoor* of [[Acheh|Atjeh]].

## Publication and occasion

The volume was published at [[Batavia|Batavia]] in [[1934]] by the *Algemeen Comité van Voorbereiding* — the General Committee of Preparation for the jubilee, formed by the Central Council of Catholic Social Bonds (*Centrale Raad van Kath. Sociale Bonden*) in response to an article by Mgr. [[Visser|Visser]] in *De Koerier* calling for a general celebration in the Indies of the four-hundredth anniversary of the first permanent Catholic mission station. The committee — under the name *Algemeen Comité van Voorbereiding tot de Feestelijke Herdenking van 400 jaar Missie in Ned.-Indië* — solicited the formation of an *Eere-comité* (Honorary Committee) and, through circulars to the Catholic Social Bonds and the clergy, organised both the ecclesiastical and the extra-ecclesiastical celebrations of the jubilee.

## Scope and structure

The volume is organised by vicariate (Batavia, [[Padang|Padang]], etc.) and, within each vicariate, by mission station (*statie*). The chapters cover the institutional history of the Catholic mission from the Portuguese period (with the [[Martyrdom of Dionysius and Redemptus at Atjeh (1638)|martyrdom of Dionysius and Redemptus at Atjeh in 1638]]) through the Jesuit, Capuchin, and Franciscan periods, to the early-twentieth-century division of the mission field among the various congregations. Each vicariate chapter concludes with an enumeration of the *staties* — including, for the [[Padang|Padang]] vicariate, the [[Catholic Mission of Koeta Radja-Atjeh|Koeta Radja-Atjeh station]].

## The Atjeh-related content

The volume is the principal documentary source for several Atjeh-related subjects:

- The [[Martyrdom of Dionysius and Redemptus at Atjeh (1638)|martyrdom of the Carmelites Dionysius and Redemptus at Atjeh in 1638]] — recorded in the chapter on the Portuguese period (pp. 54–55).
- The beatification of the two martyrs by Pope [[Leo XIII|Leo XIII]] on [[25 March]] [[1900]] — recorded in the chapter on the vicariate of Mgr. [[E. S. Luypen|E. S. Luypen]] (p. 53).
- The [[1885|1885]] consecration of the first Catholic church at [[Koeta Radja|Kota-Radja]] by [[Pastoor H. C. Verbraak|Pastoor Verbraak]] — recorded in the chapter on the Jesuit period (p. 52).
- The [[1896|1896]] building of the chapel-of-ease at [[Olehleh|Olehleh]] — recorded in the same chapter (p. 52).
- The [[Pedir Expedition (1898)|1898 Pedir-expedition]] and the extension of Verbraak's chaplaincy to [[Pedir|Pedir]] — recorded in the Verbraak sketch (p. 129).
- The [[1898|1898]] account of the Koeta Radja-Atjeh station under the [[Zusters van Schijndel|Zusters van Schijndel]] — recorded in the chapter on the [[Padang|Padang]] vicariate (p. 96).
- The biography of [[Pastoor H. C. Verbraak|Pastoor H. C. Verbraak]] — the sketch "*Pastoor H. C. Verbraak*" (pp. 127–133), reproducing [[S. Kalff|S. Kalff]]'s "*Een trits Indische Pastoors*" from *Koloniaal Missie Tijdschrift*, Jaargang XII.
- The [[Unveiling of the Verbraak bronze statue (27 January 1922)|unveiling of the Verbraak bronze statue at Bandoeng on 27 January 1922]] — recorded at the close of the Verbraak sketch (p. 133).
- The foundation of the [[Pastoor Verbraak-Stichting|Pastoor Verbraak-Stichting]] in the early [[1920s]] — recorded in the chapter on Mgr. [[A. P. F. van Velsen|van Velsen]]'s vicariate (p. 62).

## Significance

The *Vierhonderd jaren missie* volume is significant for the encyclopedia of [[Acheh|Achehnese]] history as the principal documentary source for the Catholic institutional presence in Atjeh — and, in particular, for the biography of [[Pastoor H. C. Verbraak|Pastoor H. C. Verbraak]] and the history of the [[Catholic Mission of Koeta Radja-Atjeh|Catholic mission of Koeta Radja-Atjeh]]. The volume's reproduction of [[S. Kalff|Kalff]]'s sketch of Verbraak — the principal biographical source for the *soldatenpastoor* — and its record of the [[1885|1885]] Kotta-Radja church, the [[1896|1896]] Olehleh chapel, the [[1898|1898]] Pedir extension, the [[Zusters van Schijndel|Zusters van Schijndel]] schools, the [[1922|1922]] Bandoeng statue, and the [[Pastoor Verbraak-Stichting|Pastoor Verbraak-Stichting]] together constitute the documentary foundation of the Catholic mission record of Atjeh, and the principal source on which the present encyclopedia's articles on those subjects rest.
