**The *Ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm*** ("The Straight Path") is a Malay handbook of religious duties (*plichtenleer*) composed by [[Raniri|Nuruddin ar-Raniri]] at the command of [[Iskandar Thani|Sultan Iskandar Thani]] of [[Acheh]]. [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] noted that the work was written in March [[1638]] — a date which provides one of the firm chronological anchor-points in the early history of the [[Acheh|Achehnese Sultanate]]. In the [[1955]] edition of Raniri's two Malay treatises, [[P. Voorhoeve|Voorhoeve]] corrected the long-standing assumption that the *Ṣirāṭ* had been composed before Raniri's arrival in Acheh: although begun in [[1634]], the work was — as [[H. Djajadiningrat|Djajadiningrat]] demonstrated in *TBG* 69 — only completed in [[1644]]. The treatise survives in an edition printed at [[Mecca]] in 1316 Hegira on the margin of an edition of the *Fatḥ al-muʿīn*.

## Composition and chronology

Voorhoeve's introduction to the *Twee Maleise geschriften* confesses candidly that he had himself, in an earlier publication (*BKI* 107, p. 357), erroneously supposed the *Ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm* to have been composed before Raniri's arrival in Acheh. Djajadiningrat's article in *TBG* 69, pp. 109–111 — which Voorhoeve had overlooked — established that although the work was begun in [[1634]], it was only completed ten years later, in [[1644]], the very year in which Raniri returned to [[Ranir]]. The knowledge of Malay letters displayed in the *Ṣirāṭ* need not, therefore, have been acquired before [[1637]]. The date of completion in [[1644]] makes the *Ṣirāṭ* one of the last works Raniri composed in Acheh before his departure.

## Purpose and content

The *Ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm* is, in genre, a Malay handbook of religious duties — a Shāfiʿite *fiqh* of the ʿibādāt adapted for Malay-speaking Muslims. It was composed, like the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān*]] and the [[Hudjdjat as-siddiq li-daf az-zindiq|*Ḥujjat aṣ-ṣiddīq*]], at the command of [[Iskandar Thani]] and as part of the same project of orthodox reform: to provide the Achehnese faithful with a reliable guide to the practical observances of Islam according to the Shāfiʿite school, in the *[[Bahasa Djawi]]* that was the language of the Achehnese court. The work is printed on the margin of the *Fatḥ al-muʿīn* in the Mecca edition of 1316 H — that is, in the same Mecca edition in which Raniri's testimony concerning his arrival in Acheh in [[1637]] was preserved by Snouck Hurgronje.

## Significance

The *Ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm* is the first systematic Malay handbook of Shāfiʿite *fiqh* and a foundational text of Malay religious prose. Its composition at the command of [[Iskandar Thani]] places it at the very centre of the project of orthodox reform that produced the suppression of the [[Wujudiyyah]] mysticism of [[Hamzah Pansuri]] and [[Shaikh Shamsuddin of Sumatra|Shamsuddin of Pasai]]: it provided, for the new dispensation, the manual of observances by which the faithful were to regulate their daily conduct. The Mecca edition of 1316 H preserved the work in a form free from the local Achehnese tendency to idealise or rearrange the past, and it is from this edition that Snouck Hurgronje drew Raniri's testimony fixing the death of [[Sultan Iskandar Muda|Meukuta Alam]] in [[1636]].
