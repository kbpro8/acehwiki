**Atjeh als land voor handel en bedrijf** is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Book sharply criticising the 'dienst van den schijn' of the Swart era; advises economic development and unification of chiefs for common deliberation.

## Subject

Book sharply criticising the '[[Dienst van den schijn|dienst van den schijn]]' of the [[Gouverneur Swart|Swart]] era; advises economic development and unification of chiefs for common [[Mupakat|deliberation]].

## Significance

Atjeh als land voor handel en bedrijf is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
