**De Atjehers** is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Scholarly study of the Acehnese (cited extensively; written ~45 years before this book)

## Subject

Scholarly study of the Acehnese (cited extensively; written ~45 years before this book)

## Significance

De Atjehers is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
