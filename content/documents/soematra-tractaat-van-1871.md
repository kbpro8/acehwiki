**Soematra-tractaat van 1871** (1871) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Sumatra Treaty giving Holland the free hand in Atjeh, resolving the difficulties created by the 1824 treaty.

## Date

[[1871]]

## Parties

[[Engeland (England)|England]] and [[Netherlands|the Netherlands]]

## Subject

Sumatra Treaty giving [[Leyden (Leiden)|Holland]] the free hand in Atjeh, resolving the difficulties created by the [[1824]] treaty.

## Significance

Soematra-tractaat van [[1871]] is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
