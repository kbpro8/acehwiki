**Weker-artikelen in the Avonpost** (during Van Daalen's governorate) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Critical articles that prompted a high-level investigation leading to Lt.-Gen.

## Date

during [[Gouverneur Van Daalen|Van Daalen]]'s governorate

## Subject

Critical articles that prompted a high-level investigation leading to Lt.-Gen. [[Gouverneur Van Daalen|Van Daalen]]'s dismissal and the [[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]]'s exile to [[Amboina (Z.M. stoomschip)|Amboina]].

## Significance

Weker-artikelen in the Avonpost is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
