**Atjeh (book by H. C. Zentgraaff)** was the book on [[Atjeh]] by [[H. C. Zentgraaff]] from which [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]] borrowed several of the fullest narrative passages of *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart]]*.

Its judgement stands as the epigraph of du Croo's chapter on the patrols: "The truth is that the Acehnese, men and women alike, have in general fought splendidly for what they saw as their national or religious ideal. Among those fighters there is a very great number of men and women who would be the pride of any people; they yield nothing to the most brilliant of our own figures of war." Du Croo reprinted from it the death of [[Pang Nanggròë]] (p. 110), the pursuit by [[W. J. Mosselman]] and the deaths of [[Tgk. Sjeh di Paja Bakong]] and [[Tjoet Meutia]], the tracking and death of [[Tgk. di Barat]], and the career and death of [[Tgk. di Mata Ië]] (p. 97).
