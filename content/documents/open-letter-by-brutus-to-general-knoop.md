**Open Letter by Brutus to General Knoop** was a high-profile, anonymous political and military pamphlet published in the Netherlands in [[1875]] under the pseudonym "Brutus," addressed to Dutch military historian General [[W. J. Knoop]]. The pamphlet launched a bitter attack on the generalship of [[J. van Swieten]] during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]].

## Arguments and Attack on Van Swieten

The author writing as "Brutus" accused General [[J. van Swieten]] of excessive caution, lack of tactical decisiveness, and premature declarations of victory after the capture of [[Kraton of Aceh]] in January [[1874]]. The pamphlet contrasted [[J. van Swieten]]'s deliberate style with the energetic, offensive tactics pursued by General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]] during [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]] in [[1875]] and [[1876]].

## Rebuttals in Contemporary Historiography

The publication of the [[Open Letter by Brutus to General Knoop]] triggered extensive rebuttals. General [[G. G. Booms]] noted in [[1875]] that the editorial invitation in *De Gids* to review "Brutus" prompted him to address broader misconceptions regarding both the first and second expeditions. 

Furthermore, the anonymous author of *Atjeh gedurende de tweede expeditie en later* ([[1876]]) wrote his entire treatise to dismantle the claims of "Brutus," demonstrating through official dispatches and troop statistics that General [[J. van Swieten]]'s methods were fully justified by logistical and environmental realities in the [[Aceh Sultanate]].
