***Geschiedenis en genealogie van het geslacht Van Son, eertijds geheeten Van Broechoven*** ("History and Genealogy of the Van Son Family, formerly called Van Broechoven") was a Dutch family genealogy compiled by **H. J. A. van Son** — Deel III: *De Oudere Linie (Vervolg tot heden)* (Volume III: The Older Line, Continuation to the Present) — printed at [[Dordrecht|Dordrecht]] by the Schefferdrukkerij in [[1951]]. The genealogy traces the older line of the Van Son family from the Tilburg *Van Broechovens* of the fourteenth century through to the mid-twentieth century, and it is the principal documentary source for the Atjeh-related careers of **[[Livinus Diederik Mattheus van Son|Livinus Diederik Mattheus van Son]]** (veteran of the [[First Aceh Expedition (1873)|first]] and [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)|second]] Atjeh expeditions) and of his son-in-law **[[Otto Louis Brewer|Otto Louis Brewer]]** (K.N.I.L. artillery officer, Atjeh posting [[1904]]).

## Publication

The genealogy was printed at [[Dordrecht|Dordrecht]] by the **Schefferdrukkerij** in [[1951]]. The volume is Deel III (Volume III) of the family history, covering the *Oudere Linie* (Older Line) from its continuity into the nineteenth and twentieth centuries. The compiler, H. J. A. van Son, was a member of the family whose genealogy he edited. The volume is preserved in the [[Koninklijke Bibliotheek|Koninklijke Bibliotheek]] under the shelfmark BIT TH 0338 0919.

## Scope and structure

The genealogy traces the older line of the Van Son family from **Rutger Jans van Son** — who left [[Tilburg|Tilburg]] around the end of the sixteenth century — through sixteen generations to the mid-twentieth century. The family's successive seats are recorded as [[Besoyen|Besoyen]], [['s-Grevelduin-Kapelle|'s-Grevelduin-Kapelle]], [[Willemstad|Willemstad]], [[Klundt|Klundt]], [[Dordrecht|Dordrecht]], [[Haarlem|Haarlem]], [[Negapatnam|Negapatnam]], [[Rotterdam|Rotterdam]], and [[Gellicum|Gellicum]]. The nineteenth-century family divides into four branches — Tak A, Tak B, Tak C, and Tak D — of which Tak B (the "Breda–Amsterdam" branch) contains the Atjeh-related entries.

## The Atjeh-related content

The genealogy's Atjeh-related content is concentrated in Tak B, generation XIa — the entry for [[Livinus Diederik Mattheus van Son|Livinus Diederik Mattheus van Son]] (born [[Amsterdam|Amsterdam]], [[3 February]] [[1845]]; died ['s-Gravenhage|'s-Gravenhage]], [[20 December]] [[1917]]) and the entries for his children and grandchildren. The principal Atjeh-related documentary facts preserved in the genealogy are:

- [[Livinus Diederik Mattheus van Son|L. D. M. van Son]]'s participation in the [[First Aceh Expedition (1873)|first]] and [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)|second]] Atjeh expeditions of [[1873]] and [[1874]] with the 9th Battalion Infantry and the *expeditionnaire Bataillon Barisan van Madura*, and his receipt of the *Eereteeken voor belangrijke krijgsbedrijven* with clasp and the [[Atjeh-medaille|Atjeh-medaille]] — explicitly identified as "*vervaardigd uit het in Atjeh veroverde geschut*" ("manufactured from the artillery captured in Atjeh").
- The marriage of his daughter [[Leonie van Son|Leonie van Son]] to [[Otto Louis Brewer|Otto Louis Brewer]] at [[Salatiga|Salatiga]] on [[26 June]] [[1899]].
- The death of [[Leonie van Son|Leonie van Son]] at [[Koeta Radja|Koeta Radja]] (Atjeh, Sumatra) on [[17 November]] [[1904]] — the genealogy's explicit topographic gloss "*Koeta Radja (Atjeh, Sumatra)*" is the documentary fix of the family's Atjeh residence.
- The birth of [[Yvonne Caroline Brewer|Yvonne Caroline Brewer]] — daughter of [[Otto Louis Brewer|Otto Louis Brewer]] and [[Leonie van Son|Leonie van Son]] — at [[Indrapoera|Indrapoera]] (groot Atjeh) on [[1 Augustus]] [[1904]].
- The second marriage of [[Otto Louis Brewer|Otto Louis Brewer]] to his sister-in-law [[Adeline van Son|Adeline van Son]] at ['s-Gravenhage|'s-Gravenhage]] on [[27 October]] [[1908]].
- [[Otto Louis Brewer|Otto Louis Brewer]]'s documented rank — *gepens. generaal majoor tit. der Artillerie, Kon. Ned. Indisch Leger* — and his decoration as *ridder 4e klasse Militaire Willemsorde*.

## Significance

The *Geschiedenis en genealogie van het geslacht Van Son* is significant for the encyclopedia of [[Acheh|Achehnese]] history on three counts. First, it is the principal documentary source for the biography of [[Livinus Diederik Mattheus van Son|Livinus Diederik Mattheus van Son]] — and therefore for the documented company of the [[First Aceh Expedition (1873)|first]] and [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)|second]] Atjeh expeditions. Second, it is the rare primary source that explicitly identifies the [[Atjeh-medaille|Atjeh-medaille]] as struck from captured Atjehnese cannon — a documentary fact of the practice of striking campaign medals from captured enemy metal. Third, it is the documentary source for the multi-generational Atjeh connection of the Van Son–Brewer family — the campaigns of the [[1870s]] and the family residence of [[1904]] — which exemplifies the way the Atjeh war-zone, opened by the military, became in the next generation a site of colonial family life. The genealogy is thus both a family document and a documentary source for the colonial penetration of Atjeh.
