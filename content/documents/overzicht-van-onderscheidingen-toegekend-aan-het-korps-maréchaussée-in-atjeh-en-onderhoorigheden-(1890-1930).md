The **Overzicht van Onderscheidingen toegekend aan het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden van 1890-1930** was the register of honours conferred on members of the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]*, printed on the final two pages of the [[1930]] jubilee number of *[[Het Nieuws van den Dag voor Nederlandsch-Indië]]*. It lists, year by year, awards of the *[[Militaire Willemsorde|Militaire Willems-Orde]]* in the third and fourth classes, of the *[[Eeresabel]]*, and of the *[[Orde van Oranje-Nassau]]*.

Although its heading claims the period 1890–1930, the entries begin in [[1897]] with the award of the Militaire Willems-Orde 3rd class to Kapt. Mar. Jhr. [[G. J. W. C. Graafland]] — the officer whose name, as the editorial observed, "prijkt bovenaan op de lijst van geridderde Maréchaussées."

## The editors' note on the limits of the list

A footnote in the jubilee number explains that the editors would gladly have reproduced the long list in its entirety, but that lack of space compelled them to confine themselves to the officers and *onderluitenants*, and to report only those non-commissioned officers and men who had been granted the knight's cross of the *Militaire Willems-Orde* 3rd class — "een zeldzame onderscheiding, die in totaal slechts aan elf leden van het Korps Maréchaussée is toegekend" — a rare distinction, granted in total to only eleven members of the Corps Maréchaussée: five officers, one *onderluitenant*, four sergeants and one soldier, "den zoo bekenden [[Nussy]]."

The editors expressed the hope that in a memorial volume to be issued one day, all the decorated, without distinction, would be named. A further editorial note recorded that the forthcoming number of the *[[Indisch Militair Tijdschrift]]* was to be devoted entirely to the Korps Maréchaussée.
