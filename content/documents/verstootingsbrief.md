**verstootingsbrief** is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Letter by which a Muslim husband in Aceh notifies the village authorities that he has repudiated his wife; the wife need not be present.

## Parties

husband to village authorities

## Subject

Letter by which a Muslim husband in Aceh notifies the village authorities that he has repudiated his wife; the wife need not be present. He may even pronounce the three repudiations simultaneously.

## Significance

verstootingsbrief is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
