**Nieuwsblad voor Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden ('Atjeh blaadje')** (from 1900 (twice weekly)) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Twice-weekly newspaper carrying key events of the gewest and the mutatiestaat (transfer list) for civil and military landsdienaren.

## Date

from [[1900]] (twice weekly)

## Parties

Published by [[N.V. Atjeh-Drukkerij]] (filiaal of [[N.V. de Deli Courant te Medan]])

## Subject

Twice-weekly newspaper carrying key events of the [[gewest]] and the mutatiestaat (transfer list) for civil and military landsdienaren. Telegram service less important since radio became common.

## Significance

Nieuwsblad voor Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden ('Atjeh blaadje') is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
