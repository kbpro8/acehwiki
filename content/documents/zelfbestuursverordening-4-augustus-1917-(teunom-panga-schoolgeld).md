**The Zelfbestuursverordening of [[4 August]] [[1917]] (Teunom-Panga schoolgeld)** — printed in the [[1930]] compilation of *Zelfbestuursverordeningen Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden* as Regulation No. 15a — is the *schoolgeld* (school-fee) verordening (ordinance) of the *landschap* (territory) [[Teunom-Panga]] in the *onderafdeeling* (subdivision) [[Tjalang]] of the [[Westkust van Atjeh|afdeeling Westkust van Atjeh]] (district West Coast of Atjeh), issued by the *Zelfbestuur Teunom-Panga* (*Het Zelfbestuur van Teunom Panga*) for the levying of school fees on the *volksscholen* (village folk-schools) of the *landschap*. Approved by the *Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden* (Governor of Atjeh and Dependencies) on the same date, the regulation is accompanied — uniquely in the compilation — by a same-day *Gouverneursbesluit* (Governor's decree) (Reg. No. 15b) issued by the [[Civiel en Militair Gouverneur|Civiel en Militair Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] himself, which prescribes the administrative *voorschriften* (instructions) by which the school heads, the *kashouders* (cashiers) of the *onderafdeelingskas* (subdivision-treasury), and the *volksschool-opziener* (village-school inspector) are to administer the school fees. Reg. 15a took effect with the start of the school year 1917–1918.

## The school-fee structure

The substantive tariff is set out in *Fatsal 2 / Artikel 2* (Article 2) of the regulation. The recital announces the general policy: "*Soedah menimbang, perloe akan mengoetáp wang sekolah dari se- kolah desa dalam Landschap Teunom Panga. Overwegende, dat het aanbeve- ling verdient over te gaan tot de heffing van schoolgeld op de volksscholen in het Ländschap Teunom Panga*" — "Whereas it is desirable to proceed to the levying of *schoolgeld* on the *volksscholen* in the *landschap* Teunom-Panga." The effective date is fixed in *Fatsal 1 / Artikel 1*: "*Moelai dari permoelaan taheen sekolah 1917-1918 dikoctip wang sekolah dari sekolah* desa didalam Landsehap Teunom Panga. Met ingang van den sanvang van het schooljaar 1917-1918 zal op Volksscholen in het Landsehap Teunom Panga schoolgeld worden geheven*" — "With effect from the start of the school year 1917–1918 *schoolgeld* shall be levied on the *volksscholen* of the *landschap* Teunom-Panga."

The tariff itself is a three-class structure, set by the *Zelfbestuurder* *in overleg* (in consultation) with the *Controleur* (Controller) of the *landschap*:

> "De heffing van dit school- geld geschiedt volgens het onder- staand tarief."

The three classes are defined by payer-status: the first-class fee per single pupil is *f 0.50*, per second pupil *f 0.25*, per additional pupil *f 0.08*; the second-class *f 0.75 / f 0.15 / f 0.15*; the third-class *f 0.10 / f 0.35 / f 0.05*. The *Zelfbestuurder* is empowered under *Fatsal 2(b) / Artikel 2(b)* to reduce the fee, for pupils from the *hoogere standen der Inheemsche bevolking* (the higher ranks of the indigenous population) for whom the prescribed minimum would be difficult to pay, down to one-quarter of the minimum fee. The poor are exempted under *Fatsal 3 / Artikel 3*. *Fatsal 4 / Artikel 4* exempts the *poeasa-vacantie* (Ramadan fasting-month vacation) month and any school-closure weeks longer than one week; *Fatsal 5 / Artikel 5* allows the removal of a pupil after three months' non-payment.

## The accompanying Gouverneursbesluit (Reg. No. 15b)

The accompanying *Gouverneursbesluit van [[4 August]] [[1917]]* (Reg. No. 15b) is the only regulation in the compilation issued directly by the *Gouverneur* himself — rather than by the *oeleebalang* (territorial chiefs) with the *Gouverneur*'s *goedkeuring* (approval) — and the only one to retain the older dual title "*Civiel en Militair Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden*" (the pre-1920s form, superseded in the 1920s civil-government reform by the simpler "*Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden*"). The *besluit* is titled "*GOUVERNEURSEESLUTT van 4 Augustus 1917, houdende vaststel- ling van voorschriften nopens de door de hoofden der volksscholen te voeren administratie, de wijze van ver- antwoording en overstorting der door hen ontvangen schoolgelden, alsmede van het toezicht te dezer zake door den schoolopaiener uit te oefenen.*" Its recital explicitly cross-references the *schoolgeld*-regelingen (school-fee regulations) that the *Zelfbesturen* of the various *landschappen* had already adopted:

> "Gelezen de door de Zelfbesturen in dit gewest getroffen en door hem Gouverneur goedgekeurde Regelingen betreffende de heffing van schoolgeld op de volksscholen in bovengenoende landschappen; Willende overgaan tot het vaststellen van voorschriften nopens de door de hoofden dier volksseholen te voeren administratie, de wijze van verant- woording en overstorting in de onderafeeelings kas van de door hen out- landschaps vangen sehoolgelden alsmede van het toezicht te dezer zake door den volks- schoolopziener uit te oefenen"

— "Having read the Regelingen concerning the levying of *schoolgeld* on the *volksscholen* in the above-named *landschappen*, adopted by the *Zelfbesturen* of this *gewest* (region) and approved by him, the Gouverneur; Willing to proceed to the establishment of *voorschriften* concerning the administration to be kept by the heads of those *volksscholen*, the manner of accounting for and overpaying into the *onderafdeelingskas* the *schoolgelden* received by them, as well as the supervision in this matter to be exercised by the *volksschool-opziener*." Reg. 15b establishes three model-forms — Model A (the *staat* [statement] of exemptions), Model B (the *register* of *schoolgeld*-receipts), and Model C (the *opgave*-form [submission-form] for monthly *stortingen* [deposits], with *specificatie* [specification] on the back) — and prescribes their use by every school head, *kashouder*, and *Administrateur der Landschapskassen* (Administrator of the Territorial Treasuries) of the *Gouvernement*.

## Role of the schoolopziener

The *volksschool-opziener* (village-school inspector) is the colonial official charged with *toezicht* (supervision) over the administration of the *schoolgeld*. Reg. 15b *Artikel 3* confers an unrestricted inspection-right: "*De volksschoolopaienes is ten allen tijde bevoegd het register bedoeld bi alinea a van het voergaand artikel te examineeren*" — "The *volksschool-opziener* is at all times competent to examine the register mentioned in paragraph (a) of the preceding article." The *schoolopziener*'s supervision is supplemented by the *kashouder der onderafdeelingskas* (cashier of the subdivision-treasury), who physically receives the monthly *schoolgeld*-stortingen from the school heads, signs the *register* (Model B), and forwards the *opgave* (Model C) to the *Administrateur der Landschapskassen te Koeta Redja* — the colonial bookkeeper at [[Kutaradja|Koeta Redja]] who serves as the destination-point of the entire *schoolgeld* bookkeeping system of the *Gouvernement*. Reg. 15a's *Fatsal 5 / Artikel 5* explicitly cross-references the *voorschriften* to be issued by the *Civiel en Militair Gouverneur*: "*Civiel dan Militair Gouverneur tanah Atjeh en ta'loeknja*" / "*door den Civiel en Militair Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden, uit te vaardigen voorschriften*" — i.e. Reg. 15b itself.

## Significance

The *Zelfbestuursverordening van [[4 August]] [[1917]]* is the originating *schoolgeld*-regeling of the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Gouvernement]], and — together with its accompanying *Gouverneursbesluit* (Reg. No. 15b) of the same date — establishes the administrative framework that would govern the levying of school fees on the *volksscholen* of every *zelfbesturend landschap* of [[Atjeh]] through the late colonial period. The comparative table of *GELIJKE VERORDENINGEN* (Parallel Ordinances) on p. 113 extends parallel *schoolgeld*-regelingen to [[Sigli]] (1919), [[Meureudu|Meureudoe]] (25-7-1917), [[Lammeulo]] (25-7-1917), [[Langsa]] (1917), Takingeun (July–December 1917), Gajo LoeBus (July–December 1917), Alaslanden (July–December 1917), [[Tjalang]] (8 and 30-8-1917), [[Tapatoean|Tapaktoean]] (July–December 1917), and the *Zuidelijke Atjehsche landschappen* (1-12-1917) — within months of the originating Teunom-Panga regulation. The dual issuing mechanism — Reg. 15a by the *Zelfbestuur* with the *Gouverneur*'s *goedkeuring*, Reg. 15b by the *Civiel en Militair Gouverneur* himself — is unique in the compilation, and reflects the constitutional division of competence under the [[Staatsblad 1919 No. 822|Zelfbestuursregelen 1919]]: the *Zelfbestuur* fixes the rate and the conditions of liability; the *Gouverneur* fixes the uniform administrative *voorschriften* that make the *schoolgeld*-heffing auditable across the entire *Gouvernement*.
