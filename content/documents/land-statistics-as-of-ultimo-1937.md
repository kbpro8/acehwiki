**Land statistics as of ultimo 1937** (end 1937) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Table of landbouwconcessies (69 perceelen, 71,069 H.A.), erfpachtperceelen (74 perceelen, 67,287 H.A.), optieperceelen (13 perceelen, 287,000 H.A.); total 143 perceelen / 138,356 H.A.

## Date

end [[1937]]

## Parties

Colonial government of Aceh

## Subject

Table of landbouwconcessies (69 perceelen, 71,069 H.A.), erfpachtperceelen (74 perceelen, 67,287 H.A.), optieperceelen (13 perceelen, 287,000 H.A.); total 143 perceelen / 138,356 H.A.

## Significance

Land statistics as of ultimo [[1937]] is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
