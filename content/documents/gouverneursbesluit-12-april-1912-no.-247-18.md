**The Gouverneursbesluit of [[12 April]] [[1912]] No. 247/18** — printed in the [[1930]] compilation of *Zelfbestuursverordeningen Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden* as Regulation No. 3 — is the *besluit* (decree) of the [[Civiel en Militair Gouverneur|Civiel en Militair Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] by which the *Kepala negeri* (head of the territory) [[Meulaboh]] was authorised to levy the [[Pantjang Alas]] tax on the *boschproducten* (forest products) of the *landschap* (territory) Meulaboh, and which simultaneously established the practical *heffing*- and *innings-voorschriften* (levy- and collection-instructions) for the *Pantjang Alas* across the entire [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]]. Cited verbatim in the preamble of the originating *Pantjang Alas* verordening (ordinance) of [[5 April]] [[1912]] — [[Zelfbestuursverordening 5 April 1912 (Meulaboh boschproducten)|Reg. No. 2 of the same compilation]] — it is the foundational authorisation for the entire *Pantjang Alas* regime.

## The besluit's role as authorisation

The *besluit* occupies the third place in the compilation, immediately after the originating [[Zelfbestuursverordening 5 April 1912 (Meulaboh boschproducten)|Meulaboh verordening of 5 April 1912]] which it authorises. The preamble of that verordening, in the bilingual Malay-Dutch columns, names the *besluit* explicitly as the immediate authorising act:

> "Overwegende, dat blijkens het besluit van den Civiel en Militair Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onder- hoorigheden ddo. 12 April 1912 No. 247/18 de Gonverneur-Generaa van vergunning Nederlandsch-India heeft verleend eene belasting te heffen van eenige van de in het landsehap Meulaboh gewonnen boschprodueten"

— "Whereas, by the *besluit* of the Civiel en Militair Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden of [[12 April]] [[1912]] No. 247/18, the Gouverneur-Generaal of Nederlandsch-Indië has granted *vergunning* (permission) to levy a tax on some of the *boschproducten* won in the *landschap* Meulaboh." The Malay column renders the metropolitan grant as "jang maha moelia Tocan Besar Goonern tanah Hindia Belanda mengixinkan mendjalankan belusting" — "His Excellency the Governor-General of the Netherlands-Indies has granted permission to levy this tax." The *besluit* of the Civiel en Militair Gouverneur thus functions as the local conduit through which the metropolitan authority of the [[Gouverneur-Generaal van Nederlandsch-Indië|Gouverneur-Generaal]] reached the [[Zelfbesturen|*Zelfbestuur*]] (self-government) of the *landschap* Meulaboh; without it the *Kepala negeri* would have had no constitutional warrant to levy the tax.

## The Governor-General's prior permission

The *besluit* is itself an act of transmission. It records — and converts into local legal form — the prior *vergunning* (permission) granted by the [[Gouverneur-Generaal van Nederlandsch-Indië]] in response to a request transmitted by the *1ste Gouvernements Secretaris* (First Government Secretary) via telegram of the same date. The full text of the *besluit*, on pages 19–24 of the compilation, opens with the recital:

> "Gelezen het telegram van den 1sten Gonvernements Secretaris ddo. 12 April 1912, waarbij de mededeeling wordt gedaan, dat de [Gouverneur-Generaal] van Ned.- Indië vergund heeft, dat de Zelf- besturen in het Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden onder den naam ,,,antjang Alas" cene belas- ting heffen, ten bedrage van 10%"

— "Having read the telegram of the *1ste Gouvernements Secretaris* of [[12 April]] [[1912]], whereby the communication is made that the Gouverneur-Generaal of Ned.-Indië has granted that the *Zelfbesturen* (self-governments) in the Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden may levy, under the name 'Pantjang Alas', a tax to the amount of 10%." The Gouverneur-Generaal's *vergunning* — transmitted by the *1ste Gouvernements Secretaris* — is thus the ultimate constitutional source of the *Pantjang Alas*; the Civiel en Militair Gouverneur's *besluit* of the same date translates that metropolitan grant into local *heffing-voorschriften* (levy-instructions) binding on all the *Zelfbesturen* of the *Gouvernement*, not on the *landschap* Meulaboh alone.

## Citation in the preamble of the Meulaboh verordening

The *besluit* is cited twice in close association with the originating Meulaboh verordening. First, in the preamble of Reg. No. 2, as the immediate authorising act ("*blijkens het besluit van den Civiel en Militair Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onder- hoorigheden ddo. 12 April 1912 No. 247/18*"). Second, in its own right as Reg. No. 3 of the compilation, where the full text is reproduced on pages 19–24 — including the recital of the Gouverneur-Generaal's *vergunning*, the quarterly valuation mechanism ("*De waarde der artikelen waar-voor de belasting wordt geheven, wordt elke drie maanden door het Hoofd van gewestelijk bestuur na overleg met het Zelfbestuur vast- gesteld.*"), the *kwitantie*- (receipt-) and *stortingsstaat*- (deposit-statement-) procedure, and the quarterly *Plaatselijk Besturende Ambtenaar* (Local Administering Official) *contrôle*. The *besluit* was later modified by *Gouverneursbesluit van [[16 July]] [[1925]] No. 793/L.K.*, which is noted on the table of contents of the compilation as the standing modification of Reg. No. 3.

## Significance

The *Gouverneursbesluit van [[12 April]] [[1912]] No. 247/18* is the foundational act of the *Pantjang Alas* regime. It converted the metropolitan *vergunning* of the [[Gouverneur-Generaal van Nederlandsch-Indië]] — itself communicated by telegram of the *1ste Gouvernements Secretaris* on the very same day — into the local *heffing-voorschriften* that would govern the ten-per-cent levy on forest products across all the *zelfbesturende landschappen* of the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Gouvernement]]. It is the immediate authorisation cited in the preamble of the originating [[Zelfbestuursverordening 5 April 1912 (Meulaboh boschproducten)|Meulaboh verordening of 5 April 1912]], and remained in force — as modified by the *Gouverneursbesluit van [[16 July]] [[1925]] No. 793/L.K.* — throughout the period covered by the compilation. Every subsequent *landschap*-level *Pantjang Alas* verordening of the *Gouvernement* — the parallel regelingen of April [[1912]] for Idi, Langan, Tamiang, Tjulung, Tapaktoean, and the *Zuidelijke Atjehsche landschappen*; the comprehensive [[Zelfbestuursverordening 27 Juli 1925 (Pidie Pantjang Alas)|Pidië regeling of 27 July 1925]] (Reg. No. 4a); and the parallel *uitvoeringsbesluiten* (implementing decisions) of 1925–1926 for the *onderafdeelingen* (subdivisions) of Pidië, Takingeun, Serbeudjadi, Gajo LoeBus, and the Alaslanden — is in constitutional terms an exercise of the authorisation embodied in this *besluit*.
