**Verspreide Geschriften IV** was the fourth volume of the collected writings of [[Christiaan Snouck Hurgronje]], in which his formula for the pacification of [[Atjeh]] and his condemnation of [[H. N. A. Swart]] both stand recorded.

Snouck's formula, quoted throughout *Generaal Swart*, was "the combination of means of pacification derived from sound principles of government with the unavoidably hard measures that must break the resistance." [[Maurice Henri du Croo|Du Croo]] noted with irritation that Snouck's judgement of Swart — "it may well be that he would be in favour of it; behind it he is not, and behind it he will not come" — "still stands recorded in his Collected Writings," although "the facts and the years from [[1918]] to [[1939]] have proved that Swart could indeed do it."
