**Mutatiestaat voor civiele en militaire landsdienaren** (ongoing (published in Atjeh blaadje)) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Personnel transfer/state list for civil and military government servants; main news content of the Atjeh blaadje.

## Date

ongoing (published in Atjeh blaadje)

## Parties

Government personnel register

## Subject

Personnel transfer/state list for civil and military government servants; main news content of the Atjeh blaadje.

## Significance

Mutatiestaat voor civiele en militaire landsdienaren is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
