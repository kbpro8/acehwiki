**Painting: Van Heutsz and his staff at the Battle of Batèë Ilië** (depicts c.1898-1904 battle) is a document described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Famous painting depicting General Van Heutsz and his staff (including several well-known figures) on the Batèë Ilië hill, observing the fierce battle on the plain of Samalanga.

## Date

depicts c.[[1898]]-[[1904]] battle

## Parties

by [[Hoynck van Papendrecht]]

## Subject

Famous painting depicting [[Generaal J. B. van Heutsz|General Van Heutsz]] and his staff (including several well-known figures) on the [[Batèë Ilië]] hill, observing the fierce battle on the plain of [[Samalanga]].

## Significance

Painting: Van Heutsz and his staff at the Battle of Batèë Ilië is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the documentary record of [[Aceh]].
