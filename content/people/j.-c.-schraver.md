**J. C. Schraver** was a **[[lieutenant of the marines|luitenant der mariniers]]** who served under **[[J. M. P. Kluit]]** during the **[[Edi River expedition (1890)]]**. He commanded the right‑hand marine platoon that stormed an Acehnese fortified position on the left bank of the **[[Edi River|Edi]]** in **[[June]] [[1890]]**.  

Schraver’s troops suffered nine wounded, but they succeeded in driving the enemy from their earthworks, after which a **[[klewang]]**‑armed Acehnese combatant was killed by **[[Van der Hegge Spies]]**.
