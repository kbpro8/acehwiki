**Teuku Nya Mohamadad** (historically written Toekoe Nja Mohamadad) was an Acehnese nobleman, hulubalang of the [[XX Moekims]], and son-in-law of [[Teuku Nek of Meuraxa]] during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]].

## Submission in 1874

In January [[1874]], following the defection of his father-in-law [[Teuku Nek of Meuraxa]], [[Teuku Nya Mohamadad]] accompanied his brother-in-law [[Teuku Nya Abas]] to Dutch headquarters in [[Kraton of Aceh]] to make their formal submission to General [[J. van Swieten]].

Their visit on [[2 February]] [[1874]] was recorded in official colonial returns and cited in the [[General van Swieten War Telegrams]] as evidence that indigenous nobility in coastal Aceh were acknowledging the sovereignty of the [[Dutch East Indies Government]].
