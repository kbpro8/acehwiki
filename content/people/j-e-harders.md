**J. E. Harders** was the author of the [[1939]] article *[[De tocht van Overste van Daalen door de Gajo-, Alas- en Bataklanden 1904 (Harders, 1939)|De tocht van Overste van Daalen door de Gajo-, Alas- en Bataklanden 1904]]* ("The march of Colonel van Daalen through the Gayo, Alas, and Batak lands in 1904"), published in the *Orgaan van de Nederlandsch-Indische Officiersvereeniging* (Organ of the Netherlands Indies Officers' Association) in [[1939]], with a cross-reference ("zie bij TICHELMAN, G. L.") indicating a connection to the work of [[G. L. Tichelmann|G. L. Tichelmann]]. The article is recorded as No. 568 in [[H. van Meurs|Van Meurs]]'s *[[Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939 (Van Meurs, 1940)|Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939]]* (The Hague, [[1940]]), in the Sumatra chapter.

## The 1939 article

Harders's article, published in the *Orgaan van de Nederlandsch-Indische Officiersvereeniging* — the principal professional journal of the Netherlands Indies officer corps — recorded the [[Colonne van Daalen naar de Gajolanden en Alaslanden (1904)|campaign of Colonel (Overste) van Daalen]] through the [[Gajoland|Gajo-]], [[Alaslanden|Alas-]], and [[Bataklanden|Bataklanden]] in [[1904]]. The article covers the same campaign as the [[De tocht van Overste van Daalen door de Gajo-, Alas- en Bataklanden (Geusau, 1939)|Geusau article of 1939]] (No. 566), but published in a different venue — the officers' association journal rather than the *Indisch Militair Tijdschrift*. The double publication of retrospective accounts of the same campaign in two distinct professional military journals in the same year marks [[1939]] as a year of sustained retrospective interest in the van Daalen campaign. The cross-reference to [[G. L. Tichelmann|Tichelmann]] — the Dutch colonial ethnographer and artist represented elsewhere in the Sumatra chapter by entries on [[In Atjeh opgedolven glazen kruikjes (Tichelmann, 1939)|Atjeh glass jugs]] (No. 625) and on Batak antiquities — indicates a connection between Harders's article and Tichelmann's ethnographic and illustrative work, suggesting that Tichelmann may have contributed illustrations or ethnographic observations to the Harders article.

## Significance

For AcehWiki, J. E. Harders is significant as the author of the [[1939]] retrospective on the [[Colonne van Daalen naar de Gajolanden en Alaslanden (1904)|van Daalen campaign of 1904]] in the officers' association journal — a companion to the [[De tocht van Overste van Daalen door de Gajo-, Alas- en Bataklanden (Geusau, 1939)|Geusau account]] (No. 566) of the same campaign, and an independent source that complements the corpus's existing accounts. The cross-reference to [[G. L. Tichelmann|Tichelmann]] connects the article to the wider ethnographic and antiquarian work on northern Sumatra represented in the same chapter of Van Meurs's survey.
