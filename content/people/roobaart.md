**Roobaart** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Tgk. Léjman]]

It was "a blow such as few have been struck": thirty-six men fell and fourteen rifles were taken, and the European sergeants [[Loeff]] and [[Roobaart]] earned the [[Militaire Willemsorde]] in the action.

### From [[Meergebied]]

Improvement began only in [[1911]], "onder 'Hein' [[Hein Meijer|Meijer]]'s krachtige marechaussee-hand." In January the influence of [[Tgk. di Barat]] still made itself felt; the bands showed heightened activity, the Telongbende fired on patrols, and there was another *[[klewang]]* attack at [[Linggö]]. But at last the Telongbende received the blow long intended for it: [[Jenae]] with a patrol of the 5th Division struck "a blow such as few have been struck" — the entire band of [[Tgk. Léjman]], successor of [[Tgk. di Tjòt Tjitjém]], was rendered harmless, thirty-six men falling and fourteen rifles being taken; the European sergeants [[Loeff]] and [[Roobaart]] earned the [[Militaire Willemsorde]].

### From [[The resistance in Atjeh 1908–1912]]

Improvement began only in [[1911]], under the vigorous *marechaussée* hand of [[Hein Meijer]]. In January the influence of Tgk. di Barat still made itself felt; the bands showed heightened activity, the Telongbende fired on Dutch patrols, and there was another *klewang* attack at [[Linggö]]. But at last the Telongbende received the blow long intended for it: [[Jenae]] with a patrol of the 5th Division struck "a blow such as few have been struck" — the entire band of [[Tgk. Léjman]], successor of the well-known [[Tgk. di Tjòt Tjitjém]], was rendered harmless in a surprise attack in which thirty-six men fell and fourteen rifles were taken; the European sergeants [[Loeff]] and [[Roobaart]] earned the [[Militaire Willemsorde]] there.
