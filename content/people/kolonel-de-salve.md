**Kolonel De Salve** commanded the Dutch expeditionary force that operated from **Nieuw‑Amsterdan** (now **Paramaribo**) and established a fortified headquarters surrounded by a five‑company cordon. He directed the **purge** of the rebel‑occupied **Wikkie‑gebied**, employing former rebel captains **[[Accara]]** and **[[Gouffarie]]** as auxiliaries under a conditional **volmacht** (proxy authority) issued by **[[Governor Van Hoogenheim|Governor Van Hoogenheim]]**.

## Strategy
De Salve preferred containment and voluntary surrender over mass killings, instructing his troops to avoid killing **Negers** unless they resisted. He coordinated a major offensive scheduled for **[[21 March]]**, dispatching forces under **[[Maajoor De Brauw|Major De Brauw]]**, **[[Luitenant‑Kolonel Douglas|Douglas]]**, and other officers.

## Significance
His approach reflects a nuanced colonial policy that balanced military pressure with diplomatic incentives, a pattern later echoed in Dutch campaigns in **[[Aceh]]**.
