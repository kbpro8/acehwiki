---

title: "Teungku Ba' Jeuleupe"
tags: [person, author]
---

A disciple of Cheh Marahaban who died about thirty years before Hurgronje's writing, living in the gampong of Ba' Jeuleupe in Daya. He wrote a brief, terse nalam on the twenty attributes of God.

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
