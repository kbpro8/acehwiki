**Jongejans' Wife** (active) was a Wife of author; long-time companion in outer provinces mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Wandered with Jongejans for a quarter century through the buitengewesten (outer provinces); supported him strongly in writing the book.

## Role

Wife of author; long-time companion in outer provinces

## Dates

active

## Account from Jongejans

Wandered with [[Resident Jongejans|Jongejans]] for a quarter century through the [[Buitengewesten|buitengewesten]] (outer provinces); supported him strongly in writing the book.

## Significance

Jongejans' Wife appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
