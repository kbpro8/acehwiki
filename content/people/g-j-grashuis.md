Published the Sundanese story "Aki Bolong" in "Soendaneesch Leesboek" (Leiden [[1874]]), a story identical to the Si Kabayan tale among most Sundanese and a close parallel of the Acehnese Si Meuseukin story.

- [[Si Meuseukin]]

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
