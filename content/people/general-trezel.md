**General Trezel** was a French general in the Algerian conquest whose colonial campaigns were referenced in Dutch military treatises regarding the [[Aceh Sultanate]].

## Reference in Aceh Historiography

In *Atjeh gedurende de tweede expeditie en later* ([[1876]]), the author cited General Trezel's military setbacks in Algeria alongside those of [[General Clauzel]] to argue that European public opinion often unfairly maligned colonial commanders who faced insurmountable geographical and logistical obstacles, drawing a parallel to criticisms of General [[J. van Swieten]].
