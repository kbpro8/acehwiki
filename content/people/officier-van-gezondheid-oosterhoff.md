**Officier van Gezondheid Oosterhoff** was the Dutch military surgeon who rushed from the adjacent field ambulance to [[J. H. R. Köhler|General Köhler]]'s side the moment the general was shot on [[14-april|14 April]] [[1873]] at the [[mesigit|Missigit]], and carried him into one of the field hospitals, where Köhler died in his arms within minutes.
