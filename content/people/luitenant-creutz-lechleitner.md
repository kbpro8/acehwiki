**Luitenant Creutz Lechleitner** was the Dutch post commandant of [[Lam Koenjit]] in [[Groot-Atjeh]] at the time of the defection of [[Teukoe Oemar]] in [[March]] [[1896]], described by [[G. B. Hooijer]] as *de immer waakzame postcommandant* — the ever vigilant post commander.

## Defence of Lam Koenjit

Hooijer recorded that Creutz Lechleitner hoped to be able to prevent the enemy from penetrating the post. Above the outwork surrounded by *[[IJzerdraadversperring|ijzerdraadversperring]]* (wire entanglement), within which the kitchen stood, rose a watch-house from which the enemy's approach could be barred. That watch-house bore as its inscription the name *Eikhoren*, and other signboards — such as *In de Villa Sans Souci* and *In den Soldatenhemel: alles vrij behalve muskieten* ("In the Soldiers' Heaven: everything free except mosquitoes") — proved sufficiently, in Hooijer's judgment, that the Dutch soldiers did not yet consider the situation desperate and had kept their humour.

Entering the gate of the outwork, above which the name of the post was written, one had to turn left to enter the *[[Benteng|benteng]]* itself, within whose bastions two 7½ cm. field-pieces and a *[[Cochoornmortier|cochoornmortier]]* were mounted.

## The night assault of 4–5 April 1896

In the night of [[4 April]] to [[5 April]] [[1896]] one of the bastions was stormed by the enemy, and, Hooijer wrote, had the gun there not been loaded with case-shot, that attack might have proved fatal to the Dutch.
