**Khoé Tjian Gie** was a Chinese guide who assisted the Dutch forces during the [[First Aceh Expedition]] in [[1873]].

## Role
He provided crucial geographical intelligence to the Dutch high command and physically guided the [[Reconnaissance of the Kraton]] on [[9 April]] [[1873]], helping to identify the position of [[Atjeh's Groote Missigit]].
