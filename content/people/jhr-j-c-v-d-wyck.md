**Jhr. J. C. v. d. Wyck** was a Dutch colonial administrator who served as Civil and Military Governor of the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] from [[1904]] to [[1905]], the brief interregnum between the powerful governorships of [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|van Heutsz]] ([[1898]]–[[1904]]) and [[G. C. E. van Daalen|van Daalen]] ([[1905]]–[[1908]]). The Encyclopaedisch Bureau's *De Buitenbezittingen* lists him in its Bijlage I — the chronological list of *Hoofden van Gewestelijk Bestuur* — as the holder of the *Civ. en Mil. Gouverneur* title for this transitional period.

## The interregnum

Van der Wijck's short tenure fell between the departure of [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|van Heutsz]], who had broken the back of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-krijg]] and begun the consolidation of the Gouvernement, and the arrival of [[G. C. E. van Daalen|van Daalen]], who would complete the military subjugation with the Gayo-Alas campaign of [[1904]] and pursue the harsh policies that provoked the [[1907-1908 Aceh resistance|1907–1908 flare-up of resistance]]. The brevity of his term — a single year, in a sequence of governors whose tenures typically ran from four to six years — reflects the transitional nature of the period. *De Buitenbezittingen* does not single out any particular policy or event of his governorship; the structural developments of these years — the continuing construction of the administrative infrastructure of the Gouvernement, the preparations for the Gayo-Alas campaign that van Daalen would lead in [[1904]] — were continuations of the van Heutsz programme rather than new initiatives.

## Place in the succession

The complete succession of governors recorded in Bijlage I runs:

- [[1874]]–[[1876]]: [[J. L. J. H. Pel|J. L. J. H. Pel]], Mil. en Civ. bevelhebber
- [[1876]]: G. B. Th. Wiggers V. Kerchem, id.
- [[1876]]–[[1877]]: A. J. E. Diemont, id.
- [[1877]]–[[1881]]: K. Heyden v. D., Wd. id.; in [[1878]] Civ. en Mil. Gouverneur
- [[1881]]–[[1882]]: A. Pruys v. d. Hoeven, Civ. Gouverneur
- [[1882]]–[[1884]]: Ph. F. Laging Tobias, id.
- [[1884]]–[[1886]]: H. Demmeni, Civ. en Mil. Gouverneur
- [[1886]]–[[1891]]: H. K. F. v. Teyn, id.
- [[1891]]–[[1892]]: F. Pompe v. Meerdervoort, id.
- [[1892]]–[[1896]]: C. Deykerhoff, id.
- [[1896]] (April–July): Lt. Gen. J. A. Comm. Vetter V. h. leger; Reg. Comm.
- [[1896]] (July): J. J. K. de Moulin, Civ. en Mil. Gouverneur
- [[1896]] (July–October): J. W. Stemfoort, Wd. id.
- [[1896]]–[[1898]]: C. P. J. van Vliet, id.
- [[1898]]–[[1904]]: [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|J. B. v. Heutsz]], id.
- [[1904]]–[[1905]]: Jhr. J. C. v. d. Wyck, id.
- [[1905]]–[[1908]]: [[G. C. E. van Daalen|G. C. E. van Daalen]], id.
- [[1908]]–: [[H. N. A. Swart|H. N. A. Swart]], id.
