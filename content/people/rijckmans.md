**Rijckmans** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[H. N. A. Swart]]

Swart took over the civil government from Assistant Resident [[Rijckmans]], Van Daalen having already departed. His account of the first weeks is preserved in the biography: "Then I went at once by tram with Colijn eastward as far as Langsa, to get an impression for myself." He discussed everything with [[Hendrikus Colijn|Colijn]], who had been attached to him as *adjunct regeeringscommissaris voor de decentralisatie* for the first months, and together they determined what should be brought to the notice of the Governor-General; Colijn then wrote the letter.

### From [[1908]]

By decree of [[4 May]] [[1908]], with thanks for his services in [[Celebes]], Swart was appointed Military Commander of Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden and simultaneously Governor of that province, with the title *Civiel en Militair Gouverneur* at 1,500 guilders a month. He assumed the government on [[10 June]] [[1908]], taking over the civil administration from Assistant Resident [[Rijckmans]], [[Gotfried Coenraad Ernst van Daalen|Van Daalen]] having already departed.
