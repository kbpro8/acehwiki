---
title: Teuku Ba'et
aliases: [uleebalang of the VII Mukims of the XXII]
tags: [tokoh, pejuang]
---

The uleebalang who conducted the actual negotiations with Teuku Uma for the release of Mrs. Hansen, managing through several discussions to reduce the ransom demand from $40,000 to $25,000, of which he personally received $500.
