**Van der Hoop (advocaat-fiscal)** was a Dutch legal officer who, between [[1788]] and [[1792]], submitted proposals for the creation of a permanent corps of ship artillerymen and a marine infantry corps for the [[Oost-Indische Compagnie]].

## Proposals for Military Reform
Together with [[Vice-admiraal Reynst]] and [[Schout-bijnaacht Van Kinsbergen]], Van der Hoop advocated for a standing force of 140 men organised into six companies. The plan was ultimately rejected due to cost concerns.

## Historical Context
His efforts occurred against the backdrop of the French Revolution (see [[1792]]) and the shifting political climate in Europe, which affected Dutch colonial policy.
