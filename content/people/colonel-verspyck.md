**Colonel Verspyck** was a senior officer and brigade commander of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] who participated in command decisions during the Aceh War.

## Role in Campaign Debates

In *Atjeh gedurende de tweede expeditie en later* ([[1876]]), [[Colonel Verspyck]] is cited in contemporary analyses regarding order of battle and operational planning during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]]. His service illustrated the administrative and tactical responsibilities borne by senior KNIL officers operating under General [[J. van Swieten]].
