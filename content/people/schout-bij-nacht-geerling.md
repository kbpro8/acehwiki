**Schout-bij-nacht Geerling** was the Kommandant der Zeemacht (commander of the naval forces) in the Netherlands Indies at the time of the Aceh War's beginning. [[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]], discussing the traditionally neglected state of the Indies squadron, recalls that Geerling in his report of [[17 October]] [[1874]] pointed out that the condition of the squadron that had in the first place to support energetic action against Atjeh was "allertreurigst" (most deplorable), and adds that his predecessor — the then commander of the naval force — had, "and rightly so", pointed out in the council meeting of [[21 February]] [[1873]] (which decided the [[First Aceh Expedition (5-29 April 1873) Kreemer's account|first Atjeh expedition]]) the inadequate state of the navy in the Netherlands Indies, a state to which the government's attention had so often been drawn before; the state of the Indies squadron, Verstege remarks, was again made painfully clear by the [[Blokkade|blockade policy of 1884-1885]], without any sign that improvement was being thought of (after generaal [[Generaal Booms|Booms]]' *Historische kritiek*, p. 101).
