---
title: Shaikh Shamsuddin of Sumatra
aliases: [Shamsuddin, Pasei]
tags: [tokoh, mistikus]
---

One of the best-known representatives of pantheistic mysticism in Acheh, who enjoyed much consideration at the court of Sultan Meukuta Alam (Eseukanda Muda) and died in 1630. He wrote commentaries on works of his predecessor Hamzah Pansuri. His successor Raniri later opposed his teaching at the instigation of the sultan's successor, who persecuted his disciples.
