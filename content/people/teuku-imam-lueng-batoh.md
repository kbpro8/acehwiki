**Teuku Imam Lueng Batoh** (historically written Toekoe Iman Longbattah or Toekoe Iman Loong of the [[XXV Moekims]]) was a celebrated Acehnese military commander and hulubalang, universally recognized in contemporary Dutch colonial literature as one of the bravest and most formidable warriors of the [[Aceh Sultanate]].

## Command in the XXV Moekims

During the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] in [[1873]] and [[1874]], [[Teuku Imam Lueng Batoh]], supported by his principal lieutenant [[Panglima Mah Saleh]], commanded resistance forces in the [[XXV Moekims]], defending fortified redoubts at [[Lemboe]] and Longbattah near [[Kraton of Aceh]].

In early February [[1874]], unconfirmed reports reached Dutch headquarters that [[Teuku Imam Lueng Batoh]] had been killed in action, rumors that were temporarily cited in the [[General van Swieten War Telegrams]] of [[2 February]] [[1874]] before it became clear that his followers remained unconquered.
