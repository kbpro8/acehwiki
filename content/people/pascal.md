**Pascal** was a French philosopher (quoted by Kreemer) mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Quoted by Kreemer as encouragement regarding Aceh: 'Le temps passe, l'eau coule et le coeur oublie'.

## Role

French philosopher (quoted by [[J. Kreemer|Kreemer]])

## Account from Jongejans

Quoted by [[J. Kreemer|Kreemer]] as encouragement regarding Aceh: 'Le temps passe, l'eau coule et le coeur oublie'.

## Significance

Pascal appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
