**Christoffel (Captain Christoffel)** was a Captain, marechaussée commander; relentless pursuer of resistance leaders mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Pursued Panglima Polém with Colijn and Breewer.

## Role

Captain, [[Marechaussée|marechaussée]] commander; relentless pursuer of [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] leaders

## Account from Jongejans

Pursued [[Panglima Polém]] with [[Hendrikus Colijn|Colijn]] and [[Breewer]]. Captured [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] Poetrèë (wife of [[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]]) in [[November]] [[1902]] at [[Gloempang Pajong]]. Captured [[24 December]] [[1902]] [[Sultan of Aceh|the Sultan]]'s favorite and son at [[Lammeulo]]. Placed by [[H.E. Swart|Governor Swart]] as [[Marechaussée|marechaussée]]-commandant at Pantonlaboe; acted with 'unbending strictness' against the population of the disorganised [[Keureutòë]] region that colluded with enemies of the government.

## Significance

Christoffel (Captain Christoffel) appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
