**Overmeiren** was an unyielding and courageous Fuselier in the Dutch forces who participated in the assaults on [[Atjeh's Groote Missigit]]. The historical account written by [[J.F.D. Bruinsma]] was specifically published to raise funds for Overmeiren's children.
