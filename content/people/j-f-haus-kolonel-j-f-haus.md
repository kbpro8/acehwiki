**J. F. Haus (Kolonel J. F. Haus)** was a Colonel of infantry; military commander under Governor Pruys van der Hoeven mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. 

## Role

Colonel of infantry; military commander under Governor [[Pruys van der Hoeven]]

## Significance

J. F. Haus (Kolonel J. F. Haus) appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
