**T. J. A. van Zijll de Jong** was a Dutch *Majoor* of the artillery serving in [[Acheh|Atjeh]] in [[1874]] who, together with [[W. J. C. Leers|Majoor W. J. C. Leers]] (engineers), sited the [[Kuta Alam|Benting Kota Alam]] fortification on [[13 November]] [[13 November]] [[1874]] on a bend of the [[Acheh River|Atjeh River]] east of [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]].

## The Kota Alam Operation, 13 November 1874

The two officers chose a site that commanded both the fortified edge of Pinang to the northeast and [[Longbatta|Longbatta]] to the south, while dominating a long stretch of the river — one of the best crossing points. The artillery's contribution to the site selection was decisive: the position allowed the mountain guns to cover the river crossings, the village of Pinang, and the road to [[Longbatta|Longbatta]] simultaneously. The full operation is described on the [[Benting Kota Alam operation (13 November 1874)|dedicated event page]]; the sapper section in the vanguard was commanded by [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede Luitenant C. J. Snijders]].
