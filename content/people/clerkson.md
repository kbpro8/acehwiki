**Clerkson** was an infantry captain of the [[Oost-Indisch Leger|Netherlands East Indies Army]], *compagnies-commandant* of the 2nd Company — a native company — of the [[6e Bataljon Infanterie]], who led the flanking peloton in the storming of the Achehnese position past the ruins of [[Kepaja Doewa]] on [[13 August]] [[1879]], the opening combat of the [[Selimoen Expedition (August 1879)|march to Selimoen]], as recorded in [[W. Cool]] and [[G. B. Hooijer]]'s *[[Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis (Cool & Hooijer, 1889)|Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis]]* ([[1889]]).

## The storm of 13 August 1879

When, in the early afternoon of [[13 August]] [[1879]], the advance guard of the [[Lamtoba column (1879)|column]] came under a fire from the strong Achehnese entrenchment astride the march route — an earthen trench more than a hundred paces long, fronted by a heavy abatis and flanked by a palisaded *tamboer* or blockhouse near the footpath — and the fire grew so heavy that the men, unprotected in the covered terrain, could no longer be held facing it, Majoor [[W. A. Coblijn|Coblijn]] gave the order to storm. The 1st peloton of the 2nd Company with its company commander, Captain Clerkson, at the head, drew round the flank of the position through a marshy stretch of ground — the dammed water of a spring — and between the bamboo abatis the enemy had placed before his own work, while the second peloton of the company, to which Coblijn had attached himself, engaged the work in front. The fortification was reached, the defenders fled in haste into a deep ravine, and so close did the stormers follow that one soldier caught an Achehnese by the head, retaining — to his regret — only the man's head-cloth in his hands. The rapidity of the assault kept the loss to three wounded, among them the 2e Luitenant [[Van Erpen Royaards]] of the 1st peloton. An indigenous sergeant, [[Ramoudin]], was the first, the luitenant-adjudant [[A. G. Popelier|Popelier]] the second man inside the work.
