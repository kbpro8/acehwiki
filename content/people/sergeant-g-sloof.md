**Sloof, G.** was a Dutch marine sergeant who received the fourth class of the **Militaire Willemsorde** for his actions during the Atjeh expeditions of [[1873]] and [[1874]]. His citation appeared in the royal decree of [[6 October]] [[1874]].

## Recognition

Sergeant Sloof’s award reflects the high level of valor displayed by marine troops in the difficult terrain of Aceh.
