**Agam (elephant)** (had to be put down) was a Army elephant at Tangsé mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Male army elephant used for military transport between Tangsé and Geumpang until recent years.

## Role

Army elephant at [[Tangsé]]

## Dates

had to be put down

## Account from Jongejans

Male army elephant used for military transport between [[Tangsé]] and [[Geumpang]] until recent years. Named '[[Agam]]' (= man, in [[Acehnese]]/Malay). Became recalcitrant and dangerous, had to be put down. One of two female elephants: one given to [[Rotterdamsche Diergaarde]], the other sold to the Soesochoenan ([[Soesochoenan's court|Susuhunan of Solo]]).

## Significance

Agam (elephant) appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
