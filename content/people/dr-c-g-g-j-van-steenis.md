**Dr. C. G. G. J. van Steenis** (1936-1937 expedition) was a Botanist at the Herbarium in Buitenzorg; expedition leader mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Plantkundige (botanist) at the Herbarium in Buitenzorg.

## Role

Botanist at the Herbarium in [[Buitenzorg]]; expedition leader

## Dates

[[1936]]-[[1937]] expedition

## Account from Jongejans

Plantkundige (botanist) at the Herbarium in [[Buitenzorg]]. Led the scientific expedition that climbed and investigated [[Goenoeng Löser]] ([[Goenoeng Löser|Gunung Leuser]]) in [[1936]]-[[1937]].

## Significance

Dr. C. G. G. J. van Steenis appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
