---
title: Fatimah Chami
aliases: [Fatimah from Sham]
tags: [person, religious-figure]
---

A woman from Syria who learns of the descending spirit of prophecy on Abdallah and journeys to Mecca intending to become the mother of the last prophet, arriving just too late.
