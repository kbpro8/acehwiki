**Studer** — major Studer — was the Dutch military officer present at Singapore during the [[Singapore incident (1873)|contacts between Atjehsche envoys and foreign consuls]] in [[1873]]. His conduct at Singapore was characterised by [[Kuyper|Kuyper]] in the [[Tweede Kamer debate on Atjeh war origins (April 1874)|Tweede Kamer debate]] as that of a "fool" — though [[I.D. Fransen van de Putte|the Minister of Colonies]] interpreted this as meaning Studer had been deceived rather than that he was foolish. The stenographic records showed Studer as someone who "meddled with everything, did things that were not his concern, and behaved foolishly."
