title: Putroe Phang
aliases: [Pahang consort]
tags: [person, literary-work]
---

The consort of Sultan Eseukanda Muda in the Hikayat Malem Dagang, formerly the wife of Raja Raden, given to the Acehnese king when Raja Raden converted to Islam. She advises the Sultan before his departure, warning him not to land in Si Ujut's territory due to its inhabitants' skill in witchcraft — advice that proves correct though not fully followed.
