**Johan Wøller** was a Danish physician who served as an officer of health (*officier van gezondheid*) in the Netherlands-Indies army and later wrote *Als officier van gezondheid naar Nederlandsch-Indië*, the source for these Aceh entries.

## Arrival in the Indies

In [[September]] [[1903]] he responded to a Danish newspaper advertisement inviting Danish doctors to serve. He married a young nurse and departed. At [[Colombo]] the ship received news of the outbreak of the [[Russo-Japanese War]], "that must therefore have been in [[February]] [[1904]]." A few days later they entered the new harbor of [[Sabang]].

After training in [[Surabaya]], he was posted to [[Atjeh en Onderhorigheden]] about [[1905]] (the introduction notes the Aceh chapter describes conditions "where the writer must have arrived about 1905").

## Service in Aceh

Stationed at [[Kotta Loempoer]], Wøller observed the [[Aceh War]]'s final phase, the three categories of officers, and the death of his friend [[Luitenant ten Brink]]. His account provides detailed descriptions of the town's climate, the garrison of 1,200–1,400 men, and the social life of the officers' club.
