**Teuku Nya Abas** (historically written Toekoe Nja Abas) was an Acehnese nobleman, hulubalang of the [[XX Moekims]], and son of [[Teuku Nek of Meuraxa]] during the Aceh War.

## Submission in 1874

In January [[1874]], following the defection of his father [[Teuku Nek of Meuraxa]] during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]], [[Teuku Nya Abas]] and his brother-in-law [[Teuku Nya Mohamadad]] visited Dutch headquarters in [[Kraton of Aceh]] to formally make their submission to General [[J. van Swieten]].

Their appearance on [[2 February]] [[1874]] was documented in official reports and cited in the [[General van Swieten War Telegrams]] as proof that coastal nobility in the [[XX Moekims]] were aligning with colonial authorities.
