**Sultan of Langkat** — the ruler of the *Landschap* (self-governing Malay territory) of [[Langkat]] on Sumatra's East Coast — was elevated from the rank of *Radja* to *Sultan* in [[1887]] by the Dutch colonial authorities in recognition of his active resistance to the [[Acehnese raids on East Sumatra|Acehnese raiding parties]] that had threatened the northern plantation and petroleum belt throughout the [[1870s]] and [[1880s]]. The *Landschap* of Langkat was also the territory where [[Aeilco Zijlker|Zijlker]] had begun petroleum test-borings in [[1883]], leading to the concession of [[1884]] on which the [[Koninklijke Petroleum Maatschappij]] was founded in [[1890]].

## Resistance to Acehnese raids

When [[Acehnese raids on East Sumatra|Acehnese raiding parties]] attacked the tobacco plantations of [[Glen Bervi]], [[Tamaram]], and [[Ludwigsburg]] in [[1876]] — wounding the manager of [[Tamaram]] and killing one of his assistants — the *Kedjoeroean* (district head) of [[Bohorok]] collaborated with the raiders and subsequently fled to the [[Alas]] lands. The ruler of Langkat took the opposite course: he resisted the invaders actively. Dr. T. Volker records that the Langkat ruler "had resisted the Acehnese raids actively and was elevated from Radja to Sultan in [[1887]]" in acknowledgement of these services.

## Role in the 1878 Langkat–Tamiang transfer

The *Landschap* of Langkat was also the subject of the [[1878 Langkat–Tamiang transfer]], by which the Dutch colonial government renegotiated its relationship with the Langkat ruling house and the adjacent [[Tamiang]] zone. The Langkat ruler's cooperation with the Dutch — in contrast to the *Kedjoeroean* of Bohorok's collaboration with the Acehnese — was a significant factor in the stability of the northern plantation frontier.

## Petroleum concession

The Sultan's territory included the area near [[Pangkalan Brandan]] where [[Aeilco Zijlker|Zijlker]] began petroleum test-borings in [[1883]]. The [[1884]] concession granted by the Langkat ruling authority was the legal instrument on which the [[Koninklijke Petroleum Maatschappij]] was founded in [[1890]], making the Sultan of Langkat — however indirectly — one of the legal progenitors of the enterprise that eventually became [[Royal Dutch Shell]].
