**D. van Peppel** was a **Marine 2e klasse** who received the **Militaire Willemsorde 4e klasse** for his conduct during the **[[1893]]** **Tamiang** operations. His award is recorded in the Dutch naval yearbook of **[[1897]]‑[[1898]]**.

## Role

Peppel served in the landing division, participating in assaults on enemy **redoutes**.
