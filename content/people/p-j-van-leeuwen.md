**P.J. van Leeuwen** was a Dutch academic who, in his dissertation on the *Maleise Alexanderroman* (1937), argued that the Malay Alexander romance constituted an exception to [[Christiaan Snouck Hurgronje]]’s claim that “oudere verhalen” were purely Arab in origin. Van Leeuwen’s ninth proposition challenged the “stelling” of Snouck Hurgronje and suggested that some Malay narratives might retain indigenous elements.

## Dissertation Highlights
In his 1937 Utrecht dissertation, Van Leeuwen classified the *Hikayat Iskandar DzŌe KI‑Karnain* as a “belangrijke uitzondering” to Snouck Hurgronje’s thesis, thereby reopening debate on the Arabic versus indigenous roots of Malay literary tradition.
