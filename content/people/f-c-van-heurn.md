**F. C. van Heurn** was a chemical engineer (*scheikundig ingenieur*) formerly attached to the [[Avros|Avros proefstation]] at [[Medan]], and author of studies on Sumatran soils.

## Assessment of Temiang soils

In his *Studiën betreffende den bodem van Sumatra*, quoted by [[Dr. R. Broersma]] on page 103, Van Heurn wrote about [[Temiang]]: the tertiary hill country of Tamiang distinguishes itself from the mountain of same formation because at surface it consists mainly of clays and claystones, while marls or sandstones, represented as sandy hills, are in minority. The little sandy parts are as infertile as the sandy tertiary soils of [[Bengkalis]], the clays have proven to possess excellent properties for European cultures with proper drainage.

Broersma questioned the proof, asking how those clays proved it, perhaps through existing plantings that were of too small extent.
