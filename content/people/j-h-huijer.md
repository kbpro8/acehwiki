**J.H. Huijer** was a tall and heroic Captain of the 4th company of the [[9e Bataljon Infanterie]] who distinguished himself during the [[Second Conquest of the Great Missigit]] on [[14 April]] [[1873]].

## Action at the Missigit
When his unit was ordered to attack, Huijer rushed the [[Stormladders]]. Taking two rungs at a time, he reached the top of the wall amidst a barrage of Acehnese lances and swords. Huijer grabbed a rifle from a soldier, and while an indigenous soldier continuously loaded cartridges for him, he unleashed a rapid fire against the defenders. During this action, a lance pierced his right wrist. Later, in a profound display of camaraderie, Huijer and Lieutenant [[J.G. Blanken]] leaped into the mosque enclosure under heavy fire to retrieve the body of a fallen Amboinese fuselier. Huijer survived the war and retired in Amsterdam.
