**Mohamad Ali Alamsjah** was a brother of [[Mohamad Djohan Alamsjah|Mohamad Djohan Alamsjah]], the *[[Oeléebalang|oeléebalang]]* of [[Peusangan|Peusangan]], and held the office of *[[Oeléebalang tjoet|oeléebalang tjoet]]* of [[Djeumpa|Djeumpa]], a sub-district within the *landschap* of Peusangan. Like his brother the *oeléebalang* and his brother the *landbouwkundige* [[Teukoe Boestaman|Teukoe Boestaman]], he had received a good education and spoke Dutch.

## Significance
The presence of three educated, Dutch-speaking brothers in the ruling house of Peusangan — one as *oeléebalang*, one as *landbouwkundige*, one as *oeléebalang tjoet* — is, in Broersma's view, the strongest single piece of evidence for the proposition that the post-pacification reforms could take root in Atjeh under good chiefs. The Alamsjah brothers are the type of the new ruling family.
