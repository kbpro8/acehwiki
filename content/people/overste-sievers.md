**Overste Sievers** was the Dutch lieutenant-colonel commanding the [[3de Bataljon Infanterie|3rd battalion]] which in [[October]] [[1893]] advanced to relieve the beleaguered Dutch post at [[Kroeng Kali]] in the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] and to drive the *[[Moslemin]]* from their three positions on the connecting road with [[Toengkoeb]].

## The relief operation at Kroeng Kali

After [[Toekoe Nja Banta]] removed the wives of [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb|Toekoe Tjoet]] from the [[Toengkoeb]] district to [[Lamreng]], causing the general dispersal of that population, the *Moslemin* occupied the abandoned terrain and cut communications between [[Kroeng Kali]] and [[Toengkoeb]]. Hooijer wrote that by yielding to the exiled Toekoe's earlier demand for the occupation of Kroeng Kali the Dutch had *ons in een wespennest gestoken* — thrust themselves into a wasps' nest.

No time was allowed to pass before relieving the post. The 3rd battalion under Overste Sievers marched thither and was to attempt to drive the enemy from three positions on the connecting road with Toengkoeb.

## The fighting

Sievers found, as Hooijer put it, that the *geestdrijvers* (religious zealots) had lost nothing of their courage when they entered the lists against the *Kafirs*, although [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]] could usually chase them from one *kampong* (village) to another without much trouble. They defended themselves so obstinately and so long that on the first day he could drive them from no more than two of the three positions. One of those positions was the [[Masdjid Siem|Messigit (Masdjid) Siem]], in the taking of which [[Luitenant Weyerman]] and three men of lower rank fell and two officers and fifty men were wounded on the broad *sawah* (wet rice-field) plain before the mosque.

Instead of opium-smoking *Moslemin*, the mosque that evening sheltered tired Dutch soldiers who were to advance the next day to take the third enemy position as well; but during the preceding night the enemy evacuated it without waiting for them. [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]]'s troops were to garrison the mosque, and on [[21 October]] [[1893]] he appeared with a hundred fighters to take the point over and to quarter his men in the *benteng* of [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb|Toekoe Tjoet]] near Kroeng Kali.

## Context

Hooijer noted that, apart from the failed attempt on [[Kaloet]], this was only the second occasion in the ten-year *concentratie* period on which the Dutch, driven by necessity, had operated outside the line against the *Moslemin* with a relatively strong force. The earlier occasion, some five years before, had been the attack on [[Kota Toeankoe]].
