**F.P. Cavaljé** was a Major in the Dutch Infantry and the commanding officer of the [[9e Bataljon Infanterie]] vanguard during the [[First Conquest of the Great Missigit]], and later commanded the [[3e Bataljon Infanterie]] during the [[Second Conquest of the Great Missigit]].

## Tactical Leadership
Cavaljé was noted for his calm and decisive leadership. On [[10 April]] [[1873]], he directed the initial approach and assault. On [[14 April]] [[1873]], he implemented the tactical adaptation of attacking in closed columns rather than in open skirmish lines (tirailleur) to better withstand the devastating Acehnese klewang charges.
