---
title: Abul-Laith as-Samarqandi
tags: [tokoh, ulama]
---

An Arabic pandit of about the 10th century A.D., author of the Tambih al-Ghafilin ("Admonition to the Thoughtless"), an extensive 95-chapter work of religious instruction translated into Acehnese verse by Teungku di Lam Gut in 1827 — the only Acehnese book lithographed in print up to Hurgronje's time.
