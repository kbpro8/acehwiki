---
title: Teungku Lam Peuneuenn
tags: [person, saint]
---

The patron saint of pepper, honored with annual kanduris during the pepper season and promoted by Teungku Kuta Karang. Vows are also made to him for recovery from sickness or completion of a child's Quranic studies.
