**Van Haften** was a Dutch second lieutenant of infantry in [[Atjeh]] in [[1876]], serving at [[Boekit Daroe]], who led the relief patrol of 28 August [[1876]] to the detachment attacked in the [[Lo-oe ambush (28 August 1876)|Lo-oe ambush]].

## 28 August 1876

When heavy rifle fire was heard from the post in northwestern direction, about half an hour after the escort of corporal [[Korporaal De Jong (1876)|De Jong]] had marched, the post commander of Boekit Daroe at once sent a patrol of 25 bayonets under Van Haften to hurry to its aid. Taking the short road by kampong Tjabang, the lieutenant first met the fleeing corporal with two fusiliers, then — guided along by them — found [[Wirokromo]] standing in closed order with his nine Javanese fusiliers at 250 paces from the ambush place, and the field with the fallen: the African fusilier Zerk, a dead Atjeher, and five wounded fusiliers. His men collected the wounded, searched in vain for the last missing, drowned European fusilier, and escorted the terribly thinned escort — now completed by part of his patrol and placed under the African corporal Bamberg — on to its destination. By half past nine his patrol was back behind the palisades of Boekit Daroe.

The events are recorded by [[Kolonel Meijer|H. F. Meijer]] in *[[Atjeh van 26 December 1875 tot 4 September 1876]]* ([[1883]]).
