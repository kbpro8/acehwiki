**H.E. Swart** was a Lieutenant-Colonel in the [[Dutch East Indies Army]] who succeeded [[Gotfried Coenraad Ernst van Daalen]] as Governor of Aceh in [[1908]].

## Credit Policies and Administration

In [[August]] [[1908]], Governor Swart reversed Van Daalen's direct government lending policy. In a famous administrative memorandum, Swart argued that direct state loans constituted unwarranted interference in Acehnese society and undermined the authority of native territorial lords (*uleebalang*). He established a standardized credit contract mandating a 4% annual interest rate and requiring planters to sell pepper at regulated market centers (*keude*).
