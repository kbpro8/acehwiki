**Jonkheer G. J. W. C. Graafland** was a Dutch military officer and Captain of the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]], celebrated in colonial historiography as the 'father of the Marechaussees' (*vader der Maréchaussées*).

## Military Career and Service in Aceh

In [[1896]], following the [[Treason of Teuku Umar]], Captain Graafland took the military initiative to convert the Marechaussee from static police guards into aggressive mobile pursuit units. On [[29 June]] [[1896]], he led 224 Marechaussee soldiers in the decisive [[Battle of Aneuk Galung]], capturing the Acehnese fort in 15 minutes. In [[1897]], he became the first Marechaussee officer awarded the Knight 3rd Class Military William Order (*M.W.O.*).
