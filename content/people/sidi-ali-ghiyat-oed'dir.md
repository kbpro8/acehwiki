**Sidi Ali Ghiyat oed'dir** — also **Sidi Ali Ghiyatuddin** — was the envoy dispatched by the elders of [[Pasej-Samodra|Pasaj]] to the court of [[Sjahroe'n Noewi]] to recover the captured sultan [[Sultan Malik at-Tahir of Pasej|Malikoe't-Tahir]] of [[Pasej-Samodra|Pasej-Samudra]], in chapter VIII of the [[Sadjarah Malajoe]]. His three embassies — each bearing a fabulous golden gift — and his final, successful request for the sultan's return are the closing movement of the [[Sjahroe'n Noewi]] chronicle episode, and the principal instance in the [[Sadjarah Malajoe]] of an [[Pasej-Samodra|Pasej]] religious figure conducting diplomacy by *bedekte taal*.

## The three embassies

After [[Awi Ditjoe|Awi Ditjoe's]] capture of the radja of [[Pasej-Samodra|Samudra]] and his humiliation as "tender of chickens", [[Sidi Ali Ghiyat oed'dir]] and the elders of [[Pasej-Samodra|Pasaj]] fitted out a ship, bought Arab merchandise (for "in those days the people of Pasaj understood Arabic"), and dispatched Sidi Ali — dressed in Arabic clothes — to the court of [[Sjahroe'n Noewi]].

His first embassy presented a tree of gold, with gemstones as fruit, worth a *bahara* of gold; on being asked "what is your desire?", Sidi Ali answered "Nothing". The king was much pleased but wondered what they wanted. The second embassy presented a chess-board of gold with gemstone pieces, again worth a *bahara* of gold; the king again offered to grant their wish; Sidi Ali again declined. The third embassy, presented as the monsoon turned and the time to return came, presented two golden ducks — a drake and a female — on a golden bowl of water, that swam, dived, and chased each other. Astonished, the king asked: "tell me now frankly, what is your desire?" Sidi Ali answered: "from your grace we ask for our king, the tender of the chickens, back". "The radja of Pasej? Good, I give him back to you", the king answered — "because he is a Muslim", Sidi Ali added, "we address this request to you". The king then graciously returned sultan [[Sultan Malik at-Tahir of Pasej|Malikoe't-Tahir]] to Sidi Ali, who brought him back to [[Pasej-Samodra|Samoedra]].

## Significance

For AcehWiki, Sidi Ali Ghiyat oed'dir is the principal [[Pasej-Samodra|Pasej]] religious envoy of the [[Sadjarah Malajoe|Sadjarah Malajoe's]] [[Sjahroe'n Noewi]] episode — the figure whose three embassies recover the captured sultan, and whose Arabic dress and speech attest the [[Pasej-Samodra|Pasej]] sultanate's Arabic-Islamic orientation in the pre-[[Aceh Sultanate|Atjehnese]] age. His use of *bedekte taal* — the fabulous golden gifts that, in [[P. de Roo de la Faille|De Roo de la Faille]]'s reading, encode the older Hindu-Buddhist sacred geography — is also the principal instance of a [[Pasej-Samodra|Pasej]] diplomat employing the veiled-language register in the chronicle.
