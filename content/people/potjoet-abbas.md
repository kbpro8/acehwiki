**Potjoet Abbas** was an Atjehsche *hoeloebalang* (war chief, district head) whose submission was reckoned among the most remarkable pledges of the quiet that followed [[Karel van der Heijden|generaal van der Heyden]]'s campaigns of [[1879]]–[[1880]]. Guided by [[Toekoe Moeda Daoed|Toekoe Moeda Daoed]] in July [[1880]] from the [[XXII Moekims]] to [[Kuta Raja|Kota Radja]], he offered his submission to the Dutch government; in the survey of the pacification given by [[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] in [[1886]] — after [[B. C. P. Kielstra|Kielstra]]'s official history — his accession is named, together with the swearing-in of the Panglima Sagi [[Toekoe Nja Abbas Setia Oelama]] on [[24 January]] [[1880]] and the reception of the aged ulama [[Toengkoe Sjech Marabahan]] on [[7 March]] [[1881]], as one of the foremost submissions of the period.
