**J. Boon Jzn.** (circa 1930s) was a Firma / Agent of the Bataafsche Petroleum Mij. (BPM) for Aceh mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Agent of BPM for the whole of Aceh (with exception of Singkel and Simeuloeë, served from Sibolga/Tapanoeli).

## Role

Firma / Agent of the Bataafsche Petroleum Mij. (BPM) for Aceh

## Dates

circa 1930s

## Account from Jongejans

Agent of BPM for the whole of Aceh (with exception of [[Singkel]] and [[Simeuloeë]], served from [[Sibolga]]/[[Tapanoeli]]). One of three wholesale firms in [[Koetaradja]].

## Significance

J. Boon Jzn. appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
