Sultan of Acheh, reigned 1735-1760, eldest son and successor of Alaedin Ahmat Shah. In the epic Hikayat Pochut Muhamat he appears as Poteu Ue' ("our lord the coconut," his pre-accession name) — an indolent ruler content to watch the Arab pretender Poteu Jeumaloy govern unopposed. It was his youngest brother, Pochut Muhamat, who forced the issue, defeated Jeumaloy, and made Poteu Ue' master of all Acheh despite himself, after which he rewarded Pochut Muhamat with half the port dues and arranged his marriage.
