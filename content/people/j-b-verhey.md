**J. B. Verhey** succeeded **Captain Cramer von Baumgarten** as commander of the marine company after the latter’s evacuation on [[5 January]] [[1876]]. He retained command for the remainder of the expedition (see [[page 136]]).

## Later Career

Verhey later became a member of the **Tweede Kamer der Staten‑Generaal** (the Dutch House of Representatives) for the **Rotterdam III** district (see footnote in [[page 136]]).

## Significance

His transition from colonial officer to national politician illustrates the close ties between the Dutch colonial military elite and metropolitan political institutions.
