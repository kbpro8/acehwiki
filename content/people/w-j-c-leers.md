**W. J. C. Leers** was a Dutch officer of the [[Korps Ingenieurs, Mineurs en Sappeurs|Genie (Engineers)]] who held the rank of *Majoor der Genie* in [[Acheh|Atjeh]] in [[1874]] and, as the senior engineer officer at [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] in the autumn of [[1874]], received the newly-arrived [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede Luitenant C. J. Snijders]] on [[8 November]] [[8 November]] [[1874]] and assigned him to the *Compagnie Mineurs en Sappers* garrisoned there.

## The Kota Alam Operation, 13 November 1874

Together with [[T. J. A. van Zijll de Jong|Majoor T. J. A. van Zijll de Jong]] (artillery), Leers sited the [[Kuta Alam|Benting Kota Alam]] fortification on [[13 November]] [[13 November]] [[1874]] — the operation described in detail on the [[Benting Kota Alam operation (13 November 1874)|dedicated event page]]. The two officers chose a site near a bend of the [[Acheh River|Atjeh River]] that commanded both the fortified edge of Pinang to the northeast and [[Longbatta|Longbatta]] to the south, while dominating a long stretch of the river — one of the best crossing points. The site selection was the engineer's contribution to a rapid field-engineering action whose sapper section was commanded by [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] himself.
