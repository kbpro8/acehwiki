**Prophet Jonah** appears in the Malay translation of the *Burda* as the “lofty one” whose swallowing by a fish is likened to the miraculous power of the prophetic messenger. The poem uses the story to illustrate divine protection of the faithful.

## Role in the *Burda*
In verses 72–73, the text draws a parallel between the Prophet’s ability to hurl stones from his palms and the miracle of Jonah being swallowed and later released by the great fish, emphasizing the messenger’s authority over nature.

## Significance for Acehnese Belief
Jonah’s narrative was a popular motif in Acehnese *nasyid* and *syair* (poetic songs), reinforcing themes of repentance and divine mercy.
