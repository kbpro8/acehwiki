**Van der Lith** — *Professor Van der Lith*, *P. A. van der Lith* — was the Dutch orientalist, professor at Leiden, who promised [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje] his help for the journey to Arabië — by taking over Snouck Hurgronje's courses at the Leiden [[Indische Instelling|Indische Instelling] during his absence — and so enabled the [[Snouck Hurgronje's Mekka journey (1884-1885)|Mekka journey of 1884–1885]]. Van der Lith's promise of help was, with the help of the consul [[J. A. Kruijt (consul)|Kruijt]] and the permission of the minister, one of the three principal enabling conditions of Snouck Hurgronje's Mekka journey.

## The Promise of Help

When Snouck Hurgronje, having resolved to study Islam at its source, inquired of the secretary-general of foreign affairs at The Hague whether he might be attached to the consulate at [[Djidda|Djidda], he was refused: in The Hague they had no ears for that. Then Snouck went to the consul [[J. A. Kruijt (consul)|Kruijt]] from Djidda, when that official was on leave at 's-Gravenhage; and that visit was a success: Kruijt would help the young scholar; he would introduce him at Djidda. Also Professor Van der Lith at Leiden promised his help: he would, during Snouck's absence in Arabië, take over his courses at the Indische Instelling. The required leave was requested and obtained; and when the heer Kruijt returned to his unhealthy station, Snouck Hurgronje travelled with him.

## Significance

Van der Lith is the Leiden professor whose promise of help — to take over Snouck Hurgronje's courses at the Indische Instelling during his absence in Arabië — was, with the help of the consul Kruijt and the permission of the minister, one of the three principal enabling conditions of Snouck Hurgronje's Mekka journey of [[1884]]–[[1885]]. The promise removed one of the principal obstacles to the journey — the competition between the Leiden Indische Instelling and the similar institution at Delft, which had been one of [[De Goeje|De Goeje]]'s principal objections to Snouck Hurgronje's absence.
