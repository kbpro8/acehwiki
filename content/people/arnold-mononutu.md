**Arnold Mononutu** was the Indonesian Minister of Information (*min. v. Voorlichting*) who, in May [[1952]], accompanied the Minister of Trade and Industry, Mr. [[Sujono Hadimoto]], on an inspection tour of the oil enterprises in [[Atjeh|Atjeh]] on Sumatra. The delegation also included Ir. [[Darmawan Mangunkusumo]], [[Sunu Sumosastro]], and the parliament member [[Tengku Mohamad Hassan]].
