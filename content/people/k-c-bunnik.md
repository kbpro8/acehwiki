**K. C. Bunnik** was a **[[lieutenant ter zee|lieutenant ter zee]]** of class **2c** who commanded a column of **173 men**, including **24 mariniers**, during the decisive assault on **[[Retch]]** on **[[7 November]] [[1858]]**. His column was equipped with a **[[howitzer|houwitser]]** and succeeded in surrounding the main fortification.

## Action at Retch

Under heavy rain, Bunnik’s column crossed the **[[Batang River|Batang‑kreek]]** in three sampans, approached to within 150 m of the main entrance, and participated in the breach of the **[[rotan|rattan]]** barrier.
