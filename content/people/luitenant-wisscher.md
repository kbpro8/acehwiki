**Luitenant Wisscher** was a Dutch marine lieutenant who participated in the 1784 landing at [[Salangore]]. He is noted as the first officer to disembark from a launch during the assault on [[18 June]] [[1784]].

## Actions During the Landing
According to the *Journaal van Van Braam*, Wisscher leapt from the launch ahead of the main force, followed by two grenadiers, and helped secure the initial beachhead despite intense enemy fire. He later received a commendation for his bravery, being described as “een veelbelovend officier” (a promising officer) by Van Braam.

## Later Career and Death
Wisscher continued to serve in the Dutch East Indies until he succumbed to a severe illness that struck the fleet in [[Malakka]] in [[1784]], dying shortly before the squadron’s departure for the Netherlands.
