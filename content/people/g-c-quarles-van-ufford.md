**G. C. Quarles van Ufford** was the Jonkheer and former naval officer — chief of the external service of the [[Hollandsche Stoomboot Maatschappij]] — who in [[1901]] took over the management of the [[Maatschappij Zeehaven en Kolenstation Sabang]], and whose name, in author [[J. Langhout|Joh. Langhout]]'s judgement, will remain bound to the history of [[Sabang]] as the man who devoted his life to making of Sabang an ocean harbour.

## The saving of Sabang

Where [[J. M. H. van Oosterzee]] of the Batavia firm [[De Lange & Co]] and General (then Major) [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] had given the impulse to the establishment, it was decisive for the bloom of Sabang that in 1901 Quarles van Ufford was found willing to take the leadership of the company upon himself. When the continuation of the exploitation ran into danger "because of the little desire which the money-lenders showed to put still more capital into the not yet yielding harbour", it was Quarles van Ufford who, "door zijn onverzettelijk geloof in de toekomst van Sabang, de onderneming over het doode punt heeft heengeholpen" ("through his inflexible faith in the future of Sabang, helped the enterprise over the dead point").

Under his administration the year [[1903]] formed a milestone on the road to success, when the harbour was taken up into the mail service of the [[Stoomvaart Maatschappij Nederland|Maatschappij "Nederland"]] and the first mailboat, the *Koning Willem III*, was festively piloted into the harbour on [[29 September]] [[1903]] in the presence of Governor Van Heutsz. In [[1904]]–[[1905]] the harbour was equipped with electrically driven coal transporters, and a fixed agreement with the Government was reached in [[1909]]–[[1910]] securing the harbour against foreign capital (see [[Maatschappij Zeehaven en Kolenstation Sabang]]).
