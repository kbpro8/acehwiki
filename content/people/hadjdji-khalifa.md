**Hādjdji Khalifa** was an Acehnese scholar whose commentary on the *[[Bad' al-Amali]]* appears in the edition edited by [[Flügel]] (IV:558 no 9524). The note attributes the composition of the poem to [[Siraj al-Din Abu Muhammad Ali ibn Uthman al-Ushi]] and provides a chronology of the work’s origin.

## Contribution to Acehnese Manuscript Tradition

Khalifa’s marginal notes are preserved in a 16th‑century Acehnese manuscript that contains an interlinear Malay translation of the creed poem. His work illustrates the scholarly activity in Aceh that engaged with Arabic theological texts.
