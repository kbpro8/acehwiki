**F. Stakman** was a Dutch 1st lieutenant of the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Netherlands Indies Army]] who commanded the left flank detachment of the patrol of [[7 March]] [[1896]] from [[Anak Galoeng]], which was to move parallel to the *kampong* edge followed by Captain [[H. F. T. van Blokland|Van Blokland]]. At the first encounter, when the Acehnese opened fire on the column in the open *[[Sawah|sawah]]*, Stakman and the *adjudant-onderofficier* [[Krüger]] were wounded, and only with much difficulty and under a hail of lead could they be carried to the *[[Benting|benting]]*. Beuming's casualty roll records his wound as a shot in the arm.
