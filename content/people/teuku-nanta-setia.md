**Teuku Nanta Setia** (historically written Toekoe Nanta) was the hereditary ruling nobleman (*hulubalang*) of the [[VI Moekims]] in the [[Aceh Sultanate]] and father of Acehnese heroine Cut Nyak Dhien, who played a leading role in resistance against the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]].

## Diplomatic Ambiguity and War (1874)

Following the fall of [[Kraton of Aceh]] in January [[1874]] during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]], preliminary reports reached General [[J. van Swieten]] that [[Teuku Nanta Setia]] was considering opening peace negotiations. 

However, as General [[J. van Swieten]] explicitly reported in his official dispatch of [[7 February]] [[1874]] (Annex F to the Governor-General), [[Teuku Nanta Setia]] soon broke off all contacts, withdrew into the interior of the [[VI Moekims]], and began erecting new earthwork fortifications ([[Coastal Fortifications in Aceh]]) to block Dutch advance. Throughout [[1875]] and [[1876]], his forces battled General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]] during [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]].
