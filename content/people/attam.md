**Atta (bendehoofd)** was a rebel leader (bendehoofd) who operated in the interior of [[Acheh]] during the Dutch riverine campaign of December 19–25. According to the [[Journal of Haringman]], Atta gathered a large band of rebels near the plantation “[[Markey]]” and the forested area of [[Hardenbrock]] at the entrance of the [[Wikkie creek]].

## Activities
Atta’s forces attempted to block the Dutch retreat from the [[Canje area]] and to ambush the landing parties at [[Wikkie creek]]. The rebels were reported to have forced many enslaved Africans to join their ranks, including women and children.
