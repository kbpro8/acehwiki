**Potjoet di Tjot Moerong (Potjoet Tjot Moerong)** was a Favorite of the Pretendent-Sultan mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Captured 24 December 1902 at Lammeulo together with the Sultan's son Toeankoe Ibrahim.

## Role

Favorite of the [[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]]

## Account from Jongejans

Captured [[24 December]] [[1902]] at [[Lammeulo]] together with [[Sultan of Aceh|the Sultan]]'s son [[Toeankoe Ibrahim]].

## Significance

Potjoet di Tjot Moerong (Potjoet Tjot Moerong) appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
