**Vastenou** was a *vermaarde patrouillecommandant* — a famous patrol commander — of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen]] in [[Atjeh|Aceh]], under whom the korporaal [[Nussy]] served as a *[[dardanellen|dardanel]]* before joining the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]]. Vastenou is paired in the book with [[Dersjant]] as the two "vermaarde patrouillecommandanten, die schitterende resultaten hadden geboekt" — the two famous patrol commanders who had achieved brilliant results.

## The patrouillecommandant

"*[[Nussy]]* was dardanel geweest bij Vastenou en [[Dersjant]], vermaarde patrouillecommandanten, die schitterende resultaten hadden geboekt." Nussy — the book's most fully drawn *dardanel* — served as a *dardanel* under Vastenou and Dersjant before joining the colonne Schmidt. The pairing of Vastenou and Dersjant as the two *vermaarde patrouillecommandanten* is the book's marker of the older generation of patrouillecommandanten — the generation that had developed the colonne method before Schmidt.

## The older generation

Vastenou and Dersjant belong to the older generation of patrouillecommandanten of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Marechaussee]] — the generation that had developed the colonne method in the years before the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]] of [[1910]]-[[1911]]. The *schitterende resultaten* they had achieved were the proof of the method; the *dardanellen|dardanellen* they had trained — including Nussy — were the instruments of the method's later application by Schmidt.

## Significance

Vastenou is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as one of the two *vermaarde patrouillecommandanten* under whom [[Nussy]] served as a *dardanel* before joining the colonne Schmidt. The book's argument is that the colonne method — the method that actually finished the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]] — was developed by the older generation of patrouillecommandanten like Vastenou and Dersjant, and that the *dardanellen|dardanellen* they trained were the instruments of the method's later application by Schmidt. Vastenou is therefore the book's marker of the continuity of the colonne method across the generations of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Marechaussee]].
