**Tgk. di Atjèh Ma' Amin** was de beruchte Tgk. di Atjèh Ma' Amin, overpowered and delivered up by the population of *gampong* [[Blang]] on [[24 December]] [[1909]] while foraging.

The event, four days after the end of [[Pang Aron]] in the same district, marked the point at which the protected population of [[Bireuën]] began to turn against the bands: "when the awe felt for the *[[Kompeuni]]* ("the Company (the Dutch power)") had been restored ([[G. F. B. Watrin|Watrin]] was here the commander of the *marechaussee*, the military constabulary), the population, now protected once more, itself began to turn against the bands."
