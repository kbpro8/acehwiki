**Teuku Sulaiman Daud** was the acting assistant-coordinator of [[Groot Atjeh]] who, in [[September]] [[1953]], led the whole of Groot Atjeh's accession to the [[Negara Islam Indonesia]] (N.I.I.) following [[Teungkoe Moehamad Daoed Beureu-éh|Teungkoe Moehamad Daoed Beureu-éh]]'s proclamation of Atjeh's rebellion against the Republic on [[21 September]] [[1953]].

## Leadership of Groot Atjeh's accession to the N.I.I.

Henri J. H. Alers's *Om een rode of groene Merdeka* records that by [[27 September]] [[1953]] — less than a week after the proclamation — almost the whole of Atjeh had fallen to the insurgents. Groot Atjeh, in particular, joined the [[Negara Islam Indonesia]] "in its entirety," under what Alers calls the "energetic leadership" of Teuku Sulaiman Daud, functioning at the time as acting assistant-coordinator. Government troops on Atjeh's important East Coast were, by this point, holding out only around [[Kutaradja|Kota-Radja]] in the north and [[Langsa|Langsa]] in the south, both under continual attack; insurgents even attacked an Alri naval vessel landing arms for Kota-Radja on [[24 September]] [[1953]].
