**H. L. Brusse** was a Dutch officer mentioned among the participants of the 1824 expedition against the sultanates of [[Tanette]] and [[Soepa]]. He later transferred from the marine corps to the regular colonial army.

## Service

His name appears in the list of officers who distinguished themselves during the operations on Sumatra’s southern coast.
