**Toekoe Bait** was one of the most Dutch-hostile *hoofden* (chiefs) of the [[XXII Moekims]] — the *sagi* containing "de kern van Atjeh's weerstandsvermogen," the densest population, the Sultan's *voorraadschuren*, and the most determined chiefs. His *gebied* bordered [[Toeram]], and his hostility provoked the Dutch *inval* into the XXII Sagi on [[3 February]] [[1876]] during the conquest of Atoeh.

## The Inval into the XXII Sagi

By early February [[1876]] [[Generaal Pel|generaal Pel]]'s [[Pel's offensive against the VI, IV and IX Moekims (1875–1876)|great offensive]] had carried the Dutch line from the [[Kroeng Raba]] on the west coast to Atoch, then northward to [[Pager Ajer]] on the left bank of the [[Atjeh-rivier|Atjeh-rivier]], and the entire [[XXV Moekims]] was in Dutch hands. The [[XXII Moekims]] — the *sagi* that contained the kernel of Atjeh's resistance and that had never been permanently occupied — remained outside the line. From [[Toeram]], which bordered the *gebied* of Toekoe Bait, the Dutch made an *inval* into that Sagi on [[3 February]] [[1876]]. After *heeten strijd* the enemy was driven out of [[Atoeh]]. The operation was a punitive incursion rather than a permanent occupation: the Dutch took Atoeh and the surrounding kampongs but did not attempt to garrison the XXII Moekims as a whole.

## The Catastrophe of 13 February 1876

Ten days later, on [[13 February]] [[1876]], the [[Atoeh catastrophe (13 February 1876)|catastrophe at Atoeh]] overtook [[Jan van Swieten (Kapitein)|kapitein van Swieten]]'s small detachment on the *drassige sawah* near [[Lamsajoen]]. [[G. F. W. Borel|Borel]] does not attribute the catastrophe to Toekoe Bait personally, but the context makes clear that the XXII Moekims remained the most dangerous sector of the Dutch *linie*, and that any incursion into the *sagi* — even the reinforcing of the Atoeh post — risked the kind of ambush that destroyed van Swieten's detachment. The Dutch eventually made a successful *inval* into the XXII Moekims in mid-[[1878]] under [[Karel van der Heijden|gouverneur van der Heijden]], but the *sagi* was never permanently occupied in Borel's lifetime. Borel's recommendation, in the *Naschrift* of his book, was for repeated *tuchtigingen* of the XXII Moekims — punitive expeditions — to be conducted by the proposed [[Indische Brigade (proposed)|Indische Brigade]] until the chiefs, including Toekoe Bait, "het hoofd in den schoot legden."

## Significance

Toekoe Bait is, in Borel's narrative, the archetype of the *XXII-Moekims* chief — the most hostile, the most resourceful, and the most difficult to reduce. His *gebied* bordered the line of Dutch conquest at its most exposed point, and his provocation drew the Dutch into the *inval* of [[3 February]] [[1876]] that exposed the Atoeh post and so set the stage for the catastrophe of the 13th.
