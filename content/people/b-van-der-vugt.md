**B. van der Vugt** was a **[[marinier|marine]]** of 3rd class who, alongside **[[W. Steffens]]**, scaled the breach of **[[Tabanio]]** on **[[27 August]] [[1859]]**, an act for which he received the **[[Militaire Willemsorde]]** by **[[Royal Decree 18 February 1861 No. 82]]**.
