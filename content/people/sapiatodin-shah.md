The first of the four female sovereigns of Acheh, reigning [[1639]] (or [[1641]])-[[1675]], famed for her piety, her name still extolled in royal edicts. During her reign the celebrated saint Abdurra'uf (Teungku di Kuala) settled in Banda Acheh and wrote a book at her request, dedicating it to her in extravagant terms.
