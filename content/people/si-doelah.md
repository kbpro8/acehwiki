**Si Doelah** was the son of a simple *[[oelama]]* who came to play the role of [[T. Radja Sabi]] and was accepted and brought up as future *[[oelèëbalang]]* at [[Koeta Radja]].

Why countless Acehnese recognised the substitute as T. Radja Sabi, showed him respect and submission, and even appeared before him deeply moved, "could never be answered by anyone, not even by [[H. N. A. Swart|Swart]]. Did they hope by the substitution to keep the real T. Radja Sabi safely out of the line of fire? Did those who made the substitution count on a high reward? Who shall say…? Atjeh remains Atjeh." Reports soon reached the bivouacs that the real T. Radja Sabi was still at large. When the true heir at last submitted on [[13 March]] [[1919]], "the 'false' one disappeared and ended as a *wegmandoer* (road foreman)."
