**W. Pordon** was a Dutch Infantry Captain who commanded the 4th company of the [[3e Bataljon Infanterie]] during the [[First Aceh Expedition]].

## Combat Role
He played a prominent role in the [[First Conquest of the Great Missigit]] on [[10 April]] [[1873]] and subsequently led his company in the central assault column during the [[Second Conquest of the Great Missigit]] on [[14 April]] [[1873]].
