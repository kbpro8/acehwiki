**Vice-Admiral F. de Casembroot** was a senior Dutch naval officer who, in a private letter dated [[1938]], praised the conduct of the marine landing division during the Acehnese expeditions of the late 1860s. The letter, addressed to [[Lieutenant P. S.anjaajrd]] (likely a misspelling of a marine lieutenant), highlighted the “honourable mention” awarded to marines such as [[Lieutenant D. A. van Vuuren]].

## Connection to Aceh

Although the letter was written decades after the events, it reflects the lasting recognition of the marine forces’ role in the conquest of [[Bandjar]] and other Acehnese strongholds. The correspondence was later deposited in the marine corps archive in Rotterdam but was lost in the 1940 fire.

## Significance

Casembroot’s acknowledgment underscores the importance of naval‑marine cooperation in the Dutch colonial project in Aceh.
