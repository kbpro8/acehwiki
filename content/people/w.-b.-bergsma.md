**W. B. Bergsma** — *Mr. W. B. Bergsma* — was a Dutch colonial official whose advice to [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] in [[1888]] — to address himself to the minister of colonies [[Keuchenius|Keuchenius]] with the offer to advise on the contents of the letters of [[Teungkoe di Tirò|Teungkoe di Tirò]] and other Atjèhse and Malay writings, and on the *nota* of the [[Habib Abdul Rachman El-Zahir|Habib Abdoerrachman ez-Zahir]] — was the immediate occasion of Snouck Hurgronje's offer to the minister on [[26 July]] [[26 July]] [[1888]], and so of the despatch to Indië in the spring of [[1889]] that would eventually lead to the [[July 1891|July]] [[1891]]–[[February 1892|February]] [[1892]] residence at Atjèh and the *[[Verslag omtrent de religieus-politieke toestanden in Atjeh|Verslag]]* of [[23 May]] [[23 May]] [[1892]].

## The 1888 Advice

When in [[1887]] the controleur [[Van Langen|Van Langen]] of Langen, from Atjèh, came on leave to the Netherlands and brought Snouck Hurgronje the products of his Atjèhse language studies — and, in particular, the letters of Teungkoe di Tirò and other Atjèhse and Malay writings, which Snouck Hurgronje found "*very instructive for our government*" — the question arose of how to bring those writings to the attention of the government. On the advice of Mr. W. B. Bergsma, Snouck Hurgronje addressed himself to the minister of colonies Keuchenius, with the offer to tell him what stood in those letters, as also in the *nota* of the Habib Abdoerrahman. That was on [[26 July]] [[26 July]] [[1888]] that he did that.

## Significance

W. B. Bergsma is the Dutch colonial official whose advice to Snouck Hurgronje in [[1888]] — to address himself to the minister Keuchenius — was the immediate occasion of the offer that led, in the spring of [[1889]], to Snouck Hurgronje's despatch to Indië. The despatch, though originally for the study of Islam on Java (in connection with the [[Tjilegon|Tjilegon]] murders), placed Snouck Hurgronje in a position to make the [[July 1891|July]] [[1891]]–[[February 1892|February]] [[1892]] residence at Atjèh, and so to produce the *Verslag* of [[23 May]] [[23 May]] [[1892]].
