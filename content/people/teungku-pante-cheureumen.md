---
title: Teungku Pante Cheureumen
tags: [person, saint]
---

A saint whose tomb near Kuala Doe on the shore of Ulee Lheue is regarded as of great antiquity. Great kanduris classified as kanduri tula' bala (feasts to avert calamity) are held there during epidemics, with a white buffalo always slaughtered.
