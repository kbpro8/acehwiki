**Rödjö Akoeb** is a subject named in *[[Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Lebé Grondong]]

In [[1910]] the column of [[F. Darlang|Darlang]] held the [[Péndéngvallei]], so that Lebé Grondong moved with his followers to neighbouring sub-divisions. In March [[1911]] a brigade under sergeant [[Wisse]] surprised his hiding-place between [[Tampor]] and [[Lésten]] and reported him killed with his principal *panglima* and a follower, the booty being an M.95 rifle, a Mauser repeating rifle, a Beaumont and a Mauser self-loading pistol. In September came news that he was *not* dead: he had toured the Gajò Loeö during the *hari raja* feasts extorting from the population and had obtained two carbines on the death of the resistance head of [[Badaq]]; in October he murdered [[Rödjö Akoeb]] of [[Penosan]] in revenge for the latter's journey to [[Koeta Radja|Koetaradja]] — but handed in a *panglima*, a *[[goeroe]]* and a carbine. In [[1912]] he came in submission.

### From [[Gajòlanden]]

In April Swart visited the division, inspected *gampongs* and held a great meeting with the *blah*-heads; the Kaniess column returned to garrison, its task fulfilled. In May the [[Kedjoerön Petiambang]] and fourteen prominent chiefs visited the fair exhibition at Koeta Radja under [[W. Muurling|Muurling]]'s leadership, and there [[Rödjö Tjoet Pasér]] with his *panglimas* and the *blah*-heads of Pasér and Reröbö solemnly begged the Governor's forgiveness. In September came news that Lebé Grondong was *not* dead: he had toured the Gajò Loeö during the *hari raja* feasts extorting from the population, had obtained two carbines on the death of the resistance head of [[Badaq]], and in October murdered [[Rödjö Akoeb]] of Penosan in revenge for his journey to Koetaradja — though he handed in a *panglima*, a *goeroe* and a carbine.

### From [[The resistance in Atjeh 1908–1912]]

In June and July a band under [[Ama-n-Tampog]] caused fresh trouble and murdered three *penghoeloes*, and to crown it, news came in September that Lebé Grondong was *not* killed. It shortly appeared that this dangerous opponent had made a journey through the actual [[Gajò Loeö]] during the *hari raja* feasts, extorting from the population everywhere, and that he had obtained two precious carbines through the death of the once influential resistance head of [[Badaq]]. He let no grass grow: in October he murdered [[Rödjö Akoeb]] of Penosan in revenge for the latter's journey to Koetaradja — but he handed in a *panglima*, a *goeroe* and a carbine to the *Kompeuni* ("the Company (the Dutch power)"). In October Swart made an inspection to [[Bireuën]] and [[Kr. Seumpo]], the latter being reached for the first time by motorcar along the [[Gajòweg]].
