**Radja Batoe van Singkel** was a Westkust chief; surrendered 1910 mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Submitted in 1910 during Governor Swart's administration.

## Role

[[Westkust]] chief; surrendered [[1910]]

## Account from Jongejans

Submitted in [[1910]] during [[H.E. Swart|Governor Swart]]'s administration.

## Significance

Radja Batoe van Singkel appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
