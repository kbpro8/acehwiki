**James Loudon** was Governor-General of the [[Dutch East Indies]] during the planning and departure of the [[Second Dutch Expedition to Aceh]] in [[1873]].

## Role in the campaign

Together with [[Jan van Swieten]] and Minister [[I. D. Fransen van de Putte]], he participated in the political discussions that produced the expedition’s initial instructions. He saw [[Jan van Swieten|Van Swieten]] off at [[Batavia]] on [[20 November]] [[1873]].

## Significance

The source places [[James Loudon]] within the contested military and political history of the [[Aceh War]].
