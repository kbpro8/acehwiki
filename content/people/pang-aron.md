**Pang Aron** was the most active subordinate commander of [[Tgk. di Barat]], who reported himself with eight followers in [[1910]].

The submission of Pang Aron in [[Keureutòë]] closed a year in which seventy-one resistance men were killed, fifty-one taken prisoner and eighty submitted: "the death (or submission) of so many fanatical and influential men of the resistance brought about the lasting turn." In [[Bireuën]], where respect for the *[[Kompeuni]]* had been restored under [[G. F. B. Watrin|Watrin]], the protected population turned against the bands, and that meant on [[20 December]] [[1909]] the end for a Pang Aron there.
