**A. Bangert** — *Kapitein* (Captain) of the artillery of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army|K.N.I.L.]] (Royal Netherlands East Indies Army) — was the commander of the [[Bergartillerie / Bergbatterijen|4e Bergbatterij]] (4th Mountain Battery) throughout the [[1896]] campaign in [[Groot-Atjeh]], in which capacity he participated in every major Dutch field operation of the year, from the [[Relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit (8 April 1896)|relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit]] on [[8 April]] [[1896]] through the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|great tuchtiging of the IV and VI Moekims of 23–25 May 1896]] to the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|tuchtiging of the V Moekims Montassik of 16–17 June 1896]]. He is one of the principal Dutch artillery officers of the [[1896]] campaign in *[[ATJEH '96 (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96]]* (1913).

## The 4e Bergbatterij under Bangert

The 4e Bergbatterij was, on [[1 January]] [[1896]], composed of three officers, forty-seven European and thirty-one Native men, with four *vuurmonden* (guns), three officers' horses (*rijpaarden*), and thirty-eight draught and pack animals (*trek- en draagdieren*). Bangert commanded the battery throughout the year. The *Tweede Deel* of *ATJEH '96* records the battery's reduced strength later in the year as two lieutenants, fifty-nine men, thirty horses, and four guns, reflecting the wear of the campaign. The 4e Bergbatterij was one of the three principal mountain batteries of the [[Militair Commando van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Military Command of Atjeh and Dependencies]] in [[1896]], alongside the 1e Bergbatterij under [[G. J. E. Nauta|Kapitein Nauta]] and the 2e Bergbatterij under [[P. J. Dibbets|Kapitein Dibbets]].

## The 1896 campaign

Bangert's 4e Bergbatterij was, throughout [[1896]], one of the principal Dutch artillery units in [[Groot-Atjeh]]. The *1e Deel* of *[[ATJEH '96 (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96]]* records the battery's participation in:

- the [[Relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit (8 April 1896)|relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit]] on [[8 April]] [[1896]];
- the [[Bombardment of Lampisang (27 April – 23 May 1896)|bombardment of Lampisang of 27 April – 23 May 1896]];
- the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|great tuchtiging of the IV and VI Moekims of 23–25 May 1896]];
- the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|tuchtiging of the V Moekims Montassik of 16–17 June 1896]];
- the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|tuchtiging of the Lam-Krak Moekims of 9–10 June 1896]].

The *Tweede Deel* records the battery's participation in the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|tuchtiging of the V Moekims Montassik]] and other operations of the second semester. In each engagement Kruisheer records the expenditure of *Granaat* (shells) and *Granaat-Kartets* (shrapnel) by the battery.

## Significance

Bangert is one of the principal Dutch artillery officers of the [[1896]] campaign and the commander of the 4e Bergbatterij throughout the year. His battery was one of the principal Dutch artillery units in [[Groot-Atjeh]] and participated in every major operation of the first semester. The mountain guns of the 4e Bergbatterij — light, mobile pieces capable of operating in the broken country of the Atjehnese interior — were the principal Dutch artillery support for the infantry and [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|marechaussée]] columns of the [[1896]] campaign.
