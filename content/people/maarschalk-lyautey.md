**Maarschalk Lyautey** was a French Marshal, military administrator mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. In an interview by Van Blankenstein said: 'Wegenaanleg is de ware pénétration pacifique.

## Role

French Marshal, military administrator

## Account from Jongejans

In an interview by [[Van Blankenstein]] said: 'Wegenaanleg is de ware [[Pénétration pacifique|pénétration pacifique]]. Waar de weg begint, houden de vijandelijkheden op... Wat men aan wegen uitgeeft, spaart men dubbel en dwars aan troepen.'

## Significance

Maarschalk Lyautey appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
