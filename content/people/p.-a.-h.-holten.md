**P. A. H. Holten** was an officer of the *[[Korps Marechaussee te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* in [[Atjeh]] recorded in the *[[Overzicht van Onderscheidingen toegekend aan het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden (1890-1930)|Overzicht van Onderscheidingen]]* of the [[1930]] jubilee number as knight of the *[[Militaire Willemsorde|Militaire Willems-Orde]]* 4th class for the [[Gajolanden|Gajo]] and [[Alaslanden|Alas]] lands.

## Entry in the register

The register lists him under the separate heading **Gajo- en Alaslanden**, together with *Kapt. Mar.* [[W. B. J. A. Scheepens]], who received the 3rd class. The awards relate to the operations following the [[Tocht naar de Gajo-Loëös (1904)|march to the Gajo-Loëös]] of [[1904]] and the [[Dempen van den opstand in de Gajo- en Alaslanden (1905)|revolt of 1905]].
