**Lieutenant Mirandolle** served as a marine officer in the [[Korps Mariniers]] during the Aceh expedition of [[1875]]–[[1876]]. He commanded a marine peloton that provided cover for artillery units and participated in the assault on the enemy camp of [[Daroe]].

### Actions at Daroe
During the fighting on [[28 January]] [[1876]], Mirandolle’s peloton protected the artillery section and later, on [[30 January]] [[1876]], he assembled about twenty marines on a height to fire upon the enemy, sustaining a wound in the process.

### Later Service
Mirandolle continued to serve with the marine corps after the Aceh campaign, later taking part in other colonial expeditions in the Dutch East Indies.
