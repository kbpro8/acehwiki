Daughter of Muhammad and wife of Ali, central to two hikayats — briefly suspected of adultery in the Hikayat peudeueng before being cleared, and the recipient, in the Tambih tujoh blaih, of her father's instructions on a wife's duties to her husband, one of the most popular themes in Acehnese popular literature.

## Account from *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*

Elderly. Made debts; was [[Banta Ahmat|Amat]]'s wife on day of [[Atjeh-moord|atjeh-moord]]. Reported [[Banta Ahmat|Amat]] was 'uithuizig' (out late) days before attack, came home late at night, including night before attack.

*Source: J. Jongejans, Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu (Baarn: Hollandia Drukkerij N.V., c. 1938), pp. 384, 385.*

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
