Daughter of Muhammad and wife of Ali, central to two hikayats — briefly suspected of adultery in the Hikayat peudeueng before being cleared, and the recipient, in the Tambih tujoh blaih, of her father's instructions on a wife's duties to her husband, one of the most popular themes in Acehnese popular literature.
