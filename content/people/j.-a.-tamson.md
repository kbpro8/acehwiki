**J. A. Tamson** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[H. N. A. Swart]]

Having been dressed by the *officier van gezondheid* [[J. A. Tamson]], Swart resumed command of his section and took part in the rest of the day's operations. He remained with the troop movements with his arm in a sling and returned to [[Magelang]] after six weeks. By royal decree of [[30 August]] [[1891]] no. 2 he was appointed Knight 4th Class of the [[Militaire Willemsorde]] for his conduct at various encounters with the enemy in Atjeh during [[1890]].

### From [[Attack on the Boekit Roembia (11 June 1890)]]

Having had a shot through his left upper arm, lieutenant Swart let himself be dressed by the *officier van gezondheid* [[J. A. Tamson]], returned to the troops, resumed the command of his section and took part in the further operations of the column for the remainder of the day. The left wing, first to debouch from the palm gardens, came under fire, wounding an Ambonese fusilier of the 3rd company; that company's commander at once had the fortification from which the shot came stormed. The 1st and 2nd companies attacked the enemy's main fortification on the summit of the Roembia to the playing of the battalion band.
