**Luitenant Tuinenburg** was a Dutch lieutenant of the garrison of the [[Geconcentreerde Stelling|concentrated line]] at [[Acheh|Atjeh]] in [[1885]]. In majoor [[M. T. H. Perelaer|Perelaer]]'s list he is recorded as wounded at [[Lampermej]], close by the tramway bridge (see [[Guerilla raids inside the concentration line (1885)]]).
