---
title: Muhamat Napiah
aliases: [Muhammad ibn al-Hanafiyyah]
tags: [tokoh, agama]
---

Son of Ali by his Hanafite wife, designated in a dream as the avenger of Hasan and Husain. He defeated Yadib and his allies at Kerbela despite losing his two principal panglimas, pursuing a remnant of Yadib's followers into a cave that closed of its own accord — he and his horse remain within, awaiting resurrection.
