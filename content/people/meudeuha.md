---
title: Meudeuha'
aliases: [Maudeuha']
tags: [tokoh, karya-sastra]
---

A man of keen wit and justice who, despite persecution by four envious royal teachers laying riddles and false accusations before him, catches all his enemies in their own nets, supported by his wife Putroe Chindu Kaseumi. Made supreme judge, he leads king Wadihirah's war against Jiran of Panjalarah, defeating him in both strategy and theological dispute.
