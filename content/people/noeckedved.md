**Nöckedved** was an *officier van gezondheid* (medical officer) serving in [[1879]] as the junior of the two medical officers of the [[Lamtoba column (1879)|Lamtoba column]] — a Dane, as the narrative expressly remarks — during the expedition to [[Lampanas]], [[Lamtoba]] and [[Selimoen Expedition (August 1879)|Selimoen]] recorded in [[W. Cool]] and [[G. B. Hooijer]]'s *[[Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis (Cool & Hooijer, 1889)|Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis]]* ([[1889]]). The form of the name in the digitised text varies (Nöckedved, Nöckendved); the officer is everywhere the same.

## Services of the campaign

On the [[March from Lampanas to Lamtoba (28–29 July 1879)|march from Lampanas to Lamtoba]] of [[28 July]] [[1879]] Nöckedved marched with the advance guard, in charge of the ambulance of ten *tandoes* attached to the main body of the *voorhoede* under Majoor [[W. A. Coblijn|Coblijn]]. His principal recorded service of the campaign came on [[16 August]] of that year, in the bivak at [[Selimoen]]: when Kapitein [[Kapitein Van Rijn|Van Rijn]] marched with one and a half companies and all the available coolies to [[Ajer-Alang]] to forage, the column at the same time evacuated four wounded and thirteen heavy sick, and because these could not remain at Ajer-Alang, it was Nöckedved — the officer of health, noted the authors, "een Deen" — who accompanied the transport all the way through to [[Indrapoeri]], returning the same evening to Ajer-Alang. The movement was made through a country that a few days before had been wholly unknown and had always been accounted the heart of the resistance; white flags now stood out everywhere, the population crowded towards the marching column, and the sick of the kampongs were brought for advice and medicine.
