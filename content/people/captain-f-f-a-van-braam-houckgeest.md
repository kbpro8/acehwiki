**Captain F. F.A. van Braam Houckgeest** led a detachment of the [[Dutch Marine Corps|Korps Mariniers]] that was transferred to the [[Indische Leger]] on [[17 May]] [[1873]]. The detachment, comprising 5 officers and 294 non‑commissioned officers and men, embarked aboard the steamship **Koning der Nederlanden** of the **Maatschappij Nederland** and sailed to [[Padang]] for integration into the colonial army.

The unit later formed part of the first brigade stationed at [[Fort de Kock]] and participated in subsequent operations in Aceh throughout 1873‑1874.
