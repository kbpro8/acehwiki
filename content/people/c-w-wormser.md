**C. W. Wormser** — published as Dr. C. W. Wormser — was a Dutch colonial administrator and author who, in [[1946]], edited and compiled the volume *[[Wat Indië Ontving en Schonk]]* ("What the Indies Received and Gave") under the auspices of the [[Indisch Instituut]] and published by [[Wereldbibliotheek N.V.]] in Amsterdam. The volume brought together fourteen specialist contributors — among them Dr. [[F. H. van Naerssen|Van Naerssen]], Prof. Dr. [[W. J. A. Kernkamp|Kernkamp]], Dr. [[K. J. Brouwer|Brouwer]], Dr. [[G. J. van Hall|Van Hall]], Mr. Dr. [[L. J. J. Caron|Caron]], Ir. [[F. P. C. S. van der Ploeg|Van der Ploeg]], Ir. [[W. J. Utermark|Utermark]] and Pater [[L. van Rijckevorsel|Van Rijckevorsel]] — to produce a synthetic survey of the cultural, religious, educational, agricultural, mining and economic life of the [[Nederlandsch-Indië|Dutch East Indies]] on the threshold of Indonesian independence.

## Role as editor of *Wat Indië Ontving en Schonk*

Wormser is named on the title page of the volume as the volume's *samensteller en verzorger* (compiler and editor), with the contributors listed as his *medewerkers* (collaborators). The book appeared in [[1946]] — that is, in the immediate aftermath of the [[Jappentijd (Japanese occupation, 1942-1945)|Japanese occupation of the Indies]] and the proclamation of the Republic of Indonesia, but before the formal [[Souvereiniteitsoverdracht (27 December 1949)|transfer of sovereignty]] in [[1949]]. The volume therefore reflects the late-colonial Dutch scholarly apparatus at its last moment of unchallenged self-presentation, even as the political ground was shifting beneath it.

For the [[Acheh|Atjeh]]-focused reader, Wormser's editorial hand is most visible in the chapters that draw on Atjeh material: the *oudste cultuurstroomen* (Van Naerssen), the *invloed van den Islam* (Kernkamp), the *bestuursvormen* (Caron), and the *landbouw* and *mijnbouw* chapters. Each of these digests Atjeh-related material from the standard colonial scholarship — chiefly [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]]'s *De Atjéhers* ([[1893]]–[[1894]]) and [[J. Kreemer|Kreemer]]'s *Atjèh* ([[1922]]–[[1923]]) — for a general Dutch readership.

## Significance

Wormser's significance in the AcehWiki corpus is largely editorial: as the volume's compiler he is responsible for the survival, in mid-twentieth-century Dutch, of a particular synthesis of Atjeh material that would otherwise have remained buried in specialist monographs. His decision to include detailed Atjeh-specific material in chapters on Islam and colonial administration — including the [[Meuseugit Raya|Meuseugit Raja]] of [[Kutaradja|Koeta Radja]], the [[Meunasah]], the [[Teungku|Teungkoe]], the [[Toean di boh]], the [[Adat and Hokm Atjeh proverb|Atjeh proverb on *adat* and *hokm*]], and the [[Korte Verklaring|*korte verklaring*]] policy of [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] — made the volume a useful point of entry for any educated Dutch reader in the late 1940s who wanted to know what had been written about Atjeh.
