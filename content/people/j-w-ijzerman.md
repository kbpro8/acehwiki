**J. W. IJzerman** was a Dutch engineer and author of *Dwars door Sumatra* ("Across Sumatra"), published in Amsterdam in [[1895]] with plates, in royal octavo format. The work was offered in [[1902]] by the bookseller Martinus Nijhoff at 11 florins in commemoration of the third centenary of the Dutch East India Company.

## Work

IJzerman's *Dwars door Sumatra* described a trans-Sumatra journey, crossing the island from east to west or vice versa. Such expeditions through the interior of Sumatra — traversing the territories of various Batak, Gayo, and Acehnese peoples — were significant undertakings in the late nineteenth century, contributing to geographical and ethnographic knowledge of the island. The work complemented the more regionally focused studies of [[Atjeh]] by [[C. Snouck Hurgronje]] and the topographical work of [[R. D. M. Verbeek]] on Sumatra's West Coast.
