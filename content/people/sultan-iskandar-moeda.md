**Sultan Iskandar Moeda** (took power 1607 at age 17) was a Greatest Sultan of Atjeh mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Aceh's greatest ruler.

## Role

Greatest [[Sultan of Atjeh (d. 1874)|Sultan of Atjeh]]

## Dates

took power [[1607]] at age 17

## Account from Jongejans

[[Aceh]]'s greatest ruler. Took power in [[1607]] at age 17. During his reign conquered half of [[Sumatra]]'s coastal area and an equal portion of the [[Malay Peninsula|Malacca peninsula]]. Reputed to have organized/introduced the '[[Nanggroe / Familie-stelsel (institution)|nanggroe]]' ([[Nanggroe / Familie-stelsel (institution)|family system]]) in [[Atjeh]]. Fought [[Portuguese Traders|the Portuguese]] as 'ungeloovigen' (unbelievers). Golden age [[1550]]-[[1650]] is centered on his reign. His death triggered crisis - a successor's brief reign was followed by a female Sultana (and three more female sultanas in the 17th century), evidence of the post-Iskandar crisis.

## Significance

Sultan Iskandar Moeda appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
