**Tengku Dhamrah** was an Indonesian civil servant who served as head of the Information Service (*Voorlichtingsdienst*) of the [[Negara Sumatera Timur]] (N.S.T.). He was a member of the N.S.T. delegation that returned on 15 May [[1950]] from Djakarta, where the delegation — led by the Wali Negara, Dr. T. Mansoer, and including the Wakil Wali Negara [[Radja Kaliamsjah Sinaga]] — had on 13 May handed a mandate to Prime Minister Hatta for conducting negotiations with the Republic of Indonesia regarding the formation of a unitary state encompassing all of Indonesia.
