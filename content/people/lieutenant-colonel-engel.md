**Lieutenant‑Colonel Engel** led a reconnaissance column on [[5 January]] [[1876]] that explored the **kloof** (gorge) leading to the **IV Moekims**. He later received permission from **General Pel** to withdraw the column after encountering heavy enemy fire (see [[page 136]]).

## Reconnaissance Mission

Engel’s party, comprising infantry and marine units, attempted to cross the **Beradin** gorge but was forced to retreat due to sustained attacks from Acehnese positions on the surrounding hills.

## Significance

His cautious approach and reliance on higher‑command authorization reflect the Dutch emphasis on coordinated, hierarchical decision‑making in difficult terrain.
