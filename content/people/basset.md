**Louis Basset** was a French scholar who published *La Bordah du Cheikh al‑Būsīrī* (Paris, 1894). The source cites his introduction as a key reference for the study of the *Burda* poem. His bibliographic work contributed to the corpus of knowledge that later Malay translators accessed when rendering the poem for the Sultanate of [[Acheh]].
