**Sergeant Rijser** was a Dutch [[genietroepen|military engineering (Genie)]] sergeant who saved the life of 1st Lieutenant Cramer (of the Genie) during the heavy fighting around **Garouw** (28 June 1874). When Cramer's revolver misfired as an Acehnese warrior rushed him and the lieutenant was about to be cut down with a klewang after breaking his sword on the attacker's blade, Rijser seized the raised arm of the Acehnese and killed him with his own weapon after a short struggle.
