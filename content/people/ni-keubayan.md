---
title: Ni Keubayan
aliases: [Ni Kubayan]
tags: [tokoh, karya-sastra]
---

A well-known figure in Malay tales, appearing in the Hikayat Malem Diwa as the elderly woman who brings together the hero and Putroe Bungsu. She also appears under other names — Ni Kemaya and Mande Rubiah — as the helpful old woman or adoptive mother in other hikayats.
