**Dr. C. Winkler** was a Dutch physician and bacteriologist who served as assistant to Professor [[C. A. Pekelharing|Pekelharing]] on the Dutch government mission to the [[netherlands|Netherlands Indies]] in [[1886]] to investigate the causes of **beri-beri**, which was then ravaging the [[Indische Leger|Indies army]] garrison in [[atjeh|Atjeh]].

## The Mission

According to [[P. Brooshooft|Brooshooft]], Pekelharing — "een onzer beste bacteriologen" — was sent out by the government after earlier work by Indies medical officers and a Japanese doctor at [[koeta-radja|Kota Radja]] had failed to identify the disease's aetiology. Winkler accompanied him as his assistant ("met Dr. C. Winkler als assistent") to carry out the bacteriological and other researches that it was hoped would disclose the cause and lead to effective preventive measures.
