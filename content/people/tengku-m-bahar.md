**Tengku M. Bahar** was an Indonesian civil servant and politician who served as a member of the College of Delegates (*College van Gedelegeerden*) of the Provisional Council (*Voorlopige Raad*) of the [[Negara Sumatera Timur]] (N.S.T.). On 1 April [[1950]], following the honourable discharge of [[J. F. Keulemans]], Tengku Bahar was appointed acting head of the Department of Cultural Affairs (*wnd. hoofd van het Dep. van Cult. Zaken*) of the N.S.T.

## Role in the N.S.T.

The Department of Cultural Affairs under the N.S.T. encompassed Education, Indonesian Culture, Public Health, and Social Affairs. Tengku Bahar's elevation to this position reflected the broader transfer of administrative responsibilities from Dutch to Indonesian personnel that characterised the final months of the N.S.T.'s existence, prior to its dissolution on 15 August [[1950]] and the incorporation of its territory — including the pre-war residencies of [[Atjeh]], [[Tapanuli]], and East Sumatra — into the autonomous province of North Sumatra.
