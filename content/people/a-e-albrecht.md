**A.E. Albrecht** was a 1st Lieutenant in the [[3e Bataljon Infanterie]].

## Service
Albrecht commanded the 2nd company during the [[Second Conquest of the Great Missigit]] on [[14 April]] [[1873]], supporting the right wing of the assault under the overarching command of Major [[F.P. Cavaljé]].
