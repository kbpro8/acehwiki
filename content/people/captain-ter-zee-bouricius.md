**Captain ter zee Bouricius** commanded the marine landing division during the third Bali expedition of [[1849]] (page 107). His division, comprising roughly 600 men equally split between marines and sailors, was organized into four companies and played a key role in the assaults on [[Djagaraga]] and later on [[Kasoemba]] (page 107).

## Marine Operations

Bouricius’s forces secured the beachhead at [[Boeleleng]] and later participated in the inland advance toward the fortified positions. After the death of General‑Major Michiels, Bouricius continued to lead his troops during the subsequent actions at [[Soenla Lawas]] (page 108).

## Recognition

He was among the officers awarded the *Militaire Willemsorde* by the royal decree of [[11 December]] [[1849]] (page 108).
