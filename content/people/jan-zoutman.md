**Jan Zoutman** was a Dutch admiral who commanded the Dutch fleet during the Battle of [[Doggersbank]] on [[5 August]] [[1781]]. His cautious tactics—allowing the British fleet under Rear‑Admiral [[Hyde Parker]] to close in before delivering a broadside—were later criticized, but his fleet’s disciplined fire contributed to the Dutch holding its position.

## Command at Doggersbank
Zoutman’s flagship formed the centre of the Dutch line. When British Rear‑Admiral Hyde Parker attempted a columnar approach, Zoutman “let the opportunity pass,” allowing the British to fire the first broadside.
