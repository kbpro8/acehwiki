**Captain van Aerssen** was a Dutch *kapitein* of the [[Indische Leger|Indische leger]] who, on [[15 April]] [[1874]], conquered a *benteng* on which the Atjehneezen had mounted an iron cannon — the small success that opened the brief series of reconnaissances culminating in the [[Battle of Lampoe Oek (16 April 1874)|échec at Lampoe Oek]] on the following day.

## The Capture of the Benteng

By mid-April [[1874]] the Dutch, under [[Generaal Pel|kolonel Pel]]'s pressure for daily patrols in execution of [[Jan van Swieten|van Swieten]]'s 7 April instruction, were pushing their reconnaissances further from the *hoofdwal*. On [[15 April]] [[1874]] van Aerssen conquered a *benteng* — an Atjehneesche fortified strongpoint — on which an iron cannon had been mounted in *batterij*. The capture is recorded by [[G. F. W. Borel|Borel]] as a small but significant success: the presence of the iron cannon shows that the Atjehneezen had begun to mount artillery on their forward works, a development the Dutch had not anticipated. The next day, [[16 April]] [[1874]], [[Majoor Romswinckel|Romswinckel]] was given *lithofracteur* — the new blasting explosive — "om te vernielen," proof that the Dutch expected to find more *versterkingen* in the same area.

## The Lampoe Oek Échec

The very next day Romswinckel, sent to support two patrols that had been heavily engaged, discovered the strongly fortified enemy stelling near [[Longbatta]] — later known as [[Lampoe Oek]] — of which the Dutch had known nothing. The [[Battle of Lampoe Oek (16 April 1874)|échec at Lampoe Oek]] cost nearly a hundred Dutch dead and wounded, of whom five were *vermist*. The date was "noodlottig" — exactly one year after the [[First Aceh Expedition (April 1873)|first expedition]]'s failure. Van Aerssen's success of the 15th thus stands in Borel's narrative as the last Dutch success before the worst Dutch reverse of [[1874]] — a reminder that the enemy's positions around [[Kota Radja]] were more formidable than van Swieten's *afwachtende houding* had allowed the Dutch to believe.
