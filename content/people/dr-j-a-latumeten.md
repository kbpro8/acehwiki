**Dr. J. A. Latumeten** was a Director of the Sabang asylum 1926-1935 mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Provided many good services to the institution from 1926 to 1935.

## Role

Director of the [[Sabang]] asylum [[1926]]-[[1935]]

## Account from Jongejans

Provided many good services to the institution from [[1926]] to [[1935]].

## Significance

Dr. J. A. Latumeten appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
