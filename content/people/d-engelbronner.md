**D'Engelbronner** was a Dutch *controleur* (controller), a civil colonial official in [[Atjeh]] in [[1876]], attached with guides to Major [[Majoor Voigt|Voigt]]'s [[Voigt expedition to the IV Moekims (1-3 June 1876)|column of 1–3 June 1876]] into the [[IV Moekims]] against the band of [[Toekoe Tjihiq Lamngga]].

## June 1876

D'Engelbronner went with the column as its civil officer. At [[Boekit Seboen]] he reported in the evening that he expected an Atjehnese head with guides at ten; the head was fetched from his house by a patrol towards midnight, carrying the news that Toekoe Tjihiq had moved to Naga Oembang with 200 men. When the disappointed column came at the last refuge of the band and Major Voigt proposed to burn the two abandoned houses as enemy hiding places, it was at D'Engelbronner's request that the major desisted — an act of forbearance towards the ostensibly friendly population that [[Kolonel Meijer|H. F. Meijer]] recorded without comment in *[[Atjeh van 26 December 1875 tot 4 September 1876]]* ([[1883]]).
