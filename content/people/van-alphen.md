**Van Alphen** was a **[[lieutenant ter zee]]** who commanded a landing division of 250 men, including 101 marines, in **April [[1859]] during the **[[First Boni Expedition 1859]]** against the kampong **[[Saribanoa]]** on the island of **[[Sepora]]**.

## Action

His forces captured the settlement after a coordinated amphibious assault.
