**J. J. Staat** was a Dutch captain of engineers (*Kapitein der Genie*) in [[Atjeh]] in [[1876]], who reconnoitred — with Captain of the General Staff [[F. C. E. Meijer]] — the crossing of the [[Kroeëng Raba]] on [[11 July]] [[1876]] and then supervised the first fortification work of the new post at the mouth of that river.

## The reconnaissance of 11 July 1876

Before the encircling march against [[Toekoe Tjihiq Lamngga]], a precise reconnaissance of the Kroeëng Raba was indispensable. Staat was one of the two officers charged with it, escorted by a company: from Pakan Bing across the brook at Anaq Paja to kampong Lamlom, then up the river. Near the kampong Raba they found the place shown on the map where a raft crossing was easy; and near Loeknga they found, and Staat himself with some soldiers tested, a one-metre foot-bridge — found already in January — that infantry could still use, before the exchange of shots with Atjehers hurrying to the left bank ended the reconnaissance and betrayed the troops' presence.

## The fortification of Kroeëng Raba

From [[14 July]] Staat directed the fortification work at kampong Moesang at the river mouth — built almost wholly out of the materials of the kampong itself after the hired steamer *Marion* could land nothing through the surf of the bay. On [[19 July]] he handed the direction over to First Lieutenant of Infantry [[H. W. Nijenhuis]], who had arrived with engineering materials from Kota Radja; the palisade was up by that date, and by [[28 July]] the work was reported fit to receive its garrison.
