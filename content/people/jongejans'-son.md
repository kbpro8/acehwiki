**Jongejans' Son** (active) was a Son of author mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Made the bandomslag (book's binding/cover design) for the book.

## Role

Son of author

## Dates

active

## Account from Jongejans

Made the bandomslag (book's binding/cover design) for the book.

## Significance

Jongejans' Son appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
