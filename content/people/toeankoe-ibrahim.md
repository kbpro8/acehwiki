**Toeankoe Ibrahim** was a Son of the Pretendent-Sultan mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Captured 24 December 1902 at Lammeulo together with his father's favorite Potjoet di Tjot Moerong.

## Role

Son of the [[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]]

## Account from Jongejans

Captured [[24 December]] [[1902]] at [[Lammeulo]] together with his father's favorite [[Potjoet]] di Tjot Moerong. Received with his father by the Governor on [[20 January]] [[1903]]. Later visits Aceh from time to time; banishment provisions for him recently lifted.

## Significance

Toeankoe Ibrahim appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
