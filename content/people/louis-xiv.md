**Louis XIV** was the King of **[[France]]** whose aggressive foreign policy in the **[[1660s]]** and **[[1670s]]** prompted the formation of the **[[Triple Alliance (1668)]]** and the secret **[[Treaty of Dover (1670)]]** with **[[England|England]]**. His ambitions threatened the **[[United Provinces (Dutch Republic)]]**, weakening Dutch capacity to protect its interests in the **[[Sultanate of Aceh]]** and other Asian trading posts.

## Relations with the Dutch Republic
Louis XIV’s conquest of parts of **[[Flanders]]** and **[[Franche‑Comté]]** in **[[1667]]** alarmed the Republic, leading to diplomatic realignments that indirectly affected the balance of power in the Indian Ocean, where the **[[VOC]]** competed for Aceh’s pepper.
