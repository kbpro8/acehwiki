**Abdul Kadir** was an Indonesian official who served as the representative of the Ministry of Transport and Public Works (*min. v. Verkeer en Openbare Werken*) of the Republic of the United States of Indonesia (R.S.S.). He officially opened the new railway connection at the ceremony at Besitang, at which the Atjeh-related incident involving [[B. de Jong]] and [[C. A. Zandbergen]] was noted.
