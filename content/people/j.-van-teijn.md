**J. van Teijn** was the Dutch Major-General who commanded in [[Atjeh]] in [[1890]] and whose partnership with [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|van Heutsz]] demonstrated, in [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]]'s judgement, "that the Acehnese adversary had respect only for purposeful force".

As commander-in-chief van Teijn sent the order to advance at 6.08 a.m. on [[11 June]] [[1890]] in the [[Attack on the Boekit Roembia (11 June 1890)|attack on the Boekit Roembia]] near [[Idi]], and came with van Heutsz into the captured fortification where the colours were planted and the *Wilhelmus* played. After his retirement the Government again chose the policy of waiting and negotiating, so that "after a short and feeble administration by General [[Pompe van Meerdervoort]], General [[C. Deijckerhoff|Deijckerhoff]] took office."
