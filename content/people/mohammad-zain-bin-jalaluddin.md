---
title: Mohammad Zain bin Jalaluddin
tags: [tokoh, penulis]
---

An Acehnese author who wrote in Malay, producing an edition of Sanusi's catechism and, most notably, the Bab an-nikah, a Malay treatise on Mohammedan marriage law lithographed at Constantinople in 1304 A.H. that includes an appendix of Acehnese legal precepts.
