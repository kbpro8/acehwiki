**Lieutenant Van Pelt** was a Dutch marine officer who was killed in action during the 1824 expedition against the Padri forces on [[Celebes]]. His death was mourned by his comrade Lieutenant P. Troost, who recorded the loss in his memoirs.

## Death

Van Pelt fell during a fierce engagement when the enemy opened fire after the Dutch troops had approached the breastwork.
