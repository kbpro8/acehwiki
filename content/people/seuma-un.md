---

title: "Seuma'un"
aliases: [the Acehnese Simeon]
tags: [person, literary-work]
---

Son of Halet, who as an unweaned infant spoke and converted his parents to Islam. He slew the hero Patian, defeated Abu Jahl's army, and fought for Muhammad against king Kobeuti, winning Abu Jahl's daughter in marriage.

- [[Mohammad]]

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
