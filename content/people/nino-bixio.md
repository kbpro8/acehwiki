**Nino Bixio** was a general who died of cholera while commanding a transport ship at the roadstead of [[Acheh|Atjeh]] in [[January 1874|January 1874]], and whose robbed corpse was sought for through the intervention of the Dutch ally [[Toekoe Nek Radja Moeda Setia|Toekoe Nek of Marassa]]. The source work does not give further particulars of his identity or of the circumstances of his death, beyond noting that he was "the general Nino Bixio, then commander of a transport ship." The name suggests he was an Italian — possibly a descendant or relative of the famous Italian patriot Nino Bixio (1828-1870), who had been a comrade of Garibaldi — but the source work does not elaborate.

## The Recovery of the Corpse

[[Toekoe Nek Radja Moeda Setia|Toekoe Nek]] had already before [[11 January]] [[11 January]] [[1874]] "promised his intervention in the recovery of the robbed corpse of the general Nino Bixio who had died of cholera, then commander of a transport ship." The cholera had first raged among the Dutch troops and now made its ravages among the Atjehers; it was the same disease of which the sultan himself would die on [[26 January]] [[26 January]] [[1874]] at Pagger Ajer. The intervention of Toekoe Nek on behalf of the Dutch — at the very moment when the Dutch were seeking to take the [[Kraton of Aceh|Kraton]] — was one of the principal tokens of his alliance.
