**Sarah** was one of the three female elephants of the colonial army's [[transport-olifanten (Aceh)|elephant transport]] in [[Atjeh|Aceh]] — with [[Agam]] the male and her fellow female elephants [[Isah]] and [[Rimba]]. In *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* by Merac ([[1947]]), Sarah is one of the four elephants — "Agam, the male, and Isah, Sarah, and Rimba, the female elephants" — that were disposed of in the time when the narrator was detached there.

## The Female Elephants and the European Zoo

"The future of the two other female elephants was also a European zoo." The future of the two other female elephants (besides the one sent to Medan) was also a European zoo — that is, the female elephants were sent to European zoos when the [[transport-olifanten (Aceh)|elephant transport]] was abolished.

## Significance

Sarah is significant in *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* as one of the three female elephants of the [[transport-olifanten (Aceh)|elephant transport]]. The book's argument — that the female elephants were sent to European zoos when the elephant transport was abolished — is the book's marker of the end of the old days' elephant transport.
