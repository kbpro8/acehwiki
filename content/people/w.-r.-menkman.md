**W. R. Menkman** — W. R. Menkman — was the author of Chapter I of *[[Nederlanders over de Zeeën (De Graaf et al., 1955)|Nederlanders over de Zeeën]]* ([[1955]] second edition), titled *Nederland in Amerika en West-Afrika* (The Netherlands in America and West Africa). Although his chapter does not directly concern [[Acheh|Atjeh]], his contribution to the collaborative volume is part of the editorial frame within which the Atjeh-related chapters (II, IV) are situated.

## Authorship of Chapter I

In the [[1955]] edition of *Nederlanders over de Zeeën*, [[W. R. Menkman|Menkman]] was responsible for Chapter I — the chapter covering the 350-year Dutch maritime enterprise in the Atlantic: the Caribbean, Suriname, Berbice, Demerara, Essequibo, the Gold Coast of Africa, and the brief Dutch Brazil episode. The chapter is, in the volume's structure, the Atlantic counterpart to [[H. J. de Graaf|De Graaf]]'s Chapter II on the Indies.

## The collaborative volume

The [[1955]] edition of *Nederlanders over de Zeeën* was a collaborative work edited by [[H. J. de Graaf|Dr. H. J. de Graaf]]. [[W. R. Menkman|Menkman]] was one of the three contributing authors alongside [[H. J. de Graaf|De Graaf]] and [[G. Gonggrijp|Gonggrijp]]; the chapter on South Africa (Chapter VII) was contributed by [[P. J. van Winter|Prof. Jhr Dr P. J. van Winter]].

## Significance

[[W. R. Menkman|Menkman]]'s significance for the encyclopedia of [[Acheh]] history is indirect: he is one of the four contributors to the [[1955]] *Nederlanders over de Zeeën*, the source of the present Aceh Wiki articles. His chapter on the Atlantic provides the comparative frame within which the Indies chapters are situated.
