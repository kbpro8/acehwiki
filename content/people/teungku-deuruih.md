---
title: Teungku Deuruih
tags: [person, scholar]
---

A teacher of South Indian origin who guided students at Chot Paya to a higher standard of Quran recitation than village schools could provide, an example of the foreign teachers who brought specialized expertise to Acheh.

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
