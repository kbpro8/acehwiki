**T. Bén Blang Pidië** was den bekenden verzetsleider who kept the whole [[Tapa' Toean]] district in unrest in [[1908]] and who submitted with his sons and hundreds of followers in the course of that year.

The action of the *[[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|marechaussée]]* under [[Th. J. Veltman|Veltman]] soon produced submissions: in June T. Bén's eldest son with a hundred followers and nine rifles; a few weeks later T. Bén himself with two other sons and 160 men and another nine rifles; and towards the end of the year his two last wandering sons, whom he brought to the administration in person — "and Tapa' Toean became quiet again."
