**Karel van der Heyden** was a Dutch general and Civil and Military Governor of Aceh during the early phase of the Aceh War.

## Military Career and Service in Aceh

General Van der Heyden conducted major military campaigns across the valley of [[Groot-Atjeh]] prior to [[1884]] and led early assaults against the mountain stronghold at [[Batèë Ilië]]. His tenure was followed on [[19 August]] [[1884]] by the adoption of the defensive [[Concentratie-Stelsel]].
