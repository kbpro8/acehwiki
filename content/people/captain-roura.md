---
title: Captain Roura
tags: [tokoh]
---

Captain of the brig Eagle, present in the roads of Rigaih at the time of the Hok Canton attack. When Teuku Uma failed to return from the steamer, Roura boarded the Hok Canton himself and brought her to safety at Olehleh.
