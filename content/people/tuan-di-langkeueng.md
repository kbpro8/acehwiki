title: Tuan di Langkeueng
aliases: [Tuan Sulah Naima]
tags: [tokoh, wali]
---

Known euphemistically as "the saint with the wrong name," his tomb at Lam Tisang is said to contain only his organ of generation. He is the patron of married couples seeking children, who pay vows and drink water mixed with earth from his grave — a cult Habib Abdurrahman is said to have opposed.
