**Teuku Pakeh** was the ruling prince (*Raja*) of [[Pidie]] (Pedir) who mobilized over 1,000 warriors to reinforce the [[Kraton of Aceh]] during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] in [[1873]]–[[1874]].

## Military Role and Naval Retaliation

Teuku Pakeh led coastal contingents from Pidie to defend the capital against General [[Jan van Swieten]]. Following the murder of Dutch envoy [[Mas Soemo Widikdjo]] inside the Kraton, Van Swieten blamed Teuku Pakeh and ordered a naval squadron to bombard his capital at Pedir on [[28 December]] [[1873]]. Hearing of the destruction, Teuku Pakeh withdrew his forces from the Kraton on [[10 January]] [[1874]] to protect his home district.

In [[June]] [[1876]], Teuku Pakeh formally submitted to Dutch authority. Recognizing that the [[1873]] bombardment had hit innocent civilians, the colonial government paid him 50,000 guilders in financial compensation.
