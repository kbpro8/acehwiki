**Lieutenant‑colonel Van Swieten** acted as the senior army officer after the death of General‑Major Michiels during the third Bali expedition of [[1849]] (page 108). He assumed command of the main force, requested further instructions from the colonial government, and oversaw the withdrawal to [[Padang Cove]] before resuming operations against the Balinese at [[Kasoemba]] (page 108).

## Command Decisions

Van Swieten’s hesitation to await further orders from the government led to a temporary cessation of hostilities, but he later renewed the offensive on [[10 June]] [[1849]], capturing the temple of [[Soenla Lawas]] and the settlement of [[Kasoemba]] (page 108).

## Diplomatic Interaction

He negotiated with the radja of [[Kloenkoeng]], whose submission after the third expedition helped restore Dutch authority in the region (page 108).
