**Lieutenant Colonel Meijer** was an officer and tactical column commander of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] who led vanguard forces during [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]] in December [[1875]] and January [[1876]].

## Leadership in the IV Moekims Offensive

On [[26 December]] [[1875]], following the [[Attack on Mibouw]], General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]] appointed [[Lieutenant Colonel Meijer]] to command an elite field column comprising the [[3rd Infantry Battalion]], the [[Marine Corps in Aceh]], one barissan company, and two artillery sections.

In the [[Crossing of Blankala Pass]], [[Lieutenant Colonel Meijer]] led his troops across steep mountain defiles under heavy Acehnese fire, descending into the valley of the [[IV Moekims]] and establishing fortified Dutch garrisons in the interior.
