---
title: Teungku Lam Paloh
tags: [tokoh, wali]
---

A Javanese from Yogyakarta, died 1894, who married and raised a family in Acheh and came to be regarded as a saint by a certain coterie despite little claim to learning, revered as kramat in his own lifetime. He borrowed his name from the gampong within the Dutch-controlled "linie" where he lived.
