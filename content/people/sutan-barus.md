**Sutan Barus** was an Indonesian official who served as inspector-general of enterprises (*insp.-generaal van de ondernemingen*) in [[Atjeh|Atjeh]] during the early 1950s. In February [[1950]], he accompanied the second survey party (*survey-party*) of some twenty planters who travelled by jeep via P. Brandan to [[Langsar Estate]] to assess the condition of plantation enterprises in Atjeh following the Dutch-Indonesian hostilities.

## Role in the Atjeh survey parties (February 1950)

The survey parties were dispatched following the decisions of an initial local arbitral commission for labour disputes and a corresponding central commission (respectively designated P4D and P4P). During discussions between representatives of the Entrepreneurs' Association for Indonesia (*Ondernemersbond voor Indonesië*) on 9 and 10 December [[1949]] at [[Kotaradja]], it was resolved to send survey parties to assess the situation of the enterprises in Atjeh.

Sutan Barus accompanied the second group, which travelled to [[Langsar]], along with his secretary [[G. Kars Gultom]] and [[N. W. L. Stofkoper]], advisor for agricultural enterprises to His Excellency (*E.Z.*). The first group had flown from Medan to Kotaradja on 2 February [[1950]], comprising seven planters from the Société Financière des Caoutchoucs, the Netherlands Trading Company (*Ned. Handel Mij.*), and the State Agricultural Enterprises (*Landslandbouwbedrijven*), accompanied by [[Rasjid Siregar]], commissioner of the [[Sarbupri]].
