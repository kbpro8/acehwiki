**F. Ch. Frank** was a Dutch non-commissioned officer and Sergeant who served nine years in the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]].

## Military Career and Service in Aceh

Born in [[1865]], Frank enlisted in [[1893]] and served extensively in Aceh. He was awarded the Knight 4th Class Military William Order (*M.W.O.*) in [[1905]], received an Honourable Mention in [[1906]], and was promoted to Knight 3rd Class M.W.O. in [[1908]]. He later served as a Police Commissioner 2nd Class.
