**Colonel Ruempol** was a Dutch *overste* (colonel) of the [[Indische Leger|Indische leger]] who commanded the column that took [[Kota Alam]] on [[13 November]] [[1874]] in the [[Conquest of Kota Alam (13 November 1874)|conquest of Kota Alam]] and the column that conquered [[Blang Tjoet]] on [[18 January]] [[1875]] in the [[Conquest of Blang Tjoet (18 January 1875)|conquest of Blang Tjoet]]. In [[G. F. W. Borel|Borel]]'s narrative he is one of [[Generaal Pel|Pel]]'s reliable column commanders, repeatedly entrusted with the consolidation of the eastern face of the *stelling-Pel*.

## The Conquest of Kota Alam

Six days after [[Kolonel Diepenbrock|Diepenbrock]]'s [[Conquest of Lemboe (7 November 1874)|conquest of Lemboe]] of [[7 November]] [[1874]], Ruempol with a half-battalion and an artillery section attacked the south-eastern part of the kampong called Kota Alam, where the [[Atjeh-rivier|Atjeh-rivier]] bends. The aim was to *enfilade* the river upstream, shell the enemy stelling of [[Longbatta]], and bring the densely populated [[III Moekims]] under Dutch fire. Although the Atjehneezen had had a week to prepare — having watched Lemboe fall — they offered only weak resistance. Kota Alam itself cost little; but during the fortification work the Dutch were fired on from all sides, and the day's cost reached more than twenty dead and wounded. By evening the post was *verdedigbaar* and a company was left as garrison. The post, armed with two [[Voorlaadkanonnen in Atjeh|12 c.M. vóórlaadkanonnen]], immediately began to enfilade the river and shell Longbatta.

## The Conquest of Blang Tjoet

Three weeks after the [[Battle of Longbatta (31 December 1874)|battle of Longbatta]] of [[31 December]] [[1874]] — in which [[Majoor van der Meer|van der Meer]]'s column had been routed and [[Majoor van Zijll de Jong|van Zijll de Jong]] had recaptured the lost *benteng* — Ruempol was again entrusted with a consolidation. On [[18 January]] [[1875]] a column of 150 bayonets and an artillery section under his command, with a reserve infantry company, marched out against Blang Tjoet, the kampong between [[Pendetti]] and the [[Longbatta-Mesigit]]. The enemy was permanently driven from the kampong. At a suitable point near the river bank, some four to five hundred paces from the Mesigit, work immediately began on a fortification, using the abandoned Atjehneesche *benteng*. After a few days of clearing, the enemy could be prevented from crossing the river by day, and transport between the Mesigit and Pendetti was now "practically safe."

## Significance

Ruempol's two columns together secured the eastern face of what would become the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]]: Kota Alam sealed the bend of the Atjeh-rivier, and Blang Tjoet bridged the gap between the Mesigit and Pendetti. Both were *betrekkelijk* cheap victories, in Borel's reading, because Pel's combined-*colonne* tactics gave the Atjehneezen no time to mass their forces.
