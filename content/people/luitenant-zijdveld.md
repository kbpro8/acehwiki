**Luitenant Zijdveld** was a Dutch officer of the [[Oost-Indisch Leger|Netherlands East Indies Army]] serving in the outposts of [[Groot-Atjeh]] during the alliance period of [[1893]]–[[1896]], recorded by [[G. B. Hooijer]] in *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]*.

## Service in the *buitenposten* (outer posts)

He was among the officers photographed on the bench at [[Biloel]]. On the afternoon of [[29 March]] [[1896]] he accompanied the patrol of sixty men led by [[Kapitein Schadée]] from [[Biloel]] towards [[Tjot Goeë]] to establish the state of affairs after the defection of [[Teukoe Oemar]]. When the point had advanced some three hundred paces beyond the covered ground north of Biloel, it came under heavy fire from all sides across the flooded rice-fields. Zijdveld, struck by a bullet in the lower jaw (*een kogel in de onderkaak*), was compelled to withdraw, and from that moment the post remained invested by the enemy.
