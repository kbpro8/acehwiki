**N. A. vau Blom** was a **Marine le klasse** who was knighted in the **Militaire Willemsorde 4e klasse** for his actions during the **Tamiang** expeditions of **[[1893]]**. His commendation appears in the Dutch naval yearbook of **[[1897]]‑[[1898]]**.

## Service

Blom’s role involved leading marine troops in assaults on fortified positions along the **Tamiang** river.
