**J.F.D. Bruinsma** was a Major in the Dutch Infantry and the author of the detailed historical account concerning the battles for [[Atjeh's Groote Missigit]].

## Authorship
Bruinsma published his account, *De verovering van Atjeh's groote Missigit*, to memorialize the extraordinary courage, sacrifices, and military tactics of the Dutch soldiers during the conflicts of [[1873]] and [[1874]]. The proceeds from his publication were donated to the children of Fuselier [[Overmeiren]], a brave soldier who fought in the campaigns.
