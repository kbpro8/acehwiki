**Sumangkut** was a *maréchaussée* of the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* who, though severely wounded in the [[Gevecht waarin kapitein Paris sneuvelde (Westkust van Atjeh, 1925-1927)|engagement in which Captain J. Paris fell]] on the [[Westkust van Atjeh|west coast of Atjeh]] during the action of [[1925]]–[[1927]], spotted a fleeing opponent driven out of cover and passed the alarm to [[Suwoek]].

## The incident

After the wounded [[Kromo-di-Koro]] had ordered shots to be fired into the surrounding *[[Alang-alang|alang-alang]]* to flush out any concealed Atjehnese, Sumangkut "zag, als gevolg daarvan, een djahat met een klewang uit de hooge alang-alang vluchten" — saw, in consequence, an opponent flee with a *[[Klewang|klewang]]* out of the high cogon grass. Being himself severely wounded, he could not pursue the man, and warned [[Suwoek]], who took up the chase and brought the fugitive down fifty metres further on.

The term *djahat* used in the account is the corps's word for an armed opponent — literally an evil-doer.
