**Teungkoe Lam Peuneu-eun** is a legendary Acehnese Islamic saint whose tomb is located in the [[IX Mukims]] of Great Aceh. According to Acehnese folklore, he is revered as the spiritual founder of pepper cultivation (*seunebok lada*) in Aceh.

## Legendary Origin of Pepper

Legend holds that Teungkoe Lam Peuneu-eun sowed the seeds of a kapok tree (*panjou*), from which the first pepper vine (*Piper nigrum*) miraculously sprang. In his honor, Acehnese pepper planters historically held an annual feast (*kanduri* or *chandoeri*) in pepper gardens across Aceh prior to the harvest season.
