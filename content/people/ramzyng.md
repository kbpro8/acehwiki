**Ramzyng (spiritual leader)** was a religious figure (referred to as “geistelijke herder”) who, according to the [[Journal of Haringman]], abandoned the colonial settlement and was blamed for the rebels’ retreat from the plantation “[[Markey]]”. Haringman’s entry calls him “Do. Ramzyng”, indicating a possible clerical title.

## Role
Ramzyng is portrayed as a “colony‑weakening” shepherd whose departure may have demoralised the colonial troops and emboldened the rebel forces.
