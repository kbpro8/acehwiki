---
title: Raja Raden
tags: [tokoh, karya-sastra]
---

The younger, loyal brother of Si Ujut in the Hikayat Malem Dagang. Unlike his treacherous brother, Raja Raden converted to Islam, gave his Pahang princess wife to Sultan Eseukanda Muda, and remained loyal to his Acehnese host, ultimately loading his own brother with chains. His tomb is pointed out near the Gunongan close to the Dalam.
