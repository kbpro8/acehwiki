**Van Dijk** was a **Lieutenant ter zee 2c klasse** in the **Royal Netherlands Navy** who was wounded during the **[[30 March]] [[1893]]** assault on the **Tamiang** river blockades aboard the **[[Koerier (ship)]]**. He sustained a light wound alongside two other marines.

## Service

His injury is recorded in the Dutch naval yearbook of **[[1893]]**, reflecting the hazards faced by naval officers in riverine operations.
