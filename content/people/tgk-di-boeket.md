**Tgk. di Boekét** was an Acehnese religious leader, the third son of *[[Teungkoe Sjech Saman di Tiri]]* and one of the two *voornaamste Teungkoes* of the *Tiriō bende* (with *[[Tgk. Tjhi Majét]]*). He was mortally wounded in the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]]'s overval on the schuilplaats in [[May 1911]] — escaped the assault but later died of his wounds. His death, with that of *[[Tgk. Tjhi Majét]]* in the same month, left only the boy *[[Tgk. Tjhi Ma'at]]* of the whole Tiriō bende.

## The third son

"Van de vier zoons van *[[Teungkoe Sjech Saman di Tiri]]* … Tngk. Tjhi Majét en Tgk. di Boekét." The third son of Saman di Tiri, di Boekét was one of the two *voornaamste Teungkoes* of the bende (with *[[Tgk. Tjhi Majét]]*). The eldest son had been killed at the [[Battle of Aneu Galong (1896)|defence of Aneu Galong]] in [[1896]]; *[[Tgk. Lambada]]* had been killed in a gevecht with a patrouille under *[[Luitenant Darlang|luitenant Darlang]]*; Tgk. di Boekét and Tgk. Tjhi Majét lived on as the two voornaamste Teungkoes.

## The overval on the schuilplaats (May 1911)

In the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]]'s overval on the schuilplaats in [[May 1911]], eleven vijanden were killed. *[[Tgk. di Boekét]]* escaped the assault but was mortally wounded. "Later kwam nog het bericht dat ook Tgk. di Boekét aan bekomen verwondingen was overleden en dat bleek juist te zijn" — later the report came that Tgk. di Boekét too had died of the wounds he had received, and the report proved true. With his death, the second of the two *voornaamste Teungkoes* was gone.

## The disintegration of the bende

With *[[Tgk. Tjhi Majét]]* killed in the chase after the schuilplaats assault, and *[[Tgk. di Boekét]]* dead of his wounds, "slechts de jonge Tgk. Tjhi Ma'at was er over van heel de Tiri bende" — only the young Tgk. Tjhi Ma'at was left of the whole Tiriō bende. The two *voornaamste Teungkoes* were both gone; the boy was alone.

## Significance

Tgk. di Boekét is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as the third son of *[[Teungkoe Sjech Saman di Tiri]]* and one of the two *voornaamste Teungkoes* of the *Tiriō bende*. His death — mortally wounded in the overval on the schuilplaats in [[May 1911]], dead of his wounds later — is the book's marker of the disintegration of the bende's leadership: with *[[Tgk. Tjhi Majét]]* killed in the chase and Tgk. di Boekét dead of his wounds, only the boy *[[Tgk. Tjhi Ma'at]]* was left.
