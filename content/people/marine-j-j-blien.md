**Blien, J. J.** was a Dutch marine of the first class who was listed among the honored participants of the Atjeh expeditions of [[1873]] and [[1874]] in the royal decree of [[6 October]] [[1874]].

## Recognition

His inclusion demonstrates the recognition of rank‑and‑file marines in the Dutch honors system.
