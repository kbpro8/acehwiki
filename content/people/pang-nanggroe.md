**Pang Nanggroë** — known to the Dutch troops after [[1907]] as *"de Watergeus"* — was a [[panglima]] of the resistance in [[Keureutoe]] and the surrounding lands from [[September]] [[1905]] to his death in [[1910]]. He came from Matang Teungoh and had been only *toeha peuë* (member of the kampong bestuur) — not of high birth, short and thick-set, no figure like the late [[T. Tjhi Toenong]]; yet he was the man who precisely fitted [[Tjoet Meuthia]], because he had shown himself a brave and energetic fighter in the days of the war, with a heartfelt hatred of the *kaphé*. He was the third husband of Tjoet Meuthia.

## Sworn to Tjoet Meuthia at Tjhi Toenong's death

In the days before the execution of [[T. Tjhi Toenong|Tjhi Toenong]] on [[5 March]] [[1905]], Tjoet Meuthia, then pregnant, was sworn by her husband to bring up their son in hatred of the *kaphé* and, after the execution, to marry Pang Nanggroë. This she swore, and she kept her oath. After the forty-four days of Atjehsche purification, Pang Nanggroë and Pang Lateh appeared with a strong bende in the kampong where Tjoet Meuthia and her son waited; she was placed in a *tandoe*, the eagle's child entrusted to the care of some ten *kawans*. They withdrew to the upper course of the Djambo Ale, a wild but free land with countless hiding-places for men familiar with the forest.

## Earlier action under Tjhi Toenong

Pang Nanggroë's name is already attached to the famous *klewangaanval* of [[22 August]] [[1902]] at [[Simpang Olim|Simpang Olim]] — though at that time he acted under T. Tjhi Toenong — when an Atjehsche bende fell upon a Dutch transport in the high *alang-alang* near Meunasah Djeuro and butchered it. He attempted also a *prauw* attack on Idi. These actions gave him his place in the line of great enemy commanders of the time.

## Activity September 1905 onwards

From [[September]] [[1905]] he developed a tremendous activity. His first success of significance was the overval of a bivak of coolies working on the tram on [[6 May]] [[1907]], in which his twenty men inflicted 2 dead and 4 wounded on the guard detachment and made ten Dutch rifles and 750 cartridges booty, besides a hunting gun and a Winchester carbine. He gave his men a *kandoerie* (slametan) that rang like a bell. On [[15 June]] [[1907]] he overvalled the *marschbivak* at Keudé Bawang (Idi) with the same twenty men: again a Dutch defeat, the loss of a rifle, and only some old iron left on the field. After these exploits his name was established beyond his own region; he could now cooperate with the great oelama's of the resistance, among them [[Tengkoe Sjech di Paja Bakong|Seupot Mata]] and [[Tengkoe di Paja Bakong|Mata Ië]], and [[Tengkoe di Barat]].

In [[1907]] he earned his nickname *de Watergeus* among the Dutch troops by a bold water-borne stroke. The *colonne matjan* and the sharpened military action of [[G.C.E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] did him some harm — chiefly by reducing the stream of volunteers and of *ewang sabil* contributions — but they did not bring him to his knees.

## The end, 26 September 1910

He was now constantly pursued; the times in which he could stay long in one schuilplaats were over. Usually [[Tjoet Meuthia]] was with him. On [[26 September]] [[1910]] [[Van Slooten]]'s brigade, on the trail of the bende, came to a place where Pang Nanggroë with two women was staying. When Van Slooten arrived the hunted ones had disappeared in the direction of Boekit Hagoe; but although it was night and raining hard, Van Slooten pressed on. The next morning, on the spoor, the spits came under fire from a group of enemies it had flushed. After a difficult pursuit through the Paja Tjitcem moeras — the men almost unable to advance in the densely grown swamp — at a distance of about two hundred metres Pang Nanggroë received his bullet and fell. After some searching the brigade found him dead, and the guide said: "Pang Nanggroë." Because of the great importance of the death of the Watergeus, his body was carried along so that the bevolking could identify it. Of Tjoet Meuthia and the boy no trace.

## Significance

His grave, with that of Pang Lateh — his energetic fellow-fighter killed on another occasion — lies near the *keudé* in a *kandang*. With his death the Keureutoe resistance lost its most capable leader; but the *adelaarsjong* [[T. Radja Sabi]] still lived, and was carried on through the forests by a succession of self-sacrificing protectors until [[1919]].
