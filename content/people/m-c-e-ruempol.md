**M. C. E. Ruempol** was a Dutch *Luitenant-Kolonel* of the infantry who, on [[13 November]] [[13 November]] [[1874]], commanded the column dispatched from [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] by the civil and military governor [[J. L. J. H. Pel|Kolonel Pel]] to construct the [[Kuta Alam|Benting Kota Alam]] fortification on the [[Acheh River|Atjeh River]] east of [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]].

## The Kota Alam Operation, 13 November 1874

Ruempol's column — the 9th Battalion L.H., a mountain artillery section, a section of [[Bataillon Mineurs en Sappeurs|Mineurs en Sappers]] (commanded in the vanguard by [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede Luitenant C. J. Snijders]]), and an ambulance detachment — departed [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] in the pre-dawn darkness of [[13 November]] [[13 November]] [[1874]] and followed the [[Pedir]] dike to the [[Acheh River|Atjeh River]] near the Rivierbenting, then proceeded upstream to the pre-selected site near a river bend where [[W. J. C. Leers|Majoor Leers]] (engineers) and [[T. J. A. van Zijll de Jong|Majoor van Zijll de Jong]] (artillery) had sited the fortification. The full operation is described on the [[Benting Kota Alam operation (13 November 1874)|dedicated event page]].
