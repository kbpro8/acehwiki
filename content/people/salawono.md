**Salawono** was an Ambonese sergeant of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Royaal Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger's Marechaussee]] — a *Ridder derde klas* of the [[Militaire Willemsorde (M.W.O.)]] — who earned the *Eervolle Vermelding* and the *[[Medaille voor Moed en Trouw]]* at the Marechaussee korps, then the M.W.O. 4e klas at the [[Storming of Limboer (22 June 1903)|storming of Limboer]] in [[Djambi]] on [[22 June]] [[1903]] (where he was the first over the wall, alone, and held it with rifle fire until the wounded luitenant [[Van Hoogenhuyzen]] could rejoin), and the M.W.O. 3e klas on [[Celebes]] for the *Verovering van de Alla-stellingen (Celebes, 1905-1908)|verovering van de berucht Alla-stellingen]] and the overval on *[[Andi Noni]]*'s band. He is one of the four retired M.W.O.-ridders who acted as *slippendragers* at the [[Funeral of Dominee Thenu (11 May 1937)|funeral of Dominee Thenu]] in [[Koeta-Radja]].

## The Marechaussee korps and the early decorations

"Voor het leger kunnen we dit zien aan sergeant Salawono, Ridder 3e klas." At the Marechaussee korps itself Salawono had "een *Eervolle Vermelding* en de *Moed en Trouw* verdiend" — an Honorable Mention and the Medal for Courage and Loyalty. These were the standard decorations of a distinguished Marechaussee career; they would be the prelude to the higher M.W.O. decorations of his later service.

## The storming of Limboer (22 June 1903)

"In Djambi werden op 22 Juni 1903 de versterkingen van [[Limboer]] genomen." At Limboer, "ontstond weifeling in de sectie van luitenant *[[Van Hoogenhuyzen]]*." The luitenant rushed forward with a few men but fell into the gracht of the versterking. "Salawono was de eerste die de wal beklom" — Salawono was the first to climb the wall — alone, and held it with rifle fire until the wounded luitenant, pulled out of the gracht by the Ambonese korporaal *[[Dirks]]*, could rejoin him inside. The next day *[[Dirks]]* was killed. Salawono was awarded the M.W.O. 4e klas.

## The Celebes campaign (1905-1908) and the 3e klas

"Salawono verkreeg de derde klas voor zijn optreden bij de verovering van de berucht *Alla-stellingen*, van een heuvelstelling en bij de overval van de bende van *[[Andi Noni]]* en de daarop volgende achtervolging van dat aanvankelijk ontkomen hoofd." The three actions recognised by the M.W.O. 3e klas were: the taking of the notorious Alla-stellingen (fortified hill positions on Celebes); the taking of a separate heuvelstelling; and the overval on Andi Noni's band followed by the achtervolging of the initially escaped hoofd. The decoration elevated Salawono from Ridder 4e klas to Ridder 3e klas — a rare progression within the order.

## The slippendragers at Thenu's funeral

When [[Dominee Thenu]] died on [[11 May]] [[1937]], the four slippendragers at his funeral were "vier oude, gepensioneerde Marechaussees … alle vier ridders M.W.O.: *Salawono* (3e kl.), *[[Pattiasina]]*, *[[Kazen]]* en *[[Pattipuluhu]]*." Salawono's place as the first-named — and the only one whose klas is specified — marks him as the most senior of the four. The choice of four M.W.O.-ridders to carry the dominee's bier is the book's most resonant image of the moral economy of the colonial army: the men who had been decorated for courage under fire laid their decorations, in effect, at the feet of the man who had blessed them.

## Significance

Salawono's significance in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* is as the most fully-decorated of the book's named Ambonese soldiers, and as the type of the Marechaussee Ridder — the man whose career can be read through his decorations, from the *Eervolle Vermelding* and *Moed en Trouw* of the korps, through the 4e klas at Limboer, to the 3e klas on Celebes. His presence as a slippendrager at Thenu's funeral ties the decorated courage of the old Marechaussee to the moral gezag of the Soldaten-Dominee.
