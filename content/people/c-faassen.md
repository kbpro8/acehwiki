**C. Faassen** was a **Marine officer** who commanded the **[[Koning der Nederlanden (ship)]]** during a voyage with **[[Koerier (ship)]]** on the **Tamiang** river in **[[December]] [[1893]]**. He received a commendation from the commander of the Dutch navy in the Dutch East Indies for his performance.

## Action

Faassen’s leadership during the riverine operation earned him a formal thank‑you note in **[[December 1893]]**.
