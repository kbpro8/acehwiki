**Teuku Nja** was the chieftain (*Uleebalang*) of Marassa who adopted a neutral and cooperative stance toward Dutch forces during the [[First Aceh Expedition]] and [[Second Aceh Expedition]].

## Role During the Conflict

In [[March]] [[1873]], Teuku Nja offered his mediation services to Dutch Commissioner [[Frederik Nieuwenhuijzen]] through his father-in-law, the ruler of Trumon. During the [[Second Aceh Expedition]], he provided critical intelligence to General [[Jan van Swieten]] regarding Acehnese troop movements, the location of fortifications, and the death of [[Sultan Alauddin Mahmud Syah IX]].

Due to his neutral position, Marassa villages were subjected to retaliatory attacks by Acehnese patriotic forces under [[Teuku Nanta]] in [[February]] [[1874]], forcing Dutch troops under Lieutenant-Colonel [[Karel van der Heijden]] to execute the [[Battle of Bital]] to protect his territory.
