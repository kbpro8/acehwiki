**Jules Bosch** was the deviser of a new rubber-tapping method (*rubber-tapmethode*) recently invented at the time of the [[1930]] Maréchaussée jubilee, in whose exploitation the former Maréchaussée officer [[W. V. Rhemrev]] took part.

## The tapping method

The [[1930]] jubilee number of *[[Het Nieuws van den Dag voor Nederlandsch-Indië]]*, in its portrait notice of W. V. Rhemrev, records that besides being director of the building company *[[De Rijzende Zon]]*, Rhemrev "neemt tevens deel aan de exploitatie van de nieuwe rubber-tapmethode, onlangs gedaan door den heer Jules Bosch" — also took part in the exploitation of the new rubber-tapping method recently devised by Mr. Jules Bosch.

The notice gives no further particulars of the method or of Bosch himself.
