**Van Eck** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[H. N. A. Swart]]

Three of his subordinates he placed above the rest: [[G. K. Dijkstra|Dijkstra]], [[W. B. J. A. Scheepens|Scheepens]] and [[H. J. Schmidt|Schmidt]]. He placed Dijkstra and later Scheepens at [[Sigli]], the most important troop command on the north coast; the principal bivouac in the plain of Pidië, [[Lam Meulò]], he put under captain Jhr. [[J. J. Boreel|Boreel]]; when resistance revived in the [[Gajò Loeös]] after some painful setbacks of the infantry garrison he sent the 1st Division there under the General Staff captain [[W. Muurling|Muurling]], with his two Atjeh lieutenants [[C. A. Snell|Snell]] and [[Van Eck]].

### From [[W. Muurling]]

**W. Muurling** was the captain of the General Staff whom [[H. N. A. Swart]] sent to the [[Gajò Loeös]] with the 1st Division of the *[[Corps of Military Constabulary of Atjeh and Dependencies|Korps Marechaussee]]* when resistance revived there after painful setbacks of the infantry garrison, and who as Civil Authority at [[Blang Kedjeren]] restored order with his two well-known Atjeh lieutenants [[C. A. Snell|Snell]] and [[Van Eck]].

### From [[C. A. Snell]]

Snell served with [[Van Eck]] under captain Muurling when the 1st Division of *[[Corps of Military Constabulary of Atjeh and Dependencies|marechaussée]]* was sent to the Gajò Loeös after setbacks of the infantry garrison, and "soon restored order." In May [[1911]], while Muurling accompanied the [[Kedjoerön Petiambang]] and fourteen chiefs to the fair exhibition at [[Koeta Radja]], Snell temporarily conducted the administration — "and he, while acting, brought in eight more men from the resistance and four muzzle-loaders."
