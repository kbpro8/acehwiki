The Acehnese form of Jamlichus, one of the Seven Sleepers of Quran chapter 18. The seven saints' names, and that of their dog, are regarded as ajeumats (charms) that avert evil; the hikayat also includes the tale of three devout men in a cave, narrated by Ali.
