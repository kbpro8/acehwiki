**Vallon** was one of the two French travellers murdered in the [[Tenom|Tenom river]] on [[9 March]] [[1880]] by the people of the Tenom pirate prince, together with his companion [[Guillaume (Tenom, 1880)|Guillaume]]. [[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] recalled the fact in 1886 against the Dutch "Atjeh-friends" who went on claiming that all the Dutch misfortunes in the Archipelago were merely the consequence of an aggressive and cruel conduct of war: before 1884, he pointed out, the Dutch had not fought against the Tenomsche robber prince (see [[Murder of Vallon and Guillaume (9 March 1880)]]), and the murder of the two travellers had preceded by four years the [[Nisero affair (1883–1885)|Nisero affair]] in which the Tenomsche China's coast was once more the centre.
