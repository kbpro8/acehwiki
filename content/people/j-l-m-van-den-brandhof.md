**J. L. M. van den Brandhof** was the author of the [[1939]] article *[[Kleiland-aanwinning op Atjeh's Westkust in 1902 (Brandhof, 1939)|Kleiland-aanwinning op Atjeh's Westkust in 1902]]* ("Land reclamation on Atjeh's West Coast in 1902"), published in *De Indische Gids*, No. 61, p. 866 *e.v.*, *etc.*, recording an episode of land reclamation on [[Atjeh's Westkust|Atjeh's Westkust]] in the year [[1902]]. The article is recorded as No. 557 in [[H. van Meurs|Van Meurs]]'s *[[Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939 (Van Meurs, 1940)|Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939]]* (The Hague, [[1940]]), in the Sumatra chapter.

## The 1939 article

Van den Brandhof's article, published in *De Indische Gids* (The Indies Guide) — one of the principal monthly review journals of the Netherlands Indies — recorded an episode of *kleiland-aanwinning* (the gaining of new cultivable clay-soil land) on [[Atjeh's Westkust|Atjeh's Westkust]] in [[1902]], the middle phase of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. The choice of venue — *De Indische Gids*, read by colonial officials, planters, merchants, and the interested Dutch public — signals that the article was addressed to the practical colonial readership interested in the land-management and geographical aspects of the colonial engagement with the Atjeh west coast. The pagination "p. 866 e.v., etc." indicates that the article ran from p. 866 onward and was continued in subsequent issues.

## Significance

For AcehWiki, J. L. M. van den Brandhof is significant as the author of the [[1939]] retrospective on the [[1902]] land reclamation on [[Atjeh's Westkust|Atjeh's Westkust]] — a reminder that the colonial engagement with Atjeh was not solely military but also involved the practical work of land-management and the extension of cultivable area. The article furnishes an independent source for the geographical and administrative history of the Atjeh west coast in the middle phase of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]].
