**Behrens (Luitenant Behrens)** was a Lieutenant, patrouille commander mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Patrol commander pursuing Teungkoe di Mata Ië; in March 1913 wounded him in the leg (but Teungkoe escaped, leaving his turban and rosary).

## Role

Lieutenant, [[Patrouille (Maréchaussée)|patrouille]] commander

## Account from Jongejans

Patrol commander pursuing [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] di [[Mata Ië]]; in [[March]] [[1913]] wounded him in the leg (but [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] escaped, leaving his turban and rosary). In [[January]] [[1912]] received reports of [[Tgk. di Barat|Teungkoe di Barat]]'s fourageeren; February [[1912]] located and surrounded the schuilplaats, killing [[Tgk. di Barat|Teungkoe di Barat]] and his wife.

## Significance

Behrens (Luitenant Behrens) appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
