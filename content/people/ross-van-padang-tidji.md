**Ross van Padang Tidji** was a Well-known Acehnese woman, photographed in old-time festive dress mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. A formerly very well-known Acehnese woman photographed by Hageman with a man in old-time festive dress.

## Role

Well-known Acehnese woman, photographed in old-time festive dress

## Account from Jongejans

A formerly very well-known Acehnese woman photographed by [[Hageman]] with a man in old-time festive dress.

## Significance

Ross van Padang Tidji appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
