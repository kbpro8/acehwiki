**Keutjhi' Bén Bangkèh** is a subject named in *[[Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Keudjroeën Leupoe]]

[[H. N. A. Swart]]'s letter to [[H. J. Schmidt]] of [[26 December]] [[1909]] had already named him as the principal quarry there: "When you have fulfilled your commission in the [[Tangsé]] country, you may go to Geumpang. Here the big game consists, of course, of T. Keudjroeën Leupoe and his sons [[T. Adé]] and [[T. Ma'E]], and after them [[Keutjhi' Bén Bangkèh]] and [[Keutjhi' Linto]]."

### From [[Geumpang]]

In August [[1910]] the bivouac commander of Geumpang, *onderluitenant* [[A. H. C. Roelofsen]], "at last laid low the long-sought and notorious resistance leader in that district, [[Keudjroeën Leupoe]]" [[H. N. A. Swart]]'s letter to Schmidt of [[26 December]] [[1909]] named the further quarry there: Keudjroeën Leupoe's sons [[T. Adé]] and [[T. Ma'E]], then [[Keutjhi' Bén Bangkèh]] and [[Keutjhi' Linto]]. The great [[Keutjhi' Bén]], for whom Geumpang had become too hot, withdrew to the [[Boven Moegò]] district and was killed in [[Pameu]] in September [[1911]].
