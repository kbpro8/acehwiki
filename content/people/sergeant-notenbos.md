**Notenbos, Sergeant** was a Dutch marine sergeant who, during the assault on the village of [[Lemboeq]] on [[31 December]] [[1874]], helped defend the forward breastwork together with [[Marine Jansma]] and [[Lieutenant Cadet]]. His actions were recorded in the after‑action report of [[W. Sutherland]].

## Combat role

Sergeant Notenbos was noted for his steadiness under fire while the marines secured the newly captured fortifications at Lemboeq.
