**S. A. Roos** was a **[[lieutenant der mariniers|lieutenant of the marines]]** who served as **adjutant** to **[[W. K. van Gennep]]** during the **[[First Boni Expedition 1859]]**. He later compiled a detailed **[[Report of S. A. Roos|report]]** on the campaign, which serves as a primary source for the operations.

## Contributions

Roos’s report described the landing, the deceptive tactics, and the subsequent engagements at **[[Badjoa]]**, **[[Laoni]]**, and **[[Tjinrana River|Tjinrana‑rivier]]**.
