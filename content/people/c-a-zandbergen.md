**C. A. Zandbergen** was a Dutch plantation official of the Société Financière des Caoutchoucs who, on 22 May [[1950]], was seriously wounded alongside his colleague [[B. de Jong]] by an Atjehse amokmaker (*Atjehse amokmaker*) at [[T. Pura]] on the East Coast of Sumatra.
