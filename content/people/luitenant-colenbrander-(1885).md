**Luitenant Colenbrander** was a Dutch lieutenant of the garrison of the [[Geconcentreerde Stelling|concentrated line]] at [[Acheh|Atjeh]] in [[1885]]. In majoor [[M. T. H. Perelaer|Perelaer]]'s list of incidents inside the line he appears in the night of [[24 March|24]]–[[25 March]] [[1885]], when at the clearing of the ground (*terrein openkappen*) the workers and their cover were fired upon and three men were wounded, among them luitenant Colenbrander (see [[Guerilla raids inside the concentration line (1885)]]).
