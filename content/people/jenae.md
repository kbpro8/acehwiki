**Jenae** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Keutjhi' Saman]]

His death fell in the month in which [[Jenae]] destroyed the band of [[Tgk. Léjman]] and [[H. J. Schmidt]] found [[Keudjroeën Oebit]] in the [[Tangsé]] valley — the turning-point of the campaign in the [[Meergebied]].

### From [[Tgk. Léjman]]

**Tgk. Léjman** was the successor of the well-known [[Tgk. di Tjòt Tjitjém]], whose entire band was destroyed in the [[Meergebied]] in February [[1911]] by [[Jenae]] with a patrol of the 5th Division.

### From [[Tgk. di Tjòt Tjitjém]]

Tgk. Léjman's whole band was destroyed by [[Jenae]] in February [[1911]] with thirty-six killed and fourteen rifles taken.
