---
title: Dari
aliases: [Dahri, the Atheist]
tags: [tokoh, karya-sastra]
---

An impious heretic whose unequalled reasoning silenced Muslim teachers and endangered the creed, until the great teacher Ahmat's child-disciple Imeum Hanapi answered two theological catch-questions so brilliantly that Dari was shamed into permanent retirement.
