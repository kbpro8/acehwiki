**Kroesen** was a [[Netherlands|Dutch]] *contrôleur* serving with the civil administration of the *geconcentreerde linie* in [[Groot-Atjeh]] in [[1896]] who, in [[March]] of that year, received from [[Toekoe Ketjik Oemar]] — an Atjehnese auxiliary of the defecting warlord [[Toekoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan Pahalawan]] — the warning that allowed the Dutch to take *onmiddellijk krachtige maatregelen* (immediate forceful measures) in response to [[Toekoe Oemar's defection (1896)|Djohan's defection]]. He is named in the [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] in the chapter *Een Trouwe Inlander* — *A Faithful Native* — as the Dutch officer to whom the warning was delivered.

## The warning of 1896

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that, in the immediate aftermath of [[Toekoe Oemar|Djohan]]'s defection, almost all of the Atjehnese chiefs on the Dutch side went over to the defector. Against this background, [[Toekoe Ketjik Oemar]] — one of Djohan's *volgelingen* (followers) — secretly warned the *contrôleur* Kroesen that Djohan would not march on [[Lamkrak]] as he had been ordered, but that he instead intended to attack the Dutch troops unexpectedly at the most favourable opportunity. The warning was, in the album's reading, the proximate cause of the Dutch ability to respond effectively to the defection.

## The Dutch response

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that Kroesen's receipt of the warning *deed den toeleg mislukken* (made the design fail) and allowed the *regeering* to take *onmiddellijk krachtige maatregelen* — the [[Relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit (April 1896)|relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit]] and the [[Battle of Tjot Rang (14 April 1896)|battle of Tjot Rang]] of [[April]] [[1896]]. The album's tribute to Kroesen's informant — *En aan Toekoe Ketjik Oemar is het te danken, dat de regeering onmiddellijk krachtige maatregelen kon nemen, en dat het verraad van Djohan nog niet ernstiger gevolgen heeft gehad* — places the *contrôleur* at the centre of the Dutch response to the defection, and it identifies the civil administration of the *geconcentreerde linie* as the channel through which the warning was transmitted.

## Significance

Kroesen's significance in the documentary record of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] is as the Dutch *contrôleur* who received [[Toekoe Ketjik Oemar]]'s warning of [[Toekoe Oemar|Djohan]]'s planned attack, and who transmitted it to the *regeering* in time for the Dutch to take effective measures. His appearance in the [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] is brief, but it identifies the civil administration of the *geconcentreerde linie* as the institutional channel through which Atjehnese intelligence reached the Dutch military command in the spring of [[1896]], and it establishes the role of the *contrôleur* as the principal Dutch intermediary with the *oeloebalang* class.
