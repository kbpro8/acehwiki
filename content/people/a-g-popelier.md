**A. G. Popelier** was a lieutenant-adjudant of the Indies army who distinguished himself in the [[Expedition against Simpang Olim and Tandjong Semantoh (November-December 1876)|expedition against Simpang Olim and Tandjong Semantoh]] of November [[1876]], when, with only six Ambonese soldiers, he entered unobserved the bastion of the hill-benting of [[Toekoe Paja|Toekoe Paja]], regarded as impregnable, and carried it without loss on the Dutch side — a feat of arms that the source work describes as "undeniably brilliant." His exploit opened the way for the submission of the radja of [[Simpang Olim|Simpang Olim]] and was one of the most celebrated single acts of bravery of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]].

## The Storming of Toekoe Paja, 14 November 1876

After the storming of the gedei of [[Tandjong Semantoh|Tandjong Semantoh]] on [[14 November]] [[14 November]] [[1876]], Lieutenant-adjudant Popelier was ordered to approach the hill-benting of [[Toekoe Paja|Toekoe Paja]] with a section. The source work describes his exploit:

> "Climbing the south-eastern slope of the hill, this officer was soon followed only by six Ambonese soldiers, who, without waiting for their comrades who stayed behind on account of the steep slopes and the light brushwood, and without firing a shot, went forward. Two of them, creeping closest along the edge of the steep excavation, came, without being observed by the enemy, before the stone parapet, from which cannon, lillas and rifles were still being fired at the troops fighting at the gedei; almost at the same time Lieutenant Popelier, with the four other Ambonese, equally unobserved, arrived at the foot of the south-eastern bastion adjoining that parapet. In this bastion an enemy was just about to fire a gun loaded with cartonne. Not regarding the small number of his men, but convinced that swift and bold action was the best means of victory, the named officer did not hesitate for a moment to jump inside, so that he came to stand right next to the Atjeh cannonier. This uttered a cry of fright, recoiled in astonishment and threw away his fuse. Meanwhile the six soldiers had also jumped over the parapet, so that no time was left to the enemy to recover from his surprise at their unexpected appearance. Three armed with lance and klewang, probably the leaders, came out of the house within the fortification, and made an attempt to gather the enemies still bewildered behind the parapet and to press on the seven; but the rapid fire at once opened drove all soon to flight along a footpath in a north-western direction, and the impregnable benting of Toekoe Paja was thus, without loss on our side, in our hands. Undeniably a brilliant feat of arms!"

## Significance

A fortified position, strong by its location, well-armed and defended by 80 Atjehers, was conquered by one officer and six men. The exploit was the most celebrated single act of bravery of the [[Expedition against Simpang Olim and Tandjong Semantoh (November-December 1876)|expedition against Simpang Olim and Tandjong Semantoh]], and made possible the submission of the radja of Simpang Olim and the appointment of a new radja on [[5 February]] [[5 February]] [[1877]].
