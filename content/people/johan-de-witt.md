**Johan de Witt** was the Grand Pensionary of the **[[United Provinces (Dutch Republic)]]** who, in **[[1668]]**, helped draft the **[[Triple Alliance (1668)]]** between the Republic, **[[England|England]]**, and **[[Sweden]]**. His diplomatic work aimed to curb the expansionist ambitions of **[[Louis XIV]]** and to protect Dutch commercial interests in the **[[Indian Ocean]]**, including the **[[Sultanate of Aceh]]**.

## Diplomatic Activity
De Witt negotiated the secret **[[Treaty of Dover|Treaty of Dover (1670)]]** indirectly by attempting to balance English and French pressures. His policies influenced the Republic’s ability to maintain a naval presence off Aceh’s coast during the volatile **[[Rampjaar|Disaster Year]]** [[1672]].
