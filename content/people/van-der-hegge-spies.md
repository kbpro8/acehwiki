**Van der Hegge Spies** was a **[[lieutenant of the sea|luitenant ter zee]]** (2nd class) who participated in the **[[Edi River expedition (1890)]]**. During the June assault, his right‑hand marine platoon engaged an Acehnese **[[klewang]]**‑armed defender; despite being struck by a **[[klewang]]** blow to the head, he continued fighting and was among the first to breach the enemy fortification.  

## Notable Incident  

- **[[June]] [[1890]]**: Received a non‑lethal **klewang** wound while leading his men, then resumed combat and helped secure the position.
