**Keudjroeën Oebit** was den onvindbaren Keudjroeën Oebit of [[Tangsé]], whom [[H. J. Schmidt]] undertook to take within three months and found in February [[1909]].

In June [[1908]] Schmidt argued against his transfer from Tangsé to the pacified [[Padang Tidji]] on the ground that he was just then on the trail of Keudjroeën Oebit — "and if you transfer me now, you may still have trouble from that band-leader for years to come." [[H. N. A. Swart]] seized the *[[Register van Bendehoofden]]*, found the name and asked "how long do you need to get that man into your hands?" — "Drie maanden, Gouverneur." — "Agreed, then you may stay for the present where you are." Schmidt found him in the Tangsé valley in February [[1909]] and struck him from the register: "the first piece of work which this long-serving collaborator of Swart's, his "first tracker," delivered to the new Governor."
