**Teukoe Agam** was the Acehnese formerly head of [[H. J. Schmidt]]'s intelligence service at [[Djeuram]], whom Schmidt brought from [[Meulaboh]] to conduct the investigation into the [[Resistance in Daja and Lhong (1914)|resistance in Daja and Lhong]] in [[1914]].

Maar die kapitein had van Meulaboh meegebracht den hem dóór en dóór bekenden Teukoe Agam … En met Teukoe Agam begon hij op eigen wijze het onderzoek. The investigation, using knowledge of language, customs and family relations and skilful, confidence-inspiring dealings with the population, uncovered the motive of [[Nja' Asém]] and the intrigue of [[Teukoe Mamat]].
