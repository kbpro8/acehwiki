**W. F. Braun** was a major of the infantry of the [[Koninklijk Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Netherlands Indies Army]] who, in the *Indisch Militair Tijdschrift* No. 11 of [[1885]], published his thoughts on the origin of [[Beri-beri|beri-beri]] and the means which in his view might be tried against that much-feared disease. [[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] borrowed from the article the striking army figures for that year: at the beginning of [[1885]] the Indies army had a surplus of about a thousand native soldiers over establishment, but already in November [[1885]] that surplus had changed into a deficit of twelve hundred natives, an important loss created chiefly by the beri-beri (see [[Koninklijk Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger]]).
