**Major van Zijll de Jong** was a Dutch *majoor* of the [[Indische Leger|Indische leger]] and *commandant der artillerie* under [[Generaal Pel|Pel]] in Atjeh, whom [[G. F. W. Borel|Borel]] calls "the hero of the day" of [[31 December]] [[1874]] for proposing and executing the counter-attack with some sixty infantry and a four-gun battery that retook the lost *benteng* at the [[Longbatta-Mesigit]] and so saved the *colonne-van der Meer* from certain destruction in the [[Battle of Longbatta (31 December 1874)|battle of Longbatta]]. He is also the officer who had repeatedly proposed the bombardment of kampongs that [[Jan van Swieten|van Swieten]] had always refused.

## The Proposal to Bombard Kampongs

Van Zijll de Jong is introduced in Borel's narrative as the *commandant der artillerie* who, during van Swieten's command, had "herhaaldelijk voorgesteld" the bombardment of the kampongs around [[Kota Radja]] from which the Dutch were fired on. Van Swieten had always refused. Kolonel Pel "dacht er evenwel geheel anders over." As early as [[28 April]] [[1874]] — two days after van Swieten's [[Departure of Jan van Swieten from Atjeh (April 1874)|departure]] — Pel ordered the *vesting-artillerie* to open fire from the [[Voorlaadkanonnen in Atjeh|12 c.M. voorlaadkanonnen]] on all kampongs within range, daily, at varying hours, to damage the enemy and prevent "samenscholen van groote massa's volk." Half of the 12 c.M. *puntgranaten* failed to explode, and the bombardment led directly to Pel's request to return the [[Achterlaadkanonnen van 12 c.M.|achterlaadgeschut]] from Java — a vindication of van Zijll de Jong's long-standing advice.

## The Counter-Attack at Longbatta

On the afternoon of [[31 December]] [[1874]] the situation was desperate. [[Majoor van der Meer|Van der Meer]]'s column had abandoned the captured Mesigit *benteng* without a garrison, marched over the open *sawah*, and was being cut to pieces by an enemy who had streamed back into the abandoned work. Pel's staff tried in vain to recall van der Meer by signals. At this critical moment van Zijll de Jong, at the staff of the supreme commander, made the bold proposal to recapture the lost *benteng* with the only available force: some sixty infantry and a four-gun battery. With so much at stake Pel did not hesitate. By a well-planned and well-executed attack van Zijll de Jong and his handful retook the *benteng* and so covered the retreat of the *colonne-van der Meer*, saving it from certain destruction. Borel calls him "the hero of the day."

## Borel's Judgement

Van Zijll de Jong is, in Borel's narrative, the model of the Indische artillery officer — tactically astute, technically proficient, and personally fearless. His counter-attack at Longbatta turned a rout into a costly retreat; his long-standing advice on the bombardment of kampongs, refused by van Swieten and adopted by Pel, became the foundation of the active defence that allowed the *bezettingskorps* to hold [[Kota Radja]] through the worst months of [[1874]]. The contrast with van Swieten's *afwachtende houding* — and with van der Meer's loss of cohesion on the *sawah* — could not be sharper.
