**W. H. Loef** was a sergeant and *brigade-commandant* of the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]*, knight of the *[[Militaire Willemsorde|Militaire Willems-Orde]]* 3rd class, whose *eereteekens* record service in [[Jambi|Djambi]], Central [[Sumatra]] and [[Atjeh]] across four campaign periods, and who at the time of the [[1930]] jubilee was concierge of the Christian Lyceum at [[Bandung|Bandoeng]].

## Service record

Loef was born on [[16 February]] [[1881]]. He departed for the Indies as a soldier on [[12 May]] [[1899]], and was promoted sergeant on [[8 September]] [[1903]]. He left the army with a pension on [[6 November]] [[1916]].

As sergeant and *brigade-commandant* of Maréchaussée he received on [[23 February]] [[1917]] the knight's cross of the Militaire Willems-Orde 3rd class.

## Campaign decorations

Loef was further decorated with the *eereteeken voor belangrijke krijgsbedrijven* for: [[Jambi|Djambi]] [[1901]]–[[1904]]; Central Sumatra [[1903]]–[[1907]]; Atjeh [[1906]]–[[1910]]; and Atjeh [[1911]]–[[1914]].

## Later life

At the time of the [[1930]] jubilee number he was concierge at the *Christelijk Lyceum* at [[Bandung|Bandoeng]].
