**Teungkoe Tapa'** was een geestdrijver uit het [[Gajòland]] who threw the [[Oostkust van Atjeh|east coast]] into commotion in [[1898]] and was dealt with by [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|van Heutsz]] in a few days.

Before turning on [[Tangsé]], van Heutsz "they first settled an account with Teungkoe Tapa' … which was accomplished, to the Teungkoe's detriment, within a few days. And so conclusively that this religious leader could reach the safer [[Westkust van Atjeh|West Coast]] only with great difficulty and with a mere handful of followers."
