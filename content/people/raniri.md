A religious pandit of Arab-Gujarati origin who played an important role in Acehnese religious and literary life, arriving in Acheh in November [[1637]] and resolving the disputed mystical questions of the day by opposing Shamsuddin of Sumatra's pantheism. At the command of Sultan Iskandar Thani, he wrote the Sirat al-mustaqim ("The Straight Path") in March [[1638]], as well as the Bad' khalq as-samawat wal-arwah.
