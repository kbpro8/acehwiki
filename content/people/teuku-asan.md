---
title: Teuku Asan
aliases: [son of Teuku Paya]
tags: [tokoh, pejuang]
---

A young guerrilla leader who, with his father's blessing, gathered panglimas and fixed his headquarters near his birthplace of Lam Bada. He executed the traitor kupala Punteuet as a lesson and died at Ulee Lheue under circumstances Achehnese regarded as martyrdom, although the fatal shot was actually fired by soldiers occupying the mosque there.
