**Tengku M. Daudsjah** was an Indonesian administrator who, during the transition from the [[Negara Sumatera Timur]] (N.S.T.) to the unitary Republic of Indonesia, served as the acting resident (*dd. res.*) of [[Atjeh|Atjeh]]. He was one of three members of the advisory college that assisted the acting governor (*dd. gouverneur*) [[Sarimin Reksodihardjo]] in the provisional government of North Sumatra.

## Position in the transitional government

When the N.S.T. was dissolved on 15 August [[1950]] and its territory incorporated into the autonomous province of North Sumatra — comprising the pre-war residencies of [[Atjeh]], [[Tapanuli]], and East Sumatra (*Oost-Sumatra*) — the existing administrative apparatus continued to function normally. The acting governor [[Sarimin Reksodihardjo]] was assisted by a college of three:

1. **Tengku M. Daudsjah** — acting resident of [[Atjeh]] (until the establishment of the Republican emergency government at the beginning of the second police action, *bij de aanvang der tweede politionele actie*);
2. **Binanga Siregar** — deputy resident of [[Tapanuli]];
3. **[[Radja Kaliamsjah Sinaga]]** — former deputy head of state (*Wakil Wali Negara*) of the N.S.T.
