---
title: Chut Nya' Patimah
tags: [person, ruler]
---

A woman who served as uleebalang (territorial chief) of Laboh Haji (Labuan Haji) for a number of years, mentioned in a sadati kisah-dialogue: "The uleebalang of that place is a woman; she keeps us all in her protection."

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
