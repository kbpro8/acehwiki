---
title: Si Meuseukin
aliases: [Si Gasin Meuseukin, Pa' Pande]
tags: [person, literary-work, folklore]
---

The Acehnese trickster-fool, related to the Sundanese Si Kabayan, the Javanese Jaka Bodo, and the German Eulenspiegel. The hero of several habas, he variously plays a diviner, a hapless bridegroom, and a trickster who eventually becomes a monarch.
