**De Rochemont** was a Dutch military officer, commentator, and contemporary analyst of colonial warfare in the Netherlands East Indies during the 1870s.

## Views on the Aceh Campaigns

In *Atjeh gedurende de tweede expeditie en later* ([[1876]]), [[De Rochemont]] is cited in debates surrounding fortifications and siege tactics during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]]. His commentaries contributed to the professional assessment of KNIL engineering and artillery methods against Acehnese bentengs ([[Coastal Fortifications in Aceh]]).
