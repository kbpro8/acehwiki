**J. Copius Peereboom** was a **Lieutenant der mariniers** who led a detachment of thirty marines during the **Tamiang** expedition of **[[February]] [[1893]]**. His force embarked on three steam‑powered boats and fought alongside the infantry column. Peereboom succumbed to exhaustion and sunstroke on **[[5 May]] [[1893]]** at **[[Seroeway]]**.

## Action

Peereboom’s marines provided covering fire on the right flank of the infantry and helped capture a series of enemy fortifications. His death was noted in the Dutch naval yearbook of **[[1892]]‑[[1893]]**.

## Significance

His sacrifice exemplifies the harsh environmental conditions faced by Dutch marines in the tropical interior of **[[Aceh]]**.
