**Scheurleer** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Pang Bintang]]

Swart had earlier, in his letter to Schmidt of [[26 September]] [[1911]], instructed [[Scheurleer]] to gather information about Pang Bintang, attaching him for form's sake to the Provincial Bureau at [[Koeta Radja]] so that he could go wherever he himself thought necessary.

### From [[A. Geertsema Beckeringh]]

Improvement on the west coast came only "when at last in 1909 the civil authority, the well-known constabulary captain Geertsema Beckeringh, could dispose of sufficient means of power": a mobile *marechaussée* column under captain [[F. Darlang|Darlang]] temporarily occupied first the mouth of the [[Wòjla]] and later [[Boebon]]; [[H. J. Schmidt|Schmidt]] cleared up in [[Teunòm]]; and the pacification of the difficult [[Boven Wòjla]] under [[Scheurleer]] and from the bivouac [[Tanòh Mirah]] produced the submission of two hundred resistance men, who were helped with rice and seed-*padi*. Thereafter only [[Beneden Wòjla]], Boebon and [[Seunagan]] remained unquiet in the Meulaboh country.

### From [[Koeta Radja]]

At Koeta Radja stood the *[[Gewestelijk Bureau]]*, to which officers could be attached for form's sake while operating freely elsewhere, as [[Scheurleer]] was in [[1911]] while collecting information about [[Pang Bintang]]. It was to Koeta Radja that [[H. J. Schmidt]] came in June [[1908]] to plead against his posting to [[Padang Tidji]], and there that Swart consulted the *[[Register van Bendehoofden]]*.

## Entry in the Maréchaussée register of honours ([[1930]])

The *[[Overzicht van Onderscheidingen toegekend aan het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden (1890-1930)|Overzicht van Onderscheidingen]]* printed in the [[1930]] jubilee number of *[[Het Nieuws van den Dag voor Nederlandsch-Indië]]* records under [[1915]] the award to *1ste Luit. Mar. H. Scheurleer* of the knight's cross of the *[[Militaire Willemsorde|Militaire Willems-Orde]]* 4th class — the only entry for that year.
