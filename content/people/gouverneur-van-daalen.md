**Gouverneur Van Daalen** (1905-1908) was a Governor of Aceh mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Governor 1905-1908 who initiated the pacification work (pacificatie-werk) that continued under his successor Swart.

## Role

Governor of [[Aceh]]

## Dates

[[1905]]-[[1908]]

## Account from Jongejans

Governor [[1905]]-[[1908]] who initiated the pacification work ([[Pacificatie|pacificatie]]-werk) that continued under his successor [[Gouverneur Swart|Swart]].

## Significance

Gouverneur Van Daalen appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
