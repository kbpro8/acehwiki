**Toekoe Imam** was one of the moekim heads of [[Gigiëng]] on the north coast of [[Atjeh]], who lived on a good footing with the Dutch officials — and, the Dutch suspected, at odds with his own prince. His report that Prince [[Toengkoe Bintara Kamangan]] had invited the heads of the realm to collect money for the *[[Prang sabi|prang sabil]]* against the Netherlands, and that he had replaced the market master [[Toekoe Moeda Rajat]] by the hostile [[Toekoe Hasjim]], was one of the three independent testimonies on which the Dutch acted in the [[Cancellation of friendship with Gigiëng (19 August 1876)|cancellation of friendship of 19 August 1876]].
