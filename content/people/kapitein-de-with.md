**Kapitein De With** was a Dutch naval captain whose squadron was stationed at the northern mouth of the [[Rivier van Riouw]] near the island of [[Mars]] during the 1784 Dutch expedition. His vessels, a frigate and several Company ships, were attacked by indigenous boats, prompting urgent assistance from [[Jacob Pieter van Braam|Van Braam (commander)]].

## Engagement at Mars
When the enemy launched a surprise attack on De With’s squadron, Van Braam dispatched landing craft to support him. The encounter resulted in a brief but intense skirmish, after which De With’s forces withdrew to the safety of their ships.

## Significance
De With’s involvement illustrates the coordination between naval and land forces in Dutch operations against local resistance in the Riau‑Sulu region.
