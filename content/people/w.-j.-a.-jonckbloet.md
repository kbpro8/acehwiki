**W. J. A. Jonckbloet** was a Dutch scholar, politician, and member of parliament who authored the influential [[Jonckbloet Note of 10 May 1873]] criticizing the political assumptions of the [[First Aceh Expedition]].

## Critique of the First Expedition

Writing on [[10 May]] [[1873]], immediately after the withdrawal of General [[E. C. van Daalen]]'s forces from [[Kraton of Aceh]], [[W. J. A. Jonckbloet]] published a scathing analysis of the [[Dutch East Indies Government]]. He famously wrote that the government had attempted to conquer the [[Aceh Sultanate]] "with a coup de théâtre," expecting immediate capitulation.

General [[G. G. Booms]] prominently cited [[W. J. A. Jonckbloet]] in his [[1875]] monograph to prove that contemporary parliamentary observers recognized that the primary causes of the expedition's defeat lay in governmental overhaastiness rather than military errors.
