**Lieutenant G. C. C. Simmermacher** commanded a marine company that led a frontal assault during the advance over the **Blang Kala** pass on [[17 January]] [[1876]]. He was killed in action during the attack (see [[page 137]]).

## Action and Death

His company was part of the force that stormed a heavily defended **aarden borstwering** (earth breastwork) on the mountain slope. The loss of Simmermacher contributed to the overall Dutch casualties of two dead and seven wounded on that day.

## Significance

Simmermacher’s death illustrates the high cost of mountain assaults and the vulnerability of Dutch officers in close combat against entrenched Acehnese forces.
