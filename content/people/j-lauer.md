**J. Lauer** was a Captain commanding the 3rd company of the [[3e Bataljon Infanterie]].

## Service
He led his forces on the right wing during the [[Second Conquest of the Great Missigit]] on [[14 April]] [[1873]], advancing on the southern walls of [[Atjeh's Groote Missigit]].
