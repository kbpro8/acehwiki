**Lieutenant P. Colombon** was a Dutch marine officer who distinguished himself during the Dutch expedition against the rulers of [[Tanette]] and [[Soepa]] in [[June]] [[1824]]. For his conduct he was later appointed a knight of the [[Militaire Willemsorde|Military William Order]] 4th class.

## Expedition of June 1824

Colombon participated in the landing on the main town of Tanette on [[19 June]] [[1824]] and the subsequent assault on Soepa on [[14 August]] [[1824]] with a force of roughly one hundred and fifty men.
