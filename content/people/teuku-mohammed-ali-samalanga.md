**Teuku Mohammed Ali Samalanga** was an Atjèhnese *[[Uleebalang|oelèëbalang]]* who, in the early 1950s, led the [[Badan Keinsjafan Rakjat|Badan Keinsjafan Rakjat]] (B.K.R.), the association formed by the surviving *oelèëbalang* class of Atjeh to press for the reversal of the political gains made by the [[Poesa|Poesa]] during the [[Ulama–Uleëbalang social revolution in Atjeh (1945–1946)|1945–1946 social revolution]].

## Leadership of the Badan Keinsjafan Rakjat

Henri J. H. Alers's *Om een rode of groene Merdeka* records that, once the new resident-coordinator [[Danubroto|Danubroto]] began attempting to restore *oelèëbalang*-class and non-Atjèhnese officials to posts held by the Poesa following Atjeh's incorporation into the province of [[North Sumatra]] in [[1951]], the *oelèëbalang* class took fresh heart and organised itself under Teuku Mohammed Ali Samalanga's leadership as the Badan Keinsjafan Rakjat. Its programme sought the abolition of the [[Madjelis Penimbang|Madjelis Penimbang]], the removal of Poesa members from the civil-service corps, and the holding of general elections — together amounting, in Alers's reading, to an attempt to unwind the entire social revolution of [[1946]].
