---

title: Sayyid Abdallah bin Tahir Ba Alawi
tags: [person, scholar, western-scholar]
---

A Hadramite teacher who died in December [[1855]], author of a collection of treatises criticizing the gap between Islamic law and actual religious practice in Hadramaut, extensively quoted by Hurgronje to show that the tension between adat and hukom is universal across the Mohammedan world, not unique to Indonesia.

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
