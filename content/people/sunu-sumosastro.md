**Sunu Sumosastro** was a senior Indonesian official at the Ministry of Trade and Industry who, in May [[1952]], accompanied the minister [[Sujono Hadimoto]] on an inspection tour of the oil enterprises in [[Atjeh|Atjeh]] on Sumatra.
