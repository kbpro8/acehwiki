**Majoor Van der Maaten** was a Major, captured Panglima Polém's family; patrouille commandant mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. In May 1903 captured Panglima Polém's wife, mother and sister - beginning of the end for Polém.

## Role

Major, captured [[Panglima Polém]]'s family; [[Patrouille (Maréchaussée)|patrouille]] commandant

## Account from Jongejans

In May [[1903]] captured [[Panglima Polém]]'s wife, mother and sister - beginning of the end for Polém. Also led colonne [[Van der Maaten]] at [[Tangsé]] that overran Polém's mother and other family, leading to Polém's surrender. Mentioned in [[1903]] action against [[Teungkoe di Tjot Plieëng]].

## Significance

Majoor Van der Maaten appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
