---

title: "Sane'"
aliases: ["sane Che'bre'"]
tags: [person, legend]
---

Evil spirits of the sea and rivers referenced in the sadati kisah, including a terrible sane' in the Daroy river and the sane Che'bre' of the Raja Umong cultivated lands, whom no human strength can overcome. A dream of being aboard ship signifies conflict with a sane survived unhurt.

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
