**Stubbeman** was one of the two newly appointed civilian officials (alongside **[[Sollicofre]]**) who, together with the remaining member of the **Hof van Politie en Justitie**, were tasked by **[[Governor Van Hoogenheim|Governor Van Hoogenheim]]** to begin processing the captured rebels at **[[Dageraad]]**.

## Administrative Role
Stubbeman’s involvement illustrates the civil‑military collaboration in the colonial justice system during the **[[1760s]]**.
