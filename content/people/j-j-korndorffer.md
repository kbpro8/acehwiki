**J. J. Korndörffer** was a Dutch marine **Sergeant‑Major** who received the [[Ridder Militaire Willemsorde]] 4th class for his actions during the **Krijgverrichtingen in Atjeh (1874–1875)**. The award roll (page 185) lists him among the officers honoured for “krijgverrichtingen in Atjeh in het bijzonder gedurende het tijdvak medio Aug. 1874 tot Feb. 1875,” and again for the year [[1875]].

## Service Record

Korndörffer served in the [[Korps Mariniers (Netherlands)]]. His citation notes that he displayed “uitstekende daden van moed, beleid en trouw” (outstanding deeds of courage, policy and loyalty) during the campaigns.

## Significance

His decoration illustrates the Dutch emphasis on rewarding senior non‑commissioned officers who played key roles in the suppression of Acehnese resistance.
