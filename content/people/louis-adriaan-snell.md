**Louis Adriaan Snell** was a distinguished Dutch military officer who served 16 years as an officer in the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]], popularly known in military circles as 'Old Hand'.

## Military Career and Service in Aceh

Snell served through all officer ranks in the Marechaussee. In [[1926]], he was dispatched from Java to command the [[West Coast of Aceh Action 1925–1927]], successfully restoring colonial order near [[Meulaboh]] and [[Bakongan]]. He was decorated with the Knight 4th Class Military William Order, two Honourable Mentions, and expedition crosses for Aceh ([[1906]]–[[1910]], [[1911]]–[[1914]], [[1925]]–[[1927]]) and New Guinea ([[1907]]–[[1915]]). In [[1930]], as Colonel commanding the 2nd Infantry Regiment in Bandung, he represented Marechaussee veterans at the 40th anniversary banner ceremony in [[Kuta Raja]].
