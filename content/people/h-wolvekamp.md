**H. Wolvekamp** was a Dutch mining engineer who, with [[P. J. J. Jansen|Jansen]] and [[B. B. Lindberg|Lindberg]], conducted the [[1922]] mineralogical survey of [[Acheh|Atjeh]] whose joint report is the principal source for the colonial-era mineral geography of the residency. The three engineers are uniformly cited together in the standard form "JANSEN, LINDBERG and WOLVEKAMP, 1922", which suggests a single field-party under the authority of the [[Netherlands Indies Geological Survey]] or the [[Bureau of Mines in Indonesia]].

## The 1922 Atjeh survey

The contributions of the Jansen–Lindberg–Wolvekamp party, as recoverable from the citations in [[Van Bemmelen's The Geology of Indonesia (1949)|Van Bemmelen's *Geology of Indonesia*]] ([[1949]]), cover the full range of Atjeh mineral occurrences: the primary gold of the [[S. Assai]] and [[S. Meranging]] quartz-veins; the iron-ore localities of [[Kr. Geuenteuet]], [[Gle Mon Ampeuet]], [[Kr. Ligan (Babah Loh)|Kr. Ligan (Babah Loh)]], [[Kr. Rigaih]], [[Alu Talu]], [[Kwala Boe Tjot Plaj|Kwala Boe and Tjot Plaj]], [[A. Pentuwa Gade]] and [[Kuto Buloh]]; the copper occurrences of Pulu Bras, Gle Broë, Aer Talu, Beutong, Aloe Baru, Aer Si Hajo, Muara Sipongi; and the lead-zinc assays at [[Krueng Heureueng]] and [[Krueng Isep]]. For a fuller account see the entry on [[P. J. J. Jansen]].

## Significance

Wolvekamp's significance for the encyclopedia of [[Acheh]] history is co-extensive with that of the Jansen–Lindberg–Wolvekamp report as a whole. He is one of the three Dutch engineers whose [[1922]] field-work supplied the baseline mineralogical description of Atjeh on which all later colonial and post-colonial work has depended.
