**Generaal H. N. A. Swart** was a Governor of Aceh 1908-1918 (Colonel then Lieutenant-General); photo caption page 397 mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Photo caption: 'Generaal H.

## Role

Governor of Aceh [[1908]]-[[1918]] (Colonel then Lieutenant-General); photo caption page 397

## Account from Jongejans

Photo caption: 'Generaal H. N. A. Swart, Gouverneur van Atjeh van [[1908]]-[[1918]].' Succeeded [[Gouverneur Van Daalen|Van Daalen]]. Stayed 10 years as Governor of this [[gewest]]. Began by placing the well-known Captain [[Christoffel]] as [[Marechaussée|marechaussée]]-commandant at Pantonlaboe. Pursued oelamas and band leaders with force, but also tried to meet population needs - reconciled feuding [[oelèëbalang]] families of [[Keureutòë]], paid out wrongfully retained traktementen and deposited waarborgsommen, supplied padi to populations suffering from failed harvests. 'Iron hand in velvet gloves.' Won population's confidence. On [[Westkust]] [[Teukoe Banta]], [[Teukoe Bin Blang Pidië]], and ([[1910]]) [[Radja Batoe van Singkel]] submitted. Helped resisters financially immediately after submission, sometimes in 'bedenkelijke vormen' - many malcontents bought off with large sums, creating '[[Bedelzucht|bedelzucht]]' (mendicancy) still noticeable. From [[1909]]-[[1913]] the [[Colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]] inflicted sensitive losses on [[Oelama|oelama]]-party in [[Pidië]]; sons of the famous [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] [[Tiro]] [[Teungkoe Sjech Saman di Tiri|Tjhèh Saman]] ([[Teungkoe di Boekit]] and [[Teungkoe Tjhi' Maid]]) were eliminated. With the death of the [[Tiro]] Teungkoes the Aceh war could be considered ended ([[1910]]-[[1913]]). [[H.E. Swart|Governor Swart]] governed until his appointment to the [[Raad van Indië]] on [[2 September]] [[1918]]. Listed among [[Marechaussée|marechaussée]] men.

## Significance

Generaal H. N. A. Swart appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
