**Luitenant Hageman** was a Dutch lieutenant of the [[Oost-Indisch Leger|Netherlands East Indies Army]] killed together with [[Luitenant Veerman]] in the assault on the Achehnese *benteng* of [[Kota Toeankoe]] in [[Groot-Atjeh]], an action fought some five years before [[1893]] and recalled by [[G. B. Hooijer]] as the only occasion besides the relief of [[Kroeng Kali]] and the attempt on [[Kaloet]] on which the Dutch had operated in strength outside the *[[Geconcentreerde linie|geconcentreerde linie]]* during the ten-year *concentratie* period.

## Death before Kota Toeankoe

Hooijer recorded that Hageman and [[Luitenant Veerman|Veerman]], with several of their men, fell some hours before the main action in their attempt to seize the *benteng*, which they hoped to find abandoned because an enveloping battalion was to approach it from the far side. Only a small outwork, in which thirty dead Achehnese lay heaped, fell to the Dutch, at a cost of three times as many dead and wounded of their own.
