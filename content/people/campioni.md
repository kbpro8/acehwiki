**Campioni** was a Marechaussee commander of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen]] in [[Atjeh|Aceh]], one of the great patrol commanders of the *prang* (the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]]). In *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* by Merac ([[1947]]), Campioni is the third-named in [[Musket]]'s list of the Marechaussee commanders: "Gilles Graafland, Van Daalen, Campioni, Christoffel, Van der Maaten, Scheepens, Watrin, Webb, Colijn, Geertsema Beckering, Darlang and the sergeants Nussy, Salowono, Pongoh … a colourful row." The names of these famous tough guys and Marechaussee commanders "rang through the Indies like carillon music," and their "stories of brave deeds, adventures, and peculiarities went the rounds, as if they were legends."

## Significance

Campioni is significant in *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* as one of the Marechaussee commanders whose name "rang through the Indies like carillon music." The book's argument — that the names of these fellows of the old time rang through the Indies like carillon music and that their stories went the rounds, as if they were legends — is the book's frame for the colonial memory of the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]]: the Marechaussee commanders whose names were legend. Campioni is therefore one of the book's fellows of the old time: the Marechaussee commander whose name was carillon music.
