**Majoor Gerlach** was an officer and battalion commander of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] who served in the [[Aceh Sultanate]] during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] and subsequent occupation operations.

## Command in Aceh

In *Atjeh gedurende de tweede expeditie en later* ([[1876]]), [[Majoor Gerlach]] is recorded as commanding infantry detachments during defensive and clearing operations around [[Kotta Alam]] and [[Penajoeng]], maintaining supply communications for General [[J. van Swieten]] and General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]].
