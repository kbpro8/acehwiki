**Richard James Wilkinson** ([[1867]]–[[1941]]) was a British colonial administrator, Malay scholar, and Orientalist who served as **Inspector of Schools in the [[Federated Malay States|Federated Malay States]]** at the time when the English translation of *The Achehnese* was prepared for the press. His name appears on the title-page of the work as the compiler of the **Index** that closes the second volume of the 1906 edition, and to whose patient labour of identification every user of the book is heavily indebted.

Wilkinson was a member of the Malayan Civil Service from [[1889]] onwards, and his career as a scholar of the Malay world ran parallel to his official duties. His *An Abridged Malay-English Dictionary (Romanised)* first appeared in [[1901]], and the larger *A Malay-English Dictionary* in [[1903]]–[[1905]]; these were the dictionaries on which the British administration of the [[Straits Settlements|Straits Settlements]] and the [[Federated Malay States|Federated Malay States]] relied for the conduct of native affairs, and they established Wilkinson's reputation as the leading British Malayologist of his generation. He was also the author of the *Papers on Malay Subjects* series (history, Malay literature, life and customs, law, industries) issued by the Government of the F.M.S. in the early years of the twentieth century, and of the still-useful *A History of the Peninsular Malays* ([[1920]]). His later career took him to the Colonial Office in London, where he served as Reader in Malay at the School of Oriental Studies.

In *The Achehnese*, Wilkinson's role was that of indexer. The index he compiled is far more than a bare register of names: it identifies persons, places, institutions and Achehnese terms, gives their Arabic or Malay equivalents where appropriate, and supplies the kind of cross-referencing that makes the two dense volumes navigable as works of reference. The title-page of the 1906 edition records that the work is "Translated by the late A. W. S. O'Sullivan, Assistant Colonial Secretary, Straits Settlements, with an Index by R. J. Wilkinson, Inspector of Schools, Federated Malay States" — a combination of Straits Settlements and F.M.S. Malay-learning that, together with the Dutch author [[Christiaan Snouck Hurgronje|Christiaan Snouck Hurgronje]] and the [[Leyden|Leyden]] publisher [[E. J. Brill|E. J. Brill]], reflects the international character of the scholarship that lay behind the book.

Wilkinson's later career as Governor of Sierra Leone ([[1916]]–[[1921]]) and as a senior official in the Colonial Office took him away from Malay studies, but his earlier writings — the dictionary, the *Papers on Malay Subjects*, and the index to *The Achehnese* — remained standard works of reference for the next half-century. Students of Achehnese and Malay history still rely on his identifications, and his name stands, with those of Snouck Hurgronje and O'Sullivan, on the title-page of one of the great monuments of colonial Orientalism.
