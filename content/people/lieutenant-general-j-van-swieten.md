**Lieutenant-General J. van Swieten** was a Lieutenant-General, Regeringscommissaris, supreme commander of the second Aceh expedition mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Led the second Aceh expedition.

## Role

Lieutenant-General, Regeringscommissaris, supreme commander of the [[Second Aceh Expedition|second Aceh expedition]]

## Account from Jongejans

Led the [[Second Aceh Expedition|second Aceh expedition]]. Military goal: capture the [[Kraton of Aceh|Kraton]]. Political goal (formulated) included concluding an agreement based on the contract of [[21 February]] [[1858]] with [[Siak]] Sri Indrapoera; otherwise deposing [[Sultan of Aceh|the Sultan]] and appointing another or providing for the government of the kingdom. [[24 January]] [[1874]]: [[Kraton of Aceh|Kraton]] conquered. Then began the [[Policy of afwachting (waiting) and toenadering (approach)|policy of afwachting (waiting) and toenadering (approach)]]. Mentioned as one of 11 governors of Aceh in [[1893]].

## Significance

Lieutenant-General J. van Swieten appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
