**Teuku Umar** was a prominent Acehnese military leader, noble chieftain, and national hero who led resistance against Dutch colonial expansion during the Aceh War.

## Military Career and Service in Aceh

Initially allying with Dutch colonial authorities in [[1893]] under the title *Teuku Johan Pahlawan*, Umar commanded a colonial-funded indigenous legion. In [[March]] [[1896]], in the famous [[Treason of Teuku Umar]], he defected back to the Acehnese resistance with 800 modern rifles, ammunition, and f 18,000 in cash. His defection forced the Dutch to abandon the [[Concentratie-Stelsel]] and deploy the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]] in active offensive counter-insurgency warfare.
