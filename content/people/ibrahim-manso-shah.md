Sultan of Acheh, reigned approximately [[1838]]-[[1870]]. At his instance, the religious-political leader Teungku Kuta Karang (Shaikh Abbas) wrote the Taj-ul-mulk, an important compendium of Islamic mystic arts, prognostications, and native medicine.
