**Weers** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Pang Manap]]

After the submissions of [[Lebé Grondong]] and [[Tgk. Moedö Koetö Serei]] in [[1912]], "now only Pang Manap with eight followers remained — "and that could hardly be called anything," as sergeant [[Weers]] would say." He appears in the [[1913]] list of the last unsubmitted resistance leaders. "October brought the end for Pang Manap in the Gajò lands … the greater part of his band, including his brother, reported themselves and surrendered a carbine."

### From [[The resistance in Atjeh 1908–1912]]

So [[1911]] ended, and [[1912]] was to bring the final settlement with the still-wandering small bands of any significance. That happened promptly: both Lebé Grondong and Tgk. Moedö Koetö Serei came in submission, so that only [[Pang Manap]] with eight followers remained — "and that could hardly be called anything," as sergeant [[Weers]] would say.

### From [[1912]]

In the [[Dörötstreek]] the band submitted, so that the condition of the Meergebied and the Dörötstreek could henceforth be judged from favourable to very favourable. In the [[Gajò Loeös]] both [[Lebé Grondong]] and [[Tgk. Moedö Koetö Serei]] came in submission, so that only [[Pang Manap]] with eight followers remained — "and that could hardly be called anything", as sergeant [[Weers]] would say. In [[Pidië]] the garrison could be reduced by two companies.
