**Wilkinson** was a British colonial administrator and scholar of Malay language and literature, known for his Malay dictionary and works on Malay proverbs. He is mentioned in the source as the compiler of a Malay vocabulary in which variant readings of a lexical item are discussed.

## Scholarly Work
The source notes that in Wilkinson’s Malay vocabulary (*Mal. Wibk. van Wilkinson*) both variant readings of a certain term are attested, and his comparison of the term with Sundanese *kumaha* (“how”) is referenced. His lexicographic contributions are frequently cited in studies of classical Malay.

## Connection to Aceh
Although Wilkinson’s primary focus was the Malay Peninsula, his dictionaries and grammatical works have been used by researchers studying Acehnese Malay and the literary traditions of Sumatra, including the [[Hikayat Raja-raja Pasai]] and related texts.
