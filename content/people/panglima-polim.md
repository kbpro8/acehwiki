**Panglima Polim** — properly *Seri Madoe Perkasa Teukoe Panglima Polem* — was the very influential [[panglima]] and [[Uleebalang|oeléëbalang]] of the XXII Moekims, the great federation of the [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] interior. He was a brother-in-law of Toeankoe Radja Keumala, who was likewise of the Sultans-family; the two, with Toeankoe Machmoed (also of the former Sultans-family), were the three most prominent submitted chiefs of Atjeh in the years [[1907]]–[[1910]]. By his side Colijn — then a young lieutenant — appears in the famous photograph of the submission at [[Lho Seumawé]].

## The 1907 letter to the Tiro-oelama's

Before governor [[H.N.A. Swart|Swart]] set the destructive apparatus of lieutenant [[Schmidt]] in motion against the [[Tiro-oelama|Tiro-oelama's]], he wished to make one last attempt to bring the Tiro-tengkoe's to submission. He had the three most prominent chiefs of Atjeh write a letter advising the tengkoe's to give up further resistance. Those chiefs were: Toeankoe Machmoed, member of the former Sultans-family; Panglima Polim, the very influential head of the XXII Moekims; and his brother-in-law Toeankoe Radja Keumala, also of the Sultans-family. The [[Brief van 18 Radjab 1325|brief]], properly sealed, was brought by a prominent Atjeher who also had the confidence of the Tiro-oelama's, to their schuilplaats in the forest. It opens:

> *"Assalmoé 'alaikoem warahmatoellahi Ta'ala wabarakatoeh! With a thousand greetings from us, Toeankoe Machmoed, Toeankoe Radja Keumala and Seri Madoe Perkasa Teukoe Panglima Polem, now residing at Koetaradja, Groot-Atjeh, to our Penghoeloe (Lord) [[Habib Teupin Wan|Habib Abdoerrachman Teupin Wan]], and our Teachers Teungkoe Mahjoeddin and [[Tengkoe di Boekit|Teungkoe di Boekit]], son of Moekarram Maulana Almoedabbar Almalik Teungkoe di Tiro, Teungkoe Hasjim, Teungkoe Oeleë Toetoeë, Tengkoe Ibrahim, as well as to their followers who still live as survivors of those who took part in the holy war (perang-sabil) in the great forest-regions."*

The three chiefs recount that they had been able to perform the *hadj* four times, had seen the prevailing poverty among the Islamic race settled there, and that the world now nears its end. They had fought against the Kompeuni Belanda and, despite all efforts, had had to acknowledge defeat — and so they had to submit to the Dutch authority. They cite the precedent of other Islamic powers — *Negeri Hindi* (British India) to the English Kompeuni, *Magribi* (Morocco) to the French Kompeuni, *Mesri* (Egypt) allied with the English — and ask the Tiro-mannen to follow their example, surrendering no weapon. The brief was dated at Koetaradja, Kampoeng Keudah, on the 18th of Radjab 1325. It actually reached [[Tengkoe Majet di Tiro|Tgk. Tjhi Majet]], and it is known that he discussed this advice of the three submitted chiefs with the other Tiro-mannen. None was willing to give up the fight; they would fight to the end, and with them would be done according to Allah's will.

## Identification of Tgk. Tjot Plieëng

When [[Tengkoe Tjot Plieëng|Tgk. Tjot Plieëng]] — the holiest and highest of the strijdbare geestelijke hoofden — was killed accidentally by a patrol of lieutenant Terwogt, the body was brought to a bivak for identification. After a few days it still showed no sign of decomposition; even Panglima Polim was called in for the identification, and he bent over the corpse that did not stink, in the midst of a group of Atjehers dumb with awe at this proof of the grace of Allah. *"This was God's secret,"* said the old Polem at Zentgraff's last meeting with him.

## Significance

The submission of Panglima Polim at Lho Seumawé, with Colijn at his side, is one of the emblematic images of the close of the great phase of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. After his submission he became the most prominent of the great chiefs through whom the Dutch bestuur sought to consolidate its authority; the letter of [[1907]] shows the use the bestuur made of his prestige. His identification of the body of Tgk. Tjot Plieëng is one of the small but revealing episodes by which the great Panglima steps out of the official record into the human one.
