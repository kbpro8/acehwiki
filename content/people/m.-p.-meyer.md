**M. P. Meyer** was a colonel of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] who authored a comprehensive account of Dutch military operations in [[Aceh]] from [[26 December]] [[1875]] to [[4 September]] [[1876]].

## Aceh Campaign 1875–1876

Meyer’s work, titled *Atjeh van 26 Dee. [[1875]] tot 4 Sept. [[1876]]*, presents a day‑by‑day chronicle of engagements, troop movements, and logistical challenges faced by Dutch forces. He records that “the mariniers, under the command of lieutenant Verhey, played a crucial role in securing the coastal forts while the Indische Leger advanced inland.”

## Strategic Assessment

Meyer evaluates the campaign as a turning point in the [[Aceh War]], arguing that the combined use of naval firepower and marine infantry allowed the Dutch to capture several key strongholds in northern Aceh. His analysis of supply lines through [[Batavia]] and the use of steam‑powered vessels is frequently referenced in studies of colonial military logistics.
