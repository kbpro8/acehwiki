**Van Swieten, General** was a Dutch army commander who captured the seat of the Sultan of [[Croot-Atjeh|West Aceh]] during the 1874 campaign. After leaving a garrison in the captured kraton, he withdrew with the main expeditionary force. He admitted limited knowledge of the internal political situation of Aceh, noting that there was no central authority and that the sultan was merely a “figurehead”.

## Campaign actions

Van Swieten’s seizure of the western kraton was one of several simultaneous operations that year, but it did not lead to lasting control because the Acehnese continued guerrilla resistance.
