**Fuselier Wolf** was a Dutch **[[fusilier]]** who was severely wounded while attempting to breach the **[[rotan|rattan]]** barrier at the main entrance of **[[Retch]]** during the **[[Expedition to Retch 1858|1858 assault]]**. He fell heavily after being struck.

## Combat Actions

Wolf’s effort to open the barrier was part of the coordinated attack that eventually allowed **[[L. F. Tuckerman]]** and other marines to enter the fort.
