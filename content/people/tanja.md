**Tanja** was a native fusilier — *inlandsch fuselier* — of the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Netherlands Indies Army]] severely wounded on [[25 September]] [[1897]] at the mouth of the [[Goeah Teroes]] cave on the [[Glé Betoes]], during the [[Patrol to the Goeah Teroes on the Glé Betoes (25 September 1897)|patrol from Lho-Nga]] under Captain [[J. N. C. Baron van Heerdt]].

He was wounded in the volley fired *à bout portant* by about fifteen Acehnese who appeared from behind a rock face on the party led by 1st Lieutenant [[J. van Greuningen]], who was killed with the European fusiliers [[Winkelman]], [[Tuk]] and [[Dissel]]. [[P. H. R. Beuming]] names him with [[Tanja]] and [[Bongsowikromo]] among the severely wounded.

The wounded were carried down in *[[Tandoe|tandoes]]*, a descent Beuming calls breakneck work, and treated on arrival at [[Lok-Nga|Lho-Nga]] by the officer of health.
