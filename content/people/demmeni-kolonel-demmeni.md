**Demmeni (Kolonel Demmeni)** was a Civil-military Governor (after Laging Tobias) mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. After a short government of Laging Tobias, civil-military Governor Kolonel Demmeni came, which was much needed.

## Role

Civil-military Governor (after [[Laging Tobias]])

## Account from Jongejans

After a short government of [[Laging Tobias]], civil-military Governor [[Demmeni|Kolonel Demmeni]] came, which was much needed.

## Significance

Demmeni (Kolonel Demmeni) appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
