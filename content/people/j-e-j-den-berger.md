**J. E. J. den Berger** was a Dutch 1e luitenant (first lieutenant) of the [[Koninklijk Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Indies army]] who, in the action of [[22 July]] [[1880]] during the [[Batoe Iliq Expedition (July 1880)|expedition against Batoe Iliq]], took with his section the [[Benting|benting]] (fortified enclosure) lying south-east of the kampong [[Batoe Iliq]] and dominating it, which the 1e luitenant-adjudant [[A. G. Popelier|A. G. Populier]] had discovered while reconnoitring the terrain. The work, which appeared to be held by a score of Atjehers, was carried; the enemy left some dead, several guns and lances, and about 1100 cartridges, while the Dutch had two wounded. [[W. A. Coblijn|Coblijn]], who quoted the account in *[[Hoe Atjeh te onderwerpen (Coblijn, 1893)|Hoe Atjeh te onderwerpen]]*, set the capture beside the success of kapitein [[Von Lübtow]] as proof that the fortified kampong could have been turned and mastered had the column been used as a whole.
