**Charles II of England** was the monarch who, in **[[1670]]**, entered into a secret treaty with **[[Louis XIV]]** (the **[[Treaty of Dover (1670)]]**) that committed England to support French ambitions against the **[[United Provinces (Dutch Republic)]]**. The treaty’s provisions, including financial subsidies, aimed to undermine Dutch trade, affecting the **[[VOC]]**’s operations in the **[[Sultanate of Aceh]]**.

## Domestic Context
Charles II’s need for funds after the **[[Restoration]]** led him to seek French assistance, a policy that provoked the **[[Triple Alliance (1668)]]** and heightened tensions that culminated in the **[[Rampjaar|Disaster Year]]** [[1672]].
