**Teukoe Tjhi' Moehamad Ali Akbar** was the [[oelèëbalang|oelèëbalang]] — self-governing ruler — of the [[Kawaj XVI|Kawaj XVI]] on the West Coast of Atjèh, one of the two loyal Dutch self-governors — the other being [[Teukoe Bin of Seunagan|Teukoe Bin of Seunagan]] — whose displacement by the governing commission assembled by the F-propagandist [[Said Aboe Bakar|Said Aboe Bakar]] at [[Meulabóh|Meulabóh]] in March [[1942]] marked the political reorganisation of the West Coast under the Japanese occupation. Piekaar's *Atjèh and the War with Japan* treats Teukoe Tjhi' Moehamad Ali Akbar as one of the principal casualties of the F-organisatie's activation — the loyal *oelèëbalang* whose removal illustrated the costs of the Dutch loss of Atjèh.

## The loyal self-governor of the Kawaj XVI

Teukoe Tjhi' Moehamad Ali Akbar was, Piekaar records, one of "the two loyal self-governors" of the West Coast — the other being Teukoe Bin of Seunagan. The Kawaj XVI — the federation of sixteen Westkust chiefs under the customs-collectorship of the *Keudjeuroeën Tjhï'* of [[Meulabóh|Meulabóh]] — was the principal territorial chieftaincy of the West Coast, and its *oelèëbalang* was therefore one of the most prominent figures in the region. The loyalty of Teukoe Tjhi' Moehamad Ali Akbar to the Dutch administration was, in the events of March [[1942]], the principal asset the Dutch lost on the West Coast.

## The governing commission at Meulabóh

When the first Japanese troops entered Meulabóh in March [[1942]] — with the F-propagandists Said Aboe Bakar and Said Doellah among them — "a governing commission was immediately formed, consisting mainly of opponents of the two loyal self-governors Teukoe Bin of Seunagan and Teukoe Tjhi' Moehamad Ali Akbar of the Kawaj XVI." The composition of the commission — drawn from the political opponents of the loyal *oelèëbalang*'s — reflected the F-organisatie's pre-invasion strategy of mobilising the dissident network against the Dutch-aligned rulers. The displacement of Teukoe Tjhi' Moehamad Ali Akbar, like that of Teukoe Bin of Seunagan, was the political reorganisation that the F-organisatie's activation had been designed to produce.

## The Tjhi' family

The Tjhi' family — to which Teukoe Tjhi' Moehamad Ali Akbar belonged — was one of the most prominent *oelèëbalang* families of Atjèh, with branches in multiple *landschap*'s. [[Teukoe Hadji Tjhi' Moehamad Djohan Alam Sjah|Teukoe Hadji Tjhi' Moehamad Djohan Alam Sjah]] of [[Peusangan|Peusangan]] — the patron of the founding of the [[Poesa|Poesa]] in [[1939]] — was the most prominent figure of the family; [[Teukoe Tjhi' Moehamad Daoed Sjah|Teukoe Tjhi' Moehamad Daoed Sjah]] of [[Idi Rajeu'|Idi Rajeu']] was another. Teukoe Tjhi' Moehamad Ali Akbar of the Kawaj XVI was, in this sense, the West Coast representative of a family whose branches spanned the residency.
