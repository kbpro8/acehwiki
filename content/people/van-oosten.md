**Van Oosten** was a European sergeant of the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Netherlands Indies Army]], *Algemeen Stamboek* no. 36662, killed in the [[Patrol of Captain Van Blokland (7 March 1896)|patrol of Captain Van Blokland]] near [[Lam Bada|Anak Baté]] on [[7 March]] [[1896]].

He heads the roll of the killed given by [[P. H. R. Beuming]]. His body was the object of the attempt by the sergeant [[Israëls]], himself struck by several bullets, to save it from the enemy's hands; the effort failed, and the body remained with those of two European and one native fusilier in Acehnese hands.

The other Europeans killed in the same action were the corporal [[Willems]] (no. 40845) and the fusiliers [[Van Manden]] (no. 34176), [[Laurens]] (no. 19396), [[Roosjen]] (no. 30288), [[Van Groenekan]] (no. 36143), [[Voegeli]] (no. 27330) and [[Magi]] (no. 45564).
