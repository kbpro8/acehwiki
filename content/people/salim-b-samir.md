**Salim b. Samir** was a Malay author whose work *[[Safinat al-nadja']]* (“The Ship of Salvation”) was printed in [[Singapore]] in [[1295]]/[[1878]]. The text was widely read in the Indonesian archipelago, including Aceh, and formed part of the literary milieu in which the *[[Bad' al-Amali]]* translation was produced.

## Publication History

The edition printed in Singapore (the year [[1295]]/[[1878]]) became a popular devotional work throughout the region, influencing later Acehnese religious literature.
