**Frederik Nieuwenhuijzen** was Vice-President of the Council of the Indies who served as the Dutch Government Commissioner for Political Affairs during the [[First Aceh Expedition]] in [[1873]].

## Political Mission to Aceh

In [[March]] [[1873]], Governor-General James Loudon appointed Nieuwenhuijzen to lead political negotiations with [[Sultan Alauddin Mahmud Syah IX]]. Tasked with compelling Aceh to recognize Dutch sovereignty and eliminate foreign diplomatic overtures, Nieuwenhuijzen arrived off the Aceh roadstead aboard a warship on [[22 March]] [[1873]].

After receiving evasive replies to his formal notes from the Acehnese court, Nieuwenhuijzen issued an official Declaration of War on [[26 March]] [[1873]]. He accompanied Supreme Commander [[Johan Harmen Rudolf Köhler]] during the landing at [[Pante Cermin]] on [[8 April]] [[1873]]. Following Köhler's death and the failed attack on the [[Kraton of Aceh]], Nieuwenhuijzen recommended the total withdrawal of the expeditionary force to Batavia in [[April]] [[1873]].
