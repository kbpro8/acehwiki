---

title: Teungku Mat Amin
aliases: [Nya' Amin, Nya' Mat]
tags: [person, warrior]
---

The son of Cheh Saman (Teungku Tiro), placed in command of his father's forces. After his father's death he and his guerrilla bands acted independently, causing Teungku Tiro's large following to disperse. He and about a hundred followers were killed in [[1896]] at the surprise of Aneu' Galong by Dutch troops.
