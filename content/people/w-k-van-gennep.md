**W. K. van Gennep** was a **[[lieutenant ter zee|lieutenant ter zee]]** of class **le** who led a landing division of 250 men, including 101 marines, during the **[[First Boni Expedition 1859]]** on **[[12 February]] [[1859]]** near **[[Badjoa]]**.

## Operations

Van Gennep’s division executed a deceptive maneuver with warships, landing successfully and engaging Boni forces in a series of skirmishes.
