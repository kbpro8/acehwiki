---
title: Nya' Bintang
tags: [tokoh, pejuang]
---

One of the principal guerrilla panglimas who took up arms under Teuku Asan's leadership in the holy war against the Dutch, named alongside Teuku Usen of Pagaraye, Teuku Ali, and Teuku Usen of Lueng Bata.
