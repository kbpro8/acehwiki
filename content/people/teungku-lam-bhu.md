---
title: Teungku Lam Bhu'
tags: [tokoh, ulama]
---

The wife of the Malay teacher Abduggamad Patani and herself a pious teacher, she composed the prose treatise Sipheut dua ploh on the twenty attributes of God for her disciples — one of only two Acehnese prose works discovered by Hurgronje.
