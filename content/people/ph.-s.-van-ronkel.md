**Philippus Samuel van Ronkel** — published as **Ph. S. van Ronkel** — was a Dutch Malay scholar whose 1944 article "Rānīrī's Maleische geschrift: Exposé der religies" in *BKI* 102, pp. 461 ff., provided the first detailed synopsis of the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān fī maʿrifat al-adyān*]] of [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]] and remained, until the [[1955]] edition of [[P. Voorhoeve|Voorhoeve]], the principal European study of the work. Van Ronkel was also the cataloguer of the manuscript of the *Tibjān* designated **A** in Voorhoeve's apparatus — Koninklijk Instituut voor de Tropen, Amsterdam, Anthropol. Afd. no. 0/271 — which he described in *Meded. Afd. Volkenkunde* no. 7 (1935), p. 158.

## The 1944 article on the *Tibjān*

Van Ronkel's 1944 article — "Rānīrī's Maleische geschrift: Exposé der religies" — was composed in wartime under difficult conditions, and Voorhoeve notes that it requires much correction of printing and other errors. Van Ronkel's opinion that the manuscript reproduced in the [[1955]] edition exhibits numerous lacunas, and that the (announced) enumerations of religions, sects, and dogmata are often defective, is however not shared by Voorhoeve: the absence of the third sect of the "Copts" is, Voorhoeve shows, indeed the result of a small lacuna in the Leiden manuscript, but the absence of the fourth group of the Tanāsuḥiyya is to be attributed to Raniri's source — the [[Tamhid fi bayan at-tawhid|*Tamhīd*]] of [[Abu Syakur as-Salimi|as-Sālimī]] — and not to the manuscript. The article nonetheless remained, until the [[1955]] edition, the principal European study of the *Tibjān*.

## The cataloguer of manuscript A

Van Ronkel was also the cataloguer of the *Tibjān* manuscript designated **A** in Voorhoeve's apparatus — Koninklijk Instituut voor de Tropen, Amsterdam, Anthropol. Afd. no. 0/271. He described the manuscript in *Mededeelingen van de Afdeeling Volkenkunde van het Koninklijk Koloniaal Instituut* no. 7 (1935), p. 158. The manuscript is on paper watermarked Leveratto (Churchill no. 573: 1794); it is a slightly more careful copy than the Leiden manuscript B, and ends at p. 105 r. 7 of B. Van Ronkel was also the cataloguer, in *BKI* 103, p. 599, of the Achehnese manuscript of the Koninklijk Instituut voor Taal-, Land- en Volkenkunde at The Hague, designated **D** in Voorhoeve's apparatus — the Achehnese adaptation of the *Tibjān* by [[Teungku Haron|Teungku Harõn]] (c. 1929).
