**Captain Pietersen** was a Dutch naval officer who commanded the fleet element that participated in the 1825 expedition against [[Boni]] and the surrounding region. His ship contributed to the transport of troops and supplies during the campaign.

## Service

Pietersen’s fleet supported the land forces of General‑Major Van Geen and the marine detachments during the March‑April [[1825]] operations.
