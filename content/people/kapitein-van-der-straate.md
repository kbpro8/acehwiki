**Captain van der Straate** was a Dutch *kapitein* of the [[Indische Leger|Indische leger]] who commanded the 180-man column that tried to penetrate the *wildernis* before sunrise on [[13 October]] [[1874]] in the [[Battle outside the ooster-face (13 October 1874)|battle outside the ooster-face]], the operation whose repulse proved that the Atjehneezen had become "complete master of the terrain" around the *zuider-* and *ooster-facen* of [[Kota Radja]].

## The Battle of 13 October 1874

On [[12 October]] [[1874]] the Dutch had again lost some ten dead and wounded clearing brush. [[Kapitein van Steenveld|Van Steenveld]], *commandant* of the *oosterwacht*, had reported on [[8 October]] [[1874]] that the Atjehneezen were digging approach trenches toward the Dutch works. [[Generaal Pel|Pel]] decided to clear the *wildernis* before it brought the enemy within storming distance of the *ooster-face*. On the morning of [[13 October]] [[1874]] van der Straate's strong column of 180 men tried to penetrate the *wildernis* before sunrise, in the hope of finding the far edge of the wood. The hope was disappointed; the *wildernis* extended so far that the troops had to set up in its midst. At six o'clock the woodcutters began, but the Atjehneezen discovered them and attacked from all sides. The [[Compagnie mariniers in Atjeh|mariniers]], in their first real fight, wasted ammunition and had to give up their position.

## The Repulse and Its Consequences

The whole covering force had to retire; by eight o'clock the enemy's fire was overwhelming. Pel ordered retreat to the Kraton. Only when the artillery on the *zuider-* and *ooster-bastions* opened fire were the Atjehneezen stopped. The day cost several dead and wounded and proved that the enemy was "complete master of the terrain" near the *zuider-* and *ooster-facen*. After this, clearing work was halted. The repulse of van der Straate's column was, in [[G. F. W. Borel|Borel]]'s narrative, a strategic reverse: it showed that the Dutch could no longer clear the ground in front of their own walls without a major operation, and that the Atjehneezen — by their *nadering* under cover of the dense *bamboe-doeri* — had neutralised the Dutch *stormvrijheid* (freedom from assault by storming). The lesson Borel draws is that the *afwachtende houding* had allowed the enemy to creep up to the very walls of the Kraton, and that only the [[Aanvallende tactiek in Atjeh|aanvallende tactiek]] of the [[Conquest of Langkroek (28 September 1874)|conquest of Langkroek]] and the [[Conquest of Lemboe (7 November 1874)|conquest of Lemboe]] could restore the Dutch position.
