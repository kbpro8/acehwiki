**Teungkoe Tirò (Teungkoe Moehamat Anim)** was a First Teungkoe Tirò encountered by the Kompeuni; oelama of Tirò (Pidië) mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Oelama from gampong Tirò in Pidië.

## Role

First [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] Tirò encountered by the [[Kompeuni]]; [[Oelama|oelama]] of Tirò ([[Pidië]])

## Account from Jongejans

[[Oelama]] from [[Gampong|gampong]] Tirò in [[Pidië]]. Died [[1886]]; his son was too young to succeed him, so the title passed to a relative [[Teungkoe Sjech Saman di Tiri|Tjhèh Saman]].

## Significance

Teungkoe Tirò (Teungkoe Moehamat Anim) appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
