**Raden Ario Soeraning Prang** was a **majoor** (major) of the **harisans** (native auxiliary troops) from **Soemenap**, who served in the Dutch colonial forces during the **Aceh War** in the mid‑1870s. He is recorded as wielding a hereditary poisoned lance while leading his men during the assault on **Adjoen** on [[30 December]] [[1875]] (see [[page 135]]). His role was pivotal in breaking the resistance of the **Atjehers** at the kampong edge, after which the Dutch column entered the settlement.

## Military Role

Raden Ario commanded a contingent of **barisans** (native soldiers) and was noted for his personal weapon, a **poisoned lance**—an **erfstuk** (family heirloom) of his lineage. During the attack on **Adjoen**, his troops were the first to breach the kampong’s outer defenses, allowing the **VIIIe bataljon** (8th Battalion) to occupy the village (see [[page 135]]).

## Significance

His participation illustrates the Dutch strategy of employing local elite leaders to command native auxiliaries, a common practice in the later phases of the **Aceh War**. The use of traditional weapons alongside modern firearms underscores the hybrid nature of the conflict.
