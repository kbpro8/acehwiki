**Luitenant Zon** was a Dutch officer of the [[Oost-Indisch Leger|Netherlands East Indies Army]] serving in the outposts of [[Groot-Atjeh]] during the alliance period of [[1893]]–[[1896]], recorded by [[G. B. Hooijer]] in *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]*.

## Service in the *buitenposten* (outer posts)

He was among the officers photographed on the bench at [[Biloel]], within the white wall of the old *[[Messigit|Messigit]]* around which the fortification had been raised. [[G. B. Hooijer]] noted of him only, in a parenthesis, that he *later sneuvelde* — later fell in action.
