**Ir. F. J. J. Dootjes** was a Dutch engineer and archivist who served as Secretary-Archivist (*Secretaris-Archivaris*) of the [[Oostkust van Sumatra-Instituut]] (East Coast of Sumatra Institute) in Amsterdam. He was the compiler of *Deli-Figuren uit de jaren 1950 t/m 1952*, published as Mededeling No. 37 of the Institute in [[1953]], as well as its predecessor *Deli-Data 1938-1951*.

## Work

Dootjes' compilations of "Deli-Figuren" and "Deli-Data" are valuable reference works documenting the personnel, institutions, organisations, and events of the East Coast of Sumatra — including the [[Atjeh]] region — during the late colonial, Japanese occupation, and early independence periods. They record the mutations (transfers, arrivals, departures, deaths) of civil servants, military personnel, plantation officials, and other figures active in the region, providing a prosopographic resource for the social and administrative history of northern Sumatra.

## Source

*Oostkust van Sumatra-Instituut Mededeling No. 37: Deli-Figuren uit de jaren 1950 t/m 1952* (Amsterdam, [[1953]]).
