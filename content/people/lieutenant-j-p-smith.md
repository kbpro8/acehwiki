**Smith, J. P.** was a Dutch marine lieutenant who was wounded in the left arm by a shot on [[23 January]] [[1874]] and later died on [[26 January]] [[1874]] in the bivouac at [[Penajoeng]]. He had entered the [[Militaire Academie]] at [[Breda]] as a second lieutenant in [[August]] [[1857]] and had previously been appointed captain, despite the usual rule that only graduates of the Royal Naval Academy could hold such rank.

## Service and death

Smith’s death was marked by a solemn burial on [[27 January]] [[1874]] with a wooden memorial placed on his grave. His loss was felt keenly within the small officer corps of the Dutch marines.
