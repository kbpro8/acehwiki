**Bart van Hoven** (active) was a Sculptor mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Sculpted the bust of Pastoor Verbraak at Koetaradja.

## Role

Sculptor

## Dates

active

## Account from Jongejans

Sculpted the bust of [[Pastoor Verbraak]] at [[Koetaradja]].

## Significance

Bart van Hoven appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
