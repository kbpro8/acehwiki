---
title: Abdussamad of Palembang
aliases: [Talem-bang]
tags: [tokoh, ulama]
---

A pandit from Palembang who gained high reputation about a century before Hurgronje's writing, translating al-Ghazali's mystical works into Malay and studying under Mohammad as-Samman at Medina. His Nasihat al-muslimin supporting the holy war was the direct model for Nya' Ahmat's Acehnese poem.
