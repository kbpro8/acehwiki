**Radja Kaliamsjah Sinaga** was an Indonesian politician who served as the deputy head of state (*Wakil Wali Negara*) of the [[Negara Sumatera Timur]] (N.S.T.) and subsequently, after the dissolution of the N.S.T. on 15 August [[1950]], as a member of the advisory college assisting the acting governor of North Sumatra, [[Sarimin Reksodihardjo]].

## Role in the N.S.T. and its dissolution

Sinaga played a central role in the negotiations leading to the dissolution of the N.S.T. He represented the N.S.T., together with [[Tengku Arifin]], in the discussions with minister [[Anak Agung Gde Agung]] (representing the R.I.S. and the Republic) and [[Sarimin Reksodihardjo]] (representing the Preparation Committee). The resulting agreement, announced on 5 August [[1950]], stipulated that East Sumatra would become part of the autonomous province of North Sumatra, which would comprise the pre-war residencies of [[Atjeh]], [[Tapanuli]], and East Sumatra.

On 18 February [[1951]], Sinaga departed for Djakarta, where the position of acting resident (*res. t.b.*) at the Ministry of Internal Affairs (*ministerie van Binnenlandse Zaken*) awaited him, and where he could also complete his studies at the Law School (*Rechtshogeschool*).
