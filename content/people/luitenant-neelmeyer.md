**Luitenant Neelmeyer** was a Dutch lieutenant of the [[Oost-Indisch Leger|Netherlands East Indies Army]] serving as acting *[[Controleur|controleur]]* in the territory of [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb]] in the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]], whose night ride of [[March]] [[1896]] to warn the garrisons of [[Toengkoeb]] and [[Tjot Rang]] of the defection of [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Oemar]] was singled out by [[G. B. Hooijer]] as an act of exceptional coolness.

## Acting controleur

Hooijer described Neelmeyer as the acting *controleur* in Toekoe Tjoet's district, and as an officer who *zooveel slag had met hem om te gaan* — who had so good a knack of dealing with him. He appears on the plate showing Toekoe Tjoet's dwelling, seated on a visit beneath the house with another officer, the host himself being absent.

## The warning ride of March 1896

When the going-over of the [[Panglima Prang Besar]] — [[Teukoe Oemar]] — became known to him, Neelmeyer set out in the evening in the company of [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb|Toekoe Tjoet]] and some of the latter's followers from [[Pakan Kroeng Tjoet]], a point inside the *[[Geconcentreerde linie|geconcentreerde linie]]*, to [[Toengkoeb]] and [[Tjot Rang]], in order to warn the garrisons of those posts. His purpose was to prevent [[Toengkoeb]] from being overrun by the Achehnese auxiliary bands which, as he knew in his capacity as *controleur*, were to assemble there early the next morning to undertake a patrol with the Dutch.

Hooijer emphasised that he thereby placed his life defenceless in the hands of *dien weinig betrouwbaren Atjeher* — that little trustworthy Achehnese — who two days later followed [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Oemar]]'s example and defected. Whether because the Toekoe was not yet fully informed of what had occurred, or because he had not yet determined on which side to place himself, Neelmeyer carried out his intention undisturbed and was back in [[Tjot Iri]], a post inside the Dutch line, before daybreak — just before communication with the posts he had warned was cut. *Zijn kameraden op die posten vonden dat een kranige streek en wij zijn van hetzelfde gevoelen*, Hooijer wrote: his comrades at those posts thought it a plucky stroke, and the author was of the same opinion.

## The evacuation of Toengkoeb

Later, under the leadership of the same officer, the garrison was withdrawn from [[Toengkoeb]] and the post evacuated *zonder dat daarbij een enkel schot viel* — without a single shot being fired.
