**Teuku Nek** was an Acehnese leader who visited the Dutch camp after the fall of the [[Kraton of Aceh]].

## Role in the campaign

On [[27 January]] [[1874]] he brought a letter from [[Nja Abas]], *Galar Sia Oelama* and panglima of the [[XXV Mukim]], offering unconditional submission and asking mercy for his subjects. He subsequently sought permission for cultivation and harvest, which [[Jan van Swieten]] treated as evidence for a conciliatory policy.

## Significance

The source places [[Teuku Nek]] within the contested military and political history of the [[Aceh War]].
