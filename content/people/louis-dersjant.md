**Louis Dersjant** was a Dutch military officer and Infantry Colonel who served in the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]].

## Military Career and Service in Aceh

Born in [[1877]], Dersjant graduated from KMA Breda and joined the Marechaussee in [[February]] [[1903]]. After receiving two Honourable Mentions, he was awarded the Knight 4th Class Military William Order (*M.W.O.*) in [[1906]] as a First Lieutenant for conspicuous gallantry in Aceh.
