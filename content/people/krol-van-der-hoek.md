**Krol van der Hoek** was a Dutch lieutenant of the engineers (*genie*) at [[Acheh|Atjeh]] in [[1885]]. By a Batavia telegram of [[16 December]] [[1885]] — carried by the Indian mail after [[M. T. H. Perelaer|Perelaer]]'s speech — the Governor of Atjeh reported that Genie troops had repaired damage to the tramway between [[Lamara]] and [[Lampeneroet]]; two men were killed and four wounded, among them the engineer lieutenant Krol van der Hoek (see [[Guerilla raids inside the concentration line (1885)]]).
