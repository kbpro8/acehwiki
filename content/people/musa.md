title: Musa
aliases: [Moses]
tags: [tokoh, agama]
---

Hero of the Hikayat Pra'un, which gives an elaborate, fantastical Acehnese account of the conflict between Moses and Pharaoh, following the Malay version of the history of the prophets with variations — including Allah's instruction that Moses break Pharaoh of his three remaining virtues through "diabolical advice" before he can be wholly overthrown.
