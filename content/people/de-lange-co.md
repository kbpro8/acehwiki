**de Lange & Co.** (pre-1896) was a Bataviaasche firma (Batavian trading firm) mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Laid the first stone for the Sabang harbor enterprise; founded a coal station with financial support from the Nederlandsche Handel Maatschappij.

## Role

Bataviaasche firma (Batavian trading firm)

## Dates

pre-[[1896]]

## Account from Jongejans

Laid the first stone for the [[Sabang]] harbor enterprise; founded a coal station with financial support from the [[Nederlandsche Handel-Maatschappij|Nederlandsche Handel Maatschappij]]. Its interests were later transferred to the N.V. '[[Vrijhaven]] en [[Kolenstation]] [[Sabang]]'.

## Significance

de Lange & Co. appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
