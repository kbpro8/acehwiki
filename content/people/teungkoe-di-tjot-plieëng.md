**Teungkoe di Tjot Plieëng** was a Tirò follower; blood relative of Tjhēh Saman; revered as a saint mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. In some regions held in even higher esteem than Teungkoe Tirò himself.

## Role

Tirò follower; blood relative of Tjhēh Saman; revered as a saint

## Account from Jongejans

In some regions held in even higher esteem than [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] Tirò himself. Revered as a saint; gave countless 'djimats' (amulets) bearing his seal, found on many fallen Acehnese. In [[1903]] an action was launched against him with [[Darlang]], [[Stoop]], [[Van der Maaten]] as [[Patrouille (Maréchaussée)|patrouille]]-commandanten. Contact occurred several times and many of his followers fell, but Teungkoe di Tjot Plieëng remained at large. Finally an infantry patrol under Lieutenant [[Dr. W. A. Terwogt|Terwogt]] ran into a small band; three were killed, and the seal carried by one proved Teungkoe di Tjot Plieëng was among the fallen ([[1905]]).

## Significance

Teungkoe di Tjot Plieëng appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
