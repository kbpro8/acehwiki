**Winkelman** was a European fusilier of the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Netherlands Indies Army]] killed on [[25 September]] [[1897]] at the mouth of the [[Goeah Teroes]] cave on the [[Glé Betoes]], during the [[Patrol to the Goeah Teroes on the Glé Betoes (25 September 1897)|patrol from Lho-Nga]] under Captain [[J. N. C. Baron van Heerdt]].

He fell with 1st Lieutenant [[J. van Greuningen]] and the fusiliers [[Winkelman]], [[Tuk]] and [[Dissel]] when about fifteen Acehnese appeared suddenly from behind a rock face and opened fire *à bout portant* on the party that had approached close to the cave. The native fusiliers [[Bongsowikromo]] and [[Tanja]] were severely wounded in the same volley.

The dead were carried down the mountain in *[[Tandoe|tandoes]]* and taken the same day to [[Koeta Radja]] for burial on the [[Peutjoet|Petjoet cemetery]].
