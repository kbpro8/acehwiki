**C. Redevoded-Hendriks** was a Dutch woman described as "bekend uit Atjeh" ("known from Atjeh") who was posthumously awarded a decoration by Royal Decision (*Koninklijk Besluit*) of 23 May [[1950]]. The insignia were presented on 2 December [[1950]] to her daughter, Mrs. Mess Höhscheid.

## Connection to Atjeh

The precise nature of Redevoded-Hendriks' connection to [[Atjeh]] is not detailed in the source, but the posthumous award of a royal decoration indicates that she had performed notable service during her time there. The fact that the decoration was awarded posthumously and that the insignia had to be presented to her daughter suggests that she died in the period between the Japanese occupation and [[1950]], possibly during the Japanese internment of European civilians or in the violent aftermath of the war.
