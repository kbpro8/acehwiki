**Rijnen** was a Dutch lieutenant of the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Netherlands Indies Army]] who commanded the platoon of fusiliers, armed with repeating rifles, that strengthened the *[[Korps Marechaussee|marechaussée]]* in the [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|surprise of Anak Galoeng]] on [[29 June]] [[1896]], and who was the only officer severely wounded in that action.

[[P. H. R. Beuming]] calls him *den dapperen luitenant Rijnen*. In the fighting inside the *[[Benting|benting]]* he struck down the Acehnese who had set upon Lieutenant [[C. F. A. Wagener|Wagener]] with the *[[Klewang|klewang]]*; at the same moment Rijnen himself was wounded by a shot in the hip and a *klewang* blow across the back, and Wagener in his turn shot down the assailants with two revolver shots and saved him from certain death.

Of the four officers wounded in the action, Beuming records, *was alleen de luitenant Rijnen zwaar gewond* — only Lieutenant Rijnen was severely wounded — while the wounds of the twenty-nine *marechaussées* all proved not life-threatening.
