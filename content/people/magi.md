**Magi** was a European fusilier of the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Netherlands Indies Army]], *Algemeen Stamboek* no. 45564, killed in the [[Patrol of Captain Van Blokland (7 March 1896)|patrol of Captain Van Blokland]] near [[Lam Bada|Anak Baté]] on [[7 March]] [[1896]].

He is named in the casualty roll given by [[P. H. R. Beuming]] in *[[Schetsen uit den strijd op Groot-Atjeh (1911)|Schetsen uit den strijd op Groot-Atjeh]]*, which lists by name and service number the European sergeant [[Van Oosten]] (no. 36662), the corporal [[Willems]] (no. 40845) and the fusiliers [[Van Manden]] (no. 34176), [[Laurens]] (no. 19396), [[Roosjen]] (no. 30288), [[Van Groenekan]] (no. 36143), [[Voegeli]] (no. 27330) and [[Magi]] (no. 45564), together with the severely wounded fusilier [[Debats]] (no. 27702) and two native fusiliers, and twenty-one lightly wounded other ranks.

Three of the European dead, with one native fusilier, were left in the enemy's hands when the attempt of the sergeant [[Israëls]] to recover the body of Van Oosten failed.
