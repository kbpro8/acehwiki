**Joed Mohmed Lamtengah** was an elderly Acehnese man who, according to [[H. van Kol]] speaking in the [[Tweede Kamer]] on [[17 November]] [[1897]], had shortly before been carried outside on a *[[Bali-bali|bali-bali]]* — a low bamboo platform-bed — and then shot dead.

## The allegation

Van Kol's words were: "Nog kort geleden werd een oude man Joed Mohmed Lamtengah op een bali-bali naar buiten gedragen en toen doodgeschoten." — Quite recently an old man, Joed Mohmed Lamtengah, was carried outside on a bali-bali and then shot dead. The sentence closed his catalogue of specific atrocities, which he summed up in the words "In één woord, ons oorlogvoeren bestaat in branden en zengen, in moorden en verwoesten." — In one word, our waging of war consists in burning and scorching, in murdering and destroying.

The source gives no date, no place beyond the toponymic element *Lamtengah* in the name, and no indication of the unit or officer responsible. The prefix *lam-* is among the commonest Acehnese topographic prefixes and identifies the *[[Kampong|kampong]]* from which the man took his designation.

## Context

The case was one of a series Van Kol adduced as breaches of the international law of war as codified at the [[Brussels Conference of 1874|Conference of Brussels of 1874]], which forbade the killing of an enemy *hors de combat* and of one powerless to defend himself. The others were the shooting of [[Njahim]], the treacherous arrests of the [[Radja van Samalangan]], the head of [[Bintara Peunang]] and [[Toekoe Njak Amat]], the taking of the daughter of [[Wakil Joesoef]] as a hostage, and the killing of [[Toekoe Tjoet Machmoed Lam Tenzah]] with his family.
