**Teungkoe Abdoel Hamid Orangkaja Sri Maharadja Mangkoe Boemi** was *mbaharadja* of the small landscape [[Lho Seumawe]], a once prominent prince bearing title *mbaharadja* indicating former prestige at sultan and whole [[Atjeh]].

## Encounter with Dutch

About 50 years before [[1923]], when representative of Netherlands government wished to meet him and came ashore from ship in bay, mbaharadja, leaned against tree, proudly awaited him. He and many heads stood with loaded revolvers in girdles, signs of subordination far. He appeared as "rude savage" said eyewitness [[J. A. Kruyt|J. A. Kruyt]]: large heavy long loose hair dressed in black jacket with gold sovereigns as buttons.

## Colonial period income

In [[1904]] signed [[Korte Verklaring]]. Landscape may be small, influence however whole *gewest* as great as title. At income from landscape and compensation from government monthly 1000 gulden, plus incomes own property and trade.
