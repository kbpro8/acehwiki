**General‑Major A. A. V. Michiels** commanded the third Dutch expedition to [[Bali]] in [[1849]] (page 106). He led an initial force of 700 men, including 178 marines, from the landing at [[Boeleleng]] to [[Singa‑Radja]] and subsequently to the fortified position of [[Djagaraga]] (page 107).

## Campaign Overview

After the landing at Boeleleng on [[29 March]] [[1849]], Michiels advanced to [[Singa‑Radja]] without resistance (page 107). Negotiations with the Balinese princes of Boeleleng and [[Karang‑Assana]] failed, prompting a move toward [[Djagaraga]], where his forces suffered heavy casualties until the decisive action of Colonel C. A. de Brauw turned the tide (page 107).

## Interaction with Marine Units

Michiels coordinated closely with the marine battalion under Captain ter zee Bouricius, integrating naval firepower with infantry assaults. The expedition’s success led to the awarding of the *Militaire Willemsorde* to several officers (page 108).
