**Sultan Iskander Moeda** was a Sultan of Aceh, ancestor of the Polém lineage mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Ancestor (through his son Panglima Polém Moeda Sertia Perkasa and an Abyssinian slave girl) of the Polém family.

## Role

[[Sultan of Aceh (captured 1824)|Sultan of Aceh]], ancestor of the Polém lineage

## Account from Jongejans

Ancestor (through his son [[Panglima Polém Moeda Sertia Perkasa]] and an Abyssinian slave girl) of the Polém family.

## Significance

[[Sultan Iskander Moeda]] appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
