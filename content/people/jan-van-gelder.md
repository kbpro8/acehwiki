**Jan van Gelder** was a Dutch sea‑captain and son‑in‑law of **[[Michiel de Ruyter]]** who perished in the **[[Battle of Schooneveld]]** (1666).  The source lists him among the officers killed when the Dutch fleet suffered heavy losses (see [[275]]).  As a member of the naval elite, his service contributed to the protection of the **[[Indische retourvloot]]**, which regularly called at **[[Aceh]]** for the transport of spices and other goods.

## Connection to Aceh
Van Gelder’s participation in the fleet that defended the North Sea and the English Channel helped maintain the safe passage of the **[[Dutch East India Company]]**’s merchant ships to the East Indies, including the Sultanate of **[[Aceh]]**.
