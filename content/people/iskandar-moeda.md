**Iskandar Moeda** (reign in 1550-1650 golden age) was a Sultan of Aceh — 'greatest of all sultans' mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Reigned during the golden age of Aceh (1550-1650).

## Role

[[Sultan of Aceh (captured 1824)|Sultan of Aceh]] — 'greatest of all sultans'

## Dates

reign in [[1550]]-[[1650]] golden age

## Account from Jongejans

Reigned during the golden age of Aceh ([[1550]]-[[1650]]). After his reign four female sultans ascended the throne, a period of deep decline.

## Significance

Iskandar Moeda appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
