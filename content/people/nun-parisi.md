---
title: Nun Parisi
tags: [tokoh, karya-sastra]
---

Son of Raja Sarah of Chamtalira, who journeyed with companions Lidam and Arian to study, saved a king from a murderous naga, and discovered his betrothed's infidelity on his return. After wars and reconciliations he married both his own betrothed and his companion's, becoming king and fathering a son, Useuman Areh.
