**Raja Abdullah of Aceh** (historically written Radja Doelah) was an Acehnese nobleman, eldest son, and designated heir of Sagie Chief [[Panglima Polem]] of the [[XXII Moekims]] during the Aceh War.

## Role in the Second Expedition

Fighting alongside his father in defense of the [[Aceh Sultanate]], [[Raja Abdullah of Aceh]] led warriors from the [[XXII Moekims]] against General [[J. van Swieten]] during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] in [[1873]] and [[1874]]. 

In early February [[1874]], rumors circulated in Dutch camps that both [[Panglima Polem]] and [[Raja Abdullah of Aceh]] had died of cholera or combat wounds, reports cited in contemporary analyses of the [[General van Swieten War Telegrams]].
