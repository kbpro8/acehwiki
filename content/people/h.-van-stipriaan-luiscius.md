**H. van Stipriaan Luiscius** was an officer of the *[[Korps Marechaussee te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* in [[Atjeh]] recorded in the *[[Overzicht van Onderscheidingen toegekend aan het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden (1890-1930)|Overzicht van Onderscheidingen]]* of the [[1930]] jubilee number as knight of the *[[Militaire Willemsorde|Militaire Willems-Orde]]* 4th class, awarded in [[1902]].

## Entry in the register

The register lists him under [[1902]] as *Kapt. Mar. H. van Stipriaan Luiscius*, in the same year in which [[K. van der Maaten]] and [[J. C. Heijnen]] received the 3rd class.
