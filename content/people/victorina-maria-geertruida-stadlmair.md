**Victorina Maria Geertruida Stadlmair** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[H. N. A. Swart]]

He married on [[22 May]] [[1901]] Miss [[Victorina Maria Geertruida Stadlmair]], then twenty-three years old. Swart had asked to be reposted to Atjeh at the end of his leave; at [[Padang]] he learned that the request had been granted — but the newly married officer had been given a station to which his wife was not permitted to follow him. So it was that on [[17 September]] [[1901]] Swart arrived in Atjeh for the third time, alone. He returned from leave on [[21 August]] [[1901]] aboard the *s.s. Koning Willem III* and was posted to the 12th Battalion.

### From [[1901]]

[[H. N. A. Swart]] married [[Victorina Maria Geertruida Stadlmair]] on [[22 May]] **[[1901]]**, returned from leave on [[21 August]] aboard the *s.s. Koning Willem III*, and arrived in Atjeh for the third time — alone — on [[17 September]] [[1901]]. On [[6 December]] [[1901]] he received the distinguishing mark for long Netherlands service as an officer.

### From [[Extract from the service record of H. N. A. Swart]]

Born [[12 October]] [[1863]] at [[Tjibitoeng]] in the East Indies. Father: [[Klaas Swart|Klaas]]. Mother: [[Cornelia Maria Johanna Maria Gouay]]. Afflicted with myopic astigmatism of 3/4 D. in both eyes. Married on [[22 May]] [[1901]] to Miss [[Victorina Maria Geertruida Stadlmair]], then twenty-three years old. These notes on the *Staat van Dienst* were signed on [[30 September]] [[1918]] by Lieutenant-General [[Van Rietschoten]] as army commander.
