**Vice‑admiral J. P. Machielsen** served as the commander of the Dutch naval forces in the [[Nederlandsch Indië|Dutch East Indies]] during the third Bali expedition of [[1849]] (page 106). He led a fleet of sixty war‑ and transport ships, carrying 2 700 sailors, and coordinated the amphibious landings at [[Boeleleng]] and later at [[Djagaraga]].

## Leadership of the 1849 Expedition

Machielsen’s fleet provided the naval firepower and logistical support for General‑Major A. A. V. Michiels. The expedition assembled roughly 5 000 colonial troops and 3 000 local auxiliaries (*koelies*) (page 106). His command was crucial in securing the coastal approaches and enabling the marine battalion’s operations.

## Naval Operations

The navy’s role included blockading the island’s coasts, transporting troops, and delivering artillery support during the assaults on fortified positions such as [[Djagaraga]] and [[Kasoemba]] (pages 106‑107).
