**Pruys van der Hoeven** (1881-1883 (approx., two-year tenure)) was a Governor of Atjeh (civiel bestuur); ex-Resident of Palembang mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. First civilian Governor of Atjeh and Onderhoorigheden after the 1881 splitting of civiel- en militair bestuur.

## Role

Governor of Atjeh (civiel bestuur); ex-Resident of [[Palembang]]

## Dates

[[1881]]-[[1883]] (approx., two-year tenure)

## Account from Jongejans

First civilian Governor of Atjeh and [[Onderhoorigheden]] after the [[1881]] splitting of civiel- en militair bestuur. Established the first administrative division into three afdeelingen ([[Groot-Atjeh]], Noord- en [[Atjeh's Oostkust (East Coast of Aceh)|Oostkust]], [[Westkust]]) and ten onderafdeelingen. Achieved important results in the [[Onderhoorigheden|onderhoorigheden]] but made errors in [[Groot-Atjeh]] by replacing the military with police too soon.

## Significance

Pruys van der Hoeven appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
