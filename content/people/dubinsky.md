**Dubinsky** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[W. B. J. A. Scheepens]]

Swart was at once informed by telephone and travelled to Sigli by special tram with the well-known surgeon [[Dubinsky|dr. Dubinsky]]. But surgical intervention could no longer help. Scheepens died on [[17 October]] [[1913]].

### From [[Pidië]]

On [[10 October]] [[1913]] the Civil Authority of Pidië, overste [[W. B. J. A. Scheepens]], was stabbed at Sigli during a sitting of the *[[Landschapsgerecht]]* by the *[[oelèëbalang]]* of the [[II Moekims Titeuë]], [[T. Béntara Titeuë]]; he died on [[17 October]]. Swart travelled to Sigli by special tram with the surgeon [[Dubinsky]], but surgical intervention could no longer help.

### From [[Sigli]]

On [[10 October]] [[1913]] Scheepens, presiding over the *[[Moesapat]]* at Sigli in his capacity as Civil Authority, was stabbed in the belly by the *[[oelèëbalang]]* [[T. Béntara Titeuë]] and died on [[17 October]]. Swart travelled to Sigli by special tram with the surgeon [[Dubinsky|dr. Dubinsky]]; the *officier van gezondheid* at Sigli had given first aid, but surgical intervention could no longer help.
