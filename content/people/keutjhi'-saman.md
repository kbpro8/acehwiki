**Keutjhi' Saman** was den langgezochten *panglima* ("war-leader") van den ex-pretendent sultan, surprised and killed with three adherents in the [[Meergebied]] in February [[1911]].

His death fell in the month in which [[Jenae]] destroyed the band of [[Tgk. Léjman]] and [[H. J. Schmidt]] found [[Keudjroeën Oebit]] in the [[Tangsé]] valley — the turning-point of the campaign in the [[Meergebied]].
