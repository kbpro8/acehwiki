**Klinkert** was a Dutch missionary and lexicographer who authored *Drie Maleische gedichten* (Leiden [[1886]]), which includes the Malay version of Chintabuhan, a source for the Acehnese Hikayat Chintabuhan; he also compiled a *Bloemlezing* (Leiden [[1890]]) and a Malay‑Dutch dictionary (*Wdbk.*). His dictionary is cited for providing the correct usage of a particular Malay term “*in vragen of bii on‑zekerheid*” (“in questions or in uncertainty”), a usage discussed alongside the work of [[Wilkinson]] in Malay semantic studies.

## Lexicographic Contribution
According to the source, Klinkert’s *Wdbk.* states that the term in question is used “*in vragen of bii on‑zekerheid*” (“in questions or in uncertainty”). His work is referenced alongside those of [[Wilkinson]] in discussions of Malay semantic fields.

## Literary Contributions
He authored *Drie Maleische gedichten* (Leiden [[1886]]), which includes the Malay version of Chintabuhan, a source for the Acehnese Hikayat Chintabuhan. He also compiled a *Bloemlezing* (Leiden [[1890]]).

## Relevance to Acehnese Studies
Klinkert’s dictionary, like other colonial‑era lexica, has been employed by scholars interpreting Malay manuscripts from Sumatra, including the [[Hikayat Raja-raja Pasai]]. His definitions assist in understanding the linguistic milieu of the Acehnese‑Malay literary world.
