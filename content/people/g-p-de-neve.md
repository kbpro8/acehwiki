**G. P. de Neve** was a Kolonel of the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger]] who, in [[July 1873|July]] [[1873]], acted as mentor to the detachment of metropolitan officers sailing to the Indies aboard the steamship *Conrad* of the Maatschappij "Nederland," including [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede-Luitenant C. J. Snijders]]. During the ten-day enforced stop at [[Gibraltar|Gibraltar]] for repairs after storm damage in the Bay of Biscay, De Neve conducted the younger officers on an excursion to [[Tangier|Tangier]].

## The *Conrad* Voyage of July–August 1873

The *Conrad* (2500 tons) left [[Nieuwediep|Nieuwediep]] on [[10 July]] [[10 July]] [[1873]] carrying a reinforcement detachment of 400 men, officers returning from European leave, and most of the metropolitan officers being detached to the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger]] for the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)|second Aceh expedition]] — among them [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] and [[J. W. N. Cramer|J. W. N. Cramer]]. In the Bay of Biscay the *Conrad* suffered damage and put in at [[Gibraltar|Gibraltar]] for ten days of repair. The officers profited by their stay to visit the British garrison and, "onder geleide van den, hen als mentor toegevoegden Kolonel van het Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger G. P. DE NEVE," to make an excursion to [[Tangier|Tangier]]. After Gibraltar the *Conrad* called only at Port Said and Aden, and reached [[Batavia|Batavia]] on [[31 August]] [[31 August]] [[1873]] after seven and a half weeks.
