**Panglima Besar Soeloong** was the chief of a band of **[[pirates|sea‑rovers]]** who had established a fortified camp at **[[Retch]]** on the east coast of **[[Sumatra]]**. He declared his willingness to submit to the Dutch **[[government|government]]**, but was ultimately killed during the **[[Expedition to Retch 1858|1858 assault]]**.

## Conflict with the Dutch

Soeloong’s forces controlled the riverine approaches to Retch and resisted the landing force led by **[[A. J. Kroef]]**. The Dutch overcame a heavy **[[rotan|rattan]]** barrier at the main entrance, after which Soeloong and about 70 of his followers were slain, with 40 taken prisoner.
