**Malik Ibrahim** was a merchant and Islamic missionary who was among the earliest propagators of Islam in the Indonesian Archipelago. He died in [[1419]] at [[Gresik]], [[Java]], where his tomb is preserved.

## Account from *Nederlandsch Indië Platen Atlas* (1926) — Malik Ibrahim

The atlas describes him as follows: "Malik Ibrahim was een handelaar, vermoedelijk een Pers uit Kashan, die gedurende zijn leven op Java zich inspande, om de bevolking te Islamiseeren. Hij overleed in 1419 te Gresik." (Malik Ibrahim was a merchant, presumably a Persian from Kashan, who during his life on Java applied himself to converting the population to Islam. He died in 1419 at Gresik.)

The atlas also notes the unusual architecture of the entrance gate to his tomb: "Eigenaardig is dat deze poort in het geheel niet in Arabischen bouwtrant is opgetrokken." (Peculiar is that this gate is not at all built in the Arabian architectural style.) This observation is consistent with the broader pattern described in the atlas, whereby early Islamic architecture in the Archipelago continued to follow Hindu models: "de bouwkunst van den Mohammedaanschen godsdienst geen nieuwe inspiratie kreeg. Men bleef de oude, bestaande Hindoesche modellen volgen, zelfs bij moskeeën." (Mohammedan religious architecture received no new inspiration. One continued to follow the old, existing Hindu models, even for mosques.)

## Significance for Achehnese History

Malik Ibrahim's mission in the early 15th century predates the establishment of the Muslim sultanates of [[Perlak]] and [[Pasé]] on the North Sumatran coast (around [[1500]]). His presence in Gresik, however, illustrates that Islam was entering the Archipelago from multiple directions simultaneously — from the Indian Ocean trade routes that touched both Java and Sumatra. The later connection between North Sumatran Islam and Javanese Islam, exemplified by [[Soenan Goenoeng Djati]]'s presumed origin in [[Pasé]], suggests that the Sumatran sultanates built upon foundations that had been partly laid by missionaries like Malik Ibrahim.
