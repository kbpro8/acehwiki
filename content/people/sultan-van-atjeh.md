**The Sultan of Aceh** was the Acehnese sovereign to whom, in [[Snouck Hurgronje]]'s *De Atjehers* and in the chapter *Jagerslatijn* of *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* by Merac ([[1947]]), a manté married couple was brought captive in the time of his grandfather. The Sultan of Aceh is the book's — and Snouck Hurgronje's — marker of the precolonial Acehnese sovereign who received the manté couple and whose efforts to persuade them failed.

## The Manté Couple and the Sultan

"One informant knew to tell that, in the time of his grandfather, a 'manté' married couple was brought captive to the Sultan of Aceh. Despite all the efforts that were employed to persuade them, those forest-people refused to speak or to eat and finally died the voluntary death by starvation." One informant knew to tell that, in the time of his grandfather, a manté married couple was brought captive to the Sultan of Aceh. Despite all the efforts that were employed to persuade them, those forest-people refused to speak or to eat and finally died the voluntary death by starvation. The Sultan of Aceh — the precolonial Acehnese sovereign — is therefore the book's — and Snouck Hurgronje's — marker of the sovereign who received the manté couple and whose efforts to persuade them failed.

## The Sultan and the Sultan's Court

"In the period of greatest splendour of the Acehnese empire, the Sultans had a thousand elephants as riding, pack, and fighting animals." In the period of greatest splendour of the Acehnese empire, the Sultans had a thousand elephants as riding, pack, and fighting animals. The Sultan of Aceh — the precolonial Acehnese sovereign of the golden age — is the book's marker of the Sultan's court that had a thousand elephants and that preceded the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]] and the colonial state.

## Significance

The Sultan of Aceh is significant in *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* as the precolonial Acehnese sovereign who received the manté couple and whose Sultan's court had a thousand elephants. The book's argument — that the Sultan of Aceh was the precolonial sovereign of the golden age whose efforts to persuade the manté couple failed — is the book's marker of the precolonial Acehnese empire. The Sultan of Aceh is therefore the book's marker of the precolonial Acehnese sovereign.
