**J. H. C. Vastenou** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Onderneming tegen Soembawa (1908)]]

Swart made "the apparently more than reckless undertaking against Bima and Soembawa with a handful of *[[Corps of Military Constabulary of Atjeh and Dependencies|military constabulary]]* into a classic example of swift and conclusive pacification." By royal decree of [[2 September]] [[1909]] no. 41 he was promoted Commander of the [[Militaire Willemsorde]] for his distinction in the operations on Soembawa in that period. The *1ste luitenant* der *marechaussee* ("military constabulary") [[J. H. C. Vastenou]], Knight 4th Class of the M.W.O. and after his death entered in the registers as Knight 3rd Class, fell on Soembawa on [[19 February]] [[1908]]; his grave monument stands in the cemetery at [[Makassar]]. Rear-Admiral [[C. de Vos van Steenwijk|C. Baron de Vos van Steenwijk]] distinguished himself as *Luitenant ter Zee* on the expedition and was decorated with the M.W.O. 4th class.

### From [[Militaire Willemsorde]]

[[H. N. A. Swart]] was appointed Knight 4th Class by royal decree of [[30 August]] [[1891]] no. 2 for his conduct in [[Atjeh]] in [[1890]]; Knight 3rd Class by decree of [[13 July]] [[1900]] no. 29 for the operations of [[1899]]; and Commander by decree of [[2 September]] [[1909]] no. 41 for the [[Onderneming tegen Soembawa (1908)|Soembawa]] operations of [[1908]]. Many of his subordinates named in du Croo's book were knights of the order, several with the *Eeresabel*: [[H. J. Schmidt]], [[W. B. J. A. Scheepens]], [[F. Darlang]], [[Th. J. Veltman]], [[A. Geertsema Beckeringh]], [[G. F. B. Watrin]], [[J. J. Boreel]], [[A. H. C. Roelofsen]], [[F. P. A. van Gheel Gildemeester]], [[H. Behrens]], [[W. J. Mosselman]], [[M. Schouten]], [[C. A. Snell]], [[G. F. P. Gosenson]], [[G. K. W. F. de Vaynes van Brakell Buys]] and [[J. H. C. Vastenou]]. The sergeants [[Loeff]] and [[Roobaart]] earned it in the destruction of the band of [[Tgk. Léjman]] in [[1911]]. The Ambonese old soldier [[Jacob Lawalata]] celebrated thirty-five years as a knight in [[1937]].
