**Slooten** was the Dutch sergeant and brigade commander who killed [[Pang Nanggròë]] in the [[Paja Tjitjém]] on [[26 September]] [[1910]].

Ordered on [[24 September]] to look for Pang Nanggròë in the Bird Marsh, "de baas" marched the same day with his brigade through [[Peutèë]] and heard from an Acehnese woman that three of Pang Nanggròë's men were under the *[[meunasah]]* of *gampong* [[Aloeë]]. Though it was night and pouring with rain, Slooten pressed on into the forest to *gampong* [[Aloeë Awé]]. He gave his dead-tired brigade only a few hours' rest and resumed the hunt before dawn on [[25 September]], holding the tracks fast into the marsh and following them on [[26 September]] through mud and water sometimes up to the arms. At midday voices were heard; a *marechaussée* fell in the tangle of roots and the loud splash alarmed the camp; the brigade rushed forward and a fugitive already two hundred metres off was hit and fell — "en de gids zegt: Pang Nanggròë."
