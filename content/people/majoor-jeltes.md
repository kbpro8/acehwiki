**Major Jeltes** was a Dutch *majoor* of the [[Indische Leger|Indische leger]] who commanded the *midden-colonne* — the 12de bataljon, the remainder of the [[Bataljon Barissans van Madura|Barissans]] and two sections of artillery — on [[13 February]] [[1876]] in [[Generaal Pel|generaal Pel]]'s three-*colonne* operation against the [[III Moekims]], advancing from [[Kota Alam]] along the right bank of the [[Atjeh-rivier|Atjeh-rivier]] against the enemy-held [[Pango]] and [[Longbatta-Mesigit]].

## The Three-Colonne Operation against the III Moekims

Pel granted the troops only a couple of days' rest at [[Kota Radja]] after the conquest of Atoeh of [[3 February]] [[1876]] and the operations that had put the entire [[XXV Moekims]] in Dutch hands. On [[13 February]] [[1876]] he advanced in *oostelijke richting* with three *colonnes* against the III Moekims. The *rechter-colonne* under [[Majoor B. E. Mekern|B. E. Mekern]] — the 13de bataljon, part of the Barissans and one section of artillery — was to march *stroomopwaarts* along the left bank of the Atjeh-rivier. The *midden-colonne* under Jeltes was to advance from Kota Alam along the right bank against Pango and the Longbatta-Mesigit, the fortified stone mosque that had been in Dutch hands since [[31 December]] [[1874]] but was now again enemy-held. The *linker-colonne* under [[Majoor Diepenheim|Diepenheim]] — the *rechter halve bataljon* and a section of artillery — was to cover the flank of the *midden-colonne*.

## Outcome

On [[13 February]] [[1876]] [[Pango]] was conquered and a post *opgericht*. The kampong, lying opposite the Dutch post at [[Blang Tjoet]], became the *uiterste steunpunt* (outermost support point) of the new *linie* toward [[Kwala Gigieng]]. The same day, however, the [[Atoeh catastrophe (13 February 1876)|catastrophe at Atoeh]] overtook [[Jan van Swieten (Kapitein)|kapitein van Swieten]]'s small detachment on the *drassige sawah* near [[Lamsajoen]]. Pel pressed on. On [[14 February|14]]–[[15 February]] [[1876]] the troops conquered [[Meroe]], [[Lampermej]] and [[Tjap Octoe]] after *hevigen tegenstand*. At Tjap Octoe the troops *stuitten* on a sterke *benteng* with artillery, *krachtig verdedigd*, which was only taken "met veel moeite en opoffering." The losses were enough to make Pel decide not to push further eastward but to designate Pango as the *uiterste steunpunt* of the new *linie* — judged *voldoende*.

## Significance

Jeltes's *midden-colonne* was the principal thrust of the operation, and the conquest of Pango by his column gave the Dutch the eastern pivot they had sought since [[26 February]] [[1876]], when [[Luitenant-kolonel Engel|lt.-kol. Engel]] had reached Kwala Gigieng. The line Pango–Kwala Gigieng closed the eastern face of Pel's projected *afsluitingslinie* and was, in Borel's reading, the high-water mark of the *aanvallende* campaign before Pel's death ten days later.
