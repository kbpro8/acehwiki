**Teukoe Tjhi Peureula** was the *radja* and [[Uleebalang|oeléëbalang]] of the landscape [[Peureula|Peureula]] during the first war years [[1873]]-[[1875]], of Groot-Atjeh origin whose ancestor had settled in [[Peureula]] and acquired government in that land, which later became a *landschap* under Dutch rule.

## Ancestry and rule

According to [[Dr. R. Broersma]], his ancestor came from [[Groot-Atjeh]] to [[Peureula]], settled there and acquired rule, existing one and a half centuries compared with many later risen landscapes. He was an autocrat who would accept letters of recognition from the [[Sultan of Atjeh]] in [[Groot-Atjeh]] if necessary, but otherwise act according to own insight. Happy insight was to place himself on good footing with Dutch government. He possessed his war-force and his eldest son, [[Teukoe Tjhi Moeda Peusangan|teukoe moeda Peusangan]], was a warlike leader when needed against [[Idi]], but while noise of internecine Atjeh war-business was large, business itself was theatrical. Sharp, hellish, full death-contempt only became struggle when going against *kafir* demanding submission.
