**Commander Stavorinus** was a Dutch naval officer who commanded the bark “[[St Eustatius]]” during the December 19–25 expedition against rebel forces in the interior of [[Acheh]]. He was ordered to maintain a post at the plantation “[[Cornelia Jacoba]]” after the detachment of [[Captain‑lieutenant Smit]] had marched overland.

## Duties
Stavorinus’s bark provided fire support for land forces, patrolled the [[Wironje creek]], and helped secure communication lines between the riverine fleet and inland posts. His journal entries (as cited in the [[Journal of Haringman]]) note the lack of significant rebel activity at the time of his arrival.
