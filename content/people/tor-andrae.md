**Tor Andrae** (1885 – 1965) was a Swedish scholar of Islamic studies who authored *Die Person Muhammeds in lehre und glauben seiner Gemeinde* (Stockholm, 1918). The source mentions his analysis of the development of the *Burda*’s theological background, which informs contemporary Acehnese understandings of prophetic veneration.
