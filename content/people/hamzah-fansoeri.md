**Hamzah Fansoeri** (also rendered Hamzah Fansuri) is identified in the OCR text as one of the editors who supplied emendations to the Malay translation of the *Burda*. His contributions appear in footnotes such as “80) hs. manusjia; Ar. fPl‑djasam, in het vette der aarde. De tekestverbetering is ingegeven door Hamzah Fansoeri ed. J. Doorenbos bl. 46: lemah manis terlalu njaman.” These notes show his work in correcting archaic Malay spellings and providing Arabic glosses. Though little biographical detail is given in the source, his editorial role places him among the Malay scholars engaged in preserving and interpreting Islamic devotional texts during the early modern period.
