**Enoch** was a Menadonese Marechaussee of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen]] who served in the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]] of [[1910]]-[[1911]] as the *[[klewang]]* man — the soldier whose role, after the korporaal [[Nussy]] had shot, was to "werken af met de *klewang*" — to finish off with the klewang. Enoch's pairing with Nussy — the shot and the blade — is the book's small symbolic register of the colonne method's two-man *[[dardanellen|dardanel]]* system.

## The killing of Peutoeha Gam Masén (May 1911)

When *[[Peutoeha Gam Masén]]* was omsingeld near the [[Kroeëng Seukoeleh]] in [[May 1911]] and summoned to surrender, "hij trok zijn *[[reuntjong]]*, de Atjehse kris" and threw himself on the two voorste Marechaussees — the *[[dardanellen|dardanellen]]* of the luitenant. "*[[Nussy]]* schoot en Enoch werkte af met de *[[klewang]]*." The pairing — Nussy's shot and Enoch's blade — is the book's set-piece of the two-man *dardanel* system: the *dardanel* who shot first and the *dardanel* who finished with the klewang.

## The schuilplaats assault (May 1911)

In the overval on the schuilplaats of the *[[Teungkoe Sjech Saman di Tiri|Tiriō bende]]* later in May, Enoch was one of the seven who climbed the steile waterral under fire: "*[[Nussy]], [[Gomies]], [[Liklikwatil]], Enoch, [[Kamagi]], [[Paliaman]] en [[Oesoel]] klommen er tegenop*." The seven who climbed the waterral were the colonne's *beste mensen* — its best men — and Enoch was one of them.

## The killing of Tgk. Tjhi Majét (May 1911)

In the chase of the four vluchtelingen after the schuilplaats assault, Enoch shot two of them, including *[[Tgk. Tjhi Majét]]*. "Nog twee ervan door Enoch, waaronder ook Tgk. Tjhi Majét di Tiri." The corpse was carried to [[Tangsé]] and the bandages torn into hundreds of pieces as holy relics.

## Significance

Enoch is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as the *klewang* man of the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]] — the soldier whose role, after the korporaal [[Nussy]] had shot, was to "werken af met de *klewang*." The pairing of Nussy's shot and Enoch's blade is the book's small symbolic register of the colonne method's two-man *dardanel* system: the *dardanel* who shot first and the *dardanel* who finished with the klewang.
