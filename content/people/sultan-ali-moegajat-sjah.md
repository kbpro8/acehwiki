**Sultan Ali Moegajat Sjah** (early 16th century, before 1500 to c.1530) was a Second underking (stadhouder) of Atjeh under Pidië, then first great Sultan of unified Atjeh mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Second 'stadhouder' (underking/viceroy) over Atjeh under Pidië suzerainty.

## Role

Second underking (stadhouder) of [[Atjeh]] under [[Pidië]], then first great [[sultan|Sultan]] of unified [[Atjeh]]

## Dates

early [[16th century]], before [[1500]] to c.[[1530]]

## Account from Jongejans

Second 'stadhouder' (underking/viceroy) over [[Atjeh]] under [[Pidië]] [[Suzerainty question|suzerainty]]. Had vision and energy: set aside his father the stadhouder, conquered the neighboring kingdom of [[Daja]] on the [[Westkust]] to cover his rear, then conquered virtually the whole Noord- and [[Atjeh's Oostkust (East Coast of Aceh)|Oostkust]]. Founded [[Groot-Atjeh]] ([[Groot-Atjeh|Greater Aceh]]). His successors followed in his footsteps but could not extend further. The unified grip on [[Aceh]] was achieved and would persist.

## Significance

Sultan Ali Moegajat Sjah appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
