**Schiff** was the Dutch *resident* designated as one of the two original *gouvernements-commissaris* to be dispatched to [[Acheh|Atjeh]] in [[1873]], together with [[Van de Wall|Van de Wall]]. When Van de Wall fell mortally ill, [[I.D. Fransen van de Putte|the Minister]] expressed doubts about whether Schiff alone was sufficient for such a weighty mission. Schiff's *missive* confirmed that the purchase of a ship by the Atjehsche envoys at Singapore was genuine, contradicting [[Read (consul-generaal at Singapore)|Read]]'s belief that it was merely a fabrication, and identifying the broker Angelbeek and even naming the purchase price.
