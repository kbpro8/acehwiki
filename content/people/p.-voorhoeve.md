**Petrus Voorhoeve** (1899–1975) — published as **P. Voorhoeve** — was a Dutch orientalist and Malay scholar, the editor of the [[1955]] facsimile edition of the two Malay treatises of [[Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]] published as No. 16 of the *Uitgaven van de [[Stichting De Goeje]]*. His edition — *Twee Maleise geschriften van Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī: in facsimile uitgegeven met aantekeningen door P. Voorhoeve* — gave scholars, for the first time, a complete facsimile of the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān fī maʿrifat al-adyān*]] (from Leiden Cod. Or. 3291, the legacy of [[Herman Neubronner van der Tuuk|Van der Tuuk]]) and of the [[Hudjdjat as-siddiq li-daf az-zindiq|*Ḥujjat aṣ-ṣiddīq li-dafʿ az-zindīq*]] (from the Maxwell collection of the [[Royal Asiatic Society]]). Voorhoeve's introduction to the edition is itself a substantial contribution to Malay religious philology.

## The edition of Raniri's two Malay treatises

Voorhoeve's [[1955]] edition — published at [[Leiden]] by E. J. Brill as No. 16 of the *Uitgaven van de [[Stichting De Goeje]]* — is the standard edition of the two principal Malay polemical treatises of [[Raniri]]. The volume contains:

1. A substantial introduction (pp. 5–55) divided into three sections: §1 ("De auteur"), on the life and writings of Raniri; §2 ("Tibjān fī maʿrifat al-adyān"), on the content, sources, sects, and apparatus criticus of the *Tibjān*; and §3 ("Ḥujjat aṣ-ṣiddīq li-dafʿ az-zindīq"), on the content and notes of the *Ḥujjat*.
2. A facsimile of the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān*]] (pp. 1–128) from Leiden Cod. Or. 3291.
3. A facsimile of the [[Hudjdjat as-siddiq li-daf az-zindiq|*Ḥujjat*]] (pp. 1–27) from Maxwell collection no. 93 of the [[Royal Asiatic Society]].
4. An errata leaf.

## The identification of the sources of the *Tibjān*

Voorhoeve's most original contribution in the [[1955]] edition is the identification of the principal sources of the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān*]]. The examination of the sources had, until Voorhoeve, "delivered only the negative result that the *Tibjān* is not an excerpt from any of the well-known extensive works on religions and sects." Voorhoeve identifies four sources:

1. The [[Tamhid fi bayan at-tawhid|*at-Tamhīd fī bayān at-tawḥīd*]] of [[Abu Syakur as-Salimi|Abū Shakūr as-Sālimī]], an eleventh-century Persian Shāfiʿite treatise — the principal source, from which more than a quarter of the text of the *Tibjān* is translated literally.
2. The [[al-Insan al-Kamil|*al-Insān al-kāmil*]] of [[Abd al-Karim al-Jili|ʿAbd al-Karīm al-Jīlī]], the fourteenth-century Sufi cosmology — the source of Raniri's notice on the Barāhima.
3. The [[Ma'rifat al-madhahib|*Maʿrifat al-madhāhib*]], a popular schematic overview of the seventy-two Islamic sects — the source of Raniri's classification of the sects.
4. The *Kitāb fī bayān madhāhib al-mutaṣawwifa* of [[Najm ad-Din an-Nasafi|Najm ad-Dīn an-Nasafī]] — the source of the section on the heretical Ṣūfī sects.

The identification is supported by an exhaustive apparatus criticus in which the readings of the *Tibjān* are compared with those of the manuscripts of its sources — including, in the case of the *Tamhīd*, the very Leiden manuscript Cod. Or. 5664 that preserves the errors of Raniri's own exemplar.

## Other scholarly contributions

Voorhoeve's other contributions to Malay and Acehnese scholarship include his notes on Raniri in *BKI* 107 (1951), pp. 353 ff. — where he had advanced the now-withdrawn hypothesis that Raniri sojourned in [[Mecca]] before his departure for [[Acheh]] — and his earlier work in the cataloguing of Malay manuscripts. The [[1955]] edition of Raniri's two Malay treatises is his principal monument.

## Acknowledgments

In the introduction to the [[1955]] edition, Voorhoeve records his acknowledgments to a number of scholars: to [[G. W. J. Drewes|Drewes]] for the identification of *Śrāwaka* as Sanskrit for Buddhist; to Professor [[J. Schacht|J. Schacht]] for his guidance in the reading, explanation, and collation of the manuscripts of the *Tamhīd*; to [[C.A.O. van Nieuwenhuijze|C. A. O. van Nieuwenhuijze]], whose dissertation *Sjamsu'l-Dīn van Pasai* and study in *BKI* 104 had earlier treated the side of Raniri's work directed against the [[Wujudiyyah]]; to [[Ph. S. van Ronkel|Ph. S. van Ronkel]] for his earlier article on the *Tibjān* in *BKI* 102 (1944); to [[H. Djajadiningrat|H. Djajadiningrat]] for the chronological study of Raniri's Malay works in *TBG* 69; to [[Sir R. O. Winstedt|Sir R. O. Winstedt]] for help in the study of the Maxwell manuscript; to the [[Royal Asiatic Society]] for permission to reproduce the text of the *Ḥujjat*; to Dr. **R. Roolvink** for the recovery of the Arabic manuscript of the *Ḥujjat* (KBG 420 Mal.); and to **H. T. Damsté** for the summary of [[Teungku Haron|Teungku Harõn]]'s Achehnese adaptation of the *Tibjān*.
