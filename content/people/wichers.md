**Wichers** was a Dutch *luitenant ter zee 2de klasse* (lieutenant of the navy, second class) who, in [[1893]] — the despatch being reported in *Het Vaderland* and quoted by [[W. A. Coblijn|Coblijn]] in the naschrift of *[[Hoe Atjeh te onderwerpen (Coblijn, 1893)|Hoe Atjeh te onderwerpen]]* — commanded the joint sloop flotilla of the *Benkoelen* in an engagement in the Riouw bay at [[Leuhong|Lehong]] on the West Coast of [[Atjeh]]. Under his leadership the flotilla had chased a *prauw* (native vessel) belonging to [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Oemar]] and attempted to bring her up from the strand; the landing division was fired upon from the shrub, whereupon the fire was silenced with the support of the guns of two sloops, and the prauw was shot to pieces. The [[Sloops of the Benkoelen in action at Lehong (1893)|affair at Lehong]] was cited in the press discussion of the same months in which the radja of Pasangan, on the East Coast, was rewarded with fifty Martini-Henry rifles, ammunition, and tin for fishing-nets for having hoisted the Dutch flag and returned the *staatjes* (small states) to the princes expelled by Toekoe Oemar — a conjunction that Van Heutsz's brochure denounced and Coblijn quoted.
