**Henri Nicolas Alfred Swart** was a Dutch military officer, Civil and Military Governor of Aceh, and Vice-President of the Council of the Indies (*Raad van Indië*).

## Military Career and Service in Aceh

As Governor of Aceh, Swart oversaw extensive post-expedition consolidation, constructing public roads, bridges, and infrastructure across Aceh while utilizing the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]] to maintain security across inland divisions.
