**Schout-bijnaacht Van Kinsbergen** was a Dutch magistrate who participated in the 1788‑1792 discussions on forming a permanent corps of ship artillerymen for the [[Oost-Indische Compagnie]].

## Role in Military Planning
Together with [[Van der Hoop]] and [[Vice-admiraal Reynst]], Van Kinsbergen helped draft proposals for a 140‑man artillery corps, but the plans were abandoned because of budgetary concerns.
