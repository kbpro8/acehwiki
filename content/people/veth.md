tags: [person, western-scholar]

Author of "Atchin," a work on Acheh, cited by Hurgronje for historical data on Sultan Iskandar Muda's attempt to drive the Portuguese from Malacca and on the [[1699]] abolition of the line of female sovereigns and the dynastic wars that followed.
