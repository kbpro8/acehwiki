**J. van Hasselt** was a Dutch 1st lieutenant of the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Netherlands Indies Army]] who commanded the part of the garrison that remained in the *[[Benting|benting]]* [[Anak Galoeng]] on [[7 March]] [[1896]] while Captain [[H. F. T. van Blokland|Van Blokland]] took the patrol out. When the patrol was pinned down he at once left the *benting* with reinforcement to help the column; scarcely arrived on the ground, he was wounded by a shot in the hand, *maar dit belette hem niet zijn dapperen te blijven aanvoeren* — but this did not prevent him from continuing to lead his brave men.
