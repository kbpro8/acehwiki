**O. van Rees** was a Dutch colonial administrator and Governor-General of the Netherlands East Indies who oversaw executive policy and military budgeting during the continuation of the Aceh War in the mid-1870s.

## Administration During Pel's Campaigns

Serving as Governor-General in [[Batavia]], [[O. van Rees]] coordinated executive support and financial allocations for General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]] during [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]] in [[1875]] and [[1876]]. 

As discussed in *Atjeh gedurende de tweede expeditie en later* ([[1876]]), the administration of [[O. van Rees]] managed the recruitment of forced convict laborers (*dwangarbeiders*) ([[Forced Labor in Aceh Military Campaigns]]) and authorized the deployment of reinforcement battalions, including the [[12th Infantry Battalion]] and [[13th Infantry Battalion]], to sustain interior offensive operations against [[Panglima Polem]].
