**J. M. P. Kluit** was a **[[lieutenant of the sea|luitenant ter zee]]** (2nd class) who commanded the marine landing division during the **[[Edi River expedition (1890)]]**. Under his leadership, a force of roughly one hundred men disembarked from the warship **[[Hendrik der Nederlanden (ship)|Hendrik der Nederlanden]]** at **[[Edi River|Edi]]** on **[[8 March]] [[1890]]**.  

He is noted for his decisive action during the June 1890 assaults on the left bank of the **[[Edi River|Edi]]**, where his troops captured enemy positions and repelled a **[[klewang]]**‑armed Acehnese counter‑attack.  

## Notable Actions  

- **[[8 March]] [[1890]]**: Led the initial landing at **[[Edi River|Edi]]**.  
- **[[June]] [[1890]]**: Coordinated the capture of fortified positions on the left bank of the **[[Edi River|Edi]]**.
