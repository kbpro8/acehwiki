---
title: Jamishah
tags: [tokoh, karya-sastra]
---

King of Aramiah, father of Banta Beuransah and his two elder brothers, whose name corrupts the Persian Jamshid. He dreams of the princess Ruhon Apenlah and sends his three sons to find her, setting the Hikayat Banta Beuransah in motion.
