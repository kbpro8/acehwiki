**Fortuyn**, sometimes referred to as **Goed Fortuyn**, was a rebel commander mentioned among the leaders whose outposts (**Quacco**, **Baubé**, **Fortuyn**) were reclaimed by Dutch forces during the **[[1760s]]** campaign in the **Wikkie‑kreek** area. His exact role beyond the mention is not detailed in the source.

## Context
Fortuyn’s inclusion among the rebel chiefs underscores the fragmented nature of the resistance against Dutch colonial authority.
