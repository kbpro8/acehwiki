The son of Malem Diwa and Putroe Bungsu, who developed vicious tendencies as a child but found his mother's hidden flying garment, enabling her escape to the sky realm. He later fought alongside his father, became a sub-king of the aerial realm, and married Janagaru, daughter of the Raja Muda of that kingdom.
