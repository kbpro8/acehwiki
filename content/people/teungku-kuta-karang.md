---

title: Teungku Kuta Karang
aliases: [Shaikh Abbas]
tags: [person, scholar, warrior]
---

The greatest rival of Teungku Tiro and one of the most important religious-political figures of Acheh's resistance to the Dutch, real name Shaikh Abbas. He wrote the Taj-ul-mulk on Islamic mystic arts at the instance of Sultan Manso Shah, and the Tadkirat ar-rakidin, in which he proposed fitting out a fleet of warships and was the first to conceive of placing bombs beneath Dutch railway lines. Unlike Teungku Tiro, he permitted his followers contact with Dutch-controlled territory to gather intelligence.
