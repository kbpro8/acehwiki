**Buyskes, Schout-bij-nacht** was a senior Dutch naval officer who, together with the corvette [[Priss Frederik]], provided support to the Dutch landing forces on [[Saparoea]] during the [[17 May]] [[1817]] uprising. His involvement is noted in the account of the failed expedition led by [[Majoor Beetjes]] (page 92).

## Naval Support

Buyskes coordinated the deployment of naval gunfire that helped the Dutch troops hold the fort at [[Duurstede]] after the initial landing by [[Ellinghuyzen]].
