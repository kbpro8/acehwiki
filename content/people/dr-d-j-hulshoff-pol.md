**Dr D. J. Hulshoff Pol** was the Dutch medical officer to whom [[P. de Roo de la Faille|De Roo de la Faille]] dedicated *[[Historische Curiositeiten uit Malajoe en Java]]* (Den Haag, [[March]] [[1954]]), and whose early career — as an Officier van Gezondheid (Medical Officer) at [[Tandjoeng Poera|Tandjoeng Poera]] with the [[K.N.I.L.|K.N.I.L. Leger]] — gave him a personal acquaintance with the [[Aroe]] country that the book places at the centre of early Malay history. De Roo de la Faille's dedication frames Hulshoff Pol as the ideal reader of the book — a man with "open ear for the chatter of the earthly parrot Bajan Boediman", i.e. for the *bedekte taal* of Malay-Javanese tradition, and with first-hand knowledge of the very east-coast landscape the book seeks to recover.

## Dedication of the *Curiositeiten*

The dedication of *[[Historische Curiositeiten uit Malajoe en Java]]* opens: "Aan Dr D. J. Hulshoff Pol, die, tot mijn groote blijdschap open oor hebbend voor het gesnap van het aardige papegaaitje Bajan Boediman, mij aanmoedigde tot het voortzetten van de publicatie daarvan" — i.e. "To Dr D. J. Hulshoff Pol, who, to my great joy, having an open ear for the chatter of the pleasant little parrot Bajan Boediman, encouraged me to continue the publication thereof". The "Bajan Boediman" is the Speaking Parrot of Malay folk tradition — the figure whose chatter conceals, in *bedekte taal* form, the older sacred history of the [[Malay Archipelago]].

## Hulshoff Pol and Tandjoeng Poera

Hulshoff Pol began his career as Officier van Gezondheid at [[Tandjoeng Poera|Tandjoeng Poera]] — the very east-coast polity whose identification with [[Sjahroe'n Noewi]] of the [[Sadjarah Malajoe]] is the central claim of De Roo de la Faille's chapter *Over Sjahroe'n Noewi*. The dedication thus frames Hulshoff Pol as the man whose first-hand knowledge of the [[Aroe|Aru]] country gave him the standing to judge De Roo de la Faille's *bedekte taal* identifications — and whose encouragement sustained the publication of the book.

## Significance

For AcehWiki, Dr D. J. Hulshoff Pol's significance is as the dedicatee and ideal reader of *[[Historische Curiositeiten uit Malajoe en Java]]* — the figure whose medical-officer's acquaintance with [[Tandjoeng Poera|Tandjoeng Poera]] and the [[Aroe|Aru]] country gave him the standing to encourage and judge De Roo de la Faille's *bedekte taal* reading. His career at [[Tandjoeng Poera|Tandjoeng Poera]] also makes him the principal Dutch colonial witness to the survival of the east-coast place-world that the [[Atjeh-Aroe War (1539)|Atjehnese conquest of Aroe]] had overwritten.
