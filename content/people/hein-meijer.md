**Hein Meijer** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[atjeh|Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[h-n-a-swart|H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[meergebied|Meergebied]]

Improvement began only in [[1911]], "onder 'Hein' [[Hein Meijer|Meijer]]'s krachtige marechaussee-hand." In January the influence of [[tgk.-di-barat|Tgk. di Barat]] still made itself felt; the bands showed heightened activity, the Telongbende fired on patrols, and there was another *[[Klewang|klewang]]* attack at [[linggö|Linggö]]. But at last the Telongbende received the blow long intended for it: [[jenae|Jenae]] with a patrol of the 5th Division struck "a blow such as few have been struck" — the entire band of [[tgk.-léjman|Tgk. Léjman]], successor of [[tgk.-di-tjòt-tjitjém|Tgk. di Tjòt Tjitjém]], was rendered harmless, thirty-six men falling and fourteen rifles being taken; the European sergeants [[loeff|Loeff]] and [[roobaart|Roobaart]] earned the [[militaire-willemsorde|Militaire Willemsorde]].

### From [[meergebied|Meergebied]]

Swart's inspection of the Meergebied under [[hein-meijer|Hein Meijer]] produced one of the anecdotes du Croo collected: crossing the lake by motorboat from Takèngön to Bintang with the European fusilier [[boonstra|Boonstra]] at the wheel, Swart timed the engine for a minute and announced "that engine is making seventy revolutions a minute." — "Tachtig, generaal," said the engineman promptly. — "Well I'll be blowed! Would you contradict me? Did I not count them myself?" — "Quite so, General, but then your minutes are shorter than mine." — "Hm," said Swart.

### From Takèngön

Formerly the mountain region "where the bands found good and safe hiding-places everywhere," Takèngön became the seat of the sub-division. [[seureulo|Seureulo]], where [[tgk.-di-barat|Tgk. di Barat]] hid and where a great meeting of the resistance party was held and dispersed in January [[1911]], lay in the Meergebied, "thans onderafd. Takengon". In April [[1914]] Takèngön reported the killing of [[pang-intan|Pang Intan]] with four followers at the [[waih-ni-wis|Waih ni Wis]]. [[h-n-a-swart|H. N. A. Swart]] crossed the lake by motorboat from Takèngön to Bintang with [[hein-meijer|Hein Meijer]] on the inspection that produced the anecdote of the engine revolutions.

## In Brooshooft (1886)

Brooshooft lists H. F. [[Hein Meijer|Meijer]]'s *Atjeh van 26 December 1875 tot 4 September 1876* (Breda: P. B. Nieuwenhuijs) among his principal secondary sources, drawing on it for the period between Pel's death and the assumption of the offensive by [[General van der Heijden|Van der Heijden]].
