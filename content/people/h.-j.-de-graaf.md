**H. J. de Graaf** — Dr. Hermanus Johannes de Graaf — was the editor of the [[1955]] second wholly revised edition of *[[Nederlanders over de Zeeën (De Graaf et al., 1955)|Nederlanders over de Zeeën: 350 Jaar geschiedenis van Nederland buitengaats]]* and the author of the chapters on the pre-Company Indies (II.1), the VOC (II.2-6), the transition from Company to Government (III), the war with Japan (V), and the Indies after the Japanese surrender (VI). His narrative is the principal source of the present Aceh Wiki History articles derived from *Nederlanders over de Zeeën*.

## Authorship of the Atjeh-relevant chapters

In the [[1955]] edition of *Nederlanders over de Zeeën*, [[H. J. de Graaf|De Graaf]] was responsible for the chapters covering:
- *Azië voor de komst der Nederlanders* (Asia before the coming of the Dutch) — Chapter II.1, which situates sixteenth-century [[Acheh|Atjeh]] in the wider context of Muslim-state formation in the archipelago.
- *De Geschiedenis der Nederlandse Oost-Indische Compagnie* (The History of the Dutch East India Company) — Chapters II.2-6, which cover Atjeh's trade with [[Gujerat|Gujerat]] and [[Suratte|Suratte]] and the VOC's conflict with Atjeh on Sumatra's Westkust.
- *De overgang van Compagnie tot Gouvernement* (The transition from Company to Government) — Chapter III.
- *De oorlog met Japan en de Japanse bezetting van Nederlands-Indië* (The war with Japan and the Japanese occupation of the Netherlands Indies) — Chapter V.
- *Nederlands-Indië na de Japanse overgave* (The Netherlands Indies after the Japanese surrender) — Chapter VI, which records the [[1948]] Atjeh exemption from the second *politionele actie*.

## The collaborative volume

The [[1955]] edition of *Nederlanders over de Zeeën* was a collaborative work. [[H. J. de Graaf|De Graaf]] was the editor; the chapter on the Indies under the State and as part of the Kingdom (Chapter IV) — the principal source of the present Atjeh-related articles on the nineteenth-century colonial state — was contributed by [[G. Gonggrijp|Prof. G. Gonggrijp]]. The chapter on South Africa in the Dutch period (Chapter VII) was contributed by [[P. J. van Winter|Prof. Jhr Dr P. J. van Winter]]. [[W. R. Menkman|W. R. Menkman]] contributed the chapter on the Netherlands in America and West Africa (Chapter I).

## Significance

[[H. J. de Graaf|De Graaf]]'s significance for the encyclopedia of [[Acheh]] history lies in his editorship of the [[1955]] *Nederlanders over de Zeeën* — the source from which the present Aceh Wiki articles on the pre-Company Indies, the VOC period, the Japanese occupation, and the Indonesian revolution are derived. His narrative situates [[Acheh|Atjeh]] in the wider 350-year history of Dutch maritime enterprise.
