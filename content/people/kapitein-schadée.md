**Kapitein Schadée** was a Dutch officer of the [[Oost-Indisch Leger|Netherlands East Indies Army]] who served in the outposts of [[Groot-Atjeh]] during the alliance period and who led the patrol from [[Biloel]] on the afternoon of [[29 March]] [[1896]] — the first Dutch reconnaissance after the defection of [[Teukoe Oemar]] — which established that the post was surrounded.

## At Biloel and Kroeng Gloempang

Hooijer identified Schadée among the officers photographed on the bench at [[Biloel]], together with [[Officier Dijkstra|Dijkstra]], [[Luitenant Zon|Zon]] (who later fell), [[Luitenant Zijdveld|Zijdveld]], [[Hendrikus Colijn|Colyn]], [[Kapitein Engelbert van Bevervoorde]], [[Kapitein Van der Wedden|Van der Wedden]], the lieutenants [[Luitenant Van Delden|Van Delden]] and [[Luitenant Kastens|Kastens]], and the medical officers [[Officier van gezondheid Nanta|Nanta]] and [[Officier van gezondheid Tromp|Tromp]]. He was also identified on the plate of the interior of [[Kroeng Gloempang]], where he appears at the left of the passage between the barracks named *Heerengracht* by the garrison, [[Kapitein Van der Wedden|Van der Wedden]] being at the right.

## The patrol of 29 March 1896

On the morning of [[29 March]] [[1896]] nobody appeared at the *[[Passer|passer]]* under the great tree at [[Biloel]] — not even the police *oppasser* — where [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]] was to have assembled a thousand warriors and a thousand coolies for the action against [[Lamkrak]]; the nearby *[[Blokhuis|blokhuizen]]* held by friendly Achehnese were found abandoned. Towards half past four in the afternoon Kapitein Schadée went with a patrol of sixty men towards [[Tjot Goeë]] to satisfy himself of the state of affairs. When the point had pushed some three hundred paces ahead, beyond the covered ground surrounding Biloel on the north side, it received heavy fire from every side across the flooded rice-fields. [[Luitenant Zijdveld]], struck by a bullet in the lower jaw, was compelled to fall back, and from that moment [[Biloel]] remained surrounded by the enemy.
