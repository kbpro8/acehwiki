**Lieutenant-Colonel Engel** was a Dutch *luitenant-kolonel* of the [[Indische Leger|Indische leger]] who commanded the column that advanced along the right bank of the [[Kroeng Daroe]] on [[26 December]] [[1875]] and occupied [[Pakan Badak]] on [[28 December]] [[1875]] in [[Generaal Pel|generaal Pel]]'s [[Pel's offensive against the VI, IV and IX Moekims (1875–1876)|great offensive against the VI, IV and IX Moekims]], who arrived at [[Lamjoeng]] with the 9de bataljon to reinforce the *ageerende troepen* before Pel's death, and who as the oldest *hoofdofficier* in Atjeh assumed *tijdelijk oppergezag* after [[Death of Generaal Pel (24 February 1876)|Pel's death at Lamjoeng]] on [[24 February]] [[1876]] and continued the march to [[Kwala Gigieng]], reached on [[26 February]] [[1876]].

## The December 1875 Offensive

On [[26 December]] [[1875]] Pel launched the great *drie-colonne* offensive against the VI, IV and IX Moekims. Engel advanced along the right bank of the Kroeng Daroe while [[Majoor Vetter|Vetter]] *bezette* [[Mibouw]] and [[Majoor F. J. E. Mekern|F. J. E. Mekern]] took the stelling between Mibouw and the Kroeng Daroe — and was *doodelijk gewond* the same day. On [[28 December]] [[1875]] Engel *bezette* Pakan Badak; the kampongs Lamtermin and Bital were *ontruimd* by the enemy, as were [[Toekoe Nanta]]'s *geduchte versterkingen* west of [[Sinangri]]. "Nergends hadden de Atjehneezen ernstigen tegenstand geboden," Borel records. Communication with Sinangri was opened in early January [[1876]]. The column then proceeded with the conquest of [[Lamtengah]] ([[8 January]] [[1876]]) and with the submission of [[Lampagger]] ([[10 January]] [[1876]]).

## The Lambaroe orders, March 1876 (Meijer 1883)

The official reports cited by [[Kolonel Meijer|H. F. Meijer]] in *[[Atjeh van 26 December 1875 tot 4 September 1876]]* ([[1883]]) preserve Engel's exact dispositions as acting commander after Pel's death. When the besieged column of Major [[Majoor B. E. Mekern|B. E. Mekern]] at Kajoe-Lē smuggled its report to his headquarters through the [[Bugis couriers from Kajoe-Lē (6 March 1876)|two Bugis couriers]] on 6 March [[1876]], Engel, then with the operating main force at [[Kwala Gigieng|Koewala Gigiëng]], answered at once: *"1 company of the 2nd battalion barisan to be joined to the 8th. The 5th battalion with a section of Artillery... shall advance immediately to Longbatah-Mesigit... Lieutenant-Colonel Meijer commander of the whole... Communication to be restored, post at Lambaroe to be established."* He added that he would come himself *"probably at once with 2 companies barisan; tomorrow the rest of the 12th battalion and the barisan come."* The [[Storming of Lambaroe (7 March 1876)|storming of Lambaroe]] the next day restored the line.

Engel arrived at Lamjoeng with the 9de bataljon to reinforce the *ageerende troepen* at the moment of Pel's sudden death from a *slagaderbreuk* in the night of [[24 February]] [[1876]]. As the oldest *hoofdofficier* in Atjeh he assumed *tijdelijk oppergezag* — temporary supreme command. He immediately took the required measures and continued the march via Tjade to Kwala Gigieng, which was reached on [[26 February]] [[1876]] and where a *bivouak* was *opgeslagen*. Posts were *opgericht* at Kwala Gigieng and on the island of [[Kota Pohama]] in the [[Gigieng lagune|Gigieng lagune]], to completely seal the *gemeenschap* between the sea and the mouth of the [[Kroeng Tjoet]] — the contraband supply channel that had given the Atjehneezen access to the [[XXVI Moekims]].

## Significance

Engel's assumption of command after Pel's death preserved the continuity of the offensive in its final, most critical days. His arrival at Kwala Gigieng closed the north-eastern end of Pel's projected *afsluitingslinie*, and the post on Kota Pohama sealed the Kroeng Tjoet mouth. He handed over to [[Generaal Wiggers van Kerchem|generaal Wiggers van Kerchem]] on [[10 March]] [[1876]], when the *linie* Kroeng Raba–Kwala Gigieng — the line whose closure had been Pel's secret plan — was at its furthest extent, and was then allowed by Wiggers to remain "nagenoeg onveranderd."
