**J.P. van Lier** was a Captain in the [[3e Bataljon Infanterie]] who commanded the vanguard during the [[First Conquest of the Great Missigit]] on [[10 April]] [[1873]].

## Military Exploits
Van Lier played a crucial role during the first assault on [[Atjeh's Groote Missigit]]. His troops were the first to breach the walls. When multiple attempts by his soldiers to climb the [[Stormladders]] failed or stalled, Van Lier personally scaled a ladder to lead by example. As he peered over the wall, he sustained a severe klewang (sword) or stone wound to his forehead, bleeding profusely. He was carried down by his men. Despite his wounds, he requested that his unit be the last to execute the [[Retreat from the Missigit]], marching back with immense pride and receiving a musical salute ordered by [[J.H.R. Köhler]].
