**Intjah** was a First wife of Amat Leupon; deaf-mute mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Deaf-mute woman married to Amat Leupon with bruidschat of one tahil (~f 40.-).

## Role

First wife of [[Amat Leupon]]; deaf-mute

## Account from Jongejans

Deaf-mute woman married to [[Amat Leupon]] with [[Bruidschat|bruidschat]] of one [[Tayl|tahil]] (~f 40.-). Mother of his two daughters. Repudiated by [[Banta Ahmat|Amat]] on accusation of infidelity. Later divorced her second husband.

## Significance

Intjah appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
