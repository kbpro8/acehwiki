**Teukoe Bin Peukan** was a Major resistance leader from the Pidië-vallei mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. One of the principal resistance leaders from the Pidië valley.

## Role

Major [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] leader from the [[Pidië]]-vallei

## Account from Jongejans

One of the principal [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] leaders from the [[Pidië]] valley. Surrendered with his entire band after the [[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]]'s and [[Panglima Polém]]'s submissions; within two months delivered 450 muzzle-loading guns and donderbussen.

## Significance

Teukoe Bin Peukan appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
