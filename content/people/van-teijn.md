**Van Teijn** — properly General Van Teijn — was a [[Netherlands|Dutch]] military officer who served as Governor of [[Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] in succession to [[Demmenie]] in the [[1880s]]. He is named in the [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] as the governor under whom the *concentratie-stelsel* continued to be applied and as the governor who *ging oorlogen op de Oostkust* — *went warring on the East Coast* — while his contemporary [[Deykerhoff]] took [[Toekoe Oemar]] under his arm.

## The governorship

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that the succession of governors of Atjeh — *Pel, Wichers van Kerchem, Diemont, Van der Heyden, Pruys van der Hoeven, Demmenie, Van Teijn, Deykerhoff* — represented a succession of *stelsels* (systems) that the Dutch had applied in Atjeh. The album's account places Van Teijn as the successor to Demmenie and the predecessor of Deykerhoff, and it identifies his principal military undertaking as the *oorlogen op de Oostkust* — the warring on the East Coast. The album does not specify the engagements of the East Coast campaigns, but the reference places Van Teijn as the governor under whom the Dutch extended their operations beyond the *geconcentreerde linie* of [[Groot-Atjeh]] to the [[Noord- en Oostkust]] of Atjeh.

## The Lamgoet campaign

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that [[Lamgoet]] — the former seat of [[Teungku di Tiro]] — *den generaals Demmenie en Van Teijn zoo lastig* (so troubled the generals Demmenie and Van Teijn). The album's reference places Van Teijn — together with Demmenie — as the governor under whom the Dutch were unable to dislodge the resistance from Lamgoet, and it identifies the Lamgoet campaign as one of the principal failures of the *concentratie* period.

## The Kota Toean Koe conflict

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that the *Moslemin* — the religious-warrior bands — had, during the *concentratie* period, constructed *versterkingen* (fortifications) from which they *bestookten* (bombarded) the Dutch posts with *artillerievuur* (artillery fire), and that against these Van Teijn — *voor Kota Toean Koe* — had *het hoofd gestooten* (butted his head — i.e., had been unsuccessful). The album's reference places the Kota Toean Koe conflict as one of the principal engagements of Van Teijn's governorship, and it identifies the *artillerievuur* of the *Moslemin* as one of the principal tactical novelties of the late *concentratie* period.

## The repair of the ceintuurbahn

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that the *Moslemin* *verontrustten onze ceintuurbahn zoo zeer, dat het noodig was een gedeelte daarvan buiten gebruik te stellen en een nieuwe straallijn aan te leggen* — *so troubled our belt-line that it was necessary to take part of it out of use and to lay a new radial line*. The reference is to the [[Atjeh-tram]] — the military tramway that ran along the *geconcentreerde linie* — and it identifies Van Teijn's governorship as the period in which the *ceintuurbahn* was first damaged by *Moslemin* action and first required the construction of a *straallijn* (radial line) as a bypass.

## Significance

Van Teijn's significance in the documentary record of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] is as the governor under whom the *concentratie-stelsel* was continued, the East Coast campaigns were undertaken, and the *ceintuurbahn* was first damaged by Atjehnese action. His appearance in the [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] is brief, but it identifies the principal engagements of his governorship and the principal failures of the *concentratie* period — failures that would, in the album's reading, ultimately produce the [[Toekoe Oemar's defection (1896)|1896 defection]] of [[Toekoe Oemar]] and the collapse of the *concentratie-stelsel*.
