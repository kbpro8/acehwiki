**Koesen Tjokrosentono** was an Indonesian civil servant who served as the acting head of the Information Service (*wnd. hoofd der voorlichting*) in [[Atjeh|Atjeh]] during the early 1950s, before being promoted in March [[1950]] to head of General Affairs (*hoofd Alg. Zaken*) of the Provisional Service (*Voorl. dienst*) of North Sumatra (*Nd.-Sumatra*).

## Career

Koesen Tjokrosentono's career illustrates the administrative reorganisation that followed the dissolution of the [[Negara Sumatera Timur]] (N.S.T.) on 15 August [[1950]] and the formation of the autonomous province of North Sumatra, which encompassed the pre-war residencies of [[Atjeh]], [[Tapanuli]], and East Sumatra. His transfer from the Atjeh information service to the provincial-level General Affairs department in Medan reflected the centralisation of administrative functions in the new provincial structure.

His appointment was made by the governor of North Sumatra (*gouv. v. Nd.-Sumatra*) by decision of 7 March [[1950]]. In the same month, [[Jusuf Abdullah Puar]], a senior official (*hoofdambtenaar*) at the Provisional Service, was appointed head of the Publicity Department (*afd. Publiciteit*) of North Sumatra.
