**J. H. Zeeman** was a **Lieutenant ter zee 2e klasse** who was wounded during the **[[30 March]] [[1893]]** fighting on the **Tamiang** river. He sustained injuries alongside seven other marines.

## Service Record

His wound is recorded in the Dutch naval yearbook of **[[1893]]**, reflecting the heavy combat experienced by junior officers.
