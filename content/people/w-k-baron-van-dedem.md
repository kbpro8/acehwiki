**W. K. Baron van Dedem** — *Mr. W. K. Baron van Dedem* — was the Dutch Minister of Colonies (*Minister van Koloniën*) who, in his *memorie van antwoord* (memorandum of reply) on the Indies budget of [[1892]], declared to the Tweede Kamer (Second Chamber of the States-General) that he expected no good from "toegeven en zachtheid" — yielding and softness — toward the "fanatieken Atjeher" (fanatical Atjehnese), and that he would pursue a firm policy in the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. He is recorded in [[Dr. W. A. Terwogt|Terwogt]]'s *[[Het Land van Jan Pieterszoon Coen (Terwogt)|Het Land van Jan Pieterszoon Coen]]* — a work closed at the end of [[1891]] — as the figure in whom the author places his hope for the conclusion of the Atjeh-oorlog.

## The memorie van antwoord of 1892

The [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] had, by the end of [[1891]], continued "onverzwakt" (unabated) for nearly two decades since the [[Tweede Atjeh-expeditie (1873-1874)|second expedition]] of [[1873]]–[[1874]], thanks — in [[Dr. W. A. Terwogt|Terwogt]]'s judgment — to the "wisselende houding" (wavering attitude) of the Dutch government, which sought salvation now in a "krachtdadig" (vigorous) and now in a "verzoenend" (conciliatory) approach to the Atjehnese, who "slechts voor overmacht bukkende" (bowed only before superior force). The appointment of Van Dedem as Minister of Colonies was, for [[Dr. W. A. Terwogt|Terwogt]], a hopeful sign: Van Dedem had, in his *memorie van antwoord* on the Indies budget of [[1892]], "onomwonden" (unambiguously) declared to the Tweede Kamer that he expected no good from "toegeven en zachtheid tegenover den fanatieken Atjeher" (yielding and softness toward the fanatical Atjehnese).

[[Dr. W. A. Terwogt|Terwogt]]'s closing judgment — "Wij hebben goed vertrouwen dat voor het taaie geduld van den Hollander en de zich nooit verloochenende dapperheid van ons leger, eindelijk ook Atjeh het hoofd in den schoot zal leggen" (we have good confidence that, for the stubborn patience of the Dutchman and the never-disowning bravery of our army, finally also Atjeh will lay its head in its lap — i.e., submit) — is thus explicitly tied to the firm policy announced by Van Dedem. The hope expressed in the closing pages of *Het Land van Jan Pieterszoon Coen* is, in this sense, the hope placed in the new Minister of Colonies.

## Significance

For AcehWiki, W. K. Baron van Dedem is significant as the Minister of Colonies whose [[1892]] declaration of a firm policy toward the Atjehnese — "geen heil van toegeven en zachtheid" (no good from yielding and softness) — is recorded by [[Dr. W. A. Terwogt|Terwogt]] as the basis of the author's hope for the conclusion of the Atjeh-oorlog. The declaration marks a turn in the Dutch political posture toward the war — from the "wisselende houding" (wavering attitude) that [[Dr. W. A. Terwogt|Terwogt]] blames for the protraction of the war, to the firm policy that would, within a few years, be embodied in the governorship of [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] and the pacification of Atjeh. Van Dedem's declaration is thus the political prelude to the final phase of the Atjeh-oorlog.
