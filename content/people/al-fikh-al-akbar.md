**The *al-Fiqh al-akbar*** ("The Great Fiqh") is one of the principal texts of early Sunni *kalām*, attributed to [[Abu Hanifa|Abū Ḥanīfa]] (d. 150 H / 767 C.E.). The tract is named by [[P. Voorhoeve|Voorhoeve]] in the introduction to the [[1955]] edition of the two Malay treatises of [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]] as the source of one of the additional sects that the *[[Ma'rifat al-madhahib|Maʿrifat al-madhāhib*]] adds, in some manuscripts, to the seventy-two Islamic sects: the *Jismiyya* or *Mujassimiyya*.

## The addition to the *Maʿrifat al-madhāhib*

The *[[Ma'rifat al-madhahib|Maʿrifat al-madhāhib*]] — the schematic overview of the seventy-two Islamic sects that is one of the principal sources of the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān*]] of [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|Raniri]] — adds, in some manuscripts, one further sect derived from the *al-Fiqh al-akbar* of [[Abu Hanifa|Abū Ḥanīfa]]: the *Jismiyya* or *Mujassimiyya* — those who attribute a body (*jism*) to God. The addition is recorded by Voorhoeve in the introduction to the [[1955]] edition: "after the seventy-two sects, some manuscripts add seven further sects drawn from a work of [[Abu'l-Kasim ar-Razi|Abū'l-Qāsim ar-Rāzī]] — a mystic who died in 378 H — and one sect drawn from the *al-Fiqh al-akbar* of Abū Ḥanīfa: the *Jismiyya* or *Mujassimiyya*."

## Significance

The *al-Fiqh al-akbar* is one of the principal texts of early Sunni *kalām* and one of the sources of the additional sects that the *[[Ma'rifat al-madhahib|Maʿrifat al-madhāhib*]] adds, in some manuscripts, to the seventy-two Islamic sects. Its attribution to [[Abu Hanifa|Abū Ḥanīfa]] makes it one of the earliest authorities cited in the textual tradition of the schematic classification of Muslim heresies — a tradition that was carried, through the *Maʿrifat al-madhāhib*, to the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān*]] of Raniri and so to the Malay-Indonesian world.
