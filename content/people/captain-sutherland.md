**Captain Sutherland** was the commanding officer of the marine companies that landed on the banks of the [[Pedir River]] during the [[Atjeh Expedition 1873-1876|Atjeh Expedition]] in [[January]] [[1874]]. His troops were forced to fight a defensive battle after the tide went out, suffering three dead and nine wounded. Later, his marines participated in the siege of the [[Kraton (Aceh)]] under [[General major Verspijk]] and were involved in the capture of the sultan’s seat on [[23 January]] [[1874]].

## Role in the expedition

Sutherland’s forces were among the first Dutch marine units to engage in the inland operations of the 1874 campaign, later joining the infantry brigades from [[Padang]] and taking part in the construction of a bivouac at [[Penajoeng]].
