**Pang Brahim** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[T. Dagang]]

T. Dagang recurs repeatedly in [[Swart's letters to Schmidt (1909-1913)|Swart's private letters to Schmidt]]. In the letter of [[26 December]] [[1909]] Swart wrote: "During your stay at Tangsé you will of course do everything to put an end to the existence of T. Dagang and his war-leader [[Pang Tjoet Bén]]. Once T. Dagang has fallen, [[Keutjhi' Bén Man]] will come back of his own accord, while [[Keutjhi' Maha]] and [[Pang Brahim]] must then simply be cleared up by the garrison of Tangsé. So once more: take T. Dagang and Pang Tjoet Bén specially upon your own account."

### From [[Tangsé]]

In [[1910]] Schmidt at Tangsé and [[J. J. Boreel|Boreel]] at [[Lam Meulò]] became the pace-setters in Pidië. Swart's letter of [[26 December]] [[1909]] instructed him: "During your stay at Tangsé you will of course do everything to put an end to the existence of [[T. Dagang]] and his *panglima* [[Pang Tjoet Bén]]. Once T. Dagang has fallen, [[Keutjhi' Bén Man]] will come back of his own accord, while [[Keutjhi' Maha]] and [[Pang Brahim]] must then simply be cleared up by the garrison of Tangsé." T. Dagang eluded Schmidt for years and finally submitted in [[1913]], when Schmidt was on leave in Holland: "Tangsé was clean."
