**Mevrouw Van Stockum** was a Netherlands-Indies medical researcher whose findings on the vitamin A content of the milk of indigenous mothers, and whose interpretation of the nature of *[[Xerophthalmie|xerophthalmie]]*, were adopted by Dr. [[C. E. Gomperts]] in [[Oriënteerend oogheelkundig onderzoek bij de Atjehsche bevolking van Groot-Atjeh gedurende 1937|Gomperts's report of 1940]].

## The vitamin A content of mothers' milk

Through the investigations of Mevrouw Van Stockum, Dr. [[J. H. de Haas]] and Ir. [[Meulemans]] among indigenous mothers on [[Java]], it was established that the vitamin A content of the milk of these women was much lower than that of European women.

Gomperts applied the finding to [[Groot-Atjeh]]: since the nutrition of the Atjehnese population was "zeker minderwaardiger" than that of the population on Java, the vitamin A content of the mothers' milk of this population group was to be expected low as well. Combined with the Atjehnese custom — as on Java — of *geprotraheerde borstvoeding* (protracted breast-feeding, eighteen to twenty months), this explained the concentration of xerophthalmia in the 0–5 age group: the low vitamin A level sufficed up to a certain point, after which growth, other developmental processes and probably other factors depressed it further into a state of hypo- or avitaminosis "die de deur opendoet voor de xerophthalmie."

## The nature of xerophthalmia

Van Stockum held that xerophthalmia was the consequence of "een gemeenschappelijke grond-oorzaak en een coïncidentie" — a common underlying cause and a coincidence — as against [[J. Tijssen|Tijssen]]'s view that it was a *constitutie-ziekte* (constitutional disease). Gomperts declared himself inclined to share this latter view: "Schrijver is geneigd dit laatste inzicht te deelen."

The position became the fourth point of his closing summary: xerophthalmia and *[[Keratomalacie|keratomalacie]]* were to be regarded as eye conditions with one common underlying cause — an a- or hypovitaminosis A — complicated by a coincidence of a more drastic nature in keratomalacia than in xerophthalmia.

## Her report of Mulock Houwer's work

It was through a communication of Mevrouw Van Stockum that Gomperts cited the investigations of [[Mulock Houwer]] at the [[C.B.Z.]] at [[Batavia]], and those at [[Semarang]] and [[Bandoeng]], showing a proportionally greater frequency of xerophthalmia among toddlers than among infants.
