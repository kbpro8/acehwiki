**Dr. van Heuvel** was a Director of the Sabang asylum in its early years (1925-) mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Led the small institution in an old hospital, later expanded with old military barracks, on small scale.

## Role

Director of the [[Sabang]] asylum in its early years ([[1925]]-)

## Account from Jongejans

Led the small institution in an old hospital, later expanded with old military [[Tangsi|barracks]], on small scale.

## Significance

Dr. van Heuvel appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
