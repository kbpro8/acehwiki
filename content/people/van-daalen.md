**Van Daalen** (active 1904 march) was a Dutch colonial officer, Major (later General) in the Aceh campaign[[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Led his colonne on the famous march straight through Atjeh in 1904, breaking resistance in the Gajo- and Alaslanden.

## Role

Dutch colonial officer, Major (later General) in the [[Aceh]]Overste (Lieutenant-Colonel)

## Dates

active [[1904]] [[March|march]]

## Account from Jongejans

Led his colonne on the famous [[March|march]] straight through [[Atjeh]] in [[1904]], breaking [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] in the [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]]- and [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]]. This campaign brought the inland of [[Atjeh]] staatsrechtelijk (constitutionally) under [[Nederlandsch-Indië]], completing the annexation of [[Atjeh]]. He is also credited with attempts to transform [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gayo]]/[[Alas]] bestuur from genealogisch to territoriaal.

## Significance

[[Gouverneur Van Daalen|Van Daalen]] appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
