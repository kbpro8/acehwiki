**A. G. N. van Sluys** was a First civilian Governor of Aceh (1918) mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. After Swart's appointment to the Raad van Indië (2 September 1918), the government appointed a civilian Governor.

## Role

First civilian Governor of Aceh ([[1918]])

## Account from Jongejans

After [[Gouverneur Swart|Swart]]'s appointment to the [[Raad van Indië]] ([[2 September]] [[1918]]), the government appointed a civilian Governor. The separation of civil and military authority was thus accomplished. Direct interference of military authorities ceased; civil government had daily leadership, supported by military force.

## Significance

A. G. N. van Sluys appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
