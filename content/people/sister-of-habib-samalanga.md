The **Sister of Habib Samalanga** was the Achehnese woman who commanded the fortified position of [[Senelop]] in the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]] against the assault of [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]] on [[12 March]] [[1894]], and who with fifteen other women had devoted herself to death in its defence.

## The defence

[[G. B. Hooijer]] recorded that at Senelop *de zuster van wijlen [[Habib Samalanga]] het bevel voerde en zich met 15 andere fanatieke vrouwen ten dood had gewijd* — the sister of the late Habib Samalanga held the command and, with fifteen other fanatical women, had devoted herself to death. The struggle that Toekoe Djohan had to wage there was, in Hooijer's word, *ernstig* — serious. It was only when he placed himself at the head of his troops with his sunshade in his hand and, together with [[Toekoe Hoessin Longbattah]] waving a handkerchief, ran in upon the fortification that he succeeded in mastering it. Dutch troops occupied Senelop three days later, on [[15 March]] [[1894]].

## Significance

The episode is one of the few in *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]* in which women appear as combatants and commanders rather than as camp followers, and it illustrates the ideology of *[[Prang sabi]]* — the holy war whose participants sought death in battle — as it operated among the *[[Moslemin]]* of the eastern *moekims*.
