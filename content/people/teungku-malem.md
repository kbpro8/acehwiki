---
title: Teungku Malem
tags: [tokoh, penulis]
---

A native of Trumon, married to a woman of Peunaga, author of the Hikayat Teungku di Meuke', which celebrates the 1893-1894 conflict in which Dutch-friendly Meulaboh chiefs fought the resistance led by Teungku di Meuke', portraying him as a holy martyr despite fighting on the opposing side.
