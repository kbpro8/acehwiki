**Pang Manap** was the last band-head of any significance in the [[Gajò Loeös]], laid low in October [[1913]] in an encounter with police *oppassers* and the population.

After the submissions of [[Lebé Grondong]] and [[Tgk. Moedö Koetö Serei]] in [[1912]], "now only Pang Manap with eight followers remained — "and that could hardly be called anything," as sergeant [[Weers]] would say." He appears in the [[1913]] list of the last unsubmitted resistance leaders. "October brought the end for Pang Manap in the Gajò lands … the greater part of his band, including his brother, reported themselves and surrendered a carbine."
