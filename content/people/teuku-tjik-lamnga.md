**Teuku Tjik Lamnga** was an Acehnese war leader from the [[XXVI Mukim]] and son-in-law of [[Teuku Nanta]].

## Role in the campaign

[[Jan van Swieten]] described him as an outsider whose influence contributed to [[Teuku Nanta]]’s ambivalent attitude toward Dutch approaches. The characterization belongs to Van Swieten’s political case that a less aggressive occupation might have separated local communities from the war party.

## Significance

The source places [[Teuku Tjik Lamnga]] within the contested military and political history of the [[Aceh War]].
