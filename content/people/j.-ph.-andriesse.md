**J. Ph. Andriesse** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Lho' Soekon]]

In [[1917]] a leper at Lho' Soekon murdered the *officier van gezondheid* [[J. Ph. Andriesse|dr. J. Ph. Andriesse]] during examination; the perpetrator was sentenced to death. In June [[1915]] the *officier van gezondheid* [[De Vos]] was lightly wounded at Lho' Soekon during vaccination, the perpetrator being killed by bystanders. On [[11 March]] [[1918]] the Civil Authority, lieutenant [[M. Schouten|Schouten]], was attacked there; he soon recovered and the perpetrator was laid low.

### From [[1917]]

In **[[1917]]** [[Tgk. di Mata Ië]], last of the three great resistance leaders of the north coast, was killed near [[Samarkilang]] on [[10 July]]; a leper murdered the *officier van gezondheid* [[J. Ph. Andriesse|dr. J. Ph. Andriesse]] at [[Lho' Soekon]]; at [[Langsa]] an Acehnese killed [[Jahnke]] in a fit of religious mania. In December a religious movement at [[Serbödjadi]] under [[Penghoeloe Koeala]] cost the *gezaghebber* of [[Lököp]] his life, and on [[29 December]] lieutenant [[G. F. P. Gosenson]] was gravely wounded at [[Djeuram]] in an attack organised by [[Tgk. Poeti']]. "More and more the population is applying itself to the planting of rubber", the *[[Koloniaal Verslag]]* recorded.
