**Jusuf Abdullah Puar** was an Indonesian civil servant who, in March [[1950]], was appointed head of the Publicity Department (*hoofd der afd. Publiciteit*) of the province of North Sumatra (*Nd.-Sumatra*). He had previously served as a senior official (*hoofdambtenaar*) at the Provisional Service (*Voorl. dienst*). His appointment was made by the governor of North Sumatra in the same month that [[Koesen Tjokrosentono]] — formerly acting head of information in [[Atjeh]] — was appointed head of General Affairs.
