**Holle** — *Adviseur-Honorair voor inlandsche zaken* (Honorary Advisor for Native Affairs) — was the Dutch colonial adviser to whom [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]], on [[27 January]] [[27 January]] [[1889]], submitted the judgement of his proposed incognito journey to Atjèh. The minister, having no objection if Indië had no objection, found the matter not urgent but made no objection; and Indië made no objection. The judgement of Holle — as the official adviser on native affairs — was sought on the travel plan, and his advice was among the considerations that allowed the plan to proceed to the point of Snouck Hurgronje's sailing for Pinang.

## The 27 January 1889 Submission

When Snouck Hurgronje wished to visit Atjèh incognito — either as Abd al-Gaffār or as unofficial European, avoiding the Dutch administrative posts on Atjèh's North Coast — the question arose of whether the government would permit it. On the Plein in The Hague, men were not enthusiastic about that travel plan, but they made no objection. The minister had no objection, if Indië had no objection; and Indië made no objection. That it was a dangerous thing that Snouck Hurgronje wished to undertake, did not so strike the minister. What was dangerous, what not — thereabouts one had on the Plein curious conceptions. The inspecting of pesantrèn's (Mohammedan religious schools on Java), that some years later was called life-endangering! — such inspections were later ordinarily assigned to the administrative officials, and never did accidents come of them — but the private walk through Atjèh that Snouck Hurgronje proposed to make, thereon was not dwelt.

The judgement of Holle — as the official adviser on native affairs — was sought by Snouck Hurgronje on [[27 January]] [[27 January]] [[1889]]. The matter was found not urgent, but no objection was made.

## Significance

Holle is the Dutch colonial adviser on native affairs whose judgement, sought by Snouck Hurgronje on [[27 January]] [[27 January]] [[1889]], was among the considerations that allowed the incognito Atjèh journey plan to proceed to the point of Snouck Hurgronje's sailing for Pinang. The plan would be frustrated at Pinang by the Buitenzorg message of [[9 April]] [[9 April]] [[1889]] — the consequence of the advice of the *assistent-resident* [[Goossens|Goossens]] of Groot-Atjèh — but the seeking of Holle's judgement is one of the principal episodes of the plan's metropolitan phase.
