**Vice-admiraal Reynst** was a senior Dutch naval officer who, alongside [[Van der Hoop]] and [[Schout-bijnaacht Van Kinsbergen]], proposed the establishment of a permanent ship‑artillery corps for the [[Oost-Indische Compagnie]] in the period [[1788]]‑[[1792]].

## Advocacy for a Standing Corps
Reynst’s involvement reflected the Dutch desire to professionalise colonial forces in the East Indies, a goal that was ultimately shelved due to financial constraints.
