**Youngest son of Ma'Anim (last Teungkoe Tirò)** was a Last Teungkoe Tirò; barely adult mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Had not actually undertaken anything against the Kompeuni; Schmidt wanted to spare him and called in the mediation of an aunt (a daughter of the great Tjhēh Saman).

## Role

Last [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] Tirò; barely adult

## Account from Jongejans

Had not actually undertaken anything against the [[Kompeuni]]; [[Schmidt]] wanted to spare him and called in the mediation of an aunt (a daughter of the great Tjhēh Saman). She found him but returned unsuccessful: the young man refused to submit, wanted to die as a [[Sjahid|sjahid]]. He died as a [[Sjahid|sjahid]] on [[9 December]] [[1911]].

## Significance

Youngest son of Ma'Anim (last Teungkoe Tirò) appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
