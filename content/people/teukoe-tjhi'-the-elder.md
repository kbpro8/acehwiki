**Teukoe Tjhi' (the elder)** (not specified) was a Previous/self-administrator of Peusangan; reference figure mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Mentioned as a benchmark for self-administrator caliber - the current Alamsjah is described as 'worth of a bestuurder van Teukoe Tjhi's formaat'.

## Role

Previous/self-administrator of [[Peusangan]]; reference figure

## Dates

not specified

## Account from Jongejans

Mentioned as a benchmark for self-administrator caliber - the current Alamsjah is described as 'worth of a bestuurder van Teukoe Tjhi's formaat'. May refer to the father/predecessor of the current Tjhi' ([[Mohamad Djohan Alamsjah]]).

## Significance

Teukoe Tjhi' (the elder) appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
