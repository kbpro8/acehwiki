**T. Maharadja Djeumpa** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[T. Tjhi' Moehammad Djohan Alamsjah]]

In [[1909]] Swart was authorised to dispose of 10,000 guilders as compensation to [[T. Maharadja Djeumpa]], acting *oelèëbalang* of Peusangan, for the loss of his possessions through the doings of ill-disposed persons.

### From [[Peusangan]]

In [[1909]] [[H. N. A. Swart]] was authorised to pay 10,000 guilders in compensation to [[T. Maharadja Djeumpa]], acting *[[oelèëbalang]]* of Peusangan, for the loss of his possessions through the doings of ill-disposed persons. [[Maurice Henri du Croo|Du Croo]] cited "T. Tjhi' Peusangan" as the example of the chiefs with whom "was het uiteindelijk mogelijk een begin-vorm van *[[zelfbestuur]]* te bereiken". A photograph shows Swart and his wife visiting the *landschapshoofd* of Peusangan during their journey through the Indies in [[1928]].

### From [[Het Pacificeerende Bestuurswerk 1908-1918]]

At [[Lam Njong]] a busy *pasar* cattle market arose on the site of the former *[[benteng]]*, and Swart was again authorised to dispose of 10,000 guilders as compensation to [[T. Maharadja Djeumpa]], acting *oelèëbalang* of [[Peusangan]], for the loss of his possessions through the doings of ill-disposed persons.
