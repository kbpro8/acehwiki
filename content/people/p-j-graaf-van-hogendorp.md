**P. J. graaf van Hogendorp** was a **Lieutenant ter zee le klasse** who commanded the landing division during the **Tamiang** expedition of **[[March]]‑[[April]] [[1893]]**. He later received a knighthood in the **Militaire Willemsorde** (4th class) for his conduct.

## Command

Hogendorp directed the assault on a heavily defended **redoute** on the left wing, coordinating infantry and marine units.

## Honour

His award of the **Militaire Willemsorde 4e klasse** reflects Dutch recognition of bravery in the **Aceh War**.
