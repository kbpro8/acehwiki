**Lewe van Aduard, Captain ter zee** commanded the frigate [[Van der Werff]] during the [[1821]] Dutch expedition against [[Palembang]]. When the ship came under heavy enemy fire, van Aduard ordered the [[Royal Netherlands Marine Corps|mariniers]] to disembark and seize the enemy batteries, a maneuver that resulted in significant casualties among his crew (page 94).

## Naval Command

Van Aduard’s decision to deploy marines from his ship illustrated the integration of naval and infantry forces in Dutch colonial warfare, a practice also employed in earlier actions in the Aceh‑adjacent islands.
