**A. H. E. von und zu Egloffstein** was an officer of the *[[Korps Marechaussee te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* in [[Atjeh]] recorded in the *[[Overzicht van Onderscheidingen toegekend aan het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden (1890-1930)|Overzicht van Onderscheidingen]]* of the [[1930]] jubilee number as *onderluitenant* of Maréchaussée, knight of the *[[Militaire Willemsorde|Militaire Willems-Orde]]* 4th class and recipient of the *[[Eeresabel]]*, both in [[1902]].

## Entry in the register

The register lists him under [[1902]] as *Onderluit. der Mar. A. H. E. von und zu Egloffstein, Alg. Stb. No. 43741*, receiving both the knight’s cross 4th class and the Eeresabel.
