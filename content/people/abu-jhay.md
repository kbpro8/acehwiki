---
title: Abu Jhay
aliases: [Abu Jahl]
tags: [person, religious-figure]
---

Made king of Mecca in Acehnese religious hikayats, a role he did not historically hold. He wages war against the young Muhammad in the Hikayat nubuet, invokes the hero Patian against the Prophet in the Hikayat Seuma'un, and appears in the Hikayat Dari as an example of impiety.
