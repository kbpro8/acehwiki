**Lieutenant P. Spanjaard** was a junior officer of the [[Royal Netherlands Marine Corps]] who commanded a marine detachment during the 1868 expedition against the Acehnese settlement of [[Bandjar]]. He led the **Marine Landings Division** in a series of assaults on fortified kampongs, including [[Kali-Anget]] and [[Kampong Djentarek]].

## Service in Aceh (1868)

During the attack on [[24 October]] [[1868]], Spanjaard’s detachment, comprising 11 officers and 170 marines, advanced along steep, slippery mountain paths to seize enemy fortifications. His conduct earned him an **Eervolle Vermelding** (Honorable Mention) for bravery and effective leadership.

## Reputation

Contemporary reports (Cambier, p. 188) praised his “eervol vermeld” (honorable mention) while also noting the overall poor discipline of the landing division, which he attempted to enforce with a “heavy stick”.

## Significance

Spanjaard’s actions contributed to the Dutch capture of key Acehnese strongholds, demonstrating the importance of marine officers in colonial amphibious warfare.
