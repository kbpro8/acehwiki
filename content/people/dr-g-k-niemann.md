title: Dr. G.K. Niemann
aliases: [Niemann]
tags: [tokoh, sarjana]
---

A Dutch scholar who published portions of the Acehnese chronicles ("Bloemlezing uit Maleische geschriften"), through which Hurgronje learned about Acheh's religious-philosophical life in the 16th-17th centuries, and who also reviewed the Batak story of "Malin Deman" in the Bijdragen (1866).
