**Lieutenant Le Mahieu** served as an adjutant of the marine battalion during the third Bali expedition of [[1849]] (page 108). He was noted for his organisational talent and for being among the first to respond to alarms during night attacks (page 108). He fell ill from exhaustion on [[24 May]] [[1849]] and died on [[22 June]] [[1849]] (page 108).

## Role in the Expedition

Le Mahieu coordinated the preparation of the landing craft and oversaw the deployment of the marine battalion during the assaults on [[Djagaraga]] and [[Kasoemba]] (page 108).

## Posthumous Recognition

Although a recommendation for his promotion to the *Militaire Willemsorde* was made, it was not acted upon because he had died before the royal decree of [[11 December]] [[1849]] (page 108).
