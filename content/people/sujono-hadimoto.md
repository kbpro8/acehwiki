**Mr. Sujono Hadimoto** was the Indonesian Minister of Trade and Industry (*min. van Handel en Industrie*) who, on 16 May [[1952]], departed from Medan on an inspection tour of the oil enterprises in [[Atjeh|Atjeh]] on Sumatra. His delegation included the Minister of Information, [[Arnold Mononutu]]; the senior officials of the Ministry of Trade and Industry, Ir. [[Darmawan Mangunkusumo]] and [[Sunu Sumosastro]]; and the parliament member [[Tengku Mohamad Hassan]].

## Visit to Atjeh

The purpose of the visit was to inspect the oil enterprises on Sumatra, particularly those in the [[Atjeh]] region, including the oil fields at [[Langsa]] and other locations. The results of this ministerial inspection were not made public. The visit reflected the central government's ongoing concern with the rehabilitation and utilisation of the oil resources of North Sumatra, which had been damaged during the Japanese occupation and the subsequent revolutionary period.
