**Dr. Sahar** was an Indonesian physician, politician, and the general chairman of the Masjumi party in North Sumatra. In February [[1950]], he was a member of the official delegation that accompanied the acting governor of North Sumatra, [[Sarimin Reksodihardjo]], on an inspection visit to [[Atjeh|Atjeh]]. He was also a member of the governing board (*Dewan Pimpinan*) of the Jajasan Universitas Sumatera Utara, the foundation established to fund the proposed university at Medan.
