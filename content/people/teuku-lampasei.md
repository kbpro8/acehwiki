**Teuku Lampasei** (historically written Toekoe Lampasei) was an influential Acehnese nobleman and territorial chieftain from the [[XXV Moekims]] in the [[Aceh Sultanate]] during the 1870s.

## Diplomacy Following the Fall of the Kraton

In late January [[1874]], immediately following the [[Capture of the Aceh Kraton]] by General [[J. van Swieten]] ([[Second Aceh Expedition]]), an official delegate representing [[Teuku Lampasei]] arrived at Dutch headquarters bearing a formal letter in which the chief offered his submission to the [[Dutch East Indies Government]].

As recorded in *Atjeh gedurende de tweede expeditie en later* ([[1876]]), this letter was cited by General [[J. van Swieten]] in his dispatches of [[28 January]] [[1874]] ([[General van Swieten War Telegrams]]) to support his assessment that major leaders of the XXV Moekims were abandoning armed resistance.
