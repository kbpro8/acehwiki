**J. Paris** was a captain of the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* who was killed during the [[Westkust Resistance (1925-1927)|action on the west coast of Atjeh]] of [[1925]]–[[1927]], in the [[Gevecht waarin kapitein Paris sneuvelde (Westkust van Atjeh, 1925-1927)|engagement]] in which his brigade lost all but two of its men unwounded and the command devolved successively upon the wounded Javanese *maréchaussée* [[Kromo-di-Koro]] and the Menadonese *maréchaussée* [[Suwoek]].

## Death in the Westkust action

The [[1930]] jubilee number records that the Maréchaussée demonstrated its indispensability anew during the action on the west coast of Atjeh in [[1925]]–[[1927]], "waarbij kapitein J. Paris het leven liet" — in which Captain J. Paris lost his life. On the same occasion the *brigade-commandant* also fell.

## The engagement

Of the brigade only two men remained unhurt. Besides Captain Paris, five other soldiers of lower rank were killed, and twelve were more or less severely wounded. Notwithstanding those losses, some twenty *kwaadwilligen* (ill-disposed persons) were laid low and twenty-six *[[Klewang|klewangs]]* captured.

The wounded Javanese *maréchaussée* [[Kromo-di-Koro]] took over the command until loss of blood threatened to make him collapse, handing it on to [[Suwoek]] after first ordering shots to be fired into the surrounding *[[Alang-alang|alang-alang]]*. [[Sumangkut]], himself severely wounded, spotted a fleeing opponent and warned Suwoek, who brought the man down fifty metres further on; and [[Leleng]], having lost the use of his left hand, fired his *[[Karabijn (M. 95)|karabijn]]* by gripping it between his knees.

It was after this engagement that Colonel [[L. A. Snell]] was sent from [[Java]] to the west coast, where he brought the resistance to an end.
