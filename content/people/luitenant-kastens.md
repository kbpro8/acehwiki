**Luitenant Kastens** was a Dutch officer of the [[Oost-Indisch Leger|Netherlands East Indies Army]] serving in the outposts of [[Groot-Atjeh]] during the alliance period of [[1893]]–[[1896]], recorded by [[G. B. Hooijer]] in *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]*.

## Service in the *buitenposten* (outer posts)

He was identified by [[G. B. Hooijer]] among the officers photographed at [[Biloel]], alongside [[Luitenant Van Delden|Van Delden]].
