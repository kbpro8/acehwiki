**Schröder** is a subject named in *[[Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[G. K. Dijkstra]]

**G. K. Dijkstra** — later Lieutenant-General K.N.I.L. b.d. — was the officer who served under [[H. N. A. Swart]] in [[Borneo]], [[Celebes]] and [[Atjeh]], took over the administration of [[Pidië]] from major [[Schröder]] in [[1908]], and in [[1943]] wrote the foreword to [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]]'s *[[Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]*. Swart placed him, with [[W. B. J. A. Scheepens]] and [[H. J. Schmidt]], among the three collaborators he ranked above the rest.

### From [[G. K. Dijkstra]]

He took over the government of Pidië from major [[Schröder]] in [[1908]], having previously served under Swart in Borneo and Celebes. Swart placed him at [[Sigli]], the most important troop command on the north coast, before Scheepens.

### From [[Pidië]]

In this district, "the cradle of the irreconcilable religious leaders, the Tirò Teungkoes," the *[[oelama]]* party took heavy blows in [[1908]], the Tirò band having fled from [[Geumpang]] to Pidië at the end of [[1907]] before the patrols of [[H. J. Schmidt]]. Captain [[G. K. Dijkstra]] took over the administration from major [[Schröder]]. On [[8 February]] [[1908]] Schmidt surprised the band's hiding-place on the *kr.* [[Meu']], where five of the principal Teungkoes fell.
