**Van der Bunt, H. J.** was a Dutch marine corporal who was knighted in the fourth class of the **Militaire Willemsorde** for his service in the Atjeh expeditions of [[1873]] and [[1874]], as recorded in the royal decree of [[6 October]] [[1874]].

## Honors

His decoration placed him among the distinguished marine personnel recognized for bravery in Aceh.
