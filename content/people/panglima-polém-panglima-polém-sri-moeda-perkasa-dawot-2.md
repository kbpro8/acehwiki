**Panglima Polém (Panglima Polém Sri Moeda Perkasa Dawot)** was a Sagi head (Sagihoofd) of the XXII Moekims, Groot-Atjeh; great Acehnese strategist mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Still alive at time of writing; lives at Lam Sie near Seulimeum, ~40 km from Koetaradja.

## Role

[[Sagi]] head (Sagihoofd) of the [[XXII Moekims]], [[Groot-Atjeh]]; great [[Acehnese]] strategist

## Account from Jongejans

Still alive at time of writing; lives at [[Lam Sie]] near [[Seulimeum]], ~40 km from [[Koetaradja]]. Very pious, strict in faith. Full title: [[Panglima Polém]] Sri Moeda Perkasa Dawot. Tradition says he descends from [[Sultan Iskander Moeda]] via that [[sultan|Sultan]]'s son [[Panglima Polém Moeda Sertia Perkasa]] and an Abyssinian slave girl. The Polém lineage rivalled the Sultans in power; several times they besieged Sultans in their [[Dalem]], drove them out and proclaimed new Sultans more compliant to them. Honest and loyal both in [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] and after submission. Resisted [[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]]'s attempt to reconcile him with [[Teukoe Oemar]]; refused Polém's sister's marriage to [[Teukoe Oemar]]; even returned gifts. Eventually limited combined action with [[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]] and [[Teukoe Oemar]] collapsed over dispute about firearms exchange. [[July]] [[1896]]: [[Prang sabi|Prang Sabil]] proclaimed; [[August]] [[1896]] Dutch ultimatum delivered to him and the oelèëbalangs of the [[XXVI Moekims|XXVI moekims]]; Polém asked for a month's delay (denied). End [[August]] [[1896]]: chase began, his possession at [[Glé Jeuëng]] burned; retreated to [[Seulimeum]]; pushed through [[Pidië]], [[Peusangan]], [[Pasai|Pasé]]; rested briefly behind [[Keureutòë]] and [[Geudong]] with brother-in-law [[Toeankoe Radja Keumala]]; [[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]] joined. Pursued by marechausée brigades; fled to [[Gajo-landen]], then [[Peutòë]]; constantly evaded capture while men like [[Webb]], [[Hendrikus Colijn|Colijn]], [[Christoffel]], [[Geertsema Beckering]], [[H. T. Aukes|Aukes]] pursued him like terriers. May [[1903]]: his wife, mother and sister captured by Major [[Van der Maaten]]. [[September]] [[1903]]: surrendered at [[Lho'Seumawé|Lho' Seumawé]] to [[Hendrikus Colijn|Colijn]] together with [[Toeankoe Radja Keumala]] and 150 followers. Restored as Sagihoofd der [[XXII Moekims|XXII moekims]]; wears Knight's Cross of Order of [[Oranje-Nassau (Orde van Oranje Nassau)|Oranje-Nassau]] and the Great Golden Star for loyalty and merit.

## Significance

[[Panglima Polém (Panglima Polém Sri Moeda Perkasa Dawot)]] appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
