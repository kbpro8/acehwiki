**J. C. Heijnen** was a European sergeant and *brigade-commandant* of the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* in [[Atjeh]], who in [[1902]] received both the fourth and the third class of the *[[Militaire Willemsorde|Militaire Willems-Orde]]* within six months, and was the third non-commissioned officer — and the fifth member of the corps as a whole — to be granted that high distinction.

## Service and decorations

Heijnen was born on [[16 October]] [[1869]]. As *brigade-commandant* of Maréchaussée he received on [[18 February]] [[1902]] the knight's cross of the Militaire Willems-Orde 4th class, and on [[31 July]] of the same year the 3rd class. The [[Overzicht van Onderscheidingen toegekend aan het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden (1890-1930)|register of honours]] in the [[1930]] jubilee number records the 3rd-class award under [[1902]] jointly with that to his division commander, *Kapt. Mar. [[K. van der Maaten]]*, and gives his register number as *Alg. Stb. No. 31519*.

He was, the jubilee notice states, "de derde onderofficier, wien deze hooge onderscheiding werd toegekend en no. 5 van het geheele Korps" — the third non-commissioned officer to whom this high distinction was granted, and number five of the whole corps.

## The photograph dedicated by Van der Maaten

The jubilee number reproduced a photograph of Captain [[K. van der Maaten]], who had been promoted knight of the Militaire Willems-Orde 3rd class at the same time as his brigade commander; the caption records that the photograph "werd door zijn oud-divisiecommandant aan sergeant Heijnen opgedragen" — was dedicated by his former division commander to Sergeant Heijnen.

## Later career

Heijnen afterwards transferred into the service of the police, and was pensioned as *adjunct-hoofdcommissaris*, after the knight's cross of the *[[Orde van Oranje-Nassau]]* had been conferred upon him.

## Note on spelling

The jubilee number spells the name both *Heijnen* and *Heynen*; the same variation occurs in other Atjeh literature.
