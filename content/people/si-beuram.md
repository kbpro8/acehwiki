**Si Beuram** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[The resistance in Atjeh 1908–1912]]

December brought the settlement for October: besides many prominent resistance men reporting themselves, among them two *oelama's* notorious for their religious school where resistance against the *Kompeuni* ("the Company (the Dutch power)") was preached, sergeant [[Bossert]] surprised a *ladang* in the night of [[5 November|5]]–[[6 November]], arresting a band-head with six followers; the same patrol shortly afterwards laid low the band-head who had dealt lieutenant Van Eendenburg the fatal blow in October. On that occasion the well-known Acehnese guide [[Si Beuram]] fell on the Dutch side and the patrol commander sergeant Bossert and two men were severely wounded. A patrol under lieutenant [[J. van Arkel|Van Arkel]] surprised an enemy band in [[Pameu]], in which four well-known band-heads fell. And so December became the closing piece: at last Tgk. Poeti' submitted with all his *teungkoes*, *panglimas* and sixty followers.
