---

title: Teungku Krueng Kale alias Haji Muda
aliases: [Haji Muda]
tags: [person, scholar]
---

A teacher who studied at Mecca as well as under Teungku di Lam Nyong, representative of the generation of teachers active before the Dutch war's complete disruption of Acehnese religious education.

## See Also

- [[Teungku di Lam Nyong]]
- [[Mecca]]

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
