---
title: Indra Bangsawan
tags: [person, literary-work]
---

One of twin sons of King Indra Bungsu of Chahrilah, separated from his brother Chahpari by a storm while seeking a magic musical instrument. Disguised as a mangy forest mannikin with a giant's help, he won the favor of princess Sangirah, slew the monster Bura'sa guarding her, and was eventually made his father's successor.

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
