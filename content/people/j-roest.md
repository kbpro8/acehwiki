**J. Roest** was a Dutch colonial official and author of railway and harbour studies for the *Tijdschrift voor het Binnenlandsch Bestuur* (Journal for Home Administration) at Batavia. He wrote — in the December [[1912]] issue of that journal — the article *Belawanhaven en Atjeh-tram* (Belawan harbour and the Atjeh tram) and, four years later at Weltevreden on [[4 December]] [[1915]], its sequel *[[De spoorwegverbinding tusschen Deli, Atjeh en de Aroebaai]]* (The railway connection between Deli, Atjeh and the Aru Bay), published in volume XLIX of the same journal in December [[1916]].

## The author of the Aru Bay railway history

In his [[1916]] article Roest judged the whole dispute between the [[Cultuurraad]] of the East Coast of Sumatra, the [[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij]], and the Netherlands Indies Government on its merits: he showed why the Government's choice of the [[Aroebai|Aru Bay]] had been reasonable while the assumption that [[Telok Taboeban|Telok Taboehan]] would become the ocean harbour was weakened year by year; he analysed the cost estimates of the rival connection lines (Pematang Pandjang–[[Pangkalan Brandan]], [[Besitang]]–[[Tandjong Slamat]]); he preserved the Cultuurraad debates of [[26 September]] [[1912]] and [[20 February]] [[1913]] and the Government decisions of [[17 June]] and [[16 October]] [[1913]]; and he defended the Government's course that the interest of the company in avoiding competition must yield to the general interest if a state connection between the [[Atjeh-tram]] and the Deli railway were ever required. His articles are the principal printed sources for the [[Deli-Atjeh railway connection controversy (1912-1916)|railway controversy]] and the history of the Aru Bay ports.
