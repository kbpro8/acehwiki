**van Langen** (not specified) was a Author of standard work on Aceh mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Named as author of one of the standard works on Aceh (likely K.F.H.

## Role

Author of standard work on [[Aceh]]

## Dates

not specified

## Account from Jongejans

Named as author of one of the standard works on [[Aceh]] (likely K.F.H. van Langen).

## Significance

van Langen appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
