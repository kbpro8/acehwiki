**W. van Kregten** was the Dutch *1ste luitenant* and Civil Authority murdered with two soldiers at [[Lam No]] in the [[Daja]] country on [[20 May]] [[1914]].

He was killed by a band of twelve men armed with *[[rentjong]]* and *[[klewang]]* from the *[[landschap]]pen* [[Lam No]], [[Panté Tjeureumèn]], [[Djeunamprong]] and [[Oenga]], who fled to the mountains with the soldiers' weapons. The night quarters had been chosen and occupied "in the most utterly careless manner", the weapons of both soldiers lying on the floor of two different unwalled rooms; in the morning, while the soldiers had left their weapons to drink coffee and the lieutenant was innocently winding his puttees, [[Nja' Ali|Si Ali]] and four others attacked him. The cause lay in the dismissal of Si Ali, at Van Kregten's instigation, from his post as *mandoer* of a coconut garden, and in a personal grudge against the *[[oelèëbalang]]* of [[Lambeusdë]]. [[Maurice Henri du Croo|Du Croo]] added the note: "Van Kregten was a quiet, calm, well-balanced man."
