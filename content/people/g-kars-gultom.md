**G. Kars Gultom** was an Indonesian official who served as secretary to [[Sutan Barus]], the inspector-general of enterprises in [[Atjeh|Atjeh]]. In February [[1950]], he accompanied the second survey party of some twenty planters who travelled by jeep via P. Brandan to [[Langsar Estate]] to assess the condition of plantation enterprises in Atjeh following the Dutch-Indonesian hostilities.
