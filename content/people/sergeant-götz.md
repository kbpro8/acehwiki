**Sergeant Götz** was a Sergeant, killed Teungkoe di Tjot Tjitjém mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Led 'meesterlijken overval' (masterly raid) in 1906 in which Teungkoe di Tjot Tjitjém fell.

## Role

Sergeant, killed [[Teungkoe di Tjot Tjitjém]]

## Account from Jongejans

Led 'meesterlijken [[Overval|overval]]' (masterly raid) in [[1906]] in which [[Teungkoe di Tjot Tjitjém]] fell.

## Significance

Sergeant Götz appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
