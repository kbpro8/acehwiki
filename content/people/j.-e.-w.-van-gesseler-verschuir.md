**J. E. W. van Gesseler Verschuir** was an officer of the *[[Korps Marechaussee te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* in [[Atjeh]] recorded in the *[[Overzicht van Onderscheidingen toegekend aan het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden (1890-1930)|Overzicht van Onderscheidingen]]* of the [[1930]] jubilee number as entered in the registers as knight of the *[[Militaire Willemsorde|Militaire Willems-Orde]]* 4th class in [[1899]], and again recorded in [[1902]].

## Entry in the register

The register lists him under [[1899]] with the formula “Ingeschreven in de registers als Ridder Militaire Willems-Orde 4e Kl.”, and again under [[1902]], in the shortened form *J. E. W. Verschuir*, among the recipients of the 4th class.
