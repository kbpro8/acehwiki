**General‑Major Van Geen** was a senior Dutch commander who arrived in [[Celebes]] in the spring of [[1825]] with a large expeditionary force. He later led operations in southern Sumatra, including the March [[1825]] campaign against the kingdom of [[Boni]].

## Campaigns

Van Geen’s forces captured the main town of [[Boni]] after a series of engagements, notably the battle at [[Mangarabombang]] on [[15 March]] [[1825]] and the landing at [[Badjoa]] on [[27 March]] [[1825]].
