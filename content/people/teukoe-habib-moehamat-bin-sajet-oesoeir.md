**Teukoe Habib Moehamat bin Sajet Oesoeir** was [[Uleebalang|oeléëbalang]] of the small landscape [[Peudawa Rajeu]], of Arab descent pointing to religious nobility (*sajjids* or *sajets* have religious nobility and great influence, per [[C. Snouck Hurgronje]]; *habib* means beloved).

## Life and Dutch relations

When Dutch authority came in those regions and prevented war of [[Idi]] and [[Peureula]] over [[Peudawa Rajeu]], this Moehamat, boy maybe 12 years, already head, while mother already remarried with head of [[Baya]]. His upbringing may under those circumstances have suffered, his attachment to government not damaged thereby.

In [[1875]] he received from government large presentation sheet of wrought silver; later coffeepot milkpot added, whole service, gladly shows old man today what remains of what he as child received. Such gifts government gave then more; per [[J. A. Kruyt]], prince of [[Edi]] received silver coffee service, another head gold watch, third silver rice dish.

In [[1895]] received small star, in [[1913]] large star of loyalty and merit; documents kept. In [[1899]] signed [[Korte Verklaring]] in 3 articles replacing long contract 18 articles. Then sent yearly felicitation on [[31 August]] to Queen, Governor-General and Governor of Atjeh with much pomp and Arabic terms. Then after some time letter from controller of [[Idi]] that he was thanked; order via Kabinet of Queen via ministry Colonies.

According to Broersma, around [[1923]] grey-beard about 63 years, sharp face sign gesture. Fixed income landscape with 400 souls but small: 155 gulden monthly. House neither big nor beautiful, poor, has debt and advance. But rich in love: with 14 lawful wives begot 17 children. His eastern patriarchate no unique among heads in Atjeh.
