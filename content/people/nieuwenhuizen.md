**Nieuwenhuizen** — the Vice-President of the *Raad van Indië* (Council of the Indies) — was the Dutch colonial official appointed as *Commissaris* (Commissioner) and dispatched with four warships to [[Acheh|Atjeh]] in [[1873]] to bring the diplomatic prelude to the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] to a conclusion and, after the failure of the negotiations, to declare war and to direct the opening phase of the [[Eerste Atjeh-expeditie (1873)|first Atjeh expedition]]. He is recorded in [[Dr. W. A. Terwogt|Terwogt]]'s *[[Het Land van Jan Pieterszoon Coen (Terwogt)|Het Land van Jan Pieterszoon Coen]]* as the principal Dutch representative in the Atjehnese theatre in the critical weeks of March–April [[1873]].

## The mission to Atjeh

After the failure of the earlier Dutch envoy — who was "eenvoudig niet ontvangen" (simply not received) by the Sultan — and after the discovery of the Atjehnese appeals to the Sultan of Turkey and to France, the Dutch government resolved on doortastend optreden (decisive action). The Vice-President of the Raad van Indië, Nieuwenhuizen, was appointed Commissaris and dispatched with four warships to Atjeh, while at the same time the necessary troop force was assembled under the command of Generaal-Majoor [[J. H. R. Köhler|Köhler]] — so that, after the declaration of war, the military could immediately begin operations.

## The arrival and the declaration of war

Nieuwenhuizen arrived at the roadstead of Atjeh on [[22 March]] [[1873]]. His first letter was answered with the *uitvlucht* (excuse) that no decision could be taken before the answer from Turkey was received. His second letter, in which Atjeh was summoned to war if no satisfactory answer followed within 24 hours, was answered with new excuses. Thereupon, on [[26 March]] [[1873]], the declaration of war followed. At the Atjehnese request, two more days' delay were granted; but as the Atjehnese continued meanwhile to fortify their defence-works, these were bombarded by the marine ships on [[28 March]] [[1873]]. The troops — 3,600 strong — arrived on [[5 April]] [[1873]], and the military operations of the [[Eerste Atjeh-expeditie (1873)|first expedition]] began under the military command of [[J. H. R. Köhler|Köhler]], with Nieuwenhuizen as the political commissioner.

## Significance

For AcehWiki, Nieuwenhuizen is significant as the Dutch Commissaris who directed the diplomatic prelude to the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] and who, on [[26 March]] [[1873]], declared war on Atjeh — the act that opened the [[Eerste Atjeh-expeditie (1873)|first Atjeh expedition]] and, with it, the decades-long Atjeh-oorlog. His mission is the source for the diplomatic chronology of March [[1873]] — the arrival of [[22 March]], the excuses of the Sultan, the ultimatum, the declaration of war of [[26 March]], and the bombardment of [[28 March]] — that frames the opening of the war.
