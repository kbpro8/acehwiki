---
title: Chah Kubat
aliases: [Shah Kubat]
tags: [person, literary-work]
---

A young hero urged in a dream to end his father's humiliating tribute to Blia Indra, king of the apes. After overcoming supernatural obstacles with his grandfather Beureuma Sa'ti's help, he conquered the kingdom of apes and married four princesses.

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
