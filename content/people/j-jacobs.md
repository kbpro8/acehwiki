**J. Jacobs** was a Dutch ethnographer and author of *Het familie- en kampongleven op Groot-Atjeh* ("Family and Kampong Life in Greater Atjeh"), published in two volumes in Leiden in [[1894]]. The work was offered in [[1902]] by the bookseller Martinus Nijhoff at 17 florins in commemoration of the third centenary of the Dutch East India Company.

## Work

Jacobs' two-volume study of family and village life in [[Groot-Atjeh]] (Greater Atjeh, the heartland of the sultanate) provided one of the most detailed late-nineteenth-century accounts of Acehnese domestic and social organisation. It complemented the larger-scale ethnographic and political studies by [[C. Snouck Hurgronje]] (*De Atjehers*, [[1893]]-[[1894|94]]) by focusing on the micro-level of household and community structure. Jacobs' work remains a valuable source for the social history of the [[Acehnese People|Acehnese people]] during the late colonial period.
