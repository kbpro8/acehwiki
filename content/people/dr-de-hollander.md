A Dutch scholar who published the Malay hikayat of Johar Manikam (Breda, [[1845]]), to which the Acehnese Hikayat Putroe Baren bears parallels, and who also contributed the "Handleiding bij de beoefening der Maleische taal- en letterkunde."

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
