**Luitenant‑Kolonel Douglas** was a senior Dutch officer who commanded forces in the **Wikkie‑gebied** and later led expeditions from **[[Stevensburg]]** and **[[Hardenbrock]]**. He coordinated attacks against **[[Atta (rebel leader)|Atta]]** and received reports of rebel movements around the **Wikkie‑kreek**, **Canje**, and **Wironje‑kreek**. Douglas also reported the capture of **[[Accara]]** and the surrender of numerous **Negers**.

## Operations
Douglas organized multiple detachments, including a naval element to the plantation **[[Maarkey]]**, and oversaw the distribution of supplies for eight days of campaigning.
