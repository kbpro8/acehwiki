**Baron Van Heeckeren van Molecaten** was a Dutch first lieutenant of infantry, a member of the Dutch nobility, in [[Atjeh]] in [[1876]]. He commanded the escort of the convoy attacked in the [[Convoy ambush near Atoë (21 March 1876)|ambush of 21 March 1876]] between [[Atoeh|Atoë]] and [[Kajoe-Lē]], and was wounded the following night in the [[Night attack on Atoë Noord (22-23 March 1876)|night alarms at the new bivouac of Atoë Noord]].

## 21 March 1876

Ordered by Major [[Majoor J. Visser|J. Visser]] to bring a transport of 5 sick men and 65 free coolies and forced-labourers, with some soldiers' wives, from Atoë to Kajoe-Lē with one other officer and 60 bayonets, Van Heeckeren was caught on the sodden sawah between the kampongs in thick fire from ditch and fence. He formed his four small sections on all four fronts, held the enemy off while the non-combatants lay flat on the ground behind them, and decided to fight back to Atoë. Wounded while about to take personal command of the rear covering party, he handed it to his native sergeant Suiringa — the official report praises both him and the steadiness of his men. The convoy reached Atoë with 5 killed and 8 wounded.

## 22–23 March 1876

The next morning the new post of Atoë Noord was begun on the ground of the battle, with the attacked troops bivouacking around it; that night the enemy returned in great numbers, firing into the bivouac from three sides, and Lieutenant Van Heeckeren, among the 1 officer, 3 non-commissioned officers and men and 1 overseer wounded, was hit a second time within two days.

Both appearances are recorded by [[Kolonel Meijer|H. F. Meijer]] in *[[Atjeh van 26 December 1875 tot 4 September 1876]]* ([[1883]]).
