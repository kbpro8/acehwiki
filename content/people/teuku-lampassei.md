**Teuku Lampassei** was the half-brother of [[Teuku Nek]] and an Acehnese chief connected with early approaches to the Dutch camp in [[1874]].

## Role in the campaign

A letter received on [[28 January]] [[1874]] declared his submission to the Dutch Government and intention to return to his village; the source quotes his hope that past actions would not be held against him.

## Significance

The source places [[Teuku Lampassei]] within the contested military and political history of the [[Aceh War]].
