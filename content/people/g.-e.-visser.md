**G. E. Visser** was a Dutch 2nd lieutenant of the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Netherlands Indies Army]] who commanded the rear-guard of forty bayonets on the patrol of [[25 September]] [[1897]] from [[Lok-Nga|Lho-Nga]] to the [[Goeah Teroes]] cave on the [[Glé Betoes]].

On the descent from the cave, after the death of Lieutenant [[J. van Greuningen]] and four fusiliers, Visser's rear-guard covered the retreat of the column while the *[[Tandoe|tandoes]]* carrying the killed and wounded were brought down in the middle. [[P. H. R. Beuming]] records that the descent went extremely slowly and that with the *tandoes* it was breakneck work, the swaying drawing many a cry from the wounded.

He is to be distinguished from the *marechaussée* lieutenant [[Vis]] who took part in the [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|surprise of Anak Galoeng]] of [[29 June]] [[1896]].
