**Haringman (journal)** is the contemporaneous diary kept by the Dutch naval officer [[Haringman]] during the December 19–25 expedition against rebel forces in the interior of [[Acheh]]. The journal provides a day‑by‑day account of naval movements, encounters with “negro” rebels, the burning of sugar plantations such as [[Prosperiteit]] and [[Peereboom]], and the fatal mis‑communication at the landing on [[Wikkie creek]] on [[25 December]].

## Content
The entries describe the deployment of patrols from the bark “[[Zeven Provinciën]]”, the use of “tent‑boats” for landing, and the capture of a Surinamese border detachment at [[Beresteyn]] plantation. Haringman also records the testimony of enslaved Africans about rebel activities and the involvement of the “Indian guides”.

## Historical Value
As a primary source, the journal is frequently cited in studies of Dutch colonial military tactics in [[Acheh]] and illustrates the challenges of riverine warfare in the early nineteenth century.
