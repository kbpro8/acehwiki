**Mirandolle** — a **Lieutenant** in the [[Royal Netherlands Marine Corps|Korps Mariniers]] — was a Dutch marine officer who served in the [[Aceh War|Aceh expedition]] of [[1875]]–[[1876]]. He commanded a marine *peloton* (platoon) that provided covering fire for artillery units, took over command of [[Lieutenant Ahn]]'s artillery-covering section after Ahn was wounded at [[Adjoen]] on [[30 December]] [[1875]], and was himself wounded on [[30 January]] [[1876]] while leading about twenty marines against the enemy camp of [[Daroe]].

## The Aceh Expedition of 1875-1876

Mirandolle's marine section formed part of the [[VIIIe battalion]] during the [[Aceh War|Acehnese campaign]] of [[1875]]–[[1876]]. The section's role was to provide covering fire for the artillery units attached to the column, and Mirandolle is recorded in two distinct episodes — at [[Adjoen]] in December [[1875]] and at [[Daroe]] in January [[1876]] — in the operational history of the [[Royal Netherlands Marine Corps|Korps Mariniers]].

## Action at Adjoen (30 December 1875)

On [[30 December]] [[1875]], at the assault on the kampong of [[Adjoen]], [[Lieutenant Ahn]] — the officer commanding the artillery-covering section — was wounded. Mirandolle immediately assumed command of Ahn's section and led the marines in providing fire support for the [[VIIIe battalion]] during the assault on the kampong, coordinating with [[Lieutenant Cambier]] and [[Captain Cramer von Baumgarten]] (DLP-55, p. 135).

## Action at Daroe (28-30 January 1876)

During the assault on the enemy camp of [[Daroe]], Mirandolle's *peloton* protected the artillery section. On [[28 January]] [[1876]], the *peloton* formed the covering force for the artillery during the fighting. Two days later, on [[30 January]] [[1876]], Mirandolle assembled about twenty marines on a height to fire upon the enemy, sustaining a wound in the process (Cambier, *Verslagen der Marine-Vereeniging* III 1889-1890, pp. 229-233; DLP-55, pp. 138-141).

## Later Service

Mirandolle continued to serve with the [[Royal Netherlands Marine Corps|marine corps]] after the [[Aceh War|Aceh campaign]], later taking part in other colonial expeditions in the [[Dutch East Indies|Netherlands Indies]].

## Significance

Mirandolle's quick assumption of command at [[Adjoen]] after [[Lieutenant Ahn]]'s wounding illustrates the flexibility of Dutch marine units in the field — a factor contributing to their ability to maintain pressure on Acehnese positions throughout the [[Aceh War|Aceh campaign]]. His subsequent wounding at [[Daroe]] on [[30 January]] [[1876]] places him among the marine officers decorated or wounded in the closing phase of the [[1875]]–[[1876]] expedition.
