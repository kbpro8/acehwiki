**Mirandolle** was a Dutch marine officer who assumed command of **Lieutenant Ahn**’s artillery‑covering section after Ahn was wounded at **Adjoen** on [[30 December]] [[1875]] (see [[page 135]]).

## Command Role

He led the marine section in providing fire support for the **VIIIe battalion** during the assault on the kampong, coordinating with **Lieutenant Cambier** and **Captain Cramer von Baumgarten** (see [[page 135]]).

## Significance

Mirandolle’s quick assumption of command illustrates the flexibility of Dutch marine units in the field, a factor contributing to their ability to maintain pressure on Acehnese positions.
