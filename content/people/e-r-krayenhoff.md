**E. R. Krayenhoff** was a controller of the Indies government who in [[September 1871]] went aboard the *Djambi* to [[Acheh|Atjeh]] to deliver a letter from the Governor of [[Sumatra's Westkust|Sumatra's Westkust]] to the [[Sultan of Aceh|sultan]]. His mission was the immediate prelude to the long series of negotiations and confrontations that culminated in the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] of [[1873]]-[[1903|1903]], and was undertaken at a time when the previous sultan had died in [[1870]] and had been succeeded by the fourteen-year-old [[Aladin Mahmood Shah|Aladin Mahmood Shah]] under the regency of the Arab sayyid [[Habib Abdoerrahman|Habib Abdoe'r Rachman]].

## The Mission of September 1871

In [[1870]] the Indies government had decided on a more vigorous course of action, in order to fulfil its obligations and to maintain safety in the Acehnese waters. Warships were sent thither to exercise sharper supervision, and the controller E. R. Krayenhoff went in [[September 1871]] aboard the *Djambi* to Atjeh to deliver a letter from the Governor of Sumatra's Westkust to the sultan. "Just in 1870 the previous sultan had died and had been succeeded by the fourteen-year-old Aladin Mahmood Shah, under the voogdij of the first rijksbestuurder, the Arabier Habib Abdoe'r Rachman. The latter assumed a high tone, demanded the return of former Acehnese territory on the east and west coasts of Sumatra, and threatened the intervention of England, France and Turkey. Although he later became more polite, it appeared that at the same time he had asked the Governor of the Straits Settlements for advice and help against us."

## Significance

Krayenhoff's mission of [[September 1871]] was the first direct Dutch approach to the new sultan and his regent, and its failure — the Habib's high tone, his demands, and his secret approaches to the British — set the course for the events that led to the [[Aceh Envoys' Visit to Singapore (January 1873)|Acehnese envoys' visit to Singapore]] of [[January 1873|January 1873]] and to the [[First Aceh Expedition (1873)|first Aceh expedition]] of [[April 1873|April 1873]].
