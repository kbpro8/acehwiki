**Franciscus Darlang** was a Dutch military officer and Captain in the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]].

## Military Career and Service in Aceh

Darlang was awarded an Honorary Sabre (*Eeresabel*) in [[1898]] and the Knight 4th Class Military William Order (*M.W.O.*) in [[1904]] as a First Lieutenant. He is commemorated with a stone memorial bust at [[Kerkhof Peutjoet]] in [[Kuta Raja]].
