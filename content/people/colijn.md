**Colijn** — *luitenant Colijn*, *later minister, staatsman* — was the Dutch officer who in [[1902]] *was opgerukt via Isaq, doch had niet verder kunnen komen dan even bij den Boer ni Intem-Intem en toen moeten terugtrekken* (had advanced via Isaq, but could not get further than just by the *Boer ni Intem-Intem* and then had to withdraw). The *Boer ni Intem-Intem* was the mountain-pass through which the road to the Gajoeland lay; on it the Gajoes had built their *zwaar versterkte bentengs*. Colijn's *terugtrekking* — his retreat — was, in Zentgraff's verdict, one of the principal causes of the Gajoes' false confidence when [[G.C.E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] came in [[1904]].

## The 1902 advance via Isaq

In [[1902]] Colijn advanced *via Isaq* — the northern approach to the Gajoeland, by the *Boer ni Intem-Intem* (the *Mountain of Intem-Intem*). *Dit heeft er veel toe bijgedragen dat de Gajoes in 1904 besloten tot uiterst verzet; zij dachten dat de Kompeuni dan wel weer afdeinzen zou.* — This contributed much to the Gajoes' decision in 1904 to offer extreme resistance; they thought the Kompeuni would then again recoil. Colijn's *terugtrekking* was, for the Gajoes, proof that the Dutch could not force the *Boer ni Intem-Intem*.

## The Gajoes' false confidence in 1904

When Van Daalen's *colonne* set out in [[March]] [[1904]], the Gajoes *hadden het niet ongelukkiger kunnen treffen* — could not have hit it more unfortunately. Their *zwaar versterkte bentengs op den Boer ni Intem-Intem* — the *zwaar versterkte* (heavily fortified) bentengs on the *Boer ni Intem-Intem* — were *den vijand nutteloos geweest* (useless to the enemy) because Van Daalen approached from a different direction, by an *omtrekkende beweging* (flanking movement) through wild uninhabited *bergland*. *Colijn was in 1902 opgerukt via Isaq, doch had niet verder kunnen komen dan even bij den Boer ni Intem-Intem en toen moeten terugtrekken. Dit heeft er veel toe bijgedragen dat de Gajoes in 1904 besloten tot uiterst verzet; zij dachten dat de Kompeuni dan wel weer afdeinzen zou.* — Colijn advanced via Isaq in 1902, but could not get further than just by the Boer ni Intem-Intem and then had to withdraw. This contributed much to the Gajoes' decision in 1904 to offer extreme resistance; they thought the Kompeuni would then again recoil.

## At the 1930 jubilee dinner

Colijn — *Min. Staat Colijn* (Minister of State Colijn) — appears at no. 5 in the 40-year jubilee photograph of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Korps Maréchaussée]] of [[2 April]] [[1930]]: *„5. Min. Staat Colijn"*. He stands between *„4. Luit. Gen. Kroesen"* and *„6. Luit. Gen. H. N. A. Swart"*. The dinner was the *Kandoeri Rajeu* at the *Witte* in [[The Hague]] — the great *slametan* of the 40-year commemoration of the korps.

## Significance

Colijn's *terugtrekking* of [[1902]] is the strategic prelude to the [[Gajoetocht (1904)|Gajoetocht]] of [[1904]]. Without that earlier failure the Gajoes would not have believed the *Boer ni Intem-Intem* invincible; without that belief, they might not have resolved on *uiterst verzet*. The *stalen* Van Daalen's *omtrekkende beweging* was, in this sense, an answer to Colijn's *terugtrekking*. Colijn's later career — minister, staatsman — is, for Zentgraff, a footnote to the early Atjeh career.
