**Wintgens** was a member of the States General who, on [[16 November]] [[1882]], took part in the interpellation over the very worrying state of Atjeh. In [[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]]'s review of that debate the aim of the members [[Rutgers van Rozenburg]], [[Insinger]], Wintgens and [[A. de Casembroot]] is described as having been "to shake government and Chamber awake over the lamentable state of things in Atjeh" (see [[Insinger]]).
