**C. A. Bannink** was a Dutch military writer whose series *Grepen uit den Atjeh-oorlog* ("Episodes from the Aceh War"), printed in the *Orgaan der Officiersvereeniging* in [[1936]], is a frequently cited secondary source for the middle phase of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. Bannink drew on the eyewitness accounts of surviving officers, including the lecture of [[T. W. G. Merz|Eerste-Luitenant T. W. G. Merz]] of October [[1935]], and his work was in turn used by [[D. van den Berg|D. van den Berg]] in the [[1944]] biography of [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Generaal Snijders]].

## The Series *Grepen uit den Atjeh-oorlog*

The series *Grepen uit den Atjeh-oorlog* appeared in the *Orgaan der Officiersvereeniging* in [[1936]]. It is an episodic treatment, drawing on oral recollection and surviving documents, of operations of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] from the [[First Aceh Expedition (1873)|Eerste Atjeh-expeditie]] of [[April 1873|April]] [[1873]] onward. The episode devoted to the [[Missigit Longbatta|Missigit Longbatta]] operation of [[31 December]] [[31 December]] [[1874]]–[[January 1875|January]] [[1875]] cites at length the [[1935]] lecture of [[T. W. G. Merz|T. W. G. Merz]] on the construction of the redoubt by [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede-Luitenant C. J. Snijders]].

## Use by Later Writers

The biographer [[D. van den Berg|D. van den Berg]] refers readers to Bannink's series as a principal source for the participation of [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] in the war: "Voor de deelneming van den Luitenant SNIJDERS aan krijgsbedrijven zie men o.m. Grepen uit den Atjeh-oorlog door C. A. BANNINK in het Orgaan der Officiersvereeniging van 1936." Van den Berg's quotation of the Merz lecture on the Missigit Longbatta work is taken via Bannink.
