**Willem Joseph, baron van Ghent** was a Dutch **[[admiral]]** and the first colonel of the **[[Royal Netherlands Marine Corps|Mariniers]]**, who in **[[1668]]** commanded a squadron that intercepted the English warship **[[Merlin]]** near the Dutch coast. The incident, in which van Ghent refused to salute the English vessel, became a **casus belli** that heightened Anglo‑Dutch tensions during the lead‑up to the **[[Rampjaar|Disaster Year]]** [[1672]].

## Connection to Aceh
The heightened naval rivalry between England and the **[[United Provinces (Dutch Republic)]]** strained the **VOC**’s ability to protect its merchant fleet travelling to the **[[Sultanate of Aceh]]**, illustrating how European conflicts reverberated in Southeast Asian trade.
