**Batteké, Lieutenant** was a Dutch marine officer who remained with a detachment of thirty marines on [[Poeloe Bras]] after the majority of the force returned to [[Fort de Kock]] in July [[1874]]. His small garrison continued to hold the island while other marine companies were reassigned.

## Role on Poeloe Bras

Batteké’s command exemplified the Dutch practice of maintaining a minimal occupational presence on strategic islands after the main expedition had moved on.
