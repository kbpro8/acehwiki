**Abraha** was an Aksumite (Abessynian) governor of Yemen in the early sixth century CE, famed in Islamic tradition for his failed expedition against the Kaʿba in Mecca. The source mentions him on [[83]] in the context of the *Sūra al‑Fīl* (the “Chapter of the Elephant”), noting that “Bedoeld is de Abessijnse heerser Abraha uit Zuid‑Arabie, die een expeditie naar Mekka op touw zou hebben gezet, volgens de legende in het jaar waarin de profeet Muhammad geboren werd, 570.”

Although not directly an Acehnese figure, Abraha’s story entered Acehnese Islamic literature and is recited in sermons (*khutbah*) as an example of divine intervention.

## Historical background

Abraha’s campaign, dated to [[570]], is narrated in the Qur’an (Sūra al‑Fīl, [[105]] of the Qur’an) and in later Malay‑Acehnese chronicles.
