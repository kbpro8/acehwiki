**W. Leendertz** was a former captain of Artillery of the Netherlands-Indies army who, with the former Maréchaussée sergeant [[W. J. Mosselman]], was one of the first two Netherlands-Indies military aviators to obtain the *Internationaal vliegbrevet* (international flying licence).

## The first two military aviators

The [[1930]] Maréchaussée jubilee number records, in its portrait notice of Mosselman, that after transferring in [[1916]] to the *[[Luchtvaart-Afdeeling (Netherlands Indies)|Luchtvaart-Afdeeling]]* he "behoort met den ex-kapitein der Artillerie W. Leendertz tot de eerste twee militaire vliegers die het Internationaal vliegbrevet behaalden" — belongs, with the former captain of Artillery W. Leendertz, to the first two military aviators to obtain the international flying licence.

The notice supplies no further particulars of Leendertz.
