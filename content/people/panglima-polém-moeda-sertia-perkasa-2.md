**Panglima Polém Moeda Sertia Perkasa** was a Son of Sultan Iskander Moeda, progenitor of the Polém lineage mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. 

## Role

Son of [[Sultan Iskander Moeda]], progenitor of the Polém lineage

## Significance

[[Panglima Polém Moeda Sertia Perkasa]] appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
