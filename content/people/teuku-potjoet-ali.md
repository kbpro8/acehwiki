**Teuku Potjoet Ali** was panglima and de facto *uleebalang* of the [[III Mukim]] during the early [[Aceh War]].

## Role in the campaign

According to [[Jan van Swieten]], he had been appointed military chief by his brother-in-law [[Teuku Langgoegoeb]], then killed him and took the leadership while retaining the title *panglima*. He was said to have considered submission after the [[Capture of the Kraton of Aceh]], but died on [[30 January]] [[1874]] before a promised declaration.

## Significance

The source places [[Teuku Potjoet Ali]] within the contested military and political history of the [[Aceh War]].
