**Teungku Hasbullah Indrapura** was an Atjèhnese Ulama who, together with [[Teungku Hadji Hasan of Krueng Kalé|Teungku Hadji Hasan of Krueng Kalé]], led an internal opposition within the [[Poesa|Poesa]] against the uncontrolled appropriation, by triumphant Ulama, of property confiscated from the defeated *[[Uleebalang|oelèëbalang]]* class during the [[Ulama–Uleëbalang social revolution in Atjeh (1945–1946)|social revolution]] of [[1945]]–[[1946]]. Henri J. H. Alers's *Om een rode of groene Merdeka* records the two men's objection that such seizures were unlawful acts of plunder — "*haram*" by the standards of Islamic law — and credits their advocacy, alongside Teungku Hadji Hasan's, with the central government's decision to establish the [[Madjelis Penimbang|Madjelis Penimbang]], the body intended to review and redistribute the disputed properties.
