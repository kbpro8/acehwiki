**Ch. Polman van den Broek** was the Dutch *controleur* at [[Sigli]] during the [[Sigli expedition (August 1897)|Sigli expedition]] of [[1897]] and the subsequent operations in [[Pidië|Pidie]] in [[1897]]-[[1898]]. He was also the creator of the sketch maps on which the operations in Pidie were conducted, and his photographs of the [[Sigli expedition (August 1897)|Sigli expedition]] — including the image of [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] standing on a munitions box directing the action, and the image of the fleeing defenders of the most western fortification of the [[Peukan Baroe|Peukan Baroe]]-[[Peukan Sòt|Peukan Sòt]] series — were preserved in the published account of [[H. T. Damsté|Damsté]].

## The photographs

Polman van den Broek's photographs include: (1) an image showing [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] standing on a munitions box, directing the operations during the second day after the expulsion of the enemy from Peukan Baroe-Peukan Sòt, with the second battalion and the marechaussee marching along Koelo Soekoen over the dry sawahs in southern direction; (2) an image of [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]'s adjutant and another officer on the bridge (over the creek connecting Koeala Baraboh and Koeala Sigli) by the most western fortification of the Peukan Baroe-Peukan Sòt series, with the garrison having just fled on the approach of the Dutch troops.
