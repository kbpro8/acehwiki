**Radja Boerhan Oedin** was an informant whose reports on Acehnese preparations were used by [[Jan van Swieten]] before the [[Second Dutch Expedition to Aceh]].

## Role in the campaign

He reported a determined wartime mood in the [[Kraton of Aceh]], described coastal works and river defences, and estimated that the Sultan expected about 60,000 defenders. He judged that capture of the Kraton would end the conflict; the subsequent war showed that this prediction was mistaken.

## Significance

The source places [[Radja Boerhan Oedin]] within the contested military and political history of the [[Aceh War]].
