**J. H. Hengeveld** was a Dutch geologist or mining engineer whose [[1918]]/[[1919]] report on the coal occurrences of northern [[Sumatra]] — cited in the standard form "HENGEVELD, 1918/1919" — is one of the principal sources for [[J. Zwierzycki|Zwierzycki]]'s [[1922]] inventory of Sumatran coal localities, and accordingly for [[Van Bemmelen's The Geology of Indonesia (1949)|Van Bemmelen's *Geology of Indonesia*]] ([[1949]]) in its treatment of the [[Coal in Atjeh|Atjeh coal occurrences]]. Hengeveld's report is specifically cited for the [[Langkat]] coal localities of [[Besitang River]] (No. 5), [[Lepan]] (No. 6) and [[Sarangan]] (No. 7), where the seams are 0.5–3 ft thick and "quality good".

## The 1918/1919 coal report

The recoverable contents of Hengeveld's report, as cited by Zwierzycki and reproduced by Van Bemmelen, cover the Langkat-residency coal localities that lie immediately to the south of the Atjeh coal zone. These localities — Besitang River, Lepan and Sarangan — are geologically continuous with the East-Atjeh coal series, and Hengeveld's report on them accordingly supplies the immediate southern extension of the Atjeh coal record. His recorded analytical figures for these Langkat coals — moisture 1–1.5 %, ash 12–24 %, coke 60–70 %, calorific value 6000–7000 — are the standard colonial benchmarks for the northern-Sumatran Paleogene coal series.

## Place in the Atjeh coal record

Hengeveld's report occupies, in the documentary sequence of Atjeh and northern-Sumatran coal exploration, the middle position between the foundational survey of [[Rinaud]] ([[1885]]) and the consolidated inventory of [[J. Zwierzycki|Zwierzycki]] ([[1922]]). The sequence runs:

1. [[Rinaud|Rinaud]] ([[1885]]): the West-Atjeh coal seams (Krueng Kluet, Tapatuan, Krueng Teunom).
2. [[Philippi|Philippi]] ([[1918]]): the coastal-region coal occurrences south of Atjeh (Ajer Padang, Ajer Lais, Ajer Besi, Ajer Nepal, etc.).
3. **Hengeveld ([[1918]]/[[1919]])**: the Langkat-residency coal localities (Besitang River, Lepan, Sarangan).
4. [[H. Fryling|Fryling]] ([[1921]]): additional Langkat and East-coast data.
5. [[Van Loheezen|Van Loheezen]] ([[1929]]): later supplementary observations.
6. [[J. Zwierzycki|Zwierzycki]] ([[1922]]): the consolidated inventory.

Hengeveld's contribution to this sequence is the systematic analytical data — moisture, ash, coke, calorific value — for the Langkat coals, which supplied the quantitative benchmarks against which the Atjeh coal occurrences were subsequently assessed.
