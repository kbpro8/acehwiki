**Majoor Romswinckel** (also read as *Riomswinckel*) was a major of the [[Koninklijk Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Netherlands Indies Army]] who arrived at [[Acheh|Atjeh]] in December [[1875]]. In the manuscript of the contrôleur [[Kroesen (controleur at Oleh-leh)|Kroesen]] — quoted by [[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] in his 1886 pamphlet — the major figures in the opening of the sampan incident that Kroesen gave as a small but telling specimen of the Atjehnese character: newly arrived at Atjeh, Romswinckel asked the contrôleur [[Tadema (controleur at Oleh-leh)|Tadema]] at [[Oleh-leh]] to help him to a vessel for his baggage; Tadema turned to [[Toekoe Nja Mohamad (Oleh-leh 1875)|Toekoe Nja Mohamad]], one of the Atjehnese greats who happened to be with him, who hailed a countryman for a sampan and received, as Kroesen recorded with astonishment, the answer from which Dutch readers would learn something of the morals of the people against whom they were at war (see [[Toekoe Nja Mohamad (Oleh-leh 1875)]] for the anecdote itself).
