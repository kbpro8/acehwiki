**W. Muurling** was the captain of the General Staff whom [[H. N. A. Swart]] sent to the [[Gajò Loeös]] with the 1st Division of the *[[Corps of Military Constabulary of Atjeh and Dependencies|Korps Marechaussee]]* when resistance revived there after painful setbacks of the infantry garrison, and who as Civil Authority at [[Blang Kedjeren]] restored order with his two well-known Atjeh lieutenants [[C. A. Snell|Snell]] and [[Van Eck]].

## The Gajò Loeös

The situation was most unfavourable in September [[1909]]: throughout the sub-division bands of ill-disposed men wandered, terrorising the population and threatening with death everyone on good terms with the administration. "With this in view the infantry garrison of [[Blang Kedjerèn]] was replaced by military constabulary (the first division under captain Muurling), while a mobile column of three brigades (under [[Kaniess]]) was sent to the western part of the sub-division."

Du Croo recorded Swart's practice of putting the right man in the right place: "when, after some painful setbacks of the infantry garrison, the resistance in the [[Gajò Loeös]] flared up again, he sent the 1st Division there under the command of the General Staff captain Muurling, who with his two well-known Atjeh lieutenants Snell and Van Eck soon restored order."

In May [[1911]] the [[Kedjoerön Petiambang]] and fourteen prominent chiefs visited, under Muurling's leadership, the *jaarmarkttentoonstelling* at [[Koeta Radja]]; the administration was temporarily conducted by lieutenant Snell, who while acting brought in eight more *uitgewekenen* and four muzzle-loaders. During that visit [[Rödjö Tjoet Pasér]] with his *panglimas* and the *[[blah]]*-heads of [[Pasér]] and [[Reröbö]] solemnly begged the Governor's forgiveness.
