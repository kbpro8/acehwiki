**Raja of Gighen** was the ruling indigenous chieftain (*radja*) of the coastal port state of [[Gighen]] along the northeast coast of Sumatra during the 1870s.

## Submission to General van Swieten in 1873

During the [[Second Aceh Expedition]], following the fall of coastal redoubts near [[Meuraxa]], the [[Raja of Gighen]] drafted and signed the [[Raja of Gighen Letter of Submission]] on [[30 December]] [[1873]] (11 Dzoelkangidah 1290 AH). 

In this letter, he formally offered the submission of [[Gighen]] to Commander-in-Chief General [[J. van Swieten]] and the [[Dutch East Indies Government]], a major diplomatic event cited in *Atjeh gedurende de tweede expeditie en later* ([[1876]]) as evidence of colonial progress in coastal Aceh.
