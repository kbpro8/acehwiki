**Teukoe Bin Blang Pidië** was a Westkust chief who surrendered mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Submitted during Governor Swart's administration on the Westkust.

## Role

[[Westkust]] chief who surrendered

## Account from Jongejans

Submitted during [[H.E. Swart|Governor Swart]]'s administration on the [[Westkust]].

## Significance

Teukoe Bin Blang Pidië appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
