**De Kock, General‑majoor** was the commander of the Dutch forces during the second expedition against [[Palembang]] in [[1821]]. He directed the naval squadron that included the frigates [[Dageraad]] and [[Van der Werff]] and coordinated the riverine assault on the Soensang River (page 94).

## Leadership of the 1821 Expedition

De Kock’s campaign faced numerous obstacles, such as obstructed waterways, fortified enemy batteries on the Peladjoe, and the island of [[Gombora]] being used as a defensive position. Despite these challenges, the expedition succeeded in forcing the enemy to retreat.
