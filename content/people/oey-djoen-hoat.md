**Oey Djoen Hoat** was a Netherlands-Indies physician who investigated the occurrence of *[[Xerophthalmie|xerophthalmie]]* in the *Regentschap* [[Keboemen]] on [[Java]], and whose figures Dr. [[C. E. Gomperts]] set beside his own from [[Groot-Atjeh]] as a comparison strongly in Java's favour.

## The Keboemen figures

Oey Djoen Hoat found among 4,532 boys examined 88 cases of xerophthalmia, and among 3,595 girls 20 cases — that is, about 2 per cent and ½ per cent of the children examined respectively.

Gomperts remarked that these values "steken zeer gunstig af bij onze statistiek" — compare very favourably with our statistics. His own survey of [[1937]] had found 459 cases among 11,400 children in Groot-Atjeh, about 4 per cent, with the rate among boys aged 1–2 reaching 7½ per cent.

The comparison forms part of Gomperts's wider observation that the proportions in Groot-Atjeh appeared to differ from those on Java.
