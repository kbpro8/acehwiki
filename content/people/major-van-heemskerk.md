**Major Van Heemskerk** was a senior officer of the Dutch army who commanded the XIV bataljon infantry during the 1868 assaults on Acehnese fortified villages, notably the attacks on [[Bandjar]] and [[Kali-Anget]]. His battalion launched two “fruitless” assaults on enemy positions before being reinforced by the marine landing division.

## Operations in Aceh (1868)

- **First assault**: The XIV battalion under Van Heemskerk attempted a direct attack on the enemy’s position at Bandjar but was repulsed.
- **Second assault**: A repeat attack also failed, prompting the deployment of the marine landing division under [[Lieutenant P. Spanjaard]] and [[Lieutenant D. A. van Vuuren]].

## Outcome

After the marine forces succeeded, Van Heemskerk’s battalion finally occupied the kraton (royal palace) and the nearby kampong of Kali‑Anget, leading to the surrender of the local leader [[Ida Madé Rahi]].

## Significance

Van Heemskerk’s coordination with marine forces illustrates the joint army‑marine operations that were essential to Dutch success in Aceh.
