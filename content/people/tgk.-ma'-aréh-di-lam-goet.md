**Tgk. Ma' Aréh di Lam Goet** was the leader of the last remnant of the [[Tirò Teungkoes|Tirò band]] in [[Pidië]], taken prisoner in January [[1913]].

He appears in the [[1913]] list of the last unsubmitted resistance leaders. In January of that year, after the remnants of the Tirò band had twice been surprised, his hiding-place was found by *[[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|marechaussée]]* from [[Lam Meulò]]: "the Teungkoe was taken prisoner, five men fell. And then the rest reported the very next day, surrendering all firearms: Central Pidië had been cleared."
