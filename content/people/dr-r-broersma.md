**Dr. R. Broersma** was a Dutch economist and author of *Atjeh als land voor handel en bedrijf* ([[1925]]), the economic survey of the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] republished from articles entitled "Verkenningen in Atjeh" in the newspaper [[De Locomotief]] between [[July]] and [[October]] [[1923]].

## Explorations in Aceh

Broersma stated that his explorations aimed to examine the condition of enterprise and trade in [[Atjeh]] and the means to strengthen the Acehnese population. He traveled from the south via the [[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij|Deli-spoor]] and [[Atjeh Tram|Atjeh-tram]] meeting at [[Besitang]], entering [[Temiang]] at [[Koeala Simpang]], then northward through [[Langsa]], [[Peureula]], [[Idi]], [[Lho Soekon]], [[Lho Seumawe]], [[Bireuen]], and into the [[Gajo-landen]] via the [[Gajo-weg]] to [[Takengon]] and [[Laut Tawar]].

He argued that the regions he saw were poor in land and poor in people, and that the "richness of Atjeh" still had to be created. Help from the Netherlands administration should consist only in giving correct indications and smoothing tracks where progress was possible.

## Critique of contemporary propaganda

In his foreword, Broersma criticized the jubilee publication [[Vijftig jaren economische staatkunde in Atjeh]] ([[1923]]) by journalist [[J. Langhout]], which he described as ordered advertising supported by the Department of Colonies counting on [[H. N. A. Swart]] as guardian. He presented his own work as free from ulterior motives, sprung from own observation, discussions with [[Uleebalang|oeléëbalangs]] and officials, and reading of official documents.
