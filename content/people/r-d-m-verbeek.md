**R. D. M. Verbeek** was a Dutch geologist and author of *Topographische en geologische beschrijving van Sumatra's Westkust* ("Topographical and Geological Description of Sumatra's West Coast"), published at The Hague in [[1883]], in royal octavo format with an atlas. The work was offered in [[1902]] by the bookseller Martinus Nijhoff at 25 florins in commemoration of the third centenary of the Dutch East India Company.

## Work

Verbeek's geological survey of Sumatra's West Coast was a foundational work in the geology of the island, covering the coastal regions that included the western coast of [[Atjeh]]. The work, accompanied by an atlas of maps and geological sections, provided the first systematic geological description of this part of Sumatra, including areas that had only recently been brought under effective Dutch control following the [[Aceh War|Atjeh War]]. Verbeek was also known for his geological work on Java (together with Fennema) following the [[1883]] eruption of Krakatoa.
