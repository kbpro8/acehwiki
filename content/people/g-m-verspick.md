**G. M. Verspick** was a Dutch writer on the [[Aceh War|Atjeh War]] who published the pamphlet *Generaal van Swieten en de waarheid* (General van Swieten and the truth) as a contribution to the public controversy in the Netherlands over the conduct and early course of the war. He is not to be confused with General [[Verspijck]] (also spelled Verspyck), who served as second-in-command during the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)|second Aceh expedition]].

## Place in the Controversy over Van Swieten

Verspick's pamphlet belonged to the body of polemical literature provoked by the early campaigns and by the policy of conciliation associated with General [[Jan van Swieten|van Swieten]]. [[P. Brooshooft|Brooshooft]] listed it among the pamphlet sources for his Brooshooft (1886), alongside [[Jan van Swieten|van Swieten]]'s own *De waarheid over onze vestiging in Atjeh* and [[J. J. W. E. Versteege|Versteege]]'s *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk in zake het Atjeh-vraagstuk*.
