---

title: Ni Towong
tags: [person, legend]
---

A Javanese spirit invoked in a ritual using a creel-and-broom figure with a coconut-shell head, placed in a cemetery on Thursday evening amid burning incense and believed to answer questions about marriage and healing by gesture — a superstition Hurgronje notes survives in children's games long after belief in it faded.

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
