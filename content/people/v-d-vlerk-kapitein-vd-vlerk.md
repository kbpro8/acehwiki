**v. d. Vlerk (kapitein v.d. Vlerk)** was a Captain, wounded by Teungkoe di Mata Ië mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Slightly wounded when Teungkoe di Mata Ië's group beslipped a bivak; one marechaussée was killed.

## Role

Captain, wounded by [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] di [[Mata Ië]]

## Account from Jongejans

Slightly wounded when [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] di [[Mata Ië]]'s group beslipped a [[Bivak|bivak]]; one [[Marechaussée|marechaussée]] was killed.

## Significance

v. d. Vlerk (kapitein v.d. Vlerk) appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
