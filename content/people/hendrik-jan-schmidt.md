**Hendrik Jan Schmidt** was a Dutch military officer and Captain in the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]].

## Military Career and Service in Aceh

Schmidt was decorated repeatedly for bravery in Aceh: Knight in the Order of Oranje-Nassau ([[1902]]), Knight 4th Class Military William Order ([[1908]]), Honorary Sabre ([[1911]]), and Knight 3rd Class Military William Order ([[1913]]).
