**Teukoe Sabi** was a Onderhoofd (sub-chief) of Oelèë Glé mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Wished not to be placed under the supremacy of the oelèëbalang of Samalanga; part of the 'onderhoofden-actie' separatism phenomenon of recent years.

## Role

Onderhoofd (sub-chief) of [[Oelèë Glé]]

## Account from Jongejans

Wished not to be placed under the supremacy of the [[oelèëbalang]] of [[Samalanga]]; part of the '[[Operette van Tjaleuë (the underhoofden-action interception)|onderhoofden-actie]]' separatism phenomenon of recent years.

## Significance

Teukoe Sabi appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
