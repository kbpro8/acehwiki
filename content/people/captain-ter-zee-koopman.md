**Captain-terr zee Koopman** commanded the Dutch naval squadron that provided gunfire support for the marine landing on [[5 April]] [[1873]]. His fleet, consisting of the warships **Antwerpen**, **Marnix**, **Soerabaja**, and **Sumatra**, opened fire at [[6 o’clock]] [[morning]] on the landing beaches, suppressing Acehnese positions and allowing the marine companies to disembark with minimal opposition.

Koopman’s coordination with the land forces under [[General-Major Köhler]] and [[Captain-terr zee Binkes]] was praised in contemporary dispatches for its effectiveness.
