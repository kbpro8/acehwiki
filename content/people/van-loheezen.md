**Van Loheezen** was a Dutch geologist or mining engineer whose [[1929]] report on the coal occurrences of northern [[Sumatra]] — cited in the standard form "VAN LOHEEZEN, 1929" — is the latest of the pre-[[Van Bemmelen's The Geology of Indonesia (1949)|Van Bemmelen]] sources on the [[Coal in Atjeh|Atjeh and Langkat coal localities]], and is specifically cited in [[J. Zwierzycki|Zwierzycki]]'s consolidated inventory (and reproduced by Van Bemmelen) for the [[Langkat]] coal localities of [[Besitang River]] (No. 5), [[Lepan]] (No. 6) and [[Sarangan]] (No. 7).

## The 1929 coal report

Van Loheezen's [[1929]] report, as cited by Zwierzycki and reproduced by Van Bemmelen, supplies supplementary observations on the Langkat-residency coal occurrences that lie immediately to the south of the Atjeh coal zone. For the localities at Besitang River, Lepan and Sarangan — where the seams are 0.5–3 ft thick and "quality good" — Van Loheezen's report is listed alongside the earlier reports of [[J. H. Hengeveld|Hengeveld]] ([[1918]]/[[1919]]), [[H. Fryling|Fryling]] ([[1921]]) and Zwierzycki's own [[1922]] synthesis. Van Loheezen's contribution is accordingly the post-1922 supplement that brought the northern-Sumatran coal record up to date for the late colonial period.

## Place in the Atjeh coal record

Van Loheezen's report occupies, in the documentary sequence of Atjeh and northern-Sumatran coal exploration, the latest position before the consolidated Van Bemmelen survey of [[1949]]. The sequence runs: [[Rinaud]] ([[1885]]) → [[Philippi]] ([[1918]]) → [[J. H. Hengeveld|Hengeveld]] ([[1918]]/[[1919]]) → [[H. Fryling|Fryling]] ([[1921]]) → [[J. Zwierzycki|Zwierzycki]] ([[1922]]) → **Van Loheezen** ([[1929]]) → Van Bemmelen ([[1949]]).
