**Van Heutsz, J. B.** was a Dutch army officer who, after serving as a junior officer in the early phases of the [[Atjeh Expedition 1873-1876|Atjeh Expedition]], rose to prominence for his “field‑craft talent” and insight into the “mestuursmatregels” (governance rules) that were deemed necessary to break the Acehnese resistance. His policies eventually led to the recognition by the Acehnese that cooperation with the Dutch colonial administration served their own interests, bringing an end to the longest and bloodiest wars in the Indonesian archipelago.

## Early career

Van Heutsz first appeared in the records of the 1874 campaign, serving under senior commanders such as [[General major Verspijk]] and later distinguishing himself in the operations around the [[Kraton (Aceh)]].

## Legacy

He later became the architect of the “*pacification*” of Aceh, culminating in the 1903‑1904 final subjugation of the sultanate.
