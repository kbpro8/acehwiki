---
title: Teuku Lam Nga
aliases: [Teuku Che']
tags: [person, warrior]
---

One of the fighting heroes of the Hikayat Prang Gompeuni, who fought with valor in the defense before being defeated and killed near Peukan Bada — a death blamed on the Habib's treachery. He tried in vain to hold back people returning to Dutch-occupied territory.
