---
title: Tuan di Lungkeueng
tags: [person, saint]
---

A saint whose tomb, surrounded by thick roots, lies in Blang Bintang (XXVI Mukims). During his life he watered grazing flocks at midday; sick cattle are now brought to his tomb, or earth from it mixed with their drinking water.

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
