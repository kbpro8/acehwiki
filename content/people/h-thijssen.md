**H. Thijssen** was a **[[korporaal der mariniers|corporal of the marines]]** who participated in the **[[Expedition to Retch 1858|1858 assault]]**. He was among the 32 marines nominated for the **[[Militaire Willemsorde]]** after the campaign, though the nomination was not acted upon.

## Recognition

Thijssen’s bravery was noted in the official report of the expedition, but no award was ultimately granted.
