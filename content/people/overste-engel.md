**Overste Engel** was a lieutenant colonel (*overste*) of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] who commanded infantry battalions during [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]] in [[1875]] and [[1876]].

## Field Operations in 1876

As recorded in official order-of-battle reports on [[30 January]] [[1876]] in *Atjeh gedurende de tweede expeditie en later*, [[Overste Engel]] commanded the [[8th Infantry Battalion]] in mobile field columns operating out of [[Pekan Badak]] and Fort [[Sinangri]]. His forces engaged in continuous skirmishing against Acehnese guerrilla bands along the Aceh River plain under General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]].
