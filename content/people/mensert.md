**Mennert** (spelled *Mennert* in the source) was a **Lieutenant ter zee le klasse** who suffered a severe wound during the **[[30 March]] [[1893]]** engagement on the **Tamiang** river. He was subsequently replaced as commander of the landing division by **[[Broers (naval officer)]]**.

## Role

Mennert’s injury illustrates the intense combat experienced by Dutch naval officers during the **Aceh War** river campaigns.
