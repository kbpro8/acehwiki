**J. C. Heynen** was a Dutch non-commissioned officer and Sergeant Brigade Commander in the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]].

## Military Career and Service in Aceh

Born in [[1869]], Heynen was awarded the Knight 4th Class Military William Order (*M.W.O.*) on [[18 February]] [[1902]] and elevated to Knight 3rd Class M.W.O. on [[31 July]] [[1902]], becoming only the third NCO in the history of the corps to receive this high honor. He later transferred to the colonial police force, serving as Adjunct-Hoofdcommissaris and receiving the Order of Oranje-Nassau.
