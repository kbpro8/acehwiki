---
title: Eumpee Lulu
tags: [tokoh, legenda]
---

A legendary old woman assailed by raging thirst, who prayed for more water until torrential rain fell; she drank the rain as it fell and walked into the sea, transforming into the mountain now named after her near the IV Mukims of the XXV. Her descendants are said to include a woman who inherits the art of rain-making, performing a frenzied procession to the sea during droughts.
