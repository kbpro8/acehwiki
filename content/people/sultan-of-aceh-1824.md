**The Sultan of Aceh (captured 1824)** was the reigning monarch of the Aceh Sultanate who was seized by Dutch forces during their 1824 riverine expedition and subsequently exiled to [[Ternate]]. His capture marked a decisive moment in the Dutch effort to subdue Aceh and restore order on [[Sumatra]] after years of unrest.

## Capture and Exile

After the Dutch forces broke through the river defenses and reached the royal palace, the sultan was taken prisoner and transferred to the island of [[Ternate]] where he remained in exile.

## Significance

The removal of the sultan temporarily weakened Aceh’s central authority, allowing the Dutch to consolidate their control over the lower reaches of the Aceh river system.
