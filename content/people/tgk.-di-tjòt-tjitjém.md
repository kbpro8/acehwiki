**Tgk. di Tjòt Tjitjém** was the well-known band-head of the [[Meergebied]] whose successor was [[Tgk. Léjman]].

Tgk. Léjman's whole band was destroyed by [[Jenae]] in February [[1911]] with thirty-six killed and fourteen rifles taken.
