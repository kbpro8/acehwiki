---
title: Teungku Lam Rukam
tags: [tokoh, penulis]
---

Author of the Hikayat Pochut Muhamat, the greatest Acehnese epic, who lived in the gampong of Lam Rukam in the XXV Mukims and composed his poem about the mid-18th century, drawing on eyewitness accounts. Hurgronje considered him a literary genius of the highest order.
