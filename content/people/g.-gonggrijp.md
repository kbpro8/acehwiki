**G. Gonggrijp** — Prof. G. Gonggrijp — was the author of Chapter IV of *[[Nederlanders over de Zeeën (De Graaf et al., 1955)|Nederlanders over de Zeeën]]* ([[1955]] second edition), titled *Ned.-Indië onder de Staat en als deel van het Koninkrijk* (Netherlands India under the State and as part of the Kingdom). His chapter is the principal source of the present Aceh Wiki History articles on the nineteenth-century colonial state — including the [[Anglo-Dutch Treaty of 1824|1824 London Treaty]], the [[1857]] contract, the [[Sumatra-tractaat (1871)|1871 Sumatra-tractaat]], the [[Atjehoorlog|Atjehoorlog]] of [[1873]]-[[1904]], and the post-[[1904]] consolidation.

## Authorship of Chapter IV

In the [[1955]] edition of *Nederlanders over de Zeeën*, [[G. Gonggrijp|Gonggrijp]] was responsible for Chapter IV — the chapter covering the Indies from the assumption of colonial authority by the Dutch State (after the dissolution of the VOC in [[1799]]) through the incorporation of the Indies as part of the Kingdom of the [[Netherlands]] to the twentieth century. The chapter is, in Atjeh-related terms, the spine of the book's colonial-period narrative.

## The Atjehoorlog account

[[G. Gonggrijp|Gonggrijp]]'s account of the [[Atjehoorlog|Atjehoorlog]] of [[1873]]-[[1904]] is the longest single Atjeh-related passage in the book. It covers the [[Anglo-Dutch Treaty of 1824|1824 undertaking]] and its impossible double commitment; the [[1857]] contract and its non-observance; the [[Sumatra-tractaat (1871)|1871 Sumatra-tractaat]] and its narrow anti-piracy framing; the [[Declaration of war against the Sultan of Atjeh (1873)|1873 declaration of war]]; the two expeditions of [[1873]] (under [[General Köhler|Köhler]] and [[General Van Swieten|Van Swieten]]); the [[1880]] false dawn and the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentratie]] period; the [[1892]] blockade and the [[Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] advice; the [[1896]] defection of [[Teungku Um|Teungku Um]] and the [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] appointment; and the [[Korte verklaring|korte verklaring]] that concluded the consolidation.

## The Snouck Hurgronje analysis

[[G. Gonggrijp|Gonggrijp]]'s chapter is the source of the [[Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] analysis of Atjehnese social structure preserved in the present Aceh Wiki articles: the *kampong*/*mukim*/*uléebalang*/*sagi*/*panglima sagi* hierarchy, and the verdict that the sultan's authority over the *panglima's sagi* and the *uléebalangs* existed "alleen in naam" (in name only). The analysis is the intellectual basis of the post-[[1892]] Dutch strategy of ignoring the sultan and striking the *uléebalangs*.

## Other works

[[G. Gonggrijp|Gonggrijp]] is also the author of *Schets eener economische geschiedenis van Ned.-Indië* (Haarlem, 1949), cited in the bibliography of the [[1955]] edition of *Nederlanders over de Zeeën*.

## Significance

[[G. Gonggrijp|Gonggrijp]]'s significance for the encyclopedia of [[Acheh]] history lies in his authorship of Chapter IV of *Nederlanders over de Zeeën* — the principal source of the present Aceh Wiki articles on the nineteenth-century colonial state and the [[Atjehoorlog|Atjehoorlog]]. His narrative situates the war in the wider context of Dutch colonial state-formation in the nineteenth-century archipelago.
