**Tjhèh Saman (Teungkoe Tjhēh Saman, Teungkoe Tjhi' di Tirò, Teungkoe Tirò)** was a Oelama, leader of organised Acehnese resistance; second Teungkoe Tirò mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Relative who inherited the title of Teungkoe Tirò in 1886 after Teungkoe Moehamat Anim's death.

## Role

[[Oelama]], leader of organised Acehnese [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]]; second [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] Tirò

## Account from Jongejans

Relative who inherited the title of [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] Tirò in [[1886]] after [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] Moehamat Anim's death. Played a very great role in organising the war. Deep faith and strong character gave him enormous influence. Bound people to him and transmitted his dynamic force. Had small well-[[Ghaib (Acehnese concept)|hidden]] fortifications constructed all around the Dutch line, from which Dutch troops were harassed. Died [[1890]]; title passed to his son Ma'Anim.

## Significance

Tjhèh Saman (Teungkoe Tjhēh Saman, Teungkoe Tjhi' di Tirò, Teungkoe Tirò) appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
