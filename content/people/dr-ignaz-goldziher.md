---

title: Dr. Ignaz Goldziher
aliases: [Goldziher]
tags: [person, western-scholar]
---

Author of an essay on Moslem saint-worship in the Muhammedanische Studien (Halle [[1890]]), which Hurgronje calls "the best sketch of the Moslim saint-worship in general" and uses to open his own discussion of Acehnese saint-worship. He also wrote "Die Zahiriten" ([[1884]]), cited for the Zahirite view on touching the Quran in ritual impurity.

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
