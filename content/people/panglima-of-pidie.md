---
title: Panglima of Pidie
tags: [tokoh, karya-sastra]
---

The bravest and most distinguished general of the fleet in the Hikayat Malem Dagang, who prefers a subordinate post to chief command. He and Malem Dagang swear mutual fidelity before the final battle; clothed entirely in white as preparation for martyrdom, he is mistaken by Si Ujut for the great guru Ja Pakeh and dies a martyr's death — the main advantage Si Ujut scores in the whole battle.
