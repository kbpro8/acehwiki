**L. P. de Bussy** was the Director of the [[Afdeeling Handelsmuseum]] of the [[Koninklijke Vereeniging Koloniaal Instituut]] in [[Amsterdam]] in the late [[1920s]] and early [[1930s]], and the signatory of the [[Circulaire of 5 May 1930 to the V.V.V.F.|circular letter of 5 May 1930]] by which sample *H.M. no. 1607* of Atjeh-turpentine was distributed to the members of the *[[Vereeniging van Vernis- en Verffabrikanten en -Handelaren in Nederland]]*. As the Director of the Afdeeling Handelsmuseum, de Bussy was the senior officer responsible for the Koloniaal Instituut's commodity-evaluation programme, and his signature on the circular of [[5 May]] [[1930]] — *W. g. L. P. DE BUSSY, Directeur der Afdeeling Handelsmuseum ten Koloniaal Instituut* — is the principal documentary evidence of the institutional authorship of the [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn evaluation of 1930]].

## The Circulaire of 5 May 1930

The [[Circulaire of 5 May 1930 to the V.V.V.F.|Circulaire of 5 May 1930]] — preserved as Bijlage II of the [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet]] — is addressed *Aan de leden van de Vereeniging van Vernis- en Verffabrikanten en -Handelaren in Nederland* and signed by de Bussy. It explains that, in [[1924]], the Afdeeling Handelsmuseum had consulted with the board of the *V.V.V.F.* on the quality of Atjeh-turpentine; that the exploitation of the Atjeh pine forests had since been converted from an experimental to a definitive enterprise at [[Baleq]]; and that the factory-scale product since October [[1928]] could now be evaluated against the standard of American and French turpentines. The circular encloses sample *H.M. no. 1607* and the chemical data on samples 1504, 1553 and 1607, notes the price of f 22 per 50 kg, and refers readers to the Vierde Afdeeling of the [[Nederlandsche Handel-Maatschappij]] at Amsterdam for further commercial details.

## Other activities

The list of *Berichten van de Afdeeling Handelsmuseum* preserved at the back of the [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet]] records that de Bussy was the author of *Bericht* No. 1 (*Enkele nieuwe gegevens over Java-jute (Hibiscus cannabinus L.)*, [[1920]]) — the first publication in the series. His position as the author of the first *Bericht* and the Director of the Afdeeling Handelsmuseum by [[1930]] indicates a continuous institutional career at the Koloniaal Instituut from the early [[1920s]] through the early [[1930s]]. Several photographs in the [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet]] are credited *opn. L. P. de Bussy* — *photograph by L. P. de Bussy* — indicating that de Bussy personally visited the [[Baleq]] enterprise and the [[Pinus Merkusii]] forests of Atjeh in the course of his duties.

## Significance

De Bussy's significance in the documentary record of colonial Aceh is as the institutional author — in his capacity as Director of the Afdeeling Handelsmuseum — of the [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn evaluation of 1930]]. His signature on the [[Circulaire of 5 May 1930 to the V.V.V.F.|circular of 5 May 1930]] is the principal documentary evidence of the institutional authorship of the evaluation, and his photographs of the [[Baleq]] enterprise and the [[Pinus Merkusii]] forests are the principal published images of the early factory-scale production of Indonesian turpentine.
