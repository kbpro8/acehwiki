**Nussy** was an Ambonese [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Marechaussee]] of the [[K.N.I.L.]] who served as a *dardanel* under the vermaarde patrouillecommandanten [[Vastenou]] and [[Dersjant]] before becoming the best-known spoorzoeker and overval specialist of the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]] in [[1910]]-[[1911]], for which he was awarded the [[Militaire Willemsorde (M.W.O.)|M.W.O.]] 3e klas — the *Ridder derde klas* — in [[July 1902]] for an earlier gevecht and again recognized for his part in the destruction of the [[Teungkoe Sjech Saman di Tiri|Tiriō bende]]. After his retirement as sergeant he was taken to the Netherlands by a *controleur* and afterwards served as *mantri-politie* on [[Ambon]] under the civil bestuur of his former commander, the Assistent-Resident [[Schmidt]].

## Dardanel and patrouillecommandant

Nussy entered the colonne Schmidt already a veteran. He "was dardanel geweest bij Vastenou en Dersjant, vermaarde patrouillecommandanten, die schitterende resultaten hadden geboekt" — that is, he had served as the point-man (the two voorste Marechaussees at the front of any patrol were the *[[dardanellen|dardanellen]]*) under two of the most famous patrouillecommandanten of the older generation in Aceh. This background made him the unsurpassed spoorzoeker of the colonne: when faint bekas of *[[Peutoeha Gam Masén]]*'s fourageurs were found near the [[Kroeëng Seukoeleh]], "Nussy, Kamagi, Paliaman lieten zich niet misleiden" by the listige verberging of sporen. He is repeatedly the man who first sights the enemy — at one point spotting a [[Beaumontgeweer]] aimed at him on the third day of a patrol and shooting first.

## The colonne Schmidt campaign (1910-1911)

It was in the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt's]] two-year hunt for the [[Teungkoe Sjech Saman di Tiri|Tiriō Teungkoes]] that Nussy's reputation was made. In [[May 1911]], when the small resistance band of *[[Peutoeha Gam Masén]]* was omsingeld in a ladang near the [[Kroeëng Seukoeleh]], Gam Masén drew his *[[reuntjong]]* (the Acehnese kris) and threw himself on the two voorste Marechaussees — the *[[dardanellen|dardanellen]]* of the luitenant. "Nussy schoot en [[Enoch]] werkte af met de [[klewang]]." The leader was neergelegd.

When the colonne overvalled the schuilplaats of the Tiriō bende later that same month, Nussy was again in the voorste linie. He climbed the steile waterral under fire alongside [[Gomies]], [[Liklikwatil]], [[Enoch]], [[Kamagi]], [[Paliaman]] and [[Oesoel]]; on the rand he was shot at from "vijf pas afstand" and missed — "Over geluk viel niet te klagen." When the luitenant entrusted an omtrekking with the six best soldiers, Nussy was given the command: "Gomies, Kamagi, Womor, Paliaman, Liklikwatil en [[Rampisela]]." On the rand above the schuilplaats "Nussy en Paliaman namen nog drie man voor hun rekening."

In the chase of the four vluchtelingen after the schuilplaats assault, Nussy and Enoch shot all but one. "Nog twee ervan door Enoch, waaronder ook [[Tgk. Tjhi Majét di Tiri]]." The last, [[Tgk. Tjhi Ma'at]], would fall to Nussy's carbine on [[3 December]] [[1911]].

## The killing of Tgk. Tjhi Ma'at (3 December 1911)

On [[3 December]] [[1911]], Nussy — now korporaal — went out with his spits, who soon turned back. Alone, he gave the vogelgeluid signaal and waited. Three armed Acehnese approached; the voorste was [[Tgk. Tjhi Ma'at]], who aimed his *revolver* at Nussy. Nussy shot first, hitting him in the *borst*; the boy fell but rose and fled. The second Acehnese was killed outright. "Rampisela en [[Koentjoeng]] legden de man neer, terwijl ook de derde nog werd ingehaald" — Tgk. Tjhi Ma'at was brought down and became *[[sjahid]]*, geloofsmartelaar in the holy war. "Hier had Tgk. Tjhi Ma'at het lot van zijn Vader, zijn ooms en zovele andere verwanten gedeeld."

## The Ridder and the tears

When the news of his decoration arrived, Nussy was coming back from the *[[passar]]* in [[Tangsé]] with pakjes in his hands. "„Nussy dapat ridder klas tiga!" Nussy heeft het ridder derde klas gekregen." The hardened dardanel, the man who had shot first at three armed Acehnese, "heeft het uitgehuild van ontroering." The moment is one of the set-pieces of *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]*.

## Later life on Ambon

After his retirement from the army, "een controleur hem nog meegenomen naar Nederland" — a Dutch civil administrator brought him on a visit to the mother country. He then returned to [[Ambon]] as *mantri-politie* under the Assistent-Resident Schmidt, who had transferred to the civil bestuur. The army kept a watchful eye on him: the *gevaarlijke communist Patty* (the [[Communistische Partij (PKI)|Partai Komunis Indonesia]]) was active on Ambon, and the army had to ensure "dat hij op hem de gevaarlijke communist Patty niet zijn hoofd af zou slaan." For Nussy "was altijd soldaat gebleven, soldaat sadja" — always a soldier, nothing but a soldier.

The author notes the deeper contrast: "De politieman gebruikt zijn wapen slechts, als hij rechtstreeks wordt aangevallen, de soldaat gaat met de wapens naar voren en eist gehoorzaamheid." Nussy, the soldier, had become a mantri-politie; the mode of his new life was therefore against the grain of what he was.

## Significance

Nussy is the most fully drawn of the named Ambonese soldiers in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* and one of the most fully drawn Ambonese soldiers in any Dutch colonial source. His career tracks the whole arc of the late-colonial Marechaussee in Aceh: from the *dardanel* on patrol under the old patrouillecommandanten, through the long hunt for the last Acehnese resistance, to the strange peace of post-war police work under his former commander. His tears at the news of his decoration, and his lifelong identification as "soldaat sadja," give the entry its human centre. The author's contrast between the soldier and the mantri-politie is also the book's quietest argument about what was being lost in the late-colonial transition.
