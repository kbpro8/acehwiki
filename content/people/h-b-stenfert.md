**H. B. Stenfert** was a Dutch marine officer who received the [[Ridder Militaire Willemsorde]] 4th class for his participation in the **War against the Aceh Sultanate (1873–1874)**. The award is recorded in the royal decree of [[185]] (page 185) which cites “Oorlog tegen het rijk van Atjeh, 1873 en 1874.” His distinction reflects the high level of personal bravery recognised by the Dutch crown during the early phase of the Aceh War.

## Military Career

Stenfert served as a marine officer in the [[Korps Mariniers (Netherlands)]], taking part in several amphibious assaults along the Acehnese coast. The source does not provide further biographical details beyond his award citation.

## Significance

His award exemplifies the Dutch practice of honouring individual officers for successful actions against the Aceh Sultanate, reinforcing the prestige of the [[Ridder Militaire Willemsorde]].
