**H. B. Stenfert** was a Dutch marine lieutenant whose name was listed honourably in the royal decree of [[6 October]] [[1874]] and who was decorated with the [[Ridder Militaire Willemsorde]] (Knight, fourth class) for his participation in the [[Atjeh Expedition 1873-1876|Atjeh expeditions]] of [[1873]] and [[1874]] — the first phase of the [[Atjeh Expedition 1873-1876|Aceh War]]. The award citation, in the standard formula of the decree, named the campaign under which the decoration was granted as *"Oorlog tegen het rijk van Atjeh, 1873 en 1874"* ("War against the realm of Aceh, 1873 and 1874").

## Service in the Korps Mariniers

Stenfert served as a marine officer in the [[Korps Mariniers (Netherlands)|Netherlands Marine Corps]] — the Dutch naval-infantry corps that supplied the amphibious assault forces for the Atjeh landings of [[1873]]–[[1874]]. He took part in several amphibious assaults along the Atjehnese coast during the early phase of the war. The source — the history of the Korps Mariniers — does not provide further biographical details beyond his award citation.

## Recognition

The royal decree of [[6 October]] [[1874]] was the formal instrument by which the Dutch crown recognised the officers — and the junior officers in particular — who had fought in the first Atjeh expedition. Stenfert's inclusion in the list reflects the broad acknowledgment by the Dutch crown of junior marine officers in the early Atjeh campaigns. His award exemplifies the Dutch practice of honouring individual officers for distinguished conduct in the war against the Atjeh Sultanate, reinforcing the prestige of the [[Militaire Willemsorde|Ridder Militaire Willemsorde]].
