**Henry Demmeni** was a Colonel and later Major-General in the [[Dutch East Indies Army]] who served as Military Governor of Aceh from [[November]] [[1884]] until his death in [[December]] [[1886]].

## Establishment of the Concentrated Line

Appointed Governor following the failure of civil administration, Demmeni oversaw the retreat of Dutch forces to a compact defensive perimeter known as the [[Concentrated Line]] (*Geconcentreerde Linie*). He constructed a ring of 16 heavily fortified redoutes around [[Kraton of Aceh|Kota Raja]], linked by an armed tramway network (*ceintuurbaan*) and surrounded by a 1,000-meter cleared zone.

Demmeni repeatedly warned colonial authorities against passive waiting, advocating strict naval blockades along the coast. He died in office in [[December]] [[1886]] due to severe health strain from managing defense operations.
