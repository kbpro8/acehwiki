**Toekoe Moehamat Dawot** — the Sultan Toeankoe Moehamat Dawot — was the sultan of the Atjeh royal house who resided at [[Keumala|Keumala]] in [[Pidië|Pidie]] during the later phase of the [[Atjeh-oorlog (1873-1904)|Atjeh War]]. As the titular head of the sultanate, Dawot served as a rallying point for the resistance, maintaining contact with [[Panglima Polém|Panglima Polém]] at [[Padang Tidji|Padang Tidji]] and other resistance leaders. Other members of the sultan's family had established themselves elsewhere in Pidie, including at [[Batèë|Batèë]], where the robber chief Toeankoe Moehamat was based.

## The youthful figures at Padang Tidji

A youthful Mohamed Daoed and a youthful [[Panglima Polém|Polim]] were also mentioned as residing at [[Padang Tidji|Padang Tidji]] (Moekim VII) in [[Pidië|Pidie]], where they were considered safe from Dutch attack until the planned [[Pidië-expedition (1898)|Pidië expedition]] of [[1898]]. Van Heutsz wrote on [[27 October]] [[1896]] that these dwelling-places must be attacked so that their occupants would discover that they were not safe anywhere.
