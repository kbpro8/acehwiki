**Lieutenant F. Zegers Veeckens** was a second‑class marine lieutenant who served in the **Marine Landings Division** during the Dutch expeditions against Acehnese fortified settlements in 1868‑1869. He is listed among the four second‑class lieutenants (H. Quispel, F. W. Hudig, F. Zegers Veeckens, H. I. Cadet) who commanded landing troops.
