**A. J. Kroef** was a Dutch **[[lieutenant ter zee|lieutenant ter zee]]** of class **A** who commanded the steamship **[[Zr.Ms. Somiingg]]** during the **[[Expedition to Retch 1858|expedition to Retch]]** in the autumn of [[1858]]. He led a detachment of 24 corporals and marines from the **[[Royal Netherlands Marine Corps|Dutch Marine Corps]]**, of which four were killed and seven wounded, and later received a nomination for the **[[Militaire Willemsorde|Military Order of William]]**. His actions are recorded in the Dutch naval reports of 1858.

## Military Action

Kroef organised the landing at **[[Retch]]**, a coastal settlement on the east coast of **[[Sumatra]]**, north of **[[Djambi]]**. He coordinated with **[[K. C. Bunnik|Lieutenant ter zee K. C. Bunnik]]** and **[[L. F. Tuckerman|Lieutenant ter zee L. F. Tuckerman]]** to assault the fortified main entrance, which was blocked by a heavy **[[rotan|rattan]]** barrier. The assault succeeded after intense close‑quarter fighting.

## Recognition

Although four members of his detachment were nominated for the **[[Militaire Willemsorde]]**, the nominations were not acted upon.
