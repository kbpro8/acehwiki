**Koopman, Vice‑Admiraal** was the senior Dutch naval commander whose steamship *Vice‑Admiraal Koopman* transported the 4th marine company under [[Clifford Kooq van Breugel]] to [[Poeloe Bras]] on [[20 April]] [[1874]] for lighthouse construction. The vessel returned to [[Java]] on [[22 April]] and [[26 April]] [[1874]] after delivering the troops.

## Naval logistics

Koopman’s ship provided the logistical backbone for the Dutch marine operations along the Acehnese coast during the mid‑1870s.
