---
title: Kupala Punteuet
tags: [person]
---

The "arch-traitor" kupala (Dutch-appointed gampong headman) executed by Teuku Asan as a lesson — an act that caused all remaining headmen to embrace Teuku Asan's cause, either openly or in secret.
