**Toeankoe Moehamad Dawot (Pretendent-Sultan, ex-Pretendent-Sultan)** was a Pretender Sultan of Aceh mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Youth chosen as Sultan after fall of the Dalem (when previous Sultan fled and soon died); came under guardianship (voogdij) of one of the most hostile vorstelijken, Toeankoe Hasjim.

## Role

Pretender [[Sultan of Aceh (captured 1824)|Sultan of Aceh]]

## Account from Jongejans

Youth [[Corea|chosen]] as [[sultan|Sultan]] after fall of the [[Dalem]] (when previous [[sultan|Sultan]] fled and soon died); came under guardianship (voogdij) of one of the most hostile vorstelijken, [[Toeankoe Hasjim]]. Was a child at the time, lacked capacity and ambition to lead as he aged. Sought pleasure in worldly and frivolous amusements; misused his ninefold seal ([[Tjab thikoereueng|tjab thikoereueng]]) and purely traditional authority to extract money for extravagances and petty wars against rebellious chiefs. In [[1898]] sought contact with the Dutch government; told to come to [[Koetaradja]] to hear conditions for accepted submission; knew restoration of [[sultanate|Sultanate]] was impossible and did not appear. Instead convened meeting with [[Panglima Polém]] and other chiefs, deciding on joint action; [[Teukoe Oemar]] joined. After [[Batèë Ilië]] (February [[1901]]) withdrew to [[Gajo-landen]]; harassed by flying [[Marechaussée|marechaussée]] columns; fled to [[Pameuë]]; then to [[Pidië]] (ostensibly to marry off his son [[Toeankoe Ibrahim]]). On this trip his wife [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] Poetrèë was captured by [[Christoffel]]. [[24 December]] [[1902]]: his favorite [[Potjoet]] di Tjot Moerong and his son captured at [[Lammeulo]]. [[Generaal J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] then threatened to intern son and wives at [[Koetaradja]] or banish them from Aceh. He submitted. [[20 January]] [[1903]]: he and son [[Toeankoe Ibrahim]] received by the Governor at [[Koetaradja]] (having been fetched and escorted to [[Sigli]] by chiefs of [[Groot-Atjeh]], then travelling by warship). In [[1907]] exiled to [[Batavia]] because he was found to be supporting [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] leaders behind the scenes; later moved to [[Amboina (Z.M. stoomschip)|Amboina]] under Governor [[Gouverneur Van Daalen|Van Daalen]]. Lives there as an old man. Footnote: died in [[Batavia]] on 6 [[April]] of the year of the book's writing (j.l. = jaar liep / 'last year'), giving the [[sultanate|Sultanate]]-restoration question a wholly different aspect.

## Significance

Toeankoe Moehamad Dawot (Pretendent-Sultan, ex-Pretendent-Sultan) appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
