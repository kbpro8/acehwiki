**Abdul Wahab Siregar** was an Indonesian civil servant who served as head of the Information Service (*Voorlichtingsdienst*) of the province of North Sumatra. He had previously served, from 1 July [[1950]], as the representative of the Ministry of Information (*min. v. Voorlichting*) of the Republic of the United States of Indonesia (R.I.S.) at the corresponding department of the [[Negara Sumatera Timur]] (N.S.T.).

In February [[1950]], he was a member of the delegation that accompanied the acting governor [[Sarimin Reksodihardjo]] on an official visit to [[Atjeh|Atjeh]].
