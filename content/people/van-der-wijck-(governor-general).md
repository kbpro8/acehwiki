**Van der Wijck** — governor-general of the [[Nederlandsch-Indië|Nederlandsch-Indië]] from 1893 to 1899 — is, in the source's narrative, the governor-general who "begreep, dat Snouck Hurgronje's adviezen gevolgd moesten worden" (understood that Snouck Hurgronje's advice had to be followed). His tenure is the institutional reversal of the [[Pijnacker Hordijk|Pijnacker Hordijk]]-[[Deykerhoff|Deykerhoff]] gamble that had armed and subsidised [[Teungku Um|Teungku Um]].

## The Snouck Hurgronje reversal

[[Van der Wijck|Van der Wijck]]'s understanding — "dat Snouck Hurgronje's adviezen gevolgd moesten worden" — was, in the source's narrative, the institutional act by which the [[Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] advice of [[1892]] was finally implemented. The reversal was, however, too late to prevent the [[1896]] defection of [[Teungku Um|Teungku Um]] — the warlord whom [[Pijnacker Hordijk|Pijnacker Hordijk]] and [[Deykerhoff|Deykerhoff]] had armed and subsidised against [[Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]]'s explicit warning.

## The Van Heutsz appointment

The source records that [[J. B. van Heutsz|overste Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz]] "had dit begrepen" (had understood this) too. "In 1896 werd hij belast met de leiding der militaire operaties in Groot-Atjeh" (In 1896 he was charged with the direction of military operations in Groot-Atjeh). The [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] appointment is, in the source's narrative, the operational consequence of the [[Van der Wijck|Van der Wijck]] reversal: the [[Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] advice was now to be implemented, and [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] was the officer charged with its implementation.

## The Lombok expedition

[[Van der Wijck|Van der Wijck]]'s tenure also covers the [[1894]] Lombok expedition, which the source narrates in the same passage. The Lombok expedition — prompted by the appeal of the Sasak population of [[Lombok]] against their Balinese overlords — ended in the [[1894]] Dutch defeat at Tjakranegara, in which the under-commander [[General Van Ham|generaal Van Ham]] was killed. [[Van der Wijck|Van der Wijck]]'s immediate dispatch of reinforcements reversed the defeat; by the following year Lombok was under Dutch administration. The Lombok episode is, in the source's narrative, the parallel to the Atjeh reversal: both were post-[[1892]] implementations of a more vigorous Dutch posture in the *buitengewesten*.

## Significance

[[Van der Wijck|Van der Wijck]]'s significance for the encyclopedia of [[Acheh]] history lies in his role as the governor-general whose understanding that the [[Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] advice had to be followed produced the [[1896]] [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] appointment. The reversal is the institutional hinge between the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentratie]] period and the post-[[1896]] offensive.
