**J. B. van Heutsz (Generaal Van Heutsz)** was a Governor of Aceh (May 1898), later Governor-General; 'the greatest' of military administrators in Aceh mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Already appeared on the scene before becoming Governor in the time of General Deyckerhoff and the founding of the marechaussée corps.

## Role

Governor of Aceh (May [[1898]]), later Governor-General; 'the greatest' of military administrators in Aceh

## Account from Jongejans

Already appeared on the scene before becoming Governor in the time of General Deyckerhoff and the founding of the [[Marechaussée|marechaussée]] corps. The man who saw more sharply than anyone else the mistakes in our Aceh policy, the preacher of the offensive and of force in the struggle against oriental peoples. In [[1893]] wrote his famous brochure 'De onderwerping van Atjeh' with the motto '[[De Atjeh-Oorlog (Kruijt, 1896)|De Atjeh-oorlog]] knaagt aan ons koloniaal bezit, hij moet eindigen. Laten wij eindelijk aan de beschaafde wereld toonen, dat wij daartoe in staat zijn.' Already under Governor Van Vliet had performed brave deeds. In May [[1898]] became Governor with [[Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje]] as advisor. [[August]] [[1898]] led the great [[Pedir Expedition|Pedir-expeditie]] personally. Also operated in the Idische; [[Tangsé]] tochten. Pursued [[Teukoe Oemar]] so hard that he fled to [[Lageuën]] - 'Oemar's doodentocht'. Personally led the [[Pasai|Pasé]]-expeditie. [[Samalanga-expeditie, attack on Batèë Ilië, capture of Paja Reubèë fortifications]], then the great Gajotocht under [[Gouverneur Van Daalen|Van Daalen]]. [[6 September]] [[1904]] left as Governor of Aceh (eervol ontheven) on his appointment as Governor-General. In [[1908]] (as GG) set the requirements for the future administrator of Aceh (quoted at length, [[Koloniaal Verslag]] [[1908]]). As GG authorised arrest and deportation of ex-[[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]].

## Significance

J. B. van Heutsz (Generaal Van Heutsz) appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
