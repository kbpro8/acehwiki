**Corporal J. E. Bruins** distinguished himself aboard the steamship *Vesuvius* during an anti‑piracy operation in [[1847]] (page 108). His actions were recognized later with the *Eervolle Vermelding* (Honourable Mention) by a royal decree of [[21 August]] [[1850]] (page 108).

## Anti‑piracy Action of 1847

In [[1847]] the steamship *Vesuvius*, commanded by Lieutenant‑baron *De Constant Rebeeque*, engaged local sea‑raiders in the Indonesian archipelago. Bruins’ conduct during the engagement was singled out for bravery (page 108).

## Honors

The *Eervolle Vermelding* awarded to Bruins was recorded in the royal decree of [[21 August]] [[1850]] (page 108).
