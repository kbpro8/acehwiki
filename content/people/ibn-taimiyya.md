**Ibn Taimiyya** (1263 – 1328) was a Syrian jurist and theologian whose strict monotheistic doctrine gave rise to [[Wahhabism]]. The source cites him as the intellectual forebear of the Wahhabi critique of the *Burda* poem’s “excessive” veneration of the Prophet. His teachings filtered into the religious discourse of the Sultanate of [[Acheh]] during the 19th century.
