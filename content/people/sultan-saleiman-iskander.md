**Sultan Saleiman Iskander** — *Sultan Sulaiman Iskandar* — was the Sultan of [[Atjeh]] whose death in [[1837]] precipitated the succession crisis out of which [[Habib Abdurrahman Zahir|Habib Abdoe'r Rachman]]'s regency for the young [[Toeankoe Machmoed]] emerged. He is named in the [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] as the deceased sultan whose widow [[Potjoet]] — daughter of [[Toekoe Moeda Baid]] — was afterwards married by the Habib, and as the father of the prince whom the Habib wished to install as Sultan.

## The succession of 1837

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that the death of Sultan Saleiman Iskander in [[1837]] opened a disputed succession. The late Sultan's son — [[Toeankoe Machmoed]] — was a minor, and was in the custody of [[Toekoe Moeda Baid]], *oeloebalang* of the [[VII Moekims]], at his *kampong* of [[Tjot Bada]]. [[Habib Abdurrahman Zahir|Habib Abdoe'r Rachman]] wished to install the prince as Sultan and to act as regent during his minority. To this end the Habib married the prince's mother — the widow of Saleiman Iskander, [[Potjoet]], daughter of Toekoe Moeda Baid — thereby binding himself to the kinship network of the late Sultan and consolidating his claim to the regency. The marriage was, however, deferred by Toekoe Moeda Baid, who accepted a payment of 400 dollars for the marriage promise but repeatedly postponed the actual delivery of the bride, until the Habib was forced to march on [[Tjot Bada]] with a force of two thousand men to compel the surrender.

## The Habib's regency

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that, after the death of Sultan Saleiman Iskander, the installation of [[Toeankoe Machmoed]] as Sultan under the Habib's regency was the principal achievement of the Habib's early career at the Atjehnese court. The regency was, in turn, the period of the Habib's greatest political influence — the period during which he was confirmed as *Grootvizier* and during which he proposed to the young Sultan that the Dutch be admitted to Atjehnese territory for the purposes of trade.

## Significance

Sultan Saleiman Iskander's significance in the documentary record of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] is as the Sultan whose death in [[1837]] opened the succession crisis that produced the Habib's regency — the period of the most sustained centralisation of authority in the late Atjehnese sultanate. His appearance in the [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] is brief, but his death is the structural event on which the Habib's career turns, and his widow [[Potjoet]]'s remarriage to the Habib is the marriage alliance that bound the Habib to the kinship network of the late Sultan.
