**Tgk. Aron** was de handige Tgk. Aron, the band-head of [[Pidië]] who carried out "an almost perfect hussar's trick" at [[Keudé Garot]] in the night of [[8 December|8]]–[[9 December]] [[1908]].

Partly disguised as soldiers in captured uniforms and equipment, his band gave itself out at Keudé Garot as a patrol, was admitted by the village watch, captured eight rifles lent to the *[[oelèëbalang]]*, wounded the [[Imeum]] of Garot, head of the watch, and disappeared with the booty — "eight rifles was an enormous amount in those days." In August [[1910]] [[J. J. Boreel|Boreel]] settled with Tgk. Aron, though not completely: six followers were rendered harmless and two of the eight rifles returned to the *[[Kompeuni]]*.
