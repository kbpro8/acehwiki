**General Van der Wijck** was the commander of the [[Indische Leger|Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] during the second Bali expedition of [[1848]] (page 106). He commanded a force of roughly 2 500 men but lacked sufficient local auxiliaries (*koelies*), limiting the expedition’s effectiveness.

## Command of the 1848 Expedition

Van der Wijck’s army was tasked with enforcing a Dutch ultimatum to the Balinese princes after the 1846 campaign. The Dutch navy, under Vice‑admiral J. P. Machielsen, supported the operation with sixty war‑ and transport ships (page 106). Despite initial successes, the expedition suffered a serious defeat at the fortified position of [[Djagaraga]] and was forced to withdraw to [[Java]].

## Interaction with Local Leaders

The general’s negotiations with Balinese princes failed, leading to renewed hostilities. His inability to secure reinforcements contributed to the Dutch decision to launch a third expedition in [[1849]] (page 106).
