**William Temple** was the English ambassador to **[[Brussels]]** who, in **[[1668]]**, represented **[[England|England]]** in the negotiations of the **[[Triple Alliance (1668)]]**. His diplomatic activities contributed to the shifting balance of power that affected the **[[United Provinces (Dutch Republic)]]** and, by extension, the **[[VOC]]**’s position in the **[[Sultanate of Aceh]]**.

## Role in the Triple Alliance
Temple worked alongside the Swedish envoy **[[Graf von Dohna]]** and the Dutch negotiators to formalise the alliance that sought to counter **[[Louis XIV]]**’s expansion, a development that indirectly impacted Dutch trade routes to Aceh.
