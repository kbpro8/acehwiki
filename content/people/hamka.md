**Hamka (Abdul Malik Karim Amrullah)** was a prominent Indonesian Islamic scholar, writer, and former teacher of [[Abdul Karim Amrullah]] (Hadji Rasul). In his biography of his father, *Ajahku* (published by Widjaya, [[Jakarta]] [[1950]]), Hamka described the traditional practice of singing Arabic kasidas, such as the *Burda*, during religious festivals in Minangkabau villages.

## Cultural Observations
Hamka noted that during the celebration of the Prophet’s birth (*Mawlid*) and the *Midrādj* (Prophet’s ascension), children dressed as *hadji* would lead processions while chanting kasidas, illustrating the integration of Arabic poetic forms into local devotional life.
