**Panglima Mah Saleh** was an Acehnese military commander (*panglima*) and primary lieutenant (*hulubalang*) serving under [[Teuku Imam Lueng Batoh]] in the [[XXV Moekims]] during the Aceh War.

## Combat Service in 1874

In *Atjeh gedurende de tweede expeditie en later* ([[1876]]), [[Panglima Mah Saleh]] is recorded among the most prominent Acehnese combat leaders who organized benteng defenses at [[Lemboe]] and around [[Kraton of Aceh]] during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]]. 

In early February [[1874]], Dutch intelligence briefly received false reports of his death alongside that of [[Teuku Imam Lueng Batoh]], illustrating the intense fog of war surrounding Acehnese leadership during the [[1874]] campaign.
