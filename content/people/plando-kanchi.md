The celebrated mouse-deer of Indonesian fable, hero of the written Hikayat Plando' Kanchi and numerous oral habas. He serves as assessor (waki) to the prophet-king Solomon under nicknames including Teungku Waki and Waki Saba, and the 26-section fable collection includes a lawsuit judged before Solomon and a bridge of crocodiles.
