**General‑Major Van Teyn** was the **civil and military governor of [[Atjeh]]** who personally assumed command of the combined Dutch forces during the **[[Edi River expedition (1890)]]**. He coordinated the arrival of reinforcements, including the warships **[[Samarang (ship)|Samarang]]** and **[[Bennkoeenn (ship)|Bennkoeenn]]**, and oversaw the subsequent assaults on Acehnese positions along the **[[Edi River|Edi]]**.

## Command Role

- **[[June]] [[1890]]**: Directed the combined land‑sea operation that ultimately forced the Acehnese to abandon their fortified hill‑tops and villages such as **[[Pedawa Pondong]]**.

## Account in Hooijer's *De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh* ([[1897]])

**General‑Major Van Teyn** was the Dutch general who granted a salary to [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb|Toekoe Tjoet Toengkoeb]], *[[Oeleebalang|oeleebalang]]* of the [[XIII Moekims Toengkoeb]], on condition that he should no longer fight against the Netherlands — the act by which that chief was detached, at least nominally, from the priestly party of [[Tengkoe Sjech Saman di Tiro|Teungkoe di Tiro]].

## The pacification of Toekoe Tjoet

[[G. B. Hooijer]] recorded that Toekoe Tjoet had for years made common cause with the priestly party under Teungkoe di Tiro, then supreme in the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]], and that only after *Generaal van Teyn hem een bezoldiging had toegekend, op voorwaarde, dat hij niet meer tegen ons zou vechten* — General Van Teyn had granted him a salary on condition that he no longer fight the Dutch — had he laid aside the *[[Klewang|klewang]]*.

The arrangement proved fragile: by [[1893]] Toekoe Tjoet was again giving grounds for suspicion, arbitrarily evacuating the *benteng* at [[Lamgoet]] and thereby playing it back into the enemy's hands, and he was ultimately banished to [[Malaboeh]].

## Significance

The episode is an example of the Dutch policy of *bezoldiging* — the paying of salaries to Achehnese chiefs in exchange for neutrality or cooperation — which underlay the whole alliance system described in *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]*, from Van Teyn's stipend to Toekoe Tjoet through to the salaried *[[Legioen van T. Djohan (T. Oemar's Legion)|legioen]]* of [[Teukoe Oemar]].
