**The Willink Ketjen brothers** — Second Lieutenant of Infantry **H. Willink Ketjen** and First Lieutenant of Infantry **W. E. Willink Ketjen** — were Dutch officers in [[Atjeh]] in [[1876]], who both figure in the official history of the war for deeds done at the checks of March and June [[1876]].

## H. Willink Ketjen

In the [[Ambush of the Buijs detachment (4 March 1876)|ambush of 4 March 1876]] in the narrows of [[Lamsajoen|Lamsajoeng]] and Tjot Bēhēl, when the detachment of Lieutenant [[T. W. J. Buijs]] was attacked by an overwhelming enemy, Second Lieutenant H. Willink Ketjen carried the alarm to [[Kajoe-Lē]]: alone, followed only by a mounted orderly, he forced a way through the enemy line to the post, whence the relieving company of infantry marched and arrived just in time to save the detachment.

## W. E. Willink Ketjen

First Lieutenant W. E. Willink Ketjen was serving with the troops at [[Oleh Karang]] in June [[1876]]. In the [[Ambush between Oleh Karang and Kota Alam (18 June 1876)|ambush of 18 June]] he immediately marched out with 30 men on hearing the firing that had caught sergeant-major Deingens' fouraging party, met the fleeing wounded, and by his arrival brought the last stand of the hard-pressed detachment to safety. On [[22 June]], walking outside the work at [[Kajoe-Lē|Kajoe-Lē]] — where he had moved with the garrison — he ventured too far into the kampong: an Atjeher fired on him at close range and he was lightly wounded, but could still bring down his assailant with a shot from his revolver.

Both appear in [[Kolonel Meijer|H. F. Meijer]]'s *[[Atjeh van 26 December 1875 tot 4 September 1876]]* ([[1883]]) with their initials recorded.
