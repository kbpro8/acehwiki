**H. Colijn (Dr. H. Colijn)** was a Captain, later Colonel/Minister; pursuer of Panglima Polém; first military commander at Tapatoean; later civil gezaghebber at Lho' Seumawé mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Pursued Panglima Polém.

## Role

Captain, later Colonel/Minister; pursuer of [[Panglima Polém]]; first military commander at [[Tapatoean]]; later civil gezaghebber at [[Lho'Seumawé|Lho' Seumawé]]

## Account from Jongejans

Pursued [[Panglima Polém]]. First commander of the military post at [[Tapatoean]] (with rank of captain). Drew up a plan for bestuursregeling (administrative arrangement) of various regions (including [[Laut Tawar]]) after the [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]]-tocht. As civiel-gezaghebber at [[Lho'Seumawé|Lho' Seumawé]], received [[Panglima Polém]]'s surrender on [[6 September]] [[1903]]. [[Panglima Polém]] later recalls him with affection. Author of the book's foreword ([[Dr. H. Colijn]]).

## Significance

H. Colijn (Dr. H. Colijn) appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
