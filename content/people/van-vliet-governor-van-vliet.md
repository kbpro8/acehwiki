**Van Vliet (Governor Van Vliet)** was a Governor of Aceh before Van Heutsz mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Under him Van Heutsz already performed brave military deeds.

## Role

Governor of Aceh before [[Generaal J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]

## Account from Jongejans

Under him [[Generaal J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] already performed brave military deeds.

## Significance

Van Vliet (Governor Van Vliet) appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
