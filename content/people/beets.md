**Beets** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Atjehtram]]

Several of the [[Atjeh-moord|Atjeh murders]] were committed against tramway staff. In September [[1913]] a train conductor was murdered; in June [[1915]] an overseer of the tram at [[Pantòn Laboe]] was attacked by an Acehnese woman; in [[1916]] the tram clerk [[Beets]] and his wife were attacked, Beets dying of his wound and the assailant being sentenced to death. Attacks on soldiers occurred repeatedly at tram halts.

### From [[1916]]

In **[[1916]]** the smallpox epidemic was brought to a standstill; a redactor of the *[[Deli Courant]]* visited Atjeh and described in ten articles, *Atjeh in Vogelvlucht*, a country wholly at rest and in flower. In December the [[Sarekat Atjeh]] was founded at [[Koeta Radja]] under Swart's patronage. [[M. Schouten|Schouten]] attempted to bring in [[T. Radja Sabi]]; unrest at [[Linggö]] in the [[Meergebied]] was suppressed and [[Penghoeloe Möngkör]] laid low by the population in September. The tram clerk [[Beets]] was fatally attacked. On [[7 April]] [[1916]] Swart was made Adjutant in Extraordinary Service to the Queen.
