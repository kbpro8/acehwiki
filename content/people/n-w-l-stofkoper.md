**N. W. L. Stofkoper** was a Dutch official who served as advisor for Outer Islands agricultural enterprises (*adviseur voor de Ond.-landbouw*) to His Excellency (*E.Z.*). In February [[1950]], he joined the second survey party that travelled from Medan via P. Brandan to [[Langsar Estate]] in [[Atjeh|Atjeh]] to assess the condition of plantation enterprises following the Dutch-Indonesian hostilities. The party was led by [[Sutan Barus]], inspector-general of enterprises in Atjeh, and included [[G. Kars Gultom]] as secretary.
