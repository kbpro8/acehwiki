**Toekoe Moeda Rajat** was the head of the *pasar* (market master) of [[Gigiëng]] until his dismissal by Prince [[Toengkoe Bintara Kamangan]] on the ground of his good disposition towards the Dutch, one of the signs by which the prince's secret transit to the war party was detected in July [[1876]] — the report, made by [[Toekoe Imam of Gigiëng]], stating expressly that the well-disposed Moeda Rajat had been replaced by the hostile **Toekoe Hasjim**.

His dismissal was one of the small facts by which the Dutch, lacking establishments on that coast, kept their finger on the pulse of the Atjehnese coast states; it preceded by a month the [[Cancellation of friendship with Gigiëng (19 August 1876)|breach of 19 August 1876]] in which the harbour of Gigiëng was closed and the prince declared an open enemy.
