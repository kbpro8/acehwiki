**J.G. Blanken** was a Lieutenant in the Dutch forces who exhibited immense bravery during the [[Second Conquest of the Great Missigit]] on [[14 April]] [[1873]].

## Act of Heroism
Alongside Captain [[J.H. Huijer]], Blanken risked his life by jumping over the walls into the courtyard of [[Atjeh's Groote Missigit]] while under intense enemy fire. Their mission was to retrieve the body of a fallen Amboinese soldier, preventing it from being mutilated by the enemy. He passed away on [[4 September]] 1882 in The Hague.
