---

The Prophet's uncle, known in Acheh as "Amdah," whose grave on Mount Uhud near Medina is the first holy place mentioned in the Hikayat Soydina Amdah, recited by women during the rateb Saman. The legendary Amir Hamzah cycle of his deeds is popular in Acheh in Malay rendering.
