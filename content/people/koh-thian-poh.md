**Koh Thian Poh** was a Penang Chinese who rendered services to the English side in the [[Nisero affair (1883–1885)|Tenom question]]. At the end of [[1885]], as [[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] noted in passing, a golden watch was presented to him with great ceremony, on behalf of the Queen of England, for the services he had proved in the Tenom affair; at the same time [[Sir Julian Pauncefote|Sir Julian Pauncefote]], the British staatssecretaris known to the Dutch side as the principal leader of the Tenom negotiations, received the Grand Cross of the Order of St Michael and St George — decorations which Verstege cited as part of the wary judgement of England's conduct in the whole affair (see [[Nisero affair (1883–1885)]]).
