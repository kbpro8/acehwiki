**W. V. Rhemrev** was a Netherlands-Indies officer of the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]*, knight of the *[[Militaire Willemsorde|Militaire Willems-Orde]]* 4th class for the [[Dempen van den opstand in de Gajo- en Alaslanden (1905)|suppression of the 1905 revolt]] in the [[Gajolanden|Gajo]] and [[Alaslanden|Alas]] lands, later an organiser of the *[[Indië Weerbaar]]* movement, co-founder of the *[[Vereeniging Nederlandsch-Indische Jaarbeurs]]*, and suppressor of the [[Communistische opstand ter Sumatra's Westkust (1926-1927)|communist rising]] on [[Westkust|Sumatra's Westkust]] in [[1926]]. The [[1930]] Maréchaussée jubilee number carried a portrait notice of his career.

## Education and commissioning

Rhemrev received his education at the former *[[Gymnasium Willem III]]* at [[Weltevreden]] and at the *[[Koninklijke Militaire Academie]]* at [[Breda]]. In [[1902]] he became an officer.

## Karo plateau and the Gajo-Alas revolt

In [[1904]] he received an honourable mention (*eervolle vermelding*) for his military operations on the [[Batak Karo|Karo plateau]].

In [[1905]] he quelled the revolt that broke out after the [[Tocht naar de Gajo-Loeös (1904)|famous march]] of Lieutenant-Colonel [[G. C. E. van Daalen]] through the Gajo and Alas lands, and was decorated for it with the Militaire Willems-Orde 4th class. The [[Overzicht van Onderscheidingen toegekend aan het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden (1890-1930)|register of honours]] in the same issue records the award under [[1906]], to *2e Luit. Mar. W. V. Rhemrev*.

Because Rhemrev had at that time been *levensgevaarlijk* — mortally dangerously — wounded, he was declared unfit for active service; but at the request of Governor-General [[J. B. van Heutsz]] and of [[H. Colijn|Colijn]] he continued to serve.

## The Albanian episode

When the [[Prins von Wied]] at that period asked for Maréchaussée officers for [[Albanië|Albania]], Rhemrev at once made himself available. Only officers from [[Netherlands|Holland]] were, however, designated; they went to Albania under the command of [[Lodewijk W J K Thomson|Kolonel Thomson]], who later fell.

## Indië Weerbaar and public life

In [[1917]] Rhemrev went to Holland with a deputation as organiser of the *[[Indië Weerbaar]]* ("Defensible Indies") movement.

He was co-founder of the *[[Vereeniging Nederlandsch-Indische Jaarbeurs]]* (Netherlands-Indies Trade Fair Association), and through his intervention four million guilders were raised by trade and industry in the Netherlands for the establishment of the *[[Technische Hoogeschool te Bandoeng]]*.

## Sumatra's Westkust, 1926

In [[1926]] Rhemrev was sent to Sumatra's West Coast, and shortly after his arrival the communist rising broke out there, which he managed to suppress by vigorous action. The *Landvoogd* sent him a telegram of congratulation, and he received his third decoration: appointment as Officer in the *[[Orde van Oranje-Nassau]]* with the swords. At the same time he was placed on the army reserve.

## Later career

At the time of the [[1930]] jubilee Rhemrev was director of the building company *[[De Rijzende Zon]]*, and also took part in the exploitation of the new rubber-tapping method recently devised by [[Jules Bosch]].
