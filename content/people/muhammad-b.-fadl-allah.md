**Muḥammad b. Faḍl Allāh** was a Sufi author of the school of the unity of being, the author of the *Tuḥfa al-mursala ilāʾn-nabī* ("The Gift Addressed to the Prophet"). He is cited by [[Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]] in the [[Hudjdjat as-siddiq li-daf az-zindiq|*Ḥujjat aṣ-ṣiddīq li-dafʿ az-zindīq*]] as one of the orthodox authorities against the heretical [[Wujudiyyah]] of [[Acheh]]. The citation is one of the principal channels by which the orthodox Wujūdī position — the position that distinguishes the orthodox Sufi from the heretical Wujūdiyyah — was transmitted to the Malay-Indonesian world.

## The *Tuḥfa al-mursala*

The *Tuḥfa al-mursala ilāʾn-nabī* of Muḥammad b. Faḍl Allāh is a Sufi treatise on the unity of being (*waḥdat al-wujūd*) in the tradition of [[Ibn Arabi|Ibn ʿArabī]]. The work is listed by Brockelmann in *GAL* II, 418, and is cited by Raniri in the [[Hudjdjat as-siddiq li-daf az-zindiq|*Ḥujjat aṣ-ṣiddīq*]] (p. 25 of the Voorhoeve facsimile) for the doctrine that the mystic, even in the highest ecstasy, remains a creature. The passage cited is: "Whoever teaches that the mystic, even in the highest state, departs from the condition of the created, or attains the degree of divinity, is wrong, contrary to what some fools suppose."

## The orthodox Wujūdī position

The citation of Muḥammad b. Faḍl Allāh in the [[Hudjdjat as-siddiq li-daf az-zindiq|*Ḥujjat aṣ-ṣiddīq*]] is one of the principal channels by which the orthodox Wujūdī position — the position that distinguishes the Sufi from the heretical Wujūdiyyah — was transmitted to the Malay-Indonesian world. Like [[Ahmad al-Mahaimi|al-Mahāʾimī]] and [[Ibn Arabi|Ibn ʿArabī]], Muḥammad b. Faḍl Allāh is a Sufi of the school of the unity of being whom Raniri cites as an authority *against* the Wujūdiyyah — a remarkable fact, since the same school is the principal authority of the [[Wujudiyyah]] of [[Hamzah Pansuri]] and [[Shaikh Shamsuddin of Sumatra|Shamsuddin of Pasai]]. The distinction between the orthodox and the heretical Wujūdī is the key to Raniri's polemical strategy.

## Significance

Muḥammad b. Faḍl Allāh is one of the Sufi authorities cited by Raniri in support of the orthodox distinction between the Sufi and the heretical Wujūdiyyah. His *Tuḥfa al-mursala* is one of the principal channels by which the orthodox Wujūdī position was transmitted to the Malay-Indonesian world. The fact that a Sufi of the school of the unity of being could be cited, by Raniri, as an authority against the Wujūdiyyah of Hamzah Pansuri's school is a measure of the difficulty of distinguishing the orthodox from the heterodox position in the scholastic Sufism of the seventeenth century, and of the role of commentary and interpretation — rather than the choice of authorities — in the religious controversy of [[Acheh]].
