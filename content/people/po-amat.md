---
title: Po Amat
tags: [person, literary-work]
---

A student of Klibeuet, hero of the Hikayat Po di Amat, whose dream predicts success in his studies under Malem Jawa. The story ends with him winning the hand of the Gayo king's daughter and becoming ruler of the Gayos.
