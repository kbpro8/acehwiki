**Ir. Meulemans** was a Netherlands-Indies researcher, co-author with Mevrouw [[Van Stockum]] and Dr. [[J. H. de Haas]] of the investigation among indigenous mothers on [[Java]] which established that the vitamin A content of their milk was much lower than that of European women.

## The finding and its application to Atjeh

The result of the joint investigation was cited by Dr. [[C. E. Gomperts]] as the basis for his explanation of why *[[Xerophthalmie|xerophthalmie]]* in [[Groot-Atjeh]] was concentrated in the age group 0–5. Since the nutrition of the Atjehnese population was certainly poorer than that of the Javanese, the vitamin A content of Atjehnese mothers' milk was to be expected lower still; and with the Acehnese custom of protracted breast-feeding of eighteen to twenty months, the low level would suffice only up to a point before growth and other factors depressed it into hypo- or avitaminosis.
