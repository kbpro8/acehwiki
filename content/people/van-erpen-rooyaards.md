**Van Erpen Royaards** was a 2e Luitenant (second lieutenant) of the infantry of the [[Oost-Indisch Leger|Netherlands East Indies Army]], serving with the 1st peloton of the 2nd Company — a native company — of the [[6e Bataljon Infanterie]] in the [[Lamtoba column (1879)|Lamtoba column]] of [[1879]]. He is named in [[W. Cool]] and [[G. B. Hooijer]]'s *[[Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis (Cool & Hooijer, 1889)|Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis]]* ([[1889]]) as the only officer among the three wounded of the storming of the Achehnese entrenchment past [[Kepaja Doewa]] on [[13 August]] [[1879]], the principal combat of the [[Selimoen Expedition (August 1879)|march to Selimoen]].

## The affair of 13 August 1879

In the storm ordered by Majoor [[W. A. Coblijn|Coblijn]] against the Achehnese position on the Selimoen road — the luitenant's peloton turning the work by its flank under the company commander Captain [[Clerkson]], while the second peloton pinned it in front — Van Erpen Royaards was wounded, with two men, in a total loss which the swiftness of the assault kept down to three. In all, 970 cartridges were expended by the column in the affair. The position, when it was afterwards examined, proved to be of great strength: an earthen trench — the thrown-up earth set upright to a thickness of a metre or more between hurdles and furnished with bamboo loops — a heavy abatis in front, and a palisaded *tamboer*, a kind of blockhouse, standing near the footpath. Had it been defended by the numbers for which it was evidently prepared, the authors observed, its capture would have demanded great sacrifices; it had been held by an estimated thirty men.
