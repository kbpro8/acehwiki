**Kolonel Schäfer** was the Dutch military commander of Atjeh whom [[H. Demmeni|Demmeni]] succeeded as Militair Commandant in [[1884]] before being promoted to [[Governor of Atjeh]] on the departure of the civil governor [[P. F. Laging Tobias|Laging Tobias]]. Brooshooft records that Governor-General [[O. van Rees|'s Jacob]] had so valued Demmeni's service at [[Ambon|Amboina]] and [[Ternate]] that when Schäfer left the Atjeh military command he appointed Demmeni in his place, and warmly recommended him to his successor van Rees for the governorship should that office again be given to a military officer.
