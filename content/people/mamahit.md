**Mamahit** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Tjoet Meutia]]

The "five-hour theory" proved correct: at ten o'clock they found a place where the band had bivouacked. Losing and refinding the trail along the *kr. Peutòë*, over waterfalls, boulders and slippery stones, Mosselman saw smoke above the trees towards four o'clock after a stiff climb. Some 150 metres up a side-creek they found a marching bivouac under construction, hurriedly abandoned with all supplies left behind. They stormed on and came upon the band about to cross the stream some thirty metres ahead. [[Mamahit]], then in front, pointed out an old man being helped along by three others, trying to escape into the forest, and fired; the old man fell wounded.

### From [[Tgk. Sjeh di Paja Bakong]]

Provisions ran out; men fell out; Mosselman was reduced to ten and finally to seven, against a band certainly sixty strong. Towards four in the afternoon of [[25 October]], after a stiff climb, he saw smoke above the trees. Some 150 metres up a side-creek they found a marching bivouac under construction, hurriedly abandoned. Storming on, they came upon the band about to cross the stream some thirty metres ahead. [[Mamahit]], then in front, pointed out an old man being helped along by three others, trying to escape into the forest, and fired; the old man fell wounded.

### From [[W. J. Mosselman]]

All seven charged at top speed to the *aloeë*, tumbling and falling over the stones, and up the side-brook. Some 150 metres from its mouth they found a marching bivouac under construction, hastily abandoned with all supplies left behind. They ran on and came upon the band about to cross the stream some thirty metres ahead. [[Mamahit]], then in front, pointed out an old man being helped along by three others, trying to escape into the forest, and fired; the old man fell wounded.
