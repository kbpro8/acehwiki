**Raja of Pedir** was the sovereign indigenous ruler (*radja*) of the historical coastal kingdom of [[Pedir]] (modern Pidie Regency) on the northeast coast of Sumatra during the Aceh War.

## Negotiations in 1874

In January [[1874]], during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]], the [[Raja of Pedir]] initiated diplomatic contacts with General [[J. van Swieten]]. As recorded in official telegrams from [[6 January]] and [[2 February]] [[1874]] ([[General van Swieten War Telegrams]]), the raja expressed his desire to return to [[Pedir]] and negotiate a peace treaty with the [[Dutch East Indies Government]].
