**Greeve** was a Dutch *controleur* (district officer) stationed at [[Bireuën]] in the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] in the years of the definitive peace. In November [[1913]], during *[[Moens' journey through Atjeh (November 1913)|Moens' journey through Atjeh]]*, the traveller [[W. Moens]] recorded that *de controleur Greeve te Bireun* supplied him, there as elsewhere in the most obliging manner, with all the information he desired. Moens met him at the time of his visit to [[Bireuën]], the starting-point of the [[Gajo-weg|Gajoe-weg]], where the extensive new buildings and the busy market showed the rapid growth of the place and where the grave and projected monument of the murdered road-builder [[Heinzenknecht]] stood by the beginning of his great work.

The controleur Greeve of Bireuën is not to be confused with the mining engineer [[De Greve]] who served at the government coal-mine of [[Sabang]].
