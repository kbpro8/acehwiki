**E. C. van Daalen** was a colonel and major general of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] who assumed second-in-command and subsequently Commander-in-Chief of the [[First Aceh Expedition]] following the death of General [[J. H. R. Köhler]] on [[14 April]] [[1873]].

## Command and Withdrawal Decision

Following Köhler's death during the [[Battle of Baiturrahman Mosque]], [[E. C. van Daalen]] took command of the invasion force. Evaluating his overflowing hospital tents, depleting ammunition, and the approaching [[Southwest Monsoon in Aceh Operations]], van Daalen concluded that remaining ashore risked the complete destruction of his army. 

After convening a council of war, [[E. C. van Daalen]] ordered the evacuation of the [[Baiturrahman Grand Mosque]] and the withdrawal of the [[First Aceh Expedition]] after [[16 April]] [[1873]].

## Defense by General Booms

Following the retreat, [[E. C. van Daalen]] was subjected to harsh questioning during the [[Commission of Inquiry into the First Aceh Expedition]] in [[1874]]. In his [[1875]] treatise, General [[G. G. Booms]] defended van Daalen against critics like General [[F. V. A. de Stuers]], proving that van Daalen's decision to withdraw was a prudent military necessity caused by the failings of the [[Dutch East Indies Government]].
