**J. Paris** was a Dutch military officer and Captain in the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]].

## Military Career and Service in Aceh

In [[1925]], Captain Paris commanded a Marechaussee detachment operating on the West Coast of Aceh near [[Meulaboh]]. During a fierce surprise ambush in the [[West Coast of Aceh Action 1925–1927]], Captain Paris was killed in action alongside five of his men.
