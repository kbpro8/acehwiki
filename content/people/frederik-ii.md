**Frederick II, Holy Roman Emperor** (1194 – 1250) was a medieval European ruler whose questions to scholars in [[Ceuta]] are referenced in the source as an example of the wide‑reaching scholarly interest that the *Burda* poem attracted. Although not directly linked to Aceh, the mention illustrates the poem’s global reputation, which later filtered into Acehnese religious culture through Arabic and Malay translations.
