**W. A. Herold** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Pidië]]

In July [[1911]] the *controleur* [[G. J. van Dongen]] took over the administration of the sub-division from the civil authority, the *marechaussée* captain [[W. A. Herold]]. In [[1912]] the garrison of Pidië could be reduced by two companies. In December [[1911]] Schmidt completed his commission with the death of Tgk. Ma'at.

### From [[The resistance in Atjeh 1908–1912]]

In July [[1911]] Swart could report in the monthly report: "towards the end of the month the *controleur* [[G. J. van Dongen]] arrived to take over the administration of the sub-division from the civil authority, the constabulary captain [[W. A. Herold]]." In [[1912]] the garrison of Pidië could be reduced by two companies.

Geertsema Beckeringh]], [[F. P. A. van Gheel Gildemeester]], [[A. H. C. Roelofsen]], [[H. Behrens]], [[W. Muurling]], [[C. A. Snell]], [[Van Eck]], [[Hein Meijer]], [[G. F. P.
