**T. H. Kroesen** was an army general and Commander of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] who repeatedly advocated for institutional reforms and naval reconnaissance prior to the Aceh War.

## Proposals for Reconnaissance and General Staff

In [[1871]], anticipating conflict with the [[Aceh Sultanate]] following the Anglo-Dutch Sumatra Treaty, General [[T. H. Kroesen]] submitted a formal proposal to the [[Dutch East Indies Government]] to send a military staff officer aboard the steamship *Djambi* to survey the coast and approaches to [[Kraton of Aceh]]. As General [[G. G. Booms]] noted in [[1875]], this proposal was rejected over minor administrative disputes.

Furthermore, General [[T. H. Kroesen]] repeatedly lobbied for the establishment of a permanent [[General Staff of the KNIL]] to professionalize [[Military Intelligence in the Aceh War]]. His proposals were ignored until after the defeat of the [[First Aceh Expedition]] in [[1873]].
