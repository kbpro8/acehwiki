**H. C. Reuter** was the oldest European planter in [[Temiang]], active in the early rubber period of the [[Oostkust van Atjeh]].

## Activity in Temiang

Reuter owned the rubber estate [[Tandjong Semantoh]], which he later transferred to the Japanese entity [[Borneo Rubber Compagnie]]. He was also owner of the young enterprise [[Rimba Sawang]]. According to [[Dr. R. Broersma]], Reuter had recently died at the time of writing in [[1923]].

His career illustrates the turnover of estates in [[Temiang]] after the failure of tobacco, from [[Deli-Maatschappij]] and [[Arendsburg]] attempts to rubber and oil palm.
