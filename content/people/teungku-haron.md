**Teungku Haron** — in [[P. Voorhoeve|Voorhoeve]]'s text **Teungku Harõn** — was an Achehnese religious scholar who, about [[1929]], composed an Achehnese treatise on the history of Islam in [[Acheh]], working from a manuscript of the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān fī maʿrifat al-adyān*]] of [[Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]]. His work — preserved in the manuscript of the Koninklijk Instituut voor Taal-, Land- en Volkenkunde at The Hague, designated **D** in Voorhoeve's apparatus — is the principal witness to the survival of the *Tibjān* as a living text of Achehnese religious scholarship in the early twentieth century.

## The Achehnese adaptation of the *Tibjān*

About [[1929]], Teungku Haron composed — working from a manuscript of the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān*]] — an Achehnese treatise on the history of Islam in [[Acheh]]. The work is described by Voorhoeve as "allerlei verteld over de Islam in Atjèh" — "all sorts of things related about Islam in Acheh" — and is, in effect, an Achehnese adaptation and continuation of the *Tibjān*. An extensive summary, almost a translation, was made of the work by **H. T. Damsté**; with the help of this reliable guide, Voorhoeve was able to recover the passages of the *Tibjān* in Achehnese, and to use them for the textual criticism of the work.

## The Achehnese author's critical use of the *Tibjān*

Teungku Haron did not accept the text of the *Tibjān* uncritically. Voorhoeve records that he used, among other sources, the *al-Farq baina al-firaq* of ʿAbd al-Qādir al-Baghdādī (probably the printed edition) to correct corrupt names of sects. This critical use of the *Tibjān* is itself a document of the continuing vitality of the scholastic tradition in Achehnese religious scholarship in the early twentieth century, more than two centuries after Raniri's departure from Acheh in [[1644]].

## Limitations of the adaptation

The Achehnese adaptation does not carry the *Tibjān* further than the first sect of the Jabriyya (p. 57 of the Voorhoeve text). For the remainder of the *Tibjān*, the Achehnese adaptation is therefore not available as a witness. Within these limits, however, it is a reliable guide to the Achehnese reading of the *Tibjān* in the early twentieth century, and a valuable witness to the textual criticism of the work.

## Significance

Teungku Haron is the principal witness to the survival of the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān*]] as a living text of Achehnese religious scholarship in the early twentieth century. His Achehnese adaptation, preserved in the manuscript of the Koninklijk Instituut voor Taal-, Land- en Volkenkunde at The Hague, is one of the principal witnesses collated by Voorhoeve in the apparatus criticus of the [[1955]] edition, and is the only one of his witnesses that preserves the Achehnese reading of the work. His critical use of the *Tibjān* — and his correction of corrupt names of sects by reference to the *al-Farq baina al-firaq* of ʿAbd al-Qādir al-Baghdādī — is itself a document of the continuing vitality of the scholastic tradition in Achehnese religious scholarship.
