**Lieutenant‑Colonel Raaff** was a Dutch army officer who commanded the forces sent against the Padri insurgents in the interior of [[Sumatra]] during the early 1820s. His initial expedition in [[1821]] achieved little, but after reinforcement in [[February]] [[1823]] he led a larger force that attempted to capture the Padri stronghold at [[Marapalm]].

## Campaign against the Padri

Raaff’s troops, reinforced by a detachment of the [[Royal Netherlands Marine Corps|Dutch Marine Corps]] under Lieutenant J. A. van den Velden, fought a bloody engagement from [[13 April]] to [[17 April]] [[1824]] at Marapalm, ultimately forced to retreat after heavy casualties.

## Later Service

After the failure at Marapalm, Raaff withdrew his forces to [[Pagar Roejong]] and later participated in operations on [[Celebes]] and the southern Sumatra expeditions of 1824‑1825.
