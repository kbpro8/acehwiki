---
title: Tuan di Keude
aliases: [Teungku tujoh blaih hdih]
tags: [person, saint]
---

Named for the former keude (market) of Meura'sa near his tomb, and for his unusually long grave, whose two tombstones lie 17 yards apart.

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
