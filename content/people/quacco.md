**Quacco** was a rebel commander whose outpost was recaptured by Dutch forces under **[[Kolonel De Salve|Colonel De Salve]]** during the suppression of the **Wikkie‑kreek** uprising in the **[[1760s]]**. His position, together with that of **[[Baubé]]**, formed part of the network of rebel strongholds.

## Capture
The Dutch reported that Quacco’s outpost fell after a concerted attack, contributing to the overall weakening of the insurgency.
