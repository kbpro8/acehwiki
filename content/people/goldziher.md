**Goldziher** was a pioneering Hungarian scholar of Islamic studies, renowned for his works on Arab poetry and religious literature. The source cites his treatise *Abh. Arab. Philologie I* in a note comparing the Arab poetic motif of a necklace to shame and disgrace.

## Cited Work
The reference appears on page 84: “*Spot, smaad en schande worden in de Arabische poëzie dikwijls met een halsketen vergeleken… (zie: Goldziher, Abh. Arab. Philologie I, 100).*” This illustrates the use of Goldziher’s scholarship in explaining metaphorical language within Arabic poetic tradition.

## Connection to Aceh
While Goldziher’s research centred on the Arab world, his methodological approaches to literary motifs have influenced the study of Malay and Acehnese Islamic literature, particularly in interpreting poetic imagery found in texts such as the *Burda* of [[Al-Busiri]] and its Malay translations.
