**Radja Dewa** was a Menangkabau immigrant chief in Alaslanden, brought Islam to Alassers mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Came from Rau (Menangkabau) to Alaslanden and brought Islam there.

## Role

[[Menangkabau]] immigrant chief in [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]], brought [[Islam]] to [[Alas|Alassers]]

## Account from Jongejans

Came from Rau ([[Menangkabau]]) to [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]] and brought [[Islam]] there. Became the stamvader (founding father) of the influential geslacht (lineage) of the [[penghoeloe Tjiq]].

## Significance

Radja Dewa appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
