**Sergeant W. J. van Ackeren** was a Dutch marine sergeant who earned the [[Militaire Willemsorde|Military William Order]] 4th class for his actions during the 1824 expedition against the sultanates of [[Tanette]] and [[Soepa]].

## Award

His citation notes his steadfast conduct during the assault on Soepa, after which he was formally recognized by the Dutch crown.
