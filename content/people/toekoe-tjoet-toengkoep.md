**Toekoe Tjoet Toengkoep** was an [[Achehnese|Atjehnese]] *[[Uleebalang|oeloebalang]]* of the [[XIII Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]] who, together with [[Toekoe Noq van Marassa]] and [[Toekoe Nju Banta]] (the *panglima* of the XXVI Moekims), was entrusted by the Dutch with the leadership of the *driemanschap* (three-man directorate) charged with the purification of the [[XXVI Moekims]] of *Moslemin* bands. He is named in the [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] as the *oeloebalang* who, in the course of the operation, took *zulk een vreemde houding aan* (such a strange attitude) that he was *tijdelijk naar Melaboe moest verbannen worden* (had to be temporarily banished to [[Melaboe]]).

## The driemanschap of the XXVI Moekims

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that, when the Dutch attempted to extend the system of using Atjehnese auxiliaries against the *Moslemin* to the [[XXVI Moekims]] — a terrain in which [[Toekoe Oemar]] had *weinig invloed* (little influence) — the leadership of the operation was entrusted to a *driemanschap* of friendly *hoofden*: [[Toekoe Noq van Marassa|Toekoe Neq van Marassa]], [[Toekoe Nju Banta]] (the *panglima* of the XXVI Moekims) and Toekoe Tjoet Toengkoep (the *oeloebalang* of the [[XIII Moekims]]). The album's characterisation of the *driemanschap* — *dat liep heel wat minder gunstig af, dan in de XXV Moekims het geval was geweest* (that went much less favourably than had been the case in the XXV Moekims) — identifies the XXVI Moekims operation as a relative failure.

## The strange attitude and the banishment

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that the failure of the *driemanschap* in the XXVI Moekims was due in part to the *vreemde houding* (strange attitude) of Toekoe Tjoet Toengkoep, who was *tijdelijk naar Melaboe moest verbannen worden* (had to be temporarily banished to Melaboe). The album does not specify the nature of the *vreemde houding* — whether it was overt sympathy with the *Moslemin*, a refusal to cooperate with the other two members of the *driemanschap*, or some other form of obstruction. The banishment to [[Melaboe]] — the principal port of the [[Westkust van Atjeh|Westkust]] — was, however, the Dutch response, and it is one of the principal documented instances of the Dutch use of internal banishment as a disciplinary instrument against unreliable *oeloebalang* allies.

## The cost of the XXVI Moekims operation

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that the failure of the *driemanschap* in the XXVI Moekims required the Dutch to intervene with their own troops. The album records that the Dutch *waren genoodzaakt met een geheel bataljon en eenige artillerie uit te rukken, om de gemeenschap met een dier door ons bezette punten te herstellen, bij welke gelegenheid 1 officier en 3 minderen sneuvelden en 3 officieren en 32 minderen gewond werden* — *were compelled to march out with an entire battalion and some artillery, in order to restore communication with one of the points occupied by us, on which occasion 1 officer and 3 subordinates were killed and 3 officers and 32 subordinates were wounded*. The XXVI Moekims operation was thus one of the costliest engagements of the late *concentratie* period.

## Significance

Toekoe Tjoet Toengkoep's significance in the documentary record of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] is as the *oeloebalang* of the XIII Moekims whose *vreemde houding* and consequent banishment to [[Melaboe]] were among the principal causes of the failure of the *driemanschap* in the XXVI Moekims. His appearance in the [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] is brief, but it identifies the *driemanschap* as the institutional form of the Dutch attempt to extend the Atjehnese-auxiliary system to the XXVI Moekims, and it identifies the banishment to Melaboe as one of the principal Dutch disciplinary instruments against unreliable *oeloebalang* allies.
