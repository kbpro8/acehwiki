**Ala-ad-Din Mahmoedsjah** (died early 1874 (shortly after 31 January 1874)) was a Sultan of Atjeh mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Sultan of Atjeh at the time of the Dutch assault on the Kraton.

## Role

[[Sultan of Atjeh (d. 1874)|Sultan of Atjeh]]

## Dates

died early [[1874]] (shortly after [[31 January]] [[1874]])

## Account from Jongejans

[[Sultan of Atjeh (d. 1874)|Sultan of Atjeh]] at the time of the Dutch assault on the [[Kraton of Aceh|Kraton]]. The Dutch government considered itself to have stepped into his place upon his death from cholera, days after the [[Kraton of Aceh|Kraton]] was captured.

## Significance

Ala-ad-Din Mahmoedsjah appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
