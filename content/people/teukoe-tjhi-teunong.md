**Teukoe Tjhi Teunong** was a Resistance leader; previously oelèëbalang of Keureutòë mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Was fusilleerd (executed by firing squad) in 1905.

## Role

[[Bantah (Acehnese term)|Resistance]] leader; previously [[oelèëbalang]] of [[Keureutòë]]

## Account from Jongejans

Was fusilleerd (executed by firing squad) in [[1905]]. His widow [[Tjoet Meuthia]] and ~6-year-old son were taken to the forest by his party; [[Pang Nanggròë]] married [[Tjoet Meuthia]] and the boy was proclaimed [[oelèëbalang]] of [[Keureutòë]].

## Significance

Teukoe Tjhi Teunong appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
