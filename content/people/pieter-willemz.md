**Pieter Willemz** (also written *Willemsz*, *Willemsoen*) was a senior merchant (*Opperkoopman*) of the [[Vereenigde Oost-Indische Compagnie|VOC]] who arrived from [[Batavia]] in [[1641]] as the designated replacement of [[Jacob Compostel|Jacob Compostel]] as *Opperhoofd* (chief) of the [[Dutch lodge at Atjeh|Company's lodge in Atjeh]]. He was the third-ranking Dutchman in [[Pieter Soury|Soury]]'s [[Pieter Soury's mission to Atjeh (1641)|embassy to Atjeh]] and a member of the six-man suite that presented the *missive* of the Gouverneur-Generaal to the [[Sultana of Atjeh (1641)|Sultana]] on [[1 July]] [[1641]].

## Role in the Soury legation

The legation suite, in order of precedence, was: *Commissaris* [[Pieter Soury|Soury]]; the *Opperkoopman* [[Jacob Compostel|Compostel]], outgoing *Opperhoofd* of the lodge; the *Opperkoopman* Willemz, Compostel's recently-arrived replacement from Batavia; the *Secretaris* [[Jan van Riebeeck|Jan van Riebeeck]]; and two *Onderkooplieden* (junior merchants). Godee Molsbergen describes the six as "een ernstige groep … de gezichten strakgetrokken onder den indruk van de plechtigheid, wetend dat het minste verzuim of gebrek aan decorum zou worden opgemerkt door Atjehers, Engelschen, Mooren en anderen, die de Compagnie geen goed hart toedroegen en haar succes misgunden" — a serious group, faces tense under the impression of the ceremony, knowing that the slightest neglect or want of decorum would be noticed by Atjehers, English, Moors and others who bore the Company no good will and grudged its success.

## The hand-over

The presence of both Compostel and Willemz in the legation suite at the audience of [[1 July]] [[1641]] was the formal moment of the hand-over of the [[Dutch lodge at Atjeh|Atjeh lodge]]. The Atjeh court saw both men together — the outgoing and the incoming *Opperhoofd* — and the Sultana's gift of an *[[Atjehsche feestjas|Atjehsch gewaad]]* with three golden rings on the collar went to the new embassy including Willemz, but pointedly not to Compostel. (See [[Jacob Compostel]].)

## Significance

For [[Acheh|Atjeh]]'s history, Willemz is the *Opperhoofd*-designate of the [[Dutch lodge at Atjeh|lodge]] under the newly-acceded [[Sultana of Atjeh (1641)|Sultana]] — the resident face of the VOC at the moment when the sultanate was transitioning from the rule of Sultan [[Iskander Tsani]] to the long era of female rule. His surviving mention in the Soury embassy record preserves the otherwise obscure fact of the Compostel–Willemz handover at this critical juncture.
