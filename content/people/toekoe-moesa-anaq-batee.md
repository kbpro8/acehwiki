**Toekoe Moesa Anaq Batee** was an Achehnese leader hostile to the Netherlands who in [[1896]] attacked the *passer*-goers travelling between [[Lam Barih|Lambarih]] and [[Anagaloeng]] in [[Groot-Atjeh]] and penetrated into the [[IX Moekims]], one of the signs by which [[G. B. Hooijer]] marked the collapse of Dutch security in the eastern outposts.

## Attacks of 1896

Hooijer recorded that after the hope of continued support from [[Toekoe Moeda Soleiman]], guardian of the young [[Panglima Polim]], had proved vain, the population of the southern part of the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] took flight. Toekoe Moesa Anaq Batee then attacked the people going to and from the *[[Passer|passer]]* between [[Lam Barih|Lambarih]] and [[Anagaloeng]] and forced his way into the [[IX Moekims]]. The Dutch post on [[Boekit Daroe]] was continuously fired upon, and everything indicated, in Hooijer's words, that the *[[Moslemin]]* still needed a lesson.

In response the *onmisbare* — indispensable — [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]] was summoned, and to support him the Dutch erected the temporary fortification of [[Kroeng Gloempang]] between [[Tjot Rang]] and [[Anagaloeng]], garrisoned with a company of infantry and a detachment of fortress artillery.
