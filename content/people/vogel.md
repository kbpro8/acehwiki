**Vogel** was a Dutch sergeant of the *[[Korps Marechaussee|marechaussée]]*, called by the [[Ambonese soldiers|Ambonese]] of his corps *Oom-Pandjang* — long uncle — who is remembered for his attempt to save an Acehnese boy during the [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|surprise of Anak Galoeng]] on [[29 June]] [[1896]].

## The episode of the burning hut

[[P. H. R. Beuming]] relates that in the confused fighting inside the *[[Benting|benting]]*, which was filled with open huts that made the struggle more difficult, Vogel became aware of half a dozen Acehnese and a small boy in one of them. He summoned them to come out and surrender, and they refused.

*Goede raad was duur.* As little firing as possible was permitted, so as not to hit the comrades posted outside, and moreover Vogel wished at any price to save the child. He therefore had the hut set on fire and went inside himself. Under a rain of *[[Klewang|klewang]]* blows, which he parried masterfully while dealing killing blows right and left, he seized the boy and dragged him out of the hut under a thundering cheer from his men.

*De jongen was gered, doch slechts voor een oogenblik. Toen men hem los liet, sprong hij de hut weer binnen en kwam met de anderen om in de vlammen* — the boy was saved, but only for a moment; when they let him go he sprang back into the hut and perished with the others in the flames. Beuming records that it grieved the good non-commissioned officer greatly that he had not been able to prevent this sad end.
