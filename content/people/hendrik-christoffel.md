**Hendrik Christoffel** was a Dutch military officer and Captain in the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]].

## Military Career and Service in Aceh

Christoffel served as an Under-Lieutenant and Captain in the Marechaussee, receiving the Knight 3rd Class Military William Order (*M.W.O.*) in [[1904]]. He gained fame for organizing specialized elite mobile pursuit detachments (*Colonne Christoffel*) that conducted relentless long-distance tracking of Acehnese guerrilla leaders in the deep jungle.
