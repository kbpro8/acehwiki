**Gotfried Coenraad Ernst van Daalen** was a Dutch colonial officer who served as Civil and Military Governor of the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] from [[1905]] to [[1908]], and is best remembered for the military expedition of [[1904]] — when still a colonel — that brought the [[Gajoland|Gajoland]] and [[Alaslanden|Alaslanden]] under Dutch control in a single *zegetocht* (victorious campaign). His name is commemorated in the *van Daalengebergte* — the mountain range of the central Barisan that forms the eastern wall of the Gayo high plateau — and in the *systeem Graafland, van Daalen en van Heutsz*, the combined military-political doctrine credited with breaking the back of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-krijg]].

## The Gayo-Alas campaign of 1904

Before the [[1904]] campaign, *De Buitenbezittingen* records, the Dutch "wisten goed als niets" — knew effectively nothing — of the inhabitants of the Alas valley. The expedition van Daalen led in June [[1904]] brought the country "ineens geheel onder onze macht" (at once entirely under our power), but not without resistance; the years that followed saw stubborn defiance, particularly in the early period, before a condition of order and rust was established that benefited the population. The same campaign traversed the Gayo lands, where the colonel installed the [[Kédjoerön Bédén|minor Kédjoerön Bédén]] — a child taken from Tamiang by his father, a previously deposed Kédjoerön, and paraded through [[Koeta Radja]] to sign the [[Korte Verklaring]] without the required consultation of the other *rédjë's* of [[Gajoland|Gayo Loeës]] — as the legitimate territorial chief of the Gayo Loeës in [[1904]].

## Governorship, 1905–1908

Promoted to the governorship in [[1905]] in succession to [[Jhr. J. C. v. d. Wyck|Jhr. J. C. v. d. Wyck]], van Daalen continued the consolidation of the territory begun under [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|van Heutsz]]. His tenure, however, was marked by "al te hard doorgevoerde represaille-maatregelen" — overly harsh reprisal measures — and by "scherpe besnoeiing der adat-inkomsten," the sharp curtailment of the customary revenues of the native chiefs. These policies provoked the *vrij hevige opflikkering van het verzet* (fairly fierce flare-up of resistance) of [[1907]] and [[1908]] that his successor [[H. N. A. Swart|Swart]] would have to overcome through a more conciliatory approach.

## Toponymic legacy

The *van Daalengebergte*, described in the geographical section of *De Buitenbezittingen* as the southeastern spur of the [[Wilhelmina-keten]] penetrating deep into the eastern coastal plain, perpetuates his name in the physical geography of northern Sumatra. It separates the basin of the upper [[Djamboe Aje]] from the high plateau of [[Serbëdjadi|Serbëdjadi]] and forms the eastern rim of the great *Laoet Tawar* basin.
