**Teukoe Tjhi' Mohamad Ali Akbar** was a Zelfbestuurder (self-ruler) of the landschap Kaway XVI (Aceh's Westkust) mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Founded the atjehsche vereenigingen van rubberplanters (Acehnese rubber-planters' associations) in Kaway XVI on his own initiative, kept them under his leadership and supervision.

## Role

[[zelfbestuurder|Zelfbestuurder]] (self-ruler) of the [[landschap]] [[Kaway XVI]] (Aceh's [[Westkust]])

## Account from Jongejans

Founded the [[Atjeh (Aceh)|atjehsche]] vereenigingen van rubberplanters (Acehnese rubber-planters' associations) in [[Kaway XVI]] on his own initiative, kept them under his leadership and supervision. Result: [[Kaway XVI]] became the most important rubber producer of the entire [[afdeeling]] [[Westkust van Atjeh]].

## Significance

Teukoe Tjhi' Mohamad Ali Akbar appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
