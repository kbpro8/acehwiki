**Toekoe Moeda Ia Malim** was an Acehnese notable — the title *Toekoe Moeda* (Teuku Muda) indicating a position of some authority, possibly an [[Uleebalang|uleebalang]] or subordinate territorial lord — whose personal seal was preserved among the gold and silver objects in Cabinet 3 (Atjeh) of the [[Koninklijk Bataviaasch Genootschap van Kunsten en Wetenschappen|Batavian Society's]] treasure room in [[Koeta Radja|Batavia]].

## The seal

The seal of Toekoe Moeda Ia Malim is catalogued in the 1928 treasure-room guide as item no. 7 of Cabinet 3, listed simply as *"Zegel van Toekoe Moeda Ia malim"* — "Seal of Toekoe Moeda Ia malim." The guide does not specify the material of the seal, though most personal seals in the Acehnese section are of silver or silver-gilt, consistent with the broader assemblage of Acehnese metalwork in the collection.

In the traditional Acehnese political system, personal seals served as instruments of authority and authentication. An [[Uleebalang|uleebalang]] or territorial lord would use his seal to validate letters, land transactions, and other official acts. The presence of Toekoe Moeda Ia Malim's seal in the Batavian Society's collection suggests it was acquired during the colonial period, likely as a result of military operations, surrender, or the dismantling of a local court's possessions during the [[Aceh War]].

## Name and title analysis

The name *Ia Malim* is notable: *Malim* (from Arabic *muʿallim*, "teacher" or "instructor") is a title frequently associated with religious functionaries or scholars in Acehnese society. The combination of the secular-political title *Toekoe Moeda* (Young Lord) with the religious title *Malim* may indicate a figure who held both territorial and religious authority, a dual role not uncommon in certain Acehnese districts. Alternatively, *Ia Malim* may be a personal name incorporating the title.
