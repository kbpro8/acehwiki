---
title: Teungku di Lam Gut
aliases: [Jalaluddin]
tags: [tokoh, ulama]
---

Proper name Jalaluddin, who received his title from his wife's gampong of Lam Gut. A pupil of the Malay teacher Abduggamad, he became kali of the XXVI Mukims and wrote, in 1826-27, the Tambih al-ghafilin, a comprehensive translation of Abul-Laith as-Samarqandi's Arabic work — the only Acehnese book lithographed in print up to Hurgronje's time. His son-in-law was Cheh Marahaban, who eventually succeeded to his teaching and kali-ship.
