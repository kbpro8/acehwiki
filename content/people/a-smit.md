**A. Smit** was a **Corporal der mariniers** who fell during the **[[30 March]] [[1893]]** assault on a **redoute** on the **Tamiang** river. His death, together with that of **[[Viergever (corporal)]]**, is noted in the Dutch naval yearbook of **[[1893]]**.

## Significance

His sacrifice underscores the fierce resistance encountered by Dutch forces in the interior of **[[Aceh]]**.
