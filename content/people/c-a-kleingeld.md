**C. A. Kleingeld** was a Dutch plantation inspector (*inspecteur*) of the Sipef (Sumatran Planters' Association) who, following the survey of plantation enterprises in [[Atjeh|Atjeh]] in early [[1950]], remained for several months at [[Langsar Estate]] to provide technical advice to the newly appointed Indonesian manager [[Said Abubakar]]. His presence exemplified the transitional arrangement whereby European technical expertise was retained while management was transferred to Indonesian administrators.
