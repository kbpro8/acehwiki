**Debats** was a European fusilier of the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Netherlands Indies Army]], *Algemeen Stamboek* no. 27702, severely wounded in the [[Patrol of Captain Van Blokland (7 March 1896)|patrol of Captain Van Blokland]] near [[Lam Bada|Anak Baté]] on [[7 March]] [[1896]].

He is named by [[P. H. R. Beuming]] in the casualty roll of the action as the only European severely wounded, alongside two native fusiliers; twenty-one other ranks were lightly wounded, and eight Europeans were killed.
