**A. W. P. Weitzel** was a Dutch politician appointed Minister of War before the treatment of the Staatsbegrooting, who took up his function on [[6 October]] [[6 October]] [[1873]]. His budget was treated on [[17 December]] [[17 December]] [[1873]], in the immediate aftermath of the failure of the [[First Aceh Expedition (1873)|first Aceh expedition]] and of the rejection of the budget of Marine of [[11 December]] [[11 December]] [[1873]] which had led to the discharge of the Minister of Marine [[Brocx|Brocx]].

## Appointment, 6 October 1873

Before the treatment of the Staatsbegrooting, the new Minister of War, A. W. P. Weitzel, was appointed, who took up his function on [[6 October]] [[6 October]] [1873]. His budget was treated on [[17 December]] [[17 December]] [1873], in the immediate aftermath of the failure of the first Aceh expedition and of the parliamentary crisis occasioned by the rejection of the budget of Marine.

## Significance

Weitzel's appointment as Minister of War in [[October 1873|October 1873]] came at a critical juncture in the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. The [[First Aceh Expedition (1873)|first Aceh expedition]] had failed in [[April 1873|April 1873]] with the death of [[J. H. R. Köhler|General Köhler]]; the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)|second expedition]] was being prepared; the condition of the Indies army had been exposed by the failure of the first expedition; and the Dutch navy had been shown to be inadequate for the blockade of the coast of Atjeh. The Minister of War in this period had therefore to preside over the levying of troops in the Netherlands to bring the Indies army up to strength, the equipment of all troops with breech-loading rifles, and the reinforcement of the artillery with newer breech-loading guns from Europe.
