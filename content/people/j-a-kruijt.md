**J. A. Kruijt** (also rendered **Kruyt** — both spellings occur in the text of *The Achehnese*) was a Dutch author whose work *Atjeh en de Atjehers* ("Acheh and the Achehnese") is cited by [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] in Vol. I of *The Achehnese* as one of the standard sources on the country and people of [[Acheh]]. The name appears in the chapter dealing with the official documents of the Achehnese sultanate, where Snouck Hurgronje refers to Kruijt's *Atjeh en de Atjehers* (p. 58) in connection with the formal letters patent issued under the Sultan's nine-fold seal.

The reference occurs in the long note appended to Snouck Hurgronje's analysis of the **letters patent given to the [[Panglima Meuseugit Raya]]**. This document, composed in a mixture of Malay and bad Arabic, opens with a long invocation of blessings — on the [[Prophet Muhammad|Prophet]], on his four companions [[Abu Bakr]], [[Omar]], [[Uthman]], and [[Ali]], on "the glorious Pole of the world" [[Abdul-Qadir Jilani|Shaikh Abdul-Qadir Jilani]], and on the whole line of deceased sultans whose names also appear in the *chab sikureu'eng* (the nine-fold seal). It is dated Monday, the 4th of Rabi' al-Awwal 1307 Hijrah — equivalent to the 28th of October [[1889]] — and is issued in the name of [[Sultan Alaedin Muhamad Daud Shah Juhan|Sultan Alaedin Muhamat Dawot Shah Juhan]] by his "Keureukon Katiboy Muluk" or royal secretary (see [[Keureukon Katiboy Muluk]]). For comparison of the formal features of such documents, Snouck Hurgronje refers the reader to Kruijt's work.

Kruijt's *Atjeh en de Atjehers* is also cited elsewhere in Vol. I in connection with the civil wars of Acheh and the material culture of the Achehnese (especially their dress and weapons). On the latter subject, Snouck Hurgronje groups Kruijt's work with the *Atjehsch Staatsbestuur* of [[K. F. H. Van Langen|Van Langen]] and the *Notulen van het Bataviaasch Genootschap* of 1892 as one of the principal Dutch-language sources for Achehnese material culture. The same work is commended for "a very correct estimate of the weight to be attached to civil wars in Acheh" — a characteristically sober assessment of the limited scale and local character of Achehnese hostilities, which Snouck Hurgronje considers essential to a proper understanding of the [[Acheh War|Achehnese War]].

Kruijt's writings thus belong to the late-nineteenth-century Dutch colonial literature on Acheh, alongside the works of [[K. F. H. Van Langen|Van Langen]], [[G. H. Niemann|Niemann]], [[Coolsma]], and [[L. W. C. van den Berg|Van den Berg]]. The variant spellings **Kruyt** and **Kruijt** (and the OCR-garbled **Kniijt**) all refer to the same author.
