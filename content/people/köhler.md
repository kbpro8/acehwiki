**Köhler** (killed 1873 (implied)) was a Dutch colonial general, commanded first Aceh expedition mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Stood under a tree (later the 'Kölee boom' / Köhler boom) in front of the meusigit at Koeta Radja when he was mortally struck.

## Role

Dutch colonial general, commanded [[First Aceh Expedition|first Aceh expedition]]

## Dates

killed [[1873]] (implied)

## Account from Jongejans

Stood under a tree (later the 'Kölee boom' / [[Generaal-Köhler-boom|Köhler boom]]) in front of the [[meusigit]] at [[Koeta-Radja|Koeta Radja]] when he was mortally struck. The tree itself was lost but pieces preserved in the [[Legermuseum]].

## Significance

Köhler appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
