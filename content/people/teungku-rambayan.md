---
title: Teungku Rambayan
tags: [person, literary-work]
---

A learned Teungku with hundreds of disciples, summoned in the Hikayat Pochut Muhamat by Pangulee Beunaroe to interpret the prince's letter; he diplomatically softens its content and advises Beunaroe to welcome the prince.
