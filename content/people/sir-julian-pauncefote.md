**Sir Julian Pauncefote** was the British permanent under-secretary who, from the Dutch side, was well known as the principal leader (*voorname leider*) of the negotiations of [[1884]] in the [[Nisero affair (1883–1885)|Tenom question]]. At the end of [[1885]] he was awarded the Grand Cross of the Order of St Michael and St George, as [[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] noted together with the honouring of the Penang Chinese [[Koh Thian Poh]] and the elevation of the English negotiator Mr. [[Sir William Maxwell|Maxwell]] to the English peerage — tokens by which he judged England's conduct in the whole affair (see [[Nisero affair (1883–1885)]]).
