**Maajoor Pusch** served as a **major** in the Dutch colonial forces stationed in the **Wikkie‑kreek** region. He died before the **[[1760s]]** campaign and was succeeded by **[[Majoor Hamell|Captain Hamell]]**, **[[Maajoor De|Major De]]**, and **[[Luitenant‑Kolonel Douglas|Lieutenant‑Colonel Douglas]]**.

## Succession
Pusch’s death prompted a re‑organisation of command, influencing the subsequent Dutch strategy against the rebels.
