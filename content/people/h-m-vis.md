**H. M. Vis** — *1e Luitenant* (First Lieutenant) of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army|K.N.I.L.]] — was the commander of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|marechaussée brigades]] attached to the Dutch columns in [[Groot-Atjeh]] during the first semester of [[1896]], in which capacity he led the marechaussée at the [[Relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit (8 April 1896)|relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit]] on [[8 April]] [[1896]], at the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|great tuchtiging of the IV and VI Moekims of 23–25 May 1896]], and at the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|tuchtiging of the Lam-Krak Moekims of 9–10 June 1896]]. He is the principal marechaussée officer of the first semester of [[1896]] in *[[ATJEH '96 (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96]]* (1913).

## The 1896 campaign

Vis commanded the marechaussée brigades — three brigades of about 54 men each — attached to the relief column of [[G. F. Soeters|Lt.-Kol. Soeters]] for the [[Relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit (8 April 1896)|relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit]] on [[8 April]] [[1896]]. The *1e Deel* of *[[ATJEH '96 (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96]]* records his leadership of the marechaussée through the actions at [[Nesoch]], [[Lam-Hasan]], [[Lam-Permai]], and the [[Kroeng Lingkar]] crossings, and again at the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|great tuchtiging of the IV and VI Moekims of 23–25 May 1896]]. The *Tweede Deel* records his participation in the [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|surprise of Ana' Galoeng]] of [[29 June]], where his brigades were among the first over the palisade of the *benteng* (fort).

## Personal leadership under fire

Vis is repeatedly mentioned in the after-action reports of *ATJEH '96* for his personal leadership under fire, his tactical initiative, and his care for his men. At the [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|surprise of Ana' Galoeng]] on [[29 June]] [[1896]], Vis and [[K. J. C. Rijnen|1e Luit. Rijnen]] with the marechaussée brigades and the Ambonese marechaussée [[Walewangko]] (No. 9487) were the first over the palisade. After the gate was opened from inside, Vis led his brigades in pursuit of the fleeing Acehnese defenders along the riverbank.

## Significance

Vis is the principal marechaussée officer of the first semester of [[1896]] in *[[ATJEH '96 (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96]]*. His brigades were the principal manoeuvre element of the Dutch columns of the first semester, and his personal leadership at the [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|surprise of Ana' Galoeng]] placed him at the centre of the most celebrated Dutch tactical success of the year.
