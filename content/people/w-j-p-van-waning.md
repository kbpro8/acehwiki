**W. J. P. van Waning** was a **Captain ter zee** who commanded the Dutch naval squadron comprising **Hr.Ms. “Pri Hendrik der Nederlanden”**, **Hr.Ms. “Mataram”**, **Hr.Ms. “Nias”**, and **Hr.Ms. “Batavia”** during the **[[1896]]** expedition against **[[Pedir (place)]]** and the **[[Selimoen-colonne]]**.

## Command

His squadron provided riverine transport and fire support for the landing division during the **[[1896]]** campaign in the **[[Aceh]]** highlands.

## Significance

Van Waning’s leadership illustrates the integration of naval firepower into Dutch ground operations during the later phases of the **Aceh War**.
