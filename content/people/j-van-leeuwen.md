**J. van Leeuwen** was a **[[marinier|marine]]** of 1st class who received the **[[Eervolle Vermelding]]** by **[[Royal Decree 8 February 1859 No. 64]]** for his actions during the **[[First Boni Expedition 1859]]**.

## Service

His commendation reflects the Dutch effort to suppress resistance on **[[Boni]]**.
