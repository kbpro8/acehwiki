**Luitenant Veerman** was a Dutch lieutenant of the [[Oost-Indisch Leger|Netherlands East Indies Army]] killed, together with [[Luitenant Hageman]] and several men, in the attack on the Achehnese fortification of [[Kota Toeankoe]] in [[Groot-Atjeh]] some five years before the events of [[1893]] described in *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]*.

## Death before Kota Toeankoe

Hooijer recorded that nearly three battalions with the necessary cavalry and artillery had entered the same terrain in which [[Overste Sievers]] later operated — north of the *kampong* (village) strip — in order to take [[Kota Toeankoe]], which threatened the Dutch line of posts. The lieutenants Veerman and [[Luitenant Hageman|Hageman]], with several of their men, had fallen some hours earlier in their attempt to take possession of the *benteng*, which they had hoped to find evacuated by its garrison since it was to be approached from the other side by an enveloping battalion.

After a long and furious fight, all that fell into Dutch hands, at a cost of three times as many dead and wounded, was an outwork of ten by fifteen metres of interior space, within which lay a heap of thirty fallen Achehnese.
