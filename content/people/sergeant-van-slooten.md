**Sergeant Van Slooten** was a Sergeant, killed Pang Nanggròë mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. In September 1910 his brigade surprised Pang Nanggròë in marshland; Pang Nanggròë fell.

## Role

Sergeant, killed [[Pang Nanggròë]]

## Account from Jongejans

In [[September]] [[1910]] his [[Brigade (Maréchaussée)|brigade]] surprised [[Pang Nanggròë]] in marshland; [[Pang Nanggròë]] fell.

## Significance

Sergeant Van Slooten appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
