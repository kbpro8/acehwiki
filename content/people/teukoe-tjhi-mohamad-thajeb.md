**Teukoe Tjhi Mohamad Thajeb** was the *[[Oeléebalang|oeléebalang]]* of [[Peureula (Perlak)|Peureula (Perlak)]] from [[1913]] onward, succeeding his father [[Teukoe Tjhi Perlak|Teukoe Tjhi Perlak]] in the chieftaincy at the moment when the oil-cijns was regularised into the *landschapskas* and the chief received a fixed income. He is the principal example in Broersma's *[[Verkenningen in Atjeh (1923)|Verkenningen in Atjeh]]* of the *verlichte hoofden* — the enlightened chiefs — *'die niet vreemd wenschten te blijven aan Westersche begrippen'* — who do not wish to remain strangers to Western ideas.

## Education and Accession
Mohamad Thajeb had been sent to school at [[Bandoeng|Bandoeng]] in West Java, where his father visited him in [[1910]] and used the occasion to inspect the blind institute, the girls' school and the hat-plaiting workshops. Broersma notes that *'had hij geen Atjehsch verleden toen hij als bestuurder ontrad'* — *he had no Atjehnese past when he entered office as ruler* — meaning that his schooling had detached him from the older ways of his country and made him at home in *'de sferen, waarin het jongere geslacht van Inlandsche bestuurders moet kunnen verkeeren.'* His father, who followed affairs closely, was *'niet ouderwets is in den minder goeden zin des woords'* — *not old-fashioned in the worse sense of the word* — and supported his son's modernising tendencies.

## Volksraad Membership
In [[1918]] and [[1919]] Mohamad Thajeb was a member of the [[Volksraad|Volksraad]] in Batavia. During his absence the *oeléebalangschap* was held by his *[[Banta|banta]]* — the eldest of his brothers or relatives, a deputy whose position Snouck Hurgronje had compared to that of the *patih* in a Javanese *regentschap*.

## Cooperation with the Government
Broersma notes that, whenever trials or measures are needed for the improvement of the *volkscultures*, Mohamad Thajeb's cooperation may be reckoned upon. He belongs to the enlightened chiefs who do not wish to remain strangers to Western ideas, *'en hij is dat ook niet gebleven'* — *and he has not remained so.* The motor-ploughs of the landschap — *'het landschap Peureula (Perlak), belang hebbend bij een flinke peperteelt, heeft nu motorploegen'* — are the most visible instance of his modernising policy. The landschap ploughs a half-*bouw* of ground for its people at a fee (f 5 in [[1922]], f 7.50 in [[1923]]); the landschap has lost a little money on the operation but has gained an indirect benefit.

## Significance
Mohamad Thajeb's chieftaincy is a representative case of the second generation of post-pacification *oeléebalangs* — the sons sent to Dutch schools in Java, who returned to rule their *landschappen* with Western habits and Dutch-speaking colleagues. Broersma treats him alongside [[Mohamad Djohan Alamsjah|Mohamad Djohan Alamsjah]] of [[Peusangan]] as the type of the new chief.
