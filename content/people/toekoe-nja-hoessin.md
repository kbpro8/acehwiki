**Toekoe Nja Hoessin** was a chief of the [[XXVI Moekims]] who, after the Dutch [[Campaign against the XXVI Moekims (July 1879)|conquest of the XXVI Moekims]] of [[1 July|1]]–[[4 July]] [[1879]], fled with [[Toekoe Moeda Baij]], [[Toekoe Paya]] and a following of some two hundred persons of rank to the valley of [[Lamtoba]], as recorded in [[W. Cool]] and [[G. B. Hooijer]]'s *[[Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis (Cool & Hooijer, 1889)|Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis]]* ([[1889]]).

## Circuit of flight

The chiefs and their following, the Dutch learned at Lamtoba, had established themselves outside the kampongs in small encampments which the local population had not dared to hinder, the materials for the camps being drawn from the kampongs themselves. On the approach of the troops they withdrew higher up — to the mountain kampong of [[Kepaja Doewa]], or to [[Selimoen]], three day-marches further in the Atjeh-valley. It was on account of the reception given to Toekoe Nja Hoessin and his companions that the [[Lamtoba column (1879)|Lamtoba column]] had been sent to punish the district; the continued refusal of the chiefs to submit was the immediate cause of the [[March order for the Selimoen expedition (11 August 1879)|march order against Selimoen]] of [[11 August]] [[1879]].
