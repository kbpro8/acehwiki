**Viergever** was a **Corporal der mariniers** who attempted to breach the gate of a fortified **redoute** during the **[[30 March]] [[1893]]** action on the **Tamiang** river. He was killed by a bullet in the engagement.

## Action

His death is recorded alongside that of **Corporal A. Smit**, highlighting the high casualty rate among Dutch marines in close‑quarter assaults.
