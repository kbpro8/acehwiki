**Van Oordt, Captain-h lieutenant ter zee** was a Dutch naval officer who commanded the squadron that ascended the [[Pedir River]] in the early days of the [[Atjeh Expedition 1873-1876|Atjeh Expedition]] in [[January]] [[1874]]. Under his orders the marines attempted to land on the riverbank, but the river was shallow and the tide receded, forcing the troops to fight a defensive action that resulted in three dead and nine wounded among the marines. After the tide returned the squadron withdrew to [[Groot-Atjch|Great Atjeh]].

## Military actions

Van Oordt’s brief riverine operation illustrated the logistical difficulties faced by the Dutch forces in the low‑lying terrain of Aceh. His squadron’s withdrawal on [[29 January]] [[1874]] marked the end of the first phase of the 1874 campaign, after which the marines were redeployed to guard the mouth of the [[Atjeh River]].
