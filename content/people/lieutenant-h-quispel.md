**Lieutenant H. Quispel** was a second‑class marine lieutenant who served in the **Marine Landings Division** during the Dutch expeditions against Acehnese fortified settlements in 1868‑1869. He is listed among the four second‑class lieutenants (H. Quispel, F. W. Hudig, F. Zegers Veeckens, H. I. Cadet) who commanded landing parties.

## Aceh Operations

Quispel participated in the assault on [[Bandjar]] on [[24 October]] [[1868]], where his unit provided infantry support and helped breach the stone walls of the kampong.

## Significance

His service exemplifies the junior officer cadre that enabled the Dutch marine corps to conduct coordinated amphibious assaults in Aceh’s difficult terrain.
