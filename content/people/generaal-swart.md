**General Swart** — *Toean Gouverneur Swart* (in the Acehnese idiom of the old hunter [[Datoek]]) — was the Aceh-connoisseur general whose ten-year administration — "order and peace were consolidated in that province" — was the phase that "brought the Aceh War to an end." In *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* by Merac ([[1947]]), Swart is the figure whose administration consolidated order and peace in Aceh, and the *Toean Gouverneur* who was a *sobat kras* (heavy friend) of the old hunter [[Datoek]].

## The ten-year administration

"The history of Aceh, for example, repeatedly showed how the most effective ally of the Acehnese against the Government was the Government itself, which constantly struck a new direction, which constantly changed systems of administration, and only during the ten-year administration of the Aceh-connoisseur, General Swart, were order and peace consolidated in that province." The history of Aceh, for example, showed repeatedly how the most effective ally of the Acehnese against the Geumpenie (the colonial government) was the Geumpenie itself, which constantly struck a new direction, which constantly changed systems of administration, and only during the ten-year administration of the Aceh-connoisseur, General Swart, were order and peace consolidated in that province.

## Under the administration of Governor Swart

"Under the administration of Governor Swart the Aceh War came to an end," Buyckjens resumed again, "then gradually regulated conditions entered, and everywhere peace, order, and security began to prevail." Under the administration of Governor Swart the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]] came to an end, Buyckjens resumed: then gradually regulated conditions entered and everywhere peace, order, and security began to prevail. The administration of Governor Swart is therefore the book's — and the colonial memory's — marker of the end of the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]]: the ten-year administration that consolidated order and peace and that brought the Aceh War to an end.

## Datoek's sobat kras

"I was a 'heavy friend' with Toean Kristoppel, and Toean General Van Heutsz, and Toean Scheepens, and Toean Darlang, and Toean Boreel, and Toean Governor Swart." The old hunter [[Datoek]]'s list of his *sobat kras* (heavy friends) includes *Toean Gouverneur Swart* — Swart. The *Toean Gouverneur* — the Acehnese idiom for the Governor — is the book's — and Datoek's — memory of Swart as the heavy friend of the old hunter: the *Toean Gouverneur* who was a *sobat kras* of the Acehnese hunter of [[Blang Rakal]].

## Significance

General Swart is significant in *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* as the Aceh-connoisseur whose ten-year administration consolidated order and peace in Aceh and brought the Aceh War to an end. The book's argument — that Swart was the Aceh-connoisseur who, after the Geumpenie's constantly striking a new direction, consolidated order and peace and brought the Aceh War to an end — is the book's — and the colonial memory's — marker of the end of the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]]. Swart is therefore the book's marker of the administration that ended the war: the *Toean Gouverneur* whose ten-year administration consolidated order and peace and whose *sobat kras* was the old hunter Datoek.
