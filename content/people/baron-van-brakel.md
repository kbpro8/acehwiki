**Baron Van Brakel** was a Dutch officer who arrived from the **Canje‑gebied** with two companies to reinforce the **[[Stevensburg]]** post. His presence allowed the post to be held by a reduced garrison while the main effort focused on the **[[Accabré]]** expedition.

## Contribution
Van Brakel’s reinforcement helped secure the **[[Stevensburg]]** position without further Dutch casualties.
