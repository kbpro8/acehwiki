---

title: Abu Samaih
aliases: [Abu Shahmah]
tags: [person, literary-work]
---

Son of Caliph Umar, an excellent Quranic reciter who became self-conceited. A Jew persuaded him to drink and seduced him into an affair with his daughter; when the resulting child was shown to Umar, the Caliph had his own son scourged to death.

## See Also

- [[Nawawi]]

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
