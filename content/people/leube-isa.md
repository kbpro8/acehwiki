---
title: Leube Isa
aliases: [Teungku Bambi]
tags: [tokoh, penulis]
---

Author of the Hikayat ranto, who lived in Pidie at gampong Bambi and later at Klibeuet, and spent part of his life in the West Coast pepper colonies (rantos). His poem warns that no Acehnese returns from the rantos unharmed in body or soul, describing the suffering of deserted wives and children.
