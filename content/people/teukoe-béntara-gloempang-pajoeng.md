**Teukoe Béntara Gloempang Pajoeng** was een van de gevaarlijkste tegenstanders in [[Pidië]], lifted by the *[[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|marechaussée]]* on [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|van Heutsz]]'s orders in [[1898]].

After returning barefoot to [[Sigli]] on [[28 August]] [[1898]] and holding a reception at [[Koeta Radja]] on [[31 August]], van Heutsz returned to Pidië "and had one of the most dangerous opponents — Teukoe Béntara Gloempang Pajoeng — seized by the constabulary: in the space of four months Pidië was subjected."
