An Acehnese scholar credited with a century-old collection of the principal legal rules regarding marriage, preserved in three copies known to Hurgronje at the Berlin Royal Library and the Leiden Library.

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
