**Jacob de Keyser** was the [[Vereenigde Oost-Indische Compagnie|Compagnie]]'s deputatie-officer who, together with [[Balthasar Bort|Balthasar Bort]], conducted the Atjeh negotiation that produced the [[Treaty of Atjeh-VOC (5 October 1659)|peace of 5 October 1659]] between the [[Sultana of Atjeh (Taj ul-Alam)|sultane of Atjeh]] and the Compagnie. In Dr. F. W. Stapel's *Geschiedkundige Atlas van Nederland* he is the diplomat who, alongside Bort, formalised the end of the [[Blockade of Atjeh (1656)|1656 blockade]] crisis and obtained the ratification of the sultane.

## The 1659 deputation

The [[Blockade of Atjeh (1656)|1656 blockade]] and the [[Arrest of Anthony van Voorst|arrest of Anthony van Voorst]] had produced, by [[1657]]–[[1659]], an Acehnese desire to negotiate. "Atjeh sent an embassy to Batavia, to discuss there a new treaty of peace and friendship, on which one agreed on 20 June 1659" (*Atjeh een gezantschap naar Batavia zond, om daar een nieuw verdrag van vrede en vriendschap te bespreken, waarover men het 20 Juni 1659 eens werd*).

With the Atjehsche envoys, Batavia then sent a deputation to Atjeh to obtain the sultane's ratification. "With the envoys a deputation under Jacob de Keyser and Balthasar Bort then travelled to Atjeh, and obtained on 5 October the ratification by the sultane" (*Met de gezanten trok nu een deputatie onder Jacob de Keyser en Balthasar Bort naar Atjeh, en verkreeg den 5den October de ratificatie door de sultane*).

## The treaty

The [[Treaty of Atjeh-VOC (5 October 1659)|peace of 5 October 1659]] had three principal elements:

**Compensation.** "The Compagnie would receive compensation for the damage suffered in the arrest of her people and goods" — i.e., compensation for the [[Arrest of Anthony van Voorst|Van Voorst affair]].

**Westkust monopoly.** "On the West Coast trade was to be carried on only by Atjehers and the Compagnie" — i.e., re-confirmation of the [[Treaty of Atjeh-VOC (November 1649)|Truytman formula]].

**Lodge at Atjeh.** "The latter would re-establish a lodge (*loge*) at Atjeh" — the Compagnie's resident, withdrawn after the 1623 closure, was to be reinstated.

A separate regulation covered [[Perak]]: half of the tin to Atjeh, half to the Compagnie, at 30 reals the bar (rising to 32½ once Dutch arrears were satisfied).

## Significance

De Keyser's role, with Bort, is to bring the 1656–1659 crisis to a formal close. The [[Treaty of Atjeh-VOC (5 October 1659)|1659 peace]] is the last great co-operative settlement between the [[Sultana of Atjeh (Taj ul-Alam)|sultane]] and the Compagnie: within four years the Compagnie was in secret negotiation with the Menangkabau chiefs through [[Jan van Groenewegen|Jan van Groenewegen]], negotiations that produced the [[Painansch Contract]] of [[6 July]] [[1663]] and effectively dismantled the Atjehsche sovereignty that the 1659 treaty had just confirmed.

De Keyser and Bort are therefore the diplomats who closed one phase — the 1656–1659 coercion crisis — at the very moment the next phase — the 1662–1663 defection of the Menangkabau — was opening. Their treaty is the hinge between the two.
