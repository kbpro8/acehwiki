**Ma'Anim (Teungkoe Ma'Anim / Moehamat Anim's son, third Teungkoe Tirò)** was a Son of Tjhèh Saman; war leader (panglima) more than scholar mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. More a warlord (panglima) than a scholar; lacked his father's piety and sobriety.

## Role

Son of [[Teungkoe Sjech Saman di Tiri|Tjhèh Saman]]; war leader ([[Panglima|panglima]]) more than scholar

## Account from Jongejans

More a warlord ([[Panglima|panglima]]) than a scholar; lacked his father's piety and sobriety. But the fire of hatred animated him. Led the assault on [[Aneuë Galong]] ([[1896]]); killed in the assault, which was repulsed in a quarter hour with the Acehnese leaving >100 dead. Died [[1896]].

## Significance

Ma'Anim (Teungkoe Ma'Anim / Moehamat Anim's son, third Teungkoe Tirò) appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
