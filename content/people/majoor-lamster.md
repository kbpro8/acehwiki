**Majoor Lamster** was a Major; knight MWO 4th class (1898); author mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Already in 1898 earned knighthood of 4th class of MWO as 1st lieutenant of marechaussée in Aceh.

## Role

Major; knight MWO 4th class ([[1898]]); author

## Account from Jongejans

Already in [[1898]] earned knighthood of 4th class of MWO as 1st lieutenant of [[Marechaussée|marechaussée]] in Aceh. Wrote 'Atjeh vóór en na de [[Pacificatie|pacificatie]]' in [[Haagsch Maandblad]] of May [[1928]], defending the [[Binnenlandsch Bestuur (B.B.)|civil administration]] against over-pessimism about the [[1926]] [[Bakóngan uprising (1925–1927)|Bakongan uprising]]: 'some unrest on the [[Westkust]]... limited to a few small areas entirely isolated by mountain outliers... with a past like this, we may well be very satisfied that now a more confidential relationship between population and administration begins to break through...'. Called 'a knight' (ridder) by author.

## Significance

Majoor Lamster appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
