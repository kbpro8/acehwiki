**Tgk. Imeum Sradeuë'** was the well-known band-head of [[Wòjla]] whose band was surprised in April [[1910]] and who fell with four followers in [[1908]] in the [[Tapa' Toean]] district.

In [[1908]] "moreover the important Tgk. Imeum Sradeuë' fell with four followers" in the Tapa' Toean campaign of [[Th. J. Veltman|Veltman]]. In April [[1910]] "in Wòjla the band of the well-known Tgk. Imeum Sradeuë' was surprised, and that cost him his son and two rifles."
