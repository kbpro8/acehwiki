**Boreel** — *Toean Boreel* (in the Acehnese idiom of the old hunter [[Datoek]]) — was a patrol commander of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen]] in [[Atjeh|Aceh]], one of the great patrol commanders of the *prang* (the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]]). In *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* by Merac ([[1947]]), Boreel is one of the *sobat kras* (heavy friends) whom [[Datoek]] names in the chapter *Jagerslatijn*.

## Datoek's sobat kras

"I was a 'heavy friend' with Toean Kristoppel, and Toean General Van Heutsz, and Toean Scheepens, and Toean Darlang, and Toean Boreel, and Toean Governor Swart. Very many, and all very brave." Datoek's list of his *sobat kras* (heavy friends) includes *Toean Boreel* — Boreel. The *Toean Boreel* — the Acehnese idiom for Boreel — is the book's — and Datoek's — memory of the patrol commander who was a heavy friend of the old hunter.

## Significance

Boreel is significant in *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* as one of Datoek's *sobat kras* — the patrol commander who was a heavy friend of the old hunter. The book's argument — that Datoek was a heavy friend of the great patrol commanders of the *prang* — is the book's marker of the Acehnese hunter's *sobat kras* with the colonial army. Boreel is therefore one of the book's patrol commanders of the *prang*: the *Toean Boreel* who was a heavy friend of Datoek.
