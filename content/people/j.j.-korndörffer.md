**J.J. Korndörffer** was a captain in the [[Royal Netherlands Navy]] who submitted a formal report to the Minister of Marine concerning a Dutch voyage from the Netherlands via [[Batavia]] to [[Aceh]].

## Voyage to Atjeh

In his *Rapport aan Z.E. den Minister van Marine over de reis van Nederland via Batavia naer Atjeh*, Korndörffer details the logistical preparation, the route taken through the Sunda Strait, and the disembarkation of troops at the port of [[Aceh]] in the late 19th century. The document emphasizes the importance of naval superiority for the success of subsequent marine and army operations in the region.

## Role in the Aceh Campaigns

Korndörffer’s report is frequently cited alongside the later *Marine operations on Sumatra 1874–1876* as evidence of coordinated naval‑ground cooperation during the Aceh War. He notes that “the presence of a well‑armed fleet off the coast of Aceh was decisive in securing supply lines for the mariniers and the Indische Leger.”
