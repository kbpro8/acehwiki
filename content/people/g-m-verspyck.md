**G. M. Verspyck** was a major-general of the Indies army who served as second-in-command of the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)|second Aceh expedition]] under [[J. van Swieten|Lieutenant-general Van Swieten]], distinguished himself in the storming of Tiban on [[14 December]] [[1873]], led the column that executed the omtrekking of the [[Kraton of Aceh|Kraton]] on [[23 January]] [[1874]], and was designated by [[J. Loudon|Governor-General Loudon]] to succeed [[J. H. R. Köhler|General Köhler]] as supreme commander of the Indies army before the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)|second expedition]] was decided upon. He had already been appointed as Köhler's successor on [[20 April]] [[1873]], the day the news of the failure of the first expedition reached Batavia.

## Background and Köhler's Successor

When on [[20 April]] [[1873]] Governor-General Loudon convened an extraordinary meeting of the [[Council of Netherlands India|Raad van Nederlandsch-Indië]] with the commanders of land and sea forces and Major-general Verspyck, who had been designated as Köhler's successor, Verspyck declared himself ready to depart for Atjeh at once. The vice-admiral's opinion, however, prevailed: the roadstead of Atjeh would offer no safe anchorage, and communication between roadstead and beach would be seriously threatened; in the first two months it would be impossible to maintain that communication, and then the rain-monsoon would set in with force. The Governor-General accordingly authorised the expeditionary force to return immediately, with the firm intention of resuming the campaign in the favourable season. The fleet left the roadstead of Atjeh on [[29 April]] [[1873]].

## Second-in-Command of the Second Expedition

When the second expedition was decided upon, the retired Lieutenant-general [[J. van Swieten|Van Swieten]] was restored to active service and placed in supreme command, with Verspyck as second-in-command: "As military supreme commander and civil government commissioner he would unite in himself the political and military authority. As second commander of the expedition was appointed the major-general G. M. Verspyck, at the same time designated to replace the supreme commander in case of absence, illness or non-appearance."

## The Storming of Tiban, 14 December 1873

After the landing east of the Atjeh river on [[9 December]] [[1873]], [[Kota Moesapi|Kota Moesapi]] was occupied on [[13 December]]. The next day the long line of enemy fortifications south of Kota Moesapi was taken by "the bold storming of Tiban by General Verspyck." On [[15 December]] the mouth of the Atjeh river was reached.

## The Omtrekking of the Kraton, 23 January 1874

The omtrekking of the Kraton was entrusted to a column under Lieutenant-colonel [[K. van der Heyden|Van der Heijden]], while Verspyck was to keep the enemy occupied. The Krueng Daroe, which also ran through the Kraton, was crossed; various kampongs from which shots were fired were taken and punished. From kampong Moendjing Van der Heijden moved north-east and reached the burial-mound east of the Kraton, by which the omtrekking was in fact accomplished. The guide, however, had lost his way; the column returned, and operations were suspended for the day. The next day the enterprise was resumed; Van der Heijden moved through kampong Longbatta, when he received the news that the Kraton had already been taken.

## The Fall of the Kraton, 24 January 1874

From Kota Goenoengan, "between the brushwood, one saw a broken piece of wall with a watch-house behind it, largely stripped of its roof covering. From there Kota Goenoengan had been continually and sometimes rather heavily fired upon during the previous days. But about 9 in the morning this fire slackened and soon ceased entirely." Van Swieten suspected that the Kraton was not or only weakly occupied; he ordered a reconnaissance to enter the Kraton and to hoist the Dutch flag on the watch-house. When the reconnaissance had advanced for some time without seeing the enemy, the supreme commander himself came up with his staff and the other companies. "Soon the heart of the Kraton was reached, and a repeated hoerah of our men and the *Wilhelmus*, played by the band of the right half of the 14th bataljon, proclaimed that the Kraton was in our possession." Verspyck, hearing the hoerah and Wilhelmus, turned towards the Kraton and found [[Pakan Atjeh|Pakan Atjeh]] and the entrenchment of [[Panglima Polim|Panglima Polim]] wholly abandoned by the enemy; Van der Heijden was ordered to return.
