**J. Doorenbos** is named in the OCR text as the co‑editor who worked with **Hamzah Fansoeri** on the Malay translation of the *Burda*. Footnotes credit him with providing the basis for textual improvements, for example: “De tekestverbetering is ingegeven door Hamzah Fansoeri ed. J. Doorenbos bl. 46: lemah manis terlalu njaman.” His involvement reflects the collaboration between Malay and Dutch scholars in the study of Malay manuscripts during the colonial era, particularly in the production of critical editions of religious texts.
