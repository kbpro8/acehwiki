title: Al-Ghazali
aliases: [Abu Hamid al-Ghazali]
tags: [tokoh, ulama]
---

The great Muslim scholar, died A.D. 1111, considered one of the most important figures in Islamic theology for reconciling mysticism with orthodox Sunni Islam. His Minhaj al-'Abidin was translated into Achehnese by Cheh Marahaban, and his larger mystical works are studied throughout Acehnese religious literature.
