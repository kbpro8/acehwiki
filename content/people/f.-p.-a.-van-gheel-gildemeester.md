**F. P. A. van Gheel Gildemeester** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[J. J. Boreel]]

Swart placed Boreel in command of the principal bivouac in the plain of [[Pidië]], [[Lam Meulò]]. In [[1910]] Boreel at Lam Meulò and [[H. J. Schmidt|Schmidt]] at [[Tangsé]] became "de gangmakers" of the campaign in Pidië, Schmidt confining himself by commission to the destruction of the remaining [[Tirò Teungkoes]]. In August [[1910]] Boreel settled with [[Tgk. Aron]], though not completely: six followers were rendered harmless and two of the eight rifles taken at [[Keudé Garot]] returned to the *[[Kompeuni]]*. He was subsequently transferred to the [[Gajòland]], being succeeded at Lam Meulò by captain [[F. P. A. van Gheel Gildemeester]].

### From [[Tgk. Moehamat Haroen]]

**Tgk. Moehamat Haroen** was den beruchten en gevreesden hoofdaanvoerder der benden in [[Lam Meulò]], rendered harmless in [[1911]] by captain [[F. P. A. van Gheel Gildemeester]] of the 4th Division.

### From [[Pidië]]

Among those killed were the *[[kramat]]*-venerated nestor of the religious resistance leaders, [[Habib Teupin Wan]] — so holy that the fingers of those who dared point at him would stiffen — three scholars of great name, and two well-known band-heads; and the notorious [[Tgk. Moehamat Haroen]] of Lam Meulò, laid low by captain [[F. P. A. van Gheel Gildemeester]] of the 4th Division.
