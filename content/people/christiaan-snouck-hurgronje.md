**Christiaan Snouck Hurgronje** was a renowned Dutch Orientalist scholar, Islamic scholar, and official advisor on Native and Arab Affairs to the Netherlands East Indies government.

## Military Career and Service in Aceh

Snouck Hurgronje lived in Aceh and wrote extensive academic studies on Acehnese society, law, and religion. His strategic recommendation to bypass traditional secular chieftains (*uleebelang*) and ruthlessly target religious leaders (*ulama*) provided the intellectual blueprint for [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]'s offensive campaigns and the operations of the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]].
