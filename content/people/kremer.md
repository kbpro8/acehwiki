**Kremer** (pre-1930s) was a Scholar of Islam in Atjeh mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Together with Snouck Hurgronje, described the Atjeh Koran education system, on which the book's account is largely based.

## Role

Scholar of [[Islam in Aceh|Islam in Atjeh]]

## Dates

pre-1930s

## Account from Jongejans

Together with [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]], described the Atjeh Koran education system, on which the book's account is largely based.

## Significance

Kremer appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
