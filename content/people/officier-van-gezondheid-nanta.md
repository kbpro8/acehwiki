**Officier van gezondheid Nanta** was a Dutch officer of the [[Oost-Indisch Leger|Netherlands East Indies Army]] serving in the outposts of [[Groot-Atjeh]] during the alliance period of [[1893]]–[[1896]], recorded by [[G. B. Hooijer]] in *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]*.

## Service in the *buitenposten* (outer posts)

He was one of the two *officieren van gezondheid* (medical officers) identified by [[G. B. Hooijer]] on the plate of [[Biloel]], the other being [[Officier van gezondheid Tromp|Tromp]]. Hooijer noted elsewhere that at the smaller post of [[Toengkoeb]] no medical officer was present at all, and that when one was needed he had to be fetched from the nearest post in the [[Geconcentreerde linie|geconcentreerde linie]], at [[Lamjong]].
