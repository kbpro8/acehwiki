**T. Bén Peureumeuë** is a subject named in *[[Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[A. Geertsema Beckeringh]]

[[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] recorded in his letter to minister [[Alexander Willem Frederik Idenburg|Idenburg]] of July [[1908]] that Geertsema Beckeringh in the [[Meulaboh|Meulabohsche]] travelled without military escort as regards [[Wòjla]] and did not report it because it was forbidden — but that "after the removal of [[T. Bén Peureumeuë]], Geertsema Beckeringh no longer dared go up to upper Meulaboh alone either."
