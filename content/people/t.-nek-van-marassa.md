**T. Nek van Marassa** — *Teuku Nek of Marassa* — was an Atjehese chieftain whose territory lay north of the [[Kraton of Aceh|kraton]] of [[Kota Radja|Koeta Radja]], on the left bank of the [[Atjeh River|Atjeh-rivier]], and who, having submitted to [[Jan van Swieten|General van Swieten]] in [[January 1874|January]] [[1874]] — immediately after the [[Conquest of the Kraton (24 January 1874)|fall of the kraton]] — became, under [[J. L. J. H. Pel|Colonel Pel]] from [[April 1874|April]] [[1874]] onwards, the principal Dutch ally within [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] and the immediate cause of the first expansion of the Dutch *stelling* (defensive position) northward from the kraton.

## Submission and Territory (January 1874)

T. Nek van Marassa submitted to General Van Swieten in [[January 1874|January]] [[1874]], in the immediate aftermath of the [[Conquest of the Kraton (24 January 1874)|fall of the kraton]] of [[Kota Radja|Koeta Radja]] to the Dutch on [[24 January]] [[24 January]] [[1874]]. His territory lay north of the kraton, on the left bank of the [[Atjeh River|Atjeh-rivier]] — a position that, from the Dutch point of view, commanded the northern approaches to the kraton and the riverine communication with the sea.

## The Defense of T. Nek (April 1874 onwards)

Immediately after the departure of General Van Swieten for Java on [[26 April]] [[26 April]] [[1874]], Colonel Pel found that the *stelling* (defensive position) bequeathed by the second expedition was wholly inadequate to afford the garrison, under active defence, security and undisturbed communication with the sea. To this was added the obligation to protect T. Nek van Marassa, whose territory, lying north of the kraton on the left bank of the [[Atjeh River|Atjeh-rivier]], was attacked from [[27 April]] [[27 April]] [[1874]] onwards. The enemy grew ever bolder and displayed a restless activity and watchfulness; the coastal statelets took vigorous part in these attacks.

The defense of T. Nek's territory — together with the need to secure the right bank of the [[Atjeh River|Atjeh-rivier]] and to establish better communication with the roadstead through [[Oleh-leh|Oleh-leh]] — was the immediate cause of the first expansion of the Dutch *stelling* beyond the kraton. A new line of fortifications was constructed from [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] outward, by which T. Nek's friends would be protected and by which better communication with the roadstead would be obtained through [[Oleh-leh|Oleh-leh]]. A head was soon built there, and Oleh-leh became, as it remains, the port of [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]].

## Significance

T. Nek van Marassa is the first Atjehese chieftain to submit to the Dutch in [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] proper after the fall of the kraton, and his submission establishes the precedent for the Dutch policy of protecting submitted chiefs — a policy that would oblige every subsequent Dutch governor to expand the *stelling* in order to defend the submitted population from the hostile majority. The defense of T. Nek's territory was the immediate cause of the construction of the [[Noorderlinie|Noorderlinie]] (Northern Line) — the series of posts north of the kraton that would become one of the three principal axes of the Dutch *postenlinie* around [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]].
