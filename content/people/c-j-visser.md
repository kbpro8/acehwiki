**C. J. Visser** was a Dutch marine lieutenant of the [[Royal Netherlands Marine Corps|Korps Mariniers]] who served as adjudant to [[Captain-terr zee Binkes]] during the [[1873]] Aceh campaign and was honorably mentioned in the royal decree of [[6 October]] [[1874]] for his service in the Aceh expeditions of [[1873]] and [[1874]].

## Service in the 1873 Campaign

Visser participated in the initial amphibious landing on [[5 April]] [[1873]] and subsequently acted as liaison between the marine landing division and the overall army command under [[General-Major Köhler]]. His after-action reports from the field, dated [[10 April]] and [[12 April]] [[1873]], provide detailed accounts of the heavy resistance encountered at the *Mesigit* (the Great Mosque of Banda Aceh) and of the subsequent casualties among the marine ranks, including the death of [[Sergeant Rood]].

## Contributions

- Authored after-action reports that clarified discrepancies in troop numbers between the marine and army records.
- Assisted in the coordination of naval gunfire support during the assault on the *Kraton* (the Acehnese royal palace).

## Recognition

Although not awarded a knighthood, Visser's name appeared among the officers distinguished for their actions in Aceh in the [[Royal Decree of 6 October 1874]], the comprehensive honour list that recognised service across the [[Aceh Expedition 1873-1876|Aceh expeditions of 1873–1874]].
