**C. J. Visser** was a lieutenant of the [[Dutch Marine Corps|Korps Mariniers]] who served as adjudant to [[Captain-terr zee Binkes]] during the 1873 Aceh campaign. He participated in the initial amphibious landing on [[5 April]] [[1873]] and later acted as liaison between the marine landing division and the overall army command under [[General-Major Köhler]].

Visser’s reports from the field, dated [[10 April]] and [[12 April]] [[1873]], provide detailed accounts of the heavy resistance encountered at the **Mesigit** and the subsequent casualties among the marine ranks, including the death of [[Sergeant Rood]].

## Contributions
- Authored after‑action reports that clarified discrepancies in troop numbers.
- Assisted in the coordination of naval gunfire support during the assault on the Kraton.
