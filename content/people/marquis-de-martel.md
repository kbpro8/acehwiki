**Marquis de Martel** was a French naval officer whose sharp criticism of **[[Michiel de Ruyter]]** appears in the source (see [[275]]).  He accused De Ruyter of “deshonouring the nation” after the Dutch fleet’s actions at **[[Schooneveld]]**.  The dispute reflects the broader rivalry between the **[[French fleet]]** and the **[[Dutch Navy]]** during the **[[Dutch–French War|Franco‑Dutch War]]**.

## Relevance to Aceh
The French attempts to challenge Dutch sea power threatened the safety of the **[[Indische retourvloot]]**, which carried valuable cargoes from **[[Aceh]]**.  French naval pressure therefore had indirect economic implications for the **[[Dutch East India Company]]**’s Aceh trade.
