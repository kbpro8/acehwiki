**Leleng** was a *maréchaussée*-soldier of the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* who, having lost the use of his left hand through a wound, continued to take part in the firing during the [[Gevecht waarin kapitein Paris sneuvelde (Westkust van Atjeh, 1925-1927)|engagement in which Captain J. Paris fell]] on the [[Westkust van Atjeh|west coast of Atjeh]] in the action of [[1925]]–[[1927]].

## The incident

The [[1930]] jubilee number records: "De Maréchaussée-soldaat Leleng, die door een verwonding het gebruik van zijn linkerhand miste, nam aan het vuur op de alang-alang deel door zijn karabijn tusschen de knieën te plaatsen en die met de rechterhand af te schieten!" — the Maréchaussée-soldier Leleng, who through a wound lacked the use of his left hand, took part in the fire on the *[[Alang-alang|alang-alang]]* by placing his *[[Karabijn (M. 95)|karabijn]]* between his knees and discharging it with his right hand.

The firing into the cogon grass had been ordered by the wounded [[Kromo-di-Koro]] before he handed over command to [[Suwoek]], in order to drive out any Atjehnese who might be lying concealed there.

The episode was cited by the jubilee number as evidence that "de oude geest van het Korps Maréchaussée was bij zijn manschappen blijven voortleven" — the old spirit of the corps had continued to live on among its men.
