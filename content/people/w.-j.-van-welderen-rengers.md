**W. J. van Welderen Rengers** — Willem Jan, baron Van Welderen Rengers ([[1834]]-[[1916]]) — was a Dutch liberal politician and the original author of the *Schets eener parlementaire geschiedenis van Nederland van 1849 tot 1901* of which the [[1955]] fourth edition is the source of the present Aceh Wiki History articles. His interventions in the colonial parliamentary debates of the [[1891]]-[[1918]] period are cited throughout the [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] section of the fourth edition.

## The "eerste pas" maxim

In the [[1897]] [[Tweede Kamer]] debate on credits for [[Nieuw Guinea]], [[W. J. van Welderen Rengers|Van Welderen Rengers]] (lib.) formulated what became a frequently-quoted maxim of colonial parliamentary debate:

> "In koloniale zaken [geldt] het allerminst het spreekwoord: *c'est le premier pas qui coûte*. De eerste stap wordt gemakkelijk gedaan, het zijn juist de volgende stappen waartoe men ter wille van het prestige gedwongen wordt, welke ons geld en helaas ook mensenlevens kosten."

— In colonial affairs it is least of all true that the proverb *c'est le premier pas qui coûte* applies. The first step is taken easily; it is precisely the subsequent steps, to which one is driven for the sake of prestige, that cost us money and, alas, also human lives.

[[W. J. van Welderen Rengers|Van Welderen Rengers]] pointed, as the type-specimen, to the [[Sumatra-tractaat ([[1871]])|sluiting van het Sumatra-tractaat]] of [[1871]], when some chambermembers had feared "moeilijkheden met Atjeh" but had been reassured by minister [[P. P. van Bosse|Van Bosse]]. In [[1873]] the [[Netherlands]] had nevertheless declared war on the [[Sultan of Acheh|sultan of Atjeh]].

## Authorship of the *Schets*

[[W. J. van Welderen Rengers|Van Welderen Rengers]] is the original author of the *Schets eener parlementaire geschiedenis van Nederland van 1849 tot 1891* that, in the [[1955]] fourth revised edition, was extended to cover the period up to [[1918]]. The fourth edition comprises four volumes: the first three cover the Netherlands itself; the fourth volume, edited by [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] (Oost- en West-Indië) and [[C. W. de Vries|de Vries]] (Nederland 1914-1918), carries the colonial story forward to [[1918]].

## Significance

[[W. J. van Welderen Rengers|Van Welderen Rengers]]'s authorship of the *Schets* makes him — alongside [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] — the principal narrator of the parliamentary history of the [[Atjeh-oorlog ([[1873]]-[[1904]])|Atjeh-oorlog]]. His "eerste pas" maxim is the type-specimen of the liberal parliamentary critique of the diplomatic prelude to the Atjehnese war.
