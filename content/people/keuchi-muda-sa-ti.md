---

title: "Keuchi' Muda Sa'ti"
aliases: [Panglima of the XXII Mukims]
tags: [person, literary-work]
---

A renowned brave chief who ridiculed the king for his inability to control Pochut Muhamat and was given full power to prevent civil war, but found he could do nothing against the prince and fled back to his territory in shame.

- [[Pochut Muhamat]]
- [[Poteu Jeumaloy]]

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
