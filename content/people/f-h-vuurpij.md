**F. H. Vuurpij** was a **[[sergeant der mariniers|sergeant of the marines]]** who received the **[[Eervolle Vermelding]]** by **[[Royal Decree 18 February 1861 No. 82]]** for his conduct during the **[[Second Boni Expedition 1860]]**.
