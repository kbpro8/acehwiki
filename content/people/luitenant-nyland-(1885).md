**Luitenant Nyland** was a Dutch lieutenant of the garrison of the [[Geconcentreerde Stelling|concentrated line]] at [[Acheh|Atjeh]] in [[1885]], twice named in the incident-list kept by majoor [[M. T. H. Perelaer|Perelaer]] of what befell the Dutch *inside* their line in that year. During the ground-clearing (*terrein opruimen*) in the [[VI Moekims]] the workers were fired upon and four men were wounded, among them luitenant Nyland; on [[13 May]] [[1885]] a patrol of 21 men under luitenant Nyland, marching between [[Lamara]] and [[Kuta Raja|Kota Radja]], fell upon the enemy and lost two killed and seven wounded, of whom three of the wounded died afterwards (see [[Guerilla raids inside the concentration line (1885)]]).
