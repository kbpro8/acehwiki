**Guillaume** was one of the two French travellers murdered in the [[Tenom|Tenom river]] on [[9 March]] [[1880]] by the people of the Tenom pirate prince, together with his companion [[Vallon (Tenom, 1880)|Vallon]]. His and Vallon's deaths are recorded by [[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] as an early symptom of the Tenom coast's defiance which four years later fed the [[Nisero affair (1883–1885)|Nisero affair]] (see [[Murder of Vallon and Guillaume (9 March 1880)]]).
