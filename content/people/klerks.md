**Klerks** (active 1938) was a Official; contributed to chapters V and VII mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. One of the officials who helped Jongejans gather newest data; contributed to chapters V (economics) and VII (administration and clergy).

## Role

Official; contributed to chapters V and VII

## Dates

active [[1938]]

## Account from Jongejans

One of the officials who helped [[Resident Jongejans|Jongejans]] gather newest data; contributed to chapters V (economics) and VII (administration and clergy).

## Significance

Klerks appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
