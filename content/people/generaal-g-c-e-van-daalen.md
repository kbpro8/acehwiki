**Generaal G. C. E. van Daalen** was a General; Governor of Aceh; India's great strategist (photo caption page 396) mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Photo caption: 'Generaal G.

## Role

General; Governor of Aceh; [[British India|India]]'s great strategist (photo caption page 396)

## Account from Jongejans

Photo caption: 'Generaal G. C. E. van Daalen, Indië's groote strateeg.' Led the 163-day [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]]-tocht - a great success. After [[Generaal J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] became GG, [[Gouverneur Van Daalen|Van Daalen]] succeeded (after [[Jhr. Van der Wyck]]'s brief tenure) as Governor. Under him it was 'long not pais en vrée'; [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] revived, small army could do little, much criticism. Had ex-[[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]] arrested and transported to [[Batavia]] (later [[Ambon]]). Mentioned by many as [[British India|India]]'s greatest strategist; honourably dismissed from his function; succeeded by Colonel [[H. N. A. Swart]]. Listed among [[Marechaussée|marechaussée]] men.

## Significance

Generaal G. C. E. van Daalen appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
