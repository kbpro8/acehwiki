**Thomas Nussy** was a Moluccan (Ambon) private and NCO in the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]].

## Military Career and Service in Aceh

Nussy was one of the most famous non-European soldiers in the KNIL. In [[1911]], he was awarded the Knight 3rd Class Military William Order (*M.W.O.*), becoming one of only eleven members in the entire forty-year history of the Marechaussee corps to receive this prestigious award.
