**Bayazid** was a Sufi mystic and the founder of the [[Rawshaniyya]] sect. He is mentioned in the source as the subject of a biography by [[Akhond Derwizeh]].

## Influence

The Rawshaniyya school contributed to the intellectual milieu of the Islamic world, whose texts—including the creed poem of [[Siraj al-Din Abu Muhammad Ali ibn Uthman al-Ushi]]—were later transmitted to Aceh.
