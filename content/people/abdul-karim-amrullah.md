**Abdul Karim Amrullah**, better known as **Hadji Rasul**, was a renowned Minangkabau Islamic teacher whose life was chronicled by his son [[Hamka]]. He was celebrated for his role in promoting Arabic devotional poetry, including the *Burda* and other kasidas, within Minangkabau religious education.

## Educational Influence
Hadji Rasul’s teaching emphasized memorisation and recitation of Arabic poetry, a practice that persisted into the mid‑20th century as described in Hamka’s *Ajahku*.
