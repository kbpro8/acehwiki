---

title: Molla Ibrahim
tags: [person, scholar]
---

The successor of Ahmad Qushashi at Medina. After Qushashi's death, Molla Ibrahim granted Abdurra'uf of Singkel permission to found his own school of mysticism, and Abdurra'uf later cited a treatise of Molla Ibrahim in his own writing.

## Source
