**Teungkoe Poetrèë (Teungkoe Poetrdë)** was a Wife of the Pretendent-Sultan mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Captured by Christoffel in November 1902 at Gloempang Pajong while travelling to Pidië where the Sultan wanted to marry off his son.

## Role

Wife of the [[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]]

## Account from Jongejans

Captured by [[Christoffel]] in [[November]] [[1902]] at [[Gloempang Pajong]] while travelling to [[Pidië]] where [[Sultan of Aceh|the Sultan]] wanted to marry off his son.

## Significance

Teungkoe Poetrèë (Teungkoe Poetrdë) appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
