**Tgk. Moehamat Haroen** was den beruchten en gevreesden hoofdaanvoerder der benden in [[Lam Meulò]], rendered harmless in [[1911]] by captain [[F. P. A. van Gheel Gildemeester]] of the 4th Division.

He was killed with his principal subordinate commanders and six followers, [[J. J. Boreel|Boreel]] having meanwhile been transferred to the [[Gajòland]].
