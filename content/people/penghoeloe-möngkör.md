**Penghoeloe Möngkör** was the still-hostile chief in the mountains of the [[Meergebied]] whom a dozen villagers of [[Linggö]] joined in [[1916]], and whom the population itself laid low in September of that year.

The band, led into the mountains by a female *[[teungkoe]]* whom the population regarded as very holy, attacked a marching bivouac, which held and inflicted three dead and one severely wounded at the cost of three wounded soldiers. Then it was soon "*mèl sadja*" again: two more encounters, two more dead, eight submissions — "and then in September the population laid Penghoeloe Möngkör low."
