**C. Japing** was a [[Netherlands|Dutch]] forestry researcher of the [[Dienst van het Boschwezen]] in [[Nederlandsch-Indië]] who, with [[C. Brandts Buys]] and [[D. Fernandes]], was the co-author of the [[Bijdrage tot de kennis van Pinus Merkusii Jungh et de Vr.|monograph on Pinus Merkusii Jungh et de Vr., more particularly in the Gajo-Landen]] — the principal early scientific study of the Sumatran pine and of its exploitable stands in the [[Gajo-landen|Gajo-Landen]] region. He is named in the [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet of 1930]] as one of the co-authors of the monograph that is the principal source of the description of the *quarre* tapping system applied at the [[Baleq]] enterprise.

## The Pinus Merkusii monograph

The [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet]] cites Japing's monograph — *Bijdrage tot de kennis van Pinus Merkusii Jungh et de Vr., meer in het bijzonder in de Gajo-landen*, by [[C. Brandts Buys]], C. Japing and [[D. Fernandes]] — for an *uitvoerige beschrijving* (extensive description) of the *[[quarre]]* tapping system and of the results obtained with it. The monograph was published as *Mededeeling no. 19 van het Proefstation voor het Boschwezen* in [[1928]], and it is the principal published source on the practical application of French turpentine-tapping methods in Atjehnese conditions.

## Significance

Japing's significance in the historiography of colonial Aceh is as a co-author — with [[C. Brandts Buys|Brandts Buys]] and [[D. Fernandes|Fernandes]] — of the principal early scientific study of the [[Pinus Merkusii|Sumatran pine]] in the [[Gajo-landen|Gajo-Landen]]. The monograph is cited in the [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet of 1930]] as the authority for the *quarre* tapping system, and the work of the three co-authors is the principal published source on the practical application of French turpentine-tapping methods in Atjehnese conditions.
