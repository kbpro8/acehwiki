**Teuku Kadli** was the hereditary supreme religious judge (*Qadi*) of the [[Sultanate of Aceh]] and one of the four principal state ministers (*Mantri Hari-Hari*).

## Role in the Aceh War

Teuku Kadli exercised administrative authority over the eastern bank of the Aceh River and was the father-in-law of [[Sultan Alauddin Mahmud Syah IX]]. Initially disposed toward peace, his grand residence at [[Peunayong]] was accidentally burned down during the [[Capture of Peunayong]] on [[18 December]] [[1873]], destroying the historic state archives of the Sultanate of Aceh.

Disillusioned by the destruction of his ancestral home and documents, Teuku Kadli joined the active resistance party under [[Tuanku Hashim]]. However, following subsequent campaigns at [[Ketapan Doewa]], he formally submitted to Dutch authorities in [[February]] [[1874]].
