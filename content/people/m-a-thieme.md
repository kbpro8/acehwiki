**M. A. Thieme** was a Dutch captain of infantry in [[Atjeh]] in [[1876]], commanding the company of *barisan* (native auxiliary troops) of [[Sumenep|Soemenēp]] that took part in the [[Storming of Lambaroe (7 March 1876)|operation against Lambaroe]] of 6–8 March [[1876]] and in the guarding, on [[7 March]], of the withdrawal of the wounded from the field to the hospital at [[Kota Radja]].

## Lambaroe, March 1876

His company of Soemenēp barisan was joined to the column of the [[8e Bataljon|8th Infantry Battalion]] for the relief of the besieged south-east line: it marched with the main body to [[Longbatah Zuid]] on the evening of [[6 March]] and formed part of Major [[Majoor Diepenheim|Diepenheim]]'s attacking column at the storming of the enemy work at [[Lambaroe]] the next morning. When the fight was won, the wounded were, under the protection of Captain Thieme's company — which had already been busy that morning — transported as soon as possible to the hospital at Kota Radja, where they were admitted before noon. His name is preserved, in this charge, by [[Kolonel Meijer|H. F. Meijer]] in *[[Atjeh van 26 December 1875 tot 4 September 1876]]* ([[1883]]).
