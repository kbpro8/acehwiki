**J. C. Bongers** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Pacificatie van Atjeh]]

Du Croo dated the closing stone of the pacification eighteen years after Swart's retirement, in the speech Governor [[Van Aken]] delivered at the handover of the government to Resident [[J. Jongejans|Jongejans]] on Saturday [[7 March]] [[1936]]: "…I also express my particular thanks to the officers exercising administrative authority … and the finest compliment I can pay to you, Sir, as Provincial Military Commander (Colonel [[J. C. Bongers]]), and to your officers in this field, is that I now consider the sub-divisions [[Alaslanden]], [[Lam Meulò]], [[Meulaboh]] and [[Tapa' Toean]] entirely ripe for civil administration, and that only considerations of economy have restrained me from making proposals to that effect." Those four were the last sub-divisions still governed by officers. "The pacification was complete; Swart's great life's work was accomplished."
