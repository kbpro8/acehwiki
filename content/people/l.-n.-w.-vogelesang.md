**L. N. W. Vogelesang** was a captain of the Netherlands-Indies army who served as adjutant to Colonel [[L. A. Snell]], commander of the 2nd Regiment of Infantry at [[Bandung|Bandoeng]], at the time of the [[1930]] jubilee of the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]*.

## In the Snell interview

The journalist of *[[Het Nieuws van den Dag voor Nederlandsch-Indië]]* who conducted the interview *Een boom met "Old Hand"* with Colonel Snell recorded that he had himself announced through Snell's adjutant, Captain L. N. W. Vogelesang: "'k Had me door zijn adjudant, kapitein L. N. W. Vogelesang, laten aandienen."

At that date Snell was colonel and commander of the 2nd Regiment of Infantry at Bandoeng, having been promoted major on [[2 May]] [[1926]] and lieutenant-colonel on [[21 December]] [[1926]].
