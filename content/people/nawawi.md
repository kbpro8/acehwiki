title: Nawawi
aliases: [Imam Nawawi]
tags: [person, scholar]
---

Author of the Minhaj al-talibin, known in Acheh as "Menhot," one of the most authoritative Shafi'ite law books studied there. His Tahdhib al-Asma is also cited in connection with the biographies of Abu Shahmah and Tamim ad-Dari.
