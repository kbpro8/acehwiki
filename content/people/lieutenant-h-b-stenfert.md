**Stenfert, H. B.** was a Dutch marine lieutenant whose name was listed honorably in the royal decree of [[6 October]] [[1874]] for his participation in the Atjeh expeditions of [[1873]] and [[1874]].

## Recognition

His inclusion reflects the broad acknowledgment of junior officers in the Aceh campaigns.
