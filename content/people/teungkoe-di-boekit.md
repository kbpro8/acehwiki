**Teungkoe di Boekit** was a Son of the famous Teungkoe Tiro Tjhèh Saman; spiritual leader and resistance chief mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Recognised as spiritual leader and resistance chief; eliminated by colonne Schmidt (1909-1913).

## Role

Son of the famous [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] [[Tiro]] [[Teungkoe Sjech Saman di Tiri|Tjhèh Saman]]; spiritual leader and [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] chief

## Account from Jongejans

Recognised as spiritual leader and [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] chief; eliminated by [[Colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]] ([[1909]]-[[1913]]).

## Significance

Teungkoe di Boekit appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
