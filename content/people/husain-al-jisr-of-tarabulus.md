title: Husain al-Jisr of Tarabulus
aliases: [al-Jisr]
tags: [person, scholar, western-scholar]
---

A 19th-century Syrian author of the Risalah Hamidiyyah, presented by Hurgronje as a sign of Islam engaging with modernity — defending the humanity and reasonableness of Islamic law against European philosophical challenges including Darwinism, while still defending the obligation of the holy war.

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
