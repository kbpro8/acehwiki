**Van Vuuren** was a Military pursuing Teungkoe di Barat mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. 

## Role

Military pursuing [[Tgk. di Barat|Teungkoe di Barat]]

## Significance

Van Vuuren appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
