**T. P. J. Berg** was a Dutch 2e luitenant (second lieutenant) of the [[Koninklijk Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Indies army]] who distinguished himself in the action of [[22 July]] [[1880]] during the [[Batoe Iliq Expedition (July 1880)|expedition against Batoe Iliq]] in [[Samalanga]]: he took with the storming troops a fortification of the enemy whose rear stood open, so that the defenders fled in time; the enemy left there three dead, while on the side of the attackers one soldier was wounded. The citation is preserved in the account of the operations that [[W. A. Coblijn|Coblijn]] quoted and discussed in *[[Hoe Atjeh te onderwerpen (Coblijn, 1893)|Hoe Atjeh te onderwerpen]]*, where the conduct of the subaltern officers — Berg, [[J. E. J. den Berger|Den Berger]], and the reconnoitring [[A. G. Popelier|1e luitenant-adjudant Populier]] — is set against the failure of the direction of the attack.
