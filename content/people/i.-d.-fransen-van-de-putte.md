**I. D. Fransen van de Putte** was a Dutch statesman, member of parliament, and Minister of Colonies who presented the official [[Parliamentary Note on Relations with Aceh 1872-1873]] to the States-General and authorized the dispatch of the [[First Aceh Expedition]] in [[1873]].

## Policy and the Declaration of War

During the winter of [[1872]] and spring of [[1873]], Minister [[I. D. Fransen van de Putte]] oversaw the diplomatic confrontation with the [[Aceh Sultanate]] following the Anglo-Dutch Sumatra Treaty of [[1871]]. 

In his [[1875]] historical critique, General [[G. G. Booms]] cited Fransen van de Putte's parliamentary note to demonstrate that the executive government in The Hague and [[Batavia]] declared war precipitously without providing the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] with sufficient naval blockade forces or a [[General Staff of the KNIL]].
