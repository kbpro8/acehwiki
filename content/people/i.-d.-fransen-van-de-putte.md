**I. D. Fransen van de Putte** was the Dutch Minister of Colonies associated with the political instructions for the [[Second Dutch Expedition to Aceh]].

## Role in the campaign

[[Jan van Swieten]] represented the discussions among Fransen van de Putte, [[James Loudon]], and himself as endorsing restraint: no unlimited conquest and no immediate direct administration of [[Aceh]].

## Significance

The source places [[I. D. Fransen van de Putte]] within the contested military and political history of the [[Aceh War]].
