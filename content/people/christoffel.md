**Christoffel** — *Toean Kristoppel* (in the Acehnese idiom of the old hunter [[Datoek]]) — was a Marechaussee commander of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen]] in [[Atjeh|Aceh]], one of the great patrol commanders of the *prang* (the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]]). In *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* by Merac ([[1947]]), Christoffel is the fourth-named in [[Musket]]'s list of the Marechaussee commanders, and the first of the *sobat kras* (heavy friends) whom [[Datoek]] names — "I was a 'heavy friend' with Toean Kristoppel" — noting that Christoffel was, in Datoek's time, still a corporal.

## The list of Marechaussee commanders

Christoffel is the fourth-named in Musket's list: "Gilles Graafland, Van Daalen, Campioni, Christoffel, Van der Maaten, Scheepens, Watrin, Webb, Colijn, Geertsema Beckering, Darlang and the sergeants Nussy, Salowono, Pongoh … a colourful row."

## Datoek's sobat kras

"I was a 'heavy friend' with Toean Kristoppel, and Toean General Van Heutsz, and Toean Scheepens, and Toean Darlang, and Toean Boreel, and Toean Governor Swart. Very many, and all very brave." Datoek's list of his *sobat kras* — his heavy friends — among the great patrol commanders and governors of the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]] begins with *Toean Kristoppel* — Christoffel. The book notes that Christoffel was, in Datoek's time, still a corporal — that is, Christoffel rose from a corporal in Datoek's time to a Marechaussee commander in the colonial memory. The *Toean Kristoppel* — the Acehnese idiom for Christoffel — is the book's — and Datoek's — memory of the Marechaussee commander who had been, in Datoek's time, a corporal.

## Significance

Christoffel is significant in *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* as the Marechaussee commander who had been, in Datoek's time, a corporal. The book's argument — that Christoffel rose from a corporal to a Marechaussee commander — is the book's marker of the colonial army's promotion from the ranks in [[Atjeh|Aceh]]. Christoffel is therefore the book's — and Datoek's — memory of the Marechaussee commander who had been a corporal: the *Toean Kristoppel* who was a heavy friend of the old hunter.
