**Christoffel** — *Toean Kristoppel* (in the Acehnese idiom of the old hunter [[Datoek]]), *kapitein Christoffel* — was a Dutch maréchaussée officer and commander of the ***[[Colonne Matjan]]*** (Tiger-Colonne) of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen]] in [[Atjeh|Aceh]], selected in [[1908]] to form the special twelve-brigade division raised to break the revolt spreading from [[Lho Soekon]], [[Keureutoe]], and Pasé. In *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* by Merac ([[1947]]), Christoffel is the fourth-named in [[Musket]]'s list of the Marechaussee commanders, and the first of the *sobat kras* (heavy friends) whom [[Datoek]] names — "I was a 'heavy friend' with Toean Kristoppel" — noting that Christoffel was, in Datoek's time, still a corporal. He was chosen, [[H.C. Zentgraff|Zentgraff]] writes, as a man *hard en ongenadig als een rots* (hard and merciless as a rock), who would receive *eene vrijwel onbeperkte volmacht* (an almost unrestricted mandate) and would, *ten koste van wat dan ook* (at whatever cost), restore order with *uiterste gestrengheid* (utmost severity).

## Datoek's sobat kras

"I was a 'heavy friend' with Toean Kristoppel, and Toean General Van Heutsz, and Toean Scheepens, and Toean Darlang, and Toean Boreel, and Toean Governor Swart. Very many, and all very brave." Datoek's list of his *sobat kras* begins with *Toean Kristoppel* — Christoffel. The book notes that Christoffel was, in Datoek's time, still a corporal — he rose from a corporal in Datoek's time to Marechaussee commander and commandant of the Colonne Matjan.

## Account from *Atjeh* by H.C. Zentgraff (1938)

### Selection of the force at Tjimahi

Christoffel was given leave to compose his own colonne. *Christoffel ging naar Java, waar vooral in het groote garnizoen Tjimahi zeer veel oudere maréchaussée's dienden* — Christoffel went to [[Java]], where in the great garrison of [[Tjimahi]] many older maréchaussée's served, men of great experience in the Atjeh work who had rested in a healthy peace-garrison for a while but were now *zat* (sated) with it and longed to return to the *rimboe*. He picked out the best *brigadecommandanten* too — men who had had *den buik vol* (their belly full) of peace-garrison silliness: *zooveel malen daags appèl, en theorie* — of which they knew less than a *milicien-korporaal* because they understood the practice so well.

### The red neckerchief and the boedjangs

To the *bloedvingers op den kraag van hun jas* (blood-fingers on the collar of their coat) that all maréchaussée's wore, Christoffel gave his colonne *nog een apart distinctief: een rooden zakdoek welken zij zich om den hals knoopten* (a separate distinction: a red neckerchief which they knotted about their necks), *ten teeken dat zij een ziertje bloederiger zouden optreden dan de maréchaussée-braaf, de gewone* — as a sign that they would act a trifle bloodier than the ordinary maréchaussée. The colonne was twelve brigades. So far as possible the front section (*voorste sectie*) was made up of *boedjangs* — bachelors who had nothing behind them to fret about.

### Systematic harshness

The *vijand kende den naam Christoffel* (the enemy knew the name Christoffel); when the colonne was transported per tram to its bivak Panton Leubeuë, the rails were torn up before and behind it. *Het bracht zelfs geen uitstel van executie* (it caused not even a delay of execution). The colonne's task was *tot elken prijs de situatie meester te worden, en het verzet te breken*, and it did this *systematisch en met uiterste hardheid*: permanent execution-squads (*parate executie*) were kept ready, and the malcontents fell by the dozen. The *schoonmaak* began in the *vlakte* (plain) and was *summier en grondig* (summary and thorough); soon the war lost its popularity with much of the population.

### Succession

After the situation was restored, [[H.N.A. Swart|Swart]] replaced [[G.C.E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] as governor, and such sharp measures were no longer needed. *Christoffel ging heen en de colonne kwam onder Van der Vierk* — Christoffel departed and the colonne came under [[Van der Vlerk|Van der Vierk]]; it gradually assumed the character of an ordinary maréchaussée-divisie, but *ondanks de verdwijning der roode halsdoeken wekte zij nog lang bijzondere vrees* (despite the disappearance of the red neckerchiefs it long aroused special fear). The [[Colonne Matjan]] is the colonial-Atjeh symbol of *uiterste gestrengheid*.
