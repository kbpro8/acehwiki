**Toengkoe Sjech Marabahan** was a very aged Atjehsche *oelama* (scholar of the scriptures) who stood in special esteem with the Atjehnese. His accession was one of the most important tokens of the pacification achieved under generaal [[Karel van der Heijden|K. van der Heyden]]: on [[7 March]] [[1881]] — four days before the [[Normale bestuursinrichting (Atjeh, 1881-1884)|cabinet letter of 11 March 1881]] that replaced the general by a civil governor — he was received by Van der Heyden in the presence of a large number of chiefs and religious teachers at [[Kuta Raja|Kota Radja]]. In [[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]]'s review of the period (after [[B. C. P. Kielstra|Kielstra]]) his reception closed the series: the swearing of [[Toekoe Nja Abbas Setia Oelama]] ([[24 January]] [[1880]]), the coming over of [[Potjoet Abbas]] (July [[1880]]), and the homage paid to the general by many heads on [[4 October]] [[1880]] after the close of the fast.
