**Kapitein Perrin de Salleneuve** was a Dutch **captain** who confronted the rebel leader **[[Atta (rebel leader)|Atta]]** in the **Wikkie‑kreek** area. After the engagement, Atta retreated, allowing Dutch forces to regain control of the **Quacco** and **Baubé** outposts. Perrin’s actions are recorded in the Dutch colonial reports of the **[[1760s]]**.

## Role in the Campaign
Perrin’s encounter with Atta marked a turning point, after which the Dutch perceived the rebellion as weakened, leading to a temporary cessation of hostilities.
