**H. B. van Daalen** was the editor of the [[De Java Bode|Java-Bode]] who, in [[1873]], published and then reprinted [[De enquête over den mislukten tocht naar Atjeh]], one of the earliest sharp attacks on Dutch policy in the [[Aceh War]]. In the months after the failure of the [[First Aceh Expedition (1873)]], he used the colonial press to criticize the war, the public defense of [[James Loudon|Governor-General Loudon]], and the way the government investigated the defeat.

## Editor of the Java-Bode

The title page of [[De enquête over den mislukten tocht naar Atjeh]] identified Van Daalen as editor of the *[[De Java Bode|Java-Bode]]*. The pamphlet was made from two newspaper articles, which shows how the first large argument about the war moved directly from the press into pamphlet form.

## Critic of the first expedition and its inquiry

Van Daalen argued that the defeat in [[Aceh|Atjeh]] should not be hidden behind patriotic language. He attacked the anonymous pamphlet [[Bemoediging]], which he believed tried to protect [[James Loudon|Loudon]], and he criticized the secret questioning of officers and the later [[Commission of Inquiry into the First Aceh Expedition]].

He also used unusually strong language about the Acehnese side, praising the vigilance of the ruler and the courage of the people. This made his pamphlet stand out in the early Dutch literature on the war.

## Significance

**H. B. van Daalen** is important in Aceh-related print history because he helped turn the first Dutch defeat into a political scandal. His pamphlet preserved the voice of colonial press opposition in [[1873]], when the meaning of the defeat was still being fought over in public.
