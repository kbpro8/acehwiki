**Tjoet Nja Din (Tjoct Nja Din, Cut Nyak Dhien)** was a Wife of Teukoe Oemar, resistance leader mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Even more fanatical than her husband; constantly urged him on.

## Role

Wife of [[Teukoe Oemar]], [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] leader

## Account from Jongejans

Even more fanatical than her husband; constantly urged him on. Travelled with him through all hardships. After his death continued her wandering life with ever-shrinking following; old and blind, lived as a hunted animal. Eventually captured after betrayal by a chief who could no longer watch her suffering; the betraying chief first obtained from the commanding officer a guarantee that her life would be spared and that she would be treated as befitted a woman of her rank. When the patrol found her she was so weakened by deprivation that [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] was impossible; she nevertheless attempted to give the betrayer a fatal rentjong stab under the pretext of feeling his face to memorise his features.

## Significance

Tjoet Nja Din (Tjoct Nja Din, Cut Nyak Dhien) appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
