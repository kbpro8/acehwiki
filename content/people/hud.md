**Hūd (prophet)** is an early Qur’anic prophet mentioned in the Malay translation of the *Burda* as part of a didactic enumeration of past messengers. The poem recounts Hūd’s mission to the people of *‘Ad* and his eventual destruction for rejecting divine warning.

## Qur’anic Background

Hūd is described in the Qur’an (e.g., [[Qur’an 11:50‑60]]) as a messenger sent to the tribe of *‘Ad* in the Arabian Peninsula. His story serves as a moral exemplar of obedience to God.

## Acehnese Literary Usage

In the Acehnese Malay version of the *Burda*, Hūd’s narrative is invoked to contrast the steadfastness of the Prophet [[Muhammad]] with the fate of earlier peoples who denied divine guidance. This reflects the Acehnese tradition of using Qur’anic exempla to reinforce contemporary religious teachings.
