**Lieutenant-Colonel J. J. de Rochemont** was a retired (*gepensioneerd*) Dutch *luitenant-kolonel* and military publicist, author of two well-known pamphlets on the Aceh War: *Onze oorlog met Atjin* (one volume only, covering the First Expedition, published at The Hague by Joh. Ykema), and *Onze vestiging in het rijk van Atjin* ("Our establishment in the kingdom of Aceh"), both cited by [[P. Brooshooft|Brooshooft]] in his [[Geschiedenis van den Atjeh-oorlog 1873–1886 (Brooshooft, 1886)|popular history of 1886]]. He is also cited by [[G. F. W. Borel|Borel]] as the source for the text of [[Jan van Swieten|van Swieten]]'s verbal instruction to [[J. L. J. H. Pel|Pel]] ("zooveel mogelijk vechten weinig, afwachtende houding bewaren" — "fight as little as possible, maintain an expectant attitude").

## Brochures

De Rochemont's first pamphlet, *Onze oorlog met Atjin*, treated the [[April 1873 Atjeh Expedition|first Aceh expedition]] under [[J. H. R. Köhler|Köhler]] and [[Gotfried Coenraad Ernst van Daalen|van Daalen]]. According to Brooshooft it was the only published volume of what appears to have been planned as a longer work. Its successor, *Onze vestiging in het rijk van Atjin*, treated the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)|second expedition]] and the consolidation of the Dutch position at [[koeta-radja|Kota Radja]].

A third brochure, *Wat hebben partijschappen* enz., was published in defence of Van Swieten's conduct of the war. Its most consequential passage was the quotation of Van Swieten's verbal instruction to Pel on his departure — the *afwachtende houding* (wait-and-see) instruction that Borel treats as the original sin of the *bezettingskorps*'s inheritance. Borel uses De Rochemont's own quotations against him to demolish the claim, put about by Van Swieten's partisans, that Pel had merely carried out Van Swieten's programme.

## The Pel Letter and Its Publication

De Rochemont's brochures also entered the controversy over [[J. L. J. H. Pel|Pel]]'s *particulier schrijven* (private letter) to Van Swieten, in which Pel had attributed his success to Van Swieten's *beleid* (policy). Van Swieten had made the letter public and used it as a weapon against his critics. Borel explains the letter as Pel's *nederigheid, erkentelijkheid* and *deferentie* — a private courtesy seized upon by Van Swieten's partisans as a public testimonial. De Rochemont's use of the letter in his brochure is, in Borel's reading, an example of how the partisans of Van Swieten were willing to use Pel's name in his lifetime to defend a system Pel had abandoned.

## Significance

De Rochemont stands, in the polemical literature of the Aceh War, as one of the principal pamphleteers of Van Swieten's cause. His writings are an indispensable source not because they are impartial — they are frankly partisan — but because they reproduce documents (Van Swieten's verbal instruction, Pel's private letter) that otherwise survive only through quotation. Brooshooft drew on both *Onze oorlog met Atjin* and *Onze vestiging in het rijk van Atjin*; Borel cited *Wat hebben partijschappen* at length in order to refute it.
