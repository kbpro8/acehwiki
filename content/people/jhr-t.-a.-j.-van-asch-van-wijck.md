**Jhr T. A. J. van Asch van Wijck** — Jonkheer Theo Alexander Adriaan van Asch van Wijck ([[1849]]-[[1902]]) — was a Dutch Anti-Revolutionary politician who served as minister of Colonies from [[1902]] to [[1905]] in the [[Abraham Kuyper|Kuyper]] cabinet. His ministry covered the [[1904]] [[Van Daalen's Gajo-Alas-Batak expedition (1904)|Gajoe-Alas-Batak expedition of Van Daalen]] and the [[Indische Comptabiliteitswet amendment (26 June 1903)|Indische Comptabiliteitswet amendment]] of [[26 June]] [[1903]] that abolished the *bijdrage* of [[Nederlandsch-Indië]] to the finances of the realm.

## The Comptabiliteitswet amendment

The *wetsontwerp* amending the [[Indische Comptabiliteitswet]] so as to abolish the *bijdrage* (contribution) of [[Nederlandsch-Indië]] to the finances of the realm had been submitted by [[J. T. Cremer|Cremer]], minister of Colonies in the [[kabinet Pierson]]. It came into force, however, only in the sittings-year [[1902]]/3 under his successor [[Jhr T. A. J. van Asch van Wijck|Van Asch van Wijck]], with enactment on [[26 June]] [[1903]] (S. 172). Neither [[J. T. Cremer|Cremer]] nor [[Jhr T. A. J. van Asch van Wijck|Van Asch van Wijck]] would hear of an [[Ereschuld|ereschuld]]: "geen terugtrede in hetgeen door de daartoe volkomen bevoegde macht vroeger in wettige vorm is geschied" — no retreat from what was done in lawful form by the authority fully competent to do so. The existing state of affairs was to be the starting-point for financial policy; the past could only strengthen the conviction that, when the Indies were in need, the motherland must support them according to its ability.

## The 1904 inheritance

[[Jhr T. A. J. van Asch van Wijck|Van Asch van Wijck]]'s ministry covered the [[Van Daalen's Gajo-Alas-Batak expedition (1904)|Gajoe-Alas-Batak expedition of Van Daalen]] of [[1904]]. The expedition — formally under the responsibility of [[A. W. F. Idenburg|Idenburg]] (a.r.), minister of Colonies [[1902]]-[[1905]] in the [[Abraham Kuyper|Kuyper]] cabinet — was an inheritance from the [[J. T. Cremer|Cremer]] liberal ministry that had launched the post-[[Toekoe Oemar's defection ([[1896]])|Oemar]] pursuit of the resistance leaders to [[Pedirse|Pedir]] and the [[Gajoland]] interior.

## Significance

[[Jhr T. A. J. van Asch van Wijck|Van Asch van Wijck]]'s brief ministry closes the [[Batig Slot|batig-slot]] era and bridges the [[J. T. Cremer|Cremer]] liberal and [[A. W. F. Idenburg|Idenburg]] anti-revolutionary colonial administrations. The [[Indische Comptabiliteitswet amendment (26 June 1903)|Comptabiliteitswet amendment]] of [[26 June]] [[1903]] is one of the foundation acts of the [[Ethische Politiek|ethical-policy]] state.
