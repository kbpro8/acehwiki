**S. P. Sterkman** was a **[[korporaal der mariniers|corporal of the marines]]** who received the **[[Eervolle Vermelding|“rervolle Vermelding”]]** for the second time by **[[Royal Decree 8 February 1859 No. 64]]** following his conduct in the **[[First Boni Expedition 1859]]**.

## Honors

Sterkman’s double commendation highlights the intensity of the Boni campaigns.
