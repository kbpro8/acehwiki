**H. Bendien** — *Luitenant-Kolonel der Infanterie* — was, in Kruisheer's *ATJEH '96*, a Dutch field commander who served as commander of the [[6e Bataljon]] (2 companies) in [[J. W. Stemfoort|Stemfoort]]'s [[Relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit (8 April 1896)|relief column of 8 April 1896]] and as commander of the [[9e Bataljon]] in subsequent operations. Bendien's command record in the spring of [[1896]] — multiple columns including the [[10 April 1896 reconnaissance|10 April reconnaissance]], the [[tuchtiging van Lam Goet (14 April 1896)|2e Colonne on 14 April 1896]], the [[20 April 1896 multi-column operation|Zuidercolonne on 20 April 1896]], the [[Battle of Senelop (17 April 1896)|reserve colonne on 17 April 1896]], the [[30 April 1896 demonstration in the XIII Moekims Toengkoeb|demonstration in the XIII Moekims Toengkoeb on 30 April 1896]], and the [[4 May 1896 expedition to XIII Moekims Toengkoeb|second tocht to XIII Moekims Toengkoeb on 4 May 1896]] — made him one of the most continuously active Dutch *luitenant-kolonels* of the spring operations, and his progression from the 6e to the 9e Bataljon reflects the operational reorganisation which followed the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (29 March 1896)|defection of Teuku Umar]].

## The 6e Bataljon and the Relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit

Bendien's first command in *ATJEH '96* is as commander of the [[6e Bataljon]] (2 companies) in [[J. W. Stemfoort|Stemfoort]]'s [[Relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit (8 April 1896)|relief column of 8 April 1896]] — the multi-column operation which broke the sieges of [[Biloel]] (under [[P. H. van der Wedden|Kapitein Van der Wedden]]) and [[Lam-Koenjit|Lamkoenjit]] (under [[K. H. J. Creutz Lechleitner|1e Luit. Creutz Lechleitner]]). The 6e Bataljon's strength in the column was 10 officers and 170 European and 135 native ranks — a substantial contribution to a multi-battalion relief force which also included the [[3e Bataljon]], [[9e Bataljon]], [[12e Bataljon]] and [[14e Bataljon]]. Bendien's role in the relief operation was followed by his command of the [[10 April 1896 reconnaissance|10 April reconnaissance]] — a patrol operation under his personal command which included [[v. d. Meer Mohr|Kapitein v. d. Meer Mohr]]'s 4e compagnie of the 9e Bataljon as *voorhoede* and [[Koedijk|Kapitein Koedijk]]'s 2e compagnie of the 9e Bataljon — which sustained some casualties, including the wounding of *Off. van Gesondheid 2e kl.* [[F. E. Coster|Coster]] and the death of *inl. korporaal* [[Sarijo, No. 22228|Sarijo, No. 22228]]. The reconnaissance established the operational pattern which Bendien would follow through April and May: aggressive patrolling combined with multi-column *tuchtiging* operations.

## The 9e Bataljon and the April–May Operations

Bendien's subsequent commands in April and May [[1896]] demonstrate the operational reorganisation which followed the relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit. He commanded the [[tuchtiging van Lam Goet (14 April 1896)|2e Colonne on 14 April 1896]] — the *tuchtiging* against [[Toekoe Hoesin Lhongbatta]]'s residence at [[Lam Goet]] which included the [[6e Bataljon]] under [[D. A. Okhuyzen|Maj. Okhuyzen]] and which produced the storming of the Lam Goet fortification and the destruction of an iron 9 cm cannon with dynamite. He commanded the [[20 April 1896 multi-column operation|Zuidercolonne on 20 April 1896]] — the multi-colonne operation which completed the wholesale evacuation of the Dutch buitenlinie — and the [[Battle of Senelop (17 April 1896)|reserve colonne on 17 April 1896]] (the Senelop operation under [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]). His progression to the command of the [[9e Bataljon]] — the same battalion which had arrived with [[J. A. Vetter|Vetter]] on [[7 April]] [[1896]] aboard the *Carpentier* — reflects the operational reshuffling which placed Bendien at the head of one of the principal Dutch infantry battalions of the spring operations. His [[30 April 1896 demonstration in the XIII Moekims Toengkoeb|demonstration in the XIII Moekims Toengkoeb on 30 April 1896]] and his [[4 May 1896 expedition to XIII Moekims Toengkoeb|second tocht to XIII Moekims Toengkoeb on 4 May 1896]] — both directed against the territory of [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb]] — were the principal April–May operations in the eastern sector, and they included [[H. T. de Moulin|Kapitein De Moulin]]'s 1e compagnie of the 9e Bataljon as *voorhoede* on [[30 April]] [[1896]] and [[H. M. Vis|1e Luit. Vis]]'s 6 brigades *maréchaussée* on [[4 May]] [[1896]].

## Significance

Bendien's significance in *ATJEH '96* lies in his embodiment of the *luitenant-kolonel* whose command record spans the full operational arc of the spring of [[1896]] — from the [[Relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit (8 April 1896)|relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit on 8 April]] to the [[4 May 1896 expedition to XIII Moekims Toengkoeb|second tocht to XIII Moekims Toengkoeb on 4 May]]. His progression from the [[6e Bataljon]] to the [[9e Bataljon]] reflects the operational reorganisation which followed the defection crisis, and his multiple column commands — including the [[tuchtiging van Lam Goet (14 April 1896)|tuchtiging of Lam Goet on 14 April]], the [[20 April 1896 multi-column operation|Zuidercolonne on 20 April]], the [[Battle of Senelop (17 April 1896)|reserve colonne on 17 April]], and the [[30 April 1896 demonstration in the XIII Moekims Toengkoeb|30 April]] and [[4 May 1896 expedition to XIII Moekims Toengkoeb|4 May]] expeditions to XIII Moekims Toengkoeb — gave him an unusually broad exposure to the operational landscape of the spring.
