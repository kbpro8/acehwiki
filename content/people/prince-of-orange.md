**Prince of Orange (William III)** was the Dutch stadtholder who, in September 1674, directed the siege of **[[Naarden]]** and coordinated the Dutch‑Spanish alliance against French forces (see [[276]]‑[[277]]).  He ordered Colonel **[[François Palm]]** and Colonel **[[George de Hertoge, Heer van Valkenburgh]]** to capture strategic positions such as the **Huizerpoort** and later the city of **[[Bonn]]**.

## Strategic importance for Aceh
William III’s policies strengthened the **[[Dutch Republic]]**’s naval power, which in turn protected the **[[Indische retourvloot]]** and the **[[Dutch East India Company]]**’s trade routes to **[[Aceh]]**.  By securing the home waters, he ensured that the fleet could escort merchant ships to the East Indies without interference from French or English privateers.
