**G. K. W. F. de Vaynes van Brakell Buys** — called *Karel*, and by [[H. N. A. Swart]] *Carolus* — was, in the caption of his portrait in [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]]'s book, "a highly deserving *[[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|constabulary]]* officer and connoisseur of Atjeh, who from [[1914]] to [[1918]] held the difficult post of adjutant to the Civil and Military Governor." He was a *Ridder van de [[Militaire Willemsorde]]*.

## The soerats

The Acehnese, du Croo wrote, "are mad about a *soerat*, a letter, a written "certificate of good conduct"," and Swart was continually accosted on tour by chiefs and ordinary people asking for one. "Ja, dat is goed," the Governor would say, and turning to his adjutant: "Carolus, geef den man een *soerat* ("letter of certification")." And Carolus promptly wrote: "The Acehnese Joesoef of the village of So-and-so in Pidië is known to me — De C. en M. Gouverneur," and Swart signed just as promptly, "waarop een doodgelukkige Atjeher met dat kostbare paperas naar huis wandelde — honderden van dergelijke 'soerats' worden waarschijnlijk nu nog als *poesaka* bewaard!"

## The zwartzwier inspection

De Vaynes accompanied Swart on the inspection tour that produced one of the best-known Swart anecdotes. Swart was fond of *zwartzwier* — jugged duck — "and the maddest of all was perhaps the Governor of Atjeh." At the first post he was satisfied, and there was *zwartzwier* in the evening: "dat zal smaken." At the second post he was satisfied, and again *zwartzwier*: "heel lekker." At the third post he was less satisfied, and still *zwartzwier*: "Hm." Nine days he inspected; nine days *zwartzwier*. On the last day Swart sighed: "Carulus, ik kan dat goed niet meer zien!" — "But we are going home now, General," Carolus consoled him. — "That is the only consolation," said Swart, "that at least this afternoon I shall at last eat properly again." That afternoon Carolus was telephoned by the Governor: "Carolus, what have you had to eat?" — "To eat, Governor? Beefsteak with fried potatoes." — "And do you know what I have had, Carolus?" — and the truth dawned on Carolus, who barely turned a grin into an "oooohhhh" — before the other end steamed: "Zwartzwier, Carolus! Voor de tiende keer. En als welkom thuis!"
