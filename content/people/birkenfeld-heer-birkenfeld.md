**Birkenfeld (Heer Birkenfeld)** was a Victim of atjeh-moord (1935) at Sigli mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Murdered in 1935 at the stationsemplacement at Sigli.

## Role

Victim of [[Atjeh-moord|atjeh-moord]] ([[1935]]) at [[Sigli]]

## Account from Jongejans

Murdered in [[1935]] at the stationsemplacement at [[Sigli]].

## Significance

Birkenfeld (Heer Birkenfeld) appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
