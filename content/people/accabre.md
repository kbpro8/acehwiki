**Accabré** was a rebel **bende (gang) leader** who opposed **[[Atta (rebel leader)|Atta]]** and commanded a force of **200–300 Guango‑Negers** (cannibalistic African slaves) stationed behind the plantation **[[Arendd]]**. He fortified his position with bamboo spikes and prepared to resist Dutch incursions.

## Conflict
Colonel **[[Kolonel De Salve|De Salve]]** planned an expedition against Accabré’s stronghold, assigning **[[Maajoor De Brauw|Major De Brauw]]** to lead the attack.
