**Captain‑lieutenant Smit** was a Dutch naval officer who led a land detachment during the December 19–25 riverine campaign against rebel forces in the interior of [[Acheh]]. Acting under the orders of [[Captain Van Oyen]], he marched from the plantation “[[Antonaa]]” to the [[Cimbia creek]] and later to [[Cornelia Jacoba]] plantation, where his forces encountered little resistance but reported the burning of several sugar plantations.

## Operations
Smit’s detachment was tasked with scouting the creeks, securing posts at plantations such as [[Altenklingen]] and [[Alexandria]], and supporting the bark “[[St Eustatius]]”. On [[24 December]] his men attempted to reach the plantation “[[Peereboom]]”, only to find it destroyed by fire.

## Reports and Communication
His reports, recorded in the [[Journal of Haringman]], noted the presence of “negro” (enslaved) rebels, the capture of a Surinamese border detachment, and the discovery of a silver candlestick bearing the name of the preacher [[Ramringt]].
