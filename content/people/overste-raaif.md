**Overste Raaif** was a Dutch army officer who commanded the forces that withdrew to [[Pagar Roejong]] after the failed assault on [[Marapalm]] in [[April]] [[1824]]. His troops later re‑entered the campaign in early [[March]] [[1824]] as part of the marine‑infantry contingent.

## Withdrawal and Re‑deployment

Following the heavy losses at Marapalm, Raaif’s command fell back to the fortified position at Pagar Roejong, where he awaited reinforcements before resuming operations later in the year.
