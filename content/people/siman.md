**Siman** was a native fusilier — *inlandsch fuselier* — of the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Netherlands Indies Army]] killed on [[25 September]] [[1897]] at the [[Goeah Teroes]] cave on the [[Glé Betoes]].

[[P. H. R. Beuming]] records that as the remainder of the advance-guard clambered up, firing continually, and at last reached the entrance of the cave, the patrol was fired on from every corner of it: *de inlandsche fuselier Siman werd bij deze gelegenheid in 't hoofd getroffen en viel dood neer* — the native fusilier Siman was struck in the head on this occasion and fell dead.

Captain [[J. N. C. Baron van Heerdt]], who had taken over the command of the advance-guard after the death of Lieutenant [[J. van Greuningen]], then ordered rapid fire into the cave, driving the Acehnese deeper inside. Siman's body was carried down with the others in a *[[Tandoe|tandoe]]* and taken the same day to [[Koeta Radja]] for burial on the [[Peutjoet|Petjoet cemetery]].
