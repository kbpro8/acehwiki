**Van Braam Houckgeest, F. A.** was a Dutch marine captain who was knighted in the **Order of the Dutch Lion** (fourth class) for his conduct during the Atjeh expeditions of [[1873]] and [[1874]], as listed in the royal decree of [[6 October]] [[1874]].

## Honors

The award highlighted the contribution of senior marine officers to the Dutch colonial war effort in Aceh.
