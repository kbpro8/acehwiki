**P. Nutters** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Corps of Military Constabulary of Atjeh and Dependencies]]

Among the officers and brigade commanders of the corps named in du Croo's book, in addition to Schmidt, are Jhr. [[J. J. Boreel]], [[F. Darlang]], [[G. F. B. Watrin]], [[Th. J. Veltman]], [[A. Geertsema Beckeringh]], [[F. P. A. van Gheel Gildemeester]], [[A. H. C. Roelofsen]], [[H. Behrens]], [[W. Muurling]], [[C. A. Snell]], [[Van Eck]], [[Hein Meijer]], [[G. F. P. Gosenson]], [[J. van Arkel]], [[W. A. Herold]], [[Kaniess]], [[Scheurleer]], [[Van der Vlerk]], [[G. K. W. F. de Vaynes van Brakell Buys]], [[M. Schouten]], [[W. J. Mosselman]], [[Slooten]], [[Jenae]], [[Bossert]], [[Gackstätter]], [[Wisse]], [[Loeff]], [[Roobaart]], [[Hanff]], [[J. H. C. Vastenou]] and [[P. Nutters]].
