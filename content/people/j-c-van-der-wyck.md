**J. C. van der Wyck** was a Dutch major-general (Jonkheer) who governed [[Acheh|Atjeh and Onderhoorigheden]] from [[11 June]] [[1904]] to [[6 May]] [[1905]], between the departure of Governor [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Johannes van Heutsz]] and the accession of Colonel [[Gotfried Coenraad Ernst van Daalen|G. C. E. van Daalen]]. During his short administration he had the opportunity — and, in author [[J. Langhout|Joh. Langhout]]'s words, "made a grateful use of it" — to make the measures recommended by his predecessor in the political field subservient to the furtherance of the province's economic growth.

## Administration

Van der Wyck accepted the government on 11 June [[1904]], after Van Heutsz had left for Holland at the beginning of May to negotiate about the Governor-Generalship. It fell to him to consolidate the opening of the ocean harbour [[Sabang]] and therewith of Atjeh to the direct mail connection with Europe: the mail steamers of the [[Stoomvaart Maatschappij Nederland|Stoomvaart Maatschappij "Nederland"]], which since September [[1903]] took the route over Sabang and [[Singapore]] on the outward and homeward voyages, gave a laudable proof of cooperation by considerably lowering their freight prices from the Netherlands to Sabang, whereupon the post offices at Sabang, [[Oeleë Lheuë|Oelèë Lheue]] and [[Koeta Radja]] had to be enlarged and equipped for the full traffic service.

Under Van der Wyck much building went on especially at Koeta Radja: stone houses for the assistant-residents of Groot-Atjeh and of the Dependencies were completed and occupied, and a new, tasteful hotel was finished beside numerous private dwellings. With the support of the Government an abattoir was opened in the capital and a pepper-washing establishment was erected, which the [[Nederlandsche Handel-Maatschappij]] administered and whose prepared product it caused to be shipped to [[Amsterdam]]. To show how high the trade traffic had risen, Langhout cites the figure of the import of piece-goods at Oelèë Lheue, which in value had been f 162,175 in [[1898]] and by [[1904]] had risen to f 4,889,522.

## Departure

On [[1 October]] [[1904]] the government of the Netherlands Indies passed from [[W. Rooseboom]] to Lieutenant-General [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|J. B. van Heutsz]]. On 6 May [[1905]] Governor Van der Wyck was appointed army commander (*legercommandant*), and on the same date the government of Atjeh was entrusted to Colonel G. C. E. van Daalen.
