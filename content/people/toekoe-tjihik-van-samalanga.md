**Toekoe Tjihik van Samalanga** was an Achehnese chief of [[Samalanga]] on the north coast of [[Atjeh]] who was brought over to [[Groot-Atjeh]] with a hundred fighters in [[1893]] to reinforce [[Toekoe Nja Banta]] in his struggle against the *[[Moslemin]]* in the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]].

## Transfer from the North Coast

Hooijer recorded that the prospects of [[Toekoe Nja Banta]] acting more vigorously improved when *Toekoe Tjihik van Samalanga met 100 strijders van de Noordkust werd overgevoerd om hem te helpen* — Toekoe Tjihik of Samalanga was brought over from the north coast with a hundred fighters to help him. The improvement was offset, however, by [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb|Toekoe Tjoet]] abandoning Nja Banta entirely at the same moment, becoming insolent towards Dutch officials, and finally being banished to [[Malaboeh]].

## Context

The transfer illustrates the Dutch practice of moving allied Achehnese contingents by sea between the north coast and [[Groot-Atjeh]]. Hooijer noted the reverse movement as well: from Samalanga alone an opponent of the pro-Dutch *[[Oeleebalang|oeleebalang]]* of that district crossed to Groot-Atjeh with two hundred followers to fight for [[Mat Amin|Mad Amin]] against [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]] and the Dutch.
