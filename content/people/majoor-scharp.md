**Major Scharp** was a Dutch *majoor* of the [[Indische Leger|Indische leger]] who commanded the half-battalion that attacked [[Lampoeloe]] on [[20 June]] [[1874]] in the [[Battle of Lampoeloe (20 June 1874)|battle of Lampoeloe]], the first Dutch offensive operation since the [[Expedition to Ketapang Doewa (15 February 1874)|expedition to Ketapang Doewa]] of [[15 February]] [[1874]] four months earlier. In [[G. F. W. Borel|Borel]]'s narrative he is the officer who opened the *aanvallende* phase of [[Generaal Pel|Pel]]'s command.

## The Battle of Lampoeloe

By mid-June [[1874]] the [[Crisis at Kota Radja (May–June 1874)|crisis at Kota Radja]] was at its depth. Some companies could field fewer than thirty men of an original 125; only the gravest sick could be taken into the ambulances; the Atjehneezen crept closer, "feasting under gamelan and drumming at 300–400 paces from the parapet, eating stolen Dutch slaughter-oxen." On [[19 June]] [[1874]] a column of 150 bayonets and an artillery section had marched from [[Kota Radja]] to clear the left bank north of [[Kampong Djawa]], while [[Luitenant Sloet van Zwanenburg|luitenant Sloet van Zwanenburg]] led a patrol from [[Penajoeng]] on the right bank; Sloet was discovered in his ravine, badly shot up, and died of his wounds days later. The next morning, [[20 June]] [[1874]], the enemy shelled Kota Radja from the east with unusual violence; the whole garrison stood to arms; *salvoes* of [[Beaumont-geweer|Beaumont rifles]] were needed at [[Kota Podjoet]] and [[Pakan Atjeh]] to drive back the attackers. Then Scharp with his half-battalion attacked Lampoeloe itself.

## Outcome and Significance

The enemy was driven from his positions; a half-built *benteng* by the river was captured. The cost was thirty-four Dutch dead and wounded. Borel judges the action "the first step on a new road": the first offensive since Ketapang Doewa, and the proof that, even at the lowest point of the *bezettingskorps*'s strength, the Atjehneezen could be driven from advanced positions when a Dutch commander chose to attack. Scharp's half-battalion thus inaugurated the series of *aanvallende* operations — [[Battle of Garouw (28 June 1874)|Garouw]] ([[28 June]]), [[Battle of Soerian (26 July 1874)|Soerian]] ([[26 July]]), [[Conquest of Langkroek (28 September 1874)|Langkroek]] ([[28 September]]) — by which Pel rebuilt the Dutch position in the second half of [[1874]].
