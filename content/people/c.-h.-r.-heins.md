**C. H. R. Heins** was the overseer of the *B.O.W.* (public works) murdered at [[Lhong]] in the sub-division [[Lho' Nga]] on the morning of [[13 July]] [[1914]] by seven persons of that *[[landschap]]*.

A Chinese mason was severely wounded in the same attack; the murderers fled to the mountains with two of the overseer's sporting guns and ammunition. That same morning the *controleur* of Lho' Nga left overland for Lhong with two groups, while [[H. N. A. Swart]] himself went by the *s.s. [[Dog]]* in the afternoon, and two further groups went to [[Leupoeëng]] to prevent the affair spreading. The murder was for Swart "a very sharp and painful disappointment indeed — after the attack at [[Daja]], now murder and band-formation in quiet [[Groot Atjeh]] as well."
