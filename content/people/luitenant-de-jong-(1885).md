**Luitenant de Jong** was a Dutch lieutenant of the garrison of the [[Geconcentreerde Stelling|concentrated line]] at [[Acheh|Atjeh]] in [[1885]] — not to be confused with [[Korporaal de Jong (1876)|korporaal De Jong]] of ten years earlier. In majoor [[M. T. H. Perelaer|Perelaer]]'s list he appears on [[17 August]] [[1885]], when a patrol 30 men strong was surprised by Atjehnese who had lain in ambush behind the tramway in the [[Oosterlinie|eastern sector]]: six men were wounded, among them luitenant de Jong (see [[Guerilla raids inside the concentration line (1885)]]).
