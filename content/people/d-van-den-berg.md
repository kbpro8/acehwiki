**D. van den Berg** was a Luitenant-Kolonel der Genie (Lieutenant-Colonel of Engineers) of the [[Korps Ingenieurs, Mineurs en Sappeurs|Korps Ingenieurs, Mineurs en Sappeurs]] — at the time of writing, "in leven Luitenant-Kolonel der Genie" — who in [[September 1944|September]] [[1944]] published the principal biography of [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Generaal C. J. Snijders]] under the title *Cornelis Jacobus Snijders (1852–1939): Een leven in dienst van zijn Land en zijn Volk*. The biography, the most substantial published source on Snijders' early engineer service in [[Acheh|Atjeh]] in [[1874]]–[[1875]] and on his later career as [[Opperbevelhebber van Land- en Zeemacht|Opperbevelhebber van Land- en Zeemacht]] during the [[First World War|First World War]], draws on Snijders' own unfinished memoirs (*Herinneringen uit mijn leven*, completed to [[August 1914|August]] [[1914]]), on family papers provided by Mrs. W. M. A. Snijders-Meyer and Ir. C. J. Snijders, and on the cooperation of Luitenant-Generaal b.d. C. van Tuinen.

## The Snijders Biography

Van den Berg's biography, published at Voorburg in [[September 1944|September]] [[1944]], is divided into three parts: the first covers Snijders' youth and education ([[1852]]–[[1872]]); the second his career from commissioning to Opperbevelhebber ([[1872]]–[[1918]]); the third his retirement work for civil aviation and national defence ([[1918]]–[[1939]]). The Atjeh chapters, occupying pp. 21–36, give an unusually detailed ground-level account of engineer operations in the middle phase of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] — including the [[Benting Kota Alam operation (13 November 1874)|Benting Kota Alam operation of 13 November 1874]], the [[Missigit Longbatta|Missigit Longbatta]] work of [[31 December]] [[31 December]] [[1874]]–[[January 1875|January]] [[1875]], the [[Lamara Olejlo|Lamara Olejlo]] fortification of [[15 February]] [[15 February]] [[1875]], and the [[Mandarsah Poetih|Mandarsah Poetih]] and [[Pungej Blang Tjoet|Pungej Blang Tjoet]] works — and quote at length the eyewitness account of [[T. W. G. Merz|T. W. G. Merz]] as transmitted by [[C. A. Bannink|C. A. Bannink]]. The chapters on Snijders' Indies journeys of [[1920]], [[1928]], and [[1929]] (pp. 127–138) include the [[1920]] visit to [[Acheh|Atjeh]] during which Snijders laid a wreath on the grave of [[J. L. J. H. Pel|Generaal-Majoor Pel]] at [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]]. A chronological *levensloop* is given at pp. 172–173.

Van den Berg writes in the third person and reproduces verbatim a number of Snijders' own *Herinneringen*, marking them with the siglum "(H. L.)" — *Herinneringen Loudon*, after the form of address used by the memoirist for himself.

## Place in the Atjeh-Historiography

Van den Berg's biography, although not primarily a work of Atjeh historiography, is an important source for the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] precisely because it records in detail the engineer's side of operations that are otherwise known mainly from the staff and infantry perspective. The technical descriptions of [[benting|benting]] construction — sandbag breastworks, [[bamboe-doerie|bamboe-doerie]] hedges, [[ranjoe|ranjoe]] plantings, traverses with Friesche ruiters, the use of vrije koelies and dwangarbeiders under fire — are otherwise sparsely documented. The biography also preserves the names of a number of engineer officers and infantry officers — [[W. J. C. Leers|Leers]], [[M. C. E. Ruempol|Ruempol]], [[T. J. A. van Zijll de Jong|van Zijll de Jong]], [[J. H. Kromhout|Kromhout]], [[J. W. N. Cramer|Cramer]], Van den Blijck, Bartelds, [[T. W. G. Merz|Merz]] — whose own service records are otherwise thin.
