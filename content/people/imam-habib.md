**Imam Habib** (printed in the pamphlet as "Imam Hab") was an Atjehsche chief who was reckoned among the Dutch-friendly heads and was a deliverer (*leverancier*) of supplies, but who — as the Koloniaal Verslag recorded and [[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] cited in [[1886]] — had his followers unexpectedly attack a Dutch patrol of 27 bayonets on its return march to [[Tjot-Goe]] on [[29 July]] [[1884]]. Nine men were killed, eight wounded, and ten rifles with ammunition were taken as booty. The act, committed in the same month in which [[Toekoe Oemar (Tenom)|Toekoe Oemar]] had deceived and destroyed the landing party at [[Lambesi]] ([[3 July]] [[1884]]), was quoted by Verstege as proof of how the "continuous attempts from the Dutch side to knit and maintain peaceable relations with chiefs and population were only answered with betrayal" — the month of the secret decision on the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration]] (see [[Concentration decision (16-17 June 1884)]]).
