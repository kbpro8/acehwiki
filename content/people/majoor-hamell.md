**Majoor Hamell** was a Dutch major serving under [[Van Braam]] during the 1784 operations against [[Salangore]] and [[Riouw]]. He commanded the landing forces on the morning of [[18 June]] [[1784]] and later supervised the capture of enemy batteries on the western side of the town.

## Role in the Salangore Landing
On the night before the assault, Hamell received overall command of the landing from [[Van Braam]]. His troops, comprising 291 native soldiers and additional marines, embarked at three o’clock in the morning and successfully secured a foothold despite heavy enemy fire. Hamell’s battery was later engaged in a fierce fight at the royal palace of the local ruler, where the revered figure [[Radya Hadgè]] was killed.

## Later Service and Rewards
After the campaign, Hamell fell ill in [[Malakka]] and was replaced by [[Kapitein-luitenant Van Hogendorp]] for the subsequent landing at Salangore. Upon his return to the Netherlands, Hamell was awarded 2 500 guilders for his service (see the reward list of [[1786]]).
