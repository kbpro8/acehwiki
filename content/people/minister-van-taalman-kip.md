**Van Taalman Kip** was the Dutch Minister of Marine who, in response to a telegraphic request from the colonial government, ordered the marines to be employed as fully as possible on the war‑front in Aceh. His directive, sent in [[October]] [[1874]], led to the redeployment of a marine company from [[Fort de Kock]] to [[Kota‑Radja]] where it was incorporated as the 4th company of the Left Half of the 3rd Infantry Battalion.

## Influence on deployment

The minister’s order reflected the high demand for disciplined troops in the ongoing Aceh campaigns and the flexibility of the marine corps in colonial warfare.
