---
title: Banta Ahmat
aliases: [Amat]
tags: [tokoh, karya-sastra]
---

Son of Ansari, king of Nabati, raised in poverty after his father's death while his uncle Tapeuhi stole his inheritance. He reared a dragon that helped him catch a magical bird who turned out to be the disguised princess Putroe Indra, whom he wed; he later defeated his uncle and married a second princess, Chahya.
