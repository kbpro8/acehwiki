**Ir. H. Meijer** was a Dutch plantation official of the Société Financière des Caoutchoucs who, during the opening ceremony of a new railway connection at Besitang, expressed his regret that his colleagues [[B. de Jong]] and [[C. A. Zandbergen]] could not be present, as they had been seriously wounded on 22 May [[1950]] by an Atjehse amokmaker at [[T. Pura]].
