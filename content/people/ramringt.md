**Ramringt (preacher)** was a Dutch‑language preacher whose name was found engraved on a silver candlestick recovered by a “negro” patrol at the plantation “[[Prosperiteit]]” during the Dutch expedition in December. The candlestick is mentioned in the [[Journal of Haringman]] as evidence of the presence of Christian missionaries in the region.

## Significance
The discovery of the candlestick suggests that religious figures had a foothold in the interior plantations, possibly influencing local populations and the dynamics between rebels and colonial forces.
