**Theodorus Jacobus Veltman** was a Dutch military officer and Major who served extensively in the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]].

## Military Career and Service in Aceh

Veltman was awarded the Knight 4th Class Military William Order (*M.W.O.*) in [[1897]], promoted to Knight 3rd Class M.W.O. in [[1906]], and created Officer in the Order of Oranje-Nassau in [[1909]] for distinguished leadership in Acehnese campaigns.
