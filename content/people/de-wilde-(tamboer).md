**De Wilde** — the *tamboer* (drummer), to be distinguished from the Javanese lexicographer [[de Wilde]] — was the drummer — *tamboer* — of the patrol of Captain [[H. F. T. van Blokland|Van Blokland]] who on [[7 March]] [[1896]] went alone from [[Anak Galoeng]] to [[Lambaroe]] to fetch reinforcement during the [[Patrol of Captain Van Blokland (7 March 1896)|engagement near Anak Baté]].

[[P. H. R. Beuming]] records: *Ook de tamboer de Wilde toonde grooten moed. Geheel alleen begaf hij zich op weg van Anak Galoeng naar Lambaroe, een afstand van ongeveer 1 uur, om versterking te halen* — the drummer De Wilde also showed great courage; entirely alone he set out from Anak Galoeng to Lambaroe, a distance of about an hour, to fetch reinforcement.

He is named with the sergeants [[Hoogeboom]] and [[Israëls]] among the instances of individual conduct in the action.
