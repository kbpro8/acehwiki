**Zijlker** — so spelled in the volume (also spelled *Zijklker* in some secondary sources) — was the Dutch petroleum prospector who, in [[1884]], secured the first petroleum concession in the *Landschap* (self-governing territory) of [[Langkat]] on the Sumatra East Coast, immediately adjacent to [[Acheh|Atjeh]]. The concession — the foundation of the modern Indonesian petroleum industry — is recorded in Ir. [[F. P. C. S. van der Ploeg|Van der Ploeg]]'s chapter *Mijnbouw* in *[[Wat Indië Ontving en Schonk]]* ([[1946]]).

## Van der Ploeg's account

Van der Ploeg's wording: "The first to drill oil in exploitable quantity was Zijlker, and indeed in a concession in the Landschap Langkat in the year 1884. But it was not until 1890 that the [[Koninklijke Petroleum Maatschappij|Koninklijke Petroleum Maatschappij]] founded on this came into being." The six-year gap between the 1884 concession and the 1890 founding of the Koninklijke reflects the time required to assemble the capital and organisation for a petroleum-refining operation in a region where no such operation had previously existed.

## Significance for Atjeh

For the [[Acheh|Atjeh]]-focused reader, Zijlker's significance is the placement of the Langkat oilfield — directly adjacent to Atjeh — at the head of the modern Indonesian petroleum story. The [[1884]] concession that Zijlker secured from the Sultan of Langkat was the legal-bureaucratic foundation on which the [[Koninklijke Petroleum Maatschappij]] was built in [[1890]], and from which the late-colonial Dutch petroleum industry expanded into the wider Indies. The same concession's location in Langkat — a self-governing sultanate on the Sumatra East Coast, immediately adjacent to Atjeh — placed the head of the modern Indonesian petroleum industry in the same territorial complex that had, since the seventeenth century, supplied pepper (and later tobacco) to the world market, and that was to become, in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, the operational core of the [[Royal Dutch Shell]] concern.
