**Radja Mangkoeta** was a head of [[Pedir|Pidir]] on the north coast of [[Atjeh]], described in the official reports as one of the few Pidir chiefs who were very well disposed to the Netherlands Indies administration. His statements were one of the three independent witnesses by which the Dutch gained certainty, in July [[1876]], that the prince of [[Gigiëng]], [[Toengkoe Bintara Kamangan]], was secretly in league with the war party — itself the ground for the [[Cancellation of friendship with Gigiëng (19 August 1876)|breach of 19 August 1876]].

His name is preserved by [[Kolonel Meijer|H. F. Meijer]] in *[[Atjeh van 26 December 1875 tot 4 September 1876]]* ([[1883]]).
