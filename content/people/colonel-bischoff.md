**Colonel Bischoff** was a Dutch officer who led the landing party that seized the battery on the island of [[Gombora]] and raised the Dutch flag during the [[1824]] expedition against the Sultan of [[Aceh]]. His operation cleared the way for further Dutch advances up the river to the Aceh capital and the capture of the sultan.

## Capture of the Gombora Battery

On the morning of [[27 August]] [[1824]], Colonel Bischoff sailed with a small squadron, including the vessels *Venus* and *Ajjax*, and forced a passage through a palisade barrier to storm the battery on Gombora. After a brief fight the Dutch forces planted their flag and silenced the enemy guns.

## After‑effects

The success allowed the Dutch navy to continue upriver, eventually reaching the royal palace (the *kraton*) where the Aceh sultan was taken into custody and later exiled to [[Ternate]].
