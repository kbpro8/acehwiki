**Dr. C. P. Gunning** was the director of [[Volkslectuur]] (the Bureau for Popular Reading) in [[Weltevreden]], [[Java]]. Under his direction, the *[[Nederlandsch Indië Platen Atlas (1926)]]* was produced and published.

## Account from *Nederlandsch Indië Platen Atlas* (1926) — Gunning

The foreword of the atlas states that the work was published "door de goede zorgen van Dr. C. P. Gunning en van Volkslectuur [...] bezorgd" (through the good offices of Dr. C. P. Gunning and of Volkslectuur). Gunning's role in commissioning and overseeing the production of the atlas placed him, alongside the [[Volkslectuur]] institution he headed, at the centre of the colonial government's effort to produce an affordable, accessible, and popularly-written account of the Dutch East Indies for a wide audience.
