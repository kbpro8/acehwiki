**Hoogeboom** was a European sergeant of the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Netherlands Indies Army]] who distinguished himself in the [[Patrol of Captain Van Blokland (7 March 1896)|patrol of Captain Van Blokland]] near [[Lam Bada|Anak Baté]] on [[7 March]] [[1896]].

[[P. H. R. Beuming]] records that *bij dit gevecht onderscheidde zich in 't bijzonder de Europeesche sergeant Hoogeboom. Zelf door niet minder dan vier kogels getroffen, bleef hij met verbrijzeld linkerbeen doorvuren totdat een kogel in den rechter schouder hem dwong zich naar de benting te doen vervoeren* — himself struck by no fewer than four bullets, he continued firing with a shattered left leg until a bullet in the right shoulder compelled him to have himself carried to the *[[Benting|benting]]*.

Beuming names him beside the sergeant [[Israëls]] and the drummer [[De Wilde (tamboer)|De Wilde]] as instances of conduct in an action where, he writes, it was impracticable to sketch all the courage displayed, *ze waren te talrijk*.
