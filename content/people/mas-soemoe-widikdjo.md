**Mas Soemoe Widikdjo** was an intermediary held by the Dutch during the [[Second Dutch Expedition to Aceh]] and used in the unsuccessful attempt to open peace negotiations.

## Role in the campaign

He questioned Dutch representatives closely about the strength of their army, fleet, and artillery. The episode illustrates the intelligence-gathering and mistrust surrounding the negotiations before the capture of the [[Baiturrahman Grand Mosque|Missigit]].

## Significance

The source places [[Mas Soemoe Widikdjo]] within the contested military and political history of the [[Aceh War]].
