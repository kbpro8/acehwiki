---
title: Si Bunto'
tags: [tokoh]
---

A woman of great repute as a singer in Pidie around 1892, especially skilled in improvising pantons containing covert allusions to the private history of uleebalangs. Her husband Pang Pasi figures as her buffoon, and she reportedly gave successful performances at the "court" at Keumala.
