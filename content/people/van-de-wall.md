**Van de Wall** was the Dutch colonial official designated as one of the two original *gouvernements-commissaris* to be dispatched to [[Acheh|Atjeh]] in [[1873]], together with [[Schiff|Schiff]]. When the telegram of [[16 February]] [[1873]] arrived informing the Hague of the impending mission, Van de Wall was already on his deathbed. His illness necessitated the substitution of [[T.N. Nieuwenhuyzen|vice-president Nieuwenhuyzen]] as sole commissioner, a change that [[I.D. Fransen van de Putte|the Minister]] used to point to Nieuwenhuyzen as the most suitable replacement.
