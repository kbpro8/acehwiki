**Lieutenant‑baron W. de Constant Rebeeque** commanded the steamship *Vesuvius* during the Dutch anti‑piracy operation of [[1847]] (page 108). Under his leadership the vessel successfully engaged local sea‑raiders, earning commendations for its crew, including Corporal J. E. Bruins.

## Command of *Vesuvius*

Rebeeque’s orders emphasized aggressive pursuit of pirates, a policy that reflected the Dutch navy’s shift from sail to steam propulsion in the archipelago (page 108).

## Recognition

His actions contributed to the issuance of a royal decree on [[21 August]] [[1850]] awarding the *Eervolle Vermelding* to several crew members (page 108).
