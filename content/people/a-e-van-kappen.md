**A. E. van Kappen** — *Kapitein* (Captain) of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army|K.N.I.L.]] — was the commander of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Korps Marechaussee]] (the marechaussée corps) in [[Groot-Atjeh]] during the second semester of [[1896]], in which capacity he commanded the [[Punitive expedition to Batoe-Mengkoerah (19 November 1896)|punitive expedition to Batoe-Mengkoerah]] of [[19 November]] [[1896]] and participated in the [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|surprise of Ana' Galoeng]] of [[29 June]] [[1896]] and the [[Excursie to Lepong and Lohong (10–14 August 1896)|expedition to Lepong]] of August [[1896]]. He is the principal marechaussée commander of the second semester of [[1896]] in *[[ATJEH '96 (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96]]* (1913).

## Career in 1896

Van Kappen is first mentioned in *[[ATJEH '96 (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96]]* as a *1e Luitenant* serving as adjutant (*1e Luit.-Adj. E. A. van Kappen*) on the staff of [[Kolonel Stemfoort|Kolonel J. W. Stemfoort]] in [[April]] [[1896]]. He was promoted to *Kapitein* and given command of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|marechaussée corps]] — strength four officers and 221 men — for the [[Excursie to Lepong and Lohong (10–14 August 1896)|expedition to Lepong]] of August [[1896]].

## The 1896 campaign

The *Tweede Deel* of *ATJEH '96* records Van Kappen's command of the [[Punitive expedition to Batoe-Mengkoerah (19 November 1896)|tuchtiging of Batoe-Mengkoerah]] of [[19 November]] [[1896]] — a marechaussée operation against the grottoes of the [[Batoe Mergati|Batoe-Mengkoerah]] hill in the [[XXII Moekims|XXII-Moekim federation]] — and his participation in the [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|surprise of Ana' Galoeng]] of [[29 June]], where he was wounded in the assault on the *benteng* (fort). The *Tweede Deel* also records his role in the [[Excursie to Lepong and Lohong (10–14 August 1896)|expedition to Lepong]] of August [[1896]], where the marechaussée corps under his command was the principal manoeuvre element of [[J. B. van Heutsz|Lt.-Kol. Van Heutsz]]'s column.

## Significance

Van Kappen is the principal marechaussée commander of the second semester of [[1896]] in *[[ATJEH '96 (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96]]*. His command of the [[Punitive expedition to Batoe-Mengkoerah (19 November 1896)|tuchtiging of Batoe-Mengkoerah]] of [[19 November]] [[1896]] was the principal marechaussée operation of the second half of the year, and his participation in the [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|surprise of Ana' Galoeng]] of [[29 June]] [[1896]] placed him at the centre of the most celebrated Dutch tactical success of the year.
