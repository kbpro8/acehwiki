**Bilgrām** — in English **Bilgram** — is a town in present-day Uttar Pradesh, India, the place of death of [[Sajjid Abu Hafs Umar Ba Sjaiban|Sayyid ʿUmar Bā Shaybān]] in [[1656]]. Bā Shaybān — the shaikh of [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]] and of [[Yusuf Makasar|Shaikh Yūsuf al-Makassar]] in the [[Rifa'iyah order|Rifāʿiyya]] — had lived chiefly at [[Bijapur|Bījāpūr]] under the protection of the ruler Muḥammad ʿĀdilshāh, but died at Bilgrām. The place of his death is recorded by al-Muḥibbī in *Khulāṣat al-athār* III, p. 214 ff., cited by [[P. Voorhoeve|Voorhoeve]] in the introduction to the [[1955]] edition of Raniri's two Malay treatises.

## Significance

Bilgrām is the place of death of [[Sajjid Abu Hafs Umar Ba Sjaiban|Sayyid ʿUmar Bā Shaybān]] — the shaikh of [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|Raniri]] and of [[Yusuf Makasar|Shaikh Yūsuf al-Makassar]] — and is the final chronological peg of the Gujarati-Ḥadramī Sufi network from which the orthodox reaction of [[Acheh]] drew its principal Malay-Indonesian agents. The death of Bā Shaybān in [[1656]] removes the last of the spiritual masters who had formed Raniri in the [[Rifa'iyah order|Rifāʿiyya]]. The place of his death at Bilgrām — a town of present-day Uttar Pradesh — is recorded by al-Muḥibbī, and is the principal authority for the date of his death.
