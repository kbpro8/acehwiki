**Habib Abdurrahman az-Zahir** was a prominent Arab-Acehnese religious scholar, statesman, and foreign diplomat who served as an influential political advisor and envoy for the [[Aceh Sultanate]] prior to and during the Aceh War.

## Diplomatic Missions and Role in 1872-1873

During [[1872]] and early [[1873]], [[Habib Abdurrahman az-Zahir]] represented the court of [[Kraton of Aceh]] in diplomatic missions abroad, attempting to secure foreign intervention and arms purchases in [[Penang]] following the Anglo-Dutch Sumatra Treaty of [[1871]]. 

His diplomatic activities were closely monitored by the [[Dutch East Indies Government]] in [[Batavia]] and formed part of the background debates in the [[Parliamentary Note on Relations with Aceh 1872-1873]] discussed by General [[G. G. Booms]] in [[1875]].
