**A. Pruijs van der Hoeven** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Van Militair naar Civiel Bestuur]]

The *spadille* was the warning reference to the "civielen tijd" of [[A. Pruijs van der Hoeven|Pruijs van der Hoeven]] and [[Laging Tobias]].
