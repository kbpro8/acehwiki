**Teukoe radja Hoesin** was the *[[Oeléebalang|oeléebalang]]* of [[Reubéë|Reubéë]] in the *onderafdeeling* [[Padang Tidji|Padang Tidji]] of the *Afdeeling Pidië]], a chief whom Broersma singles out for praise as *'beseffend welke volksbelangen zijn gemoeid met orde en netheid'* — *understanding what people's interests are involved in order and neatness* — and as one who keeps his roads in good condition. The roads around [[Padang Tidji|Padang Tidji]] are particularly well maintained.

## Landschap and Roads
Broersma notes Teukoe radja Hoesin in passing, in his account of the *onderafdeeling* Padang Tidji, as an example of the kind of chief who understands that good roads are not a luxury but a *volksbelang*. The great road runs from Padang Tidji some kilometres westward and then stops; the only large north-south road has a great gap, so that on that stretch in the direction of [[Seulimeum|Seulimeum]] the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]] is the only large traffic-road and over-land connection. There is a path, but it is desirable that the connection over land for heavy transport be completed.

## Significance
Teukoe radja Hoesin is one of the small *oeléebalangs* of Pidië whom Broersma praises, alongside the great chiefs, for taking seriously the ordinary business of *wegenbouw* — road-building — and *orde en netheid*. His *landschap* is the type of the well-kept small chiefdom in the great rice-plain.
