**Van Blankenstein** was a Interviewer of Marshal Lyautey mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. 

## Role

Interviewer of Marshal Lyautey

## Significance

Van Blankenstein appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
