**Fatimah bint Abdarrhaman 'Aidid** was a Sayyid woman of the famous clan of **'Aidid**, and the consort of the great Achehnese saint [[Teungku Anjong|Teungku Anjong]] (Sayyid Abu Bakar bin Husain Bil-Faqih). She is better known to the Achehnese by the popular honorific **Aja Eseutiri** — also written **Aja Bseutiri** — meaning "my lady the consort". Her tomb stands close to that of her husband at [[Gampong Jawa]], and is the occasion of one of the three principal annual *kanduris* held in the *deah* (prayer-house) at that sacred tomb.

## Birth, descent, and death

Fatimah was a daughter of a Sayyid of the famous clan of 'Aidid — a Hadrami Arab lineage of [[Hadramaut|Hadramaut]] origin, branches of which are to be found throughout the Indian Archipelago and especially at [[Batavia|Batavia]] (Luar Batang) and [[Acheh]]. She appears to have died on the 18th of Rajab 1235 H, corresponding to May [[1820]]. Her husband, [[Teungku Anjong|Teungku Anjong]], had himself died — according to the Achehnese chronology recorded by [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] — on the 14th of Ramadhan 1196 H (August [[1782]]); she thus survived him by some thirty-eight years.

## The annual kanduri of Aja Eseutiri

On the 18th of Rajab each year, one of the three principal annual *kanduris* is held in the *deah* at Teungku Anjong's tomb in honour of Aja Eseutiri. The other two are held on the 12th of [[Mo'lot|Rabi' al-awwal]] (in honour of the saint himself, the so-called [[Kanduri T'eungku Anjong|kanduri Těungku Anjong]]) and on the 14th of [[Puasa (Ramadhan fasting month)|Puasa]] (also dedicated to the saint). The three together form the most important cycle of religious feasts at this most venerated of Achehnese tombs; the *kanduri Aja Eseutiri* is described as resembling the other two exactly.

## Place in the Achehnese hagiological calendar

The annual kanduri of Aja Eseutiri is one of the fixed feasts of the Achehnese year, and is woven into the calendar of the month of [[Kanduri Apam|Rajab]] — itself known in Acheh as *Kanduri Apam* from the round flat apam-cakes baked in every house during the month. The conjunction of the *kanduri Aja Eseutiri* with the observance of the Prophet's celebrated "journey to heaven" — the [[Miraj (Ascension of Mohammad)|mi'raj]] — commemorated on the night of the 27th of Rajab, places the saint's consort within the same ritual month as one of the principal events of the Mohammedan sacred history.
