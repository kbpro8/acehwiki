**C.J. Marinkelle** was a 1st Class Naval Lieutenant who served as an adjutant to General [[J.H.R. Köhler]] during the [[First Aceh Expedition]].

## Service
On [[10 April]] [[1873]], during the [[First Conquest of the Great Missigit]], Marinkelle delivered the critical report to General Köhler that the [[Atjeh's Groote Missigit]] could no longer be held, prompting Köhler to order the immediate [[Retreat from the Missigit]].
