**P. Mijer** was a Dutch colonial administrator who served as [[Governor-General of the Netherlands India|Governor-General of Netherlands India]] immediately before [[J. Loudon|Mr. J. Loudon]], from whom Loudon took over the administration on [[1 January]] [[1 January]] [[1872]]. The source work does not give further particulars of Mijer's administration beyond noting that Loudon's governorship is "principally of great significance become through the outbreak of the Atjeh-oorlog"; the implication is that Mijer's governorship, by contrast, did not produce events of comparable importance in the period covered by the source work.

## The Hand-over, 1 January 1872

On [[1 January]] [[1 January]] [[1872]] the newly appointed Governor-General of Netherlands India, Mr. J. Loudon, took over the administration from the hands of his predecessor Mr. P. Mijer. Loudon's governorship is principally of great significance through the outbreak of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]].
