**Lieutenant F. A. van Braam Houckgeest** served as the **adjutant** of the Dutch expeditionary force during the 1868‑1870 campaigns in [[Acheh]]. He was the senior marine officer of the **Marine Landings Division**, coordinating the assault on fortified villages such as [[Kali-Anget]] and [[Kampong Djentarek]].

## Campaign Highlights

- **1868**: Acted as adjutant to the corps commander, overseeing the landing division’s operations during the attack on [[Bandjar]] on [[24 October]] [[1868]].
- **1870**: Received an **Eervolle Vermelding** for his conduct and was later appointed a knight of the [[Militaire Willemsorde]] (3rd class) after the successful subjugation of the Acehnese resistance.

## Significance

Van Braam Houckgeest’s role illustrates the importance of staff officers in directing complex amphibious operations and in maintaining discipline within the marine landing forces.
