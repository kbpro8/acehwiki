**Van der Stroom, A.** was a Dutch marine corporal who was mentioned in the royal decree of [[6 October]] [[1874]] for his service during the Atjeh expeditions of [[1873]] and [[1874]].

## Recognition

His name appears among the enlisted ranks honored for bravery in Aceh.
