**Pang Lateh** was a determined resistance fighter (*panglima*) and the energieke medestrijder of [[Pang Nanggroë]] in the [[Keureutoe|Keureutoë]] mountains. In [[September]] [[1905]], together with Pang Nanggroë, he brought [[Tjoet Meuthia]] and the young [[T. Radja Sabi]] — the *adelaarsjong* — from the vlakte to the bergen, where they established themselves along the upper Djambo Ale in a wild but free land.

## Role in the resistance

Pang Lateh was one of the leading figures of the verzetspartij that formed around Tjoet Meuthia and her son after the [[Execution of T. Tjhi Toenong (1905)|execution of T. Tjhi Toenong]] on [[5 March]] [[1905]]. The bende, initially under the opperste leiding of Teukoe Ben Pira (a brother of Tjoet Meuthia), came entirely under Pang Nanggroë when Pira became *sjahid* in [[1907]]. Pang Lateh was one of the chief assistants throughout this period.

## Death and burial

Pang Lateh was killed at a later occasion (the book does not specify the date) and was buried behind the keudé of [[Lho Soekon]], within a wooden *kandang* enclosure. He lies there *vlak naast* Pang Nanggroë, who was killed on [[26 September]] [[1910]] by sergeant Van Slooten's brigade in the Paja Tjitcem moeras. As Zentgraff writes: *„So in death they are together again, as on many an old campaign.“*

The two graves within their *kandang* enclosures remain at Lho Soekon as memorials to the resistance.

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart (1943)

Pang Nanggròë's body, carried back after his death in the [[Paja Tjitjém]] on [[26 September]] [[1910]] so that the population might recognise it, was buried behind the *keudé* within a covered wooden enclosure, a *[[kandang]]*, "and right beside it, likewise in such a kandang, rests the body of Pang Latèh … So in death they are together again, as on many an old campaign."
