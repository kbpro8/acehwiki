**W. Allirol** was a **Lieutenant ter zee** (naval lieutenant) of the **Royal Netherlands Navy** who commanded an armed **eel‑flotilla** during the **Tamiang** expedition of **[[February]] [[1893]]**. Originating from the steamships **[[Flores (ship)]]**, **[[Soembawa (ship)]]**, and **[[Sindoro (ship)]]**, his unit cleared river obstructions of rotan, rope and bamboo under heavy enemy fire.

## Naval Action

Allirol’s flotilla cleared the **Tamiang** river’s blockades on **[[15 February]] [[1893]]**, enabling the infantry column to advance. His vessel sustained light wounds, and his actions were recorded in the Dutch naval yearbook of **[[1892]]‑[[1893]]**.

## Significance

Allirol’s role illustrates the importance of riverine warfare in the **Aceh War**, where naval forces often had to remove natural and man‑made barriers to support land operations.
