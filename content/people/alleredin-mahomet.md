**Alleredin Mahomet** was the [[Atjehsche vloot|Atjehnese fleet]] commander who led the descent on [[Aroe]] in [[1539]] and so brought the [[Atjeh-Aroe War (1539)|Atjeh-Aru war]] to its crisis. He is named by [[Mendez Pinto]] as the *beau frère du mesme Roy, marié avec une sienne coeur*, "brother-in-law of the [Atjehnese] king, married to a sister of his", and as holding the office of *Gouverneur du Royaume de Baarros* — i.e. governor of the kingdom of [[Barus|Baarros]] on the Sumatran west coast, a post that placed him in command of Atjeh's western sphere at the same moment that the sultan was extending his power over the east coast.

## Command of the Atjehsche vloot, 1539

Pinto's account, retold by De Roo de la Faille, places Alleredin Mahomet in command of the [[Atjehsche vloot|fleet of about 130 vessels]] that arrived at [[Aroe-baai|the bay of Aroe]] three days after Pinto's own arrival there. The fleet anchored in the river of [[Poelo Kampi|Panetican]], where the [[Radja of Aroe|radja of Aroe]] had drawn up his 6,000 men. The military weight of the force — its size and the fact that its commander was the sultan's brother-in-law — left Pinto in no doubt that Aroe would fall, and he sought leave to depart. After Pinto's withdrawal, the end came quickly: the radja held out at first with success, but treason by an Atjeh-bribed *Cacis* opened the gates; the radja fell; panic ensued.

## The Governorship of Baarros

The title *Gouverneur du Royaume de Baarros* ties Alleredin Mahomet to [[Barus|Barus]] — the great west-coast emporium of [[kapoer baroer|camphor]] and a long-standing node of Indian, Arabic and Chinese trade. His command of the Atjehsche vloot against Aroe, combined with his governorship of Barus, marks the breadth of the [[Sultan of Atjeh (16th century)|sultan of Atjeh]]'s design in [[1539]]: not merely to chastise a single neighbour but to bring the whole circuit of Sumatran coast, west and east, under Atjehnese command — and so to close the [[Straits of Malacca|Strait of Malacca]] to Portuguese shipping.

## Significance

For AcehWiki, Alleredin Mahomet is the only Atjehnese commander of the [[Atjeh-Aroe War (1539)]] to be named by a European eyewitness, and the only high officer of the early-sixteenth-century [[Aceh Sultanate|Atjehnese sultanate]] whose portfolio — fleet command, royal kinship, and governorship of [[Barus|Barus]] — can be reconstructed from a single source. His name is thus the principal personal anchor for the Atjehnese side of the [[1539]] war.
