---

title: Teungku Tanoh Abie
tags: [person, scholar]
---

A celebrated teacher known for his learning and independence, who held the position of kali of the XXII Mukims, and was father of Teungku Tanoh Mirah.

## See Also

- [[Teungku Tanoh Mirah]]

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
