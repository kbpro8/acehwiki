**Teukoe Gam** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Maurice Henri du Croo]]

As acting *controleur* of [[Meulaboh]] in [[1925]] he was struck by the improbably numerous entries on the budget headed *onderstand* (support) to relatives of chiefs, to *[[Teukoe Gam]]* and *[[Teungkoe Poetoes]]*. It looked, he admitted, strange and wrong, and seemed to confirm the accusation that the resistance had been bought to rest. But diligent search in archives, reports and memoranda showed that each of those items had its own history and its own "the why and the wherefore", and that they had as a rule been just and wise policy, "a practical and inexpensive bridge to normal conditions."

### From [[Het Pacificeerende Bestuurswerk 1908-1918]]

Du Croo answered from his own experience. As acting *controleur* of [[Meulaboh]] in [[1925]] he was struck by the improbably numerous entries on the budget headed *onderstand* (support) to relatives of chiefs, to [[Teukoe Gam]] and [[Teungkoe Poetoes]]. "That did indeed look odd and wrong. Had those people, then, been bought into quiet after all?" But diligent search in archives, reports and memoranda showed that each of those items had its own history, its own "the why and the wherefore." It had as a rule been just and wise policy, which had supported such people and thereby laid "a practical and inexpensive bridge to normal conditions."
