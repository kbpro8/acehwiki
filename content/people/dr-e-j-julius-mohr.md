**Dr. E. J. Julius Mohr** (active early 20th c.) was a Agro-geologist mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Cited for agro-geological considerations; he assumed the volcanoes of Aceh were somewhat acidic and thus one would expect acidic volcanic rocks in the soil.

## Role

Agro-geologist

## Dates

active early 20th c.

## Account from Jongejans

Cited for agro-geological considerations; he assumed the volcanoes of Aceh were somewhat acidic and thus one would expect acidic volcanic rocks in the soil. Other geologists disputed this view.

## Significance

Dr. E. J. Julius Mohr appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
