**Kolonel Gosenson** was a Colonel; only currently active officer to wear the Willemsorde mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Of the currently still actively serving officers, Colonel Gosenson alone wears the Willemsorde.

## Role

Colonel; only currently active officer to wear the [[Willemsorde]]

## Account from Jongejans

Of the currently still actively serving officers, Colonel Gosenson alone wears the [[Willemsorde]].

## Significance

Kolonel Gosenson appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
