---

title: "Teungku Lho' Tapa' Tuan"
tags: [person, saint]
---

The most celebrated coastal saint of the West Coast, said to have pursued a dragon into the sea. His footsteps, the rocks formed from his turban and staff, and two islands rent asunder by the fleeing dragon are pointed out as evidence of the chase; deliberately approaching the rocks is dangerous, though touching them accidentally is a lucky omen.
