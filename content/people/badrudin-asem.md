---
title: Badrudin Asem
tags: [person, ruler, sultan]
---

A Sultan of Acheh recorded in the register of Acehnese rulers compiled by Hurgronje, among the line of sultans whose reigns are documented with varying degrees of detail.

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
