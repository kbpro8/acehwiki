**Teuku Nek of Meuraxa** (historically written Toekoe Nek Radja Setia of Maraksa) was a prominent Acehnese nobleman, hulubalang, and hereditary ruler of the coastal port and principality of [[Meuraxa]] who defected to the [[Dutch East Indies Government]] during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]].

## Submission and Alliance in 1873-1874

During the initial landings of the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] in December [[1873]], [[Teuku Nek of Meuraxa]] recognized Dutch military superiority and formally submitted to Commander-in-Chief General [[J. van Swieten]]. 

On [[27 January]] [[1874]], following the [[Capture of the Aceh Kraton]], Teuku Nek visited Dutch headquarters in [[Kraton of Aceh]] to reaffirm his allegiance and provide political intelligence regarding neighboring rulers.

## Role as Intelligence Informant

As documented in *Atjeh gedurende de tweede expeditie en later* ([[1876]]), [[Teuku Nek of Meuraxa]] served as General [[J. van Swieten]]'s primary indigenous informant ([[Military Intelligence in the Aceh War]]). He reported that his son [[Teuku Nya Abas]], his son-in-law [[Teuku Nya Mohamadad]], and other chiefs of the [[XX Moekims]] were ready to submit. 

These reports formed the factual basis for the [[General van Swieten War Telegrams]] and journal excerpts published in the *Javasche Courant* ([[Commander's Journal of the Second Aceh Expedition]]).
