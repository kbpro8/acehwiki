**C. C. Brown** was the author of *Peruk Malay*, a work cited in the source for information on Malay kinship terms, specifically the term for women or household members (*ai‑harim*).

## Cited Reference
The source notes: “*isi, ramah, huisgenoten, vrouwen (A. ai‑harim), 97 (cf. C. C. Brown, Peruk Malay, p. 96 nt. 4, p. 98 nt. 2);*” indicating that Brown’s lexicographic work provides supplementary detail on the Malay term *ai‑harim*.

## Significance for Acehnese Lexicon
Although *Peruk Malay* focuses on general Malay usage, its entries are relevant to the study of Acehnese Malay, as the language shares a substantial lexical base with the broader Malay world. Scholars of Acehnese historiography may consult Brown’s work when analysing terms appearing in Malay manuscripts from the Aceh region.
