**Triebel** was a Dutch captain (*kapitein*) of the [[Koninklijk Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Indies army]] who, in the night of 26–27 July [[1892]], led the second Dutch attack upon the fortified Atjehnese kampong [[Kaloet]] in the [[XXV Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]] and suffered *échec*, five days after kapitein [[Jhr. Graafland]] had failed before the same kampong on [[21 July]] [[1892]]. [[W. A. Coblijn|Coblijn]], recording the succession in *[[Hoe Atjeh te onderwerpen (Coblijn, 1893)|Hoe Atjeh te onderwerpen]]*, noted that for the third attempt, in October following — the [[Battle of Kaloet (1 October 1892)|battle of Kaloet]] — again only a single company was used at first, so that reinforcements had to be sent piecemeal; he asked why it was again with merely one company, and placed the three fruitless attacks in his indictment of the conduct of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]].
