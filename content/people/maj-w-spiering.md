**Major W. Spiering** was a Dutch military officer who commanded the 3rd Battalion of Infantry (*bat. Inf. III*) of the KNIL, which had been stationed at [[Sabang]] (the island off the north-western tip of [[Atjeh]]) before the transfer of Dutch military responsibilities to the Indonesian armed forces (APRIS). On 30 May [[1950]], a company of the battalion was handed over to the APRIS at Stabat.

## Transfer ceremony

The handover ceremony at Stabat was conducted by Lt.-Col. Dom and Major Kartakusuma. Major Spiering transferred command of the battalion to Major Kartarusuma, while the adjutant Arpassa was promoted to captain and assumed command of a company. The adjutant-under-officer Salamony was promoted to first lieutenant and would act as company commander.
