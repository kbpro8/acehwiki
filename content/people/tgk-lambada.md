**Tgk. Lambada** was an Acehnese religious leader, the second son of *[[Teungkoe Sjech Saman di Tiri]]*, who was killed in a gevecht with a patrouille under the *luitenant* [[Luitenant Darlang|Darlang]]. His death, between the [[Battle of Aneu Galong (1896)|death of the eldest son at Aneu Galong]] in [[1896]] and the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]] of [[1910]]-[[1911]], is part of the steady attrition of the Tiriō family's patriarchal generation.

## The second son

"Een tweede, Tgk. Lambada was gesneuveld in een gevecht met een patrouille onder de luitenant [[Luitenant Darlang|Darlang]]." The second son of *[[Teungkoe Sjech Saman di Tiri]]*, Lambada was killed in a gevecht with a patrouille under the luitenant [[Luitenant Darlang|Darlang]]. The book does not give a date, but the placement — between the [[Battle of Aneu Galong (1896)|death of the eldest son at Aneu Galong]] in [[1896]] and the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]] of [[1910]]-[[1911]] — places the death in the intermediate period.

## The attrition of the patriarchal generation

The death of Tgk. Lambada is part of the steady attrition of the Tiriō family's patriarchal generation. The eldest son had been killed at [[Battle of Aneu Galong (1896)|Aneu Galong]] in [[1896]]; Tgk. Lambada was killed in a gevecht with a patrouille under [[Luitenant Darlang|Darlang]]; *[[Tgk. di Boekét]]* and *[[Tgk. Tjhi Majét]]* lived on as the two *voornaamste Teungkoes* until the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]] of [[1910]]-[[1911]]. The attrition is the book's marker of the long, slow working-out of the colonial army's pressure on the Tiriō family.

## Significance

Tgk. Lambada is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as the second son of *[[Teungkoe Sjech Saman di Tiri]]* and as part of the steady attrition of the Tiriō family's patriarchal generation. The book's argument is that the long, slow working-out of the colonial army's pressure on the Tiriō family — the eldest son killed in [[1896]], the second son killed by [[Luitenant Darlang|Darlang]]'s patrouille, the third and fourth sons killed in the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]] of [[1910]]-[[1911]] — was the precondition of the colonne Schmidt's destruction of the bende.
