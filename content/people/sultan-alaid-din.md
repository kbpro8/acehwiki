**Sultan Alaid-din** (circa 1600) was a Sultan of Aceh (~1600) mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Sultan under whom the golden coinage of Aceh was already in use; golden mint pieces known from his time.

## Role

[[Sultan of Aceh (captured 1824)|Sultan of Aceh]] (~[[1600]])

## Dates

circa [[1600]]

## Account from Jongejans

[[sultan|Sultan]] under whom the golden coinage of Aceh was already in use; golden mint pieces known from his time. Indicates that gold-winning and metal currency tradition in Aceh predated [[1600]].

## Significance

Sultan Alaid-din appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
