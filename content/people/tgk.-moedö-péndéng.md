**Tgk. Moedö Péndéng** is a subject named in *[[Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Lebé Grondong]]

**Lebé Grondong** was een berucht verzetsleider of the [[Gajò Loeös]], formerly the right hand and later the successor of [[Tgk. Moedö Péndéng]], twice reported dead and finally submitting in [[1912]].

### From [[The resistance in Atjeh 1908–1912]]

The monthly report for January [[1911]] partly confirmed the favourable expectations: "after mutual deliberation 22 uitgewekenen from Penosan reported, surrendering their firearms, among them all the panglimas and members of distinguished families. The submission of the Pasér Reröbö party was delayed because a few followers with a rifle had not yet returned from the Lake District; the situation in Péndéng after the death of Tgk. Moedö is not yet clear, since two panglimas went to the Lake District with the carbines and have not yet returned. In the remaining part of Gajò Loeös submission is being deliberated everywhere; the Gajò ödöt (adat), which grants every interested party a voice in any weighty decision, means that mutual deliberation costs much time. The principal religious leader Tgk. Moedö Koetö Serei too is said to be inclined to submit."
