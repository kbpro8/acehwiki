**Mahmoed (young prince of Riouw)** was the legitimate heir to the throne of [[Riouw]] who, after the flight of [[Radya Aly]], entered into a peace treaty with the Dutch commander [[Jacob Pieter van Braam|Van Braam (commander)]] on [[31 October]] [[1784]].

## Restoration of a Pro‑Company Ruler
Mahmoed sent a white flag to Van Braam, signalling his willingness to negotiate. The resulting treaty reinstated his authority under Dutch suzerainty, ending the brief Buginese‑led resistance.

## Significance
The treaty with Mahmoed secured Dutch control over the Riau archipelago and paved the way for further Dutch expansion toward [[Aceh]] and the surrounding regions.
