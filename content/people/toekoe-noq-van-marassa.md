**Toekoe Noq van Marassa** — *Teungku Ne' of Meura'sa* — was an [[Achehnese|Atjehnese]] chief (*Ne'* — literally "grandfather", an honorific for a senior chief) of [[Marassa]] in the [[XXV Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]] who, in the years before the Dutch attack of [[1873]], served as one of the three principal advisers consulted by the Sultan [[Toeankoe Machmoed]] on the question of whether to open negotiations with the Dutch. He is named in the [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] in the chapter on [[Habib Abdurrahman Zahir|Habib Abdoe'r Rachman]] as one of the three *hoofden* — together with [[Toekoe Imam Longbattah]] and [[Toekoe Kadli]] — who declared themselves in agreement with the Habib's position.

## The council of three

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that, when the Sultan [[Toeankoe Machmoed]] commissioned [[Panglima Tibang]] to open negotiations with the Dutch steamship *Maar en Waul* then lying at the roadstead of [[Groot-Atjeh]], the Habib insisted that *zulke gewichtige saangelegenheid* (such a weighty matter) could not be decided by the Sultan alone. The matter was referred to a council of three chiefs — *Toekoe Imam Longbattah, Toekoe Kadli en Toekoe Noq van Marassa* — who declared themselves in agreement with the Habib and accompanied him to [[Batavia]] on the steamship *Patti* (Captain *Roura*) to treat with the Governor-General.

## The territory of Marassa

[[Marassa]] was the territory in the [[XXV Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]] that, in the administrative geography of the sultanate, formed part of the immediate territory of the Sultan — together with the [[Kraton of Aceh|Kraton]] and [[Kota Radja]], the [[Taman (Kota Goenoengan)|Taman]], [[Longbatta|Longbattah]], the [[III Moekims]], the [[IV Moekims]], the [[VI Moekims]] and the [[IX Moekims]]. As *Ne'* of Marassa, Toekoe Noq held one of the senior *oeloebalang* positions in the immediate Sultan's territory, and his place in the council of three reflects the seniority of his office.

## Significance

Toekoe Noq van Marassa's significance in the documentary record of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] is as one of the three named advisers consulted by the Sultan on the question of whether to open negotiations with the Dutch, and as the *Ne'* of [[Marassa]] — a senior *oeloebalang* of the Sultan's immediate territory. His appearance in the [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] is brief, but it identifies the council of three as the principal advisory body of the late sultanate, and it places the Habib's pro-Dutch policy within the broader framework of Atjehnese court politics.
