**Sultan Alaedin Muhamat Shah** was an Achehnese sultan who reigned from [[1824]] to [[1838]]. He is chiefly remembered in the surviving Achehnese diplomatic tradition as one of the monarchs whose mediation (*afwah*) is invoked in the preamble of the sultans' letters patent (*sarakata*), in addition to those whose names appear on the [[Sarakata|ninefold seal]].

As [[C. Snouck Hurgronje]] explains, the *chab sikureu'eng* — the ninefold seal placed at the head of sultans' edicts — consists of a central circle with the reigning sultan's name surrounded by eight smaller circles each bearing the name of a celebrated predecessor. The choice of the eight names lies with the reigning sultan, but four recur on almost every seal: [[Sultan Sayyidi al-Mukamnal]] (Alaedin al-Qahhar), [[Sultan Meukuta Alam]] (Iskandar Muda), [[Sultan Tajul-alam]] (Sapiatodin), and [[Sultan Ahmat Shah]].

Alaedin Muhamat Shah's name is not on that core list. He is exceptional rather in the opposite sense: he is the *only* sultan whose mediation is invoked in the letters patent *in addition* to those whose names appear on the ninefold seal. As the footnote to the preamble of the letters patent given to [[Panglima Meuseugit Raya]] observes, "the blessing is invoked of the identical sultans whose names appear in the *chab sikureu'eng*. The only additional one is this Muhamat ([[1824]]–[[1838]])." As a rule, only three or four of the names contained in the seals are repeated in such documents.

The reign of Alaedin Muhamat Shah fell within the decadent period of the Achehnese sultanate, when the effective power of the port-kings had been reduced to little more than the limits of the [[Dalam]] (the Sultan's Court at [[Banda Acheh]]). He was succeeded by [[Sultan Jauhar Alain Shah]] ([[1802]]–[[1824]]) — though these dates are confusingly given in the native sources — and ultimately by [[Sultan Ibrahim Mansur Shah|Sultan Mansur Shah]] ([[1838]]–[[1870]]), whose son [[Tuanku Muhamat Dawot|Sultan Alaedin Muhamad Daud Shah Juhan]] was the last sultan recognised by the Achehnese before the [[Achehnese War|Dutch war]].
