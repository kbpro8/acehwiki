**Mohamed Djohan Alamsjah**, titled [[Teukoe Tjhi Peusangan]], was [[Uleebalang|oeléëbalang]] of large landscape [[Peusangan]] with more than 32,000 souls, in underafdeeling [[Bireuen]] of afdeeling [[Noordkust van Atjeh]].

## Administration and family

Living at [[Matang Gloempang Doea]] in nice but not Atjeh house amid well-planted garden at road and tram, found support among brothers who like him received good education and all speak Dutch. Brother [[Teukoe Boestaman]] received training at cultuurschool [[Soekaboemi]] and now holds office of agriculturalist in landscape. Another brother [[Mohamad Ali Alamsjah]] is oelèëbalang tjoet of [[Djeumpa]].

According to Broersma, head with much authority and good insight in interests of his land, giving all zeal. In population of Peusangan somewhat more agricultural feeling than most other landscapes. About third of ground irrigable i.e., from swamp, some *wadoeks*.

He attempted irrigation from [[Peusangan River|Peusangan]] which at [[Panté Lhong]] flows in two arms to sea, damming one arm by [[Burgerlijke Openbare Werken|B.O.W.]] After damming embankment, no water more in main line and all water runs other arm. Work cost 3 tons and fruitless. Would require new work maybe dam over whole width river, expensive due high daily wage 60 cent versus Atjeher buying off heerendienst for only 3 gulden whole year.
