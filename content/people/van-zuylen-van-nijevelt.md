**Van Zuylen van Nyevelt** (printed in the pamphlet as "Van Zullen van Nhevelt") was a member of the States General who, in the secret sitting of the Tweede Kamer of [[17 April]] [[1874]] on the origin of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh war]], took the floor against the accusation of the Minister of Colonies [[I. D. Fransen van de Putte|Fransen van de Putte]] that some members of the people's representation "meer Atjehsch waren dan de Atjehers zelven" ("were more Atjehnese than the Atjehers themselves") and "that they had more love for Atjeh than for the Netherlands" (see [[C. van Nispen van Sevenaar]] and [[Stieltjes]], cited from the same debate's official report, p. 25).
