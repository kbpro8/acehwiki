**A. de Casembroot** was a member of the States General (not to be confused with [[Vice-Admiral F. de Casembroot|Vice-Admiraal F. de Casembroot]]) who, at the end of [[1882]], presented to the government a special Nota drawing attention to the signs of demoralisation which had appeared in the [[Koninklijk Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Netherlands Indies Army]] as a result of the false situations in which the Atjeh army had been placed under the civil administration — ernstig feit ("a serious fact") which he thought the government ought to be made to attend. As [[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] records, the Minister of Colonies [[Willem Maurits de Brauw|de Brauw]] kept denying that demoralisation in a counter-memorandum — a denial maintained, in Verstege's prediction, "after the old system, certainly still in the year 1886". De Casembroot also took part, with [[Rutgers van Rozenburg]], [[Insinger]] and [[Wintgens]], in the interpellation of [[16 November]] [[1882]] over the worrying state of Atjeh.
