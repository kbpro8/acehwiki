**E. Junson** was a **[[mariner 2nd class|marinier 2e klasse]]** who was **honourably mentioned** in a **[[Royal Decree of 17 December 1878|Koninklijk Besluit van 17 December 1878]]** for his conduct during the **[[Simpang Olim (1876)]]** operations.  

## Recognition  

- **[[17 December]] [[1878]]**: Received a royal commendation for bravery in the **[[Simpang Olim (1876)]]** campaign.
