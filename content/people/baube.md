**Baubé** was a prominent **rebellion leader** operating in the **Wikkie‑kreek** region. Dutch reports from the **[[1760s]]** note that his outpost, alongside that of **[[Quacco]]**, was retaken by colonial troops. Later, a **Neger captain named Baubé** was captured by **[[Luitenant‑Kolonel Douglas|Lieutenant‑Colonel Douglas]]**, though his wife was not.

## Military Engagements
Baubé’s forces were part of the larger rebel coalition that resisted Dutch attempts to re‑establish control over the plantations.
