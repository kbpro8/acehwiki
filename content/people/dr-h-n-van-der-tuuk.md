---

title: Dr. H.N. van der Tuuk
aliases: [van der Tuuk]
tags: [person, western-scholar]
---

A Dutch linguist and orientalist whose essay on Malay manuscripts and notes in Bijdragen ([[1866]]) are extensively cited by Hurgronje, particularly regarding the literary works and the history of religious teachers in Acheh, including Raniri, Shamsuddin, and Abdurra'uf.

## See Also

- [[Raniri]]
- [[Shaikh Shamsuddin of Sumatra]]
- [[Abdurra'uf of Singkel]]

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
