**Wokas** was the one genuine tracker in [[W. J. Mosselman]]'s brigade during the pursuit of October [[1910]], whose enforced detachment nearly cost Mosselman his success.

Mosselman had largely young soldiers and only one real *spoorzoeker*, Wokas; thanks to him the true trail of the fanned-out band was found. When a young *marechaussée* fell twenty metres at a waterfall and gave out, Mosselman had to leave six men, his native sergeant, the convicts and — for fear of their losing the way — Wokas as well. "And that nearly cost Mosselman his success and… cost Wokas his amply and thoroughly deserved chance of a decoration. But Wokas was a marechaussee and bore that reverse as such."
