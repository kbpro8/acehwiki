**1ste Luitenant Bordes** was a Dutch officer who commanded the second company assigned to cover the rear of Captain [[J.P. van Lier|Van Lier]]'s storming column during the second assault on the [[mesigit|Missigit]] on [[14-april|14 April]] [[1873]], during which [[J. H. R. Köhler|General Köhler]] was killed.
