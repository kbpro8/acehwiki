**F. V. A. de Stuers** was a general and former commander of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] who became a prominent critic of military field commanders following the defeat of the [[First Aceh Expedition]] in [[1873]].

## Controversy with General Booms

In April [[1875]], [[F. V. A. de Stuers]] published an essay in *De Gids* titled "Nog iets over Atjeh," defending the executive policy of the [[Dutch East Indies Government]] in [[Batavia]]. De Stuers argued that the expeditionary force dispatched in April [[1873]] possessed sufficient troop strength and time to capture [[Kraton of Aceh]], attributing the withdrawal entirely to a lack of tactical perseverance by General [[J. H. R. Köhler]] and General [[E. C. van Daalen]].

His assertions were systematically dismantled by General [[G. G. Booms]] in the [[1875]] monograph [[General Booms Answer to General de Stuers]]. Booms proved from official parliamentary records that de Stuers had ignored systemic governmental failures, including the lack of a [[General Staff of the KNIL]] and the hazards of the [[Southwest Monsoon in Aceh Operations]].
