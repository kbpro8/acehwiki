**Captain Cramer von Baumgarten** was a senior officer of the **Dutch Marine Corps** who participated in the 1875‑1876 **Aceh** expedition. He joined the assault on **Adjoen** alongside **Lieutenant Cambier** and later led a marine company during the advance on **Lamhasan** (see [[page 135]], [[page 136]]).

## Illness and Evacuation

Captain von Baumgarten fell ill and was evacuated from the field on [[5 January]] [[1876]] to **Kota Radja**, and subsequently to **Padang**, where he boarded the steamship **Kroesen** for further evacuation (see [[page 136]]).

## Military Actions

He coordinated the marine sections that supported artillery batteries and participated in the capture of several kampongs, including **Pasoch** and **Lamhasan** (see [[page 135]]).

## Significance

His leadership exemplifies the role of marine officers in combined‑arms operations during the later phases of the **Aceh War**, integrating infantry, artillery, and native troops.
