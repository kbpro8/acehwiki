**Toekoe Nja Abbas Setia Oelama** was the *Panglima Sagi* (war lord of the province) of the [[XXV Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]], whose submission and oath at [[Kuta Raja|Kota Radja]] on [[24 January]] [[1880]] was, in [[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]]'s review of the pacification of [[1879]]–[[1881]] (after [[B. C. P. Kielstra|Kielstra]]'s history), mentioned in the first place among the heads who, after the campaigns of generaal [[Karel van der Heijden|van der Heyden]], came to Kota Radja to submit themselves to the Dutch government. Many of the heads who swore allegiance in that period belonged, like him, to the XXII and XXV Moekims whose resistance had been broken in the field.
