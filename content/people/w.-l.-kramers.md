**W. L. Kramers** was an officer of the *[[Korps Marechaussee te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* in [[Atjeh]] recorded in the *[[Overzicht van Onderscheidingen toegekend aan het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden (1890-1930)|Overzicht van Onderscheidingen]]* of the [[1930]] jubilee number as knight of the *[[Militaire Willemsorde|Militaire Willems-Orde]]* 4th class, awarded in [[1906]].

## Entry in the register

The register lists him under [[1906]] among the six recipients of the 4th class that year, with [[Vastenou|J. H. Ch. Vastenou]], [[G. K. W. F. de Vaynes van Brakell Buys]], [[O. L. Brewer]], [[Dersjant|L. Dersjant]] and [[W. V. Rhemrev]].
