**Pang Meurandéh** is a subject named in *[[Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[T. Dagang]]

The end came in [[1913]] without a fight. T. Dagang's hiding-place was found and cleared; his principal subordinate leader [[Pang Meurandéh]] submitted with nine men; and then T. Dagang gave up as well — the reason, as du Croo noted with some irony, being that Schmidt was on leave in Holland and his threat to bury T. Dagang beside the *landschapshoofd* whom T. Dagang had murdered "had now lost its force!" *"Tangsé was clean."*

### From [[The resistance in Atjeh 1913–1918]]

Simultaneously T. Dagang's hiding-place was found and cleared; [[Pang Meurandéh]], his principal subordinate commander, submitted with nine men; and then T. Dagang too gave up, since Schmidt was on leave in Holland and his threat to bury T. Dagang beside the *landschapshoofd* he had murdered had lost its force: "Tangsé was clean."
