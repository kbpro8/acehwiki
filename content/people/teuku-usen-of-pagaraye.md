---
title: Teuku Usen of Pagaraye
aliases: [Teuku Ali]
tags: [person, warrior]
---

Two brothers who served as panglimas under Teuku Asan's guerrilla leadership against the Dutch, conducting feats of arms usually directed at Dutch provision convoys.
