**Luitenant Terwogt** was a Lieutenant of infantry patrol mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. His infantry patrol ran into a small band of Teungkoe di Tjot Plieëng; three were killed, one of them bearing the seal that proved Teungkoe di Tjot Plieëng was among the fallen (1905).

## Role

Lieutenant of infantry patrol

## Account from Jongejans

His infantry patrol ran into a small band of [[Teungkoe di Tjot Plieëng]]; three were killed, one of them bearing the seal that proved [[Teungkoe di Tjot Plieëng]] was among the fallen ([[1905]]).

## Significance

Luitenant Terwogt appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
