**Captain‑Lieutenant Buys** was a Dutch naval officer who reinforced the expedition against the sultanates of [[Tanette]] and [[Soepa]] after the arrival of the frigate *Euridice* in [[1824]]. His forces helped to stabilize the Dutch position during the renewed attacks.

## Role

Buys commanded a landing division that supported the infantry and marine troops during the fighting around Soepa.
