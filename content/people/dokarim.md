---

title: Dokarim
aliases: [Abdulkarim of Glumpang Dua]
tags: [person, author]
---

The illiterate composer of the Hikayat Prang Gompeuni, the Acehnese heroic poem of the Dutch war, who orally composed the work over five years, adding fresh material as eyewitnesses provided new information. Formerly a director of sadati-performances and master of ceremonies at marriages, he recited the poem to Hurgronje, who produced from it the first complete written copy.
