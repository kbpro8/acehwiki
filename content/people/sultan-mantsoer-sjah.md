**Sultan Mantsoer Sjah** (reigned [[1838]]–[[1870]]) was the voorlaatste (next-to-last) Sultan of [[Aceh|Atjèh]], who issued *sarakata*'s to the heads of Paté and Lhö Gloempang and to the bestuurder of Lhö Kroeët. The compilation *[[Tadj-oel-moelk|Tadj-oel-moelk]]* was allegedly composed at his request by [[Teungku Kutakarang|Teungkoe Koetakarang]].

## Account from *Atjèh* by J. Kreemer (Deel II)

Sultan Mantsoer Sjah issued *[[Sarakata|sarakata]]*'s (royal charters) to:
- The heads of Paté and Lhö Gloempang
- The bestuurder of Lhö Kroeët
- The Kali Malikön Adé, who obtained half of Panglima Meuseugit Raja's territory from the Sultan

## The Tadj-oel-moelk

The *[[Tadj-oel-moelk|Tadj-oel-moelk]]* — a 141-page Atjèhnese-popular Malay compilation by [[Teungku Kutakarang|Teungkoe Koetakarang]], containing chronology, astrology (*èleumèë noedjöm*), medical recipes, and magical formulas — was allegedly compiled at the request of Sultan Mantsoer Sjah. The work's full title translates as "Crown of the realm, set with all manner of ordered pearls and precious stones."

## Court connections

Sultan Mantsoer Sjah's reign ([[1838]]–[[1870]]) preceded that of the last ruling Sultan, [[Sultan Ala ad-dïn Mahmoed Sjah|Ala ad-dïn Mahmoed Sjah]] ([[1870]]–[[1874]]). The prominent woman **Pö Tjoet Meurah** (Tjoet Nja' Meurah) had an aunt who married Sultan Mantsoer Sjah, connecting the royal house to the Pidiësche Pakèh-geslacht.
