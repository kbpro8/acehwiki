title: Amat Shah Johan
aliases: [Ama Shah Juhan]
tags: [person, ruler, sultan]
---

Described in Acehnese tradition as "the first Sultan of the last dynasty," a transitional figure in the sequence of Acehnese rulers recorded by Hurgronje.

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
