---

title: Nya' Ahmat
aliases: [Uri bin Mahmut bin Jalaludin bin Abdosalam]
tags: [person, author]
---

Author of the Nasihat ureueng muprang, "Admonition to those engaged in the war," a 2000-verse fanatical exhortation composed in August [[1894]] from the gampong of Chot Paleue, modeled on Abdussamad of Palembang's Nasihat al-muslimin and ranking the holy war above all other religious obligations.

- [[Abdussamad of Palembang]]
- [[Aceh War]]

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
