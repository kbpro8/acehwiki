**C. A. Snell** — *Ridder van de [[Militaire Willemsorde]]* — was one of [[W. Muurling]]'s two well-known Atjeh lieutenants in the [[Gajò Loeös]], and the officer who on [[5 August]] [[1914]] destroyed the united bands of [[Nja' Ali]] and [[Nja' Asém]] at [[Koeala Daja]] in the sub-division [[Tjalang]]. He was later pensioned as a colonel.

## The Gajò Loeös

Snell served with [[Van Eck]] under captain Muurling when the 1st Division of *[[Corps of Military Constabulary of Atjeh and Dependencies|marechaussée]]* was sent to the Gajò Loeös after setbacks of the infantry garrison, and "soon restored order." In May [[1911]], while Muurling accompanied the [[Kedjoerön Petiambang]] and fourteen chiefs to the fair exhibition at [[Koeta Radja]], Snell temporarily conducted the administration — "and he, while acting, brought in eight more men from the resistance and four muzzle-loaders."

## Koeala Daja, [[5 August]] [[1914]]

During the [[Resistance in Daja and Lhong (1914)|resistance in Daja and Lhong]], the relentless pursuit in the plain of Lhong drove the band of Nja' Asém either into the caves of the limestone hills or into the *[[landschap]]* [[Daja]], where another *marechaussée* column was pursuing the band of Nja' Ali. Nja' Asém fled to Daja and joined Nja' Ali's band.

"It proved a short-lived joy, for already on 5 August the marechaussee commander in Daja, Lieutenant Snell, after a neatly prepared action, surprised the bands assembled at the holy grave near the mouth of the Daja river; fifteen band members fell, among them both band leaders Nja' Ali and Nja' Asém, while the repeating carbines lost by the Daja column and the overseer's Mauser rifle were recaptured."

That blow broke the last resistance: on [[9 August]] five more men of the Lhong band reported themselves and on [[18 August]] the remaining nine came to offer their submission, handing in the missing double-barrelled sporting gun.
