**Lebé Grondong** was een berucht verzetsleider of the [[Gajò Loeös]], formerly the right hand and later the successor of [[Tgk. Moedö Péndéng]], twice reported dead and finally submitting in [[1912]].

In [[1910]] the column of [[F. Darlang|Darlang]] held the [[Péndéngvallei]], so that Lebé Grondong moved with his followers to neighbouring sub-divisions. In March [[1911]] a brigade under sergeant [[Wisse]] surprised his hiding-place between [[Tampor]] and [[Lésten]] and reported him killed with his principal *panglima* and a follower, the booty being an M.95 rifle, a Mauser repeating rifle, a Beaumont and a Mauser self-loading pistol. In September came news that he was *not* dead: he had toured the Gajò Loeö during the *hari raja* feasts extorting from the population and had obtained two carbines on the death of the resistance head of [[Badaq]]; in October he murdered [[Rödjö Akoeb]] of [[Penosan]] in revenge for the latter's journey to [[Koeta Radja|Koetaradja]] — but handed in a *panglima*, a *[[goeroe]]* and a carbine. In [[1912]] he came in submission.
