**Colonel Egter van Wissekerke** was the chief of staff (*chef van den staf*) of the [[April 1873 Atjeh Expedition|first Aceh expedition]] under General [[J. H. R. Köhler|Köhler]] in [[1873]]. After Köhler was mortally wounded at the [[mesigit|Missigit]] on [[14-april|14 April]] [[1873]], Egter van Wissekerke took provisional command in the Missigit until Colonel [[Gotfried Coenraad Ernst van Daalen|Van Daalen]] arrived from the beach camp that afternoon to assume the supreme command.
