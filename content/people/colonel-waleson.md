**Colonel Waleson** was a colonel of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] who commanded infantry formations during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] in [[1873]] and [[1874]].

## Tactical Leadership

Serving under General [[J. van Swieten]], [[Colonel Waleson]] commanded infantry brigades during the advance from [[Koerong Raba Bay]] to [[Pekan Badak]] and during the [[Capture of Kotta Alam]] in January [[1874]], contributing to the successful [[Capture of the Aceh Kraton]].
