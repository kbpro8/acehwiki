**King Willem I** was the monarch of the Kingdom of the Netherlands who, as sovereign, awarded the [[Militaire Willemsorde|Military William Order]] 4th class to several Dutch marines for their bravery in the Padri campaigns of the early 1820s, including Corporal A. Hosdorft and Marine N. Vinchent.

## Role in Colonial Honors

His decrees of honor were transmitted to the Dutch East Indies, though some recipients died before the medals could be delivered.
