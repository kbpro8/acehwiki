**J. H. Hoeksteijn** was a Dutch 1st lieutenant of the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Netherlands Indies Army]] who was wounded on [[8 April]] [[1896]] in the excursion to restore communication with the besieged forward posts [[Biloel]] and [[Lamkoenjit]], the day after the arrival of the Java troops with General [[J. A. Vetter|Vetter]]. The march cost the life of Captain [[F. P. A. Geluk]] and three European and four native fusiliers; wounded with Hoeksteijn was 1st Lieutenant [[R. ten Seldam]], together with ten European and thirteen native fusiliers.
