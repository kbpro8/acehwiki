**Kamp** was a *controleur* of the [[Controleur Binnenlandsch Bestuur|Binnenlandsch Bestuur]] who marched, in August [[1879]], with the column of Majoor [[Majoor Lubeck|Lubeck]] from [[Indrapoeri]] to [[Selimoen]] during the [[Selimoen Expedition (August 1879)|Selimoen expedition]], as the civil officer charged with the political handling of the chiefs. He is mentioned once by name in [[W. Cool]] and [[G. B. Hooijer]]'s *[[Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis (Cool & Hooijer, 1889)|Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis]]* ([[1889]]).

## Mediation at Selimoen

At the occupation of [[Selimoen]] on [[15 August]] [[1879]], when the [[Lamtoba column (1879)|Lamtoba column]] arrived a day after Lubeck's force, it was "door tusschenkomst van den bij die colonne ingedeelden controleur Kamp" — through the mediation of the controleur posted with that column — that Majoor Lubeck had given [[Toekoe Moeda Daoed|Toekoe Moeda Daud]], the newly submitted head of Selimoen, ground to hope that the bivak for the second column would be laid wholly outside the kampong, as a mark of respect to the population that had remained within it. The hope was not realised: Overste [[Majoor Gerlach|Gerlach]], who now assumed the general command, judged that his soldiers, after the hardships they had endured and must yet endure, had a claim to better lodging — "hetgeen de overwinnaar voorzeker ook het recht had te eischen" — and quartered the bivak against the kampong's edge on the right bank of the Selimoen river, assigning certain houses of the departed refugees of the [[XXII Moekims|XXII]] and [[XXVI Moekims]], with Toekoe Moeda Daud's consent, to the use of the troops.
