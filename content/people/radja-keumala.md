**Radja Keumala** (pre-1912) was a Keudjroeën Radja in Alaslanden (the only one recognized by Atjeh) mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. The only keudjroeën (territorial chief) recognized by Atjeh in the Alas valley.

## Role

[[keudjroeën|Keudjroeën]] Radja in [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]] (the only one recognized by Atjeh)

## Dates

pre-[[1912]]

## Account from Jongejans

The only [[keudjroeën]] (territorial chief) recognized by Atjeh in the [[Alaslanden|Alas valley]]. After abdicating administrative power, the [[Alaslanden|Alas valley]] was divided into a northern and southern realm, each under a [[keudjroeën]].

## Significance

Radja Keumala appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
