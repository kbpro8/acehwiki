**Teukoe Hadji Tjhi' Mohamad Djohan Alamsjah** (25-year jubilee as zelfbestuurder celebrated November 1937) was a Zelfbestuurder (self-administrator) of Peusangan landschap mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Self-administrator of Peusangan, celebrated 25-year jubilee in November 1937.

## Role

[[zelfbestuurder|Zelfbestuurder]] (self-administrator) of [[Peusangan]] [[landschap]]

## Dates

25-year jubilee as [[zelfbestuurder]] celebrated [[November]] [[1937]]

## Account from Jongejans

Self-administrator of [[Peusangan]], celebrated 25-year jubilee in [[November]] [[1937]]. Made [[Peusangan]] nearly a model-[[landschap]]. Resides over a fertile land worthy of a 'bestuurder van [[Teukoe Tjhi' (the elder)|Teukoe Tjhi's formaat]]'.

## Significance

Teukoe Hadji Tjhi' Mohamad Djohan Alamsjah appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
