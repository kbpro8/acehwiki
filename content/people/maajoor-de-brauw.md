**Maajoor De Brauw** was a Dutch **major** who commanded troops in the **Wikkie‑gebied** and later led the expedition against **[[Accabré]]**’s bende. He received orders on **[[21 March]]** to mobilise forces, including artillery and mortars, for a coordinated assault on rebel positions.

## Operations
De Brauw’s forces were to be equipped with **36 cartridges per man**, **mortars**, and **hangmatten** for night rest, reflecting the logistical planning of the campaign.
