title: F. Valentijn
aliases: [Valentijn]
tags: [person, western-scholar]
---

The Dutch historian and theologian whose "Beschrijvinge van Sumatra," part of his Oud en Nieuw Oost-Indien, supplies historical data on the conquests of Sultan Iskandar Muda, including Johor ([[1613]]) and Pahang ([[1618]]).

- [[Sultan Iskandar Muda]]

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
