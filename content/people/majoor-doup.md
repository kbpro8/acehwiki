**Majoor Doup** (active 1938) was a Major; leader of the Legermuseum (Army Museum) at Koetaradja mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Leader of the Army Museum at Koetaradja.

## Role

Major; leader of the [[Legermuseum]] ([[Legermuseum|Army Museum]]) at [[Koetaradja]]

## Dates

active [[1938]]

## Account from Jongejans

Leader of the [[Legermuseum te Koetaradja|Army Museum at Koetaradja]]. Provided [[Resident Jongejans|Jongejans]] with much data concerning the military section. Museum grew under his 'voortreffelijke leiding' (excellent leadership) into a documentary of enormous value.

## Significance

Majoor Doup appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
