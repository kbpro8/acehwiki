**Bootsman Coppel** was a Dutch **[[bootsman]]** who entered the fort of **[[Retch]]** alongside **[[L. F. Tuckerman]]** after the **[[rotan|rattan]]** barrier had been breached during the **[[Expedition to Retch 1858|1858 assault]]**.

## Role in the Battle

Coppel’s participation contributed to the close‑quarters fighting that secured the Dutch victory.
