**Toegimin** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Tgk. di Mata Ië]]

It was, as du Croo observed, almost chance that a patrol under the native sergeant [[Toegimin]] passed through [[Samarkilang]] on [[10 July]] [[1917]] and found in the forest an insignificant *ladang* with two little houses on it. Two unknown Acehnese were shot down; the leg of one corpse was much swollen, presumably the result of an earlier wound. Although Tgk. di Mata Ië had been wounded in the leg in [[1913]], there was still doubt — until a few Acehnese from Samarkilang, seeing the body, threw themselves to the ground in deep reverence, afraid of the curse of the dead man, and cried out: "Teungkoe, be not angry — it was not our fault." The Teungkoe's wife finally recognised him by his ankle rings.

### From [[Samarkilang]]

Als onze jagers het hem ál te lastig maakten, week hij voor een tijdje uit naar het toen nog vrijwel onbekende gebied van Samarkilang en het Oostelijke Gajoland, [[H. C. Zentgraaff]] wrote. On [[24 December]] [[1909]] a group of infantry returning from Samarkilang was surprised at [[Tjampoeron]], the captured carbines forming the [[Telongbende]]. In [[1911]] *ladangs* and hiding-places of [[Tgk. di Barat]] were cleared up in the Samarkilang country. On [[10 July]] [[1917]] a patrol under the native sergeant [[Toegimin]] found in the forest near Samarkilang an insignificant *ladang* with two houses; two unknown Acehnese were shot, and Acehnese of Samarkilang seeing the body threw themselves to the ground in reverence, crying "Teungkoe, be not angry — it was not our fault." From the Teungkoe's last followers formed the [[Samarkilang-bende]], whose head was captured only in [[1937]].

### From [[The resistance in Atjeh 1913–1918]]

It was, Zentgraaff wrote, almost chance that a patrol under the native sergeant [[Toegimin]] passed on [[10 July]] [[1917]] through Samarkilang and found in the forest an insignificant *ladang* with two little houses on it. Two unknown Acehnese were shot; the leg of one corpse was much swollen, presumably from an earlier wound. Doubt remained until a few Acehnese of Samarkilang, seeing the body, threw themselves to the ground in deep reverence, afraid of the curse of the dead, and cried out: "Teungkoe, be not angry — it was not our fault." The Teungkoe's wife finally recognised him by his ankle rings. "His spirit long went on working; out of his last followers there formed the well-known Samarkilang band, whose leader was only taken prisoner in 1937 — twenty years later!"
