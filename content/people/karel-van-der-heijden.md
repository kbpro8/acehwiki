**Karel van der Heijden** was a Dutch *kolonel* who later became *Gouverneur van Atjeh*, in which capacity he led the sea-expedition to the mouth of the [[Kroeng Raya]] on the north coast under the administration of [[Generaal Diemont|generaal Diemont]] and twice *tuchtigde* the vassal state of [[Gedoeng]] in June [[1878]]. His absence from [[Groot-Atjeh]] with the bulk of the mobile *strijdmacht* during the second Gedoeng expedition was the immediate occasion of the [[Enemy breakthrough in the IV, VI and IX Moekims (June 1878)|enemy breakthrough of June 1878]], the worst Dutch reverse since the [[Battle of Longbatta (31 December 1874)|disaster at Longbatta]].

## The Kroeng Raya Sea-Expedition

[[Generaal Pel|Pel]]'s secret plan, lost with his death at [[Lamjoeng]] on [[24 February]] [[1876]], had been to extend the Dutch line from the mouth of the [[Kroeng Raba]] on the west coast to the mouth of the [[Kroeng Raya]] on the north coast, so isolating [[Atjeh (Borel account)|Atjeh]] from the sea and reducing it to a *binnenstaat*. Under [[Generaal Diemont|generaal Diemont]]'s administration Van der Heijden was entrusted with the sea-borne part of the design: he *debarkeerde* troops at the mouth of the Kroeng Raya, where the Dutch *dapper gestreden* have, Borel concedes. The expedition, however, "leidde tot geene bevredigende uitkomst" and did not link up with the Tongah–[[Kwala Gigieng]] line that [[Luitenant-kolonel Engel|lt.-kol. Engel]] had reached on [[26 February]] [[1876]]. The Pel plan was therefore not fully executed, and the eastern closure of Atjeh remained incomplete.

## The Gedoeng Expeditions and the June 1878 Breakthrough

In June [[1878]] Van der Heijden, by then Gouverneur van Atjeh, left his headquarters with most of the *mobiele strijdmacht* to *tuchtigen* the vassal coastal state of [[Gedoeng]] — an operation that Borel describes as a *schitterend succes*. After the second expedition the chief of Gedoeng laid down the *bestuur* and his son came in to submit. But barely had the governor departed when the Atjehneezen attacked the Dutch *linie*, broke through at several places, fell into the [[IV Moekims]], [[VI Moekims]] and [[IX Moekims]], *verjoegen* the pro-Dutch *bevolking*, plundered and burned, *sneed* the *gemeenschap* between [[Kota Radja]] and several posts, and caused "schrik en verwarring." The enemy even perpetrated, Borel records, a "gruwelijk moordtooneel" in one of the Dutch hospitals. Van der Heijden had to rush back "in aller ijl" and with *geweld* restore communication with the posts in the IV Moekims. New *bataljons* were summoned from [[Java]]. The enemy was eventually driven out and a successful *inval* made into the [[XXII Moekims]] in punishment, but the *verloopen* of the friendly *bevolking* could not be undone: they had lost *vertrouwen* in Dutch protection. Borel uses the episode as proof that posts alone can never prevent a *linie-doorbraak* and that mobile forces must never be reduced.
