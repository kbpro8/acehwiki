**J.J. Veltman** was a Captain and colonial administrator in [[Meulaboh]] on the West Coast of Aceh who authored influential ethnographical reports on Acehnese social structures and pepper cultivation.

## Analysis of Tribal Leadership

Captain Veltman documented the unique institution of the *Kapala Kaom* (clan heads) among West Coast settler communities. He recommended that colonial credit and agricultural advances be channeled through these informal clan leaders rather than territorial chiefs (*uleebalang*), arguing that planters maintained stronger personal loyalty to their *kaom* leaders.
