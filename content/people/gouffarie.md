**Gouffarie** was another former rebel who, like **[[Accara]]**, entered the service of **[[Kolonel De Salve|Colonel De Salve]]** under a conditional **volmacht**. He was tasked with entering the forested zones, capturing escaped **Negers**, and delivering them to the Dutch command.

## Role
Gouffarie’s collaboration helped the Dutch authorities to reduce the number of active insurgents without resorting to large‑scale massacres.
