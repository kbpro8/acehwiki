**Greve** was a *Kapitein-Luitenant* (captain-lieutenant) of the [[Marine (Dutch Navy)|Royal Netherlands Navy]], commander of the warship [[Merapi (Z.M. stoomschip)|Merapi]], and the senior naval officer present on the roadstead of [[Lampanas]] with whom the commander of the [[Lamtoba column (1879)|Lamtoba column]], Luitenant-Kolonel [[Majoor Gerlach|Gerlach]], arranged the repatriation of the troops in September [[1879]], as recorded in [[W. Cool]] and [[G. B. Hooijer]]'s *[[Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis (Cool & Hooijer, 1889)|Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis]]* ([[1889]]). He is to be distinguished from the mining engineer [[De Greve]] of the central-Sumatran coal discoveries.

## The embarkation arrangement of September 1879

The Militaire Bevelhebber had determined that the return of the troops from the [[Quarantine at Lampanas (August–September 1879)|Lampanas quarantine]] should be effected exclusively by the transport [[Graaf van Bijland (stoomschip)|Graaf van Bijland]], and that the column-commandant should arrange the particulars with the senior naval officer on the roadstead of Lampanas. "In overleg dus met den Kapitein-Luitenant Greve, commandant van het oorlogschip de Merapi," it was settled that the embarkation should take place in three parts, beginning at six o'clock in the morning of [[2 September]] [[1879]]: the transport should land the troops at [[Oleh-leh]] in the afternoon, and immediately set course again for Lampanas, so that the embarkation might be continued undisturbed at the same hour on the two following days. On [[4 September]], in accordance with the stipulation of the commander of the Marine, everything was finished by ten o'clock, and Greve's Merapi — which with the [[Amboina (Z.M. stoomschip)|Amboina]] had kept the station at Lampanas during the troops' stay — steamed back to her station before [[Pedir]].
