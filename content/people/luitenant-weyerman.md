**Luitenant Weyerman** was a Dutch lieutenant of the [[Oost-Indisch Leger|Netherlands East Indies Army]] killed in [[October]] [[1893]] during the attack of the [[3de Bataljon Infanterie|3rd battalion]] under [[Overste Sievers]] on the fortified mosque of [[Masdjid Siem]] in the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]].

## Death at Masdjid Siem

Hooijer recorded that the defenders of Masdjid Siem drew strength from the sanctity the place possessed in their eyes, having so often served them as a meeting-point for religious and worldly festivals, and that Dutch bullets had to fly like howling hailstorms across the broad *sawah* (wet rice-field) plain behind the mosque and through the foliage of the heavy fruit-trees surrounding it before they gave up their sanctuary. *Luitenant Weyerman en 3 minderen waren op die vlakte gesneuveld* — Lieutenant Weyerman and three men of lower rank had fallen on that plain — and two officers and fifty men of lower rank were wounded there.
