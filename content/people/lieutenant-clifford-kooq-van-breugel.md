**Van Breugel, Clifford Kooq** was a Dutch marine lieutenant who commanded the 4th company of the Left Half of the 1st Battalion, departing on [[20 April]] [[1874]] aboard the steamship *Vice‑Admiraal Koopman* for the island of [[Poeloe Bras]] to assist in the construction of a lighthouse there. After the main expedition returned to [[Java]] on [[22 April]] and [[26 April]] [[1874]], his company remained on the island as part of the occupation force.

## Lighthouse mission

The presence of Van Breugel’s company on Poeloe Bras exemplified the Dutch practice of establishing permanent coastal installations to support naval operations in Aceh.
