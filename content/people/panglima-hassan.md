**Panglima Hassan** — also written *Panglima Hassim* — was, in Kruisheer's *ATJEH '96*, the "*hoofdpanglima van T. Moeda Baid*" (chief panglima of [[Toekoe Moeda Baid]]) and one of the three co-commanders (alongside the Kling [[Habib Lhong]] and the *oelama* [[T. Mad Amin]]) of the approximately 250-man Achehnese force which engaged the [[Patrol of Captain Van Blokland (7 March 1896)|patrol of Captain Van Blokland on 7 March]] [[1896]] at [[Ana' Batée]]. His identification as the *hoofdpanglima* of Toekoe Moeda Baid — the *hoeloebalang* of the [[VII Moekims Baid]] — places him in the western sector of [[Groot-Atjeh]], in the *landschap* which contained the historic [[Lamkrak]] mukims, and gives him a formal military command position subordinate to Baid but with operational authority over a substantial force. The combination of his territorial command in the VII Moekims Baid and his co-command of the 7 March engagement establishes him as one of the most active Achehnese field commanders in the pre-defection period.

## Position as Hoofdpanglima of Toekoe Moeda Baid

The chief panglima was the senior military commander subordinate to a *hoeloebalang*, responsible for the operational command of the territorial chief's military forces. Hassan's service as *hoofdpanglima* to [[Toekoe Moeda Baid]] therefore placed him at the head of Baid's military household — a position of considerable authority within the VII Moekims Baid, where Baid's "promise to ensure peace in his *landschap*" had been a key element of the pre-defection Dutch *bindingspolitiek*. The VII Moekims Baid contained the [[Lamkrak]], [[Kroeng Mak]], [[Loetoe]] and [[Lam-Lheue|Lam Lheue]] mukims — "*welke steeds eene verblijfplaats van kwaad volk waren*" (which had always been a refuge of bad people) — and Hassan's *hoofdpanglima*-ship would have given him operational responsibility over this persistently hostile area. The fact that Hassan was *hoofdpanglima* to a Baid who was himself "initially neutral" before he "later conferred with Oemar" suggests that Hassan's own military activity on 7 March [[1896]] may have preceded Baid's full commitment to the Achehnese coalition — that is, the *hoofdpanglima* was more aggressive than his titular master, and his co-command of the 250-man force at [[Ana' Batée]] represented an early assertion of the VII Moekims Baid's military capacity.

## Co-Command of the 7 March 1896 Engagement

The [[Patrol of Captain Van Blokland (7 March 1896)|patrol of 7 March 1896]] was, as Kruisheer narrates it, one of the most intense small engagements of the first semester of [[1896]]. The approximately 250-man Achehnese force under Hassan, [[Habib Lhong]] and [[T. Mad Amin]] engaged the [[15e Bataljon]] patrol of [[H. F. T. van Blokland|Kapitein Van Blokland]] — which included 1e Luit. [[C. T. J. Stakman|Stakman]] (wounded in the right arm), *Adj. ond. off.* [[Krüger, No. 14477|Krüger]] (wounded), *Off. van gesondh. 2e klasse* [[C. van der Meer|Dr. Van der Meer]] (wounded, head), 72 *bajonetten*, 2 *minderen* of the medical service, 9 *dwangarbeiders* with 3 stretchers and 1 *hoornblazer* — for five hours. The engagement produced heavy Dutch casualties: the severely wounded fusilier [[Monden, No. 31470|Monden]] was "afgemaakt" (finished off) by the enemy when he could no longer defend himself, and the fusilier [[Laurens, No. 19336|Laurens]] was likewise "finished off" and his body left on the field. The relief detachment under [[Kronouer|Kapitein Kronouer]] — 40 bayonets from [[Lamsoet]]/[[Lambarih]] under [[K. H. Westendorp|Lt. Westendorp]] (severely wounded) and [[F. Ketjen|Lt. Ketjen]] — eventually allowed the patrol to withdraw to Ana' Galong. Hassan's co-command of this engagement, alongside the Kling Habib Lhong (who would be killed at [[Senelop]] on [[17 April]] [[1896]]) and the *oelama* T. Mad Amin, places him in the most active Achehnese military command circle of the pre-defection period.

## Significance

Panglima Hassan's significance in *ATJEH '96* lies in his embodiment of the *hoofdpanglima* — the professional military commander subordinate to a *hoeloebalang* — whose operational activity on 7 March [[1896]] preceded the formal commitment of his titular master [[Toekoe Moeda Baid]] to the Achehnese coalition. His co-command of the 250-man force at Ana' Batée, alongside the Kling Habib Lhong and the *oelama* T. Mad Amin, illustrates the multi-confessional, multi-ethnic composition of the Achehnese military coalition in the western sector before Umar's defection. The engagement itself was one of the most intense small actions of the first semester of [[1896]] and produced some of the heaviest Dutch casualties of the spring operations.
