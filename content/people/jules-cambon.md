---

title: Jules Cambon
tags: [person, western-scholar]
---

Governor-general of Algeria, who in [[1893]] submitted to the Meccan muftis a question on whether Algerian Muslims should emigrate to Syria to escape infidel rule, comparable to that raised about British India; the muftis responded in similarly ambiguous terms.

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
