**Schmidt (Kolonel)** was a [[K.N.I.L.]] (Royal Netherlands East Indies Army) officer who collaborated with [[Maurice Henri du Croo]] on two military books: *[[Marechaussée in Atjeh (book)|Marechaussée in Atjeh]]* and *[[Generaal Swart (book)|Generaal Swart]]*. No first name or initials are given in the memorial booklet for du Croo, though he likely served in [[Aceh|Atjeh]] during the [[1920s]] and held the rank of *Kolonel* at the time of the publications. He is also referenced in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* (Dames, [[1954]]) as the commander of the *colonne Schmidt* of [[1910]]–[[1911]] against the Tiriō Teungkues, and as one of the older printed authorities on the [[Aceh War]] that Dames acknowledges in his *Nawoord*.

## Role as Co-Author

His role as co-author on a book about the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Marechaussee]] in the [[Aceh War]] suggests direct experience with the military police corps that fought in the Acehnese jungle. Together with [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]], he contributed to the Dutch colonial literature on the Aceh conflict — the two-volume output comprising *[[Marechaussée in Atjeh (book)|Marechaussée in Atjeh]]* (on the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Marechaussee corps]] and its patrols) and *[[Generaal Swart (book)|Generaal Swart]]* (a biography of the [[H. N. A. Swart|Swart]] administration that closed the war).

## The Colonne Schmidt ([[1910]]–[[1911]])

In *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* Dames names Schmidt as the commander of the *colonne Schmidt* — the campaign of [[1910]]–[[1911]] against the Tiriō Teungkoes — which is the book's set-piece example of the small-patrouille method of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Marechaussee]]. Dames further records that Schmidt received the *Eresabel* for [[1910]] and the *Militaire Willemsorde (M.W.O.)* 3e klas for [[1911]] in recognition of this campaign. Schmidt's published report on the [[1910]]–[[1911]] campaign was among the printed sources Dames drew on for his own account.

## Significance

Schmidt is significant both as a primary actor in the [[Aceh War]] — the commander of the *colonne Schmidt* of [[1910]]–[[1911]] against the Tiriō Teungkoes, decorated with the *Eresabel* and the *M.W.O.* — and as a co-author (with [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]]) of two of the book-length Dutch accounts of the war's military institutions and its closing administration. His career and writings span the active pacification era (the *colonne Schmidt*) and the interwar literary commemoration of that era (*[[Marechaussée in Atjeh (book)|Marechaussée in Atjeh]]* and *[[Generaal Swart (book)|Generaal Swart]]*), making him a key link between the operational and the historiographical phases of the Dutch memory of the [[Aceh War]].
