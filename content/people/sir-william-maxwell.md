Cited in a footnote in Hurgronje's medical section for his description of Malay medicinal "blowing" or "bespuing" practices, drawn from "Notes and Queries" in the Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society, December [[1884]].

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
