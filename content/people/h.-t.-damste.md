**H. T. Damsté** — Hendrik Thijs Damsté — was a Dutch colonial administrator and scholar of Achehnese, whose summary of [[Teungku Haron|Teungku Harõn]]'s Achehnese adaptation of the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān fī maʿrifat al-adyān*]] of [[Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]] was, as [[P. Voorhoeve|Voorhoeve]] records, "a reliable guide" by whose help it was possible to recover the passages of the *Tibjān* in Achehnese, and to use them for the textual criticism of the work.

## The summary of Teungku Haron's adaptation

About [[1929]], the Achehnese scholar [[Teungku Haron]] composed — working from a manuscript of the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān*]] — an Achehnese treatise on the history of Islam in [[Acheh]]. Damsté made an extensive summary of the work — described by Voorhoeve as "bijna een vertaling" ("almost a translation") — and this summary was the principal means by which Voorhoeve was able to use Teungku Haron's Achehnese adaptation as a witness in the apparatus criticus of the [[1955]] edition. Damsté's summary is, in effect, the principal European access to the Achehnese reading of the *Tibjān* in the early twentieth century.

## Significance

Damsté's summary of [[Teungku Haron|Teungku Harõn]]'s Achehnese adaptation is one of the principal channels by which the Achehnese reading of the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān*]] was made accessible to European scholarship. The summary — characterised by Voorhoeve as a "reliable guide" — is the principal means by which the Achehnese witness to the *Tibjān* could be used in the textual criticism of the work, and is the basis of the readings designated **D** in Voorhoeve's apparatus.
