**Christan** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Alaslanden]]

Van Daalen crossed the passes of the [[Boekit Barisan|Barisan]] ranges with two hundred *[[Corps of Military Constabulary of Atjeh and Dependencies|marechaussées]]* carrying six days' provisions and operated almost independently for five and a half months. Under the [[Administrative Division of Atjeh (1908)|division of 1908]] the Alaslanden formed part of the *afdeeling* [[Bambél]]; in [[1910]] they became an independent *afdeeling* under an officer-*gezaghebber*. In December [[1914]] two young [[Alassers]] forced their way into the *[[Moesapat]]* hall at Koeta Tjanè and wounded *controleur* [[Christan]]. [[F. Darlang|Darlang]] operated successfully in the Alaslanden in [[1911]]. In [[1936]] the Alaslanden were one of the last four sub-divisions still governed by officers.

### From [[Bambél]]

Under the [[Administrative Division of Atjeh (1908)|administrative division of 1908]] Bambél was one of the five *afdeelingen*, each under an Assistant Resident. "In [[1910]] the division of Bambél was abolished; Singkel was joined to the [[Westkust van Atjeh|West Coast]], while the Gajò Loeös and the Alas lands became independent divisions, each under an officer serving as district authority." In December [[1914]], after two young [[Alassers]] wounded *controleur* [[Christan]] at [[Koeta Tjanè]], the investigation pointed towards the acting *[[Kedjoerön]]* of Bambél; the arrest of the presumed instigator prompted an attack on the bivouac guard in March [[1915]], but the investigation finally showed the *Kedjoerön* entirely innocent.

### From [[Koeta Tjanè]]

**Koeta Tjanè** was the administrative point of the [[Alaslanden]] where two young [[Alassers]] forced their way into the *[[Moesapat]]* hall in December [[1914]] and wounded *controleur* [[Christan]].
