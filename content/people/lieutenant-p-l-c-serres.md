**Lieutenant P. L. C. Serres** was a Dutch marine officer whose name was entered into the registers of the [[Militaire Willemsorde]] after his death in the 1824 operations against the sultanates of [[Tanette]] and [[Soepa]].

## Registry Entry

His death is recorded among the casualties of the expedition, and his name appears in the order’s official register.
