**J. B. Zon** was a [[Netherlands|Dutch]] lieutenant of the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger]] who was killed in action at [[Senelop]] on [[19 April]] [[1896]] during the spring [[1896]] crisis that followed [[Toekoe Oemar's defection (1896)|Toekoe Oemar's defection]]. He is named in the [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] as one of the seven officers killed in the spring [[1896]] operations, and his portrait is one of the six reproduced in the album's closing plates.

## The second siege of Senelop

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that the temporary post at [[Senelop]] — like the post at [[Anagaloeng]] — was repeatedly attacked during the spring [[1896]] crisis. The album's narrative of the relief of the besieged temporary posts records that the *fierce struggle around Senelop* — *het verwoede gevecht dat om Senelop geleverd was* — produced heavy casualties: Major [[Okhuysen]], Lieutenant Zon and fifty subordinates were wounded, and thirteen killed. The roll of the fallen gives Zon's date of death as [[19 April]] [[1896]] — ten days after the [[Relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit (April 1896)|relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit]] in which Captain [[E. P. A. Geluk|Geluk]] had been killed.

## The chronology of the spring 1896 fallen

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]]'s roll of the fallen gives Zon's place in the chronology of the spring casualties as the fifth fatality:

- [[K. H. Westendorp|Lieutenant K. H. Westendorp]] — died of wounds [[11 March]] [[1896]]
- [[J. W. Vuijk|Lieutenant J. W. Vuijk]] — killed at Senelop [[30 March]] [[1896]]
- [[E. P. A. Geluk|Captain E. P. A. Geluk]] — killed at Biloel-Lamkoenjit [[9 April]] [[1896]]
- [[H. L. Maarschalk|Lieutenant H. L. Maarschalk]] — killed at Tjot Rang [[14 April]] [[1896]]
- [[J. B. Zon|Lieutenant J. B. Zon]] — killed at Senelop [[19 April]] [[1896]]
- [[C. G. A. C. Krohne|Lieutenant C. G. A. C. Krohne]] — died of wounds [[4 May]] [[1896]]
- [[H. O. W. L. Kramer|Captain H. O. W. L. Kramer]] — killed at Lampisang [[23 May]] [[1896]]

## Burial at Kota Potjoet

The album records that Zon was buried at the cemetery of [[Kota Potjoet]], whose entrance bears the inscription *Aan onze Kameraden, gevallen op het Veld van Eer*.

## Significance

Zon's significance in the documentary record of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] is as the second officer fatality at the post of [[Senelop]] — the post that, with [[Anagaloeng]], had been the principal target of the Atjehnese siege in the early days of the [[1896]] crisis. His death — together with that of [[J. W. Vuijk|Vuijk]] at the same post three weeks earlier — establishes Senelop as one of the costliest single posts of the spring crisis, and one of the principal focal points of the Dutch defensive effort.
