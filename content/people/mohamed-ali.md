**Mohamed Ali (Malaysian prince)** was a Malay noble installed by the Dutch [[Oost-Indische Compagnie]] as a client ruler over the coastal region of [[Salangore]] after the 1784 Dutch expedition. He had previously governed the kingdom of [[Siak]].

## Installation by the Dutch
After the capture of Salangore, Van Braam dispatched two frigates, two kotters, and two armed vessels to enforce the Dutch claim. Mohamed Ali was granted a lease over the territory and his descendants were recognised as the legitimate authority by the Company.

## Role in Regional Politics
Mohamed Ali’s appointment was part of a broader Dutch strategy to secure the Straits of Malacca and to counter Buginese influence in the archipelago. His rule facilitated Dutch trade and military logistics in the area.
