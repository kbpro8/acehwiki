**P. J. van Winter** — Prof. Jhr Dr P. J. van Winter — was the author of Chapter VII of *[[Nederlanders over de Zeeën (De Graaf et al., 1955)|Nederlanders over de Zeeën]]* ([[1955]] second edition), titled *Zuid-Afrika in de Hollandse tijd* (South Africa in the Dutch period). Although his chapter does not directly concern [[Acheh|Atjeh]], his contribution to the collaborative volume is part of the editorial frame within which the Atjeh-related chapters (II, IV) are situated.

## Authorship of Chapter VII

In the [[1955]] edition of *Nederlanders over de Zeeën*, [[P. J. van Winter|Van Winter]] was responsible for Chapter VII — the chapter covering the Dutch period at the Cape of Good Hope (1652-1795, with the brief 1803-1806 reoccupation). The chapter is, in the volume's structure, the South-Atlantic counterpart to [[H. J. de Graaf|De Graaf]]'s Chapter II on the Indies and [[W. R. Menkman|Menkman]]'s Chapter I on America and West Africa.

## The collaborative volume

The [[1955]] edition of *Nederlanders over de Zeeën* was a collaborative work edited by [[H. J. de Graaf|Dr. H. J. de Graaf]]. [[P. J. van Winter|Van Winter]] was one of the four contributors alongside [[H. J. de Graaf|De Graaf]], [[G. Gonggrijp|Gonggrijp]] and [[W. R. Menkman|Menkman]].

## Significance

[[P. J. van Winter|Van Winter]]'s significance for the encyclopedia of [[Acheh]] history is indirect: he is one of the four contributors to the [[1955]] *Nederlanders over de Zeeën*, the source of the present Aceh Wiki articles. His chapter on South Africa provides the comparative frame within which the Indies chapters are situated.
