**Ds. Thenu** was a Protestant minister (Dominee), great clergyman of Aceh's action-time mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Photo caption (page 376): 'Pastoor Verbraak en Ds.

## Role

Protestant minister (Dominee), great clergyman of Aceh's action-time

## Account from Jongejans

Photo caption (page 376): '[[Pastoor Verbraak]] en Ds. Thenu, Atjeh's groote geestelijken uit den [[actie-tijd]].'

## Significance

Ds. Thenu appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
