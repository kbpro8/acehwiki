---
title: Putroe Aloih
tags: [tokoh, karya-sastra]
---

A princess of Nata (Natal) whose hand was the reward for plucking two betel-nuts from a fabulously tall areca-palm, a feat that had killed 99 princes before Malem Diwa succeeded with magical aid. She was later carried off by the King of China in a crystal chest and rescued after the great war in China.
