**J. van Braam Morris** was an officer of the *[[Korps Marechaussee te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* in [[Atjeh]] recorded in the *[[Overzicht van Onderscheidingen toegekend aan het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden (1890-1930)|Overzicht van Onderscheidingen]]* of the [[1930]] jubilee number as recipient of the *[[Eeresabel]]* in [[1908]].

## Entry in the register

The register lists him under [[1908]] as the sole recorded award of that year.
