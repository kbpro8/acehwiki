**Sutherland, D. C. W.** was a Dutch marine captain who commanded forces during the 1874‑1875 operations in Aceh. He was awarded the fourth class of the **Militaire Willemsorde** for his distinguished conduct in the two Atjeh expeditions of [[1873]] and [[1874]].

## Honors

The royal decree of [[6 October]] [[1874]] listed Captain Sutherland among those knighted in the Military Order of William (fourth class) for bravery in Aceh.
