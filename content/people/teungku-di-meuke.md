---
title: Teungku di Meuke'
tags: [person, warrior]
---

A religious leader of the resistance party in the Meulaboh/West Coast area, whose stronghold was at Runeng. He led the holy war against the Dutch and their Acehnese allies; the poem celebrating his struggle ends with his death, portraying him as a holy martyr and representative of Islam.
