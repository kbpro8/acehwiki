**Officier van gezondheid Tromp** was a Dutch officer of the [[Oost-Indisch Leger|Netherlands East Indies Army]] serving in the outposts of [[Groot-Atjeh]] during the alliance period of [[1893]]–[[1896]], recorded by [[G. B. Hooijer]] in *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]*.

## Service in the *buitenposten* (outer posts)

He was one of the two *officieren van gezondheid* (medical officers) identified by [[G. B. Hooijer]] on the plate of [[Biloel]], the other being [[Officier van gezondheid Nanta|Nanta]].
