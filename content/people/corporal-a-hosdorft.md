**Corporal A. Hosdorft** was a member of the [[Royal Netherlands Marine Corps|Dutch Marine Corps]] who, together with Marine N. Vinchent, was decorated with the [[Militaire Willemsorde|Military William Order]] 4th class by [[King Willem I]] for his conduct during the Padri campaign of [[1824]].

## Decoration

The award was announced after the expedition, but both Hosdorft and Vinchent succumbed to their wounds before the decorations could reach them in the Dutch East Indies.
