**J. A. Kruyt** was a Dutch author whose work *Atjeh en de Atjehers* is cited repeatedly by [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] in Vol. I of *The Achehnese* as a useful source of information on the country and people of [[Acheh]]. His name appears in the footnotes of the Introduction as one of the small body of Dutch writers whose observations on Achehnese life and history had been published before Snouck Hurgronje's own investigations.

The first of the two specific citations refers to Kruyt's estimate of the weight to be attached to civil wars in Acheh. In a footnote relating to the [[Acheh War|Achehnese War]], Snouck Hurgronje commends "a very correct estimate of the weight to be attached to civil wars in Acheh" to be found in *Atjeh en de Atjehers* (p. 144). The remark bears on the essentially local and personal character of Achehnese hostilities: wars in Acheh, as Snouck Hurgronje elsewhere observes, were "of small importance and little blood was spilt," and cannot be compared in scale or consequence to the wars of European powers.

The second citation concerns Achehnese material culture. For an account of Achehnese weapons, Snouck Hurgronje refers the reader to [[K. F. H. Van Langen|Van Langen]]'s article on Acheh's west coast in the *Tijdschrift van het Nederlandsch Aardrijkskundig Genootschap*, together with J. A. Kruyt's *Atjeh en de Atjehers* (p. 56) and an appendix to the *Notulen van het Bataviaasch Genootschap* of 1892. The same work is therefore treated as a standard reference for the description of Achehnese dress and weaponry.

Kruyt's *Atjeh en de Atjehers* belongs to the literature produced by Dutch officials and observers in the closing decades of the nineteenth century, alongside the *Atjehsch Staatsbestuur* of [[K. F. H. Van Langen|Van Langen]] and the writings of [[G. H. Niemann|Niemann]] and [[Coolsma]]. These works, together with the early monographs of [[L. W. C. van den Berg|Van den Berg]] and the later researches of [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] himself, form the principal Dutch-language corpus on Achehnese society.

The variant spelling **J. A. Kruijt**, which appears elsewhere in Vol. I (p. 225) in connection with the same work, is likely an alternative rendering of the same author's name.
