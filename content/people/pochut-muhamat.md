The youngest of three brothers of King Poteu Ue' (Alaedin Juhan Shah) and central hero of the 18th-century epic Hikayat Pochut Muhamat. Outraged by the existence of two kings in one country, he gathered allies in Pidie including the reluctant Pangulee Beunaroe and conducted a brilliant campaign against the pretender Poteu Jeumaloy, including the siege of Gampong Jawa. He showed magnanimity in sparing Jeumaloy's life and rewarded his allies generously, receiving half the port dues and a royal marriage for his service.
