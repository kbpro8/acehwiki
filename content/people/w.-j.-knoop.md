**W. J. Knoop** was a lieutenant general of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]], military historian, and member of the Dutch States-General, who became the recipient and addressee of the anonymous [[1875]] polemical pamphlet [[Open Letter by Brutus to General Knoop]].

## Role in the Aceh Controversies

As a leading military intellectual in The Hague, General [[W. J. Knoop]] was addressed by the anonymous pamphleteer "Brutus," who used open letters to Knoop to attack General [[J. van Swieten]]'s cautious generalship during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]]. 

In *Over de eerste expeditie tegen Atjeh* ([[1875]]), General [[G. G. Booms]] noted that the editorial invitation in *De Gids* to review "Brutus's Open Letter to General Knoop" provided the direct impetus for Booms to publish his own defense of military commanders in the [[First Aceh Expedition]].
