**W. Steffens** was a **[[lieutenant ter zee|lieutenant ter zee]]** who, together with **[[B. van der Vugt|B. van der Vugt]]**, breached the fortifications of **[[Tabanio]]** on **[[27 August]] [[1859]]**. For this action he was appointed **[[Militaire Willemsorde|knight of the Military Order of William]]** by **[[Royal Decree 18 February 1861 No. 82]]**.

## Action at Tabanio

The assault on Tabanio was a decisive moment in the **[[Second Boni Expedition 1860]]**, leading to the capture of the enemy position.
