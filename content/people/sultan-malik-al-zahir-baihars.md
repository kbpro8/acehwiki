**Sultan Malik al‑Zahir Baihars** was the fourth ruler of the Sultanate of [[Acheh]] in the early 17th century, noted in the source as the “fourth Mamläken” (Mamluk‑styled) sultan who counted the renowned wezier [[Tādj al‑Dīn b. Hinna]] among his confidants. His reign coincided with the flourishing of the *Burda* poem’s reputation in Aceh, as the sultan’s court received copies of the poem and the water‑soaked cloth from the Ottoman custodians of the Prophet’s cloak.

## Patronage of Religious Literature
The sultan’s court is recorded as having a “ribāt” (monastic house) built in Cairo for the collection of relics, reflecting the close ties between Aceh and the wider Islamic world. The popularity of al‑Būsīrī’s *Burda* among Acehnese elites was enhanced by the sultan’s endorsement.
