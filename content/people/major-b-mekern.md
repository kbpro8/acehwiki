**Major B. Mekern** commanded the central column of the Dutch expeditionary force in Aceh during the winter of [[1875]]–[[1876]]. He directed the march from [[Bockit Daroe]] toward the [[Atjeh-rivier]] and oversaw engagements at [[Daroe]] and [[Kajoe‑leh]].

### Leadership
Mekern’s column included marine units, infantry companies, and artillery sections. On [[30 January]] [[1876]] his troops were forced to cross a swampy sawah, suffering casualties from both enemy fire and the difficult terrain.

### Strategic Decisions
After encountering the Atjeh River on [[5 February]] [[1876]], Mekern halted further advance, ordering a retreat to the north to avoid further exposure to enemy fire.
