**Tengku Mohamad Hassan** was an Indonesian parliament member and lawyer (*mr.*) who, in May [[1952]], accompanied the Minister of Trade and Industry, Mr. [[Sujono Hadimoto]], on an inspection tour of the oil enterprises in [[Atjeh|Atjeh]] on Sumatra. The delegation included the Minister of Information, [[Arnold Mononutu]], senior officials of the Ministry of Trade and Industry — Ir. [[Darmawan Mangunkusumo]] and [[Sunu Sumosastro]] — and Tengku Mohamad Hassan as the parliamentary representative.
