**Teungkoe Ma'jid** was a Brother of Ma'Anim; fourth Teungkoe Tirò mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Became Teungkoe Tirò after Ma'Anim's death (1896).

## Role

Brother of Ma'Anim; fourth [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] Tirò

## Account from Jongejans

Became [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] Tirò after Ma'Anim's death ([[1896]]). In February [[1910]] Lt. [[Schmidt]] set out to capture him. Twice engagements occurred with significant followers killed but Ma'jid escaped. The second time his young son fell into Dutch hands; the child felt at home and babbled information. In [[September]] [[1910]] Teungkoe Ma'jid was tracked down and killed.

## Significance

Teungkoe Ma'jid appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
