**Pö Rachman** was a rich Klingalese (Chulia) trader who in 1185 A.H. / [[1771]] A.D. went to [[Koeta Radja|Koeta Radja]] with sixteen notables and received a *sarakata* (royal charter) from Sultan Mahmoed Sjah (reigned [[1760]]–[[1781]]), designating him **Keudjroeën Tjhi** and tax collector. This *sarakata* established the **[[Kawaj XVI|Kawaj XVI]]** federation of sixteen dignitaries in the [[Meulaböh|Meulaböh]] region.

## The sarakata and Kawaj XVI

The *sarakata* given to Pö Rachman by Sultan Mahmoed Sjah designated him as Keudjroeën Tjhi (a title of old Javanese origin, *Këdjoeroean*) and made him the principal tax collector in the Meulaböh region. The sixteen dignitaries who accompanied him or were designated in the sarakata formed the **Kawaj XVI** federation — a council of sixteen that governed the Meulaböh region under the leadership of the Keudjroeën Seunagan.

The Kawaj XVI included: Keudjroeën Seunagan (chief), Keudjroeën Toenöng Seunagan, Pangoelèë Sidé Seunagan, and others. The federation was renamed from "Meulaböh" to "Kawaj XVI" by Gouvernementsbesluit [[6 June]] [[1911]] N°. 11.

## Klingalese origins

Pö Rachman's Klingalese (Chulia, from the Coromandel coast of India) origin reflects the important role that Klingalese traders played in Atjèhnese commerce. The Klingalese were among the principal *opkoopers* (buyers-up) of Atjèhnese products, alongside Chinezen and Arabieren, and their network extended to [[Pinang|Pinang]] and [[Singapore|Singapore]]. The title "Tjhi" ("oldest" or "grandfather") was used in Meulaböh for the Keudjroeën, reflecting the assimilation of Klingalese notables into the Atjèhnese administrative structure.
