**W. H. Loeb** was a Dutch non-commissioned officer and Sergeant Brigade Commander in the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]].

## Military Career and Service in Aceh

Born in [[1881]], Loeb enlisted in [[1899]] and served in campaigns across Jambi ([[1901]]–[[1904]]), Central Sumatra ([[1903]]–[[1907]]), and Aceh ([[1906]]–[[1910]], [[1911]]–[[1914]]). On [[23 February]] [[1917]], he was awarded the Knight 4th Class Military William Order (*M.W.O.*) for extraordinary combat leadership.
