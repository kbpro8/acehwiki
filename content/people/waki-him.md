**Waki Him** was the Acehnese who committed "an old-fashioned *[[klewang]]* charge" in [[Seunagan]] in March [[1909]].

The attack cost the Dutch eleven wounded, "but which for Waki Him and three of his men brought the end as a *[[sjahid]]* (martyr)."
