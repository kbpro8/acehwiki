**Sultan Ala ad-dïn Mahmoed Sjah** (reigned [[1870]]–[[1874]]) was the **last ruling Sultan of [[Aceh|Atjèh]]**. His single seal is on the spine of [[J. Kreemer|Kreemer]]'s work. He died without children when the [[Kraton Atjeh|kraton]] fell on [[24 January]] [[1874]].

## The last Sultan

Ala ad-dïn Mahmoed Sjah succeeded his father [[Sultan Ibrahim Mansur Shah|Mantsoer Sjah]] ([[1838]]–[[1870]]) as Sultan in [[1870]]. His reign coincided with the diplomatic crisis that led to the [[Aceh War|Atjèh-oorlog]] in [[1873]]. He died without children when the kraton fell to Dutch forces under [[Generaal van Swieten|generaal Van Swieten]] on [[24 January]] [[1874]].

## Family connections

The prominent woman **Pö Tjoet Meurah** (Tjout Nja' Meurah) first married Sultan Mahmoed Sjah, then Toeankoe Mahmoed (brother of Toeankoe Asem). Pö Tjoet Meurah was the daughter of Teukoe Pakèh Dalam (former Oelèëbalang of XII Moekim's Pidië), and her aunt had married Sultan [[Sultan Ibrahim Mansur Shah|Mantsoer Sjah]].

## The end of the sultanate

After the kraton fell, the pretender Sultan [[Tuanku Muhamat Dawot|Moehamat Dawöt]] fled to [[Keumala|Keumala]] in Pidië and continued to claim the sultanate. His daughter **Teungkoe Djam** had married the pretender Sultan. The sultanate was effectively ended when Moehamat Dawöt submitted in [[1903]] and was banished in [[1907]].
