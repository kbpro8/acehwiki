**Hotz** was a Source/author cited mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Cited (in parentheses) on the Raad van Achten in Penang and on 'Oemar's doodentocht' and on Gajo contributions to the prang sabil and the colonisation of the interior.

## Role

Source/author cited

## Account from Jongejans

Cited (in parentheses) on the [[Council of Eight|Raad van Achten]] in [[Penang]] and on 'Oemar's doodentocht' and on [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]] contributions to the [[Prang sabi|prang sabil]] and the colonisation of the interior.

## Significance

Hotz appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
