---

title: Teungku Muhamat Amin
aliases: ["Teungku di Tiro", "Teungku Chi' di Tiro"]
tags: [person, scholar]
---

The foremost member of an old family of pandits at Tiro, and Teungku di Tiro par excellence until his death in [[1886]]. He was the right-hand man of the energetic Cheh Saman, who succeeded him when his own son proved too young to take his place. He was a central figure in the scholarly and spiritual life of Tiro.
