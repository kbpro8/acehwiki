**W. Spoon** — properly Ir. W. Spoon, an engineer of the [[Koninklijke Vereeniging Koloniaal Instituut]]'s [[Afdeeling Handelsmuseum]] — was the author of the [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|1930 pamphlet on Atjeh-turpentine]] extracted from *De Indische Mercuur* of [[19 November]] [[1930]] and published as No. 57 of the *Berichten van de Afdeeling Handelsmuseum*. As the principal contemporary monographer of the [[Baleq]] enterprise — the colonial government's turpentine and resin factory in the [[Gajo-landen|Gajo-Landen]]-[[Atjeh]] border region — Spoon is the source of the published production figures, chemical analyses, and European market evaluations that document the early factory-scale production of Indonesian turpentine.

## The Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet

The [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet]] is Spoon's principal publication on the subject of Atjeh turpentine. It is organised into four sections — production, chemical composition, testing against the Pharmacopoeia and the draft *Normaalblad V 597*, and practical evaluations from the Dutch varnish and paint industry — and it draws on three samples of factory-scale Atjeh-turpentine: *H.M. no. 1504* (received December [[1929]] directly from [[Baleq]]), *H.M. no. 1553* (drawn from a January [[1930]] Amsterdam shipment by the [[Nederlandsche Handel-Maatschappij]]) and *H.M. no. 1607* (an original tin from the April [[1930]] shipment per m.s. *Poelau Roebiah*).

The pamphlet concludes that, while the technical properties of the new Baleq product had markedly improved relative to the trial samples of [[1924]] — principally through the fourfold reduction in carene content from 15% to about 4% — the residual difference in odour from American and French turpentines, combined with the very small price differential (about f 22 per 50 kg), left most Dutch varnish and paint manufacturers unwilling to switch. The general verdict is summed up in the closing sentence of the pamphlet: *Vastgesteld is intusschen, dat de technische eigenschappen van het tegenwoordige Baleq-product een ruimere toepassing in Europa niet meer in den weg behoeven te staan* — *It has, however, been established that the technical properties of the present Baleq product no longer stand in the way of a wider application in Europe*.

## Other publications

The list of *Berichten van de Afdeeling Handelsmuseum* preserved at the back of the [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet]] records that Spoon was a prolific contributor to the series. His publications include *Bericht* No. 29 (*Eenige mededeelingen over het conserveeren van versche vruchten met rubbermelksap*, [[1927]]), No. 32 (*Eenige opmerkingen over exploitatie van Euphorbia's in Zuid-Afrika*, [[1927]]), No. 33 (*Iets over de samenstelling van Balata-melk uit Suriname*, [[1927]]), No. 34 (*Verdere opmerkingen over exploitatie van Euphorbia's in Zuid-Afrika*, [[1928]]), No. 35 (*Invoer van Sawo Manila te Amsterdam*, [[1928]]), No. 37 (*Invoer van Doerian-vruchten in Nederland*, [[1928]]), No. 38 (*Verslag van in 1927 genomen proeven tot invoer van Oost-Indische ananas in Nederland*, [[1928]]), No. 39 (*Verdere gegevens over den invoer van Sawo Manila te Amsterdam*, [[1928]]), No. 42 (*Eerste beoordeelingen van sucade-vruchten uit Suriname*, [[1929]]), No. 43 (*Enkele waarnemingen over het conserveeren van versche Indische vruchten met latex*, [[1929]]), No. 45 (*Beschrijving van enkele verzendingsproeven met manggistans van Java*, [[1929]]), No. 46 (*Barnes-kamfer, enkele bijzonderheden naar aanleiding van een nieuwe aanwinst*, [[1929]]), No. 47 (*Versche Ananas uit Suriname*, [[1929]]), No. 48 (*Enkele bijzonderheden over de koeling van proefpartijen Indisch fruit*, [[1929]]), No. 49 (*Een en ander over de nieuwe vezelsoort "Artificial Cotton" of "Kunstkatoen"*, [[1929]]), No. 50 (*Proeven over de verscheping in koelkamer van een viertal soorten mangga van Java*, [[1930]]), No. 51 (*Enkele bijzonderheden verbonden aan den invoer der Oost-Indische pompelmoes of "djerook besar" in Nederland*, [[1930]]), No. 52 (*Enkele waarnemingen over de conserveering van versche Indische vruchten door middel van lakachtige omhulsels*, [[1930]]), No. 55 (*Een proef over de conserveering van klamboe-goed, ten behoeve van de tabakscultuur en andere tropische culturen*, [[1930]]) and No. 56 (*Proeven over de verscheping van enkele oliehoudende tropische grondstoffen in geperste balen*, [[1930]]).

The range of Spoon's publications — from Atjeh-turpentine to Surinamese balata-milk, from *sawo manila* to *doerian*, from *kunstkatoen* to *barnes-kamfer* — illustrates the breadth of the Afdeeling Handelsmuseum's remit: the systematic evaluation and promotion of colonial commodities on behalf of the metropolitan Dutch industry.

## Significance

Spoor's significance in the historiography of colonial Aceh is as the principal contemporary monographer of the [[Baleq]] enterprise — the only detailed published account of the early factory-scale production of Indonesian turpentine. His pamphlet is the principal source for the production figures, chemical analyses, and European market evaluations that document the early history of the [[Baleq]] enterprise, and it is the document of a particular moment in Dutch colonial science: the moment at which the Koloniaal Instituut's Afdeeling Handelsmuseum was systematically evaluating the commercial prospects of colonial commodities in the metropolitan market.
