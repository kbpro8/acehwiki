**Muhammad Jilani Hamid** was the paternal uncle of [[Nuruddin ar-Raniri]] who first brought the family's scholarly contacts to [[Acheh]], arriving between [[1580]] and [[1583]].

He intended to teach logic, rhetoric, dogmatics and ethics, but found "little interest" — great crowds gathered only for teachers of mysticism. He therefore sailed to [[Mecca]], returned some years later with a licence to teach mystical theology, and then had great success.

Voorhoeve cites this episode from Niemann's *Bloemlezing* to explain why Nuruddin came to Acheh already prepared in both Malay and Sufi initiation.
