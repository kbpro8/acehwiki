**Generaal J. H. R. Köhler** was a General, leader of the first Aceh expedition mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Led the first Aceh expedition.

## Role

General, leader of the [[First Aceh Expedition|first Aceh expedition]]

## Account from Jongejans

Led the [[First Aceh Expedition|first Aceh expedition]]. The great [[meuseugit]] was conquered but [[Generaal Köhler|General Köhler]] was killed. Troops disembarked at [[Batavia]] on [[10 May]] [[1873]].

## Significance

Generaal J. H. R. Köhler appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
