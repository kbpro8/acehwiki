**A. Wee Westenberg** was a Dutch marine who received the [[Militaire Willemsorde|Military William Order]] 4th class for his conduct during the 1824 expedition against the sultanates of [[Tanette]] and [[Soepa]].

## Award

His name appears in the list of marines honored for the successful capture of the main town of Tanette and the subsequent landing on Soepa.
