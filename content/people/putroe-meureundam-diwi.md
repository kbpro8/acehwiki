---
title: Putroe Meureundam Diwi
tags: [tokoh, karya-sastra]
---

A princess of Lho' Sinibong on the Jambo Aye river, daughter of Raja Angkasa, hidden by her father after the geureuda (garuda-griffin) devastated her kingdom. Malem Diwa slew the geureuda and married her; she was later carried off by the Raja Jawa and rescued after a great war.
