**Colonel M. F. Meyer** commanded the Dutch expeditionary force that operated in **Atjeh** from [[26 December]] [[1875]] to [[4 September]] [[1876]]. His campaign involved the capture of **Mesigit**, the occupation of **Pakan Badak**, and the arduous march through the **Blang Kala** pass (see [[page 137]]).

## Campaign Overview

Meyer’s forces advanced from **Djempit** towards **Kota Radja**, establishing bivouacs at **Djempit**, **Pakan Badak**, **Boekit Seboen**, and other locations. He oversaw the construction of **bentings** and coordinated with **General Pel** and other subordinate officers.

## Significance

Meyer’s expedition is a key episode of the **Aceh War**, demonstrating the Dutch shift to systematic territorial penetration and fortified base‑building in the highlands.
