---
title: Putroe Bungsu
aliases: [the Sky Princess]
tags: [person, literary-work]
---

The youngest daughter of Raja Din, ruler of the aerial kingdom, and the first princess won by Malem Diwa, who stole her flying garment while transformed into a fish to prevent her returning to the sky. They married and settled near Piadah, where their son Ahmat was born; after Ahmat's misbehavior caused a rupture, she flew back to the aerial realm until Malem Diwa rescued her from a forced marriage to the Raja Muda.
