**George de Hertoge, Heer van Valkenburgh** was a Dutch colonel of the **[[Royal Netherlands Marine Corps|Mariniers]]** who, in September 1674, led two regiments of infantry and two regiments of dragoons to **[[Bonn]]**, forcing its surrender after a brief siege (see [[278]]).  His daring assault earned him praise in contemporary Dutch chronicles and a promotion to **bevelhebber der stad** (city commander).

## Connection to Aceh
After his service in the European theater, de Hertoge was appointed to the **Dutch East India Company**’s overseas forces, where he participated in the defense of Dutch trading posts on **[[Aceh]]** against local resistance during the early 1680s.  His experience in siege warfare was considered valuable for the Dutch colonial administration in the **East Indies**.
