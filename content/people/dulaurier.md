**Dulaurier** was a nineteenth‑century French orientalist known for his edition of the [[Hikayat Raja-raja Pasai]], which he published with a French translation and notes. His work made the Pasai chronicle accessible to European scholars and contributed to the early study of Malay historiography.

## Editorial Work
Dulaurier’s edition of the Hikayat Raja-raja Pasai appears in the source text as the reference for passages cited on pages 59, 60, 64, 70, 78, 79, and 82 of the original manuscript. His editorial approach involved presenting the Malay text alongside a literal translation, facilitating comparative linguistic and historical analysis.

## Legacy
Although Dulaurier’s edition is now superseded by later critical editions, it remains an important milestone in the dissemination of Malay literary texts to Western academia. His name is frequently cited in bibliographies of the Hikayat and in discussions of early Malay manuscript studies.
