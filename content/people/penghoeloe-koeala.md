**Penghoeloe Koeala** was een zekeren Penghoeloe Koeala, een [[Gajòs|Gajò]] uit [[Leuboe Sigeunab]], die ook alweer stood in an odour of sanctity, who set on foot the religious movement at [[Serbödjadi]] in December [[1917]].

The *gezaghebber* of [[Lököp]] went with the chiefs and a *bevolkingspatrouille* to the *[[gampong]]* [[Semboeang]], which the Penghoeloe had worked upon, but was killed in an encounter with the unwilling, five men of his patrol being wounded. The Penghoeloe then organised a three-day religious feast and associated himself with the guardian of a holy grave and the guardian's daughter; the convinced withdrew; the patrols of the *[[Kompeuni]]* came; four fell, among them the guardian of the holy grave; the Penghoeloe was taken prisoner, "and then it was over."
