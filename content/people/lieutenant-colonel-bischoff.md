**Bischoff, Lieutenant‑colonel** was a Dutch army officer who commanded a column of 800 men, including a rear guard of 50 [[Dutch Marine Corps|mariniers]], during the [[17 June]] [[1821]] phase of the second Dutch expedition against [[Palembang]]. His forces attempted a rear attack on the enemy fortifications at the Peladjoe but were repulsed (page 94).

## The 1821 Palembang Expedition

Bischoff’s column formed part of the larger force under [[General‑majoor De Kock]] and [[Captain Lewe van Aduard]]. The operation faced numerous logistical difficulties, including obstructed waterways and strong enemy batteries.
