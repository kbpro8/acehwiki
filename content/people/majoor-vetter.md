**Major Vetter** was a Dutch *majoor* of the [[Indische Leger|Indische leger]] who commanded the column that occupied [[Mibouw]] south of [[Kota Radja]] on [[26 December]] [[1875]], the opening move of [[Generaal Pel|generaal Pel]]'s great [[Pel's offensive against the VI, IV and IX Moekims (1875–1876)|three-colonne offensive against the VI, IV and IX Moekims]]. In [[G. F. W. Borel|Borel]]'s narrative he is the officer whose *bezetting* of Mibouw set in motion the chain of conquests that would carry the Dutch line from the [[Kroeng Raba]] on the west coast to [[Kwala Gigieng]] on the north-east in less than two months.

## The Launch of the New Offensive

After his return to Atjeh on [[9 November]] [[1875]] from his [[Pel's Buitenzorg planning session (1875)|Buitenzorg planning session]], Pel had repulsed the [[Night attack on Longbatta-Mesigit (22 November 1875)|night attack on Longbatta-Mesigit]] ([[22 November]]) and the enemy attack along the entire *ooster-linie* ([[26 November]]), and had received reinforcements — the [[Bataljon Barissans van Madura|bataljon Barissans]] on [[10 December]] and the 9th battalion on [[21 December]] [[1875]]. With these in hand, and with the [[Gouverneur-Generaal van Lansberge|gouverneur-generaal van Lansberge]]'s endorsement of his plan to "den oorlog met Atjeh op de meest doortastende wijze te voeren," Pel launched on [[26 December]] [[1875]] the great *drie-colonne* offensive. Three *colonnes* in *verbond* moved against the VI, IV and IX Moekims. Vetter *bezette* Mibouw ten zuiden van Kota Radja. [[Majoor F. J. E. Mekern|Majoor F. J. E. Mekern]] took the stelling between Mibouw and the [[Kroeng Daroe]] but was *doodelijk gewond* the same day. [[Luitenant-kolonel Engel|Lt.-kol. Engel]] advanced along the right bank of the Kroeng Daroe.

## Outcome and Significance

Vetter's *bezetting* of Mibouw opened the way for the fall of [[Ketapang Doewa]] on [[27 December]] [[1875]] — "andermaal en nu voorgoed" — and the [[Conquest of Djempit and Pakan Badak (28 December 1875)|conquest of Djempit and Pakan Badak]] on [[28 December]], when Engel *bezette* Pakan Badak and the kampongs Lamtermin and Bital were *ontruimd* by the enemy, as were [[Toekoe Nanta]]'s *geduchte versterkingen* west of [[Sinangri]]. "Nergends hadden de Atjehneezen ernstigen tegenstand geboden" — proof again, in Borel's reading, that they could not withstand a well-planned combined-*colonne* attack. Vetter's column was the eastern pivot of the operation; from Mibouw the line of conquest rolled south-westward through the VI Moekims in January [[1876]].
