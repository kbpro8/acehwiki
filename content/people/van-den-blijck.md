**Van den Blijck** was an Eerste-Luitenant der Infanterie of the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger]] who, in [[November 1874|November]] [[1874]], commanded the *Compagnie Mineurs en Sappeurs* at [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] during the middle phase of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. He was an infantry officer temporarily detached to the engineers for want of trained engineer officers, and was the immediate commander of [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede-Luitenant C. J. Snijders]] in the first weeks after the latter's arrival in [[Acheh|Atjeh]] on [[8 November]] [[8 November]] [[1874]].

## Command of the Mineurs Compagnie at Kota Radja

The *Compagnie Mineurs en Sappeurs* stationed at [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] in late [[1874]] was, like most of the engineer establishment in Atjeh, commanded by infantry officers on temporary detachment, the [[Korps Ingenieurs, Mineurs en Sappeurs|Korps Ingenieurs, Mineurs en Sappeurs]] not having enough trained officers to spare. Van den Blijck commanded the company; his second-in-command was the Eerste-Luitenant der Infanterie Bartelds. When [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] arrived at [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] from [[Oleh-leh|Olehleh]] on [[8 November]] [[8 November]] [[1874]] and reported to [[W. J. C. Leers|Majoor der Genie W. J. C. Leers]], Leers assigned him to the company, where Van den Blijck and Bartelds "ontvingen hun nieuwen collega hartelijk en bleken ook in den vervolge uitstekende kameraden." The two infantry officers formed a lasting rapport with Snijders, who would otherwise have served his first months in Atjeh without senior mentorship from his own arm.
