---

title: Noh
aliases: [Noah]
tags: [person, religious-figure]
---

Referenced in the Hikayat asay pade in connection with the rainbow (beuneung raja thnoh), explained as a token of storm, rain, and prosperity, with the writer warning against a pagan conception of the phenomenon prevalent among the ancient Arabs.

## See Also

- [[Adam and Hawa]]

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
