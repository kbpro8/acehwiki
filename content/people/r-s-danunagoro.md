**Ir. R. S. Danunagoro** was an Indonesian engineer and civil servant who, in February [[1950]], was a member of the official delegation that accompanied the acting governor of North Sumatra, [[Sarimin Reksodihardjo]], on an inspection visit to [[Atjeh|Atjeh]]. He was also a member of the governing board (*Dewan Pimpinan*) of the Jajasan Universitas Sumatera Utara, the foundation established to fund the proposed university at Medan.
