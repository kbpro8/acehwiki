**C. Schmid** (killed 10 July 1933) was a Dutch colonial army captain mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Last (at time of writing) victim of a typical Acehnese aanslag (attack) at Lho'Soekon — stabbed by a passing Atjeher on 10 July 1933.

## Role

Dutch colonial army captain

## Dates

killed [[10 July]] [[1933]]

## Account from Jongejans

Last (at time of writing) victim of a typical [[Acehnese]] aanslag (attack) at [[Lho'Soekon]] — stabbed by a passing [[Atjeh (Aceh)|Atjeher]] on [[10 July]] [[1933]].

## Significance

C. Schmid appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
