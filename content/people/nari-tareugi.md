---
title: Nari Tareugi
aliases: [the demon of the Meuseugit Raya]
tags: [person, legend]
---

A mysterious mounted warrior evoked in a sadati kisah, who performed tapa (penance in seclusion) in Acheh's early days and has woken again "since the infidel came." Described with white eyes like black bayam-seed and red pupils, wielding a squared iron club no man can resist, he is called "the demon of the Meuseugit Raya."
