**Teungkoe di Mata Ië (Teungkoe Moehamad Chatib)** was a Oelama, fanatic resistance leader of the Keureutòë region mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Born leader, fanatic; had high standing among the people; influence extended beyond Keureutòë deep into the Gajo-land.

## Role

[[Oelama]], fanatic [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] leader of the [[Keureutòë]] region

## Account from Jongejans

Born leader, fanatic; had high standing among the people; influence extended beyond [[Keureutòë]] deep into the [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]]-land. Led attacks on the [[Atjeh-tram]], destruction of telephone network; constantly pursued by patrolling soldiers ([[Hendrikus Colijn|Colijn]], [[Stoop]], [[Sloos]], [[Christoffel]], [[Behrens]], [[Dompselaar]]). Always managed to slip through the net. In [[1908]] left [[Keureutòë]] for good and withdrew to schuilplaats between [[Samarkilang]] and [[Serbödjadi]]. Lost his weaponsmith, his diviner, his brother-in-law ([[Panglima|panglima]]) and his 17-year-old son and his [[krani]]. Personally led a [[Bivak|bivak]] sneak attack killing one [[Marechaussée|marechaussée]] and lightly wounding Captain v.d. Vlerk. [[March]] [[1913]]: encountered Lt. [[Behrens]] patrol, wounded in leg but escaped, leaving turban and rosary. Long silence; rumour of his death. About 4 years later a patrol under Javanese sergeant [[Toegimin]] hit a [[Ladang|ladang]] hut, killing two Acehnese. Body identified by [[Samarkilang]] population as [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] di [[Mata Ië]] (by foot rings and wounded leg). Last 7 followers became the 'Samarkilangbende'; one killed, one surrendered to [[J. Paris|Captain Paris]] in [[1923]], leader arrested in [[1937]] and died shortly after in [[Langsar|Langsa]].

## Significance

Teungkoe di Mata Ië (Teungkoe Moehamad Chatib) appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
