---
title: Ra'na Jamin
tags: [tokoh, karya-sastra]
---

Daughter of Sultan Ahli of Keureutoe, who fell in love with Ali Juhari (Eseukanda Ali) but was betrothed to another. After escaping with him on her wedding day, she disguised herself as a man, became Raja Muda of Tahtanun, and won a princess in a horse race before revealing her true sex.
