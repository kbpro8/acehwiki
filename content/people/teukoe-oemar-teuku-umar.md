**Teukoe Oemar (Teuku Umar)** (active 1882-1899; killed near Meulaboh 1899) was a Westkust (West Coast) head, oelèëbalang of Leupoeëng, resistance leader, auxiliary commander for the Dutch, defector mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Descended from a chief lineage (hoofdengeslacht) of the Westkust.

## Role

[[Westkust]] ([[Seunagan|West Coast]]) head, [[oelèëbalang]] of [[Leupoeëng]], [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] leader, auxiliary commander for the Dutch, defector

## Dates

active [[1882]]-[[1899]]; killed near [[Malaboeh|Meulaboh]] [[1899]]

## Account from Jongejans

Descended from a chief lineage (hoofdengeslacht) of the [[Westkust]]. Repeatedly invaded [[Groot-Atjeh]] from the [[Leupoeëng]] area in [[1882]]-[[1884]]. Made several false submissions to the Dutch; in [[1884]] tricked Governor [[Laging Tobias]], seized weapons and ammunition, hijacked the ship Hok Canton in the [[Rigaih-baai]] (taking the captain, his wife and the second engineer captive, releasing them for a ransom of 25000 rijksdaalders/Spanish matten). His submission was accepted in [[September]] [[1893]] after advice from [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]]. From [[September]] to [[December]] [[1893]] helped clear [[Groot-Atjeh]] of [[Kwaadwilligen|kwaadwilligen]]. Won the trust of General Deyckerhoff, received titles, allowances, a paid legion, a house, ~900 rifles and money. Appointed [[oelèëbalang]] of [[Leupoeëng]] in [[January]] [[1896]]. In [[March]] [[1896]] defected to the [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] with many followers. Fought another 4 years until killed near [[Malaboeh|Meulaboh]] (at [[Oedjoeng Kala]]) while attempting to attack and burn that kota. An obelisk [[Marks|marks]] the spot where he fell; his body was carried off by followers; his grave was later discovered by Captain [[Schmidt]]. Author poses whether he was adventurer, fanatical Muslim [[Kafir|kafir]]-hater, or faith-hero.

## Significance

Teukoe Oemar (Teuku Umar) appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
