**Toekoe Hoessin Longbattah** was an Achehnese chief associated with [[Longbatta]] who served as a Dutch and allied auxiliary in [[Groot-Atjeh]] during the period of the alliance with [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Oemar]], guiding the Dutch column to [[Biloel]] in [[May]] [[1894]] and leading the storm of [[Senelop]] alongside Oemar in [[March]] [[1894]].

## Guide to Biloel

Hooijer recorded that the post of [[Biloel]] was erected in the night of [[20 May]] to [[21 May]] [[1894]] at the request of [[Toekoe Nja Mohammad]], head of the [[IX Moekims]], and that he and Toekoe Hoessin Longbattah — *wiens moeder daar woonde*, "whose mother lived there" — showed the Dutch column the way to the site.

## The storm of Senelop

At [[Senelop]] on [[12 March]] [[1894]], where the sister of the late [[Habib Samalanga]] commanded with fifteen other women who had devoted themselves to death, the fighting was severe. Only when [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]] placed himself at the head of his troops with his sunshade in his hand and ran in upon the fortification together with Toekoe Hoessin Longbattah — who waved a handkerchief as he came on — did the attack succeed. Dutch troops occupied the position three days later.
