**Lieutenant J. H. van Wely** was a Dutch marine officer who designed the **Adams‑Francotte** revolver conversion used by the marine landing division during the Acehnese expeditions of the late 1860s. His invention allowed the marine infantry to employ rear‑loading revolvers (caliber 11.2 mm) until they were replaced in [[1890]].

## Connection to Aceh

The upgraded firearms were deployed in the 1868 assault on [[Bandjar]] and the subsequent attacks on [[Kali-Anget]] and [[Kampong Djentarek]], enhancing the firepower of the Dutch marines.

## Significance

Van Wely’s technical contribution improved the effectiveness of Dutch marine troops in Aceh, illustrating the interplay between weapon innovation and colonial warfare.
