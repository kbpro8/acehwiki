**Generaal Stemfoort** was a Dutch military officer who, together with [[J. J. van Vliet|Van Vliet]], advised on the disposition of the Dutch forces in [[Gouvernement Atjeh|Atjeh]] during [[1897]]. Stemfoort's proposed reorganisation of the Atjeh garrison — submitted to [[C. A. van der Wijck|Van der Wijck]] for consideration alongside [[J. J. van Vliet|Van Vliet]]'s counter-proposal — formed part of the strategic debate that preceded the expansion of mobile operations beyond the [[Geconcentreerde linie|concentrated line]].

## The proposed reorganisation

Stemfoort's plan for the reorganisation of the Atjeh garrison came down to four points, as recorded in [[C. A. van der Wijck|Van der Wijck]]'s letter to [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck]]:

1. Abolish the posts at [[Pakan Kroeng Tjoet|Pakan Kroeng Tjoet]], [[Boekit Karang|Boekit Karang]], [[Roempit|Roempit]], [[Sirón|Sirón]], Lamreng, [[Blang|Blang]], [[Lamsih|Lamsih]], and [[Olehleh-Oost|Olehleh-Oost]], and move [[Kota Pohama|Kota Pohama]] to [[Kroeëng Raja|Kroeëng Raja]].
2. Keep a half battalion at Loknga and a battalion at Tjot Mantjang.
3. Station two battalions at [[Indrapoeri|Indrapoeri]] to secure order there and in the 7 Moekims Baid, and a battalion at [[Selimoen|Selimoen]] to destroy the war party in the upper regions of the [[XXII Moekims|XXII Moekims]].
4. Conduct an expedition to the 7 Moekims Pedir and [[Peukan Baroe|Peukan Baroe]]–[[Peukan Sot|Peukan Sot]].

[[J. J. van Vliet|Van Vliet]] preferred to keep a half battalion at [[Samagani|Samahani]] and one and a half battalions at [[Indrapoeri|Indrapoeri]] until a [[Tramweg aanleg in Atjeh (1897)|tramway]] to Indrapoeri was constructed, from which a later establishment at [[Selimoen|Selimoen]] could be prepared.

## The Lhong expedition

Both Van Vliet and Stemfoort *ordeelden* (agreed) that the [[Lhong expedition (January 1897)|Lhong expedition]] was unavoidable, to inflict a blow on the increasing influence of [[Toekoe Oemar|Teukoe Oemar]] — though [[C. A. van der Wijck|Van der Wijck]] would have preferred to postpone it.
