**Alexander** was the second mate of the steamer *[[Raijah (steamer)|Raijah]]*, killed on the bridge on the night of [[20 July]] [[1893]] during the [[Raijah piracy (20 July 1893)|seizure of the ship]] off the north coast of [[Acheh|Atjeh]].

[[P. H. R. Beuming]] records that after the pirates had killed the helmsman with a *[[Rentjong|rentjong]]*, *daarop snelden de bandieten naar de brug en overvielen den tweeden stuurman, den heer Alexander, die de wacht had; ook hij werd na een kort handgemeen afgemaakt* — the bandits then hurried to the bridge and fell upon the second mate, Mr. Alexander, who had the watch; he too was despatched after a short hand-to-hand struggle. There followed the general massacre in which the captain also died of two stab wounds.
