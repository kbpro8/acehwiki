---

title: Syed Amir Ali
tags: [person, western-scholar]
---

A British-Indian writer on the life of Mohammed and Muslim law who sought to make Islam palatable to a "civilized European public," a strategy Hurgronje criticizes as something "no true son of Islam would agree" to.

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
