**R. G. Doorman** was the author of the [[1939]] article *[[De gevangeneming van den ex-pretendent Sultan van Atjeh, Toeanangkoe Mohammad Dawat (Doorman, 1939)|De gevangeneming van den ex-pretendent Sultan van Atjeh, Toeanangkoe Mohammad Dawat, na 32 jaar ballingschap den 7en Febr. 1939 overleden te Mr.-Cornelis]]* ("The capture of the ex-pretender Sultan of Atjeh, Toeanangkoe Mohammad Dawat, who after 32 years of exile died at Meester-Cornelis on 7 February 1939"), published in *De Indische Gids*, vol. 61, p. 308 *e.v.* The article is recorded as No. 563 in [[H. van Meurs|Van Meurs]]'s *[[Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939 (Van Meurs, 1940)|Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939]]* (The Hague, [[1940]]), in the Sumatra chapter.

## The 1939 article

Doorman's article, published in *De Indische Gids* (The Indies Guide) in [[1939]] — the year of the death of the ex-pretender Sultan [[Toeanangkoe Mohammad Dawat|Toeanangkoe Mohammad Dawat]] — recorded the capture (*gevangeneming*) of the pretender, the circumstances of his thirty-two-year exile, and the fact of his death at [[Mr.-Cornelis|Meester-Cornelis]] (now [[Jatinegara|Jatinegara]], a suburb of [[Batavia|Batavia]]) on [[7 February]] [[1939]]. The article was a prompt obituary notice, published in the same year as the death it records, and it is the source for the date [[7 February]] [[1939]] in the corpus — the terminus ad quem of the colonial-period Atjehnese sultanate. The thirty-two-year duration of the exile, counted back from [[1939]], places the beginning of the exile in [[1907]] — the year of the [[Atjéh-enquête|Atjéh-enquête]] and of the [[1907-1908-aceh-resistance|1907–1908 flare-up of resistance]] under [[G. C. E. van Daalen|van Daalen]]'s governorship — suggesting that the pretender's capture and banishment were part of the Dutch response to the late phase of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]].

## Significance

For AcehWiki, R. G. Doorman is significant as the author of the [[1939]] obituary notice of the last Atjehnese pretender Sultan, [[Toeanangkoe Mohammad Dawat|Toeanangkoe Mohammad Dawat]] — the source for the date [[7 February]] [[1939]] in the corpus, and thus for the terminus ad quem of the colonial-period Atjehnese sultanate.
