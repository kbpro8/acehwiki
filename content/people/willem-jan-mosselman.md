**Willem Jan Mosselman** was a Dutch military officer, Reserve Infantry Lieutenant, and early pioneer military aviator in the Netherlands East Indies.

## Military Career and Service in Aceh

Born in [[1884]], Mosselman enlisted in [[1900]] and served as a Sergeant in the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]] from [[December]] [[1906]] to [[January]] [[1912]]. In [[1912]], he was awarded the Knight 3rd Class Military William Order (*M.W.O.*). He later became one of the first two licensed military pilots in the Dutch East Indies. He authored detailed literary memoirs describing Marechaussee jungle patrols and camp culture in the Barisan mountains.
