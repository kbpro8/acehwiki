**Mangkoe Negoro** was a Javanese auxiliary prince (*pangeran*) whose *hulptroepen* — auxiliary troops raised in the principalities of central [[Java]] — were sent to Atjeh as part of the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873–1874)|second Dutch expedition]] and, together with those of [[Pakoe Alam]], were returned to Java in February [[1874]] as part of [[Jan van Swieten|generaal van Swieten]]'s *afwachtende houding* force reduction.

## The Auxiliaries in Atjeh

The Javanese *hulptroepen* of Mangkoe Negoro and Pakoe Alam arrived in Atjeh in the early weeks of the second expedition as part of the auxiliary force attached to the Dutch *bezettingskorps*. The [[Bataljon Barissans van Madura|Barissans van Madura]] — inlandsche *hulptroepen* from Madura — had arrived separately on [[29 January]] [[1874]] and would remain in Atjeh through the [[Pel's offensive against the VI, IV and IX Moekims (1875–1876)|great offensive of December 1875 – February 1876]] (one bataljon arrived as reinforcement on [[10 December]] [[1875]]). The Javanese auxiliaries of Mangkoe Negoro and Pakoe Alam, by contrast, were sent back to Java in February [[1874]], together with a *veld-batterij*, as part of van Swieten's reduction of the Dutch force after the [[Fall of the Kraton (24 January 1874)|fall of the Kraton]] of [[24 January]] [[1874]] and the [[Death of the Sultan of Atjeh and annexation proclamation (1874)|death of the sultan and annexation proclamation]] of early February. The reduction was part of the broader *afwachtende houding* — "geen vechten meer met den vijand, maar werken met schop en spade."

## The Return to Java and Its Consequences

The return of the Javanese auxiliaries to Java in February [[1874]] was, in [[G. F. W. Borel|Borel]]'s narrative, part of the systematic run-down of the Dutch striking force that began with the return of the vesting-artillerie and the achterlaadkanonnen and culminated in the [[Departure of Jan van Swieten from Atjeh (April 1874)|departure of van Swieten with the hoofdmacht]] on [[26 April]] [[1874]]. The result was the [[Crisis at Kota Radja (May–June 1874)|crisis of May–June 1874]], when the *bezettingskorps* was reduced to a state in which some companies could field fewer than thirty men of an original 125, and the Atjehneezen crept to within 300–400 *pas* of the parapet. The auxiliaries of Mangkoe Negoro and Pakoe Alam were, in Borel's reading, the first reduction; the *hoofdmacht* was the second. The contrast with the [[Bataljon Barissans van Madura|Barissans van Madura]] — retained, reinforced, and used in the great offensive — is sharp.
