**T. Maharadja Mangkoeboemi** was the *zelfbestuurder* (self-governing ruler) of the *[[landschap]]* [[Lho' Seumawè]] on the [[Noordkust van Atjeh|north coast of Atjeh]], and with [[T. Tjhi' Moehammad Djohan Alamsjah]] of [[Peusangan]] the drafter, first signatory and joint executor of the [[Letter of the Acehnese Chiefs to General Swart (26 September 1933)|letter of the Acehnese chiefs]] addressed to [[H. N. A. Swart]] at [[Den Haag]] on [[26 September]] [[1933]], on the occasion of his seventieth birthday on [[12 October]] [[1933]].

## The letter

The letter was signed by thirty-two chiefs and dignitaries of the north coast — the region where the resistance of [[1908]]–[[1912]] had raged most fiercely. It recorded that they had not forgotten "all the former services and benefactions of Your Honour towards us and also for the land of Atjeh, our birthplace, which has become pacified, peaceful and prosperous and received proper education, which forms the pillar on which the culture of a country rests, beginning with the coming to power of His Honour the Governor H. N. A. Swart in Atjeh, from the year [[1908]] to [[1918]]."

They offered as a memento of the day "a smoking set consisting of a cigar case, an ashtray and a match holder," and closed with the wish that God might prolong the years of Swart, his wife and family.

The letter was signed, "in the name of all those mentioned above and as the executors of their commission," by T. Maharadja Mangkoeboemi, *zelfbestuurder* of Lho' Seumawè, and T. Tjhi Moehammad Djohan Alamsjah, *zelfbestuurder* of Peusangan.

His son, [[T. Tjoet Lizamsjah]], was the seventeenth signatory.

## Significance

The letter of [[1933]] — drawn up fifteen years after Swart's departure — was, in [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]]'s presentation, the principal Acehnese testimony to Swart's memory in Atjeh: "They wrote to him, they told him their opinion, they did not forget their *Bapa Swart* (Father Swart)."
