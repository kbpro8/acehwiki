**Tādj al‑Dīn b. Hinna** was a devout wezier (religious scholar) and close confidant of [[Sultan Malik al‑Zahir Baihars]] of the Sultanate of [[Acheh]]. The source records that he was a great admirer of the Prophet and an avid collector of relics, having established a large collection and a personal [[ribāt]] in [[Cairo]]. After hearing al‑Būsīrī’s *Burda*, he vowed to listen to the poem barefoot and with an uncovered head, a practice that later spread among Acehnese religious circles.

## Role in Disseminating the Burda
His enthusiastic endorsement of the *Burda* contributed to its prestige in Aceh, where the poem was regarded as a source of divine favor and protection for the sultanate’s elite.
