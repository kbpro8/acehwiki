**Visser, C. J.** was a Dutch marine lieutenant who was mentioned honorably in the royal decree of [[6 October]] [[1874]] for his service in the Atjeh expeditions of [[1873]] and [[1874]].

## Recognition

Although not awarded a knighthood, his name appeared among the officers distinguished for their actions in Aceh.
