**Accara** was formerly the **captain of Coffy** before offering his services to **[[Kolonel De Salve|Colonel De Salve]]**. Described as a notorious **murderer and arsonist**, he was granted a **volmacht** by **[[Governor Van Hoogenheim|Governor Van Hoogenheim]]** on the condition that he would infiltrate the forests, capture fleeing **Negers**, and bring them back to the Dutch headquarters.

## Activities
Accara regularly delivered groups of **30–40** former rebels to the headquarters, facilitating the Dutch “purge” of the area. His cooperation was seen as a key factor in the relative success of the campaign.
