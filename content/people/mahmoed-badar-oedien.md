**Mahmoed Badar Oedien** was the brother of the Sultan of [[Palembang]] who, after being installed by the Dutch in [[1819]], launched a surprise attack on the Dutch fort in [[June 1819]]. His assault was repelled, prompting a Dutch punitive expedition under [[Schout-bij-nacht Wolterbeek]] (page 93).

## Conflict with the Dutch

The attack demonstrated the volatile loyalties within the sultanate and the broader challenge of Dutch authority in the Aceh‑adjacent region.
