**A. W. K. ter Beek** was a Dutch writer on colonial-military affairs who authored *De vier eerste jaren van den Atjeh-oorlog* (The first four years of the Aceh War), published at The Hague by Gebroeders van Cleef. The work was one of the secondary sources used by [[P. Brooshooft|Brooshooft]] in compiling his Brooshooft (1886) of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh War]] published at Utrecht in [[1886]].

## Work

Ter Beek's book treated the opening phase of the Aceh War from the outbreak of hostilities in [[1873]] through the first four years of campaigning. In Brooshooft's judgement it belonged, together with the pamphlet literature of [[Jan van Swieten|van Swieten]], [[G. M. Verspick|Verspick]], [[J. J. W. E. Versteege|Verstege]] and others, to the category of works that treated "only short periods or fragments of the war," and which therefore could not by themselves give the Dutch public the comprehensive and affordable overview that Brooshooft set out to provide.
