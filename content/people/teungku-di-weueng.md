---
title: Teungku di Weueng
tags: [person, saint]
---

A saint buried on the mountain of that name in the XXII Mukims, regarded as an implacable punisher of thieves, with stories of votive offerings of stolen goods bringing sudden death to the donor.

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
