**Corporal J. Moleman** was a Dutch marine who was awarded the [[Militaire Willemsorde|Military William Order]] 4th class for his bravery during the June‑August [[1824]] operations against the rulers of [[Tanette]] and [[Soepa]].

## Recognition

His decoration was recorded alongside those of Lieutenant P. Colombon and Sergeant W. J. van Ackeren.
