**Kapitein-luitenant Van Hogendorp** was a Dutch naval officer who commanded the landing forces at [[Salangore]] on [[18 June]] [[1784]] after [[Majoor Hamell]] fell ill in [[Malakka]].

## Command of the Landing
Van Hogendorp led a force of 517 sailors and marines together with 305 native troops. Despite heavy fire from enemy batteries, his troops secured the beachhead and captured the higher‑lying battery, enabling the Dutch to consolidate their position.

## Later Service
After the Salangore operation, Van Hogendorp continued to serve in the Dutch East Indies, though his later career is not detailed in the source.
