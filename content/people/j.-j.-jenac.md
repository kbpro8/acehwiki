**J. J. Jenac** was an officer of the *[[Korps Marechaussee te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* in [[Atjeh]] recorded in the *[[Overzicht van Onderscheidingen toegekend aan het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden (1890-1930)|Overzicht van Onderscheidingen]]* of the [[1930]] jubilee number as knight of the *[[Militaire Willemsorde|Militaire Willems-Orde]]* 4th class, awarded in [[1909]].

## Entry in the register

The register lists him under [[1909]] together with *1ste Luit. Mar.* [[H. J. Schmidt]], in the same year in which the sergeant [[F. Ch. Frank]] received the 3rd class.
