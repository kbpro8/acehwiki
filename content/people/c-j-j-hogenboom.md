**C. J. J. Hogenboom** was a Dutch civil servant who served as a member of the College of Delegates (*College van Gedelegeerden*) of the [[Negara Sumatera Timur]] (N.S.T.) and as a senior administrative member (*hoofdbestuurslid*) of the N.I.B.E.G. (the Dutch educational institution in the Indies). He was awarded a royal decoration by Royal Decision (*Koninklijk Besluit*) of 23 May [[1950]].

## Activities

In June [[1950]], Hogenboom delivered a lecture before the J.C.J. world (the Dutch Christian trade union movement) on the then-precarious problem of education for Dutch children in Indonesia. He had been appointed a member of the N.S.T. College of Delegates in December [[1948]], and was also a former chairman of the Dutch Association (*Nederl. Vereniging*) on the East Coast.

On 4 June [[1951]], Hogenboom was appointed government commissioner (*regeringscommissaris*) of the Dutch colony in Brazil — the settlement known as Holambra.
