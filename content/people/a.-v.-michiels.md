**A. V. Michiels** was a celebrated early nineteenth-century general of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] who commanded colonial expeditions in Bali and West Sumatra, whose historical campaigns were frequently cited in comparisons with the Aceh War.

## Comparative Military Historiography

In the [[1876]] study *Atjeh gedurende de tweede expeditie en later*, the author compared General [[J. van Swieten]]'s conduct of the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] with General [[A. V. Michiels]]'s famous Bali expedition of 1849 (such as the capture of Djagaraga). 

The author demonstrated that even renowned historical commanders like [[A. V. Michiels]] had relied upon cautious, deliberate siege operations when confronting entrenched indigenous fortifications, thereby refuting critics who accused [[J. van Swieten]] of unprecedented hesitation at [[Kraton of Aceh]].
