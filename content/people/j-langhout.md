**J. Langhout** was a Dutch journalist who authored the jubilee publication *Vijftig jaren economische staatkunde in Atjeh* ([[1923]]) titled "fifty years of economic policy in Atjeh", commissioned during Swart's afterglow.

## The jubilee book

Langhout's work appeared in [[1923]] when Atjeh was to have its jubilee, fifty years after the first Dutch expedition of [[1873]]. According to [[Dr. R. Broersma]], Langhout had never seen land and people of [[Atjeh]] when he composed the overview from data placed before him. The true responsible was [[H. N. A. Swart]], oud-governor [[1908]]-[[1918]], whose administration period was treated most extensively.

Broersma dismissed the work as not containing land and people essentially, and as unclean sketch of volkshuishouding.
