**Governor Van Hoogenheim** was the Dutch colonial governor who corresponded with **[[Kolonel De Salve|Colonel De Salve]]** regarding the conduct of the anti‑rebellion campaign in the **Wikkie‑kreek** region. In a letter dated **[[28 February]] [[1765]]**, he expressed conditional support for De Salve’s use of former rebels **[[Accara]]** and **[[Gouffarie]]**, while insisting on impartial judicial oversight.

## Administrative Actions
Van Hoogenheim ordered the dispatch of a fiscal and impartial judges from the Netherlands to address the legal vacuum at the **[[Hof van Politie en Justitie]]**.
