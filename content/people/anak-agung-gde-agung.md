**Anak Agung Gde Agung** was the Indonesian minister who, on 14 July [[1950]], established at Medan the Committee for Preparation (*Comité van Voorbereiding*) charged with the orderly liquidation of the [[Negara Sumatera Timur]] (N.S.T.) and its incorporation into the unitary Republic of Indonesia. The committee consisted of [[Sarimin Reksodihardjo]], Mr. [[S. M. Amin]], and Mr. [[M. Joesoef]].

## Role in the N.S.T. dissolution

The committee, under Anak Agung's authority, negotiated the terms of the N.S.T.'s dissolution. The resulting agreement, announced on 5 August [[1950]], stipulated that East Sumatra would become part of the autonomous province of North Sumatra, comprising the pre-war residencies of [[Atjeh]], [[Tapanuli]], and East Sumatra. The committee was granted full authority (*volledige volmacht*) to act on behalf of the central government at Djakarta.
