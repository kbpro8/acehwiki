**Jansma, Marine** was a Dutch marine who fought in the battle for [[Lemboeq]] on [[31 December]] [[1874]]. He served alongside [[Sergeant Notenbos]] and [[Lieutenant Cadet]] in defending the forward breastwork of the newly taken fortifications.

## Participation

Jansma’s presence in the combat report of [[W. Sutherland]] highlights the involvement of ordinary marines in the final phases of the 1874 campaign.
