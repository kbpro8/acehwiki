**H. P. Lucas** was a **[[sergeant der mariniers|sergeant of the marines]]** who received the **[[Eervolle Vermelding|honorable mention]]** by **[[Koninklijk Besluit|Royal Decree]]** dated **[[8 February]] [[1859]] No. 64**. He later died of exhaustion during the **[[Second Boni Expedition 1860]]**.

## Honors

Lucas was recognised for his conduct during the **[[First Boni Expedition 1859]]** and again during the later Boni campaign.
