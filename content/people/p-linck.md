**P. Linck** — also written *J. P. Linck*, *Kapitein Linck* — *Kapitein* and commander of the 4e compagnie of the [[5e Bataljon]], was, in Kruisheer's *ATJEH '96*, one of the most continuously engaged Dutch company commanders of the first semester of [[1896]], the officer whose compagnie stormed key Achehnese *versterkingen* in three of the principal engagements of May and June [[1896]]. Linck's command record includes the [[4 May 1896 expedition to XIII Moekims Toengkoeb|investigation of a bende south of Klat on 4 May 1896]], the [[Excursie to Moekim Lamtengah (21 May 1896)|storming of the versterking Belang on 21 May 1896]], the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|entry into Lam-Pisang on 24 May 1896]] (right after 1e Luit. [[M. D. J. de Jongh|de Jongh]]), the [[17 June 1896 attack on Lam-Goet|attack on Lam-Goet on 17 June 1896]], the [[20 June 1896 four-column machtsvertoon|command of two companies of 5e Bataljon through III Moekims Daroe on 20 June 1896]], and the [[Battle of Kroeng-Kali (23 June 1896)|storm of the Kroeng-Kali benteng on 23 June 1896]] — the culminating Dutch tactical success of the first semester. His compagnie's role in the storm of the Kroeng-Kali *benteng*, with 2e Luit. [[Heyligers]] the first to enter, marks the high point of his command record and one of the most celebrated Dutch infantry assaults of the spring operations.

## May 1896: The Versterking Belang and the Entry into Lam-Pisang

Linck's first major command in *ATJEH '96* is on [[4 May]] [[1896]] during the [[4 May 1896 expedition to XIII Moekims Toengkoeb|second tocht to XIII Moekims Toengkoeb]], when he was "sent to investigate a bende south of [[Klat]]" — a reconnaissance task which placed his compagnie at the forward edge of the Dutch advance into the eastern sector. His most famous May engagement was the [[Excursie to Moekim Lamtengah (21 May 1896)|excursie to Moekim Lamtengah on 21 May 1896]], in which his 4e compagnie stormed the *versterking Belang* — the principal Achehnese fortification in the territory of [[Toekoe Tjoet Lamtengah]]. The excursie, under [[Bisschoff van Heemskerck|Lt.-Col. Bisschoff van Heemskerck]] with 6 brigades *maréchaussée* under [[Jhr. G. J. W. C. H. Graafland|Kapitein Graafland]], two companies of the [[5e Bataljon]] (including Linck's 4e compagnie), a *bergartillerie* section under [[A. Bangert|Kapitein Bangert]], and *genietroepen*, was the Dutch response to the hollow goodwill professions of Lamtengah, whose territory had offered heavy fire to a Dutch column earlier that day. Three days later, on [[24 May]] [[1896]], during the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day offensive]], Linck led his sectie into [[Lam-Pisang]] "right after [[M. D. J. de Jongh|de Jongh]]" — that is, immediately after 1e Luit. [[M. D. J. de Jongh|de Jongh]] of the 5e Bataljon had reached [[Teuku Umar|Umar]]'s *verblijf* at 10:35 AM. The capture of Umar's house at Lam-Pisang was the principal tactical objective of the three-day offensive, and Linck's entry immediately after de Jongh placed his compagnie at the centre of the culminating Dutch tactical success of the May operations.

## June 1896: The Kroeng-Kali Benteng and the End of the First Semester

The June [[1896]] operations consolidated Linck's reputation as one of the most capable Dutch company commanders of the spring. On [[17 June]] [[1896|17 June]] he led the [[17 June 1896 attack on Lam-Goet|attack on Lam-Goet]] during the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|tuchtiging of V Moekims Montassik]], occupying the Lam-Goet *benteng* without resistance and burning it subsequently. On [[20 June]] [[1896|20 June]] he commanded two companies of the [[5e Bataljon]] through the [[III Moekims Daroe]] during the [[20 June 1896 four-column machtsvertoon|four-column machtsvertoon]] — the operation in which [[T. Panglima Bintang]] made his first appearance before the Dutch administration and [[Teuku Umar]] held his *chandoeri* at Lepong — and his passage through the III Moekims Daroe encountered no resistance, confirming the relative stability of that sector after the V Moekims Montassik *tuchtiging*. Linck's culminating command was the [[Battle of Kroeng-Kali (23 June 1896)|storm of the Kroeng-Kali benteng on 23 June 1896]] — the final engagement of the first semester. The Kroeng-Kali *benteng* was a square earthen redoubt with 15 m sides, walls 1.5 m high and 0.75 m thick, with a 4 m-wide *bamboe-doeri pagger* and a small swivel gun; Linck's compagnie stormed the work under fire from the *lilla*, with 2e Luit. [[Heyligers]] the first to enter (with *hoornblazer* [[van Spanje]] and fusilier [[Veeges, No. 31895|Veeges No. 31895]]). 1e Luit. [[P. van der Molen|Van der Molen]] and two European fusiliers were wounded in the assault, and the *benteng* was destroyed. The Kroeng-Kali assault forced the retreat of [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb]] and [[Panglima Nja Makam]] to [[Lam-Tobah]] and effectively closed the first-semester operations.

## Significance

Linck's significance in *ATJEH '96* lies in his embodiment of the *kapitein* whose compagnie provided the storm assault capacity in three of the most important Dutch tactical successes of the first semester of [[1896]] — the [[Excursie to Moekim Lamtengah (21 May 1896)|storming of the versterking Belang on 21 May]], the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|entry into Lam-Pisang on 24 May]], and the [[Battle of Kroeng-Kali (23 June 1896)|storm of the Kroeng-Kali benteng on 23 June]]. His compagnie's role in the Kroeng-Kali assault — with 2e Luit. [[Heyligers]] the first to enter the redoubt — marks the high point of his command record and one of the most celebrated Dutch infantry assaults of the spring operations. His sustained command of the 4e compagnie of the [[5e Bataljon]] through the full operational arc of the first semester confirms his institutional centrality to the Dutch infantry war effort.
