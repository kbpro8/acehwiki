**G. B. Hooyer** was a Dutch military historian and author of *Krijgsgeschiedenis van Nederl.-Indië, 1811-1894* ("War History of Netherlands-India, 1811-1894"), published in three volumes in Batavia in [[1895]], with an atlas in quarto format. The work was offered in [[1902]] by the bookseller Martinus Nijhoff at 15.35 florins in commemoration of the third centenary of the Dutch East India Company.

## Work

Hooyer's comprehensive three-volume war history of the Netherlands Indies covered the entire period from the British interregnum of 1811 to the campaigns of the 1890s, including the [[Aceh War|Atjeh War]]. The accompanying atlas provided cartographic documentation of the various military campaigns discussed in the text. The work was part of a tradition of Dutch colonial military historiography that included the more specialised studies of the Atjeh War by [[E. B. Kielstra]].
