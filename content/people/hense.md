**Hense** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Koeta Radja]]

The town also housed the [[Atjehclub]], in whose reading room hung the portraits of all the Atjeh generals, and the [[Oranjehotel]], whose manager [[Hense]], a former *marechaussée* sub-officer, was murdered on [[12 February]] [[1919]]. In the former military encampment at [[Peuniti]] a native trade school was established.

### From [[1919]]

On [[12 February]] **[[1919]]** [[Hense]], manager of the [[Oranjehotel]] at [[Koeta Radja]], was murdered — the last of the [[Atjeh-moord|Atjeh murders]] listed by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]]. On [[13 March]] [[1919]] [[T. Radja Sabi]], then eighteen, was recognised at [[Lho' Seumawè]] and brought to his uncle [[T. Tjhi' Béntara]], ending nine years of wandering. On [[20 May]] [[1919]] the *[[Nieuwe Rotterdamsche Courant]]* published [[I. Feenstra]]'s account of Swart's departure. A photograph shows Swart as Vice-President of the [[Raad van Nederlandsch-Indië]] in his study at [[Batavia]] in [[1919]].
