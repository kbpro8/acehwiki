**Teuku Nek Purba** (historically written Toekoe Nek Poerba) was a prominent Acehnese chieftain and hulubalang from the [[XX Moekims]] in the [[Aceh Sultanate]].

## Diplomatic Overtures in 1874

Following the [[Capture of the Aceh Kraton]] on [[24 January]] [[1874]] during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]], [[Teuku Nek of Meuraxa]] informed General [[J. van Swieten]] that [[Teuku Nek Purba]], alongside [[Teuku Nanta Setia]], was preparing to submit to the [[Dutch East Indies Government]]. This intelligence was recorded in *Atjeh gedurende de tweede expeditie en later* ([[1876]]) as part of the documentary record justifying Dutch dispatches in early [[1874]].
