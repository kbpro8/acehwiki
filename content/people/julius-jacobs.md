**Dr. Julius Jacobs** was author of *Het familie- en kampongleven op Groot-Atjeh* ([[1894]]), cited by [[Dr. R. Broersma]] for social history of [[Groot-Atjeh]] and for leprosy customs.

## On leprosy in Aceh

Jacobs noted that sufferers in [[Groot-Atjeh]] were inexorably expelled and could go to a specific kampong with sulfur hot springs. Real estate of leprosy sufferer passed to family and spouse considered legally divorced. The lepers' kampong formed small society of unfortunates, maintaining fields, crafts, fisheries as far as able. Disease occurred less frequent in Groot-Atjeh than eastern coast where [[Pidie]] and [[Pasei]] region were worse visited.

He also reported that in [[1893]] no pepper of significance was harvested, illustrating volatility of pepper culture.
