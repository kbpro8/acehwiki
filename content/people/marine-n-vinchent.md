**Marine N. Vinchent** was a second‑class marine of the [[Dutch Marine Corps]] who received the [[Militaire Willemsorde|Military William Order]] 4th class from [[King Willem I]] for his actions during the Padri operations of [[1824]]. He died of his wounds before the decoration could be presented.
