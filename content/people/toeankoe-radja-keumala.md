**Toeankoe Radja Keumala** was a Brother-in-law (zwager) of Panglima Polém; resistance leader mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Gave Polém strong support behind Keureutòë and Geudong.

## Role

Brother-in-law (zwager) of [[Panglima Polém]]; [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] leader

## Account from Jongejans

Gave Polém strong support behind [[Keureutòë]] and [[Geudong]]. [[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]] joined them. Surrendered [[6 September]] [[1903]] with [[Panglima Polém]] at [[Lho'Seumawé|Lho' Seumawé]] to [[Hendrikus Colijn|Colijn]], with 150 followers.

## Significance

Toeankoe Radja Keumala appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
