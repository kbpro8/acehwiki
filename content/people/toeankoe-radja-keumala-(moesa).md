**Toeankoe Radja Keumala** (real name **Moesa**) was the son of **Toeankoe Asém** (Pangéran Asem), the fiercest opponent in the [[Aceh War|Atjèh-oorlog]]. He lived at Keudah near [[Koeta Radja|Koeta Radja]] on ƒ50 monthly *onderstand* (support pension), having submitted to Koeta Radja in [[1903]]. He served as geestelijk adviseur (spiritual adviser) to the governor and was a member of the [[Raad agama|Raad agama]].

## Scholarly profile

Toeankoe Radja Keumala was scholarly in Arabic and mystic studies, and had made the *hajj* (pilgrimage to Mecca) twice. His scholarly credentials made him a valued geestelijk adviseur to the governor, and he was appointed to the [[Raad agama|Raad agama]] established by Stbl. [[1920]] N°. 398.

## Family connections

Toeankoe Radja Keumala was connected to some of the most prominent Atjèhnese families:
- His father was **Toeankoe Asém** (Pangéran Asem), "the fiercest opponent in the Atjèh-oorlog"
- His sister was **Teungkoe Ratna**, wife of [[Panglima Polem|Panglima Pölém]] (sagi-head of the XXII Moekim's)
- His aunt was **Pö Tjoet Meurah** (Tjout Nja' Meurah), a prominent woman raised at the kraton court
- His niece **Teungkoe Djam** (daughter of Pö Tjoet Meurah) married the pretender Sultan [[Tuanku Muhamat Dawot|Moehamat Dawöt]]

## The Raad agama

As a member of the [[Raad agama|Raad agama]], Toeankoe Radja Keumala served alongside [[Teungkoe Tjhèh Ibrahim|Teungkoe Tjhèh Ibrahim]] (hatib of the great mosque at Koeta Radja). The *Raad agama* was not a priesterraad with judicial authority (as on Java) but merely a college of advies (advisory body) to the governor.
