**Lieutenant D. A. van Vuuren** was a marine lieutenant of the [[Royal Netherlands Marine Corps]] who served in the **Marine Landings Division** during the 1868‑1869 campaigns in [[Acheh]]. He participated in the assault on [[Bandjar]] on [[24 October]] [[1868]] and later received an **Eervolle Vermelding** for his conduct.

## Aceh Campaigns

Van Vuuren’s detachment was part of the force that attacked the fortified kampong of [[Kali-Anget]], where he helped coordinate the storming of stone‑filled walls. His actions were noted in the official reports, and he was later honored alongside other officers such as [[Lieutenant P. Spanjaard]].

## Honors

Awarded the **Eervolle Vermelding** for bravery during the expedition; later, his name appears among those recommended for the [[Militaire Willemsorde]] after the 1870 operations.

## Significance

Van Vuuren exemplifies the junior officer cadre that enabled the Dutch marine corps to execute complex amphibious assaults in Aceh’s rugged terrain.
