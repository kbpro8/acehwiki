**Teungkoe di Tjot Tjitjém** was a Tirò follower; panglima with ~400-man army mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. A panglima who had drilled his army of >400 men well; even had a sort of uniform and a deserter from Dutch lines served as bugler.

## Role

Tirò follower; [[Panglima|panglima]] with ~400-man army

## Account from Jongejans

A [[Panglima|panglima]] who had drilled his army of >400 men well; even had a sort of uniform and a deserter from Dutch lines served as bugler. Had the airs of highway pirates. Tram and telephone connections suffered, but their own gampongs also suffered. He remained invisible while his followers defied death. Rumours of horrible mistreatment of faithful prisoners by the [[Kafir|kafir]] suited to temper fear of death. Killed in [[1906]] in a masterly raid under [[Sergeant Götz|sergeant Götz]].

## Significance

Teungkoe di Tjot Tjitjém appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
