---
title: Si Kabayan
aliases: [Si Buta-Tuli]
tags: [person, folklore]
---

The most familiar Sundanese version of the Indonesian Eulenspiegel trickster, whose tomb is pointed out at Pandeglang and elsewhere in Banten. Hurgronje compared some 70 dongengs about him in detail with the Acehnese Si Meuseukin and Pa' Pande.
