---
title: Mr. H.N. Ridley
tags: [person, western-scholar]
---

Director of the Botanic Gardens, Singapore, who provided botanical identifications and notes on several Acehnese medicinal simples in Hurgronje's list of the forty-four herbs, including anise, Piper sarmentosum, and the root of cost (Saussaurea Lappa).
