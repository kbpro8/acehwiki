**Pattiasina** was a retired Ambonese Marechaussee of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen]] — a *Ridder [[Militaire Willemsorde|M.W.O.]]* living at [[Koeta-Radja]] — who was one of the four *slippendragers* (pall-bearers) at the [[Funeral of Dominee Thenu (11 May 1937)|funeral of Dominee Thenu]] on [[11 May]] [[1937]]. The four — [[Salawono]] (3e kl.), Pattiasina, [[Kazen]] and [[Pattipuluhu]] — were all old, retired Marechaussees, all *Ridders M.W.O.* The choice of four M.W.O.-ridders to carry the dominee's bier is the book's most resonant image of the moral economy of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Marechaussee]].

## The slippendragers

"Als slippendragers traden vier oude, gepensioneerde Marechaussees op, alle vier ridders M.W.O.: [[Salawono]] (3e kl.), Pattiasina, [[Kazen]] en [[Pattipuluhu]]." The four retired M.W.O.-ridders living at [[Koeta-Radja]] were chosen to carry the dominee's bier. The choice is the book's most explicit statement of the moral economy of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Marechaussee]]: the men who had been decorated for courage under fire laid their decorations, in effect, at the feet of the man who had blessed them.

## Significance

Pattiasina is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as one of the four retired M.W.O.-ridders who acted as *slippendragers* at the [[Funeral of Dominee Thenu (11 May 1937)|funeral of Dominee Thenu]]. The book's argument is that the choice of four M.W.O.-ridders to carry the dominee's bier was the moral economy of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Marechaussee]] made visible: the men of courage carrying the man of faith.
