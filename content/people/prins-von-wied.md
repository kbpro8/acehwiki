**Prins von Wied** was the ruler of [[Albanië|Albania]] who, at the period following [[1905]], requested Maréchaussée officers from the Netherlands Indies for service in that country — a request to which the former Maréchaussée officer [[W. V. Rhemrev]] at once responded.

## The request for Maréchaussée officers

The [[1930]] Maréchaussée jubilee number records that "toen de Prins Von Wied destijds maréchaussée-officieren vroeg voor Albanië, stelde de heer Rhemrev zich direct beschikbaar" — when the Prince von Wied at that time asked for maréchaussée officers for Albania, Mr. Rhemrev at once made himself available.

Only officers from [[Netherlands|Holland]] were, however, designated. They went to Albania under the command of [[Lodewijk W J K Thomson|Kolonel Thomson]], who later fell.

## Significance

The episode is recorded in the jubilee number as an instance of the international reputation acquired by the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* as a model of counter-insurgency organisation.
