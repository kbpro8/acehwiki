**Van der Pauwert** was a Dutch captain of infantry in [[Atjeh]] in [[1876]], serving in the column of Major [[Majoor B. E. Mekern|B. E. Mekern]] at [[Kajoe-Lē]] when, on [[15 February]] [[1876]], he was killed in the daily fighting of the patrols that tried to keep open the road between the new posts of the south-east line.

## Kajoe-Lē, February 1876

After the [[Atoeh catastrophe (13 February 1876)|Atoeh catastrophe]], Mekern's column had taken its stand at Kajoe-Lē and begun the fortification site, sending daily patrols towards [[Atoeh|Atoë]] and [[Mesigit Pagar Ajer]]. Those patrols were fired upon continually from the kampong edges in which the enemy hid. Meijer lists the toll on that road by date:

- [[15 February]] — Captain of Infantry Van der Pauwert fell;
- [[18 February]] — Second Lieutenant of Infantry [[L. H. Kooy]] was wounded;
- [[27 February]] — Captain of Infantry [[Witewaall van Stoetwege|Jhr. Witewaall van Stoetwege]] received a wound of which he died.

The continuing enemy pressure severed the road altogether, provoking the siege of the column at Kajoe-Lē in the first week of March and the relief operation of [[Storming of Lambaroe (7 March 1876)|7 March]]. The record of Van der Pauwert's fall is from [[Kolonel Meijer|H. F. Meijer]], *[[Atjeh van 26 December 1875 tot 4 September 1876]]* ([[1883]]).
