**Israëls** was a European sergeant of the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Netherlands Indies Army]] who distinguished himself in the [[Patrol of Captain Van Blokland (7 March 1896)|patrol of Captain Van Blokland]] near [[Lam Bada|Anak Baté]] on [[7 March]] [[1896]].

[[P. H. R. Beuming]] records that *niet minder flink gedroeg zich de kranige Europeesch sergeant Israëls, die, ofschoon zelf door meerdere kogels getroffen, het lijk van zijn gesneuvelden collega [[Van Oosten]] uit 's vijands handen trachtte te redden* — no less gallantly bore himself the sergeant Israëls, who, though himself struck by several bullets, tried to save the body of his fallen colleague Van Oosten from the enemy's hands.

*Te vergeefs waren zijn pogingen* — his efforts were in vain: the body remained, with those of two European and one native fusilier, in the enemy's hands.
