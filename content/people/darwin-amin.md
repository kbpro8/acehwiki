**Darwin Amin** was an Indonesian civil servant who, in February [[1950]], was a member of the official delegation that accompanied the acting governor of North Sumatra, [[Sarimin Reksodihardjo]], on an inspection visit to [[Atjeh|Atjeh]]. The delegation, which included some ten civil servants and journalists, also comprised [[R. S. Danunagoro]], [[Abdul Wahab Siregar]], [[Ismail Daulay]], [[Moh. Diri Harahap]], Dr. [[Sahar]], and [[Zainul Basri]].
