**F. W. Stapel** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[G. K. Dijkstra]]

Dijkstra opened his foreword with the words *"Watchful and industrious"* and with a citation of [[F. W. Stapel|Dr. F. W. Stapel]]'s remark in the introduction to *De Gouverneurs-Generaal van Nederlandsch-Indië* that, faithful to the good custom of biographical works, no life-notice is included for those still living. Notwithstanding his own view on the matter, Dijkstra had no objection to placing a short foreword before du Croo's work, which he regarded "not as a biography of General Swart but as a broadly conceived description of the so-called Van Heutsz period with regard to the Outer Provinces]]." That so large a place was accorded in the book to the said senior officer, one of the leading collaborators of Governor-General [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|van Heutsz]], would surprise nobody competent in the matter.

### From [[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)]]

In his foreword Dijkstra explicitly declined to treat the work as a life of Swart in the ordinary sense. Citing [[F. W. Stapel|Dr. F. W. Stapel]]'s remark in the introduction to *De Gouverneurs-Generaal van Nederlandsch-Indië* that biographical works faithfully omit notices of the living, Dijkstra wrote that he regarded du Croo's book "not as a biography of General Swart but as a broadly conceived description of the so-called Van Heutsz period with regard to the Outer Provinces* ("Outer Provinces")" — not as a biography but as a broadly conceived description of the so-called Van Heutsz period with respect to the [[Buitengewesten]].
