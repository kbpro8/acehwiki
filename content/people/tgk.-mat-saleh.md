**Tgk. Mat Saleh** was the son of [[Tgk. Sjeh di Paja Bakong]], killed with his father and with [[Tjoet Meutia]] in the surprise attack of [[W. J. Mosselman]] at the [[Kroeëng Peutòë]] on [[25 October]] [[1910]].

The action ended the resistance in [[Keureutòë]]: "thus fell the great blind resistance leader, revered as a saint, Teungkoe Sjeh di Paja Bakong (Seupot Mata), his son Tgk. Mat Saleh, and Tjoet Meutia as well."
