**Napoleon I** (fall 1813) was a French Emperor mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Mentioned for his fall in 1813 which ended the threat to Dutch independence and led England to return Dutch colonies.

## Role

French Emperor

## Dates

fall [[1813]]

## Account from Jongejans

Mentioned for his fall in [[1813]] which ended the threat to Dutch independence and led [[Engeland (England)|England]] to return Dutch colonies.

## Significance

Napoleon I appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
