**Kapitein Hamell** assumed command of the **Wikkie‑gebied** after the death of **[[Maajoor Pusch|Major Pusch]]**. He served alongside **[[Maajoor De|Major De]]** and **[[Luitenant‑Kolonel Douglas|Lieutenant‑Colonel Douglas]]** during the Dutch suppression of the rebel uprising in the **[[1760s]]**.

## Role
Hamell’s leadership contributed to the re‑establishment of Dutch authority over the plantation zones.
