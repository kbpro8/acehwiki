**Tgk. di Reubèë** was de geleerde en fanatieke Tgk. di Reubèë, one of eleven resistance men taken prisoner in [[Keureutòë]] in [[1909]].

In the course of [[1909]] the situation improved throughout the district, "but 269 opponents still fell — among them 17 band-leaders or commanders and 7 Teungkoes; 11 were taken prisoner, among them the learned and fanatical Tgk. di Reubèë, while 72 submitted, simply reporting themselves."
