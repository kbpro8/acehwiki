**Teukoe Oemar (oelèëbalang of Keumangan)** was a Oelèëbalang of the landschap Keumangan (different from the famous Teukoe Oemar) mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Expressed his wish about insane care: wondered why the Government had built such fine expensive prisons that 'orang nakal' (mischievous/criminals) longed to be there, but had not used part of the money to give the sick or insane man a good shelter, which he needed more than all those evildoers.

## Role

[[oelèëbalang|Oelèëbalang]] of the [[landschap]] [[Keumangan]] (different from the famous [[Teukoe Oemar]])

## Account from Jongejans

Expressed his wish about insane care: wondered why the Government had built such fine expensive prisons that '[[Orang nakal|orang nakal]]' (mischievous/criminals) longed to be there, but had not used part of the money to give the sick or insane man a good shelter, which he needed more than all those evildoers. Quoted by Van Loon.

## Significance

Teukoe Oemar (oelèëbalang of Keumangan) appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
