---

title: Teungku di Keureuse'
tags: [person, saint]
---

One of a series of saints whose tombs line the coasts, islands and cliffs of Acheh, alongside Tuan di Payel, Tuan di Kala, Teungku di Buket, Teungku di Ujong Eumpee, Tuan di Pulo Bunta, Teungku di Ujong, and Teungku di Ujong Riteung. All are revered by seafarers passing their tombs.

- [[Teungku Lho' Tapa' Tuan]]

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
