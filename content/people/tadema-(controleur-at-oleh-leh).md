**Tadema** was a contrôleur of the Binnenlandsch Bestuur posted at [[Oleh-leh]] in December [[1875]]. In the manuscript of his fellow official [[Kroesen (controleur at Oleh-leh)|Kroesen]] — afterwards governor of Sumatra's Westkust — Tadema appears when the newly arrived [[Majoor Romswinckel (1875)|Majoor Romswinckel]] applied to him for help to a vessel for his baggage: Tadema asked [[Toekoe Nja Mohamad (Oleh-leh 1875)|Toekoe Nja Mohamad]], one of the Atjehnese greats who was just then with him, to provide a sampan, and so became the unwitting witness of the sampan incident that Kroesen quoted to illustrate the Atjehnese character (see [[Kroesen (controleur at Oleh-leh)]]).
