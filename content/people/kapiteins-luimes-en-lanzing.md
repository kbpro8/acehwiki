**Captains Luimes and Lanzing** were the two Dutch company commanders whose companies were the first formed up on the beach west of the [[Kwala Atjeh|Atjeh river]] during the [[April 1873 Atjeh Expedition|landing of 8 April 1873]] at the opening of the [[aceh-war|Aceh War]]. More than five hundred Acehnese charged them from [[Battle of Pantei Tjermin and Kota Mogat (8 April 1873)|Kota Pantei Tjermin]] and from behind the dunes in an effort to destroy the landing party before the rest of the force could come ashore; Luimes's and Lanzing's companies stood fast, received the charge with rapid fire from their [[beaumont-geweer|breech-loaders]], and then drove the attackers back with bayonet and sabre.
