**Koningin Wilhelmina** — Wilhelmina (1880-1962), Queen of the Netherlands from 1890 to 1948 — was the sovereign whom [[Dominee Thenu]] served and by whom he was received in audience in the Netherlands after his retirement in [[1934]]. In *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* Koningin Wilhelmina is the figure whose handshake Thenu refused to wash — "Lang heeft het geduurd eer hij de hand heeft gewassen, die door de Koningin was gedrukt" — and whose [[7 December]] [[1942]] speech (in exile in London) gave its name to the [[Zeven December Divisie]] of the Indonesian revolution.

## The audience with Thenu (1934)

When [[Dominee Thenu]]'s "veertig Atjeh-jaren" were over in [[1934]], he was sent on a reis naar Nederland and was received by Koningin Wilhelmina. "Lang heeft het geduurd eer hij de hand heeft gewassen, die door de Koningin was gedrukt" — for a long time he refused to wash the hand the Queen had shaken. For more than ten days no one else — until [[Generaal Swart]] — was permitted to shake that hand. The episode places the Queen, with [[Generaal Swart|Swart]], in the small circle of figures whose touch the *Soldaten-Dominee* counted as sacramental.

## The 7 December 1942 speech

The [[7 December]] [[1942]] speech of Koningin Wilhelmina — in exile in London — promised a post-war round-table conference on Indonesia. The speech gave its name to the [[Zeven December Divisie]] of the Indonesian revolution, raised in [[1946]]-[[1947]] from Dutch volunteers. The naming was therefore both a political reference (to the Queen's promise) and a military designation: the division named for the promise of negotiation was fighting the war that the negotiation would, in [[1949]], finally conclude.

## The loyalty triad

The *[[Marechausseelied]]* that [[Dominee Thenu]] taught stressed "*trouw aan Koningin, trouw aan het vaandel en trouw aan de vlag*" — loyalty to the Queen, loyalty to the standard, and loyalty to the flag. The triad was the Soldaten-Dominee's framing of the colonial soldaat's loyalty: the Queen, the standard, and the flag — the sovereign, the regiment, and the state.

## Significance

Koningin Wilhelmina is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as the sovereign whom [[Dominee Thenu]] served and by whom he was received in audience. The book's argument is that the Queen's handshake — the touch that Thenu refused to wash — was the political honour accorded to the *Soldaten-Dominee* at his retirement, and that the [[7 December]] [[1942]] speech gave its name to the division that would fight the war the speech had promised to conclude by negotiation.
