title: A.F. Von de Wall
aliases: [Von de Wall]
tags: [person, western-scholar]
---

A Dutch scholar who published the stories of Pak Belalang and Lebai Malang in "Hunga Rampai" vol. IV (Batavia [[1894]]); his collection of Malay manuscripts at Batavia is frequently cited.

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
