**Toekoe Nja Mohammad** was the head (*hoofd*) of the [[IX Moekims]] in [[Groot-Atjeh]] and a Dutch ally during the alliance period of [[1893]]–[[1896]], at whose request the post of [[Biloel]] was erected in the night of [[20 May]]–[[21 May]] [[1894]] and whose territory, together with that under the influence of [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]], formed the [[XXV Moekims]].

## Request for the post at Biloel

Hooijer recorded that [[Biloel]] was established in the night of [[20 May]] to [[21 May]] [[1894]] *op verzoek van Toekoe Nja Mohammad, hoofd der IX Moekims* — at the request of Toekoe Nja Mohammad, head of the IX Moekims. He and [[Toekoe Hoessin Longbattah]], whose mother lived at the place, showed the Dutch column the way there. The fortification was raised around the old *[[Messigit|Messigit]]* (mosque), whose roof had been burnt and renewed, and within whose white wall the officers' quarters were established.

## Position within the alliance

Hooijer noted that the territory standing under [[Teukoe Oemar]]'s influence together with that of Toekoe Nja Mohammad jointly constituted the [[XXV Moekims]], and that when both had been cleared of the enemy the result was so favourable, and the resulting rest so complete, that the [[Civiel en Militair Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Governor of Atjeh and Dependencies]] resolved on [[1 September]] [[1893]] to extend the same method to the terrain east of the Dutch line under [[Toekoe Nja Banta]].
