**J. J. W. E. Versteege** was a Dutch writer on colonial affairs and the author of the pamphlet *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk in zake het Atjeh-vraagstuk* (An appeal to the Dutch people in the matter of the Aceh question), one of the polemical publications generated by the public controversy over the [[Aceh War|Atjeh War]] in the 1870s and 1880s.

## Place in the Atjeh Controversy

[[P. Brooshooft|Brooshooft]] listed Versteege's pamphlet among the secondary sources he consulted when compiling his Brooshooft (1886) of the war, published at Utrecht in [[1886]]. He grouped it with the writings of [[Jan van Swieten|van Swieten]], [[G. M. Verspick|Verspick]], [[luitenant-kolonel de Rochemont|de Rochemont]], and "Misopseudes" as part of the rich pamphlet literature to which the prolonged conflict gave rise.
