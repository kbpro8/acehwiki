**Corporal Donselaar** served in the **Dutch Marine Corps** and was tasked with leading a detachment of **14 mariniers** that provided covering fire for an artillery section positioned at the kampong **Pasoch** during the December 1875 operations (see [[page 135]]).

## Role in the Battle

His men were placed behind the **ambulance** and reserve‑munitions, forming a defensive line while the main column advanced. Donselaar’s unit contributed to the successful occupation of **Pasoch** after heavy fighting (see [[page 135]]).

## Significance

The involvement of a non‑commissioned officer in coordinating fire support reflects the Dutch reliance on experienced NCOs to maintain cohesion among mixed marine and native forces.
