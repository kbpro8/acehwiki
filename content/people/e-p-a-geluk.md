**E. P. A. Geluk** was a [[Netherlands|Dutch]] captain of the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger]] who was killed in action on [[9 April]] [[1896]] at the [[Relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit (April 1896)|relief of the besieged temporary posts of Biloel and Lamkoenjit]] in the [[XXV Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]]. He is named in the [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] as one of the seven officers killed in the spring [[1896]] crisis, and his portrait is one of the six reproduced in the album's closing plates.

## The relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that the relief expedition of [[April]] [[1896]] — the [[Relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit (April 1896)|relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit]] — was undertaken by a column of two battalions that marched from the *geconcentreerde linie* to disengage the besieged posts. The column was preceded by the *vuren* — heavy fire — of the enemy, and the engagement was severe. The album records the cost of the relief in a single sentence: *Kapitein Geluk en zeven minderen, die sneuvelden* — *Captain Geluk and seven subordinates, who fell* — and *de Luitenants ten Seldam en Hockstein, die met 23 minderen werden gewond* — *the Lieutenants ten Seldam and Hockstein, who with 23 subordinates were wounded*.

## The chronology of the spring 1896 fallen

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]]'s roll of the fallen gives the dates of the seven officer fatalities of the spring [[1896]] crisis as follows:

- [[K. H. Westendorp|Lieutenant K. H. Westendorp]] — died of wounds [[11 March]] [[1896]]
- [[J. W. Vuijk|Lieutenant J. W. Vuijk]] — killed at Senelop [[30 March]] [[1896]]
- [[E. P. A. Geluk|Captain E. P. A. Geluk]] — killed at the relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit [[9 April]] [[1896]]
- [[H. L. Maarschalk|Lieutenant H. L. Maarschalk]] — killed at Tjot Rang [[14 April]] [[1896]]
- [[J. B. Zon|Lieutenant J. B. Zon]] — killed at Senelop [[19 April]] [[1896]]
- [[H. O. W. L. Kramer|Captain H. O. W. L. Kramer]] — killed at the storming of Lampisang [[23 May]] [[1896]]
- [[C. G. A. C. Krohne|Lieutenant C. G. A. C. Krohne]] — died of wounds [[4 May]] [[1896]]

The roll establishes Geluk's death as the third of the spring casualties, and the first at the relief expeditions of April.

## Burial at Kota Potjoet

The album records that Geluk was buried at the cemetery of [[Kota Potjoet]], whose entrance bears the inscription *Aan onze Kameraden, gevallen op het Veld van Eer*.

## Significance

Geluk's significance in the documentary record of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] is as the highest-ranking officer fatality of the [[Relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit (April 1896)|relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit]] — the first major Dutch offensive of the post-defection period. His death — together with the seven subordinates killed and the 23 wounded, including Lieutenants [[Ten Seldam|ten Seldam]] and [[Hockstein]] — established the cost of the relief operations and set the pattern for the engagements that would follow.
