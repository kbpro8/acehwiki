---
title: Raja Beureuhat
tags: [person, legend]
---

A marvellous hero invoked in a sadati kisah, whose footsteps shake the ground and whose raised hands cause earthquakes, possessing ships on the sea and steeds on land — a metaphor for the challenging performance of the sadati troupe.
