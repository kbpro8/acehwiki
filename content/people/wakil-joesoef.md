**Wakil Joesoef** was an Acehnese whose daughter was taken as a hostage by the Dutch, according to the allegation made by [[H. van Kol]] in the [[Tweede Kamer]] on [[17 November]] [[1897]].

## The allegation

Van Kol's words were: "Men nam de dochter van Wakil Joesoef als gijzelares, en heeft vroeger wel eens vaders gevangen genomen om van de dochters te genieten" — the daughter of Wakil Joesoef was taken as a hostage, and formerly fathers had sometimes been imprisoned in order to enjoy the daughters.

The statement immediately followed his citation of the latest [[Koloniaal Verslag]], which spoke of the laying of *[[Kampong|kampongs]]* in ashes, the destruction and burning of hiding-places and the extirpation of forests, and immediately preceded his charge concerning the some fifty thousand persons sentenced to labour who had been exposed to hostile fire.

## The title

*Wakil* is an Arabic-derived term for a deputy, agent or representative, used in Acehnese and Malay official usage for the deputy of a chief or of a religious functionary. The source does not state of whom Joesoef was *wakil*, nor his district, nor the date or circumstances of his daughter's seizure, nor her fate.

## Reception

The Minister of Colonies [[J. T. Cremer|Cremer]] did not answer this case specifically, replying to the general charge that he had heard nothing of cruelties committed in cold blood and did not believe they had been committed.
