**Sultan Alaedin Muhamad Daud Shah Juhan** — pronounced in Achehnese **Muhamat Dawot Shah Juhan**, and commonly called **Tuanku Muhamat Dawot** — was the last pretender to the sultanate of [[Acheh]]. Elected Sultan in 1296 H (A.D. [[1879]]) as a child, he was the son of [[Sultan Mansur Shah|Sultan Alaedin Manso Shah Juhan]] (reigned 1838–70); he maintained a wandering "court" first at [[Keumala]] and then in various refuges, until his [[Submission of Tuanku Muhamat Dawot|submission]] to the Dutch Government in January [[1903]]. For his biography and conduct during the [[Achehnese War|war]], see [[Tuanku Muhamat Dawot]]; this article treats of the formal style, seals, and letters patent under which he acted, as illustrated in Chapter I of Vol. I.

## The ninefold seal (*chab sikureu'eng*)

The latest [[Sarakata with chab sikureu'eng|ninefold seal]] of Sultan Alaedin Muhamad Daud Shah Juhan is engraved at the head of the [[Letters patent with the ninefold seal to Panglima Meuseugit Raya|letters patent to the Panglima Meuseugit Raya]] translated in the appendix to Chapter I. It consists of one large circle surrounded by eight smaller ones. The large circle contains the inscription:

> *May Allah give good guidance unto His Majesty Sultan ʿAlauʾddīn (pronounced in Ach. Alaedin) Muhamad Daud Shah Juhan (pronounced in Ach. Muhamat Dawot Shah Juhan) the Blessed, the shadow of Allah in the world 1296* (i.e. 1879, the year of his election as Sultan).

In the eight smaller circles surrounding the central one appear the names of the following Sultans: [[Sultan Sayyidi al-Mukamnal|Sultan Sayyidi al-Mukamnal (Alaedin al-Qahhar, 1530–52/57)]]; [[Sultan Meukuta Alam|Sultan Meukuta Alam (Iskandar Muda, 1607–26)]]; [[Sultan Tajul-alam|Sultan Tajul-alam (Sapiatodin, the first sultana, 1639/41–1675)]]; [[Sultan Ahmat Shah|Sultan Ahmat Shah (the first prince of the present dynasty, 1723/26–1735)]]; [[Alaedin Juhan Shah|Sultan Juhan Shah (1735–60)]]; [[Sultan Mahmut Shah|Sultan Mahmut Shah (1781–1795)]]; [[Sultan Jauhar Alain Shah|Sultan Jauhar Alain Shah (1802–24)]]; and [[Sultan Mansur Shah|Sultan Mansur (Manso) Shah (1838–70)]]. The first four of these names recur on most of the nine-fold seals of the sultans; they are those of the rulers to whom the former prosperity of Acheh and her adat-institutions are generally ascribed. The others vary according to the taste of each sultan, who decides for himself the contents of his seal.

## The private seal and the "rightful possessor"

To the right of the ninefold *chab* is to be seen the small oblong private seal of the sultan, which is regarded as more or less establishing the legality of the large seal, and bears the words *as-Sultan Muhamat Dawot Shah*. In the left-hand top corner of the document is written *al-mustahiqq* (pronounced in Achehnese *al-meuseutaha*), "the rightful possessor," by which expression Tuanku Muhamat Dawot makes known his claims to the throne of Acheh. Less weighty documents, which are not considered worthy of the ninefold seal, exhibit in its place a single seal of the sultan. Round its border run the words "Allah" and "His word is the truth and to him belongeth dominion." In the centre appears: "This is His Majesty Sultan Alaedin Muhamat Dawot Shah Juhan, the Blessed, Allah's shadow in the world."

## The letters patent to the Panglima Meuseugit Raya

The letters patent given to the Panglima Meuseugit Raya are dated the 4th day of the month Rabīʿ al-Awwal, A.H. 1307 (28 October [[1889]]). The preamble invokes the help of Allah, the blessing of [[Prophet Mohammad|the Prophet]] "the Lord of Men," of his four companions [[Abu Bakr|Abu Bahr]], [[Omar]], [[Uthman]] and [[Ali]], of "the glorious Pole of the world and the brilliant framework of the skies, the immutable knower, the true Beloved, the lord Shaikh [[Abdul-Qadir Jilani|Abdul-Qadir Jilani]]," and of all the devout *walis*. The text then introduces "our lord and master His Majesty Sultan Alaedin Muhamat Dawot Shah, son of the late Sultan Alaedin Manso Shah Juhan, the Blessed, Allah's shadow in the world; while His Highness sat upon the throne in Kuta Keumala, His exalted Majesty, the Prince of the world, spoke unto the [[Keureukon Katiboy Muluk]], bidding him draw up a document with the thunder-seal, to be vouchsafed unto Our uleebalang, who is called the Panglima of the Meuseugit Raya of [[Banda Acheh]], the Seat of Peace."
