**Snouck Hurgronje** (1857 – 1936) was a Dutch orientalist whose extensive fieldwork in [[Acheh]] produced seminal studies such as *Mekka II*. The source cites his observations on the performance of the *Burda* in Meccan rituals, which illuminate the poem’s broader Islamic context that Acehnese Muslims inherited. His scholarship remains a cornerstone for modern Aceh historiography.
