**Majoor Knoote** was a Dutch infantry major who served with the [[April 1873 Atjeh Expedition|first Aceh expedition]] of [[1873]]. He is mentioned by Brooshooft as one of the commanding officers during the 10 April march on the [[mesigit|Missigit]] and as the leader of a sharp reconnaissance in action towards the [[Kraton (institution)|Kraton]] on [[12-april|12 April]] [[1873]], in which his column was heavily engaged by Acehnese fighters firing unexpectedly from behind the *paggers* (fences) of their kampongs.
