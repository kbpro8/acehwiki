**H.L.J. Ligtenberg** was a 1st Lieutenant of the [[3e Bataljon Infanterie]] who fought under Captain [[J.P. van Lier]] during the [[First Conquest of the Great Missigit]] on [[10 April]] [[1873]].

## Service
He assumed command or actively rallied the troops on the ladders immediately after Captain Van Lier was severely wounded in the face.
