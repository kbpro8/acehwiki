**Pang Intan** was the last band-head of the [[Meergebied]], killed with four followers at the [[Waih ni Wis]] on [[2 April]] [[1914]].

He appears in the [[1913]] list of the last unsubmitted resistance leaders as the one remaining figure in the Meergebied. In April [[1914]] [[Takèngön]] reported: "contact was at last obtained on 2 April with those in resistance under Pang Intan; the said band, seven men strong, was surprised by a patrol at the [[Waih ni Wis]]; [[Pang Intan]] fell with four followers."
