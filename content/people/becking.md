**Becking** (circa 1930s) was a Photographer mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Photographer credited for the photograph 'Zwaartap van een dennenboom' (heavy tapping of a pine tree).

## Role

Photographer

## Dates

circa 1930s

## Account from Jongejans

Photographer credited for the photograph 'Zwaartap van een dennenboom' (heavy tapping of a pine tree).

## Significance

Becking appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
