**Overste Scheepens** was a Dutch *overste* (colonel) of the [[K.N.I.L.]] in [[Atjeh|Aceh]] who, in [[1913]], was wounded by an Acehnese during a *rechtzitting* (court session) — and who died of his wounds in [[1933]], twenty years later. The long delay — twenty years between the wounding and the death — is the book's marker of the lethality of the Acehnese *[[reuntjong]]* wound: a wound that could kill in [[1913]] or in [[1933]].

## The wounding (1913)

"Scheepens 1913 tijdens een rechtzitting dodelijk..." The *Overste* Scheepens was wounded in [[1913]] during a *rechtzitting* — a court session — by an Acehnese. The *rechtzitting* was the colonial army's judicial proceeding in the bivak; the wounding of the *overste* during a *rechtzitting* was therefore an attack on the colonial army's judicial authority. The weapon was an Acehnese *[[reuntjong]]* — the traditional bladed weapon of the Acehnese.

## The death (1933)

"Aan dit soort wonden stierf in [[1933]] de Overste Scheepens." The *Overste* Scheepens died of his wounds in [[1933]], twenty years after the wounding. The long delay is the book's marker of the lethality of the Acehnese *reuntjong* wound: a wound that could kill in [[1913]] or in [[1933]]. The *reuntjong* wound was therefore both an immediate and a long-term danger: the soldaat who was wounded by the *reuntjong* might die of the wound decades later.

## Significance

Overste Scheepens is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as the *overste* whose death in [[1933]] — twenty years after his wounding in [[1913]] — is the book's marker of the lethality of the Acehnese *[[reuntjong]]* wound. The book's argument is that the *reuntjong* wound was both an immediate and a long-term danger: the soldaat who was wounded by the *reuntjong* might die of the wound decades later. Scheepens is therefore the book's marker of the long tail of the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]]'s wounds — the wounds that could kill in [[1913]] or in [[1933]].
