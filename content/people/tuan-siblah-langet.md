---

title: Tuan Siblah Langet
tags: [person, saint]
---

A saint whose tomb near Kuala Changkoy lies irregularly east-west rather than the prescribed north-south orientation of Mohammedan graves, yet is revered despite this irregularity.

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
