**Sultan Mahmud Syah of Aceh** was the reigning sovereign Sultan of the [[Aceh Sultanate]] who reigned from [[Kraton of Aceh]] during the outbreak of the Aceh War in [[1873]] and the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] in [[1874]].

## Reign and European Invasion

Reigning during the Anglo-Dutch Sumatra Treaty of [[1871]], Sultan [[Sultan Mahmud Syah of Aceh]] oversaw Acehnese rearmament from [[Penang]] in [[1872]]. In spring [[1873]], his forces defeated the [[First Aceh Expedition]] under General [[J. H. R. Köhler]].

When General [[J. van Swieten]] launched the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] in December [[1873]], Sultan [[Sultan Mahmud Syah of Aceh]] directed the defense of the royal capital. Prior to the [[Capture of the Aceh Kraton]] on [[24 January]] [[1874]], the sultan evacuated the palace, retreating into the interior mukims alongside [[Panglima Polem]]. His death of cholera shortly thereafter left the sultanate without an immediate crowned successor during early [[1874]].
