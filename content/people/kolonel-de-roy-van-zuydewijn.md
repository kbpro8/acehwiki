**Colonel de Roy van Zuydewijn** was a Dutch *kolonel* of the [[Indische Leger|Indische leger]] who led the frontal assault on the *voorwerk* of the [[Mesigit of Atjeh|Mesigit]] on [[6 January]] [[1874]] during the [[Storming of the Mesigit (6 January 1874)|storming of the Mesigit]], the bloodiest single action of the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873–1874)|second expedition]] against Atjeh. In [[G. F. W. Borel|Borel]]'s account his is the name attached to the *borstwering* on which the first Dutch footing was gained at the cost of 220 dead and wounded.

## The Storming of the Mesigit

The Mesigit — the fortified mosque that served as *voorwerk* of the [[Kraton of Atjeh|Kraton]] — lay "als een tijger die op zijn prooi loert" at "niet meer dan 1200 passen" from the Dutch bivouak at [[Penajoeng]]. The Atjehneezen had built a *meesterlijk* work: a 180-metre earthen *borstwering* reinforced with bamboo, *dwarswallen* and *schietkokers*, fronted by a dry *gracht* filled with *bamboe-doeri versperringen*. Without proper reconnaissance and "tegen alle Indische traditiën in, zonder noemenswaardige voorbereiding, alleen in front, waar ze natuurlijk het sterkst was," [[Jan van Swieten|generaal van Swieten]] ordered the assault. Battalion after battalion was repulsed by "moorddadig vuur." After a "hevigen strijd" De Roy van Zuydewijn's column at last gained footing on the *borstwering*, while [[Generaal Pel|luitenant-kolonel Pel]] flanked the work and entered the Mesigit unopposed from the rear.

## Borel's Judgement

The Mesigit cost 220 Dutch dead and wounded; enemy losses, Borel records, were "vermoedelijk slechts onbeduidend." Borel's strictures fall principally on van Swieten, who ordered the assault without reconnaissance and "alleen in front" against the strongest face of the work, and not on De Roy van Zuydewijn, who carried out the order with the courage and tenacity the situation demanded. The colonel's name survives in Borel's narrative as the symbol of the Dutch infantry's hard-won footing on the Mesigit wall — the action that, in Borel's account, ought to have been the prelude to an immediate *omtrekkende beweging* but was instead followed by nineteen days of [[Bombardment of the Kraton (January 1874)|desultory bombardment]] before [[Generaal Verspijck|Verspijck]] finally completed the encirclement on [[24 January]] [[1874]].
