**Nicolo de Conti** — more properly **Niccolò de' Conti** (Venice, c. [[1395]]–[[1469]]) — was the Venetian merchant-traveller whose ~[[1430]] voyages through the [[Malay Archipelago|Malay Archipelago]] produced the first European-language account of [[Sumatra]]'s east coast and the [[Acheh|Atjehnese]] sphere, and the first European record of the place-name **Cernovem** — which [[P. de Roo de la Faille|De Roo de la Faille]] identifies with [[Sjahroe'n Noewi]]/[[Tandjoengpoera]]-[[Aroe]]. His account, preserved in the *Historia rerum ubique gestarum* of [[Aeneas Sylvius Piccolomini|Pope Pius II]] (the apostolic secretary to whom De Conti confessed his travels), is the principal pre-Portuguese European witness to the [[Aroe|Aru]]-[[Pasej-Samodra|Pasej]] complex.

## Voyage to Sciamthera and Cernovem

Sailing from [[Coromandel Coast|Mailapoer]] (later Portuguese São Tomé) on the [[Coromandel Coast|Coromandel coast]], De Conti reached "the isles deinceps Taprobanes, which in the language of the Sciamuthera-people is called the city of the island" — i.e. [[Sumatra]] — and stayed a full year in that "*nobilissimum emporium*". The name *Sciamthera* [[P. de Roo de la Faille|De Roo de la Faille]] decodes as *syam-oe-thera*, a *bedekte taal* form for *syama Tara*, the Buddhist goddess who was patroness of [[Sumatra]]. De Conti then proceeded to [[Tenasserim|Tenasserim]], a Malay land under Siamese sovereignty, and from there — by several land and sea journeys — up a "Ganges" so broad that one sailing in mid-stream could see neither bank, through heavy reed-beds and rich fisheries, to the city of **Cernovem**.

## Decoding: *Cernovem* and the "Ganges"

The "Ganges" up which De Conti sailed is, in [[P. de Roo de la Faille|De Roo de la Faille]]'s reading, no Indian river but the river of [[Tandjoengpoera]] at the head of [[Aroe-baai|the bay of Aroe]] — the same *Moeware perhentian* or *Moeware Sija-na-ma-tara* whose *minjak tanah* (white earth-oil) the bay produced. The *arundo* (reed) on the banks is read as *bamboe petoeng*; the fishery apparatus as the *sero*'s of the [[Aroe|Aru]] coast. The city of **Cernovem** De Roo de la Faille identifies with [[Sjahroe'n Noewi]]/[[Tandjoengpoera]] — the Persian "New City" of the [[Sadjarah Malajoe|Sadjarah Malajoe's]] [[Awi Ditjoe|Awi Ditjoe episode]] — and with the *Xarnauz* of the *Roteiro* of [[Vasco da Gama]].

## *Marahatian* and the products of the land

Three months up the "Ganges", De Conti reached *Marahatian* (alternative reading *Maarazia*), where there was "a great plenty" of aloë-wood, gold, silver, precious stones, and pearls. De Roo de la Faille decodes *Marahatian* as *m-oe-aram-oe-ara sija-mata-ra* — i.e. the *perhentian* (stopping-place) of the goddess [[Cyama-Tara|Tara]] — which fixes the location as [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]] at the head of [[Aroe-baai|the bay of Aroe]].

## Significance

For AcehWiki, De Conti is the principal pre-Portuguese European witness to the [[Aroe|Aru]]-[[Pasej-Samodra|Pasej]]-[[Acheh|Atjeh]] complex, and the only European traveller to record the place-name **Cernovem** — the *bedekte taal* form that [[P. de Roo de la Faille|De Roo de la Faille]] decodes as [[Sjahroe'n Noewi]]/[[Tandjoengpoera]]. His *Marahatian* identification with [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]] also fixes the [[Minjak tanah|white petroleum]] of the bay as the trade-good whose fame reached pre-Portuguese Europe.
