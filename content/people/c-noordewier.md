**C. Noordewier** was a 1st Class Medical Officer (Medical Officer) attached to the [[Marine-landingsdivisie]] during the [[First Aceh Expedition]].

## Service and Death
During the brutal combat of the [[First Conquest of the Great Missigit]] on [[10 April]] [[1873]], Noordewier heroically attempted to revive a mortally wounded fellow naval officer, Lieutenant Engelvaart, under heavy fire but was unable to save him. Noordewier's medical service highlighted the severe casualties suffered by the Dutch forces.
