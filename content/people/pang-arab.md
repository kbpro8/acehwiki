**Pang Arab** — literally *Panglima Arab*, "the Arab commander" — was an Acehnese band chief (*bendehoofd*) of Arab descent of the [[V Moekims Montassik|V Moekims Montassik federation]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]] who, after the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (29 March 1896)|defection of Teuku Umar of 29 March 1896]], gathered a hostile band in the [[V Moekims Montassik|Montassik country]] and was one of the principal Acehnese military antagonists of the Dutch in the first semester of the [[1896]] campaign. He is one of the principal Acehnese band chiefs of *[[ATJEH '96 (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96]]* (1913).

## Role in the 1896 campaign

Pang Arab was, in [[1896]], one of the band chiefs of the [[V Moekims Montassik|V Moekims Montassik federation]]. The *1e Deel* of *[[ATJEH '96 (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96]]* records that the hostile bands of the [[V Moekims Montassik|Montassik country]], led by [[Toeankoe Brahim|T. Brahim Montassik]] and Pang Arab and [[Tengkoe Tanah-Abé]] and [[T. Seuneuboe]], had gathered in the federation after the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (29 March 1896)|defection of Teuku Umar]] and were raiding the surrounding country, robbing their own countrymen. The [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|tuchtiging of the V Moekims Montassik of 16–17 June 1896]] under [[J. W. Stemfoort|Kolonel Stemfoort]] was mounted to break these bands.

## The Arab-Acehnese community of resistance

The presence of a band chief identified as "Arab" in the ranks of the Montassik resistance is consistent with the wider pattern of Hadhrami sayyid involvement in the Acehnese resistance throughout the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. Hadhrami Arabs — descendants of immigrants from the Hadramaut in southern Arabia — had settled in Atjeh over the centuries and played prominent roles in the religious and military life of the sultanate. The title *Habib* (literally "my beloved", an honorific for sayyids claiming descent from the Prophet) is borne by several other resistance figures in the wiki, including [[Habib Lhong]], [[Habib Samalanga]], [[Habib Abdoerrachman el-Zahir]], and others. Pang Arab may have been a *habib* or a *sayyid* of the same Hadhrami diaspora, though Kruisheer does not specify his religious status.

## Significance

Pang Arab is one of the principal Acehnese band chiefs of *[[ATJEH '96 (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96]]* and one of the principal Acehnese military antagonists of the Dutch in the first semester of the [[1896]] campaign. His band was one of the principal targets of the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|tuchtiging of the V Moekims Montassik of 16–17 June 1896]]. His identification as "Arab" places him in the wider context of Hadhrami Arab involvement in the Acehnese resistance.
