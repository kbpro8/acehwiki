**Willem Bernard Johan Alexander Scheepens** was a Dutch military officer and Captain in the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]].

## Military Career and Service in Aceh

Scheepens was awarded an Honorary Sabre (*Eeresabel*) in [[1897]] and the Knight 3rd Class Military William Order (*M.W.O.*) during the [[Gayo and Alas Lands Expedition of 1904]]. After falling in combat in Aceh, a stone memorial bust was erected in his honor at [[Kerkhof Peutjoet]] in [[Kuta Raja]].
