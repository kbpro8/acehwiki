**C.H. Kool van Heerens** (born [[16 October]] 1845, Huizum) was a brave Dutch gunner (kanonnier) of the artillery whose heroic death became legendary during the [[Second Conquest of the Great Missigit]] on [[14 April]] [[1873]].

## Heroic Death
During the intense combat at the walls of [[Atjeh's Groote Missigit]], Kool van Heerens realized that the 12-second fuses on the hand grenades allowed the Acehnese to throw them back at the Dutch troops. To prevent this, he lit a fuse, held the live grenade in his hand, and climbed the [[Stormladders]] until his shoulders cleared the wall. He waited until the last possible moment to throw the grenade into the enemy ranks. At that exact instant, an Acehnese defender thrust a lance entirely through his throat and neck. Kool van Heerens fell backward and died in the arms of his comrades, his action saving many Dutch lives and neutralizing numerous defenders.
