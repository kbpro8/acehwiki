**C.H. Visscher** was a Captain of the 5th company of the [[3e Bataljon Infanterie]] in the Dutch military.

## Service
Alongside Captain [[W. Pordon]], Visscher commanded the central column during the fierce assaults of the [[Second Conquest of the Great Missigit]] on [[14 April]] [[1873]].
