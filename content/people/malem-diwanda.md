---
title: Malem Diwanda'
tags: [tokoh, karya-sastra]
---

Son of Sulutan Roih of Panjalarah, who won and then wrongly executed his wife Siti Chahya for adultery; she was secretly restored to life by the bidadari Mande Rubiah. After further adventures and marriages, including to the celestial princess Santan Meuteupi, he was eventually reunited with his original family.
