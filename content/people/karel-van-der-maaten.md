**Karel van der Maaten** was a Dutch military officer and Captain in the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]].

## Military Career and Service in Aceh

Van der Maaten received the Knight 4th Class Military William Order (*M.W.O.*) in [[1899]] and was promoted to Knight 3rd Class M.W.O. in [[1902]] alongside Sergeant [[J. C. Heynen]] for outstanding tactical leadership in central Aceh.
