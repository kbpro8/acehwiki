**J. A. de Bruyne** was a Dutch historian, director of the Hoogere Burgerschool at Zierikzee, and author of *De geschiedenis van Nederland in onzen tijd* ("The History of the Netherlands in Our Time"), a multi-volume work of which Volume IV (1872-1883, new edition, Schiedam: J. F. Dupont, 1912) is the source for the present article. The work is a comprehensive history of the Netherlands — domestic and colonial — in the period covered, and its first chapter is devoted to the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] and to the administration of the [[Governor-General of the Netherlands India|Governors-General]] [[J. Loudon|Loudon]] and [[J. W. van Lansberge|Van Lansberge]].

## The Work

*De geschiedenis van Nederland in onzen tijd* is a multi-volume work; Volume IV covers the period 1872-1883 in 516 pages. The first chapter (Hoofdstuk I) is devoted to the Atjeh-oorlog and to Nederlandsch-Indië under the Governors-General Loudon and Van Lansberge, and runs from page 1 to page 115. The chapter is organised in two principal sections: the first (pp. 1-52) covering the war under Loudon — the outbreak, the [[First Aceh Expedition (1873)|first expedition]] of [[April 1873|April 1873]], the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)|second expedition]] of [[November 1873|November 1873]] – [[January 1874|January 1874]], the [[Capture of the Kraton (24 January 1874)|fall of the Kraton]] on [[24 January]] [[24 January]] [[1874]], the operations under [[J. L. J. H. Pel|General Pel]] until Loudon's departure, the operations in other parts of the archipelago ([[Deli|Deli]], [[Sintang|Sintang]], [[Kraeng Bonto-Bonto|Kraeng Bonto-Bonto]]), and Loudon's domestic reforms; the second (pp. 52-115) covering the war under Van Lansberge — the [[Conversiequaestie|conversiequaestie]], the [[Tandjong Priok|harbour at Tandjong Priok]], the campaigns under [[G. B. T. Wiggers van Kerchem|Wiggers van Kerchem]], [[A. J. E. Diemont|Diemont]], and [[K. van der Heyden|Van der Heyden]], the [[Batak War (1878)|Batak War]] of [[1878]], the [[Submission of Habib Abdurrahman (13 October 1878)|submission of Habib Abdurrahman]] of [[13 October]] [[13 October]] [[1878]], the [[Campaign against the XXII Moekims (1879)|campaign against the XXII Moekims]] of [[1879]], the [[Campaign against the XXVI Moekims (July 1879)|campaign against the XXVI Moekims]] of [[July 1879|July 1879]], the [[Selimoen Expedition (August 1879)|Selimoen expedition]] of [[August 1879|August 1879]], the [[Batoe Iliq Expedition (July 1880)|Batoe Iliq expedition]] of [[July 1880|July 1880]], and the [[Establishment of Civil Government in Aceh (6 April 1881)|establishment of civil government]] on [[6 April]] [[6 April]] [[1881]].

## Method and Sources

The work is based on the official colonial reports (*koloniale verslagen*), the published correspondence and notes of the principal actors (notably the note of [[J. L. J. H. Pel|General Pel]] of [[30 April]] [[30 April]] [[1875]] to the Governor-General, and the cabinet letter of [[J. W. van Lansberge|Van Lansberge]] of [[21 March]] [[21 March]] [[1879]]), and the communications of [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje]] — in particular the contents of the [[Hikajat prang Kompeuni|Hikajat prang Kompeuni]] communicated by Snouck Hurgronje. The work is written in the neutral, scholarly register of Dutch historical writing of the early twentieth century, and is among the principal Dutch secondary sources for the early years of the Atjeh-oorlog.
