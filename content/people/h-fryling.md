**H. Fryling** was a Dutch geologist or mining engineer whose [[1921]] report on the coal occurrences of northern [[Sumatra]] — cited in the standard form "FRYLING, 1921" — is one of the sources for [[J. Zwierzycki|Zwierzycki]]'s [[1922]] inventory of Sumatran coal localities, and accordingly for [[Van Bemmelen's The Geology of Indonesia (1949)|Van Bemmelen's *Geology of Indonesia*]] ([[1949]]) in its treatment of the [[Coal in Atjeh|Atjeh and Langkat coal occurrences]]. Fryling's report is specifically cited for the [[Langkat]] coal localities of [[Besitang River]] (No. 5), [[Lepan]] (No. 6) and [[Sarangan]] (No. 7), where it is listed alongside the earlier [[1918]]/[[1919]] report of [[J. H. Hengeveld|Hengeveld]].

## The 1921 coal report

Fryling's [[1921]] report, as cited by Zwierzycki and reproduced by Van Bemmelen, supplies supplementary observations on the Langkat-residency coal occurrences that lie immediately to the south of the Atjeh coal zone. For the localities at Besitang River, Lepan and Sarangan — where the seams are 0.5–3 ft thick and "quality good" — Fryling's report is listed as a co-source alongside Hengeveld's [[1918]]/[[1919]] report and Zwierzycki's own [[1922]] synthesis. Van Bemmelen also cites Fryling for additional coal data in the East-coast-of-Sumatra localities (No. 10, the [[Kualu River]] occurrence, "FRYLING, 1928" — possibly a separate later report).

## Place in the Atjeh coal record

Fryling's report occupies, in the documentary sequence of Atjeh and northern-Sumatran coal exploration, the position immediately before [[J. Zwierzycki|Zwierzycki]]'s [[1922]] consolidated inventory. The sequence runs: [[Rinaud]] ([[1885]]) → [[Philippi]] ([[1918]]) → [[J. H. Hengeveld|Hengeveld]] ([[1918]]/[[1919]]) → **Fryling** ([[1921]]) → Zwierzycki ([[1922]]) → [[Van Loheezen|Van Loheezen]] ([[1929]]). Fryling's contribution is the immediately-pre-Zwierzycki field-observation that Zwierzycki drew on for his consolidated account.
