**Teukoe Bintara Keumangan** (1874 submission) was a Acehnese regional chief mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Acehnese chief who after Van Swieten's proclamation of 31 March 1874 set as condition for his written submission that the rule of his land must remain in his and his descendants' hands - an early example of how chiefs tried to preserve their authority under Dutch rule.

## Significance

Teukoe Bintara Keumangan appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
