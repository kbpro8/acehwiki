---

title: Tamim Ansa
aliases: [Tamim ad-Dari]
tags: [person, religious-figure]
---

Derived from Arabic-Moslem tradition about a Christian convert to Islam, Tamim is kidnapped by an infidel jinn while bathing at Medina and carried through the upper and lower worlds for 100 years, encountering Dajjal and the prophet Khidr, before being restored to his wife at Caliph Omar's command.

- [[Khidr]]
- [[Medina]]

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
