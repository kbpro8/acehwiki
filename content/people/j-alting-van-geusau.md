**J. Alting van Geusau** was the author of the [[1939]] article *[[De tocht van Overste van Daalen door de Gajo-, Alas- en Bataklanden (Geusau, 1939)|De tocht van Overste van Daalen door de Gajo-, Alas- en Bataklanden (8 Febr.–23 Juli 1904)]]* ("The march of Colonel van Daalen through the Gayo, Alas, and Batak lands, 8 February–23 July 1904"), published in *Indisch Militair Tijdschrift* (Netherlands Indies Military Journal), No. 30, p. 593 *e.v.*, with illustrations. The article is recorded as No. 566 in [[H. van Meurs|Van Meurs]]'s *[[Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939 (Van Meurs, 1940)|Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939]]* (The Hague, [[1940]]), in the Sumatra chapter.

## The 1939 article

Geusau's article, published in *Indisch Militair Tijdschrift* — the principal professional military journal of the Netherlands Indies — recorded the [[Colonne van Daalen naar de Gajolanden en Alaslanden (1904)|campaign of Colonel (Overste) van Daalen]] through the [[Gajoland|Gajo-]], [[Alaslanden|Alas-]], and [[Bataklanden|Bataklanden]] between [[8 February]] and [[23 July]] [[1904]]. The campaign — the [[Colonne van Daalen naar de Gajolanden en Alaslanden (1904)|Colonne van Daalen]] of the corpus's existing entry — was the military *zegetocht* (victorious campaign) by which the Gayo and Alas highlands of central Sumatra were brought under Dutch control. Geusau's article is an independent retrospective account, published thirty-five years after the campaign, and it gives the precise dates of the campaign ([[8 February]]–[[23 July]] [[1904]]) and the inclusion of the Bataklands in the traverse. The article is a companion to the [[De tocht van Overste van Daalen door de Gajo-, Alas- en Bataklanden 1904 (Harders, 1939)|Harders account]] (No. 568) of the same campaign, published in the same year in the *Orgaan van de Nederlandsch-Indische Officiersvereeniging*.

## Significance

For AcehWiki, J. Alting van Geusau is significant as the author of the [[1939]] retrospective on the [[Colonne van Daalen naar de Gajolanden en Alaslanden (1904)|van Daalen campaign of 1904]] in the *Indisch Militair Tijdschrift* — an independent source for the campaign that complements the corpus's existing accounts and the [[De tocht van Overste van Daalen door de Gajo-, Alas- en Bataklanden 1904 (Harders, 1939)|Harders account]] (No. 568).
