**Lieutenant Verhey** was a junior officer of the [[Korps Mariniers]] who served under [[General Pel]] during the Aceh campaign of [[1875]]–[[1876]]. He led the marine detachment that acted as a protective guard for the general’s staff and participated in actions at [[Daroe]], [[Bockit Daroe]], and the crossing of the [[Loëng Lemo]] river.

### Role in the Campaign
On [[27 January]] [[1876]], Verhey received Pel’s directive to keep the marine corps at the coast. He commanded the marine peloton that covered artillery sections and later guarded the headquarters and prisoners after the capture of the IX Moekims.

### Engagements
Verhey’s unit was engaged in heavy fighting on [[28 January]] [[1876]] at the enemy camp of [[Daroe]], where the marines suffered one fatality and ten wounded. He also helped construct a bridge over the [[Loëng Lemo]] for the artillery on [[30 January]] [[1876]].
