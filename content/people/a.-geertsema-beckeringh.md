**A. Geertsema Beckeringh** — *Ridder van de [[Militaire Willemsorde]] met Eeresabel* — was the *[[Corps of Military Constabulary of Atjeh and Dependencies|marechaussée]]* captain who commanded the 5th and later the 2nd Division and was twice Civil Authority at [[Meulaboh]], and under whose administration the pacification of the [[Westkust van Atjeh|west coast]] began in [[1909]].

## The turn of [[1909]]

Improvement on the west coast came only "when at last in 1909 the civil authority, the well-known constabulary captain Geertsema Beckeringh, could dispose of sufficient means of power": a mobile *marechaussée* column under captain [[F. Darlang|Darlang]] temporarily occupied first the mouth of the [[Wòjla]] and later [[Boebon]]; [[H. J. Schmidt|Schmidt]] cleared up in [[Teunòm]]; and the pacification of the difficult [[Boven Wòjla]] under [[Scheurleer]] and from the bivouac [[Tanòh Mirah]] produced the submission of two hundred resistance men, who were helped with rice and seed-*padi*. Thereafter only [[Beneden Wòjla]], Boebon and [[Seunagan]] remained unquiet in the Meulaboh country.

## Travel without escort

[[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] recorded in his letter to minister [[Alexander Willem Frederik Idenburg|Idenburg]] of July [[1908]] that Geertsema Beckeringh in the [[Meulaboh|Meulabohsche]] travelled without military escort as regards [[Wòjla]] and did not report it because it was forbidden — but that "after the removal of [[T. Bén Peureumeuë]], Geertsema Beckeringh no longer dared go up to upper Meulaboh alone either."
