**Wisse** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Lebé Grondong]]

In [[1910]] the column of [[F. Darlang|Darlang]] held the [[Péndéngvallei]], so that Lebé Grondong moved with his followers to neighbouring sub-divisions. In March [[1911]] a brigade under sergeant [[Wisse]] surprised his hiding-place between [[Tampor]] and [[Lésten]] and reported him killed with his principal *panglima* and a follower, the booty being an M.95 rifle, a Mauser repeating rifle, a Beaumont and a Mauser self-loading pistol. In September came news that he was *not* dead: he had toured the Gajò Loeö during the *hari raja* feasts extorting from the population and had obtained two carbines on the death of the resistance head of [[Badaq]]; in October he murdered [[Rödjö Akoeb]] of [[Penosan]] in revenge for the latter's journey to [[Koeta Radja|Koetaradja]] — but handed in a *panglima*, a *[[goeroe]]* and a carbine. In [[1912]] he came in submission.

### From [[Gajòlanden]]

[[1911]] brought steady submissions — twenty-two of Penosan in January with all their *panglimas*, twenty-five of [[Pasér]] and [[Reröbö]] in February — and in March the apparent end of Lebé Grondong, surprised between [[Tampor]] and [[Lésten]] by a brigade under sergeant [[Wisse]] with a haul of one M.95 rifle, a Mauser repeating rifle, a Beaumont and a Mauser self-loading pistol. The same month brought the murder of [[Rödjö Kemala]] at the instigation of his kinsman [[Taib|Si Taib]], and Taib's escape from the transport escort at Lésten and death, wounded and starving, on the twenty-sixth.

### From [[The resistance in Atjeh 1908–1912]]

March brought the provisional end for Lebé Grondong: "a brigade under Sergeant Wisse surprised on the 16th the hiding place, between Tampor and Lésten, of Lebé Grondong, formerly the right hand and later the successor of Tgk. Moedö Péndéng. Laid low: Lebé Grondong himself, together with his principal panglima and one follower; booty 1 M. 95 rifle, 1 Mauser repeating rifle and a Beaumont and 1 Mauser self-loading pistol."
