**Broers** was a **Lieutenant ter zee 2e klasse** who assumed command of the Dutch landing division after **[[Mennert (naval officer)]]** was wounded during the **[[30 March]] [[1893]]** operation on the **Tamiang** river.

## Command

Broers directed the continuation of the assault on enemy fortifications and coordinated with the infantry under **[[Colonel Van de Pol]]**.
