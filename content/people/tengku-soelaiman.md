**Tengku Soelaiman** was an Indonesian civil servant who, by decision of 7 March [[1950]], was appointed secretary to the governor of North Sumatra (*gouv. v. Nd.-Sumatra*). The appointment was made by the acting governor [[Sarimin Reksodihardjo]] as part of the administrative reorganisation following the dissolution of the [[Negara Sumatera Timur]] and the incorporation of the pre-war residencies of [[Atjeh]], [[Tapanuli]], and East Sumatra into the autonomous province of North Sumatra.
