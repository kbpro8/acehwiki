**Verhofstad** was a Dutch second lieutenant of infantry in [[Atjeh]] in [[1876]], who on [[15 February]] [[1876]] escorted a munition transport — carried by about 150 Chinese coolies — from [[Kota Radja]] to [[Biloel]] through the threatened south-east line.

## 15 February 1876

A day after Major [[Majoor B. E. Mekern|B. E. Mekern]] had marched from Pagar-Ajer to Atoë and Kajoe-Lē, Verhofstad marched with 44 armed men as escort of the munition transport. Near Atoë the transport was attacked: first fired upon, then pressed by the enemy, so that the coolies twice dropped their loads and fled into the kampong. By effective measures the lieutenant kept his men together and the enemy off; the fled coolies returned and were collected, and the detachment — supported by a patrol from the post at Atoë — delivered the munition and returned without loss to Pagar-Ajer that same afternoon.

The feat — one of the few fully successful escort actions of the month — is recorded by [[Kolonel Meijer|H. F. Meijer]] in *[[Atjeh van 26 December 1875 tot 4 September 1876]]* ([[1883]]).
