**T. Adam** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Meulaboh]]

In [[1910]] more than a hundred *uitgewekenen* reported themselves in March, among them many adherents of [[Tgk. Poeti']]; in April the band of [[Tgk. Imeum Sradeuë']] was surprised in Wòjla and [[T. Adam]] fell on the Beneden Wòjla; in December Tgk. Poeti' at last submitted with all his *teungkoes*, *panglimas* and sixty followers. In May [[1911]] the bivouacs [[Arongan]], [[Tanòh Mirah]] and [[Reusa']] could be abolished in consequence of the favourable political situation in Wòjla and Boebon.

### From [[Wòjla]]

In [[1908]] an official report recorded that in the Wòjla district politics had been "gemodderd". In [[1909]] a mobile *[[Corps of Military Constabulary of Atjeh and Dependencies|marechaussée]]* column under [[F. Darlang|Darlang]] temporarily occupied the mouth of the Wòjla, and the pacification of the Boven Wòjla under [[Scheurleer]] from [[Tanòh Mirah]] produced the submission of two hundred resistance men. In April [[1910]] the band of [[Tgk. Imeum Sradeuë']] was surprised in Wòjla and [[T. Adam]] fell on the Beneden Wòjla. By [[1918]] Wòjla had so flourished that [[H. J. Schmidt]] "zelfs hoofden uit andere streken 'ter instructie' nu en dan naar Wòjla dirigeerde": the roads were exceptionally well maintained, the *[[gampong]]s* excelled in order and cleanliness, and tax collection and school attendance left nothing to be desired.

### From [[Beneden Wòjla]]

After [[A. Geertsema Beckeringh]] obtained sufficient means of power in [[1909]], only the Beneden Wòjla, [[Boebon]] and [[Seunagan]] remained unquiet in the Meulaboh country. In April [[1910]] [[T. Adam]] fell on the Beneden Wòjla, at the cost of one more rifle to the resistance. In May [[1911]], in consequence of the favourable political situation in Wòjla and Boebon, the bivouacs [[Arongan]], [[Tanòh Mirah]] and [[Reusa']] were abolished.
