**Toon Dupuis** (active c.1930s) was a Sculptor mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Sculptor of the bust of General Van Heutsz at Koetaradja, the bust of soldaten-dominée Thenu (finished by Ingen Housz), and the bust of kapitein Heinzenknecht at Bireuën.

## Role

Sculptor

## Dates

active c.1930s

## Account from Jongejans

Sculptor of the bust of [[Generaal J. B. van Heutsz|General Van Heutsz]] at [[Koetaradja]], the bust of [[Soldaten-dominée Thenu|soldaten-dominée Thenu]] (finished by [[Ingen Housz]]), and the bust of [[Kapitein Heinzenknecht|kapitein Heinzenknecht]] at [[Bireuën]].

## Significance

Toon Dupuis appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
