**Van Drongelen** was an Ambonese *sergeant 2e klasse* of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen]] who served in the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]] of [[1910]]-[[1911]] — the *sergeant 2e kl.* (the *pang* in the brigade hierarchy on [[Atjeh|Aceh]]) who made the omtrekking with six men that omsingelde the ladang of *[[Peutoeha Gam Masén]]* near the [[Kroeëng Seukoeleh]], and who was one of the five (with [[Nussy]], [[Gomies]], [[Enoch]] and [[Kamagi]]) who made the *levensgevaarlijke afdaling* along the steile wand that gained the M. 95 and the small boy — the son of *[[Tgk. Tjhi Majét]]*.

## The omsingeling of the ladang

"De Amb. sergeant 2e kl. Van Drongelen maakte met zes man een omtrekking en zette de rand af" — the Ambonese sergeant 2e kl. Van Drongelen made a flanking movement with six men and set the edge off. The ladang was omsingeld within half an hour. The omsingeling was the colonne method's tactical payoff: the *bekas* read, the ladang surrounded, the bende engaged.

## The levensgevaarlijke afdaling

"De Amb. sergeant 2e kl. van Drongelen, Nussy, Gomies, Enoch en Kamagi deden een levensgevaarlijke afdaling langs een steile wand." The five — Van Drongelen, Nussy, Gomies, Enoch and Kamagi — made a life-threatening descent along a steep wall. The descent gained the M. 95 (a *[[karabijn (M. 95)|karabijn]]) and the small boy — the son of *[[Tgk. Tjhi Majét]]* — who "vertelde in zijn onschuld van alles aan de luitenant" (told the luitenant, in his innocence, all sorts of things).

## Significance

Van Drongelen is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as the Ambonese *sergeant 2e kl.* of the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]] — the *pang* of the brigade hierarchy — who made the omtrekking that omsingelde Gam Masén's ladang and the *levensgevaarlijke afdaling* that gained the M. 95 and the small boy. The book's argument is that the colonne method depended on the *sergeant 2e kl.*'s tactical work — the omtrekking and the afdaling — and that Van Drongelen was the instrument of that work in the colonne Schmidt.
