**L. F. Tuckerman** was a **[[lieutenant ter zee|lieutenant ter zee]]** who entered the main fort of **[[Retch]]** after the **[[rotan|rattan]]** barrier had been cut. He followed **[[Coppel (bootsman)]]** and other marines into the interior of the fort during the **[[Expedition to Retch 1858|1858 assault]]**.

## Role in the Assault

Tuckerman’s entry into the fort marked the turning point of the battle, leading to the capture of the enemy position and the death of **[[Panglima Besar Soeloong]]**.
