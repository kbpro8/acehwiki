**Jhr. Witewaall van Stoetwege** was a Dutch captain of infantry — a member of the Dutch titled nobility (*jonkheer*) — in [[Atjeh]] in [[1876]]. On [[27 February]] [[1876]] he was wounded in the daily fighting of the patrols of Major [[Majoor B. E. Mekern|B. E. Mekern]]'s column at [[Kajoe-Lē]], in the struggle to keep open the road between the new posts of the south-east line, and died of the wound.

[[Kolonel Meijer|H. F. Meijer]] lists the three officer casualties of that road in February [[1876]]: Captain [[Van der Pauwert]] killed on [[15 February]], Second Lieutenant [[L. H. Kooy]] wounded on [[18 February]], and Captain Jhr. Witewaall van Stoetwege wounded on [[27 February]] at the consequences of which he died. The same toll — endemic musketry from the kampong edges on every daily patrol — sealed the isolation of the Kajoe-Lē column that was raised only by the [[Storming of Lambaroe (7 March 1876)|storming of Lambaroe]] on [[7 March]].
