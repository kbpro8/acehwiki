**J. W. N. Cramer** was a Dutch engineer officer of the [[Korps Ingenieurs, Mineurs en Sappeurs|Korps Ingenieurs, Mineurs en Sappeurs]] selected, with [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede-Luitenant C. J. Snijders]], for detachment to the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger]] by [[Koninklijk Besluit of 10 June 1873 No. 33|Koninklijk Besluit of 10 June 1873 No. 33]], in preparation for the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)|second Aceh expedition]]. The two officers were instructed together at the Mineurskamp at [[Zeist|Zeist]] in the [[J. H. Kromhout|Kromhout]] optical-telegraph system in June [[1873]] and sailed together for the Indies later that summer. After their return from the Indies both were decorated with the [[Militaire Willemsorde|Ridderkruis der Militaire Willemsorde]] 4th class for their services in Atjeh, and the two crosses were conferred on them together, with full ceremony, at the garrison parade on the Maliebaan at [[Utrecht|Utrecht]] on [[19 February]] [[19 February]] [[1876]], the parade held for the King's birthday.

## The 1873 Detachment

The call for volunteer officers from the metropolitan army, issued after the failure of the [[First Aceh Expedition (1873)|Eerste Atjeh-expeditie]] under [[J. H. R. Köhler|Generaal-Majoor Köhler]] in [[April 1873|April]] [[1873]], drew applications from four engineer officers. By [[Koninklijk Besluit of 10 June 1873 No. 33|the Royal Decree of 10 June 1873]] the four were selected; Cramer and the younger Snijders were then detached to [[Zeist|Zeist]] for instruction in the [[J. H. Kromhout|Kromhout]] optical telegraph. Both subsequently sailed for the Indies on the steamship *Conrad* of the Maatschappij "Nederland" from [[Nieuwediep|Nieuwediep]] on [[10 July]] [[10 July]] [[1873]].

## Decoration and the 1876 Parade

By *Koninklijk Besluit* of [[12 November]] [[12 November]] [[1875]] No. 18 — see [[Koninklijk Besluit of 12 November 1875 No. 18|Koninklijk Besluit of 12 November 1875 No. 18]] — both Cramer (by then Eerste-Luitenant-Ingenieur) and Snijders were appointed Ridder 4e klasse der Militaire Willemsorde for their services during the Atjeh operations in the period mid-August [[1874]] to end-February [[1875]]. When the crosses were simply sent to the recipients without the prescribed ceremony, Snijders submitted a request that they be conferred "met den meest mogelijken militairen luister" and with the prescribed riddereed. The request was granted, and at the garrison parade on the Maliebaan at Utrecht on [[19 February]] [[19 February]] [[1876]] the two officers received their crosses together, under the full ceremonial of the [[Militaire Willemsorde|Order]] and before great public interest.
