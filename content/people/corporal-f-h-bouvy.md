**Corporal F. H. Bouvy** was a Dutch marine who was recognized for his “excellent conduct” during the 1824 campaign and subsequently granted permission to study at the *Instituut der Marine* in *Medemblik* with the aim of becoming an officer.

## Education

His award and subsequent study illustrate the Dutch practice of promoting capable marines through formal naval education.
