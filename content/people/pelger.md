**Pelger** (active 1938) was a Official; contributed to chapter I mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Helped Jongejans with chapter I (history of Aceh).

## Role

Official; contributed to chapter I

## Dates

active [[1938]]

## Account from Jongejans

Helped [[Resident Jongejans|Jongejans]] with chapter I (history of [[Aceh]]).

## Significance

Pelger appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
