**Wolterbeek, Schout-bij-nacht** was a senior Dutch naval officer who commanded the expeditionary squadron—including the corvette [[Trop]] and four chartered vessels—sent in [[September 1819]] to suppress the rebellion of [[Mahmoed Badar Oedien]] in the [[Palembang]] region. The expedition suffered heavy losses from enemy batteries and disease, leading to its withdrawal (page 93).

## 1819 Palembang Expedition

Wolterbeek’s force, comprising roughly 1 400 men under the overall command of [[Lieutenant‑colonel Bisschoff]], was unable to secure the riverine stronghold due to logistical difficulties and strong enemy fire.
