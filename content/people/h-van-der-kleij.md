**H. van der Kleij** was a **[[marinier|marine]]** of 2nd class in the **[[Royal Netherlands Marine Corps|Korps Mariniers]]** who fought in the **[[Expedition to Retch 1858|1858 assault]]**. He was cited among the marines nominated for the **[[Militaire Willemsorde]]**.

## Service Record

Van der Kleij took part in the breach of the **[[rotan|rattan]]** barrier and subsequent hand‑to‑hand combat inside the fort.
