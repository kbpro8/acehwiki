---

title: Khidr
aliases: [Chidhr]
tags: [person, religious-figure]
---

The immortal prophet who lives in the waters, whose body never becomes wet through God's favor. He appears in rapa'i performance texts, and the Hikayat Edeurih Kholani reveals that his long life owes partly to multiplied repetition of certain Quranic passages.

## See Also

- [[Tamim Ansa]]

## Source

*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
