**Pang Nanggròë** was a Band leader (bendeleider) from Lho' Soekonsche mentioned in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Born in Lho' Soekonsche, not of distinguished origin or education.

## Role

Band leader (bendeleider) from Lho' Soekonsche

## Account from Jongejans

Born in Lho' Soekonsche, not of distinguished origin or education. One of the '[[Petocha peuët|petocha peuët]]' (four leaders) of Teungoh. Brave and cunning. After death of [[Teukoe Tjhi Teunong]] (executed [[1905]]), married his widow [[Tjoet Meuthia]]. The party of [[Teukoe Tjhi Teunong]] took [[Tjoet Meuthia]] and her ~6-year-old son into the forest; Pang Nanggròë soon followed. His leadership capacities came to the fore; soon became acknowledged commander. Called the 'Watergeus' (Water Beggar) by the Dutch because in one of his first attacks on military seaside establishments he came with praus. Long list of attacks: [[Ara Koendo]] attack (10 carbines lost), [[Overval|overval]] on a marchbivak in the Idische, bombardments of bivaks [[Lho'Soekon|Lho' Soekon]] and [[Panton Laboe]], attacks on tram [[Coolies|coolies]] and personnel, destruction of tramway. His stepson was proclaimed [[oelèëbalang]] of [[Keureutòë]]; the child's presence and the reverence for the mother ([[Tjoet Meuthia]]) strengthened his influence. Became a political danger. In [[September]] [[1910]] a [[Brigade (Maréchaussée)|brigade]] under [[Sergeant Van Slooten]] surprised him in marshland; he was killed.

## Significance

Pang Nanggròë appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s account as one of the figures shaping the history of [[Aceh]] during the period covered by the book.
