**Insinger** was a member of the States General who, on [[16 November]] [[1882]], took part in the interpellation over the very worrying state of Atjeh. In the review of that debate given by [[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] — after the ministerial declaration of [[Willem Maurits de Brauw|de Brauw]] that the undivided sovereignty over Sumatra had been conquered "in these times" — the aim of several members, among whom Insinger, [[Rutgers van Rozenburg]], [[Wintgens]] and [[A. de Casembroot|de Casembroot]], is described as having been "to shake government and Chamber awake over the lamentable state of things in Atjeh; to give a powerful hint to make the government break with its carelessness and its optimism".
