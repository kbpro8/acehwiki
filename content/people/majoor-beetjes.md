**Majoor Beetjes** was a Dutch army officer who commanded a mixed force of land and sea troops during the [[17 May]] [[1817]] uprising on the island of [[Saparoea]], an episode of the broader Dutch effort to pacify the [[Aceh]] region and its surrounding archipelago. His leadership of the expedition, which ended in a disastrous failure with heavy casualties, is recorded in the histories of the [[Royal Netherlands Marine Corps|Dutch Marine Corps]] (see page 92).

## The Saparoea Expedition

Majoor Beetjes led a detachment drawn from the [[Indische leger]] and the [[Royal Netherlands Marine Corps|Dutch Marine Corps]] to suppress the rebellion that erupted on [[Saparoea]] on [[17 May]] [[1817]]. The landing site was poorly chosen, and the operation suffered from inadequate reconnaissance. The expedition resulted in the death of Beetjes, ten officers, and 350 other ranks, as well as marines from the frigates [[Evertsen]] and [[Nassan]] (page 92).

## Consequences

The failure underscored the difficulties of amphibious warfare in the rugged coastal terrain of the Aceh‑adjacent islands and prompted a reassessment of Dutch landing tactics in the region.
