**Bisschoff van Heemskerck** — also written *E. W. Bisschoff van Heemkerck*, *Bisschoff*, *Bisschof* — *Luitenant-Kolonel der Infanterie*, line commander of the concentrated line in early [[1896]], was one of the most continuously active Dutch field commanders of the first semester of [[1896]], as recorded in A. Kruisheer's *ATJEH '96*. Bisschoff talso office as *liniecommandant* early in [[1896]], ordered the post commanders at [[Ana' Galong]] to patrol at least once during their detachment period, personally came to [[Ana' Galong]] at 10:30 AM on [[7 March]] [[1896]] to take charge after the [[Patrol of Captain Van Blokland (7 March 1896)|patrol of Van Blokland]] got into trouble, reorganised the *benteng*, and held the reserve at [[Lambaroe]] with 4 companies infantry and 1 section *bergartillerie*. Through April, May and June [[1896]] he commanded the [[tuchtiging van Lam Goet (14 April 1896)|1e Colonne on 14 April (Lam Goet operation)]], the [[Excursie to Moekim Lamtengah (21 May 1896)|excursie to Moekim Lamtengah on 21 May 1896]], the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|1e Colonne in the Lam-Krak expedition on 9–10 June 1896]], the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|1e Colonne in the 16–17 June V Moekims Montassik tuchtiging]], and a *machtsvertoon* column on [[26 June]] [[1896]]. His combination of *liniecommandant* responsibilities with field command made him one of the most institutionally central Dutch officers of the spring operations.

## Liniecommandant and the 7 March 1896 Crisis

Bisschoff's tenure as *liniecommandant* of the concentrated line in early [[1896]] placed him in direct command of the Dutch defensive perimeter around [[Koeta Radja]] — the *geconcentreerde linie* of posts which included [[Ana' Galong]], [[Lambarih]], [[Lamsoet]], [[Senelop]], [[Toengkoeb]], [[Tjot Rang]], [[Kroeng Gloempang]], [[Lampeneroet]] and the other buitenposts whose progressive evacuation would define the April [[1896]] operations. His order that post commanders at Ana' Galong patrol at least once during their detachment period was the immediate context for the [[Patrol of Captain Van Blokland (7 March 1896)|patrol of 7 March 1896]] — the engagement in which [[H. F. T. van Blokland|Kapitein Van Blokland]]'s patrol was surrounded by approximately 250 Achehnese under [[Habib Lhong]], [[T. Mad Amin]] and [[Panglima Hassan]] at [[Ana' Batée]]. Bisschoff personally came to Ana' Galong at 10:30 AM on [[7 March]] [[1896]] to take charge after the patrol got into trouble, and he was accompanied by [[Kronouer|Kapitein Kronouer]] of the General Staff (who talso command of the relief detachment of 40 bayonets from [[Lamsoet]]/[[Lambarih]] under [[K. H. Westendorp|Lt. Westendorp]] and [[F. Ketjen|Lt. Ketjen]]). Bisschoff's reorganisation of the *benteng* after the engagement and his maintenance of the reserve at Lambaroe with 4 companies infantry and 1 section *bergartillerie* were the immediate institutional responses to the [[7 March]] [[1896]] crisis, and they established his reputation for direct operational engagement with the line.

## April, May and June Field Commands

Bisschoff's field command record in the spring and early summer of [[1896]] is one of the most extensive in the balso. He commanded the [[tuchtiging van Lam Goet (14 April 1896)|1e Colonne on 14 April 1896 (Lam Goet operation)]] — the *tuchtiging* against the residence of [[Toekoe Hoesin Lhongbatta]] which stormed the fortification and destroyed an iron 9 cm cannon with dynamite. He commanded the [[Excursie to Moekim Lamtengah (21 May 1896)|excursie to Moekim Lamtengah on 21 May 1896]] — the operation against the territory of [[Toekoe Tjoet Lamtengah]] which included 6 brigades *maréchaussée* under [[Jhr. G. J. W. C. H. Graafland|Kapitein Graafland]], two companies of the [[5e Bataljon]] under [[P. Linck|Kapitein Linck]] (whose 4e compagnie stormed the *versterking Belang*), a *bergartillerie* section, and *genietroepen*. He commanded the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|1e Colonne in the Lam-Krak expedition on 9–10 June 1896]] — with the [[3e Bataljon]], [[6e Bataljon]], [[7e Bataljon]], a *bergbatterij*, and 12 brigades *maréchaussée* under [[Jhr. G. J. W. C. H. Graafland|Graafland]] — which fought the [[Battle of Ateuh Raja / Lam-Krak / Lam-Djamé (9 June 1896)|battle of Ateuh Raja on 9 June 1896]] and the [[Combat at Baha (9 June 1896)|combat at Baha]] (where the *koeta Baha* was stormed and sergeant [[Stemfoort, No. 40785|Stemfoort]] and *amb. fus.* [[Pelitimoe, No. 41233|Pelitimoe]] were the first to penetrate). He commanded the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|1e Colonne in the 16–17 June V Moekims Montassik tuchtiging]] — with [[G. A. Hansen|Lt.-Col. Hansen]]'s [[3e Bataljon]], [[D. A. Okhuijzen|Maj. Okhuijzen]]'s [[6e Bataljon]], [[A. Bangert|Kapitein Bangert]]'s [[4e Bergbatterij]], and *genietroepen* — which burned [[Lam-Soet]] on [[17 June]] [[1896|17 June]] and cleared the eastern sector. On [[26 June]] [[1896|26 June]] — two days before Vetter's departure — he commanded a *machtsvertoon* column which patrolled both sides of the [[Kroeng-Lingkar]] between [[Lam-Soet]] and [[Senelop]], received some fire at Senelop (one wounded), and demolished the western faces of the *versterkingen* [[Tjot Gloempang]] and [[Lam-Soet]].

## Significance

Bisschoff van Heemskerck's significance in *ATJEH '96* lies in his embodiment of the *liniecommandant* whose responsibilities spanned both the static defence of the concentrated line and the active field commands of the spring and summer operations. His personal intervention at [[Ana' Galong]] on [[7 March]] [[1896]] established his reputation for direct operational engagement, and his subsequent commands — at [[Lam Goet]] on [[14 April]], at [[Moekim Lamtengah]] on [[21 May]], at [[Lam-Krak]] on [[9 June|9]]–[[10 June]] [[1896]], at [[V Moekims Montassik]] on [[16 June|16]]–[[17 June]] [[1896]], and at [[Lam-Soet]]/[[Senelop]] on [[26 June]] [[1896]] — covered the full operational arc of the first semester. His partnership with the *maréchaussée* under [[Jhr. G. J. W. C. H. Graafland|Kapitein Graafland]] in three of these operations confirms the central role of the *korps maréchaussée* in the spring [[1896]] operations.
