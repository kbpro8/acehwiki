---
title: Sayyid Abdallah al-Haddad
tags: [tokoh, ulama]
---

The great saint of Hadramaut, author of a didactic poem translated into Acehnese verse by Cheh Marahaban. A separate biographical hikayat (Kisah Abdolah Hadat) and an Acehnese version of one of his poems (Hikayat Habib Hadat) describe the blessings bestowed during his life and from his grave at Trim.
