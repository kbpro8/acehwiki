**Commander Huygens** was a Dutch naval commander who operated the bark “[[Zeven Provinciën]]” during the December 19–25 riverine campaign in the interior of [[Acheh]]. He received orders to patrol the [[Wironje creek]] and to explore plantations such as [[Prosperiteit]] and [[Cornelia Jacoba]].

## Actions
Huygens dispatched officer patrols to the riverbanks, reported the presence of “negro” rebels, and coordinated with [[Captain Van Oyen]] and [[Captain‑lieutenant Smit]] for joint operations. His reports noted that the plantation “[[Prosperiteit]]” had recently been abandoned by rebels.
