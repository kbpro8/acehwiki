**Bartelds** was an Eerste-Luitenant der Infanterie of the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger]] who, in [[November 1874|November]] [[1874]], served as second-in-command of the *Compagnie Mineurs en Sappeurs* at [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] during the middle phase of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. Like the company's commander, [[Van den Blijck|Eerste-Luitenant Van den Blijck]], Bartelds was an infantry officer temporarily detached to the engineers for want of trained engineer officers. He and Van den Blijck together received [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede-Luitenant C. J. Snijders]] on his arrival in [[Acheh|Atjeh]] on [[8 November]] [[8 November]] [[1874]] and formed a lasting rapport with him.
