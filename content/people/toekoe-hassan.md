**Toekoe Hassan** was the Acehnese leader of a band that in [[1897]] continually made unsafe the mountains between [[Lok-Nga|Lho-Nga]] and [[Lepong]] on the west of [[Groot-Atjeh]], and against whom the Dutch patrol of [[25 September]] [[1897]] from the *[[Benting|benting]]* Lho-Nga was directed.

According to the intelligence reports on which the patrol was based, his band was lodged in the [[Goeah Teroes]] — the great cave — on the [[Glé Betoes]], a cave lying high in the mountains and resembling *een waar arendsnest*, a veritable eagle's nest.

The attempt to surprise the band failed: the Acehnese detected the approaching column and gave the shrill call that the echoes prolonged endlessly through the mountains, and [[P. H. R. Beuming]] records the suspicion that the Acehnese guide was not a stranger to the failure. In the fighting at the cave mouth the band killed Lieutenant [[J. van Greuningen]] and three European fusiliers and severely wounded two native fusiliers, before withdrawing deeper into the cave and escaping through its several exits. The pursuit by *[[Korps Marechaussee|marechaussée]]* brigades under Lieutenant [[C. F. A. Wagener]] on the far side of the mountain led to a violent fight in which Wagener and two *marechaussées* were killed and the band again escaped, carrying its dead and wounded with it.

The source records nothing further of Toekoe Hassan's district, standing or subsequent fate.
