**Dersjant** was a *vermaarde patrouillecommandant* — a famous patrol commander — of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen]] in [[Atjeh|Aceh]], paired in the book with [[Vastenou]] as the two "vermaarde patrouillecommandanten, die schitterende resultaten hadden geboekt" — the two famous patrol commanders who had achieved brilliant results. Under Dersjant, as under Vastenou, the korporaal [[Nussy]] served as a *[[dardanellen|dardanel]]* before joining the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]].

## The patrouillecommandant

"*[[Nussy]]* was dardanel geweest bij [[Vastenou]] en Dersjant, vermaarde patrouillecommandanten, die schitterende resultaten hadden geboekt." Nussy — the book's most fully drawn *dardanel* — served as a *dardanel* under Vastenou and Dersjant before joining the colonne Schmidt. The pairing of Vastenou and Dersjant as the two *vermaarde patrouillecommandanten* is the book's marker of the older generation of patrouillecommandanten — the generation that had developed the colonne method before Schmidt.

## The older generation

Dersjant and Vastenou belong to the older generation of patrouillecommandanten of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Marechaussee]] — the generation that had developed the colonne method in the years before the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]] of [[1910]]-[[1911]]. The *schitterende resultaten* they had achieved were the proof of the method; the *dardanellen|dardanellen* they had trained — including Nussy — were the instruments of the method's later application by Schmidt.

## Significance

Dersjant is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as one of the two *vermaarde patrouillecommandanten* under whom [[Nussy]] served as a *dardanel* before joining the colonne Schmidt. The book's argument is that the colonne method — the method that actually finished the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]] — was developed by the older generation of patrouillecommandanten like Dersjant and Vastenou, and that the *dardanellen|dardanellen* they trained were the instruments of the method's later application by Schmidt. Dersjant is therefore the book's marker of the continuity of the colonne method across the generations of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Marechaussee]].

## In the Maréchaussée Jubileum-nummer ([[1930]])

The *[[Overzicht van Onderscheidingen toegekend aan het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden (1890-1930)|Overzicht van Onderscheidingen]]* printed in the [[1930]] jubilee number of *[[Het Nieuws van den Dag voor Nederlandsch-Indië]]* records under [[1906]] the award to *1ste Luits. Mar. L. Dersjant* of the knight's cross of the *[[Militaire Willemsorde|Militaire Willems-Orde]]* 4th class, among the six recipients of that class in that year — with [[Vastenou|J. H. Ch. Vastenou]], [[G. K. W. F. de Vaynes van Brakell Buys]], [[W. L. Kramers]], [[Otto Louis Brewer|O. L. Brewer]] and [[W. V. Rhemrev]].

A portrait notice in the same issue styles him *oud-kolonel der Infanterie L. Dersjant* and gives his career: born on [[23 April]] [[1877]], he was appointed second lieutenant of Infantry in [[1900]] after completing the *[[Koninklijke Militaire Academie|K.M.A.]]*, and was placed with the Korps Maréchaussée in February [[1903]]. After having twice been *eervol vermeld* (honourably mentioned), he was awarded the knight's cross of the Militaire Willems-Orde 4th class as first lieutenant in [[1906]]. He then served in the successive ranks, to be pensioned as colonel.
