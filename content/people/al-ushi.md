**Al‑Ushi** was a 12th‑century Arabic poet whose kasida on theological doctrine (*k. al‑lāmiyya fi ‘I‑lawii*) is preserved in the Cambridge manuscript of the Malay *Burda* translation. The poem, dated to [[569]]/[[1173]], reflects early Islamic scholastic poetry that later entered the Indonesian literary sphere through translation.

## Manuscript Presence
The Cambridge copy (catalogue no. 3116 (1) and 5823 (2)) contains Al‑Ushi’s work alongside the *Burda* and other devotional poems, evidencing the transmission of Arabic theological poetry to Southeast Asia.
