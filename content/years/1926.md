**[[1926]]** was the year of the [[Communist uprisings of 1926|communist uprisings]] on [[Java]] — and of the *nasmeulend verzet* (smouldering resistance) that flared up again on the [[Westkust (of Atjeh)|Westkust of Atjeh]] at the same time — which together forced a partial restoration of the [[K.N.I.L.]]'s strength after the cutbacks of the post-First-World-War period. It is one of the political-military years of the inter-war period in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]*.

## The communist uprisings

"Gelukkig vonden de communisten in [[1926]], dat dit ver genoeg was gegaan en begonnen een paar opstanden, terwijl op de Westkust van Atjeh nog wat nasmeulend verzet even oplaaide…" The communists in [[1926]] judged that the cutbacks had gone far enough and began a few uprisings, while on the Westkust of Atjeh some smouldering resistance flared up again. The two events — the communist uprisings on Java and the nasmeulend verzet on the Westkust of Atjeh — were independent but simultaneous, and together they forced a partial restoration of the colonial army's strength.

## The cutbacks and the restoration

The cutbacks that provoked the communist judgement had been the post-First-World-War policy of "*geen man en geen cent*" — no man and no cent — by which the colonial army's strength had been reduced to 36,000 (then cut by 6,000) for an archipelago of 70 million. The communist uprisings of [[1926]] and the Westkust resistance forced a partial restoration of the strength; then the economic crisis hit, communism was conveniently declared "done with," and the *legermacht* was reduced again. "En na [[1936]] maar weer uitbreiden."

## The Westkust resistance

The *nasmeulend verzet* on the [[Westkust (of Atjeh)|Westkust of Atjeh]] in [[1926]] was the last flicker of the Acehnese armed resistance after the [[Handover of Aceh to a civil Gouverneur (1918)|handover of 1918]]. The book notes that the *Westkust verzet 1925-27|Westkust verzet of 1925-1927]] was the last significant Acehnese resistance episode of the inter-war period; the *Troemon tragedy 1926|Troemon tragedy of 1926]] is also mentioned in this context.

## Significance

[[1926]] is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as the year of the communist uprisings on Java and of the *nasmeulend verzet* on the Westkust of Atjeh. The book's argument is that the two events together forced a partial restoration of the colonial army's strength, and that the cutbacks that provoked the communist judgement were the post-First-World-War policy of "*geen man en geen cent*." The year [[1926]] is therefore the book's marker of the inter-war political-military context: the communist uprisings, the Westkust resistance, and the partial restoration of the colonial army's strength.
