Figures, places, and events in Acehnese history associated with the year 1521 — the year of the fall of [[Pasej-Samodra|Pasej]] to the Portuguese, which drove the [[Faletahan|Paseier Faletahan]] to [[Mecca]] and then to [[Java]] (where he became [[Sunan Gunung Jati]]), and which cleared the way for the [[Aceh Sultanate|Atjehnese sultanate]]'s absorption of the north Sumatran coast.

## People

- [[Faletahan]] / [[Sunan Gunung Jati]] — Paseier who left Pasej on the Portuguese conquest
- [[Pati Oenoes]] / [[Soenan Kalidjaga]] — lord of Japara who would marry his sister to Faletahan (c. 1523-1524)
- [[Pigafetta]] — chronicler of the Magellan voyage, who recorded the state of Java in this year
- [[Tome Pires]] — author of the *Suma Oriental* (~1515), recording the state of Java and Pasej before the fall

## Places

- [[Pasej-Samodra]] — fell to the Portuguese
- [[Pasej-Samodra|Pasej]] — north Sumatran sultanate
- [[Acheh]] — absorbing power on the north Sumatran coast
- [[Demak]]
- [[Japara]]
- [[Mecca]] — Faletahan's intermediate destination
