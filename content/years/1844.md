The year **1844** is the year of the [[Bombardment of the Atjehse coast by the British (1844)|British bombardment of the Atjehse coast]], in which the British government followed the precedent of the [[American bombardment of the Atjehse coast (1831)|American bombardment of 1831]] and chastised the [[Achehnese|Atjehse]] coastal population for Atjehese piracy against British merchant shipping. The action is recorded by [[J. Frijling|Ir. J. Frijling]] in his [[1946]] sketch *Een stukje geschiedenis der Indische Buitengewesten*. The same year [[1844]] also saw the British adventurer [[James Brooke|James Brooke]] conquer North-West Borneo with his crew and have himself proclaimed Rajah, an event Frijling cites as a parallel instance of British willingness to act unilaterally in the archipelago when the Dutch failed to do so.

## Events of the Year

- The [[Bombardment of the Atjehse coast by the British (1844)|British bombardment of the Atjehse coast]] — a punitive naval action by the British government against the Atjehse coastal population, following the precedent of the [[American bombardment of the Atjehse coast (1831)|American bombardment of 1831]].
- The conquest of North-West Borneo by the British adventurer [[James Brooke|James Brooke]], who had himself proclaimed Rajah — an event Frijling cites as a parallel instance of British unilateral action in the archipelago.

## Significance for Atjeh

The year [[1844]] marks the second foreign reprisal against the Atjehse coast for the failure of the [[Netherlands|Netherlands]] to suppress Atjehese piracy. Together with the [[American bombardment of the Atjehse coast (1831)|American bombardment of 1831]], the British action is one of the two precedents for foreign naval action against the Atjehse coast in the period before the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]], and forms part of the international-pressure background to the [[Anglo-Dutch Treaty of 1824|Sumatra-contract of 1871]] and the [[Dutch military measures against Atjehse piracy (1872)|Dutch military measures of 1872]].
