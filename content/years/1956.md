Figures, places, and events in Atjehnese history associated with the year 1956.

## People

- [[J. Kreemer]] — publication of his late synthesis [[Kreemer's De Karbouw (1956)|*De Karbouw*]] in 1956, with the *Voorbericht* dated 's-Gravenhage, October 1955.

## Documents

- [[Kreemer's De Karbouw (1956)]] — Kreemer's comparative ethnography of the water-buffalo in the Indonesian archipelago, published by N.V. Uitgeverij W. van Hoeve in 's-Gravenhage and Bandung.

## Concepts

- The book documents a wide range of Atjeh-specific buffalo-practices — see [[Atjehs buffalo vocabulary]], [[Poephon blang]], [[peuteumbôn]], [[poewôê siê]], [[teungkoe meunasah]], [[Pantang of the albino buffalo in Atjeh]], [[koeta djaq]], [[baris-Laksamana]], [[sakét oelèe neuraja]], [[geluksbuffel in Atjeh]], [[keubeuë peunoelang]], [[Atjehse Id al-Adha cow-not-buffalo choice]], [[Rabò annual seven-day feast]], [[Atjeh sale rite of grasping the nose-rope]], [[Hakikah and Koeroebeuën pre-Islamic preparation in Atjeh]], [[Atjeh adat schimpstraf (buffalo-riding wife-beater)]], [[getah dadih]], [[Atjeh folk-veterinary practice]], [[Marga Ramin legend (Boven-Singkel)]].

## Sources

*De Karbouw — Zijn betekenis voor de volken van de Indonesische Archipel*, by J. Kreemer ('s-Gravenhage / Bandung: N.V. Uitgeverij W. van Hoeve, 1956).
