## Account from *Schets eener parlementaire geschiedenis* (1955)


The following account is drawn from the parliamentary history of the [[Netherlands|Dutch]] [[Tweede Kamer der Staten-Generaal (colonial business)|Second Chamber]] as narrated by [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] in the colonial section of the [[Schets eener parlementaire geschiedenis van Nederland van 1849 tot 1901 (Rengers-Vermeulen, 1955)|1955 fourth revised edition of Rengers's *Schets*]].


**1905** is the year in which [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] was appointed military commander and governor of Atjeh, succeeding [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] in the latter role when [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] became governor-general. It is also the year of the Dutch annexation of [[Celebes]] and the year of the [[D. Fock|Fock]]-[[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] debate on the proper size of the Atjeh gendarmerie.

### Van Daalen's appointment


In [[1905]] [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] was appointed *militair commandant en gouverneur van Atjeh*. The [[Raad van Indië]] had recommended Colonel Swart, but governor-general [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] had persuaded the minister to appoint Van Daalen instead. According to testimony preserved in *Schets eener parlementaire geschiedenis*, [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] said to [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] on the occasion: "Ga je gang, ik vertrouw je. Ge hebt goede en kwade eigenschappen; ik geef u die post en als gij nu maar zorgt, dat er geen gedonder komt dan kunt gij zolang uw gang gaan, maar als het aan de grote klok komt dan voor jou een ander".

### The Thomson debate on police strength


The liberal MP [[H. C. Thomson|Thomson]], who had served as a captain in Atjeh for several years, argued in the [[Tweede Kamer]] that the Dutch forces in Atjeh were too small for the tasks they were assigned. To patrol the whole of [[Gajoland]] with seventy men was, in his view, "verregaand onvoldoende" — grossly insufficient. As an alternative to garrisoning he recommended strengthening the police (*maréchaussée*) in Atjeh, and warned against skimping on the cost. The warning would prove prescient: the [[Avondpost "Wekeer" articles (October 1907)|"Wekeer" affair]] of [[1907]] was to expose precisely the kind of atrocities that undermanning invited.

### Beyond Atjeh


In the wake of the [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacificatie]], Dutch authority was consolidated elsewhere in the archipelago: [[Djambi]] and [[Korintji]] in [[1901]], [[Borneo]] in [[1904]], [[Celebes]] in [[1905]], [[Bali]] in [[1906]]. The post-[[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] consolidation was thus synchronous with the [[Ethische Politiek|ethical-policy]] proclamation of [[Troonrede of 1901|1901]].

## Account from *Atjeh* by H.C. Zentgraff (1938)


[[H.C. Zentgraff|Zentgraff]] records three key events of [[1905]]. On [[26 January]] [[1905]] the [[Meurandeh Paja massacre (1905)|Meurandeh Paja massacre]]: sergeant Vollaers's patrol of sixteen *bajonetten*, bivouacked in the *meunasa* of Meurandeh Paja east of [[Lho Soekon]], neglected all security measures; armed Acehnese were admitted inside the *pagger* as fruit-sellers; *plotseling werd het signaal gegeven voor den overval; de geheele troep werd met het blanke wapen afgemaakt, de sergeant was de eerste*. Of seventeen military, sixteen were killed with the traditional *houw bovenop* klewang-stroke. [[H.N.A. Swart|Swart]] then *commandant te Lho Seumawé*, ran with a patrol in a forced march of nearly eighty kilometres a day to Meurandeh Paja. On [[5 March]] [[1905]] [[T. Tjhi Toenong]] was arrested and shot by firing-squad on the strand at [[Lho Seumawé]]. The double gravestone at [[Bakongan]] commemorates [[Bakongan klewangaanvallen (1905)|luitenant Donner's two patrols]], both surprised on the same night on 12 August 1905.

*Atjeh*, door H.C. Zentgraff. Gedrukt bij Drukkerij "De Unie", [[1938]], Batavia.

## Account from *Verkenningen in Atjeh* (1923)

**1905** was the year in which the *Afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden* was formed in the aftermath of [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]]'s expeditions of [[1901]] and [[1904]]. The *afdeeling* was re-allotted in [[1908]] among other administrative areas but was restored in [[1922]] with chief place [[Takengon|Takengon]], comprising the *onderafdeelingen* [[Takengon|Takéngon]], [[Gajoe Loeös|Gajoe Loeös]], [[Serbödjadi|Serbödjadi]] and [[Alaslanden|Alaslanden]].
