The year **1717** is, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's survey of the [[Buitenbezittingen|Outer Possessions]], the year of [[Radja Ketjil|Radja Ketjil]]'s conquest of the throne of [[Djohor|Djohore]]. The conquest — by which [[Radja Ketjil|Radja Ketjil]], having already seized [[Siak|Siak]] from [[Djohor|Djohore]], made himself master of the [[Djohor|Djohorese]] throne — was, in the Bureau's framing, the political event that briefly united [[Siak|Siak]] and [[Djohor|Djohore]] under a single ruler, before [[Radja Ketjil|Radja Ketjil]]'s rapid loss of the Djohore throne and his consolidation of an independent [[Siak|Sultanate of Siak]].

## Events

- [[Radja Ketjil|Radja Ketjil]]'s conquest of the throne of [[Djohor|Djohore]]. The Encyclopaedisch Bureau records: "Met behulp van den vorst van Pagarroejoeng veroverde hij eerst Siak en maakte zich daarna zelfs meester van den troon van Djohore ([[1717]])."
- [[Radja Ketjil|Radja Ketjil]]'s loss of the Djohore throne and consolidation of an independent [[Siak|Sultanate of Siak]]. The Encyclopaedisch Bureau records: "Deze laatste verloor Radja Ketjil echter spoedig weer, doch in Siak wist hij zich te handhaven, zoodat dit sedert dien tijd onafhankelijk was van het rijk Djohore."

## Significance

The year [[1717]] is, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's survey, the chronological hinge between the long Djohorese presence at the mouth of the [[Siak|Siak River]] (which had begun in the early sixteenth century, after the [[atjehsche-verovering-van-gasip-(begin-16e-eeuw)|Atjehnese destruction of Gasip]]) and the independent [[Siak|Sultanate of Siak]] that would, in turn, impose its own overlordship over the East Coast statelets under [[Sultan Ismail of Siak|Ismail]] and [[Sultan Said Ali|Said Ali]]. The year [[1717]] thus closes the long Djohorese-Atjehnese arrangement at the [[Siak|Siak River]] mouth and inaugurates the independent [[Siak|Sultanate of Siak]] — the political entity that would, in the late eighteenth and early nineteenth centuries, become the principal rival of [[Acheh|Atjeh]] for the East Coast overlordship.
