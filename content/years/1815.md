## Account from *Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.* (1954) — 1815

**[[1815]]** was the year of the establishment of the [[Militaire Willemsorde (M.W.O.)]] — the Military Order of William — by King Willem I of the Netherlands, for "*Moed, Beleid en Trouw*" (Courage, Judgment and Loyalty) in the field. The M.W.O. would become the highest Dutch military decoration and the central decoration of the colonial army's moral economy. In *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* [[1815]] is the founding year of the M.W.O., and therefore the opening of the decoration arc that the book narrates: the *Ridders M.W.O.* who carried [[Dominee Thenu]]'s bier at his [[Funeral of Dominee Thenu (11 May 1937)|funeral of 1937]] all earned their decorations in the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]] and the related colonial pacifications, long after the 1815 establishment. The M.W.O. had four classes — *Ridder 4e klas*, *Ridder 3e klas*, *Commandeur*, *Grootkruis* — of which the 4e and 3e klassen were the decorations of the rank-and-file soldaat and the onderofficier.

*Source: Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.*, by G. W. T. Dames, Maj. Inf. K.N.I.L. b.d. ('s-Gravenhage: Koninklijke Bibliotheek, 1954), chapter "Oom en de Bintang."
