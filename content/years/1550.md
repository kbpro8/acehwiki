The year **c. 1550** is the date given in *[[Wat Indië Ontving en Schonk]]* ([[1946]]) for the rapid, wholesale conversion of the [[Minangkabau]] of central Sumatra to Islam, effected — in the account of both Prof. Dr. [[W. J. A. Kernkamp|Kernkamp]] and Dr. [[F. H. van Naerssen|Van Naerssen]] — under the direct influence of the [[Acheh|Atjeh]] sultanate. See [[Conversion of Minangkabau via Atjeh (c. 1550)]].

## The c. 1550 conversion

Kernkamp's wording in the chapter *De invloed van den Islam*: "the Minangkabauers, who around 1500 were still heathens, were converted from Atjeh, after the realm of that name had arisen there. About 1550 they took up Islam in rapid tempo and in their entirety, and have remained fervent Muslims since." The conversion marks the moment at which the Atjeh sultanate's religious-cultural reach extended beyond its own territorial boundaries to the most populous interior people of Sumatra. The Minangkabau, in turn, became the principal agents of Islamisation for the southern Bataks — a process that, on Kernkamp's account, "long continued to resist" and "was accompanied by violence and plunder."

## Significance for Atjeh

For the [[Acheh|Atjeh]]-focused reader, the c. 1550 date fixes a specific moment at which the Atjeh sultanate's wider religious influence is documented, some three centuries before the [[Aceh War|Dutch-Aceh war]] of [[1873]] and four centuries before the [[1946]] publication of *Wat Indië Ontving en Schonk*. The date brackets the [[1415]] Chinese reports (which document Islam on the whole north coast) and the [[1297]] grave of the founder of [[Samudra]]: by 1550, the long Atjeh-led Islamisation of northern Sumatra was effectively complete.

## Account from *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*

Start of Atjeh's golden age ([[1550]]-[[1650]]). Wealth from trade and traffic, scholars from [[Egypt]]/Syrië/Arabië, translations, encyclopedic works, fiqh books.[[1550]]–[[1650]], the golden age of Aceh under the greatest of all sultans, [[Iskandar Moeda]].

*Source: J. Jongejans, Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu (Baarn: Hollandia Drukkerij N.V., c. 1938), pp. 28, 122.*
