## Account from *Schets eener parlementaire geschiedenis* (1955)

The following account is drawn from the parliamentary history of the [[Netherlands|Dutch]] [[Tweede Kamer der Staten-Generaal (colonial business)|Second Chamber]] as narrated by [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] in the colonial section of the [[Schets eener parlementaire geschiedenis van Nederland van 1849 tot 1901 (Rengers-Vermeulen, 1955)|1955 fourth revised edition of Rengers's *Schets*]].


**1907** is the year of the [[Avondpost "Wekeer" articles (October 1907)|"Wekeer" affair]] — the publication in *[[De Avondpost]]* in October [[1907]] of a series of articles by an anonymous *maréchaussée*-officer from Atjeh, exposing "nodeloze wreedheden" — needless cruelties — committed against the Atjehnese population, "toegelaten zo niet aangemoedigd door Van Daalen" — tolerated if not encouraged by [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]]. The year culminated in the resignation of [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] in December and the dispatch of governor-general [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] on an inspection tour of Atjeh the same month.

### The "Wekeer" articles

Under the pseudonym "Wekeer" — a *maréchaussée*-officer from Atjeh — a series of articles appeared in *[[De Avondpost]]* in October [[1907]] detailing needless cruelties allegedly committed against the Atjehnese population and tolerated, if not encouraged, by [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] in his capacity as governor and military commander. Minister [[D. Fock|Fock]] advised the chambermembers not to waste their time on these articles. The governor-general, he said, had full confidence in Van Daalen, and both he and Van Daalen considered reinforcement of the troop strength in Atjeh unnecessary.

### Van Heutsz's inspection tour

Pressed by the minister, [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] undertook an inspection tour of Atjeh in December [[1907]], "opdat ook het publiek overtuigd worde van datgene waarvan ook de gouverneur-generaal overtuigd is, dat wij in Atjeh op de goede weg zijn" — so that the public too may be convinced of that of which the governor-general is already convinced, that we are on the right path in Atjeh. The inspection proved otherwise. In the same month [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] submitted his resignation.

### What "the great bell" meant

The "grote klok" — great bell — of [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]'s warning to [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] at the time of his appointment in [[1905]] was, in the event, "Wekeer": the public exposure of atrocities in the press. The 1907 affair is the moment at which the contradiction between the [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacificatie]] rhetoric and the reality of occupation became impossible to contain.

### Significance

[[1907]] is the political crisis-year of the Atjehnese occupation. The fall of [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] — the most consequential single event of the post-conquest period — cleared the way for the [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] report of [[1908]] that would set the course of the [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacificatie]] for the next decade.
