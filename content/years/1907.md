## Additional Information from 1907

Figures, places, and events in Atjehnese history associated with the year 1907.

## People

- [[J. Kreemer]] — publication of his first scholarly article, [[Kreemer's De Maleier en zijn karbouw (1907)|*De Maleier en zijn karbouw*]], in volume I of *De Indische Gids* in 1907, composed when Kreemer was a young *Kontroleur B.B.* on [[Sumatra's Westkust|Sumatra's Westkust]].

## Documents

- [[Kreemer's De Maleier en zijn karbouw (1907)]] — Kreemer's first scholarly article, the predecessor of his [[Kreemer's Atjeh (1922-23)|*Atjeh* (1922-23)]] and [[Kreemer's De Karbouw (1956)|*De Karbouw* (1956)]].

## Places

- [[Sumatra's Westkust]] — the under-district in which Kreemer served as *Kontroleur B.B.* and on which his 1907 article was based.

## Sources

"De Maleier en zijn karbouw," by J. Kreemer, in *De Indische Gids* 1907, vol. I, pp. 953-964.

*De Karbouw — Zijn betekenis voor de volken van de Indonesische Archipel*, by J. Kreemer ('s-Gravenhage / Bandung: N.V. Uitgeverij W. van Hoeve, 1956), *Voorbericht* (pp. 5-6).
