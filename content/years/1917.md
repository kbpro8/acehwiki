## Statistical and Administrative Record
The year [[1917]] is primarily documented in the *Atjeh up to date* compilation through multi-year colonial spending plans. The surviving ledgers from this period focus on standardized, predictable provincial budgets:

*   **Budgetary Projections:** Colonial ledgers outline specific allocations for [[1917]], recording initial project or administrative spending at 154,650 guilders, with a broader aggregate budget category reaching 386,625 guilders.
*   **Administrative Consolidation:** These figures reflect a colony transitioning from the extreme, unpredictable costs of active guerrilla warfare to a stabilized, revenue-generating colonial province. The financial overhead of maintaining the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army|KNIL]] garrisons and local infrastructure was being absorbed into routine multi-year provincial budgets.


## The Atjehsche hoofden opleiding of 1916

The year [[1917]] is also the year in which the G.B. of 27 June [[1916]] No. 7 — by which, for the school year 1916/1917, up to 20 sons of Atjehsche chiefs could also be trained at the training school for Inlandsch teachers at Fort de Koek for the administrative sphere — came into effect. The Atjehsche hoofden opleiding of [[1916]]–[[1917]] is the staff history's principal instance of the Dutch colonial state's programme for the indigenisation of the Atjeh administrative officials, and the principal evidence of the Bureau's [[1917]] judgement that the programme was insufficient — both quantitatively and qualitatively.

## Account from *Atjeh* by H.C. Zentgraff (1938)

[[H.C. Zentgraff|Zentgraff]] records [[1917]] as the year the *vermaarde oelama* [[Tengkoe di Paja Bakong (Mata Ië)|Tengkoe di Paja Bakong]] — alias *Tgk. di Mata Ië*, the *Heer der bronnen* — was killed. On [[16 July]] [[1917]] a patrol under the Inlandsche sergeant Toegimin, patrolling near [[Samarkilang]], found an *onbegroeide ladang* with two *hutjes*. Two Atjehers were shot; one had a strongly-*opgezet been* from an earlier wound (the [[1913]] wound by [[Behrens]] at Aloeë Garöt). The Tengkoe's wife identified him by his *voetringen*. His followers formed the Samarkilang *bende*, whose head was not *gevangen genomen* until [[1937]], twenty years later.

Zentgraff also notes that [[1917]] was the year the *oelama's* of [[Peureula]] formally declared: *Er is geen oorlog, dus men kan geen sjahid (martelaar voor het geloof) worden* — "there is no war, so one cannot become a *[[sjahid]]* (martyr of the faith)." *Dacht het volk er anders over*, Zentgraff observes, *deze oorlog zou in de latere jaren een geheel ander karakter hebben gehad* — had the people thought differently, the war would have taken a wholly different character in the later years.

*Atjeh*, door H.C. Zentgraff. Gedrukt bij Drukkerij "De Unie", [[1938]], Batavia.
