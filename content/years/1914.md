**[[1914]]** was the year of a series of administrative reorganisations in the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] — the dissolution of the [[Kawaj XII|Kawaj XII]] federation on the Pedir highlands, the regularisation of the territorial boundaries of the [[Alaslanden|Alaslanden]], the relocation of the *Kédjoerön* of [[Batoe Mboelan|Batoe Mboelan]] to [[Poeloe Nas|Poeloe Nas]], and the redrawing of the boundary between [[Batoe Mboelan|Batoe Mboelan]] and [[Bambél|Bambél]] — that together represent the high-water mark of the Dutch policy of consolidating the *landschap* structure of the territory into regular territorial units amenable to indirect rule.

## The dissolution of the Kawaj XII

The *Kawaj XII* federation — the twelve gold-seeker settlements on the Pedir highlands between [[Beutông|Beutông]] and Keumala Dalam — had been depopulated by the decline of the gold workings and the cholera epidemic of [[1878]]. By [[1911]] the *geregistreerden* (registered inhabitants) numbered fewer than a thousand. On the grounds of this very small remaining population, the Dutch administration in [[1914]] brought an end to the independent existence of the twelve-membered *landschapje* by dividing it between the *onderafdeelingen* of [[Sigli|Sigli]] and [[Meulabôh|Meulabôh]]. At Sigli were joined: 1. Keumala Dalam, united with Keumala Raja to form the II Moekims Keumala; 2/3. Tangsé with Beunga; 4/5. Geumpang with Lamjeuë. At Meulabôh were joined: 6/7. Anoë with Pameuë; 8. Reunggeuët, attached to the [[Kawaj XVI|Kawaj XVI]]; 9/10. Toengkób with Ara. The territories of the two remaining *keudjeuroeëns* of the twelve could no longer be identified with certainty.

## The Alaslanden reorganisation

The same year saw the regularisation of the territorial boundaries of the [[Alaslanden|Alaslanden]]. The territory of the *Radja Tjèq of Alas|Radja Tjèq* in [[Batoe Mboelan|Batoe Mboelan]] was transferred to [[Bambél|Bambél]], ending the *Radja Tjèq*'s anomalous dual position in both *landschappen*. The *Kédjoerön* of [[Batoe Mboelan|Batoe Mboelan]] — who had moved his residence to [[Poeloe Nas|Poeloe Nas]] — signed a new [[Korte Verklaring|Korte Verklaring]] for the *landschap* of that name. The two *landschappen* were each divided into four *mérga*'s under *Péngoeloe Si-émpat* as *bezoldigde hoofden* (salaried chiefs): the *mérga*'s of Poeloe Nas were *Klis* under *Imom Balei*, *Képala Dèsa* under *Batin*, *Kling Kérétan*, and *Tjébéro* under *Béntjirim*; the *mérga*'s of Bambél were *'ngKran* under *'ngKran*, *Béroeh* under *Biaq Moeli*, *Sékédang* under *'nDjaar*, and *Sélian* under the *Radja Tjèq Batoe Mboelan*.

## The handwerkscholen and the A.V.R.O.S.

[[1914]] was also the year of the founding of the *handwerkscholen* — handicraft schools — in [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] and [[Pedir|Pedir]], on the initiative of [[H. N. A. Swart|Governor Swart]], and the year in which most of the rubber *ondernemingen* of the East Coast joined the [[A.V.R.O.S.|Algemeene Vereeniging van Rubberplanters ter Oostkust van Sumatra]] (A.V.R.O.S.), the planters' association that would coordinate labour recruitment and other common concerns.
