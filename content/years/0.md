**0** was a year of significance in the history of [[Aceh]], as recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Beginning of our era: Hindu contact begins.

## Events

Beginning of our era: Hindu contact begins. Hindus from Voor-Indië sought these lands for camphor, ivory, gold, [[Kajoe garoe|agarwood]], spices. Atjeh was the first land they reached. Foundation of [[Samoedra-Pasè]] kingdom in first 12 centuries CE.

## Significance

The year 0 is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the chronological framework of [[Aceh]]'s history.
