The approximate year in which the Leiden manuscript Cod. Or. 3291 of the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān fī maʿrifat al-adyān*]] was copied for [[Herman Neubronner van der Tuuk|Van der Tuuk]], from a good older exemplar of Achehnese provenance. The Leiden manuscript is the only complete manuscript of the *Tibjān* and is the basis of [[P. Voorhoeve|Voorhoeve]]'s facsimile edition of [[1955]]. Its Achehnese provenance is betrayed by such forms as *sidjuk* (Ach. *sidjueʾ*) for *sedjuk*, and by the consistent replacement of *kesembilan* with *kesemilan* (cf. Ach. *seumah*, Mal. *sembah*).
