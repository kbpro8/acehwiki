## Founding of the Baleq turpentine enterprise (1924)

The year [[1924]] was the founding year of the [[Baleq|Baleq]] enterprise — the colonial government's turpentine and resin factory in the [[Gajo-landen|Gajo-Landen]]-[[Atjeh|Atjeh]] border region. By the [[Besluit van 27 June 1924 on Atjeh-turpentine exploitation|Besluit (government decision) of 27 June 1924]], the Regeering (colonial government) of the [[Nederlandsch-Indië|Netherlands Indies]] placed funds at the disposal of *'s Lands Caoutchouchedrijf* (the Government Rubber Enterprise) for an *exploitatie-proef* (exploitation trial) of Atjeh-turpentine, to be carried out on behalf of the *Dienst van het Boschwezen* (the Forestry Service).

The implementation began on [[14 September]] [[1924]] in the *Semnenboschcomplexen* (Sumatran-pine forest complexes) of [[Paja Djorok|Paja Djorok]] and [[Blangtekoer|Blangtekoer]] — both near the road from [[Takengon|Takengon]] to [[Bireuen|Bireuen]] in Atjeh. The two complexes were separated by the river [[Baleq|Baleq]], from which the enterprise took its name. The trial exploited the [[Pinus Merkusii|Pinus Merkusii]] (Sumatran pine) forests of the two *Semnenboschcomplexen*, and the first trial samples were produced in the laboratory of the *Afdeeling Nijverheid* (Industry Department) at [[Buitenzorg|Buitenzorg]] from the balsam collected by the foresters of the *Dienst van het Boschwezen*.

The same year, the [[Afdeeling Handelsmuseum|Afdeeling Handelsmuseum]] (Commercial Museum Department) of the [[Koninklijke Vereeniging Koloniaal Instituut|Koninklijke Vereeniging Koloniaal Instituut]] (Royal Colonial Institute Society) exchanged thoughts with the Board of the *[[Vereeniging van Vernis- en Verffabrikanten en -Handelaren in Nederland|Vereeniging van Vernis- en Verffabrikanten en -Handelaren in Nederland]]* (V.V.V.F. — the Association of Varnish and Paint Manufacturers and Traders in the Netherlands) on the quality of the Sumatran turpentine oil derived from [[Pinus Merkusii|Pinus Merkusii]]. The V.V.V.F. considered the odour of the trial product *te zeer afwijkende geacht van dien van zuivere terpentijnolie uit Frankrijk of Noord-Amerika* ("too different from that of pure turpentine oil from France or North America"). The consultation would be revisited in [[1930]].

- [[Takengon]] — Town near the [[Baleq|Baleq]] enterprise.
- [[Bireuen]] — Town near the [[Baleq|Baleq]] enterprise.

## Concepts (additional)

- [[Pinus Merkusii]] — The Sumatran pine, the principal raw material of the new enterprise.
- [[Atjeh-terpentijn]] — The turpentine oil of the new enterprise.
- [[Quarre]] — The French tapping method applied at the new enterprise.
