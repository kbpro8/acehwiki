## Account from *Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.* (1954) — 1880

**[[1880]]** is mentioned in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* in connection with the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]] — "[[Samalanga]] in [[1880]]. Tot driemaal toe werd..." — as the year of the Dutch attempt(s) on [[Samalanga]], which [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] would finally capture in [[1901]]. The persistence of Samalanga as a target across twenty years marks the slow pace of the early colonial campaigns.

*Source: Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.*, by G. W. T. Dames, Maj. Inf. K.N.I.L. b.d. ('s-Gravenhage: Koninklijke Bibliotheek, 1954).
