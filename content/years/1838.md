**1838** was a year in which the [[Dutch colonial administration]] intensified its military campaign against the *Padri* insurgency on *[[Sumatra]]*, and in which several notable figures, events, and documents are recorded in Acehnese history.

## Key Event  

- **Expedition against the Padri's (1838)** – detailed in *De Geschiedenis van het Nederlandsche Korps Mariniers* (p. 104), this operation involved marine officers such as [[Lieutenant ’s Jacob]], [[W. A. Lans]], and [[A. van Vloten]] and sought to suppress the Padri insurgency.

## Wider Context  

The year fell within a period of relative peace for the Dutch marine corps after the separation of Belgium ([[1830]]–[[1846]]) and before the intensification of colonial expeditions to [[Bali]] ([[1846]]–[[1849]]) and later to [[Aceh]] ([[1873]]–[[1904]]).
