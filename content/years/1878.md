## Account from *De Buitenbezittingen* (1917) — 1878

**[[1878]]** was the year in which the [[Sultan of Langkat|Sultan of Langkat]] formally transferred his rights over the [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] landschapjes to the Netherlands-Indies Government, extinguishing the Langkat claim to suzerainty that had been the source of intermittent conflict since the early nineteenth-century intervention of [[Soetan Moeda Indra Kesoema|Soetan Moeda Indra Kesoema]]. The Encyclopaedisch Bureau's *De Buitenbezittingen* treats [[1878]] as the terminus of a long Langkat-Tamiang dispute and the beginning of the formal Dutch administration that would, in [[1908]], be regularised through the incorporation of Tamiang into the *indirect bestuurd gebied* of the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]].

## The antecedents of the transfer

The Langkat claim to Tamiang rested on the fact that *Tjoet Gát* — the warrior who had led the combined Tamiang forces to victory against adventurers from [[Meureudoe|Meureudoe]] and had died in that battle — had been a subject of the [[Sultan of Langkat|Sultan of Langkat]]. The grant of the [[Bandahara|Bandahara]] territory to Tjoet Gát's heir, made by the two *Keudjeuroeën* of Karang and Moeda in gratitude for his service, was thus exploited by Langkat as a *voorwendsel* — a pretext — for proclaiming its own suzerainty over the new *landschapje*. The subsequent appointment of [[Soetan Moeda Indra Kesoema|Soetan Moeda Indra Kesoema]] as ruler of part of the Bandahara territory provoked the armed resistance of the next Radja Bandahara, supported by [[Atjeh|Atjeh]] and [[Langsa|Langsa]] — a war that remained unresolved for years.

## The 1861 appeal and its aftermath

In [[1861]] the [[Sultan of Langkat|Sultan of Langkat]] exploited the presence of Dutch warships off [[Poeloe Kompai|Poeloe Kompai]] to summon the Tamiang princes and induce them to appeal to the Dutch *Gouvernement* and offer their submission. The Keudjeuroeën Karang and Moeda answered the summons, and at the same occasion recognised Soetan Moeda Indra Kesoema as the legitimate ruler of the Bandahara fragment. In [[1865]] the Dutch Government issued formal acts of confirmation to the Keudjeuroeën Karang, the Keudjeuroeën Moeda, and Soetan Moeda Indra Kesoema.

## The 1878 transfer

The formal transfer of [[1878]] extinguished the remaining Langkat rights over the Tamiang *landschapjes*. The Dutch *Gouvernement*, having acquired a *vrije hand* in the administration of the territory, placed a *bestuursambtenaar* — a civil administrator — in the Tamiang lands. The continuous military action required by the presence of *vele uit Atjeh verdreven kwaadwilligen en avonturiers* — many evildoers and adventurers driven out of Atjeh — in these largely wilderness *landschapjes* eventually compelled the Dutch to unify the command, and in [[1908]] Tamiang was formally incorporated into the *indirect bestuurd gebied* of the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]], though its economic ties remained with the *Cultuurgebied* of the [[Oostkust van Sumatra|Oostkust van Sumatra]].
