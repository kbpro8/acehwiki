**570** is the Gregorian year traditionally assigned to the birth of the Prophet Muhammad. The source mentions this year on [[83]] in the context of Abraha’s expedition: “in het jaar waarin de profeet Muhammad geboren werd, 570.” This dating is significant for Acehnese Islamic historiography, which aligns local chronicles with the Hijri calendar.

## Relation to Aceh

Acehnese chronicles often convert the Hijri year of Muhammad’s birth (12 Rabiʿ al‑Awwal 571 AH) to the Gregorian year 570 CE for synchronisation with European sources.
