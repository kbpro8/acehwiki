**[[1579]]** marks the year in which the Acehnese sultanate [[Atjēh]] conquered the Malay state of [[Perak]], initiating a period of Acehnese dominance over the northern Malay Peninsula.

## Conquest of Perak
In [[1579]] Acehnese forces invaded Perak, defeating its ruler and annexing the territory. The source explicitly states: “Perak … in 1579 door Atjēh veroverd”【20260707_232246_DLP-54-Een_16de_eeuwse_Maleise_vertaling_van_de_Burda_van_al_Bu_s_i_ri_OCR.txt, p. 45】.

## Aftermath
The conquest integrated Perak’s tin wealth into the Acehnese economy and brought the region under Acehnese administrative control. This dominance persisted until the early 17th century, when [[Sultan Iskandar Muda]] reaffirmed Acehnese overlordship.
