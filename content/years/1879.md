## Account from *Oost-Indisch Journael* (1947) — 1879

**[[1879]]** was the year of the laying of the first stone of the [[Meusigit Raja]] of [[Koeta-Radja|Koeta Radja]] on [[9 October]] [[1879]] — in the presence of Governor-General [[Van Lansberge|Van Lansberge]], all the civil and military authorities, and many Acehnese chiefs and clergy. The first stone — the colonial state's formal beginning of the rebuilding — is the marker of the colonial state's consent and cooperation in the rebuilding of the Meusigit Raja, which had been promised in [[generaal Van Swieten|Van Swieten]]'s missive of [[8 February]] [[1874]] following the [[1873]] storming of the mosque.

The book's sequence is: the [[1873]] storming of the mosque (the burning-down and demolition) — the [[1874]] Van Swieten missive (the well-known lines) — the [[1879]] laying of the first stone (in the presence of Van Lansberge) — the [[1881]] completion and the [[27 December]] [[1881]] solemn transfer (by [[Pruys van der Hoeven|Pruys van der Hoeven]]). The [[1879]] first stone is therefore the marker of the formal beginning of the rebuilding: the colonial state's laying of the first stone of the Meusigit Raja.
