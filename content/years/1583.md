The end of the first visit of [[Muhammad Djailani Hamid]] to [[Acheh]]. Finding little interest in his teaching of logic, rhetoric, dogmatics, and the doctrine of duties, the scholar sailed on to [[Mecca]] to obtain the *venia docendi* as a teacher of mystical theology, with which he returned to Acheh and then had great success. The experience was decisive for his nephew [[Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]], who, when he himself left for Acheh in [[1637]], was thoroughly prepared in mysticism, in the *[[Bahasa Djawi]]*, and in the production of Malay religious writings.
