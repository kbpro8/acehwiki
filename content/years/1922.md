Figures, places, and events in Atjehnese history associated with the year 1922.

## People

- [[J. Kreemer]] — publication of volume I of his [[Kreemer's Atjeh (1922-23)|*Atjèh*]] monograph in 1922.
- [[L. C. Westenenk]] — publication of his [[Westenenk's Rëntjong-schrift, II (1922)|*Rëntjong-schrift, II, Beschreven hoorns in het landschap Krintji*]] article in 1922.

## Documents

- [[Kreemer's Atjeh (1922-23)]] — volume I published in 1922 (volume II followed in 1923).
- [[Westenenk's Rëntjong-schrift, II (1922)]] — article on the rencong / surat ulú script family and the practice of inscribing buffalo horns.

## Concepts

- [[rëntjong-schrift]] — the pre-Islamic Sumatran script family (rencong / ulú / surat ulú) documented by Westenenk in 1922.

## Sources

*Atjèh*, by J. Kreemer, vol. I (1922).

"Rëntjong-schrift, II, Beschreven hoorns in het landschap Krintji," by L. C. Westenenk, in *Tijdschrift van het Bataviaasch Genootschap van Kunsten en Wetenschappen*, 1922.
