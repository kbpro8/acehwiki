**1672** marks the **[[Rampjaar]]**, when the **[[United Provinces (Dutch Republic)]]** faced coordinated attacks by **[[England|England]]**, **[[France|France]]**, and **[[Munster (Germany)|Munster]]** following the secret **[[Treaty of Dover (1670)]].** The crisis weakened Dutch naval power, reducing the protection of **[[VOC]]** shipping to the **[[Sultanate of Aceh]]** and other overseas possessions.
