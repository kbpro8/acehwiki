Figures, places, and events in Acehnese history associated with the year [[1953]].

## Events

- [[Atjèh's accession to the Negara Islam Indonesia (1953)|Atjèh's accession to the Negara Islam Indonesia]] — the armed rebellion proclaimed on [[21 September]] [[1953]] by [[Teungkoe Moehamad Daoed Beureu-éh|Teungkoe Moehamad Daoed Beureu-éh]], by which Atjeh joined the [[Negara Islam Indonesia]] led by the Imam [[Kartosuwirjo|Kartosuwirjo]] and took up arms against the Republic of Indonesia. The *[[De staatkundige ontwikkeling van Indonesië in de 20e eeuw|staatkundige ontwikkeling]]* survey ([[Koninklijk Instituut voor de Tropen|Koninklijk Instituut voor de Tropen]], revised ed. [[1955]]) records the outbreak in its chronological survey under "Augustus 1953 — Onder leiding van Daoed Beurouch breekt een opstand uit in Atjeh" — In August 1953, under the leadership of Daud Beureu'eh, a revolt breaks out in Atjeh — the August date reflecting the beginning of the unrest, while the formal proclamation followed on [[21 September]] [[1953]].
- The reconquest by Colonel [[Simbolon|Simbolon]] of the Bukit Barisan territorial command proceeded through October and November [[1953]] — [[Seulimeum|Seulimeum]] and [[Bajeunen|Bajeunen]] on [[3 October]], [[Meureudu|Meureudu]] on [[10 October]], [[Blangkedjeren|Blangkedjeren]] and [[Bireuën|Biruën]] on [[20 October]], and finally [[Takengon|Takengon]] on [[24 November]] [[1953]].
- On [[1 August]] [[1953]] the cabinet of [[Mr. Ali Sastroamidjojo|Ali Sastroamidjojo]] was formed, from which the Masjumi was excluded — the political trigger that, according to Alers, removed Daud Beureu'eh's last motive for patience.
- On [[1 August]] [[1953]], [[Kahar Muzakkar|Kahar Muzakkar]], the guerrilla leader in South Celebes, joined the Negara Islam Indonesia.
- On [[20 October]] [[1953]], [[Mr. S. M. Amin|Mr. S. M. Amin]] took office as the new Governor of [[North Sumatra]].
