The date is established by [[H. Djajadiningrat|Djajadiningrat]] in *TBG* 69, pp. 109–111; it corrects the earlier assumption of [[P. Voorhoeve|Voorhoeve]] (*BKI* 107, p. 357) that the *Ṣirāṭ* had been composed before Raniri's arrival in [[Acheh]]. The knowledge of Malay letters displayed in the *Ṣirāṭ* need not, therefore, have been acquired before [[1637]].

## Account from *Over Maleise Literatuur* (1947) — 1634

In [[C. Hooykaas|Hooykaas]]'s survey of Malay literature, the year [[1634]] is the year in which [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]] began the composition of the *[[Sirat al-mustaqim|Sirāṭ al-mustaqīm]]* — the Malay handbook of religious duties (*fiqh*) of the Shāfiʿite school. The date was established by [[H. Djajadiningrat|Djajadiningrat]] in *TBG* 69, pp. 109-111, and corrects the earlier assumption of [[P. Voorhoeve|Voorhoeve]] (*BKI* 107, p. 357) that the *Sirāṭ* had been composed before ar-Rānīrī's arrival in [[Acheh|Atjeh]] (which is dated to [[1637]]).

### The Beginning of the Sirat al-mustaqim

The *[[Sirat al-mustaqim|Sirāṭ al-mustaqīm]]* — begun in [[1634]] and completed only in [[1644]] — is the principal Malay-language *fiqh* handbook produced at the Atjehnese court. Its composition, begun three years before ar-Rānīrī's formal arrival in Atjeh in [[1637]], suggests that ar-Rānīrī had begun his Malay *fiqh* project before his formal appointment at the Atjehnese court — perhaps during an earlier visit to the region, or in correspondence with the Atjehnese court from his native Gujarat.

### The Place in ar-Raniri's Project

The [[1634]] beginning of the *Sirāṭ* falls in the early part of ar-Rānīrī's Atjehnese period — the period that would culminate, in [[1638]], with the composition of the *[[Sirat al-mustaqim|Sirāṭ]]* (completed in March [[1638]]), the *[[Bustanu's-Salatin|Bustān as-Salāṭīn]]* ([[1638]]), and the *Bad' khalq as-samawat wal-arwah* ([[1638]]). The three works together constitute the literary apparatus of ar-Rānīrī's orthodox reform of the Atjehnese court — the reform that targeted the *Wujudiyyah* mystics who had been patronised under [[Sultan Iskandar Muda|Iskandar Muda]].

### The Correction of Voorhoeve's Dating

The [[1634]] date — established by Djajadiningrat — corrects the earlier assumption of Voorhoeve that the *Sirāṭ* had been composed before ar-Rānīrī's arrival in Atjeh. The correction establishes that "the knowledge of Malay letters displayed in the *Sirāṭ* need not, therefore, have been acquired before [[1637]]" — that is, ar-Rānīrī could have learned the Malay letters he needed for the *Sirāṭ* after his arrival in Atjeh, not before.

*The Achehnese*, Vol. I, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
