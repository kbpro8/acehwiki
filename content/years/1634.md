The year in which [[Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]] began the composition of the [[Sirat al-mustaqim|*Ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm*]], the Malay handbook of religious duties that he was to complete only ten years later, in [[1644]]. The date is established by [[H. Djajadiningrat|Djajadiningrat]] in *TBG* 69, pp. 109–111; it corrects the earlier assumption of [[P. Voorhoeve|Voorhoeve]] (*BKI* 107, p. 357) that the *Ṣirāṭ* had been composed before Raniri's arrival in [[Acheh]]. The knowledge of Malay letters displayed in the *Ṣirāṭ* need not, therefore, have been acquired before [[1637]].
