**[[1875]]** is mentioned in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* in connection with the colonial administration of [[Atjeh|Aceh]] — "Regent Jane Sowale in [[1875]] opdracht om met zijn 200..." — as one of the years of the early Dutch colonial administration of Aceh after the [[Tweede Atjeh-expeditie (1873-1874)|Tweede Atjeh-expeditie]].
