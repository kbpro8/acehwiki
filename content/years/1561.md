The year of the birth of [[Muhammad al-Addars|Muḥammad al-ʿAddārs]] at [[Tarim]] in South Arabia. Al-ʿAddārs, the spiritual grandfather of [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]] through the latter's shaikh [[Sajjid Abu Hafs Umar Ba Sjaiban|Sayyid ʿUmar Bā Shaybān]], was to come to Gudjarāt at the age of nineteen, succeeding his famous grandfather as religious teacher first at [[Ahmadabad]] and later at [[Surat]], where he died in [[1620]].
