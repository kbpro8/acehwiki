**[[1932]]** was the year in which the author first met [[Dominee Thenu]] on the beach of [[Bakongan]] — "De eerste maal, dat ik Dominee Thenu ontmoette, was eind [[1932]] op het strand van Bakongan." Thenu was then "al vierenzestig" years old. The year is the book's marker of the beginning of the author's personal acquaintance with the *Soldaten-Dominee*.
