**[[1932]]** was the year in which the author first met [[Dominee Thenu]] on the beach of [[Bakongan]] — "De eerste maal, dat ik Dominee Thenu ontmoette, was eind [[1932]] op het strand van Bakongan." Thenu was then "al vierenzestig" years old. The year is the book's marker of the beginning of the author's personal acquaintance with the *Soldaten-Dominee*.

## Publication of *Het recht op den grond in Atjeh* ([[5 January]] [[1932]])

The instalments of [[D. W. N. de Boer]]'s study *[[Het recht op den grond in Atjeh (De Boer)|Het recht op den grond in Atjeh]]* appeared in print in [[1932]], across pages 59 to 80 of a Netherlands-Indies colonial periodical. The running head of page 65 of the offprint carries the notation "1932 Januari 5," fixing the instalment containing that page to [[5 January]] [[1932]].

The study itself had been completed and signed at [[Scheveningen]] in November [[1931]]. The offprint, catalogued as DLP-141, entered the library of the [[Koninklijk Instituut voor Taal-, Land- en Volkenkunde]] at Galewstraat 159, 's-Gravenhage, where it bears the address stamp of the institute and the accession marks "BIBLIOTHEEK KITLV 0060 4460 60 081470 428." It remains one of the few systematic descriptions of Atjehnese customary land law written from the bench rather than the study, preserving a body of technical Atjehnese legal vocabulary — *[[Haq radja taléë|haq radja taléë]]*, *[[Thiplah tano|thiplah tano]]*, *[[Haq toeleuèng|haq toeleuèng]]*, *[[Padé kandoeri|padé kandoeri]]*, *[[Aboen meu'oeë|aboen meu'oeë]]*, *[[Atra peunoelang|atra peunoelang]]* — not recorded in comparable detail elsewhere.

## Account from *Verkenningen in Atjeh* (1923)

**1932** was the year of publication of [[Ir. W. Spoon|Ir. W. Spoon]]'s *[[Atjeh Patchouli-Olie (1932)|Atjeh Patchouli-Olie]]* — *Bericht* No. 71 of the [[Afdeeling Handelsmuseum|Afdeeling Handelsmuseum]] of the [[Koninklijke Vereeniging Koloniaal Instituut|Kon. Vereeniging Koloniaal Instituut]], reprinted from *De Indische Mercuur* of [[20 July]] [[1932]]. The pamphlet is the second of Spoon's two *Berichten* on the patchouli industry of Atjeh, the first being *Bericht* No. 21 of May [[1925]]. Together they form the principal printed source for the early history of the [[Patchouli-olie industry in Atjeh|patchouli-oil industry in Atjeh]].
