The year **[[1616]]** marked the lifting of the [[VOC trading post at Aceh]] by the [[Dutch East India Company]] because of the "tyranny and bad treatment" of the sultan towards Dutch merchants, as recorded in the Company's documents: "overmits de tyrannye ende het quaet tractement." This was not the final closure; the factorij was re-established in [[1620]], but it was a significant setback for Dutch trade with Aceh.

## Aceh Context

The sultan of Aceh at that time was [[Sultan Iskandar Muda|Iskander Moeda]], who reigned from 1607 to 1636. His militant policies and interference in trade made him a difficult partner. The lifting of the factorij in 1616 reflected the ongoing tensions between the VOC and Aceh, which would continue to fluctuate over the following decades. The post was reopened in 1620 only to be lifted again in 1623.
