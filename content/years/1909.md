## Account from *Atjeh up to date* (c. 1914) — 1909

**1909** was a year of continued administrative recording and logistical tracking in [[Acheh]], situated within the [[Dutch East Indies]]. Following the major military campaigns of the preceding years, the colonial authorities focused on quantifying the economic output and the ongoing costs of garrisoning the region during the latter stages of the [[Aceh War]].

## Statistical and Administrative Record

According to the *Atjeh up to date* compilation, [[1909]] is heavily documented in colonial financial and logistical ledgers. The surviving data from this year highlights the scale of Dutch operations in northern [[Sumatra]]:

*   **Financial Expenditures:** Aggregate colonial financial lines for [[1909]] record massive outlays, with total figures reaching 6,077,025 guilders across various administrative and military departments. Specific departmental ledgers show distinct allocations, such as 1,283,439 and 767,017 in separate accounting categories.
*   **Trade and Logistics:** Registries of vessel arrivals and tonnage indicate a steady flow of colonial supplies and export commodities. Monthly and annual tracking shows figures such as 96,093 and 48,240 in primary logistical categories, alongside 102,402 and 123,325 in secondary trade metrics.
*   **Military Overhead:** The financial burden of maintaining the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army|KNIL]] garrisons and conducting localized patrols is reflected in the high baseline expenditures, a trend that would continue to fluctuate in the years leading up to [[1914]].
