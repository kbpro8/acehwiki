**1290** was a year of significance in the history of [[Aceh]], as recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Marco Polo (c.

## Events

[[Marco Polo]] (c. 1290) mentioned six 'koninkrijken' (kingdoms) in the Atjeh region - evidence of multiple polities with extensive foreign trade.

## Significance

The year 1290 is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the chronological framework of [[Aceh]]'s history.
