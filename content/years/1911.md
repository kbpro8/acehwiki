**1911** was a year of complex administrative accounting and ongoing logistical management in [[Acheh]], situated within the [[Dutch East Indies]]. As the Dutch administration continued to stabilize its control over northern [[Sumatra]], colonial records meticulously tracked the financial overhead of the [[Aceh War]]'s aftermath, alongside the movement of export commodities and colonial supplies.

## Statistical and Administrative Record

The *Atjeh up to date* compilation preserves extensive tabular data for [[1911]], illustrating the material scale of the colonial enterprise:

*   **Financial Expenditures:** Aggregate colonial financial lines for [[1911]] record outlays reaching 7,757,821 guilders. Specific departmental ledgers show distinct allocations, such as 1,374,616 and 1,898,980 in separate accounting categories, alongside secondary ledgers showing 971,916 and 933,063.
*   **Trade and Logistics:** Monthly and annual tracking of shipping and trade metrics shows highly variable figures, including 79,850; 166,450; 108,678; 208,104; and 300,365 across various regional and commodity categories.
*   **Shipping Registries:** Vessel arrivals and tonnage registries for the year record specific logistical movements indexed at 107, 243, and 75 in primary tracking columns, with further metrics at 106,600 and 180,929.

## Source

*Atjeh up to date* (Statistical and Administrative Compilation, c. [[1914]]). Archival reference DLP-10. Fragmentary OCR extraction containing financial ledgers, shipping registries, and colonial budget tables for [[1911]].

## Account from *Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.* (1954) — 1911

**[[1911]]** was the closing year of the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]]'s counter-insurgency phase — the year in which the [[Colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]] destroyed the *[[Teungkoe Sjech Saman di Tiri|Tiriō bende]]*. In May 1911 the small resistance band of *[[Peutoeha-Gam-Masen|Peutoeha Gam Masén]]* was surrounded near the [[Kroeng-Seukoeleh|Kroeëng Seukoeleh]] and Gam Masén was killed by the korporaal [[Nussy]]. Later in May the colonne surprised the schuilplaats of the Tiriō bende. On [[3 December]] [[1911]] the korporaal [[Nussy]] — alone after his *spits* turned back — shot and brought down *[[Tgk. Tjhi Ma'at]]*, the last leader of the bende, who became *[[sjahid]]*: "De Tiri-bende was niet meer." The militaire closure of the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]] was complete; the political closure would come with the [[Handover of Aceh to a civil Gouverneur (1918)|handover of 1918]].

*Source: Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.*, by G. W. T. Dames, Maj. Inf. K.N.I.L. b.d. ('s-Gravenhage: Koninklijke Bibliotheek, 1954), chapter "De colonne Schmidt."

## Account from *De Buitenbezittingen* (1917) — 1911

**[[1911]]** was the year in which the [[Bureau van Financieele aangelegenheden|Bureau van Financieele aangelegenheden (Bureau of Financial Affairs)]] was established by Gouvernementsbesluit (G. B.) of [[30 January]] [[1911]] No. 3 — the institutional instrument of the [[1910]] "mijlpaal" (milestone) of centralised oversight of the [[Landschapskassen|landschapskassen (territorial treasuries)]] of the [[Zelfbesturen|zelfbesturen (self-governing territories)]] of the [[Buitenbezittingen|Outer Possessions]], including the [[Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh and Dependencies]] residency. The establishment of the Bureau van Financieele aangelegenheden in [[1911]] was the formal institutional translation of the [[1910]] mijlpaal — the bureau through which the central Gouvernement exercised its oversight of the territorial treasuries of the Buitenbezittingen.

The Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account of the [[Landschapskassen|landschapskassen]] notes that the [[1910]] mijlpaal was followed, in [[1911]], by the establishment of the [[Bureau van Financieele aangelegenheden|Bureau van Financieele aangelegenheden]]: "[[1914|to that end]] was established by G. B. of [[30 January]] [[1911]] No. 3 the 'Bureau van Financieele aangelegenheden,' from which the central leadership (since [[1913]] also including actual oversight of the technical management) of the [[Landschapskassen|landschapskassen]] proceeds."

The Bureau's statistical tables for the [[Landschapskassen|landschapskassen]] show that, in [[1911]], the [[Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh and Dependencies]] residency had total landschapskas income of f 355,752 — a substantial increase from the [[1910]] figure of f 264,910, and an index of the rapid consolidation of the Dutch administrative order in Atjeh in the immediate aftermath of the [[1910]] mijlpaal. The [[1911]] figure would rise further to f 748,802 by [[1913]] — the principal financial index of the post-[[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacification]] consolidation.

## Significance

[[1911]] is, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, the year of the formal institutional translation of the [[1910]] mijlpaal of centralised oversight of the [[Landschapskassen|landschapskassen]] — the year in which the [[Bureau van Financieele aangelegenheden|Bureau van Financieele aangelegenheden]] was established to provide central leadership and oversight of the territorial treasuries of the Buitenbezittingen, including the [[Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh and Dependencies]] residency. The [[1911]] establishment of the Bureau is one of the principal administrative consolidations of the post-[[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacification]] order in Atjeh.
