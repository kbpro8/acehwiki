The year **[[1620]]** saw the re-establishment of the [[VOC trading post at Aceh]] by the [[Dutch East India Company]], after it had been lifted in [[1616]]. The re-establishment was short-lived; the factorij was again lifted in [[1623]], and thereafter only a single assistant remained.

## Aceh Context

The sultan [[Sultan Iskandar Muda|Iskander Moeda]] was still in power, and the Dutch sought to maintain a presence in Aceh despite the difficult relations. The re-establishment in 1620 indicates that the Company still valued the pepper and other trade opportunities, but the repeated closures reflect the volatile nature of the relationship. After 1623, the Company abandoned the idea of a permanent factorij and relied on periodic visits and treaties.
