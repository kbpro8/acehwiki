**16e-17e eeuw (16th-17th century)** was a year of significance in the history of [[Aceh]], as recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. 

## Significance

The year 16e-17e eeuw (16th-17th century) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the chronological framework of [[Aceh]]'s history.
