**The year 1940** was, in the [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|Dutch staff history's]] account (Deel VI, Hoofdstuk V), the year of the opening phase of the Dutch pre-invasion force drawdown in [[Atjeh|Atjeh]] — the year in which the [[4de Garnizoensbataljon|4de Garnizoensbataljon (the "Mobiele Reserve")]] was dissolved, the [[Westkustcolonne|Westkustcolonne]] was formed and transferred out of Atjeh, and the structural foundations of the Dutch pre-invasion force were dismantled. The year [[1940]] is the *terminus a quo* of the Dutch pre-invasion drawdown that would hollow out the Atjeh garrison on the eve of the [[Japanese invasion of Atjèh|Japanese invasion of March 1942]].

## The 1 January 1940 troop strength

As of [[1 January]] [[1940]] the Dutch garrison in Atjeh comprised:

- **Territoriale Staf** at [[Kotaradja|Kotaradja]]
- **1ste Garnizoensbataljon** (27 brigades): staff Kotaradja; [[Tapatoean|Tapatoean]] 4, [[Blang Pidië|Blang Pidië]] 4, [[Meulaboh|Meulaboh]] 4, [[Tjalang|Tjalang]] 4, [[Lam No|Lam No]] 3, [[Lokop|Lokop]] 4, [[Kotatjane|Kotatjane]] 4.
- **2de Garnizoensbataljon** (30 brigades): staff Kotaradja with 23 brigades; [[Lho'Nga|Lho'Nga]] 3, [[Seulimeum|Seulimeum]] 4.
- **3de Garnizoensbataljon** (34 brigades): staff Kotaradja; [[Padang Tidji|Padang Tidji]] 3, [[Sigli|Sigli]] 8, [[Meureudoe|Meureudoe]] 3, [[Samalanga|Samalanga]] 4, [[Bireuën|Bireuën]] 4, [[Lho Seumawé|Lho Semawe]] 4, [[Boeloe Blang Ara|Boeloe Blang Ara]] 4, [[Langsa|Langsa]] 4.
- **4de Garnizoensbataljon** (16 brigades): staff Kotaradja with 16 brigades (2 at [[Sabang|Sabang]] for police duties); the "Mobiele Reserve."
- **Korps Maréchaussee** (60 brigades): Iste Divisie (12 brigades at [[Blangkedjeren|Blangkedjeren]] and [[Koeala Bee|Koeala Bee]]); IIde Divisie (8 at [[Djeuram|Djeuram]] and [[Lam Ie|Lam Ie]]); IIIde Divisie (15 at [[Takengon|Takengon]] and [[Lam Meulo|Lam Meulo]]); IVde Divisie (6 at [[Tangsé|Tangsé]] and [[Geumpang|Geumpang]]); Vde Divisie (12 at [[Lho Soekoen|Lho Soekoen]], [[Paja Bakong|Paja Bakong]] and [[Panton Laboe|Panton Laboe]]); VIde Divisie (7 at [[Bakongan|Bakongan]]).

**Total**: 107 brigades of garrison infantry (about 2,250 men) + 60 brigades of Maréchaussee (1,200 men) = 3,450 men.

## The 1940 reductions

The [[1940]] drawdown comprised the following reductions:

- **April 1940**: One company of the 4de Garnizoensbataljon sent to the Riouw Archipelago; in May a second company sent to Java. The 4de Garnizoensbataljon, the "Mobiele Reserve," disappeared entirely from Atjeh.
- **Shortly after the outbreak of war with Germany (May 1940)**: The outposts at [[Bireuën|Bireuën]], [[Langsa|Langsa]] and [[Kotatjane|Kotatjane]] (12 brigades) were evacuated and assigned to a wartime task outside Atjeh. The [[Westkustcolonne|Westkustcolonne]] of nine brigades was formed from the Tapatoean, Meulaboh and Tjalang detachments; Tapatoean and Meulaboh were dissolved; Tjalang was reduced by one brigade. The Westkustcolonne was sent to the Oostkust van Sumatra, eventually to [[Lawe Sigalagala|Lawe Sigalagala]] for the German internee camp. The soldiers never returned to Atjeh; their families were moved to Kotaradja.
- The established strength of all remaining Atjeh brigades was reduced by 2 men per brigade, for the formation of one company also sent to the Oostkust van Sumatra.

The staff history records that, as a result of the above transfers, the strength of the troops stationed in Atjeh had already been reduced by one quarter.

## Significance

The year [[1940]] is, in the [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|staff history's]] Atjeh chapter, the *terminus a quo* of the Dutch pre-invasion force drawdown that hollowed out the Atjeh garrison on the eve of the [[Japanese invasion of Atjèh|Japanese invasion of March 1942]]. The staff history's emphasis on the dissolution of the [[4de Garnizoensbataljon|4de Garnizoensbataljon ("Mobiele Reserve")]], on the formation and removal of the [[Westkustcolonne|Westkustcolonne]], and on the reduction of the established strength of all remaining brigades frames the [[1940]] drawdown as the structural cause of the Dutch inability to suppress the [[F-beweging|F. beweging]] rising of February–March [[1942]] or to mount a sustained guerrilla after the Japanese landings. The year [[1940]] thus marks the beginning of the end of the Dutch colonial military presence in Atjeh.
