**The year [[1940]]** was, in the [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|Dutch staff history's]] account (Deel VI, Hoofdstuk V), the year of the opening phase of the Dutch pre-invasion force drawdown in [[Atjeh|Atjeh]] — the year in which the [[4de Garnizoensbataljon|4de Garnizoensbataljon (the "Mobiele Reserve")]] was dissolved, the [[Westkustcolonne|Westkustcolonne]] was formed and transferred out of Atjeh, and the structural foundations of the Dutch pre-invasion force were dismantled. The year [[1940]] is the *terminus a quo* of the Dutch pre-invasion drawdown that would hollow out the Atjeh garrison on the eve of the [[Japanese invasion of Atjèh|Japanese invasion of March 1942]]. It was also the year of publication of [[H. van Meurs|H. van Meurs]]'s *[[Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939 (Van Meurs, 1940)|Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939]]* — the fourth annual literature survey of the [[Koninklijk Instituut voor de Taal-, Land- en Volkenkunde van Nederlandsch-Indië|Koninklijk Instituut]] — issued by [[M. Nijhoff|M. Nijhoff]] of [['s-Gravenhage|'s-Gravenhage]] notwithstanding the "bijzondere tijdsomstandigheden" (special wartime conditions) of the German occupation of the Netherlands.

## The 1 January 1940 troop strength

As of [[1 January]] [[1940]] the Dutch garrison in Atjeh comprised:

- **Territoriale Staf** at [[Kotaradja|Kotaradja]]
- **1ste Garnizoensbataljon** (27 brigades): staff Kotaradja; [[Tapatoean|Tapatoean]] 4, [[Blang Pidië|Blang Pidië]] 4, [[Meulaboh|Meulaboh]] 4, [[Tjalang|Tjalang]] 4, [[Lam No|Lam No]] 3, [[Lokop|Lokop]] 4, [[Kotatjane|Kotatjane]] 4.
- **2de Garnizoensbataljon** (30 brigades): staff Kotaradja with 23 brigades; [[Lho'Nga|Lho'Nga]] 3, [[Seulimeum|Seulimeum]] 4.
- **3de Garnizoensbataljon** (34 brigades): staff Kotaradja; [[Padang Tidji|Padang Tidji]] 3, [[Sigli|Sigli]] 8, [[Meureudoe|Meureudoe]] 3, [[Samalanga|Samalanga]] 4, [[Bireuën|Bireuën]] 4, [[Lho Seumawé|Lho Semawe]] 4, [[Boeloe Blang Ara|Boeloe Blang Ara]] 4, [[Langsa|Langsa]] 4.
- **4de Garnizoensbataljon** (16 brigades): staff Kotaradja with 16 brigades (2 at [[Sabang|Sabang]] for police duties); the "Mobiele Reserve."
- **Korps Maréchaussee** (60 brigades): Iste Divisie (12 brigades at [[Blangkedjeren|Blangkedjeren]] and [[Koeala Bee|Koeala Bee]]); IIde Divisie (8 at [[Djeuram|Djeuram]] and [[Lam Ie|Lam Ie]]); IIIde Divisie (15 at [[Takengon|Takengon]] and [[Lam Meulo|Lam Meulo]]); IVde Divisie (6 at [[Tangsé|Tangsé]] and [[Geumpang|Geumpang]]); Vde Divisie (12 at [[Lho Soekoen|Lho Soekoen]], [[Paja Bakong|Paja Bakong]] and [[Panton Laboe|Panton Laboe]]); VIde Divisie (7 at [[Bakongan|Bakongan]]).

**Total**: 107 brigades of garrison infantry (about 2,250 men) + 60 brigades of Maréchaussee (1,200 men) = 3,450 men.

## The 1940 reductions

The [[1940]] drawdown comprised the following reductions:

- **April 1940**: One company of the 4de Garnizoensbataljon sent to the Riouw Archipelago; in May a second company sent to Java. The 4de Garnizoensbataljon, the "Mobiele Reserve," disappeared entirely from Atjeh.
- **Shortly after the outbreak of war with Germany (May 1940)**: The outposts at [[Bireuën|Bireuën]], [[Langsa|Langsa]] and [[Kotatjane|Kotatjane]] (12 brigades) were evacuated and assigned to a wartime task outside Atjeh. The [[Westkustcolonne|Westkustcolonne]] of nine brigades was formed from the Tapatoean, Meulaboh and Tjalang detachments; Tapatoean and Meulaboh were dissolved; Tjalang was reduced by one brigade. The Westkustcolonne was sent to the Oostkust van Sumatra, eventually to [[Lawe Sigalagala|Lawe Sigalagala]] for the German internee camp. The soldiers never returned to Atjeh; their families were moved to Kotaradja.
- The established strength of all remaining Atjeh brigades was reduced by 2 men per brigade, for the formation of one company also sent to the Oostkust van Sumatra.

The staff history records that, as a result of the above transfers, the strength of the troops stationed in Atjeh had already been reduced by one quarter.

## Additional account from *Literatuur-Overzicht* ([[1940]]) — the Van Meurs survey

[[1940]] was also the year of publication of [[H. van Meurs|H. van Meurs]]'s *[[Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939 (Van Meurs, 1940)|Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939 van de Taal-, Land- en Volkenkunde en Geschiedenis van Nederlandsch-Indië]]* — the fourth annual (*4e jaarlijksche*) literature survey of the [[Koninklijk Instituut voor de Taal-, Land- en Volkenkunde van Nederlandsch-Indië|Koninklijk Instituut]], issued by [[M. Nijhoff|M. Nijhoff]] of [['s-Gravenhage|'s-Gravenhage]]. In his *Voorbericht* (foreword), Van Meurs records that the Institut issued the volume "in de overtuiging, dat de bijzondere tijdsomstandigheden niet den minsten ongunstigen invloed mochten uitoefenen op het verschijnen van deze publicatie" — in the conviction that the special wartime circumstances should not exert the slightest unfavourable influence on the appearance of this publication. The reference is to the German occupation of the Netherlands, which had begun in May [[1940]]; the survey is thus a document of the persistence of Dutch colonial scholarship under occupation. The survey's Sumatra chapter (Hoofdstuk III, Nos. 548–658) and Sumatra Addenda (Nos. 1056–1062) record the principal [[1939]] publications on Atjeh, including the [[H. T. Damsté|Damsté]] edition of the [[Hikajat Eitia Toedjoh|Hikajat Eitia Toedjoh]], the [[J. J. Jongejans|Jongejans]] survey *[[Land en volk van Atjeh vroeger en nu (Jongejans, 1939)|Land en volk van Atjeh vroeger en nu]]*, the [[R. G. Doorman|Doorman]] notice of the death of [[Toeanangkoe Mohammad Dawat|Toeanangkoe Mohammad Dawat]], and the [[Luc. van der Linden|van der Linden]] commemoration of the [[Bij het derde eeuwfeest van de martelaren van Atjeh (1138-1938)|third centenary of the Atjeh martyrs]]. The publication of the survey in [[1940]] — alongside the simultaneous drawdown of the Atjeh garrison — marks the year as one in which the Dutch colonial order on Atjeh was, in both its scholarly and its military dimensions, being dismantled.

## Significance

The year [[1940]] is, in the [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|staff history's]] Atjeh chapter, the *terminus a quo* of the Dutch pre-invasion force drawdown that hollowed out the Atjeh garrison on the eve of the [[Japanese invasion of Atjèh|Japanese invasion of March 1942]]. The staff history's emphasis on the dissolution of the [[4de Garnizoensbataljon|4de Garnizoensbataljon ("Mobiele Reserve")]], on the formation and removal of the [[Westkustcolonne|Westkustcolonne]], and on the reduction of the established strength of all remaining brigades frames the [[1940]] drawdown as the structural cause of the Dutch inability to suppress the [[F-beweging|F. beweging]] rising of February–March [[1942]] or to mount a sustained guerrilla after the Japanese landings. The year [[1940]] thus marks the beginning of the end of the Dutch colonial military presence in Atjeh. It also marks the publication of the Van Meurs *Literatuur-Overzicht* — the last harvest of the pre-war Dutch colonial scholarly order on Atjeh, issued under the conditions of the German occupation that would, within five years, end the colonial state itself.
