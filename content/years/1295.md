**1295** is the approximate year of death for the Egyptian poet‑scholar [[Al‑Busiri]], author of the **Al‑Burda**. His passing marked the end of a prolific career that produced the poem later incorporated into Acehnese devotional practice during the **Mawlid** (*dikië jadjandji*).
