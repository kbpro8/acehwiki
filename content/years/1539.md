Figures, places, and events in Acehnese history associated with the year 1539 — the year of the [[Atjeh-Aroe War (1539)]], the [[Atjehsche vloot|Atjehnese fleet]]'s descent on [[Aroe]], and [[Mendez Pinto|Pinto]]'s first Sumatran mission.

## People

- [[Mendez Pinto]] — Portuguese adventurer, eyewitness to the Atjeh-Aru war
- [[Pero de Faria]] — Portuguese governor of Malacca who dispatched Pinto
- [[Alleredin Mahomet]] — commander of the Atjehsche vloot, governor of Barus
- [[Sultan of Atjeh (16th century)]] — the Atjehnese sultan who launched the war
- [[Radja of Aroe]] — the east-coast ruler killed in the fall of Aroe
- [[Queen of Aroe]] — the queen-consort who fled to Malacca, Bentan, and Johor
- [[Sultan of Djohor]] — who married the Queen of Aroe and briefly retook Aroe
- [[P. de Roo de la Faille]] — modern source on the year 1539
