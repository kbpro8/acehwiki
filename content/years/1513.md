Figures, places, and events in Acehnese history associated with the year 1513 — the year of [[Pati Oenoes|Pati Oenoes's]] great Javanese naval attack on Portuguese [[Malacca]] from [[Japara|Japara]], the first Muslim attempt to retake the city from the Portuguese, and a strategic echo of the [[Acheh|Atjehnese]] sultanate's own [[Atjeh-Aroe War (1539)|war on Aroe]] of [[1539]].

## People

- [[Pati Oenoes]] / [[Soenan Kalidjaga]] — Javanese commander of the attack
- [[Tome Pires]] — author of the *Suma Oriental*, recording Pati Oenoes around this date

## Places

- [[Japara]]
- [[Demak]]
- [[Malacca]]
- [[Pasej-Samodra]]
- [[Acheh]]
