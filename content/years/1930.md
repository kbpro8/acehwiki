**1930** was marked in Acehnese colonial history by two notable events: the fortieth anniversary (*veertig-jarig bestaan*) of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen]], celebrated on [[2 April]] [[1930]], and the publication of the [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet]] by Ir. [[W. Spoon]] of the [[Afdeeling Handelsmuseum]] — the principal contemporary monograph on the early factory-scale production of Indonesian turpentine from the [[Baleq]] enterprise.

## Events

- **40-year jubilee of the Korps Marechaussee ([[2 April]] [[1930]])** — The [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen]] celebrated its fortieth anniversary on [[2 April]] [[1930]]. The army commander (*legercommandant*) Lieutenant-General H.A. Cramer travelled in person to Atjeh for the ceremonies; all available officers from posts across Atjeh and Onderhoorigheden gathered at [[Koetaradja]]. In Den Haag a grand dinner was held at *De Witte* — described as a *[[Kandoerie|Kandoeri Rajeu]]* (great feast) with a menu of [[Gajoeland]] dishes. Captain [[Struijvenberg]] wrote the historical overview for the occasion.

- **Publication of the Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet ([[19 November]] [[1930]])** — The [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet]] by Ir. [[W. Spoon]] was published as *Berichten van de Afdeeling Handelsmuseum van de Kon. Vereeniging Koloniaal Instituut* No. 57, extracted from *De Indische Mercuur* of [[19 November]] [[1930]] and published by J. H. de Bussy of [[Amsterdam]]. The pamphlet is the principal contemporary monograph on the [[Baleq]] enterprise.

- **Chemical investigation of Atjeh-turpentine samples ([[1930]])** — Dr. [[D. B. Spoelstra]] of the Afdeeling Handelsmuseum performed the fractional distillation of 2500 g of Atjeh-turpentine (sample *H.M. no. 1607*) under 30 mm pressure, establishing the 85–90% α-pinene and approximately 4% Δ3-carene composition of the factory-scale product. [[M. A. van Royen]] tested the samples against the draft *Ontwerp-normaalblad V 597*.

- **European market-reception survey (May [[1930]])** — The [[Afdeeling Handelsmuseum]] addressed a [[Circulaire of 5 May 1930 to the V.V.V.F.|circular letter of 5 May 1930]] to the 28 members of the *[[Vereeniging van Vernis- en Verffabrikanten en -Handelaren in Nederland]]*, enclosing sample *H.M. no. 1607* and chemical data. Eleven written evaluations were received — the principal documentary record of the European market reception of factory-scale Atjeh-turpentine.

- **Production at the Baleq enterprise ([[1930]])** — By [[1930]] the area under exploitation at the [[Baleq]] enterprise was approximately 1,600 ha, entirely in production. The production figures for [[1929]] — the latest available in the pamphlet — were 356 tons of resin and 104 tons of turpentine.

## People

- [[Struijvenberg]] — Captain; author of the historical overview for the Korps Marechaussee jubilee.
- H.A. Cramer — Lieutenant-General (*legercommandant*); attended the jubilee ceremonies in Atjeh.
- [[W. Spoon|Ir. W. Spoon]] — Author of the [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet]].
- [[L. P. de Bussy]] — Director of the Afdeeling Handelsmuseum; signatory of the circular of 5 May 1930.
- [[D. B. Spoelstra|Dr. D. B. Spoelstra]] — Chemist who performed the fractional distillation.
- [[M. A. van Royen]] — Chemist who tested the samples against the draft *Ontwerp-normaalblad V 597*.
- [[W. M. van der Veer]] — Superintendent of the [[Baleq]] enterprise.

## Places

- [[Koetaradja]] — Gathering point for the Korps Marechaussee jubilee ceremonies.
- [[Gajoeland]] — Theme of the jubilee banquet menu in Den Haag.
- [[Baleq]] — Site of the colonial government's turpentine and resin factory, operated by *'s Lands Caoutchouchedrijf*.
- [[Takengon]] — Town near the [[Baleq]] enterprise.
- [[Amsterdam]] — City of publication of the [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet]].
