The year **1929** is the year of [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Generaal Snijders]]'s [[Snijders' third Indies journey (1929)|third Indies journey]], undertaken as delegate of the Raad van Bestuur of the [[K.N.I.L.M.|K.N.I.L.M.]] to negotiate with the Indies government over modifications to the [[K.N.I.L.M.|K.N.I.L.M.]] statutes and route. The journey resulted in the opening of the Weltevreden–Surabaya line and the first west-to-east Java flight on [[1 November]] [[1 November]] [[1929]], in Snijders' presence.

## Events of the Year

- [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]]'s [[Snijders' third Indies journey (1929)|third Indies journey]]. The Raad van Bestuur of the [[K.N.I.L.M.|K.N.I.L.M.]], of which Snijders had become a member in [[1928]], asked him to return to the Indies to negotiate with the Indies government over a desired modification to the statutes and to several parts of the route. After initial hesitation on grounds of health, Snijders sailed again on [[19 September]] [[19 September]] [[1929]], arriving [[12 October]] [[12 October]]. Discussions at [[Bandoeng|Bandoeng]] with the Director of the Department of Gouvernementsbedrijven reached their goal.
- On [[1 November]] [[1 November]] [[1929]] Snijders attended the opening of the [[K.N.I.L.M.|K.N.I.L.M.]] Weltevreden–Surabaya line and the first west-to-east Java flight.
- At [[Semarang|Semarang]] in November Snijders again attended the *jaarfeest* of the Nationalen Bond "Het Mobilisatiekruis." At [[Medan|Medan]] a slight attack of dysentery reminded him of his doctor's warning, and he declined a reception by the Vereeniging van Nederlandsch-Indische Officieren on Sumatra's Oostkust.
- Snijders returned to the Netherlands in late December [[1929]].
