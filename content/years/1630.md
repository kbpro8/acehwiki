The year of the death of [[Shaikh Shamsuddin of Sumatra|Shamsuddin of Pasai]], the pantheistic mystic who dominated the religious life of the court of [[Sultan Iskandar Muda|Iskandar Muda]] of [[Acheh]]. Even after his death his disciples remained in honour with the sultan, and the [[Wujudiyyah]] mysticism he had developed from the heritage of [[Hamzah Pansuri]] continued to flourish at the Achehnese court until the death of Iskandar Muda in [[1636]] and the arrival of [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]] in [[1637]].
