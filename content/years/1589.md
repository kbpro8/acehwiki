The year **1589** is, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's survey of the [[Buitenbezittingen|Outer Possessions]], the approximate date of the [[atjehsche-vernietiging-van-het-batakrijk-aroe-(c-1589)|Atjehsche destruction of the Batakrijk Aroe]] — the cataclysm by which the [[Acheh|Atjehers]] shattered the great [[Batak]] kingdom of [[rijk Aroe|Aroe]] that had extended along the [[Straits of Malacca|Strait of Malakka]] from the present cultivation district of the [[Oostkust van Sumatra|East Coast of Sumatra]] south-eastward into south-eastern [[Acheh|Atjeh]].

## Events

- [[atjehsche-vernietiging-van-het-batakrijk-aroe-(c-1589)]] — the destruction of the Batakrijk Aroe by the [[Acheh|Atjehers]], placed by the Encyclopaedisch Bureau "probably" in [[1589]] on the basis of Batak saga.
- [[atjehsche-invasie-in-tamiang-en-noordelijk-langkat-(c-1589)]] — the Atjehnese seizure of [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] and Northern [[Langkat|Langkat]] under a *vertegenwoordiger* of the [[Sultan of Acheh|Sultan of Atjeh]], the immediate sequel to the destruction of Aroe.

## Significance

The year [[1589]] is, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's survey, the chronological anchor of the [[atjehsche-overheersing-der-noordelijke-kuststaatjes|long Atjehnese overlordship of the East Coast]]. The destruction of [[rijk Aroe|Aroe]] and the Atjehnese seizure of [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] and northern [[Langkat|Langkat]] together mark the moment at which the [[Acheh|Atjehnese]] presence on the East Coast shifts from military incursion to formal administration — the moment that inaugurates the long century of [[Acheh|Atjehnese]] East Coast dominance that would be consolidated under [[Sultan Iskander Muda|Sultan Iskander Moeda]] and only shaken off — briefly — in [[1769]].

The Encyclopaedisch Bureau's attribution of the events of [[1589]] to "saga" (*sage*) and its qualifier "probably" (*waarschijnlijk*) on the year itself signal that the date rests on traditional Batak memory rather than on a dated contemporary chronicle. The year [[1589]] is, in the Bureau's framing, the chronological approximation of a traditional Batak memory of the cataclysm — the date around which the destruction of [[rijk Aroe|Aroe]] and the Atjehnese seizure of [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] and northern [[Langkat|Langkat]] are best placed.
