**1925-1927** was a year of significance in the history of [[Aceh]], as recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. 

## Significance

The year [[1925]]-[[1927]] is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the chronological framework of [[Aceh]]'s history.
