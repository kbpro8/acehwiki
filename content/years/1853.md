**1853** was a year of intensified Dutch naval activity on **[[Borneo]]**. The steamship **[[Celebess]]** under **[[Geerling|Lieutenant ter zee Geerling]]** conducted operations along the **[[Sambas River]]** and **[[Taykong]]**, targeting Chinese **[[Kongsi]]** settlements. These actions were part of a broader strategy to protect the **[[Sultanate of Aceh]]**’s commercial interests from disruption by illicit trade.
