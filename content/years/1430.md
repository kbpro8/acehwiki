Figures, places, and events in Acehnese history associated with the year c. 1430 — the approximate year of the Venetian traveller [[Nicolo de Conti|Niccolò de' Conti]]'s visit to the [[Malay Archipelago|Malay Archipelago]] and his voyage up the "Ganges" to the city of [[Sjahroe'n Noewi|Cernovem]] — which [[P. de Roo de la Faille|De Roo de la Faille]] identifies with [[Sjahroe'n Noewi]]/[[Tandjoengpoera]]-[[Aroe]].
