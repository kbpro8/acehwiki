**9** denotes the ninth year of the Hijri calendar (approximately [[630]] CE), the year in which the poet [[Ka'b ibn Zuhair]] is traditionally said to have composed his celebrated *qasīda* “Bānat Sūcād”. This early composition later entered the Acehnese poetic repertoire and is sometimes performed during the **Mawlid** (*dikië jadjandji*) in Aceh.
