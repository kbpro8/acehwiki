## Account from *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*

[[London|Londensche]] Conventie of [[1814]]: [[Engeland (England)|England]] returned most Dutch colonies but kept some parts. Atjeh remained an independent native state.

*Source: J. Jongejans, Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu (Baarn: Hollandia Drukkerij N.V., c. 1938), pp. 35.*
