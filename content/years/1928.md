The year **1928** is the year of the founding of the [[K.N.I.L.M.|K.N.I.L.M.]] (Koninklijke Nederlandsch-Indische Luchtvaart Maatschappij) on [[16 July]] [[16 July]] [[1928]] as an independent Indies aviation company, separate from the [[K.L.M.|K.L.M.]], and of [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Generaal Snijders]]'s [[Snijders' second Indies journey (1928)|second Indies journey]] to attend the festive opening of the K.N.I.L.M. lines. The year also saw the centenary of the [[Koninklijke Militaire Academie|Koninklijke Militaire Academie]], which was celebrated at [[Weltevreden|Weltevreden]] in October and which Snijders attended in the course of his Indies tour.

## Events of the Year

- The founding of the [[K.N.I.L.M.|K.N.I.L.M.]] on [[16 July]] [[16 July]] [[1928]] as an independent Indies aviation company, separate from the [[K.L.M.|K.L.M.]], by some tens of companies and bodies in the Indies representing trade, industry, shipping, and finance.
- [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]]'s [[Snijders' second Indies journey (1928)|second Indies journey]]. He sailed on [[19 September]] [[19 September]] on the *Christian Huygens* and arrived at [[Tanjung Priok|Tanjung Priok]] on [[12 October]] [[12 October]]. The arrival had the character of a triumphant return — a thousand-headed crowd, a military band, and circling aircraft welcomed him at the quay. On [[1 November]] [[1 November]] the inauguration of the [[K.N.I.L.M.|K.N.I.L.M.]] lines took place at the airfield Tjikolitan near Meester Cornelis, where Snijders as adviser to the company delivered one of his characteristically forceful speeches.
- The centenary of the [[Koninklijke Militaire Academie|Koninklijke Militaire Academie]], celebrated at [[Weltevreden|Weltevreden]] on [[18 October]] [[18 October]] in the course of Snijders' Indies tour.

## The Baleq factory-scale turpentine enterprise (October 1928)

The year [[1928]] was the year in which the modern French distillation installation was brought into service at the [[Baleq|Baleq]] enterprise of *'s Lands Caoutchouchedrijf* (the Government Rubber Enterprise) in the [[Gajo-landen|Gajo-Landen]]-[[Atjeh|Atjeh]] border region — the principal event in the conversion of the [[Baleq|Baleq]] enterprise from an experimental trial to a fully factory-scale operation. After the [[1926]] decision to convert the trial into a definitive exploitation, *maatregelen genomen [werden] om een goede destilleer-installatie te verkrijgen. Weldra werd dan ook in Frankrijk een zoo modern mogelijke installatie aangeschaft. Doordat het vervoer naar de onderneming met veel tegenslag te kampen had, kon de nieuwe fabriek eerst in October 1928 in bedrijf worden gesteld* ("measures were taken to acquire a proper distillation installation. Before long, then, an as modern as possible installation was purchased in France. Because the transport to the enterprise had much setback to contend with, the new factory could only be brought into service in October 1928").

The new factory distilled the balsam (raw resinous exudate) of [[Pinus Merkusii|Pinus Merkusii]] with steam from two horizontal Jonkers steam boilers, with a yield of approximately 75% colophonium (resin) and 20% turpentine. The production figures for [[1928]] were 222 tons of resin (hars) and 62 tons of turpentine.

The same year saw the publication of the *Mededeeling no. 19 van het Proefstation voor het Boschwezen* — the [[C. Brandts Buys|Brandts Buys]] monograph *Bijdrage tot de kennis van Pinus Merkusii Jungh et de Vr., meer in het bijzonder in de Gajo-landen* ("Contribution to the knowledge of Pinus Merkusii Jungh et de Vr., more particularly in the Gajo-Landen") — the principal early scientific study of [[Pinus Merkusii|Pinus Merkusii]] in the [[Gajo-landen|Gajo-Landen]] and the principal published source on the *quarre* tapping method as applied at the [[Baleq|Baleq]] enterprise.

## People (additional)

## Concepts (additional)

- [[Atjeh-terpentijn]] — The factory-scale product of the new factory.
- [[Colophonium]] — The solid resinous residue produced by the new factory.
- [[Pinus Merkusii]] — The Sumatran pine, the raw material of the new factory.
- [[Quarre]] — The French tapping method applied at the [[Baleq|Baleq]] enterprise.
