The year **1928** is the year of the founding of the [[K.N.I.L.M.|K.N.I.L.M.]] (Koninklijke Nederlandsch-Indische Luchtvaart Maatschappij) on [[16 July]] [[16 July]] [[1928]] as an independent Indies aviation company, separate from the [[K.L.M.|K.L.M.]], and of [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Generaal Snijders]]'s [[Snijders' second Indies journey (1928)|second Indies journey]] to attend the festive opening of the K.N.I.L.M. lines. The year also saw the centenary of the [[Koninklijke Militaire Academie|Koninklijke Militaire Academie]], which was celebrated at [[Weltevreden|Weltevreden]] in October and which Snijders attended in the course of his Indies tour.

## Events of the Year

- The founding of the [[K.N.I.L.M.|K.N.I.L.M.]] on [[16 July]] [[16 July]] [[1928]] as an independent Indies aviation company, separate from the [[K.L.M.|K.L.M.]], by some tens of companies and bodies in the Indies representing trade, industry, shipping, and finance.
- [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]]'s [[Snijders' second Indies journey (1928)|second Indies journey]]. He sailed on [[19 September]] [[19 September]] on the *Christian Huygens* and arrived at [[Tandjong Priok|Tandjong Priok]] on [[12 October]] [[12 October]]. The arrival had the character of a triumphant return — a thousand-headed crowd, a military band, and circling aircraft welcomed him at the quay. On [[1 November]] [[1 November]] the inauguration of the [[K.N.I.L.M.|K.N.I.L.M.]] lines took place at the airfield Tjikolitan near Meester Cornelis, where Snijders as adviser to the company delivered one of his characteristically forceful speeches.
- The centenary of the [[Koninklijke Militaire Academie|Koninklijke Militaire Academie]], celebrated at [[Weltevreden|Weltevreden]] on [[18 October]] [[18 October]] in the course of Snijders' Indies tour.
