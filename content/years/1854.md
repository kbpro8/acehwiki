Figures, places, and events in Acehnese history associated with the year **1854** — the year in which [[Acheh|Atjeh]] reimposed its overlordship over the East Coast statelets of [[Langkat]], [[Deli]] and [[Serdang]], as recorded by the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's *De Buitenbezittingen*, Deel II, Afl. 3 (1918).

## Events

- [[Atjeh as opperheer in Langkat, Deli and Serdang (1854)]] — the reimposition of Atjehnese overlordship over Langkat, Deli and Serdang. The Encyclopaedisch Bureau records: "Serdang, Deli en Langkat, welke zich los hadden weten te maken van Siak's invloed, waren al sinds 1854 gedwongen geworden in plaats daarvan dien van Atjeh toe te laten, welk rijk daar sedert dat jaar inderdaad opperheer was."
