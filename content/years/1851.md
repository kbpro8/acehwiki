**1851** was a year of notable Dutch anti‑smuggling activity on the west coast of **[[Borneo]]**, as well as a point in the career of [[Governor Van Heutsz]] within the broader Dutch colonial administration in **[[Aceh]]**. In [[May]] [[1851]] Dutch naval forces, including the frigate **[[Rijn]]**, attempted to navigate the **[[Sambas River]]**, encountering armed resistance from Chinese **[[Kongsi]]** groups. The operation aimed to secure the maritime trade routes that were vital to the **[[Sultanate of Aceh]]** and to suppress illicit commerce along the Bornean shoreline.

## People

- [[Governor Van Heutsz]]

## Events

- **Dutch anti‑smuggling expedition (May 1851)** – Dutch forces, under colonial command, deployed the frigate **[[Rijn]]** to enter the **[[Sambas River]]**. The expedition met resistance from Chinese **[[Kongsi]]** settlements, leading to several engagements recorded in contemporary colonial reports.
