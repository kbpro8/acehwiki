**1648** marks the signing of the **[[Treaty of Münster (1648)]]**, which concluded the **[[Eighty Years' War]]** and recognised the independence of the **[[United Provinces (Dutch Republic)]].** The peace enabled the Republic to expand its overseas trade, strengthening the **[[Dutch East India Company|VOC]]** presence in the **[[Sultanate of Aceh]]**.
