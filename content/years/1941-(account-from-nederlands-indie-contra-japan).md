**The year 1941** was, in the [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|Dutch staff history's]] account (Deel VI, Hoofdstuk V), the year of the second phase of the Dutch pre-invasion force drawdown in [[Atjeh|Atjeh]] — the year in which 28 brigades of garrison infantry were dissolved in a single reduction (15 February 1941), in which the city guards were raised, and in which the [[Major W. F. Palmer van den Broek|Palmer van den Broek]]–[[C. J. Bekkers|Bekkers]] command transition at the [[2de Garnizoensbataljon|2de Garnizoensbataljon]] was effected. The year [[1941]] is the *terminus ad quem* of the Dutch pre-invasion drawdown, and the year of [[Panglima Polem|Panglima Polem]]'s death.

## The 15 February 1941 reduction

On 15 February of that year, the detachments at [[Blang Indië|Blang Indië]], [[Lam No|Lam No]], [[Lho'Nga|Lho'Nga]], [[Seulimeum|Seulimeum]], [[Padang Tidji|Padang Tidji]], [[Meureudoe|Meureudoe]], [[Samalanga|Samalanga]] and [[Boeloe Blang Ara|Boeloe Blang Ara]] (totalling 28 brigades) had to be dissolved. Of the brigades thereby released, a column of 15 brigades had to be formed immediately for assignment elsewhere outside Atjeh. The 2de Garnizoensbataljon at Kotaradja was reinforced with 8 brigades from the dissolved Sabang war detachment; the 3de Garnizoensbataljon was reduced to its [[Sigli|Sigli]] (8) and [[Lho Seumawé|Lho Seumawé]] (4) detachments; the 1ste Garnizoensbataljon staff was moved to [[Medan|Medan]]. The 3de Garnizoensbataljon no longer had any troops and was formally dissolved.

## The city guards and the Maréchaussee shortfall

In the course of [[1941]], city guards were established at Kotaradja (6 brigades), Sabang (3 brigades), Sigli (3 brigades), Takengon (3 brigades), Langsa (3 brigades) and Koeala Simpang (3 brigades). A portion of the city guardsmen — Atjehnese — had to be dismissed soon afterwards owing to proven untrustworthiness; the proficiency of the remainder was insufficient by the outbreak of war. The Maréchaussee's shortfall reached 200 men; the brigade strength was reduced from 20 to 15 carbines.

## The Palmer van den Broek–Bekkers transition

From July [[1941]], Major W. F. Palmer van den Broek acted as commander of the Korps Maréchaussee, previously commander of the 2de Garnizoensbataljon, whose command over the latter corps he handed over to Lieutenant-Colonel C. J. Bekkers, who had not previously served in Atjeh. The transition — placing a non-Atjeh-experienced officer in command of the Kotaradja garrison — is the staff history's principal instance of the loss of Atjeh experience at the top of the 2de Garnizoensbataljon.

## The death of Panglima Polem

The death of [[Panglima Polem (d. 1941)|Panglima Polem]] in [[1941]] — he had been decorated, among other things, with the Knight's Cross of the [[Oranje Nassau (Orde van Oranje Nassau)|Order of Oranje Nassau]] for his great support to the colonial administration — placed the Dutch government before a great problem. Panglima Polem was the *sagihoofd* of the [[XXII Moekims|XXII moekims]] whose conduct as a legendary hero had sustained Dutch authority in the Seulimeumse through the consolidation of the [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacification]]. The succession of his eldest son — politically almost outspokenly anti-Dutch in orientation — was the principal Dutch political risk of [[1941]].

## Significance

The year [[1941]] is, in the [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|staff history's]] Atjeh chapter, the *terminus ad quem* of the Dutch pre-invasion drawdown, and the year of the structural loss of Atjeh experience at the top of the 2de Garnizoensbataljon. The staff history's emphasis on the [[15 February 1941]] reduction, on the Maréchaussee shortfall of 200 men, on the Palmer van den Broek–Bekkers transition, and on the death of [[Panglima Polem (d. 1941)|Panglima Polem]] frames [[1941]] as the year that closed the structural preconditions of the Dutch pre-invasion garrison: the year by which the Atjeh garrison had been reduced to about one-third of its [[1 January 1940]] strength, the year by which the Maréchaussee's established strength had been reduced to 15 carbines per brigade, and the year by which the political anchor of the Seulimeumse had been lost.
