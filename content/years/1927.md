**[[1927]]** was the end year of the *Westkust verzet 1925-1927|Westkust verzet]] of [[1925]]-[[1927]] — and the end of the [[1906]]-[[1927]] communist period on [[Java]]. "[[1927]] viel, volgden weer..." — 1927 fell, and again followed... The year is the book's marker of the end of the inter-war resistance and the communist period.

## Account from *In Memoriam Maurice Henri du Croo* (1951) — 1927

In [[1927]] in Aceh, [[Maurice Henri du Croo]] was appointed *Stafofficier* (staff officer) and awarded the [[Ridder O.N.O. met de Zwaarden]] (Knight of the [[Orde van Oranje-Nassau]] with Swords), recognising his military service in the [[Aceh War]] region. This decoration underscores the continued active Dutch military presence in [[Aceh|Atjeh]] during the late [[1920s]] — the same interwar period in which the [[Bakóngan uprising (1925–1927)|Bakóngan uprising]] was being suppressed — and places du Croo's Atjeh service within the same chronological frame as the closing phase of that rising.

*Source: In Memoriam Maurice Henri du Croo* (1951), p. 5.
