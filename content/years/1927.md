**[[1927]]** was the end year of the *Westkust verzet 1925-1927|Westkust verzet]] of [[1925]]-[[1927]] — and the end of the [[1906]]-[[1927]] communist period on [[Java]]. "[[1927]] viel, volgden weer..." — 1927 fell, and again followed... The year is the book's marker of the end of the inter-war resistance and the communist period.

## Account from *In Memoriam Maurice Henri du Croo* (1951) — 1927

In [[1927]] in Aceh, [[Maurice Henri du Croo]] was appointed *Stafofficier* (staff officer) and awarded the [[Ridder O.N.O. met de Zwaarden]] (Knight of the [[Orde van Oranje-Nassau]] with Swords), recognising his military service in the [[Aceh War]] region. This decoration underscores the continued active Dutch military presence in [[Aceh|Atjeh]] during the late [[1920s]] — the same interwar period in which the [[Bakóngan uprising (1925–1927)|Bakóngan uprising]] was being suppressed — and places du Croo's Atjeh service within the same chronological frame as the closing phase of that rising.

*Source: In Memoriam Maurice Henri du Croo* (1951), p. 5.

## Account from *Atjeh* by H.C. Zentgraff (1938)

[[H.C. Zentgraff|Zentgraff]] records [[1927]] as the year of the death of [[Tjoet Ali]] and the [[Mengamat attack (1927)|Mengamat attack]]. On the night of Thursday-on-Friday in [[May]] [[1927]], [[Tjoet Ali]] launched the Mengamat attack on *luitenant* Harting's *marschbivak*: *zes of zeven Atjehers kwamen binnen, doch de militairen hielden het hoofd koel*. The seven were all killed; *wij kregen acht gewonden, waarvan vier zwaar, allen met klewangwonden*. [[Imam Sabi]] was wounded in the chest, the bullet going through his back — he treated the chest wound with a *modder-papje* and wore a *rottan gevlochten korfje* over the back wound, fastened with thin rattan.

On [[25 May]] [[1927]] [[Gosenson]] received a report that a *bendetje* had been seen in the *boschrand* three hours from [[Kandang]]. He went out that same night; by morning the brigade reached the place. The chase ran to the *Aloeë* near Kandang, where, *in het water*, six bodies were found — among them [[Tjoet Ali]] himself. *Bij de lijken zaten eene kip en een jong aapje; het waren de mascottes van het bendehoofd en zijn vrouw*. Gosenson buried Tjoet Ali as *een respectabel en moedig tegenstander* — *vlak vóór het marschbivak Kandang*, with metselwerk and a plaat, face toward Mekka. *Zóó was Tjoet Ali "sjahid" geworden op 25 Mei 1927.*

*Atjeh*, door H.C. Zentgraff. Gedrukt bij Drukkerij "De Unie", [[1938]], Batavia.
