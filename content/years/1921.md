## Account from *Verkenningen in Atjeh* (1923)

**1921** was the year of the founding of the first factory for indigenous processing of patchouli leaves into essential oil at [[Tapa Toean|Tapa Toean]] on the [[Westkust van Atjeh|Westkust van Atjeh]], with government support and the cooperation of the *Afdeeling Nijverheid* of the *Landbouwconsulent* at [[Buitenzorg|Buitenzorg]] — the beginning of the [[Patchouli-olie industry in Atjeh|patchouli-oil industry in Atjeh]]. The [[Familie Veenhuizen|Familie Veenhuizen]] began the cultivation of potatoes and vegetables at [[Paja Toempi|Paja Toempi]] on the [[Gajo-weg|Gajo-weg]] in [[1921]]. The Lho' Seumawé toll-office receipts show declining trade: 120 ships were cleared in [[1921]] against 108 in [[1922]]. The estate [[Beuridi|Beuridi]] in [[Peusangan|Peusangan]] was abandoned in [[1921]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart (1943)

On [[18 October]] **[[1921]]** the *[[Java-Bode]]* and on [[15 October]] the *[[De Locomotief|Locomotief]]* published their assessments of [[H. N. A. Swart]] on his retirement as Vice-President of the [[Raad van Nederlandsch-Indië]]. On [[27 August]] [[1921]] he was appointed Grand Officer in the [[Orde van Oranje-Nassau]]. In October [[1921]] Swart closed his period of service.
