## Account from *Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.* (1954) — 1884

**[[1884]]** is mentioned in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* in connection with the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]] as one of the years of the middle phase of the war, when the Dutch were extending their control over the inland areas — "[[1884]], na als eerste de stelling in Tenom te zijn binnengedrongen" — and when the [[Derde Bataljon (Atjeh-Bataljon)|Derde Bataljon]] and other colonial formations were engaged in the systematic reduction of Acehnese strongholds.

*Source: Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.*, by G. W. T. Dames, Maj. Inf. K.N.I.L. b.d. ('s-Gravenhage: Koninklijke Bibliotheek, 1954).
