## Additional Information from 1636

Figures, places, and events in Atjehnese history associated with the year 1636.

## People

- [[Sultan Iskandar Muda]] — death in 1636; his reign (1607-1636) had defined the [[Atjeh bloeitijdperk (1607-1636)|Atjeh *bloeitijdperk*]] (golden age) of the sultanate.

## Events

- [[Atjeh bloeitijdperk (1607-1636)]] — end of the Atjehnese golden age with the death of [[Sultan Iskandar Muda]].

## Places

- [[Banda Acheh]] — the Atjehnese capital (Koetaradja), where [[Sultan Iskandar Muda]] died.
- [[Acheh]] — the sultanate at the close of its golden age.
