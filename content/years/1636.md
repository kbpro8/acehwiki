The year of the death of [[Sultan Iskandar Muda|Meukuta Alam]] (Iskandar Muda), the greatest of the sultans of [[Acheh]], and the accession of his successor [[Iskandar Thani]]. The date is fixed by the testimony of [[Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]] in his treatise [[Sirat al-mustaqim|*Ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm*]] (printed at [[Mecca]] in 1316 H on the margin of an edition of *Fatḥ al-muʿīn*), who relates that he came to Acheh in [[1637]] while Iskandar Thani was king. The death of Iskandar Muda marks the end of the golden age of Acheh and the beginning of the orthodox reaction against the [[Wujudiyyah]] mysticism of [[Hamzah Pansuri]] and [[Shaikh Shamsuddin of Sumatra|Shamsuddin of Pasai]], in which Raniri himself was the principal actor.
