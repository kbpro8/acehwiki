## Additional Information from 1888

Figures, places, and events in Atjehnese history associated with the year 1888.

## People

- [[K. F. H. van Langen]] — publication of his article [[Van Langen's Atjeh's Westkust (1888)|"Atjeh's Westkust"]] in volume II of the *Tijdschrift van het Koninklijk Nederlandsch Aardrijkskundig Genootschap* in 1888.

## Documents

- [[Van Langen's Atjeh's Westkust (1888)]] — Van Langen's article on the [[Atjeh's Westkust|Westkust van Atjeh]], one of the earliest systematic Western geographical accounts of the region.

## Places

- [[Atjeh's Westkust]] — the West Coast of Atjeh, the subject of Van Langen's 1888 article.

## Concepts

- The [[Atjeh adat schimpstraf (buffalo-riding wife-beater)|Atjeh *adat* schimpstraf]] for wife-beaters (buffalo-riding punishment) is documented at p. 481 of Van Langen's 1888 article.
