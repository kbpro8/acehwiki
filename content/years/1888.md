Figures, places, and events in Acehnese history associated with the year 1888 — including the Controleur S.B. [[Van Rijn van Alkemade|Van Rijn van Alkemade]]'s official visit to [[Aroe-baai|the bay of Aroe]] from [[Bengkalis|Bengkalis]], in which he recorded the *benteng* of the [[Atjehsche avonturier at Aroe-baai (1862)|Atjehscher avonturier]] of [[1862]], the folk tradition of the [[Queen of Aroe|Queen of Aroe]]'s flight, and the inland *bentengs* of the earlier rajas of [[Aroe]].
