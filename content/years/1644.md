The year in which [[Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]] completed the [[Sirat al-mustaqim|*Ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm*]] — the Malay handbook of religious duties that he had begun in [[1634]] — and returned from [[Acheh]] to [[Ranir]]. The date of completion is established by [[H. Djajadiningrat|Djajadiningrat]] in *TBG* 69, pp. 109–111, and corrects the earlier assumption of [[P. Voorhoeve|Voorhoeve]] that the *Ṣirāṭ* had been completed before Raniri's arrival in Acheh. In the same year, [[Yusuf Makasar|Shaikh Yūsuf al-Makassar]] left [[Makassar|Celebes]] for [[Mecca]] — a journey that would bring him, like Raniri, to the discipleship of [[Sajjid Abu Hafs Umar Ba Sjaiban|Sayyid ʿUmar Bā Shaybān]]. The composition of the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān*]] and the [[Hudjdjat as-siddiq li-daf az-zindiq|*Ḥujjat aṣ-ṣiddīq*]] falls within the joint reigns of [[Iskandar Thani]] and [[Sultan Tajul-alam|Taj al-Ālam]].
