## Account from *Snijders* (1944) — 1852

**[[1852]]** was the birth year of [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Cornelis Jacobus Snijders]] — born on [[29 September]] [[29 September]] [[1852]] in [[Nieuwe Tonge]] on the island of [[Goeree-Overflakkee]] — who, as a young *Tweede Luitenant* of the [[Korps Ingenieurs, Mineurs en Sappeurs|Genie (Engineers)]], would serve in [[Acheh|Atjeh]] during the early phase of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] in [[1874]]–[[1875]] and, four decades later, rise to become [[Chef van de Generale Staf|Chef van de Generale Staf]] ([[1910]]–[[1918]]) and [[Opperbevelhebber van Land- en Zeemacht|Opperbevelhebber van Land- en Zeemacht]] ([[August 1914|August]] [[1914]]–[[November 1918|November]] [[1918]]) during the [[First World War]].

Snijders was the fifth of eight sons of Cornelis Jacobus Snijders Sr., a physician who had served as a naval surgeon before establishing a practice in [[Zeeland]], and Sara Maria Muller. The family was intellectually oriented; the elder brother W. G. F. Snijders would later become *Luitenant-Generaal* and Inspector of Infantry, providing the model that the younger Snijders would follow into the [[Koninklijke Militaire Academie|KMA]] at [[Breda]] in [[September 1869|September]] [[1869]]. The birth year [[1852]] thus opens, in the biographer's framing, an arc that would carry a child of the [[Zeeland|Zeeland]] medical bourgeoisie from the island of [[Goeree-Overflakkee]] to the command of the entire Dutch armed forces during the European war of [[1914]]–[[1918]].
