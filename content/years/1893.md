**1893** was a pivotal year in the Dutch‑Aceh theatre. In [[1893]] the first volume of Christiaan Snouck Hurgronje’s foundational ethnography *De Atjehers* was published, while the Dutch colonial navy launched two coordinated marine operations – the **Tuchtiging der Aroe‑cilanden** against the [[Aroe Islands]] and the **Expedition against Tamiang** in the neighbouring [[Tamiang|Tamiang (Sumatra)]] region. These actions reflected the widening scope of the Dutch pacification effort beyond the immediate borders of [[Atjeh]] and were accompanied by notable political and religious events within Aceh.

## Publications

- [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Christiaan Snouck Hurgronje]] – publication of volume I of his [[Snouck Hurgronje's De Atjehers ([[1893]]-[[1894]])|*De Atjehers*]] in [[1893]], the foundational ethnography of the Atjehnese. Volume II followed in [[1894]].

## Military Operations

- **Tuchtiging der Aroe‑cilanden** – a punitive expedition against the [[Aroe Islands]] to suppress a rebellion that threatened Dutch shipping in the region.
- **Expedition against Tamiang** – a combined land‑and‑sea operation in the [[Tamiang|Tamiang (Sumatra)]] area aimed at neutralising cross‑border insurgents.

Both operations were part of the broader Dutch strategy to extend control over the peripheral islands and riverine zones surrounding Aceh.
