## Account from *De Buitenbezittingen* (1917) — 1873

**[[1873]]** was the year of the first Dutch military expedition against [[Koeta Radja|Koeta Radja]] — the campaign that ended the formal authority of the [[Sultans of Atjeh|Sultanate of Atjeh]] and inaugurated the protracted conflict known as the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-krijg]], which would — with intermittent pauses — rage for the next thirty years and produce the colonial [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] that the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's *De Buitenbezittingen* describes. The year [[1873]] thus stands as the terminus of the pre-colonial order in Atjeh and the starting-point of the colonial order.

The first Dutch military expedition against [[Koeta Radja|Koeta Radja]] in [[1873]] was the culmination of a long diplomatic dispute over Atjehsche sovereignty that had pitted the Netherlands against the interests of other European powers — particularly the United Kingdom and the United States — that had begun to court the [[Sultans of Atjeh|Sultan of Atjeh]]. The Dutch response, in [[1873]], was military: an expeditionary force sent against the Sultan's capital. The campaign, which ended with the death of the Sultan — succeeded, in the same year, by a new Sultan who would lead the resistance — established the pattern of the conflict that would follow.

The [[Aceh War|Atjeh-krijg]] that followed [[1873]] was a *hardnekkige godsdienst-oorlog* — a stubborn religious war — that, "door een telkens te snel de overhand krijgend pacifisme der moederlandsche Regeering herhaaldelijk weer den kop op kon steken." With some quieter intervals, the war raged through the last thirty years of the nineteenth century. The formal establishment of the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] in [[1874]] — the year following the first expedition — formalised the transition from ad hoc military occupation to the institutional structure of a colonial administrative unit.

## Account from *Oost-Indisch Journael* (1947) — 1873

**[[1873]]** was the year of the [[Eerste Atjeh-expeditie (1873)|First Aceh Expedition]] and of the historic storming of the [[Meusigit Raja|mosque]] of [[Koeta-Radja|Koeta Radja]] by the Dutch troops in [[April 1873|April]], when the building burned down and later was completely demolished. The storming of the mosque — the First Aceh Expedition — is the marker of the opening of the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]]: the colonial army's storming of the mosque that the Acehnese population had built on the place of the first mosque of [[Sultan Iskandar Muda]]. The aftermath of the [[1873]] storming — the [[generaal Van Swieten|Van Swieten]] letter of [[8 February]] [[1874]] and the rebuilding of the [[Meusigit Raja]] — is the marker of the colonial state's religious politics in [[Atjeh|Aceh]].
