## Account from *Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.* ([[1954]]) — [[1894]]
**[[1894]]** was the year in which [[Dominee Thenu]] came to [[Atjeh|Aceh]] as the *Inlands Godsdienstleraar* attached to the garrison of [[Koeta-Radja|Koeta-Radja]], at the age of twenty-six. He would hold the post for forty full years, until his retirement in [[1934]]. "Hij aanvaardde hem in [[1894]], toen hij net zes en twintig was geworden. Hij zou hem veertig volle jaren bekleden." The mood of the troops in Aceh in [[1894]] was somber; Thenu "voelde aan dat de troep afleiding nodig had en bracht de mensen het soldatenlied" — and in doing so composed the opening couplets of the [[Marechausseelied]]. The year [[1894]] also saw the Dutch [[Lombok expedition ([[1894]])|Lombok expedition]] against the Rija of Lombok. It is the book's marker of the beginning of the Thenu era in Aceh.

*Source: Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.*, by G. W. T. Dames, Maj. Inf. K.N.I.L. b.d. ('s-Gravenhage: Koninklijke Bibliotheek, [[1954]]), chapter "Leve het Zesde Bataljon!" and chapter "Dominee Thenu."
