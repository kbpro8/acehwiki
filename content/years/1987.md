**1987** was the year in which a *Koninklijk Besluit* dated [[February]] [[1987]] posthumously awarded the *Bronzenn Kruis* to *Lieutenant‑colonel J. G. M. Nass* for his extraordinary bravery during the 1942 actions near [[Grisseh]] and [[Lamongan]]. The decree cited his “bijzonder moedige en beleid‑valle daden” (particularly brave and prudent deeds) in the face of overwhelming enemy forces.

## Award Context
Although the award was granted decades after the war, it reflected continued recognition of the sacrifices made by Dutch officers in the defence of the Dutch East Indies, including the Aceh region.

## Significance
The 1987 decree underscores the lasting legacy of World War II military honours within Dutch historical memory.
