The year of publication of *Twee Maleise geschriften van Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī: in facsimile uitgegeven met aantekeningen door P. Voorhoeve*, No. 16 of the *Uitgaven van de Stichting De Goeje*, published at [[Leiden]] by E. J. Brill. The volume — [[P. Voorhoeve|Voorhoeve]]'s edition of the two Malay treatises of [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]] — gave scholars, for the first time, a complete facsimile of the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān fī maʿrifat al-adyān*]] (from Leiden Cod. Or. 3291, the legacy of [[Herman Neubronner van der Tuuk|Van der Tuuk]]) and of the [[Hudjdjat as-siddiq li-daf az-zindiq|*Ḥujjat aṣ-ṣiddīq li-dafʿ az-zindīq*]] (from the Maxwell collection of the [[Royal Asiatic Society]]). Voorhoeve's introduction identifies, for the first time, the principal sources of the *Tibjān*: the [[Tamhid fi bayan at-tawhid|*Tamhīd*]] of [[Abu Syakur as-Salimi|Abū Shakūr as-Sālimī]], the [[al-Insan al-Kamil|*al-Insān al-kāmil*]] of [[Abd al-Karim al-Jili|ʿAbd al-Karīm al-Jīlī]], and the [[Ma'rifat al-madhahib|*Maʿrifat al-madhāhib*]].

## Account from *Schets eener parlementaire geschiedenis* (1955)

The following account is drawn from the parliamentary history of the [[Netherlands|Dutch]] [[Tweede Kamer der Staten-Generaal (colonial business)|Second Chamber]] as narrated by [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] in the colonial section of the [[Schets eener parlementaire geschiedenis van Nederland van 1849 tot 1901 (Rengers-Vermeulen, 1955)|1955 fourth revised edition of Rengers's *Schets*]].


**1955** is the publication year of the second wholly revised edition of *Nederlanders over de Zeeën: 350 Jaar geschiedenis van Nederland buitengaats*, edited by Dr. H. J. de Graaf, with the cooperation of Prof. G. Gonggrijp, W. R. Menkman and Prof. Jhr Dr P. J. van Winter (Utrecht: W. de Haan N.V., MCMLV). The [[1955]] edition is the source of the present Aceh Wiki History articles derived from the book.

### The 1955 edition

The [[1955]] second edition of *Nederlanders over de Zeeën* is, in the source's imprint, the "Tweede geheel herziene druk" (second wholly revised edition). The book comprises seven chapters covering the 350-year history of Dutch maritime enterprise from the late sixteenth century to the Indonesian revolution. Chapter II (by [[H. J. de Graaf|De Graaf]]) covers the Dutch in East Asia and the VOC; Chapter III (by [[H. J. de Graaf|De Graaf]]) the transition from Company to Government; Chapter IV (by [[G. Gonggrijp|Gonggrijp]]) the Indies under the State and as part of the Kingdom; Chapter V (by [[H. J. de Graaf|De Graaf]]) the war with Japan; Chapter VI (by [[H. J. de Graaf|De Graaf]]) the Indies after the Japanese surrender; Chapter VII (by [[P. J. van Winter|Van Winter]]) South Africa in the Dutch period.

### The Atjeh-relevant content

The Atjeh-relevant content of the [[1955]] edition is concentrated in:
- The introductory survey of pre-Company Asia (Chapter II.1, pp. 99-100), which situates sixteenth-century Atjeh in the wider context of Muslim-state formation in the archipelago.
- The chapter on the VOC in the western part of the archipelago (Chapter II.5, pp. 154-159), which covers Atjeh's trade with Gujerat and Suratte, the elephant-fight illustration of [[1613]], and the VOC's conflict with Atjeh on Sumatra's Westkust.
- The chapter on the Indies under the State (Chapter IV, by [[G. Gonggrijp|Gonggrijp]], pp. 197, 205, 209, 216-219), which covers the [[Anglo-Dutch Treaty of 1824|1824 London Treaty]], the [[1857]] contract, the [[Sumatra-tractaat (1871)|1871 Sumatra-tractaat]], the [[Atjehoorlog|Atjehoorlog]] of [[1873]]-[[1904]], and the post-[[1904]] consolidation.
- The chapter on the ethical policy period (Chapter IV, p. 238), which covers the post-Atjeh establishment of effective Dutch authority.
- The chapter on the Indonesian revolution (Chapter VI, p. 267), which records the [[1948]] Atjeh exemption.

### Significance

[[1955]] is significant for the encyclopedia of [[Acheh]] history because it is the publication year of the source from which the present Aceh Wiki articles are derived. The [[1955]] edition is the canonical citation form for *Nederlanders over de Zeeën* in Aceh Wiki references.
