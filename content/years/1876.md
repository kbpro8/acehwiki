## Account from *Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.* (1954) — 1876

**[[1876]]** was the year of the *hevige gevechten van de eerste maanden van 1876* — the heavy fighting of the first months of 1876 — in the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]], and the year for which the [[Derde Bataljon (Atjeh-Bataljon)|Derde Bataljon]] was *in het bijzonder vermeld* in its [[1877]] [[Militaire Willemsorde (M.W.O.)|M.W.O.]] decoration. In November 1876 an expedition went to a new area, requiring the *colonne* method that would become Van Heutsz's *systeem*.

*Source: Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.*, by G. W. T. Dames, Maj. Inf. K.N.I.L. b.d. ('s-Gravenhage: Koninklijke Bibliotheek, 1954).
