**1876** was the year of the death of [[J. L. J. H. Pel|General Pel]] on [[24 February]] [[24 February]] at the kampong Tonga in the [[XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]]; of the arrival of [[Habib Abdoerrahman|Habib Abdurrahman Zahir]] in Atjeh in March; of the commands of [[G. B. T. Wiggers van Kerchem|General Wiggers van Kerchem]] (March-November) and [[A. J. E. Diemont|General Diemont]] (November-); of the [[Expedition against Simpang Olim and Tandjong Semantoh (November-December 1876)|expedition against Simpang Olim and Tandjong Semantoh]] of November-December; of the completion of the iron screw-pile sea-head at [[Oleh-leh|Oleh-leh]] in September and of the railway between Oleh-leh and [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] in November; and of the appearance of the koffiebladziekte in [[Sumatra's Westkust|Sumatra's Westkust]]. The year also saw the submission of the vorst of [[Gighen|Gighen]] on [[29 February]] [[29 February]] and the appointment of [[K. van der Heyden|Colonel Van der Heyden]] as commandant of the [[Zuidoosterlinie|Zuidoosterlinie]] on [[1 April]] [[1 April]].

## January-February: Pel's Last Campaign and Death

In [[January 1876|January 1876]] the troops resumed operations under General Pel for the completion of his program: the conquest of the [[IV Moekims|IV Moekims]] over the [[pas van Blang Kala|pas of Blang Kala]] on [[15 January]] [[15 January]], the [[IX Moekims|IX Moekims]] in ten days from [[28 January]] [[28 January]] to [[7 February]] [[7 February]], and the [[XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]]. On [[24 February]] [[24 February]] [1876] Pel died suddenly of an arterial rupture at the kampong Tonga, where he had his headquarters. The command passed temporarily to Lieutenant-colonel [[F. T. Engel|F. T. Engel]].

## March-April: The Arrival of Habib Abdurrahman and of Wiggers van Kerchem

In [[March 1876|March 1876]] [[Habib Abdoerrahman|Habib Abdurrahman]] arrived in Atjeh: "From Pulau Penang he went, completely disguised as a matelot, aboard a schooner that took him to [[Edi-Tjoet|Edi Tjoet]], whence he reached Great Acheh overland. On the way he collected funds for the war, while a number of fighters from the various coastal states followed him. Later he gradually acquired an important influence on the course of affairs." On [[10 March]] [[10 March]] [1876] Major-general [[G. B. T. Wiggers van Kerchem|Wiggers van Kerchem]] arrived in Atjeh to take over the command. On [[1 April]] [[1 April]] [1876] Colonel [[K. van der Heyden|K. van der Heyden]] took command of the [[Zuidoosterlinie|Zuidoosterlinie]].

## February: The Submission of Gighen

On [[29 February]] [[29 February]] [1876] the vorst of [[Gighen|Gighen]], who had fought against the Dutch in Atjeh but now began to give up hope of driving them out, signed the act of submission and hoisted the Dutch flag. The Dutch then decided to set up a fortification at [[Pedir|Pedir]] on the north coast. The vorst, however, later betook himself to the [[XXII Moekims|XXII Moekims]] to fight against the Dutch there; on [[19 August]] [[19 August]] [1876] the tractaat of friendship with him was cancelled, the Dutch flag was taken down, and the country was again closed to trade.

## May-October: The Operations

Operations of [[May 1876|May 1876]] in the [[IX Moekims|IX Moekims]] and of [[July 1876|July 1876]] in the [[IV Moekims|IV]] and [[VI Moekims|VI Moekims]] had the desired success, but every success was only temporary. In [[September 1876|September 1876]], after the arrival of new reinforcements, the Dutch troops again took more offensive action. The object of the operations was to better secure the [[Noordoosterlinie|Noordoosterlinie]], and to obtain a better boundary line by the occupation of the [[Kroëng Tjoet|lagune Kroëng Tjoet]]. On [[4 September]] [[4 September]] operations began in the [[XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]]. The [[missigit Lambada|missigit Lambada]] of the Noordoosterlinie was the object of a Dutch échec on [[12 October]] [[12 October]].

## November-December: Diemont's Command and the Simpang Olim Expedition

The disagreement between Wiggers van Kerchem and the government over the policy toward the [[Onderhoorigheden|Onderhoorigheden]] led to his resignation; he was succeeded by [[A. J. E. Diemont|Major-general A. J. E. Diemont]], who took over the command on [[6 November]] [[6 November]] [1876]. Diemont at once took up the [[Expedition against Simpang Olim and Tandjong Semantoh (November-December 1876)|expedition against Simpang Olim]] with vigour. On [[14 November]] [[14 November]] the chief place of [[Tandjong Semantoh|Tandjong Semantoh]] was stormed; the impregnable hill-benting of [[Toekoe Paja|Toekoe Paja]] was carried by a single officer, [[A. G. Popelier|A. G. Popelier]], and six Ambonese soldiers. On [[10 December]] [[10 December]] an ultimatum was issued to the radja of [[Simpang Olim|Simpang Olim]]; on [[11 December]] [[11 December]] the Dutch ally [[Toekoe Moeda Angkasa|Toekoe Moeda Angkasa]] was treacherously murdered; on [[12 December]] [[12 December]] the radja was declared deprived of his authority.

## The Engineers' Works

The engineers completed the iron screw-pile sea-head at [[Oleh-leh|Oleh-leh]] in [[September 1876|September 1876]] and the railway between Oleh-leh and [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] in [[November 1876|November 1876]].

## The Koffiebladziekte

In [[1876]] the koffiebladziekte — which had previously ravaged [[Ceylon|Ceylon]] — first appeared in [[Sumatra's Westkust|Sumatra's Westkust]]. In [[1879]] it would penetrate to Java, at first only sporadically, but soon more generally and of a serious character.
