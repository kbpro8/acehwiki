## Account from *Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.* (1954) — 1936

**[[1936]]** is mentioned in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* in two contexts: as the year in which it became clear that [[Dominee Thenu]]'s *ziekbed* was his *doodsbed*, and as the year after which the colonial army began to expand again after the cutbacks of the crisis years — "And after [[1936]] to expand again." The colonial army that had shrunk during the economic depression now rebuilt, only to be dissolved six years later by the Japanese invasion of [[1942]].

*Source: Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.*, by G. W. T. Dames, Maj. Inf. K.N.I.L. b.d. ('s-Gravenhage: Koninklijke Bibliotheek, 1954).

## Account from *Atjeh* by H.C. Zentgraff (1938)

[[H.C. Zentgraff|Zentgraff]] records [[1936]] as the year the befaamde *Köhler-boom* at [[Koetaradja]] — under which gen.-maj. [[J.H.R. Köhler|Köhler]] had stood when he *sneuvelde* on [[8 April]] [[1873]] — was *geveld*. A table was made from the *onderste deel van den stam* and placed in the Atjeh-museum; two *kogels* were found *in de schors, ongeveer ter manshoogte*. Late in [[1936]] the *jonge oeléëbalang* of [[Keureutoe]] — son of [[T. Tjhi Bentara]] — was *geschorst* for *mislukt Westersch gedrag*, opening the way for [[T. Radja Sabi]] to be appointed *waarnemend oeléëbalang* in [[1937]].

*Atjeh*, door H.C. Zentgraff. Gedrukt bij Drukkerij "De Unie", [[1938]], Batavia.

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart (1943)

On [[7 March]] **[[1936]]** Governor [[Van Aken]] handed over the government of Atjeh to Resident [[J. Jongejans|Jongejans]], declaring the sub-divisions [[Alaslanden]], [[Lam Meulò]], [[Meulaboh]] and [[Tapa' Toean]] — the last still governed by officers — fully ripe for civil administration: "the keystone of the pacification". On [[12 September]] [[1936]] [[W. J. Mosselman]] wrote to Swart from [[Tandjoengsari]]. In [[1936]] the son of [[T. Tjhi' Béntara]] was suspended as *[[oelèëbalang]]* of [[Keureutòë]]. [[H. T. Damsté]]'s *Drie Atjehmannen* appeared in the *Koloniaal Tijdschrift* of [[1936]]–[[1938]].

## Account from *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*

[[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] died ('in [[1936]] ontslapen'). [[August]]: [[vloedgolf|Vloedgolf]] (tidal wave) and [[aardbeving]] (earthquake) destroyed [[Oelèë Lheuë]]. Start of [[Van Steenis expedition to Goenoeng Löser]] ([[1936]]-[[1937]]).[[September]] [[1936]] — earthquake in the [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]] cost several lives and did considerable material damage. [[1936]] — BPM abandoned the [[Leuboe]] oil exploration terrain near [[Bireuën]].

*Source: J. Jongejans, Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu (Baarn: Hollandia Drukkerij N.V., c. 1938), pp. 40, 47, 62, 84, 85, 207.*
