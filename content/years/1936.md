## Account from *Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.* (1954) — 1936

**[[1936]]** is mentioned in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* in two contexts: as the year in which it became clear that [[Dominee Thenu]]'s *ziekbed* was his *doodsbed*, and as the year after which the colonial army began to expand again after the cutbacks of the crisis years — "En na [[1936]] maar weer uitbreiden." The colonial army that had shrunk during the economic depression now rebuilt, only to be dissolved six years later by the Japanese invasion of [[1942]].

*Source: Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.*, by G. W. T. Dames, Maj. Inf. K.N.I.L. b.d. ('s-Gravenhage: Koninklijke Bibliotheek, 1954).
