**The year 1943** was, in Piekaar's *Atjèh and the War with Japan*, the year of the consolidation of the Japanese occupation of [[Atjèh|Atjèh]] — the year in which the [[M-organisatie|M-organisatie]] under [[Masubuchi|Masubuchi]] took its final form, in which the [[Maibkatra|Maibkatra]] was installed on [[10 March]] [[1943]] as the official Islamic council for the welfare of [[Asia Timoer Raja|Greater East Asia]] in Atjèh, and in which the [[Atjèh Council|Atjèh Council]], the [[people's army|people's army]] and the welfare office were established as the principal institutional innovations of the occupation.

## The installation of the Maibkatra (10 March)

The most consequential single event of 1943 was the installation of the [[Maibkatra|Maibkatra]] — the *Madjelis agama Islam oentoek bantoean kemakmoeran Asia Timoer Raja di Atjèh* — on [[10 March]] [[1943]]. The installation speech was delivered by Resident lino, who summarised the council's task as "providing support — to the extent that it does not conflict with the Islamic religion — in the promotion of the welfare of Greater East Asia." As chairman of the main board was appointed [[Teukoe Abdoel Azis|Teukoe Abdoel Azis]]; as first and second deputy chairmen [[Teungkoe Moehamad Hasbi|Teungkoe Moehamad Hasbi]] and [[Teungkoe Moehamad Daoed Beureu-éh|Teungkoe Moehamad Daoed Beureu'éh]]; as secretary [[Teungkoe Moehamad Joenoes Djamil|Teungkoe Moehamad Joenoes Djamil]]; with [[Teukoe Djohan Meura'sa|Teukoe Djohan Meura'sa]] as representative of the [[Muhammadijah|Muhammadijah]] and [[Teukoe Moehamad Amin|Teukoe Moehamad Amin]] as representative of the [[Poesa|Poesa]] movement. The same day, [[10 March]] [[1943]], saw the surrender of [[Lieutenant Van Zanten|Lieutenant Van Zanten]] — "almost a year after the official surrender" of [[28 March]] [[1942]] — after his year-long guerrilla in the Atjèhnese highlands.

## The first changes among the gun-chó

The first half of 1943 brought the first reshuffles among the *gun-chó*s. In [[February]] [[1943]], [[Teukoe Nja' Banta|Teukoe Nja' Banta]] of [[Lhóng|Lhóng]] was appointed *gun-chó* of [[Simaloer|Simaloer]]. In [[May]] [[1943]], [[Teukoe Nja' Arif|Teukoe Nja' Arif]] was relieved of his administrative functions; he was succeeded as *controleur* of [[Koetaradja|Koetaradja]] by his brother-in-law [[Teukoe Ali Keureukón|Teukoe Ali Keureukón]]. The first list of F-people was published in the *[[Atjèh Shimbun|Atjèh Shimbun]]* of [[15 December|15]]–[[25 December]] [[1943]]. [[8 December]] [[1943]] saw the presentation of "*soerat poedjian*" (certificates of distinction) at [[Koetaradja|Koetaradja]] to those who had distinguished themselves in the resistance movement — a ceremony at which [[Teukoe Sabi|Teukoe Sabi]] of [[Lageuën|Lageuën]] appeared as the great man of the occasion.

## The people's army and the Atjèh Council

The month of [[November]] [[1943]] brought "three important measures": the establishment of the [[people's army|people's army]] (*giyúgun*) on [[22 November]] [[1943]], the institution of the [[Atjèh Council|Atjèh Council]], and the institution of the welfare office. On [[9 November]] [[1943]] Resident lino addressed a thousand-strong crowd at the great mosque in Koetaradja, declaring: "The intention is that the people's army will perform the light work, while the heavy work will be done by the Japanese army" — and "when the Japanese army advances to London and Washington, the people's army will ensure the defense of the fatherland Sumatra." The first session of the [[Atjèh Council|Atjèh Council]] followed on [[8 December|8]]–[[9 December]] [[1943]].

## The kempeitai strikes

The same year brought the first great kempeitai purges. On [[16 May]] [[1943]] the leaders of the [[Ten Velde-Woudenberg organisation|Ten Velde-Woudenberg organisation]] were arrested. On [[20 September]] [[1943]] the large raid conducted throughout Sumatra "led to the elimination of most remaining resistance nuclei." The execution of the protest leaders [[H. Wolff|Wolff]], [[M. J. Croes|Croes]], [[K. Stoltz|Stoltz]] and [[H. F. Voss|Voss]] at [[Koeta Tjané|Koeta Tjané]] on [[28 May]] [[1943]] — Voss's last words "Long live the Queen" — marked the suppression of the [[Lawé Sigalagala|Lawé Sigalagala]] colonists' protest against enlistment in the *heiho*.

## Significance

1943 was, in Piekaar's reading, the year in which the Japanese occupation definitively took shape: the [[M-organisatie|M-organisatie]] was consolidated, the [[Maibkatra|Maibkatra]] institutionalised the [[Poesa|Poesa]]–*oelama* ascendancy, and the *kempeitai* extinguished the last Dutch underground. By the close of the year the occupation had acquired its enduring form.
