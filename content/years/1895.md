Figures, places, and events in Acehnese history associated with the year [[1895]].

**1895** was the final full year of the [[Stelsel van alliancepen|Dutch allied policy]] of [[September]] [[1893]]–[[April]] [[1896]] and, as described in [[A. Kruisheer|Kruisheer]]'s *ATJEH '96*, the year in which [[Teuku Umar]]'s [[Legioen van T. Djohan (T. Oemar's Legion)|legioen]] reached the height of its operational activity under Dutch patronage while, at the same time, the religious and political conditions for his [[Defection of Teuku Umar (late March 1896)|defection of March 1896]] were quietly maturing. The year saw Umar's *legioen* cooperate with Dutch troops at [[Tjot Goeë]] on [[9 May]] [[1895]] under [[J. F. T. van Bloemen Waanders|Maj. Van Bloemen Waanders]], the [[Recapture of bentengs Glé Broë and Koeta T. Rajoet (4 August 1895)|recapture of bentengs Glé Broë and Koeta T. Rajoet]] on [[4 August]] [[1895]] under Umar's personal direction, the [[November]] [[1895]] visit of the [[Legercommandant]] (the future [[J. A. Vetter|Lt.-Gen. Vetter]]) to [[Ana' Galong]] — where his carriage was shot at near [[Boekit Karang|Boekloet]] despite maréchaussée cover — and the death on [[8 December]] [[1895]] of the great *oelama* [[Teungkoe Koeta Karang]], whose bought-off preaching had hitherto neutralised Achehnese religious opposition to Umar and whose succession by the hostile [[Teungkoe Tanah Abée]] would, within months, expose Umar to the charge of *kafr* and help tip him into open revolt.
