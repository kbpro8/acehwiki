Figures, places, and events in Acehnese history associated with the year 1621.

## People

- [[Augustin de Beaulieu]]
- [[Robert Gravé]]
- [[Jan Pieterszoon Coen]]

## Places

- [[Acheh]]
- [[Tikoe]]
- [[Sumatra's Westkust]]
- [[Jakarta|Jakatra]]

## Events

- [[Augustin de Beaulieu's Atjeh mission (1620-1621)]]
- De Beaulieu's arrival in Atjeh in early [[1621]] from [[Tikoe]]
- Difficult negotiations with the Sultan and the *Rijksgrooten* for permission to buy pepper — De Beaulieu complaining of the extortion of gifts
- The November [[1621]] appearance of an English ship at the *Montmorency*'s cleaning-bay, bearing the gravely ill Vice-Admiral [[Robert Gravé|Robert Gravé]]
- The Dutch recovery of the *l'Espérance* — found "gants machteloos" with 74 souls aboard, of whom only [[Robert Gravé|Gravé]] and three others were on their feet
- The death of [[Robert Gravé|Robert Gravé]] in Atjeh in the night of [[9 November]] [[1621]] and his burial there
