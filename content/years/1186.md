The year of the Hijra in which the only known complete manuscript of the [[Hudjdjat as-siddiq li-daf az-zindiq|*Ḥujjat aṣ-ṣiddīq li-dafʿ az-zindīq*]] of [[Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]] was copied, on Sunday 12 Shaʿbān 1186 (corresponding to late [[1772]]/early [[1773]]), the year *Wāw*, after the morning prayer, by **Encik ʿAbdul ʿAzīz**. The manuscript — Maxwell collection no. 93 in the library of the [[Royal Asiatic Society]] in London — is the basis of [[P. Voorhoeve|Voorhoeve]]'s facsimile edition of [[1955]].
