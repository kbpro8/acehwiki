## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart (1943)

[[C. Lulofs]]'s figures, quoted by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]], take **[[1845]]** as the base year for comparing Dutch troop deployments in the [[Buitengewesten]]: after [[1845]], Atjeh left out of account, only the period [[1871]]–[[1890]] is noteworthily distinguished from the twenty-five preceding and the sixteen following years.
