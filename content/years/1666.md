The year before which [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]] must have died. Neither Raniri's year of birth nor that of his death has been preserved; the latest attested date of his Malay writings is [[1644]], the year in which he returned from [[Acheh]] to [[Ranir]]. The terminus ante quem of [[1666]] is established by [[Hoesein Djajadiningrat|Djajadiningrat]] in *TBG* 69, pp. 109–111.
