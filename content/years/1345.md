Figures, places, and events in Acehnese history associated with the year 1345 — the year of the Arab traveller [[Ibn Battuta|Ibn Battuta]]'s visit to the sultanate of [[Pasej-Samodra|Pasej]] on the north coast of [[Sumatra]], where he was received by [[Sultan Malik at-Tahir of Pasej|Sultan Malik at-Tahir]] and recorded the principal Arabic-language account of the pre-[[Aceh Sultanate|Atjehnese]] north Sumatran sultanate.

## People

- [[Ibn Battuta]] — Arab traveller from Bengal, who visited Pasej
- [[Sultan Malik at-Tahir of Pasej]] — Ibn Battuta's host at Pasej
- [[P. de Roo de la Faille]] — modern source on Ibn Battuta's voyage

## Places

- [[Pasej-Samodra]]
- [[Pasej-Samodra|Sarha]] — port of Pasej, four miles from the capital Samatra
- [[Pasej-Samodra|Samatra / Sjemoe-t]] — capital of Pasej
- [[Moel Djawa]] — kingdom whose capital was Qakola
- [[Bengal]] — Ibn Battuta's point of embarkation
- [[Bengal|Djaouah]] — Ibn Battuta's name for Sumatra

## Events

- [[Ibn Battuta's visit to Pasej (1345)]]

## Concepts

- [[Bedekte taal]] — Ibn Battuta's account is, in De Roo de la Faille's reading, pervaded by it
- [[Wangsallan]]
