The beginning of the first visit of [[Muhammad Djailani Hamid]] — the uncle of [[Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]] — to [[Acheh]]. The visit, which lasted until [[1583]], was the first link of Raniri's family with the Achehnese court. The scholar wished to teach logic, rhetoric, dogmatics, and the doctrine of duties, but found little interest: there was a great influx only for teachers of mysticism. So he sailed on to [[Mecca]], returning some years later provided with the *venia docendi* as a teacher of mystical theology, and then had great success.
