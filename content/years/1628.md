The year of the death of [[Abd al-Qadir al-Aidaros|ʿAbd al-Qādir al-Aydārūs]] at [[Ahmadabad]]. Al-Aydārūs — the uncle by marriage of [[Muhammad al-Addars|Muḥammad al-ʿAddārs]] and so a kinsman of [[Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]] — was a prolific author of the Indian-Arab al-Aydārūs family. Many of his writings are preserved, including correspondence exchanged with a scholar at [[Mocha]]. His father had come from South Arabia to India in [[1551]]; his mother was an Indian. He is one of the figures of the Gujarati milieu from which Raniri himself sprang.
