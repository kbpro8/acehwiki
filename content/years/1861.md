Minister [[Loudon]] wrote to the Governor-General in **[[1861]]**: "I regard every extension of our authority in the Indies archipelago as a step nearer to our fall, and the more so because we have already grown far beyond our strength" — the classic statement of the [[Non-interventiepolitiek|policy of non-intervention]].
