**1674** was a pivotal year in the **[[Dutch Republic]]**’s military history, marked by the **[[Siege of Naarden (1674)]]** and the **[[Siege of Grave (1674)]]** against French and Spanish forces (see [[276]]‑[[277]]).  The successful Dutch operations secured the home front, allowing the **[[Dutch Navy]]** to continue protecting the **[[Indische retourvloot]]**, which carried spices from **[[Aceh]]** and other East Indies ports to Europe.

## Broader Aceh context
During this year the **[[Dutch East India Company]]** continued its profitable trade with **[[Aceh]]**, sending regular shipments of pepper that fed the European market.  The stability achieved by the Dutch victories at home indirectly supported the VOC’s overseas activities in Aceh.
