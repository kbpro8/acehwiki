**The year 1933** was, in Piekaar's *Atjèh and the War with Japan*, the year of the [[Lhóng action (1933)|Lhóng action of 1933]] — the "last of significance" of the armed actions "which repeatedly flared up after the conclusion of the [[Atjèh War|Atjèh War]]," fought in the remote *oelèëbalangship* of [[Lhóng|Lhóng]] in the *sagi* of the [[XXV Moekims|XXV Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjèh|Groot-Atjèh]]. With the suppression of the rising and the installation of [[Teukoe Radja Djoema'at|Teukoe Radja Djoema'at]] as *oelèëbalang* of Lhóng, 1933 closed the cycle of armed resistance that had run intermittently since [[1873]].

## The rising in Lhóng

Lhóng lies in the wild mountain country where the [[Barisan|Barisan]] range "reaches here, as in the adjacent area of the sagi of the XXV Moekims … right down to the coast." Its isolation preserved, even into the 1930s, the religious mentality of the [[Atjèh War|Atjèh War]] generation: "the standstill of time was reflected in the religious instruction. Here no modernization of religious education, as one found in Groot-Atjèh and on the North Coast." The [[Lhóng action (1933)|Lhóng action of 1933]] was the last flicker of the *[[prang sabi|prang sabi]]* tradition that had animated the [[Bakóngan uprising (1925–1927)|Bakóngan uprising]] of [[1925]]–[[1927]] and, before it, the [[Atjèh War|Atjèh War]] itself. Garrisons at [[Tjalang|Tjalang]] and [[Lam Nó|Lam Nó]] and a detachment at [[Lam Soedjèn|Lam Soedjèn]] in Lhóng had been maintained precisely to make "intensive patrolling possible" in this volatile country.

## The settlement

The rising's suppression inaugurated two decades of peace in Lhóng. The new *oelèëbalang*, [[Teukoe Radja Djoema'at|Teukoe Radja Djoema'at]], was confirmed as an "energetic forty-year-old" who "had restored confidence and authority in this area and had brought about orderly conditions." His installation was, in turn, a quiet re-foundation of the *oelèëbalang* class — the very class that, in [[March]] [[1942]], would be purged by [[G. F. V. Gosenson|G. F. V. Gosenson]] in the celebrated "coup d'état," and again by the [[kempeitai|kempeitai]] in [[1945]].

## Other events of the year

1933 was also the year in which a nationalist protest against the introduction of Acehnese as the medium of instruction in the primary school (the so-called *Acehnese language* controversy of [[1931]]) was still reverberating in the Atjèhnese political consciousness — a controversy Piekaar cites as the first clear expression of an "all-encompassing Indonesian national consciousness" among the more developed *oelèëbalang*s. The persistent refusal of the *oelèëbalang* of [[Ba'ét|Ba'ét]] to pay the customary *[[hari raja puasa|hari raja puasa]]* respects to [[Panglima Polem|Panglima Polem]] of [[Seulimeum|Seulimeum]] continued throughout the year, marking the survival of the older antagonisms even in the supposedly pacified *sagi* of the [[XXII Moekims|XXII Moekims]].

## Significance

1933 closed the era of armed resistance in colonial Atjèh. When the next rising came — the [[revolt in Great Atjèh|revolt in Great Atjèh]] of [[1942]] — it would no longer be a localised, religiously motivated *[[prang sabi|prang sabi]]* in the mountain fastnesses, but a coordinated fifth-column movement aligned with the [[Japanese invasion of Atjèh|Japanese invasion]].

## Account from *Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.* (1954) — 1933

**[[1933]]** was the approximate year of the opening anecdote of *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* — the captain of the [[1e Divisie Marechaussee uit Blang Kedjeren]]'s [[Hotel de Boer]] story at the *Soos* in [[Koeta-Radja|Koeta-Radja]] — and the year of the kolonel's visit to [[Bakongan]] at which [[Thijs de Fretes]] promised the kolonel the tanden of a rogue elephant and shot it at five metres' distance. It is also the year of the death of the *Overste* [[Overste-Scheepens|Scheepens]] — who had been wounded by an Acehnese during a court session in [[1913]] and died of his wounds in [[1933]] after twenty years.

## Account from *Atjeh* by H.C. Zentgraff (1938)

[[H.C. Zentgraff|Zentgraff]] records [[1933]] as a year of new resistance and notable deaths. At [[Lhong]], dertien mannen went over to *verzet*; one who *zich bedacht en zich meldde* was rejected by his wife — *"Itoe man? Ik heb geen man."* — and the episode produced *elf weduwen en twintig weezen*, a new kernel of *haat* for the future. *Tgk. Doellah* was *neergeslagen* at Lhong; a [[hikajat prang]] was found on his body. [[Potjoet Barin]] — *oeléëbalang* of [[Toengkob]] on the Westkust, who had fought the Dutch with a *peudeueng* (sword) in the field and lost a foot to a Dutch bullet that was later replaced by a wooden leg from Holland — died in [[1933]].

*Atjeh*, door H.C. Zentgraff. Gedrukt bij Drukkerij "De Unie", [[1938]], Batavia.
