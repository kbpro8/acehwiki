**889** marks the year in which the celebrated Arabic poet **Thābit ibn Kūtaibā** (also rendered as *Thn Kutaiba*) died in Baghdad. His works on the theory of the *qasīda* were later cited by later scholars, and his definitions of the *nasīb* and *raḥīl* informed the classical structure that would be transmitted to Acehnese *kasida* traditions.
