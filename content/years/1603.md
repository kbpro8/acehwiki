Figures, places, and events in Acehnese history associated with the year 1603.

## People

- [[Frederik de Houtman]]
- [[Sebald de Weert]]
- [[Joris van Spilbergen|Joris van Spilbergh]]

## Places

- [[Acheh]]
- [[Batticaloa]]
- [[Kandi]]

## Events

- Publication of [[Frederik de Houtman|Frederik de Houtman]]'s [[Spraeck ende woord-boeck (1603)|*Spraeck ende woord-boeck* (1603)]] — the first Dutch-Malay dialogue-book, composed from notes taken during his Atjeh captivity of [[1599]]–[[1601]]
- The return of [[Sebald de Weert|De Weert]]'s fleet (with Atjeh ambassadors aboard) to Batticaloa in April [[1603]]
- The fatal banquet at Batticaloa and the murder of [[Sebald de Weert|Sebald de Weert]] at the hands of the King of Kandi's retinue — with the Atjeh ambassador at the King's ear egging him on
- The arrival of the first [[Vereenigde Oost-Indische Compagnie|VOC]] fleet at Atjeh (the Company having been founded [[20 March]] [[1602]])
