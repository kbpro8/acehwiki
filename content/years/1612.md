**The year 1612** was the year of the commissioning of the *[[Sadjarah Malajoe]]* (the Malay Annals) — the principal Malay chronicle of the [[Malacca|Malacca]]-[[Djohor|Johor]] sultanate — at [[Batoe Sawar]] on the *Mawlid* (Mūlūd) festival of Sunday 13 Rabīʿ I 1021 AH, that is, [[13 May]] [[1612]]. The commission — by *Radja 'Abdoellāh*, the younger brother of the reigning Sultan Alā'uddīn Ri'āyat Shāh III of Johor — was the event that inaugurated the composition of the principal Malay chronicle of the early-modern Malay-Indonesian world, and the year [[1612]] is, in [[C. Hooykaas|Hooykaas]]'s reading in [[Over Maleise Literatuur (1947)|his survey]] of Malay literature, the principal literary date of the late-Batoe Sawar period of Johor history.

## The Commissioning of the Sadjarah Malajoe

The *[[Sadjarah Malajoe]]* was commissioned on the *Mawlid* festival of Sunday 13 Rabīʿ I 1021 AH — that is, [[13 May]] [[1612]] — at [[Batoe Sawar]], the capital of Johor. The commission was by *Radja 'Abdoellāh*, the younger brother of the reigning Sultan Alā'uddīn Ri'āyat Shāh III, who asked the *[[Bendahara Sri Lanang|Bendahara]]* — *Tun Sri Lanang*, also styled *Paduka Raja* — to "beteren" (improve) an existing *Hikajat Malajoe* that had been brought to Batoe Sawar "door mensen uit Goa" (by people from Goa).

The commissioning, in [[C. Hooykaas|Hooykaas]]'s reading, was for a "Geslachtslijst der Soeltanen" (Genealogy of the Sultans) — a work that was, in its conception, modelled on the *[[Tādj oes-Salatina|Tādj us-Salāṭīn]]* of [[1607]] that the *Bendahara* would later encounter in Atjeh after his [[1613]] captivity. The completed work — 34 stories — was finished after the *Bendahara*'s return from Atjeh in [[1614]], with later stories (35-38) added to cover the [[1615]] Atjehnese attack and the subsequent exile of the Johor court.

## The Atjehnese Context

The commissioning of the *[[Sadjarah Malajoe]]* in [[1612]] falls in the middle of the [[Sultan Iskandar Muda|Iskandar Muda]] reign ([[1607]]-[[1636]]) — the *bloeitijdperk* (golden age) of Atjeh — and just one year before the [[1613]] Atjehnese attack on Batoe Sawar that would interrupt the work and carry off the *Bendahara* to Atjeh. The Atjehnese context of the commissioning is thus the immediate threat: the court of Johor, under the shadow of the rising Atjehnese power of [[Sultan Iskandar Muda|Iskandar Muda]], commissioned the *Sadjarah Malajoe* as a record of the Malay sultanate's past greatness in the face of the Atjehnese threat to its future.

The commissioning of the *Sadjarah Malajoe* in [[1612]] is, in Hooykaas's reading, also the moment at which the *Bendahara* would have become aware of the *[[Tādj oes-Salatina|Tādj us-Salāṭīn]]* of [[1607]] — the Atjehnese "mirror for princes" completed in the year of [[Sultan Iskandar Muda|Iskandar Muda]]'s accession. The *Bendahara*'s model for his "Geslachtslijst der Soeltanen" — in Hooykaas's reading — was the *Tādj*, and the *Bendahara* would have become acquainted with the *Tādj* either through the Atjehnese attack of [[1613]] (which carried him off to Atjeh) or through the regular Malay-Atjehnese literary exchange of the early seventeenth century.

## Significance

The year [[1612]] is, in Hooykaas's reading, the principal literary date of the late-Batoe Sawar period of Johor history. The commissioning of the *[[Sadjarah Malajoe]]* on [[13 May]] [[1612]] inaugurated the composition of the principal Malay chronicle of the early-modern Malay-Indonesian world, and the year [[1612]] is the chronological anchor of the *Sadjarah Malajoe*'s composition history — the year before the [[1613]] Atjehnese attack, and the year in which the *Bendahara*'s project of a "Geslachtslijst der Soeltanen" was first conceived.
