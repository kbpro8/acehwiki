The year in which [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] sent to [[Leiden]] the collection of manuscripts from which the Leiden Cod. Or. 5664 — the manuscript of the [[Tamhid fi bayan at-tawhid|*Tamhīd*]] of [[Abu Syakur as-Salimi|Abū Shakūr as-Sālimī]] — derives. This manuscript, almost entirely of [[Banten]] provenance, was shown by [[P. Voorhoeve|Voorhoeve]] to preserve a text practically identical to that of [[Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]]'s own exemplar of the *Tamhīd*, including the shared errors (such as *Kubtiyya* for *al-Muʿattila*) that allowed Voorhoeve to identify the *Tamhīd* as the principal source of the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān*]]. The year [[1906]] is also the year of publication of the English translation of *The Achehnese* by Snouck Hurgronje.

## Account from *De Buitenbezittingen* (1917) — 1906

**[[1906]]** was the year in which the second tramway of the [[Atjeh's Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh Dependencies]] — the line under construction from [[1899]] onward — was "successively completed" from [[Sigli]] to the harbour of [[Langsa]]. According to the Encyclopaedisch Bureau, "from [[1899]] onward, [the Gouvernement] was already proceeding to the construction of a second tramway, in the [[Atjeh's Onderhoorigheden|Onderhoorigheden]], where between [[1899]]–[[1906]] the line was successively completed from [[Sigli]] to the harbour of [[Langsa]], to which between [[1910]]–[[1912]] the [[Langsa]]–[[Koeala Simpang]] section was added, while already in [[1899]] from [[Sigli]] a lijntje was built westward over [[Padang Tidji]] to [[Keude Breue]]."

The [[1906]] completion of the [[Sigli]]–[[Langsa]] line marked a major logistical milestone in the [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacification of Atjeh]]. The line opened a direct railway connection from [[Sigli]] — the central junction of the second tramway — to the eastern harbour of [[Langsa]] in the [[Atjeh's Onderhoorigheden|Dependencies]]. The [[1904]]–[[1908]] construction of the [[Berglijntje Keude Breue–Seulimeum|berglijntje]] was still in progress, so the [[1906]] completion of the [[Sigli]]–[[Langsa]] line preceded the integration of the [[Atjeh's Onderhoorigheden|Dependencies]] network with the [[Groot-Atjeh|Great Atjeh]] network by two years.

The Encyclopaedisch Bureau's statistical tables for the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]] show that the line's route length grew from 326 K.M. in [[1905]] to 401 K.M. in [[1906]] — a 75 K.M. jump that reflects the completion of the [[Sigli]]–[[Langsa]] line. The aanlegkapitaal likewise rose from f 9,873,000 in [[1905]] to f 12,694,000 in [[1906]].


## Account from *Oost-Indisch Journael* (1947) — 1906

**[[1906]]** was the year in which the colonial army, after the failed trials with foreign elephants in [[1880]]–[[1881]], tried to train indigenous elephants and put them in service for the military transports in the Region — the beginning of the [[transport-olifanten (Aceh)|elephant-transport]] of [[Atjeh|Aceh]]. After the narrator believes that military trials with foreign elephants as pack-animals had failed in [[1880]]–[[1881]], in [[1906]] the colonial army tried to train indigenous elephants and put them in service for the military transports in the Region. This trial turned out favourably. The [[1906]] trial — the trial with indigenous elephants — is the marker of the beginning of the elephant-transport: the year that the colonial army, after the [[1880]]–[[1881]] failure with foreign elephants, tried the indigenous elephants and the trial turned out favourably. By [[1913]] the transport-elephants would be transported to [[Lam-toeulo]] to serve the transport to [[Tangsé]] and the still more southerly post [[Geumpang]].

## Significance

[[1906]] is, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, the year of the completion of the [[Sigli]]–[[Langsa]] line — the second tramway of the [[Atjeh's Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh Dependencies]] in its original form. The completion marks a major logistical milestone in the [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacification of Atjeh]], opening a direct railway connection from the central junction at [[Sigli]] to the eastern harbour of [[Langsa]].
