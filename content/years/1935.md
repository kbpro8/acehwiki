**[[1935]]** was the year in which [[Dominee Thenu]] became ill — the beginning of the ziekbed that would be his doodsbed. "In [[1935]] werd hij ziek." The year is the book's marker of the beginning of Thenu's decline, two years before his death on [[11 May]] [[1937]].
