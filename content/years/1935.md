**[[1935]]** was the year in which [[Dominee Thenu]] became ill — the beginning of the ziekbed that would be his doodsbed. "In [[1935]] werd hij ziek." The year is the book's marker of the beginning of Thenu's decline, two years before his death on [[11 May]] [[1937]].

## Account from *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*

Verzetsgebied ([[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] area) near [[Geunteuët]] (also [[1933]]). [[Sigli ambachtsleergang opened]], initially teaching only metaalbewerking.

*Source: J. Jongejans, Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu (Baarn: Hollandia Drukkerij N.V., c. 1938), pp. 49, 59.*
