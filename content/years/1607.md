## Additional Information from 1607

Figures, places, and events in Atjehnese history associated with the year 1607.

## People

- [[Sultan Iskandar Muda]] — accession as Sultan of Atjeh in 1607; his reign (1607-1636) inaugurates the [[Atjeh bloeitijdperk (1607-1636)|Atjeh *bloeitijdperk*]] (golden age).

## Events

- [[Atjeh bloeitijdperk (1607-1636)]] — beginning of the Atjehnese golden age with the accession of [[Sultan Iskandar Muda]].

## Places

- [[Banda Acheh]] — the Atjehnese capital (Koetaradja), seat of [[Sultan Iskandar Muda]].
- [[Acheh]] — the sultanate at the start of its golden age.
