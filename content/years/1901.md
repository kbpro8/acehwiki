## Account from *Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.* (1954) — 1901

**[[1901]]** was the year in which [[Governor Van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] went to [[Samalanga]] and, on 3 February of that year, finally captured it after the three earlier failed attempts of [[1880]]. The year [[1901]] is one of the key years of the late-[[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]] Dutch offensive, and the year for which [[Dominee Thenu]] was made *Ridder in de Orde van Oranje Nassau* at age 33 — his first major decoration.

*Source: Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.*, by G. W. T. Dames, Maj. Inf. K.N.I.L. b.d. ('s-Gravenhage: Koninklijke Bibliotheek, 1954).
