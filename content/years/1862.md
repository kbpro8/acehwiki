Figures, places, and events in Acehnese history associated with the year 1862 — the year in which an [[Atjehsche avonturier at Aroe-baai (1862)|Atjehscher avonturier]] threw up a *benteng* on the hill of [[Poelo Kampi]] in [[Aroe-baai|the bay of Aroe]] and defiantly flew the [[Atjehsche vlag|Atjehsche flag]] from it, recalling the [[Atjeh-Aroe War (1539)|Atjehnese conquest of Aroe]] of [[1539]] to the [[Van Rijn van Alkemade|Van Rijn]] visit of [[1888]].

## People

- [[Atjehsche avonturier at Aroe-baai (1862)|Atjehscher avonturier]] — the unnamed Atjehnese adventurer
- [[Van Rijn van Alkemade]] — recorded the benteng in 1888
