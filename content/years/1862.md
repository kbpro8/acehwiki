Figures, places, and events in Acehnese history associated with the year **1862** — a year that brackets two distinct Atjeh-related episodes on [[Sumatra's East Coast]]: the [[Langkat's appeal to Siak against Atjeh (1862)|Langkat appeal to Siak against Atjeh]] and the [[Netscher|Netscher]] mission to the East Coast statelets, recorded by the Encyclopaedisch Bureau (1918); and the [[Atjehsche avonturier at Aroe-baai (1862)|Atjehscher avonturier]]'s defiance at [[Aroe-baai]], recorded by [[P. de Roo de la Faille|De Roo de la Faille]] (1954).

## Events

- [[Atjehsche avonturier at Aroe-baai (1862)]] — the Atjehnese adventurer threw up a *benteng* on the hill of Poelo Kampi in the bay of Aroe and defiantly flew the Atjehsche flag from it, recalling the Atjehnese conquest of Aroe of 1539.
- [[Langkat's appeal to Siak against Atjeh (1862)]] — the formal request by the Radja of Langkat to Sultan Tongkoe Ismail of Siak for an act of Siak recognition that would unlock Dutch protection against Atjeh.
- The [[Netscher|Netscher]] mission to the East Coast statelets — Resident Netscher of Riouw, "op aandrang van Tongkoe Ismail van Siak", was instructed "in 1862 opgedragen, meer aanrakingen te zoeken met de verschillende hoofden der in het Siak-tractaat als onderhoorigheden van Siak genoemde staatjes, teneinde deze te bewegen tot eene erkenning van die onderhoorigheid." Deli, Serdang, Langkat en enkele kleinere kuststaatjes gingen hiertoe over; Asahan and its vassals refused.
