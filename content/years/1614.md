The year of the volcanic eruption that destroyed [[Pulo Ai]] — one of the islands of the Banda group — on Monday 3 Rabīʿ I 1023, that is, [[14 April]] [[1614]]. The eruption is recorded in a note on f. 1v of Leiden Cod. Or. 1255, a Moluccan manuscript of the *[[Ma'rifat al-madhahib|Maʿrifat al-madhāhib*]] with interlinear Malay translation, written on paper bearing the watermark of Thann in Alsace (in use from [[1612]]). This manuscript, which [[P. Voorhoeve|Voorhoeve]] identifies as Raniri's source for the second part of his [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān*]], is one of the oldest witnesses to the diffusion of Malay religious prose in the Indonesian archipelago before Raniri's arrival in [[Acheh]].
