## People
- [[J. F. Keulemans]] — honourable discharge as head of Cultural Affairs N.S.T.; departed for Netherlands 9 May; pre-war businessman in [[Atjeh]].
- [[Tengku M. Bahar]] — appointed acting head of Cultural Affairs N.S.T. 1 April.
- [[Tengku M. Daudsjah]] — acting resident of [[Atjeh]] in the transitional North Sumatra government.
- [[Koesen Tjokrosentono]] — promoted from head of information in [[Atjeh]] to head of General Affairs, Nd.-Sumatra, March.
- [[J. S. Broers]] — Société Financière inspector; pre-war service at Westkust van [[Atjeh]].
- [[N. Breukels]] — repatriated 6 Nov.; formerly at [[Atjeh-drukkerij]] in [[Kotaradja]] 1920-1942.
- [[B. de Jong]] — seriously wounded by Atjehse amokmaker at [[T. Pura]], 22 May.
- [[C. A. Zandbergen]] — seriously wounded by Atjehse amokmaker at T. Pura, 22 May.
- [[C. Redevoded-Hendriks]] — posthumous royal decoration; known from [[Atjeh]].
- [[J. W. de Veer]] — former administrator of Redelong tea factory in Nd. [[Takengon]]; royal decoration.
- [[Sutan Barus]] — inspector-general of enterprises in [[Atjeh]]; led second survey party Feb.
- [[C. A. Kleingeld]] — Sipef inspector; stationed at [[Langsar Estate]] for several months.
- [[W. J. J. A. van den Bosch]] — B.P.M. survey party to [[Atjeh]], Feb.
- [[Rasjid Siregar]] — Sarbupri commissioner; accompanied first survey party to [[Atjeh]], Feb.
- [[Said Abubakar]] — appointed manager (*beheerder*) of enterprise in [[Atjeh]].
- [[F. F. L. Lingen]] — repatriated 21 June; belonged to Atjeh-party during Japanese occupation.
- [[A. W. Wortman Jr.]] — formerly resident lt. artillery of [[Atjeh]]; royal decoration June.
- [[C. J. J. Hogenboom]] — N.S.T. College of Delegates member; royal decoration 23 May.
- [[Sarimin Reksodihardjo]] — acting governor of North Sumatra; visited [[Atjeh]] Feb.; headed N.S.T. liquidation committee.
- [[Radja Kaliamsjah Sinaga]] — former Wakil Wali Negara; member of transitional advisory college.
- [[Anak Agung Gde Agung]] — established N.S.T. liquidation committee 14 July.
- [[Maj. W. Spiering]] — KNIL battalion commander formerly at [[Sabang]]; company handed over to APRIS 30 May at Stabat.

## Places
- [[Kotaradja]] — site of survey-party discussions Dec. 1949 and V.V.R.O.S. delegation Aug.
- [[Atjeh-drukkerij]] — printing office in [[Kotaradja]]; former workplace of [[N. Breukels]].
- [[Langsar Estate]] — key destination for survey parties; enterprise returned to management.
- [[T. Pura]] — scene of amok incident, 22 May.
- [[Westkust van Atjeh]] — pre-war posting of [[J. S. Broers]].
- [[Redelong]] — tea factory in Nd. [[Takengon]]; former workplace of [[J. W. de Veer]].

## Events
- [[Integration of Atjeh into North Sumatra Province (1950)]] — dissolution of N.S.T.; Atjeh incorporated into province of North Sumatra.
- [[Survey Parties to Atjeh (February 1950)]] — assessment of plantation enterprises in [[Atjeh]].
- [[T. Pura Amok Incident (22 May 1950)]] — [[B. de Jong]] and [[C. A. Zandbergen]] wounded.
- [[V.V.R.O.S. Delegation to Kotaradja (August 1950)]] — negotiations on return of enterprises.
- [[Governor Sarimin's Visit to Atjeh (February 1950)]] — first impressions favourable politically, difficult economically.

## Organizations
- [[Atjeh-drukkerij]] — principal printing office in [[Kotaradja]]; former workplace of [[N. Breukels]] 1920-1942.
- [[Oostkust van Sumatra-Instituut]] — published *Deli-Figuren* compiled by [[F. J. J. Dootjes]].

## Concepts
- [[Atjeh-party (POW group)]] — POW group from [[Atjeh]] sent to Pakan Baroe railway.
- [[Survey-parties (plantation assessment)]] — mechanism for evaluating plantation conditions.
