**[[1931]]** is mentioned in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* in connection with the *bivak* at [[Bakongan]] — "een brigadecommandant, die vandaar in [[1931]] met pensioen was gegaan, maar die lezen noch schrijven kon" — the brigadecommandant who retired from Bakongan in 1931 and could neither read nor write, but who dictated his patrol reports from memory. "In [[Juli]] [[1931]] viel de laatste" — the last of the Westkust resistance fell in July 1931.
