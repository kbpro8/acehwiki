**The year 1943** was, in the [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|Dutch staff history's]] account (Deel VI, Hoofdstuk V), the year of the Japanese counter-operation against Lt [[H. van Zanten|lieutenant H. van Zanten]]'s post-capitulation resistance in [[Atjeh|Atjeh]] — the year of the November [[1942]] arrests of fourteen Redelong soldiers, of the [[26 November]] [[26 November 1942|26 November 1942]] Japanese attempt on Van Zanten's bivouac, and of Van Zanten's [[10 March]] [[10 March 1943|10 March 1943]] capture, court-martial at [[Fort de Rock|Fort de Rock]] and execution. The year [[1943]] is the *terminus ad quem* of the Dutch post-capitulation resistance in Atjeh.
