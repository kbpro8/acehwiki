**1500** was a year of significance in the history of [[Aceh]], as recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Around 1500: Islamization of Atjeh coast complete (transition not violent).

## Events

Around 1500: [[Islam comes to Atjeh / first Muslim sultan at Samoedra-Pasè|Islamization of Atjeh]] coast complete (transition not violent). Before 1500: [[Pidië]] conquered Atjeh and made it a vassal state. Portuguese appeared in [[East Indian Archipelago|the archipelago]] around 1500with plans to control all trade.1500–[[1550]], the rise of the Aceh [[sultanate]].

## Significance

The year 1500 is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the chronological framework of [[Aceh]]'s history.
