**1670** is notable for the secret **[[Treaty of Dover (1670)]]** signed on **[[3 December]] [[1670]]** between **[[England|England]]** and **[[France|France]]**, in which **[[Charles II of England]]** received a two‑million‑livre subsidy from **[[Louis XIV]]**. The treaty’s clandestine nature contributed to the **[[Rampjaar|Disaster Year]]** [[1672]] and disrupted the **[[Dutch East India Company|VOC]]**’s protection of its trade routes to the **[[Sultanate of Aceh]]**.
