**-2000** was a year of significance in the history of [[Aceh]], as recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Ancestors of the Atjehners probably already settled in the land, perhaps a couple of thousand years before the beginning of our era.

## Events

Ancestors of the Atjehners probably already settled in the land, perhaps a couple of thousand years before the beginning of our era. Came from elsewhere in Asia, with related peoples of [[East Indian Archipelago|the archipelago]], with a certain degree of civilization. Were farmers, chose permanent settlements.

## Significance

The year -[[2000]] is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the chronological framework of [[Aceh]]'s history.
