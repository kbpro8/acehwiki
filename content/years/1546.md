Figures, places, and events in Acehnese history associated with the year 1546 — the year of [[Demak's attack on Pasoeroewan (1546)|Demak's attack on Pasoeroewan]] in East Java, the death of the king of [[Demak|Demak]] in the war-council, and the close of the Hindu-Javanese age in Java — the Javanese counterpart of the [[Atjeh-Aroe War (1539)|Atjehnese war on Aroe]] of [[1539]].

## People

- [[Mendez Pinto]] — eyewitness, in Pinto's *Peregrinacam*
- [[Pati Oenoes]] / [[Soenan Kalidjaga]] — king of Demak (in De Roo de la Faille's identification)
- [[Faletahan]] / Sunan Gunung Jati — summoned to the campaign
- [[Djaka Tingkir]] — later Sultan of Padjang, hanged the rioters
- [[Njahi Pombaja]] — envoy of the king of Demak at Banten
- [[P. de Roo de la Faille]] — modern source

## Places

- [[Demak]]
- [[Pasuruan|Pasoeroewan]]
- [[Japara]]
- [[Banten]]
- [[Surabaya]]
- [[Sidayu]]

- [[Demak's attack on Pasoeroewan (1546)]]

## Sources
