The year of the death of [[Sajjid Abu Hafs Umar Ba Sjaiban|Sayyid ʿUmar Bā Shaybān]] at [[Bilgram|Bilgrām]]. Bā Shaybān, of Hadrami descent but born in India, was the shaikh of [[Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]] in the [[Rifa'iyah order|Rifāʿiyya]] and the teacher of [[Yusuf Makasar|Shaikh Yūsuf al-Makassar]]. He had studied in Arabia but later settled in India, where he was received into the Rifāʿiyya by [[Muhammad al-Addars|Muḥammad al-ʿAddārs]] at [[Surat]]. He lived chiefly at [[Bijapur|Bījāpūr]], where he enjoyed the protection of the ruler Muḥammad ʿĀdilshāh. His death in [[1656]] removes the last of the spiritual masters who had formed Raniri in the Gujarati-Ḥadramī network of the Rifāʿiyya.
