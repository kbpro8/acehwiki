## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart (1943)

By royal decree of [[13 July]] **[[1900]]** [[H. N. A. Swart]] was promoted Knight 3rd Class of the [[Militaire Willemsorde]]. He remained on operations in Atjeh until May [[1900]], was granted a year's European leave on [[6 July]] and departed on [[18 July]] aboard the *s.s. Lawoe*. Dutch colonial administration in the [[Buitengewesten]] could, [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]] argued, be set as an example to all colonial powers after [[1910]]; before [[1900]] the Netherlands Government "by no means felt itself the fairy-tale prince".
