**1668** saw the signing of the **[[Triple Alliance (1668)]]** between the **[[United Provinces (Dutch Republic)]], [[England|England]]**, and **[[Sweden|Sweden]]**, a coalition designed to counter **[[Louis XIV]]**’s expansionist aims. The alliance helped preserve Dutch naval dominance, which was crucial for safeguarding **[[VOC]]** commerce with the **[[Sultanate of Aceh]]**.
