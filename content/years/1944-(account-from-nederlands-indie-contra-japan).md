**The year 1944** was, in the [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|Dutch staff history's]] account (Deel VI, Hoofdstuk V), the year of Lt [[L. C. G. F. Onvlee|L. C. G. F. Onvlee]]'s designation for transport to Japan and the temporary hand-over of the *vaandel* (colours) of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Korps Maréchaussee]] to the Dutch camp commander of his Birma prisoner-of-war camp. The year [[1944]] is the staff history's principal narrative instance of the vaandel's temporary loss and recovery in the Onvlee episode.

## The vaandel under the camp commander

The camp commander held the vaandel from 2 March [[1944]] until its return to Onvlee on [[10 May 1945]] — a period of more than fourteen months. The staff history does not record any incident during this period of temporary custody, and the vaandel passed undetected through the Japanese searches and unannounced inspections that continued throughout [[1944]] and into [[1945]].
