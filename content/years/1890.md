## Account from *The Geology of Indonesia* ([[1949]]) — 1890

### The founding of the Koninklijke Maatschappij

Van Bemmelen's account of the [[1890]] founding reads:

> "[[1890]] the concession became the first oil property of the newly founded 'Koninklijke Maatschappij tot ontginning van Petroleum terreinen in Nederlandsch Indië' (Royal Company for the exploitation of oil fields in the Netherlands East Indies). This concern is now known all over the world by the name of Royal Dutch."

The [[1890]] founding of the *Koninklijke Maatschappij* — the future Royal Dutch — took place in the immediate aftermath of the [[1885]] [[Ph. C. Fennema|Fennema]]-led drilling success at [[Telaga Said]]. The first oil property of the new company was accordingly the Telaga Said concession itself, which the company acquired in [[1890]] and which became the founding asset of Royal Dutch. The northern-Sumatran oil industry is, in this sense, the originating locus of the entire Royal Dutch (and subsequently Royal Dutch Shell) oil empire.

### Place in the northern-Sumatran oil sequence

The [[1890]] founding of the *Konoinlijk Maatschappij* occupies, in the documentary sequence of northern-Sumatran oil exploration, the position immediately after the [[1885]] Telaga Said drilling and immediately before the [[1907]] amalgamation with Shell. The sequence runs:

1. **[[1883]]**: [[J. Zulkler|J. Zulkler]] obtains the first North-Sumatran oil concession.
2. **[[1885]]**: The [[Ph. C. Fennema|Fennema]]-led government crew drills the [[Telaga Said]] well to success.
3. **[[1890]]**: Founding of the *Koninklijke Maatschappij* (the future Royal Dutch), with the Telaga Said concession as its first oil property.
4. **[[1907]]**: Amalgamation of Royal Dutch with the Shell Transport and Trading Company, creating the [[Royal Dutch Shell]] group; the [[B.P.M.]] is created as the Royal Dutch subsidiary for the East Indian oil interests.
5. **[[1912]]**: The [[Standard Oil Company of New Jersey]] enters the Indies through its subsidiary the [[N.K.P.M.]]
6. **[[1931]]**: The [[Standard Oil Company of California]] enters through its subsidiary the [[N.P.P.M.]]
7. **[[1940]]–[[1941]]**: The [[Van Bemmelen's The Geology of Indonesia (1949)|Van Bemmelen]] production tables record the [[East Atjeh oil block|East Atjeh]], [[Tamiang oil block|Tamiang]] and [[Atjeh oil complex]] fields on Atjeh territory.

### Significance

[[1890]] is significant for the encyclopedia of [[Acheh]] history because it is the year of the founding of the *Koninklijke Maatschappij* (the future Royal Dutch) — the founding corporate episode of the northern-Sumatran oil industry, and the originating act of the corporate sequence that would lead, via the [[1907]] amalgamation with Shell, to the [[1940]]–[[1941]] production of the [[East Atjeh oil block|East Atjeh]], [[Tamiang oil block|Tamiang]] and [[Atjeh oil complex]] oil fields on Atjeh territory. The fact that the originating asset of the entire Royal Dutch (and subsequently Royal Dutch Shell) oil empire was the [[1885]] Telaga Said drilling on the [[1883]] Zulkler concession also makes the northern-Sumatran oil industry the founding locus of the company's global presence, and [[1890]] the year in which that founding locus was translated into a corporate structure.
