The year of [[Raniri's arrival in Acheh (1637)|the arrival of Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī in Acheh]], in the first year of the reign of [[Iskandar Thani]]. Raniri states in his [[Sirat al-mustaqim|*Ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm*]] that he came to Acheh in [[1637]] while Iskandar Thani was king; since Iskandar Thani succeeded [[Sultan Iskandar Muda|Meukuta Alam]] (Iskandar Muda) on the latter's death in [[1636]], this testimony fixes the death of the great Sultan at 1636 — a date that [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] accepts as confirmed against the uncertainties of native chronicle and tradition. Shortly after the accession of Iskandar II, Raniri [[Raniri's public debate against the Wujudiyyah in Acheh|refuted the adherents of the extreme Wujūdiyyah mysticism in a public debate]] in the presence of the sultan, and from that time forth he enjoyed royal favour, both from Iskandar II and from his successor [[Sultan Tajul-alam|Ṣalīyatu'd-Dīn]] (Taj al-Ālam). The composition of the [[Sirat al-mustaqim|*Ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm*]] (March [[1638]]) and of the [[Bad' khalq as-samawat wal-arwah|*Badʾ khalq as-samāwāt wal-arwāḥ*]] was to follow at the sultan's command.
