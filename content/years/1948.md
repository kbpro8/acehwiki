**1948** is the year of the second Dutch *politionele actie* (police action) of [[18 December]] [[1948]], in the course of which — the source records — "alle grote plaatsen op Java en Sumatra, behalve in Atjeh, dat men om zijn bijzondere mentaliteit voorlopig ontsag, werden bezet" (all the major places on Java and Sumatra, except in Atjeh, which was for the time being spared on account of its particular mentality, were occupied). The [[1948]] exemption of Atjeh from military occupation is, in the source's narrative, the final Dutch acknowledgment of the Atjehnese particularism that had shaped Dutch-Atjehnese relations for three-quarters of a century.

## The second politionele actie

The second *politionele actie* began at midnight on [[18 December]] [[1948]]. The first day saw paratroopers capture [[Jogjakarta|Jogjakarta]]; [[Soekarno|Soekarno]] and his followers were arrested and taken without difficulty to [[Banka|Banka]]. The action was, the source records, "een groot militair succes" (a great military success). A guerrilla broke out, but "deze schaadde de bevolking meer dan de Nederlandse troepen" (this harmed the population more than the Dutch troops).

## The Atjeh exemption

The Atjeh exemption is the source's most significant Atjeh-related observation for the [[1948]] year. Atjeh — alone of the major regions of [[Sumatra|Sumatra]] — was not occupied in the second *politionele actie*. The reason given is "zijn bijzondere mentaliteit" (its particular mentality). The phrase is the source's only explicit characterisation of Atjehnese particularism in the revolutionary period.

## The 1948 guerrilla

The guerrilla that broke out after the [[1948]] action is the source's only reference to armed resistance in the post-[[1945|1945]] Indonesian revolution. The source's verdict — that the guerrilla "deze schaadde de bevolking meer dan de Nederlandse troepen" (harmed the population more than the Dutch troops) — is the standard Dutch official reading of the revolutionary guerrilla. The Atjeh exemption, however, indicates that the source's confidence in this reading did not extend to a recommendation that Atjeh be occupied.

## Significance

[[1948]] is the year of the second Dutch *politionele actie* and of the Atjeh exemption. The exemption is the final Dutch acknowledgment of Atjehnese particularism in the source's narrative — the institutional recognition that the [[Acheh|Atjeh]] of the [[1945]]-[[1949|49]] revolution was, in Dutch military planning, a special case.
