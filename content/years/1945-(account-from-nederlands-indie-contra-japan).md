**The year 1945** was, in the [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|Dutch staff history's]] account (Deel VI, Hoofdstuk V), the year of the Japanese capitulation of [[15 August]] [[15 August 1945|15 August 1945]] and of Lt [[L. C. G. F. Onvlee|Onvlee]]'s November [[1945]] transfer to [[Bangkok|Bangkok]], where the disposition of the *vaandel* (colours) of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Korps Maréchaussee]] was settled. The year [[1945]] is the staff history's *terminus ad quem* of the Atjeh chapter's narrative of the Dutch wartime presence in Atjeh.

**The year 1945** was, in the [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|Dutch staff history's]] account (Deel VI, Hoofdstuk V), the year of the return of the *vaandel* (colours) of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Korps Maréchaussee]] to Lt [[L. C. G. F. Onvlee|Onvlee]] on [[10 May 1945]], of the Japanese capitulation of [[15 August 1945]], and of Onvlee's November [[1945]] transfer to [[Bangkok|Bangkok]] where the final disposition of the vaandel was settled.
