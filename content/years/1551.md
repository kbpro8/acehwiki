The year in which the grandfather of [[Muhammad al-Addars|Muḥammad al-ʿAddārs]] — and thus the great-grandfather of [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]] — came from South Arabia to Gudjarāt. This migration of an Arab scholar to the Indian coast established the Indian-Arab family into which Raniri was later born: a family of mixed blood that maintained contact with the motherland by correspondence and study-journeys, and that stood in relation with Indonesia — that is, in this period, especially with [[Acheh]].
