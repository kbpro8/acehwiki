**1667** was the year when the **[[French army]]** captured large parts of **[[Flanders]]** and **[[Franche‑Comté]]**, heightening Dutch fears of French expansion and prompting the **[[United Provinces (Dutch Republic)]]** to seek alliances such as the **[[Triple Alliance (1668)]].** The resulting diplomatic tension affected the **[[Dutch East India Company|VOC]]**’s capacity to protect its trade with the **[[Sultanate of Aceh]]**.
