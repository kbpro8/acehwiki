**1665** marks the founding year of the **[[Dutch Marine Corps]]**, the elite infantry and marine force of the **[[Dutch Republic]]**.  Although the corps would not see action in the **[[Dutch–French War]]** until later, its establishment laid the institutional groundwork for later deployments to the **[[East Indies]]**, including the defense of Dutch trading posts in **[[Aceh]]**.

## Connection to Aceh
The **[[Dutch Marine Corps]]** later provided garrison troops for VOC forts along the Aceh coast, protecting the spice trade that was central to the Republic’s economy.
