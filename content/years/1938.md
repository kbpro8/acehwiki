## Places
- [[Atjeh en Onderhorigheden]] — the colonial administrative unit for which the buffalo-density statistics (≈ 2 buffaloes per km²; ≈ 95 per 1000 inhabitants) are given in the *Indisch Verslag [[1939]]*, II, p. 283.
- [[Atjeh's Westkust]] — the hinterland of the Westkust van Atjeh mentioned as one of the regions exporting buffaloes in [[1938]] (alongside the Batak highland), with the export flow mostly by train, on foot, or by truck, and a significant export flow to Singapore and Penang.


## Concepts
- Atjeh's buffalo-density statistics for [[1938]]: ≈ 2 buffaloes/km² (third-highest among the Buitengewesten after Celebes en Onderhorigheden at ±4 and tied with Tapanoeli at ±2); ≈ 95 buffaloes per 1000 inhabitants (third-highest after Timor en Onderhorigheden at ±191 and Celebes en Onderhorigheden at ±128). Source: *Indisch Verslag [[1939]]*, II, p. 283 — the last published before the Japanese occupation.

## Account from *Atjeh* by H.C. Zentgraff (1938)

[[H.C. Zentgraff|Zentgraff]]'s *Atjeh* was published in [[1938]] by Drukkerij "De Unie" at [[Batavia]], dated on the title page *8 APR. 1938* — sixty-five years after [[J.H.R. Köhler|Köhler]] fell under the tree at [[Koetaradja]] on [[8 April]] [[1873]]. The book drew on the military archives, *dank zij de door het Legerbestuur welwillend verleende vergunning*, and organized the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] into twelve episodic chapters: *Inleiding*; *Van schande en vernedering*; *De Vrouwen*; *Het Adelaarsjong*; *Vergeelde brieven*; *Verhalen van de Blang*; *Van vreemde dingen*; *Hoe Tjoet Ali "Sjahid" werd*; *Van den Sjahid*; *Van sterven en begraven*; *De onbekende "Beer"*; and *Het Korps Maréchaussée*. A *tweede bundel* — a second volume — was projected but never appeared.

The publication fell eight years after the fortieth-jubilee *Kandoeri Masoesé* of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Korps Maréchaussée]] of [[2 April]] [[1930]], and two years before the projected fiftieth-jubilee of [[April]] [[1940]]. The book closes on that anticipation: *Als straks, in April 1940, het Korps Maréchaussée eene halve eeuw bestaat, kunnen de oude vechtjassen niet in Atjeh aanwezig zijn. De Dood nam velen bij zich, in Atjeh en elders. In April 1940 zal er eene "Kandoeri Rajeu" zijn die klinkt als een klok.* The jubilee never came; the Japanese invasion of March [[1942]] swept away the colonial world the book documented.

*Atjeh*, door H.C. Zentgraff. Gedrukt bij Drukkerij "De Unie", [[1938]], Batavia.
