## Places
- [[Atjeh en Onderhorigheden]] — the colonial administrative unit for which the buffalo-density statistics (≈ 2 buffaloes per km²; ≈ 95 per 1000 inhabitants) are given in the *Indisch Verslag [[1939]]*, II, p. 283.
- [[Atjeh's Westkust]] — the hinterland of the Westkust van Atjeh mentioned as one of the regions exporting buffaloes in [[1938]] (alongside the Batak highland), with the export flow mostly by train, on foot, or by truck, and a significant export flow to Singapore and Penang.


## Concepts
- Atjeh's buffalo-density statistics for [[1938]]: ≈ 2 buffaloes/km² (third-highest among the Buitengewesten after Celebes en Onderhorigheden at ±4 and tied with Tapanoeli at ±2); ≈ 95 buffaloes per 1000 inhabitants (third-highest after Timor en Onderhorigheden at ±191 and Celebes en Onderhorigheden at ±128). Source: *Indisch Verslag [[1939]]*, II, p. 283 — the last published before the Japanese occupation.
