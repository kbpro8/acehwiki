The year in which [[Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]], at the command of [[Iskandar Thani|Sultan Iskandar Thani]], wrote the [[Sirat al-mustaqim|*Ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm*]] (in March [[1638]]), as well as the [[Bad' khalq as-samawat wal-arwah|*Badʾ khalq as-samāwāt wal-arwāḥ*]]. The composition of these two works in the second year of Iskandar Thani's reign — at the very moment of the [[Raniri's public debate against the Wujudiyyah in Acheh|public disputation]] against the [[Wujudiyyah]] — marks the beginning of Raniri's project of orthodox reform: to provide the Achehnese faithful with the literary apparatus of a Shāfiʿite sultanate.

## Account from *Over Maleise Literatuur* (1947) — 1638

In [[C. Hooykaas|Hooykaas]]'s survey of Malay literature, the year [[1638]] is the year of the composition of the *[[Bustanu's-Salatin|Bustān as-Salāṭīn]]* — the Malay "mirror for princes" written by [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]] at the command of Sultan Iskandar II of [[Acheh|Atjeh]] — and the year of the completion of the *[[Sirat al-mustaqim|Sirāṭ al-mustaqīm]]* (in March [[1638]]) and the *Bad' khalq as-samawat wal-arwah*. The three works together constitute the literary apparatus of ar-Rānīrī's orthodox reform of the Atjehnese court.

### The Bustān as-Salāṭīn

The *[[Bustanu's-Salatin|Bustān as-Salāṭīn]]* — Arabic *Bustān as-Salāṭīn*, Malay *Boestan oes-Salāṭīn*, "Garden of Kings" — was written in [[1638]] by [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|ar-Rānīrī]] "op order van Sultan Iskandar II van Atjeh daar te lande" (on the order of Sultan Iskandar II of Atjeh there in that country). The work is the second of the two principal Atjehnese "mirrors for princes" — the earlier being the *[[Tādj oes-Salatina|Tādj us-Salāṭīn]]* of [[1607]]. Hooykaas contrasts the two: "terwijl BOCHARI's Tādj meer eenvoudig stichtelijk gehouden is, is daarentegen RANĪRI's Boestān meer het werk van een geleerde" (while Bochari's *Tādj* is more simply edifying, Rānīrī's *Bustān* is more the work of a scholar).

The *Bustān*'s historical chapter (II.m) on "de vorsten van Atjéh en de godsdienstleraars daar te lande, voorgangers en tijdgenoten uit 16de en 17de ceuw" (the sultans of Atjeh and the religious scholars there, leaders and contemporaries of the 16th and 17th century) is, in Hooykaas's judgement, occasionally more reliable than the *[[Sadjarah Malajoe]]*: "de geschiedkundige gegevens, steeds van een jaartal voorzien, een betrouwbare indruk maken, wel eens de voorkeur blijken te verdienen boven de voorstelling van de SM" (the historical data, always provided with a year-date, make a reliable impression, sometimes prove to deserve the preference over the representation of the *Sadjarah Malajoe*).

### The Sirāṭ al-mustaqīm (March 1638)

The *[[Sirat al-mustaqim|Sirāṭ al-mustaqīm]]* — begun in [[1634]] — was completed in March [[1638]]. The work is the principal Malay-language *fiqh* handbook produced at the Atjehnese court, and its completion in [[1638]] — at the very moment of ar-Rānīrī's public disputation against the *Wujudiyyah* mystics — marks the beginning of ar-Rānīrī's project of orthodox reform.

### The Project of Orthodox Reform

The composition of the *Sirāṭ*, the *Bustān*, and the *Bad'* in [[1638]] — at the command of Sultan Iskandar II, in the second year of his reign — coincides with ar-Rānīrī's public disputation against the *Wujudiyyah*. The three works together constitute the literary apparatus of ar-Rānīrī's orthodox reform — to provide the Achehnese faithful with the literary apparatus of a Shāfiʿite sultanate.
