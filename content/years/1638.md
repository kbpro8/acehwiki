The year **1638** in [[Acheh|Atjeh]] was the year in which [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]], at the command of [[Iskandar Thani|Sultan Iskandar Thani]], completed the [[Sirat al-Mustaqim|*Ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm*]] (in March [[1638]]) and composed the [[Bustan al-Salatin|*Bustān as-Salāṭīn*]] and the [[Bad' khalq as-samawat wal-arwah|*Badʾ khalq as-samāwāt wal-arwāḥ*]] — the literary apparatus of ar-Rānīrī's orthodox reform of the Atjehnese court — and the year of the [[Martyrdom of Dionysius and Redemptus at Atjeh (1638)|martyrdom of the Carmelites]] [[Pater Dionysius of the Nativity|Pater Dionysius of the Nativity]] (born **Petrus Berthelot**) and [[Brother Redemptus à Cruce|Brother Redemptus à Cruce]] on the sea-shore of [[Acheh|Atjeh]] on [[29 November]] [[1638]], in the course of the Portuguese embassy of [[Francisco de Sosa de Castro|Francisco de Sosa de Castro]] to the [[Sultan of Acheh|sultan of Atjeh]]. The composition of the three orthodox-reform works in the second year of Iskandar Thani's reign — at the very moment of the [[Raniri's public debate against the Wujudiyyah in Acheh|public disputation]] against the [[Wujudiyyah]] — and the execution of the Carmelite embassy together mark [[1638]] as a year in which the Atjehnese court defined both its Islamic orthodoxy and its posture toward Christian-European diplomacy.

## Events

- The completion of the [[Sirat al-Mustaqim|*Sirāṭ al-mustaqīm*]] in March [[1638]] — the principal Malay-language *fiqh* handbook produced at the Atjehnese court, begun in [[1634]].
- The composition of the [[Bustan al-Salatin|*Bustān as-Salāṭīn*]] — the Malay "mirror for princes" written by [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|ar-Rānīrī]] at the command of [[Iskandar Thani|Sultan Iskandar II]].
- The composition of the [[Bad' khalq as-samawat wal-arwah|*Badʾ khalq as-samāwāt wal-arwāḥ*]].
- The [[Raniri's public debate against the Wujudiyyah in Acheh|public disputation]] of [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|ar-Rānīrī]] against the [[Wujudiyyah]] mystics — the orthodox-reform context of the three works.
- The [[Martyrdom of Dionysius and Redemptus at Atjeh (1638)|martyrdom of the Carmelites]] [[Pater Dionysius of the Nativity|Pater Dionysius of the Nativity]] and [[Brother Redemptus à Cruce|Brother Redemptus à Cruce]] — together with the rest of the company of the Portuguese embassy of [[Francisco de Sosa de Castro|Francisco de Sosa de Castro]] to the [[Sultan of Acheh|sultan of Atjeh]], saving only the envoy himself — on the sea-shore of [[Acheh|Atjeh]] on [[29 November]] [[1638]], by *pijlsehoten* and *krissteken* of the Atjehnese executioners. The embassy had put to sea from [[Goa|Goa]] on [[25 September]] [[1638]] on two warships carrying some seventy Portuguese.

## Account from *Over Maleise Literatuur* (1947) — 1638

In [[C. Hooykaas|Hooykaas]]'s survey of Malay literature, the year [[1638]] is the year of the composition of the *[[Bustan al-Salatin|Bustān as-Salāṭīn]]* — the Malay "mirror for princes" written by [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]] at the command of Sultan Iskandar II of [[Acheh|Atjeh]] — and the year of the completion of the *[[Sirat al-Mustaqim|Sirāṭ al-mustaqīm]]* (in March [[1638]]) and the *Bad' khalq as-samawat wal-arwah*. The three works together constitute the literary apparatus of ar-Rānīrī's orthodox reform of the Atjehnese court.

### The Bustān as-Salāṭīn

The *[[Bustan al-Salatin|Bustān as-Salāṭīn]]* — Arabic *Bustān as-Salāṭīn*, Malay *Boestan oes-Salāṭīn*, "Garden of Kings" — was written in [[1638]] by [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|ar-Rānīrī]] "op order van Sultan Iskandar II van Atjeh daar te lande" (on the order of Sultan Iskandar II of Atjeh there in that country). The work is the second of the two principal Atjehnese "mirrors for princes" — the earlier being the *[[Tādj oes-Salatina|Tādj us-Salāṭīn]]* of [[1607]]. Hooykaas contrasts the two: "terwijl BOCHARI's Tādj meer eenvoudig stichtelijk gehouden is, is daarentegen RANĪRI's Boestān meer het werk van een geleerde" (while Bochari's *Tādj* is more simply edifying, Rānīrī's *Bustān* is more the work of a scholar).

The *Bustān*'s historical chapter (II.m) on "de vorsten van Atjéh en de godsdienstleraars daar te lande, voorgangers en tijdgenoten uit 16de en 17de ceuw" (the sultans of Atjeh and the religious scholars there, leaders and contemporaries of the 16th and 17th century) is, in Hooykaas's judgement, occasionally more reliable than the *[[Sadjarah Malajoe]]*: "de geschiedkundige gegevens, steeds van een jaartal voorzien, een betrouwbare indruk maken, wel eens de voorkeur blijken te verdienen boven de voorstelling van de SM" (the historical data, always provided with a year-date, make a reliable impression, sometimes prove to deserve the preference over the representation of the *Sadjarah Malajoe*).

### The Sirāṭ al-mustaqīm (March 1638)

The *[[Sirat al-Mustaqim|Sirāṭ al-mustaqīm]]* — begun in [[1634]] — was completed in March [[1638]]. The work is the principal Malay-language *fiqh* handbook produced at the Atjehnese court, and its completion in [[1638]] — at the very moment of ar-Rānīrī's public disputation against the *Wujudiyyah* mystics — marks the beginning of ar-Rānīrī's project of orthodox reform.

### The Project of Orthodox Reform

The composition of the *Sirāṭ*, the *Bustān*, and the *Bad'* in [[1638]] — at the command of Sultan Iskandar II, in the second year of his reign — coincides with ar-Rānīrī's public disputation against the *Wujudiyyah*. The three works together constitute the literary apparatus of ar-Rānīrī's orthodox reform — to provide the Achehnese faithful with the literary apparatus of a Shāfiʿite sultanate.

## Account from *Vierhonderd jaren missie* (1934) — 1638

The [[Vierhonderd jaren missie in Nederlandsch Indië (1934)|mission jubilee volume of 1934]] records the [[Martyrdom of Dionysius and Redemptus at Atjeh (1638)|martyrdom of the Carmelites]] in the chapter on the Portuguese period of the Indian mission. A *gezant van den onderkoning van Goa* — an envoy of the Viceroy of Portuguese India at [[Goa|Goa]] — was to proceed to [[Acheh|Atjeh]] to offer the homage of Portugal to the newly chosen [[Sultan of Acheh|sultan of Atjeh]]. The envoy designated was [[Francisco de Sosa de Castro|Francisco de Sosa de Castro]], who made his acceptance conditional on the appointment of [[Pater Dionysius of the Nativity|Pater Dionysius]] — born **Petrus Berthelot**, a former navigator — as *scheepshevelhebber* (ship's commander) and confessor; and the lay brother [[Brother Redemptus à Cruce|Redemptus à Cruce]] volunteered to accompany the priest. The embassy put to sea on [[25 September]] [[1638]] on two warships carrying some seventy Portuguese, accompanied by the two Carmelites, two Franciscan fathers, and an indigenous priest.

Upon arrival at [[Acheh|Atjeh]], the embassy was destroyed by distrust — "a slanderous report, as would this homage conceal only a cunning design directed against the independence of Atjeh and the power of the crescent" — and the [[Sultan of Acheh|sultan]] ordered the execution of the company, saving only the envoy himself. The martyrdom on [[29 November]] [[1638]] is the principal seventeenth-century European clerical martyrdom on Atjehnese soil documented in the Catholic mission record; the two Blesseds were beatified by Pope [[Leo XIII|Leo XIII]] on [[25 March]] [[1900]], and their feast is kept on [[29 November]].
