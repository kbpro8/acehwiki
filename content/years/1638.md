The year in which [[Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]], at the command of [[Iskandar Thani|Sultan Iskandar Thani]], wrote the [[Sirat al-mustaqim|*Ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm*]] (in March [[1638]]), as well as the [[Bad' khalq as-samawat wal-arwah|*Badʾ khalq as-samāwāt wal-arwāḥ*]]. The composition of these two works in the second year of Iskandar Thani's reign — at the very moment of the [[Raniri's public debate against the Wujudiyyah in Acheh|public disputation]] against the [[Wujudiyyah]] — marks the beginning of Raniri's project of orthodox reform: to provide the Achehnese faithful with the literary apparatus of a Shāfiʿite sultanate.
