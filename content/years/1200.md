**1200** was a year of significance in the history of [[Aceh]], as recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Around 1200: Islam began to win adherents in the archipelago, first in Atjeh.

## Events

Around 1200: [[Islam]] began to win adherents in [[East Indian Archipelago|the archipelago]], first in Atjeh. First Mohammedan prince mentioned at [[Samoedra-Pasè]].

## Significance

The year 1200 is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the chronological framework of [[Aceh]]'s history.
