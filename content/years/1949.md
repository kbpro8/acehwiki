**[[1949]]** was the year of the [[Souvereiniteitsoverdracht (27 December 1949)|transfer of sovereignty]] of [[27 December]] [[1949]] — the formal end of the Dutch colonial state in the Indonesian archipelago and the dissolution of the [[K.N.I.L.]] — and the year that closes the colonial arc of *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]*. The transfer of sovereignty handed the federal Indonesia the Dutch had constructed over to the unitary Republic, and the Ambonese soldiers who had served the Dutch were abandoned.

## The Tweede Politionele Actie and its aftermath

The [[Tweede Politionele Actie|Tweede Politionele Actie]] of [[December 1948]] had been the last Dutch military offensive of the Indonesian revolution. Its aftermath — through [[1949]] — was the steady realisation that the military offensive could not be translated into a political victory. The *Renville Agreement]] and the *Van Mooklijn]] had failed; the *Ronde Tafel Conference|Round Table Conference]] at the Hague was the negotiation that would produce the transfer of sovereignty.

## The Souvereiniteitsoverdracht (27 December 1949)

The [[Souvereiniteitsoverdracht (27 December 1949)|Souvereiniteitsoverdracht]] — the transfer of sovereignty — took place on [[27 December]] [[1949]]. The federal Indonesia the Dutch had constructed — the *[[Negara Pasundan]]*, the *Negara Indonesia Timur* and the rest — was handed over to the unitary Republic. The [[K.N.I.L.]] was dissolved; its Ambonese soldiers were transferred into the *Federale Legers* and *Negaratroepen* of the federal Indonesia, and then, with the federal Indonesia's dissolution into the unitary Republic, into the army of the Republic that had been their enemy.

## The abandonment of Oom Ambon

The closing arc of *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* is the steady disbandment of the [[K.N.I.L.]] and the abandonment of its Ambonese soldiers. The book's quiet point is that the Ambonese soldiers who had served the colonial army since the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]] were, in [[1949]], used in a war whose political outcome would leave them stateless. The [[Souvereiniteitsoverdracht (27 December 1949)|transfer of sovereignty]] of [[27 December]] [[1949]] handed the federal Indonesia the Dutch had built over to the unitary Republic, and the Ambonese soldiers who had served the Dutch were left without the institution that had made them.

The chapter *Tamaela* — the last narrative chapter of the book — is the figure through whom this argument is made: the Ambonese korporaal whose *[[tangsi]]* culture was dissolved in the new federal formations, and who was, in [[1949]], left without the institution that had made him.

## The Republic of the South Moluccas

The book's closing chapters also register the *Republic of the South Moluccas|South Moluccas self-determination]] — the unilateral declaration of independence of the *Republik Maluku Selatan* by the Ambonese soldiers who refused to be handed over to the unitary Republic. The declaration was the political consequence of the abandonment: the Ambonese soldiers who had served the Dutch and were now to be handed over to their enemy refused, and declared their own state.

## Significance

[[1949]] is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as the year of the end of the colonial state and the dissolution of the [[K.N.I.L.]]. The book's argument is that the Ambonese soldier of the colonial army was, in [[1949]], used in a war whose political outcome would leave him stateless, and that the *[[tangsi]]* culture that had made him was, with the disbandment of the [[K.N.I.L.]], dissolved. The year [[1949]] is therefore the book's political closure: the end of the colonial army, and the abandonment of the soldiers who had served it.
