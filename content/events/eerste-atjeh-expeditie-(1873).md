**The Eerste Atjeh-expeditie (1873)** — the first Dutch military expedition against the Sultanate of [[Atjeh|Aceh]] — was the opening action of the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]], conducted against the [[Missigit (Meusigit)|Missigit]] (the great mosque) of [[Koeta-Radja]] in [[April 1873|April]] [[1873]] and ending in failure. The expedition would be followed by the [[Tweede Atjeh-expeditie (1873-1874)|Tweede Atjeh-expeditie]] of [[1873]]-[[1874]] — which took the Missigit on [[6 January]] [[1874]] — and by the forty-five-year war that would end with the [[Handover of Aceh to a civil Gouverneur (1918)|handover of 1918]].

## The expedition

The [[Eerste Atjeh-expeditie (1873)|Eerste Atjeh-expeditie]] of [[April 1873|April]] [[1873]] was the first Dutch military expedition against the Sultanate of [[Atjeh|Aceh]]. The expedition, conducted against the [[Missigit (Meusigit)|Missigit]] of [[Koeta-Radja]], failed. The book notes that "[[April 1873|April]] [[1873]]" was the date of the first attack, and that the Missigit was not taken until [[6 January]] [[1874]].

## The Tweede Atjeh-expeditie (1873-1874)

The [[Tweede Atjeh-expeditie (1873-1874)|Tweede Atjeh-expeditie]] — which began in [[1873]] and concluded with the taking of the Missigit on [[6 January]] [[1874]] — opened the forty-five-year war. The book notes that "de hevige gevechten van de eerste maanden van [[1876]]" followed, and that the war would continue, in one form or another, until the [[Handover of Aceh to a civil Gouverneur (1918)|handover of 1918]].

## The forty-five-year war

The [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]] that opened with the [[Eerste Atjeh-expeditie (1873)|Eerste Atjeh-expeditie]] of [[April 1873|April]] [[1873]] would last forty-five years — from [[1873]] to the [[Handover of Aceh to a civil Gouverneur (1918)|handover of 1918]] — and would be the principal theatre of the [[K.N.I.L.]] for the next half-century. The war would be pronounced over several times before it actually was; it would be the subject of political controversy in the Netherlands; it would make the reputation of [[Gouverneur-Generaal Van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]].

## Significance

The [[Eerste Atjeh-expeditie (1873)|Eerste Atjeh-expeditie]] is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as the opening action of the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]]. The book's argument is that the war would last forty-five years — from [[1873]] to [[1918]] — and that it would be the principal theatre of the [[K.N.I.L.]] for the next half-century. The [[Eerste Atjeh-expeditie (1873)|Eerste Atjeh-expeditie]] of [[April 1873|April]] [[1873]] is therefore the book's opening of the colonial arc that the rest of the book narrates.
