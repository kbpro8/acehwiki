**The declaration of war against the Sultan of Atjeh (26 March 1873)** was the act by which the [[Netherlands]] opened the [[Atjehoorlog|Atjehoorlog]] of [[1873]]-[[1904]]. The declaration followed the unsatisfactory response of the [[Sultan of Acheh|Sultan of Atjeh]] to the Dutch demand for clarification of the Atjehnese chiefs' visits to the Italian and American consuls in [[Singapore]].

## The Singapore consul-visits

The immediate Dutch grievance was the discovery, in [[1872]], that the Atjehnese chiefs had used a diplomatic postponement to visit, in [[Singapore]], the consuls of [[Italy]] and the [[United States]]. With the American consul, "was zelfs een voorlopige overeenkomst gesloten" (a preliminary agreement was even concluded). The Dutch demand for clarification produced no satisfactory response.

## The 26 March declaration

The source records: "Onmiddellijk werd aan de Sultan van Atjeh opheldering gevraagd en toen geen bevredigend antwoord ontvangen werd volgde op 26 Maart 1873 de oorlogsverklaring" (Immediately clarification was sought from the Sultan of Atjeh, and when no satisfactory answer was received, the declaration of war followed on 26 March 1873). The [[26 March]] [[1873]] declaration is the formal opening of the [[Atjehoorlog|Atjehoorlog]].

## The first expedition

"Tien dagen later landde generaal Köhler met 3600 man" (Ten days later general Köhler landed with 3,600 men). The first military expedition, under [[General Köhler|generaal Köhler]], landed in early April [[1873]]. Köhler was killed in the assault on the "versterkte vorstenverblijf" (fortified royal residence) — the kraton of the [[Sultan of Acheh|Sultan of Atjeh]]. The first expedition, feeling itself too weak to continue, returned.

## The second expedition

A second expedition, 8,000 strong under [[General Van Swieten|generaal Van Swieten]], landed in December [[1873]]. After a bombardment of the royal residence, it was surrounded; the residence proved to have been abandoned. By proclamation of the commander, [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] was declared government territory; the fortified kraton was renamed "[[Koeta Radja|Kota-Radja]]".

## Significance

The [[26 March]] [[1873]] declaration of war is the formal opening of the [[Atjehoorlog|Atjehoorlog]]. The declaration, the first expedition's defeat, and the second expedition's apparent success together set the pattern for the next thirty-one years: formal Dutch declarations of sovereignty alternating with persistent Atjehnese resistance.
