**The Batavian Society treasure room Aceh collection** was a major assemblage of Acehnese gold, silver, and precious-metal objects housed in the [[Koninklijk Bataviaasch Genootschap van Kunsten en Wetenschappen|Batavian Society's]] treasure room (*schatkamer*) in [[Koeta Radja|Batavia]]. The collection was displayed in three dedicated cabinets (Kast 3, 4, and 5), all labelled "Atjeh," and was comprehensively catalogued in the Society's 1928 visitor's guide, *Gids voor den bezoeker van de schatkamer*.

## Scope and organisation

The three cabinets together held over one hundred individually numbered objects, spanning the full range of Acehnese personal adornment, weaponry, and ceremonial metalwork. The objects can be broadly categorised as follows:

### Weapons and martial equipment
- Swords (*zwaard*), sabres (*sabel*), lances (*lans*), and daggers (*dolk*) with gold and silver fittings
- [[Kris|Kris]] of various types, including *kris pandjang* (long kris) and flamed-blade kris
- Two revolvers decorated with [[Spinsbek|spinsbek]] work
- A silver-and-gold belt chain, possibly a sword-belt

### Personal ornaments
- Rings (*ring*), finger rings (*vingerringen*), and ankle rings (*enkelring*, *enkelband*) in gold, silver, and [[Spinsbek|spinsbek]]
- Bracelets (*armband*, *armbandje*) in various styles
- Chest ornaments (*borstsieraad*), necklaces (*borstketen*, *keten*), and ear buttons (*oorknop*)
- Brooches (*broche*), buttons (*knoop*, *knoopje*), and slide buttons (*schuifknoop*)
- Hair pins (*haarnaald*) and pins in various forms
- Children's jewellery, including bracelets, neck ornaments, and a rattle chain (*rinkelsnoer*)

### Ceremonial and authority objects
- The [[Sultanszegel van Atjeh|nine-fold sultan's seal]] (*negenvoudige zegel*)
- The personal seal of [[Toekoe Moeda Ia Malim]]
- A gold chain bearing the Dutch coat of arms, formerly belonging to [[Teukoe Moeda Baid Setia Maharadja]]
- Gold kopiah crown ornaments (*koepiah kruin-versiersel*)

### Amulets and magical objects
- Amulets (*amulet*) on chains, in boxes (*amulet-doosje*), and as pendants
- [[Wonderkogel|Wonderkogels]] — gold bullets with human faces and diamond eyes
- A seal for stamping amulets

### Betel and domestic equipment
- Betel-lime boxes (*kalkdoosje*) in silver, gold, and [[Spinsbek|spinsbek]]
- Boxes (*doos*, *doosje*) of chased silver and gold for various purposes
- A pair of gold spectacles with extendable parts
- Candlesticks (*kandelaar*), bowls (*kom*, *napje*), and other domestic items

### Textiles
- A gold-embroidered mosquito-net strip (*klamboe-strook met goud geborduurd*)
- A woven pandan-leaf sitting mat from [[Seunagan]]

## Provenance

The objects entered the collection through various channels. The largest single group — the [[Radja of Simpang Olim|jewellery of the radja of Simpang Olim]] (Cabinet 3, items 2–42) — was recovered from an abandoned house in [[1877]]. Other items were gifts from colonial officials, confiscated from disloyal rulers (such as Baid Setia Maharadja's chain), or acquired through purchase or donation during the decades of the [[Aceh War]].

## Significance

The collection is one of the most comprehensive surviving assemblages of nineteenth-century Acehnese precious-metal work, and the 1928 catalogue provides an invaluable record of the types, styles, and materials of Acehnese personal adornment and martial equipment during the late pre-colonial and early colonial period. The dominance of [[Spinsbek|spinsbek]] work (a technique of fine, granulated or beaded metalwork) throughout the collection is particularly noteworthy, as is the presence of both Islamic-influenced and indigenous Acehnese decorative motifs.
