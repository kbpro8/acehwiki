**Teukoe Panglima Polem's submission** (May 1904) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Teukoe Panglima Polem, the Sagi chief, after being a fanatical opponent who led his followers in fierce resistance, submitted to then-captain Colijn in May 1904. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to May [[1904]].

## Account

[[Teukoe Panglima Polem]], the [[Sagi]] chief, after being a fanatical opponent who led his followers in fierce [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]], submitted to then-captain [[Hendrikus Colijn|Colijn]] in May [[1904]]. Since then he refrained from any action against the Dutch and used his far-reaching influence for good. (At time of writing he had held the dignity for nearly 35 years.)

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
