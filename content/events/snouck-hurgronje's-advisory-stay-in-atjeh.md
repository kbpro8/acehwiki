**Snouck Hurgronje's advisory stay in Atjeh** (1892-1893) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Christiaan Snouck Hurgronje stayed in Atjeh in 1892-1893 to advise on the political situation. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1892]]-[[1893]].

## Account

[[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Christiaan Snouck Hurgronje]] stayed in Atjeh in [[1892]]-[[1893]] to advise on the political situation. His advice in short: never negotiate with the enemy, only strike them, never give them the chance to recover. After that, there would be every opportunity for measures to show [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Gouvernement|the Gouvernement]]'s peaceful intentions. Logical and simple advice, but a sensitive shock was needed to demonstrate it - and that shock was delivered by [[Teukoe Oemar]].

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
