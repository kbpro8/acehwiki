**The Conquest of Blang Tjoet (18 January 1875)** was the operation by which [[Kolonel Ruempol|overste Ruempol]], at the head of a column of 150 bayonets and an artillery section with a reserve infantry company, permanently drove the Atjehneezen from the kampong of [[Blang Tjoet]] on the left bank of the [[Atjeh-rivier]] between [[Pendetti]] and [[Longbatta-Mesigit]], and built there a post — using the abandoned Atjehnese *benteng* — that made transport between Mesigit and Pendetti "practically safe" by day.

## Background

The [[Battle of Longbatta (31 December 1874)|battle of Longbatta]] of [[31 December]] [[1874]] had ended in the disastrous retreat of [[Majoor van der Meer|van der Meer]]'s column, but the fortified [[Longbatta-Mesigit|Mesigit]] had remained in Dutch hands, garrisoned by two companies. On [[4 January]] [[1875]] Pel, with two columns, had reconnoitred and cleared the terrain south of Pendetti and the kampongs [[Lampoe Idjoe]], [[Lampoe Oek]], Longbatta and Blang Tjoet, without resistance; the houses were burned. On [[7 January]] [[1875]] a convoy had been heavily attacked near Blang Tjoet, and [[Majoor Romswinckel|Romswinckel]] had been sent to its aid; sharp fighting followed, with several Dutch dead and wounded, and the Atjehneezen could not be dislodged from positions in the dense brush.

## The conquest

On [[18 January]] [[1875]] Ruempol marched out. The enemy was permanently driven from Blang Tjoet. At a suitable point near the river bank, about four to five hundred paces from Longbatta-Mesigit, work immediately began on a fortification, using the abandoned Atjehnese *benteng* as its core. After a few days of clearing, the Atjehneezen could be prevented from crossing the river by day. Transport between Mesigit and Pendetti was now "practically safe" — a small but, in Borel's view, important gain, since the route formed the left-bank spine of the future [[Stelling-Pel|stelling]].

## Borel's verdict

Borel praises the economy of the operation — a small column, a careful choice of ground, the reuse of an enemy work — but notes that Blang Tjoet, like so much else in Atjeh, had to be conquered twice: once briefly in the wake of Longbatta, and once definitively on [[18 January]] [[1875]]. The new post formed, with *Poengej Blang Tjoet* (established [[23 January]] [[1875]]), the western anchor of the ring completed in [[Completion of the defensive ring around Kota Radja (May 1875)|May 1875]].
