**Capture of the Baiturrahman Grand Mosque (1874)** was the Dutch seizure of the [[Baiturrahman Grand Mosque|Missigit]] during the [[Second Dutch Expedition to Aceh]], an operation presented by [[Jan van Swieten]] as a major step toward the [[Kraton of Aceh]].

## Course

The episode followed the death of a Dutch parliamentarian and siege works. Its capture had immediate military consequences, but the source’s later political argument made clear that possession of a symbolic central building did not itself secure Acehnese consent or end resistance.

## Significance

The episode belongs to the continuing [[Aceh War]] and the contested consequences of the [[Capture of the Kraton of Aceh]].
