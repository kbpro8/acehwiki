**Van Swieten's proclamation** (31 March 1874) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Lieutenant-General J. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[31 March]] [[1874]].

## Account

[[Lieutenant-General J. van Swieten]] issued a proclamation on [[31 March]] [[1874]] establishing Dutch authority in the conquered territory. Following this, [[Teukoe Bintara Keumangan]] set as condition for his written submission that the rule of his land must remain in his and his descendants' hands - an early example of how chiefs tried to preserve their authority.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
