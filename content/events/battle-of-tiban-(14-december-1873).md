**The Battle of Tiban (14 December 1873)** was the Dutch success on [[14 December]] [[1873]] during the opening land phase of the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)]]. After the landing east of the [[Aceh River|Aceh River]], Dutch troops under [[General Verspijck|Verspijck]] stormed the Acehnese line at [[Tiban]] and opened the way toward [[Penajoeng]] and the inner defenses of [[Aceh|Atjeh]].

The battle later became important in the pamphlet debate around the expedition. [[Brutus]] argued that the victory at Tiban should have been followed at once by stronger reconnaissance and a faster advance. In his view, the pause after Tiban was the first lost chance of the campaign.

## The attack

By [[14 December]] [[1873]], the Dutch landing force had already secured its beachhead and occupied [[Kota Moesapi]]. The next obstacle was the long Acehnese defensive line south of that position. On [[14 December]] [[1873]] the Dutch attacked at [[Tiban]]. The line was taken, and the Acehnese defenders were pushed back.

In Dutch accounts of the expedition, this was one of the first clear battlefield successes of the renewed invasion. It showed that the second expedition was much stronger and better prepared than the failed expedition of earlier in the same year.

## The pause that followed

Brutus later gave the battle a second meaning. He argued that the Dutch should have spent the same afternoon and the next day in close reconnaissance, to see how far the enemy had truly withdrawn and to prepare the march toward [[Penajoeng]]. Instead, he said, the advance did not move forward quickly enough. Every day of delay gave the Acehnese more time to strengthen the next line.

## Significance

**The Battle of Tiban** mattered for two reasons. First, it was an important early success of the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)]]. Second, it became the starting point for later criticism that the Dutch command did not exploit victory fast enough. That criticism shaped the later judgement that the expedition had taken the [[Kraton of Aceh|Kraton]] but had not finished the war.
