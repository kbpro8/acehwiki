**The Conquest of Mandarsa Poeti (28 January 1875)** was the three-column operation by which [[Generaal Pel|kolonel Pel]] finally occupied the old stone *gebedenhuis* (prayer-house) of [[Mandarsa Poeti]] — already a ruin since being destroyed by 12 c.M. fire on [[5 October]] [[1874]] — as a Dutch post, an action whose sudden initial success was nearly undone when the Atjehneezen streamed back in from the [[IX Moekims]] in the course of the afternoon.

## The plan

Three columns marched simultaneously from [[Kota Radja|K. Radja]]. The middle, or main, column under [[Majoor van der Meer|van der Meer]] marched straight on the stone *gebedenhuis* and took it without resistance. The other columns moved left on Nesoch and right on [[Setee]] to cover the flanks. Since the walls of the *gebedenhuis* had already been shot to rubble by [[Achterlaadkanonnen van 12 c.M.|12 c.M. achterlaadgeschut]] the previous October, and the interior was too small for a garrison, a suitable nearby point was chosen and construction of a new fortification begun.

## The afternoon counter-attack

The conquest was so sudden that the Atjehneezen had no time to resist. Later in the day, however, when it became clear that the Dutch meant to stay, the Atjehneezen streamed in from all sides, especially from the [[IX Moekims]]. They occupied the south-west *kampong-rand* toward Lamlagan and opened fire. The day's cost was three dead and ten wounded; one man was killed in the western flank column under kapitein Sutherland.

## Borel's verdict

For Borel the operation illustrates both the strength and the limitation of Pel's method. A well-planned multi-column advance could carry the object in minutes; but, unless the enemy was pursued and broken, he would return within hours, from the surrounding *Moekims*, in numbers that the small Dutch garrison could barely hold. The post at Mandarsa Poeti would be the base from which [[Kapitein von Ende|kapitein von Ende]], on the night of [[14 February|14]]–[[15 February]] [[1875]], launched his unauthorized but brilliant [[Surprise of Lamara Olejlo (14–15 February 1875)|surprise of Lamara Olejlo]]. Mandarsa Poeti also became a southern anchor of the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]] completed in [[Completion of the defensive ring around Kota Radja (May 1875)|May 1875]].
