**The Trip to XXII Moekims** — *tocht naar de XXII Moekims van 23 tot en met 27 Augustus* [[1896]] — was a five-day Dutch reconnaissance of the [[XXII Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]], undertaken by a colonne under [[G. F. Soeters|Luitenant-Kolonel G. F. Soeters]] between [[23 August]] [[1896|23]] and [[27 August]] [[1896]]. The trip formed part of the broader Dutch strategy of the second semester of [[1896]] — the slow extension of effective Dutch knowledge of, and influence over, the [[XXII Moekims]], the *sagi* (territorial confederation (sagi)) of [[Panglima Polim]], in preparation for the operations of October–November [[1896]] which would include the [[Temporary Occupation of Indrapoeri (28 October 1896)|temporary occupation of Indrapoeri]] and the [[Capture of Kota Ba Teuē (28 October 1896)|capture of Kota Ba Teuē]].

## The Strategic Context

By late August [[1896]], the Dutch, under [[J. W. Stemfoort|Stemfoort]]'s second tenure, had extended their effective control from the *geconcentreerde linie* (concentrated line) into the [[XXVI Moekims]] and the [[IV Moekims|IV Moekims (Kroeng-Raba)]]. The [[XXII Moekims]] — the *sagi* (territorial confederation (sagi)) of [[Panglima Polim]], one of the principal Acehnese commanders of the war — remained, however, only partially under Dutch control. The trip to the XXII Moekims of [[23 August|23]]–[[27 August]] [[1896]] was thus both a reconnaissance and a *machtsvertoon* (show of force) — designed to ascertain the disposition of the Acehnese forces in the XXII Moekims and to demonstrate the Dutch capacity for force projection into the *sagi* (territorial confederation (sagi)) of Panglima Polim. The trip was undertaken in the same period as the [[Occupation of Samahani (late August 1896)|occupation of Samahani]] in late August [[1896]] — the occupation of a forward position in the [[VII Moekims Baid]] which would serve as a base for further operations in the XXII Moekims.

## The Colonne under Soeters

The colonne for the trip to the XXII Moekims was commanded by [[G. F. Soeters|Luitenant-Kolonel Soeters]] — the same officer who had commanded the 2e colonne at the [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|surprise of Ana' Galoeng]] of [[29 June]] [[1896]] and who had conducted the [[Reconnaissance of the XXVI Moekims (24 July 1896)|reconnaissance of the XXVI Moekims]] of [[24 July]] [[1896]]. The composition of the colonne is not separately narrated by Kruisheer in the detail given for the surprise of Ana' Galoeng, but the operation is recorded in the *Inhoud* (Table of Contents) of *ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel* as one of the principal operations of August [[1896]]. The trip traversed the XXII Moekims over five days, from [[23 August]] to [[27 August]] [[1896]], and assessed the political disposition of the *sagi* — the disposition of the *hoeloebalang* (territorial chief (uleebalang)) class and the extent of Panglima Polim's following.

## Significance

The Trip to XXII Moekims of [[23 August|23]]–[[27 August]] [[1896]] is significant in the documentary record of *ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel* as the most extensive Dutch engagement with the political base of [[Panglima Polim]] in the second semester of [[1896]]. The trip — undertaken in the same period as the [[Occupation of Samahani (late August 1896)|occupation of Samahani]] — formed part of the broader Dutch strategy of the second semester: the slow extension of effective Dutch knowledge of the XXII Moekims in preparation for the operations of October–November [[1896]]. The intelligence gathered on the trip would inform the Dutch planning for the [[Temporary Occupation of Indrapoeri (28 October 1896)|temporary occupation of Indrapoeri]] and the [[Capture of Kota Ba Teuē (28 October 1896)|capture of Kota Ba Teuē]] — operations which would establish the third Dutch forward position beyond the *geconcentreerde linie* (concentrated line) and which would open the way for the [[Second Selimoen Expedition (3–6 November 1896)|second Selimoen expedition]] of [[3 November|3]]–[[6 November]] [[1896]].
