The **excursie in het Samalangasche** was the expedition into the territory of [[Samalanga]] in which the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* took part, and during which the stronghold of *[[Batee Ilie|Batee Iliek]]* — notorious since the time of General [[K. van der Heijden|Van der Heyden]] — was taken by storm.

## The expedition

The jubilee number of [[1930]] records that the corps was not lacking at the excursion into the Samalangasche, "waarbij o.a. het uit den tijd van generaal Van der Heyden beruchte Batoe-Iliek stormenderhand werd genomen" — at which, among other things, the Batee Iliek made notorious in the time of General Van der Heyden was taken by storm.

The position had acquired its reputation during the governorship of [[K. van der Heijden|General K. van der Heijden]], under whose administration the Dutch action had shortly before extended over the valley of [[Groot-Atjeh]], prior to the *[[Concentratiestelsel|concentratie]]* decision of [[19 August]] [[1884]].

## Aftermath: the pursuit inland

The jubilee account places the storming of Batee Iliek within the corps's larger pattern of operations in these years. The Maréchaussée, it records, "dreef de verzetslieden steeds meer het binnenland in, waar zij zich schuil wisten te houden, doch ook daar werden opgespoord" — drove the resistance fighters ever further into the interior, where they contrived to keep themselves hidden, but where they were tracked down as well. Most of the corps's dead, the account adds, fell during the patrols.
