**Sigli ambachtsleergang opened** (1935) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. In 1935, a vocational training (ambachtsleergang) was opened at Sigli, initially teaching only metaalbewerking (metalworking). 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1935]].

## Account

In [[1935]], a vocational training ([[Ambachtsleergang|ambachtsleergang]]) was opened at [[Sigli]], initially teaching only metaalbewerking (metalworking). Graduates find ready work at the 'winkel' (workshop) of the [[Atjeh-tram]] as fully qualified workers.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
