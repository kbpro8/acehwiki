**Crossing of Blankala Pass** was a decisive mountain maneuver executed in late December [[1875]] and early [[1876]] during [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]], in which a mobile column of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] under [[Lieutenant Colonel Meijer]] traversed the rugged Blankala Pass to invade the [[IV Moekims]].

## Narrative of the Mountain March

Following the successful [[Attack on Mibouw]] on [[26 December]] [[1875]], General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]] ordered an advance across the coastal mountain barrier separating Kotta Radja from the interior valley of the [[IV Moekims]]. 

Column commander [[Lieutenant Colonel Meijer]] assembled a strike force comprising the [[3rd Infantry Battalion]], one company of the [[8th Infantry Battalion]], the [[Marine Corps in Aceh]], one barissan company, and two artillery sections, accompanied by General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]] and his staff. 

Navigating steep, muddy jungle defiles under constant harassment from Acehnese snipers, Dutch infantry and forced laborers ([[Forced Labor in Aceh Military Campaigns]]) hauled mountain guns across the pass, descending triumphantly into the fertile valley of the IV Moekims.

## Strategic Consequences

The successful [[Crossing of Blankala Pass]] severed communications between Acehnese coastal resistance forces and interior bases, establishing permanent Dutch garrisons in the [[IV Moekims]] and preparing the staging ground for the subsequent [[March through the XXII Moekims]] in February [[1876]].
