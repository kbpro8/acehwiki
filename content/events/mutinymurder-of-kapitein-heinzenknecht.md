**Mutiny/murder of Kapitein Heinzenknecht** (during Gajoweg construction (after 1913)) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Kapitein Heinzenknecht, 'the gemütliche Beier' (good-natured Bavarian), one of the capable workers on the great Gajoweg construction, was murdered by coolies. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to during [[Gajoweg (Gayo Road)|Gajoweg]] construction (after [[1913]]).

## Account

[[Kapitein Heinzenknecht]], 'the gemütliche Beier' (good-natured Bavarian), one of the capable workers on the great [[Gajoweg (Gayo Road)|Gajoweg]] construction, was murdered by [[Coolies|coolies]]. His loyal friends erected a bust by [[Toon Dupuis]] at [[Bireuën]] at the start of the [[Gajoweg (Gayo Road)|Gajoweg]], his face turned toward the road.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
