The **dempen van den opstand in de Gajo- en Alaslanden** in [[1905]] was the suppression of the rising that broke out in the [[Gajolanden|Gajo]] and [[Alaslanden|Alas]] highlands after the [[Tocht naar de Gajo-Loeös (1904)|famous march]] of Lieutenant-Colonel [[G. C. E. van Daalen]] through those lands, and the action for which the young officer [[W. V. Rhemrev]] received the *[[Militaire Willemsorde|Militaire Willems-Orde]]* 4th class.

## The rising and its suppression

The [[1930]] Maréchaussée jubilee number records that Rhemrev "dempte in 1905 den opstand, die uitbrak na den beroemden tocht door de Gajo- en Alas-landen van Overste van Daalen" — quelled in 1905 the revolt which broke out after the celebrated march through the Gajo and Alas lands of Lieutenant-Colonel Van Daalen — and was decorated for it with the Militaire Willems-Orde 4th class.

Van Daalen's expedition had run from February to July [[1904]], with an initial ten and eventually fourteen *[[Brigade (Maréchaussée)|brigades]]* of Maréchaussée, the division commanders being at the same time charged with the civil administration.

## Rhemrev's wound and continued service

In the course of the suppression Rhemrev was wounded in a manner described in the jubilee account as *levensgevaarlijk* — mortally dangerous. He was in consequence declared unfit for active service. He nevertheless continued to serve, at the express request of Governor-General [[J. B. van Heutsz]] and of [[H. Colijn|Colijn]].

The year before, in [[1904]], Rhemrev had received an honourable mention for his military operations on the [[Batak Karo|Karo plateau]]. The register of honours in the same issue records that in [[1906]] he was, as second lieutenant of Maréchaussée, made a knight of the Militaire Willems-Orde 4th class.
