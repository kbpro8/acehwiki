**Koninklijk Besluit of 26 July 1872 No. 63** (Royal Decree of 26 July 1872, No. 63) was the Dutch royal decree by which the cadet-sergeant [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Cornelis Jacobus Snijders]] — then still two months short of his twentieth birthday — was appointed *Tweede Luitenant* (Second Lieutenant) in the [[Korps Ingenieurs, Mineurs en Sappeurs|Korps Ingenieurs, Mineurs en Sappeurs]] and posted to the *Eerste Stelling der Genie* at [[Utrecht|Utrecht]]. The decree is the legal basis of Snijders' commissioning and of his subsequent engineer career, which included his Atjeh service of [[1874]]–[[1875]] under [[Koninklijk Besluit of 10 June 1873 No. 33|KB of 10 June 1873 No. 33]] and his decoration with the [[Militaire Willemsorde|Ridderkruis der Militaire Willemsorde]] under [[Koninklijk Besluit of 12 November 1875 No. 18|KB of 12 November 1875 No. 18]].

## The Commissioning

By the decree of [[26 July]] [[26 July]] [[1872]] No. 63, the cadet-sergeant Snijders, who had passed the officers' examination as No. 1 of his four-man *rotatie* of engineers on [[14 July]] [[14 July]] [[1872]] and had been awarded two honorary prizes at the [[Koninklijke Militaire Academie|KMA]] (a portrait of King Willem III for excellence in linear drawing, and a revolver for excellence in practical aptitude), was appointed Tweede Luitenant in the Korps Ingenieurs, Mineurs en Sappeurs and posted to the Eerste Stelling der Genie at [[Utrecht|Utrecht]]. He reported for duty on [[10 August]] [[10 August]] [[1872]] to the Kolonel-Ingenieur Jhr. J. F. van Thije Hannes, who administered the oath of office in his lodgings before a clerk on crutches as sole witness.
