title: Surprise of Aneu' Galong
aliases: [Battle of Aneuk Galong]
tags: [event]
---

A Dutch military operation in [[1896]] in which Teungku Mat Amin, son of the resistance leader Cheh Saman (Teungku Tiro), was killed along with about a hundred of his followers, an event that further fragmented the organized resistance movement his father had built.
