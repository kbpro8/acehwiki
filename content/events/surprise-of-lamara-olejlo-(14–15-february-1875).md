**The Surprise of Lamara Olejlo (14–15 February 1875)** was the unauthorized night attack by which [[Kapitein von Ende|kapitein von Ende]], commandant of the new post at [[Mandarsa Poeti]], slipped across the *sawah* with part of his garrison on the night of [[14 February|14]]–[[15 February]] [[1875]], occupied the abandoned Atjehnese *benteng* at [[Lamara Olejlo]] before dawn, and held it against the enemy's morning return — receiving from [[Generaal Pel|Pel]] both formal praise and a reprimand, for, as Borel puts it, "he had fully deserved both; the achieved success made everything good."

## Von Ende's reconnaissance

Von Ende had noticed that the Atjehneezen left the fortification at the northern *rand* of [[Lamara]] each evening and returned in the morning. He verified this personally, creeping across the *sawah* on a dark night with a few men. Without asking permission, he resolved to seize the empty works.

## The night march

On the night of [[14 February|14]]–[[15 February]] [[1875]] von Ende crossed the *sawah* with part of the Mandarsa Poeti garrison and occupied the abandoned fortification. He at once reported to Pel and asked for reinforcements. Between eight and four in the morning the Atjehneezen arrived, as usual — and to considerable surprise met with [[Beaumont-geweer|Beaumont]] salvoes. Von Ende beat off their attempt to retake the works and held with a handful until reinforcements under overste van Dentekom arrived, then [[Majoor Romswinckel|Romswinckel]]. Fighting continued all morning as the Atjehneezen streamed in from all sides. The whole Lamara Olejlo stelling remained permanently in Dutch hands.

## The booty

Three or four enemy *benteng*s, several cannons and *lilla*s fell into Dutch hands, including a 4-pounder that had fired on Mandarsa Poeti — it had no carriage but was ingeniously suspended on ropes, so that it could be elevated and trained by hand. The device is one of Borel's recurring exhibits of Atjehnese mechanical ingenuity.

## Borel's verdict

Von Ende received from Pel both formal praise for courage and a reprimand for the unauthorized action; Borel's verdict is characteristically double: "he had fully deserved both; the achieved success made everything good." The day's cost was one dead, about twelve severely wounded, and two officers wounded after the fight was already over. The post at Lamara Olejlo became a south-eastern anchor of the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]] completed in [[Completion of the defensive ring around Kota Radja (May 1875)|May 1875]].
