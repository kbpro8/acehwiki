**Christoffel's expedition to Keureuto** — about fifteen years before Broersma's visit, that is, around [[1908]] — was the *marechaussée* action that broke the post-pacification resistance in the hill-country of [[Keureuto|Keureuto]] in the *onderafdeeling* [[Lho Soekon|Lho Soekon]]. The expedition was commanded by the *marechaussée* officer [[Christoffel|Christoffel]], who was called by the Atjehnese *apitan rimoeeng* — *the tiger-captain* — *'omdat zijn greep bloedig was'* — *because his grip was bloody.*

## The Expedition
The hill-country of Keureuto was difficult to reach, because the resistance *'zich uit de paja-Tjitjem telkens terugtrok naar de heuvels'* — *repeatedly withdrew from the Paja Tjitjem into the hills.* The great swamp of [[Paja Tjitjem|Paja Tjitjem]] in the south-eastern half of the *onderafdeeling* was the refuge of the insurgents; the hills of the north-western half were their fastness. Christoffel's bivouac at [[Panon Labeuë|Panon Labeuë]] commanded the approaches to both, and his *marechaussées* broke the resistance from there.

## Aftermath
The memory of the suffering of those years is still fresh in such a region as Keureuto, Broersma writes; and when *geestelijke parasieten* — spiritual parasites — misuse the doctrine of the holy war to keep minds bitter, double vigilance is required, a vigilance that is not ostentatious. And in such regions the presence of administrative officials with Atjeh-experience and knowledge of the language is highly to be valued.

## Significance
Christoffel's expedition is one of the last significant military actions of the post-pacification period, undertaken in a region whose geography — the great swamp and the hills — gave the insurgents a refuge from which only the *marechaussée* could dislodge them. The nickname *apitan rimoeeng* places Christoffel in the same series as the *'tijger van Atjeh'* [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] himself.
