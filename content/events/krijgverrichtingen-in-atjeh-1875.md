**Krijgverrichtingen in Atjeh (1875)** refers to the continuation of Dutch military actions in the Aceh Sultanate during the calendar year [[1875]]. The award list (page 185) records that several officers who had already received honours for earlier campaigns were again mentioned for their conduct in 1875, including:

* **J. J. Korndörffer** – again cited for bravery.  
* **W. F. Clifford Koek van Breugel** – repeated honourable mention.  
* **J. L. Cadet** – likewise.  
* **G. P. C. Bijsterveld** – again recognised.  
* **H. Jansma** – repeated citation.

These mentions were part of the same royal decree (No. 39, [[26 October]] [[1876]]) that covered the broader 1874‑1875 period, indicating that the 1875 actions were viewed as a distinct phase within the larger campaign.

## Significance

The 1875 operations helped to consolidate Dutch footholds established in the previous year and demonstrated the persistence of Dutch military pressure on the Acehnese resistance.
