**The treachery of Lambesi** was the act by which [[Toekoe Oemar (Tenom)|Toekoe Oemar]], an Atjehsche chief who had promised to procure the liberation of the English shipwrecked men then held by the Tenomsche (see [[Nisero affair (1883–1885)]] and the [[Attempted enlistment of Teukoe Oemar to free the Niserosschip prisoners (medio July 1884)|attempted enlistment of Teukoe Oemar]] of the same summer), destroyed the unsuspecting crew of a Dutch war sloop. Landed at [[Lambesi]], he overwhelmed the landing party that suspected no evil, killed seven of them, and made himself master of all the weapons. The fact, recorded in the Koloniaal Verslag, was quoted by [[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] in 1886 among the proofs that "the continuous attempts from our side to knit and maintain peaceable relations with chiefs and population are only answered with betrayal" — in the very month in which the government had secretly decided upon the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration of Atjeh]]. After this deed, the report adds, Toekoe Oemar again zealously harassed the Dutch in Groot-Atjeh.
