The **Capture of Lam Barih** was the seven-day struggle by which [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]] made himself master, in [[November]] [[1893]], of the *kampong* (village) of [[Lam Barih]] in the [[XXII Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]], a quarter of an hour from the *[[Geconcentreerde linie|geconcentreerde linie]]*, followed by the Dutch occupation of a temporary post there on [[30 November]] [[1893]].

## The fighting

Scarcely had [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]] finished his work in the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] when a new task was given him: an attack on that part of the [[XXII Moekims]] which fringed the Dutch concentrated position on the south-east and bordered the now cleansed XXVI Moekims. Only if the *[[Moslemin]]* were driven from it too, Hooijer wrote, could lasting rest be counted on in the liberated territory.

*De tegenstand, dien hij hier ondervond, overtrof verre dien in de XXVI Moekims ontmoet* — the resistance he met here far exceeded that encountered in the XXVI Moekims — and only after seven days of fighting and considerable losses could he make himself master of Lam Barih.

## The Dutch post

At his repeated insistence a temporary post was occupied by the Dutch on [[30 November]] [[1893]] on the existing road from [[Lambaroe]] to [[Anagaloeng]]. The position lay at the edge of the *kampong* chain through which the [[Acheh River|Atjeh-rivier]] rolls its yellow waters, with high watch-houses commanding the wide rice-fields of [[Lamkrak]] at which a 12 cm. gun was directed.
