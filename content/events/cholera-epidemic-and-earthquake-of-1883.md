The **cholera epidemic and earthquake of 1883** were the twin visitations that, together with the falling-away of trusted chiefs and the decline of Dutch influence, stamped the year [[1883]] as a "rapjaar" — "een jaar dat ook voor de economische ontwikkeling van het gewest rampsspoedig geweest is" ("a year that for the economic development of the province has been disastrous") — in the history of [[Acheh|Atjeh]].

## The visitations

To the discouraging events of the first civil years — the ever more religious character of the Atjehnese struggle, the failure of the civilian government's attempts at approach to the hostile chiefs, and the marked decline of the population's good disposition accompanied by the defection of some chiefs who had enjoyed the Government's confidence — must be added, writes author [[J. Langhout|Joh. Langhout]], "dat het geheele gewest bezocht werd door een geweldige cholera-epidemie, die haar slachtoffers bij duizenden telde, en van de niet minder geweldige aardbeving die Koeta Radja tot in verre omgeving teisterde" — that the whole province was visited by a violent cholera epidemic which counted its victims by thousands, and by the no less violent earthquake which tormented [[Koeta Radja]] to a wide distance. Sickness had haunted the Dutch in Atjeh from the first years: particularly cholera had raged frightfully in [[1874]]–[[1875]] around the new settlements (an evil answered by the [[Oeleë Lheuë|Oelèë Lheue]] artesian waterworks of [[22 June]] [[1875]]).

## Consequences

In 1883 the restrictive harbour resolution of [[29 May]] was issued (see [[Establishment of Civil Government in Aceh (6 April 1881)|Introduction of civil government in Atjeh (1881)]]); the attacks on [[Meulaboh]] and [[Teunom]] intensified; and the year closed with the stranding of the *Nisero* ([[Nisero affair (1883–1885)|10–11 November 1883]]), whose ten-month sequel spanned the last months of the civil experiment. "Neemt men bij die ontmoedigende gebeurtenissen nog kennis van het feit, dat ... bij in de Atjeh-geschiedenis het jaar 1883 als een 'rapjaar' te boek staat."
