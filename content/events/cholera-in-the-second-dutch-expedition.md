**Cholera in the Second Dutch Expedition** was the epidemic that afflicted transports of the [[Second Dutch Expedition to Aceh]] before and after their arrival off [[Aceh]] in [[1873]].

## Course

The disease had already appeared aboard vessels before anchors were raised. Quarantine constrained the provisioning ship and delayed departure indirectly; aboard the *Sloet van den Beele* and *Willem Kroonprins der Nederlanden*, carrying parts of the 3rd battalion from [[Central Java]], it proved especially destructive. [[Jan van Swieten]] emphasized this emergency as a material condition of the campaign.

## Significance

The episode belongs to the continuing [[Aceh War]] and the contested consequences of the [[Capture of the Kraton of Aceh]].
