---
title: Hikayat Prang Gompeuni
aliases: [Hikayat Prang Kompeni]
tags: [peristiwa, karya-sastra]
---

The great Acehnese heroic poem narrating the war against the Dutch, composed orally over five years by the illiterate poet Dokarim of Glumpang Dua, who added fresh material as eyewitnesses provided new information. Hurgronje had the poem taken down directly from Dokarim's recitation, producing the first complete written copy. It is the principal literary source for the careers of Teuku Uma, Teungku Tiro, Habib Abdurrahman, Panglima Tibang, and many other figures of the Aceh War.
