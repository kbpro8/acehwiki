**The verbanning van drie hoofden uit Lho Seumawe (1922)** — *the banishment of three chiefs from Lho' Seumawe in 1922* — was the colonial government's intervention against the [[Sarikat Islam in Atjeh (1918-1922)|Sarikat Islam movement]] in the *onderafdeeling* [[Lho Seumawe|Lho' Seumawé]] of the *afdeeling* [[Noordkust van Atjeh|Noordkust van Atjeh]]. Three chiefs of Lho' Seumawe were banished, an intervention Broersma describes as *'geen dag te vroeg gekomen'* — *not a day too soon.*

## Background
In the whole *afdeeling* Noordkust van Atjeh the Sarikat Islam showed a vigorous life after [[1918]]. Traders, school-teachers — *'laat ons zeggen de intellectueelen'* — but also several chiefs were members and took part. A chief of [[Samalanga|Samalanga]] had joined by way of a move on the political chessboard on which he was playing against the influential [[Sri Tengkoe Abdoel Hamid Orangkaja Sri Maharadja Mangkoe Boemi|Maharadja of Lho' Seumawé]], who had also become a member of the Sarikat Islam in order to strengthen his chances of retaining a *landschap Plimbang*. The movement in Lho' Seumawe took the direction of bringing the distinction between believers and unbelievers to the fore, of undermining the Dutch government, and of working towards a union of *oeléebalangs*.

## The Intervention
Three chiefs of Lho' Seumawe were banished in [[1922]]. Broersma's comment is brief: *'Tot bedaring is men gekomen, toen verleden jaar drie hoofden uit Lho' Seumawe zijn verbannen, een ingrijpen dat geen dag te vroeg is gekomen.'* The implication is that the banishment was a necessary and belated act of colonial discipline.

## Significance
The banishment of the three chiefs of Lho' Seumawe is the most forceful intervention of the colonial government against an Atjehnese political movement in the post-pacification period, and the type-case of the government's response to the alliance of *oeléebalangs* and *oelama* in the Sarikat Islam. The intervention is also the political condition of the relatively quiet state of the *Afdeeling Noordkust van Atjeh* in the years immediately before Broersma's visit.
