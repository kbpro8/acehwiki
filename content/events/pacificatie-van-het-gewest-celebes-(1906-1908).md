**Pacification of the Province of Celebes (1906–1908)** was the pacification of the province of [[Celebes]] carried out by [[H. N. A. Swart]] as Civil and Military Governor between [[3 September]] [[1906]] and [[1908]].

Educated in [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|van Heutsz]]'s school, Swart could "pacify Celebes in a few years." Operations in Celebes are recorded in his *[[Extract from the service record of H. N. A. Swart|stamboek]]* for [[1906]], [[1907]] and [[1908]]. Those who knew only [[Atjeh]] considered the work there and in [[Borneo]] easier and not comparable — "Atjeh was Atjeh! What proved right elsewhere did not on that account have to be right for Atjeh." Swart denied it: "it was all the application of the same insights — though under different circumstances. And those circumstances he did not consider insuperable."
