**The tennis selection trials including Atjeh** were held from 10 to 13 September [[1951]] to select the best Indonesian tennis players from the East Coast of Sumatra, [[Atjeh|Atjeh]], and [[Tapanuli]] for the national championships at Djakarta. The selection was conducted by the Persatuan Lawn Tennis Indonesia (the Indonesian Lawn Tennis Association).

## Outcome

The selection for the women's events fell on Mrs. P. St. Namora of Siantar. The trials were part of the broader post-independence development of organised sport in North Sumatra, integrating the Atjeh region into the provincial and national sporting framework.
