**Court's flight from Dalem to Keumala (twice)** (1878 and 1898) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Keumala was historically the region where Sultansmacht (Sultan's authority) was most honored. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1878]] and [[1898]].

## Account

[[Keumala]] was historically the region where Sultansmacht ([[sultan|Sultan]]'s authority) was most honored. The court sought and found refuge in [[Keumala]] twice when forced to flee the [[Dalem]]: in [[1878]] and in [[1898]]. From here [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] was long sustained and for years it was the breeding ground of fanaticism and [[Kafir|kafir]]-haat (hatred of unbelievers).

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
