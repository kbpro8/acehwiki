**The Destruction of the Lampoe Oek fortification (18 September 1874)** was the operation by which a single [[Achterlaadkanonnen van 12 c.M.|12 c.M. achterlaadkanon]], firing from the new ooster-batterij, demolished the parapet of the Atjehnese stelling at [[Lampoe Oek]] — the position from which [[Majoor Romswinckel]] had been repulsed at such cost on [[16 April]] [[1874]] — and so, by a few cannon shots, achieved what an infantry assault had failed to do. "What had not been obtained earlier through fierce struggle costing heavy sacrifices," Borel writes, "was now achieved by a few cannon shots."

## The clearing of the field

For some weeks the [[Vesting-artillerie in Atjeh|vesting-artillerie]] had been pushing forward a new ooster-batterij to bring Lampoe Oek under fire. To clear the field of view, 150 *klapperbomen* (coconut palms) had to be felled; the work, done under enemy sharpshooters, cost nine wounded, including one officer.

## The bombardment

On [[18 September]] [[1874]] the 12 c.M. achterlaadkanon opened fire. It soon found the range and demolished the parapet, overthrowing the *schanskorven* (gabions) and dismounting the works. The Lampoe Oek stelling — where Romswinckel had been repulsed on [[16 April]] — was abandoned in haste by the Atjehneezen, who left guns and tools behind. Borel's verdict is delivered in a single cutting sentence: the episode was, for him, final proof that the [[Battle of Lampoe Oek (16 April 1874)|échec of 16 April]] had been the fault not of Romswinckel but of [[Jan van Swieten|van Swieten]], who had recalled the vesting-artillerie and the achterlaadkanonnen to Java in the spring.

## Aftermath

The Lampoe Oek site, once cleared, became in turn a Dutch *nipa-benteng* — a fortified post built of nipa-palm — and was itself the target of an enemy dawn attack on [[1 February]] [[1875]]. It stood inside the line of the future [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]]. The lesson Borel drew — that artillery, not infantry assault, was the proper instrument against Atjehnese *benteng*s — would be repeated at [[Mandarsa Poeti]] on [[5 October]] [[1874]], when a few 12 c.M. shells brought down the whole front wall of the old stone *gebedenhuis*.
