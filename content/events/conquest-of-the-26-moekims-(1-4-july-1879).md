**The Conquest of the 26 Moekims of 1–4 July [[1879]]** — *de verovering der 26 Moekims* — was the four-day military campaign by which the Dutch forces under [[General van der Heijden|General van der Heijden]], beginning on [[1 July]] [[1 July]] [[1879]], subdued the [[26 Moekims|26 Moekims]] — one of the two seaward *Sagi's* (divisions) of [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] — and thereby, together with the [[Conquest of the 22 Moekims (March-June 1879)|conquest of the 22 Moekims]] completed in half [[June 1879|June]] [[1879]] and the subsequent expedition to [[Selimoen|Selimoen]], brought the entire valley of Groot-Atjeh under Dutch control. The campaign was completed "*met volkomen succes*" (with complete success) in four days.

## The Campaign

On [[1 July]] [[1 July]] [[1879]] the campaign against the [[26 Moekims|26 Moekims]] began, and was completed with full success in four days. Then there was still an expedition to [[Selimoen|Selimoen]], far in the interior. Remote chiefs submitted. All of Groot-Atjeh was Dutch. "*De schrik was er in*" (the terror was in them). The irreconcilable principal chiefs withdrew to [[Kemala|Kemala]], the hinterland of [[Pedir|Pedir]]. The return march from Selimoen was a true triumph.

## Significance

The conquest of the 26 Moekims, together with the [[Conquest of the 22 Moekims (March-June 1879)|conquest of the 22 Moekims]] of [[March 1879|March]]–[[June 1879|June]] [[1879]] and the [[Selimoen expedition|Selimoen expedition]], brought the entire valley of [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] under Dutch control for the first and only time before the final pacification under [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] after [[1898]]. The conquest would, however, prove transient: under the civil government of [[Pruijs van der Hoeven|Pruijs van der Hoeven]] and [[Laging Tobias|Laging Tobias]] (1881–1884), the endless patrols that [[General van der Heijden|Van der Heijden]] had deemed necessary to maintain the peace were virtually abolished, the enemy reorganised, and the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration system]] of [[1884]] contracted the Dutch *stelling* back to the perimeter of [[1876]].
