**The Atjehsche avonturier at Aroe-baai ([[1862]])** is the unnamed [[Acheh|Atjehnese]] adventurer who, in [[1862]], threw up a *benteng* (fortification) on the hill of [[Poelo Kampi|Poelo Kampi]] in [[Aroe-baai|the bay of Aroe]] and defiantly flew the Atjehsche flag from it — the only recorded instance of an Atjehnese military assertion on the east-coast Sumatran theatre in the immediate pre-[[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] period. The episode is preserved by [[P. de Roo de la Faille|De Roo de la Faille]], who notes it in connection with the [[Van Rijn van Alkemade|Van Rijn]] visit of [[1888]] and with the Tanjore inscription of [[1030]]'s "ancient Malayur situated on a high hill".

## The episode

In [[1862]] — eleven years before the [[Eerste Atjeh-Expeditie (1873)|first Dutch expedition against Atjeh]] of [[1873]] — an unnamed Atjehnese adventurer threw up a *benteng* on the hill of [[Poelo Kampi|Kampi]] in [[Aroe-baai|the bay of Aroe]], "and here defiantly let the Atjehsche flag fly". The episode is recorded in the [[Van Rijn van Alkemade|Van Rijn]] report of [[1888]], which notes that the impression of the hill — "as if one has a fortress with breastwork before one" — recalled the Tanjore inscription's "ancient Malayur situated on a high hill".

## Significance

For AcehWiki, the Atjehsche avonturier at Aroe-baai ([[1862]]) is the principal recorded instance of an Atjehnese military assertion on the east-coast Sumatran theatre in the pre-[[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] period — a token of the survival of an Atjehnese memory of the [[Atjeh-Aroe War (1539)|Atjehnese conquest of Aroe]] of [[1539]] more than three centuries after the event. The choice of [[Poelo Kampi|Kampi]] — the [[Malajoer|sacred islet]] of the older Hindu-Buddhist geography, the [[Noesa-n-ta-ra|Noesa-n-ta-ra]] of the [[Pararaton]] — for the Atjehnese flag is, in [[P. de Roo de la Faille|De Roo de la Faille]]'s reading, no accident: it marks the same place that the [[Atjehsche vloot|Atjehnese fleet]] of [[Alleredin Mahomet]] had taken in [[1539]].
