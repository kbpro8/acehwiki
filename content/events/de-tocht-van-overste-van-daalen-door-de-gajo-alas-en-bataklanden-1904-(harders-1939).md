**De tocht van Overste van Daalen door de Gajo-, Alas- en Bataklanden 1904** — *"The march of Colonel van Daalen through the Gayo, Alas, and Batak lands in 1904"* — is an article by **[[J. E. Harders|J. E. Harders]]**, published in [[1939]] in the *Orgaan van de Nederlandsch-Indische Officiersvereeniging* (Organ of the Netherlands Indies Officers' Association), recording the [[Colonne van Daalen naar de Gajolanden en Alaslanden (1904)|campaign of Colonel (Overste) van Daalen]] through the [[Gajoland|Gayo]], [[Alaslanden|Alas]], and [[Bataklanden|Batak]] lands of the central Sumatran interior in [[1904]]. The article is recorded as No. 568 in [[H. van Meurs|Van Meurs]]'s *[[Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939 (Van Meurs, 1940)|Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939]]* (The Hague, [[1940]]), in the Sumatra chapter, with a cross-reference ("zie bij TICHELMAN, G. L.") indicating its connection to the work of [[G. L. Tichelmann|G. L. Tichelmann]].

## Subject

The article's subject, as recorded by Van Meurs, is the *tocht* — the march, the campaign — of **Overste** (Colonel) [[G. C. E. van Daalen|van Daalen]] through the [[Gajoland|Gajo-]], [[Alaslanden|Alas-]], and [[Bataklanden|Bataklanden]] in [[1904]]. The article thus covers the same campaign as the [[De tocht van Overste van Daalen door de Gajo-, Alas- en Bataklanden (Geusau, 1939)|Geusau article of 1939]] (No. 566) — the [[Colonne van Daalen naar de Gajolanden en Alaslanden (1904)|Colonne van Daalen]] of the corpus's existing entry — but published in a different venue, the *Orgaan van de Nederlandsch-Indische Officiersvereeniging* rather than the *Indisch Militair Tijdschrift*. The double publication of retrospective accounts of the same campaign in two distinct professional military journals in the same year marks [[1939]] as a year of sustained retrospective interest in the van Daalen campaign, thirty-five years after the event.

The cross-reference "zie bij TICHELMAN, G. L." — "see under Tichelmann, G. L." — indicates that Harders's article is connected to, or to be read alongside, the work of [[G. L. Tichelmann|G. L. Tichelmann]], the Dutch colonial ethnographer and artist who is represented elsewhere in the Sumatra chapter of Van Meurs's survey by entries on [[In Atjeh opgedolven glazen kruikjes (Tichelmann, 1939)|Atjeh glass jugs]] (No. 625), on Batak antiquities, and on the prehistoric and ethnographic material of northern Sumatra. The nature of the connection — whether Tichelmann contributed illustrations to Harders's article, or whether the two accounts are companion pieces — is not specified in Van Meurs's entry, but the cross-reference indicates that the two authors' work on the van Daalen campaign should be read together.

## Author and venue

[[J. E. Harders|Harders]] published the article in the *Orgaan van de Nederlandsch-Indische Officiersvereeniging*, the principal professional journal of the Netherlands Indies officer corps — the same venue that carried the [[J. J. C. A. Bannink|Bannink]] article on the [[Grepen uit den Atjeh-oorlog (Bannink, 1939)|Atjeh-oorlog]] (No. 550) and the [[S. da Costa|da Costa]] survey of the [[De Padrie-oorlog (Costa, 1939)|Padrie-oorlog]] (No. 558) in the same year. The choice of venue signals the article's intended audience: serving and former officers of the [[K.N.I.L.|Koninklijk Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger]] with an interest in the campaigns of the preceding generation.

## Place in the 1939 Literatuur-Overzicht

The article is No. 568 in the Sumatra chapter (Hoofdstuk III, Nos. 548–658) of Van Meurs's survey, where it stands immediately after No. 566 — the [[De tocht van Overste van Daalen door de Gajo-, Alas- en Bataklanden (Geusau, 1939)|Geusau account of the same campaign]]. The double recording — Geusau in the *Indisch Militair Tijdschrift* and Harders in the officers' journal — is a notable feature of the Sumatra chapter of the 1939 survey, and it marks the van Daalen campaign as the retrospective military-historical subject *par excellence* of the year.

## Significance

For AcehWiki, the article is significant as a [[1939]] retrospective on the [[Colonne van Daalen naar de Gajolanden en Alaslanden (1904)|van Daalen campaign of 1904]], published in the principal officers' journal of the Netherlands Indies, and as an independent source for the campaign that complements both the [[colonne-van-daalen-1904|corpus's existing entry]] (drawing on *[[De Buitenbezittingen|De Buitenbezittingen]]* and *Dispereert Niet*) and the [[De tocht van Overste van Daalen door de Gajo-, Alas- en Bataklanden (Geusau, 1939)|Geusau account]] (No. 566). The cross-reference to [[G. L. Tichelmann|Tichelmann]] indicates a connection to the wider ethnographic and antiquarian work on northern Sumatra represented in the same chapter of Van Meurs's survey, and it suggests that the Harders article may have included Tichelmann's illustrations or ethnographic observations.
