**The Siege of Grave (1674)** was a concurrent operation to the **[[Siege of Naarden (1674)]]**, in which **[[Prince of Orange (William III)]]**’s forces attempted to dislodge French and Spanish troops from the town of **[[Grave]]**.  The source notes that on **[[9 September]] [[1674]]** the Dutch opened artillery fire on the town’s walls, and that the **[[Waterlandsche poort]]** was heavily damaged (see [[276]]).

## Relevance to Aceh
The successful relief of **Grave** and **Naarden** helped secure the Dutch heartland, allowing the **[[Dutch Navy]]** to continue its patrols of the North Sea and protect the **[[Indische retourvloot]]**.  The safety of this fleet was essential for maintaining the **[[Dutch East India Company]]**’s trade with **[[Aceh]]**, which relied on uninterrupted sea lanes.
