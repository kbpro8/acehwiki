The **Establishment of the post at Tjot Goeë** on [[8 August]] [[1893]] was the first act of the Dutch outpost policy beyond the *[[Geconcentreerde linie|geconcentreerde linie]]* in [[Groot-Atjeh]], undertaken after [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Oemar]] had cleared the whole of the [[IX Moekims]] of the *[[Moslemin]]*.

## Circumstances

[[G. B. Hooijer]] recorded that neither the road running along and over the bare hills of the [[IX Moekims]] nor the post of [[Tjot Goeë]] existed when Oemar first drove the *Moslemin* thither. *Die post werd eerst opgetrokken op den 8sten Augustus 1893, toen de geheele IX Moekims door Toekoe Oemar van vijanden gezuiverd waren* — the post was first raised on 8 August 1893, when the whole of the IX Moekims had been purged of enemies by Toekoe Oemar.

The date was placed by Hooijer one month before Oemar's submission to Dutch authority was accepted and before the dignity of *[[Panglima Prang Besar]]* was conferred on him under the name [[Toekoe Djohan Pahalawan]]. The post thus marks the point at which the alliance became a matter of Dutch military occupation rather than of Achehnese action alone.

## Consequences

The road along the hills was constructed later, when the garrison of [[Boekit Daroe]] was transferred to [[Biloel]] on [[20 May]] [[1894]]. Tjot Goeë became the northern anchor of the southern group of posts, with [[Biloel]] to the south and [[Lam Koenjit]] between them from [[4 July]] [[1894]]; it was the only post of that group retained as a permanent fortification after [[April]] [[1895]], *om te voorkomen dat de vijand er weder een tweede [[Kaloet]] zou oprichten*.
