**The Battle of Garouw (28 June 1874)** was the action by which [[Majoor Romswinckel]] carried the strongly fortified kampong of [[Garouw]], north-east of [[Lampoeloe]], and broke the Atjehnese hold on the [[Pedir-dijk]] between [[Penajoeng]] and [[Kota Radja Bedil|K. R. Bedil]] — the first permanently held Dutch advance beyond the right bank of the [[Atjeh-rivier]] since the [[Fall of the Kraton (24 January 1874)|fall of the Kraton]]. Borel calls [[28 June]] [[1874]] "een zeer belangrijk en roemrijken dag."

## Background

Intercepts of Atjehnese messages revealed that the chiefs assembled at Garouw were resolved to cut the river communication between [[Kota Radja|K. Radja]] and Penajoeng once more, and were trying to persuade [[Toekoe Nek]] of [[Marassa]] — the only Dutch ally — to turn against the *orang belanda*. Pel decided to strike first. In the early morning of [[28 June]] [[1874]] Majoor Romswinckel attacked.

## The assault

The fortification at Garouw was well laid out, but Romswinckel's half-battalion surrounded it and, after *dapperen tegenstand*, carried it. The cost was four dead and about thirty wounded, three of them officers severely. Two of those officers — the lieutenants von Massow and Zwager — died of their wounds shortly after. The enemy was so shaken by the loss that he abandoned, in succession, his other positions on the right bank, the entire Pedir-dijk, the road to Koeala, and the fort of [[Kota Radja Bedil|K. R. Bedil]] at the downstream end of the dike, all of which the Dutch occupied at once.

## Borel's verdict

Borel calls [[28 June]] [[1874]] "een zeer belangrijk en roemrijken dag" — for the first time the Atjehneezen had been permanently driven from the right bank below Penajoeng. He contrasts the result with Romswinckel's [[Battle of Lampoe Oek (16 April 1874)|échec at Lampoe Oek]] on [[16 April]] [[1874]], which had been laid by [[Jan van Swieten|van Swieten]] at Romswinckel's door, and which Borel treats as van Swieten's own fault for recalling the [[Vesting-artillerie in Atjeh|vesting-artillerie]] and the [[Achterlaadkanonnen van 12 c.M.|achterlaadkanonnen]] to Java. Garouw repaired that stain and opened the way for the [[Battle of Soerian (26 July 1874)|westward push on Soerian]] a month later. Toekoe Nek, who had refused the enemy's overtures, kept his loyalty to the Dutch and was rewarded by a quiet Marassa frontier.
