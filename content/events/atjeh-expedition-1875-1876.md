**Atjeh Expedition (1875–1876)** was a sustained Dutch marine campaign that followed the earlier 1873 operations. Conducted during [[1875]] and [[1876]], the expedition sought to establish a permanent Dutch presence in the interior of [[Atjeh]] and to dismantle the remaining organized resistance.

The expedition combined naval bombardments with infantry advances, constructing forts at strategic points such as [[Banda Aceh]] and the riverine outpost of [[Krueng Seumantok]]. The marines operated under the command of **Colonel Heuter**, whose leadership is noted in the corps’ records.

## Significance
The 1875‑1876 campaign marked a turning point in the Dutch approach to Aceh, shifting from short punitive raids to a strategy of permanent occupation and infrastructure development.
