**Founding of the Atjeh-Museum** (not specified in pages read) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. The Atjeh-Museum at Koetaradja, built in Acehnese style, holds (currently plundered/depleted) collections on land and people. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to not specified in pages [[Read (consul-generaal at Singapore)|read]].

## Account

The [[Atjeh Museum|Atjeh-Museum]] at [[Koetaradja]], built in Acehnese style, holds (currently plundered/depleted) collections on land and people. Work is ongoing to bring together a more complete and systematically organized collection. The [[Tjakra Donja]] bronze bell hangs under an awning in front of the museum building.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
