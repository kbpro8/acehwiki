The **Handboek der Pionierkunst voor het Nederlandsche Leger** (Handbook of Sapping for the Dutch Army) was the multi-volume engineers' textbook written by [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Kapitein (later Generaal) C. J. Snijders]] in the years [[1880]]–[[1887]] (with one part delayed to [[1894]]) during his first tenure as a teacher at the [[Koninklijke Militaire Academie|Koninklijke Militaire Academie]]. The Handboek drew directly on Snijders' Atjeh experience of [[1874]]–[[1875]] — particularly on the [[Benting Kota Alam operation (13 November 1874)|Benting Kota Alam]] and [[Missigit Longbatta operation (31 December 1874 – 13 January 1875)|Missigit Longbatta]] operations — for its treatment of field fortification under fire; it was introduced as the official textbook for the cadets of all arms at the [[Koninklijke Militaire Academie|KMA]] soon after the appearance of the first parts, and was also officially adopted as the handbook for the [[Bataillon Mineurs en Sappeurs|Korps Genietroepen]]. A revised second edition was published in [[1903]]–[[1908]], in main part reworked by Kapitein J. H. A. Mijsberg under Snijders' supervision.

## Composition and Adoption

The Handboek appeared in twelve parts over the years [[1880]]–[[1887]]; one part, however, was delayed by particular circumstances and did not appear until [[1894]]. Soon after the appearance of the first parts the Handboek was introduced as the official textbook for the cadets of all arms at the [[Koninklijke Militaire Academie|KMA]], and it was also officially adopted as the handbook for the [[Bataillon Mineurs en Sappeurs|Korps Genietroepen]] — in connection with which the last two parts were prepared in collaboration with Kapitein J. H. A. Mijsberg of that corps.

## The Second Edition of 1903–1908

In [[1903]]–[[1908]] a much-shortened second edition of the Handboek appeared, in main part reworked by Kapitein Mijsberg under the supervision of the then Majoor Snijders. The second edition reflects the further development of Snijders' thinking on field fortification in the decades after the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] and his work on the [[Stelling van Amsterdam|Stelling van Amsterdam]] and the [[Nieuwe Hollandsche Waterlinie|Nieuwe Hollandsche Waterlinie]].

## Significance

The Handboek is the principal published embodiment of Snijders' Atjeh experience. The technical detail of [[benting|benting]] construction under fire — the use of [[vrije koelies|vrije koelies]] and [[dwangarbeiders|dwangarbeiders]], the [[bamboe-doerie|bamboe-doerie]] hedge, the [[ranjoe|ranjoe]] planting, the sandbag parapet, the traverses with Friesche ruiters — which is recorded in the [[1944]] biography by [[D. van den Berg|Van den Berg]] from the contemporary lecture of [[T. W. G. Merz|Merz]], is systematised in the Handboek for the instruction of the next generation of Dutch engineer officers. The Handboek is thus one of the channels through which the Atjeh experience was transmitted to the Dutch army of the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries.
