**The Tweede Atjeh-expeditie (1873-1874)** — the second Dutch military expedition against the Sultanate of [[Atjeh|Aceh]] — was the expedition that began in [[1873]] and concluded with the taking of the [[Missigit (Meusigit)|Missigit]] (the great mosque) of [[Koeta-Radja]] on [[6 January]] [[1874]], opening the forty-five-year war that would end with the [[Handover of Aceh to a civil Gouverneur (1918)|handover of 1918]]. The taking of the Missigit on [[6 January]] [[1874]] is the first major Dutch military succes of the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]].

## The taking of the Missigit (6 January 1874)

"Die tocht werden de hevige gevechten van de eerste maanden van [[1876]]" — but the Missigit itself was taken on [[6 January]] [[1874]]. The [[Tweede Atjeh-expeditie (1873-1874)|Tweede Atjeh-expeditie]] — which began in [[1873]] and concluded with the taking of the Missigit on [[6 January]] [[1874]] — opened the forty-five-year war. The Missigit — the great mosque of [[Koeta-Radja]] — was the symbolic centre of the Sultanate of [[Atjeh|Aceh]]; its taking was the symbolic opening of the war.

## The Derde Bataljon and the M.W.O.

The book notes that the [[Derde Bataljon (Atjeh-Bataljon)|Derde Bataljon]] — the "Atjeh-Bataljon" — received the M.W.O. decoration "in [[1877]] voor het buitengewoon roemrijk aandeel" in the early years of the war, "in het bijzonder vermeld die van begin [[1876]]." The decorations of the early Aceh War — the M.W.O. for the Derde Bataljon, the M.W.O. for individual ridders — begin with the [[1873]]-[[1874]] expeditions.

## The forty-five-year war

The [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]] that opened with the [[Tweede Atjeh-expeditie (1873-1874)|Tweede Atjeh-expeditie]] would last forty-five years — from [[1873]] to the [[Handover of Aceh to a civil Gouverneur (1918)|handover of 1918]] — and would be the principal theatre of the [[K.N.I.L.]] for the next half-century. The [[Tweede Atjeh-expeditie (1873-1874)|Tweede Atjeh-expeditie]]'s taking of the Missigit on [[6 January]] [[1874]] was the symbolic opening of the war, but the war itself would be a long counter-insurgency that would only be militarily finished by the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]] of [[1910]]-[[1911]].

## Significance

The [[Tweede Atjeh-expeditie (1873-1874)|Tweede Atjeh-expeditie]] is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as the expedition that opened the forty-five-year war. The book's argument is that the taking of the [[Missigit (Meusigit)|Missigit]] on [[6 January]] [[1874]] was the symbolic opening of the war, but that the war itself would be a long counter-insurgency that would only be militarily finished by the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]] of [[1910]]-[[1911]] and politically closed by the [[Handover of Aceh to a civil Gouverneur (1918)|handover of 1918]].
