**Pel's Buitenzorg planning session (1875)** was the series of conversations held in July [[1875]] at Buitenzorg in Java between [[Generaal Pel|generaal Pel]] — there to recover his health — the newly appointed [[Gouverneur-Generaal van Lansberge|gouverneur-generaal van Lansberge]] and the *commandant van het leger*, in which Pel laid out his conviction that the [[Afwachtende houding|afwachtende stelsel]] was useless and proposed instead an offensive to extend the Dutch *linie* from the mouth of the [[Kroeng Raba]] on the west coast to the mouth of the [[Kroeng Raya]] on the north coast, isolating Atjeh from the sea and reducing it to a *binnenstaat*.

## The plan

While recovering at Buitenzorg, Pel explained to Borel and to the newly appointed Gouverneur-Generaal van Lansberge his conviction that the *afwachtende stelsel* was useless. His plan was, as soon as the good *moesson* returned, to resume offensives; to push the post at [[Soerian]] forward; to invade [[Toekoe Nanta]]'s [[VI Moekims]]; to occupy [[Sinangri]] and sweep the western bank of [[Kwala Tjangkoel]]; to break the strong enemy line at Doewa; to seize kampongs and *rijstvelden* (rice fields); and, later, to extend the *linie* from the mouth of the [[Kroeng Raba]] on the west coast to the mouth of the [[Kroeng Raya]] on the north coast, isolating Atjeh from the sea and reducing it to a *binnenstaat* (inland state).

## Van Lansberge's endorsement

The Gouverneur-Generaal agreed. He decided "to wage the war with Atjeh in the most decisive manner" and promised ample reinforcements. The government, Borel adds, approved Pel's plan in the second half of [[1875]]. Pel's secret intention — to seal Atjeh off from the sea by the Kroeng Raba–Kroeng Raya line — he never communicated officially, not even to his chief of staff; nothing about it was found in his archive after his [[Death of Generaal Pel (24 February 1876)|death on 24 February 1876]].

## Borel's verdict

Borel, who heard the plan from Pel's own mouth at Buitenzorg, treats it as the unifying strategic conception of the war. The plan would be partly executed in Pel's great [[Pel's offensive against the VI, IV and IX Moekims (1875–1876)|December 1875–February 1876 offensive]] through the [[VI Moekims|VI]], [[IV Moekims|IV]] and [[IX Moekims]], and the closure of the [[Kroeng Tjoet]] mouth by [[Luitenant-kolonel Engel|lt.-kol. Engel]] on [[26 February]] [[1876]]. The full Kroeng Raba–Kroeng Raya line, however, was never completed: the sea-expedition to [[Kroeng Raya]] under [[Karel van der Heijden|kolonel van der Heijden]], during [[Generaal Diemont|generaal Diemont]]'s administration, fought bravely but achieved no satisfactory result and did not link up with the Tongah–Kwala Gigieng line.
