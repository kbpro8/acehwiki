**Expedition to Boni and Loewoe (1905)** was a Dutch marine operation conducted in [[1905]] against the coastal regions of [[Boni (Sulawesi)]] and [[Loewoe (Sulawesi)]]. The expedition formed part of the Dutch effort to extend control over the eastern Indonesian archipelago following the long‑running Aceh conflict.

A detachment of the [[Dutch Marine Corps]] landed at the port of [[Boni]] and proceeded to subdue resistant villages, after which the force moved eastward to Loewoe, where it negotiated the surrender of local leaders.

## Significance
The campaign helped to secure the southern Celebes coast for Dutch trade and to prevent the use of the area as a base for anti‑colonial activity that could support Acehnese rebels.
