**Establishment of V.I.B.E.M. at Sinabang** (1917) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. In 1917 the wood exploitation company V.I.B.E.M. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1917]].

## Account

In [[1917]] the wood exploitation company [[V.I.B.E.M.]] was established at [[Sinabang]] ([[Simeuloeë]]) on a large and luxurious scale. Today its buildings and many installations still stand but in a deplorable state of neglect.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
