**Capture of the Kraton of Aceh** was the Dutch occupation of the [[Kraton of Aceh]] after the Acehnese court had left it during the [[Second Dutch Expedition to Aceh]].

## Course

The capture followed operations around the [[Baiturrahman Grand Mosque|Missigit]], [[Kota Goenoengan]], and [[Penajong]]. [[Jan van Swieten]] argued that the event had profound psychological and political effect but did not make a new sultan’s installation possible: by local understanding, rulers had to be inaugurated at the Kraton itself.

## Significance

The episode belongs to the continuing [[Aceh War]] and the contested consequences of the [[Capture of the Kraton of Aceh]].
