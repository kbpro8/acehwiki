aliases: [1903 surrender]

In January [[1903]], the young pretender-Sultan Muhammad Dawot Shah, who had held court at Keumala since childhood, renounced his claim to the Acehnese throne and submitted unconditionally to the Dutch government, an event generally regarded as marking the formal end of large-scale organized resistance in the Aceh War.
