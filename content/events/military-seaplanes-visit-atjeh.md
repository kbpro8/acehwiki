**Military seaplanes visit Atjeh** (mid-1937) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. In mid-1937, several military seaplanes visited Atjeh, landing on the Atjeh-rivier at Koetaradja and on the more van Takengon (Lake Takengon).. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to mid-[[1937]].

## Account

In mid-[[1937]], several military seaplanes visited Atjeh, landing on the [[Atjeh-rivier]] at [[Koetaradja]] and on the more van [[Takengon]] ([[Laoet Tawar|Lake Takengon]]).

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
