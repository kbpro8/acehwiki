The **Siege and relief of Tjot Goeë** was the investment of the Dutch post of [[Tjot Goeë]] in the [[IX Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]] from [[29 March]] [[1895]] until [[9 April]] [[1895]], the first serious breach of the security that the alliance with [[Teukoe Oemar]] had appeared to bring to the southern outposts.

## The engagement of 29 March 1895

Hooijer recorded that the quiet of [[Tjot Goeë]] — where ladies and gentlemen had come out from [[Kota Radja]] by carriage to enjoy the view over the posts of the *[[Geconcentreerde linie|geconcentreerde linie]]* — changed only on [[29 March]] [[1895]], *toen de drie compagnieën, uit de geconcentreerde linie ter bescherming van den post afgezonden tegen kwaad volk, dat zich in de IX Moekims verzamelde, krachtig onder vuur genomen werden*: when the three companies sent out from the concentrated line to protect the post against ill-disposed people gathering in the [[IX Moekims]] were vigorously fired upon.

## Investment and relief

From that date the *[[Benteng|benteng]]* remained surrounded by the enemy until it was relieved on [[9 April]] [[1895]].

## Consequences

Four days after the relief the two more southerly posts, [[Lam Koenjit]] and [[Biloel]], were lifted, and it was decided to keep a permanent fortification garrisoned at Tjot Goeë to prevent the enemy from erecting *een tweede [[Kaloet]]* there. The other two went up in flames as the Dutch troops hastened back over the muddy fields on which, for almost three years, they had endured all the misery of a temporary station with resignation.
