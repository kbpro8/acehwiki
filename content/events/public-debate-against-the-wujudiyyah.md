**Public debate against the Wujudiyyah** was the open disputation held in [[Acheh]] shortly after the accession of [[Sultan Iskandar II]] (post-[[1636]]), in which [[Nuruddin ar-Raniri]] refuted the adherents of extreme [[Wujudiyyah]] mysticism.

Voorhoeve places it immediately after Raniri's arrival in [[1637]]. The sultan's presence gave Raniri royal sanction, reversing the favour previously enjoyed by the pupils of [[Syamsuddin of Pasai]] under [[Sultan Iskandar Muda]].

The debate is the historical context for both the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan]] and the [[Hudjdjat as-siddik li-daf az-zindik]].
