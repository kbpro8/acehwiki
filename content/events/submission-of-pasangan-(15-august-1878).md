**The Submission of Pasangan (15 August 1878)** was the capitulation, on [[15 August]] [[1878]], of the vassal coastal state of [[Pasangan]] — one of the *Atjeh-afhankelijk kuststaatjes* that had supported the enemy of [[Groot-Atjeh]] with *hulpbenden*, weapons and *levensmiddelen* — after a bombardment by the Dutch marine, an event which Borel, writing as the news came in, hails as proof of his doctrine that "eerst moet de vijand getuchtigd worden en wel in die mate dat hem geen andere uitweg overblijft dan het hoofd in den schoot te leggen."

## Background

The vassal coastal states — Analaboe, Pedir, Samalangan, Pasangan, Simpang Olim, Edi — had supported the enemy of Groot-Atjeh throughout the war with *hulpbenden* (auxiliaries), weapons and *levensmiddelen* (foodstuffs). They had been *tuchtiged* by the Dutch in succession. The same doctrine — that the coastal vassals must be punished into submission before Groot-Atjeh itself could be reduced — had been applied, in June [[1878]], to [[Gedoeng]] by [[Karel van der Heijden|van der Heijden]], and was being applied, by naval bombardment, to Pasangan.

## The submission

On [[15 August]] [[1878]], while Borel was at his desk in 's-Gravenhage putting the last touches to his manuscript, the telegraph brought the news: Pasangan, after a bombardment by the Dutch marine, had submitted. The news is recorded in the *Naschrift* that closes the book.

## Borel's prediction

Borel uses the news to make a prediction. What had been done to Gedoeng and to Pasangan could — and, in his view, should — be done to [[Groot-Atjeh]] itself: "eerst moet de vijand getuchtigd worden en wel in die mate dat hem geen andere uitweg overblijft dan het hoofd in den schoot te leggen" — first the enemy must be chastised, and to such a degree that no other course is left him but to lay his head in the lap. The [[XXII Moekims]] — "de kern van Atjeh's weerstandsvermogen," the densest population, the Sultan's *voorraadschuren*, the most hostile chiefs — were, in his view, the proper next target; once they were tamed, the [[XXVI Moekims]] would submit without needing force.

## Significance

The submission of Pasangan, coming on the heels of the [[Enemy breakthrough in the IV, VI and IX Moekims (June 1878)|June 1878 crisis]] in Groot-Atjeh, showed both what the offensive could achieve when properly applied and what the passive *stelsel* had cost. It closes Borel's narrative. The book itself was finished at 's-Gravenhage on [[27 August]] [[1878]], twelve days later.
