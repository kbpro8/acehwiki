The **capture of Mandarsah Poetih** on [[28 January]] [[28 January]] [[1875]] was a Dutch operation of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] in which the troops of [[J. L. J. H. Pel|Kolonel PEL]] took the "Mandarsah Poetih" (the "white bedehuis," a small stone mosque already partially ruined by artillery fire) to the south of [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]]. The building was then put in a state of defence by [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede-Luitenant C. J. Snijders]] of the [[Bataillon Mineurs en Sappeurs|Compagnie Mineurs en Sappeurs]] as part of [[J. L. J. H. Pel|PEL]]'s southward extension of the Dutch perimeter in [[January 1875|January]] [[1875]]. The position continued to be harassed by Atjeh fire from the fortified kampongs of Gitjiel Olejlo, [[Lamara Olejlo|Lamara Olejlo]], and Lohong, leading directly to the [[Lamara Olejlo fortification (15 February 1875)|Lamara Olejlo fortification of 15 February]] [[1875]].

## Context

After the [[Missigit Longbatta operation (31 December 1874 – 13 January 1875)|Missigit Longbatta operation]] and the [[Capture of Pungej Blang Tjoet (23 January 1875)|capture of Pungej Blang Tjoet]] on [[23 January]] [[23 January]] [[1875]], [[J. L. J. H. Pel|PEL]] continued his southward operations. On [[28 January]] [[28 January]] the troops took the Mandarsah Poetih, the "white bedehuis," a small stone building already partially ruined by artillery fire.

## Fortification

[[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] "voerde overeenkomstige werkzaamheden uit" — that is, he put the captured building in a state of defence, as he had done at [[Pungej Blang Tjoet|Pungej Blang Tjoet]] five days earlier. The small size of the building and its partial ruin by artillery fire made it a less ambitious work than the [[Missigit Longbatta|Missigit Longbatta]] redoubt, but its position served as part of the southern line of posts covering the Dutch perimeter.

## Subsequent Operations

The Mandarsah Poetih continued to be harassed by Atjeh fire from the fortified kampongs of Gitjiel Olejlo, [[Lamara Olejlo|Lamara Olejlo]], and Lohong, leading to the reconnaissance of [[14 February|14]]/[[15 February]] [[15 February]] [[1875]] and the [[Lamara Olejlo fortification (15 February 1875)|Lamara Olejlo fortification of 15 February]].
