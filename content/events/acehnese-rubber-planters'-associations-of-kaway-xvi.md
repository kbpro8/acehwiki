**Acehnese rubber-planters' associations of Kaway XVI** (c. 1930s) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. On the initiative of the zelfbestuurder Teukoe Tjhi' Mohamad Ali Akbar, rubber-planters' associations were founded in the landschap Kaway XVI (Aceh's Westkust). 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to c. 1930s.

## Account

On the initiative of the [[zelfbestuurder]] [[Teukoe Tjhi' Mohamad Ali Akbar]], rubber-planters' associations were founded in the [[landschap]] [[Kaway XVI]] (Aceh's [[Westkust]]). He kept them under his leadership and supervision. Result: [[Kaway XVI]] is now the most important rubber producer of the entire [[afdeeling]] [[Westkust van Atjeh]]. A favorable exception to the general Acehnese inability to cooperate.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
