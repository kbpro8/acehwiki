The **onderwerping van Peusangan en Pasé** in [[1899]] was the campaign of subjection in the north-coast territories of [[Peusangan]] and [[Pase|Pasé]], in which — according to the [[1930]] jubilee account of the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* — the corps again made itself felt, and which stood under the supreme command of [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]], by then promoted major-general.

## Sequence

The operation followed the [[Pidië Expedition]] of [[1898]], which had been conducted under the supreme command of the then colonel of infantry [[J. B. van Heutsz]] and whose success the jubilee number attributed in important part to the Maréchaussée. In the following year the corps "had zich ook doen gelden bij de onderwerping van Peusangan en Pasé in 1899" — had also made itself felt at the subjection of Peusangan and Pasé in 1899. The same expedition, the account records, likewise stood under the supreme command of Van Heutsz, since promoted to major-general.

## Context in the corps's development

The year [[1899]] was also that in which the establishment of the corps was substantially enlarged: by *Gouvernementsbesluit* of [[3 March]] [[1899]] its strength was brought to 20 officers and 1,212 men, divided over five *[[Divisie (Maréchaussée)|divisies]]* of three *afdeelingen*, subdivided into four *[[Brigade (Maréchaussée)|brigades]]* each. The jubilee number treated the period [[1898]]–[[1906]] as the era in which the history of the Atjehnese Guard was marked by "bijzonder-opvallende krijgsdaden" — particularly striking feats of arms — reflected in the royal distinctions conferred during those years.
