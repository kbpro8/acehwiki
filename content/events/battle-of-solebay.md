**Battle of Solebay** was a naval engagement fought on [[7 June]] [[1672]] between the fleets of the [[Dutch Republic]] and the allied forces of [[England]] and [[France]] during the [[Third Anglo‑Dutch War]]. The Dutch fleet, commanded by [[Michiel de Ruyter]], achieved a tactical victory despite being outnumbered.

## Course of the battle
The battle took place off the coast of [[Solebay]] (now [[Suffolk]], England). Admiral de Ruyter’s fleet repelled the Anglo‑French attack, inflicting heavy losses on the enemy. The engagement demonstrated the resilience of the Dutch navy after the earlier defeat at the Thames raid.

## Aftermath and Aceh connection
The victory at Solebay allowed the Dutch Republic to retain naval resources for its overseas empire, including the VOC’s campaigns against the Sultanate of [[Aceh]]. The continued dominance of the Dutch fleet in European waters ensured that the VOC could sustain its military presence in the East Indies throughout the late seventeenth century.
