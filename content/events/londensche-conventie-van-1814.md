**Londensche Conventie van 1814** (1814) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. England returned most of the Dutch colonies after Napoleon's fall, but kept some parts. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1814]].

## Account

[[Engeland (England)|England]] returned most of the Dutch colonies after Napoleon's fall, but kept some parts. Sumatra was largely returned to Dutch administration except some regions on the [[Westkust]] and Atjeh, which still existed as an independent native state.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
