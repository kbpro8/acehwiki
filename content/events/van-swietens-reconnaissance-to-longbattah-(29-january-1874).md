**Van Swieten's reconnaissance to Longbattah** of [[29 January]] [[1874]] was the advance in considerable force undertaken by the opperbevelhebber of the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)|second Atjeh expedition]], five days after the [[Capture of the Kraton (24 January 1874)|fall of the Kraton]], in the direction of the Atjehnese inland stronghold of [[Longbatta|Longbattah]], at which his troops were *aangetast* (attacked) by a numerous enemy and obliged to withdraw. Quoted by [[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] (1886) from the course of the campaign, the reconnaissance was one of the experiences that belied the optimism of van Swieten's first proclamations: the opperbevelhebber who had declared on [[24 January]] in his [[Van Swieten's dagorder 24 January 1874|day order]] that the enemy was "wel overwonnen, doch nog niet onderworpen" ("conquered indeed, but not yet subdued"), and who proclaimed five days later, on [[29 January]], saw himself compelled to the retreat by a numerous enemy — and nevertheless, a fortnight afterwards ([[12 February]] [[1874]]), he passed over to the [[Annexation proclamation of Atjeh (1874)|annexation of the whole realm]], selling "the skin of the bear before the bear was shot".
