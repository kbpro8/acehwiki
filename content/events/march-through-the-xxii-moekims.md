**March through the XXII Moekims** was a major interior military campaign conducted in February [[1876]] during [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]], wherein columns of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] under General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]] invaded the agricultural territory of the [[XXII Moekims]] to crush forces commanded by [[Panglima Polem]].

## Narrative of the February 1876 Offensive

On morning of [[13 February]] [[1876]], General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]] deployed a combined infantry force consisting of the [[3rd Infantry Battalion]], [[12th Infantry Battalion]], [[13th Infantry Battalion]], and the [[Barissans of Bangkallan]]. Departing from fortified line around [[Sinangri]] and [[Pekan Badak]], Dutch columns advanced along the Aceh River into the heart of the [[XXII Moekims]].

Acehnese defenders loyal to [[Panglima Polem]] resisted from a succession of timber bentengs and village fortifications. Dutch artillery pulverized the defenses, while infantry battalions burned granaries and dismantled irrigation structures across the sawahs ([[Sawah Cultivation and Supply in Aceh]]).

## Tactical Limits and Logistical Strain

Despite achieving tactical victories on the battlefield, General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]]'s advance was slowed by severe logistical bottlenecks. As analyzed in *Atjeh gedurende de tweede expeditie en later* ([[1876]]), the heavy burden of transporting supplies via the [[Transport and Coolie System in Aceh]] prevented Dutch cavalry and infantry from executing rapid pursuit of [[Panglima Polem]]'s retreating guerrillas.
