**Retreat from the Missigit** occurred on the late afternoon of [[10 April]] [[1873]], concluding the [[First Conquest of the Great Missigit]].

## The Withdrawal
After hours of intense fighting inside and around the walls of [[Atjeh's Groote Missigit]], it became evident that the Dutch position was untenable against the relentless Acehnese counter-attacks. Around 5:30 PM, General [[J.H.R. Köhler]] received a report from Lieutenant [[C.J. Marinkelle]] detailing the precarious situation. Köhler subsequently ordered the withdrawal. The retreat was executed in good order by the [[9e Bataljon Infanterie]] and the [[Marine-landingsdivisie]]. The troops marched out of the western opening of the mosque and relocated to a bivouac near [[Village Merdoehati]]. The Acehnese immediately reoccupied the mosque with victorious chants.
