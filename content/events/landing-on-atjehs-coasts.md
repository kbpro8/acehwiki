**Landing on Atjeh's Coasts** was the initial amphibious operation of the [[First Aceh Expedition]] on [[8 April]] [[1873]], marking the formal start of the Aceh War.

## The Landing
On the morning of [[8 April]] [[1873]], troops from the [[12e Bataljon Infanterie]] and the [[Marine-landingsdivisie]] disembarked from their sloops onto the Acehnese shores. They were immediately met by Acehnese resistance from behind the coastal dunes. The landing forces successfully established a beachhead, setting up a strand bivouac that would serve as the staging ground for the subsequent interior push towards [[Atjeh's Groote Missigit]] and the [[Kraton of Atjeh]].
