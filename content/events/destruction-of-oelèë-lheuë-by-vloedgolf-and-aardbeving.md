**Destruction of Oelèë Lheuë by vloedgolf and aardbeving** (August 1936) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. In August 1936, a vloedgolf (tidal wave) and aardbeving (earthquake) destroyed the once-lively port town of Oelèë Lheuë, formerly the well-known badplaats (bathing resort) for old Koetaradja residents. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[August]] [[1936]].

## Account

In [[August]] [[1936]], a [[vloedgolf]] (tidal wave) and [[aardbeving]] (earthquake) destroyed the once-lively port town of [[Oelèë Lheuë]], formerly the well-known badplaats (bathing resort) for old [[Koetaradja]] residents. The town has been reduced to a 'dead Zuiderzeeplaatsje' atmosphere. Recently a platform has been built for moonlit '[[makan angin]]'.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
