The **Skirmish of April 16, 1874** was a sharp engagement fought near Kampong Lampu-Uk southeast of the [[Kraton of Aceh]] shortly before the dissolution of the [[Second Aceh Expedition]].

## Detailed Narrative

At 7:00 AM on [[16 April]] [[1874]], Captain Bardok led a 100-man patrol along the eastern face of the Kraton. Upon reaching dense jungle south of Kampong Lampu-Uk, the column came under heavy concealed fire, sustaining four wounded.

Hearing heavy firing, Major Romswinckel marched out from the Kraton with 150 men and mountain artillery. Believing Lieutenant-Colonel [[Jacobus Pel]] intended to destroy the position, Romswinckel combined forces with Lieutenant Scheltus and ordered a frontal storming charge against concealed Acehnese earthworks.

The assault was repulsed by intense musket fire. A second storming attempt also failed. Upon realizing that Romswinckel had engaged without orders, Colonel Pel ordered an immediate withdrawal. The engagement cost Dutch forces 9 dead and 86 wounded (including 10 officers), underscoring the strength of Acehnese field fortifications.
