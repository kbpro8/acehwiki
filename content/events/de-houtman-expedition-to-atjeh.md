**De Houtman expedition to Atjeh** (1599) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Cornelis and Frederik de Houtman made their discovery voyage to the East. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1599]].

## Account

Cornelis and [[Frederik de Houtman]] made their discovery voyage to the East. The reception they got in Atjeh was very disappointing ([[1599]]) - including manslaughter (death of one) and imprisonment. Marked the start of Dutch-Acehnese relations on a hostile note.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
