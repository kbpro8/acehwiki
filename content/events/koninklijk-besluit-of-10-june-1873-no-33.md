**Koninklijk Besluit of 10 June 1873 No. 33** (Royal Decree of 10 June 1873, No. 33) was the Dutch royal decree by which four engineer officers of the metropolitan army — the youngest of them [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede-Luitenant C. J. Snijders]] — were selected for a two-year detachment to the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger]] in preparation for the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)|second Aceh expedition]], after the failure of the [[First Aceh Expedition (1873)|first Aceh expedition]] under [[J. H. R. Köhler|Generaal-Majoor Köhler]] in [[April 1873|April]] [[1873]]. The decree is the legal basis of Snijders' Atjeh service of [[1874]]–[[1875]] and thus of the actions for which he was decorated with the [[Militaire Willemsorde|Ridderkruis der Militaire Willemsorde]] by [[Koninklijk Besluit of 12 November 1875 No. 18|KB of 12 November 1875 No. 18]].

## The Call for Volunteers

The call for volunteer officers from the metropolitan army, issued by the Dutch government in [[May 1873|May]] [[1873]] after the failure of the [[First Aceh Expedition (1873)|first Aceh expedition]], offered 40 officers — including 4 of the engineers — the opportunity of a two-year detachment to the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger]]. The call was posted at the [[Sociëteit Sic Semper|Sociëteit Sic Semper]] at [[Utrecht|Utrecht]], where [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] heard of it from an older comrade and immediately applied. Snijders' father, initially displeased that his minor son had not consulted him, consented on condition that Snijders not request assignment to a combat column in [[Acheh|Atjeh]] — a promise Snijders kept, although [[J. Loudon|Governor-General Loudon]] later tried to put such a request into his mouth.

## The Decree

The decree of [[10 June]] [[10 June]] [[1873]] No. 33 selected the four engineer officers from those proposed. Snijders, though the youngest of those proposed, was selected owing to the dropping out of some older colleagues. By the same decree, Snijders and his fellow selectee [[J. W. N. Cramer|J. W. N. Cramer]] were instructed to the Mineurskamp at [[Zeist|Zeist]] for some days' instruction in the [[J. H. Kromhout|Kromhout]] optical-telegraph system before departure. Snijders embarked on the steamship *Conrad* of the [[Maatschappij "Nederland"|Maatschappij "Nederland"]] from [[Nieuwediep|Nieuwediep]] on [[10 July]] [[10 July]] [[1873]].

## Significance

The decree of [[10 June]] [[10 June]] [[1873]] No. 33 is the legal foundation of Snijders' Atjeh service. Without it, the [[Benting Kota Alam operation (13 November 1874)|Benting Kota Alam operation of 13 November]] [[1874]], the [[Missigit Longbatta operation (31 December 1874 – 13 January 1875)|Missigit Longbatta operation]], the [[Lamara Olejlo fortification (15 February 1875)|Lamara Olejlo fortification]], and the [[Snijders' 1920 visit to Atjeh|1920 wreath-laying on Pel's grave]] would not have occurred.
