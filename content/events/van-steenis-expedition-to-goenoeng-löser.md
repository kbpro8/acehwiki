**Van Steenis expedition to Goenoeng Löser** (1936-1937) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. ## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1936]]-[[1937]].

## Account

In [[1936]]-[[1937]], the [[Goenoeng Löser]] ([[Goenoeng Löser|Gunung Leuser]]) was climbed and investigated by a scientific expedition under the leadership of [[Dr. C. G. G. J. van Steenis]], a botanist at the Herbarium in [[Buitenzorg]].

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
