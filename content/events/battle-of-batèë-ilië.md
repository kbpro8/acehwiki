**Battle of Batèë Ilië** (c. 1898-1904 (not specified in pages read)) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Famous battle directed by General Van Heutsz from the Batèë Ilië hill (white stone bench marks the spot). 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to c. [[1898]]-[[1904]] (not specified in pages [[Read (consul-generaal at Singapore)|read]]).

## Account

Famous battle directed by [[Generaal J. B. van Heutsz|General Van Heutsz]] from the [[Batèë Ilië]] hill (white stone bench [[Marks|marks]] the spot). The battle took place on the plain of [[Samalanga]], visible from the hill. Immortalized in the painting by [[Hoynck van Papendrecht]] depicting [[Generaal J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] and his staff (with several well-known figures) following the changes of the fierce battle. A 'Van Heutszbank' monument was erected on the hill.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
