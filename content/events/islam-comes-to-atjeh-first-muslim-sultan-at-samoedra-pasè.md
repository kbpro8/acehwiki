**Islam comes to Atjeh / first Muslim sultan at Samoedra-Pasè** (c. 1200) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. The Islam (founded in Arabia) began around 1200 to win adherents in the archipelago, first in Atjeh. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to c. [[1200]].

## Account

The [[Islam]] (founded in [[Hunoynen|Arabia]]) began around [[1200]] to win adherents in [[East Indian Archipelago|the archipelago]], first in Atjeh. The first kingdom where a Mohammedan prince is mentioned is [[Samoedra-Pasè]]. The attraction of this religion proved so powerful that within the first three centuries afterwards virtually all of Atjeh, at least the coastal area, was won over and remained so to the present day - in contrast to Hinduism which had to yield completely. The transition was not violent and was complete by [[1500]].

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
