**Expedition to Edi (1890)** was a Dutch marine operation aimed at securing the eastern coastal town of [[Edi (Atjeh)|Edi]] in [[Atjeh]]. Conducted in [[1890]], the force was part of the ongoing Dutch effort to pacify the sultanate after the earlier campaigns of the 1870s.

The marines built a small fortification at Edi and used the base to launch patrols against rebel groups operating along the [[Krueng Seumantok]] river system.

## Outcome
The expedition succeeded in establishing a Dutch foothold on the east coast, but resistance continued in the interior for several more years.
