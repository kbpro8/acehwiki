The **Relief and lifting of Anagaloeng, Lambarih and Senelop** on [[18 April]] [[1896]] ended the eighteen-day isolation of the Dutch posts in the [[XXII Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]] following the defection of [[Teukoe Oemar]], and completed the abandonment of the outpost system built up since [[1893]].

## The siege of Anagaloeng

Hooijer described the patrol of [[Van Blokland|Kapitein Blokland]] on [[7 March]] [[1896]] as the prelude to the drama that followed three weeks later, when the post of [[Anagaloeng]] — which was to have been converted into a permanently occupied fixed point — was invested by a population that had suddenly turned into enemies. *Achttien dagen lang bleef de bezetting daar aan zich zelve overgelaten, gelukkig ruimschoots voorzien van vivres en munitie* — for eighteen days the garrison was left to itself, fortunately amply supplied with *[[Vivres|vivres]]* and ammunition — and only then did a column appear which, after fighting several actions, relieved it.

## The lifting

*Zonder veel verliezen kon de post den 18 April worden opgeheven en verbrand* — without heavy losses the post could be lifted and burnt on 18 April. The same columns relieved and lifted [[Senelop]] and [[Lam Barih|Lambarih]], after the surrounding population had ranged itself on the side of [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Oemar]], who had gone over to the *[[Moslemin]]*, and after the *[[Blokhuis|blokhuizen]]* in the neighbourhood had been burnt by the Dutch.

## Hooijer's judgment

The description of the furious struggle then fought fell, Hooijer wrote, outside the framework of a narrative written to explain plates. *Met deze posten was tegelijkertijd het werk van twee jaren, dat zooveel goeds scheen te beloven, al was het ook opgetrokken op ondeugdelijken grondslag, in vlammen opgegaan* — with these posts the work of two years, which had seemed to promise so much good although raised on an unsound foundation, had at the same time gone up in flames.
