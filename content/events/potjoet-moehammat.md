The **Potjoet Moehammat** (modern Atjehnese *Putjut Muhammad* or *Hikayat Putjut Meukat*) is an Atjehnese-language historical epic, composed — in [[J. Kreemer|Kreemer]]'s dating — "omstreeks het midden der 18e eeuw" (around the middle of the eighteenth century), which is cited in [[J. Kreemer|Kreemer]]'s [[Kreemer's De Karbouw ([[1956]])|*De Karbouw* ([[1956]])]] (p. 146) — drawing on [[Kreemer's Atjeh ([[1922]]-23)|his *Atjeh*]] (vol. I, p. 307) — as the earliest literary attestation of the [[koeta djaq|*koeta djaq*]] "wandelende fort" (walking fort), the mobile buffalo-hide siege-shelter used in the inter-tribal wars of the [[Acheh|Atjeh]] cultural sphere.

## The epic and the koeta djaq
The *Potjoet Moehammat* passage in *De Karbouw* (p. 146) reads:

> *"Een in de Archipel veel toegepast belegeringsmidel in de onderlinge oorlogen was het z.g. 'wandelende fort', in Atjeh [[koeta djaq|koeta djaq]] genoemd. De belegeraars traden daar in troepjes vooruit, verscholen achter omgebogen lappen buffelhuid, die dus als grote schilden voor enige personen tegelijk dienst deden. Van zulke wandelende fortjes is reeds sprake in het bekende, omstreeks het midden der 18e eeuw opgestelde, Atjehse epos Potjoet Moehammat."*
The passage fixes three facts about the *Potjoet Moehammat* as it bears on the encyclopedia of Atjeh buffalo-history. First, the epic was composed around the middle of the eighteenth century — that is, in the period between the [[Atjeh bloeitijdperk ([[1607]]-[[1636]])|*bloeitijdperk* of Iskandar Muda]] ([[1607]]-[[1636]]) and the [[Aceh War|Dutch conquest of the late nineteenth century]]. Second, the epic is "bekende" (well-known) — that is, it was a recognised part of the Atjehnese literary-historical canon at the time of Kreemer's writing. Third, the epic mentions the [[koeta djaq|*koeta djaq*]] — the mobile buffalo-hide siege-shelter — which thus attests the device's use as a military technology from at least the mid-eighteenth century.

## Significance
The *Potjoet Moehammat* is significant for the encyclopedia of [[Acheh]] history as a mid-eighteenth-century Atjehnese-language historical epic that attests the use of the [[koeta djaq|*koeta djaq*]] buffalo-hide siege-shelter as a military technology of the Atjehnese state from at least that period. The epic is one of the principal Atjehnese historical-literary texts, and its mention of the *koeta djaq* is significant as a literary attestation of the device's place in the Atjehnese military-technological imaginary of the mid-eighteenth century — that is, in the period of Atjehnese history between the [[Atjeh bloeitijdperk ([[1607]]-[[1636]])|*bloeitijdperk* of Iskandar Muda]] and the [[Aceh War|Dutch conquest of the late nineteenth century]].
