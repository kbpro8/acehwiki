**Krijgverrichtingen in Atjeh (1874–1875)** — literally "military operations in Aceh (1874–1875)" — refers to the continuation of the Dutch offensive against the [[Acheh|Atjeh]] Sultanate in the period from mid-August [[1874]] to February [[1875]], a concentrated phase of intense fighting that earned multiple members of the [[Korps Mariniers (Netherlands)|Netherlands Marine Corps]] the [[Ridder Militaire Willemsorde]] (Knight of the Military Order of William) and other honourable mentions. The actions of the calendar year [[1875]] — the subject of a separate decoration list — were viewed at the time as a distinct phase within this larger campaign; the awards for both phases were issued together under the same *Koninklijke onderscheiding* (Royal Distinction), decree No. 39 of [[26 October]] [[1876]].

## Operational Highlights

The operations involved coordinated assaults on fortified villages, riverine engagements, and the suppression of Acehnese guerrilla activity. The source — the award roll at page 185 of the history of the Netherlands Marine Corps — notes that the awards were granted for "*krijgverrichtingen in Atjeh in het bijzonder gedurende het tijdvak medio Aug. 1874 tot Feb. 1875*" ("military operations in Aceh, in particular during the period mid-August 1874 to February 1875"), indicating a concentrated period of intense fighting that immediately followed the major Dutch setback of the first [[Aceh expedition]].

The 1874-1875 campaigns solidified Dutch control over key coastal districts and demonstrated the effectiveness of the [[Korps Mariniers (Netherlands)|Netherlands Marine Corps]] in combined land-sea operations. The extensive list of honours illustrates the personal bravery recognised by the Dutch crown.

## Decorated Officers

The award roll records the following officers of the Netherlands Marine Corps as decorated for their conduct in the 1874-1875 campaign:

- **[[J. J. Korndörffer]]** — Sergeant-Major, awarded the 4th class of the Military Order of William for his actions in the period; again cited for bravery in the calendar year 1875.
- **[[W. F. Clifford Koek van Breugel]]** — Lieutenant, likewise honoured; repeated honourable mention in 1875.
- **[[J. L. Cadet]]** — Lieutenant, recipient of the order; likewise cited in 1875.
- **[[G. P. C. Bijsterveld]]** — Captain-Lieutenant, also decorated; again recognised in 1875.
- **[[H. Jansma]]** — Captain-Lieutenant, received the order; repeated citation in 1875.
- **[[P. A. Notebos]]** — posthumously mentioned for his conduct; his award was granted after his death.

The 1875 phase of the campaign — recognised in the same royal decree — repeats the citations for the first five officers, indicating that they were again mentioned for their conduct in the calendar year 1875. The 1875 operations helped to consolidate Dutch footholds established in the previous year and demonstrated the persistence of Dutch military pressure on the Acehnese resistance.

## Significance

The 1874-1875 operations belong to the immediate aftermath of the [[War against the Aceh Sultanate (1873–1874)|first Aceh expedition of 1873-1874]] and form the connective tissue between that opening campaign and the later Dutch operations of the late 1870s, including the [[Operation Simpang Olim (1878)|Simpang Olim operation of 1878]]. The high number of Marine Corps decorations — six officers in a single award roll — reflects both the intensity of the fighting and the central role of the Marine Corps in the early years of the [[Aceh War]].
