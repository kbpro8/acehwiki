**Krijgverrichtingen in Atjeh (1874–1875)** were a continuation of the Dutch offensive against the [[Atjeh]] Sultanate, encompassing a series of battles and punitive expeditions that earned multiple members of the [[Korps Mariniers (Netherlands)]] the [[Ridder Militaire Willemsorde]] and honourable mentions. The award roll (page 185) records the following officers:

* **J. J. Korndörffer** – Sergeant‑Major, awarded the 4th class of the Military Order of William for actions in the period.  
* **W. F. Clifford Koek van Breugel** – Lieutenant, likewise honoured.  
* **J. L. Cadet** – Lieutenant, recipient of the order.  
* **G. P. C. Bijsterveld** – Captain‑Lieutenant, also decorated.  
* **H. Jansma** – Captain‑Lieutenant, received the order.  
* **P. A. Notebos** – posthumously mentioned for his conduct; his award was granted after his death.

These recognitions were issued under the **Koninklijke onderscheiding** (Royal Distinction) decree dated [[26 October]] [[1876]] (No. 39), reflecting the high value placed on the campaign’s outcomes.

## Operational Highlights

The operations involved coordinated assaults on fortified villages, riverine engagements, and the suppression of Acehnese guerrilla activity. The source notes that the awards were granted for “krijgverrichtingen in Atjeh in het bijzonder gedurende het tijdvak medio Aug. 1874 tot Feb. 1875,” indicating a concentrated period of intense fighting.

## Significance

The 1874‑1875 campaigns solidified Dutch control over key coastal districts and demonstrated the effectiveness of the [[Korps Mariniers (Netherlands)]] in combined land‑sea operations. The extensive list of honours illustrates the personal bravery recognized by the Dutch crown.
