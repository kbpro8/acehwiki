**Pepper Cultivation in Atjeh** was the dominant export agriculture on east and west coast of [[Atjeh]], with large vicissitudes due to war, market price, rice scarcity and disease, forming lifeline of [[Uleebalang|oeléëbalang]] economies.

## Narrative

Fifty years before [[1923]], coast had abundant pepper. [[J. A. Kruyt]] in [[1877]] described garden 5,000 trees yielding 5 *kojang* = 1,100 *picol*, maintained by 3-4 men receiving half produce at local price. Collection of pepper gardens together called *seuneubò* (Kruyt wrote "seleboe").

Export east coast annually 5,000-6,000 *kojang* at [[Penang]] 200-250 *pilaardollars* per *kojang* in [[August]] [[1873]], earlier 90.

In *De buitenbezitingen 1904-'14* export from Atjeh [[1913]] just over 3 million kg, less than tenth [[1873]]. Compare [[Lampoeng]] [[1913]] 12.5 million kg (200,000 *picol*). In [[1893]] no pepper significant per [[Julius Jacobs]].

Administration figures: [[1900]] export 5,365,091 kg, [[1899]] [[Pidie]] and [[Edi]] nearly half more; [[1911]] still 4,514,461 kg then big collapse. In west coast not detailed but same origin migrants from [[Pidie]], [[Samalangan]], [[Peusangan]], [[Pasei]] temporarily settling e.g., [[Peureula]].

System: group 4-6 men under leader requested ground from [[Uleebalang|oeléëbalang]], who had *oelèëbalangs tjoet*. Needed little capital *pangkai* from oeléëbalang to live 2-3 years until garden yielded. Debt earlier ~25 gulden per planter, now 100 gulden. For use ground paid *hacil radja* lordly right to oeléëbalang. Adat brought payments *wasé pangkai*, *wasé oeléëbalang*, *wasé peutoeha* and more.

Oeléëbalang privileged buyer. Manipulation possible: planter let capitalist fall into trap when market prices fell sharply and provider little desire as privileged buyer act. Then sold himself kept money or disappeared to home region leaving garden.

Netherlands bestuur after pacification regulated rights could be determined and at export via customs collected, while "landscape" established as self-government area *wasé oelèëbalang* ground right belonged landscape treasury. Broersma criticized healer weakened patient attacking life source oelèëbalang. Had no pleasure more, then no pepper.

[[1915]] new regulation customs no more interference. *Hacil tanah* divided 50% landscape 50% *pangkaigiver* and *peutoeha* who from leader pepper planters became head municipality *kepala kampong*. Also not encouraging capital.

Government came with credit institution afdeelingsbank [[Langsa]], [[Lho Seumawe]], [[Koeta Radja]] to help pepper become free volksculture. They tested credit applications to controller and to landscape head oeléëbalang. Head found spacious credit good. But banks had no collateral and suffered loss, forced stay away pepper.

Practical burdens now: *hacil* 1 gulden per *picol*; capital provider mostly oeléëbalang or oelèëbalang-tjoet f 2.50 per *picol* of which f 0.50 to *peutoeha*; freight. Risk third year first repayment.

In [[1918]] disease appeared. Fairly fleeting investigation Department Landbouw and landbouwleeraar led not discovery; landscape head held disease *djamoer oepas*. Poor maintenance in gardens probably influenced.

In [[1919]] rice scarcity each daily only one *batok* (=3/4 *katti*) rice insufficient for Atjeher eating twice rice with salt, *lombok* vegetable, no meat fish daily, accustomed consuming huge quantities. Pepper planters departed to [[Pidie]], [[Pasei]], [[Peusangan]] abundant padi.

Third cause market listlessness [[1920]] prices fell to 3-4 gulden *picol* max 7. Before war Europe 10 gulden just enough per head assurance, now 13-14 needed. In [[1923]] price reached market, here there pepper planter returns. With stable market undoubtedly pepper cultivation Atjeh revives and not much effort long duration to give withering gardens fresh look unless disease very malignant.

Broersma noted [[Langsa]] north many pepper gardens established by Atjehers from [[Pidie]], [[Samalanga]], [[Pasei]] returned when failed due disease partly maybe receding, and market drop. Heads provided principal capital *pangkai* with interest and landscape treasury income. After failure tax collection decreased even backlog irrecoverable.

In [[Peureula]], oeléëbalang encourages *sawah* ordering, demands ground processing for old pepper reuse, motor plows landscape 5 gulden half *baoe*, [[1924]] f 7.50 half *baoe*, some loss but indirect benefit.
