**Bombardment of the VI Mukim** was the naval bombardment of the [[VI Mukim]] described in the later operations of the [[Second Dutch Expedition to Aceh]].

## Course

It occurred in the period when the Dutch also established a lighthouse on [[Pulau Bras]], reconnoitred [[Maraksa]], and sought diplomatic agreements through naval station commanders. Van Swieten’s account questioned coercive methods when they undermined the hoped-for separation of peaceful inhabitants from the war party.

## Significance

The episode belongs to the continuing [[Aceh War]] and the contested consequences of the [[Capture of the Kraton of Aceh]].
