**The Machtsvertoon in IX Moekims (29 May 1896)** — the show-of-force in the district cluster of the [[IX Moekims]] — was a four-day Dutch demonstration of force conducted on [[29 May]] [[1896]] by a colonne under [[Luitenant-Kolonel J. B. van Heutsz|Lt.-Col. J. B. van Heutsz]] — Chef van den Staf [[Kapitein H. C. Kronouer|Kapt. H. C. Kronouer]]; adjudant [[1e Luit.-Adj. F. Kilian|1e Luit.-Adj. F. Kilian]] — in the immediate aftermath of the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day offensive]] and four days before the opening of the [[Razing of Lam-Asan (30 May – 2 June 1896)|razing of Lam-Asan]]. The colonne comprised the [[3e Bataljon Infanterie|3e Bataljon]] under [[Majoor G. A. Hansen|Maj. G. A. Hansen]] (15 officers, 440 men), the [[14e Bataljon Infanterie|14e Bataljon]] under [[Majoor G. P. M. van der Noordaa|Maj. G. P. M. van der Noordaa]] (16 officers, 414 men), half of the [[9e Bataljon Infanterie|9e Bataljon]] under [[Kapitein H. Ph. de Moulin|Kapt. H. Ph. de Moulin]] (6 officers, 249 men), a cavalry platoon under [[1e Luit. C. W. F. Happé|1e Luit. C. W. F. Happé]] (30 riders), the [[4e Bergbatterij]] under [[Kapitein A. Bangert|Kapt. A. Bangert]] (3 officers, 101 men, 6 *vuurmonden*), and engineers under [[1e Luit. A. S. Ruzette|1e Luit. A. S. Ruzette]] (30 men). Its objective: to destroy the *versterkingen* of [[Paja Krah]], [[Lam-Blé]], and [[Masdjid Behé]], the fortified market of [[Lam-Poe-Oe]], and to burn the houses of [[T. Gih]] and [[T. Nja Gedong]] at [[Lam-Tehen-Wer]].

## Background

By the last week of [[May]] [[1896]] the [[Luitenant-Generaal J. A. Vetter|Regeeringscommissaris]] had accomplished the principal military objective of the spring campaign: the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day offensive]] had broken [[Teuku Umar|Teungkoe Oemar]]'s *stelling* at [[Lam-Pisang]], Oemar's house had been [[Capture of Lam-Pisang and Oemar's House (24 May 1896)|captured and destroyed]], and his warriors had retreated behind the [[Kroeng-Raba]]. The strategic initiative was firmly in Dutch hands. Vetter now resolved to consolidate the political position in the *moekim*s adjacent to the area of operations — to demonstrate to the *hoeloebalang* and *oelama* of the [[IX Moekims]] that the Government was the stronger party and that cooperation with Oemar's resistance would be punished.

The [[IX Moekims]] — a cluster of nine *moekim* lying to the south-east of the [[Lampoeng]] / [[Lampisang]] region — had long been a centre of Achehnese resistance. Its *versterkingen* at [[Paja Krah]], [[Lam-Blé]], and [[Masdjid Behé]] — the latter built around the principal mosque of the district — formed a chain of fortified works that controlled the approaches to the interior. The fortified market of [[Lam-Poe-Oe]] was the commercial centre of the district and a known meeting-place of the resistance. The houses of [[T. Gih]] and [[T. Nja Gedong]] at [[Lam-Tehen-Wer]] — both prominent Achehnese figures — were symbols of the political leadership of the resistance in the IX Moekims.

## The Composition of the Colonne

The colonne for the *machtsvertoon* was placed under [[Luitenant-Kolonel J. B. van Heutsz|Lt.-Col. J. B. van Heutsz]] — recently distinguished as the commander of the Bergcolonne at the [[Slag bij Tjot Tjako (23 May 1896)|Slag bij Tjot Tjako]]. His Chef van den Staf was [[Kapitein H. C. Kronouer|Kapt. H. C. Kronouer]] of the General Staff; his adjudant was [[1e Luit.-Adj. F. Kilian|1e Luit.-Adj. F. Kilian]]. The composition was substantial: the [[3e Bataljon Infanterie|3e Bataljon]] under [[Majoor G. A. Hansen|Maj. G. A. Hansen]] (15 officers, 440 men); the [[14e Bataljon Infanterie|14e Bataljon]] under [[Majoor G. P. M. van der Noordaa|Maj. G. P. M. van der Noordaa]] (16 officers, 414 men); half of the [[9e Bataljon Infanterie|9e Bataljon]] under [[Kapitein H. Ph. de Moulin|Kapt. H. Ph. de Moulin]] (6 officers, 249 men); a cavalry platoon under [[1e Luit. C. W. F. Happé|1e Luit. C. W. F. Happé]] (30 riders); the [[4e Bergbatterij]] under [[Kapitein A. Bangert|Kapt. A. Bangert]] (3 officers, 101 men, 6 *vuurmonden* — i.e., guns); and engineers under [[1e Luit. A. S. Ruzette|1e Luit. A. S. Ruzette]] (30 men). The total force was approximately 1,260 men — a substantial column for a *machtsvertoon* in a single district.

## The Course of the Operation

The colonne advanced on [[29 May]] [[1896]] into the [[IX Moekims]]. The cavalry under [[1e Luit. C. W. F. Happé|Happé]] reconnoitred ahead; the infantry followed in march order; the artillery of the [[4e Bergbatterij]] under [[Kapitein A. Bangert|Bangert]] was brought up to shell any *versterking* that offered resistance. The *versterkingen* at [[Paja Krah]], [[Lam-Blé]], and [[Masdjid Behé]] were taken and destroyed — the latter a particularly delicate operation, given the religious sensitivity of the mosque around which the work had been built. The fortified *pasar* of [[Lam-Poe-Oe]] was occupied and its fortifications dismantled. The houses of [[T. Gih]] and [[T. Nja Gedong]] at [[Lam-Tehen-Wer]] were burned — a deliberate act of personal *tuchtiging* against two of the principal political figures of the resistance in the IX Moekims.

The operation was, in tactical terms, a success: the *versterkingen* were destroyed, the houses were burned, and the colonne returned to the consolidated line without serious loss. The *machtsvertoon* had the additional political effect of demonstrating to the *hoeloebalang* and *oelama* of the IX Moekims that the Government retained the capacity to reach into the district at will and to chastise those who cooperated with the resistance. The operation was conducted in parallel with the [[Machtsvertoon in III Moekims Daroe (29 May 1896)|machtsvertoon in the III Moekims Daroe]] under [[Luitenant-Kolonel C. J. Laceulle|Lt.-Col. C. J. Laceulle]] — a second colonne operating simultaneously in the adjacent district.

## Significance

The Machtsvertoon in IX Moekims on [[29 May]] [[1896]] was, in operational terms, a small affair — a four-day demonstration in a single district, with no major battle and no significant casualties. In strategic and political terms, however, it was an integral part of the consolidation phase that followed the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day offensive]]. The destruction of the *versterkingen* at [[Paja Krah]], [[Lam-Blé]], and [[Masdjid Behé]] degraded the Achehnese defensive infrastructure in the IX Moekims; the burning of the houses of [[T. Gih]] and [[T. Nja Gedong]] at [[Lam-Tehen-Wer]] struck at the political leadership of the resistance; and the demonstration of force — over 1,200 men, six guns, and 30 cavalry — confirmed to the *hoeloebalang* of the district that the Government was the stronger party. The *machtsvertoon* was, in this sense, a small operation with a large political purpose: the consolidation of the Dutch position in the immediate aftermath of the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day offensive]] and the preparation of the ground for the larger operations of June — the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|tuchtiging of Lam-Krak]] and the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|tuchtiging of the V Moekims Montassik]].
