**The T. Pura amok incident** of 22 May [[1950]] was an act of violence in which an Atjehse amokmaker (*Atjehse amokmaker*) seriously wounded two Dutch plantation officials of the Société Financière des Caoutchoucs — [[B. de Jong]] and [[C. A. Zandbergen]] — at [[T. Pura]] (Tjoeka Pura) on the East Coast of Sumatra.

## Context

The incident occurred in the volatile period following the transfer of sovereignty from the Netherlands to the Republic of the United States of Indonesia on 27 December [[1949]], and during the early months of the dissolution of the [[Negara Sumatera Timur]] (N.S.T.) and the formation of the unitary Republic of Indonesia. T. Pura was situated in a border area between the East Coast plantation zone and the territory of [[Atjeh]], where tensions between the plantation economy and the Atjehnese population were historically acute.

## Aftermath

The incident was publicly noted by [[Ir. H. Meijer]] of the Société Financière during a ceremony at the railway station at Besitang marking the opening of a new railway connection. The fact that the perpetrator was described specifically as "Atjehse" rather than merely as an Indonesian reflected the ongoing perception of the Atjehnese as a distinct and potentially threatening "other" in the consciousness of the East Coast European community.
