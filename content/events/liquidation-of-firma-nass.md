**Liquidation of firma Nass** (1932) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. In 1932 the well-known firma Nass, which had toko's in various places in Aceh, was liquidated. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1932]].

## Account

In [[1932]] the well-known [[Firma Nass|firma Nass]], which had toko's in various places in Aceh, was liquidated. The Hotel [[Firma Nass|Nass]] at [[Lho'Seumawé]] — well-known to old Aceh customers — disappeared. The former hotel became the logeergebouw of the [[N.K.P.M.]] (which has terrains at [[Pasè I]], 25 km from [[Lho'Seumawé]]).

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
