**Administrative redivision of Gajo/Alas regions** (1934) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Since 1934, the Gajo Loeös, Alaslanden and Serbödjadi are administratively no longer part of the Gajolanden. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1934]].

## Account

Since [[1934]], the [[Gajo Loeös]], [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]] and [[Serbödjadi]] are administratively no longer part of the [[Gajolanden (Gayo Lands)|Gajolanden]]. The first three became part of the [[afdeeling]] [[Oostkust van Atjeh]]; the [[Gajolanden (Gayo Lands)|Gajolanden]] fall under the afdeelingschef of Atjeh's [[Noordkust]]. Practical reason: the [[Assistent-Resident]] at [[Takengon]] had to drive ~750 km via [[Langsar|Langsa]], [[Medan]] and [[Kaban Djahé]] to visit these remote parts of his [[ressort]].

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
