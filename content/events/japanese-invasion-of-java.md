**Japanese invasion of Java** was a major military campaign launched by the Imperial Japanese Army in [[March]] [[1942]] that resulted in the rapid defeat of the *Koninklijk Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger* and the occupation of the island of [[Java]]. The invasion featured amphibious landings, air and sea superiority, and a numerical advantage of roughly 1 to 6‑8 over Dutch forces.

## Course of the Campaign
Japanese infantry employed mobile tactics, small highly‑motivated units, and infiltration of weak points in Dutch defenses. Dutch troops, including the *Marinebataljon* under *Overste Roelofsen* and *Lieutenant‑colonel J. G. M. Nass*, were caught off‑guard and suffered heavy losses.

## Impact on the Dutch East Indies
The fall of Java precipitated the collapse of Dutch colonial authority across the archipelago, including the Aceh region, and led to the eventual surrender of the *KNIL*.

## Significance
The invasion reshaped the geopolitical landscape of Southeast Asia, ending Dutch colonial rule and setting the stage for Indonesian independence.
