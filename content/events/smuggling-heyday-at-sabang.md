**Smuggling heyday at Sabang** (1930-1934) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. In the years 1930-1934, unheard-of sums were involved in smuggling at Sabang's free port. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1930]]-[[1934]].

## Account

In the years [[1930]]-[[1934]], unheard-of sums were involved in smuggling at [[Sabang]]'s free port. Now smuggling is no longer so profitable, so less prevalent. Effective suppression impossible without fast motorboats and a seaplane for the customs.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
