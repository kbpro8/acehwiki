**The Reconnaissance of Kaloet (23 August 1892)** was a Dutch reconnaissance operation against the Atjehnese fortification of [[Kaloet]] in the [[XXV Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]], undertaken on [[23 August]] [[1892]] by a *verkenningcolonne* (reconnaissance column) under Captain [[Jhr. Graafland]] of the *Militaire Willems-Orde*, with Lieutenant [[Brant]] of the *maréchaussée* as guide. The reconnaissance destroyed the Atjehnese fortifications in the [[Kroeng Tandjong]] and approached to within 50 metres of Kaloet's *vóórwal* (front wall) before withdrawing. It was the first Dutch operation against Kaloet, and it established the topographical and tactical knowledge that would inform the subsequent [[Battle of Kaloet (1 October 1892)|battle of Kaloet (1 October 1892)]] and the eventual [[Capture of Kaloet by Toekoe Oemar (August 1893)|capture of Kaloet by Toekoe Oemar (August 1893)]].

## The night-patrol of Brant

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] records that the [[Reconnaissance of Kaloet (23 August 1892)|reconnaissance of 23 August 1892]] was preceded by the night-patrol of Lieutenant [[Brant]] of the *maréchaussée* on the night of [[22 August|22-23 August]] [[1892]]. The patrol — a *detachement maréchaussées* — lay in ambush *tegenover Kaloet* (opposite Kaloet) in the dry bed of the [[Kroeng Kali]], with the intention of catching the Atjehnese garrison as they came in the morning. The Atjehnese, however, *versliepen zich* (overslept), and the patrol — having been *hevig beschoten* (heavily fired upon) but having *gestoord zich daarvan weinig* (been little disturbed by it) — *nestelde zich te 4 uur 's nachts in eene der versterkingen in de bedding van het riviertje de Kroeng Kali* — *nested at 4 a.m. in one of the fortifications in the bed of the river Kroeng Kali*. The patrol returned to [[Lamreng]] at 7 a.m. without having caught anyone.

## The reconnaissance column

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that, *directly na terugkomst te Lamreng van de patrouille Brunt* (immediately after the return to Lamreng of the Brunt patrol), a *verkenningcolonne* — consisting of a *gemengde compagnie* (mixed company, of European and native soldiers), *mijnen* (miners) and *maréchaussée*s — set out under Captain [[Jhr. Graafland]] of the *Militaire Willems-Orde*. Lieutenant [[Brant]] — *die reeds een half etmaal nachtelijke patrouilledienst achter den rug had* (who had already half a day of night-patrol duty behind him) — went along as guide. The reconnaissance column *trok de Kroeng Tandjong in* (entered the Kroeng Tandjong) — the river that ran eastward from Kaloet — and *de versterkingen in en nabij deze rivier werden geslecht* (the fortifications in and near this river were destroyed).

## The approach to Kaloet

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that the reconnaissance column, *kort vóór Kaloet* (shortly before Kaloet), *trok oostwaarts en déboncheerde in het front daarvan, misschien op 50 M. afstands van den nog onzichtbaren vóórwal* (turned eastward and debouched in its front, perhaps at 50 m distance from the still-invisible front wall). The terrain was *tamelijk open* (rather open) over a breadth of approximately 30 to 40 metres: *links had men struikgewas, rechts randjoe-aanplantingen, daarachter eenige rijen wolfskuilen, en dan een 5 M. breede comboeri-aanplanting, waarna de hoofdwal door struikgewas heen schemerde* — *on the left was shrubbery, on the right randjoe plantings, behind that several rows of wolf-pits, and then a 5-m-broad comboeri planting, behind which the main wall loomed through the shrubbery*.

## Significance

The Reconnaissance of Kaloet (23 August 1892) is significant in the documentary record of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] as the first Dutch operation against the Atjehnese fortification of [[Kaloet]] — the operation that established the topographical and tactical knowledge that would inform the subsequent [[Battle of Kaloet (1 October 1892)|battle of Kaloet (1 October 1892)]] and the eventual [[Capture of Kaloet by Toekoe Oemar (August 1893)|capture of Kaloet by Toekoe Oemar (August 1893)]]. The reconnaissance also established the pattern of night-patrol and day-reconnaissance that would characterise Dutch operations against Kaloet throughout the late *concentratie* period — and it produced the first detailed Dutch account of the Atjehnese defensive works around Kaloet.
