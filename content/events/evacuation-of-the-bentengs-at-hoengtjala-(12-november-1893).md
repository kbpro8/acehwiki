The **Evacuation of the bentengs at Hoengtjala** on [[12 November]] [[1893]] followed the resumption of [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]]'s advance in the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]] under cover of Dutch artillery, and was succeeded by the plundering and burning of the *kampong* (village) by the fighters of [[Toekoe Nja Banta]] and [[T. Nek van Marassa|Toekoe Neq]] against the Governor's orders.

## The advance

Hooijer recorded that on [[12 November]] [[1893]] [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]] continued his movements, supported by Dutch gunfire, *met dat gevolg, dat de bentengs te Hoengtjala, in het midden van het moerasland, door den vijand werden ontruimd*.

## The sack

Before that *kampong* [[Toekoe Nja Banta|Nja Bantah]] had twice been repulsed, and [[T. Nek van Marassa|Toekoe Neq]] had so often seen his plans thwarted from it, that the two chiefs — *tegen de bevelen van den Gouverneur in* — could not restrain themselves from giving their fighters a free hand and delivering *die voorraadschuur en veilige wijkplaats der sabilbenden* over to plunder and fire.
