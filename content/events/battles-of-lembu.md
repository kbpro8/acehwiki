The **Battles of Lembu** occurred on [[25 December]] and [[26 December]] [[1873]] when Dutch forces assaulted fortified Acehnese positions at [[Lembu]] southeast of [[Peunayong]].

## Detailed Narrative

On [[25 December]] [[1873]], Colonel [[G. Wiggers van Kerchem]] led a reconnaissance force toward a reported open grass plain near [[Peunayong]]. Upon reaching [[Lembu]], the column was ambushed by Acehnese forces entrenched behind a 5-meter-thick thorn barrier (*bamboe-doeri*).

When Colonel Wiggers was wounded, Captain G. van Daalen took command. Adjutant [[E.C.O. von Bredow]] and three soldiers forced a gap through the thorn hedges, planting the battalion colors at the base of the parapet under intense fire. Reinforced by Major-General [[G.M. Verspijck]], Dutch infantry stormed three redoutes, killing 54 Acehnese defenders at a cost of 6 killed and 78 wounded.

On [[26 December]] [[1873]], Colonel [[K. Schultze]] led a second column that located the grass plain, establishing positions for heavy siege mortar batteries facing the [[Mesjid Raya Aceh]].
