**The Temporary Occupation of Indrapoeri** — *tijdelijke bezetting van Indrapoeri op 28 October* [[1896]] — was the temporary Dutch occupation of the kampong of [[Indrapoeri]] in the [[XXII Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]], undertaken on [[28 October]] [[1896]] in conjunction with the *samenwerking met de bezetting van Tjot Mantjang tot inname van de sterkte, genaamd Kota Ba Teuē* (cooperation with the garrison of [[Tjot Mantjang]] for the capture of the fortification called [[Kota Ba Teuē]]). The occupation — a temporary extension of the Dutch forward position beyond the [[Blang Bintang]] camp — was authorised under the modified *postenstelsel* (system of posts) policy of the [[18 July|18 July 1896]] telegram of [[Jhr. C. H. A. van der Wijck|Governor-General Van der Wijck]], which permitted the temporary occupation of points beyond the *geconcentreerde linie* (concentrated line) on condition that *voortdurende aanvulling van troepen en dwangarbeiders steeds mogelijk zullen zijn* (continuous replenishment of troops and forced labourers would always be possible).

## The Strategic Context

By late October [[1896]], the Dutch, under [[J. W. Stemfoort|Stemfoort]]'s second tenure, had established the [[Occupation of Kroeng-Raba (Lok-Nga) (15 July 1896)|permanent occupation of Kroeng-Raba (Lok-Nga)]] on [[15 July]] [[1896]] and the [[Construction of Blang Bintang Camp (7 September 1896)|construction of the Blang Bintang camp]] on [[7 September]] [[1896]]. The [[XXII Moekims]] — the *sagi* (territorial confederation (sagi)) of [[Panglima Polim]] — remained, however, only partially under Dutch control. The kampong of [[Indrapoeri]] — one of the principal kampongs of the XXII Moekims, and the site of an important Acehnese *stelling* — was the object of the temporary occupation of [[28 October]] [[1896]]. The occupation was undertaken in conjunction with the [[Capture of Kota Ba Teuē (28 October 1896)|capture of the stelling Kota Ba Teuē]], a strong Acehnese fortification which controlled the approach to Indrapoeri and which had resisted earlier Dutch probes.

## The Cooperation with the Tjot Mantjang Garrison

The temporary occupation of Indrapoeri was undertaken *in samenwerking met de bezetting van Tjot Mantjang* — in cooperation with the garrison of Tjot Mantjang (Blang Bintang). The Blang Bintang garrison — which had been established on [[7 September]] [[1896]] and which had served as the base for the [[First Selimoen Expedition (6–11 September 1896)|first Selimoen expedition]] of [[6 September|6]]–[[11 September]] [[1896]] — provided the southern arm of the operation, while a colonne from the *geconcentreerde linie* (concentrated line) provided the northern arm. The two forces converged on the kampong of Indrapoeri and on the *stelling* (fortification (stelling)) Kota Ba Teuē, and the cooperation of the two forces was the key to the capture of the *stelling* (fortification (stelling)) on [[28 October]] [[1896]]. The capture of Kota Ba Teuē opened the way for the [[Trip to Ladoeng and Kroeng Raja (30–31 October 1896)|trip to Ladoeng and Kroeng Raja]] of [[30 October|30]]–[[31 October]] [[1896]] and the [[Second Selimoen Expedition (3–6 November 1896)|second Selimoen expedition]] of [[3 November|3]]–[[6 November]] [[1896]].

## The Temporary Nature of the Occupation

The occupation of Indrapoeri — like the occupation of Kroeng-Raba (Lok-Nga) and the construction of the Blang Bintang camp — was authorised as a *voorloopige* (provisional) occupation, under the modified *postenstelsel* (system of posts) policy of the [[18 July|18 July 1896]] telegram. The kampong was occupied by a Dutch garrison, but the occupation was not intended to be permanent — the Dutch sought only to deny the Acehnese the use of the kampong and its *stelling*, and to demonstrate the Dutch capacity for force projection into the XXII Moekims. The temporary nature of the occupation reflected the *geen postenstelsel* policy of the [[Notulen der conferentie van 18 Juni 1896|conference of 18 June 1896]], which had specified that *tot een postenstelsel, behalve dat van onze geconcentreerde stelling, niet mag worden overgegaan* — no system of posts, other than that of the concentrated line, was to be undertaken.

## Significance

The Temporary Occupation of Indrapoeri on [[28 October]] [[1896]] is significant in the documentary record of *ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel* as the third Dutch forward position beyond the *geconcentreerde linie* (concentrated line) in the second semester of [[1896]] — after the [[Occupation of Kroeng-Raba (Lok-Nga) (15 July 1896)|occupation of Kroeng-Raba (Lok-Nga)]] of [[15 July]] [[1896]] and the [[Construction of Blang Bintang Camp (7 September 1896)|construction of the Blang Bintang camp]] of [[7 September]] [[1896]]. The occupation — undertaken in conjunction with the [[Capture of Kota Ba Teuē (28 October 1896)|capture of the stelling Kota Ba Teuē]] — opened the way for the [[Trip to Ladoeng and Kroeng Raja (30–31 October 1896)|trip to Ladoeng and Kroeng Raja]] of [[30 October|30]]–[[31 October]] [[1896]] and the [[Second Selimoen Expedition (3–6 November 1896)|second Selimoen expedition]] of [[3 November|3]]–[[6 November]] [[1896]], and represented the slow, methodical extension of Dutch forward positions which characterised the *pacificatie* (pacification) of the second semester of [[1896]] under [[J. W. Stemfoort|Stemfoort]]'s second tenure.
