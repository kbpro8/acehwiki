The **Third Capture of Mesjid Raya** occurred on [[6 January]] [[1874]] during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]], resulting in the permanent capture of the [[Mesjid Raya Aceh]] by Dutch colonial forces.

## Detailed Narrative

At 5:00 AM on [[6 January]] [[1874]], the 2nd Brigade commanded by Colonel [[C. de Roy van Zuydewijn]] crossed the Aceh River to attack the [[Mesjid Raya Aceh]]. Siege mortar batteries at [[Peunayong]] opened fire at 7:00 AM, firing over 500 heavy artillery shells into the mosque defenses.

Advancing through dense jungle along the riverbank, the leading company of the 3rd Battalion encountered heavy musket and swivel-gun fire at 9:00 AM. Colonel De Roy van Zuydewijn deployed mountain guns at point-blank range (20 meters) and ordered a bayonet charge led by the 14th Battalion.

Dutch infantry stormed the outer parapets and entered the mosque enclosure at 10:30 AM. Sappers immediately fortified the open eastern wall with sandbags. The 3rd Battalion suffered 75% officer casualties during the advance. Dutch losses totaled 15 killed and 208 wounded, while Acehnese defenders left over 100 dead inside the complex.
