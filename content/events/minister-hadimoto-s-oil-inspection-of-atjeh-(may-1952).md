**Minister Sujono Hadimoto's oil inspection of Atjeh** was a ministerial visit that began on 16 May [[1952]], when the Indonesian Minister of Trade and Industry, Mr. [[Sujono Hadimoto]], departed from Medan for [[Atjeh|Atjeh]] to inspect the oil enterprises on Sumatra.

## Delegation

The minister was accompanied by:
- [[Arnold Mononutu]], Minister of Information;
- Ir. [[Darmawan Mangunkusumo]], senior official of the Ministry of Trade and Industry;
- [[Sunu Sumosastro]], senior official of the Ministry of Trade and Industry;
- [[Tengku Mohamad Hassan]], parliament member.

## Context

The visit reflected the central government's concern with the rehabilitation and utilisation of the oil resources of North Sumatra, particularly the oil fields at [[Langsa]] and other locations in the Atjeh region. The oil installations had been partially destroyed even before the Japanese invasion of [[1942]] and were found to be completely ruined when a B.P.M. survey party visited in February [[1950]]. The results of the ministerial inspection were not made public.
