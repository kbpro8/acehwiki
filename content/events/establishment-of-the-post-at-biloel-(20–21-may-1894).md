The **Establishment of the post at Biloel** took place in the night of [[20 May]] to [[21 May]] [[1894]] in the [[IX Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]], at the request of [[Toekoe Nja Mohammad]], head of the IX Moekims, who with [[Toekoe Hoessin Longbattah]] guided the Dutch column to the site.

## The operation

[[G. B. Hooijer]] recorded that [[Biloel]] *werd in den nacht van 20 op 21 Mei 1894, op verzoek van Toekoe Nja Mohammad, hoofd der IX Moekims, opgericht* — was erected in the night of 20 to 21 May 1894 at the request of Toekoe Nja Mohammad — and that he and [[Toekoe Hoessin Longbattah]], whose mother lived there, showed the Dutch column the way. The fortification was raised around the old *[[Messigit|Messigit]]*, whose roof had been burnt and renewed, and within whose white wall the officers' quarters were established; two field-pieces and a *[[Mitrailleur|mitrailleur]]* were placed in battery in the two bastions.

The garrison of [[Boekit Daroe]], west of [[Tjot Goeë]], was transferred to Biloel on [[20 May]] [[1894]], and the road along and over the bare hills of the [[IX Moekims]] was constructed on that occasion.
