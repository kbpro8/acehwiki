**The Murder of Toekoe Moeda Angkasa** on [[11 December]] [[11 December]] [[1876]] was the treacherous murder of the radja of [[Merbau|Merbau]], an ally of the Dutch in their conflict with the hostile coast-states of [[Simpang Olim|Simpang Olim]] and [[Tandjong Semantoh|Tandjong Semantoh]], by a handlangers of the radja of Simpang Olim. The murder was committed on the eve of the Dutch ultimatum to the radja of Simpang Olim and was followed on [[13 December]] [[13 December]] by a feast at [[Teping Sirin|Teping Sirin]] at which the radja of Simpang Olim invited his adherents and allies to plan an attack on the Dutch positions.

## Background

The Dutch government wished in this period to act vigorously against two states on the east coast that had remained hostile — [[Simpang Olim|Simpang Olim]] and [[Tandjong Semantoh|Tandjong Semantoh]]. A favourable opportunity presented itself to do damage to those states, because another prince in that region, [[Toekoe Moeda Angkasa|Toekoe Moeda Angkasa]], had been driven from his country, [[Merbau|Merbau]], by these two, and wished nothing more than to take revenge and, with Dutch help, to recover what he had lost. The government held that he should be strongly supported, and gave the commander in Atjeh to understand "that the government as little wished that Toekoe Moeda Angkasa should be defeated, as that he should conquer without our assistance; if possible, troops should therefore be sent to the [[Arakoendoer|Arakoendoer-rivier]]."

The commander, [[G. B. T. Wiggers van Kerchem|General Wiggers van Kerchem]], was little inclined to reduce further the troop strength at Atjeh, and on a new application, in November, for 350 to 400 men, he made objection to granting that application, "both with a view to the complete unusability of the auxiliary troops, and to the approaching favourable monsoon, which would soon make the continuation of operations in Great Acheh possible. Moreover, he argued, the consequences of aggressive action against Simpang Olim were incalculable, and it deserved the preference to employ all available forces for the time being against Great Acheh." This disagreement led to his resignation; his successor, [[A. J. E. Diemont|General Diemont]], at once took up the expedition against Simpang Olim with vigour.

## The Murder

Diemont sent on [[7 November]] [[7 November]] [[1876]] the necessary troops to combine with the navy, which had already taken an active part in the operations, to conquer the two hostile states. After the storming of [[Tandjong Semantoh|Tandjong Semantoh]] on [[14 November]] [[14 November]] and the submission of the radja of Simpang Olim, the radja's continued evasions of the conditions imposed — that Dutch troops should be admitted into the gedei of Simpang Olim, that a post should be established there or in the neighbourhood, and that the radja should present himself personally at [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] — led to the issue of an ultimatum on [[10 December]] [[10 December]] [[1876]]. When on [[12 December]] [[12 December]] the chiefs reported that the radja had fled to the interior, a proclamation declared him deprived of his authority and excluded for ever, together with his lineage, from the government of Simpang Olim.

"That he had remained hostile to us appeared from the fact that on [[11 December]] [[11 December]] our ally Toekoe Moeda Angkasa was treacherously murdered by one of his handlangers, and that on [[13 December]] [[13 December]] in the evening he had invited his adherents and allies to a feast at Teping Sirin, in order to devise plans for an attack on our positions."

## Significance

The murder of Toekoe Moeda Angkasa closed the Dutch alliance with Merbau in its original form, and required the appointment of his son as radja of Merbau and Tandjong Semantho. Before the Dutch troops returned, the affairs were first regulated: the son of Toekoe Moeda Angkasa was appointed radja of Merbau and Tandjong Semantho, and by the chiefs a new radja of Simpang Olim was chosen, who on [[5 February]] [[5 February]] [[1877]] solemnly swore the conditions imposed by the government, whereupon the Dutch flag was given to him. In various places further garrison was laid, and thereupon the other troops returned under Major Burgers to Atjeh on [[17 January]] [[17 January]] [1877]. "The submission of Simpang Olim made a great impression everywhere and confirmed our authority also in the other Onderhoorigheden."
