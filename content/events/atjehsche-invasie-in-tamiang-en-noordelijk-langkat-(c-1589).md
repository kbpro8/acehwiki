**The Atjehsche invasie in Tamiang en Noordelijk Langkat** — the Atjehnese invasion of [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] and Northern [[Langkat|Langkat]] — was, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's survey of the [[Buitenbezittingen|Outer Possessions]], the immediate sequel to the [[atjehsche-vernietiging-van-het-batakrijk-aroe-(c-1589)|destruction of the Batakrijk Aroe]] (c. [[1589]]), by which the [[Acheh|Atjehers]] installed a *vertegenwoordiger* — a resident representative of the [[Sultan of Acheh|Sultan of Atjeh]] — to govern the conquered districts. The invasion is the first formal imposition of Atjehnese sovereignty on the East Coast south of the [[Acheh|Atjehnese]] border, and it established the precedent of resident Atjehnese *vertegenwoordigers* that would later be invoked in support of [[atjeh-as-opperheer-in-langkat-deli-serdang-(1854)|19th-century Atjehnese claims to overlordship over Langkat, Deli and Serdang]].

## Background: the destruction of Aroe and the coastal invasion

The Bureau's account places the invasion of [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] and Northern [[Langkat|Langkat]] immediately after the destruction of the [[rijk Aroe|Batakrijk Aroe]]. The destruction had been followed, the Bureau records, by an "invasie van vreemde elementen in het kustgebied" — "invasion of foreign elements into the coastal territory." In the most northerly districts, [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] and Northern [[Langkat|Langkat]], the invading element was specifically Atjehnese: "In Tamiang en Noordelijk Langkat waren het de Atjehers, welke onder een vertegenwoordiger van hun Sultan de macht aan zich trokken" — "In Tamiang and Northern Langkat it was the Atjehers who, under a representative of their Sultan, drew the power to themselves."

## The institution of the Atjehnese *vertegenwoordiger*

The institution of a *vertegenwoordiger van den Sultan van Atjeh* — a "representative of the Sultan of Atjeh" — resident in [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] and Northern [[Langkat|Langkat]] is, in the Bureau's account, the first formal imposition of Atjehnese sovereignty on the East Coast. The *vertegenwoordiger* governed on behalf of the [[Sultan of Acheh|Sultan of Atjeh]] rather than in his own name, and his presence in the most northerly districts of the coast established the documentary and institutional basis for the later, more sweeping claims of Atjehnese overlordship that would be asserted under [[Sultan Iskander Muda|Sultan Iskander Moeda]] and revived in the [[atjeh-as-opperheer-in-langkat-deli-serdang-(1854)|1854 reimposition]].

## The parallel Malay-Djohorese movement south of Tamiang

The Atjehnese invasion of [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] and Northern [[Langkat|Langkat]] was paralleled, further south, by a movement of the [[Djohor|Maleiers of Djohore]]. The Bureau records that "meer naar het Zuiden maakten de Maleiers van Djohore, evenals na de vernietiging van het rijk Gasip gebruik van den verwarden toestand om de kuststreken (riviermonden) te bezetten en daardoor een groot economische overwicht op het binnenland te verwerven" — "more to the south, the Malays of Djohore, just as after the destruction of the realm of Gasip, made use of the confused situation to occupy the coastal regions (river mouths) and thereby to acquire a great economic preponderance over the interior." The pattern established after [[atjehsche-verovering-van-gasip-(begin-16e-eeuw)|the conquest of Gasip]] was thus repeated: Atjehnese seizure of the most northerly districts, Djohorese occupation of the river-mouths further south, and a general dispossession of the [[Batak]]s, who were "steeds meer teruggedrongen naar het binnenland, vanwaar zij vroeger uitgezwermi waren naar de kust" — "driven ever further back into the interior, from which they had earlier swarmed out to the coast."

## Consequences

The Atjehnese invasion of [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] and Northern [[Langkat|Langkat]] had two long-term consequences. First, it produced the demographic pattern that the Bureau later describes for the [[Batak-Karo|Karo Doesoens]] of [[Langkat|Langkat]], [[Deli|Deli]] and [[Serdang|Serdang]]: a Batak population driven into the hills, leaving the coastal plain to Malay and Atjehnese incomers. Second, it established the institution of the Atjehnese *vertegenwoordiger* — the resident representative of the Sultan — that would later be invoked by the [[Acheh|Atjehnese]] sultanate in support of its [[atjehsche-overheersing-der-noordelijke-kuststaatjes|claims to overlordship over the entire northern coast]].

## Significance

The invasion of [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] and Northern [[Langkat|Langkat]] is, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's survey, the moment at which the [[Acheh|Atjehnese]] presence on the East Coast shifts from military incursion to formal administration. The institution of the *vertegenwoordiger* transforms the Atjehnese role from that of an outside conqueror to that of a resident sovereign, and prepares the institutional ground for the later, more comprehensive Atjehnese overlordship that would be imposed by [[Sultan Iskander Muda|Iskander Moeda]]'s [[sultan-iskander-moeda's-oostkust-veldtocht|Oostkust-veldtocht]].
