**Atjeh campaign (26 December 1875 – 7 February 1876)** denotes the initial phase of the Dutch offensive in [[Atjeh (Aceh)]] that concluded with a temporary withdrawal on [[7 February 1876]]. This shorter timeframe is the subject of a second Cambier monograph titled *De mariniers te Atjeh van 26 December 1875 tot 7 February 1876* (see page 473).  

## Operational Outline  

- **Landing (26 December 1875)** – Same as the longer campaign, marines landed near [[Banda Aceh]].  
- **Advance (January 1876)** – Troops moved toward interior strongholds, establishing bivouacs in [[Groot Atjeh]].  
- **Withdrawal (7 February 1876)** – After encountering stiff resistance and logistical difficulties, the force withdrew to the coast.  

## Sources  

Cambier’s brief account (page 473) provides a concise chronology, while the *Atjeh Marineblad* (1890‑1891) contains supplemental operational reports.
