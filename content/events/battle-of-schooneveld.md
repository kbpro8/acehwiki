**The Battle of Schooneveld** was a naval engagement fought on **[[27 August]] [[1666]]** between the **[[Dutch Navy]]**, commanded by **[[Michiel de Ruyter]]**, and a combined **[[French fleet]]** and **[[English fleet]]**.  The source records that the Dutch fleet, despite being outnumbered, inflicted heavy casualties on the enemy while sustaining relatively few losses (see [[275]]).  The battle was part of the **[[Dutch–French War|Franco‑Dutch War]]** (1672–1678).

## Impact on Aceh‑related trade
The Dutch victory secured the North Sea and English Channel, allowing the **[[Indische retourvloot]]** to continue its voyages to the **[[East Indies]]**, including the port of **[[Aceh]]**, without disruption by French privateers.  The protection of this trade was vital for the **[[Dutch East India Company]]**’s spice monopoly.
