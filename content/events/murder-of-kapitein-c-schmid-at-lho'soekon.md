**Murder of Kapitein C. Schmid at Lho'Soekon** (10 July 1933) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Kapitein C. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[10 July]] [[1933]].

## Account

[[C. Schmid|Kapitein C. Schmid]] was stabbed (overhoop gestoken) by a passing [[Atjeh (Aceh)|Atjeher]] at [[Lho'Soekon]] on [[10 July]] [[1933]]. The author, then at [[Sigli]], rushed to [[Lho'Soekon]] on the first news of the attack and found a grey, somber scene with groups of people huddled on the spoordijk in the rain and deep dismay inside the town. The author speculates that Schmid may be the last such victim.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
