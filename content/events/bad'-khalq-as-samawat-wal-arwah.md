**The *Badʾ khalq as-samāwāt wal-arwāḥ*** ("The Beginning of the Creation of the Heavens and the Spirits") is a Malay cosmological treatise by [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|Nuruddin ar-Raniri]], composed at the command of [[Iskandar Thani|Sultan Iskandar Thani]] of [[Acheh]] in the first year of his reign. The work is named by [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] in *The Achehnese* alongside the [[Sirat al-Mustaqim|*Ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm*]] as one of the two works whose composition at the sultan's command marks the beginning of Raniri's activity at the Achehnese court in [[1637]]–[[1638]].

## Composition and date

The *Badʾ khalq as-samāwāt wal-arwāḥ* is to be dated, with the [[Sirat al-Mustaqim|*Ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm*]], to the very beginning of Raniri's Achehnese period. [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] records that Raniri "at the command of Sultan Iskandar Thani wrote the *Sirat al-mustaqim* in March [[1638]], as well as the *Badʾ khalq as-samāwāt wal-arwāḥ*." The composition of two such works in the first year of Iskandar Thani's reign — at the very moment of the [[Raniri's public debate against the Wujudiyyah in Acheh|public disputation]] against the [[Wujudiyyah]] — indicates the scale and ambition of the project of orthodox reform that the new sultan had set in motion.

## Content and character

The very title of the work indicates its subject: the beginning of God's creation, the heavens and the spirits. The treatise belongs to the genre of Islamic cosmological literature — the literature of the *badʾ al-khalq* — that sets out, from the Quran and the Sunna and from the inherited cosmological traditions of late-antique and early-Islamic civilization, the order of the created world: the throne and the footstool, the seven heavens and the seven earths, the angels and the jinn, the genesis of Adam and the physiology of the human microcosm. The work is in Malay, in the *[[Bahasa Djawi]]* of the Achehnese court, and forms part of the same body of orthodox Malay prose as the [[Sirat al-Mustaqim|*Ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm*]], the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān*]], the [[Bustan al-Salatin|*Bustān as-Salāṭīn*]], the [[Hall al-zill|*Ḥall aẓ-ẓill*]], the [[Djawahir al-'ulum|*Jawāhir al-ʿulūm*]], and the [[Hudjdjat as-siddiq li-daf az-zindiq|*Ḥujjat aṣ-ṣiddīq*]].

## Significance

The *Badʾ khalq as-samāwāt wal-arwāḥ* is the earliest of the works composed by Raniri in Acheh at the command of the sultan. Its composition in the same year as the [[Sirat al-Mustaqim|*Ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm*]] and at the very moment of the [[Raniri's public debate against the Wujudiyyah in Acheh|public disputation]] against the Wujūdiyyah places it at the head of the literary output by which Raniri carried the orthodox reaction into every department of Achehnese religious writing: cosmology, law, sects, and the polemic of mysticism.
