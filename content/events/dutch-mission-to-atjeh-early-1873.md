**Dutch mission to Atjeh (early 1873)** (early 1873) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. A last Dutch attempt to avoid war: a mission was sent to Atjeh in early 1873. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to early [[1873]].

## Account

A last Dutch attempt to avoid war: a mission was sent to Atjeh in early [[1873]]. When the negotiations came to nothing, war was immediately declared.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
