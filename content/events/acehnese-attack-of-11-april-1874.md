**Acehnese attack of 11 April 1874** was an Acehnese attack against the Dutch position during the later [[1874]] occupation phase around [[Kota Radja]].

## Course

[[Jan van Swieten]] included it alongside negotiations at [[Gighen]], the [[Radja Bandhara]], and the subsequent adverse engagement of [[16 April]] [[1874]]. These incidents formed part of his case that the reduced occupation faced a continuing, politically unresolved war.

## Significance

The episode belongs to the continuing [[Aceh War]] and the contested consequences of the [[Capture of the Kraton of Aceh]].
