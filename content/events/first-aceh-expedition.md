**First Aceh Expedition** was the initial Dutch military invasion of the Acehnese Sultanate in [[1873]], marking the outbreak of the long and bloody Aceh War.

## The Campaign
Commanded initially by General [[J.H.R. Köhler]], the expedition began with the [[Landing on Atjeh's Coasts]] on [[8 April]] [[1873]]. The forces immediately pushed towards the [[Kraton of Atjeh]], resulting in the [[First Conquest of the Great Missigit]] on [[10 April]] [[1873]] and the [[Second Conquest of the Great Missigit]] on [[14 April]] [[1873]]. The expedition was plagued by underestimation of the Acehnese defenses, heavy casualties, and the death of General Köhler. The Dutch eventually withdrew later in April, concluding the expedition in failure and setting the stage for a much larger second invasion later that year.
