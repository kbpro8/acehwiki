**Kielstra's *Beschrijving van den Atjeh-oorlog*** ("Description of the Atjeh War") is a three-volume military history of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh War]] by [[E. B. Kielstra]], published at The Hague between [[1883]] and [[1885]], with maps.

## Content

Kielstra's work provided a detailed contemporary account of the military operations in [[Atjeh]] from the beginning of the war in [[1873]] through the early 1880s. It served as a standard reference alongside the more analytical works of [[C. Snouck Hurgronje]] and the comprehensive military history of the Netherlands Indies by [[G. B. Hooyer]].

## Source

Offered by Martinus Nijhoff, The Hague, in *A List of the Best Books Relating to Dutch East India* ([[1902]]).
