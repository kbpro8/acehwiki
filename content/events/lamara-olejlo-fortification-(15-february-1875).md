The **Lamara Olejlo fortification** of [[15 February]] [[15 February]] [[1875]] was a Dutch engineer operation of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] in which [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede-Luitenant C. J. Snijders]] of the [[Bataillon Mineurs en Sappeurs|Compagnie Mineurs en Sappeurs]] was ordered to march with part of the company and a detachment of 100 dwangarbeiders (forced labourers), with tools and sandbags, to put in a state of defence a [[benting|benting]] captured in the night of [[14 February|14]]/[[15 February]] [[15 February]] on the northern edge of the kampong [[Lamara Olejlo|Lamara Olejlo]]. Under heavy fire, which cost the Dutch one killed and two officers and eighteen men wounded, the south face was completed and the west and east faces partially completed by about 4 p.m.; the work was continued in the following days by daily working parties sent from [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] under Snijders' direction, and brought to completion despite constant harassment from Atjeh rifle and gun fire. With the Lamara Olejlo action Snijders' task with the field column ended; he was then attached to the Staf der Genie at [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]].

## Context

After the [[Missigit Longbatta operation (31 December 1874 – 13 January 1875)|Missigit Longbatta]] and the capture of [[Mandarsah Poetih|Mandarsah Poetih]] on [[28 January]] [[28 January]] [[1875]] and of [[Pungej Blang Tjoet|Pungej Blang Tjoet]] on [[23 January]] [[23 January]], [[J. L. J. H. Pel|Kolonel PEL]] continued his southward clearing operations. The fortified and partly gun-armed edges of the kampongs Gitjiel Olejlo, [[Lamara Olejlo|Lamara Olejlo]], and Lohong continued to harass [[Mandarsah Poetih|Mandarsah Poetih]]. A reconnaissance in the night of [[14 February|14]]/[[15 February]] [[15 February]] led to the surprise occupation of some fortifications on the northern edge of [[Lamara Olejlo|Lamara Olejlo]].

## The Fortification, 15 February 1875

Snijders received orders at midday on [[15 February]] [[15 February]] at [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] to march with part of the company and a detachment of 100 dwangarbeiders, with a quantity of tools and sandbags, to [[Lamara Olejlo|Lamara Olejlo]] and to put the captured benting in a state of defence. On arrival at about 2 p.m., under heavy fire — to suppress which the Dutch artillery had to intervene repeatedly — the work was begun. The south face was completed and the west and east faces partially completed by about 4 p.m., and the foreground was opened up as much as possible, so that the garrison had a usable fighting position for the night. The action cost the Dutch one killed and two officers and eighteen men wounded. Snijders and his workers then returned to the Kraton with the reinforcement troops sent from [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]].

In the following days the work was continued by daily working parties sent from [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] under Snijders' direction, and brought to completion despite constant harassment from Atjeh rifle and gun fire.

## Significance

The Lamara Olejlo action was Snijders' last operation with the field column. With its completion he was attached to the Staf der Genie at [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] for what were essentially peacetime duties — the maintenance and improvement of buildings, roads, and bridges; the construction of a temporary troop camp at [[Pante Perak|Pante Perak]]; the ice factory for the Medical Service; the narrow-gauge railway to [[Oleh-leh|Olehleh]]; and so forth — in which he remained for the rest of his time in Atjeh. The action is also notable as the first operation in which Snijders commanded dwangarbeiders rather than vrije koelies; the difference in working parties — between hired coolies and forced labourers — is a recurring feature of Dutch engineer operations in the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] and is preserved in the wording of the biographer.
