**Volkstelling van 1930 (Aceh Census of 1930)** (1930) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. First census to cover all of Aceh. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1930]].

## Account

First census to cover all of Aceh. Showed Atjeh is sparsely populated: in the most densely populated [[afdeeling]] [[Noordkust]] max 50 inhabitants/km²; vast [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]]- and [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]] are very sparsely populated or uninhabited. Average for the residency: just over 18/km². 97% indigenous. The 'Voorloopige uitkomsten' suggested 36% increase since [[1920]], but for [[Groot-Atjeh]] and [[Pidië]] no [[1920]] data exist, so this is only an estimate. Next census expected [[1940]].

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
