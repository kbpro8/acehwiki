The **Banishment and recall of Toekoe Tjoet Toengkoeb** was a Dutch disciplinary measure of [[1893]] against the *[[Oeleebalang|oeleebalang]]* of the [[XIII Moekims Toengkoeb]] in [[Groot-Atjeh]], followed within months by his recall, because the population of his district would not return to their abandoned *kampongs* (villages) without him.

## The banishment

Having demanded the Dutch occupation of [[Kroeng Kali]] before assisting [[Toekoe Nja Banta]], and having then been negligent in erecting the required watch-post on a neighbouring hill, [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb|Toekoe Tjoet]] left Nja Banta entirely in the lurch, became insolent towards Dutch officials, *en maakte het eindelijk zoo erg, dat de Gouverneur hem, bij wijze van les, naar Malaboeh verbande* — and finally made matters so bad that the Governor banished him to [[Malaboeh]] by way of a lesson. Hooijer quoted the judgment of a member of the sultan's family upon him: *hij bleef een aap, die nog niet getemd is*.

## Consequences of the banishment

Under these circumstances [[Toekoe Nja Banta]] had little success in resuming hostilities, and by having the wives of his former fellow-combatant transferred from the [[Toengkoeb]] district to [[Lamreng]] inside the *[[Geconcentreerde linie|geconcentreerde linie]]* he caused a general dispersal of that chief's population. The *[[Moslemin]]* immediately occupied the abandoned terrain and broke communications between [[Kroeng Kali]] and [[Toengkoeb]].

## The recall

*Toekoe Tjoet werd uit zijn ballingschap teruggeroepen, omdat zonder hem zijn bevolking niet in de ontruimde kampongs wilde terugkeeren* — Toekoe Tjoet was recalled from his banishment because without him his population would not return to the evacuated *kampongs*. Hooijer recorded that the recall had a good effect: the abandoned terrain was repopulated, and the *Moslemin* were thereby compelled to evacuate the fortified points they still held within it.
