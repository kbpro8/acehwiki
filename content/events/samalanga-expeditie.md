**The Samalanga Expedition** was one of the great successes of the glorious period of Van Heutsz in the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]], the fourth-named of the expeditions and excursions that [[Musket]] lists in *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* by Merac ([[1947]]). The Samalanga Expedition was the expedition to the Samalanga region of [[Atjeh|Aceh]] — the region of the Samalanga mukims on the North Coast of Atjeh — conducted under [[Gouverneur-Generaal Van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] as part of the all-out offensive.

## The great successes of the glorious period

"the time also of our great successes! The Pidië expedition, the expeditions in Idi and Peusangan, the Pasé expedition, the Samalanga expedition, the expeditions to the Gayo and Alas lands, the action in the Tangsé and Geumpang area and on the North Coast of Atjeh, the patrol actions on the always restless West Coast, and finally the Battle of Batèë-Ilië, by which the organised, general resistance in Atjeh was broken for good." The Samalanga Expedition is the fourth-named of the great successes, after the [[Pidië-expeditie]], the expeditions in Idi and Peusangan, and the [[Pasé-expeditie]].

## Significance

The Samalanga Expedition is significant in *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* as one of the great successes of the glorious period of Van Heutsz. The book's argument — that the great successes of the glorious period were the expeditions and excursions by which the organised, general resistance in Atjeh was broken for good — is the book's frame for the offensive phase of the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]]. The Samalanga Expedition is therefore one of the book's — and the colonial memory's — great successes of the glorious period.
