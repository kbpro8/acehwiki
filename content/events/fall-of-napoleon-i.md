**Fall of Napoleon I** (1813) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. The fall of Napoleon I in 1813 ended the threat to Dutch independence, leading England to return the Dutch colonies (with some kept for itself) via the Londensche Conventie of 1814.. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1813]].

## Account

The fall of Napoleon I in [[1813]] ended the threat to Dutch independence, leading [[Engeland (England)|England]] to return the Dutch colonies (with some kept for itself) via the [[London|Londensche]] Conventie of [[1814]].

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
