The **Battle of Selimeum** took place on [[15 July]] [[1879]] when Dutch forces under Governor [[Karel van der Heijden]] captured the stronghold of [[Panglima Polim]] in the upper valley of the [[XXII Mukims]].

## Detailed Narrative

In [[1878]], Governor Van der Heijden launched an aggressive offensive campaign to subjugate Great Aceh. Advancing into the interior of the [[XXII Mukims]], Van der Heijden mobilized two major mobile columns to encircle the mountain fortress of [[Selimeum]], held by [[Panglima Polim]].

On [[15 July]] [[1879]], under intense artillery bombardment, Dutch columns stormed the surrounding redoutes and captured Selimeum, driving Acehnese resistance forces out of Great Aceh into the surrounding mountain ranges.
