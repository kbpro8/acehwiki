The **Dutch occupation of a benteng at Toengkoeb** on [[27 September]] [[1893]] returned Dutch soldiers to ground in the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]] which they had abandoned in [[1884]] after four years of possession, and was made necessary by the wavering conduct of the *[[Oeleebalang|oeleebalang]]* [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb]].

## Circumstances

Hooijer recorded that Toekoe Tjoet, by his wavering attitude, so often threw affairs into confusion that *het den 27sten September 1893 noodig geoordeeld werd één zijner bentengs te Toengkoeb door onze eigene troepen te doen bezetten* — it was judged necessary on 27 September 1893 to have one of his *bentengs* at [[Toengkoeb]] occupied by Dutch troops.

## The contrast with 1880–1884

*Daar zaten dus onze soldaatjes opnieuw op de plaats, die in 1884 door hen verlaten was, nadat zij er vier jaren lang heer en meester waren geweest* — thus the soldiers sat again on the place they had left in [[1884]], after being lords and masters there for four years. But where formerly the columns under [[General van der Heijden]] had raised a great encampment at [[Toengkoeb]] holding two battalions of infantry, a detachment of cavalry, three sections of artillery, sappers, survey personnel and a thousand convict labourers and coolies, the new garrison numbered only two officers and fifty men, with no gun on the ramparts and no medical officer, who had to be fetched from [[Lamjong]] in the *[[Geconcentreerde linie|geconcentreerde linie]]* when required.
