---
title: Hok Canton Incident
aliases: [Capture of the Hok Canton]
tags: [peristiwa]
---

On June 14, 1886, followers of the Acehnese resistance leader Teuku Uma, received aboard as guests, seized the British-owned steamer Hok Canton in the roads of Rigaih on the West Coast, killing the chief mate and chief engineer and fatally wounding Captain Hansen. Mrs. Hansen and the English engineer John Fay were held hostage and eventually ransomed for 62,500 guilders in September 1886, an episode recorded in detail in the Hikayat Prang Gompeuni.
