The **Assembly of the auxiliary troops at Masdjid Siem** on [[2 November]] [[1893]] gathered some 2,400 Achehnese auxiliaries under the leadership of [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]] and at Dutch expense, to drive the *[[Prang sabi|Sabil]]* bands out of the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]].

## The gathering

Hooijer recorded that beneath the trees of the terrain around [[Masdjid Siem]] — the wide fields overgrown with coconut palms, the *kampongs* (villages) closed off by hedges of areca and thorn — *verzamelden zich den 2den November ongeveer 2400 man onzer hulptroepen, die onder Toekoe Djohan's leiding en op onze kosten de Sabilbenden uit de XXVI Moekims zouden verjagen*.

## Terms of service

It was arranged that when this had been accomplished, Oemar and his fighters would maintain order in the district, again against a fixed salary; and that should he fall, he would be succeeded in the command of his fighting force by his brother and by the bravest of his officers.

## Outcome

*Boven verwachting ontmoette hij weinig tegenstand en leed hij weinig verlies* — beyond expectation he met little resistance and suffered little loss. His success was great, and when he later took a couple of days' rest from fighting, the Dutch made it easier for him to resume by complying with his request to send troops to the terrain he had conquered at [[Tjot Rang]]. Thus covered, he was able in the following days to disperse [[Mat Amin|Mad Amin]]'s bands entirely.
