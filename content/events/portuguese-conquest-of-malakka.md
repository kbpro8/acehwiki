**Portuguese conquest of Malakka** (1511) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. The Portuguese, who had appeared in the archipelago around 1500 with plans to control all trade, conquered Malakka in 1511 and made this Malay trade city their 'stapelpplaats' (entrepot) for the Far East. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1511]].

## Account

[[Portuguese Traders|The Portuguese]], who had appeared in [[East Indian Archipelago|the archipelago]] around [[1500]] with plans to control all trade, conquered [[Malakka]] in [[1511]] and made this Malay trade city their 'stapelpplaats' (entrepot) for the Far East. Collisions with Atjeh began immediately. Portuguese attempts to settle on the mainland of North Sumatra were fruitless from the start.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
