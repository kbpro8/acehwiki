The **communistische opstand ter Sumatra's Westkust** was the communist rising which broke out on [[Sumatra's Westkust|Sumatra's West Coast]] shortly after the arrival there of the former Maréchaussée officer [[W. V. Rhemrev]] in [[1926]], and which he suppressed by vigorous action.

## Rhemrev's posting and the outbreak

The [[1930]] Maréchaussée jubilee number records that in [[1926]] Rhemrev was sent to Sumatra's West Coast, and that shortly after his arrival "brak daar de communistische opstand uit, die hij met krachtig optreden wist te onderdrukken" — the communist rising broke out there, which he managed to suppress with vigorous action.

## Recognition

The *Landvoogd* (the Governor-General) sent Rhemrev a telegram of congratulation, and he received his third decoration: appointment as Officer in the *[[Orde van Oranje-Nassau|Orde van Oranje Nassau]]* with the swords. At the same time, however, he was placed on the army reserve.

## Context

The rising fell in the same period as the [[Westkust Resistance (1925-1927)|Westkust action]] in [[Atjeh]] of [[1925]]–[[1927]], in which Captain [[J. Paris]] was killed and to which Colonel [[L. A. Snell]] was despatched from [[Java]]. The jubilee number treats Rhemrev's suppression of the West Sumatra rising as one of the instances in which officers trained in the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* proved their value outside Atjeh.
