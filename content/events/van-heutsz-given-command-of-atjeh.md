**Van Heutsz given command of Atjeh** (March 1898) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. In March 1898, the civil and military command of Atjeh was given to General J. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[March]] [[1898]].

## Account

In [[March]] [[1898]], the civil and military command of Atjeh was given to General [[Generaal J. B. van Heutsz|J. B. van Heutsz]], partly on [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]]'s insistence - [[Christiaan Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck]] found in [[Generaal J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] the right man and supporter for a policy of unweakened action to the end. From then on, the enemy was actively combated and given no rest. The action took place in territory already declared Dutch in [[1873]].

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
