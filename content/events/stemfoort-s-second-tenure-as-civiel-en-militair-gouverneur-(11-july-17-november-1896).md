**Stemfoort's Second Tenure as Civiel en Militair Gouverneur** — *het tweede waarnemend-gouverneurschap van Kolonel Stemfoort* — from [[11 July]] [[1896]] to [[17 November]] [[1896]] was the four-month period during which [[J. W. Stemfoort|Kolonel J. W. Stemfoort]] served as *wd. Civiel en Militair Gouverneur* of [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] for the second time, following the death of [[J. J. K. de Moulin|Generaal-Majoor J. J. K. de Moulin]] by sunstroke on [[7 July]] [[1896]]. Stemfoort had previously served as *wd. Civiel en Militair Gouverneur* from [[28 June]] [[1896]] — the [[Departure of Vetter (28 June 1896)|departure of Lt.-Gen. Vetter as Regeeringscommissaris]] — until [[5 July]] [[1896]], when he had handed over to De Moulin and departed for Batavia on the same steamer. The death of De Moulin on [[7 July]] [[1896]] — two days after assuming the office — required Stemfoort's recall, and he *aanvaardt kolonel Stemfoort het gezag* — assumed the authority — on [[11 July]] [[1896]]. His second tenure lasted until [[17 November]] [[1896]], when the newly-appointed [[C. P. J. van Vliet|Kolonel C. P. J. van Vliet]] arrived at Koeta-Radja and the handover took place.

## The Recall of 7 July [[1896]] and the Telegram of 18 July [[1896]]

The death of Generaal-Majoor De Moulin on [[7 July]] [[1896]] was telegraphically communicated to [[Jhr. C. H. A. van der Wijck|the Governor-General]] at Buitenzorg, who immediately ordered Stemfoort's recall to Koeta-Radja. Stemfoort — who had been en route to Batavia — was redirected, and *11 Juli aanvaardt kolonel Stemfoort het gezag* — on 11 July Colonel Stemfoort assumed the authority. The Governor-General then sent a telegram of [[18 July]] [[1896]] which *eene nadere uiteenzetting zijner bedoelingen omtrent de verdere actie* (a further exposition of his intentions regarding the further action) gave. The telegram — supplementing the *Notulen der conferentie van 18 Juni [[1896]]* between Van der Wijck and the late De Moulin — laid down that *de XXV Moekims moeten in haar geheel door ons beheerscht worden, wij moeten de woelgeesten en de hen volgende benden onschadelijk maken, minstens verdrijven, vervolgens moet er naar gestreefd worden, de medegesleepte kampongbevolking en hare traditioneele hoofden te doen terugkeeren; aan die hoofden mogen geen wapens worden verstrekt, en vuurwapens zelfs slechts tot zeer gering aantal in hun bezit gelaten worden, maar hun worde dan ook alleen de eisch gesteld om tegen dieven en roovers enz. op te treden, terwijl wij de woelgeesten en benden á faire moeten nemen en langzamerhand van hoofden en bevolking gedaan moeten krijgen, daarin geholpen te worden, door geheime inlichtingen en aanwijzingen hunnerzijds.* This *beheersching* (domination) programme — more explicit than the *pacificatie* (pacification) framework of the [[18 June|18 June]] conference — was the policy within which Stemfoort's second tenure would be conducted.

## The Occupation of Kroeng-Raba (Lok-Nga) on 15 July [[1896]]

The first major operation of Stemfoort's second tenure was the [[Occupation of Kroeng-Raba (Lok-Nga) (15 July 1896)|occupation of Kroeng-Raba (Lok-Nga)]] on [[15 July]] [[1896]] — the permanent Dutch occupation of the [[IV Moekims|IV Moekims (Kroeng-Raba)]] region, which had been the objective of De Moulin's fatal *excursie* (reconnaissance (excursie)) of [[7 July]] [[1896]]. The occupation was authorised by the Governor-General's telegram of [[18 July]] [[1896]], which permitted *het bezetten dezer moekims* (the occupation of these mukims) on condition that *voortdurende aanvulling van troepen en dwangarbeiders steeds mogelijk zullen zijn* (continuous replenishment of troops and forced labourers would always be possible). The occupation of Kroeng-Raba — known to the Dutch as *Lok-Nga*, the modern [[Lho-Nga|Lho-Nga (Lhoknga)]] — was the first permanent Dutch occupation of a point beyond the *geconcentreerde linie* (concentrated line) in the second semester of [[1896]], and represented a significant departure from the *geen postenstelsel* policy laid down in the [[Notulen der conferentie van 18 Juni 1896|conference of 18 June 1896]].

## The Reconnaissances of July–August [[1896]]

The remainder of July and August [[1896]] was devoted to a series of reconnaissances and *tuchtigingen* designed to extend Dutch knowledge of, and control over, the [[XXVI Moekims]] and [[XXII Moekims]]. On [[24 July]] [[1896]], *idem in de XXVI Mockims door eene colonne onder Luitenant-Kolonel G. F. Soeters* — a reconnaissance of the XXVI Moekims by a colonne under [[G. F. Soeters|Luitenant-Kolonel Soeters]]. On [[29 July|29]]–[[30 July]] [[1896]], *tocht naar Teungkoe di Lengkong en Pièng* — a trip to [[Teungkoe di Lengkong]] and [[Pièng]] — was undertaken, in which the religious leader [[Habib Lengkong]] of the [[Oostergebergte]] was contacted. On [[4 August]] [[1896]], *verkenningen in verschillende richtingen a.a. naar de IX Moekims* — reconnaissances in various directions, including to the [[IX Moekims]] — were undertaken. On [[7 August]] [[1896]], *nieuwe verkenningen van de Blang Bintang* — new reconnaissances from [[Blang Bintang]] — were conducted. From [[10 August|10]] to [[14 August]] [[1896]], *excursie naar Lepong en Lohong* — an excursion to [[Lepong]] and [[Lohong]] — was undertaken, in which the Dutch made contact with the *kedjoeroean* (council of headmen) of Lohong and received intelligence on the movements of [[Teuku Umar|Toekoe Oemar]], who had *Lepong verlaten en zich in Lohong gevestigd* (left Lepong and settled in Lohong). On [[23 August|23]]–[[27 August]] [[1896]], *tocht naar de XXII Moekims* — a trip to the XXII Moekims — was undertaken by a colonne under [[G. F. Soeters|Soeters]].

## The Construction of Blang Bintang Camp and the Selimoen Expeditions

On [[7 September]] [[1896]], *begin van den bouw van een kampement op de Blang Bintang (Tjot Mantjang)* — the beginning of the construction of a camp at Blang Bintang (Tjot Mantjang). The camp — which would serve as the advanced base for the [[First Selimoen Expedition (6–11 September 1896)|first Selimoen expedition]] of [[6 September|6]]–[[11 September]] [[1896]] and for the [[Second Selimoen Expedition (3–6 November 1896)|second Selimoen expedition]] of [[3 November|3]]–[[6 November]] [[1896]] — was a significant forward position in the Dutch strategy of the second semester. The first Selimoen expedition, which had been ordered before the construction of the camp began, was the principal Dutch probe into the Selimoen highlands — a region which had been a centre of Acehnese resistance throughout the war. The expedition was conducted by a colonne under [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]], and represented the most ambitious Dutch operation of September [[1896]].

## The Pasenstelsel (September–October [[1896]])

In the period *van medio September tot 28 October* — from mid-September to 28 October — the Dutch introduced the [[Pasenstelsel (September–October 1896)|pasenstelsel]] (pass system) for the regulation of movement between the Dutch-held and Acehnese-held territories. The *pasenstelsel* (pass system) was a critical innovation of Stemfoort's second tenure: it required all inhabitants of the Dutch-held area to obtain a pass (*pas*) in order to travel beyond the *geconcentreerde linie*, and all inhabitants of the Acehnese-held area to obtain a pass in order to enter the Dutch-held area. The system was designed to facilitate the identification of *woelgeesten* (troublemakers) and the regulation of trade, but it also represented a significant restriction on the freedom of movement of the Acehnese population, and was a notable extension of Dutch administrative control beyond the military sphere.

## The Temporary Occupation of Indrapoeri and the Capture of Kota Ba Teuē (28 October [[1896]])

On [[28 October]] [[1896]], *tijdelijke bezetting van Indrapoeri* — the temporary occupation of [[Indrapoeri]] — was undertaken, in conjunction with the *samenwerking met de bezetting van Tjot Mantjang tot inname van de sterkte, genaamd Kota Ba Teuē* (cooperation with the garrison of Tjot Mantjang for the capture of the fortification called Kota Ba Teuē). The capture of [[Kota Ba Teuē]] — the Acehnese *stelling* (fortification (stelling)) which had controlled the approach to Indrapoeri — was the most significant Dutch military success of October [[1896]], and opened the way for the [[Trip to Ladoeng and Kroeng Raja (30–31 October 1896)|trip to Ladoeng and Kroeng Raja]] of [[30 October|30]]–[[31 October]] [[1896]] and the [[Second Selimoen Expedition (3–6 November 1896)|second Selimoen expedition]] of [[3 November|3]]–[[6 November]] [[1896]].

## The Arrival of Van Vliet and the Handover (8–17 November [[1896]])

On [[8 November]] [[1896]], *aankomst van den nieuw benoemden Civiel- en Militairen Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden den kolonel C. J. P. van Vliet* — the arrival of the newly appointed Civiel en Militair Gouverneur of Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden, Colonel C. J. P. van Vliet. The handover took place on [[17 November]] [[1896]] — *overgave en overname van het bestuur, Kolonel J. W. Stemfoort keert naar Java terug* — at which Stemfoort returned to Java and Van Vliet formally assumed the governorship. Stemfoort's second tenure thus closed on [[17 November]] [[1896]], after four months and six days in office.

## Significance

Stemfoort's Second Tenure as Civiel en Militair Gouverneur is significant in the documentary record of *ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel* as the administrative period which characterised the second semester of [[1896]]. Under Stemfoort's command, the Dutch extended their effective control from the *geconcentreerde linie* (concentrated line) into the [[XXVI Moekims]], [[XXII Moekims]], [[IV Moekims]] (Kroeng-Raba / Lok-Nga), and the Selimoen highlands; introduced the [[Pasenstelsel (September–October 1896)|pasenstelsel]] for the regulation of movement; and undertook the most ambitious operations of the second semester — the [[First Selimoen Expedition (6–11 September 1896)|first Selimoen expedition]], the [[Capture of Kota Ba Teuē (28 October 1896)|capture of Kota Ba Teuē]], and the [[Second Selimoen Expedition (3–6 November 1896)|second Selimoen expedition]]. Stemfoort's second tenure was thus the bridge between the immediate crisis of the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (29 March 1896)|Umar defection]] and the appointment of [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] as [[Civiel en Militair Gouverneur]] in [[1898]] — the slow, methodical *pacificatie* (pacification) of the second semester of [[1896]] which prepared the ground for the decisive *korte oorlog* phase.
