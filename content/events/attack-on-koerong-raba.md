**Attack on Koerong Raba** was the successful amphibious landing and coastal assault executed in December [[1873]] by the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] under General [[J. van Swieten]], which secured the strategic beachhead at [[Koerong Raba Bay]] on the Acehnese coast.

## Narrative of the Amphibious Invasion

Seeking to bypass the heavily fortified coastal redoubts guarding [[Meuraxa]] and the Aceh River estuary ([[Coastal Fortifications in Aceh]]), General [[J. van Swieten]] directed his invasion armada to anchor off [[Koerong Raba Bay]]. 

In late December [[1873]], naval gunfire suppressed Acehnese coastal batteries, and landing craft carried infantry battalions and the [[Marine Corps in Aceh]] ashore. Dutch troops charged across the surf, capturing the beachhead and securing the western flank of the Acehnese coastal plain.

## Strategic Consequences

The capture of Koerong Raba provided the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] with a safe debarkation port free from the worst hazards of the northeast monsoon. From this base, [[J. van Swieten]] organized his logistical train ([[Transport and Coolie System in Aceh]]) and launched the overland advance that led to the capture of [[Pekan Badak]] and [[Kraton of Aceh]] in January [[1874]].
