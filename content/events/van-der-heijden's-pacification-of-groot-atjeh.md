**Van der Heijden's pacification of Groot-Atjeh** (1874-1875/76) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. In 1874, General van der Heijden was given both civilian and military authority in the conquered area. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1874]]-[[1875]]/76.

## Account

In [[1874]], [[General van der Heijden]] was given both civilian and military authority in the conquered area. In slightly more than 1.5 years, this strong leader effectively subdued [[Groot-Atjeh]], banished unrest, and gave the population confidence. Had a strong but wise policy been adopted by the highest authorities in [[Netherlands|the Netherlands]], the whole territory might have been subdued in relatively short time. However, heavy war burdens and fears of outsiders led the Dutch government to adopt a hesitant, wait-and-see attitude.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
