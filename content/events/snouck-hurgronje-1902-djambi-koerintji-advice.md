**Snouck Hurgronje's 1902 Djambi-Koerintji advice** was the policy advice given by Dr. [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] — together with the relevant advices of the Residents of [[Palembang]] and [[Benkoelen]] — that led the Indische Regeering in [[1902]] to adopt "an active policy with regard to Djambi and thereby also against Koerintji." In the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, this advice is the immediate occasion of the post-[[1902]] extension of the Atjeh model of "effective authority" to other parts of the [[Buitenbezittingen|Outer Possessions]] — the moment at which the post-[[1896]] [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacification of Atjeh]] was deliberately translated into a colonial-wide policy.

## Account from *De Buitenbezittingen* (1917) — Snouck Hurgronje's 1902 Djambi-Koerintji advice

The Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account of [[Snouck Hurgronje's 1902 Djambi-Koerintji advice|Snouck Hurgronje's 1902 Djambi-Koerintji advice]] appears in the chapter on administrative organisation. The Bureau frames [[1902]] as the moment at which the post-[[Toekoe Oemar's verraad (1896)|1896]] strategic reorientation in [[Acheh|Atjeh]] was extended, by deliberate policy choice, to other parts of the [[Buitenbezittingen|Outer Possessions]] — specifically to [[Djambi]] and the adjoining region of [[Koerintji]].

The Bureau writes: "[[1902|The date]] at which a beginning was made with the complete subjection to our effective authority — by which the unfamiliarity with the greater part of the [[Buitenbezittingen|Buitenbezittingen]] was lifted — properly dates only from [[1902]], when the necessity of an active policy with regard to [[Djambi|Djambi]] and thereby also against [[Koerintji|Koerintji]] was adopted by the Indische Regeering, principally in consequence of the relevant advices of the Residents of [[Palembang|Palembang]] and [[Benkoelen|Benkoelen]] and of Dr. [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]]."

The role of [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] in both the post-[[1896]] Atjeh policy and the [[1902]] Djambi/Koerintji extension places him as the intellectual architect of the wider Buitenbezittingen project of "effective authority." The [[1902]] advice is the moment at which the Atjeh experience is deliberately translated into a colonial-wide policy — and the moment at which [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]]'s role expands from that of advisor on Atjehnese affairs to that of advisor on the wider Buitenbezittingen project.

## Significance

[[Snouck Hurgronje's 1902 Djambi-Koerintji advice|Snouck Hurgronje's 1902 Djambi-Koerintji advice]] is, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, the moment at which the Atjeh model of post-[[1896]] active pacification was deliberately extended to other parts of the Buitenbezittingen. The central role of [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] in both the Atjeh policy and the [[1902]] extension underscores the intellectual continuity of the project, and marks [[1902]] as the transition from an Atjeh-specific strategic reorientation to a colonial-wide policy of effective authority.
