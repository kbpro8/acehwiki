**The Night attack on Longbatta-Mesigit (22 November 1875)** was the surprise escalade by a band of about fifty Atjehneezen who, under cover of darkness, climbed the low *borstwering* (parapet) of the post at [[Longbatta-Mesigit]] and "met de blanke klewang" (with the bare klewang) hacked among the sleeping *manschappen* of the garrison, wounding thirteen men including two officers before the combined garrison drove them out — an attack which, Borel warns, could have been disastrous had it been made with more force and from several sides at once.

## Background

Pel had returned to Atjeh on [[9 November]] [[1875]] to find the *toestand* such that offensive action was needed "as soon as possible." The Atjehneezen had received reinforcements from all sides and were preparing to break the Dutch line with *overmacht*. After three months of relative quiet, during which they had built *versterkingen* near the Dutch posts, they had resumed harassment. Posts were *beschoten* from surrounding brush; patrols and communications between posts were attacked.

## The attack

On the night of [[22 November]] [[1875]] a band of about fifty Atjehneezen used the darkness to approach [[Longbatta-Mesigit]] unnoticed, climbed over the low *borstwering*, and "met de blanke klewang" hacked among the sleeping *manschappen*. The combined garrison managed to drive them out, leaving several dead. Dutch losses were thirteen severely wounded, including two officers.

## Borel's verdict

Borel stresses that the attack could have been disastrous if done with more force and from multiple sides — at night, in a man-to-man struggle, the Atjehnese *klewang* is "een vreeslijk wapen." He suspects *achteloosheid* (carelessness) of the *schildwachten* (sentinels) — the same failing that [[Jan van Swieten|van Swieten]]'s [[Van Swieten's legerorder on sleeping sentinels|legerorder]] on sleeping sentinels had, in Borel's view, encouraged by shielding them from court-martial under [[Crimineel Wetboek art. 92|Crimineel Wetboek art. 92]]. A similar escalade would be attempted on the post at Lemboe-N.O. on the night of [[23 January|23]]–[[24 January]] [[1876]]. The Longbatta-Mesigit attack, together with the enemy attack along the entire ooster-linie on [[26 November]] [[1875]], cemented Pel's conviction that the *afwachtende stelsel* had to be abandoned — a conviction that drove the great offensive he launched on [[26 December]] [[1875]].
