**Jacobs's *Het familie- en kampongleven op Groot-Atjeh*** ("Family and Kampong Life in Greater Atjeh") is a two-volume ethnographic study by [[J. Jacobs]], published in Leiden in [[1894]], providing one of the most detailed late-nineteenth-century accounts of Acehnese domestic and social organisation in [[Groot-Atjeh]] (Greater Atjeh), the heartland of the sultanate.

## Content

Jacobs' work complemented the larger-scale ethnographic and political studies by [[C. Snouck Hurgronje]] (*De Atjehers*, [[1893]]-[[1894|94]]) by focusing on the micro-level of household and village (*kampong*) structure. It documented family life, marriage customs, housing, and community organisation in the coastal plain of Greater Atjeh during the late colonial period, when the [[Aceh War|Atjeh War]] was still being waged.

## Source
