The **capture of Pungej Blang Tjoet** on [[23 January]] [[23 January]] [[1875]] was a Dutch operation of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] in which the troops of [[J. L. J. H. Pel|Kolonel PEL]] occupied the post of Pungej Blang Tjoet to the south of [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]]. The post was then fortified by [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede-Luitenant C. J. Snijders]] of the [[Bataillon Mineurs en Sappeurs|Compagnie Mineurs en Sappeurs]] with a detachment of mineurs and a number of coolies, in the first of the southward clearing operations of [[January 1875|January]] [[1875]] that continued with the [[Capture of Mandarsah Poetih (28 January 1875)|capture of Mandarsah Poetih]] five days later and culminated in the [[Lamara Olejlo fortification (15 February 1875)|Lamara Olejlo fortification of 15 February]].

## Context

After the [[Missigit Longbatta operation (31 December 1874 – 13 January 1875)|Missigit Longbatta operation]] was largely complete by [[13 January]] [[13 January]] [[1875]], [[J. L. J. H. Pel|PEL]] "wende hij zich meer in zuidelijke richting, waar hij op 23 Januari den post Poengej Blang Tjoet deed bezetten."

## Fortification

The post was "in de daarop volgende dagen door den Luitenant SNIJDERS — die aan de actie zelve niet had deelgenomen — met een detachement mineurs en een aantal koelies in staat van verdediging gebracht en voor logies ingericht." The action is the first of Snijders' southward engineer operations of [[January 1875|January]]–[[February 1875|February]] [[1875]] and marks the beginning of the systematic extension of the Dutch perimeter south of the [[Acheh River|Atjeh River]].
