**Teukoe Hadji Tjhi's 25-year jubilee** (November 1937) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. In November 1937, the 25-year jubilee of Teukoe Hadji Tjhi' Mohamad Djohan Alamsjah as zelfbestuurder of Peusangan was commemorated. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[November]] [[1937]].

## Account

In [[November]] [[1937]], the 25-year jubilee of [[Teukoe Hadji Tjhi' Mohamad Djohan Alamsjah]] as [[zelfbestuurder]] of [[Peusangan]] was commemorated. The event clearly showed the great significance of the self-administrator figure for his [[landschap]].

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
