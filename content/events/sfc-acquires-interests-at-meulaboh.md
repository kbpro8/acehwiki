**S.F.C. acquires interests at Meulaboh** (1926) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Since the Société Financière des Caoutchoucs (S.F.C.) acquired interests in the Meulaboh area in 1926, much more activity came to the Westkust.. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1926]].

## Account

Since the [[Société Financière des Caoutchoucs (S.F.C.)]] acquired interests in the [[Malaboeh|Meulaboh]] area in [[1926]], much more activity came to the [[Westkust]].

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
