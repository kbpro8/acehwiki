**The Bridge over the Kroeng Pengapit** — *het slaan van een brug over de Kroeng Pengapit op 8 November* [[1896]] — was the construction of a bridge over the [[Kroeng Pengapit]] river in [[Groot-Atjeh]], undertaken by the *Genietroepen* (engineers) on [[8 November]] [[1896]]. The bridge — constructed on the same day as the [[Arrival of Van Vliet as Civiel en Militair Gouverneur (8 November 1896)|arrival of Kolonel Van Vliet]] at Koeta-Radja — improved the Dutch lines of communication between the *geconcenteerd linie* and the [[Blang Bintang]] / [[Selimoen]] region, and was a significant logistical achievement of the second semester of [[1896]]. The construction of the bridge reflected the Dutch commitment to the forward movement of the second semester — the slow, methodical extension of Dutch control beyond the *geconcenteerd linie*, supported by the construction of permanent logistical infrastructure.

## Significance

The Bridge over the Kroeng Pengapit of [[8 November]] [[1896]] is significant in the documentary record of *ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel* as a logistical achievement of the second semester of [[1896]]. The bridge — constructed on the same day as the arrival of Van Vliet — improved the Dutch lines of communication between the *geconcenteerd linie* and the Blang Bintang / Selimoen region, and reflected the Dutch commitment to the forward movement of the second semester. The construction of the bridge — like the [[Construction of Blang Bintang Camp (7 September 1896)|construction of the Blang Bintang camp]] of [[7 September]] [[1896]] and the [[Occupation of Kroeng-Raba (Lok-Nga) (15 July 1896)|occupation of Kroeng-Raba (Lok-Nga)]] of [[15 July]] [[1896]] — was a step in the slow transformation of the Dutch presence in Atjeh from a military occupation into a civil administration, supported by permanent logistical infrastructure.
