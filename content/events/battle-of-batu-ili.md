The **Battle of Batu Ili** occurred in [[July]] [[1880]] during a military expedition led by General [[Karel van der Heijden]] against the fortified mountain position of Batu Ili in [[Samalanga]].

## Detailed Narrative

In [[July]] [[1880]], Dutch troops launched three successive infantry assaults against the cliffside fortress of Batu Ili in [[Samalanga]]. Acehnese defenders repulsed all three infantry storming attempts, inflicting heavy casualties.

Unable to breach the stone ramparts by direct assault, General Van der Heijden concentrated heavy artillery batteries to bombard the fort continuously. Terrified by the prolonged artillery bombardment, local chieftains surrendered in early [[August]] [[1880]].
