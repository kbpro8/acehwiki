**Kleiland-aanwinning op Atjeh's Westkust in 1902** — *"Land reclamation (literally: clay-land gaining) on Atjeh's West Coast in 1902"* — is an article by **[[J. L. M. van den Brandhof|J. L. M. van den Brandhof]]**, published in [[1939]] in *De Indische Gids* (The Indies Guide), No. 61, p. 866 *e.v.*, *etc.*, recording an episode of land reclamation — the gaining of new clay-land from the sea — on the west coast of [[Acheh|Atjeh]] in the year [[1902]]. The article is recorded as No. 557 in [[H. van Meurs|Van Meurs]]'s *[[Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939 (Van Meurs, 1940)|Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939]]* (The Hague, [[1940]]), in the Sumatra chapter.

## Subject

The article's subject, as recorded by Van Meurs, is the *kleiland-aanwinning* — literally "clay-land winning," i.e. the gaining of new cultivable clay-soil land from the sea or from swamp — on [[Atjeh's Westkust|Atjeh's Westkust]] in the year [[1902]]. The term *aanwinning* denotes the act of gaining or acquiring, and the compound *kleiland-aanwinning* is the technical Dutch term for land reclamation in which new clay-land is formed, whether by drainage, by embankment, or by natural accretion. The year [[1902]] falls within the middle phase of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]], at a moment when the Dutch colonial administration was consolidating its hold on the coastal stretches of the west coast even as the interior remained contested. The article thus records a practical episode of colonial land-management in a theatre of war.

## Author and venue

[[J. L. M. van den Brandhof|Brandhof]] published the article in *De Indische Gids*, one of the principal monthly review journals of the Netherlands Indies, which carried articles on colonial administration, economy, geography, and current affairs. The journal's audience — colonial officials, planters, merchants, and the interested Dutch public — was precisely the readership to which a technical note on land reclamation on the Atjeh west coast would be of practical interest. The pagination "p. 866 e.v., etc." indicates that the article ran from p. 866 onward and was continued in subsequent issues, a common practice in the journal.

## Place in the 1939 Literatuur-Overzicht

The article is No. 557 in the Sumatra chapter (Hoofdstuk III, Nos. 548–658) of Van Meurs's survey, where it stands among the B-letter entries of the Sumatra chapter, between the [[J. J. C. A. Bannink|Bannink]] article on the [[Grepen uit den Atjeh-oorlog (Bannink, 1939)|Atjeh-oorlog]] (No. 550) and the [[S. da Costa|da Costa]] survey of the [[De Padrie-oorlog (Costa, 1939)|Padrie-oorlog]] (No. 558). Its placement there marks it as one of the Atjeh-specific entries of the Sumatra chapter, and its subject — land reclamation rather than military operations — illustrates the breadth of the Atjeh content that Van Meurs captured: not only the war and its personalities, but also the practical and geographical aspects of the colonial engagement with the Atjeh west coast.

## Significance

For AcehWiki, the article is significant as a [[1939]] retrospective on an episode of [[1902]] land reclamation on the [[Atjeh's Westkust|Atjeh's Westkust]], and as a reminder that the colonial engagement with Atjeh was not solely military but also involved the practical work of land-management, drainage, and the extension of cultivable area. The article furnishes an independent source for the geographical and administrative history of the Atjeh west coast in the middle phase of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]], and it complements the corpus's existing entries on [[atjeh's-westkust|Atjeh's Westkust]], on [[westkust-van-sumatra|the Westkust of Sumatra]], and on the wider colonial geography of Atjeh.
