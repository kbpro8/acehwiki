**The murder of Vallon and Guillaume** was the killing, on [[9 March]] [[1880]], of the two French travellers [[Vallon (Tenom, 1880)|Vallon]] and [[Guillaume (Tenom, 1880)|Guillaume]] in the [[Tenom|Tenom river]] by the people of the Tenomsche robber prince. [[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] recalled the fact in [[1886]] against the apologists who explained every Dutch reverse in the Archipelago by Dutch aggression alone: the Netherlands had, before [[1884]], never made war on the Tenom robber prince, and yet his people murdered the two Frenchmen in 1880 — the coast that was to become the centre of the [[Nisero affair (1883–1885)|Nisero affair]] three years later, and from which eventually, in [[1884]], the most serious threats of English meddling with Atjeh emanated.
