**The Industriebond defense meeting of [[17 February]] [[1917]]** was a public gathering of the Industriebond (Industriële Club) at which former Governor-General [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|J. B. van Heutsz]] delivered his second major address on the defence of [[Netherlands India|Netherlands Indies]], reiterating the themes of his earlier [[Van Heutsz's Bond van Vrije Liberalen defense speech (13 January 1917)|speech at the Bond van Vrije Liberalen]] and setting out what he presented as the government's accepted defence system.

## The Meeting

The meeting took place at the Industriebond (Industriële Club) on [[17 February]] [[1917]] and was reported in the *Handelsblad* of [[18 February]] [[1917]]. [[C. C. Vattier Kraane]] also spoke at the meeting, addressing "De verhooging der economische ontwikkeling van Nederlandsch-Indië, in verband met zijn defensie" (The increase of the economic development of Netherlands Indies in connection with its defence).

## Van Heutsz's Speech

Van Heutsz covered the same defence themes as his [[13 January]] [[1917]] address to the [[Bond van Vrije Liberalen]]. At this meeting, however, he set out what he presented as the "*hoofdlijnen*" (main outlines) of the government's accepted defence system, drawing from the [[Memorie van Antwoord]]. He described the government's fleet plan as comprising 4 battleships (*linieschepen*), 5 cruisers, and 25 submarines, at a total acquisition cost of 275 million guilders. He dismissed the land army component as insufficient, capable only of the defence of [[Java]].

## Relationship to the Memorie van Antwoord

Van Heutsz's presentation of the government's fleet plan from the Memorie van Antwoord formed part of his broader argument that the government had accepted the principles of the [[Van der Weyden's defense brochure|Van der Weyden defence brochure]] but had failed to act on them with sufficient urgency. His criticism of the army component as adequate only for Java defence reinforced his call for the appointment of a *buitengewoon Regeerings-Commissaris* with sweeping powers.

## Provocation of Van der Maaten

[[Van der Maaten]] wrote the *Naschrift* (Postscript) to [[Van der Maaten's pamphlet on Van Heutsz and the Defense of Netherlands Indies|his pamphlet]] after reading the *Handelsblad* report of this meeting. The Industriebond speech thus provided the immediate occasion for Van der Maaten's final additions to a work that was dated [[25 February]] [[1917]] in [[Montreux]].

## Part of the Broader Campaign

The meeting was one element of the sustained post-governorship defence advocacy campaign conducted by [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|J. B. van Heutsz]] and his circle, which also included the [[Van Heutsz's Bond van Vrije Liberalen defense speech (13 January 1917)|Bond van Vrije Liberalen speech]] and the promotion of [[J. Van der Weyden]]'s defence proposals. The campaign formed a central episode in the [[Defense of Netherlands Indies debate (1917)]].

## Significance

The Industriebond meeting demonstrated the breadth of Van Heutsz's public campaign, extending beyond the party-political arena of the [[Bond van Vrije Liberalen]] into the commercial and industrial sphere. It was the *Handelsblad* report of this meeting that prompted Van der Maaten to add his *Naschrift*, making the Industriebond speech a direct trigger for one of the pamphlet's most consequential sections.
