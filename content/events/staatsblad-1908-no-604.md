**Staatsblad 1908 No. 604** was the *Staatsblad* (Government Gazette) decree that formally established and precisely demarcated the boundary between the [[Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra|Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra]] and the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]]. It is the only boundary of the [[Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra|Oostkust gewest]] that was *nauwkeurig omgeschreven* (accurately demarcated) by legal instrument; all other borders with [[Tapanoeli|Tapanoeli]], the Westkust, and [[Residentie Riouw en Onderhoorigheden|Riouw]] remained based on traditional landscape boundaries recognizable only in the terrain.

## Legal context

The *Staatsblad* (State Gazette) was the official publication for laws, decrees, and ordinances in the Netherlands East Indies. No. 604 of [[1908]] specifically fixed the *grens tusschen het gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden en het gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra*. The decree followed the administrative separation of [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] from the [[Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra|Oostkust]] and its attachment to [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh]] via [[Staatsblad 1908 No. 112|Staatsblad [[1908]] No. 112]], which moved the northern limit south to the [[Besitang|Besitang]] watershed.

## Boundary description in the decree

The text of the work summarizes the demarcated line as follows (likely quoting or paraphrasing the *Staatsblad*):

> *Overal elders gelden nog de, bij overlevering voldoende bekende, doch slechts in het terrein zelf met betrekkelijke juistheid aan te wijzen, grenzen der verschillende landschappen. Over het algemeen volgt de gewestelijke grens van het Noorden af de waterscheiding tusschen de Besitang- en Tamiang-rivier tot de Wilhelmina-keten (G. Bandahara) en vandaar den kam van dit gebergte tot de Serbolangit groep (Liang Gergas, 1888 m). Van dien top daalt de grens af naar het moerassige dal de L. Penjabrangan, welke rivier zij in den middenloop snijdt. De grens blijft nu in nagenoeg Zuidelijke richting doorlopen, tot aan de Blang Nodi, welker Oostrand zij volgt tot den G. Aboe Aboe, in welk punt nu nog de grenzen van Atjeh, Oostkust en Tapanoeli samenkomen.*

## Tripoint at Gunung Aboe Aboe

The decree confirmed Gunung Aboe Aboe (1,684 m) as the tripoint where the boundaries of three *gouvernementen* meet: [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh]], [[Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra|Oostkust van Sumatra]], and [[Tapanoeli|Tapanoeli]]. From this point, the [[Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra|Oostkust]] boundary continues south along the [[Wilhelmina-gebergte|Wilhelmina-keten]] toward the [[Tobameer|Toba Lake]] northern rim, while the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh]]–[[Tapanoeli|Tapanoeli]] boundary runs west toward the [[Alaslanden|Alas lands]].

## Survey and mapping

The boundary was surveyed by the Topografische Dienst and appears on:

- *Gewestelijke kaart van Atjeh* (Bijlage XI, afl. II of the Atjeh volume).
- Composite map of [[Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra|Oostkust]] (Bijlage XXIII, compiled by the Encyclopaedisch Bureau).

The Topografische Dienst also proposed an alternative natural boundary from Liang Gergas down a tributary of the Lau Penjabrangan to the Lau Alas, then along the Gunung Sembala and Deleng Madjid crests to the Lau Renoen, rejoining the [[Wilhelmina-gebergte|Wilhelmina-keten]] at Gunung Simpang Pajong. Both lines are shown on the maps.

## Administrative consequences

The decree fixed the jurisdiction of the *assistent-resident* of [[Langkat|Langkat]] (*onderafdeeling* Beneden-Langkat, *hoofdplaats* [[Tandjoeng Poera|Tandjoeng Poera]]) up to the [[Besitang|Besitang]] watershed. It defined the northern limit of the [[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij|Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij]] concession area ([[Pangkalan Brandan|Pangkalan Brandan]] terminus), clarified customs and police jurisdiction for the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]] extension to [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]], and provided the legal basis for the [[1915]] elevation of [[Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra|Oostkust]] to a Gouvernement under a Governor (previously a Residentie), as the territory's extent was now legally certain.

## Significance

[[Staatsblad 1908 No. 604|Staatsblad [[1908]] No. 604]] is, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, the only legal instrument that precisely demarcated a *gewest* boundary in the northern Sumatran Buitenbezittingen — and the legal foundation on which the [[Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra|Oostkust]] was, in [[1915]], elevated from a Residentie to a Gouvernement. The decree's coincidence with [[Staatsblad 1908 No. 112|Staatsblad [[1908]] No. 112]] (the Tamiang transfer) and with the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]]'s [[1908]] extension into [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] marks [[1908]] as the year in which the northern frontier of the [[Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra|Oostkust]] was simultaneously fixed, reduced, and integrated into the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh]] infrastructure.
