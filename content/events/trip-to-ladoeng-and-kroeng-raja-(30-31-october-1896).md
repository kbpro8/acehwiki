**The Trip to Ladoeng and Kroeng Raja** — *tocht naar Ladoeng en Kroeng Raja op 30 en 31 October* [[1896]] — was a two-day Dutch reconnaissance of the [[Ladoeng]] and [[Kroeng Raja]] regions of [[Groot-Atjeh]], undertaken on [[30 October|30]]–[[31 October]] [[1896]], two days after the [[Capture of Kota Ba Teuē (28 October 1896)|capture of Kota Ba Teuē]] on [[28 October]] [[1896]]. The trip formed part of the broader Dutch forward movement of late October [[1896]] — the extension of Dutch control from the [[Temporary Occupation of Indrapoeri (28 October 1896)|temporary occupation of Indrapoeri]] into the Ladoeng and Kroeng Raja regions. The trip was followed, on [[3 November]] [[1896|3]], by the [[Second Selimoen Expedition (3–6 November 1896)|second Selimoen expedition]] — the last major Dutch operation of the second semester of [[1896]].

## Significance

The Trip to Ladoeng and Kroeng Raja of [[30 October|30]]–[[31 October]] [[1896]] is significant in the documentary record of *ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel* as the immediate follow-up to the [[Capture of Kota Ba Teuē (28 October 1896)|capture of Kota Ba Teuē]] of [[28 October]] [[1896]]. The trip — undertaken two days after the capture — extended the Dutch forward movement into the Ladoeng and Kroeng Raja regions, and prepared the way for the [[Second Selimoen Expedition (3–6 November 1896)|second Selimoen expedition]] of [[3 November|3]]–[[6 November]] [[1896]]. The trip was thus part of the rapid sequence of Dutch operations in late October and early November [[1896]] — the sequence which would be brought to a halt by the [[Cholera Epidemic in Atjeh (December 1896)|cholera epidemic]] of December [[1896]].
