**Sarikat Islam Agitation in Noordkust** in [[1918]] and following years was political-religious movement taking wrong and dangerous direction in afdeeling [[Noordkust van Atjeh]], leading to banishment of some younger heads.

## Narrative

In the afdeeling [[Noordkust van Atjeh]] with her 3 underafdeelingen [[Lho Seumawe]], [[Bireuen]] and [[Lho Soekon]], after [[1918]] the [[Sarikat Islam]] movement showed fairly vigorous life. Traders, teachers of volksscholen, intellectuals said members and took part. Handelaars, onderwijzers van volksscholen zegden intellectueelen lid en namen deel. A head of [[Samalanga]] joined as move on political chessboard playing against influential [[Mbaharadja]] who also member Sarikat Islam thereby aiming keep landscape [[Plimbang]]. After consulting *oelama* (Islamic legal scholar) saying joining good deed formed department S.I. Public meetings even secret meetings. Would go direction movement [[Lho Seumawe]] where distinction between believers and unbelievers brought forward, where in S.I. circle Netherlands authority undermined and moreover worked at association of oeléëbalangs. To calm last year three heads from [[Lho Seumawe]] banished, intervention not day too early.

Younger heads too susceptible new slogans, religious advisors always ready increase own significance adding faith zeal and hatred factor, and as movement more general older heads tempted participation. Let afdeeling so far then resistance revives. In [[Lho Seumawe]] and [[Samalangan]] vigilance very required per Broersma.

Broersma also notes in [[Lho Soekon]] south-eastern paja area population on low level heads backwardness may cause block. In paja area workings Sarikat Islam earlier affected.
