The **Relief of Kroeng Kali by the 3rd battalion** in [[October]] [[1893]] was the operation by which the Dutch, under [[Overste Sievers]], broke the investment of their advanced post at [[Kroeng Kali]] in the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]] and cleared two of three *[[Moslemin]]* positions on the connecting road to [[Toengkoeb]], at the cost of [[Luitenant Weyerman]] and three men killed and two officers and fifty men wounded.

## Circumstances

The occupation of [[Kroeng Kali]] had been undertaken at the demand of [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb|Toekoe Tjoet]], who afterwards left [[Toekoe Nja Banta]] in the lurch and was banished to [[Malaboeh]]. Hooijer judged that by yielding to the exile's request the Dutch had *ons in een wespennest gestoken* — thrust themselves into a wasps' nest. When [[Toekoe Nja Banta]] removed Toekoe Tjoet's wives to [[Lamreng]], the resulting dispersal of the population allowed the *Moslemin* to occupy the abandoned terrain and cut communications between [[Kroeng Kali]] and [[Toengkoeb]].

## The operation

*Natuurlijk liet men geen tijd voorbijgaan den post te ontzetten* — naturally no time was allowed to pass before relieving the post. The [[3de Bataljon Infanterie|3rd battalion]] under Overste Sievers advanced and was to attempt to drive the enemy from his three positions on the connecting road with [[Toengkoeb]].

Sievers found that the *geestdrijvers* (religious zealots) had lost nothing of their courage when they entered the lists against the *Kafirs*, although [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]] could usually chase them from one *kampong* (village) to another without much trouble. They defended themselves so obstinately and so long that on the first day he could drive them from no more than two positions. One of these was the [[Masdjid Siem|Messigit (Masdjid) Siem]], where Dutch bullets had to fly like howling hailstorms across the broad *sawah* (wet rice-field) plain before the defenders gave up their sanctuary; [[Luitenant Weyerman]] and three men fell there and two officers and fifty men were wounded.

That evening the mosque sheltered tired Dutch soldiers instead of opium-smoking *Moslemin*. During the following night the enemy evacuated the third position without awaiting the Dutch attack.

## Aftermath

[[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]]'s troops were to garrison the mosque, since it had become clear that without his help the Dutch allies would not drive the fanatics from the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]]. On [[21 October]] [[1893]] he appeared with a hundred fighters to take over the point and to quarter his men in the *benteng* of [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb|Toekoe Tjoet]] near Kroeng Kali. Thereafter it remained quiet, and in [[January]] [[1894]] Kroeng Kali was evacuated.
