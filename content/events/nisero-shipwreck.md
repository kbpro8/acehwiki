**Nisero shipwreck** (night of 8-9 November 1883) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. In the night of 8-9 November 1883, the ship 'Nisero' stranded at Koeala Panga, 20 km south of Tjalang on the Westkust. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to night of 8-[[9 November]] [[1883]].

## Account

In the night of 8-[[9 November]] [[1883]], the ship 'Nisero' stranded at [[Koeala Panga]], 20 km south of [[Tjalang]] on the [[Westkust]]. The shipwrecked crew were taken to boven-[[Teunom]] ([[Teunom (boven-Teunom)|upper Teunom]]) in the interior. 7 of them died there and were originally buried there. Later, when the graves were threatened by the river changing direction, the bones were transferred to [[Tjalang]] and re-buried there at the English navy cemetery.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
