The **Concentratiebesluit** of [[19-august|19 August]] [[1884]] — the *besluit van 19 Augustus 1884* (decree of 19 August 1884) by which the [[Van Rees|Governor-General van Rees]] ordered the [[Concentratiestelsel|Concentratie]] (concentration policy), the withdrawal of all outlying Dutch posts to the concentrated *stelling* (defensive line) of [[groot-atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]], and promulgated it by the *proclamatie* (proclamation) of the same date to the Atjehnese — is, in [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz's]] [[1893]] tract *[[De onderwerping van Atjeh (Van Heutsz, 1893)|De onderwerping van Atjeh]]*, the principal *bête noire* of the entire Atjeh record: the *groote politieke fout* (great political mistake) that reversed the [[van der Heijden|Van der Heijden]] conquest of [[groot-atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] and condemned the colony to another fifteen years of war. The decision was taken in the secret sessions of the [[staten-generaal|Staten-Generaal]] of [[16 June|16]]–[[17-june|17 June]] [[1884]] over the head of the colonial Atjeh authorities — "*zonder dat aan de autoriteiten te Atjeh om eenig nader advies was gevraagd*" — and was executed, despite [[Laging Tobias|Gouverneur Laging Tobias]]'s *ongevraagd* (unsolicited) warning that "*een dergelijk vertoon van zwakheid, ten zeerste zou schaden aan het Nederlandsch gezag*", by his successor [[demmeni|Generaal Demmeni]] from August [[1884]]. The decision produced three operational corollaries: the abolition of the posts at [[telok-semawe|Telok Semawé]] and [[samalanga|Samalanga]]; the elimination of the *controleurs* (civil-administrative officials) in the *onderafdeelingen* (sub-divisions) of [[groot-atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]]; and the formal establishment of the [[Staatsblad No. 105 van 1884|rigorous blockade]] of the entire Atjeh coast from [[Tamiang]] on the [[east-coast-of-aceh|Oostkust]] to [[troemon|Troemon]] on the [[westkust|Westkust]].

## The 16–17 June 1884 secret Staten-Generaal sessions

The Concentratie was authorised, in the first instance, by the secret sessions of the [[staten-generaal|Staten-Generaal]] of [[16 June|16]] and [[17-june|17 June]] [[1884]] in The Hague. Van Heutsz records the parliamentary origin:

> "Dat de hangende plannen de kort daarop ingevoerde Concentratie zouden wezen, waartoe besloten was in de geheime zittingen van de Staten-Generaal van 16 en 17 Juni 1884".

The decision was taken at the metropolitan level — *opperbestuur in Nederland* (supreme government in the Netherlands) — without consultation of the colonial Atjeh authorities, "*zonder dat aan de autoriteiten te Atjeh om eenig nader advies was gevraagd*". The colony's Governor-General at the moment of the decision, [[Pijnacker Hordijk|G.-G. 's Jacob]], had himself, in August [[1883]], "dergelijke inkrimping officieel besprak" but, after hearing the opinions of the Governor and the *autoriteiten van land- en zeemacht te Kota Radja* (authorities of the land and sea forces at Kota Radja), had "openlijk verklaard, dat noch tot inkrimping van grondgebied, noch tot vermindering van troepenmacht zou overgegaan worden, zoolang in de toenmalige omstandigheden geen verandering kwam". The June [[1884]] secret sessions thus overrode both 's Jacob's August [[1883]] declaration and the colonial authority of the Atjeh government itself. The decision was, in Van Heutsz's reading, the metropolitan *opperbestuur*'s imposition on the colony of a policy the colony's own authorities had rejected.

## The 19 August 1884 proclamation by van Rees

The Concentratie was promulgated in the colony by [[Van Rees|G.-G. van Rees]] — who had succeeded [[Pijnacker Hordijk|'s Jacob]] as Governor-General and visited Atjeh in March [[1884]] — by *proclamatie* of [[19-august|19 August]] [[1884]]. Van Heutsz records the dateline and the OCR-damaged text:

> "in weerwil van de op 19 Augustus door den Gouvernnurr-Gnnraall van Rees uitgevaardigde proclamatie, waarin hij aan de Atjehers deed verkondigen edat xij er nooit in rouden slagen eons een enkelen stap terugtedringen".

The proclamation — *dat zij er nooit in zouden slagen ons een enkelen stap terugtedringen* (that they would never succeed in driving us back a single step) — was Van Rees's public assurance to the Atjehnese that the Dutch would *never* yield an inch. The Atjehnese, Van Heutsz records, "waren zoo vrij te gelooven, dat ons terugtrekken in de geconcentreerde stelling niet vrijwillig, maar gedwongen was en voortsproot uit zwakte, uit onmacht om den strijd langer vol te houden" — read the proclamation in precisely the opposite sense, as proof that the Dutch withdrawal was involuntary, the product of weakness, of inability to sustain the struggle. The formal *besluit* — *de concentratie, die bij besluit van 19 Augustus 1884 bevolen werd* (the concentration that was ordered by decree of 19 August 1884) — was the legal instrument by which the Concentratie was executed.

## The abolition of posts at Telok Semawé and Samalanga

The operational core of the Concentratie was the withdrawal of the outlying Dutch posts to the *geconcentreerde stelling* (concentrated defensive line) of [[groot-atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]]. The most consequential withdrawals were the abolition of the posts at [[telok-semawe|Telok Semawé]] and [[samalanga|Samalanga]]. Van Heutsz's catalogue of the abolished posts:

> "de betrekkingen van assistent-resident ter Noord- en Oostkust en van controleur der onderafdeeling Samalanga werden ingetrokken tegelijk met de opheffing der vestigingen op laatstgenoemde plaats en te Telok Semawé".

The abolition of the [[telok-semawe|Telok Semawé]] post — the seat of the *assistent-resident ter Noord- en Oostkust* (assistant resident for the North and East Coast) — was the most consequential single act of the Concentratie. Telok Semawé had been, under [[scherer|Scherer]]'s administration, the operational centre of the [[Scheepvaartregeling|scheepvaartregeling]] (shipping regulation) on the Noord- en Oostkust; the post was "een maand na Scherer's vertrek werd ingerukt" — was abolished one month after Scherer's own departure in early October [[1884]]. The abolition of the [[samalanga|Samalanga]] post — the seat of the *controleur der onderafdeeling Samalanga* (controller of the sub-division Samalanga) — closed the second of the two *kruisboot-bangoen* test sites of [[1880]] that had given Scherer the empirical foundation of the *scheepvaartregeling* concept.

## The elimination of controleurs in the onderafdeelingen of Groot-Atjeh

The Concentratie's second operational corollary was the elimination of the *controleurs* in the *onderafdeelingen* (sub-divisions) of [[groot-atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] and the transfer of their functions to the military commandants of the posts. Van Heutsz records the reassignment:

> "de controleurs in de onderafidellnnen van Groot-Atjeh werden afgeschaft, de functiën van assistent-resident ter Westkust (standplaats Melaboeh) en van controleur te Segli werden opgedragen aan de daar gevestigde militaire commandanten".

The *controleurs* were the civil-administrative officials on whom the *scheepvaartregeling* and the *contrôle op handel en scheepvaart* (supervision of trade and shipping) had depended; their abolition, and the transfer of their functions to the military commandants, was the operational expression of the policy of *non-interventie in de binnenlandsche aangelegenheden van de kuststaten* (non-intervention in the internal affairs of the coastal statelets) that the Concentratie made explicit. The civil administration of the Atjeh colony was, in effect, abolished along with the outlying posts: the *assistent-resident ter Westkust* at [[meulaboh|Melaboeh]] and the *controleur te Segli* were both transferred to the military commandants, and the *assistent-resident ter Noord- en Oostkust* (standplaats [[telok-semawe|Telok Semawé]]) and the *controleur der onderafdeeling Samalanga* were eliminated entirely.

## The resignation of Laging Tobias

The Concentratie was the immediate occasion of the resignation of [[Laging Tobias|Gouverneur Laging Tobias]]. Van Heutsz records the chronology: Laging Tobias had offered his resignation in February [[1884]], "*toen de Regeering het eenige middel verwierp waarvan hij in de bestaande omstandigheden een goede uitkomst verwachtte*" — when the Regeering (Government) rejected the only remedy from which he had expected a good outcome, namely his projected restoration of the sultanate under [[tuanku-muhamat-dawot|Toeankoe Mohamad Daoed]]. The resignation had been declined by [[Pijnacker Hordijk|G.-G. 's Jacob]], and Van Rees — on his March [[1884]] visit — had *betuigd* (assured), "*dat hij de inzichten van den Gouverneur Laging Tobias omtrent den met Atjeh te volgen weg vrij juist vond*". Laging Tobias, in consequence, "*besloot hij niet verder op zijn ontslag aan te dringen*". The June [[1884]] secret sessions and the [[19-august|19 August]] [[1884]] *Concentratiebesluit* were, however, "*hangende plannen*" of which the Governor "*in geenen deele*" had been warned; on perceiving, in June [[1884]], that the *Concentratie* had been decided "*zonder dat aan de autoriteiten te Atjeh om eenig nader advies was gevraagd*", Laging Tobias "*rekende het zich tot plicht nogmaals ongevraagd tegen de uitvoering er van ernstig te moeten waarschuwen, omdat hij overtuigd was, dat een dergelijk vertoon van zwakheid, ten zeerste zou schaden aan het Nederlandsch gezag en dit niet alleen te Atjeh, maar in geheel Nederlandsch-Indië*".

The warning made his position untenable: "*Met dit advies was het natuurlijk onmogelijk den Heer Laging Tobias met de uitvoering te belasten van de concentratie, die bij besluit van 19 Augustus 1884 bevolen werd*". Laging Tobias's February [[1884]] resignation was now "*ingewilligd*" — accepted — and the *bestuur* (government) "*weder in handen gegeven aan een militair, den kolonel Demmeni*". The resignation of Laging Tobias was, in Van Heutsz's reading, the second great loss of the Atjeh campaign — the loss of the only governor who had both perceived the necessity of the *scheepvaartregeling* (introduced by him in May [[1883]]) and warned, *ongevraagd*, against the *vertoon van zwakheid* (display of weakness) the Concentratie would represent.

## Significance

The Concentratiebesluit of [[19-august|19 August]] [[1884]] is, in Van Heutsz's narrative, the principal *bête noire* of the entire Atjeh record: the *groote politieke fout* (great political mistake) that reversed the [[van der Heijden|Van der Heijden]] conquest of [[groot-atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] and condemned the colony to another fifteen years of war. Its significance in the Aceh Wiki History record lies in five things. First, it was authorised by the secret sessions of the [[staten-generaal|Staten-Generaal]] of [[16 June|16]]–[[17-june|17 June]] [[1884]] — *zonder dat aan de autoriteiten te Atjeh om eenig nader advies was gevraagd* — and thus represents the metropolitan *opperbestuur*'s imposition on the colony of a policy the colony's own authorities had rejected. Second, it was promulgated by [[Van Rees|G.-G. van Rees]]'s *proclamatie* of [[19-august|19 August]] [[1884]], which the Atjehnese read in precisely the opposite sense from its intended one — as proof that the Dutch withdrawal was involuntary, the product of weakness. Third, it produced the abolition of the posts at [[telok-semawe|Telok Semawé]] and [[samalanga|Samalanga]] — the operational centres of the [[Scheepvaartregeling|scheepvaartregeling]] on the Noord- en Oostkust and on the Noordkust respectively — and thus the operational destruction of the *scheepvaartregeling* that [[scherer|Scherer]] had introduced in May [[1883]] under [[p-f-laging-tobias|Laging Tobias]]. Fourth, it eliminated the *controleurs* in the *onderafdeelingen* of [[groot-atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] and transferred their functions to the military commandants — the operational expression of the policy of *non-interventie in de binnenlandsche aangelegenheden van de kuststaten* (non-intervention in the internal affairs of the coastal statelets). Fifth, it was the immediate occasion of the resignation of [[p-f-laging-tobias|Laging Tobias]] — the governor who had both introduced the *scheepvaartregeling* and warned, *ongevraagd*, against the *vertoon van zwakheid* the Concentratie would represent — and thus the second great loss of the Atjeh campaign after the dismissal of Van der Heijden in early [[1881]]. The Concentratie's corollary — the [[Staatsblad No. 105 van 1884|rigorous blockade]] of the entire Atjeh coast from [[Tamiang]] to [[troemon|Troemon]] — was abolished within a year by [[Staatsblad No. 85 van 1885|Staatsblad No. 85 of 1885]], and the Concentratie itself was, in Van Heutsz's counter-factual, the *groote politieke fout* against which "*in de Staten-Generaal zelfs door den gewezen minister [[i-d-fransen-van-de-putte|Fransen van de Putte]] gewaarschuwd was*".

## Brooshooft's Account

Brooshooft describes the concentration decision (*concentratiebesluit*) of [[19-august|19 August]] [[1884]] as the logical consequence of the failure of the offensive campaigns of [[General van der Heijden|van der Heyden]] to produce a lasting pacification and of the financial exhaustion of the Indies treasury. He defends the decision — taken under Governor-General [[O. van Rees|Otto van Rees]] and executed by [[demmeni|Demmeni]] — against the accusation of 'abandonment,' arguing that the concentrated line, though smaller, was more defensible and was a necessary prelude to any later forward policy.
