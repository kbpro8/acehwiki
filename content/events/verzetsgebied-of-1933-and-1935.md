**Verzetsgebied of 1933 and 1935** (1933 and 1935) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. In 1933 and 1935, the area near Geunteuët on the Westkust was a verzetsgebied (resistance area); it is now patrolled by the bivakcommandant of Geunteuët.. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1933]] and [[1935]].

## Account

In [[1933]] and [[1935]], the area near [[Geunteuët]] on the [[Westkust]] was a verzetsgebied ([[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] area); it is now patrolled by the bivakcommandant of [[Geunteuët]].

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
