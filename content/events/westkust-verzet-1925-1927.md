**The Westkust verzet 1925-1927** — the Westkust (West Coast) resistance of [[1925]]-[[1927]] — was the last significant Acehnese armed resistance episode of the inter-war period, centred on the [[Westkust (of Atjeh)|Westkust of Atjeh]] and beginning on [[25 October]] [[1925]]. The resistance is mentioned in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as part of the *nasmeulend verzet* (smouldering resistance) that flared up again on the Westkust in the inter-war period, alongside the [[Communist uprisings of 1926|communist uprisings]] on [[Java]] of [[1926]].

## The beginning (25 October 1925)

"Atjeh's Westkust begon [[25 October]] [[1925]] met..." The Westkust of Aceh began on [[25 October]] [[1925]]. The *Westkust verzet 1925-1927|Westkust verzet]] was the last significant Acehnese armed resistance episode of the inter-war period, seven years after the [[Handover of Aceh to a civil Gouverneur (1918)|handover of 1918]] had politically closed the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]].

## The four months and the sergeant

"Echter, vier maanden later, op [[1 March]] [[1926]] zou een sergeant laten zien..." — however, four months later, on [[1 March]] [[1926]], a sergeant would show... The *Westkust verzet 1925-1927|Westkust verzet]] was met by the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Marechaussee]] patrouilles; the sergeant of [[1 March]] [[1926]] is one of the set-piece figures of the resistance's suppression.

## The end (1927-1931)

"[[1927]] viel, volgden weer..." — 1927 fell, and again followed... The *Westkust verzet 1925-1927|Westkust verzet]] is dated to [[1925]]-[[1927]], but the book also notes that the last of the Westkust resistance fell in [[July 1931|Juli]] [[1931]]: "In [[July 1931|Juli]] [[1931]] viel de laatste." The resistance therefore flickered on for several years after its formal end, with the last resister killed in [[1931]].

## The Sigli and Lho Soekon incidents

The book also mentions the *Sigli|Sigli]] incident — "1925 Sigli kapitein [[Van der Sluys Veer|Van der Sluys Veer]]" — and the *Lho Soekon|Lho Soekon]] incident — "wandelde in [[1933]] in Lho Soekon de..." The *Westkust verzet 1925-1927|Westkust verzet]] was therefore part of a broader cycle of Acehnese resistance in the inter-war period, which included incidents at Sigli and Lho Soekon as well.

## Significance

The *Westkust verzet 1925-1927* is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as the last significant Acehnese armed resistance episode of the inter-war period. The book's argument is that the resistance — beginning in [[1925]], flickering on until [[1931]] — was the *nasmeulend verzet* (smouldering resistance) that flared up again on the Westkust seven years after the political closure of the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]] in [[1918]]. The *Westkust verzet 1925-1927* is therefore the book's marker of the long tail of the Aceh War — the resistance that continued, in flickers, long after the war was politically over.
