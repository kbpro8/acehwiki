**Founding of Sarikat Atjeh and 'Sama mendjadi satoe'** (earlier (c. 1910s-1920s)) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Acehnese associations 'Sarikat Atjeh' and somewhat later 'Sama mendjadi satoe' were founded in earlier years. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to earlier (c. 1910s-1920s).

## Account

Acehnese associations 'Sarikat Atjeh' and somewhat later 'Sama mendjadi satoe' were founded in earlier years. The then-governors put considerable effort into them and strongly encouraged the youth movement that gave the impetus for their founding. All that survives now is a bundle of yellowed letters in the archive — i.e., the associations came to nothing, illustrative of the Acehnese individualism that resists collective organization.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
