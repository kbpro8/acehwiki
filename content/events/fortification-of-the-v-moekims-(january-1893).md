**The fortification of the V Moekims** was the erection, in the first weeks of [[1893]], of *groote versterkingen* (large fortifications) in the Vth Moekims of [[Groot-Atjeh]] by the chiefs [[Tengkoe Mat Amin di Tiro|Toekoe Mat Amin]], Mat Saleh and the [[Toekoe Imam Longbattah|Iman van Longbattah]], as reported by the *Atjehsche Courant* of [[28 January]] [[1893]] and quoted by [[W. A. Coblijn|Coblijn]] in the naschrift of his pamphlet *[[Hoe Atjeh te onderwerpen (Coblijn, 1893)|Hoe Atjeh te onderwerpen]]*.

## The prohibition of contact

The same report stated that the people were forbidden, under the severest penalties, to come within the Dutch lines: for the men the penalty was the cutting off of one of their feet (*het afhakken van een der voeten*), and for the women the cutting off of one of their breasts (*het afsnijden van een der borsten*). Coblijn closed his quotation with the newspaper's own conclusion: *De tegenstand schijnt hoe langer hoe meer een kwaadaardig karakter aan te nemen* — the resistance appears to assume an ever more malignant character.

## Significance

The fortification of the V Moekims is significant in the documentary record of the last years of the *[[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentratie-stelsel]]* as a press testimony of 1893, the year of the brochures of [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] and [[W. A. Coblijn|Coblijn]], that the war party of Groot-Atjeh — the same chiefs of the [[Teungku Mat Amin|Mat Amin]] party who would still lead the resistance of 1896 — was openly preparing new fortified points of resistance and forcibly cutting the population off from the Dutch lines, while in the capital the question of the definitive solution of the Atjeh question was under discussion. Coblijn used the report to close his pamphlet: the Atjehnese would only respect power — *het zwaard* (the sword), without untimely interference of politics, could alone achieve the subjugation.
