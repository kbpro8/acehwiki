**Atjeh expedition (1873–1876)** was a series of Dutch military operations conducted by the [[Korps Mariniers]] and the Royal Netherlands East Indies Army against the Sultanate of [[Atjeh (Aceh)]]. The expedition marked the opening phase of the protracted [[Aceh War]] and involved amphibious landings, river patrols, and the establishment of temporary camps in [[Groot Atjeh]]. Uniforms of the marines during this period are illustrated in the source (see page 471).  

## Chronology  

- **[[1873]]** – Initial naval engagements and reconnaissance missions along the Aceh coast.  
- **[[1875]]** – Intensified ground operations, including the **Atjeh campaign (26 December 1875 – 4 September 1876)**.  
- **[[1876]]** – Continued fighting in the interior; the campaign concluded with a temporary cease‑fire.  

## Operational Highlights  

Uniforms of the marines from 1873‑1876 are depicted in the illustrated plates (page 471). The *Map of Groot Atjeh* (c. [[1873]]) was employed for planning bivouac sites (see [[Bivouacs on Groot Atjeh]]).
