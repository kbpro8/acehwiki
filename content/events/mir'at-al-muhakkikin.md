**The *Mirʾāt al-muḥaqqiqīn*** ("The Mirror of the Verifiers") is a Malay Sufi treatise by [[Shaikh Shamsuddin of Sumatra|Shamsuddin of Pasai]], the pantheistic mystic who dominated the religious life of the court of [[Sultan Iskandar Muda|Iskandar Muda]] of [[Acheh]] until his death in [[1630]]. The work is one of the principal sources for the [[Wujudiyyah]] mysticism against which [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|Nuruddin ar-Raniri]] directed his polemic after his arrival in Acheh in [[1637]]; it is cited and refuted in the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān fī maʿrifat al-adyān*]].

## Content and character

The *Mirʾāt al-muḥaqqiqīn* sets out the doctrine of the unity of being (*waḥdat al-wujūd*) in the form developed by Shamsuddin of Pasai from the heritage of [[Hamzah Pansuri]]. The title — "the mirror of the verifiers" — indicates the Sufi genre to which the work belongs: a manual of *taḥqīq* ("verification"), the highest station of the gnostic (ʿārif) in which the mystic, having traversed the stations of the path, attains to a direct vision of the divine reality. The *Mirʾāt al-muḥaqqiqīn* is, in genre, a systematic exposition of the metaphysics of the unity of being, drawing on the technical vocabulary of Ibn ʿArabian Sufism and on the Malay poetic tradition of Hamzah Pansuri's school.

## Raniri's refutation

The *Mirʾāt al-muḥaqqiqīn* is named, together with Shamsuddin's *[[Chirkah|Chirkah]]* (or *Harakah*, as [[C.A.O. van Nieuwenhuijze|Van Nieuwenhuijze]] prefers to read it), among the works of [[Shaikh Shamsuddin of Sumatra|Shamsuddin of Pasai]] that Raniri refutes in the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān*]] (p. 108 of the Voorhoeve facsimile). Raniri's refutation is part of the central section of the *Tibjān* on the Wujūdiyyah, which also contains his refutation of the *[[Asrar al-'arifin|Asrār al-ʿārifīn]]* and the *[[Kitab al-Muntahi|Kitāb al-Muntahī]]* of [[Hamzah Pansuri]]. The juxtaposition is significant: Raniri treats Shamsuddin of Pasai as the direct continuator of Hamzah Pansuri, and his works as forming, with those of Hamzah, the canon of the Wujūdiyyah mysticism against which the orthodox reaction of [[Iskandar Thani]] was directed.

## Place in Shamsuddin's oeuvre

The *Mirʾāt al-muḥaqqiqīn* is, with the *[[Chirkah|Chirkah]]*, one of the two works of Shamsuddin of Pasai named by Raniri in the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān*]]. Both works belong to Shamsuddin's activity at the court of [[Sultan Iskandar Muda|Iskandar Muda]] in the first three decades of the seventeenth century. The *Mirʾāt al-muḥaqqiqīn* was composed in the *[[Bahasa Djawi]]* of the Achehnese court and forms, with the works of Hamzah Pansuri, the principal surviving corpus of the Wujūdiyyah mysticism of Acheh's golden age.

## Significance

The *Mirʾāt al-muḥaqqiqīn* is one of the principal monuments of the Wujūdiyyah mysticism of [[Acheh]] and one of the principal sources for the doctrines against which Raniri's orthodox reaction was directed. Its refutation in the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān*]] is one of the chief documents of the religious controversy that defined the transition from the age of [[Sultan Iskandar Muda|Iskandar Muda]] to that of [[Iskandar Thani]] and [[Sultan Tajul-alam|Taj al-Ālam]], and its citations by Raniri preserve the only extended fragments of Shamsuddin's text that survive in polemical sources.
