**The Conquest of Kota Alam (13 November 1874)** was the operation by which [[Kolonel Ruempol|overste Ruempol]], at the head of a half-battalion and an artillery section, carried the south-eastern part of [[Lemboe]] known as [[Kota Alam]] — at the bend of the [[Atjeh-rivier]] where the stream turns eastward — and established a post which, armed with two 12 c.M. [[Voorlaadkanonnen in Atjeh|voorlaadkanonnen]], enfiladed the river upstream and brought the enemy stelling of [[Longbatta]] and the densely populated [[III Moekims]] under shellfire.

## The object

Ruempol's aim was threefold: to take the kampong of Kota Alam at the river bend, to enfilade the Atjeh-rivier upstream from it, and to shell the enemy stelling of Longbatta and the III Moekims on the right bank. The Atjehneezen had had a full week since the [[Conquest of Lemboe (7 November 1874)|fall of Lemboe]] to prepare, and the Dutch expected stiff resistance.

## The assault

On [[13 November]] [[1874]] Ruempol marched out. Contrary to expectation, the enemy offered only weak resistance. Kota Alam itself cost little; but during the fortification work the Dutch were fired on from all sides, and a column again had to be pushed out on [[14 November]] [[1874]] to drive the Atjehneezen back. By evening the post was defensible; a company was left as garrison, and two 12 c.M. voorlaadkanonnen were mounted.

## Borel's verdict

The day's cost — more than twenty dead and wounded — was, in Borel's view, slight for what was gained. Kota Alam, once armed, gave the Dutch command of the river bend and a platform against Longbatta, which Pel had long meant to attack. The conquest is also notable as the first appearance in the Atjeh theatre of Ruempol, who would later take [[Blang Tjoet]] on [[18 January]] [[1875]]. Borel notes, however, that the voorlaadkanonnen mounted at Kota Alam were of the same obsolete pattern that van Swieten had allowed to remain in Atjeh after recalling the [[Achterlaadkanonnen van 12 c.M.|achterlaadkanonnen]] to Java, and whose *tijdbuis*-shells too often failed to burst on the wet, covered Atjehnese terrain. The post at Kota Alam would form an eastern link of the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]] completed in [[Completion of the defensive ring around Kota Radja (May 1875)|May 1875]].
