**The Conquest of Langkroek (28 September 1874)** was the surprise seizure by [[Kolonel Wiegand|overste Wiegand]] — acting on a plan drawn up by [[Generaal Pel|kolonel Pel]] — of the kampong of [[Langkroek]], which leaned against the middle of the [[Pedir-dijk]] between [[Penajoeng]] and [[Kota Radja Bedil|K. R. Bedil]], and which the Atjehneezen had heavily fortified as the linchpin of their line on the right bank of the [[Atjeh-rivier]]. The victory, Borel writes, was "of great importance" and "cost almost nothing" — but Borel's praise is qualified by Wiegand's failure to press on at once to [[Lampriet]].

## Pel's plan, Wiegand's execution

After several weeks of forced inactivity Pel resolved on a stroke. The north side of Langkroek was known to be heavily fortified — a continuous line of works armed with light guns — but intelligence suggested the works were thinly held at dawn. Pel's plan was to take the kampong by surprise before the enemy, streaming in from Pinang and the [[XXVI Moekims]], could arrive in force. Execution was entrusted to Overste Wiegand.

## The assault

In the early morning of [[28 September]] [[1874]] Wiegand's column struck. The works were un- or weakly held, and were carried before the Atjehneezen could resist. Three new posts were at once erected around the kampong, anchoring the line on the dike.

## Borel's criticism of Wiegand

Wiegand, having taken Langkroek, should have pressed on at once to seize [[Lampriet]] — the kampong between Langkroek and Pinang, "like an oasis in the *sawah*" — which could have been had without a fight, since the 12 c.M. shells of the Dutch batteries had already criss-crossed its terrain. Lampriet was in fact taken without resistance on [[4 February]] [[1875]], by [[Majoor Romswinckel|Romswinckel]] — more than four months later. The delay, Borel argues, was Wiegand's fault. The same lack of drive would mar Wiegand's conduct at the [[Battle of Longbatta (31 December 1874)|battle of Longbatta]] on [[31 December]] [[1874]], when his column lost its way in the *nipa-bosch* and failed to support the hard-pressed colonne of [[Majoor van der Meer|van der Meer]].
