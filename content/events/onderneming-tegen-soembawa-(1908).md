**Onderneming tegen Soembawa ([[1908]])** was the undertaking against [[Bima]] and the island of [[Soembawa]] between [[16 February]] and [[9 May]] [[1908]], the closing piece of [[H. N. A. Swart]]'s service in the eastern part of the archipelago.

Swart made "the apparently more than reckless undertaking against Bima and Soembawa with a handful of *[[Corps of Military Constabulary of Atjeh and Dependencies|military constabulary]]* into a classic example of swift and conclusive pacification." By royal decree of [[2 September]] [[1909]] no. 41 he was promoted Commander of the [[Militaire Willemsorde]] for his distinction in the operations on Soembawa in that period. The *1ste luitenant* der *marechaussee* ("military constabulary") [[J. H. C. Vastenou]], Knight 4th Class of the M.W.O. and after his death entered in the registers as Knight 3rd Class, fell on Soembawa on [[19 February]] [[1908]]; his grave monument stands in the cemetery at [[Makassar]]. Rear-Admiral [[C. de Vos van Steenwijk|C. Baron de Vos van Steenwijk]] distinguished himself as *Luitenant ter Zee* on the expedition and was decorated with the M.W.O. 4th class.
