**The Completion of the defensive ring around Kota Radja (May 1875)** was the linking-up, in the first days of May [[1875]], of the various Dutch posts into an unbroken line running from [[Blang Tjoet]] on the left bank of the [[Atjeh-rivier]] westward over [[Longbatta-Mesigit]], Lohong, [[Lamara]], Gitjiel, [[Setee]], Lamteboe, Poengej Blang Tjoet, [[Poe Oe]], [[Soerian]] and [[Blang Oe]] to the sea-strand at [[Oeleh Leh]] — the line thereafter known as the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]] — and the immediate granting to [[Generaal Pel|generaal Pel]] of three months' leave, on [[12 June]] [[1875]], for the recovery of his shattered health.

## The line

The desired connection between the various posts had been obtained. The Dutch now possessed an unbroken line running from Blang Tjoet on the left bank of the Atjeh-rivier, westward over Longbatta-Mesigit, Lohong, Lamara, Gitjiel, Setee, Lamteboe, Poengej Blang Tjoet, Poe Oe, Soerian and Blang Oe to the sea-strand at Oeleh Leh. K. Radja was now completely covered on south and east as well, forming the purposeful central position it should have been from the beginning. With this achieved, operations could be suspended or postponed.

## Pel's leave

On [[12 June]] [[1875]] Pel — exhausted, after "ruim een jaar" of command in which he had restored the position from *allerdonkerendst* state — was granted three months' leave to recover his health at Buitenzorg in Java. Borel praises Pel's "doorzicht, beleid en rusteloos streven" and declares that "het vaderland was hem daarvoor veel dank schuldig." Even the strongest constitution, he adds, would have succumbed in Atjeh.

## Borel's verdict

For Borel the completion of the ring is a vindication and an indictment at once. A vindication, because Pel had carried through, against the inherited [[Afwachtende houding|afwachtende houding]] and with inadequate troops, what [[Jan van Swieten|van Swieten]] should have done in February [[1874]]. An indictment, because the same line — the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]] — had been obtainable "a year earlier" at far lower cost, had van Swieten not abandoned the captured ground. The ring was strong but, as the [[Night attack on Longbatta-Mesigit (22 November 1875)|night attack of 22 November 1875]] and the [[Enemy breakthrough in the IV, VI and IX Moekims (June 1878)|breakthrough of June 1878]] would prove, not impregnable. Borel would have preferred fewer, stronger posts and a real *mobiele colonne* always ready behind the line — the doctrine he develops in his last chapters and in his March [[1878]] article *[[Een vaderlandsch belang (Borel, 1878)|Een vaderlandsch belang]]*.
