**Expedition against Tamiang (1893)** was a Dutch marine operation targeting the [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] region, launched to quell insurgent activity that threatened Dutch control of the eastern Sumatra coast and the neighboring sultanate of [[Atjeh]].

Commanded by **Heuter**, the expedition comprised a detachment of the [[Royal Netherlands Marine Corps|Dutch Marine Corps]] and a small fleet of gunboats. After securing the coastal town of [[Kuala Tamiang]], the force conducted inland sweeps that forced the rebels to disperse.

## Outcome
The operation succeeded in temporarily stabilising the frontier, but sporadic resistance persisted until the early twentieth century.
