**Erection of the gravestone of Sultan Iskandar II** took place in [[1641]] in [[Acheh]], with [[Nuruddin ar-Raniri]] acting together with a number of high court functionaries.

Voorhoeve uses this dated ceremonial role as firm evidence that Raniri was fully integrated into the Achehnese elite by the early 1640s, before his departure in [[1644]].
