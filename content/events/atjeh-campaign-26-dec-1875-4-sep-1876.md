**Atjeh campaign (26 December 1875 – 4 September 1876)** was a focused Dutch offensive conducted by the [[Korps Mariniers]] against fortified positions in the interior of [[Atjeh (Aceh)]]. The operation is documented in R. J. J. P. Cambier’s monograph *De mariniers te Atjeh van 26 December 1875 tot 4 September 1876* and referenced in the *Atjeh Marineblad* (1890‑1891).  

## Campaign Phases  

- **Landing (26 December 1875)** – Marines disembarked at the coastal base of [[Banda Aceh]] and moved inland.  
- **Bivouacs (January‑March 1876)** – Temporary camps were established in [[Groot Atjeh]], described as “*Bivaks op Groot Atjeh*” (page 471).  
- **Final Assault (April‑June 1876)** – Coordinated attacks on rebel strongholds, culminating in a cease‑fire on [[4 September 1876]].  

## Documentation  

Cambier’s detailed narrative (see page 473) provides day‑by‑day accounts, while the *Atjeh Marineblad* (1890‑1891) publishes official reports and casualty lists.
