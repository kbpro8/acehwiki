**The punitive expedition to Batoe-Mengkoerah of 19 November 1896** was a Dutch field operation mounted under the command of [[A. E. van Kappen|Kapitein A. E. van Kappen]] of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|marechaussée corps]] against the grottoes of the [[Batoe Mergati|Batoe-Mengkoerah]] hill in the [[XXII Moekims|XXII-Moekim federation]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]] on [[19 November]] [[1896]]. The expedition was one of the principal marechaussée operations of the second semester of the [[1896]] campaign and the principal Dutch attempt to break the hostile bands that had gathered in the grottoes of the [[Batoe Mergati|Batoe-Mengkoerah]] hill.

## The operations (19 November 1896)

The expedition was launched on [[19 November]] [[1896]]. The *Tweede Deel* of *[[ATJEH '96 (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96]]* records that the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|marechaussée corps]] under [[A. E. van Kappen|Kapitein Van Kappen]] advanced to the top of the [[Batoe Mergati|Batoe-Mengkoerah]] hill in the early morning of [[19 November]], where a new *pondok* (hut) was found but no enemy. At about six o'clock the enemy opened fire from a neighbouring hilltop, and the marechaussée returned fire. The marechaussée then explored the grottoes of the hill, in which a quantity of arms and ammunition was captured.

Kruisheer records that *uit de grotten der Batoe Mergati bracht men tal van voorladers en donderbussen te voorschijn. T. Tjoet-Banta haalde er zelfs* — *from the grottoes of the Batoe Mergati many muzzle-loaders and blunderbusses were brought out. T. Tjoet-Banta even fetched* — confirming that the grottoes had been used as an arms cache by the hostile bands. The collaboration of [[Teukoe Nja Banta|T. Nja Banta]] — the *sagihoofd* of the [[XXVI Moekims|XXVI-Moekim federation]] and one of the most co-operative of the [[Uleebalang|hoeloebalangs]] — illustrates the combined Dutch-Acehnese operation against the resistance caches.

## Significance

The [[Punitive expedition to Batoe-Mengkoerah (19 November 1896)|tuchtiging of Batoe-Mengkoerah of 19 November 1896]] was one of the principal marechaussée operations of the second semester of the [[1896]] campaign and the principal Dutch attempt to break the hostile bands that had gathered in the grottoes of the [[Batoe Mergati|Batoe-Mengkoerah]] hill. The expedition demonstrated the marechaussée's capacity for operations in the broken country of the interior; it captured a quantity of arms and ammunition; and it confirmed that the grottoes of the [[Batoe Mergati|Batoe-Mengkoerah]] hill had been used as an arms cache by the hostile bands.
