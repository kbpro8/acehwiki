**Enlargement of the Meuseugit Raja** (between 1899 and c. 1938) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. In 1899 Prof. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to between [[1899]] and c. [[1938]].

## Account

In [[1899]] [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Prof. Snouck Hurgronje]] called the [[Meuseugit Raja]] at [[Koeta-Radja|Koeta Radja]] 'een [[Snouck Hurgronje's ledig monument verdict|ledig monument onzer dwaasheid]]' and condemned its construction as one of the many expensive mistakes that marked the beginning of Dutch Aceh involvement. Almost 40 years later (c. [[1938]]), the [[meuseugit]] had been enlarged with two wings, and on high days the space was still too small to hold all who came to fulfil their religious duties — a sign, the author argues, of how times have changed.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
