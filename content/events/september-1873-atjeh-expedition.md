**September 1873 Atjeh Expedition** was the follow‑up Dutch marine operation to the earlier April [[1873]] landing, intended to reinforce Dutch authority in [[Atjeh]] and to press the remaining rebel forces into submission.

The expedition departed from the Dutch colonial port of [[Batavia]] in September [[1873]] and landed near the same coastal sector as the April force. It brought additional artillery and engineering units, enabling the construction of a more permanent garrison at [[Kuta (Atjeh)|Kuta]].

## Outcome
Although the September force secured a larger area than its predecessor, resistance persisted, leading to a prolonged campaign that lasted into the mid‑1870s.
