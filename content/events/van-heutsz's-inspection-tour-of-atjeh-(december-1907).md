**Van Heutsz's inspection tour of Atjeh (December 1907)** was the visit that governor-general [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] — at the request of minister [[D. Fock|Fock]] — undertook to Atjeh in December [[1907]] in response to the [[Avondpost "Wekeer" articles (October 1907)|"Wekeer" articles]] of October [[1907]]. The inspection was undertaken, in [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]'s own formulation, "opdat ook het publiek overtuigd worde van datgene waarvan ook de gouverneur-generaal overtuigd is, dat wij in Atjeh op de goede weg zijn" — so that the public too may be convinced of that of which the governor-general is already convinced, that we are on the right path in Atjeh. The inspection proved otherwise.

## The October 1907 provocation

In October [[1907]] *[[De Avondpost]]* had published the [[Avondpost "Wekeer" articles (October 1907)|"Wekeer" articles]] — a series by an anonymous *maréchaussée*-officer from Atjeh exposing needless cruelties committed against the Atjehnese population, "toegelaten zo niet aangemoedigd door [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]]". Minister [[D. Fock|Fock]] had advised the chambermembers not to waste their time on these articles; the governor-general, he said, had full confidence in [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]], and both he and [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] considered reinforcement of the troop strength in Atjeh unnecessary.

## The inspection

Pressed by the minister, [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] undertook an inspection tour of Atjeh in December [[1907]]. The official purpose was confirmatory: to convince the public that the [[Netherlands]] was "op de goede weg" — on the right path — in Atjeh. The inspection itself, however, revealed the untenability of [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]]'s administration. In the same month [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] submitted his [[Van Daalen's resignation (December 1907)|resignation]].

## The June 1908 report

In [[June 1908|June]] [[1908]] the [[Van Heutsz's report on Atjeh (June 1908)|report of the governor-general]] was published. Its verdict — "Hij heeft het beginsel van de pacificatie miskend. Hij heeft een verkeerde politiek gevoerd tegenover de hoofden, aan wie een onheuse bejegening ten deel viel. Hij heeft het goedgezinde deel der bevolking tussen twee vuren geplaatst" — is the official confirmation that the inspection had reversed both the [[D. Fock|Fock]] judgement of October and the [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] confidence of [[1905]].

## Significance

The [[Van Heutsz's inspection tour of Atjeh (December 1907)|December 1907 inspection tour]] is the immediate political-military hinge of the post-[[Van Daalen's Gajo-Alas-Batak expedition (1904)|1904 expedition]] period. Its consequence — the [[Van Daalen's resignation (December 1907)|resignation of Van Daalen]] and the [[Van Heutsz's report on Atjeh (June 1908)|June 1908 report]] — installed the new course of the [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacificatie]].
