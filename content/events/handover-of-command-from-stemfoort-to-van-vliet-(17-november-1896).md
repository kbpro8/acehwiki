**The Handover of Command from Kolonel Stemfoort to Kolonel Van Vliet** on [[17 November]] [[1896]] — *overgave en overname van het bestuur, Kolonel J. W. Stemfoort keert naar Java terug* — was the formal transfer of the office of [[Civiel en Militair Gouverneur]] van [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] from [[J. W. Stemfoort|Kolonel J. W. Stemfoort]] to [[C. P. J. van Vliet|Kolonel C. P. J. van Vliet]], on which occasion Stemfoort returned to [[Batavia|Java]]. The handover — which took place nine days after the [[Arrival of Van Vliet as Civiel en Militair Gouverneur (8 November 1896)|arrival of Van Vliet at Koeta-Radja]] on [[8 November]] [[1896]] — closed Stemfoort's second tenure (which had lasted four months and six days, from [[11 July]] to [[17 November]] [[1896]]) and opened the Van Vliet governorship, which would last until [[1898]], when Van Vliet would be succeeded by [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]].

## The Closure of Stemfoort's Second Tenure

The handover of [[17 November]] [[1896]] closed Stemfoort's second tenure as *wd. Civiel en Militair Gouverneur* — a tenure which had begun on [[11 July]] [[1896]], when Stemfoort had been recalled to Koeta-Radja after the [[Death of Generaal-Majoor De Moulin (7 July 1896)|death of Generaal-Majoor De Moulin]] by sunstroke. Stemfoort's second tenure had been characterised by the slow, methodical extension of Dutch control from the *geconcentreerde linie* (concentrated line) into the [[XXVI Moekims]], [[XXII Moekims]], [[IV Moekims]] (Kroeng-Raba / Lok-Nga), and the Selimoen highlands — a *pacificatie* (pacification) which had included the [[Occupation of Kroeng-Raba (Lok-Nga) (15 July 1896)|occupation of Kroeng-Raba (Lok-Nga)]] on [[15 July]] [[1896]], the [[Construction of Blang Bintang Camp (7 September 1896)|construction of the Blang Bintang camp]] on [[7 September]] [[1896]], the [[First Selimoen Expedition (6–11 September 1896)|first Selimoen expedition]] of [[6 September|6]]–[[11 September]] [[1896]], the introduction of the [[Pasenstelsel (September–October 1896)|pasenstelsel]] in mid-September, the [[Temporary Occupation of Indrapoeri (28 October 1896)|temporary occupation of Indrapoeri]] on [[28 October]] [[1896]], and the [[Capture of Kota Ba Teuē (28 October 1896)|capture of Kota Ba Teuē]] on the same day. The handover of [[17 November]] [[1896]] thus marked the formal closure of the post-De Moulin transitional period and the opening of the Van Vliet governorship.

## The Return of Stemfoort to Java

The handover of [[17 November]] [[1896]] was accompanied by Stemfoort's return to Java — *Kolonel J. W. Stemfoort keert naar Java terug*. Stemfoort — who had first come to Atjeh in April [[1896]] as the commander of the Dutch expeditionary reinforcements in response to the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (29 March 1896)|Umar defection]], and who had served as *wd. Civiel en Militair Gouverneur* for two tenures (28 June – 5 July [[1896]] and 11 July – 17 November [[1896]]) totalling nearly five months — departed for Java with the formal thanks of the Governor-General and the satisfaction of having overseen the slow forward movement of the second semester of [[1896]]. His departure marked the end of his direct involvement in the Atjeh command, and the beginning of the Van Vliet governorship.

## The Opening of the Van Vliet Governorship

The handover of [[17 November]] [[1896]] opened the Van Vliet governorship — a period which would last until [[1898]], when Van Vliet would be succeeded by [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]. The Van Vliet governorship was characterised by the continuation of the *pacificatie* (pacification) policy of the second semester of [[1896]] — the slow, methodical extension of Dutch control beyond the *geconcentreerde linie* — but was increasingly constrained by the *heerschende cholera* (prevailing cholera) of December [[1896]], which prevented the *colonne tegen Oemar* (column against Umar) from taking the field. The Van Vliet governorship was thus a transitional period — between the *pacificatie* (pacification) of the second semester of [[1896]] and the decisive *korte oorlog* phase which would be launched by Van Heutsz in [[1898]].

## Significance

The Handover of Command from Stemfoort to Van Vliet on [[17 November]] [[1896]] is significant in the documentary record of *ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel* as the formal closure of the post-De Moulin transitional period and the opening of the Van Vliet governorship. The handover — which took place nine days after the [[Arrival of Van Vliet as Civiel en Militair Gouverneur (8 November 1896)|arrival of Van Vliet]] on [[8 November]] [[1896]] — closed Stemfoort's second tenure and opened the period which would last until [[1898]], when Van Vliet would be succeeded by Van Heutsz. The handover thus marks a critical transition in the documentary record of *ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel* — from the *Hoofdstuk III* of the second semester under Stemfoort to the *Hoofdstuk IV* of November–December [[1896]] under Van Vliet — and prepares the ground for the *Besluit* (Conclusion) and the extensive *Bijlagen* (Annexes) which follow.
