**The *Kitāb al-Muntahī*** ("The Book of the Ultimate") is a Malay Sufi treatise by [[Hamzah Pansuri]], the celebrated pantheistic mystic of [[Acheh]]. It is the principal work of the school of Wujūdiyyah mysticism against which [[Raniri|Nuruddin ar-Raniri]] directed his polemic in the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān fī maʿrifat al-adyān*]] and the [[Hudjdjat as-siddiq li-daf az-zindiq|*Ḥujjat aṣ-ṣiddīq li-dafʿ az-zindīq*]]. The *Kitāb al-Muntahī* is one of the few works of Hamzah Pansuri to survive in Malay manuscripts, in Malay fragments, and in a Javanese translation; its text and refutation are the subject of an extended collation in the apparatus of [[P. Voorhoeve|Voorhoeve]]'s [[1955]] edition of Raniri's two Malay treatises.

## Content and character

The *Kitāb al-Muntahī* sets out, in the form of a Sufi commentary on the stages of the mystical path, the doctrine of the unity of being (*waḥdat al-wujūd*) in its most extreme Achehnese form. The very title — "the book of the ultimate" — points to the doctrine of the *fanaʾ* (passing-away) and the *baqāʾ* (subsistence) by which the gnostic attains the ultimate state in which the mystic declares, with [[al-Hallaj|al-Ḥallāj]], *anā al-ḥaqq* ("I am the Truth"). The treatise develops the metaphysics of the *maqāmāt* (stations) and *aḥwāl* (states) in a Malay of considerable poetic density, drawing on the technical vocabulary of Arabic Sufism and on Persian Sufi verse.

## Raniri's refutation

The longest and most detailed of Raniri's polemical engagements with the Wujūdiyyah is his refutation of the *Kitāb al-Muntahī*. In the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān*]] (pp. 97–112 of the Voorhoeve facsimile) Raniri cites the *Kitāb al-Muntahī* literally — section by section — and refutes each citation in turn. Voorhoeve's apparatus to this section (pp. 35–36 of the introduction, and notes ad pp. 97–105 of the facsimile) collates Raniri's citations with the surviving manuscripts of the *Kitāb al-Muntahī* itself, and so provides, in effect, a critical edition of those portions of Hamzah's treatise that Raniri chose to refute.

Notable in Raniri's refutation is his attention to a passage in which Hamzah, in the language of [[Pasai]] — that is, in the Achehnese Malay of Pasai — plays on the words *kuphō* (Arabic *kufr*, "unbelief") and *kuphu* (Arabic *kufʾ*, "equal, like"). The play, Voorhoeve notes, turns on the fact that *pada* in Achehnese means both "equal" (Javanese *pepada*) and "sufficient" (Malay *pada*). Hamzah's utterance — "if there is no *kuphō*, there is no meeting with *kuphu*" — Raniri takes as a covert profession of unbelief; Voorhoeve suggests that Hamzah's saying hangs together with a Persian verse in the *Kitāb al-Muntahī* on "the gate of the perishable world," and that Hamzah's intended meaning was that the highest mystical state is not attained so long as the gate of the perishable world is not closed. The word *kufʾ*, Voorhoeve drily notes, was naturally a tempting target for Raniri's attack.

## Manuscripts and editions

Voorhoeve collated the following manuscripts of the *Kitāb al-Muntahī*:

- **F** — Cod. Or. 7291(3), the Malay text.
- **G** — Cod. Or. 1952, a Malay fragment of the same text.
- **J** — Cod. Or. 5716(2) and 7736(1), the Javanese translation (cf. *BKI* 107, p. 358).

These manuscripts make it possible to recover the text of the *Kitāb al-Muntahī* in those passages that Raniri chose to cite, and in some further passages that are transmitted in F alone.

## Significance

The *Kitāb al-Muntahī* is the most considerable surviving work of [[Hamzah Pansuri]] and the principal monument of the [[Wujudiyyah]] mysticism of [[Acheh]] in its golden age. Its refutation by Raniri in the [[Tibjan fi ma'rifat al-adyan|*Tibjān*]] is the longest single polemical engagement in Raniri's Achehnese writings; and the collation of the surviving manuscripts of the *Kitāb al-Muntahī* with Raniri's citations — effected by Voorhoeve in his apparatus — is the most exact reconstruction of a lost work of Malay Sufism that the sources permit. The work, in its survival and in its refutation, is the central document of the religious controversy that defined the transition from the age of [[Sultan Iskandar Muda|Iskandar Muda]] to that of [[Iskandar Thani]] and [[Sultan Tajul-alam|Taj al-Ālam]].
