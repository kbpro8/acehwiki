**The Enemy breakthrough in the IV, VI and IX Moekims (June 1878)** was the crisis that broke upon the Dutch establishment in the second half of June [[1878]] — barely had the [[Karel van der Heijden|gouverneur van Atjeh, kolonel van der Heijden]], left [[Groot-Atjeh]] with most of the mobile *strijdmacht* to *tuchtigen* the vassal coastal state of [[Gedoeng]] — when the Atjehneezen attacked the Dutch *linie*, broke through at several places, fell into the [[IV Moekims|IV]], [[VI Moekims|VI]] and [[IX Moekims|IX]] *Sagi*s, drove out the pro-Dutch population, plundered and burned, cut communication between [[Kota Radja|K. Radja]] and several posts, and produced what Borel calls the "gruwelijke moordtooneel in een van onze hospitalen."

## Background

In January [[1878]] the *toestand* in Atjeh had been *bevredigend*; several *hoofden* had submitted. In the second half of June [[1878]] van der Heijden marched with most of the mobile *strijdmacht* to *tuchtigen* [[Gedoeng]] — a vassal coastal state outside [[Groot-Atjeh]] — for the support it had given the enemy with *hulpbenden*, weapons and *levensmiddelen*. Gedoeng was *tuchtiged* twice; after the second, the chief laid down his *bestuur* and his son came in to submit. But the absence of the mobile force from Groot-Atjeh opened the door.

## The breakthrough

The enemy attacked the Dutch *linie*, broke through at several places, fell into the IV, VI and IX Moekims, *verjoeg* the pro-Dutch *bevolking*, plundered and burned, *sneed* the *gemeenschap* between K. Radja and several posts, and produced *schrik* and *verwarring*. In the course of the crisis an atrocity was committed in one of the Dutch hospitals — the "gruwelijke moordtooneel" Borel cites as one of the *wrange vruchten* of the passive *stelsel*. Van der Heijden had to rush back *in aller ijl* and *met geweld* restore communication with the posts in the IV Moekims. New battalions were sent from Java.

## Borel's verdict

The Dutch eventually drove the enemy out everywhere and even made a successful *inval* in the [[XXII Moekims]] to punish them — but, Borel insists, the *verloopen* of the friendly population could not be undone: they had lost *vertrouwen* in Dutch protection. The episode proves, for Borel, that posts alone cannot prevent a *linie-doorbraak*; the mobile force must never be reduced. It is the same lesson he had drawn from the [[Night attack on Longbatta-Mesigit (22 November 1875)|night attack on Longbatta-Mesigit]] and from the [[Atoeh catastrophe (13 February 1876)|Atoeh catastrophe]], and it is the practical argument behind his [[Indische Brigade (proposed)|Indische Brigade]] proposal of March [[1878]].
