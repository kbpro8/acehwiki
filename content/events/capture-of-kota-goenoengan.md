The **Capture of Kota Goenoengan** occurred on [[12 January]] [[1874]] when Dutch forces under Colonel [[K. Schultze]] stormed the western redoute complex protecting the [[Kraton of Aceh]].

## Detailed Narrative

On [[11 January]] [[1874]], intelligence from [[Teuku Nja]] alerted General [[Jan van Swieten]] that [[Tuanku Hashim]] was mounting heavy artillery at [[Kota Goenoengan]] to flank Dutch positions at [[Mesjid Raya Aceh]].

At 5:00 AM on [[12 January]] [[1874]], Colonel Schultze marched with two half-battalions, mountain guns, and sappers toward [[Kota Rentang]]. Deploying artillery 200 meters from the enemy lines, the 9th Battalion executed a left-flank movement through jungle thickets, forcing Acehnese defenders out of Kota Rentang.

Continuing the advance, Schultze's troops reached Kota Goenoengan at 11:30 AM. Sappers cut openings through thorn hedges, enabling two infantry columns to storm the fort at noon. The capture of Kota Goenoengan eliminated the western defense sector of the royal palace, paving the way for the final assault on the [[Kraton of Aceh]].
