**Founding of Samoedra-Pasè** (first 12 centuries CE) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Foundation of a city and dynasty somewhere in the first twelve centuries of our era on the East coast of Atjeh, becoming the kingdom of Samoedra-Pasè. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to first 12 centuries CE.

## Account

Foundation of a city and dynasty somewhere in the first twelve centuries of our era on the [[Pase / Pasei|East coast]] of Atjeh, becoming the kingdom of [[Samoedra-Pasè]]. Likely has its origin in Hindu contact.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
