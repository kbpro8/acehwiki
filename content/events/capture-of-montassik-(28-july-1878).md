**The Capture of Montassik** on [[28 July]] [[1878]] was the taking by the Dutch third column under the governor [[K. van der Heyden|Major-general K. van der Heyden]] of the [[missigit Montassik|missigit Montassik]], a fortified mosque that served as the principal arsenal of [[Habib Abdoerrahman|Habib Abdurrahman]] in the [[XXII Moekims|XXII Moekims]] of [[Acheh|Atjeh]], in the opening operation of the [[Campaign against the XXII Moekims (1879)|campaign in the XXII Moekims]] of [[23 July]] [[1878]] – [[23 March]] [[1879]]. With the taking of Montassik the initial objective of the operations was attained.

## Background

Information had been received that [[Habib Abdoerrahman|Habib Abdoerrahman]] had gathered at [[Montassik|Montassik]] in July [1878] a great quantity of food and war-material and was planning thence to make a new incursion into the [[IV Moekims|IV Moekims]]. Van der Heyden therefore decided to forgo the planned expedition to [[Pasanga|Pasanga]] and to enter with all available forces the still independent and hostile territory of the [[XXII Moekims|XXII Moekims]], to seek the enemy in his own arsenal at Montassik. On [[23 July]] [1878] the important campaign began with three columns.

## The Capture

While the first column advanced from Lambaroe over Lamtenga and the [[missigit Anak Bate|missigit of Anak Bate]] towards Montassik, the second column, which had secured the river-crossing at [[Lamkali|Lamkali]], and the third, which had set out from [[Oleh Karang|Oleh Karang]], reached on [[25 July]] [1878] the strong [[Senelop|Senelop]] north of Montassik. When the first storming by the second column had been repulsed and the soldiers — mostly Frenchmen and Belgians — could not be moved to a second, the vanguard of the third column, a company of Africans, came up to help and drove the enemy from the parapet; the benting was taken. On [[28 July]] [1878] the third column, after a powerful bombardment by the artillery, took the [[missigit Montassik|missigit Montassik]], and with this the initial objective of the operations was attained.

## Significance

The neighbouring kampongs were still hostile, and Dutch transports were continually threatened. The enemy did not yet seem inclined to submit. "It was therefore considered desirable to push further on to [[Indrapoeri|Indrapuri]] with its famous missigit. For this, however, more data about that region had first to be collected, and as the rainy season was again approaching, one had for the time being to confine oneself to holding the positions taken, with preparation for later operations." The campaign continued from [[1 August]] [1878] to [[23 March]] [1879] in a system of "standfast, reconnoitre, punish," and led, after the [[Submission of Habib Abdurrahman (13 October 1878)|submission of Habib Abdurrahman on 13 October 1878]], to the [[Capture of Indrapuri (24 March 1879)|capture of Indrapuri on 24 March 1879]].
