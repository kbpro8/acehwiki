**The V.V.R.O.S. delegation to Kotaradja** was a visit on 29 August [[1950]] by a delegation of the [[V.V.R.O.S.]] (the Association of Plantation Companies on the East Coast of Sumatra) together with six representatives of the companies with interests in [[Atjeh|Atjeh]] to [[Kotaradja]], for further discussions with a commission formed by the governor of Atjeh.

## Outcome

The discussions resulted in the release of approximately 40 of the roughly 50 enterprises in Atjeh, of which 20 were immediately taken into management. This was a significant step in the rehabilitation of the Atjeh plantation economy following the hostilities. [[Said Abubakar]] was appointed as manager (*beheerder*) of one of the returned enterprises, with technical advice provided by [[C. A. Kleingeld]] of the Sipef, who remained for several months at [[Langsar Estate]].
