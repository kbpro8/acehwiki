The **Establishment of the post at Lam Koenjit** on [[4 July]] [[1894]] created a very temporary intermediate post on the road between [[Tjot Goeë]] and [[Biloel]] in [[Groot-Atjeh]], directed against the attacks of [[T. Rajoet|Toekoe Rajoet]], *[[Imam|imam]]* of the [[III Moekims Daroe]].

## Circumstances

The road connecting [[Tjot Goeë]] and [[Biloel]] had been very unsafe at first. Transports marching along it often suffered from the enemy and were obliged to push part of the covering troops forward to take post at [[Lam Koenjit]] and, with volleys into the *kampongs* (villages) where the enemy lay concealed, make possible the advance of the remainder. For that reason it was decided to erect a very temporary intermediate post there against Toekoe Rajoet's attacks; *den 4den Juli 1894 vond dit plaats*.

## Strategic effect

Together with [[Biloel]] at the foot of the steep southern mountains and [[Tjot Goeë]] further north, the new post also served to separate the [[XXII Moekims]] more effectively from those of the [[IX Moekims]].
