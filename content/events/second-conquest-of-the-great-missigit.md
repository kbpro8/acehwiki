**Second Conquest of the Great Missigit** was the renewed assault on [[Atjeh's Groote Missigit]] executed on [[14 April]] [[1873]] during the [[First Aceh Expedition]]. The operation was marked by intense combat, heroic sacrifices, and the tragic death of General [[J.H.R. Köhler]].

## Preparation and Assault
After the withdrawal on [[10 April]] [[1873]], the Dutch forces regrouped. On the morning of [[14 April]] [[1873]], Major [[F.P. Cavaljé]] again led the attack, commanding the entire [[3e Bataljon Infanterie]] along with support from the [[9e Bataljon Infanterie]] and the artillery. The attack was executed in closed columns to counter the Acehnese klewang (sword) charges. Captains like [[W. Pordon]], [[C.H. Visscher]], [[J. Lauer]], and [[J.H. Huijer]] led their respective companies against the mosque's walls.

## Heroic Actions and Tragedies
The fighting at the walls was exceptionally brutal. Captain [[J.H. Huijer]] and his men from the [[9e Bataljon Infanterie]] engaged in desperate ladder combat. A defining moment occurred when Gunner [[C.H. Kool van Heerens]] climbed a ladder to throw a grenade into the Acehnese positions, successfully detonating it but receiving a fatal lance thrust to his throat in the process. 

During the broader engagement on this day, General [[J.H.R. Köhler]] was struck and killed by an Acehnese sniper while observing the battle from near a prominent banyan tree, which later became known as the [[Generaal-Köhler-boom]]. Despite the severe losses, the Dutch forces successfully retook the mosque by overwhelming the defenders through coordinated infantry and artillery action.
