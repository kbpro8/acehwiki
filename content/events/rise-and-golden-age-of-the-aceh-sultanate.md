**Rise and Golden Age of the Aceh Sultanate** (1500–1650) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. The years 1500–1550 are the period of rise of the Aceh sultanate. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1500]]–[[1650]].

## Account

The years [[1500]]–[[1550]] are the period of rise of the Aceh [[sultanate]]. [[1550]]–[[1650]] was the golden age of Aceh, the time of the greatest of all sultans, [[Iskandar Moeda]]. After his reign, four female sultans ascended the throne, marking a period of deep decline.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
