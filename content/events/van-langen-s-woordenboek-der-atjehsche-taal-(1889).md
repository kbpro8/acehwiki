**Van Langen's *Woordenboek der Atjehsche taal*** ("Dictionary of the Atjehnese Language") is a comprehensive dictionary of the Acehnese language compiled by [[K. F. H. van Langen]], published at The Hague in [[1889]] in octavo format.

## Content

Van Langen's dictionary was the standard lexicographical reference for the Acehnese language during the colonial period. It was offered in [[1902]] by Martinus Nijhoff at 3.50 florins. Van Langen also published a companion volume, the *Handleiding voor de beoefening der Atjehsche taal* ("Guide for the Study of the Atjehnese Language"), also in [[1889]], priced at 1.75 florins, which included a reading book (*leesboek*). Together, these two works provided the essential linguistic toolkit for Dutch officials and scholars working in Atjeh.

## Source

Offered by Martinus Nijhoff, The Hague, in *A List of the Best Books Relating to Dutch East India* ([[1902]]).
