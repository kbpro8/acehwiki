**Restoration of civil administration in Atjeh** (1918) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. In 1918, civil administration was returned to a civilian (non-military) official. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1918]].

## Account

In [[1918]], [[Binnenlandsch Bestuur (B.B.)|civil administration]] was returned to a civilian (non-military) official. Practically all of Atjeh had by then developed growing trust in the honest intentions of the formerly hated Dutch intruder. The Acehnese now travel freely through their great land, trade, and can keep what they earn; find work with [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Gouvernement|the Gouvernement]] and on private large-scale agricultural enterprises (grootlandbouwbedrijf) founded in later years.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
