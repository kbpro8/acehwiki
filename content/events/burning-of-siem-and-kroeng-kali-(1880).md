The **Burning of Siem and Kroeng Kali** was the outcome of a Dutch column's advance into the northern part of the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]] in [[1880]], in which two days of fighting were required before the *kampongs* (villages) could be approached and set alight.

## The action

Hooijer recorded that the column which in [[1880]] had to penetrate the northern part of the XXVI Moekims *had daar reeds een krachtig verzet ondervonden en eerst na twee dagen strijd de kampongs Siem en Kroeng Kali kunnen naderen en in vlammen doen opgaan* — had met a strong resistance there and could approach and burn the *kampongs* of [[Siem]] and [[Kroeng Kali]] only after two days of fighting.

## Aftermath

When the population that had fled returned after some time, the head appointed over it by the Dutch was murdered by [[Toekoe Nja Hassan]]. The post that [[General van der Heijden]] afterwards caused to be erected at Kroeng Kali remained a thorn in the enemy's eye and proved so great a point of attraction for the ill-disposed that a later governor resolved to slight its walls and withdraw the garrison from that advanced position.
