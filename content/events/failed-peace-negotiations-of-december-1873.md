**Failed peace negotiations of December 1873** was the unsuccessful Dutch effort to obtain a settlement during the [[Second Dutch Expedition to Aceh]] before the assault on the [[Baiturrahman Grand Mosque|Missigit]].

## Course

The Dutch dealt through [[Mas Soemoe Widikdjo]], who requested information about their forces and armament. The failure left both sides preparing for further fighting and became central to [[Jan van Swieten]]’s later argument that political communication had been mishandled.

## Significance

The episode belongs to the continuing [[Aceh War]] and the contested consequences of the [[Capture of the Kraton of Aceh]].
