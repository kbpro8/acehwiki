**Berglijntje Keude Breue–Seulimeum** — the *berglijntje* (mountain line) built in the years [[1904]]–[[1908]] from [[Keude Breue]] over the hills to [[Seulimeum]] — was the engineering project that connected the second tramway of the [[Atjeh's Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh Dependencies]] with the old tramway of [[Groot-Atjeh|Great Atjeh]], thereby forming the integrated [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]] network as a single operational whole. In the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, the completion of the berglijntje is identified as the moment at which "the military administration had created a powerful instrument for achieving the complete [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacification of Atjeh]]."

## Account from *De Buitenbezittingen* (1917) — Berglijntje Keude Breue–Seulimeum

The Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account of the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]] explicitly frames the [[Berglijntje Keude Breue–Seulimeum|berglijntje]] as the engineering keystone of the integrated Atjeh-tram network. The Bureau's narrative runs as follows: the second tramway in the [[Atjeh's Onderhoorigheden|Dependencies]] — built from [[1899]] onward from [[Sigli]] to [[Langsa]] and from [[Sigli]] westward over [[Padang Tiji|Padang Tidji]] to [[Keude Breue]] — was "a piece of tramway in the Dependencies, somewhat hanging in the air" (*wel wat in de lucht hangende, stuk trambaan in de Onderhoorigheden*), that is, a line without direct connection to the [[Groot-Atjeh|Great Atjeh]] network. The [[Berglijntje Keude Breue–Seulimeum|berglijntje]] of [[1904]]–[[1908]] supplied this missing connection:

> "Dit, wel wat in de lucht hangende, stuk trambaan in de [[Atjeh's Onderhoorigheden|Onderhoorigheden]] werd in de jaren [[1904]]–[[1908]] door een berglijntje van [[Keude Breue]] over de heuvels naar [[Seulimeum]] met de oude trambaan in [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] verbonden, waardoor het militaire bewind een machtig middel had geschapen om te geraken tot de volledige [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacificatie]] der doorsneden streken."

The Bureau's account also includes an illustrative plate of the berglijn section of the Atjeh-tram near the [[Aloer-Pantja-viaduct|Aloer-Pantja viaduct]] — together with an illustrative plate of the bridge over the [[Lam Temot-rivier|Lam Temot river]] — identifying these as the two principal engineering works of the entire Atjeh-tram network. The berglijntje is thus both the strategic and the engineering keystone of the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]] in the Bureau's account.

## Significance

The [[Berglijntje Keude Breue–Seulimeum|berglijntje of 1904–1908]] is, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, the engineering keystone of the integrated [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]] network and the strategic instrument of the [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacification of Atjeh]]. Its completion in [[1908]] closed the geographic gap between the [[Groot-Atjeh|Great Atjeh]] and [[Atjeh's Onderhoorigheden|Dependencies]] halves of the Atjeh railway system, and gave the military administration the means to complete the pacification of the intersected regions.
