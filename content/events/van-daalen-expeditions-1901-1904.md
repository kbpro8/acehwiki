**Van Daalen Expeditions** in [[1901]] and [[1904]] were Dutch military campaigns into interior of north Sumatra to bring [[Gajo-land]] and [[Alaslanden]] under Dutch authority and end involvement of Gajos and Alassers in Atjeh war.

## Narrative

Without busy contact with Atjehers, people of north Sumatra interior already early under highness Atjeh sultans come. Once subject, [[Gajo]] and [[Alas]] had to show help to Atjeh princes as soon as in trouble, and from this derives that interior people, Mohammedan besides, was involved in weapon violence of Atjehers against Netherlands government. Around [[1900]] still case. Time that end came.

Hence expeditions of [[Van Daalen]] in [[1901]] and [[1904]]. Order placed on affairs, but only in [[1911]] state entered that may bear name pacification. Bestuur-work strengthened by presence few bivouacs, spanned only ten years. Department [[Gajo- en Alaslanden]] formed [[1905]], in [[1908]] already parceled among other administrative areas, but in [[1922]] restored with hoofdplaats [[Takengon]], counting underafdeelingen [[Takengon]], [[Gajoe Loeës]], [[Serbödjadi]] and [[Alaslanden]].

[[Van Daalen]] in [[1904]] also began construction [[Gajo-weg]] with much *heerendienst* and money.

In [[Gajo-land]], according to Broersma, name Van Daalen alone makes Gajo'er run.
