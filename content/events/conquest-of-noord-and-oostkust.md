**Conquest of Noord- and Oostkust** (1898-1899) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. In 1898 and 1899, important parts of the kingdom on the Noord- and Oostkust were permanently subdued. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1898]]-[[1899]].

## Account

In [[1898]] and [[1899]], important parts of the kingdom on the Noord- and [[Atjeh's Oostkust (East Coast of Aceh)|Oostkust]] were permanently subdued. A new spirit inspired everyone, from high to low. Many names would remain linked to deeds of loyalty, courage, and sacrifice.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
