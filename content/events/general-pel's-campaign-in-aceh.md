**General Pel's Campaign in Aceh** was the series of aggressive interior military offensives directed by General [[j-l-j-h-pel|J. L. J. H. Pel]] of the [[indische-leger|Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] between December [[1875]] and spring [[1876]], which broke out of the defensive perimeter around [[dalam|Kraton of Aceh]] to invade the agricultural heartlands of the [[iv-moekims|IV Moekims]], [[vi-moekims|VI Moekims]], and [[22-moekims|XXII Moekims]].

## Narrative of the Opening Offensive (December 1875)

On [[26-december|26 December]] [[1875]], General [[j-l-j-h-pel|J. L. J. H. Pel]] mobilized a strike force from an available garrison of 7,012 men. Resolving to punish Acehnese forces for harassing Dutch supply convoys near [[kuta-alam|Kotta Alam]] and [[oleh-karang|Oleh Karang]], Pel ordered two battalions of auxiliary troops ([[barissans-of-bangkallan|Barissans of Bangkallan]]), the [[6e-bataljon-infanterie|6th Infantry Battalion]], and artillery to execute the [[attack-on-mibouw|Attack on Mibouw]].

Following the capture of Mibouw, column commander [[kolonel-meijer|Lieutenant Colonel Meijer]] led the [[derde-bataljon-(atjeh-bataljon)|3rd Infantry Battalion]], the [[8th-infantry-battalion|8th Infantry Battalion]], and the [[marine-corps-in-aceh|Marine Corps in Aceh]] in a dramatic mountain advance during the [[crossing-of-blankala-pass|Crossing of Blankala Pass]], driving Acehnese defenders out of the [[iv-moekims|IV Moekims]].

## The March through the XXII Moekims (February 1876)

In February [[1876]], General [[j-l-j-h-pel|J. L. J. H. Pel]] expanded the offensive into the territory of [[panglima-polem|Panglima Polem]]. On [[13-february|13 February]] [[1876]], a combined force comprising the [[derde-bataljon-(atjeh-bataljon)|3rd Infantry Battalion]], [[12e-bataljon-infanterie|12th Infantry Battalion]], [[13th-infantry-battalion|13th Infantry Battalion]], and Madurese auxiliaries launched the [[march-through-the-xxii-moekims|March through the XXII Moekims]]. 

As documented in *Atjeh gedurende de tweede expeditie en later* ([[1876]]), Dutch columns systematically destroyed bentengs and granaries across the sawahs ([[sawah-cultivation-and-supply-in-aceh|Sawah Cultivation and Supply in Aceh]]).

## Historiographical Significance

In contemporary debates, anonymous pamphleteers like "Brutus" ([[open-letter-by-brutus-to-general-knoop|Open Letter by Brutus to General Knoop]]) used General [[j-l-j-h-pel|J. L. J. H. Pel]]'s offensive successes in [[1875]] and [[1876]] to disparage General [[jan-van-swieten|J. van Swieten]]'s earlier cautious strategy. 

However, veteran analysts demonstrated that [[j-l-j-h-pel|J. L. J. H. Pel]]'s advance was possible only because [[jan-van-swieten|J. van Swieten]] had already established secure coastal bases at [[pekan-badak|Pekan Badak]] and [[sinangri|Sinangri]], and that Pel's speed was still restricted by the [[transport-and-coolie-system-in-aceh|Transport and Coolie System in Aceh]].

## Brooshooft's Account

Brooshooft credits [[J. L. J. H. Pel|Pel]] with consolidating the Dutch position at [[koeta-radja|Koeta-Radja]] after [[Jan van Swieten|Van Swieten]]'s departure in [[april|April]] [[1874]] and with building the fortified perimeter (*stelling-Pel*) that was to protect Kota Radja through the late 1870s. Under Pel's command the Dutch systematically reduced the fortified belt around the town, fighting actions at [[Battle of Garouw (28 June 1874)|Garouw]], [[Battle of Soerian (26 July 1874)|Soerian]], [[battle-of-lampoe-oek-(16-april-1874)|Lampoe-oek]] and [[Battle of Longbatta (31 December 1874)|Longbatta]]; by [[may|May]] [[1875]] thirty-eight posts ringed the town, enclosing the central province of Groot-Atjeh and part of the XXV Moekims. Pel's death from cholera on [[24-february|24 February]] [[1876]] at the age of forty-six — worn out by incessant work, recurrent fever and massive doses of quinine — is treated by Brooshooft as a severe blow to the Dutch cause.
