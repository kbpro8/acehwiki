The **Battle of Bital** occurred on [[12 February]] [[1874]] during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] when a Dutch expeditionary column led by Lieutenant-Colonel [[Karel van der Heijden]] assaulted fortified Acehnese positions at Kampong Bital to protect the allied chief [[Teuku Nja]].

## Detailed Narrative

In early [[February]] [[1874]], [[Teuku Nanta]], chieftain of the VI Mukims, destroyed the bridge connecting his territory with Marassa and built stockades at Bital to attack [[Teuku Nja]] for his neutrality.

At 5:30 AM on [[12 February]] [[1874]], Lieutenant-Colonel Van der Heijden marched from [[Peunayong]] with eight half-battalions, a *Barisan* half-battalion, mountain guns, and cavalry. The column advanced along the left bank of the Kroeng Daroe to Lamtemen before turning west to flank Bital.

Coming under heavy artillery fire that disabled a Dutch field gun, infantry units advanced across open ground to within 50 paces of the stockade. At 2:00 PM, Dutch troops executed a bayonet charge, storming Bital and routing Teuku Nanta's garrison. Dutch forces suffered 2 killed and 15 wounded before returning to Peunayong at 10:30 PM.
