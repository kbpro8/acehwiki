**Sultan Iskandar Moeda's reign and conquests** (1607 onward) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. A century after Sultan Ali Moegajat Sjah, in 1607, Groot-Atjeh got a man of great format: Sultan Iskandar Moeda, who took power at age 17. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1607]] onward.

## Account

A century after [[Sultan Ali Moegajat Sjah]], in [[1607]], [[Groot-Atjeh]] got a man of great format: [[Sultan Iskandar Moeda]], who took power at age 17. During his reign he took possession of half the coastal area of Sumatra and an equal portion of the [[Malay Peninsula|Malacca peninsula]]. He is reputed to have organized/introduced the '[[Nanggroe / Familie-stelsel (institution)|nanggroe]]' ([[Nanggroe / Familie-stelsel (institution)|family system]]) in Atjeh - the very ancient [[Nanggroe / Familie-stelsel (institution)|family system]]. Demanded great organization and regnal talent. The period [[1550]]-[[1650]] is the [[Golden Age of Atjeh (1550-1650)|glanstijdperk]] (golden age).

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
