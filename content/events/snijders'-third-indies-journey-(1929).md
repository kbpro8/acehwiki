**Snijders' third Indies journey** ([[September 1929|September]]–[[December 1929|December]] [[1929]]) was the retired [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Generaal C. J. Snijders]]'s third return to [[Nederlandsch-Indië|Nederlandsch-Indië]], undertaken as gedelegeerde van den Raad van Bestuur of the [[K.N.I.L.M.|K.N.I.L.M.]] to negotiate with the Indies government over modifications to the [[K.N.I.L.M.|K.N.I.L.M.]] statutes and route. The journey resulted in the opening of the [[K.N.I.L.M.|K.N.I.L.M.]] Weltevreden–Surabaya line and the first west-to-east Java flight on [[1 November]] [[1 November]] [[1929]], in Snijders' presence.

## The Background

In the course of [[1929]] certain difficulties had arisen in the exploitation of the [[K.N.I.L.M.|K.N.I.L.M.]], particularly concerning a desired modification to the statutes and to several parts of the route, which necessitated further discussions with the Indies government. The person originally intended for this task was unable to undertake it owing to illness. The Raad van Bestuur of the [[K.N.I.L.M.|K.N.I.L.M.]], of which Snijders had become a member in [[1928]], asked him to undertake the negotiations as its delegate.

## Acceptance and Voyage

Snijders initially hesitated on grounds of health. His doctor, however, declared that he need not object to the journey provided that some moderation was observed in the participation in festivities. Snijders accepted, and sailed on [[19 September]] [[19 September]] [[1929]] — exactly one year after his departure for the second Indies journey — arriving at [[Tanjung Priok|Tanjung Priok]] on [[12 October]] [[12 October]], as before.

## The Negotiations and the West-to-East Java Flight

Discussions at [[Bandoeng|Bandoeng]] with the Director of the Department of Gouvernementsbedrijven reached their goal. On [[1 November]] [[1 November]] [[1929]] Snijders attended the opening of the [[K.N.I.L.M.|K.N.I.L.M.]] Weltevreden–Surabaya line and the first west-to-east Java flight.

## Further Engagements and Return

At [[Semarang|Semarang]] in November Snijders again attended the *jaarfeest* of the Nationale Bond "Het Mobilisatiekruis". At [[Medan|Medan]] a slight attack of dysentery reminded him of his doctor's warning, and he declined a reception by the Vereeniging van Nederlandsch-Indische Officieren on Sumatra's Oostkust. After a short stay at Medan he undertook the return journey to the [[Netherlands|Netherlands]], arriving safely in late December [[1929]].
