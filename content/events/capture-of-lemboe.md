**Capture of Lemboe** was a tactical military engagement fought in early [[1874]] during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]], in which columns of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] under General [[J. van Swieten]] captured the fortified Acehnese redoubt of [[Lemboe]] near the [[Baiturrahman Grand Mosque]].

## Narrative of the Assault

As Dutch forces advanced from coastal beachhead toward [[Kraton of Aceh]], Acehnese defenders concentrated their forces at the fortification of [[Lemboe]], which commanded the western approaches to the mosque and royal palace. 

In January [[1874]], General [[J. van Swieten]] deployed infantry battalions supported by field artillery to assault the position. 

In his [[1876]] defense of Van Swieten's generalship, the anonymous author of *Atjeh gedurende de tweede expeditie en later* emphasized that when Dutch troops stormed Lemboe and the Mesigiet, they discovered that the fortifications would not have been found in a defensive state had the army arrived even a few days earlier, validating Van Swieten's deliberate operational timing.
