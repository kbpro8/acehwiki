**De tocht van Overste van Daalen door de Gajo-, Alas- en Bataklanden (8 Febr.–23 Juli 1904)** — *"The march of Colonel van Daalen through the Gayo, Alas, and Batak lands (8 February–23 July 1904)"* — is an article by **[[J. Alting van Geusau|J. Alting van Geusau]]**, published in [[1939]] in *Indisch Militair Tijdschrift* (Netherlands Indies Military Journal), No. 30, p. 593 *e.v.*, with illustrations, recording the [[Colonne van Daalen naar de Gajolanden en Alaslanden (1904)|campaign of Colonel (Overste) van Daalen]] through the [[Gajoland|Gayo]], [[Alaslanden|Alas]], and [[Bataklanden|Batak]] lands of the central Sumatran interior between [[8 February]] and [[23 July]] [[1904]]. The article is recorded as No. 566 in [[H. van Meurs|Van Meurs]]'s *[[Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939 (Van Meurs, 1940)|Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939]]* (The Hague, [[1940]]), in the Sumatra chapter.

## Subject

The article's subject, as recorded by Van Meurs, is the *tocht* — the march, the campaign — of **Overste** (Colonel) [[Gotfried Coenraad Ernst van Daalen|van Daalen]] through the [[Gajoland|Gajo-]], [[Alaslanden|Alas-]], and [[Bataklanden|Bataklanden]] in the year [[1904]]. The dates given — [[8 February]] to [[23 July]] [[1904]] — fix the campaign's duration at approximately five and a half months. The campaign — the [[Colonne van Daalen naar de Gajolanden en Alaslanden (1904)|Colonne van Daalen]] of the corpus's existing entry — was the military *zegetocht* (victorious campaign) by which the Gayo and Alas highlands of central Sumatra were brought under Dutch control, completing the pacification of the interior of the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]]. The campaign is treated at length elsewhere in the corpus, from the perspectives of *[[De Buitenbezittingen|De Buitenbezittingen]]* (1917) and of *Dispereert Niet* (1946); Geusau's [[1939]] article is an independent retrospective account published in the principal military journal of the Netherlands Indies.

The inclusion of the **Bataklanden** in the article's title — alongside the Gajo and Alas lands — extends the scope of the account beyond the Gayo-Alas campaign proper, which in the corpus's existing entry is treated as a Gajo-Alas expedition. The Bataklands lie to the south and east of the Gayo-Alas highlands, and van Daalen's column, in its traverse of the interior, passed through or near Batak territory on its way toward [[Tarotoeng|Tarotoeng]] in [[Tapanoeli|Tapanoeli]], which the column reached on [[18 June]] [[1904]] according to the *Dispereert Niet* account. The Geusau article thus appears to treat the full traverse of the column from its starting point in [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] through the Gajo and Alas lands and into the Batak country of [[Tapanoeli|Tapanoeli]].

## Author and venue

[[J. Alting van Geusau|Geusau]] — full name J. Alting van Geusau — published the article in *Indisch Militair Tijdschrift* (Netherlands Indies Military Journal), No. 30, p. 593 *e.v.*, with illustrations (*Met ills.*). The *Indisch Militair Tijdschrift* was the principal professional military journal of the Netherlands Indies, and the choice of venue signals the article's intended audience: serving and former officers of the [[K.N.I.L.|Koninklijk Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger]] with an interest in the campaigns of the preceding generation. The article's appearance in [[1939]] — thirty-five years after the campaign — places it in the late-colonial retrospective phase, when the Atjeh-oorlog was passing from living memory into institutional history.

## Place in the 1939 Literatuur-Overzicht

The article is No. 566 in the Sumatra chapter (Hoofdstuk III, Nos. 548–658) of Van Meurs's survey, where it stands as the sole G-letter entry of the Sumatra chapter. It is immediately followed by No. 568 — the [[De tocht van Overste van Daalen door de Gajo-, Alas- en Bataklanden 1904 (Harders, 1939)|separate account of the same campaign]] by [[De tocht van Overste van Daalen door de Gajo-, Alas- en Bataklanden 1904 (Harders, 1939)|De tocht van Overste van Daalen door de Gajo-, Alas- en Bataklanden 1904 (Harders, 1939)]], published in the *Orgaan van de Nederlandsch-Indische Officiersvereeniging* in [[1939]]. The double recording of the campaign — by Geusau in the *Indisch Militair Tijdschrift* and by Harders in the officers' association journal — marks [[1939]] as a year in which the van Daalen campaign of [[1904]] was the subject of retrospective military-historical writing in two distinct professional venues.

## Significance

For AcehWiki, the article is significant as a [[1939]] retrospective on the [[Colonne van Daalen naar de Gajolanden en Alaslanden (1904)|van Daalen campaign of 1904]], published in the principal military journal of the Netherlands Indies, and as an independent source for the campaign that complements the corpus's existing accounts — the [[colonne-van-daalen-1904|colonne-van-daalen-1904]] entry (drawing on *[[De Buitenbezittingen|De Buitenbezittingen]]* and *Dispereert Niet*) and the [[Gotfried Coenraad Ernst van Daalen|van Daalen biographical entry]]. The article's inclusion of the Bataklanden in the title extends the scope of the campaign account, and the precise dates ([[8 February]]–[[23 July]] [[1904]]) furnish an independent confirmation of the campaign's duration. The article also complements the [[De tocht van Overste van Daalen door de Gajo-, Alas- en Bataklanden 1904 (Harders, 1939)|Harders account]] (No. 568) of the same campaign.
