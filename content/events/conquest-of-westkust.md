**Conquest of Westkust** (1900) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. In 1900 the Westkust followed in being conquered. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1900]].

## Account

In [[1900]] the [[Westkust]] followed in being conquered. Strong and just administration was instituted.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
