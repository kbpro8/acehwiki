**Van Kol's 1902 traverse of Atjeh** was the journey undertaken in [[1902]] by the social-democratic MP [[H. B. van Kol|Van Kol]] through [[Acheh|Atjeh]] "van Noord tot Zuid" — from north to south. The traverse gave [[H. B. van Kol|Van Kol]]'s subsequent parliamentary speeches an authority no other social-democratic or liberal critic of the [[Atjeh-oorlog ([[1873]]-[[1904]])|Atjeh-oorlog]] could match; his certification that the [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacificatie]] had progressed further than he had supposed — and that the "gruwelen" — the horrors — of the war were already past — was almost immediately dispelled by the [[Van Daalen's Gajo-Alas-Batak expedition (1904)|expedition of Van Daalen]] of [[1904]].

## The traverse

In [[1902]] [[H. B. van Kol|Van Kol]] — social-democratic member of the [[Tweede Kamer]], former colonial civil servant — travelled through Atjeh from north to south. The traverse was unusual for a parliamentary critic of the war; most chambermembers relied on official colonial reports and the "ambtelijke adviezen" — official advices — that minister [[D. Fock|Fock]] would later be accused of hiding behind.

## The certification

On his return [[H. B. van Kol|Van Kol]] reported to the [[Tweede Kamer]] that the [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacificatie]] was further advanced than he had anticipated and that the "gruwelen" — the horrors — of the [[Atjeh-oorlog ([[1873]]-[[1904]])|Atjeh-oorlog]] belonged to the past. The certification is, in the [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] narrative, the type-specimen of the parliamentary illusion of approaching peace.

## The 1904 dispelling

The illusion was almost immediately dispelled by the [[Van Daalen's Gajo-Alas-Batak expedition (1904)|expedition of Van Daalen]] to the [[Gajoland|Gajoe-]], [[Alasland|Alas-]] and [[Bataklanden|Bataklanden]] in [[1904]]. In the [[1904]] [[Tweede Kamer]] debate on the expedition — in which, according to the official figures, 2,922 inlanders (including 1,149 women and children) had been killed "in weinige dagen" — [[H. B. van Kol|Van Kol]] declared it "erger dan de dagen van Alva's schrikbewind" — worse than the days of Alva's reign of terror. The contrast between the [[1902]] certification and the [[1904]] denunciation is the measure of the shock that the [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] expedition administered to the parliamentary critics of the war.

## Significance

[[Van Kol's 1902 traverse of Atjeh|The 1902 traverse]] is the principal first-hand parliamentary inspection of Atjeh in the [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]]-period. The contrast between [[H. B. van Kol|Van Kol]]'s [[1902]] optimism and his [[1904]] denunciation is the type-specimen of the parliamentary shock at the [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] expedition.
