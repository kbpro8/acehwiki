The **Battle of the Tiban Line** occurred on [[14 December]] [[1873]] during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]], when Dutch assault columns stormed the main coastal defense line built by [[Tuanku Hashim]].

## Detailed Narrative

The Tiban Line was an extensive 1,800-meter field fortification system comprising earthworks, palisades, and log redoutes manned by 3,000 to 4,000 Acehnese defenders under [[Tuanku Hashim]].

At 7:00 AM on [[14 December]] [[1873]], Major-General [[G.M. Verspijck]] deployed three half-battalions and mountain guns in battle formation before the eastern redoutes. Supported by naval gunfire from offshore warships, Dutch infantry launched a frontal bayonet charge.

After fierce hand-to-hand combat, Dutch troops captured the central redoute at Kota Perak, suffering 8 killed and 39 wounded while Acehnese defenders left 120 dead inside the works. On [[15 December]] [[1873]], Acehnese forces evacuated the remaining positions, allowing Dutch units to reach the mouth of the Aceh River.
