**Acehnese attack on Penajong (1874)** was an Acehnese attack on the Dutch camp at [[Penajong]] after the capture of the [[Kraton of Aceh]].

## Course

It occurred amid the uncertain aftermath of the court’s flight, the escape of followers of [[Mas Soemoe Widikdjo]], and Dutch attempts to turn military success into political adhesion. The event demonstrated that occupation of the Kraton had not removed Acehnese capacity for organized attack.

## Significance

The episode belongs to the continuing [[Aceh War]] and the contested consequences of the [[Capture of the Kraton of Aceh]].
