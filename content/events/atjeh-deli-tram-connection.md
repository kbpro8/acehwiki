**The Atjeh-Deli Tram Connection** was the planned and partially realized junction between the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]] (Staatstramwegen in Atjeh, narrow gauge [[1 January|1 January 1917]] 1,067 mm) and the [[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij|Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij]] network (standard gauge 1,435 mm) at [[Bookit Koeboe|Bookit Koeboe]] between [[Besitang|Besitang]] and [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]], intended to create continuous rail transport between the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] and the [[Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra|Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra]] cultivation zone. The connection remained incomplete as of [[1918]] due to gauge incompatibility and unresolved financing.

## Background

The [[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij|Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij]] (D.S.M.), founded [[28 June]] [[1883]] as a spin-off of the Deli Maatschappij, built a standard-gauge network radiating from [[Medan|Medan]] north to [[Pangkalan Brandan|Pangkalan Brandan]] (via [[Tandjoeng Poera|Tandjoeng Poera]]) and south to [[Tandjoeng Balai|Tandjoeng Balai]]. By [[1915]] the main line Tebing Tinggi–Tandjoeng Balai (94 km) and the Deli-Towa extension to Batoe were complete, bringing the northern terminus to [[Pangkalan Brandan|Pangkalan Brandan]].

Simultaneously, the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]] (narrow gauge) expanded southeast from [[Koeala Simpang|Koeala Simpang]] through the [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] region toward [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]] (Pangkalan Susu), an oil-shipping port on the [[Aroebai|Aroebaai]] where the [[Bataafsche Petroleum Maatschappij|Koninklijke Petroleum Maatschappij]] maintained tank installations. The [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]] reached [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]] by [[1918]].

## The Bookit Koeboe junction

At [[Bookit Koeboe|Bookit Koeboe]], between [[Besitang|Besitang]] and the [[Aroebai|Aroebaai]] endpoint, the two systems were to meet. The [[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij|D.S.M.]] began construction of the section [[Pangkalan Brandan|Pangkalan Brandan]]–[[Bookit Koeboe|Bookit Koeboe]]; the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]] built toward the same point from the north. Because the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]] used narrow gauge (1,067 mm) and the [[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij|D.S.M.]] standard gauge (1,435 mm), a dual-gauge (three-rail) track was required on the shared section from the junction to [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]], allowing both systems to serve the oil port.

## Gauge incompatibility and operational plan

The [[1918]] *Dienstregeling* of the [[Koninklijke Paketvaart Maatschappij|Koninklijke Paketvaart Maatschappij]] notes the gauge difference explicitly: "De Atjehtram heeft smalspoor, de Delitram breedspoor, zoodat op het gemeenschappelijke baanvak 3 rails noodig zijn." Through-running was impossible; freight and passengers would transfer at [[Bookit Koeboe|Bookit Koeboe]] or [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]]. The [[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij|D.S.M.]] hoped the connection would draw northern cultivation-area traffic (rubber, tobacco) to its port at [[Belawan|Belawan-Deli]] rather than to [[Penang|Penang]] or [[Singapore|Singapore]].

## Status in 1918

- [[Pangkalan Brandan|Pangkalan Brandan]]–[[Besitang|Besitang]] section: complete and metalled by [[1918]] (simple sand surfacing).
- [[Besitang|Besitang]]–[[Bookit Koeboe|Bookit Koeboe]] section: "goed is aangevat" (well underway) but not finished.
- [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]] to [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]]: operational; tank steamers already calling.
- [[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij|D.S.M.]] three-rail section to [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]]: "binnenkort tot stand komt" (soon to be realized).
- Missing link [[Pangkalan Brandan|Pangkalan Brandan]]–[[Besitang|Besitang]]: the only remaining gap in overland post/telegraph connection between the two *gouvernementen*; post still traveled this segment by auto.

## Economic and strategic motives

The [[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij|D.S.M.]] saw the link as a *voeringslijn* (feeder) to pull [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh]] and northern [[Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra|Oostkust]] produce to [[Belawan|Belawan]]. The Handelsvereeniging Medan argued that [[Belawan|Belawan]]'s ocean-harbor development (Plan Nobel) made the connection essential. The [[Bataafsche Petroleum Maatschappij|Koninklijke Petroleum Maatschappij]] at [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]] benefited from dual rail access for oil evacuation. The colonial government viewed the junction as completing the *groote Atjehsche postweg* extension into the cultivation zone.

## Abandoned southern extension plans

The [[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij|D.S.M.]] originally planned a line from Kisaran via Bandar Poelau and Merbau to Kota Pinang on the Baroemoen (upper Panai/Bila), connecting eventually to a projected Trans-Sumatran state railway via Goenoeng Sahilan and Taloek. After feasibility studies showed doubtful profitability even for the Kisaran–Bandar Poelau section, the company *deze plannen thans geheel los gelaten* (abandoned these plans entirely) in favour of the Siantar–Panahatan–Balige lake connection.

## Significance

The Atjeh-Deli Tram Connection represents, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, the principal infrastructural link between the two colonial *gouvernementen* of northern Sumatra — a link whose gauge incompatibility forced the three-rail compromise at [[Bookit Koeboe|Bookit Koeboe]] and whose missing [[Pangkalan Brandan|Pangkalan Brandan]]–[[Besitang|Besitang]] section remained, as of [[1918]], the sole gap in overland communication between [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh]] and [[Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra|Oostkust]]. The connection's completion would have integrated the [[Aroebai|Aroebaai]] oil port and the [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] cultivation zone into the [[Belawan|Belawan]]-centred export economy — the same economic reorientation that the [[Aroe-baai-plannen|Aroe-baai-plannen]] of [[1913]] had sought, from the Atjeh side, to forestall.
