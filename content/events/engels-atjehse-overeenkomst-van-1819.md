**Engels-Atjehse overeenkomst van 1819** (1819) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. England made an agreement with the Sultan of Atjeh in 1819, by which control of Atjeh's foreign relations came entirely to England. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1819]].

## Account

[[Engeland (England)|England]] made an agreement with [[Sultan of Aceh|the Sultan]] of Atjeh in [[1819]], by which control of Atjeh's foreign relations came entirely to [[Engeland (England)|England]]. This complicated subsequent Dutch-English relations over Atjeh.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
