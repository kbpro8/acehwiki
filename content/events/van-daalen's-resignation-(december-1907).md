**Van Daalen's resignation (December 1907)** was the act by which [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] — governor and military commander of [[Acheh|Atjeh]] since [[1905]] — submitted his resignation in the same month as the [[Van Heutsz's inspection tour of Atjeh (December 1907)|inspection tour]] undertaken by governor-general [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] at the request of minister [[D. Fock|Fock]]. The resignation is the political climax of the [[Avondpost "Wekeer" articles (October 1907)|"Wekeer" affair]] of October [[1907]] and the immediate prelude to the [[Van Heutsz's report on Atjeh (June 1908)|Van Heutsz report of June 1908]] that installed the new course of the [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacificatie]].

## The October 1907 provocation

In October [[1907]] *[[De Avondpost]]* had published the [[Avondpost "Wekeer" articles (October 1907)|"Wekeer" articles]] — a series by an anonymous *maréchaussée*-officer from Atjeh exposing needless cruelties committed against the Atjehnese population, "toegelaten zo niet aangemoedigd door [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]]". Minister [[D. Fock|Fock]] had advised the chambermembers not to waste their time on these articles; the governor-general, he said, had full confidence in [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]].

## The inspection tour

Pressed by the minister, [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] undertook an inspection tour of Atjeh in December [[1907]], "opdat ook het publiek overtuigd worde van datgene waarvan ook de gouverneur-generaal overtuigd is, dat wij in Atjeh op de goede weg zijn" — so that the public too may be convinced of that of which the governor-general is already convinced, that we are on the right path in Atjeh. The inspection proved otherwise. In the same month [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] submitted his resignation.

## The June 1908 report

In [[June 1908|June]] [[1908]] the [[Van Heutsz's report on Atjeh (June 1908)|report of the governor-general]] was published. Its verdict on [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] was harsh:

> "Hij heeft het beginsel van de pacificatie miskend. Hij heeft een verkeerde politiek gevoerd tegenover de hoofden, aan wie een onheuse bejegening ten deel viel. Hij heeft het goedgezinde deel der bevolking tussen twee vuren geplaatst".

— He had misunderstood the principle of the pacification; he had pursued a wrong policy towards the chiefs, who had been given shabby treatment; he had placed the well-disposed part of the population between two fires.

The report did, however, acknowledge that [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] had acted vigorously against the specific atrocities for which he had been arraigned in *[[De Avondpost]]*.

## Significance

The resignation of [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] in [[December 1907|December]] [[1907]] is the political-military hinge of the post-conquest period of Atjehnese history. It cleared the way for the [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] report of [[1908]] and the new course of the [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacificatie]]: administration through the *hoofden* (chiefs), bound to Dutch authority by "tactvolle omgang".
