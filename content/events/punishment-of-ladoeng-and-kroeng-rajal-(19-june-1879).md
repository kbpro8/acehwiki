**The punishment of Ladoeng and Kroeng Rajal** was the punitive turn inflicted by Dutch troops, on [[19 June]] [[1879]], upon the population of [[Ladoeng]] and [[Kroeng Rajal]] on the North Coast of [[Acheh|Atjeh]], known from of old for its unbridled robber spirit (*teugellooze roofzucht*). The punishment was carried out without any loss on the Dutch side, and belonged, as [[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] records after [[B. C. P. Kielstra|Kielstra]]'s history, to the establishing phase that followed the triumphant campaign against the [[XXII Moekims]]: ten days after the completion of the operations at [[Gleieng]] and two days before [[Johan Willem van Lansberge|Van Lansberge]]'s cabinet letter of [[21 March]] was answered by events in the field, the coast population was taught that the arm of the government reached everywhere (see [[Campaign against the XXII Moekims (1879)]]).
