**West Coast of Aceh Action 1925–1927** was a military pacification campaign fought along the southwestern coast of Aceh between [[1925]] and [[1927]] to suppress a major resurgence of anti-colonial resistance.

## Narrative of the Ambush and Heroic Defense

In [[1925]], anti-colonial resistance flared anew along the southwestern coast near [[Meulaboh]] and [[Bakongan]]. Independent Acehnese leaders, armed with [[Klewang|klewangs]] and rifles, ambushed colonial patrols in the tall *alang-alang* grass plains and dense coastal jungle.

During one fierce engagement, a Marechaussee patrol commanded by Captain [[J. Paris]] was ambushed by a large Acehnese force. In the initial volley and hand-to-hand clash, Captain Paris, the European brigade commander, and five soldiers were killed, while twelve others received severe wounds.

With both officers dead, leadership fell upon wounded Javanese Marechaussee [[Kromo-di-Koro]]. Despite heavy blood loss, Kromo-di-Koro took charge, rallied the remaining twelve men, and ordered them to fire volley salvos into the high grass to prevent the attackers from overwhelming the survivors. When on the verge of collapsing from blood loss, Kromo-di-Koro formally handed command over to Menadonese Marechaussee [[Suwoek]].

During the ongoing clash, Marechaussee [[Leleng]], who had lost the functional use of his left hand from a blade wound, wedged his rifle between his knees and continued firing with his right hand. Meanwhile, Marechaussee [[Sumangkut]] spotted an attacker charging through the grass with a klewang; though badly injured, Sumangkut alerted Suwoek, who pursued and shot the insurgent at fifty meters distance. The small band successfully killed over twenty attackers, captured 26 klewangs, and held the position until reinforcements arrived.

## Pacification and Resolution

To restore total order, the colonial government dispatched Colonel [[Louis Adriaan Snell|L. A. Snell]] ("Old Hand") from Java to lead operations on the West Coast. Snell deployed mobile Marechaussee brigades that systematically tracked down insurgent bands. By late [[1927]], resistance was broken, and a new permanent Marechaussee division was established at [[Bakongan]] to preserve peace.
