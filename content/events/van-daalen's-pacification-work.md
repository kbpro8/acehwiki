**Van Daalen's pacification work** (1905-1908) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Under Governor Van Daalen (1905-1908), the pacification work (pacificatie-werk) was begun, continuing under his successor Swart.. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1905]]-[[1908]].

## Account

Under Governor [[Gouverneur Van Daalen|Van Daalen]] ([[1905]]-[[1908]]), the pacification work ([[Pacificatie|pacificatie]]-werk) was begun, continuing under his successor [[Gouverneur Swart|Swart]].

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
