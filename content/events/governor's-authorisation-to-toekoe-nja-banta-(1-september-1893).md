The **Governor's authorisation to Toekoe Nja Banta** of [[1 September]] [[1893]] extended the Dutch alliance policy from the [[XXV Moekims]] eastward into the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]], by permitting the friendly *sagi*-head [[Toekoe Nja Banta]] to open the struggle against the *[[Moslemin]]* in the terrain outside the eastern part of the Dutch *[[Geconcentreerde linie|geconcentreerde linie]]*.

## The decision

Hooijer recorded that the result obtained by [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]] in clearing both his own territory and that of [[Toekoe Nja Mohammad]] — together forming the [[XXV Moekims]] — was so favourable, and the rest in the cleared terrain so general, that *de Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden er den 1sten September 1893 toe overging te trachten die ook te verkrijgen in de terreinen buiten het oostelijk gedeelte onzer linie, door aan Toekoe Nja Bantah, het bevriende hoofd van de sagi der XXVI Moekims, toe te staan ook daar den strijd tegen de Moslemin te openen*.

## Terms

Rifles and powder were also to be granted to Nja Banta, and his father-in-law [[T. Nek van Marassa|Toekoe Neq van Marassa]] and his brother-in-law [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb|Toekoe Tjoet]], *[[Oeleebalang|oeleebalang]]* of the [[XIII Moekims Toengkoeb]], were to help him in the work.

## Outcome

The action did not go particularly well, although Dutch troops assisted by temporarily occupying captured points and Dutch artillery was employed against the common enemy. Within a month the Dutch had to garrison one of Toekoe Tjoet's *bentengs* at [[Toengkoeb]] themselves ([[27 September]] [[1893]]); [[Lamgoet]], captured on [[4 September]] [[1893]], was lost again through Toekoe Tjoet's arbitrary evacuation; and when Dutch soldiers appeared before [[Kroeng Kali]] on [[6 October]] [[1893]] the hill they had required to be occupied was still unoccupied. The failure demonstrated that without [[Teukoe Oemar]]'s help the Dutch allies would not drive the fanatics from the XXVI Moekims.
