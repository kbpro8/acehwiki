---
title: Hikayat Malem Dagang
tags: [event, literary-work]
---

The oldest and most celebrated of the Acehnese epic hikayats, centered on the naval expedition of Sultan Eseukanda Muda against the treacherous Malaccan prince Si Ujut. The poem's hero, Malem Dagang, is nominated chief panglima of the fleet through the mediation of the wise teacher Ja Pakeh, and the epic culminates in the capture and execution of Si Ujut after his betrayal of Acehnese hospitality.
