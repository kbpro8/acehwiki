**Expedition Samalanga and Peusangan (1901)** was a Dutch military operation in the northern part of [[Atjeh]] that resulted in the conquest of **Batoe Island** in [[January]] [[1901]] and the subjugation of the villages of Samalanga and Peusangan. The operation is noted in the award list (page 186) where **J. A. Gauw** received the [[Ridder Militaire Willemsorde]] 4th class and an honourable mention for his role.

## Objectives and Outcome

The expedition aimed to secure the coastal hinterland, disrupt Acehnese supply lines, and establish Dutch administrative control over the region. The successful capture of Batoe Island and the subsequent pacification of Samalanga and Peusangan marked a significant step toward the final phases of the Aceh War.

## Significance

The 1901 expedition demonstrated the continued Dutch commitment to fully annexing Atjeh, even after decades of resistance. It also highlighted the role of the [[Korps Mariniers (Netherlands)]] in combined amphibious‑land operations.
