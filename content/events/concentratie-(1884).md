**The Concentratie (1884)** — *the Concentration of 1884* — was the Dutch military policy in the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] by which the Netherlands forces in [[1884]] restricted themselves to a ring round [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] and a *ceintuurbaan* (girdle-road) connecting the sixteen posts with the recently shortened railway, while three *straalbanen* (ray-roads) connected the chief station at Kota Radja with the *ceintuurbaan*.

## The First Tram-Line
The first railway traffic in Atjeh ran between [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] and the harbour-place [[Oelèë Lheue|Oelèë Lheuë]] — *'begrijpelijkerwijs tusschen Kota Radja en de havenplaats Oelèë Lheue'*. That remained the only railway until the time of the *concentratie*, when the Dutch power restricted itself to a ring round Kota Radja, and the *ceintuurbaan* connected the sixteen posts with the recently shortened railway, while the three *straalbanen* connected the chief station at Kota Radja with the *ceintuurbaan*. For a telephone in that post-ring provision was likewise made. Broersma notes that he saw at [[Sigli|Sigli]] a little locomotive that had already been in service in [[1884]].

## The End of the Concentratie
When in [[1896]] other insights prevailed concerning the conduct in Atjeh — *'toen in 1896 andere inzichten omtrent het optreden in Atjeh veld wonnen'* — and consequently the line of concentration round Kota Radja was given up, there came a need for a line through [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]]. In [[1897]] and [[1898]] the tram-line was laid via [[Indrapoeri|Indrapoeri]] to [[Seulimeum|Seulimeum]]. The *concentratie* was thus the military policy of the years [[1884]]–[[1896]], and the *ceintuurbaan* and *straalbanen* its physical legacy.

## Significance
The *concentratie* is the principal military policy of the middle period of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]], and the *ceintuurbaan* and *straalbanen* are the physical frame of the Dutch military presence in Groot-Atjeh during that period. The end of the *concentratie* in [[1896]] is the political event that opened the way for the expansion of the tram-line through Groot-Atjeh and eventually to the eastern coast.
