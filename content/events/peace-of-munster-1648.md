**Treaty of Münster (1648)** was the peace agreement that concluded the **[[Eighty Years' War]]** between the **[[United Provinces (Dutch Republic)]]** and **[[Spain]]**, formally recognising Dutch independence. The treaty’s end of hostilities allowed the Republic to redirect military resources toward overseas expansion, notably strengthening the **[[Dutch East India Company|VOC]]** presence in the **[[Sultanate of Aceh]]**.

## Consequences for Aceh
With the cessation of European war in Europe, the **VOC** could increase its fleet size and protect its trading posts on the Aceh coast, facilitating the flow of pepper and other commodities to Europe.
