**Action against Kloenkoeng (1908)** was a Dutch marine operation targeting the town of **Kloenkoeng** (modern **Klungkung**) on the island of [[Bali]] in [[1908]]. The operation formed the concluding phase of the Dutch pacification of Bali and is listed in the corps’ chronology alongside the earlier Aceh campaigns.

A small force of the [[Royal Netherlands Marine Corps|Dutch Marine Corps]] landed, seized the royal palace, and forced the surrender of the local ruler, completing Dutch domination of the island.

## Significance
The action marked the end of organized Balinese resistance and allowed the Dutch colonial administration to focus resources on the remaining Aceh insurgency.
