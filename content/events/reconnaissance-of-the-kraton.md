**Reconnaissance of the Kraton** was an exploratory military mission conducted on [[9 April]] [[1873]] by the Dutch forces shortly after their [[Landing on Atjeh's Coasts]].

## The Mission
The objective of the mission was to map the interior routes leading towards the [[Kraton of Atjeh]]. Guided by the Chinese guide [[Khoé Tjian Gie]], the expedition force approached within 1200 meters of a large structure, which was identified as [[Atjeh's Groote Missigit]]. During this reconnaissance, few enemy combatants were spotted, leading the Dutch command to underesimate the formidable Acehnese defensive preparations. The intelligence gathered directly informed General [[J.H.R. Köhler]]'s decision to launch the [[First Conquest of the Great Missigit]] the very next day.
