The **dienst van de Maréchaussée in de Zuider- en Oosterafdeeling van Borneo** in [[1905]] was the despatch of three *[[Brigade (Maréchaussée)|brigades]]* of the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* to the [[Zuider- en Oosterafdeeling van Borneo|Southern and Eastern Division of Borneo]], cited in the [[1930]] jubilee number as the leading instance of the corps rendering extraordinary services outside [[Atjeh]].

## The despatch

The jubilee account records that the Maréchaussée "bewees ook buiten Atjeh haar buitengewone diensten, zooals in de Z. en O. Afdeeling van Borneo, waarheen in 1905 drie brigades werden gedirigeerd" — proved its extraordinary services outside Atjeh as well, as in the Southern and Eastern Division of Borneo, whither three brigades were directed in 1905.

## The object

The object of the three brigades was to render the *[[Pretendent-sultan|pretendent-sultan]]* harmless — "wien 't mocht gelukken den pretendent-sultan onschadelijk te maken," to whom it fell to succeed in making the pretender-sultan harmless.

## Significance

The Borneo detachment illustrates the extent to which the methods developed by the corps in Atjeh — small, mobile, lightly armed brigades operating on local intelligence — came to be regarded within the Netherlands-Indies army as transferable to other theatres of colonial warfare. The same transferability underlay the later despatch of Maréchaussée-trained officers such as [[W. V. Rhemrev]] to [[Westkust|Sumatra's Westkust]] and of Colonel [[L. A. Snell]] from [[Java]] to the [[Westkust Resistance (1925-1927)|Westkust of Atjeh]].
