**The murder of the postholder at Anak Paja** was the treacherous killing, four days after the hopeful government telegram of [[21 June]] [[1883]] — that [[Groot-Atjeh]] was "nagenoeg geheel van vijanden gezuiverd" (almost wholly cleansed of enemies) — of a Dutch postholder (*posthouder*) with eight *pradjoerits* (native soldiers), while eight more pradjoerits were severely wounded. The event, noted by [[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] in 1886 with the dry footnote "Trouwens den 21en Juni 1883 had de Gouv.-Generaal ook getelegrafeerd..." ("Moreover on 21 June 1883 the Governor-General had telegraphed..."), was one of the typical contrasts between the official optimism and the real state of Atjeh in the last years before the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration]].
