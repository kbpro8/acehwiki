**Jongejans arrives in Atjeh** (1930) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Jongejans first set foot on Acehnese soil as assistant-resident in 1930. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1930]].

## Account

[[Resident Jongejans|Jongejans]] first set foot on Acehnese soil as assistant-resident in [[1930]]. Stayed (with a brief interlude in [[Menado]]) until the time of writing, providing him objectivity lacking in long-time Atjeh-kenner circles (free from '[[adoration mutuelle]]').

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
