**The Bestorming van de Stelling Daman–Lam Tjroeng–Lam Poelau (25 May 1896)** — the storming of the Achehnese defensive position running from [[Daman]] through [[Lam-Tjroeng]] to [[Lam-Poelau]] — was the final engagement of the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day Dutch offensive]] against [[Teuku Umar|Teungkoe Oemar]]'s fortified *stelling* around [[Lam-Pisang]], executed on the third day by a force under the overall command of [[Kolonel der Infanterie J. W. Stemfoort|Kol. Stemfoort]] with the [[6e Bataljon Infanterie|6e Bataljon]] under [[Majoor D. A. Okhuizen|Maj. Okhuizen]] outflanking and attacking the enemy position at [[Daman]] and [[Lam-Tjroeng]]. The 3e compagnie (kapitein Janssen) approached Lam-Tjroeng over the hills covered; the 1e peloton under [[1e Luit. Keetell|1e Luit. Keetell]] stormed; the 12-man garrison fled toward Lam-Tjroeng; 2e luit. Lamster was first into the Lam-Tjroeng *versterking*. **By 11:45 a.m. the entire stelling Daman–Lam-Tjroeng–Lam-Poelau was *ontruimd* by the enemy.** The storming was followed by the burning of [[Anak-Paja]], [[Djirat]], [[Lho-Nga]], [[Lam-Caboes]], [[Lam-Paja]], [[Mantjang]], [[Lam-Baroe]], [[Lamlon]], and [[Noesa]].

## Background

By the morning of [[25 May]] [[1896]] the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day Dutch offensive]] had accomplished its two principal objectives: the [[Slag bij Tjot Tjako (23 May 1896)|storming of Tjot Tjako]] on [[23 May]] [[1896]] and the [[Capture of Lam-Pisang and Oemar's House (24 May 1896)|capture of Lam-Pisang and Oemar's house]] on [[24 May]] [[1896]]. The remaining objective was the enemy *stelling* on the ridge running from [[Boekit Seboen]] through [[Lam-Poelau]] and [[Lam-Tjroeng]] to [[Daman]] — the southern anchor of the Achehnese defensive position. So long as this *stelling* remained in Achehnese hands, Oemar's warriors could threaten the captured positions at [[Lam-Pisang]] and could stage a counter-offensive from the south.

[[Kolonel der Infanterie J. W. Stemfoort|Kol. Stemfoort]] planned to drive the enemy from the *heuvelstelling* [[Boekit Seboen]]–[[Lam-Poelau]]–[[Lam-Tjroeng]]–[[Daman]]. Three battalions with auxiliary weapons participated. The plan called for the enemy *stelling* to be threatened by artillery fire in front and on the left flank, plus feint attacks, with the main attack to be a flanking movement on the right flank. The reinforcements brought up for the day's operations were substantial: the [[12e Bataljon Infanterie|12e Bataljon]], 1½ *compagnieën* of the [[7e Bataljon Infanterie|7e Bataljon]], two *compagnieën* *maréchaussée*, two cavalry platoons, one engineer peloton, two sections of mountain artillery, and three ambulances.

## The Morning Reconnaissance and the Storming of Beradin

At 6:30 a.m. the cavalry under [[Ritmeester Jhr. L. D. C. de Lannoy|de Lannoy]] marched from [[Lam-Djamoe]] to reconnoitre toward [[Boekit Seboen]]. The [[9e Bataljon Infanterie|9e Bataljon]] talso stelling along the foot of the gebergte near [[Lam-Pisang]]. At 7:55 a.m. the enemy fired from a top approximately 70 m high; the 9e Bataljon occupied it. At 9:30 a.m. 2½ *compagnieën* of the [[7e Bataljon Infanterie|7e Bataljon]] under [[Majoor J. R. Jacobs|Maj. Jacobs]] were placed at [[Majoor J. F. T. Veeren|Maj. Veeren]]'s disposal. At 10:45 a.m. optical signal communication with [[Ketapan Doea]] was established.

The cavalry found [[Lam-Goe-Oe-Zuid]] (Lam Oekoe) *onbezet*; the northern *rand* of [[Beradin]] was weakly held; about 6 enemies left the southern *rand versterking* and fled to [[Lam-Poelau]]; a band of about 25–30 men on [[Boekit Seboen]] fired on [[1e Luit. C. W. F. Happé|luit. Happé]]'s patrol. Beradin was assigned to the 5de compagnie of the [[12e Bataljon Infanterie|12e Bataljon]] under [[Kapitein S. A. Drijber|Kapt. Drijber]]. The cavalry's initial report was wrong — Drijber's compagnie was met by maintained crossfire from *kampong*, *versterking*, and western hills. Thomson led a storm-action on the *versterking* with about 10 men; the hoornblazer Karel, Beekman, and van den Wollenberg distinguished themselves. Drijber was wounded, with 12 *minderen*. Five artillery sections opened fire on enemy positions. The storming of [[Beradin]] was, in tactical terms, a hard fight for a small work — a foretaste of the resistance that the main *stelling* would offer.

## The Flanking Movement: 6e Bataljon at Daman and Lam Tjroeng

The [[6e Bataljon Infanterie|6e Bataljon]] under [[Majoor D. A. Okhuizen|Maj. Okhuizen]] was ordered to outflank and attack the enemy position at [[Daman]] and [[Lam-Tjroeng]]. The 3e compagnie under kapitein Janssen was to approach Lam-Tjroeng over the hills covered, the 1e compagnie along the foot of the gebergte. Janssen marched through scorching cogon grass — the tall, sharp-edged grass that covers the abandoned *sawah* of Atjeh and that, in the heat of the day, is a particular torment to troops in heavy marching order — to a small *versterking* at Daman. After a few salvoes, the 1e peloton under [[1e Luit. Keetell|1e Luit. Keetell]] stormed. The 12-man garrison fled toward [[Lam-Tjroeng]]. The fall of Daman opened the way to Lam-Tjroeng itself.

2e luit. Lamster was first into the Lam-Tjroeng *versterking* when it was attacked next. The capture of Lam-Tjroeng — the central element of the *stelling* — was the decisive moment of the day. **By 11:45 a.m. the entire stelling Daman–Lam-Tjroeng–Lam-Poelau was *ontruimd* by the enemy.** The Achehnese, their flank turned and their position overlapped, retreated in disorder. The Dutch troops talso possession of the *stelling*. The *heuvelrug* from [[Boekit Seboen]] through [[Lam-Poelau]] to [[Daman]] — the southern anchor of Oemar's defensive position — was in Dutch hands.

## The Verbranding of the Kampongs

The afternoon of [[25 May]] [[1896]] was devoted to the destruction of the enemy's *kampong*s. The cavalry was ordered to continue reconnaissance to Lama-Paja, [[Lam-Gaboes|Lam-Caboes]], and [[Anak-Paja]], and, if *onbezet*, to burn them. The [[12e Bataljon Infanterie|12e Bataljon]] under [[Luitenant-Kolonel G. F. Soeters|Lt.-Col. Soeters]] marched to [[Djirat]], with orders to burn all *kampong*s on the return. Soeters also had the 5e compagnie burn [[Lam-Caboes]] and the 2e compagnie burn [[Lam-Paja]]. The *maréchaussée* under [[Kapitein Jhr. Graafland|Graafland]] reached [[Anak-Paja]] and burned the houses.

At [[Djirat]], [[Ritmeester Jhr. L. D. C. de Lannoy|de Lannoy]] decided to also investigate [[Lam-Kroet]] and [[Lho-Nga]]. About 10 enemies posted on the right bank of the [[Kroeng-Raja]] fired on the cavalry, preventing closer reconnaissance of the bridge. Soeters sent the order: "Keer terug, en steek so molike Lho-Nga on fire" (Return, and, if possible, set Lho-Nga on fire). After burning about 10 houses, de Lannoy halted the burning — considering the battle-readiness of his troops — and returned to [[Boekit Seboen]]. On the return, some enemies in a small *versterking* on a western *helling* fired without causing losses.

To make the punishment as complete as possible, the *bevelhebber* at 1 p.m. sent a compagnie of the [[5e Bataljon Infanterie|5e Bataljon]] from [[Lam-Poelau]] to [[Mantjang]], [[Lam-Baroe]], and [[Lamlon]]; at 1:50 p.m. a peloton of the [[6e Bataljon Infanterie|6e Bataljon]] to [[Noesa]] — all to destroy those *kampong*s. By the evening of [[25 May]] [[1896]] the *kampong*s of [[Anak-Paja]], [[Djirat]], [[Lho-Nga]], [[Lam-Caboes]], [[Lam-Paja]], [[Mantjang]], [[Lam-Baroe]], [[Lamlon]], and [[Noesa]] had been burned.

## The Withdrawal and the Bivak at Lam-Pisang

The cavalry rested until 5:30 p.m. at Oemar's house, then returned to [[Koeta-Radja]] by 8 p.m. The infantry started the return march at 4 p.m.; some fire from [[Tandjong]] forced the rearguard under Weustmann to retire by sections to [[Lam-Poelau]]. By 5 p.m. all were at [[Lam-Poelau]]. Peloton Sandberg held [[Boekit Seboen]] overnight; the [[12e Bataljon Infanterie|12e Bataljon]] marched to [[Lam-Pisang]], then to [[Lam-Djamoe]] for transport to the [[Kraton|Kraton]] by 8 p.m. [[Kolonel der Infanterie J. W. Stemfoort|Stemfoort]] decided to hold the captured positions overnight and to retire under cover of darkness from [[Lam-Poelau]] and neighbouring hills to [[Lam-Pisang]], where they would stay until the next morning. The [[9e Bataljon Infanterie|9e Bataljon]] was to retire to Lam-Pisang at 5:30 p.m.; the [[6e Bataljon Infanterie|6e Bataljon]] with a compagnie of the 14e and the sectie Vrijdag would leave at a suitable moment in the night; Sandberg's peloton at 9 p.m. The compagnie of the 5e Bataljon that had burned the western *kampong*s returned at 6 p.m. without meeting an enemy.

The engineers prepared the demolition of Oemar's house for the next day. The task for [[25 May]] [[1896]] — driving the enemy further from the *vallei van Beradin* — was accomplished; Oemar's warriors had retreated behind the [[Kroeng-Raba]]; the *kampong*s were chastised.

## Significance

The Bestorming van de Stelling Daman–Lam Tjroeng–Lam Poelau on [[25 May]] [[1896]] was the closing engagement of the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day Dutch offensive]] and the operational completion of the strategic reversal of the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (late March 1896)|defection]] of [[29 March]] [[1896]]. With the *stelling* in Dutch hands and Oemar's warriors driven behind the [[Kroeng-Raba]], the immediate military objective of the offensive had been achieved. The *verbranding* of the *kampong*s — [[Anak-Paja]], [[Djirat]], [[Lho-Nga]], [[Lam-Caboes]], [[Lam-Paja]], [[Mantjang]], [[Lam-Baroe]], [[Lamlon]], and [[Noesa]] — was a deliberate act of chastisement intended to deny the enemy the means of subsistence and to demonstrate the cost of continued resistance. The day also marked the operational debut of [[Majoor D. A. Okhuizen|Maj. Okhuizen]]'s [[6e Bataljon Infanterie|6e Bataljon]] as a flanking force — a role it would repeat, with distinction, in the subsequent operations of June [[1896]]. The Bestorming of [[25 May]] [[1896]] was, in this sense, both the close of one chapter of the spring campaign and the prelude to the next.
