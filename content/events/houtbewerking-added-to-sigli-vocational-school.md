**Houtbewerking added to Sigli vocational school** (start of 1937-1938 course) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Recently, i.e. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to start of [[1937]]-[[1938]] course.

## Account

Recently, i.e. at the start of the [[1937]]-[[1938]] course, houtbewerking (woodworking) was added to the curriculum of the [[Sigli ambachtsleergang]] alongside metaalbewerking.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
