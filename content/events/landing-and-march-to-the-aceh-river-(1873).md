**Landing and march to the Aceh River (1873)** was the opening overland operation of the [[Second Dutch Expedition to Aceh]], in which Dutch forces moved from the coast toward the [[Aceh River]] and occupied the camp at [[Penajong]].

## Course

The operation followed debates over landing sites including [[Krueng Raba]], [[Lepong]], and the coastal sector near [[Gighen]]. Intelligence warned of muddy routes, river crossings, fortified banks, and Acehnese fieldworks. The move established the base from which the Dutch attacked the religious and royal centres.

## Significance

The episode belongs to the continuing [[Aceh War]] and the contested consequences of the [[Capture of the Kraton of Aceh]].
