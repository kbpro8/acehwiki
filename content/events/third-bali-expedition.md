**Third Bali Expedition (1850)** was a Dutch military campaign launched in [[1850]] to subdue the Balinese kingdoms of **[[Bali]]**. The successful conclusion of this expedition freed Dutch resources, allowing the **[[Dutch East Indies]]** to redirect troops and naval assets toward the suppression of piracy and smuggling on **[[Borneo]]’s west coast**, a region whose stability was vital for protecting the **[[Sultanate of Aceh]]**’s trade routes.

## Link to Aceh

- The expedition’s outcome enabled the Dutch to allocate forces for the 1850‑1854 Borneo anti‑smuggling operations that indirectly safeguarded Acehnese commerce.
