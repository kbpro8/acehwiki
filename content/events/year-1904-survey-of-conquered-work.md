**Year 1904 survey of conquered work** (1904) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. In 1904 the Dutch could briefly survey the work accomplished, but the time was not yet ripe for complete rest. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[1904]].

## Account

In [[1904]] the Dutch could briefly survey the work accomplished, but the time was not yet [[Ripe|ripe]] for complete rest. In the following years it became clear the equilibrium was not yet found, and continuous activity was needed to extinguish developing centers of [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]].

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
