**The Reconnaissance in Lepong** — *verkenning in Lepong op 12 November* [[1896]] — was a Dutch reconnaissance of the [[Lepong]] region of [[Groot-Atjeh]], undertaken on [[12 November]] [[1896]], five days before the [[Handover of Command from Stemfoort to Van Vliet (17 November 1896)|formal handover of command]] from [[J. W. Stemfoort|Stemfoort]] to [[C. P. J. van Vliet|Van Vliet]]. The reconnaissance was one of the first operations of the transitional Van Vliet period, and was designed to maintain pressure on the region in which [[Teuku Umar|Toekoe Oemar]] had been located by the [[Excursie to Lepong and Lohong (10–14 August 1896)|excursie to Lepong and Lohong]] of [[10 August|10]]–[[14 August]] [[1896]]. The reconnaissance was followed, on [[29 November]] [[1896]], by the [[Trip to Tibang-Poei and Birah (29 November 1896)|trip to Tibang-Poei and Birah]] — the operation to recover the artillery lost on [[17 April]] [[1896]] during the [[Battle of Senelop (17 April 1896)|battle of Senelop]].

## Significance

The Reconnaissance in Lepong of [[12 November]] [[1896]] is significant in the documentary record of *ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel* as one of the first operations of the transitional Van Vliet period. The reconnaissance — undertaken five days before the formal handover of command — maintained Dutch pressure on the Lepong region, and demonstrated the continuity of the Dutch *pacificatie* (pacification) policy across the transition from Stemfoort to Van Vliet.
