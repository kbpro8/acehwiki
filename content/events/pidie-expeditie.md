**The Pidië Expedition** was one of the great successes of the glorious period of Van Heutsz in the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]], the first-named of the expeditions and excursions that [[Musket]] lists in *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* by Merac ([[1947]]). The Pidië Expedition was the expedition to the Pidië region of [[Atjeh|Aceh]] — the region of the Pidië mukims — conducted under [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] as part of the all-out offensive that followed the defection of Teukoe Oemar on [[29 March]] [[1896]].

## The great successes of the glorious period

"the time also of our great successes! The Pidië expedition, the expeditions in Idi and Peusangan, the Pasé expedition, the Samalanga expedition, the expeditions to the Gayo and Alas lands, the action in the Tangsé and Geumpang area and on the North Coast of Atjeh, the patrol actions on the always restless West Coast, and finally the Battle of Batèë-Ilië, by which the organised, general resistance in Atjeh was broken for good." The Pidië Expedition is the first-named of the great successes: the first of the expeditions and excursions of the glorious period of Van Heutsz.

## Significance

The Pidië Expedition is significant in *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* as the first-named of the great successes of the glorious period of Van Heutsz. The book's argument — that the great successes of the glorious period — the Pidië Expedition, the expeditions in Idi and Peusangan, the Pasé Expedition, the Samalanga Expedition, the expeditions to the Gayo and Alas lands, the action in the Tangsé and Geumpang area, the patrol actions on the West Coast, and the Battle of Batèë-Ilië — were the expeditions and excursions by which the organised, general resistance in Atjeh was broken for good — is the book's frame for the offensive phase of the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]]. The Pidië Expedition is therefore the book's — and the colonial memory's — first great success of the glorious period.
