**The Benting Kota Alam operation** of [[13 November]] [[13 November]] [[1874]] was a field-engineering action of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] in which a column under [[M. C. E. Ruempol|Luitenant-Kolonel M. C. E. Ruempol]], dispatched from [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] by the civil and military governor [[J. L. J. H. Pel|Kolonel Pel]], constructed a sandbag redoubt — the *Benting Kota Alam* — on the [[Acheh River|Atjeh River]] east of [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] under heavy Achehnese fire. The sapper section in the vanguard was commanded by [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede Luitenant C. J. Snijders]] of the [[Bataillon Mineurs en Sappeurs|Bataillon Mineurs en Sappeurs]], for whom the action was a baptism of fire; the fortification, once completed, secured the eastern flank of the Dutch positions and covered the line of communication to [[Longbatta|Longbatta]].

## Background

On [[7 November]] [[7 November]] [[1874]] Dutch troops had captured the fortified villages of Lemboecq, Lemboe, Berauw, and the Rivierbenting northeast of [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]]. Intelligence then indicated that the [[Achehnese|Atjehers]] were preparing to occupy [[Kuta Alam|Kota Alam]] further east, a move that would have threatened [[Longbatta|Longbatta]] and the Dutch line of communication. [[J. L. J. H. Pel|Kolonel PEL]] (civil and military governor) decided to forestall them by building a defensible post at [[Kuta Alam|Kota Alam]] on [[13 November]] [[13 November]] [[1874]].

## The Column

In the pre-dawn darkness of [[13 November]] [[13 November]] [[1874]], a column departed [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] under [[M. C. E. Ruempol|Luitenant-Kolonel M. C. E. Ruempol]]. The force comprised:

- the 9th Battalion L.H.;
- a mountain artillery section;
- a section of [[Bataillon Mineurs en Sappeurs|Mineurs en Sappers]];
- an ambulance detachment.

[[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede Luitenant Snijders]] commanded the sapper section in the vanguard, supported by an infantry platoon, the mountain guns, and 180 coolies with tools and materials following under a light infantry screen.

## The Site

The column followed the [[Pedir]] dike to the [[Acheh River|Atjeh River]] near the Rivierbenting, then proceeded upstream to a pre-selected site near a river bend. [[T. J. A. van Zijll de Jong|Majoor T. J. A. van Zijll de Jong]] (artillery) and [[W. J. C. Leers|Majoor W. J. C. Leers]] (engineers) sited the fortification to command both the fortified edge of Pinang to the northeast and [[Longbatta|Longbatta]] to the south, while dominating a long stretch of the river — one of the best crossing points.

## The Construction Under Fire

Snijders immediately set his coolies to filling sandbags and stacking them along the traced perimeter, while a large Achehnese house was converted into temporary shelter. A double line of outposts 150 metres forward, anchored on the river and reinforced with an infantry section and artillery, covered the work.

Scarcely had the first sandbags been laid when heavy rifle fire erupted from the scrub on three sides and from the opposite bank. The [[Achehnese|Atjehners]] twice attempted to rush the covering line but were repulsed. Snijders, though under fire for the first time, directed the construction calmly, later recalling — in the third-person memoir that became the principal source for the action — that his chief concern was not to appear fearful.

The fortification — named [[Kuta Alam|Benting Kota Alam]] — was completed and held, securing the eastern flank of the Dutch positions and covering the Dutch line of communication with [[Longbatta|Longbatta]] and the country further east.

## Significance

The [[Benting Kota Alam operation (13 November 1874)|Kota Alam action]] is, in [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]]'s later account, an exemplar of the Dutch reliance on rapid field engineering — sandbag redoubts sited to dominate river crossings and village strongholds — to consolidate territorial gains against a mobile, well-armed opponent in the swampy, jungle-covered theatre of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. For [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] personally, the action was the formative military experience of his youth: the young lieutenant who calmly directed coolies under fire at [[Kuta Alam|Kota Alam]] would, four decades later, become [[Opperbevelhebber van Land- en Zeemacht|Opperbevelhebber van Land- en Zeemacht]] ([[August 1914|August]] [[1914]]–[[November 1918|November]] [[1918]]) and hold the safety of the entire [[Netherlands|Netherlands]] in his hands during the [[First World War]].

The operation also marks the first systematic Dutch attempt to extend the fortified perimeter eastward from [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] along the [[Acheh River|Atjeh River]] in the late autumn of [[1874]], complementing the westward extension along the [[Westerlinie|Westerlinie]] (toward [[Oleh-leh|Oleh-leh]]) and the northward extension along the [[Noorderlinie|Noorderlinie]] earlier in the same year.
