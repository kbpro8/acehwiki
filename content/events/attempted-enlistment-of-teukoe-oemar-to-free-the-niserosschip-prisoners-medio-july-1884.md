**Attempted enlistment of Teukoe Oemar to free the Niserosschip prisoners (medio July 1884)** (medio July 1884) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to medio [[July]] [[1884]].

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

[[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] — after the Koloniaal Verslag — gives the measure its result in blood, in the same months: on [[3 July]] [[1884]] the Atjehnese [[Toekoe Oemar (Tenom)|Toekoe Oemar]], who had promised to deliver the English castaways held by the Tenomsche, was landed at [[Lambesi]] with captured English flag-blinkered expectations, and "overrompelde de geen kwaad vermoedende bemanning onzer oorlogssloep, 7 hunner doodde, en zich van alle wapens meester maakte" ("surprised the crew of our war sloop that suspected no evil, killed seven of them, and made himself master of all the weapons"): the same palm-court as the request and its failing English answer narrated above, in the month of the secret decision on the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration]]. "Na dit feit heeft TOEKOE OEMAR volgens het Verslag ons weder ijverig in Groot Atjeh bestookt" ("After this deed Toekoe Oemar, according to the report, again zealously harassed us in Groot-Atjeh") — and three weeks later [[Imam Habib (Tjot-Goe)|Imam Habib]] repeated the lesson near [[Tjot-Goe]] ([[Ambush at Tjot-Goe (29 July 1884)|ambush of 29 July 1884]]) (see [[Lambesi treachery (3 July 1884)]]).
