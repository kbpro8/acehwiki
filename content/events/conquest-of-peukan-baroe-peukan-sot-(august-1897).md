**The conquest of Peukan Baroe-Peukan Sòt** on [[6]]–[[10 August]] [[1897]] was the Dutch military operation during the [[Sigli expedition (August 1897)|Sigli expedition]] that destroyed [[Habib Oesén|Habib Oesén]]'s hostile fortifications (*bentengs*) in the moekims [[Peukan Baroe|Peukan Baroe]] and [[Peukan Sòt|Peukan Sòt]] near [[Sigli]]. After bombardment by warships and artillery from Sigli, the bentengs were stormed and taken by forces under [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]], resulting in the complete clearance of the area that had been the source of daily shootings against the Dutch establishment.

## Van Heutsz's speech and the battle

On the evening of [[5 August]], [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] addressed the troops at Governor [[J. J. van Vliet|Van Vliet]]'s request: "Daarginds liggen een heeleboel bentengs, en die bentengs zitten vol Atjehers. Die Atjehers moeten eruit, en jelui moeten erin! En binnen die bentengs staan tafels, waarop de Willemsorden klaar liggen voor wie ze willen gaan halen." On [[6 August]], the bentengs were taken exactly as Van Heutsz had dictated: "De Atjehers gingen eruit en de soldaten gingen erin." The *uléla's* cleared the field, leaving many dead, rifles, and white weapons.

## The Ch. Polman van den Broek photographs

[[Ch. Polman van den Broek|Controleur Polman van den Broek]]'s photographs documented the battle: the second day saw the second battalion and marechaussee marching along Koelo Soekoen over dry sawahs in southern direction, with Van Heutsz standing on a munitions box directing operations; another photograph showed Van Heutsz's adjutant and another officer on the bridge over the creek connecting Koeala Baraboh and Koeala Sigli, by the most western fortification, where the garrison had just fled on the approach of Dutch troops.
