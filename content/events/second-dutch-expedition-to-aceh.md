**Second Dutch Expedition to Aceh** was the Dutch land-and-sea expedition commanded by [[Jan van Swieten]] which sailed from [[Batavia]] in late [[1873]], landed on the Acehnese coast, captured the [[Baiturrahman Grand Mosque|Missigit]] and [[Kraton of Aceh]], and established a reduced occupation at [[Kota Radja]].

## Course

The force assembled amid a blockade and a cholera epidemic. It sailed on [[20 November]] [[1873]], concentrated before Aceh by [[1 December]] [[1873]], and faced severe sickness aboard its transports. Its operations were intended to succeed the earlier unsuccessful expedition, but its military gains did not end the wider [[Aceh War]].

## Significance

The episode belongs to the continuing [[Aceh War]] and the contested consequences of the [[Capture of the Kraton of Aceh]].
