**The Arrival of Kolonel C. J. P. van Vliet** as [[Civiel en Militair Gouverneur]] van [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] on [[8 November]] [[1896]] — *aankomst van den nieuw benoemden Civiel- en Militairen Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden den kolonel C. J. P. van Vliet op 8 November* — was the arrival of the newly-appointed governor at [[Koeta Radja|Koeta-Radja]], nine days before the formal [[Handover of Command from Stemfoort to Van Vliet (17 November 1896)|handover from Kolonel Stemfoort]] on [[17 November]] [[1896]]. The arrival of Van Vliet opened *Hoofdstuk IV* of [[A. Kruisheer|Kruisheer]]'s *[[ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel]]* and marked the beginning of the transition from [[J. W. Stemfoort|Stemfoort]]'s second tenure to Van Vliet's governorship. The appointment of Van Vliet — a *Kolonel* (Colonel) of the Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger, with a portrait opposite page 145 of the volume — represented the formal closure of the post-De Moulin transitional arrangement and the restoration of the regular [[Civiel en Militair Gouverneur]] office under a permanent (rather than acting) incumbent.

## The Appointment and the Instruction

The appointment of Kolonel Van Vliet to the governorship of Atjeh was made by the *Opperbestuur* (Supreme Government) in the autumn of [[1896]], in succession to the late [[J. J. K. de Moulin|Generaal-Majoor De Moulin]] (who had died on [[7 July]] [[1896]]) and the acting governor [[J. W. Stemfoort|Stemfoort]] (who had served since [[11 July]] [[1896]]). The appointment was accompanied by the issue of a formal *Instruction for the Civiel en Militair Gouverneur van A. en O., den kolonel C. P. J. van Vliet* — preserved as *Bijlage III* of *ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel* (pp. 168–172). The Instruction — which explicitly *de Instruction door den Regeeringscommissaris voor Atjeh- en Onderhoorigheden bij zijn vertrek uit genoemd gewest achtergelaten in zijnen brief van 27 Juni [[1896]], No. 212, zeer geheim, [wordt] ingetrokken, terwijl de tegenwoordige Instructie tevens in de plaats treedt van het afgesprokene in de conferentie van den Landvoogd met den Generaal-Majoor J. J. K. de Moulin op 18 Juni [[1896]] en het opgedragene in het regeerings-telegram van 17 Juli [[1896]], No. 273* — replaced all prior instructions and consolidated the policy framework within which Van Vliet was to govern. The Instruction was thus the definitive policy document for the Atjeh governorship, superseding both the Vetter instruction of [[28 June]] [[1896]] and the De Moulin conference of [[18 June]] [[1896]].

## The Portrait in ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel

A portrait of Kolonel C. P. J. van Vliet appears in *ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel* opposite page 145 — the opening page of *Hoofdstuk IV*. The portrait is one of the six portraits bound into the volume, and was prepared in anticipation of Van Vliet's tenure as governor — making its presence, opposite the very page that records his arrival, a counterpart to the tragic portrait of [[J. J. K. de Moulin|De Moulin]] opposite page 15. The placement of Van Vliet's portrait at the opening of *Hoofdstuk IV* thus marks the formal transition from the post-De Moulin transitional period (covered in *Hoofdstuk III*) to the Van Vliet governorship (covered in *Hoofdstuk IV*).

## The Handover of 17 November [[1896]]

The arrival of Van Vliet on [[8 November]] [[1896]] was followed, nine days later, by the [[Handover of Command from Stemfoort to Van Vliet (17 November 1896)|formal handover of command]] on [[17 November]] [[1896]] — *overgave en overname van het bestuur, Kolonel J. W. Stemfoort keert naar Java terug* — at which Stemfoort returned to Java and Van Vliet formally assumed the governorship. The nine-day interval between Van Vliet's arrival and the formal handover was presumably used for the briefing of the new governor by Stemfoort and the senior Dutch officers, and for the orientation of Van Vliet with the current state of the operations. The handover of [[17 November]] [[1896]] closed Stemfoort's second tenure — which had lasted four months and six days, from [[11 July]] to [[17 November]] [[1896]] — and opened the Van Vliet governorship, which would last until [[1898]].

## Significance

The Arrival of Van Vliet on [[8 November]] [[1896]] is significant in the documentary record of *ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel* as the opening event of *Hoofdstuk IV* and the beginning of the transition from Stemfoort's second tenure to the Van Vliet governorship. The arrival — accompanied by the issue of the *Instruction for the Civiel en Militair Gouverneur van A. en O.* (preserved as *Bijlage III*) — represented the formal closure of the post-De Moulin transitional arrangement and the restoration of the regular [[Civiel en Militair Gouverneur]] office under a permanent incumbent. The Van Vliet governorship — which would last until [[1898]], when Van Vliet would be succeeded by [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] — was the bridge between the *pacificatie* (pacification) of the second semester of [[1896]] and the decisive *korte oorlog* phase of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]], and the Instruction of [[Bijlage III]] was the definitive policy document within which that bridge would be constructed.
