**Construction of new K.P.M. steiger at Lho'Seumawé** (year of writing (c. 1930s)) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. A new steiger (pier) for K.P.M. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to year of writing (c. 1930s).

## Account

A new steiger (pier) for [[Koninklijke Paketvaart Maatschappij (K.P.M.)|K.P.M.]] ships at [[Lho'Seumawé]] was taken into use in the year of writing. [[Koninklijke Paketvaart Maatschappij (K.P.M.)|K.P.M.]] maintains busy trade relations with [[Penang]].

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
