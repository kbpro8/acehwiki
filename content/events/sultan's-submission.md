**Sultan's submission** (January 1903) was an event in the history of [[Aceh]] recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] in *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. After 1900, the Sultan, who had fled with many dissatisfied people to remaining unconquered parts of the kingdom, wandered restlessly through his former kingdom, hunted, until he finally submitted in January 1903. 

## Date and Context

The event is dated to [[January]] [[1903]].

## Account

After [[1900]], [[Sultan of Aceh|the Sultan]], who had fled with many dissatisfied people to remaining unconquered parts of the kingdom, wandered restlessly through his former kingdom, hunted, until he finally submitted in [[January]] [[1903]]. The [[sultanate]] of Atjeh, having existed for about four centuries, had effectively ceased with his [[1902]] abdication/overgaaf; his [[1903]] submission was the final act.

## Significance

The event is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the broader historical narrative of [[Aceh]] and the [[Aceh War|Atjeh conflict]] period. It illustrates the complex interplay of local [[Acehnese|Atjehers]] politics, Dutch colonial administration, and the religious and military leadership that shaped the region's history.
