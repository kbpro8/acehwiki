**The Battle of Batèë-Ilië** was the battle in the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]] by which "the organised, general resistance in Aceh was broken for good." In *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* by Merac ([[1947]]), the Battle of Batèë-Ilië is the last-named of the great successes of the glorious period of Van Heutsz — the time of the great expeditions and excursions — that [[Musket]] lists after [[Buyckjens]]'s backward glance.

## The great successes of the glorious period

"the time also of our great successes! The Pidië expedition, the expeditions in Idi and Peusangan, the Pasé expedition, the Samalanga expedition, the expeditions to the Gayo and Alas lands, the action in the Tangsé and Geumpang area and on the North Coast of Atjeh, the patrol actions on the always restless West Coast, and finally the Battle of Batèë-Ilië, by which the organised, general resistance in Atjeh was broken for good." The great successes of the glorious period of Van Heutsz — the time of the great expeditions and excursions — were: the [[Pidië-expeditie]], the expeditions in Idi and Peusangan, the [[Pasé-expeditie]], the [[Samalanga-expeditie]], the expeditions to the Gayo and Alas lands, the action in the Tangsé and Geumpang area and on the North Coast of Atjeh, the patrol actions on the always restless West Coast, and finally the Battle of Batèë-Ilië, by which the organised, general resistance in Atjeh was broken for good.

## The breaking of the organised resistance

The Battle of Batèë-Ilië is the book's — and the colonial memory's — marker of the breaking of the organised, general resistance in Atjeh. The organised resistance — the organised resistance of the Acehnese — was broken for good at Batèë-Ilië. The Battle of Batèë-Ilië is therefore the book's marker of the military end of the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]]: the battle after which the organised, general resistance was broken, and after which only the patrol actions and the column method remained.

## The sequence

The book's sequence is: the Graafland era — the all-out offensive — the defection of Teukoe Oemar on [[29 March]] [[1896]] (the critical days) — the glorious period of Van Heutsz (the time of the great expeditions and excursions, including the great successes culminating in the Battle of Batèë-Ilië) — the administration of Governor Swart (the regulated conditions and the peace, order, and security). The Battle of Batèë-Ilië is therefore the last of the great successes of the glorious period of Van Heutsz: the battle that broke the organised resistance for good and that opened the way to the administration of Governor Swart.

## Significance

The Battle of Batèë-Ilië is significant in *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* as the battle by which "the organised, general resistance in Atjeh was broken for good." The book's argument — that the Battle of Batèë-Ilië was the last of the great successes of the glorious period of Van Heutsz and that it broke the organised resistance for good — is the book's marker of the military end of the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]]. The Battle of Batèë-Ilië is therefore the book's — and the colonial memory's — marker of the breaking of the organised resistance: the battle that opened the way to the administration of Governor Swart.
