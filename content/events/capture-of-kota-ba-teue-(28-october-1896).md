**The Capture of Kota Ba Teuē** — *inname van de sterkte, genaamd Kota Ba Teuē* — on [[28 October]] [[1896]] was the Dutch storming of the Acehnese *stelling* (fortification) called [[Kota Ba Teuē]] in the [[XXII Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]], undertaken in *samenwerking met de bezetting van Tjot Mantjang* — cooperation with the garrison of [[Tjot Mantjang]] (Blang Bintang) — as part of the [[Temporary Occupation of Indrapoeri (28 October 1896)|temporary occupation of Indrapoeri]]. The capture was the most significant Dutch military victory of October [[1896]], and opened the way for the [[Trip to Ladoeng and Kroeng Raja (30–31 October 1896)|trip to Ladoeng and Kroeng Raja]] of [[30 October|30]]–[[31 October]] [[1896]] and the [[Second Selimoen Expedition (3–6 November 1896)|second Selimoen expedition]] of [[3 November|3]]–[[6 November]] [[1896]]. The *stelling* (fortification (stelling)) Kota Ba Teuē — a strong Acehnese fortification which controlled the approach to [[Indrapoeri]] — had resisted earlier Dutch probes, and its capture was a critical step in the Dutch forward movement of the second semester of [[1896]].

## The Stronghold of Kota Ba Teuē

The *stelling* (fortification (stelling)) Kota Ba Teuē — literally "Fort of the Father of the Flag" — was a strong Acehnese fortification in the [[XXII Moekims]], which controlled the approach to the kampong of [[Indrapoeri]]. The *stelling* (fortification (stelling)) had been constructed by the Acehnese resistance as part of the defensive system of the XXII Moekims, and its capture was a critical objective of the Dutch forward movement of the second semester of [[1896]]. The name *Kota Ba Teuē* — combining the Malay *Kota* (fort) with the Acehnese *Ba Teuē* (Father of the Flag) — reflects the syncretic linguistic character of the Acehnese resistance, and the *stelling* (fortification (stelling)) itself was a typical Acehnese fortification, constructed of earth and timber with a vertical barbed-wire entanglement — the same type of fortification which had been encountered at the [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|benteng of Ana' Galoeng]] on [[29 June]] [[1896]].

## The Cooperation with the Tjot Mantjang Garrison

The capture of Kota Ba Teuē was undertaken *in samenwerking met de bezetting van Tjot Mantjang* — in cooperation with the garrison of [[Tjot Mantjang]] (Blang Bintang). The Blang Bintang garrison — which had been established on [[7 September]] [[1896]] and which had served as the base for the [[First Selimoen Expedition (6–11 September 1896)|first Selimoen expedition]] of [[6 September|6]]–[[11 September]] [[1896]] — provided the southern arm of the operation, while a colonne from the *geconcentreerde linie* (concentrated line) provided the northern arm. The two forces converged on the *stelling* (fortification (stelling)) Kota Ba Teuē, and the cooperation of the two forces was the key to the capture of the *stelling* (fortification (stelling)) on [[28 October]] [[1896]]. The capture of Kota Ba Teuē opened the way for the [[Trip to Ladoeng and Kroeng Raja (30–31 October 1896)|trip to Ladoeng and Kroeng Raja]] of [[30 October|30]]–[[31 October]] [[1896]] and the [[Second Selimoen Expedition (3–6 November 1896)|second Selimoen expedition]] of [[3 November|3]]–[[6 November]] [[1896]].

## Significance

The Capture of Kota Ba Teuē on [[28 October]] [[1896]] is significant in the documentary record of *ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel* as the most significant Dutch military victory of October [[1896]]. The capture — undertaken in cooperation with the Blang Bintang garrison as part of the [[Temporary Occupation of Indrapoeri (28 October 1896)|temporary occupation of Indrapoeri]] — opened the way for the [[Trip to Ladoeng and Kroeng Raja (30–31 October 1896)|trip to Ladoeng and Kroeng Raja]] of [[30 October|30]]–[[31 October]] [[1896]] and the [[Second Selimoen Expedition (3–6 November 1896)|second Selimoen expedition]] of [[3 November|3]]–[[6 November]] [[1896]], and represented the slow, methodical extension of Dutch forward positions which characterised the *pacificatie* (pacification) of the second semester of [[1896]] under [[J. W. Stemfoort|Stemfoort]]'s second tenure. The capture of the *stelling* — a strong Acehnese fortification which had resisted earlier Dutch probes — demonstrated the increasing effectiveness of the Dutch forward movement, and the cooperation of the Blang Bintang garrison with the colonne from the *geconcentreerde linie* (concentrated line) established the operational pattern which would be repeated in the November operations.
