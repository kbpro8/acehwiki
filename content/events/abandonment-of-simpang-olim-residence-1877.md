**The abandonment of the Simpang Olim residence** occurred in [[1877]], when the [[Radja of Simpang Olim|local radja]] of [[Simpang Olim]] fled or was driven from his dwelling, leaving behind a substantial assemblage of personal ornaments that subsequently entered the collection of the [[Koninklijk Bataviaasch Genootschap van Kunsten en Wetenschappen|Batavian Society]].

## Circumstances

The 1928 treasure-room guide describes the event concisely: the objects were *"aangetroffen in diens verlaten woning in 1877"* — "found in his abandoned dwelling in 1877." The use of the word *verlaten* (abandoned, deserted) combined with *gewezen* (former) to describe the radja indicates a forced departure. The radja had already been stripped of his status by the time the catalogue was compiled, suggesting that his abandonment of the residence was a definitive rupture — either he was killed, captured, or permanently exiled.

## Context of the Aceh War in 1877

The year [[1877]] fell within the first phase of the [[Aceh War]]. The Dutch had first attacked the palace at [[Koeta Radja|Kutaraja]] in [[1873]], and by 1874 had occupied the royal capital, proclaiming the sultanate dissolved. However, the Acehnese resistance continued under the leadership of regional commanders and religious figures, and the Dutch found themselves locked in a protracted guerrilla conflict. In this phase, Dutch columns regularly penetrated into the interior, attacking and burning villages and forcing local rulers either to submit or flee.

The abandonment of the radja's house at Simpang Olim is consistent with this pattern of displacement. The rich collection of ornaments left behind — including gold and silver jewellery, amulets, daggers, and the remarkable gold spectacles — suggests a hasty departure in which the radja was unable or chose not to take his most precious possessions.

## The recovered objects

The full assemblage is catalogued in the Batavian Society's Cabinet 3 (Atjeh), items 2–42, and is described in detail under [[Radja of Simpang Olim]].
