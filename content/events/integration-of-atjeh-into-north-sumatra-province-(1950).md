**The integration of Atjeh into the Province of North Sumatra** was the process by which the pre-war residency of [[Atjeh]] — which had existed as a separate colonial administrative unit and was not part of the [[Negara Sumatera Timur]] (N.S.T.) — was incorporated into the new autonomous province of North Sumatra (*Propinsi Sumatera Utara*) following the dissolution of the N.S.T. on 15 August [[1950]].

## Background

The [[Negara Sumatera Timur]] (State of East Sumatra) had been established as a federal state within the United States of Indonesia (*R.I.S.*) on 25 April [[1948]], with its capital at Medan. It encompassed the territory of the former East Sumatra residency but not the pre-war residencies of [[Atjeh]] and Tapanuli. With the collapse of the federal system and the adoption of the unitary state by the R.I.S. parliament on 15 August [[1950]], the N.S.T. was dissolved and its territory, together with Atjeh and Tapanuli, was constituted as the autonomous province of North Sumatra.

## The agreement of 5 August 1950

On 14 July [[1950]], the minister [[Anak Agung Gde Agung]] established a Committee for Preparation (*Comité van Voorbereiding*) at Medan, consisting of [[Sarimin Reksodihardjo]], Mr. S. M. Amin, and Mr. M. Joesoef. The committee's communiqué of 5 August [[1950]] announced that:

- East Sumatra would become part of the autonomous province of North Sumatra;
- The province would comprise the pre-war residencies of [[Atjeh]], Tapanuli, and East Sumatra;
- Districts (*kabupaten*) and municipalities would enjoy autonomy within the province;
- The division of North Sumatra into districts and municipalities would be decided by the central government on the proposal of a "Panitia Persiapan Pembentukan Propinsi Sumatera Utara" (Committee for the Preparation of the Formation of the Province of North Sumatra);
- The liquidation of the N.S.T. was to be completed, if possible, before the formation of the unitary state.

## Transitional administration

[[Sarimin Reksodihardjo]] was appointed acting governor (*dd. gouverneur*) of North Sumatra. He was assisted by a college of three:
1. [[Tengku M. Daudsjah]] — acting resident of [[Atjeh]];
2. Binanga Siregar — deputy resident of Tapanuli;
3. [[Radja Kaliamsjah Sinaga]] — former Wakil Wali Negara of the N.S.T.

## Atjeh's oil fields and economic concerns

In the context of the integration, the question of the oil fields at [[Langsa]] and in [[Atjeh]] was raised. The acting governor noted that if the central government could not resolve the issue of utilising these welfare resources, it should delegate the matter to the province: "Als Djakarta deze zaak niet kan oplossen, delegeer haar dan aan ons, maar doe iets" ("If Djakarta cannot resolve this matter, delegate it to us, but do something").
