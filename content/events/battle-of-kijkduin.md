**The Battle of Kijkduin** (also known as the **Battle of the Texel**) was fought on **[[21 August]] [[1673]]** between the **[[Dutch Navy]]**, again under **[[Michiel de Ruyter]]**, and a combined **[[French fleet]]** and **[[English fleet]]**.  The source notes that the Dutch victory “settled the pleit” (see [[275]]), ensuring continued Dutch control of the seas.

## Significance for Aceh
By defeating the Franco‑English fleet, the Dutch secured the maritime routes used by the **[[Indische retourvloot]]** to reach the **[[East Indies]]**, where **[[Aceh]]** was a major source of pepper and other spices.  The battle therefore helped maintain the flow of goods that underpinned the **[[Dutch East India Company]]**’s economic power in Aceh.
