**Governor Sarimin's visit to Atjeh** took place in February [[1950]], when the acting governor of North Sumatra, [[Sarimin Reksodihardjo]], made an official inspection visit to [[Atjeh|Atjeh]] accompanied by some ten civil servants and journalists.

## Delegation

The delegation included:
- Ir. [[R. S. Danunagoro]];
- [[Darwin Amin]];
- [[Abdul Wahab Siregar]];
- [[Ismail Daulay]];
- [[Moh. Diri Harahap]];
- Dr. [[Sahar]];
- [[Zainul Basri]].

## Findings

The governor's first impressions of Atjeh were favourable: politically there were no longer any difficulties, though economically there were. The roads urgently needed improvement. The visit demonstrated the new provincial administration's engagement with the Atjeh region following the [[integration of Atjeh into North Sumatra Province (1950)|incorporation of Atjeh into the province of North Sumatra]].
