**Pel's offensive against the VI, IV and IX Moekims (1875–1876)** was the great winter campaign launched by [[Generaal Pel|generaal Pel]] in person on [[26 December]] [[1875]] — after the arrival of the [[Bataljon Barissans van Madura|Barissans]] on [[10 December]] [[1875]] and the 9th battalion on [[21 December]] [[1875]] — by which, in the space of six weeks, three Dutch colonnes in *verbond* overran the [[VI Moekims|VI]], [[IV Moekims|IV]] and [[IX Moekims|IX]] *Sagi*s of [[Groot-Atjeh]], drove out [[Toekoe Nanta]] and [[Toekoe Bait]], and carried the Dutch line from the [[Kroeng Raba]] on the west coast to [[Atoeh]] on the south-east.

## The launch

Under Pel's personal *leiding*, three colonnes in *verbond* moved on [[26 December]] [[1875]] against the VI, IV and IX Moekims. [[Majoor Vetter|Vetter]] occupied Mibouw south of [[Kota Radja|K. Radja]]. [[Majoor F. J. E. Mekern|F. J. E. Mekern]] took a stelling between Mibouw and [[Boekit Daroe|K. Daroe]] but was *doodelijk gewond* the same day. [[Luitenant-kolonel Engel|Lt.-kol. Engel]] advanced along the right bank of K. Daroe.

## The fall of the VI Moekims

On [[27 December]] [[1875]] the important kampong of [[Ketapang Doewa]] — bitterly fought over on [[15 February]] [[1874]] — fell "andermaal en nu voorgoed" into Dutch hands. On [[28 December]] [[1875]] Engel occupied Pakan Badak; the kampongs Lamtermin and [[Bital]] were *ontruimd* by the enemy, as were Toekoe Nanta's *geduchte versterkingen* west of [[Sinangri]]. "Nergends hadden de Atjehneezen ernstigen tegenstand geboden" — proof, Borel writes, that they could not withstand a well-planned combined colonne attack. On [[8 January]] [[1876]] Lamtengah fell "niet zonder krachtigen tegenstand."

## Into the IV and IX Moekims

On [[10 January]] [[1876]] the *opperhoofd* of [[Lampagger]] submitted; a post was *opgericht*. On [[15 January]] [[1876]] a colonne marched through the *kloof* (gorge) of [[Blang Kalla]] southward over *bergachtig terrein* into the IV Moekims, reaching Loenga on the right bank of the [[Kroeng Raba]] mouth, which would become the western boundary of the new *afsluitingslinie*. On [[28 January]] [[1876]] Pel invaded the IX Moekims; at [[Boekit Daroe]] there was *vinnig gestreden*, and the mountain pass of [[Glitaroem]], which gave access between the IV and IX Moekims, fell into Dutch hands.

## The closing weeks

On [[31 January]] [[1876]] troops moved eastward, took Lampermej and Oeleh Soesoe — at Oeleh Soesoe the enemy offered *hevigen tegenstand* but had to *zwichten*. On [[3 February]] [[1876]] [[Atoeh]] was carried after *heeten strijd*. On [[4 February]] [[1876]] Lamrong and [[Lamsajoen]] fell; communication with [[Longbatta-Mesigit]] was established. On [[7 February]] [[1876]] the troops returned to K. Radja after a ten-day *tocht*. Total losses were five dead and fifty-nine wounded — *betrekkelijk gering*. The entire *Sagie* of [[XXV Moekims]] was now in Dutch hands. The demarcation line ran from the mouth of the [[Kroeng Raba]] on the west coast to Atoch, then northward to [[Pager Ajer]] on the left bank of the [[Atjeh-rivier]].

## Borel's verdict

For Borel this is the campaign of the war: proof that, with adequate reinforcements, a clear plan, and the offensive *stelsel*, the Atjehneezen could be beaten in the field at small cost. The conquest would be crowned, on [[13 February]] [[1876]], by the taking of [[Pango]] — though the same day would bring the [[Atoeh catastrophe (13 February 1876)|Atoeh catastrophe]].
