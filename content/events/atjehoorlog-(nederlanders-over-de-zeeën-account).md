**The Atjehoorlog (Nederlanders over de Zeeën account)** is the narrative of the [[Atjehoorlog|Atjehoorlog]] of [[1873]]-[[1904]] given in Chapter IV of *[[Nederlanders over de Zeeën (De Graaf et al., 1955)|Nederlanders over de Zeeën]]* ([[1955]] second edition), authored by [[G. Gonggrijp|Prof. G. Gonggrijp]]. The account is the most concentrated single-paragraph summary of the war in the source and the principal basis of the present Aceh Wiki articles derived from *Nederlanders over de Zeeën*.

## The narrative arc

[[G. Gonggrijp|Gonggrijp]]'s narrative arc comprises: the [[Anglo-Dutch Treaty of 1824|1824 London Treaty]]'s impossible double commitment; the [[1857]] contract and its non-observance; the [[Sumatra-tractaat (1871)|1871 Sumatra-tractaat]]; the [[Declaration of war against the Sultan of Atjeh (1873)|1873 declaration of war]]; the two [[1873]] expeditions under [[General Köhler|Köhler]] and [[General Van Swieten|Van Swieten]]; the [[Pel|Pel]] interlude; the [[1880]] false dawn; the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentratie]] period; the [[1892]] blockade and the [[Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] advice; the [[1896]] defection of [[Teungku Um|Teungku Um]] and the [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] appointment; and the post-[[1904]] consolidation under the [[Korte verklaring|korte verklaring]].

## The Snouck Hurgronje analysis

The most distinctive feature of the [[G. Gonggrijp|Gonggrijp]] account is the embedding of the [[Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] analysis of Atjehnese social structure: the *kampong*/*mukim*/*uléebalang*/*sagi*/*panglima sagi* hierarchy, the verdict that the sultan's authority over the *panglima's sagi* and the *uléebalangs* existed "alleen in naam" (in name only), and the [[1892]] advice to ignore the sultan's party, strike the enemy sensibly, and thereafter win the population by welfare policy.

## The Deykerhoff-Pijnacker Hordijk error

The [[G. Gonggrijp|Gonggrijp]] account's principal didactic episode is the [[Deykerhoff|Deykerhoff]]-[[Pijnacker Hordijk|Pijnacker Hordijk]] decision to override the [[Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] warning and to arm and subsidise [[Teungku Um|Teungku Um]]. The [[1896]] defection of [[Teungku Um|Teungku Um]] — "wierp het masker af" (threw off the mask) — is, in the source's narrative, the consequence of this error.

## The Van Heutsz reversal

The [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] appointment of [[1896]] is the [[G. Gonggrijp|Gonggrijp]] account's institutional reversal: "De G.-G. Van der Wijck (1893-99) begreep, dat Snouck Hurgronje's adviezen gevolgd moesten worden. Ook de overste Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz had dit begrepen. In 1896 werd hij belast met de leiding der militaire operaties in Groot-Atjeh. Met mobiele kolonnes werd dit gebied onafgebroken doorkruist en de vijand geen rust gelaten" (The G.-G. Van der Wijck (1893-99) understood that Snouck Hurgronje's advice had to be followed. Overste Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz had understood this too. In 1896 he was charged with the direction of military operations in Groot-Atjeh. With mobile columns this area was traversed uninterruptedly and the enemy was given no rest).

## The korte verklaring

The [[Korte verklaring|korte verklaring]] — drawn up by [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] "in samenwerking met Snouck Hurgronje" (in cooperation with Snouck Hurgronje) — is the [[G. Gonggrijp|Gonggrijp]] account's principal administrative instrument of the post-[[1904]] consolidation. The declaration was eventually signed by 261 chiefs of self-governing territories in the *buitengewesten*.

## Significance

The [[Atjehoorlog (Nederlanders over de Zeeën account)|Atjehoorlag (Nederlanders over de Zeeën account)]] is the principal narrative of the [[Atjehoorlog|Atjehoorlog]] in *Nederlanders over de Zeeën* and the source of the present Aceh Wiki articles derived from the book. Its distinctive features are the embedding of the [[Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] analysis of Atjehnese social structure and the didactic episode of the [[Deykerhoff|Deykerhoff]]-[[Pijnacker Hordijk|Pijnacker Hordijk]] error.
