**Maastricht** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Maurice Henri du Croo]]

**Maurice Henri du Croo** — writing as **M. H. du Croo, Kolonel K.N.I.L. b.d.** — was the Dutch colonial army officer, administrator and author who wrote *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]* ([[Maastricht]]: N.V. Leiter-Nypels, [[1943]]), the biography of [[H. N. A. Swart]], with the collaboration of [[H. J. Schmidt]] and a foreword by lieutenant-general [[G. K. Dijkstra]]. With Schmidt he had earlier written *[[Military Constabulary in Atjeh (1943)|Military Constabulary in Atjeh (1943), to which the Swart biography repeatedly refers for episodes it does not narrate at length.

### From [[1943]]

**[[1943]]** was the year of publication, at [[Maastricht]] by N.V. Leiter-Nypels, of *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]* by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]] with the collaboration of [[H. J. Schmidt]] and a foreword by [[G. K. Dijkstra]]; and of *[[Military Constabulary in Atjeh (1943)|Marechaussee in Atjeh]]* by Schmidt and du Croo.

### From [[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)]]

***Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh*** was the biography of the Dutch lieutenant-general and Civil and Military Governor of [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] [[H. N. A. Swart]], written by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], *Kolonel K.N.I.L. b.d.*, with the collaboration of [[H. J. Schmidt]], *Kolonel tit. K.N.I.L. b.d.*, with a foreword by His Excellency lieutenant-general K.N.I.L. b.d. [[G. K. Dijkstra]], and published at [[Maastricht]] by N.V. Leiter-Nypels in [[1943]]. Its title-page motto was *"For courage, conduct, loyalty"* — the device of the [[Militaire Willemsorde]].
