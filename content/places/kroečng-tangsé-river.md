**Kroečng Tangsé (river)** (river) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. River at Tangsé that joins the Kroečng Geumpang to form the Kroečng Teunom.

## Type

river

## Description

River at [[Tangsé]] that joins the [[kroečng|Kroečng]] [[Geumpang]] to form the [[kroečng|Kroečng]] [[Teunom]].

## Significance

Kroečng Tangsé (river) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
