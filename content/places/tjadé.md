**Tjadé** was a Dutch encampment (*kampement*) in [[Groot-Atjeh]] to the north of the *kampong* (village) belt of the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]], to which the column operating under [[General van der Heijden]] withdrew for the night after the action at the fortified *[[Messigit|Messigit]]* — the withdrawal that provoked the general's anger.

## The withdrawal

Hooijer recorded, through the tale of an old [[Ambonese soldiers in the Atjeh outposts|Ambonese soldier]] at [[Toengkoeb]], that the column — having cut its way through hundreds of *paggers* (bamboo fences), captured a couple of *bentengs* in passing, and covered only half its road by four in the afternoon — withdrew *ter wille van een rustigen nacht voor de vermoeide soldaten* to the north, to the encampment at Tjadé, instead of pressing further or at least remaining where it was to resume the task at first light. *En dat was de reden van des Generaals toorn geweest* — that had been the reason for the general's wrath, for as much as he cared for his men, he demanded much of them.
