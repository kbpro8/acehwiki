**Peudawa** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Landschap in the onderafdeeling Idi; collaborated with BPM to construct a landing field in 1934 for aerial mapping of the Peureula' oil fields.

## Description

[[landschap|Landschap]] in the [[onderafdeeling]] Idi; collaborated with BPM to construct a landing field in [[1934]] for aerial mapping of the [[Peureula]]' oil fields.

## Significance

Peudawa is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
