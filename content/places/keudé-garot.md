**Keudé Garot** was the trading quarter of [[Garot]] in [[Pidië]] where [[Tgk. Aron]] carried out his *huzarentukje* in the night of [[8 December|8]]–[[9 December]] [[1908]].

Partly disguised as soldiers in captured uniforms and equipment, his band gave itself out as a patrol, was admitted by the village watch, captured eight rifles lent to the *[[oelèëbalang]]*, wounded the [[Imeum]] of Garot, head of the watch, and disappeared with the booty — "eight rifles was a very great deal in those days, although, now that the Kompeuni had truly become the Kompeuni again* ("the Company (the Dutch power)"had become, naturally only a 'loan'."
