**Kotta Poeah** was a fortified Acehnese settlement and defensive redoubt (*benteng*) situated in the agricultural interior of the Aceh River valley in modern Aceh Province, Indonesia.

## History During Pel's Campaign

Guarding irrigation channels and supply roads within the [[XXII Moekims]], Kotta Poeah was utilized by warriors under [[Panglima Polem]] to resist Dutch occupation forces. 

During [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]], Dutch columns comprising the [[3rd Infantry Battalion]] and [[13th Infantry Battalion]] assaulted and captured Kotta Poeah during the [[March through the XXII Moekims]] in February [[1876]], dismantling its timber ramparts.
