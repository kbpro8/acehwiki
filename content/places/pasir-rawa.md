**Pasir Rawa** (sea bath (at Sigli)) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Sea bathing place at Sigli, named alongside the ELS (Europeesche Lagere School) and electric light as one of Sigli's facilities.

## Type

sea bath (at [[Sigli]])

## Description

Sea bathing place at [[Sigli]], named alongside the ELS (Europeesche Lagere School) and electric light as one of [[Sigli]]'s facilities.

## Significance

Pasir Rawa is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
