**Kroëng Raba** (also written Krueng Raba) was a kampong in the [[IV Moekims|IV Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]], one of the points designated by [[J. L. J. H. Pel|General Pel]] in his note of [[30 April]] [[30 April]] [[1875]] as the western terminus of the coast-stretch to be occupied, and the object of repeated Dutch operations in [[January 1876|January 1876]] and [[July 1876|July 1876]]. Its fortification by the Dutch in [[July 1876|July 1876]] — for the better protection of the population of the IV Moekims — was one of the principal engineering works of the early war, and the post was repeatedly attacked by the enemy, especially under [[Habib Abdoerrahman|Habib Abdurrahman]] in [[June 1878|June 1878]].

## The Conquest of the IV Moekims, January 1876

On [[15 January]] [[15 January]] [[1876]] the advance into the IV Moekims began over the [[pas van Blang Kala|pas of Blang Kala]]. The path was so narrow that four mules with ammunition cases fell into the ravine; the guns had to be taken apart and carried by coolies. By the afternoon of [[17 January]] [[17 January]] [1876] the troops descended into the plain of the IV Moekims; on [[18 January]] [[18 January]] [1876] Kroëng Raba was reached. On [[25 January]] [[25 January]] [1876] the troops returned to [[Pakan Badak|Pakan Badak]]. In ten days, from [[28 January]] [[28 January]] to [[7 February]] [[7 February]], the [[IX Moekims|IX Moekims]] was also reduced.

## The Fortification, July 1876

Operations in [[July 1876|July 1876]] in the IV and VI Moekims and the adjoining mountains were undertaken "with the object of driving the enemy from there, destroying his dwellings and provision-shops, and at the same time establishing a post at Kroëng Raba, for the better protection of the population of the IV Moekims." The establishment of the post at Kroëng Raba was part of the wider Dutch strategy of securing the IV Moekims after the conquest of the VI and IX Moekims.

## The Attack of June 1878

By the spring of [[1878]] the [[Habib Abdoerrahman|Habib]] was once more on the offensive. As soon as [[K. van der Heyden|Van der Heyden]] had departed for [[Gedong|Gedong]], news came of the Habib's intention to make a great attack with 2,000 men in the IV Moekims on the posts at Kroëng Raba, [[Boekit Seboen|Boekit Seboen]] and [[Pakan Badak|Pakan Badak]]. On [[22 June]] [[22 June]] [[1878]] Kroëng Raba was attacked by a great multitude of Atjehers, who shot at the garrison until [[24 June]] [[24 June]] without venturing a storm. On [[23 June]] [[23 June]] the Habib sent an Atjeher with a white flag and a letter inviting the post-commandant "to leave his position and fight with him in the open field," and on [[24 June]] [[24 June]] a second letter offering free withdrawal on condition that the Dutch fortification was destroyed. At the same time various posts in other lines were heavily attacked. Van der Heyden decided to return at once; on [[23 June]] [[23 June]] he arrived at [[Oleh-leh|Oleh-leh]], and on [[24 June]] [[24 June]] a column was sent to force the [[kloof van Beradin|kloof of Beradin]], to supply the garrisons of Boekit Seboen and Kroëng Raba with food and to drive the enemy out. After a difficult march through the sawah plain, in which the column suffered considerable losses from the enemy's fire, and later through the mountains, the object was attained and both posts were relieved, whereupon the enemy withdrew entirely, only to undertake new attacks at other points.
