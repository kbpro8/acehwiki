The mountain near [[Medina]]. In the Hikayat Soydina Amdah: 'The first place mentioned is the grave of Mohammed's uncle Hamzah (Ach. Amdah) on the mountain of [[Uhud]] (Ach. Ahat).' The rapal verses also mention: 'Hamzah perished near the mountain [[Uhud]], a little distance (from [[Medina]]).'
