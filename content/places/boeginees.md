**Boeginees** (Buginese) refers to the Bugis people of South Sulawesi, known throughout the Indonesian archipelago as skilled seafarers and traders. They established trading networks and settlements across the Malay world, including connections with [[Acheh]] and the [[East Coast of Sumatra]].
