**Langsar Estate** was a plantation enterprise in [[Atjeh|Atjeh]] that served as a key destination for the survey parties sent in February [[1950]] to assess the condition of the plantation enterprises following the Dutch-Indonesian hostilities. The second group of some twenty planters, led by [[Sutan Barus]], travelled by jeep via P. Brandan to Langsar Estate, where the party split — some proceeding to [[Takengon]] to inspect resin and serpentinite enterprises.

## Post-survey management

After the [[V.V.R.O.S. Delegation to Kotaradja (August 1950)|V.V.R.O.S. delegation's visit to Kotaradja in August 1950]], approximately 40 of the roughly 50 enterprises in Atjeh were released, of which 20 were immediately taken into management. [[Said Abubakar]] was appointed manager (*beheerder*), with technical advice provided by [[C. A. Kleingeld]], inspector of the Sipef, who remained for several months at Langsar Estate.
