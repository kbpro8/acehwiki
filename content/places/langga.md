**Langga** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Place where the Pretendent-Sultan fortified himself during the Pedir expedition of August 1898.

## Description

Place where the [[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]] fortified himself during the [[Pedir Expedition|Pedir expedition]] of [[August]] [[1898]].

## Significance

Langga is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
