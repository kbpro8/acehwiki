**The 26 Moekims** — *Twenty-six Moekims*, written in Dutch *26 Moekims* — was one of the two seaward *Sagi's* (administrative divisions) of [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]], together with the [[25 Moekims|25 Moekims]] and the inland [[22 Moekims|22 Moekims]] and the [[Sultan's gebied|Sultan's own territory]]. The [[Conquest of the 26 Moekims (1-4 July 1879)|Dutch conquest of the 26 Moekims]] under [[General van der Heijden|Van der Heijden]] from [[1 July]] [[1 July]] [[1879]] — completed "*met volkomen succes*" (with complete success) in four days — was the principal military success of Van der Heijden's governorship, and brought the entire valley of Groot-Atjeh under Dutch control for the first and only time before the final pacification under [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] after [[1898]].

## Geography

The 26 Moekims was one of the two seaward *Sagi's* of [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] — together with the [[25 Moekims|25 Moekims]]. The 25 and 26 Moekims lay on the sea; the [[22 Moekims|22 Moekims]] lay inland. The Dutch had mainly occupied the 25 Moekims and the [[Sultan's gebied|Sultan's territory]]. All of Groot-Atjeh was to be brought under.

## The Conquest (1-4 July 1879)

On [[1 July]] [[1 July]] [[1879]] the campaign against the 26 Moekims began, and was completed with full success in four days. Then there was still an expedition to [[Selimoen|Selimoen]], far in the interior. Remote chiefs submitted. All of Groot-Atjeh was Dutch. "*De schrik was er in*" (the terror was in them). The irreconcilable principal chiefs withdrew to [[Kemala|Kemala]], the hinterland of [[Pedir|Pedir]].

## The Loss under Civil Government (1881–1884)

The conquest of the 26 Moekims would prove transient. Under the civil government of [[Pruijs van der Hoeven|Pruijs van der Hoeven]] (1881–1882) and [[Laging Tobias|Laging Tobias]] (1883–1884), the endless patrols that Van der Heijden had deemed necessary to maintain the peace were virtually abolished. In [[June 1878|June]] [[1878]] — even before the conquest of the 26 Moekims — [[Habib Abdul Rachman El-Zahir|Habib Abdul Rachman]] had invaded the Dutch *stelling* and the enemy had pressed into the [[22 Moekims|22]] and 26 Moekims. Under civil government, "steeds stoutmoediger drongen sterke vijandige benden de 22 en 26 Moekims binnen en op ons gebied door" (ever bolder, strong enemy bands pressed into the 22 and 26 Moekims and onto Dutch territory), even into the immediate vicinity of [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] and [[Oleh-leh|Oleh-leh]]. The [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration system]] of [[1884]] contracted the Dutch *stelling* back to the perimeter of [[1876]], abandoning the 26 Moekims to the enemy.

## Significance

The 26 Moekims is one of the two seaward *Sagi's* of [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] and a principal theatre of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. Its [[Conquest of the 26 Moekims (1-4 July 1879)|conquest by Van der Heijden]] in [[1 July|1]]–[[4 July|4 July]] [[1879]] was the principal military success of Van der Heijden's governorship; its loss under civil government was the principal disaster of the [[Civil Government Period (1881-1884)|civil government period]]; its abandonment by the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration system]] of [[1884]] contracted the Dutch *stelling* back to the perimeter of [[1876]]. The final pacification under Van Heutsz after [[1898]] would re-establish Dutch control over the 26 Moekims.
