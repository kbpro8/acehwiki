**VII Moekims Pidië** was the separately administered territory of [[Pidië]] placed under a civil administrator in [[1909]].

It was one of the sub-divisions — with the *[[sagi]]* of the [[XXII Moekims]], [[Tamiang]], [[Serbödjadi]], [[Poelò Raja]] and [[Tapa' Toean]] — which the [[Bestuursindeeling van Atjeh (1908)|administrative reorganisation]] allowed to pass to civil administrators in the year after its introduction.
