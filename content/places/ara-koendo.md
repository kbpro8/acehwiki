**Ara Koendo** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Location of one of Pang Nanggròë's attacks in which the Dutch lost 10 carbines.

## Description

Location of one of [[Pang Nanggròë's attacks]] in which the Dutch lost 10 carbines.

## Significance

Ara Koendo is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
