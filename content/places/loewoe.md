**Loewoe** (modern **Luwu**) is a region on the southeastern coast of [[Celebes]] (today [[Sulawesi]]) that, together with [[Boni (Sulawesi)]], was the focus of a Dutch marine expedition in [[1905]]. The operation sought to pacify local chieftains and to protect Dutch commercial interests in the region.
