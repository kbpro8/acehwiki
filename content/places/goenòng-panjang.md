**Goenòng Panjang** is a subject named in *[[Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[T. Radja Sabi]]

**T. Radja Sabi** was the son of [[T. Tjhi' Toenòng]] and [[Tjoet Meutia]] of [[Keureutòë]], proclaimed *[[oelèëbalang]]* of Keureutòë by [[Pang Nanggròë]] at the age of six in November [[1907]], who after the deaths of his stepfather and mother in [[1910]] wandered for nine years in the forests of the [[Goenòng Panjang]] as the last unfound irreconcilable of the district, submitted at last at [[Lho' Seumawè]] on [[13 March]] [[1919]] at the age of eighteen, and was finally installed as *oelèëbalang* of Keureutòë in [[1937]]. [[Maurice Henri du Croo|Du Croo]] called his story "the Acehnese drama of T. Radja Sabi and his people."

### From [[T. Radja Sabi]]

The condition set by the other side proved a stumbling-block: "we shall bring the real T. Radja Sabi down when it is first established that the boy at Koetaradja is not genuine — that is, when the Great Master acknowledges that a wrong person has been designated as T. Radja Sabi." And so T. Radja Sabi continued to wander in the forests of the lonely [[Goenòng Panjang]].
