**Mesigit** was a fortified structure (often described as a “temple” or “mosque”) located near the royal **Kraton** in the Aceh interior. During the April [[1873]] campaign, Dutch forces under [[Captain-terr zee Binkes]] captured the building on [[10 April]] [[1873]] after a brief but fierce fight, only to lose it again to Acehnese counter‑attacks on [[14 April]] [[1873]].

The building’s strategic position made it a focal point for both sides; its capture allowed the Dutch to establish a beach bivouac, while its loss forced a temporary retreat. Marine casualties incurred during the fighting at the Mesigit included the death of [[Sergeant Rood]] and several wounded.

## Significance
The Mesigit episode illustrated the difficulties of sustaining an amphibious foothold in Aceh’s rugged interior and highlighted the importance of coordinated naval gunfire and infantry assaults.
