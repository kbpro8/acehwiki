**Lampong** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Region in South Sumatra; source of a pepper variëteit more resistant to voetrot (foot rot).

## Description

Region in South Sumatra; source of a pepper variëteit more resistant to [[Voetrot|voetrot]] (foot rot).

## Significance

[[Lampongers|Lampong]] is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
