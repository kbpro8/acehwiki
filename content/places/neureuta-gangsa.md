In Hikayat Diwa Akaih Chahya (XXXVII): 'The hero of this tale is the son of a royal pair of celestial origin, Diwa La'sana and Mandu Diwi, king and queen of Neureuta Gangsa.' The hero eventually succeeds to the throne of Neureuta Gangsa.

## Source
*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
