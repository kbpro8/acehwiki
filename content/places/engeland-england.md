**Engeland (England)** (country / colonial power) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Colonial rival; seized Dutch colonies end 18th century; returned them 1813-1814 (Londensche Conventie); signed Aceh agreement 1819 and London Treaty 1824; Sumatra Treaty 1871 gave Holland free hand in Aceh.

## Type

## Description

Colonial rival; seized Dutch colonies end 18th century; returned them [[1813]]-[[1814]] ([[London|Londensche]] Conventie); signed Aceh agreement [[1819]] and [[London]] Treaty [[1824]]; Sumatra Treaty [[1871]] gave [[Leyden (Leiden)|Holland]] free hand in Aceh.

## Significance

Engeland (England) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
