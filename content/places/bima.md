**Bima** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Onderneming tegen Soembawa (1908)]]

**Onderneming tegen Soembawa ([[1908]])** was the undertaking against [[Bima]] and the island of [[Soembawa]] between [[16 February]] and [[9 May]] [[1908]], the closing piece of [[H. N. A. Swart]]'s service in the eastern part of the archipelago.
