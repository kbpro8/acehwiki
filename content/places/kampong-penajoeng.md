**Village Penajoeng** was a village located on the right bank of the [[Atjeh-rivier]], opposite Village Djawa.

## Role in 1873-1874
During the Second Aceh Expedition, the 2nd Brigade of the Dutch army established a massive bivouac in Penajoeng on 18 December [[1873]]. From this location, troops crossed the river to initiate the final push that culminated in the [[Third Conquest of the Great Missigit]] on [[6 January]] [[1874]].
