---
title: Sriweue
tags: ["place", "north-east-coast"]
---

A village in [[Pidie]] that was one of the centres of religious learning before the Dutch. 'Such were [[Langga and Langgo]], [[Langga and Langgo]], Sriweue, [[Simpang]], [[Ie Leubeue]] (= Ayer Labu).' The word sriweuen is also used for a fowl-run.
