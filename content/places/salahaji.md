**Salahadji** is a river — the *Salahadjirrivier* — and a small place (*het plaatsje Salahadji*) on the south-eastern approach to [[Atjeh]] from the [[Aru Bay|Aroe-baai]] (Aru bay). [[W. Moens|Moens]], who in November [[1913]] entered Atjeh from [[Pangkalan Brandan]] by steam launch, recorded the route: into the Aroe-baai again, past [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoeh]], the village of the petroleum tanks, and up the Salahadji river to the little place Salahadji itself; *verder hadden wij het ook zeker met dit transport minder getroffen* — the transport might have been worse. From there a hardened art-road, a few days' march from the Acehnese frontier, ran to [[Koeala Simpang|Kwalla Simpang]] on the [[Tamiang]] river, the first station of a government official on Acehnese soil, which was reached by carriage in a good hour.
