A bay and arm of the sea on the southern West Coast, famous for the gigantic saint Teungku Lho Tapa' Tuan. 'In old maps it is marked as Tampat Tuan, but is called by the Achehnese Tapa' Tuan (footstep of the saint).' A gigantic saint is said to have once pursued a dragon into the sea; certain traces of this chase may still be seen both on the shore and in the sea. One of his footsteps left its mark on a hill, and another lower down close to the sea-shore — the last about 75 yards long — is roofed over. Two small islands ([[Pulo Duo]]) in the bay are believed to have been originally connected but rent asunder by the dragon in his flight. The saint hurled his turban and his staff at the dragon, which sank beneath the waves, while these objects turned to stone and now project from the sea as rocks. The sadati dialogue: 'When you get to [[Laboh Haji]] (vulg. Labuan Haji), who is king there? The uleebalang of that place is a woman, she keeps us all in her protection.'
