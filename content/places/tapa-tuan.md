## Account from *The Geology of Indonesia* ([[1949]]) — Tapa Tuan

### Tapa Tuan as a geological reference-point

The mineral localities of the West-Atjeh coast that are located by reference to Tapa Tuan include:

- **[[Kuto Buloh]]** (No. 9 in Van Bemmelen's Fig. 41): 27 km North of Tapa Tuan — an iron-ore locality (no particulars available).
- **[[A. Pentuwa Gade]]** (No. 8): 68 km SE of Meulaboh — an iron-ore locality (no particulars available), in the same general region as Kuto Buloh.

The Tapa Tuan cluster of West-Atjeh iron-ore localities is, in contrast to the more substantially-documented NW-of-Meulaboh cluster (the [[Kr. Ligan (Babah Loh)|Kr. Ligan]] 700-m-long deposit, the [[Kr. Rigaih]] and [[Alu Talu]] scattered-ore localities, and the [[Kwala Boe Tjot Plaj|Kwala Boe and Tjot Plaj]] bog-iron deposits), recorded only as points on the map without detailed geological description. The Tapa Tuan cluster accordingly represents the southern, less-well-documented end of the West-Atjeh iron-ore zone, in contrast to the better-documented northern end around Meulaboh.

### Significance

Tapa Tuan is significant for the encyclopedia of [[Acheh]] history because it is the geographical reference-point for the southernmost cluster of West-Atjeh iron-ore occurrences recorded in the colonial geological literature — the [[Kuto Buloh]] locality (27 km North of Tapa Tuan) and the [[A. Pentuwa Gade]] locality (in the same general region). The Tapa Tuan cluster represents the southern end of the West-Atjeh iron-ore zone as recorded in the colonial geological literature, and its inclusion in Van Bemmelen's Fig. 41 key — despite the absence of detailed geological description — establishes it as part of the documented colonial-era iron-ore record of Atjeh.
