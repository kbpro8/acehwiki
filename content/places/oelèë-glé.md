**Oelèë Glé** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Place; its onderhoofd Teukoe Sabi wished not to be placed under the oelèëbalang of Samalanga (onderhoofden-actie).

## Description

Place; its onderhoofd [[Teukoe Sabi]] wished not to be placed under the [[oelèëbalang]] of [[Samalanga]] ([[Operette van Tjaleuë (the underhoofden-action interception)|onderhoofden-actie]]).

## Significance

Oelèë Glé is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
