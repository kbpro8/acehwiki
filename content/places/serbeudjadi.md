**Serbeudjadi** — *Serbajadi* in modern Indonesian spelling, *Serbendjadi*, *Ser-hendjadi* in OCR variants of the *[[Zelfbestuursverordeningen Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]]* — was a *zelfbesturend landschap* (self-governing territory) and the seat of the *onderafdeeling* (sub-division) of the same name in the *afdeeling* (administrative division) [[Gayo- en Alaslanden|Gajo- en Alaslanden]] in the interior highlands of [[Atjeh|Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]], ruled jointly by the *Kedjoeroen Abog* (Panta Ali Aman Lage) and *Banta Ali Aman Lage* — the two named *Zelfbestuurders* (Self-Governing Rulers) whose *Zelfbestuursbesluit* (Self-Government Decision) of [[1 January]] [[1921]] (Reg. No. 21) designated thirteen named sites in the *landschap* as *natuurmonumenten* (natural monuments) under the colonial *ordonnantie van 18 Maart 1916 Staatsblad van Nederlandsch-Indië No. 278* (ordinance of 18 March 1916, Netherlands Indies Statute Book No. 278), the only *natuurmonument* designation in the entire 1930 compilation. The signatory clause of Reg. No. 21 names both rulers personally rather than as a corporate collective: *"Kami Oelesbalang dari landschap Serbendjadi, Kedjoeroen Aboq, Banta Ali Aman Lage, dari onderafdeeling Serbeudjadi, afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden."*

## Geography and the two Zelfbestuurders

The *landschap* (territory) Serbeudjadi lies in the eastern Gayo lands of the *afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden* — the highland interior of [[Atjeh|Aceh]]. The *onderafdeeling Serbeudjadi* is the same geographical area as the *landschap*: Serbeudjadi is a single-*oeleebalang* (single-chief) *landschap* in the sense that its *Zelfbestuur* (self-government) is vested in two named rulers jointly, rather than in a corporate collective of separate *oeleebalang* (territorial chiefs). The signatory clause of Reg. No. 21 reads: *"Kami Oelesbalang dari landschap Serbendjadi, Kedjoeroen Aboq, Banta Ali Aman Lage, dari onderafdeeling Serbeudjadi, afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden."* — *we, the Oelesbalang of the landschap Serbendjadi, [namely] Kedjoeroen Aboq [and] Banta Ali Aman Lage, of the onderafdeeling Serbeudjadi, afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden*.

The Dutch side of Reg. No. 21 — through an OCR bookkeeping slip — mis-attributes Serbeudjadi to the *afdeeling Oostkust van Atjeh*: *"Panta Ali Aman Lage der onderafdeeling Serbeudjadi van de afdeeling Oostkust van Atjeh."* The Malay side correctly names the *afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden*. The OCR-mangled *"Panta"* is the printed *"Banta"* — i.e. *Banta Ali Aman Lage*. *"Kedjoeroen"* (also *Keudjroeen*) is a chiefly title in the Gajo/Alas lands (cf. the *Kedjroen* of Rädjö Boekét in Takingeun); the name *"Abog"* (also OCR'd *"Aboq"*) is the personal name. *"Banta"* is a common Acehnese/Minangkabau personal-name element.

## The Rafflesia-natuurmonument verordening of 1 January 1921 (Reg. No. 21)

The *natuurmonument*-aanwijzing (natural-monument designation) of [[1 January]] [[1921]] is the only *natuurmonument* designation in the 1930 compilation, and the only regulation in the book designating natural monuments under the colonial *ordonnantie van 18 Maart 1916 Staatsblad van Nederlandsch-Indië No. 278* (the Netherlands-Indies natural-monument ordonnantie of 1916). Its full Dutch title — reconstructed from the OCR-mangled heading *"ZEL.FEESTTUUSBEESUUTT van 1 Januari 1921 houdende nanwixxing van terreinen als maturrmmnmeaten in het landsehap Serbeudjadi"* — is *Zelfbestuursbesluit van 1 Januari 1921 houdende aanwijzing van terreinen als natuurmonumenten in het landschap Serbeudjadi* (Self-Government Decision of 1 January 1921 designating grounds as natural monuments in the territory Serbeudjadi). The regulation's *Fatsal 1* (Article 1) invokes the ordonnantie explicitly: *"Aan te wijzen als natunrmonn- ment op den veet der hepalingen van de ordonnantie van 18 Maart 1916 Staatsblad van Nederlandsch-Indic No. 278"* — *to be designated as natuurmonumenten on the footing of the provisions of the ordonnantie of 18 March 1916, Staatsblad van Nederlandsch-Indië No. 278*.

The regulation designates thirteen named sites (a–n, with one duplicated) in the inland Serbeudjadi area as *natuurmonumenten* because *Rafflesia* grows there — the world's largest individual flower, a parasitic flowering plant native to Southeast Asia. The three principal sites — *Aloer Doeren Tarotoeng*, *Aloer Kloewat*, and *Aloer Itam* — are designated in *Fatsal 1a–c*:

> *"1. Ditoendjoekun sebagai peringatan menoercet ketetapan dari ordonnantie op den voet der hepalingen van de ordonnantie van 18 Maart 1916 Staatsblad van Nederlandsch-Indic No. 278, de door het daarop voorkomen van Rufflesia geken- merkte terreingedeelten in het landsehap Serbeudjadi geheel door oer- woud omringd, gelegen: a. dipinggir kali Aloer Doeren Ta- roetoeng = 11/2 K.M. sebelah Barat Daja dari kampong Ta- roedjak. aan den oever van de Aloer Doeren Taroctoong op ongeveer 11/2 K.M. ten Z.W. van de kam- pong Taroedjak."*

The three principal sites are:

- **a. Aloer Doeren Tarotoeng** — on the bank of the Aloer Doeren Tarotoeng stream, approximately 1½ KM south-west of *kampong* (village) Taroedjak (Taroetoeng / Taroedjak).
- **b. Aloer Kloewat** — 10 minutes north of *kampong* Nalon.
- **c. Aloer Itam** — 3 minutes upstream of *bivak Aloer Itam* (the colonial transport-bivouac).

The remaining ten sites — *Aloer Soengsang*, the *Ranto Pandjang–Plawi paardenpad* (horse-path, between K.M. 40 and 41), *Djamboer Manja* (on the old transport road Loekoep–Ranto Pandjang), 20 minutes N.O. of *kampong* Boegag, 20 minutes Z. of *kampong* Leles (50 M. W. of K.M. 3 of the Loekoep–Pendeng path), *kampong* Pante Kra on the west bank of the Djerneh river, 30 M. S. of K.M. 41 of the Ranto Pandjang–Plawi path, the left bank of the Djerneh near Poelo Moento, Aloer Serpong (≈7 KM S. of *kampong* Leles), and Aloer Koembar (on the Aloer Itam–Ranto Pandjang path) — together comprise the most topographically detailed regulation in the entire compilation. Most of the toponyms are streams (*Aloer* = "stream" in Gayo/Acehnese) and small *kampongs* in the inland Serbeudjadi area; the *Djerneh* river is the largest named watercourse; *Poelo Moento* (Poelo = *pulau*, island) is an island in the Djerneh.

## Parallel regelingen extended to Serbeudjadi

Beyond the Serbeudjadi-seated *natuurmonument* designation, the *landschap* receives parallel *regelingen* (arrangements) in the comparative tables of nearly every other regulation in the compilation. The Page 109 comparative table extends the Bireuën *heerendienst*-regeling (corvée-service arrangement, Reg. No. 14) to Serbeudjadi — *"Serbeudjadi 3 Oct. 1928"* — with the synchronised *inwerkingtreding* (entry into force) of [[1 January]] [[1929]]. The Page 113 comparative table extends the Teunom-Panga *schoolgeld*-regeling (school-fee arrangement, Reg. No. 15) to Serbeudjadi — *Juli-Dec 1917*. The Page 123 comparative table extends the Alaslanden cattle-transport regulation (Reg. No. 16) to Serbeudjadi — *"Serbeudjadi October 1914"*. The Page 129 comparative table extends the Lhoksoekon *klappercultuur*-*verordening* (coconut-cultivation regulation, Reg. No. 18a) to Serbeudjadi — *"Serbeudjadi 1- 9- 1916"*. The Page 133 comparative table extends the Lhoksoekon *mantri*-instructie (petty-official instruction, Reg. No. 18b) to Serbeudjadi — *"Serbendjadi 1 9- 1916"*. The Page 29 comparative table extends the *afdeeling* Pidië *Pantjang Alas* regeling (Reg. No. 4a) to Serbeudjadi — *"Serbeudjadi 1-10-1926 31-12-1926"* (i.e. *dagteekening* / date 1 October 1926, *inwerkingtreding* 31 December 1926). The Page 144 comparative table extends the Takingeun *alang-alang en dennenbosschen-verordening* (cogon-grass and pine-forest regulation, Reg. No. 22) to Serbeudjadi — *"Serbeudjadi 29- 5-1924 29- 5-1924 1- 5-1925"* (i.e. *dagteekening* 29 May 1924, *goedkeuring* / approval 29 May 1924, *inwerkingtreding* 1 May 1925).

## Significance

Serbeudjadi's significance in the Aceh Wiki History record rests on three things. First, it is the originating seat of the only *natuurmonument* designation in the 1930 compilation — Reg. No. 21 of [[1 January]] [[1921]], designating thirteen named Rafflesia-sites as *natuurmonumenten* under the colonial *ordonnantie van 18 Maart 1916 Staatsblad van Nederlandsch-Indië No. 278*, the only regulation in the book designating *natuurmonumenten* and the only regulation in the book dealing with botanical conservation as such. The choice of Serbeudjadi as the originating seat reflects the *landschap*'s particular abundance of *Rafflesia* habitat — its inland *oerwoud* (primeval forest) and its many small streams (*Aloer*) providing the precise ecological conditions for the parasitic plant. Second, it is the demonstration — through the personally-named dual signatory clause *"Kedjoeroen Aboq, Banta Ali Aman Lage"* rather than the corporate-collective *"Kami Oeleebalang"* form — of the variable constitutional form of the *Zelfbestuur* (self-government) in the highland Gajo lands, where a *landschap* could be ruled jointly by two named *Zelfbestuurders* rather than by a single *oeleebalang* or a collective of *oeleebalang*. Third, it is the demonstration — through the OCR bookkeeping slip that mis-attributes the *landschap* to the *afdeeling Oostkust van Atjeh* on the Dutch side of Reg. No. 21, while the Malay side correctly names the *afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden* — of the recurrent administrative-territorial confusion in the colonial record-keeping between the highland *afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden* and the coastal *afdeeling Oostkust van Atjeh*, a confusion that the bilingual Malay signatory clauses often resolve in favour of the Gajo- en Alaslanden attribution. Serbeudjadi is, in this sense, the empirical demonstration of the *natuurmonument*-designation motive's only appearance and the variable constitutional form of the Gajo-lands *Zelfbestuur* in the Atjeh regulatory record.
