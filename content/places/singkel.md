A coastal town on Sumatra's west coast (also referred to as Pansur), birthplace of the great Acehnese mystic Abdurra'uf, who is consequently known by the title Teungku di Kuala or "of Singkel." The town's name is also linked, through later legend, to the mystic Hamzah Pansuri.
