**Pedawa Pondong** is a locality in the interior of [[Aceh]] referenced in the index of *De geschiedenis van het Nederlandsche Korps Mariniers van 1665‑1945* on [[112]] and [[113]]. Its mention alongside other Aceh‑related entries such as [[Pedir]] and [[Pasoeh]] indicates its relevance to Dutch military activities in the region.

## Geographic and Historical Note

The source supplies only the index citation, but the inclusion of Pedawa Pondong among places recorded by the Dutch Marine Corps suggests it was a site of strategic or logistical importance during Dutch expeditions or campaigns in Aceh, likely serving as a waypoint, settlement, or battleground.

## Significance

Recording Pedawa Pondong in the marine corps history underscores the breadth of locations that formed the theater of the Dutch‑Aceh conflict, illustrating how even relatively obscure villages were mapped and noted by colonial forces.
