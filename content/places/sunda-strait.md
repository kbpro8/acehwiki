**The Sunda Strait** — Dutch *Straat Soenda* — is the strait that separates the southern tip of [[Sumatra|Sumatra]] from the western end of [[Java|Java]], and forms one of the two main sea passages between the [[Indian Ocean|Indian Ocean]] and the Java Sea. The strait is named in the Dutch pamphlet *[[Beschouwingen over Atjeh en onzen oorlog met dat rijk|Beschouwingen over Atjeh en onzen oorlog met dat rijk]]* by [[Een Ooggetuige|Een Ooggetuige]] ([[1880s]]) in the course of the author's discussion of the sea approaches to [[Acheh|Atjeh]] and of the wider setting of the Atjehnese theatre in relation to the shipping routes of the archipelago.

## Navigation and trade

The Sunda Strait is one of the two great sea passages of the archipelago — the other being the [[Strand Malaka (Strait of Malacca)|Strait of Malacca]] — by which shipping passes between the Indian Ocean and the waters of the archipelago. The strait separates the Lampong districts of southern Sumatra — including the places [[Kroeë|Kroeë]] and [[Oosthaven|Oosthaven]] — from the western end of Java. In the nineteenth century, the Sunda Strait was one of the principal routes by which shipping passed to and from the [[Netherlands Indies|Netherlands Indies]], and its relation to the [[Strand Malaka (Strait of Malacca)|Strait of Malacca]] and to the [[Strait of Halakka|Strait of Halakka]] at the northern tip of Sumatra was a central element of the geography of the archipelago within which the [[Aceh War|Atjeh War]] was fought.

## The pamphlet's reference

The author of the *[[Beschouwingen over Atjeh en onzen oorlog met dat rijk|Beschouwingen]]* names the Sunda Strait — *Straat Soenda* — in the context of a discussion of the sea approaches to Atjeh. The reference is made in the course of the author's argument on the strategic and commercial value of Atjeh, in which the Sunda Strait is discussed as one of the elements of the wider geography of the archipelago within which the Atjehnese coast and the [[Strait of Halakka|Strait of Halakka]] held their importance. The comparison of the Sunda Strait with the Strait of Malacca and the Strait of Halakka is part of the author's wider argument that Atjeh, properly developed, could serve as a station for world trade.

## Significance

The Sunda Strait is significant within the AcehWiki corpus as a waterway named in the contemporary Dutch pamphlet literature of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh War]] in connection with the sea approaches to Atjeh. The strait's mention in the *[[Beschouwingen over Atjeh en onzen oorlog met dat rijk|Beschouwingen]]* is a document of the wider geography within which the Dutch pamphleteers of the Atjeh War framed the Atjehnese theatre.
