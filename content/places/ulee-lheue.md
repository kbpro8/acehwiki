**Ulee Lheue** is the primary seaport of [[Banda Aceh]], situated on the Malacca Strait.

## Commercial and Military History

Ulee Lheue served as the historic naval gateway to the [[Sultanate of Aceh]] where foreign merchant ships loaded pepper cargo. Following the capture of the capital in [[1874]], Dutch authorities constructed a military road and the first regional steam tramway connecting Ulee Lheue directly to [[Kraton of Aceh|Kota Raja]] in [[November]] [[1876]].
