**The Mandarsah Poetih** (literally "white bedehuis" or "white mosque") was a small stone mosque to the south of [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]], already partially ruined by artillery fire, which was captured by Dutch troops under [[J. L. J. H. Pel|Kolonel PEL]] on [[28 January]] [[28 January]] [[1875]] and put in a state of defence by [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede-Luitenant C. J. Snijders]] of the [[Bataillon Mineurs en Sappeurs|Compagnie Mineurs en Sappeurs]] as part of the southward extension of the Dutch perimeter in [[January 1875|January]] [[1875]]. The post continued to be harassed by Atjeh fire from the fortified kampongs of Gitjiel Olejlo, [[Lamara Olejlo|Lamara Olejlo]], and Lohong, leading directly to the [[Lamara Olejlo fortification (15 February 1875)|Lamara Olejlo fortification of 15 February]] [[1875]].

## The Building

The biographer [[D. van den Berg|Van den Berg]] describes the Mandarsah Poetih as "een door artillerievuur reeds ten deele vernield steenen gebouwtje" — a small stone building already partially ruined by artillery fire. The name "Mandarsah Poetih" (wit bedehuis) marks it as a mosque or prayer-house of stone — a more solid construction than the usual Atjehese timber-and-bamboo kampong mosque — and the partial ruin by artillery fire suggests that it had already been the object of one or more earlier Dutch bombardments.

## The Fortification of 28 January 1875

The capture of [[28 January]] [[28 January]] [[1875]] was followed by the engineer operation of putting the building in a state of defence. [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] "voerde overeenkomstige werkzaamheden uit" — that is, he executed the corresponding works to those he had done at [[Pungej Blang Tjoet|Pungej Blang Tjoet]] five days earlier. The small size of the building and its partial ruin made it a less ambitious work than the [[Missigit Longbatta|Missigit Longbatta]] redoubt, but its position served as part of the southern line of posts covering the Dutch perimeter.

## Subsequent Operations

The Mandarsah Poetih continued to be harassed by Atjeh fire from the fortified kampongs of Gitjiel Olejlo, [[Lamara Olejlo|Lamara Olejlo]], and Lohong, leading to the reconnaissance of [[14 February|14]]/[[15 February]] [[15 February]] [[1875]] and the [[Lamara Olejlo fortification (15 February 1875)|Lamara Olejlo fortification of 15 February]] [[1875]].
