**Bambél** was the *afdeeling* created in [[1908]] comprising the [[Gajò Loeös]], the [[Alaslanden]] and [[Singkel]], abolished in [[1910]].

Under the [[Bestuursindeeling van Atjeh (1908)|administrative division of 1908]] Bambél was one of the five *afdeelingen*, each under an Assistant Resident. "In [[1910]] the division of Bambél was abolished; Singkel was joined to the [[Westkust van Atjeh|West Coast]], while the Gajò Loeös and the Alas lands became independent divisions, each under an officer serving as district authority." In December [[1914]], after two young [[Alassers]] wounded *controleur* [[Christan]] at [[Koeta Tjanè]], the investigation pointed towards the acting *[[Kedjoerön]]* of Bambél; the arrest of the presumed instigator prompted an attack on the bivouac guard in March [[1915]], but the investigation finally showed the *Kedjoerön* entirely innocent.
