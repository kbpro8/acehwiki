**Indrapuri** was/is a historic district and former royal citadel site situated along the Aceh River in [[Groot-Atjeh]].

## Historical Context and Role

Famous for its ancient fortified mosque complex built on a pre-Islamic temple base, Indrapuri served as a strategic military outpost. In [[1898]], the 2nd Division of the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]] was established here to secure the central valley districts.
