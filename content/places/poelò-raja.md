**Poelò Raja** was the island off the [[Westkust van Atjeh|west coast of Atjeh]] where, in July [[1902]], the real administrative work of [[H. N. A. Swart]] began. It was "a relatively small island that was practically pacified, but with, on the mainland, an extensive field for action in which numerous well-known band leaders and their bands put *main prang* (the war game) into practice."

## Swart as Civil Authority

On [[17 July]] [[1902]] Swart was relieved of the government of the sub-division *[[sagi]]* of the [[XXV Moekims]] and the southern settlements and charged with the government of the sub-division Poelò Raja with the title of *Civiel Gezaghebber*. He was honourably relieved on [[12 October]] [[1903]] and transferred to [[Lho' Seumawè]].

For communication between his station and the mainland Swart even disposed of a *"ship of war"*, which he used above all for nocturnal crossings and thus for the element of surprise.

It was at Poelò Raja that Swart formulated the [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|administrative principles]] that would govern the rest of his career, and there that his first clash with [[Gotfried Coenraad Ernst van Daalen|Van Daalen]] occurred: Van Daalen, visiting as acting Assistant Resident of the *Onderhoorigheden* and taking part in a patrol, judged that an *[[oelèëbalang]]* who paid his respects in the customary manner had shown insufficient respect, which Swart could not see. According to [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|van Heutsz]] matters at Poelò Raja had all been "veel te slap," and Swart was instructed to act differently and above all more vigorously in various respects — though "Van Heutsz's 'vigorous action' … does not mean the same thing as the 'vigorous action' of his successor Van Daalen."

Van Heutsz once noted on Swart's journal, when Swart was commander of the 5th Division of the *[[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|marechaussée]]* and Civil Authority at Poelò Raja: "now it is time for Captain Swart to go to [[Teunòm]]" — beneath which Swart promptly wrote: "Captain Swart will judge that for himself."

## Later

In [[1908]] there was a great shortage of plough cattle in Poelò Raja and [[Meulaboh]], and eighteen thousand guilders were made available for the purchase of buffaloes. In the [[Bestuursindeeling van Atjeh (1908)|administrative reorganisation]] Poelò Raja was among the sub-divisions placed under a civil administrator in [[1909]]. [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] recorded in July [[1908]] that [[Boreel]] "in the Poelò Raja district" travelled without military escort and reported it now and then without receiving any remark.
