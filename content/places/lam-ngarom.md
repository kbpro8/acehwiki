---
title: Lam Ngarom
tags: ["place"]
---

The gampong in [[Acheh]] Proper where [[Teungku di Lam Ngarom]] had his abode. This place is noted as the residence of a religious teacher, consistent with the pattern of Achehnese ulamas being named after the gampong where they lived and taught.
