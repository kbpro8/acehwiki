**Seuneu'am** was the bottomless marshes near [[Meulaboh]] which, with [[Tripa]], sheltered [[T. Radja Tampo']] — the one irreconcilable never found during [[H. N. A. Swart]]'s governorship.

At Swart's retirement in [[1918]] "only one unfindable man had remained unfound: T. Radja Tampo' in the bottomless swamps of Seuneu'am and Tripa (Meulaboh), an irreconcilable who even in [[1939]] still wandered as a legendary figure, holy in the eyes of the population."
