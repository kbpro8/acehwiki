**Boekloe** was a temporary Dutch military post established on [[14 January]] [[1885]] on the road from [[Anagaloeng]] to [[Lambaroe]] to protect transport operations during the evacuation of the [[XXII Moekims]] posts.

## Purpose

Boekloe was established specifically to provide security for the ongoing transport of materials and goods from the evacuated posts. The temporary post served as a protective position during the retrograde movement.

## Location

Boekloe was situated on the road between [[Anagaloeng]] and [[Lambaroe]], covering the main route used for evacuation transport.

## Evacuation

Boekloe itself was evacuated on [[28 February]] [[1885]] along with [[Senelop]], [[Montasik]], [[Anagaloeng]], and [[Pakan-Badak]].

## Significance

The establishment and subsequent evacuation of Boekloe illustrated the temporary nature of many posts and the practical challenges of retrograde operations. The post served its purpose during the evacuation period and was then abandoned.

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

[[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] records from the Koloniaal Verslag that Boekloe was evacuated together with [[Anak Galoeng]] in the February [[1885]] series at the execution of the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration]] (see [[Completion of the concentrated position (March 1885)]]).
