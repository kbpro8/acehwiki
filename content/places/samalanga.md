---
title: Samalanga
tags: [place]
---

A district whose religious leader, known as the Habib of Samalanga, became one of the principal figures directing the holy war against the Dutch after the death of Teungku Tiro, having received from the Sultan a letter bearing the royal seal authorizing his leadership.
