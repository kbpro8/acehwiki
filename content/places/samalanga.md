---
title: Samalanga
tags: ["place", "north-east-coast"]
---

A territory and centre on the North Coast. The Habib of Samalanga (a religious leader allied with the holy war) is mentioned multiple times in the [[Hikayat Prang Gompeuni]]: 'the followers of Ma' Amin and of [[Habib Samalanga]] found themselves fighting shoulder to shoulder with those of [[Teungku Kuta Karang]].' The Habib of Samalanga punished all who had gone within the 'linie' with seven days penitential seclusion. After [[Teungku Tiro]]'s death, '[[Habib Samalanga]] obtained from the Sultan a letter with the royal seal.' The guree (teacher) of Teuku Ne' was 'a man from Bateue Hie' in Samalanga.' In the sadati dialogue: 'Dear little brother, blessed little brother, I shall go and establish thee at Samalanga. At Samalanga also there are strange doings; Keuchi' Ali has been driven into the forest.'
