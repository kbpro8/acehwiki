**Reusa'** was the bivouac in the [[Tapa' Toean]] district at which all the band-heads of [[Tgk. Pidië]] came in submission in August [[1908]] with forty-seven men and fourteen firearms.

The bivouac was abolished in May [[1911]], with [[Arongan]] and [[Tanòh Mirah]], in consequence of the favourable political situation in [[Wòjla]] and [[Boebon]].
