'The ruler of Guha' is an epithet in the [[Hikajat Malém Dagang|Hikayat Malem Dagang]] for the European enemy. 'They also allude to him not only as ruler of [[Malacca]], but also occasionally by way of variety as the ruler of Guha, which latter name refers to [[Goa]] the chief settlement of the Portuguese in [[India]].' After the battle, 'the fleet now sails to Guha. Here the inquisitive king of [[Acheh]] wishes to have a look at the country, but is restrained by [[Malem Dagang]], who reminds him of the perils predicted by his consort.' This may be explained by the fact that the Portuguese disappeared from the Achehnese field of vision while the Dutch came to represent European danger.
