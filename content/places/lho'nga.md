**Lho'Nga** (former controleursplaats (controller's seat)) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. On Westkust where outliers of the Barisan reach the sea.

## Type

former controleursplaats (controller's seat)

## Description

On [[Westkust]] where outliers of the [[Barisans|Barisan]] reach the sea. Was a controleursplaats until [[1932]], then abolished as bestuursvestiging.

## Significance

Lho'Nga is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
