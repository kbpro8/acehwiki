**Lho'Nga** (former controleursplaats (controller's seat)) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. On Westkust where outliers of the Barisan reach the sea.

## Type

former controleursplaats (controller's seat)

## Description

On [[Westkust]] where outliers of the [[Barisans|Barisan]] reach the sea. Was a controleursplaats until [[1932]], then abolished as bestuursvestiging.

## Significance

Lho'Nga is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].

## In the eye survey of Groot-Atjeh ([[1937]])

Lho-Nga appears repeatedly in Dr. [[C. E. Gomperts]]'s [[Oogheelkundig kampongonderzoek in Groot-Atjeh (Maart-December 1937)|eye survey of 1937]]. It was one of only four places in [[Groot-Atjeh]] not populated exclusively by Atjehnese — with [[Koeta Radja|Koetaradja]], [[Oeleë Lheuë|Oelee-Lheuë]] and [[Seulimeum]] — though the admixture with Malays, Padangers and others was very slight; and both the *IV Moekims* and the *IX Moekims* of Lho-Nga are named among the coastal and valley districts covered by the survey.

The *[[Volkstelling 1930]]* had supposed that in the *onderafdeelingen* Lho-Nga and Seulimeum a slight natality had long existed, connected with a slight fertility of the women through factors not certainly known — avitaminosis among this population group was suggested — and with great child mortality, which had caused the group of the non-adult to shrink so that the group of adults became relatively large.

In the *moekims* Lho-Nga, [[VI Moekims Lhong|Lhong]] and [[Lam-Kabeuë]], *[[Xerophthalmie|xerophthalmie]]* was known among the Acehnese population under the name *penjikit sisë*, while in the remainder of the surveyed area the condition bore no such name.

Lho-Nga was also among the districts where Western influence had penetrated further and traffic was busier, which gave Gomperts a measure for the frequency of venereal infection and hence for its small share among the causes of [[Blindheid in Groot-Atjeh|blindness]]; and among those where the concentration of *conjunctivitis follicularis* would have been expected had that condition been a form of [[Trachoma in Groot-Atjeh|trachoma]].
