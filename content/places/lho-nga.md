**Lho Nga** (modern Lhoknga) is a coastal settlement, bay, and maritime locality situated on the western coast of northern Sumatra southwest of [[Meuraxa]], in modern Aceh Besar Regency, Aceh Province, Indonesia.

## Strategic Geography in the Aceh Campaigns

Lho Nga provided an alternative coastal anchorage and overland approach to the western mukims of the [[Aceh Sultanate]]. 

During the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] in December [[1873]] and subsequent operations under General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]] in [[1875]], naval patrols of the [[Dutch East Indies Government]] monitored Lho Nga to prevent Acehnese resistance forces from importing arms or outflanking Dutch positions at [[Koerong Raba Bay]].
