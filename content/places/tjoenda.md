**Tjoenda** is a *landschap* in the *onderafdeeling* [[Lho Seumawe|Lho' Seumawé]] of the *afdeeling* [[Noordkust van Atjeh|Noordkust van Atjeh]], one of the beautiful *landschappen* of the *onderafdeeling*. Cattle from Tjoenda and the neighbouring *landschap* [[Nisam|Nisam]] is sold to traders from [[Medan|Medan]]. The two *landschappen* have water from the [[Kroeeng Boeloeh|Kroeeng Boeloeh]], in which one finds, 12 km west of Lho' Seumawé, a stone dam which is from time to time endangered by the *bandjirs*. The population is lukewarm towards such works and does little or nothing on its side.
