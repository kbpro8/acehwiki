**Tjot Goet** was the station of the 4th *[[Divisie (Maréchaussée)|Divisie]]* of the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* in [[Atjeh]] in [[1898]].

## The 1898 localisation

According to the [[1930]] jubilee number, the corps was in [[1898]] localised with its Staff at [[Koeta Radja]], the 1st Division at [[Seulimeum|Selimoen]], the 2nd at [[Indrapoeri]], the 3rd at [[Tjot Mantjang]] and [[Glé Broet]], the 4th at Tjot Goet, and the 5th at [[Lam Baroe]] and [[Lho Nga|Lho'-Nga]].

The name is formed with the Acehnese topographic element *tjot* (also *cot*), denoting a hill.

## Later dispositions

The [[1910]] redistribution of the corps placed its five divisions at [[Blang Kedjeren]], [[Meulaboh]], [[Takengeun|Takèngan]], [[Lam Meulo]] and [[Lho Soekon]]; Tjot Goet does not appear among them.
