---
title: Awe Geutah
tags: ["place", "north-east-coast", "river"]
---

A place on the North or East Coast. Sadati dialogue: 'If that please thee not, I shall carry thee to Awe Geutah. Brothers, I will not live at Awe Geutah, for I fear that Teungku Chut Muda would forbid me (i.e. forbid his performance as a sadati).'
