**Peutòë** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Place to which Panglima Polém fled after Gajo-landen.

## Description

Place to which [[Panglima Polém]] fled after [[Gajo-landen]].

## Significance

Peutòë is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
