---
title: Lam Guha
tags: ["place"]
---

The gampong in [[Acheh]] Proper from which [[Teungku di Lam Guha]] took his name. He was a Javanese from [[Demak]], so called because he secluded himself in a cave for many years. His tomb, which lies behind the mosque at [[Ulee Lheue]], is now visited by many pilgrims.
