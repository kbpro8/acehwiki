**Geunteuët** (marschbivak (march bivak)) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. March bivak left of the main road on Westkust.

## Type

[[marschbivak]] ([[March|march]] [[Bivak|bivak]])

## Description

[[March]] [[Bivak|bivak]] left of the main road on [[Westkust]]. Now patrols the verzetsgebied ([[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] area) of [[1933]] and [[1935]].[[Bivak]] whose commandant still functions as schakel-officier.

## Significance

Geunteuët is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
