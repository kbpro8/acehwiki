**The Peusangan-rivier** — the *Peusangan river*, also *Kroeëng Peusangan* — is one of the principal rivers of the [[Noordkust|Noordkust]] of [[Atjeh|Atjèh], and, together with the [[Arakoendo-rivier|Arakoendo-rivier]], the site of one of the principal Dutch flood-control (bandjir-freedom) works in Atjèh — works to a total of more than half a million guilders, by which some tens of thousands of hectares of sawah-land have been freed from the almost annual excess of flooding. The river and its surrounding region ([[Peusangan|Peusangan]]) are among the principal rice-producing regions of Atjèh.

## The Bandjir-Freedom Works

Important results have been achieved on the bandjir-freedom of regions in Atjèh, especially in the area of the Peusangen and Arakoendo rivers, where works for that purpose have been spent to a total of more than half a million guilders, and where the favourable consequence is that some tens of thousands of hectares of sawah-land have been freed from the almost annual excess of flooding, whereby importantly greater harvest-certainty has been obtained. The bandjir-freedom works on the Peusangen and Arakoendo rivers were the necessary precondition of the irrigation works that would bring the irrigation water to the rice fields; naturally, the latter task had, where necessary, to be fulfilled first, before the construction of irrigation works could be gone into.

## Significance

The Peusangan-rivier is one of the principal rivers of the Noordkust of Atjèh, and the site of one of the principal Dutch flood-control works in Atjèh. The more than half a million guilders spent on the bandjir-freedom works on the Peusangen and Arakoendo rivers, and the tens of thousands of hectares of sawah-land thereby freed from the almost annual excess of flooding, together constitute one of the principal Dutch investments in the post-war economic development of the Noordkust of Atjèh.
