**Marassa** was a kampong at the mouth of the [[Aceh River|Atjeh river]] to the east of the Dutch landing-place of the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)|second Aceh expedition]], whose head, [[Toekoe Nek Radja Moeda Setia|Toekoe Nek Radja Moeda Setia]], had repeatedly offered his services to the Dutch and was the principal native ally of the Dutch during the [[Capture of the Kraton (24 January 1874)|fall of the Kraton]] in [[January 1874|January 1874]]. The sparing of Marassa was one of the reasons for the choice of the eastern landing-place in the second expedition; the kampong was connected with [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] by the [[Westerlinie|Westerlinie]] in [[May 1874|May-June 1874]].

## The Dutch Alliance

When the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)|second Aceh expedition]] was being planned, it was decided that the landing should take place east of the Atjeh river rather than west of it as in the first expedition, "firstly because there was less resistance to be expected there, and secondly because one wished to spare the kampong Marassa, which was well-disposed to us and whose head, Toekoe Nek Radja Moeda Setia, had repeatedly offered us his services." After the landing at the [[Kwala Loë|Kwala Loë]] on [[9 December]] [[9 December]] [[1873]], Toekoe Nek maintained his good offices, sending word of enemy movements and offering to act as guide. He had already before then "promised his intervention in the recovery of the robbed corpse of the [[Nino Bixio|general Nino Bixio]] who had died of cholera," then commander of a transport ship.

## The Westerlinie, May-June 1874

After the departure of [[Jan van Swieten|Van Swieten]] on [[26 April]] [[26 April]] [[1874]] the war continued under [[J. L. J. H. Pel|General Pel]]. In [[May 1874|May-June 1874]] the enemy resumed the struggle "with the greatest possible boldness and perseverance," evidently in the hope of overwhelming the weakened garrison. To protect Toekoe Nek adequately, Pel occupied the Westerlinie — a series of posts west of Kota Radja to connect it with Marassa and [[Lampassei|Lampassei]]. The principal post on this line was [[Oleh-leh|Oleh-leh]].
