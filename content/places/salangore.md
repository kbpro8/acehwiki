**Salangore** was a coastal settlement on the western shore of the island of [[Mars]] (Pulau Mars) that became the focus of a Dutch military expedition in [[1784]]. The town was divided by a river obstructed by wooden piles and defended by a fort on a height and a beach battery.

## Strategic Importance
Control of Salangore gave the Dutch access to a harbour that could be blockaded by sea, and its proximity to the Straits of Malacca made it a valuable prize for the [[Oost-Indische Compagnie]].

## 1784 Dutch Expedition
Van Braam’s fleet arrived on [[20 July]] [[1784]] and, after a landing on [[18 June]] [[1784]], captured the fort and forced the local ruler to flee. The Dutch then declared the settlement a Company possession and installed [[Mohamed Ali]] as a client ruler.

## Aftermath
Following the campaign, the Dutch left a small garrison to maintain authority. The fleet later returned to [[Malakka]] where many crew members fell ill, including [[Luitenant Wisscher]].
