**Kaijoe Atoa** (historically Kayu Ato) was a fortified military redoubt and defensive outpost established by the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] in the coastal lowlands near Kotta Radja in the [[Aceh Sultanate]], modern Banda Aceh, Indonesia.

## Role in General Pel's Perimeter

Constructed during the occupation of [[1874]] and [[1875]], Kaijoe Atoa formed an integral link in the chain of defensive blockhouses guarding the western approaches to [[Kraton of Aceh]] against guerrilla incursions from the [[IV Moekims]] and [[VI Moekims]]. 

During [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]] in December [[1875]], troops garrisoned at Fort Kaijoe Atoa provided flank security for columns marching toward [[Pekan Badak]] and the [[Blankala Pass]].
