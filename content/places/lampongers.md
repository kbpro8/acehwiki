**Lampongers** (region/people) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. People who are enthusiastic feast-celebrators; in good times they brought Bantammers over for the pepper harvest.

## Type

region/people

## Description

People who are enthusiastic feast-celebrators; in good times they brought [[Bantammers]] over for the pepper harvest. Compared unfavorably to [[Atjehers]] who are not big feast-throwers.

## Significance

Lampongers is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
