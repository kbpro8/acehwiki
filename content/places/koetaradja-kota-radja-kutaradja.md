**Koetaradja (Kota Radja, Kutaradja)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Capital of Aceh; seat of the Dutch Governor.

## Description

Capital of Aceh; seat of the Dutch Governor. [[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]] was told to come here to hear submission conditions ([[1898]]). [[20 January]] [[1903]] [[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]] received by Governor here. Javasche Bank agency here. [[Peutjoet (cemetery)|Begraafplaats Peutjoet]] here.

## Significance

Koetaradja (Kota Radja, Kutaradja) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
