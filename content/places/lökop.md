**Lökop** is the chief place of the *onderafdeeling* [[Serbödjadi|Serbödjadi]] in the *afdeeling* [[Gajo- en Alaslanden|Gajo- en Alaslanden]]. A company of infantry (eight brigades of twenty men) under a captain lay at Lökop at the time of Broersma's visit. The connection of [[Blang Kedjerën|Blang Kedjerën]] with Lökop is partly by horse-track, the middle section being still a footpath.
