In the Hikayat Eseukanda Ali: 'Raja Hadan of Hidian avails himself of his absence to make war on [[Keureutoe]] in revenge for the death of his relative [[Suloyman]].' [[Aramiah]] (Banta Beuransah's kingdom) In the Hikayat Banta Beuransah: 'Jamishah, king of [[Aramiah]], had three sons; Banta Beusiah and Keureutaih by his first, Banta Beuransah by his second wife.' [[Aramiah]] is described as the home kingdom of the Beuransah family. The narrative ends: 'the poet makes him touch successively at almost all the harbours of the East, West and North Coasts of [[Acheh]] and its dependencies, and finally arrive in [[Aramiah]] at the source of the river of Singke ([[Singkel]]).'
