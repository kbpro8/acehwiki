**Kampen** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[H. N. A. Swart]]

In [[1872]] his father was pensioned. The family returned to the Netherlands by the sailing ship *Kosmopoliet II*, a voyage of 121 days round the Cape, touching at [[Mauritius]], the [[Cape of Good Hope]], [[Ascension]] and [[Saint Helena]] — where Swart heard the name Napoleon for the first time in his life. They landed at [[Brouwershaven]], lived for a time in [[Rotterdam]], and finally settled at [[Kampen]]. There Swart went to school for the first time, under the schoolmaster [[Van Duijl]], entering the lowest class with a brother three years his junior. During a free quarter of an hour he wandered into the sixth class, saw a problem in decimal fractions on the blackboard, took a piece of chalk and solved it; the master of the fourth class saw him and Swart was promoted at once to the sixth.

### From [[1872]]

In **[[1872]]** the father of [[H. N. A. Swart]] was pensioned; the family returned to the Netherlands by the sailing ship *Kosmopoliet II* in 121 days round the Cape, touching at [[Mauritius]], the [[Cape of Good Hope]], [[Ascension]] and [[Saint Helena]], landing at [[Brouwershaven]] and settling finally at [[Kampen]]. In October **[[1872]]** the Dutch believed war with Atjeh unavoidable "without possessing a single even moderately reliable datum concerning the land and its people".

### From [[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)]]

- I. From volunteer soldier to Vice-President of the [[Raad van Nederlandsch-Indië]] ([[1880]]–[[1922]]); the Dutchman in the Indies and the flowering and growth of the Indies in those years.
- II. From the childhood years in [[Soekadana]] and [[Kampen]] to the lieutenant's years at [[Magelang]] ([[1863]]–[[1890]]); [[Atjeh]] in [[1890]], the first Atjeh experiences and the [[Militaire Willemsorde]]; instructor at the [[Korps Pupillen te Gombong|Pupillenschool at Gombong]]; officer of armament and lieutenant-adjutant ([[1890]]–[[1895]]); at the IInd Division of the [[Departement van Oorlog]], promotion to captain, and the beginning of the *Buitenbestek*.
- III. [[Atjeh]] from [[1890]] to [[1898]]: from [[C. Deijckerhoff|Deijckerhoff]] to [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]; the insights of [[Christiaan Snouck Hurgronje|Dr. Snouck Hurgronje]] and the brochure *[[The Subjection of Atjeh (1892)|De onderwerping van Atjeh]]*; Van Heutsz becomes Civil and Military Governor of Atjeh.
- IV. As captain in Atjeh ([[1898]]–[[1900]]); Swart and his *Jannen*; promotion to knight 3rd class of the *Militaire Willemsorde* ("Military William Order") and leave to the Netherlands; back in Atjeh ([[1901]]); placed with the *[[Corps of Military Constabulary of Atjeh and Dependencies|marechaussee]]* ([[1902]]) and Civil Authority at [[Poelò Raja]] and [[Lho' Seumawè]] (to [[1904]]); extraordinary promotion to major and appointment as Civil and Military Resident to the [[South and East Division of Borneo]].
