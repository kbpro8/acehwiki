An ancient gampong not far from [[The Dalam]], where the tomb of [[Poteu Jeumaloy]] is situated. 'The Arab rival of the first kings of the present dynasty' is buried here. He had been the lawful and recognized sovereign of [[Acheh]], and his tomb is universally revered as a saint by the Achehnese. Teungku Shah Manso (Mansur) is another saint buried in [[Gampong Jawa]], and Tuan di Dapat in the same area.
