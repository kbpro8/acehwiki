**Roempit** was a Dutch military post in the eastern section of the geconcentreerde stelling (concentrated position) in [[Groot-Atjeh]]. The post was established on [[29 September]] [[1884]] as part of the construction of the new defensive line.

## Location

Roempit was situated in the eastern section of the geconcentreerde stelling, forming part of the outer perimeter.

## Establishment

Roempit was the first new post established during the concentration period. Construction began with the new road from [[Oleh-Karang]] to Roempit, and the post was occupied on [[29 September]] [[1884]].

No significant enemy interference was encountered during the establishment of the post.

## Fortification (1885)

Roempit was:

**Type:** A post with 150-man Infantry garrison
**Armament:** 2 × 12cm breech-loading guns and 1 Coehoorn mortar

## Infrastructure Connections

The post was connected to:
- The ceintuurtram (girdle tramway) system
- [[Lampermej]] via [[Tjot-Iri]] and [[Boekit-Karang]]
- [[Lamjong]] and the eastern posts

## Significance

Roempit's early establishment demonstrated the rapid progress of construction following the concentration decision. The post formed an important link in the new defensive line.
