**[[2 April]]** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Pang Intan]]

**Pang Intan** was the last band-head of the [[Meergebied]], killed with four followers at the [[Waih ni Wis]] on [[2 April]] [[1914]].

### From [[1914]]

**[[1914]]** brought the [[Vaccination Unrest in Groot Atjeh (April 1914)|vaccination unrest in Groot Atjeh]] in April; the murder of Civil Authority [[W. van Kregten]] at [[Lam No]] on [[20 May]] and of the overseer [[C. H. R. Heins]] at [[Lhong]] on [[13 July]], leading to the [[Resistance in Daja and Lhong (1914)|resistance in Daja and Lhong]] and its suppression by lieutenant [[C. A. Snell|Snell]] at [[Koeala Daja]] on [[5 August]] and the banishment of [[Teukoe Mamat]] on [[2 September]]. [[Pang Intan]] was killed at the [[Waih ni Wis]] on [[2 April]]; [[T. Radja Tampo']] levied tribute in [[Tadoe]] and [[Tripa]] in March. On [[6 March|6]]–[[7 March]] the *[[Sumatra Post]]* published Swart's interview; the reform replacing military by civil authority took effect on [[1 March]]. An agricultural school opened at [[Pedir]] on [[12 February]].
