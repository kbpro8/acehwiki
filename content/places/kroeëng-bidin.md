**Kroeëng Bidin** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Side river of the upper Kroeëng Djambó-Ajé, in tertiair gronden.

## Description

Side river of the upper [[Kroeëng Djambó-Ajé]], in tertiair gronden.

## Significance

Kroeëng Bidin is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
