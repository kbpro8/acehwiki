**Malakka** (modern [[Malacca]]) was a strategic port on the Strait of Malacca that served as the staging base for the Dutch expedition against [[Salangore]] and [[Riouw]] in [[1784]]. The fleet under [[Jacob Pieter van Braam|Van Braam (commander)]] departed from Malakka and later returned there after the campaign.

## Role in the 1784 Expedition
From Malakka, Van Braam organised the landing forces, dispatched frigates, and coordinated naval support. The port also became the site of a severe outbreak of disease that claimed the lives of many crew members, including [[Luitenant Wisscher]].

## Significance
Control of Malakka allowed the Dutch East India Company to monitor and protect its trade routes through the Strait of Malacca, a crucial artery for commerce between the Indian Ocean and the South China Sea.
