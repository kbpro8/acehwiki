**Karang‑Assana** was a Balinese principality that featured prominently in the Dutch expeditions of the 1840s. After the Dutch landing at [[Boeleleng]] on [[28 June]] [[1846]], negotiations were held with the princes of Karang‑Assana, though they yielded no lasting agreement (page 106). The area later fell under Dutch control following the third Bali expedition of [[1849]], after which new treaties were signed on [[15 June]] [[1849]] (page 108).

## Role in Dutch‑Balinese Relations

Karang‑Assana’s resistance contributed to the Dutch decision to launch a second expedition in [[1848]] and a third in [[1849]] (page 106). The settlement’s strategic location near the interior routes made it a target for Dutch military advances.
