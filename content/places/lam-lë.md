**Lam lë** (military post) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Military post on the Westkust road, where the road turns inland and the sea is briefly out of sight.

## Type

military post

## Description

Military post on the [[Westkust]] road, where the road turns inland and the sea is briefly out of sight.

## Significance

Lam lë is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
