**Tripa** was the river and marsh country near [[Meulaboh]] in which, with [[Seuneu'am]], [[T. Radja Tampo']] remained unfound throughout [[H. N. A. Swart]]'s governorship.

In March [[1909]], after [[T. Moeda Mat Sa'it]] fell with his men on the upper Tripa, the submission in [[Seunagan]], [[Tadoe]] and Tripa of all his *pangs* and band members followed. In March [[1914]] T. Radja Tampo' levied tribute on inhabitants of Tadoe and Tripa, set fire to a couple of *padi* barns and disappeared again. He was still wandering there in [[1939]].
