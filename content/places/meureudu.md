A town and territory on the North Coast. In the [[Hikajat Malém Dagang|Hikayat Malem Dagang]], 'the fleet shapes its course for Meureudu, which the poet depicts as a sparsely-populated and almost desert land. The want of familiarity with the great of the land withheld the people of Meureudu from fulfilling their duty of waiting on the Sultan... a teacher from [[Medina]] who lived among them, called [[Ja Pakeh]] or Ja Madinah, went to plead the cause of these simple folk.' The Sultan declared Meureudu a feudal freehold (wakeueh). In the sadati dialogue: 'If that please thee not, little brother, I shall take thee to the land of Meureudu. Brothers, I will not live at Meureudu; the whole country is in tumult and war prevails.' The [[Samalanga]] uleebalang Keuchi' Ali 'was actually driven out of house and home by his enemies from Meureudu.' Gampong Meureuduati is also mentioned as a gampong within the 'linie' where the uleebalang Teuku Aneu' Paya had a wife.

## Account from *Verkenningen in Atjeh* (1923)

**Meureudoe** — pronounced *Merdoe* — is the chief place (*hoofdplaats*) of the *onderafdeeling* of the same name in the *afdeeling* [[Pidië|Pidië]]. The *onderafdeeling*, with its 25.000 souls, of whom the *landschap* Meureudoe alone has 12.000, lies in a land of *padi*, pepper, pinang and to a lesser extent coco-palm. Besides rice-cultivation, which in some years gives a surplus, there is a busy fishery at [[Pante Radja|Pante Radja]] and in the *landschap* of that name the pepper-gardens are still maintained. Pinang and copra and whatever else is exported and imported find their harbour at [[Sigli|Sigli]] and to a lesser extent at [[Lho Seumawe|Lho' Seumawe]]. The *landschap* Meureudoe has, despite its means of livelihood, no prosperity commensurate with those means, because trade is in the hands of Klings and Chinese and Arabs, who buy up for export to [[Penang|Penang]].
