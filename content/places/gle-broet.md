**Glé Broet** was/is a mountainous terrain feature and strategic outpost located along the southern mountain fringe of [[Groot-Atjeh]].

## Historical Context and Role

Glé Broet formed a defensive link alongside [[Cot Mentjang]] for the 3rd Division of the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]], blocking insurgent infiltration routes from the west coast.
