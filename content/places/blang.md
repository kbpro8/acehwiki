**Blang** was a Dutch military post in the southern section of the geconcentreerde stelling (concentrated position) in [[Groot-Atjeh]]. The post was established on [[26 January]] [[1885]].

## Location

Blang was situated in the southern section of the geconcentreerde stelling, between [[Ketapan-Doea]] and [[Lamdjamoe]].

## Establishment

Blang was established and occupied on [[26 January]] [[1885]].

## Fortification (1885)

Blang was:

**Type:** A post with 50-man Infantry garrison
**Armament:** 2 × 12cm breech-loading guns and 1 Coehoorn mortar

## Infrastructure Connections

The post was connected to:
- The ceintuurtram (girdle tramway) system
- [[Ketapan-Doea]] and [[Lamdjamoe]]

## Significance

Blang helped complete the southern section of the perimeter, providing additional coverage between [[Ketapan-Doea]] and [[Lamdjamoe]].
