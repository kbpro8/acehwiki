**Singa‑Radja** was a Balinese settlement captured without resistance by General‑Major [[A. A. V. Michiels]] on [[29 March]] [[1849]] after the landing at [[Boeleleng]] (page 107). It served as a temporary base for Dutch negotiations with the princes of Boeleleng and [[Karang‑Assana]] (page 107).

## Military Use

Following its capture, Dutch forces used Singa‑Radja as a staging point before advancing toward the fortified position of [[Djagaraga]] (page 107).
