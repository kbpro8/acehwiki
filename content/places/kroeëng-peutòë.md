**Kroeëng Peutòë** was the river of [[Keureutòë]] in whose side-stream [[W. J. Mosselman]] surprised and killed [[Tgk. Sjeh di Paja Bakong]], [[Tgk. Mat Saleh]] and [[Tjoet Meutia]] on [[25 October]] [[1910]].

Mosselman had marched out from [[Lho' Soekon]] on [[22 October]] [[1910]] and picked up fresh tracks in a side-stream of the Kroeëng Peutòë on the third day. The pursuit ran over waterfalls, boulders and slippery stones; towards four in the afternoon of [[25 October]] he saw smoke above the trees and charged with seven men into a band certainly sixty strong. The corpse of the old blind Teungkoe, defended to the death by two young men, was recognised by the golden weapon, the famous *bawar*. In March [[1911]] a patrol under captain [[Van der Vlerk]] was attacked in a marching bivouac on the [[Keureutòë-rivier]] by old adherents of [[Tgk. di Barat]].
