An island close to [[Pulo Breueh]]. 'On the shore of that island a seven days' feast is held every year. The first six days are dedicated to piasans (secular feasts), the main features of which are music and unlawful love-making, chiefly with married women. On the seventh day a buffalo is sacrificed, and it is supposed that this festival ensures the people for a whole year against the freaks of certain malignant jens who, if its celebration be neglected, avenge themselves by causing many to fall and break their necks.' Also, the rice-field Blang Seureugong on the same island is believed to be the habitation of a jen, who must be propitiated by the giving of a yearly feast involving the slaughter of a buffalo.
