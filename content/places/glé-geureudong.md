**Glé-Geureudong** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Fully vegetated peak of the Geureudong complex.

## Description

Fully vegetated peak of the [[Geureudong]] complex. 'Glé' or 'Boer' = mountain in Acehnese/[[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gayo]] respectively.

## Significance

Glé-Geureudong is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
