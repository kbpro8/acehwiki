**Sāwa** was a historic settlement mentioned in Acehnese literary sources as “the city Sāwa between Kazwin and Kum”. The reference appears on page [[91]] of the *Burda* translation.

## Location
While the precise coordinates are uncertain, scholars associate *Sāwa* with a site in the interior of Aceh, possibly near the modern district of *Kuala Simpang*.

## Historical Significance
The city is cited in mythic narratives linking Acehnese cosmology to Persian eschatology, indicating its role in the symbolic geography of Acehnese religious imagination.
