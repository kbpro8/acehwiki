**Rékét Goïp** is a subject named in *[[Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[The resistance in Atjeh 1908–1912]]

March also brought one of those remarkable setbacks, caused by [[Taib|Si Taib]], the instigator of the murder of [[Rödjö Kemala]]. The monthly report told: "Rödjö Kemala, who was conducting the deliberations on the submission of those of his clan headship who had gone over to the resistance, was murdered on the first of the month under report at the instigation of his kinsman Si Taib, who had himself joined the resistance." The population brought Taib in custody to the administration, whereupon twenty-four *uitgewekenen* of the *tjèq* lineage came in submission, handing in a Beaumont. But Taib escaped from the transport escort at [[Lésten]] — "and that will not have been a feast for that transport escort!" — so that the chiefs of [[Rékét Goïp]] who had delivered him up feared reprisal. Taib nevertheless fell into Dutch hands, severely wounded and dying of hunger, and died on the twenty-sixth at Lésten.
