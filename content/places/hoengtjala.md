**Hoengtjala** was a *kampong* (village) with *[[Benteng|bentengs]]* in the middle of the marshland of the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]], described by [[G. B. Hooijer]] as a storehouse and safe refuge of the *[[Prang sabi|sabil]]* bands, and evacuated by the *[[Moslemin]]* on [[12 November]] [[1893]] before the advance of [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]].

## The evacuation and its sequel

On [[12 November]] [[1893]] [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]] continued his movements supported by Dutch artillery fire, with the result that the *bentengs* at Hoengtjala were evacuated by the enemy. Before that *kampong*, Hooijer recorded, [[Toekoe Nja Banta|Nja Bantah]] had twice been repulsed, and [[T. Nek van Marassa|Toekoe Neq]] had so often seen his plans thwarted from it, that the two chiefs — *tegen de bevelen van den Gouverneur in*, against the Governor's orders — could not restrain themselves from giving their fighters a free hand and delivering that storehouse and refuge over to plunder and fire.
