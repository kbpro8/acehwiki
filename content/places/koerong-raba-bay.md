**Koerong Raba Bay** was a strategic coastal bay and anchorage situated west of [[Meuraxa]] along the northern coast of the [[Aceh Sultanate]], which served as the primary invasion landing site for the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] in December [[1873]].

## Geographical and Military Significance

Protected from the heaviest surf of the northeast monsoon, [[Koerong Raba Bay]] offered deep-water anchorage for the invasion fleet of the [[Dutch East Indies Government]]. 

In December [[1873]], General [[J. van Swieten]] ordered his armada to bypass the fortified redoubts at [[Meuraxa]] and execute the [[Attack on Koerong Raba]]. Marine and infantry battalions debarked at the bay, securing a permanent coastal base from which Dutch columns launched their overland advance toward [[Pekan Badak]], [[Sinangri]], and [[Kraton of Aceh]].
