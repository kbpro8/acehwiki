**Bookit Koeboe** (also **Bukit Kubu**) is a location on the northern coastal plain of the [[Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra|Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra]], between the [[Besitang|Besitang River]] and [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]] on the [[Aroebai|Aroebaai]], designated as the junction point for the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]] (narrow gauge, 1,067 mm) and the [[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij|Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij]] network (standard gauge, 1,435 mm). A three-rail dual-gauge section from [[Bookit Koeboe|Bookit Koeboe]] to [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]] was planned to allow both systems to serve the petroleum port.

## Location and context

[[Bookit Koeboe|Bookit Koeboe]] lies on the coastal plain north of the [[Langkat|Langkat]] cultivation area, approximately midway between:

- [[Pangkalan Brandan|Pangkalan Brandan]] ([[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij|D.S.M.]] northern terminus, standard gauge).
- [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]] ([[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]] terminus / [[Bataafsche Petroleum Maatschappij|Koninklijke Petroleum Maatschappij]] oil port, narrow gauge).
- [[Besitang|Besitang River]] (the watershed boundary with [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh]] per [[Staatsblad 1908 No. 604|Staatsblad [[1908]] No. 604]]).

The site was chosen because it was the first point where the two railway systems' projected alignments converged on practicable terrain.

## Gauge incompatibility and the three-rail solution

The [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]] (Staatstramwegen in Atjeh) used narrow gauge (1,067 mm); the [[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij|D.S.M.]] used standard gauge (1,435 mm). Through-running was impossible. The solution was a dual-gauge (three-rail) track on the shared section [[Bookit Koeboe|Bookit Koeboe]]–[[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]], allowing:

- [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]] narrow-gauge rolling stock to reach the oil jetties.
- [[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij|D.S.M.]] standard-gauge wagons to access the petroleum traffic and northern cultivation produce.

The [[1918]] *Dienstregeling* of the [[Koninklijke Paketvaart Maatschappij|Koninklijke Paketvaart Maatschappij]] explicitly notes: *De Atjehtram heeft smalspoor, de Delitram breedspoor, zoodat op het gemeenschappelijke baanvak 3 rails noodig zijn.*

## Construction status (1918)

- [[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij|D.S.M.]] section [[Pangkalan Brandan|Pangkalan Brandan]]–[[Bookit Koeboe|Bookit Koeboe]]: *binnenkort tot stand komt* (soon to be completed); the [[Pangkalan Brandan|Pangkalan Brandan]]–[[Besitang|Besitang]] subsection was already complete and metalled (sand surfacing).
- [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]] to [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]]: operational; tank steamers calling regularly.
- Three-rail section [[Bookit Koeboe|Bookit Koeboe]]–[[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]]: under construction / planned.
- Missing link [[Besitang|Besitang]]–[[Bookit Koeboe|Bookit Koeboe]]: the final gap in the [[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij|D.S.M.]] line to the junction.

## Economic rationale

The [[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij|D.S.M.]] sought the connection to:

- Capture petroleum traffic from the [[Bataafsche Petroleum Maatschappij|Koninklijke Petroleum Maatschappij]] for haulage to [[Belawan|Belawan]] (though tank steamers loaded directly at [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]]).
- Draw northern cultivation produce (rubber, tobacco from [[Langkat|Langkat]], [[Besitang|Besitang]]) to [[Belawan|Belawan-Deli]] rather than [[Penang|Penang]]/[[Singapore|Singapore]] via coastal steamers.
- Integrate the northern frontier into the [[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij|D.S.M.]] feeder network (*vangarmen* pulling traffic to [[Medan|Medan]]/[[Belawan|Belawan]]).

The Handelsvereeniging Medan supported the link as complementary to the [[Belawan|Belawan]] Oceanhaven (Plan Nobel) development.

## Administrative significance

[[Bookit Koeboe|Bookit Koeboe]] lay just south of the [[Besitang|Besitang]] watershed boundary ([[Staatsblad 1908 No. 604|Staatsblad [[1908]] No. 604]]), placing it firmly in [[Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra|Oostkust]] territory (*onderafdeeling* Beneden-[[Langkat|Langkat]]). The [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]] crossed the boundary at [[Besitang|Besitang]] into [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh]] territory (*onderafdeeling* [[Tamiang|Tamiang]]) before reaching [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]]. The junction thus straddled the governmental frontier, requiring coordination between the [[Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra|Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra]] and the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]].

## Significance

[[Bookit Koeboe|Bookit Koeboe]] is, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, the principal infrastructural hinge between the [[Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra|Oostkust]] and [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh]] railway systems — the junction whose three-rail compromise was the only practicable response to the gauge incompatibility between the [[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij|D.S.M.]]'s standard gauge and the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]]'s narrow gauge. The junction's location immediately south of the [[Besitang|Besitang]] watershed boundary made it the principal frontier point of the [[Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra|Oostkust]] — the point at which the two *gouvernementen* met, not in legal abstraction, but in the practical topology of rails and gauge.
