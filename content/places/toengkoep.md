**Toengkoep** was a locality in [[Groot-Atjeh]] whose fortified mosque — the *missigit Toengkoep* — had been one of the principal fortified points of the [[XXVI Moekims]], and which became in September [[1879]] the permanent garrison of the [[6e Bataljon Infanterie]] after the battalion's return from the [[Selimoen Expedition (August 1879)|Lamtoba-Selimoen expedition]], as recorded in [[W. Cool]] and [[G. B. Hooijer]]'s *[[Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis (Cool & Hooijer, 1889)|Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis]]* ([[1889]]).

## The torrent of the 26 Moekims and the garrison of 1879

In the [[Campaign against the XXVI Moekims (July 1879)|conquest of the XXVI Moekims]] of [[1 July|1]]–[[4 July]] [[1879]], the missigit Toengkoep had been numbered among the chief fortified points — with the missigit Melajoe, the missigit Boëng Tjala and the missigit Lambaroe — taken at the first storm or already evacuated. On the return of the expedition of [[1879]], in conformity with received orders, the 6th Battalion marched immediately after its arrival at [[Kota Radja]] to Toengkoep, "om van dezen post de vaste bezetting uit te maken" — to form the permanent garrison of this post. The march completed what the campaign had begun: many of the men could not perform it and fell out along the road, and within a few days a great part of the already thinned corps was received into the hospital. From the third and last division of the returning column, landed at [[Oleh-leh]] on [[3 September]] [[1879]], the section of guns with one section of mortars likewise marched to Toengkoep, the other mortar section to [[Lambaroe]]. The post must be distinguished from the [[Toengkoeb]] of the III/XIII Moekims, whose bentengs figure in the operations of the [[1890s (decade)|1890s]].
