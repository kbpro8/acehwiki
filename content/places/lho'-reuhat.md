**Lho' Reuhat** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Tjoet Meutia]]

From [[Lho' Soekon]] the brigade commander [[W. J. Mosselman]] of the 5th Division — one of the best of the division's brigade commanders — had marched out on [[22 October]] [[1910]] to trace a band reported at [[Lho' Reuhat]]. That trail crossed Tjoet Meutia's, and on the third day Mosselman was on fresh tracks in a side-stream of the [[Kroeëng Peutòë]].

### From [[Tgk. Sjeh di Paja Bakong]]

The end came a month after the death of Pang Nanggròë. From [[Lho' Soekon]] brigade commander [[W. J. Mosselman]] of the 5th Division, 1st brigade, had marched out on [[22 October]] [[1910]] to trace a band signalled at [[Lho' Reuhat]]. That trail crossed the path of Tjoet Meutia, who was travelling with her son towards the [[Gajòland]] to seek Tgk. di Mata Ië; on the third day Mosselman was on fresh tracks in a side-stream of the [[Kroeëng Peutòë]].

### From [[W. J. Mosselman]]

Mosselman had marched out from [[Lho' Soekon]] on [[22 October]] [[1910]] with the 1st brigade of the 5th Division to trace a band reported at [[Lho' Reuhat]]. That trail crossed the path of [[Tjoet Meutia]], travelling with her son [[T. Radja Sabi]] towards the [[Gajòland]] to seek [[Tgk. di Mata Ië]]; on the third day Mosselman was on fresh tracks in a side-stream of the [[Kroeëng Peutòë]].
