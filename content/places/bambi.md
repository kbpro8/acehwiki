A village in [[Pidie]]. The author of the Hikayat ranto ([[Leube Isa]]) 'lived in [[Pidie]], first in gampong Bambi, after which he is called Teungku Bambi, and later at [[Klibeuet]].' [[Ie Leubeue]] (= Ayer Labu) A village in [[Pidie]] (or near it), a centre of religious learning. 'A teacher at [[Ie Leubeue]] (Ayer Labu) called Teungku di [[Acheh]] or Teungku [[Acheh]], since he pursued his studies for a long time in [[Acheh]].' In the sadati dialogue: 'Dear little brother, to cut the matter short, I shall take thee to Kuala [[Ie Leubeue]] (vulg. Ayer Labu). I will not live at Kuala [[Ie Leubeue]], for in the fresh-water creek at that place there are many crocodiles.'
