**Tjibitoeng** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[H. N. A. Swart]]

**H. N. A. Swart** — registered in his *stamboek* (service record) as **Henri Nicolaas Alfred Swart**, born at [[Tjibitoeng]] in the [[Preanger Regentschappen]] on [[12 October]] [[1863]] — was the Dutch *luitenant-generaal* of infantry of the [[Koninklijk Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|K.N.I.L.]] who served as **Civil and Military Governor of [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]]** from [[10 June]] [[1908]] to [[2 September]] [[1918]], the last military governor of [[Atjeh]] and the man whom Dutch colonial memory named the *Pacificator van Atjeh* ("Pacifier of Atjeh"). [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]]'s biography *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) — written with the collaboration of [[H. J. Schmidt]] and on the basis of long interviews with Swart himself, then seventy-nine years old — treats his ten-year Acehnese governorship as the summit of a career that ran from volunteer private in [[1880]] to Vice-President of the [[Raad van Nederlandsch-Indië]] in [[1918]]–[[1922]].

### From [[H. N. A. Swart]]

Swart did not come from a family of officers. His father, Klaas Swart, was a Groninger who worked in the Indies first in the *cultures* (plantation agriculture) and afterwards in the civil service; his mother was Cornelia Maria Johanna Maria Gouay. Born at [[Tjibitoeng]], Swart retained only vague memories of his earliest childhood before, in his seventh year, his father's administrative posting took the family to [[Soekadana]] in [[Borneo]] — a post under a lieutenant with a few European sergeants, from whom the boy learned "a little arithmetic and Dutch" until his ninth year.

### From [[1863]]

**[[1863]]** was the birth year of [[H. N. A. Swart]], born on [[12 October]] [[1863]] at [[Tjibitoeng]] in the [[Preanger Regentschappen]], son of Klaas Swart, a Groninger who worked in the Indies first in the *cultures* and then in the civil service, and of Cornelia Maria Johanna Maria Gouay.
