'Another (copy of the Umdat al-muhtajin) in the Royal Library at Berlin (Numbered Schumann V, 6).' Multiple Malay manuscripts in Berlin's Hofbibliothek (Royal Library, Schumann collection) are cited for various hikayats and religious texts.

## Source
*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
