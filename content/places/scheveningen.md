**Scheveningen**, the coastal district of 's-[[The Hague|Gravenhage]] in the Netherlands, was the place at which [[D. W. N. de Boer]] signed and dated his study *[[Het recht op den grond in Atjeh (De Boer)|Het recht op den grond in Atjeh]]* in November [[1931]].

## Connection with Atjehnese history

Scheveningen and the neighbouring quarters of 's-Gravenhage were a common place of retirement for returned officials of the Netherlands Indies, and the colophon "Scheveningen, November 1931. D. W. N. DE BOER." records the composition of the Atjehnese land-law study after its author's departure from the *[[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|gewest]]*. The [[Koninklijk Instituut voor Taal-, Land- en Volkenkunde]], which preserved the offprint, was likewise seated in 's-Gravenhage, at Galewstraat 159.
