**Long Lemoh** was an Atjehnese place guarded by a Dutch post which, at the execution of the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration]], stood among the outer fortifications that were given back to the field. The post was one of the fortifications maintained by the Dutch outside the new line that were returned to the field at the execution of the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration]]: it was evacuated between [[5 January|5]] and [[14 January]] [[1885]], nearly a month and a half before the clearing of the so-called [[1000-meter strook|free strip]] was finished (see [[Completion of the concentrated position (March 1885)]]).
