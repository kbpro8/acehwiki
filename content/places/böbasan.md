**Böbasan** is a Gajo *kampong* not far from [[Takengon|Takengon]], described by Broersma as the type-example of the Gajo *kampong* in contrast to the Atjehnese. The Atjehnese *kampong* has dwellings with planted yards; the Gajo *kampong* has no yards with any growth but great houses. That recalls the Batak-*kampong*; an agreement with that is also that many related families live together and each has a section. The Gajo dwelling is built on posts above the ground and has a particular interior, described in [[Dr. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]]'s *[[Het Gajo-land en zijn bewoners|Het Gajo-land en zijn bewoners]]*. Wood and *bilik* (wattle) are the building materials, and now it is remarkable that not every day a Gajo-*kampong* burns down, for stoking is done regularly. The space under the house lies full of firewood and against the cold one stokes daily. Wood, *bilik*, *atap* — everything is black with smoke — and for each family-section in the house there is still a round stove-place on the floor.
