The **Longbatta-Mesigit** was the fortified stone mosque at [[Longbatta]] taken without a fight on the morning of [[31 December]] [[1874]] in the [[Battle of Longbatta (31 December 1874)|Longbatta operation]] and afterwards converted into a strong Dutch post. It was the *tophoek* (apex) of the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]] triangle, some 6,000 metres into enemy territory perpendicular to the strand, and the target of the [[Night attack on Longbatta-Mesigit (22 November 1875)|night attack of 22 November 1875]] in which some 50 Atjehneezen climbed the low borstwering with the *blanke klewang* and hacked among the sleeping manschappen.

## The tophoek of stelling-Pel

Borel describes the *stelling-Pel* of April [[1875]] as a nearly *gelijkbenige driehoek* (isosceles triangle) whose basis was the sea-strand from [[Moesapi]] to [[Oeleh Leh]] and whose apex was the Longbatta-Mesigit in the south-east. From the Mesigit the south side of the line ran through Longbatta Z., Lohong, [[Lamara Olejlo]], [[Setee]], Lamteboe, Poengej Blang Tjoet, [[Poe Oe]] and [[Soerian]] to Oedjoeng Planggan; the east side ran through [[Blang Tjoet]], [[Kota Alam]], [[Lemboe]] O. en N.O., [[Lampriet]], [[Langkroek]] O., [[Lamara]] Z.O. en N.O., [[Tiban]] to Moesapi. The Mesigit's stone walls, loopholed for riflemen and surrounded by an earthen borstwering, made it the strongest single post of the line; its loss would have unhinged the entire stelling. It was therefore garrisoned with two companies of infantry and supported by artillery.

## The night attack of 22 November 1875

In the night of [[22 November]] [[1875]] a band of some 50 Atjehneezen used the darkness to approach the Longbatta-Mesigit unnoticed, climbed over the low borstwering, and "met de blanke klewang" — with the bare klewang — hacked among the sleeping manschappen. The combined bezetting managed to drive them out, leaving several dead; Dutch losses were 13 *zwaar gewonden* including two officers. Borel stresses that the attack could have been disastrous if done with more force and from multiple sides: at night, in man-to-man struggle, the Atjehnees *klewang* is "een vreeslijk wapen" — a terrible weapon. He suspects *achteloosheid* (carelessness) of the schildwachten. The attack was the second of its kind, the first having been the night attack on Lemboe-N.O. of [[23 January|23]]–[[24 January]] [[1876]], and was one of the warnings that convinced [[Generaal Pel|Pel]] to abandon the *afwachtende houding* for the great [[Pel's offensive against the VI, IV and IX Moekims (1875–1876)|winter offensive of 1875–1876]].
