**Geudong** was the place on the [[Pasè-rivier]] where captain [[H. N. A. Swart]] crossed the flooded river on [[21 June]] [[1899]] to support the *[[Corps of Military Constabulary of Atjeh and Dependencies|marechaussée]]*.

Halted by the greatly swollen river and under heavy fire at short range, Swart at once took energetic measures for a crossing, went over, gathered seventeen soldiers on the right bank and drove the superior enemy from his position to the south by a flank attack with heavy loss, holding the captured position until further troops arrived. The action formed the citation for his promotion to Knight 3rd Class of the [[Militaire Willemsorde]].
