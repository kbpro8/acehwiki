**Aloer-Pantja-viaduct** (also rendered **Alur Panca viaduct**) was a viaduct on the [[Berglijntje Keude Breue–Seulimeum|berglijntje (mountain line) of the Atjeh-tram]] — the line built in the years [[1904]]–[[1908]] to connect the second tramway of the [[Atjeh's Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh Dependencies]] at [[Keude Breue]] with the old tramway of [[Groot-Atjeh|Great Atjeh]] at [[Seulimeum]]. In the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's volume *De Buitenbezittingen 1904 tot 1914*, the berglijn section of the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]] near the Aloer-Pantja-viaduct is illustrated as one of the two principal engineering works of the entire Atjeh-tram network.

## Account from *De Buitenbezittingen* (1917) — Aloer-Pantja-viaduct

The Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account of the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]] includes two illustrative plates of principal engineering works of the line: "Brug over de [[Lam Temot-rivier|Lam Temot-rivier]] ([[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]])" and "Gedeelte der berglijn van de [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]], nabij het Aloer-Pantja-viaduct." The berglijn section near the Aloer-Pantja-viaduct is, together with the [[Lam Temot-rivier|Lam Temot-rivier]] bridge, one of the two principal engineering landmarks of the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]] identified by the Bureau's illustrations.

The berglijn section near the Aloer-Pantja-viaduct belongs to the [[1904]]–[[1908]] *berglijntje* from [[Keude Breue]] over the hills to [[Seulimeum]] — the line whose completion the Bureau identifies as the moment at which "the military administration had created a powerful instrument for achieving the complete [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacification]] of the intersected regions" of [[Groot-Atjeh|Great Atjeh]] and the [[Atjeh's Onderhoorigheden|Dependencies]]. The Aloer-Pantja-viaduct is thus one of the principal engineering works of the integrated Atjeh-tram network, and one of the material embodiments of the post-[[1896]] Dutch strategic reorientation in Atjeh.

## Significance

[[Aloer-Pantja-viaduct|Aloer-Pantja-viaduct]] is, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, one of the principal engineering landmarks of the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]] — the viaduct on the [[Berglijntje Keude Breue–Seulimeum|berglijntje]] whose illustration in the Bureau's volume marks it as one of the two principal engineering works of the entire network. The viaduct is part of the material infrastructure of the post-[[1896]] [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacification of Atjeh]].
