**Djambo Ajé** was the river on whose upper course [[Pang Nanggròë]] set the whole of [[Keureutòë]] in movement after [[1905]], working with [[Tgk. Sjeh di Paja Bakong]], [[Tgk. di Mata Ië]] and [[Tgk. di Barat]].

In March [[1910]] Pang Nanggròë was driven out of "the safe *paja* (swamp) of the Djambo Ajé"; on [[30 July]] he was surprised again and escaped again; and on [[26 September]] he was killed in the [[Paja Tjitjém]].
