**Lamara** was a kampong at the end of the [[Pedir-dijk]] marshes and the site of the Dutch post east of the [[Marine-benteng|marine-benteng]], beyond the dike, that was continually harassed by Atjehnees *lilla* (light swivel-gun) fire from the kampong of [[Langkroek]] across the river. The post was one of the eastern links of the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]], flanking the marshy ground between the dike and the [[Kwala Atjeh|river-mouth]] through which enemy bands tried to *verschansen* (entrench) themselves to threaten Dutch river communication.

## The post and its harassments

Lamara's exposure to *lilla* fire is repeatedly mentioned by Borel. The Atjehnees *lilla* — a small swivel-gun, lighter and more mobile than the Dutch 12 c.M. *achterlaadkanonnen* — was easily moved through the brushwood of the marshy ground east of the dike and brought to bear on the post at all hours. Its fire "caused more unrest than harm," but the constant harassment wore down the garrison and forced the Dutch to keep up an expensive watch. The location of Lamara at the end of the Pedir-dijk marshes meant that any attempt to *nest* between [[Penajoeng]] and the Marine-benteng — exactly the threat that had materialised on [[21 May]] [[1874]] — would have to come past Lamara. The post was therefore an indispensable link in the river-defence chain.

## In the stelling-Pel

By the time the continuous line around [[Kota Radja|K. Radja]] was completed in May [[1875]], the Lamara post had been strengthened and incorporated into the eastern side of the *stelling-Pel*. Borel lists the south side of the line as running from Oedjoeng Planggan through [[Soerian]], [[Poe Oe]], Poengej Blang Tjoet, Lamteboe, [[Setee]] Z., [[Lamara Olejlo]], [[Longbatta]] Z. to [[Longbatta-Mesigit]], and the east side as running from [[Blang Tjoet]] through [[Kota Alam]], [[Lemboe]] O. en N.O., [[Lampriet]], Langkroek O., Lamara Z.O. en N.O., [[Tiban]] to [[Moesapi]]. The Lamara posts (south-east and north-east) thus connected the river-defence chain of the [[Pedir-dijk]] with the eastern sea-face at Moesapi. With the completion of the line, operations could be suspended or postponed, and [[Generaal Pel|Pel]] was granted three months' leave on [[12 June]] [[1875]] to recover his shattered health.
