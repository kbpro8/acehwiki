**Mount Idam** is a mountain referenced in the *Maleise tekst van de Burda* as the source of a “[[Madinat rasul-ullah]]”‑derived wind and a “[[Idam]]” lightning strike. The text situates the mountain within a symbolic landscape that frames the poem’s emotional climax.

## Literary Context

In the Malay translation, the line “karena gemilang kilat dalam malam jang gelap dari betul bukit Idam terbitnja” (because the bright lightning in the dark night comes from the true [[Mount Idam]]) links natural phenomena to divine intervention.

## Significance

While the precise geographic location of [[Mount Idam]] is uncertain, its inclusion reflects the integration of local topography into Acehnese‑Malay religious poetry, a practice common in early modern Malay literary culture.
