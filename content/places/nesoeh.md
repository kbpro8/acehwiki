**Nesoeh** was one of the two quarters of [[Koeta Radja]] lying outside the *[[Kraton of Aceh|Kraton]]* — the other being [[Gedah]] — which were strongly fortified in the spring of [[1896]] during the crisis that followed the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (29 March 1896)|defection of Teuku Umar]].

## The quarter

[[P. H. R. Beuming]] explains that Nesoeh was the quarter *waar de woningen van de hoofdofficieren zijn gelegen* — where the dwellings of the senior officers lay: *ruime fraaie huizen met paviljoens, aan beide kanten omgeven door een fraai plantsoen* — spacious handsome houses with pavilions, surrounded on both sides by a fine garden.

## The fortification of [[1896]]

Nesoeh above all, with its extensive row of wooden houses on the south side, lay wholly unprotected and turned towards the most hostile front. To protect the quarter, a breastwork and wooden fence were erected at the weakest point — *bij de wacht en het laboratorium der artillerie*, by the guard-house and the laboratory of the artillery — and the whole quarter was then surrounded by a heavy entanglement of barbed wire.

The measure belongs to the period of extreme tension between the defection of [[Teuku Umar|Toekoe Oemar]] and the arrival of the Java battalions, when there were no troops available to relieve the enclosed forward posts because all available men were needed to hold the posts of the *[[Geconcentreerde linie|geconcentreerde linie]]* and to secure Koeta Radja and [[Oleh-leh]].
