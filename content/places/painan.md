**Painan** is a coastal town on [[Sumatra's west coast|Sumatra's west coast]] whose name is preserved in the **Painans Contract** of [[1663]]. The treaty, negotiated by the [[Verenigde Oostindische Compagnie|VOC]] and west-coast chiefs, marked a decisive shift of allegiance away from the [[Sultanate of Aceh]] and toward Dutch mediation and arbitration in local disputes.

## Significance

Painan was the focal point of VOC diplomacy in the early 1660s. A preliminary compact negotiated near the town in May [[1662]] laid the groundwork for the formal agreement concluded on [[27 July]] [[1663]]. The settlement transferred de facto authority over several west-coast polities, including [[Indrapura|Indrapoera]] and [[Padang]], from Aceh's suzerainty to a Dutch-backed confederation.
