**Goenong Löser** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Volcano in the western Barisan range where borders of Tapa' Toean, Gajo-Loeüs, and Alaslanden meet.

## Description

Volcano in the western [[Barisan (mountains)|Barisan range]] where borders of [[Tapa]]' Toean, [[Gajo-Loeüs]], and [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]] meet. Among Sumatra's highest peaks; only recognizable as volcano by some solfataras and hot springs.

## Significance

Goenong Löser is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
