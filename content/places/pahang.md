A Malay sultanate. [[Sultan Iskandar Muda]] conquered Pahang in [[1618]]. In the [[Hikayat Malem Dagang]]: 'Between [[Raja Raden]] and his royal host there soon grew up such a brotherly feeling, that the former... gave up his wife, a daughter of the ruler of Pahang to the king of [[Acheh]].' The consort of [[Sultan Iskandar Muda]], '[[Putroe Phang]],' was from Pahang and warned him to avoid certain dangerous territories. The fleet 'next proceeds to Phang (Pahang) the king of which country is overjoyed at meeting his new and his former son-in-law ([[Sultan Iskandar Muda]] and [[Raja Raden]]).' [[Asahan]] (Aseuhan) A principality on the East Coast of [[Sumatra]]. In the [[Hikayat Malem Dagang]]: 'The first principality of the Malay Peninsula at which the fleet touches is Aseuhan ([[Asahan]]), the residence of the pagan [[Raja Muda]]... In the negociations connected with this conversion [[Malem Dagang]] plays a very chivalrous part.' In the fictional Hikayat Nun [[Parisi]], 'They proceed to Aseuhan, the territory of the powerful prince Bahrun Diwa, who has married ninety-nine wives one after another.' Also: 'a relative of the king of Bangka Ulu' is mentioned in Nun [[Parisi]].
