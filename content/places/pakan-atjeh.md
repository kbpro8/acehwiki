**Pakan Atjeh** was the fortified Dutch post on the right bank of the [[Kroeng Daroe]] east of the [[Kraton of Atjeh|Kraton]], garrisoned under [[Kapitein van Randwijk|kapitein van Randwijk]] and equipped with *achterlaadkanonnen van 12 c.M.* (12 cm breech-loading guns). Its elevated observatory made it the principal Dutch artillery-watching point against the enemy's eastern *benteng* line at [[Pendetti]] and [[Lampoe Idjoe]], and from it on [[15 May]] [[1874]] the discovery of an Atjehneesche *vlag* (flag) waving above a new *benteng* triggered the bombardment of the Pendetti fortifications.

## The post and its guns

Pakan Atjeh was established after the [[Fall of the Kraton (24 January 1874)|fall of the Kraton]] as the eastern anchor of the Dutch perimeter and the principal battery against enemy works upstream on the [[Atjeh-rivier]]. Its 12 c.M. *achterlaadkanonnen* — the best Dutch gun in Atjeh, the first breech-loaders used in the Indies — outranged the enemy's *voorlaad* guns and the Atjehnees *lilla* (light swivel-guns). Borel personally supervised the battery's service, and on [[24 August]] [[1874]] it was Pakan Atjeh's 12 c.M. *achterlaadkanonnen* that silenced the enemy's guns when the Atjehneezen brought the first *lilla*'s and at least one cannon onto a covered position and fired at the Dutch flag flying high over the Kraton. On [[7 September]] [[1874]] a well-aimed 6-pound enemy ball flew over the northern part of the east face into the *vesting-artillerie* kazerne, "tore a sergeant's shoulder and half an inlandsche woman's head," and tore the papers of the sergeant-major to shreds.

## The observation post

From the high observatory at Pakan Atjeh the Dutch discovered on [[15 May]] [[1874]] the new enemy *vlag* above a *benteng* at the height of [[Lampoe Idjoe]] upstream. The artillerie at Pakan Atjeh immediately opened fire, the shots being *gecorrigeerd* from the observatory, and the Atjehneezen were soon unable to "binnen hunne werken te Pendetti geen stand houden" — hold their ground in the Pendetti works. On [[17 August]] [[1874]] kapitein van Randwijk marched out with his company at dawn and seized the *grafheuvel* (mound) east of Pakan Atjeh at the double-quick — the post later named the *ooster-benteng*. From that day the enemy could do nothing against Pakan Atjeh, which enjoyed unprecedented quiet. *Kartetsen* (canister) were used at Pakan Atjeh during the [[Occupation of the ooster-benteng (17 August 1874)|fierce fighting of 17 August 1874]].
