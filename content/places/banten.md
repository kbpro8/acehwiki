Referenced in the discussion of Javanese Islamic education: 'In Banten this principle (of sending students away for Quran study) is pursued so far that boys are even sent for their elementary studies to a pondok outside their own village.' Also: 'Haji Mangsur, the great saint of Banten is said to have one day set free a tiger which was caught in the vice of a huge shell-fish.' Sundanese collections from Banten and South Chirebon are referenced in the description of Si Kabayan stories.
