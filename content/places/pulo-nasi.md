**Pulo Nasi** is an island situated off the northwestern tip of Sumatra in the Aceh archipelago.

## Role in the Second Aceh Expedition

On [[24 November]] [[1873]], Dutch naval forces occupied Pulo Nasi without resistance to establish a quarantine field hospital for troops suffering from a severe cholera epidemic that broke out aboard troopships leaving Batavia. The 3rd Infantry Battalion was stationed on the island until heavy monsoonal rains forced their re-embarkation in [[December]] [[1873]].
