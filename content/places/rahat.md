**Rahat** was a kampong in the [[III Moekims Lamkrak]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]], which was burned by the Dutch on [[29 June]] [[1896]] in the [[Tuchtiging of Sibreh and Rahat (29 June 1896)|tuchtiging which followed the Surprise of Ana' Galoeng]]. The kampong — together with [[Sibreh]] — was the explicit object of the *tuchtiging* (punitive expedition) ordered in the operational plan of the [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|surprise of Ana' Galoeng]]: *het verbranden van de kampongs Rahat en Sibreh der VII Moekims Baid, en het vernietigen der eigendommen aldaar* (the burning of the kampongs of Rahat and Sibreh of the VII Moekims Baid, and the destruction of the properties there). The burning of Rahat was thus the closing act of the surprise attack, undertaken by the 1e colonne under [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] after the storming of the *benteng* (fort) of [[Ana' Galong]].

## Significance

Rahat is significant in the documentary record of *ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel* as one of the two kampongs (with [[Sibreh]]) burned by the Dutch on [[29 June]] [[1896]] in the [[Tuchtiging of Sibreh and Rahat (29 June 1896)|tuchtiging which followed the Surprise of Ana' Galoeng]]. The kampong — explicitly named in the operational plan of the surprise attack — was the object of the standard Dutch punitive response to Acehnese resistance, applied to the kampongs of the III Moekims Lamkrak in the immediate vicinity of the *benteng* (fort) which the Acehnese had considered the *hart van Groot-Atjeh*.
