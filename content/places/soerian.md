**Soerian** was an Atjehnees kampong west of [[Kota Radja|K. Radja]] and the principal objective of the [[Battle of Soerian (26 July 1874)|battle of 26 July 1874]], in which [[Generaal Pel|kolonel Pel]] led out the whole available force to conquer Soerian and the neighbouring kampongs of [[Setee]], Poengej Blang Tjoet, [[Poe Oe]] and Soerian, gain a forward line protecting the Marassa area and the K. Radja–[[Oeleh Leh]] communication, and threaten the flank and rear of [[Toekoe Nanta]] of the [[VI Moekims]]. The enemy abandoned Soerian within days of the battle.

## The battle

[[Generaal Pel|Pel]] marched out on [[26 July]] [[1874]] with the whole available force. His aim was to conquer Soerian and its neighbouring kampongs in one combined operation, gain a forward line protecting the Marassa area and the K. Radja–Oeleh Leh communication, bring [[Bital]] and Lamtermin under observation, and threaten the flank and rear of Toekoe Nanta. Troops assembled at [[Blang Oe]] and advanced. They met immediate stiff resistance; the enemy was prepared. The Dutch failed to take Soerian outright but managed to surround a band inside a *benteng* near Soerian and, because they refused to surrender, killed them to the last man — about 70 enemy dead. Dutch losses were six dead and more than 40 wounded, including three officers. A post was immediately erected on the conquered ground. Borel's verdict is sharp: the whole bloody fight could have been avoided if Soerian, which had been in Dutch hands in February [[1874]], had never been given back.

## Aftermath and incorporation

In the days following the battle the enemy abandoned all positions; the magnificent *sawah* (irrigated rice fields) stretching to the edge of Bital and [[Ketapang Doewa]] fell into Dutch hands. The kampong of [[Poe Oe]] west of Soerian was occupied without a shot on [[16 August]] [[1874]], giving the Dutch a wide view over the sawahs. By the time the continuous line around K. Radja was completed in May [[1875]], Soerian had become one of the western-side posts of the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]], connected through Poengej Blang Tjoet, [[Lamteboe]], [[Setee]] and Lamara Olejlo to [[Longbatta-Mesigit]] and through [[Blang Oe]] to the sea-strand at Oeleh Leh. [[Sinangri]], east of Soerian, was occupied without opposition in July [[1875]] and considerably improved the stelling at its weakest point.
