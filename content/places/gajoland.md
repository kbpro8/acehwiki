**Gajoland** (region) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Region of the Gajo people, including Gajo Loeös.

## Type

region

## Description

Region of the [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]] people, including [[Gajo Loeös]]. Subject of [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]]'s '[[Het Gajoland en zijn bewoners]]'.

## Significance

[[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajoland]] is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
