**Poe Oe** was an Atjehnees kampong west of [[Soerian]] and east of [[Blang Oe]], occupied without a shot on [[16 August]] [[1874]] in the [[Occupation of the ooster-benteng (17 August 1874)|same summer offensive]] that followed the [[Battle of Soerian (26 July 1874)|battle of Soerian]]. From its wide view over the *sawah* (irrigated rice fields) it gave the Dutch observation over the western kampong belt and formed, with Soerian and Blang Oe, the western arc of the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]] around [[Kota Radja|K. Radja]].

## The occupation

On [[16 August]] [[1874]] — the day before the [[Occupation of the ooster-benteng (17 August 1874)|occupation of the ooster-benteng]] east of [[Pakan Atjeh]] — the kampong of Poe Oe, west of Soerian, was occupied without a shot. The enemy had abandoned it in the days following the [[Battle of Soerian (26 July 1874)|battle of Soerian]] of [[26 July]] [[1874]], when the Atjehneezen gave up all their western positions and the magnificent *sawah* stretching to the edge of [[Bital]] and [[Ketapang Doewa]] fell into Dutch hands. Poe Oe's elevated position gave a wide view over the sawahs, and a post was immediately established. The fall of Poe Oe was, Borel notes, an almost bloodless complement to the heavy fighting at Soerian — proof that the enemy could not stand once his main line was broken.

## In the stelling-Pel

Poe Oe became a permanent western-side post of the *stelling-Pel*. On [[23 January]] [[1875]] a new post was built at Poengej Blang Tjoet between Poe Oe and [[Kota Podjoet]] to better secure the western side of K. Radja and prevent the enemy penetrating the forward line toward Lampasej. By the time the continuous line around K. Radja was completed in May [[1875]], the south side of the line ran from Oedjoeng Planggan through Soerian, Poe Oe, Poengej Blang Tjoet, Lamteboe, [[Setee]], [[Lamara Olejlo]], Lohong, [[Longbatta]] Z. to [[Longbatta-Mesigit]]. From Poe Oe the line continued westward through Blang Oe to the sea-strand at [[Oeleh Leh]]. With the completion of the line, operations could be suspended or postponed, and [[Generaal Pel|Pel]] was granted three months' leave on [[12 June]] [[1875]] to recover his shattered health.
