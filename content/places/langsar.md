**Langsar** (also written Langsa) was a coast-state on the east coast of [[Acheh|Atjeh]], one of the two states — together with [[Simpang Olim|Simpang Olim]] — that had remained hostile to the Dutch by the end of [[1874]]. The state was the object of an expedition in [[May 1877|May 1877]] after its population had been troubling the friendly [[Tamiang|Tamiang]], in which the Dutch flag was hoisted at Langsar on [[18 May]] [[18 May]] [[1877]]. Together with [[Modjopahit|Modjopahit]] and the whole [[Temijang|Temijang-gebied]], Langsar came under Dutch sovereignty in [[1877]]-[[1878]].

## Refusal of Submission

By the end of [[1874]] the Dutch flag had not yet been accepted by [[Telok Kroet|Telok Kroet]], [[Rigas|Rigas]] and [[Tenom|Tenom]] on the west coast, by [[Pedir|Pedir]], [[Merdoe|Merdoe]] and [[Samalanga|Samalanga]] on the north coast, and by [[Simpang Olim|Simpang Olim]], [[Arakoendoer|Arakoendoer]] and Langsar on the east coast. Langsar was thus one of the hold-outs among the coast-states of Atjeh.

## The Expedition of May 1877

In [[May 1877|May 1877]] the whole East Coast was brought under Dutch authority by an expedition to Langsar, whose population had been troubling the friendly Tamiang. The Dutch troops had thereby a very tiring march to accomplish before they had reached the chief place from the [[Birimrivier|Birimrivier]], where they had disembarked. After a failed march on [[10 May]] [[10 May]], the troops set out again on [[11 May]] [[11 May]] and arrived at Langsar only on [[14 May]] [[14 May]]:

> "The great heat to which the troops had been exposed that day had caused many to fall unconscious: an adjutant-underofficier, acting officer, and 3 soldiers had thereby succumbed, and the troop was, according to an eye-witness, about midday so lethargic that 50 enterprising Atjehers would certainly have been able to make a terrible slaughter. Fatigue, hunger and thirst stood on every face, and only when the bivouac was pitched did the officers and rank-and-file gradually come to themselves."

On [[18 May]] [[18 May]] [[1877]] the Dutch flag was hoisted at Langsar.

## The Submission of Modjopahit and the Temijang-gebied

Repeated disturbances of the peace and incursions into the Dutch enterprises in [[Langkat|Langkat]] led to an expedition in [[1877]], "of which the consequence was that also the Atjehsche statejes Langsar and Modjopahit, and the whole Temijang-gebied came under our sovereignty."
