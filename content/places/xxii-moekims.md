**The XXII Mukims** — the 22 Mukims — is the region of [[Atjeh|Aceh]] whose mountains were, in [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]]'s *De Atjehers* and in the chapter *Jagerslatijn* of *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* by Merac ([[1947]]), the habitat of the *[[Manté's|manté's]]* — the interior people who would wear no clothing and would be densely hairy over the whole body.

## The mountains of the XXII Mukims

These *manté's* would wear no clothing and would be densely hairy over the whole body. They would live in the mountains of the XXII Mukims, and all informants knew them only by hearsay. The mountains of the XXII Mukims are the book's — and Snouck Hurgronje's — marker of the habitat of the *manté's*: the mountains where the interior people lived, always one day's journey further into the interior than the researcher who wished to see them.

## Significance

The XXII Mukims are significant in *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* as the region whose mountains were the habitat of the *manté's*. The book's argument — that the *manté's* would live in the mountains of the XXII Mukims and that all informants knew them only by hearsay — is the book's — and Snouck Hurgronje's — marker of the interior people of the Acehnese mountains. The XXII Mukims are therefore the book's marker of the habitat of the *manté's*: the mountains of the 22 Mukims where the interior people lived.
