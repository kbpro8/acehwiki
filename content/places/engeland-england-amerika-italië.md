**Engeland (England), Amerika, Italië** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Foreign powers with which Acehnese nobles established contact (along with England, America, Turkey and Italy).

## Description

Foreign powers with which Acehnese nobles established contact (along with [[Engeland (England)|England]], America, [[Ottoman Empire|Turkey]] and Italy).

## Significance

Engeland (England), Amerika, Italië is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
