**Mibouw** was a fortified Acehnese village settlement and earthen redoubt located near [[Pekan Badak]] and [[Sinangri]] on the border of the [[IV Moekims]] in modern Banda Aceh, Indonesia.

## Storming of Mibouw in December 1875

Guarding the northern approach to the [[Blankala Pass]], Mibouw was heavily garrisoned by Acehnese forces who harassed Dutch transport lines throughout [[1875]]. 

On [[26 December]] [[1875]], General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]] opened [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]] by launching the [[Attack on Mibouw]]. Two battalions of [[Barissans of Bangkallan]] and the [[6th Infantry Battalion]] stormed the earthworks, capturing the settlement at the cost of two killed and twelve wounded, thereby opening the road to the interior mukims.
