**Lho Nibong** — modern *Lhok Nibong* — is a *kampong* in the *landschap* [[Idi rajeu|Idi rajeu]] in the *onderafdeeling* [[Idi|Idi]], notable for its many dwellings and its gardens full of palms, profiting from the dyke along the [[Kroeeng Djamboe Aje|Kroeeng Djamboe Aje]].
