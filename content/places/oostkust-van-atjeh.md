**Oostkust van Atjeh** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Afdeeling of East Coast of Aceh; includes Tamiang, Langsa, Idi; main rubber cultivation area; includes Alaslanden.

## Description

[[afdeeling|Afdeeling]] of [[East Coast of Aceh]]; includes [[Tamiang]], [[Langsar|Langsa]], Idi; main rubber cultivation area; includes [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]]. Cattle: 24,142 runderen / 15,622 karbouwen. Major timber exploitation and tidal forest area.

## Significance

Oostkust van Atjeh is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
