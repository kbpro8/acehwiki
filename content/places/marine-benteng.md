The **Marine-benteng** was the small Dutch fort on the right bank of the [[Atjeh-rivier]], about half-way between the mouth at [[Kwala Atjeh]] and the bivouak at [[Penajoeng]], garrisoned by the [[Compagnie mariniers in Atjeh|compagnie mariniers]] and forming with the bivouak and the river-mouth one of the early fixed points of the Dutch perimeter on the right bank. After the [[Fall of the Kraton (24 January 1874)|fall of the Kraton]] [[Jan van Swieten|van Swieten]] ordered improvements to the marine-benteng, the *vierkante gehastionneerde redoute* at Penajoeng, and the conversion of the captured [[Kraton of Atjeh|Kraton]] into the Dutch *hoofdplaats* — all part of his *werken met schop en spade* ("working with spade and shovel") programme of February [[1874]].

## Function

The Marine-benteng's purpose was to keep the lower Atjeh-rivier open for the prauwen and stoombootjes that brought heavy stores from the roadstead at [[Kwala Atjeh]] up to Penajoeng and the Kraton. Its guns enfiladed the river and the adjacent right bank; its garrison could sally against any enemy attempting to *verschansen* (entrench) along the river. On [[21 May]] [[1874]] the Dutch learned that the Atjehneezen were working between Penajoeng and the Marine-benteng precisely to render the vaart on the river unsafe; the Marine-benteng's role in frustrating this attempt was critical. The waterweg via the Soengei Aroesan — at this point still controlled by the Marine-benteng — was, in Borel's phrase, "geruimen tijd voor ons van onschatbare waarde," carrying almost all vivres, munitie, geschut, and bouwmaterialen.

## Attacks and aftermath

The Marine-benteng was repeatedly threatened through the spring of [[1874]]. On [[15 June]] [[1874]] the Dutch *waterprauw* was fired on between the Marine-benteng and Penajoeng by Atjehneezen concealed in the brush on the right bank; the sailors saved themselves only by rowing with all their might, and many bullets pierced the prauw. It was the first sign that river navigation was becoming unsafe. After [[Generaal Pel|kolonel Pel]] took command in late April [[1874]] the Marine-benteng was strengthened, and the [[Conquest of Langkroek (28 September 1874)|conquest of Langkroek]] of [[28 September]] [[1874]] finally cleared the right bank below Penajoeng of enemy positions. With the [[Pedir-dijk]] later garrisoned and the post at [[Lamara]] established beyond the dike, the Marine-benteng became a quiet link in the river-defence chain of the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]].
