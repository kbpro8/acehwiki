**Lamgoet** was a *[[Benteng|benteng]]* in the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]] captured by [[Toekoe Nja Banta]] on [[4 September]] [[1893]] and given into the keeping of [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb|Toekoe Tjoet]], who arbitrarily evacuated it and thereby played it back into the hands of the *[[Moslemin]]*.

## The episode

[[G. B. Hooijer]] recorded that *de benteng te Lamgoet, op den 4den September door Nja Bantah veroverd en aan hem in bewaring gegeven, had hij eigendunkelijk ontruimd en daardoor weder in handen des vijands gespeeld* — the *benteng* at Lamgoet, captured on 4 September by Nja Bantah and given to him in keeping, he had arbitrarily evacuated and thereby played back into the enemy's hands. It was the first of the acts by which Toekoe Tjoet renewed Dutch suspicion of him after [[General‑Major Van Teyn]] had bought his neutrality with a salary.
