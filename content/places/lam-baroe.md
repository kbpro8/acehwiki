**Lam Baroe** was a station of the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* in [[Atjeh]], one of the two posts — with [[Lho Nga|Lho'-Nga]] — at which the 5th *[[Divisie (Maréchaussée)|Divisie]]* of the corps was quartered in [[1898]].

## The 1898 localisation

The [[1930]] jubilee number gives the corps's disposition in [[1898]]: Staff at [[Koeta Radja]]; 1st Division at [[Seulimeum|Selimoen]]; 2nd Division at [[Indrapoeri]]; 3rd Division at [[Tjot Mantjang]] and [[Glé Broet]]; 4th Division at [[Tjot Goet]]; 5th Division at Lam Baroe and Lho'-Nga.

The name is formed with the Acehnese topographic prefix *lam-*, denoting a village near water.

## Later dispositions

In [[1910]] the 5th Division was stationed at [[Lho Soekon]], where it remained at the time of the [[1930]] jubilee.
