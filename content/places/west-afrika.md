**West-Afrika** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Native region of the oil palm species used in Aceh.

## Description

Native region of the oil palm species used in Aceh. The variety cultivated in [[Netherlands|the Netherlands]] Indies yields significantly more than in its native land.

## Significance

West-Afrika is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
