**Noordkust (North Coast of Aceh)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Coastal region; Teukoe Oemar's strijdmacht arrived here to support resistance at Pedir.

## Description

Coastal region; [[Teukoe Oemar]]'s strijdmacht arrived here to support [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] at [[Pedir]]. Largest number of insane among pure Acehnese population of [[Noordkust]] and [[Groot-Atjeh]]. [[Sigli]] and Lho-Soekon the places with most [[Atjeh-moorden|atjeh-moorden]].

## Significance

Noordkust (North Coast of Aceh) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
