**Mandarsa Poeti** was an old stone *gebedenhuis* (place of worship, mosque) about 1,600 paces south of the main wall of [[Kota Radja|K. Radja]], which the Atjehneezen had converted into a fortification and the Dutch destroyed by *achterlaadkanonnen van 12 c.M.* fire on [[5 October]] [[1874]] and then occupied as a post on [[28 January]] [[1875]] in the [[Conquest of Mandarsa Poeti (28 January 1875)|conquest of Mandarsa Poeti]]. From its position at the southern arc of the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]] it commanded the kampong belt of [[Lamara Olejlo]], [[Setee]] and Lamlagan.

## The destruction of October 1874

As the terrain between the *zuid-west saillant* of K. Radja and [[Kota Podjoet]] was partly cleared in the late summer of [[1874]], the old stone *gebedenhuis* called Mandarsa Poeti came into view about 1,600 paces from the main wall. The enemy had converted it into a fortification. A few shots from the 12 c.M. *achterlaadkanon* established the range; the whole front wall was then brought down. Through the breach the interior could be seen; the building was permanently evacuated by the enemy. Borel's point is that the same 12 c.M. *achterlaadkanon* which had been *recalled* to Java by [[Jan van Swieten|van Swieten]] in February [[1874]] — "the best geschut the Dutch had" — was now doing the work that nothing else could do. The lesson was not lost on [[Generaal Pel|kolonel Pel]], who immediately requested the return of the guns from Java.

## The post of January 1875

Three columns marched simultaneously from K. Radja on [[28 January]] [[1875]]. The middle, main column under [[Majoor van der Meer|majoor van der Meer]] marched straight on the stone *gebedenhuis* and took it without resistance. The other columns moved left on Nesoch and right on [[Setee]] to cover the flanks. Since the walls of the *gebedenhuis* had already been shot to rubble by the 12 c.M. fire and the interior was too small for a garrison, a suitable nearby point was chosen and construction of a new fortification begun. The conquest was so sudden the enemy had no time to resist. Later in the day, however, when it became clear the Dutch meant to stay, the Atjehneezen streamed in from all sides, especially from the [[IX Moekims]]. They occupied the *zuid-west kampong-rand* toward Lamlagan and opened fire. The cost was three dead, ten wounded, and one killed in [[Kapitein von Ende|captain Sutherland]]'s western flank column. The post of Mandarsa Poeti then became [[Kapitein von Ende|kapitein von Ende]]'s command, from which he made the [[Surprise of Lamara Olejlo (14–15 February 1875)|surprise of Lamara Olejlo]] of [[14 February|14]]–[[15 February]] [[1875]].
