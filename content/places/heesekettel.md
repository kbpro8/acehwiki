**Heesekettel** (bay (Westkust)) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. One of the many small bays where in West monsoon the ocean surf breaks thunderously on karangrotsen (coral rocks) in boiling foam.

## Type

bay ([[Westkust]])

## Description

One of the many small bays where in West monsoon the ocean surf breaks thunderously on karangrotsen (coral rocks) in boiling foam. Worth visiting during Westmoesson.

## Significance

Heesekettel is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
