**Pasar Oleh Gli** was one of the three places in [[Groot-Atjeh]] — with [[Pakan Badak]] and [[Lampisang]] — on which a powerful fire from all the pieces of the Dutch land and sea forces was opened in the early morning of [[27 April]] [[1896]], after [[Teuku Umar|Toekoe Oemar]] had failed to answer the [[Regeeringscommissaris]]'s demand that he surrender his arms and munitions, raze his fortifications and submit.
