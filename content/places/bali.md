**Bali** is an island in the [[Indonesia]] archipelago that became the scene of a Dutch marine expedition in [[1905]] and a subsequent **action against Kloenkoeng** in [[1908]]. Although the island was never incorporated into the [[Aceh sultanate]], its campaigns are recorded alongside the Aceh‑related operations of the [[Royal Netherlands Marine Corps|Dutch Marine Corps]] in the early twentieth century.

## Expedition to Bali (1905)

The [[1905]] expedition was launched by the Dutch to subdue Balinese resistance to Dutch authority. Dutch forces sought to enforce colonial control over the island’s principal kingdoms, confronting local leaders who opposed foreign rule. The operation marked a decisive step in extending Dutch influence beyond the western parts of the archipelago.

## Action against Kloenkoeng (1908)

In [[1908]], the Dutch mounted a focused action against the town of **Kloenkoeng** (now known as [[Klungkung]]), completing the final pacification of Bali. The 1908 operation targeted remaining pockets of resistance and solidified Dutch administrative dominance over the island.
