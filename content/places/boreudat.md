In the [[Hikayat Putroe Baren]]: 'Banta Sulutan is the son, and Putroe Baren (Bahren) Miga the daughter of Raja Baren Nasi, king of Boreudat ([[Baghdad]]).' Also in [[Hikayat Pha Suasa]]: 'Raja Ahmat, the king of [[Baghdad]] (Boreudat).'
