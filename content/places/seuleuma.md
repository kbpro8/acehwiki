**Seuleuma** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[T. Radja Sabi]]

In [[1916]] [[M. Schouten|Schouten]] received reports so positive that he resolved, with the *oelèëbalang* of [[Seuleuma]], to make one more attempt: the *vechttijd* was past, and why should the boy go on wandering? With the co-operation of all the chiefs and of [[T. Tjhi' Béntara]], the half-brother, who went along in person, a discussion was held "somewhere in the rimba."

### From [[M. Schouten]]

In [[1916]] Schouten received reports so positive about the whereabouts of [[T. Radja Sabi]] — the last wandering irreconcilable of [[Keureutòë]], then a boy of fifteen — that he resolved, together with the *[[oelèëbalang]]* of [[Seuleuma]], to make one more attempt to bring him in: "the fighting time was over, and why should that boy go on wandering?" With the co-operation of all the chiefs and of [[T. Tjhi' Béntara]], the half-brother, who went along in person, a discussion was held "somewhere in the rimba." The condition set by the other side — that the Governor must first acknowledge that the boy at [[Koeta Radja]] was a substitute — proved a stumbling-block, and T. Radja Sabi continued to wander until [[1919]].

### From [[T. Tjhi' Béntara]]

In [[1916]] T. Tjhi' Béntara personally accompanied [[M. Schouten|Schouten]] and the *oelèëbalang* of [[Seuleuma]] to the meeting held "somewhere in the rimba" in an attempt to bring in his wandering nephew.
