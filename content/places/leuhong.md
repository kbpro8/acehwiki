**Leuhong** was a maréchaussée post in the [[Lho Seumawé]] region, serving as the headquarters of the 5e divisie. The [[Colonne Matjan]] — the *tijger-colonne* formed under [[Christoffel]] in [[1907]] — was stationed at Leuhong after Christoffel's departure, taking on the character of an ordinary maréchaussée divisie under Van der Vierk.

## The false T. Radja Sabi investigation

When the false [[T. Radja Sabi]] arrived at [[Lho Soekon]] on [[6 December]] [[1913]], kapitein [[Behrens]] — commandant of the 5e divisie at Leuhong — came over to question the boy. Behrens inquired about the bijzonderheden of different overvallen by the troepen at which the real T. Radja Sabi had been present. The pas-gemelde knaap immediately fell door de mand: he knew nothing of those tastbare feiten. The boy was brought to the bivak at Leuhong for further questioning, where he confessed that he was an *onderschoven object* — in reality Doellah, son of Tengkoe Radja Imeum of Mbang.
