**Nesooh** (also read *Nesoch*) was a watchpost close by [[Kuta Raja|Kota Radja]], within the Dutch [[Geconcentreerde Stelling|concentrated position]] of [[1885]]. The watch at Nesooh was attacked by the Atjehnese in the autumn of [[1885]] with a loss of one wounded man, one of the incidents recorded by majoor [[M. T. H. Perelaer|Perelaer]] in his list of what befell the Dutch inside their own line in the first year of the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration]] (see [[Guerilla raids inside the concentration line (1885)]]).
