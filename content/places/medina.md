The second holiest city of Islam. [[Abdurra'uf of Singkel]] 'studied for many years at Medina,

In the [[Hikayat Soydina Usen]]: 'Asan was king at Medina; the infidel Yadib (Ach. pronunciation of Yazid) in [[Meuse]] ([[Egypt]]). Lila-majan, one of the two wives of Asan, let herself be persuaded by Yadib to poison her husband.' In the Hikayat Muhamat Napiah: 'Muhamat Napiah the son of Ali, ruled in Buniara, a subdivision of the kingdom of Medina.' The cave where Muhamat Napiah and his horse are still waiting for the resurrection is also described.
