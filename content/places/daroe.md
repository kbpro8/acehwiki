**Daroe** was a kampong (village) situated at the eastern exit of the [[Glitaroem]] mountain pass in [[Acheh]]. It was the scene of a fierce engagement on [[28 January]] [[1876]] when Dutch marines and army units forced the enemy to abandon the settlement.

### Battle Details
The terrain around Daroe was heavily marshy and covered in sawah (rice fields). After a short firefight, the Dutch forces reached the “mesigit” (temple) and a stone‑ringed wall surrounding it. The enemy retreated from the kampong, firing from the sawahs.

### Aftermath
The Dutch bivouac was later engaged and the column withdrew after suffering one dead and ten wounded.
