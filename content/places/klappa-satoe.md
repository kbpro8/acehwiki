**Klappa Satoe** — modern *Kelapa Satu*, *Coconut-One* — is a *kampong* close to [[Sigli|Sigli]] in the *afdeeling* [[Pidië|Pidië]], a place with a war-memory, where formerly there really stood a single coco-palm — *'vroeger wezenlijk één cocospalm stond'* — but where now some thousands of trees stand and are neatly maintained.
