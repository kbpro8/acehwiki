**Panté Lhong** is a place in the *landschap* of [[Peusangan|Peusangan]] where the river Peusangan, in two arms, flows to the sea. The *B.O.W.* dammed one arm of the river there, at a cost of three tons, with the intention of irrigating the *sawahs* of Peusangan; but after the damming and dyking no water came into the main conduit, and one sees at this starting-point of irrigation all the water running through the other arm. The work cost three tons and is fruitless. The failure is Broersma's type-case of the costly and useless public-works of the early civilian period.

## Account from *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*

Site of an important irrigation work and drainage canal (~20 years before writing) that greatly improved the [[Peusangan River|Peusangan river]] flooding situation in the coast strook.

*Source: J. Jongejans, Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu (Baarn: Hollandia Drukkerij N.V., c. 1938), pp. 77.*
