**The Glé Loeda pass** was a mountain pass on the west coast of [[Groot-Atjeh]], between which and the [[Kroeng Raba]] only a narrow coastal strip remained as the slope of the [[Glé Aron]] range fell away to the sea.

[[P. H. R. Beuming]] records in a footnote the Acehnese tradition attached to it: *Volgens de Atjehsche overlevering zou deze pas in één nacht ontstaan zijn door een ontzettende vloedgolf, gepaard aan een vulkanische uitbarsting. Vandaar door hen aangeduid met "Sprengpas."* — according to Acehnese tradition this pass arose in a single night through a tremendous tidal wave coupled with a volcanic eruption, whence it was designated by them the *Sprengpas*, the blasted or burst pass.

The tradition is one of the few items of Acehnese oral geography preserved in Beuming's sketches, and belongs to his description of the landscape about [[Lok-Nga|Lho-Nga]] and Kroeng Raba.
