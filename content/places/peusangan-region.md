**Peusangan (region)** (region/landschap) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Region whose inhabitants are stereotyped for gezwollenheid (arrogance) and listen (cunning); 'the people of Peusangan have 12 ways to deceive people'.

## Type

region/[[landschap]]

## Description

Region whose inhabitants are stereotyped for gezwollenheid (arrogance) and listen (cunning); 'the people of [[Peusangan]] have 12 ways to deceive people'.

## Significance

Peusangan (region) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
