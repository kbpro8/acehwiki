**Lho' Seumawé (Lhò Seumawé, Lho Seumawe)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Town on the Northkust.

## Description

Town on the Northkust. [[September]] [[1903]] [[Panglima Polém]] and [[Toeankoe Radja Keumala]] surrendered here to civiel-gezaghebber [[Hendrikus Colijn|Colijn]]. In [[Pasai|Pasé]]-expeditie a colonne remained here.

## Significance

Lho' Seumawé (Lhò Seumawé, Lho Seumawe) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
