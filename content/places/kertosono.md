**Kertosono** was a town in eastern [[Java]] that featured in Dutch defensive operations during the Japanese invasion of [[1942]]. The *Zuid‑colonne* under *Kolonel Van Dijk* was ordered to keep the bridge at Kertosono open, with a half‑squadron of cavalry and infantry positioned east of the river.

## Military Activity
*Lieutenant‑colonel K. M. R. W. A. van Heuven* received orders on [[4 March]] [[1942]] to form a provisional battalion with a company from the *Legioen Mangko Negoro* and to hold the bridge at Kertosono.

## Significance
Control of the Kertosono bridge was vital for maintaining Dutch lines of communication and delaying Japanese forces in the region.
