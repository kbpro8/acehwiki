**Langsabaai** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Bay reached by the Atjeh-tram extension in 1898-1902; the 1910 decision extended the line from there to Kwala Simpang.

## Description

Bay reached by the [[Atjeh-tram]] extension in [[1898]]-[[1902]]; the [[1910]] decision extended the line from there to [[Kwala Simpang]].

## Significance

Langsabaai is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
