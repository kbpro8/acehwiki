**Oud Singkel (Old Singkel)** (abandoned town) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Already abandoned due to malaria plague.

## Type

abandoned town

## Description

Already abandoned due to malaria plague. Predecessor of Nieuw [[Singkel]].

## Significance

Oud Singkel (Old Singkel) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
