**The Kroeng Daroe** (modern spelling Krueng Daroe or Krueng Daroy) is a river of the [[Acheh|Atjeh]] plain in the immediate vicinity of [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] and the [[Kraton of Aceh|Kraton]], whose embankment was one of the drainage works undertaken by the Staf der Genie at [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] in the early months of [[1875]] to make the Kraton free of [[bandjir|bandjir]]. The work was one of the peacetime engineer duties performed by [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede-Luitenant C. J. Snijders]] in the last months of his Atjeh service.

## The Embankment of 1875

After the close of his field engineer service with the [[Lamara Olejlo fortification (15 February 1875)|Lamara Olejlo fortification of 15 February]] [[1875]], [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] was attached to the Staf der Genie at [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] for what were essentially peacetime duties. Among the works listed by the biographer [[D. van den Berg|Van den Berg]] are "appoleinngweekanneedd tot het bandjirvrijmaken van den Kraton (o.a. door bedijking van de Kroeng Daroe ter plaatse)" — drainage works to make the Kraton free of bandjir, including by the embankment of the Kroeng Daroe at the relevant place.

## Significance

The Kroeng Daroe is the principal watercourse of the [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] plain and one of the rivers whose flooding periodically threatened the Dutch positions in and around the [[Kraton of Aceh|Kraton]]. The [[bandjir at Penajoeng (4 December 1874)|bandjir of 4 December 1874]] that nearly cost Snijders his life was a flood of the [[Acheh River|Atjeh River]] rather than of the Kroeng Daroe, but the two watercourses form a single hydrological system, and the embankment of the Kroeng Daroe in [[1875]] was part of the same general programme of bandjir-control that the bandjir of [[4 December]] [[4 December]] [[1874]] had shown to be necessary. The Kroeng Daroe is also the river whose name is preserved in the modern Krueng Daroy, the stream that flows through the [[Taman Sari (Aceh)|Taman Sari]] pleasure-garden of the sultans of Aceh.
