Home of the famous scholar [[Teungku Tanoh Abee|Teungku Tanoh Abie]], who held the position of kali of the XXII Mukims; both he and his son ([[Teungku Tanoh Mirah]], a teacher who is also kali of the IV Mukims of the VII sagi of the XXVI) are now dead. His son acquired his learning at [[Lam Nyong]].
