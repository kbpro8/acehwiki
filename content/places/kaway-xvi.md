**Kaway XVI** (landschap (afdeeling Westkust van Atjeh)) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Landschap in Aceh's Westkust whose zelfbestuurder Teukoe Tjhi' Mohamad Ali Akbar founded Acehnese rubber-planters' associations; now the most important rubber producer of the entire Westkust afdeeling.

## Type

[[landschap]] ([[afdeeling]] [[Westkust van Atjeh]])

## Description

[[landschap|Landschap]] in Aceh's [[Westkust]] whose [[zelfbestuurder]] [[Teukoe Tjhi' Mohamad Ali Akbar]] founded Acehnese rubber-planters' associations; now the most important rubber producer of the entire [[Westkust]] [[afdeeling]].

## Significance

Kaway XVI is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
