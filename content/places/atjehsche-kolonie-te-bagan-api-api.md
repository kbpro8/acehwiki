**The Atjehsche kolonie te Bagan Api Api** was, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's survey of the [[Buitenbezittingen|Outer Possessions]], an [[Acheh|Atjehnese]] settlement in the *onderafdeeling* [[Bagan Api Api|Bagan Api Api]] on [[Sumatra's East Coast|Sumatra's East Coast]], dating — the Bureau records — "uit den tijd van Atjeh's overheersching" — "from the time of Atjeh's overlordship." The colony is the only explicit Atjehnese settlement on the East Coast recorded in the Bureau's survey of the East Coast population, and it constitutes the most concrete demographic trace of the [[atjehsche-overheersing-der-noordelijke-kuststaatjes|long Atjehnese overlordship of the East Coast]] in the ethnic geography of the late colonial period.

## The colony

The Bureau records the colony in a single footnote to its discussion of the [[Batak-Karo|Karo-Batak]] *doesoen* population of [[Langkat|Langkat]], [[Deli|Deli]] and [[Serdang|Serdang]]. The footnote reads: "En onderdool der onderafdeeling Bagan Api Api. waar eene Atjehsche kolonie bestant dagteekenend uit den tijd van Atjeh's overheorsching" — "And a sub-district of the onderafdeeling Bagan Api Api, where an Atjehnese colony exists, dating from the time of Atjeh's overlordship." The Bureau's reference to the colony as "bestaande" — "existing" — in the present tense signals that the colony was, at the time of writing ([[1918]]), still a recognisable ethnic community in the Bagan Api Api *onderafdeeling*.

## Bagan Api Api

[[Bagan Api Api|Bagan Api Api]] was, in the Bureau's survey, an *onderafdeeling* of the [[Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra|Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra]], located on the southern stretch of the East Coast. The presence of an [[Acheh|Atjehnese]] colony at this considerable distance south of the [[Acheh|Atjehnese]] sphere of influence — separated from [[Acheh|Atjeh]] by the entire northern stretch of East Coast statelets — is, in the Bureau's framing, the most striking evidence of the demographic reach of the [[atjehsche-overheersing-der-noordelijke-kuststaatjes|Atjehnese overlordship]]. The colony could not have been a mere frontier settlement; it must have been an institutional planting, sustained by the Atjehnese apparatus of overlordship across the entire length of the East Coast.

## The demographic context

The colony at [[Bagan Api Api|Bagan Api Api]] is the only explicit [[Acheh|Atjehnese]] settlement on the East Coast recorded in the Bureau's survey. The Bureau's broader account of the East Coast population records the displacement of the [[Batak]]s from the coastal plain by [[Djohor|Djohorese Malays]] and [[Acheh|Atjehers]] after the [[atjehsche-vernietiging-van-het-batakrijk-aroe-(c-1589)|destruction of the Batakrijk Aroe]] and the [[atjehsche-invasie-in-tamiang-en-noordelijk-langkat-(c-1589)|Atjehnese invasion of Tamiang and Northern Langkat]], but it does not record Atjehnese settlements further south than [[Langkat|Noordelijk Langkat]]. The [[Bagan Api Api|Bagan Api Api]] colony is thus a unique exception: an [[Acheh|Atjehnese]] community at the southern end of the East Coast, sustained by the long Atjehnese overlordship of the coast.

## Significance

The Atjehsche kolonie te Bagan Api Api is, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's survey, the most concrete demographic trace of the [[atjehsche-overheersing-der-noordelijke-kuststaatjes|Atjehnese overlordship of the East Coast]]. Its survival into the late colonial period — recorded by the Bureau in the present tense in [[1918]] — is the empirical refutation of any account of the Atjehnese overlordship as a merely ceremonial or episodic arrangement. The colony is thus the demographic complement of the [[atjehsche-overheersing-der-noordelijke-kuststaatjes|Atjehnese overlordship]] and of the [[atjehsche-viervorsten-systeem|Atjehsche viervorsten-systeem]]: where the latter are the political and institutional imprints of Atjehnese power, the former is its demographic imprint.
