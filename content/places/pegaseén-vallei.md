**Pegaseén-vallei** — the *Pegasén valley* — is a valley near [[Takengon|Takengon]] to which a road runs from the chief place, passable at least to the 13th kilometre. It is one of the destinations of the walking-tours that, in Broersma's view, make Takengon the most delightful inland place of the gewest.
