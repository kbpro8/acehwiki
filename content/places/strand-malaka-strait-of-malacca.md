**Strand Malaka (Strait of Malacca)** (strait) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Strait labeled on the residency map (page 45).

## Type

strait

## Description

Strait labeled on the residency map (page 45).

## Significance

Strand Malaka (Strait of Malacca) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
