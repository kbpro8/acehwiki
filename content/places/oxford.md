**Oxford** is the English university city whose Bodleian Library preserves the *Hikayat Seri Rama* (dated [[1633]]), regarded as “the oldest Malay book in existence” by early 20th‑century scholars. The manuscript’s presence underscores the early European interest in Malay literary heritage, including works from [[Aceh]].
