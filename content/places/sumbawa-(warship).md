**The Sumbawa** was one of the three Netherlands warships detailed to take part in the bombardment of [[Lampisang]] in [[1896]] during the operations against [[Teuku Umar|Toekoe Oemar]].

[[P. H. R. Beuming]] records that the navy was also to take part in the bombardment: the warships *[[Koningin Wilhelmina (warship)|Koningin Wilhelmina]]*, the *[[Tromp (warship)|Tromp]]* and the *[[Sumbawa (warship)|Sumbawa]]*, armed respectively with 22, 32 and 6 pieces of 28, 21 and 17 cm, were *op het eerste sein, vanuit [[Oleh-leh]] en de [[Kroeng Raba Bay|Kroeng Rababaai]], het vuur openen* — to open fire at the first signal from Oleh-leh and the Kroeng Raba bay. The *Sumbawa* carried 6 pieces of 17 cm.

The naval guns joined the twenty land pieces of the siege battery at [[Lamdjamoe]] in the fire opened on [[27 April]] [[1896]] on [[Pakan Badak]], [[Pasar Oleh Gli]] and Lampisang.
