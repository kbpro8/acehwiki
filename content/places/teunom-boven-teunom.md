**Teunom (boven-Teunom)** (place (interior Westkust)) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Where the Nisero shipwreck survivors were taken.

## Type

place (interior [[Westkust]])

## Description

Where the [[Nisero shipwreck]] survivors were taken. 7 of them died there and were originally buried there before being re-interred at [[Tjalang]].

## Significance

Teunom (boven-Teunom) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
