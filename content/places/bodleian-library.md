**Bodleian Library** in [[Oxford]] houses one of the oldest known Malay manuscripts, the *Hikayat Seri Rama*, dated to [[1633]]. Although the *Hikayat Seri Rama* predates many other Malay texts, its age is contested by the older Arabic manuscripts preserved in Cambridge.

## Significance
The Bodleian manuscript provides a benchmark for dating early Malay literary production, complementing the Arabic‑Malay *Burda* translations studied by scholars of Aceh.
