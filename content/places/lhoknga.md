**Lhoknga** was/is a coastal district and bay located on the western shore of Aceh, just south of [[Kuta Raja]] (modern Aceh Besar Regency).

## Historical Context and Role

In [[1898]], Lhoknga hosted the 5th Division of the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]], securing the western maritime approaches and suppressing coastal pepper smuggling.
