**Kroeeng Djamboe Aje** is the river forming the northern border of the *landschap* [[Idi rajeu|Idi rajeu]] in the *onderafdeeling* [[Idi|Idi]] of the *afdeeling* [[Oostkust van Atjeh|Oostkust van Atjeh]]. Its overflowing causes enough trouble to require a firm dyke along it, from which the beautiful *kampong* [[Lho Nibong|Lho Nibong]] profits with its many dwellings and gardens full of palms. The prevention of swamp-formation by river-water and the effective use of it for agriculture may also in the *onderafdeeling* Idi be worked upon.
