**Matang Gloempang Doea** — modern *Matang Glumpang Dua* — is a place in the *landschap* of [[Peusangan|Peusangan]] in the *onderafdeeling* [[Bireuen|Bireuen]], the residence of the *oeléebalang* [[Mohamad Djohan Alamsjah|Mohamad Djohan Alamsjah]]. The chief lives there in an attractive but un-Atjehnese house amid a well-planted garden, situated on the road and on the tram.
