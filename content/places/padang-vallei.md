**Padang-vallei** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Gajòlanden]]

In [[1910]] the Kaniess column brought many from the [[Padang-vallei]] to submission, returned from a temporary rest at [[Koeta Radja]] on [[25 August]] and at once laid low by surprise the irreconcilable [[Imeum van Tjané Toa]]. Since women were repeatedly hit in raids, a *hoofdenvergadering* in March was charged with letting the wives of the *uitgewekenen* know that they should not wear men's clothing and should not run away during a raid.

### From [[The resistance in Atjeh 1908–1912]]

In [[1910]] the fighting and patrolling continued. The Kaniess column in the western part brought many from the [[Padang-vallei]] to submission, could even spend a temporary "verlof" in its garrison at [[Koeta Radja]], but returned on [[25 August]] and at once laid low by surprise the irreconcilable [[Imeum van Tjané Toa]]. Kaniess continued to act with success, so that the situation indeed improved. Since women were repeatedly hit during raids, a *hoofdenvergadering* in March was charged with letting the wives of the *uitgewekenen* know that they should not wear men's clothing and should not run away during a raid.
