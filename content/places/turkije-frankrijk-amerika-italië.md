**Turkije, Frankrijk, Amerika, Italië** (foreign powers) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Foreign powers with which Atjeh established relations in 1871-1873, prompting the Dutch to intervene militarily.

## Type

foreign powers

## Description

Foreign powers with which Atjeh established relations in [[1871]]-[[1873]], prompting the Dutch to intervene militarily.

## Significance

Turkije, Frankrijk, Amerika, Italië is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
