The holiest city of Islam, central to Achehnese religious life. The most important teachers returned from Mekka, and it became more and more the spiritual centre of Islam for the Indonesians. [[Cheh Marahaban]] studied at Mekka, where he acted as haji-shaikh (guide and protector of pilgrims). Many teachers from Mekka came to [[Acheh]]: 'Even up to the present time teachers and exponents of mysticism occasionally come, chiefly from Mekka, to make a profit of their learning or their sanctity among religiously disposed chiefs.' [[Acheh]] was historically 'the gate of the Holy Land' — a great halting place for almost all pilgrims from the Eastern Archipelago.
