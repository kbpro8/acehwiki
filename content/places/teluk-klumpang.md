**Teluk Klumpang** (Dutch: *Telok Kloempang*) is a bay on the west coast of [[aceh|Aceh]] north of [[rigaih|Rigas]] at which part of the Dutch punitive column under Lieutenant-Colonel [[general-major-van-teyn|Van Teyn]] was landed on [[20-june|20 June]] [[1886]] during the [[hoc-canton-affair|Hoc Canton affair]], in an effort to cut off [[Sajjid Abu Hafs Umar Ba Sjaiban|Toekoe Oemar]]'s landward retreat after the plundering of the steamship *Hoc Canton*. Heavy surf made the landing dangerous; a sloop carrying Lieutenant Matthes and six men capsized (all were rescued), and the captain of the *Devonhurst* was swept overboard and drowned.
