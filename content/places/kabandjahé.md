**Kabandjahé** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Place on the autoweg between Koeta Tjane and Medan.

## Description

Place on the autoweg between [[Koeta Tjané|Koeta Tjane]] and [[Medan]].

## Significance

Kabandjahé is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
