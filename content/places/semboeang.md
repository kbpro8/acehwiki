**Semboeang** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Penghoeloe Koeala]]

The *gezaghebber* of [[Lököp]] went with the chiefs and a *bevolkingspatrouille* to the *[[gampong]]* [[Semboeang]], which the Penghoeloe had worked upon, but was killed in an encounter with the unwilling, five men of his patrol being wounded. The Penghoeloe then organised a three-day religious feast and associated himself with the guardian of a holy grave and the guardian's daughter; the convinced withdrew; the patrols of the *[[Kompeuni]]* came; four fell, among them the guardian of the holy grave; the Penghoeloe was taken prisoner, "and then it was over."

### From [[Serbödjadi]]

The movement, set on foot by [[Penghoeloe Koeala]], a [[Gajòs|Gajò]] of [[Leuboe Sigeunab]], cost the *gezaghebber* of [[Lököp]] his life in an encounter at the *[[gampong]]* [[Semboeang]] and ended with four dead, among them the guardian of a holy grave, and the Penghoeloe's capture.

### From [[Religious movement in Serbödjadi (December 1917)]]

The *gezaghebber* of Lököp thereupon went with the chiefs and a *bevolkingspatrouille* (population patrol) to the *gampong* [[Semboeang]], which the Penghoeloe had worked upon, but was killed in an encounter with the unwilling, while five men of his patrol were wounded.
