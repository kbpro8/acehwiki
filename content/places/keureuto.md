**Keureuto** — pronounced *Kertó* — is a river and a *landschap* of the *onderafdeeling* [[Lho Soekon|Lho Soekon]] in the *afdeeling* [[Noordkust van Atjeh|Noordkust van Atjeh]]. The river runs through the middle of the *onderafdeeling* and divides it in two; the *landschap* lies in the western, more fertile half, where the population is greater and the rice-plain is watered by the Keureuto.

## The Division of the Onderafdeeling
The Keureuto divides the *onderafdeeling* Lho Soekon in two, and the two parts differ greatly in the nature of the land. The south-eastern half is all *paja* — swamp — with a population of fifteen thousand souls; the smaller northern half is free of it and counts twice the number of souls. The *landschap* of Keureuto itself lies in the western part of the *onderafdeeling*, in the hill-country where the memory of [[Christoffel's expedition to Keureuto (c. 1908)|Christoffel's expedition]] — the *apitan rimoeeng*, or *tiger-captain* — is still fresh.

## The Oeléebalang
At [[Blang Djeroeën|Blang Djeroeën]] lives the *oeléebalang* of Keureuto, [[Teukoe Bentara Keureuto|Teukoe Bentara Keureuto]], whom Broersma describes as *'een krachtig en kundig hoofd'* — *a powerful and capable chief.* He rules a large *landschap* and receives accordingly f 600 a month from the *landschapskas* in addition to f 125 for his car. He maintains a small lorry for the transport of persons and goods and fetches, for example, fish daily from the coast. His son attends the *Mulo*-school at [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]].

## The Five Unused Licences
There are also five licences in the *landschap* Keureuto which have not yet been opened — *'nog geen spa in den grond gestoken'* — though the dues are already owed. *'Met zulke bezoeken van «heet kapitaal» kan geen land zijn gediend'* — *With such visits of «hot capital» no land can be served.* The implication is that speculative land-licences, taken up by absentees who neither develop nor pay, are a curse to a *landschap*.

## The Rice-Plain
The western part of the *onderafdeeling*, in which the *landschap* Keureuto lies, is a fertile rice-plain. There one plants in the rain-months of the late year, and in April or May the harvest is done. Although the usual yield of 20 *picol* of dry *padi* per *bouw* is not exceeded, some export can take place from this part of Lho Soekon. Broersma reckoned with the chief that a couple consumes seven *picol* of *bras* (rice) a year; the calculation was real reckoning, for such quantities are expressed in Atjeh in *'bamboe'* — one *bamboe* being 2.5 *katti* of *bras*.
