**Lho' Seumawé / Lho'Seumawé** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Place in the afdeeling Noordkust van Atjeh; onderafdelingshoofdplaats.

## Description

Place in the [[afdeeling]] [[Noordkust van Atjeh]]; onderafdelingshoofdplaats. Standplaats of an [[Assistent-Resident]] from [[1878]]. Site of the surrender of [[Panglima Polém]] to [[Dr. H. Colijn|Kapitein Colijn]]. Has a landing field 7 km away donated free by the [[zelfbestuurder]].

## Significance

Lho' Seumawé / Lho'Seumawé is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
