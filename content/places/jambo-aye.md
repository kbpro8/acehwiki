---
title: Jambo Aye
tags: ["place", "aceh-proper"]
---

A river on the East Coast of [[Acheh]]. In the Hikayat [[Malem Diwa]], [[Lho' Sinibong]] is described as being on the river of Jambo Aye. In the sadati dialogue: 'If that suits thee not, my heart, go and stay at Jambu Aye. I will not stop at Jambu Aye; I fear that I may perish if a flood comes.'
