**Lampriet** was an Atjehnees kampong between [[Langkroek]] and [[Pinang]] on the right bank of the [[Atjeh-rivier]], described by Borel as "like an oasis in the sawah," which [[Majoor Romswinckel|majoor Romswinckel]] conquered without resistance on [[4 February]] [[1875]]. The new post, built to replace *Langkroek-oost*, gave command of the extensive terrain on the banks of the Kroeng Broek and was an important point close to the enemy's line-fortifications at Pinang.

## The conquest

The taking of Lampriet had been intended on [[28 September]] [[1874]] — the day of the [[Conquest of Langkroek (28 September 1874)|conquest of Langkroek]] — but was postponed. Borel criticises [[Kolonel Wiegand|overste Wiegand]] for not pressing on from Langkroek to Lampriet, which would have fallen almost without a blow. By February [[1875]] the Dutch 12 c.M. shells had criss-crossed the terrain, and the enemy was unlikely to defend it. On [[4 February]] [[1875]] [[Majoor Romswinckel|majoor Romswinckel]] marched with a column to take Lampriet, "like an oasis in the sawah." Romswinckel took it without resistance and immediately began a new fortification to replace the *Langkroek-oost* post. With Lampriet in Dutch hands, the eastern side of the Dutch perimeter at the [[Pedir-dijk]] was considerably strengthened; from the new post the Dutch could observe and shell the enemy's line-fortifications at Pinang and prevent the enemy from crossing the Kroeng Broek.

## In the stelling-Pel and after

Lampriet became one of the eastern-side posts of the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]]. By May [[1875]] the east side of the continuous line around [[Kota Radja|K. Radja]] ran from [[Blang Tjoet]] through [[Kota Alam]], [[Lemboe]] O. en N.O., Lampriet, [[Langkroek]] O., [[Lamara]] Z.O. en N.O., [[Tiban]] to [[Moesapi]]. Lampriet's exposure made it a frequent target of enemy harassment: in September [[1875]] a Dutch detachment south of K. Radja and at Lampriet was "in groot gevaar om afgesneden en in de pan gehakt te worden" — in great danger of being cut off and chopped to pieces. On [[26 November]] [[1875]] Lampriet was one of the chief points of the enemy's attack along the entire *ooster-linie*, an attack that cemented [[Generaal Pel|Pel]]'s conviction that the *afwachtende stelsel* had to be abandoned and that the great [[Pel's offensive against the VI, IV and IX Moekims (1875–1876)|winter offensive of 1875–1876]] had to be launched.
