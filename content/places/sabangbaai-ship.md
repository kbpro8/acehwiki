**Sabangbaai (ship)** (vessel) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Small ship of ~50 tons, built in 1903 at 'de Schelde' shipyard in Holland, crossed to NEI under its own power.

## Type

vessel

## Description

Small ship of ~50 tons, built in [[1903]] at [[Scheepsbouwmaatschappij 'de Schelde'|'de Schelde' shipyard]] in [[Leyden (Leiden)|Holland]], crossed to NEI under its own power. Plying the [[Sabang]]-[[Oelèë Lheuë]] connection (~3.5 hour crossing).

## Significance

Sabangbaai (ship) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
