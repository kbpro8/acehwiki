**Simaloer / Simeuloeë** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Island off the west coast of Atjeh; onderafdeeling of the afdeeling Westkust.

## Description

Island off the [[Seunagan|west coast]] of Atjeh; [[onderafdeeling]] of the [[afdeeling]] [[Westkust]]. On [[Simaloer]], the indigenous volkshoofden were reduced to subordinate positions by the Dutch, while the heads of foreigners (vreemdelingen) were made into zelfbestuurders.

## Significance

Simaloer / Simeuloeë is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
