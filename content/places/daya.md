---
title: Daya
aliases: ["Gle Putoih"]
tags: ["place", "north-east-coast"]
---

A territory to the south or west of the capital, associated with highland origins and danger. 'If that contents thee not, I take you farther still; go to Gle Putoih (in Daya) to plant pepper. I will not go to Gle Putoih, for the men of Daya are at enmity with (us) Achehnese.' (sadati dialogue). The guree (teacher) of the Panglima [[Meuseugit Raya]] 'is Teungku Gam, said to come from Daya.' Also, [[Teuku Uma]] 'passed some time at [[Rigaih]] and became master of [[Krueng Sabe]] without striking a blow.' After his treacherous act on the Hok Canton, 'the poet describes the expeditions of T. Uma with the imprisoned nyonya.' Finally: 'the poet details at some length the exploits of this hero till his return to Daya.'
