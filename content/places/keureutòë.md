**Keureutòë** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Landschap in Lho' Soekon with a peculiar administrative structure.

## Description

[[landschap|Landschap]] in [[Lho'Soekon|Lho' Soekon]] with a peculiar administrative structure. The oldest core is partly under the [[zelfbestuurder]] with the title [[Teukoe Tjhi']], partly under four [[peutoeha peuët]]. Includes [[oelèëbalang tjoet]] areas (young settlements, pepper gardens) and six onderhoorige landschappen each under an [[oelèëbalang]].

## Significance

Keureutòë is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
