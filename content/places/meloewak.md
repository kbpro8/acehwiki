**Meloewak** (military bivak) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Military bivak between Blang Kedjerèn and Koeta Tjané at ~600 m altitude; small pasanggrahan with bedding.

## Type

military [[Bivak|bivak]]

## Description

Military [[Bivak|bivak]] between [[Blang Kedjerèn]] and [[Koeta Tjané]] at ~600 m altitude; small [[Pasanggrahan|pasanggrahan]] with bedding.

## Significance

Meloewak is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
