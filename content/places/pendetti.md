**Pendetti** was the strong enemy fortification opposite [[Lemboe]] on the [[Atjeh-rivier]] and the Dutch intermediate post on the road to [[Longbatta]], discovered by the Dutch when an Atjehneesche *vlag* (flag) was seen flying above a new *benteng* at the height of [[Lampoe Idjoe]] upstream on [[15 May]] [[1874]]. From the high observatory at [[Pakan Atjeh]] the Dutch shelled the Pendetti works, and the Atjehneezen were unable to "binnen hunne werken te Pendetti geen stand houden" — to hold their ground in the Pendetti works — under the 12 c.M. fire.

## The enemy fortification

The Pendetti works were among the most elaborate Atjehnees fortifications east of the [[Kraton of Atjeh|Kraton]], built on the right bank opposite the strongly fortified kampong of [[Lemboe]] in the months after the [[Fall of the Kraton (24 January 1874)|fall of the Kraton]] while the Dutch stood idle under [[Jan van Swieten|van Swieten]]'s *afwachtende houding*. The works lay about 1,500 *pas* (paces) from the Kraton and consisted of a continuous *benteng* line with several armed salients, linked to the enemy's eastern line at [[Lampoe Oek]] and [[Longbatta]]. On [[15 May]] [[1874]] the Dutch discovery of an Atjehneesche *vlag* waving above the new *benteng* triggered the first bombardment of Pendetti: the artillerie at [[Pakan Atjeh]] immediately opened fire, the shots being *gecorrigeerd* from the observatory. The uitwerking was "moeilijk na te gaan," but it soon became clear that the Atjehneezen could not hold their ground in the works.

## In the stelling-Pel

After the [[Battle of Longbatta (31 December 1874)|Longbatta operation of 31 December 1874]] the strong stelling at Pendetti opposite Lemboe was abandoned by the enemy, who had streamed east to face [[Majoor Romswinckel|Romswinckel]]'s feint attack. On [[4 January]] [[1875]] [[Generaal Pel|Pel]] with two columns reconnoitred and cleared the terrain south of Pendetti and the kampongs of [[Lampoe Idjoe]], [[Lampoe Oek]], [[Longbatta]] and [[Blang Tjoet]]; no resistance was offered and the houses were burned. Pendetti was then converted into a Dutch intermediate post on the road to Longbatta. With the [[Conquest of Blang Tjoet (18 January 1875)|conquest of Blang Tjoet]] of [[18 January]] [[1875]] and the new post 400–500 paces from the [[Longbatta-Mesigit]], transport between the Mesigit and Pendetti became practically safe. Pendetti then formed part of the eastern side of the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]], linking [[Kota Alam]] upstream to [[Lemboe]] and the [[Longbatta-Mesigit]].
