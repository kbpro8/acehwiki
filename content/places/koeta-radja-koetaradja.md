**Koeta Radja / Koetaradja** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Capital of the Atjeh gewest; named by the Atjehers for the Kraton.

## Description

Capital of the Atjeh [[gewest]]; named by the [[Atjehers]] for the [[Kraton of Aceh|Kraton]]. Heavily fortified after [[1874]]. Standplaats of the [[Assistent-Resident]] [[Groot-Atjeh]]. Site of the [[M.U.L.O.]], the [[Christelijk militair internaat]] ([[1929]]), the inheemsch internaat ([[1920]]), Amboneesche School, handelsklasse at [[Vervolgschool|vervolgschool]], [[Ambachtsleergang|ambachtsleergang]] at [[Sigli]] was separate. Gouverneurshuis where the [[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]] surrendered. Connected by rail to [[Lambaroe]] ([[1885]]) and by heirweg to [[Medan]].

## Significance

Koeta Radja / Koetaradja is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
