**Turkije (Sultan of Turkey)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. In 1868 Acehnese heads offered sovereignty of their realm to the Sultan of Turkey.

## Description

In [[1868]] Acehnese heads offered [[Suzerainty question|sovereignty]] of their realm to [[Sultan of Turkey (16th century)|the Sultan of Turkey]].

## Significance

Turkije (Sultan of Turkey) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
