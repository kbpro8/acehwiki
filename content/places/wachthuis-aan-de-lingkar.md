**The Wachthuis aan de Lingkar** (Lingkar watchhouse; the *blokhuis* near the Kali Lengkar or Lingkar river) was one of the watch-posts of the Dutch [[Geconcentreerde Stelling|concentrated line]] around [[Kuta Raja|Kota Radja]] as completed on [[1 March]] [[1885]], included in the official post list handed down by the Koloniaal Verslag. On [[25 August]] [[1885]] the blockhouse near the Kali Lengkar was fired upon by the Atjehnese, who wounded three of the Dutch (see [[Guerilla raids inside the concentration line (1885)]]).
