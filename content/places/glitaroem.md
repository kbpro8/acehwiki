**Glitaroem** was a mountain pass in the interior of [[Acheh]] that formed a critical route for the Dutch expeditionary force in [[1875]]–[[1876]]. Columns under [[Major Diepenheim]] and [[Major B. Mekern]] traversed the pass to enter the IX Moekims territory.

### Military Use
The pass was described as the “eastern exit” leading to the kampong of [[Daroe]]. Its steep, marshy surroundings made movement difficult for infantry, artillery, and cavalry.
