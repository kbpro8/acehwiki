**Millingen** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[H. N. A. Swart]]

As a sergeant Swart once met King [[Willem III]], during a summer detachment in the camp of [[Millingen]]. Commanding a forage column to [[Apeldoorn]] on a sweltering day, he had unbuttoned his tunic and unfastened his belt when he recognised the King approaching on foot.
