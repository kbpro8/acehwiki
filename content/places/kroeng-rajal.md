**Kroeng Rajal** was a coastal area on the North Coast of [[Acheh|Atjeh]] whose population, together with that of [[Ladoeng]], was known from of old for its unbridled robber spirit and was punished by Dutch troops on [[19 June]] [[1879]], without any loss on the Dutch side (see [[Punishment of Ladoeng and Kroeng Rajal (19 June 1879)]]).
