---
title: Chot Paya
tags: ["place", "aceh-proper"]
---

A location in [[Acheh]]. 'At Chot Paya such students as desired to bring their proficiency in reciting the Quran to a higher level than could be attained in the village schools, assembled under the guidance of [[Teungku Deuruih]], a man of South Indian origin.'
