**Koeala Daja** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[C. A. Snell]]

**C. A. Snell** — *Ridder van de [[Militaire Willemsorde]]* — was one of [[W. Muurling]]'s two well-known Atjeh lieutenants in the [[Gajò Loeös]], and the officer who on [[5 August]] [[1914]] destroyed the united bands of [[Nja' Ali]] and [[Nja' Asém]] at [[Koeala Daja]] in the sub-division [[Tjalang]]. He was later pensioned as a colonel.

### From [[Teukoe Mamat]]

Through T. Mamat's intervention seven men of the Lhong band reported themselves before the end of July [[1914]]; Nja' Asém apparently considered this too high a toll and fled with his men to [[Daja]] to join the band of [[Nja' Ali]]. Both band-heads were killed by lieutenant [[C. A. Snell|Snell]] at [[Koeala Daja]] on [[5 August]].

### From [[Lhong]]

The murder, committed by seven persons of the *landschap* who fled to the mountains with two of the overseer's sporting guns, was for Swart "a very sharp and painful disappointment indeed — after the attack at [[Daja]], now murder and band-formation in quiet [[Groot Atjeh]] as well." The plain of the IV Moekims Lhong was about three hours' walk broad, backed by an inhospitable complex of limestone rocks, outliers of the [[Boekit Barisan]], in which a band could hold out only in a few caves. [[H. J. Schmidt]]'s investigation traced the affair to [[Nja' Asém]] and behind him to [[Teukoe Mamat]]; the band was destroyed at [[Koeala Daja]] on [[5 August]] [[1914]] and the last nine members submitted on [[18 August]].
