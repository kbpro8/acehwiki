**Westkust (West Coast of Aceh)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Region of Teukoe Oemar's origin; later theatre of Teukoe Banta's, Teukoe Bin Blang Pidië's, Radja Batoe's submissions, and of 1926 Bakongan uprising.

## Description

Region of [[Teukoe Oemar]]'s origin; later theatre of [[Teukoe Banta]]'s, [[Teukoe Bin Blang Pidië]]'s, Radja Batoe's submissions, and of [[1926]] [[Bakóngan uprising (1925–1927)|Bakongan uprising]]. [[Société Financière]] and H.V.A. active there.

## Significance

Westkust (West Coast of Aceh) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
