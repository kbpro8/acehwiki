**Wòjla** was the *[[landschap]]* of the [[Meulaboh]] country, divided into [[Boven Wòjla]] and [[Beneden Wòjla]], which became under its bivouac commander at [[Koeala Bhèë]] a model district of the [[Westkust van Atjeh|west coast]].

In [[1908]] an official report recorded that in the Wòjla district politics had been "gemodderd". In [[1909]] a mobile *[[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|marechaussée]]* column under [[F. Darlang|Darlang]] temporarily occupied the mouth of the Wòjla, and the pacification of the Boven Wòjla under [[Scheurleer]] from [[Tanòh Mirah]] produced the submission of two hundred resistance men. In April [[1910]] the band of [[Tgk. Imeum Sradeuë']] was surprised in Wòjla and [[T. Adam]] fell on the Beneden Wòjla. By [[1918]] Wòjla had so flourished that [[H. J. Schmidt]] "zelfs hoofden uit andere streken 'ter instructie' nu en dan naar Wòjla dirigeerde": the roads were exceptionally well maintained, the *[[gampong]]s* excelled in order and cleanliness, and tax collection and school attendance left nothing to be desired.
