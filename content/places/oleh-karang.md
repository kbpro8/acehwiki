**Oleh Karang** was an Acehnese settlement, agricultural locality, and fortified redoubt situated near [[Kotta Alam]] along the Aceh River plain in modern Banda Aceh, Indonesia.

## Combat History in 1875

During the Dutch occupation of Kotta Radja in [[1874]] and [[1875]], Oleh Karang formed a contested no-man's-land between Dutch garrisons and Acehnese forces. 

As recorded in *Atjeh gedurende de tweede expeditie en later* ([[1876]]), Acehnese fighters repeatedly ambushed Dutch supply convoys and patrols between [[Kotta Alam]] and Oleh Karang throughout late [[1875]], causing continuous casualties and compelling General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]] to open his offensive winter campaign on [[26 December]] [[1875]].

## Role as Logistics Depot (1884–1885)

During the [[Concentration Decision of August 1884|concentration period]] of [[1884]]–[[1885]], [[Oleh-Karang]] served as a depôt van materialen (materials depot) supporting the construction of new posts at [[Boekit-Karang]], [[Roempit]], and [[Tjot-Iri]]. Roads were built from Oleh-Karang to these new positions, and materials were staged there before being moved to the construction sites. The use of Oleh-Karang as a logistical hub illustrated the systematic approach to implementing the concentration: materials from evacuated posts and northern stations were efficiently redistributed to support new construction.
