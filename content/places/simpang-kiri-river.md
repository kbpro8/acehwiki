**Simpang-Kiri (river)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. River in the onderafdeeling Singkel, on which the place Roendeng is located.

## Description

River in the [[onderafdeeling]] [[Singkel]], on which the place [[Roendeng]] is located.

## Significance

Simpang-Kiri (river) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
