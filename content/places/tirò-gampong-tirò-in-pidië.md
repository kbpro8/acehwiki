**Tirò (gampong Tirò in Pidië)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Gampong in Pidië known for many legal scholars (wet-geleerden) and schools for the holy sciences; also a vrijplaats (sanctuary) for those involved in disputes.

## Description

[[Gampong]] in [[Pidië]] known for many legal scholars (wet-geleerden) and schools for the holy sciences; also a vrijplaats (sanctuary) for those involved in disputes. Origin of the [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] Tirò lineage; eldest [[Oelama|oelama]] titled [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] Tjhi' di Tirò.

## Significance

Tirò (gampong Tirò in Pidië) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
