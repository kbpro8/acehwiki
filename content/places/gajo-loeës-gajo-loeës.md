**Gajo Loeës / Gajo-Loeës** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Landschap/onderafdeeling in the afdeeling Oostkust van Atjeh.

## Description

[[landschap|Landschap]]/[[onderafdeeling]] in the [[afdeeling]] [[Oostkust van Atjeh]]. Inhabited by the [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]] people with a genealogisch (clan-based) bestuursstelsel built around the [[Blah|blah]] (clan). Still under a [[kapitein-fungeerend-controleur]]. Site of the road extending from [[Koeta Tjané|Koeta Tjane]]; potential vliegveld being sought.

## Significance

Gajo Loeës / Gajo-Loeës is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
