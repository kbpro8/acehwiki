---
title: Gampong Jawa
tags: [place]
---

A stronghold in Acheh defended by the four sons of the pretender Poteu Jeumaloy, and later besieged successfully by Pochut Muhamat in the 18th-century succession war recorded in the Hikayat Pochut Muhamat.
