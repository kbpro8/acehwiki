**Malaboeh** (modern Meulaboh) was a coast-state on the west coast of [[Acheh|Atjeh]], one of the principal centres of Dutch authority on the Westkust and the chief place of the assistant-residency of the [[Westkust van Atjeh|Westkust van Atjeh]] under the [[Establishment of Civil Government in Aceh (6 April 1881)|administrative reorganisation of March 1881]]. The Dutch established a fortified post at Malaboeh in [[April 1877|April 1877]], but the post was repeatedly attacked by the population under the hostile eldest son of the radja, and expeditions were necessary in [[May 1877|May 1877]] and [[June 1877|June 1877]] to punish the kampong [[Merbau|Merbau]] and to defend the establishment.

## The Establishment of the Post, April 1877

In the same month [[April 1877|April 1877]] as the [[Bombardment of Loöng (April 1877)|expedition to Loöng]], Dutch ships arrived at Malaboeh to set up, as had long before been determined, a fortified post. Hardly were they engaged there, however, when they were exposed to repeated attacks by the population under the hostile eldest son of the radja. Therefore in [[May 1877|May 1877]] an expedition was also sent thither to punish the kampong [[Merbau|Merbau]], the gathering-place of the malicious, as also happened on [[11 May]] [[11 May]], notwithstanding a very brave resistance by the Atjehers, who even overwhelmed a section of marines in the midst of the destruction and cut them to pieces. As the controller thought to settle the matter further by negotiation, the expedition returned, but in [[June 1877|June 1877]] a new one proved necessary to defend the establishment against the continual attacks. By its help various kampongs were again punished, and a new fortification was laid out at the mouth of the Malaboeh river, which however also later still continued to suffer from hostilities.

## The Administrative Reorganisation, March 1881

In accordance with the [[Establishment of Civil Government in Aceh (6 April 1881)|administrative reorganisation of March 1881]], Malaboeh became the chief place of the assistant-residency of the [[Westkust van Atjeh|Westkust van Atjeh]], one of the three assistant-residencies of the gewest Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden. To the landscapes belonging to this afdeeling, however, self-government was left; only in [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] was direct Dutch administration introduced.
