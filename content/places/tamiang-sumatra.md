**Tamiang** is a region on the east coast of [[Sumatra]] that bordered the sultanate of [[Atjeh]]. In [[1893]] a Dutch marine force under **Heuter** was dispatched to the area to suppress a cross‑border insurgency that had been harassing Dutch positions in Atjeh.

The operation, recorded as the **expedition against Tamiang**, involved a small naval contingent that secured the coastal town of [[Kuala Tamiang]] and conducted patrols inland.
