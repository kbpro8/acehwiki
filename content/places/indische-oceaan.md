**Indische Oceaan** (ocean) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Ocean visible from the Westkust viewpoints, especially Glé Geuroctèë.

## Type

ocean

## Description

Ocean visible from the [[Westkust]] viewpoints, especially [[Glé Geuroctèë]].

## Significance

Indische Oceaan is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
