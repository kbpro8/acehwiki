**Keureutòë (Keureutòë-streek)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Region long a broeinest (hotbed) of ungerechtigheid, where the oelamas sat and inflamed hatred against the Kompeuni and resistance.

## Description

Region long a broeinest (hotbed) of ungerechtigheid, where the oelamas sat and inflamed hatred against the [[Kompeuni]] and [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]]. Influence of [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] di [[Mata Ië]] extended beyond it deep into [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]]-land. [[Tgk. di Barat|Teungkoe di Barat]] was son-in-law of [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] di [[Mata Ië]]. [[Pang Nanggròë]]'s stepson was proclaimed [[oelèëbalang]] of [[Keureutòë]]. Under [[Gouverneur Van Daalen|Van Daalen]] / [[Gouverneur Swart|Swart]] the disorganised [[Keureutòë]]-streek was severely dealt with by Captain [[Christoffel]]. Oelèëbalangsfamilie of [[Keureutòë]] reconciled by [[Gouverneur Swart|Swart]]. On the eve of general revolt under [[Gouverneur Swart|Swart]]. Underhoofden-actie of [[Keureutòë]] onderhoofden refusing the [[zelfbestuurder]] of [[Tjoenda]].

## Significance

Keureutòë (Keureutòë-streek) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
