An island in the Babah Aroh (the strait between the islands and the mainland). The saint Tuan di Pulo Bunta has his tomb here.

## Source
*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
