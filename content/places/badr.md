In the Hikayat [[Raja Bada]] (LXIII): 'The Malays have personified the village of Badr, in the neighbourhood of which Muhammad gained his first victory, as a beautiful prince named Badar. The khandaq or canal which the Prophet had dug round [[Medina]] to defend himself against the attack of the men of Mekka, they have converted into the father of that prince, under the names Hondok, Handak etc.' In the Achehnese version: 'Raja Handa' or Keunda' with his son Bada make war upon the Prophet and his followers.'
