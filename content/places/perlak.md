**Perlak** (Peureulak) was the landscape of the East Coast of [[Acheh|Atjeh]] within which lay the petroleum field of [[Peureula]] — the 'Indische markt' (Indian market) where oil had been won from four dug wells since time immemorial — and which from the 1890s became the scene of Atjeh's petroleum industry, exploited by the [[Hollandsche Perlak Maatschappij]], the [[Zuid Perlak Maatschappij]] and finally the [[Bataafsche Petroleum Maatschappij|Bataafsche Petroleum Maatschappij]], with the oil city [[Rantau Pandjang]] in its interior.

## The landscape and its oil

In the landscape Perlak, on Atjeh's East Coast, petroleum wells had been found which were exploited "in native fashion"; Langhout gives the área the name **Peureula**, where an Indian market had existed for centuries and the petroleum won from four dug pits was brought to market — the source of the mineral oil which the [[Vereenigde Oost-Indische Compagnie|East India Company]] in the eighteenth century obtained from the Sultan of Atjeh and which was highly prized in *patria* as a medicament. The [[Hollandsche Perlak Maatschappij]] began its activity here a few years after the [[Bataafsche Petroleum Maatschappij|Bataafsche]] had founded [[Pangkalan Brandan]] over the border in [[Langkat]] ([[1890]]); the Zuid Perlak Maatschappij transferred its works to the Bataafsche some years before [[1923]], and the petroleum concession in Zuid Perlak, exploited by "de Koninklijke", was the foremost mineral concession of the province under Governor [[H. N. A. Swart|Swart]]. The [[Koninklijke Paketvaart Maatschappij|K.P.M.]] called at the Perlak coast, and agents and branches of Medan houses penetrated the district.

## Timber and estates

The chiefs of [[Langsar]] and Perlak had early conceded exploitation areas to the firm [[Handelsmaatschappij Deli-Atjeh|Nolte & Haas]], and the N.V. Handelmaatschappij "Deli-Atjeh" received in [[1905]] extensive wood-cutting concessions in Perlak among the East Coast landscapes (Radja Moeda, Radja Toewa, Manjapahit, [[Birim Rajeu]], Birim Pontong, Bajan, Simpang Anas and Perlak). The [[Société Financière des Caoutchoucs]]' estate-district lay in neighbouring [[Tamiang|Temyang]]; the whole coastal belt was served by the [[Atjeh Tram]] reaching [[Besitang]] in [[1915]]–[[1916]].
