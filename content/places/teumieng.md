The easternmost limit of [[Acheh]]. '[[Teumieng]], vulg. Tamiang' is the East Coast limit of [[Acheh]] referenced in the index. The island of [[Sampoe]] lies near Tamiang.

**Teumieng** (Malay: *Tamiang*) was the eastern boundary point of the [[Aceh Sultanate]] on the East Coast. According to [[C. Snouck Hurgronje]], the limits of the whole Acehnese kingdom were placed by the Acehnese themselves at **Teumieng** (Tamiang) on the East Coast — though this referred to the outer kingdom, not to [[Great Acheh]] proper, which was far more restricted.

Teumieng lay well beyond [[Pidie]] (Pedir) and the other coastal dependencies, marking the furthest reach of Acehnese sovereignty along the northeastern Sumatran coast. The territory between Great Acheh and Teumieng encompassed the various coast states that acknowledged the [[Aceh Sultanate|Sultan's]] nominal suzerainty.
