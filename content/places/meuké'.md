**Meuké'** is a subject named in *[[Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Het Pacificeerende Bestuurswerk 1908-1918]]

In [[1909]] Swart was authorised to dispose of 6,000 guilders for provision, by way of interest-free advances, for the first needs of persons in need who had come in submission. He was also authorised to pay 4,000 guilders to the head of [[Peudada]] ([[Bireuën]]) as compensation for the burning of two houses belonging to him and the loss of his goods during a raid on the *keudé* of Peudada by ill-disposed persons in September. In the same way 5,000 guilders were paid to [[T. Radja Tjoet]], *oelèëbalang* of [[Meuké']] ([[Tapa' Toean]]), for the loss of his possessions in a raid on his *koeta* in February.

### From [[1909]]

**[[1909]]** saw continued heavy fighting in [[Keureutòë]] — 269 opponents killed, including seventeen band-heads and seven Teungkoes, eleven prisoners including [[Tgk. di Reubèë]], and seventy-two submissions — and in the [[Meergebied]], where 132 resistance men fell and where on [[24 December]] a group of infantry was surprised at [[Tjampoeron]], the captured carbines forming the [[Telongbende]]. In February [[H. J. Schmidt]] found [[Keudjroeën Oebit]] in the [[Tangsé]] valley. On the [[Westkust van Atjeh|west coast]] [[A. Geertsema Beckeringh]] at last obtained sufficient means of power. In December Swart gave Schmidt the special commission against the [[Tirò Teungkoes]]. On [[31 March]] [[1909]] Swart was extraordinarily promoted *Kolonel*; on [[2 September]] [[1909]] he was made Commander of the [[Militaire Willemsorde]] for [[Onderneming tegen Soembawa (1908)|Soembawa]]; in May [[1909]] Knight in the [[Order of the Netherlands Lion]]. He was authorised to dispose of 6,000 guilders in interest-free advances to needy persons who had submitted, and of compensations to the head of [[Peudada]] and to [[T. Radja Tjoet]] of [[Meuké']].
