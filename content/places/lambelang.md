**Lambelang** was the point on the high road to [[Lampisang]] in [[Groot-Atjeh]] where the Dutch advance of [[24 May]] [[1896]] met a barrier during the [[Three-day battle in the Beradin pass (23-25 May 1896)|three-day battle in the Beradin pass]]. [[P. H. R. Beuming]] records that *de groote weg naar Lampisang was bij Lambelang versperd door een kunstig aangelegde zware borstwering, die na een hevig verzet genomen werd* — the high road to Lampisang was barred at Lambelang by an artfully constructed heavy breastwork, which was taken after violent resistance.
