In the Hikayat Gajah Tujoh Ulee (Seven-Headed Elephant): 'Sa'doymanan, son of To [[Suloyman]], Raja of Teuleukin, wins his four princesses.' The hero comes from this fictional kingdom and his adventures begin with rescuing princess Meureundam Hiinga.
