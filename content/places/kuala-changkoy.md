---
title: Kuala Changkoy
tags: ["place", "aceh-proper", "river"]
---

A coastal location in [[Acheh]]. The saint Tuan Siblah Langet ('saint of the direction of the sky') has his tomb here, lying unusually in an East-West orientation rather than the prescribed North-South.
