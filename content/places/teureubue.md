A gampong in [[Pidie]] from which the heretical mystic [[Teungku Teureubue]] took his name. His real name was Muhamat Sa'it, abbreviated to It. He gained great celebrity as a teacher of unorthodox mysticism (eleumee sale) about 30-40 years before Snouck's time. Men and women crowded in hundreds to listen to his teaching. He was exterminated by the orthodox chiefs at the instigation of the ulamas, but his disciple [[Teungku Gade]] (also known as Teungku di Geudong or Teungku Teupin Raya) succeeded him; his tomb in the centre of Teupin Raya is surrounded by a thick and lofty wall.
