**The Madura** was a Netherlands warship in Acehnese waters in [[1893]] which came to the assistance of the steamer *[[Raijah (steamer)|Raijah]]* after her seizure by pirates on the night of [[20 July]] [[1893]].

[[P. H. R. Beuming]] records that after the sea-rovers had departed, the European engineers — who had saved themselves by hiding in the engine-room during the massacre — came out and *die door vuurpijlen noodseinen gaven aan het in de nabijheid zijnde oorlogschip Madura, dat oogenblikkelijk hulp verleende* — gave distress signals by rockets to the warship *Madura*, which was in the neighbourhood and immediately rendered assistance.
