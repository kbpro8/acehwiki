**Garot (Sigli)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Place near Sigli; site of an old wooden bridge with carved decorations shown in book's photo.

## Description

Place near [[Sigli]]; site of an old wooden bridge with carved decorations shown in book's photo.

## Significance

Garot (Sigli) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
