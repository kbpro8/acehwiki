**Ketappen Doem** was an Acehnese village settlement, agricultural crossing, and fortified earthwork situated in the lowlands near Kotta Radja in the [[Aceh Sultanate]], modern Banda Aceh, Indonesia.

## Combat in 1875-1876

During the military operations of [[1875]] and [[1876]], Ketappen Doem served as a defensive redoubt for Acehnese fighters opposing Dutch patrols between [[Pekan Badak]] and the interior mukims. 

Troops under General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]] engaged in repeated skirmishes around Ketappen Doem during the opening phase of [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]] in December [[1875]].
