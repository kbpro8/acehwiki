**Mata Ië** (bathing place) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Idyllic bathing place near Koetaradja, at foot of a hill range from which crystal-clear water flows into two pools.

## Type

bathing place

## Description

Idyllic bathing place near [[Koetaradja]], at foot of a hill range from which crystal-clear water flows into two pools. Heavily used, especially on free days.

## Significance

Mata Ië is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
