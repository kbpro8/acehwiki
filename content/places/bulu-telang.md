**Bulu Telang** is an oil-field locality on the north-eastern coast of [[Sumatra]], in the residency of the East Coast of Sumatra immediately to the south of the [[Acheh|Atjeh]] border, recorded in [[Van Bemmelen's The Geology of Indonesia (1949)|Van Bemmelen's *Geology of Indonesia*]] ([[1949]]) as one of the producing fields of the North Sumatra oil province in the [[1940]]–[[1941]] period. The Bulu Telang field was operated by the [[B.P.M.]] (Bataafsche Petroleum Maatschappij, the Royal Dutch subsidiary), and its production of 919 tons of 1000 kg in [[1940]] and 900 tons in [[1941]] made it one of the smaller B.P.M.-operated fields of the North Sumatra oil province.

## Production figures

For the full North-Sumatran production table see the entry on the [[East Atjeh oil block]]. The Bulu Telang field's production of 919 tons in [[1940]] and 900 tons in [[1941]] was small by the standards of the late-colonial East Indian oil industry — comparable to the [[Telaga Said]] field (1,076 tons in [[1940]]) and considerably smaller than the [[Aru Bay|Aru Bay]] field (55,924 tons in [[1940]]). The decline from 919 tons to 900 tons (–2.1 %) is consistent with the gradual depletion of a small mature field. The Bulu Telang field is, like the Telaga Said field, part of the older generation of B.P.M.-operated fields in the Langkat–Deli–Aru Bay region of the East Coast of Sumatra, and its inclusion in the North-Sumatran oil province reflects the geographic contiguity of that region with the Atjeh oil fields to the north.

## Significance

Bulu Telang is significant for the encyclopedia of [[Acheh]] history because it is part of the [[B.P.M.]]-operated North-Sumatran petroleum complex of which the Atjeh fields — the [[East Atjeh oil block|East Atjeh block]] and the [[Tamiang oil block|Tamiang block]] — are the northern component. The Bulu Telang field's small mature production is typical of the older generation of B.P.M. fields in the East-Coast-of-Sumatra region, and the field accordingly illustrates the mature-producer context within which the Atjeh-proper oil fields were developed in the late colonial period.
