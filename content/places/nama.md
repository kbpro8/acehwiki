title: Nama
aliases: ["the saint with the wrong name"]
tags: ["place", "aceh-proper"]
---

situated at the foot of a mountain in Lam Tisang. His body is supposed to be on top of the hill while his organ of generation alone is in the actual tomb. He is the patron of barren couples who want children.

## Source
*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
