**Gloempang Doea** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[The resistance in Atjeh 1908–1912]]

[[1911]] brought the great improvement: "the political situation remained favourable" ("the political situation remained favourable") became a customary opening of the monthly report. The co-operation of the population with the *Kompeuni* ("the Company (the Dutch power)") against the *[[djahat]]s* — troublesome to the population too — produced incidents such as that of March: "at the beginning of March the band of [[Pang Bin Dé]] of [[Gloempang Doea]] carried off two boys by force." A few nights later the band tried through those two boys to forage in the said *gampong* but was driven off by the inhabitants, while the boys were freed from its hands.
