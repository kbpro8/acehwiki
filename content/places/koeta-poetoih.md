**Koeta Poetoih** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Teungkoe di Tjòt Plieng]]

After the fall of Garot on [[1 June]] [[1898]], [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|van Heutsz]] crossed and re-crossed [[Pidië]], and "the stubbornly defended stockades [[Koeta Poetoih]] of Teungkoe di Tjòt Plieng were taken."

### From [[Van Heutsz's Pidië campaign (1898)]]

Van Heutsz crossed and re-crossed Pidië. The stubbornly defended *[[benteng]]s* [[Koeta Poetoih]] of [[Teungkoe di Tjòt Plieng]] were taken; the bands of the other resistance leaders were relentlessly hunted; T. Oemar had to withdraw to the mountains and chose as his residence the unreachable [[Tangsé]].

### From [[benteng]]

At the [[Attack on the Boekit Roembia (11 June 1890)|Boekit Roembia]] Swart "sprang over the parapet into the stronghold* ("fortified stockade")" and was at once shot through the arm. In [[1898]] [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|van Heutsz]] took the stubbornly defended *bentengs* [[Koeta Poetoih]] of [[Teungkoe di Tjòt Plieng]]. [[I. Feenstra]] recalled in [[1918]] that in the days of the older generals "there was much shooting, and stockades, cannon and barbed wire were needed." At [[Lam Njong]] a busy *pasar* cattle market later arose on the site of the former *benteng*.
