**Lho Nga / Lho NNa** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Landing field 14 km from Koeta Radja.

## Description

Landing field 14 km from [[Koeta-Radja|Koeta Radja]].

## Significance

Lho Nga / Lho NNa is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
