**Weesberg** (mountain) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Mountain near the Goudberg, on whose slopes the road descends to the coastal plain.

## Type

mountain

## Description

Mountain near the [[Goudberg]], on whose slopes the road descends to the coastal plain. Waterscheidingspunt between [[Goudberg]] and Weesberg is a barren [[Blang|blang]] area.

## Significance

Weesberg is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
