**Tjap Octoe** was an Atjehnees kampong in the [[III Moekims]] sector conquered on [[14 February|14]]–[[15 February]] [[1876]] in [[Generaal Pel|Pel]]'s three-colonne operation against the III Moekims, whose *sterke benteng* (strong fortification) with artillery was only taken "met veel moeite en opoffering" — with much effort and sacrifice. Its losses, combined with those of the [[Atoeh catastrophe (13 February 1876)|Atoeh catastrophe]], were enough to make Pel decide not to push further eastward.

## The conquest

After the [[Atoeh catastrophe (13 February 1876)|Atoeh catastrophe]] of [[13 February]] [[1876]] and the conquest of [[Pango]] the same day, Pel pressed on. Meroe, Lampermej and Tjap Octoe were bemachtigd after *hevigen tegenstand* on [[14 February|14]]–[[15 February]] [[1876]]. At Tjap Octoe the troepen stuitten on a *sterke benteng* with artillery, *krachtig verdedigd*, only taken "met veel moeite en opoffering" — with much effort and sacrifice. The enemy had clearly prepared the defence in depth: artillery in the *benteng*, infantry in the boschrand around it, and a network of *bamboe-doeri* (thorn-bamboo) entanglements that channelled the Dutch assault into killing-grounds. The storming of Tjap Octoe was therefore among the hardest fights of the [[Pel's offensive against the VI, IV and IX Moekims (1875–1876)|winter offensive of 1875–1876]]. Borel's point is that the Atjehneezen could not withstand a well-planned combined colonne attack — but they could make each Dutch victory cost.

## The strategic decision

The losses at Tjap Octoe were enough to make Pel decide not to push further eastward but to designate [[Pango]] as the *uiterste steunpunt* of the new linie toward [[Kwala Gigieng]] — judged *voldoende*. The decision was, Borel suggests, the right one: the new linie from [[Pango]] to [[Kwala Gigieng]] and the [[Kroeng Tjoet]] mouth would, with the post on the island of [[Kota Pohama]] in the lagune, complete the isolation of [[Groot-Atjeh]] from the sea on the north-east side. From Pango Pel marched northward on [[22 February]] [[1876]] via Lamkapang, Loeëng Iek, Poeklat and Oeleh Karang to the kampong Pinang — found abandoned, "in den rug genomen" — and on to the [[Kroeng Tjoet]] and his death at [[Lamjoeng]] on [[24 February]] [[1876]]. [[Luitenant-kolonel Engel|Lt.-kol. Engel]] then continued the march to Kwala Gigieng on [[26 February]] [[1876]], and the line was closed.
