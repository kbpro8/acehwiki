**Altenklingen plantation** was a sugar plantation in the interior of [[Acheh]] that was visited by Dutch forces during the December 19–25 expedition. The Dutch ships “[[Zeven Provinciën]]” and “[[St Eustatius]]” anchored nearby, and the plantation was found largely intact, though later reports indicated that rebels had set fire to nearby plantations such as [[Landdsroon]] and [[Beresteyn]].

## Role in the Campaign
Altenklingen served as a temporary post for Dutch troops after they cleared the surrounding area of rebel activity. Its location near the [[Cimbia creek]] made it a strategic waypoint for riverine movements.
