**Seulawaih Agam** — the *Goudberg* or *Gold Mountain* — is the greater of the two mountains called *Seulawaih* (the man and the woman), which rise in the middle of the valley of [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]]. The smaller is the [[Seulawaih Inong|Seulawaih Inong]] — *the woman*. *Seulawaih Agam* means *the man*. The mountain is visible from the *landschap* [[Ndjong|Ndjong]] in the [[Pidië|afdeeling Pidië]], where it rises behind the *landschap* in a light mist. In everything of Nature the Atjehnese sees or imagines the presence and the distinction of the horse; *Seulawaih* itself means *belangrijk paard* — *important horse* — though Broersma does not gloss the name.
