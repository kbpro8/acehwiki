The fictional birthplace of [[Malem Diwa]], the immortal hero of the most popular Achehnese hikayat. '[[Malem Diwa]] was the son of Raja Tampo', a prince who ruled in the gampong of Piadah on the Krueng (river) of [[Pase]], commonly known as [[Pase]].' His mother was brought back to [[Malem Jawa]] (close to Piadah) after marriage. Historically, [[Teungku Tapa]] (an adventurer from Telog in the Gayo country who claimed to be [[Malem Diwa]]) was killed in [[1900]] in a skirmish with the Dutch troops near Piadah ([[Pase]]).

Referenced in sadati dialogue as a destination: 'If that please thee not, blessed little brother, I shall take thee to the country of Piadah. Brothers, I will not live in the country of Piadah; I will go further off; I will start now.' [[Idi]] (vulg. Edi) A mart on the East Coast. Sadati dialogue: 'If that suit thee not, little brother sadati, go yonder to [[Idi]] (vulg. Edi) that great mart. I will not live at [[Idi]], brothers; Teuku Nya' Paya is a raja who cannot be trusted.'
