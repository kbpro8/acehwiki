**Markey plantation** was a sugar plantation in the interior of [[Acheh]] that remained standing on [[26 December]] after the Dutch expedition. It became a gathering point for rebel forces under the leadership of [[Atta (bendehoofd)]] and for enslaved Africans who had fled the “Societeitsplantages”.

## Events
Rebels attempted to use the plantation as a base, but Dutch forces later secured it and reported the presence of women, children, and enslaved laborers who had been forced to join the rebels.
