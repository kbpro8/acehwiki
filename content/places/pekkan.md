**Pekkan** was a traditional Acehnese marketplace settlement and commercial gathering point located in the Aceh River valley near [[Kraton of Aceh]], in modern Banda Aceh, Indonesia.

## Economic and Military Role

Serving as a local distribution center for agricultural produce from the [[IV Moekims]] and [[VI Moekims]] ([[Sawah Cultivation and Supply in Aceh]]), Pekkan was fortified by Acehnese forces following the Dutch invasion of [[1873]]. 

During the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] and [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]], Dutch columns occupied Pekkan to control civilian grain trading and secure supply lines for the [[Intendance and Supply Service in Aceh]].
