**III Moekims Kerkoen** — written in Dutch as *III Moekims Kerkoen* and meaning "Three Mukims of Kerkoen" — was a region of [[Groot-Atjeh]] comprising three [[moekim|mukims]] situated along the right bank of the [[Atjehrivier]] in the eastern portion of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] theatre. As described in Kruisheer's *ATJEH '96*, the III Moekims Kerkoen had remained free of religious-warriors and was the home of the [[Dutch]] post of [[Lamsoet]] on the right bank of the Atjeh river. The kampongs along the right bank of the Atjehrivier of the III Moekims Kerkoen had been, from [[1878]] to [[1885]], a favourite residence of marauding bands, and the region was crossed by the [[Geconcentreerde Linie|Geconcentreerde Linie (Concentrated Line)]] of the Dutch defences.

## Description and Location

The III Moekims Kerkoen lay along the right bank of the [[Atjehrivier]] in the eastern part of the [[Sagi der XXII Moekims|XXII Moekims]] (technically) or as a separate mukim cluster between the [[Sagi der XXII Moekims|XXII Moekims]] and the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]]. The [[Dutch]] post of [[Lamsoet]] was situated on the right bank of the Atjeh river within this mukim, and served as the river crossing between [[Lambaroe]] and [[Ana' Galong]]. The [[Blokhuis Lamara]] was a blockhouse in or near this mukim. The *Binasa-Adjé* fortification was in the III Moekims Kerkoen. The mukim was crossed by the road from [[Lambaroe]] to [[Ana' Galong]] and was bounded on the east by the [[Kroeng Lingkar]] and on the south by the [[V Moekims Montassik]] and the sawah plain of [[Blang Bintang]].

## Historical Background

The III Moekims Kerkoen had been a centre of Achehnese marauding activity from [[1878]] to [[1885]], when the kampongs along the right bank of the [[Atjehrivier]] were a favourite residence of marauding bands. With the construction of the [[Geconcenteerde Linie|Geconcentreerde Linie (Concentrated Line)]] from [[1884]] onward, the III Moekims Kerkoen was enclosed within the Dutch defences, and the post of [[Lamsoet]] was built on the right bank of the Atjeh river to control the crossing. Despite this, the mukim remained a thoroughfare for *benden* moving between the [[Sagi der XXII Moekims|XXII Moekims]] and the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]]. The III Moekims Kerkoen had remained free of religious-warriors of [[Teungkoe di Tiro|Teungkoe di Tiroe]] — that is, the religious-war bands had not established a permanent presence in the mukim, distinguishing it from the more deeply hostile regions.

## Role in the 1896 Operations

The III Moekims Kerkoen was traversed by Dutch operations throughout the first semester of [[1896]]. On [[30 March]] [[1896]] the column of [[Majoor G. P. M. van der Noordaa|Van der Noordaa]] reached [[Lamsoet]] at 5:15 PM after the relief of the [[blokhuis|blokhuizen]] of [[Aloer Oe]] and [[Toei Selimbing]], and the wounded were re-bandaged there on [[31 March]] [[1896]] at 2 PM. On [[17 April]] [[1896]] [[Lamsoet]] was one of the [[buitenpost|buitenposten]] lifted by [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]'s column. On [[2 May]] [[1896]] the [[Diversie to Montassik (2 May 1896)|diversionary operation to Montassik]] re-talso the old post of [[Lamsoet]] without opposition and burned it; the [[Blokhuis Lamara]] nearby was the site of an ambush on [[25 April]] [[1896]]. On the morning of [[15 June]] [[1896]] the *versterkingen* of the III Moekims Kerkoen were found unoccupied by the Dutch preparatory to the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|tuchtiging of the V Moekims Montassik]]. The mukim was crossed again on [[17 June]] [[1896]] by returning Dutch columns.

## Significance

The III Moekims Kerkoen was a transit and crossing mukim rather than a centre of organised resistance. Its inclusion within the [[Geconcenteerde Linie|Geconcentreerde Linie (Concentrated Line)]] and the establishment of the [[Lamsoet]] post had pacified the mukim sufficiently that it remained free of *moslemin* in the period covered by *ATJEH '96*. The mukim's principal military significance was the [[Atjehrivier]] crossing at [[Lamsoet]] and its location athwart the routes between the [[Sagi der XXII Moekims|XXII Moekims]] and the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]].
