**T. Pura** (Tjoeka Pura) was a location on the East Coast of Sumatra, in the border area between the former [[Negara Sumatera Timur]] and the territory of [[Atjeh|Atjeh]]. On 22 May [[1950]], two Dutch plantation officials of the Société Financière des Caoutchoucs — [[B. de Jong]] and [[C. A. Zandbergen]] — were seriously wounded there by an Atjehse amokmaker.

## Historical context

T. Pura was situated in a zone where the East Coast plantation economy intersected with the Atjehnese hinterland. The [[T. Pura Amok Incident (22 May 1950)|incident of May 1950]] reflected the tensions that persisted in this border region during the early independence period.
