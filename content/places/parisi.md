Parisi appears as a fictional kingdom in several hikayats. In [[Hikayat Banta Ali]] (XXV): 'Kachah Peureudan, son of the king of [[Tambon Parisi]].' In [[Hikayat Chut Gambang China]] (XXXVI): 'Meureuhom Hiali, king of Parisi while on a hunting expedition lost his way and strayed into the country of the Jen Diu.' The story is set largely in Parisi and involves the kingdoms of [[Kawa Mandari]] and Ua'iron Banun. In [[Hikayat Pha Suasa]] (XXXIV): 'Banta Sulutan is the son, and Putroe Baren (Bahren) Miga the daughter of Raja Baren Nasi, king of [[Boreudat]] ([[Baghdad]])'; the story also involves Parisi as a setting.
