**Gajo Loeös** (high plateau) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. High plateau at ~800 m in the upper Tripa river basin, surrounded by 2000–3000 m mountains.

## Type

high plateau

## Description

High plateau at ~800 m in the upper [[Tripa-rivier|Tripa river]] basin, surrounded by [[2000]]–[[3000]] m mountains. Prosperous plain with cool climate, rich sawahgebied, promising for terpentijnwinning. Since [[1934]] administratively part of [[afdeeling]] [[Oostkust van Atjeh]] (no longer in [[Gajolanden (Gayo Lands)|Gajolanden]]).

## Significance

Gajo Loeös is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
