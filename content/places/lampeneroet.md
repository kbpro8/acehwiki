**Lampeneroet** was a Dutch military post in the southern section of the geconcentreerde stelling (concentrated position) in [[Groot-Atjeh]]. The post was established on [[21 February]] [[1885]].

## Location

Lampeneroet was situated in the southern section of the geconcentreerde stelling, between [[Lamrong]] and [[Ketapan-Doea]].

## Establishment

Lampeneroet was established and occupied on [[21 February]] [[1885]].

## Fortification (1885)

Lampeneroet was:

**Type:** A post with 75-man Infantry garrison
**Armament:** 2 × 12cm breech-loading guns and 1 Coehoorn mortar

## Infrastructure Connections

The post was connected to:
- The ceintuurtram (girdle tramway) system
- [[Lamrong]] and [[Ketapan-Doea]]
- Routes to [[Tjot-Goe]]

## Significance

Lampeneroet helped complete the southern section of the perimeter, providing coverage of routes toward [[Tjot-Goe]] and the southern interior.
