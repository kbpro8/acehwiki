**Lampeneroet** was a Dutch military post in the southern section of the geconcentreerde stelling (concentrated position) in [[Groot-Atjeh]]. The post was established on [[21 February]] [[1885]].

## Location

Lampeneroet was situated in the southern section of the geconcentreerde stelling, between [[Lamrong]] and [[Ketapan-Doea]].

## Establishment

Lampeneroet was established and occupied on [[21 February]] [[1885]].

## Fortification (1885)

Lampeneroet was:

**Type:** A post with 75-man Infantry garrison
**Armament:** 2 × 12cm breech-loading guns and 1 Coehoorn mortar

## Infrastructure Connections

The post was connected to:
- The ceintuurtram (girdle tramway) system
- [[Lamrong]] and [[Ketapan-Doea]]
- Routes to [[Tjot-Goe]]

## Significance

Lampeneroet helped complete the southern section of the perimeter, providing coverage of routes toward [[Tjot-Goe]] and the southern interior.

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

[[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] names Lampeneroet (also Lampernoet) one of the eighteen posts of the line of [[1885]], and registers against it two incidents of the same year: the destruction of the telephone connection between [[Lamrong]] and Lampeneroet (night of [[27 September|27]]-[[28 September]] [[1885]]), and the repair of the damaged tramway between [[Lamara]] and Lampeneroet by the engineers, in which two men were killed and four wounded, among them the engineer lieutenant [[Krol van der Hoek]] — by the Batavia telegram of [[16 December]] [[1885]] (see [[Guerilla raids inside the concentration line (1885)]]).
