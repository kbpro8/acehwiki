**Meuraxa** was an important coastal port settlement, mukim, and fortified beachhead of the [[Aceh Sultanate]] situated near the Aceh River estuary in modern Banda Aceh, Aceh Province, Indonesia.

## Description and Fortifications

Commanding the primary landing beaches of northern Aceh, Meuraxa was protected by extensive timber and coral earthworks ([[Coastal Fortifications in Aceh]]), notably Coastal Fortification No. 2, armed with artillery imported from [[Penang]] in [[1872]].

## Role in the Military Campaigns

On [[8 April]] [[1873]], the [[First Aceh Expedition]] under General [[J. H. R. Köhler]] debarked at Meuraxa under heavy surf. Acehnese defenders contested the landing, inflicting severe casualties before Dutch troops secured a beachhead bivouac.

During the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] in December [[1873]], the hereditary ruler of Meuraxa, [[Teuku Nek of Meuraxa]], defected to the [[Dutch East Indies Government]]. His submission allowed General [[J. van Swieten]] to occupy Meuraxa without opposition and turn it into a major logistical supply depot and hospital station ([[Medical Service in Aceh]]).
