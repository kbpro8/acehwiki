---
title: Lam Paloh
tags: ["place"]
---

The gampong in [[Acheh]] Proper from which [[Teungku Lam Paloh]] took his name. He was a Javanese of [[Yogyakarta]] who married and had a family in [[Acheh]], and without much claim to learning came to be regarded as a saint by a certain coterie. He died in [[1894]].
