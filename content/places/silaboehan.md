**Silaboehan** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Proposed natuurmonument of moerasbosch (swamp forest) adjacent to Langkat Zuidwest boschreserve.

## Description

Proposed [[Natuurmonument|natuurmonument]] of moerasbosch (swamp forest) adjacent to [[Langkat]] Zuidwest [[Boschreserve|boschreserve]].

## Significance

Silaboehan is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
