**Aneuë Galong** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Battlefield of 1896 assault by Teungkoe Ma'Anim; the fierce and desperate attack was repulsed in a quarter hour with the Acehnese leaving >100 dead.

## Description

Battlefield of [[1896]] assault by [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] Ma'Anim; the fierce and desperate attack was repulsed in a quarter hour with the Acehnese leaving >100 dead. [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] Ma'Anim was among the dead.

## Significance

Aneuë Galong is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
