**Poelau Banjak / Banjakeilanden** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Island district of Singkel, entirely composed of islands; originally part of Tapanoeli, separated in 1908 and joined to Atjeh.

## Description

Island district of [[Singkel]], entirely composed of islands; originally part of [[Tapanoeli]], separated in [[1908]] and joined to Atjeh.

## Significance

Poelau Banjak / Banjakeilanden is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
