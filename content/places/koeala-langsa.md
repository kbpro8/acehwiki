**Koeala Langsa** was harbour place of [[Langsa]], connected with town by 9 km side line of [[Atjeh Tram|Atjeh-tram]] running middle through *bakoe-bakoe* mangrove swamp.

## Harbour description

Long roomy jetty built, extendable at will at harbor water 15-20 metres deep. On bank at entrance sea mouth at low water still 14 foot depth. Opportunity envied by many small trade harbours Indies. Average 36 vessels monthly cleared, majority [[Koninklijke Paketvaart Maatschappij|Kon. Paketvaart]], weekly Chinese boat [[Penang]], Chinese tongkangs fetching goods.

Company [[Guntzel & Schumacher]] had own coastal shipping tug with tongkangs plying between [[Idi]] and [[Belawan]] in connection timber permits tidal forests.

Road through *bakoe-bakoe* is whole area messy plant growth in marsh maintained by sea water, classic Sumatra occurrence where man extracts wood, bark, *nipah* leaf with sacrifice health and lifespan.
