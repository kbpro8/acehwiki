**Kota Mogat** was a heavy stone coastal fortification located near [[Pante Cermin]] along the lagoon defense line of Great Aceh.

## Military Role

Kota Mogat was constructed with high stone walls backed against a tidal lagoon to guard the coastal approach to the capital. During the [[First Aceh Expedition]], Dutch troops under Colonel [[Eduard Cornelis van Daalen]] attempted to storm Kota Mogat on [[8 April]] [[1873]], but were repulsed due to the lack of storming ladders and heavy Acehnese musket fire.

On [[9 April]] [[1873]], Dutch naval gunships bombarded the stone walls, creating a breach that enabled the Madoera *Barisan* battalion to capture the fort without further casualties.
