**Gle Kambing** (also printed *Glé-Kambing*) was a place in the [[XXII Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]]: the Atjehnese post opposite the [[Missigit Indrapoeri|missigit of Indrapoeri]], at which the troops of [[Karel van der Heijden|generaal van der Heyden]] came up on [[24 March]] [[1879]] and from which the [[Capture of Indrapuri (24 March 1879)|missigit was taken by stratagem]] that day (see [[Campaign against the XXII Moekims (1879)]]). Thereafter it was kept as a Dutch fortification. The post was one of the fortifications maintained by the Dutch outside the new line that were returned to the field at the execution of the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration]]: it was evacuated between [[5 January|5]] and [[14 January]] [[1885]], nearly a month and a half before the clearing of the so-called [[1000-meter strook|free strip]] was finished (see [[Completion of the concentrated position (March 1885)]]).
