In the [[Hikayat Banta Amat]] (XXIII): 'Banta Ahmat came into the world shortly after the death of his father Ansari, king of the country of Nabati.' This is the hero's birthplace and the kingdom he eventually reclaims from his evil uncle Tapeuhi. [[Iran Supah]] (in [[Hikayat Banta Amat]]) In the [[Hikayat Banta Amat]]: 'on the way he finds the opportunity of becoming secretly betrothed to the princess Chahya in [[Iran Supah]].'
