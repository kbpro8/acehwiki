title: Klibeuet
tags: ["place", "aceh-proper"]
---

A gampong in [[Acheh]]. In the Hikayat Podi Amat (a hikayat ruhe), the hero is 'a student in the gampong of Klibeuet.' He dreams of success and goes on a journey to pursue his studies, eventually becoming ruler of the Gayos. The author of the hikayat Ranto ([[Leube Isa]]) 'passed a portion of his life in the colonies of the pepper planters on the West Coast' and later settled at Klibeuet after starting at [[Bambi]]. In the sadati performance, Klibeuet is mentioned as a centre of Islamic grammar study: 'here with a (question) in grammar, wherein I was instructed at Klibeuet at the home of Teungku Muda.'
