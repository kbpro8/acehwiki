**Bandar-Lampahan** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Coffee estate in the Takengonsche — a middelmatige (medium-sized) onderneming; other coffee enterprises in the area are klein-landbouw.

## Description

Coffee estate in the Takengonsche — a middelmatige (medium-sized) onderneming; other coffee enterprises in the area are klein-landbouw.

## Significance

Bandar-Lampahan is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
