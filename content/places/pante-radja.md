**Pante Radja** — modern *Pante Raja* — is a place in the *onderafdeeling* [[Meureudoe|Meureudoe]] of the *afdeeling* [[Pidië|Pidië]], known for its busy fishery and for the pepper-gardens of the *landschap* of that name, which are still maintained.
