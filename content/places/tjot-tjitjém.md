**Tjot Tjitjém** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Place associated with Teungkoe di Tjot Tjitjém.

## Description

Place associated with [[Teungkoe di Tjot Tjitjém]].

## Significance

Tjot Tjitjém is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
