**Lembu** was a heavily fortified village and embankment line situated behind the Pedir dike, southeast of [[Peunayong]] in Great Aceh.

## Military Role

During the [[Second Aceh Expedition]], Acehnese forces constructed three interconnected redoutes (*benteng*) at Lembu, protected by a 5-meter-thick thorn barrier (*bamboe-doeri*). On [[25 December]] [[1873]], Colonel [[G. Wiggers van Kerchem]] launched a frontal assault against Lembu, during which Adjutant [[E.C.O. von Bredow]] breached the thorn barrier to plant the battalion colors, earning the Military William Order.

The position was captured after intense hand-to-hand fighting during the [[Battles of Lembu]] on [[25 December]]–[[26 December]] [[1873]].
