**Blang Kolag** was large flat grass plain west and immediate surroundings of [[Takengon]], praised by Broersma as excellent terrain for exercise and game.

## Description

Westwards place immediate environment stretches big blang grass plain as level as would not imagine free nature. Rarely saw so excellent terrain exercise game resort human and child not unsafe ravine water open wide glory played sun cooling breeze place each day feasts renewal life it is Blang Kolag. Truthfully [[Atjeh]] blessed inland health resort and all reason accommodation increase.
