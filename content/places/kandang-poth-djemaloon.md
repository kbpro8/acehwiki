**Kandang Poth Djemaloon** was a highly fortified royal Acehnese cemetery located approximately 100 meters west-southwest of the [[Atjeh's Groote Missigit]].

## Structure and Role in Conflict
Enclosed by a massive double stone wall, it was the largest and highest of the burial grounds in the area. Because of its sturdy construction, it was utilized as a tactical stronghold during the [[First Conquest of the Great Missigit]] on [[10 April]] [[1873]]. Dutch troops from the [[9e Bataljon Infanterie]] took up covered positions near this cemetery to prevent Acehnese flanking maneuvers during the assault.
