**Batée Ilië** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Hill where Van Heutsz and his staff were photographed on march during the actie-tijd.

## Description

Hill where [[Generaal J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] and his staff were photographed on [[March|march]] during the [[actie-tijd]].

## Significance

Batée Ilië is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
