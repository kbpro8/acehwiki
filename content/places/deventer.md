**Deventer** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[H. N. A. Swart]]

Swart chose the solution many took in that period: *adspirant-onderofficier*. On [[16 October]] [[1880]], aged seventeen, he engaged as a volunteer soldier for six years, with a bounty of eighty guilders, in the battalion of the 8th Regiment of Infantry then garrisoned at [[Deventer]]. He deliberately avoided the alternative, the *Instructie Bataljon* at Kampen, because he did not wish to walk about as a private in front of the comrades who had passed for the K.M.A.

### From [[1880]]

On [[16 October]] **[[1880]]**, aged seventeen, [[H. N. A. Swart]] engaged as a volunteer soldier for six years with a bounty of eighty guilders in the 8th Regiment of Infantry at [[Deventer]]. In [[1880]] the costs of the [[Atjeh-oorlog]] amounted to 115 million guilders.
