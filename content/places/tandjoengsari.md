**Tandjoengsari** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[H. N. A. Swart]]

He kept up his correspondence with the humblest of his old subordinates. A letter of [[19 February]] [[1937]] to the Ambonese old soldier [[Jacob Lawalata]], celebrating on [[26 February]] [[1937]] his thirty-fifth year as a Knight of the *Militaire Willemsorde* ("Military William Order") at [[Jogjakarta]], is reproduced in facsimile in the biography; a letter to Swart from his old brigade commander [[W. J. Mosselman]], written from *De Hoenderhof* at [[Tandjoengsari]] near [[Bandoeng]] on [[12 September]] [[1936]], is printed beside it.

### From [[W. J. Mosselman]]

Mosselman later transferred to the flying service and was pensioned as *onderluitenant* with the rank of second lieutenant. He remained in touch with his old chief. On [[12 September]] [[1936]] he wrote to Swart from *De Hoenderhof* at [[Tandjoengsari]] near [[Bandoeng]], timing the letter for the mail boat of [[16 September]] which by the table would arrive in the Netherlands on [[11 October]], so that it would lie in the letter-box exactly on Swart's birthday; the letter closed "uw oud-brigadecommandant, Mosselman," with the promise of drinking an otherwise forbidden *oorlam* to His Excellency's health on [[12 October]].

### From [[1936]]

On [[7 March]] **[[1936]]** Governor [[Van Aken]] handed over the government of Atjeh to Resident [[J. Jongejans|Jongejans]], declaring the sub-divisions [[Alaslanden]], [[Lam Meulò]], [[Meulaboh]] and [[Tapa' Toean]] — the last still governed by officers — fully ripe for civil administration: "the keystone of the pacification". On [[12 September]] [[1936]] [[W. J. Mosselman]] wrote to Swart from [[Tandjoengsari]]. In [[1936]] the son of [[T. Tjhi' Béntara]] was suspended as *[[oelèëbalang]]* of [[Keureutòë]]. [[H. T. Damsté]]'s *Drie Atjehmannen* appeared in the *Koloniaal Tijdschrift* of [[1936]]–[[1938]].
