**Samahani** was a kampong in the [[VII Moekims Baid]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]], which was occupied by a Dutch *bataljon infanterie met hulp-wapens* (bataljon of infantry with supporting arms) in late August [[1896]] — *bezetting van Samahani door een bataljon infanterie met hulp-wapens*. The *bivak* (camp) of Samahani served as a forward Dutch position for the operations in the [[VII Moekims Baid]] and the [[XXV Moekims|XXV]] and [[XXVI Moekims]] in the second semester of [[1896]]. The occupation of Samahani formed part of the broader Dutch forward movement of the second semester of [[1896]] — the slow, methodical extension of Dutch control from the *geconcenteerd linie* into the surrounding *sagi's*, which characterised the *pacificatie* (pacification) under [[J. W. Stemfoort|Stemfoort]]'s second tenure.

## The Strategic Context

By late August [[1896]], the Dutch had established the [[Occupation of Kroeng-Raba (Lok-Nga) (15 July 1896)|permanent occupation of Kroeng-Raba (Lok-Nga)]] on [[15 July]] [[1896]] and were conducting the [[Trip to XXII Moekims (23–27 August 1896)|trip to the XXII Moekims]] of [[23 August|23]]–[[27 August]] [[1896]]. The occupation of Samahani — in the [[VII Moekims Baid]], north of the [[XXII Moekims]] — was undertaken in the same period, and formed part of the broader Dutch forward movement of the second semester. The *bivak* (camp (bivak)) of Samahani was garrisoned by a *bataljon infanterie met hulp-wapens* — a bataljon of infantry with supporting arms, typically including a section of mountain artillery and a detachment of engineers — and served as the base for the *verschillende patrouilles in de VII Moekims Baid, in de XXV en XXVI Mockims* (various patrols in the VII Moekims Baid, in the XXV and XXVI Moekims) which were conducted in the autumn of [[1896]].

## The Bivak and the Patrouilles

The *bivak* (camp (bivak)) of Samahani — *bivak Samahani* — was established as a forward Dutch position in late August [[1896]], and was maintained through the autumn of [[1896]]. The *bivak* (camp (bivak)) served as the base for the *verschillende patrouilles* (various patrols) which were conducted in the VII Moekims Baid and in the XXV and XXVI Moekims, and which formed the principal Dutch military activity of the autumn of [[1896]]. The *bivak* (camp (bivak)) was still in operation in [[1913]] — the date of Kruisheer's *ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel* — and was one of the permanent Dutch forward positions which had been established in the second semester of [[1896]].

## Significance

Samahani is significant in the documentary record of *ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel* as a forward Dutch position in the [[VII Moekims Baid]] — the third Dutch forward position beyond the *geconcenteerd linie* in the second semester of [[1896]], after the [[Occupation of Kroeng-Raba (Lok-Nga) (15 July 1896)|occupation of Kroeng-Raba (Lok-Nga)]] of [[15 July]] [[1896]] and the [[Construction of Blang Bintang Camp (7 September 1896)|construction of the Blang Bintang camp]] of [[7 September]] [[1896]]. The *bivak* (camp (bivak)) of Samahani — garrisoned by a bataljon of infantry with supporting arms — served as the base for the various patrols in the VII Moekims Baid and in the XXV and XXVI Moekims, and represented the slow, methodical extension of Dutch control which characterised the *pacificatie* (pacification) of the second semester of [[1896]] under [[J. W. Stemfoort|Stemfoort]]'s second tenure.
