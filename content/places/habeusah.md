In [[Hikayat Pha Suasa]]: 'The king of [[Habeusah]] (Abyssinia) who lays claim to the hand of his bride... a colossal conflict supervenes, ending in the conquest of the raja of [[Habeusah]] and his conversion to Islam.' Also: 'Pha Suasa is equally successful in a war with the Batak king Kabeulat, and she then subdues once more the kingdom of Habeusi Raya (Great Abyssinia).' The Abeusi (from Abyssinian) is a term used in the hikayats for a dark- complexioned character.
