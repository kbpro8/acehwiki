**Indrapoera** (modern Indrapura) was a pepper and gold-bearing polity on the [[Westkust van Sumatra|West Coast of Sumatra]], lying south of [[Padang|Padang]] and north of [[Salido|Salido]], whose ruler — usually called the "keijser" (*keijser*) of Indrapoera — was one of the four contracting landheeren of the [[Painansch Contract]] of [[6 July]] [[1663]] and a signatory of the [[Padang Conference (29 August 1680)|Padang Conference]] of [[29 August]] [[1680]]. In Dr. F. W. Stapel's *Geschiedkundige Atlas van Nederland* it is the most consistently loyal of the Painansch Contract signatories, the polity whose ruler is repeatedly found ceding territory or confirming treaties to the [[Vereenigde Oost-Indische Compagnie|Compagnie]].

## In the 1641 Schouten treaty

Indrapoera was one of the four pepper ports named in the [[Treaty of Atjeh-VOC (15 February 1641)|Schouten treaty]] of [[15 February]] [[1641]], by which the [[Sultana of Atjeh (Taj ul-Alam)|sultane of Atjeh]] conceded to the Compagnie the tolls in the pepper ports of [[Tikoe]], [[Priaman]], [[Padang]] and Indrapoera and promised to admit no other European traders there.

## The 1649 Truytman treaties

The [[Treaty of Atjeh-VOC (November 1649)|Truytman treaties]] of November [[1649]] widened the Compagnie's privileges from the four named pepper ports to the whole Westkust, "with the exclusion of all other European and native nations — except Atjehers."

## The 1659 Bort treaties

After the [[Treaty of Atjeh-VOC (5 October 1659)|peace of 5 October 1659]] with the sultane, commissioner [[Balthasar Bort|Balthasar Bort]] sailed along the Westkust and "concluded new trade treaties with the chiefs of [[Silebar|Sillebar]] — which did not stand under Atjeh, but under the [[Sultan of Bantam|sultan of Bantam]] — Indrapera and Padang" (*sloot commissaris Bort, in het bijzijn van een vertegenwoordiger der sultane, nieuwe handelsverdragen met de hoofden van Sillebar — die niet onder Atjeh, maar onder den sultan van Bantam stond — Indrapera en Padang*). On the two last-named places — Indrapera and Padang — offices (*kantoren*) were established. The Compagnie was now physically present at Indrapoera as well as at [[Tikoe]] and [[Priaman]].

## The 1663 Painansch Contract

Indrapoera was one of the four contracting landheeren in the [[Painansch Contract]] of [[6 July]] [[1663]]. The defection of the "keijser" of Indrapoera from "the croone of Atchin" to the protection of the Compagnie — negotiated secretly by [[Jan van Groenewegen|Jan van Groenewegen]] in [[1662]] — was one of the four signatories' acts that constituted the Painansch Contract.

## The 1666 cession of Salido and Poeloe Tjingkoek

After the [[Abraham Versprect|Versprect]] expedition's destruction of [[Paoeh]] in August [[1666]] the rulers of [[Bajang]], Indrapoera and [[Taroesan]] "transferred to the Compagnie in ownership the place [[Salido]] with the gold-rich surroundings, from the kali-Loemoepo to Painan, as well as [[Poeloe Tjingkoek]], in the person of [[Jacob Jorisse Pits|Jacob Jorisse Pits]]" (*droegen de vorsten van Bajang, Indrapoera en Taroesan de plaats Salido met de goudrijke ommelanden, van de kali-Loemoepo tot aan Painan, benevens Poeloe Tjingkoek, in eigendom over aan de Compagnie, in den persoon van Jacob Jorisse Pits*). The "keijser" of Indrapoera is the senior of the three ceding rulers.

## The 1670 re-cession

In [[1670]] the rulers of Bajang and Indrapoera "gave a new act of cession of the lands of Salido, and Poeloe Tjingkoek, which the government had asked for, because the original piece had been lost at Batavia" (*gaven een nieuwe acte van overdracht der landen van Salido, en Poeloe Tjingkoek, waarom de regeering gevraagd had, daar het oorspronkelijke stuk te Batavia was zoekgeraakt*).

## The 1673 summons

In [[1673]] the king of Indrapoera himself "called in the intermediation of the Governor-General, to obtain restoration of supposed wrong" (*riep de koning van Indrapoera de tusschenkomst van den Gouverneur-Generaal in, om herstel te krijgen van vermeend onrecht*). The reference suggests that the "keijser" of Indrapoera was, by the early 1670s, treating the Governor-General as his overlord in disputes with his neighbours.

## The 1680 Padang Conference

The [[Padang Conference (29 August 1680)|Padang Conference of 29 August 1680]] was signed by the "keijser" (*keijser*) of Indrapoera — together with the panglima-radja at [[Padang]] and the governors of [[Salido]] and [[Kotta Tengah]] — as the principal regents of the Westkust. The choice of the Indrapoera ruler as the senior signatory is the constitutional high-water mark of his position in the Westkust system.

## The 1681 re-cession

On [[27 February]] [[1681]] the rulers of [[Bajang]] and Indrapoera "gave a renewed confirmation of the complete cession of the lands of Salido and Poeloe Tjingkoek to the Compagnie" (*Nog gaven de vorsten van Bajang en Indrapoera den 27 Februari 1681 een hernieuwde bevestiging van den algeheleen afstand van de landen van Salido en Poeloe Tjingkoek aan de Compagnie*).

## The eighteenth century

Stapel records that in [[1716]] the Compagnie "renewed with the 'Keyser' of Indrapoera the contract of 1663, and established there again a lodge" (*vernieuwde men met den 'Keyser' van Indrapoera het contract van 1663, en stichtte er weer een loge*). The Indrapoera relationship remained a continuous thread of the Westkust system through the entire VOC period.

## Significance

Indrapoera is the most consistently loyal of the four Painansch Contract signatories. The "keijser" of Indrapoera is the senior native signatory of the [[Padang Conference (29 August 1680)|1680 Padang Conference]] and the senior of the three rulers who ceded [[Salido]] and [[Poeloe Tjingkoek]] to the Compagnie in [[1666]]. The Compagnie's relationship with Indrapoera is the model of the Painansch Contract system: native sovereignty retained, Atjehsche suzerainty repudiated, Compagnie protection and monopoly accepted.
