**Ana' Galong** — written in Dutch variously as *Ana' Galong*, *Anak Galoeng*, *Anas Galoeng*, *Ana Galong*, and *Anao Galong* — was a [[Dutch]] outpost and fort in the [[Sagi der XXII Moekims|sagi of the XXII Moekims]], described by Kruisheer in *ATJEH '96* as the place "Door de Atjehers steeds beschouwd als het hart van Groot-Atjeh" ("considered by the Atjehers as the heart of Great Aceh"). As the [[buitenpost|buitenposten]] of the [[buitenlinie]] went, Ana' Galong was the most exposed and most fought-over, the former main residence of [[Teungkoe di Tiro|Teungkoe di Tiroe]] (who died there), and the scene of the famous [[Patrol of Captain Van Blokland (7 March 1896)|patrol of Captain Van Blokland of 7 March 1896]]. Ana' Galong was conquered by [[Teuku Umar|Oemar]]'s *legioen* on [[30 November]] [[1893]], was the eastern boundary of the [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lamkrak complex]], and was lifted (evacuated) by the Dutch on [[17 April]] [[1896]].

## Description and Location

Ana' Galong lay in the [[Sagi der XXII Moekims|sagi of the XXII Moekims]] at the eastern edge of the [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lamkrak complex]], at the head of the road from [[Lambaroe]] (with a bridge approximately 1100 metres from Lambaroe that was broken by the Dutch on evacuation). The *benteng* sat on the strategic high ground that the Achehnese considered the symbolic heart of [[Groot-Atjeh]], and from it the [[Dutch]] could dominate the [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lamkrak]] and the country southward toward [[Biloel]] and the *gebergte*. The garrison in [[1895]] consisted of 1 captain, 3 lieutenants, 120 bayonets from the 15e Bataljon, 1 medical officer, 1 hospital attendant, an ambulance, and 16 men of the siege artillery. The entrance to the *benteng* in early [[1896]] was under fire so that a privy on the north side could not be used. [[Toeankoe Machmoed]] and [[Toeankoe Brahim]] settled here in late [[May]] [[1896]] after the [[Pretender Sultan|pretender sultan]]'s move.

## Historical Background

Ana' Galong had been the former main residence of [[Teungkoe di Tiro|Teungkoe di Tiroe]], the great Achehnese religious-war leader, who died there. After [[Teungkoe di Tiro|Tiroe]]'s death the post remained a symbolic centre of Achehnese resistance. The [[Dutch]] occupation of Ana' Galong was intended in part to keep "[[Toekoe Moeda Baid|T. Baid]]" of the [[VII Moekims Baid]] in line. The post was conquered by [[Teuku Umar|Oemar]]'s native *legioen* on [[30 November]] [[1893]] in the course of his operations as [[Panglima Prang Besar]] against the Lamkrak mukims. Thereafter it was continuously shot at by the enemy, and the [[buitenpost|buitenposten]] of [[Lambaroe]] and [[Blang Tjoet]] were built in part to support its operations. [[Kapitein P. H. Bodemeijer|Kapitein Bodemeijer]] was the "vindingrijke postcommandant" (inventive post-commander) who used gin bottles with messages floated down the [[Atjehrivier]] to communicate with [[Lambaroe]].

## Role in the 1896 Operations

Ana' Galong was the scene of the [[Patrol of Captain Van Blokland (7 March 1896)|famous patrol of 7 March 1896]] in which [[Kapitein der Infanterie|Kapitein]] [[H. F. T. van Blokland|Van Blokland]] led a patrol of 1e luit. [[C. T. J. Stakman|Stakman]], adjudant-onderofficier Krüger, medical officer [[C. van der Meer|Dr. v. d. Meer]], and 72 bajonetten along the kampong edges of [[Lam-Bada Anak Baté|Ana' Batée]], Klieng, and the sawah toward Sibreh, engaging for five hours a superior enemy of approximately 250 men under [[T. Mad Amin|T. Mad Amin]], [[Habib Lhong]], and [[Panglima Hassan]]. [[Kapitein der Infanterie|Kapitein]] [[H. F. T. van Blokland|Van Blokland]] personally fought with sabre ("het gelukte hem persoonlijk met de sabel twee tegenstanders neer te slaan, of which een nagenoeg in tweeën gedeeld werd"). The relief by [[Kapitein van den generalen staf Kronouer|Kapitein Kronouer]]'s detachment of 40 bayonets from [[Lamsoet]]/[[Lambarih|Lambaroe]] under luit. [[K. H. Westendorp|Westendorp]] and luit. [[F. Ketjen|Ketjen]] allowed withdrawal. On [[13 March]] [[1896]] [[Kapitein P. H. Bodemeijer|Kapitein Bodemeijer]] talso command. On [[30 March]] [[1896]] the post came under fire from [[Koeta Karang]], and on [[31 March]] [[1896]] from the *missigit* Lamdjampoe. On [[17 April]] [[1896]] the post was lifted by the second colonne under [[Kolonel der Infanterie|Kolonel]] [[J. W. Stemfoort|Stemfoort]]; the bridge was blown by the Dutch engineers, and the [[blokhuis|blokhuizen]] of Bak Tjerlak and Oedjong Boekloet were burned by their postcommandants. In late [[May]] [[1896]] [[Toeankoe Machmoed]] and [[Toeankoe Brahim]] settled at Ana' Galong after the [[Pretender Sultan|pretender sultan]]'s arrival in the region. Ana' Galong is also mentioned in [[Teuku Umar]]'s [[Second Letter of Teuku Umar (12 April 1896)|second letter of 12 April 1896]] as a place to receive "benting te Ana' Galong".

## Significance

Ana' Galong was the symbolic and strategic heart of the [[Dutch]] *buitenlinie* in the [[Sagi der XXII Moekims|XXII Moekims]], and its evacuation on [[17 April]] [[1896]] marked the effective end of the outer-line policy that had governed the Dutch enterprise since [[1890]]. The [[Patrol of Captain Van Blokland (7 March 1896)|patrol of 7 March 1896]] was the largest single engagement on the *buitenlinie* in the spring of [[1896]] and a precursor of the defection crisis. The settlement of [[Toeankoe Machmoed]] and [[Toeankoe Brahim]] at Ana' Galong in late [[May]] [[1896]] indicated the post's continued political importance in the Achehnese imagination.
