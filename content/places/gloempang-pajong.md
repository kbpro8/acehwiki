**Gloempang Pajong** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Place where in November 1902 Christoffel's colonne captured one of the Pretendent-Sultan's wives, Teungkoe Poetrèë.

## Description

Place where in [[November]] [[1902]] [[Christoffel]]'s colonne captured one of the [[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]]'s wives, [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] Poetrèë.

## Significance

Gloempang Pajong is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
