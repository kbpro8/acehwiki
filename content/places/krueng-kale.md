---
title: Krueng Kale
tags: ["place", "aceh-proper", "river"]
---

A location in [[Acheh]]. A renowned religious teacher who succeeded his father resided here. In the sadati dialogue: 'Logic is taught at [[Lam Paya]], dogma at Krueng Kale; your questions are put without consideration.' Also: 'At Krueng Kale there are many teachers, [[Teungku Meuse]] is as the lamp of the world.' [[Teungku Krueng Kale]] alias Haji Muda studied at Mekka as well. The tomb of Teungku di [[Batee Puteh]] on a hill near [[Krueng Raya]] (XXVI Mukims) is mentioned separately.
