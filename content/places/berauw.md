**Berauw** was the western part of [[Lemboe]] along the [[Pedir-dijk]] taken by the Dutch on [[7 November]] [[1874]] in the [[Conquest of Lemboe (7 November 1874)|conquest of Lemboe]] by [[Kolonel Diepenbrock|overste Diepenbrock]]. With its main dike fortifications, Berauw was the strongest section of the Lemboe defences and the key to the southern end of the Pedir-dijk.

## The western part of Lemboe

Borel describes Lemboe as a single kampong complex stretching along the [[Pedir-dijk]] from its southern bend at the [[Atjeh-rivier]] up to the marshy ground near the [[Marine-benteng|marine-benteng]]. Berauw was its western part, where the dike fortifications were strongest. The *benteng* (fortification) line of Berauw ran along the crest of the dike, with earthen parapets, *bamboe-doeri* (thorn-bamboo) entanglements, and a series of armed salients commanding the *sawah* (irrigated rice fields) to either side. The enemy had been fortifying Berauw and the rest of Lemboe continuously since the abandonment of the place by [[Jan van Swieten|van Swieten]] after the [[Battle of Lemboe (25–26 December 1873)|battle of 25–26 December 1873]] — almost a year of unopposed labour. The Berauw section was therefore, by November [[1874]], the most formidable single work on the right bank of the Atjeh-rivier.

## The conquest

On [[7 November]] [[1874]] [[Kolonel Diepenbrock|overste Diepenbrock]] led two half-battalions and a field-artillery battery against Lemboe, attacking from [[Penajoeng]] and [[Langkroek]] across open *sawah*. Contrary to expectation, resistance was light — the enemy, weakened by months of bombardment and the [[Conquest of Langkroek (28 September 1874)|loss of Langkroek]] on [[28 September]] [[1874]], did not stand. The western part of Lemboe, called Berauw, with its main dike fortifications, fell. The Dutch lost Diepenbrock and about 20 men or coolies wounded, and one officer's servant killed. Three posts were immediately built: the *Lemboe-zuid* rivier-benteng armed with an 8 c.M. rifled gun, the original *Lemboe N.O.* post (later relocated on [[10 March]] [[1875]] opposite [[Pinang]] and renamed *Nieuw Lemboe N.O.*), and the *Lemboe Oost* intermediate post established on [[12 March]] [[1875]]. Berauw itself became part of the chain of right-bank posts of the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]] along the [[Pedir-dijk|Pedir-dijk]], completing the Dutch hold on the dike from [[Kota Radja Bedil|K. R. Bedil]] at the river-mouth up to the bend at Lemboe.
