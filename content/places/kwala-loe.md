**Kwala Loë** (also written Kuala Loë) was the mouth of the Loë river on the east coast of [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] to the east of the [[Aceh River|Atjeh river]], chosen as the landing-place of the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)|second Aceh expedition]] on [[9 December]] [[9 December]] [[1873]] — partly because less resistance was expected there, partly to spare the kampong [[Marassa|Marassa]] whose head, [[Toekoe Nek Radja Moeda Setia|Toekoe Nek]], had offered his services to the Dutch. Kwala Loë was also one of the two simultaneous landing-places of the operation of [[25 January]] [[25 January]] [[1877]] for the closure of the coast from [[Kwala Gighen|Kwala Gighen]] to [[Kroëng Raja|Kroëng Raja]], under the personal direction of the commander-in-chief.

## The Landing of 9 December 1873

The landing of the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)|second Aceh expedition]] took place at Kwala Loë on [[9 December]] [[9 December]] [[1873]]. "Although the troops were received at the beach by some musketry, this did no damage and the enemy soon fled. Also most of the fortifications proved to be abandoned or offered only slight resistance." On [[13 December]] [[13 December]] [[Kota Moesapi|Kota Moesapi]] was occupied; on [[14 December]] [[14 December]] the long line of enemy fortifications south of Kota Moesapi was taken by the storming of Tiban by General [[G. M. Verspyck|Verspyck]]; on [[15 December]] [[15 December]] the mouth of the Atjeh river was reached.

## The Operation of 25 January 1877

In [[January 1877|January 1877]] the troops resumed operations for the completion of [[J. L. J. H. Pel|General Pel]]'s program: the occupation of the coast from Kwala Gighen to Kroëng Raja. On [[25 January]] [[25 January]] [1877] the operations began under the commander-in-chief's personal direction with a simultaneous landing at Kwala Loë and Kroëng Raja. The latter place was not deemed suitable for a settlement; the troops returned along the beach to Ladoeng and thence over the mountains to [[Lambaroe Angan|Lambaroe Angan]] to join the other column, which had landed at Kwala Loë under Colonel [[K. van der Heyden|Van der Heyden]]. After the junction, the strong [[Lamnga|Lamnga]] was occupied, abandoned by the enemy.
