**Tjot-Basetoel** was a Dutch military post on the communication line through the [[XXII Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]]. The post was evacuated on [[13 January]] [[1885]] as part of the concentration strategy.

## Location

Tjot-Basetoel was situated on the communication line extending eastward from [[Lambaroe]], between [[Samagani]] and [[Long-Lemoh]].

## Pre-Concentration Status

Tjot-Basetoel was one of the posts established during the expeditions of [[General van der Heyden]] in [[1878]]–[[1879]]. The post formed part of the chain of isolated positions along the 121-palen line.

## Evacuation

Tjot-Basetoel was evacuated on [[13 January]] [[1885]] along with the other posts on the communication line.

## Significance

The evacuation of Tjot-Basetoel was part of the systematic withdrawal from the extended communication line that characterized the concentration strategy.
