**Senelop** was a Dutch military post in the [[XXII Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]]. The post was evacuated on [[28 February]] [[1885]] as part of the concentration strategy.

## Location

Senelop was situated in the southern portion of the [[XXII Moekims]], away from the main communication line.

## Pre-Concentration Status

Senelop was a post that existed before the concentration decision of [[1884]]. Unlike the posts on the main line, it was not evacuated with the main group on [[13 January]] [[1885]].

## Evacuation

Senelop was evacuated on [[28 February]] [[1885]] along with [[Montasik]], [[Anagaloeng]], [[Boekloe]], and [[Pakan-Badak]].

## Significance

The evacuation of Senelop was part of the systematic withdrawal from the outer perimeter that characterized the concentration strategy.
