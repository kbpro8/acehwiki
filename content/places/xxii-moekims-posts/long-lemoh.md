**Long-Lemoh** was a Dutch military post on the communication line through the [[XXII Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]]. The post was evacuated on [[13 January]] [[1885]] as part of the concentration strategy.

## Location

Long-Lemoh was situated on the communication line extending eastward from [[Lambaroe]], between [[Tjot-Basetoel]] and [[Glé-Kamping]].

## Pre-Concentration Status

Long-Lemoh was one of the posts established during the expeditions of [[General van der Heyden]] in [[1878]]–[[1879]]. Like other posts on the line, it was isolated and vulnerable once offensive operations ceased.

## Evacuation

Long-Lemoh was evacuated on [[13 January]] [[1885]] along with the other posts on the communication line.

## Significance

The evacuation of Long-Lemoh was part of the systematic withdrawal from the extended communication line that characterized the concentration strategy.
