**Samagani** was a Dutch military post on the communication line through the [[XXII Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]]. The post was evacuated on [[13 January]] [[1885]] as part of the concentration strategy.

## Location

Samagani was situated on the communication line extending eastward from [[Lambaroe]], between [[Lepong-Ara]] and [[Tjot-Basetoel]].

## Pre-Concentration Status

Samagani was one of the posts established during the expeditions of [[General van der Heyden]] in [[1878]]–[[1879]]. The post was added to the line to improve communications and support.

## Evacuation

Samagani was evacuated on [[13 January]] [[1885]] along with the other posts on the communication line.

## Significance

The evacuation of Samagani was part of the systematic withdrawal from the extended communication line that characterized the concentration strategy.
