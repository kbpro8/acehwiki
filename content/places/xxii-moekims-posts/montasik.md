**Montasik** was a Dutch military post in the [[XXII Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]]. The post was evacuated on [[28 February]] [[1885]] as part of the concentration strategy.

## Location

Montasik was situated in the [[XXII Moekims]], between [[Panteh-Karang]] and [[Anagaloeng]].

## Pre-Concentration Status

Montasik was a post that existed before the concentration decision of [[1884]]. It was one of the posts along the extended perimeter that was eventually abandoned.

## Evacuation

Montasik was evacuated on [[28 February]] [[1885]] along with [[Senelop]], [[Anagaloeng]], [[Boekloe]], and [[Pakan-Badak]].

## Significance

The evacuation of Montasik was part of the systematic withdrawal from the outer perimeter that characterized the concentration strategy.
