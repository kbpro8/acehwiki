**Panteh-Karang** was a Dutch military post on the communication line through the [[XXII Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]]. The post was evacuated on [[14 January]] [[1885]] as part of the concentration strategy.

## Location

Panteh-Karang was situated on the communication line extending eastward from [[Lambaroe]], between [[Senelop]] and [[Montasik]].

## Pre-Concentration Status

Panteh-Karang was one of the posts established during the expeditions of [[General van der Heyden]] in [[1878]]–[[1879]]. Like other posts on the line, it was part of the chain of isolated positions that characterized Dutch presence.

## Evacuation

Panteh-Karang was evacuated on [[14 January]] [[1885]], the day after the evacuation of the main group of posts. This slight delay may have been for logistical reasons.

## Significance

The evacuation of Panteh-Karang was part of the systematic withdrawal from the extended communication line that characterized the concentration strategy.
