**Rajeu** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. NKPM 5A contract area.

## Description

[[N.K.P.M.|NKPM]] 5A contract area.

## Significance

Rajeu is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
