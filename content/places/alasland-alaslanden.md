**Alasland (Alaslanden)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Region; Alaslanders under Acehnese influence for centuries; during the war many Acehnese fled there.

## Description

Region; Alaslanders under Acehnese influence for centuries; during the war many Acehnese fled there. [[Atjeh-moorden]] also occurred there, attributed to滥用 of power by chiefs. Leprosy sufferers also committed an attack there. 2 [[Alas|Alassers]] among [[Sabang]] asylum patients.

## Significance

Alasland (Alaslanden) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
