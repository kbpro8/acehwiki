**Keumangan / Gigiëng** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Landschap that set the example by being the first to sign the souvereiniteitsverklaring; later more commonly known as Keumangan.

## Description

[[landschap|Landschap]] that set the example by being the first to sign the [[souvereiniteitsverklaring]]; later more commonly known as [[Keumangan]].

## Significance

Keumangan / Gigiëng is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
