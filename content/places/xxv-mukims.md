**XXV Mukims** was a traditional coastal federal district (*Sagi*) of Great Aceh located along the western maritime border.

## History

Governed by *Sagi* head [[Teuku Nja|Nja Abas]], the XXV Mukims contained major pepper plantations and coastal trade centers. It experienced heavy fighting during pacification campaigns led by [[Karel van der Heijden]] and [[Teuku Umar]].
