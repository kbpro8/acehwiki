**Lampoe Idjoe** was a kampong on the [[Atjeh-rivier]] upstream of the [[Kraton of Atjeh|Kraton]], near the spot where the Dutch [[Reconnaissance of the Atjeh-rivier (29 January 1874)|reconnaissance of 29 January 1874]] met unexpected enemy resistance and was forced to retire with losses. In Borel's account it is one of the recurring posts of the enemy's eastern line above the Kraton, where an Atjehneesche vlag flew above a *benteng* (fortification) from May [[1874]] and the terrain was kept under intermittent Dutch artillery fire from [[Pakan Atjeh]].

## The January 1874 échec

On [[29 January]] [[1874]], only five days after the [[Fall of the Kraton (24 January 1874)|fall of the Kraton]], a colonne of two bataljons infanterie with a batterij veld-artillerie rukte uit to reconnoitre the left bank of the Atjeh-rivier. [[Jan van Swieten|Van Swieten]] and [[Generaal Verspijck|Verspijck]] both accompanied the column. Near Lampoe Idjoe the column met unexpectedly strong resistance, was fired on from both banks, and at a kreek with a versterking "stootten bepaald het hoofd" — butted its head against a wall. The artillerie "had spoedig al hare munitie verbruikt," and van Swieten "besloot den strijd op te geven en onverrichter zake terug te trekken." Losses were two officers and "ruim 20 minderen" killed or wounded. Borel's critical verdict is that the column operated on only one bank, leaving the other bank in enemy hands.

## Later operations

From the high observatie-post at [[Pakan Atjeh]] on [[15 May]] [[1874]] the Dutch discovered an Atjehneesche vlag waving above a new benteng at the height of Lampoe Idjoe upstream — proof that the enemy had re-occupied the ground the January reconnaissance had failed to clear. The artillerie at Pakan Atjeh immediately opened fire, the shots being gecorrigeerd from the observatiepost; the uitwerking was "moeilijk na te gaan," but the Atjehneezen "binnen hunne werken te Pendetti geen stand konden houden." Lampoe Idjoe, [[Lampoe Oek]], [[Longbatta]] and [[Blang Tjoet]] were reconnoitred and cleared on [[4 January]] [[1875]] by [[Generaal Pel|Pel]] with two columns, with no resistance; the houses were burned. From [[1 February]] [[1875]] the enemy tried to retaliate with a dawn attack on the [[Lampoe Oek|nipa-benteng]] at Lampoe Oek.
