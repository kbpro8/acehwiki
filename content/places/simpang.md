---
title: Simpang
tags: ["place", "north-east-coast"]
---

A village in [[Pidie]] which was a centre of religious learning before the Dutch arrival. 'Such were [[Langga and Langgo]], [[Langga and Langgo]], [[Sriweue]], Simpang, [[Ie Leubeue]] (= Ayer Labu).' [[Cheh Marahaban]] studied in [[Pidie]] (in Simpang among other places) and later at Mekka. The author of the Hikayat ranto, [[Leube Isa]], lived first in gampong [[Bambi]], later at [[Klibeuet]].
