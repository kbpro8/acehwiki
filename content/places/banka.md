**Banka** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Island off Sumatra; source of a pepper variëteit tried in Aceh (initially successful, then wiped out by voetrot).

## Description

Island off Sumatra; source of a pepper variëteit tried in Aceh (initially successful, then wiped out by [[Voetrot|voetrot]]).

## Significance

Banka is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
