**Panton Laboe (Pantonlaboe)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Bivak bombarded by Pang Nanggròë.

## Description

[[Bivak]] bombarded by [[Pang Nanggròë]]. Captain [[Christoffel]] placed here as [[Marechaussée|marechaussée]]-commandant by [[H.E. Swart|Governor Swart]]; [[Marechaussée|marechaussée]] still stationed there at time of writing.

## Significance

Panton Laboe (Pantonlaboe) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
