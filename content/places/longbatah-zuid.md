**Longbatah Zuid** (Longbatah South) was a Dutch fortified post south of kampong [[Longbatah]] in the [[XXV Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]], a work standing since before the offensive of [[26 December]] [[1875]] and built to correct the defects of the earlier posts of Longbatah-Mesigit and Blang Tjoet.

## The work

The fortification had considerable dimensions — laid out for two companies, its southern and northern faces 64 and 74 metres, its eastern and western 70 and 64. The northern face was palisaded with coconut trees; every other face had an earth breastwork with ditch, and obstacles were made everywhere. The single bastion stood at the southeast salient; the gun placed on it had served to fire on the enemy's old position at [[Lo-ong]].

The lightly wooded ground at the east and west faces and the trees of the kampong were partly cut to open the view; the work stood moreover in sawahs except near its north face, where a nipah swamp ringed it — a natural abatis, partly cut and completed by the lengthening of the east and west faces, and used as emplacement for the outbuildings.

## Purpose and reduction

The work's purpose was to remedy the defects of the old posts of Longbatah-Mesigit and Blang Tjoet; but after the hostile line of the [[Imam Longbatah|Imam of Longbatah]] was abandoned in February [[1876]] and the Dutch fortified line pushed far forward, Longbatah Zuid lost its value and was kept only to shelter the garrison working at the building of the post at [[Lo-ong]], the demolishing of the abolished works Lohong Northeast, Southeast and South, and the building of a road through the *rawah* north of Lo-ong. Soon after March [[1876]] its garrison was reduced to one section of infantry; the fortification did not exist much longer. In March of that year the bivouac of the relief column of Lieutenant-Colonel [[Kolonel Meijer|Meijer]] stood there the night before the [[Storming of Lambaroe (7 March 1876)|storming of Lambaroe]], in the hospital and quarters of Major [[Majoor Lubeck|Lubeck]]'s still-stationed left half of the 5th battalion.
