**Gayo Lèuëos** was/is a isolated mountainous highland region in the interior of Aceh, inhabited primarily by the Gayo ethnic group.

## Historical Context and Role

Between [[February]] and [[July]] [[1904]], Gayo Lèuëos was the scene of the intense [[Gayo and Alas Lands Expedition of 1904]] commanded by Lieutenant-Colonel [[Gotfried Coenraad Ernst van Daalen|G. C. E. van Daalen]]. Ten to fourteen brigades of the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]] marched through its high mountain passes, breaking highland guerrilla resistance.
