**Kemala** was a place in [[Groot-Atjeh]] where, in August [[1884]], the dynastic candidate [[Toeankoe Mohamad Daoed]] was declared of age; afterwards the Atjehnese regarded him as their lawful Sultan (see [[Herstel van het sultanaat]]). The fact is recorded by [[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] in the conclusion of his 1886 pamphlet, where he uses it to show that any retreat with honour by handing the land over to a pretender had become impossible, "the true crown Allah has already caused us to regain" being the answer that every Atjeher would give.
