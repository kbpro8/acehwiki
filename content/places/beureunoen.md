**Beureunoen** — pronounced *Bernoen* — is a place in the [[Pidië|vallei of Pidië]] on the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]], the seat of the *oeléebalang* of [[Keumangan|Keumangan]], [[Teukoe Oemar Keumangan|Teukoe Oemar]]. From Beureunoen on the Atjeh-tram there is a connection to [[Lam Meulo|Lam Meulo]], which lies south-west and is the chief place of the *onderafdeeling* of that name. Within the yard of the chief's dwelling at Beureunoen stands a new house in the Atjehnese style, entirely decorated with carving and with the mouldings picked out in colours, which has revived, at least for a few years, the declining art of Atjehnese wood-carving.
