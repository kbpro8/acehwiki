**Alas Peurba** was a plantation estate in [[Oostkust van Atjeh|East Atjeh]], lying at 200 metres above sea level about eighteen kilometres south of [[Lho Seumawe|Lho Seumawe]], on which part of the collection of bird skins described in *[[Over eene collectie vogelhuiden van Oost-Atjeh]]* by [[F. C. van Heurn]] and [[R. C. E. G. J. Snouckaert van Schauburg]] was gathered, and on which the earlier, second Atjeh collection of Van Heurn — described in *[[Over de tweede Atjeh-collectie van Jhr. F. C. van Heurn|Over de tweede Atjeh-collectie van Jhr. F. C. van Heurn]]* by Snouckaert — was gathered in [[September]]–[[November]] [[1920]].

## The estate and its management

Like [[Aloer Djamboe|Aloer Djamboe]] in [[Tamiang|Tamiang]], the estate stood under the management of the agency of the [[Handelsvereeniging Amsterdam|Handelsvereeniging Amsterdam]], whose grant of permission and facilities the authors of the *[[Over eene collectie vogelhuiden van Oost-Atjeh|Over eene collectie vogelhuiden van Oost-Atjeh]]* acknowledged as a proof of the interest of a commercial body in purely scientific work. The collecting was done on heavy hill country at the boundary of plantation and original forest — *op zwaar heuvelland, aan de grens van plantage en oorspronkelijk bosch* — the transitional situation that the authors held to be the most productive for tropical collecting, since it gave access both to the typical primeval-forest species and to those of the cultivated zone.

The authors' description of the eastern seaboard places the estate in its setting: almost the whole plain along the coast of East Atjeh had been brought into cultivation by a fairly dense population, while further inland the population had penetrated only sporadically; but from the coastal districts the European cultures had struck *eenige diepe bressen in het oerbosch* — "some deep breaches into the primeval forest" — of which the estates at which they collected were instances.

## The second Atjeh collection of 1920

The estate was the site of the second of the two avifaunal collections made by [[F. C. van Heurn|Van Heurn]] in Atjeh in [[1920]]. The first collection — described in the *[[Avifaunistische Studien in de Gajo-landen|Avifaunistische Studien in de Gajo-landen]]* — had been made in the highland region of [[Takengon|Lake Takengon]] (1200 metres) in [[Gajo-land|Gajo-land]]. The second — described in the *[[Over de tweede Atjeh-collectie van Jhr. F. C. van Heurn|Over de tweede Atjeh-collectie van Jhr. F. C. van Heurn]]* — was made at Alas Peurba (200 metres) and [[Aloer Djamboe|Aloer Djamboe]] (50 metres) in [[September]]–[[November]] [[1920]]. As Snouckaert records, *Ditmaal viel de keuze op veel lager gelegen localiteiten meer nabij de N.O.-kust* — "This time the choice fell on much lower-lying localities nearer the northeast coast."

At Alas Peurba, Van Heurn collected from [[11 September]] [[1920]] — the date of the earliest specimens recorded in the paper (specimens of *Rollulus roulroul*, *Picus vittatus*, *Dendronanthus indicus*, and *Microtarsus melanocephalus*) — through [[September]] and into early [[October]] [[1920]]. The paper records specimens from a wide range of bird families collected at Alas Peurba, including the crested wood-partridge (*Rollulus roulroul*), the jambu fruit-dove (*Ptilinopus jambu*), the crested serpent-eagle (*Spilornis cheela bacha*), several kingfishers, trogons, woodpeckers, broadbills, and the garnet pitta.

## The *Pitta granatina vanheurni* specimen

The paper is notable for the record of *Pitta granatina vanheurni* — a subspecies of the garnet pitta named after [[F. C. van Heurn|Van Heurn]] by [[C. Boden Kloss|Kloss]] — of which Snouckaert records a juvenile male collected at Alas Peurba on [[13 September]] [[1920]]. Snouckaert notes that the Sumatran form of the garnet pitta had only recently become known to science, and that Van Heurn's two (or three) specimens were among the very few then in collections. Van Heurn himself, however, was of the opinion that the distinction of the Sumatran form from the Malayan *coccinea* was not well founded.

## Deposit

The collection described in the *[[Over de tweede Atjeh-collectie van Jhr. F. C. van Heurn|Over de tweede Atjeh-collectie]]* was deposited with the *Boekerij van het Koloniaal Instituut* — the Library of the [[Koninklijke Vereeniging Koloniaal Instituut|Koloniaal Instituut]] — in [[Amsterdam|Amsterdam]].
