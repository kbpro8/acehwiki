**The Krueng Meureubo** (also *Kr. Meureubo*, modern *Krueng Meureubo* or *Sungai Meureubo*) is a river of the [[Atjeh's Westkust|West coast of Atjeh]] whose coal-bearing deposits are recorded in [[Van Bemmelen's The Geology of Indonesia (1949)|Van Bemmelen's *Geology of Indonesia*]] ([[1949]]) as one of the two rivers — together with the [[Krueng Woyla]] — along which the Neogene-coal deposits of the West-Atjeh coast are located. The Krueng Meureubo and the Krueng Woyla are accordingly the principal fluvial reference-points for the West-Atjeh Neogene coal zone.

## The coal deposits along the Krueng Meureubo

Van Bemmelen's reference to the Krueng Meureubo occurs in the same passage of the coal chapter as his reference to the Krueng Woyla:

> "The deposits of the second group are located along the Krueng Wojla and the Krueng Meureubo in the district of West Atjeh."

For the wider context of the West-Atjeh Neogene coals see the entry on [[Krueng Woyla]]. The Krueng Meureubo deposits, like the Krueng Woyla deposits, "to the present, seem to be of no or little economical importance".

## Significance

The Krueng Meureubo is significant for the encyclopedia of [[Acheh]] history because it is one of the two principal fluvial reference-points for the West-Atjeh Neogene coal zone, and accordingly one of the two rivers whose name is canonically associated with the West-Atjeh coal occurrences in the colonial geological literature. The river's inclusion in Van Bemmelen's summary of the West-Atjeh coal occurrences is itself the historical fact that matters: the river was sufficiently well-known in the colonial geological literature to be named as one of the two principal coal-bearing rivers of the West-Atjeh coast.
