In the Hikayat Malem Diwanda': 'Prince Malem Diwanda', son of Sulutan Roih (Sultan Rus) of Panjalarah.' This is the hero's home kingdom. Also referenced in the [[Hikayat Meudeuhaa]]: the villain Jiran is king of Panjalarah who 'levies war against the ruler of [[Watu]] and a hundred other princes.'

In [[Hikayat Meudeuhaa]]: 'Jiran king of Panjalarah levies war against the ruler of [[Watu]] and a hundred other princes.' Panjalarah is also the kingdom of the hero Sulutan Roih in the Hikayat Malem Diwanda'.
