**Thingkun** was an Achehnese kampong in the neighbourhood of the valley of [[Lamtoba]], at the foot of the [[Goudberg]] behind the north coast of [[Groot-Atjeh]], known as the kampong of the chief [[Toekoe Djoe Raman]] during the Dutch occupation of the district in August [[1879]], as recorded in [[W. Cool]] and [[G. B. Hooijer]]'s *[[Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis (Cool & Hooijer, 1889)|Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis]]* ([[1889]]).

## In the occupation of 1879

From the kampong Thingkun, its chief Toekoe Djoe Raman came into the Dutch bivak at Lamtoba during the absence of the main column on the [[Selimoen Expedition (August 1879)|Selimoen expedition]], bringing a *sappi* as a gift for the column-commandant — a significant adherence, since a younger sister of the chief was married to [[Radja Koelala|Radja Koeala]], the son of the great [[Panglima Polem|Panglima Polim]]. The place itself the book does not further describe; like the *paseijs* — the little mountain hamlets passed by the reconnaissance towards [[Kroeng Raija]] — it belonged to the scattered settlement of the hill country between the north coast and the Atjeh-rivier.
