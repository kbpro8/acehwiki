**Kroečng Geumpang (river)** (river) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. River that joins the Kroečng Tangsé at Tangsé to form the Kroečng Teunom.

## Type

river

## Description

River that joins the [[kroečng|Kroečng]] [[Tangsé]] at [[Tangsé]] to form the [[kroečng|Kroečng]] [[Teunom]].

## Significance

Kroečng Geumpang (river) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
