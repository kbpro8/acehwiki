**The Rivierbenting** was a Dutch fortification (*benting*) on the [[Acheh River|Atjeh River]] north-east of [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] — so named from its position at the river — which was established on [[7 November]] [[7 November]] [[1874]] together with the fortifications at Lemboe, Berauw, and [[Lemboecq|Lemboecq]] as one of the chain of fortified villages that formed the eastern outwork of the Dutch position at [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] in the middle phase of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. The Rivierbenting marked the point at which the column of [[13 November]] [[13 November]] [[1874]] — see [[Benting Kota Alam operation (13 November 1874)|Benting Kota Alam operation]] — reached the [[Acheh River|Atjeh River]] by way of the [[Pedir|Pedir]] dike before turning upstream to the site chosen for the new [[Kuta Alam|Benting Kota Alam]].

## The Fortification of 7 November 1874

On [[7 November]] [[7 November]] [[1874]] Dutch troops "hadden zich meester gemaakt van eenige krachtig versterkte vijandelijke stellingen in de kampong Lemboecq (ten N.O. van Kota Radja) en aldaar nieuwe versterkingen Lemboe. Berauw en de Rivierbenting ingericht en bezet." The Rivierbenting was thus a newly-constructed Dutch work, named for its river-side position, and was occupied as part of the same operation as the Lemboe and Berauw works.

## The 13 November 1874 Movement

The [[Benting Kota Alam operation (13 November 1874)|Benting Kota Alam operation of 13 November]] [[1874]] began from the Rivierbenting. The column under [[M. C. E. Ruempol|Luitenant-Kolonel Ruempol]] crossed the bridge at Pakan Atjeh, advanced along the trenches "op geringen afstand ten N. daarvan gelegen," reached the benting Berauw, and then "boog de colonne in zuidelijke richting om, bereikte aldus langs den Pedirdijk de Atjeh-Rivier nabij de Rivierbenting, en volgde daarna stroomopwaarts de rivier tot Kota Alam." The Rivierbenting was thus a recognised landmark on the [[Acheh River|Atjeh River]] at the point where the column left the [[Pedir|Pedir]] dike and entered the river road to [[Kuta Alam|Kota Alam]].
