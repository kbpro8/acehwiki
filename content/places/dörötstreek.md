**Dörötstreek** was the district which, with the [[Meergebied]], formed the fourth of the six hearths of the [[Het verzet in Atjeh 1908-1912|resistance of 1908–1912]].

The [[Onrust in Linggö (1916)|Dösön valley of Linggö]] in the Meergebied had formerly been very turbulent. "And when in [[1912]] the band in the Döröt district also submitted, the situation in the Lake District and the Döröt district could thenceforth be judged as ranging from favourable to very favourable."
