**Padalarang** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Location of papierfabriek (paper factory) on Java that uses pine resin from Aceh.

## Description

Location of papierfabriek (paper factory) on [[Madiun and Surabaya|Java]] that uses pine [[Resin (Aceh)|resin]] from Aceh.

## Significance

Padalarang is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
