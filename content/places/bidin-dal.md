**Bidin-dal** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Valley in Gajoland identified for pine resin exploitation expansion; expansion began 1938.

## Description

Valley in [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajoland]] identified for pine [[Resin (Aceh)|resin]] exploitation expansion; expansion began [[1938]].

## Significance

Bidin-dal is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
