**Koeala Panga** (place (Westkust)) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Where the Nisero stranded in the night of 8-9 November 1883, 20 km south of Tjalang.

## Type

place ([[Westkust]])

## Description

Where the Nisero stranded in the night of 8-[[9 November]] [[1883]], 20 km south of [[Tjalang]].

## Significance

Koeala Panga is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
