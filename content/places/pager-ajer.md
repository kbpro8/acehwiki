**Pager Ajer** was an Atjehnees kampong in the [[III Moekims]] sector on the road between [[Kota Radja|K. Radja]] and the [[Longbatta-Mesigit]], conquered on [[4 February]] [[1876]] in [[Generaal Pel|Pel]]'s great [[Pel's offensive against the VI, IV and IX Moekims (1875–1876)|winter offensive]] — its stone mosque, the *Pager Ajer Mesigit*, became a Dutch strongpoint — and the *uiterste steunpunt* of the new linie toward [[Kwala Gigieng]] after the [[Atoeh catastrophe (13 February 1876)|Atoeh catastrophe of 13 February 1876]]. The post was besieged by the enemy in early March [[1876]] and relieved by [[Kolonel Meijer|overste Meijer]]'s colonne in the operation that also stormed [[Lambaroe]].

## The conquest of 4 February 1876

Pager Ajer was conquered on [[4 February]] [[1876]] in the closing sweep of the [[XXV Moekims|XXV Moekims]]. From Toeram the Dutch had made an *inval* into the [[III Moekims]] on [[3 February]] [[1876]], driving the enemy out of [[Atoeh]] after a *heeten strijd*. The next day the colonne continued *noordoostelijk*: Lamrong and [[Lamsajoen]] were conquered, along with Kajoe Leh, [[Lambaroe]] and the Pager Ajer Mesigit, and via Tandjoeng and Bayoer communication was opened with the Longbatta-Mesigit. On [[7 February]] [[1876]] the troepen returned to K. Radja after a 10-day *tocht*; total losses were five dead and 59 wounded, "betrekkelijk gering." The *demarcatie-linie* now ran from the mouth of the [[Kroeng Raba]] on the west coast to Atoeh, then northward to Pager Ajer on the left bank of the [[Atjeh-rivier]].

## The uiterste steunpunt

After the [[Atoeh catastrophe (13 February 1876)|Atoeh catastrophe]] of [[13 February]] [[1876]] and the conquest of [[Pango]] the same day, Pel pressed on through Meroe, Lampermej and [[Tjap Octoe]] on [[14 February|14]]–[[15 February]] [[1876]]; at Tjap Octoe the troepen stuitten on a *sterke benteng* with artillery, *krachtig verdedigd*, only taken "met veel moeite en opoffering." The losses were enough to make Pel decide not to push further eastward but to designate [[Pango]] as the *uiterste steunpunt* of the new linie toward [[Kwala Gigieng]] — judged *voldoende*. Pager Ajer became part of the same linie. In early March [[1876]] the communication between Pager Ajer and the Longbatta-Mesigit was broken and Kajoe Leh was *ingesloten* by the enemy. [[Kolonel Meijer|Overste Meijer]] marched with a colonne from K. Radja to *ontzet* the bedreigde posten; after the enemy was verdreven on the Pager Ajer side, the colonne stuitten at [[Lambaroe]] on a *sterke benteng* which was *stormenderhand* taken, the Atjehneezen leaving more than 70 dead.
