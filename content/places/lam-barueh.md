title: Lam Barueh
aliases: [Lam Jamee"]
tags: ["place", "aceh-proper"]
---

A location in [[Acheh]]. After Jeumaloy fled from [[Gampong Jawa]], he fled successively to Lam Barueh, [[Gampong Meulayu]], Krueng Raba, and Krueng Kala, and was pursued no further. Also, in the [[Hikajat Prang Kompeuni|Hikayat Prang Gompeuni]]: 'the captain of Lam Barueh (i.e. Lam Jamee) fell in an attack on [[Kuta Kandang]].'
