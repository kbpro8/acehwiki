**The Raijah** was a steamer of the [[Koninklijke Paketvaart Maatschappij|Indische Paketvaart]] seized by pirates off the north coast of [[Acheh|Atjeh]] on the night of [[20 July]] [[1893]] and run ashore near [[Keureutoe|Kerti]], in the episode narrated by [[P. H. R. Beuming]] as the [[Raijah piracy (20 July 1893)|Raijah piracy]].

She left [[Oelè Lheuë|Oleh-leh]] on [[20 July]] [[1893]] bound for [[Penang]] by way of [[Segli]], [[Telok Semawé]] and [[Edi (Atjeh)|Edi]], carrying besides her ordinary cargo a rumoured large sum of money for the *[[Controleur Binnenlandsch Bestuur|controleur]]* at Edi — a rumour that proved false, the consignment having gone with the *[[Hok Canton (ship)|Hok Canton]]*.

Seven [[Pedir|Pedirese]] were smuggled aboard at Telok Semawé by an Acehnese of the crew and hidden in the hold. That night they killed the helmsman, the second mate [[Alexander (second mate)|Alexander]] and the captain, and in the general massacre that followed twenty-four of those aboard were killed, twelve wounded and eighteen drowned. The European engineers survived by hiding in the engine-room; part of the crew was pressed to help work the ship, which was run onto the beach so that the bandits could go ashore with their booty and five women. Before leaving they stove in the boats, so that thirteen passengers attempting to land perished.

After the pirates had gone the engineers signalled with rockets to the warship *[[Madura (warship)|Madura]]*, which was in the neighbourhood and rendered immediate assistance.
