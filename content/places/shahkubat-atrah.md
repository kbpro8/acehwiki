In the Hikayat Malem Diwanda': the hero establishes himself in the country of Shahkubat. His son Indra Peukasa becomes the successor of Shahkubat. In the Hikayat Chah Kubat: 'Chah Kubat's father Chah Peurasat Indra La'srma, king of [[Atrah]].' Arab. Atraf = extremities; 'according to our hikayat this country lay close to the mountain Kah (Arab. Qaf) and marched with the territory of the jens.'
