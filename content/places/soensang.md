**Soensang River** is a tributary of the Musi River near [[Palembang]] that was the main waterway navigated by Dutch forces during the [[1821]] expedition. The fleet, including the frigates [[Dageraad]] and [[Van der Werff]], encountered strong enemy batteries and obstructed channels, which hampered their advance toward the city’s [[Kraton]] (page 94).

## Military Significance

Control of the Soensang was essential for projecting Dutch power inland and for the pacification of the Aceh‑influenced southern Sumatra region.
