**Lam Teumet** is a place in the *onderafdeeling* [[Seulimeum|Seulimeum]] of the *afdeeling* [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]], proposed as the site of a central institution for the treatment of lunatics (*krankzinnigen-inrichting*) in Atjeh. The governor of Atjeh had proposed to build such a central institution at Lam Teumet, but for the sake of economy the government did not consent. Recently, however, the government has approved that the former military hospital at [[Sabang|Sabang]] be fitted up for the admission of lunatics who require institutional care. The proposal for Lam Teumet is one of the casualties of the retrenchment of the early civilian period.
