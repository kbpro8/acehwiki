**Leiden** is a city in the Netherlands whose university library holds a significant collection of Acehnese manuscripts, including the [[Nalam Mo'lôt]] and a local translation of the [[Akidat al-awāmm]] [[Nalam Mo'lôt]] [[Akidat al-awāmm]]. The source notes: “Het boekje is in de Leidse bibliotheek aanwezig” and “de talrijke Atjéhse handschriften te Leiden” [[Leiden]] [[Nalam Mo'lôt]] [[Akidat al-awāmm]] [[Atjéhse handschriften]].
