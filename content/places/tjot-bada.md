**Tjot Bada** — *Cot Bada* — was an Atjehnese *kampong* of the [[VII Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]] — the *kampong* of [[Toekoe Moeda Baid]], *[[Uleebalang|oeloebalang]]* of the VII Moekims, in whose custody the young Sultan [[Toeankoe Machmoed]] was held after the death of Sultan [[Sultan Saleiman Iskander|Saleiman Iskander]] in [[1837]]. The *kampong* is named in the [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] as the site of [[Habib Abdurrahman Zahir|Habib Abdoe'r Rachman]]'s armed expedition against Toekoe Moeda Baid — the expedition by which the Habib compelled the surrender of the young prince and installed him as Sultan under the Habib's regency.

## The 1837 succession crisis

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] records that, after the death of Sultan [[Sultan Saleiman Iskander|Saleiman Iskander]] in [[1837]], the young prince [[Toeankoe Machmoed]] was in the custody of [[Toekoe Moeda Baid]] at his *kampong* of Tjot Bada. [[Habib Abdurrahman Zahir|Habib Abdoe'r Rachman]] wished to install the prince as Sultan and to act as regent during his minority, but Toekoe Moeda Baid refused to release him. The Habib accordingly assembled a force of two thousand men from the [[XXVI Moekims]] and marched on Tjot Bada — *met groote macht daartoe in zijn kampong Tjot Bada te komen dwingen* (with great force to come to compel him [to do so] in his kampong Tjot Bada).

## The second expedition to Tjot Bada

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that [[Habib Abdurrahman Zahir|the Habib]] made a second expedition to Tjot Bada in connection with the marriage to [[Potjoet]] — the widow of Sultan Saleiman Iskander and sister of Toekoe Moeda Baid. Toekoe Moeda Baid had accepted a payment of 400 dollars for the marriage promise, but had repeatedly deferred the actual delivery of the bride — *zoo lang, dat Habib genoodzaakt werd hem met groote macht daartoe in zijn kampong Tjot Bada te komen dwingen* — *so long, that the Habib was compelled to come with great force to compel him [to do so] in his kampong Tjot Bada*.

## Significance

Tjot Bada's significance in the documentary record of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] is as the *kampong* of [[Toekoe Moeda Baid]] — the *oeloebalang* of the [[VII Moekims]] whose resistance to [[Habib Abdurrahman Zahir|Habib Abdoe'r Rachman]]'s centralising reforms marks the limit of the centralising experiment of the [[1860s]]–[[1870s]]. The *kampong* was the site of two of the Habib's armed expeditions against Toekoe Moeda Baid — the expedition to compel the surrender of the young Sultan [[Toeankoe Machmoed]], and the expedition to compel the delivery of the bride [[Potjoet]]. The *kampong*'s appearance in the [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] is brief, but it identifies the site of two of the principal conflicts between the Habib and the *oeloebalang* class.
