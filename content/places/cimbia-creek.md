**Cimbia creek** was a waterway in the interior of [[Acheh]] that served as a key navigation route for the Dutch naval expedition of December 19–25. The bark “[[Zeven Provinciën]]” anchored near the creek on [[7 am]] and later dispatched patrols to explore surrounding plantations such as [[Altenklingen]] and [[Cornelia Jacoba]].

## Military Use
The creek was used to block rebel movements, to provide a landing point for troops, and to secure communication lines between the river fleet and inland posts.
