**Poeloe Nassie** (modern Pulo Nasi) is a maritime island situated off the northwestern tip of Sumatra near the entrance to the Aceh River estuary, in modern Aceh Besar Regency, Aceh Province, Indonesia.

## Military Supply and Hospital Base

During the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] in [[1873]] and [[1874]], General [[J. van Swieten]] utilized the sheltered anchorages of Poeloe Nassie as a naval staging area and quarantine station for the [[Dutch East Indies Government]]. 

As documented in official returns in *Atjeh gedurende de tweede expeditie en later* ([[1876]]), Poeloe Nassie served as an offshore medical evacuation center for the [[Medical Service in Aceh]], where hundreds of sick soldiers and convict laborers ([[Forced Labor in Aceh Military Campaigns]]) suffering from cholera and dysentery ([[Field Hospital and Cholera Epidemics in Aceh]]) were sent to recover away from the malaria-ridden coast.
