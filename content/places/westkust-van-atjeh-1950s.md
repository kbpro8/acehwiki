**The Westkust van Atjeh** (the West Coast of Atjeh) was a region on the western coast of the Atjeh residency that, during the late colonial and early independence period, hosted plantation enterprises of the Société Financière des Caoutchoucs and other companies. [[J. S. Broers]], a longtime Société Financière inspector, was transferred to the West Coast of Atjeh before the war, and the region was the subject of the geographical and geological surveys by [[R. D. M. Verbeek]] (*Topographische en geologische beschrijving van Sumatra's Westkust*, [[1883]]).

## Colonial administration

The West Coast of Atjeh had its own distinct colonial administrative structure, separate from the East Coast and from Groot-Atjeh (Greater Atjeh) proper. The region's ports and coastal settlements had historically been important trading points, and the colonial period saw the development of plantation enterprises along the coastal plain.
