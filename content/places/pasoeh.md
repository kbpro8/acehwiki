**Pasoeh** was a settlement in [[Aceh]] listed in the index of *De geschiedenis van het Nederlandsche Korps Mariniers van 1665‑1945* on [[103]]. Its inclusion among other Aceh‑related locations such as [[Pedir]] and [[Pedawa Pondong]] points to its relevance in Dutch military records.

## Geographic Note

The source provides only an index reference, but the recording of Pasoeh suggests it was a notable site—perhaps a village, fort, or logistical point—encountered by Dutch marine forces during their operations in the Aceh region.

## Significance

Pasoeh’s appearance in the marine corps history reflects the detailed geographical awareness of Dutch colonial forces and the extensive mapping of Acehnese terrain that underpinned their military campaigns.
