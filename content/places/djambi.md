**Djambi** was a region on the east coast of **[[Sumatra]]** that featured prominently in Dutch colonial military operations in the mid‑nineteenth century. The town of **[[Retch]]**, the site of the **[[Expedition to Retch 1858|1858 expedition]]**, lay just south of Djambi.

## Historical Context

During the 1850s the Dutch sought to suppress piracy and local resistance along the **[[Batang River|Batang‑kreek]]** and surrounding coastal settlements, including Djambi.
