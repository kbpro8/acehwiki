**Tripa (river)** (river) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Upper basin = Gajo Loeös and considered the 'bakermat' (cradle) of Gayo people.

## Type

river

## Description

Upper basin = [[Gajo Loeös]] and considered the 'bakermat' (cradle) of [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gayo]] people. A possible road to [[Westkust]] through [[Tripa]]-vallei was studied by [[Topografische Dienst]] and V&enW, found 'not unsuitable'.

## Significance

Tripa (river) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
