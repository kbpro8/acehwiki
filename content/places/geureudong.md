**Geureudong** (mountain) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Volcanic complex north of Laoet Tawar; has two prominent peaks: the fully vegetated Glé-Geureudong and the bare still-active Boer Ni Tèlông.

## Type

mountain

## Description

Volcanic complex north of [[Laoet Tawar]]; has two prominent peaks: the fully vegetated [[Glé-Geureudong]] and the bare still-active [[Boer Ni Tèlông]]. Volcanic material covers a very large area extending funnel-shaped from [[Laoet Tawar]]. Has the most extensive tufmantel of all Aceh volcanoes.

## Significance

Geureudong is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
