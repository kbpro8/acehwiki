**Groot-Atjeh (Groot-Atjeh)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. The main region around the Sultan's capital; repeatedly referenced as theatre of war and of Teukoe Oemar's invasions, clearing operations, etc.

## Description

The main region around [[Sultan of Aceh|the Sultan]]'s capital; repeatedly referenced as theatre of war and of [[Teukoe Oemar]]'s invasions, clearing operations, etc.

## Significance

Groot-Atjeh (Groot-Atjeh) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
