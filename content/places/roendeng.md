**Roendeng** (place (Westkust)) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. End of the auto-road on Westkust, 80 km south of Bakongan.

## Type

place ([[Westkust]])

## Description

End of the auto-road on [[Westkust]], 80 km south of [[Bakongan]]. From here one takes a motorboat (6 hours upstream) or prauw (not much longer downstream) to Nieuw [[Singkel]]. Connection to [[Tapanoeli]] via [[Sidikalang]]is 'under consideration' with trace set out but not officially in execution.[[Simpang Kiri]] in the [[onderafdeeling]] [[Singkel]]; the onderafdelingshoofdplaats reachable only by motorboat.

## Significance

Roendeng is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
