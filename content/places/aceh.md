**Aceh** (also known as **Atjeh** or abbreviated **Atj.**) is a region at the northern tip of Sumatra, historically known as the Sultanate of Aceh. In a 16th‑century Malay translation of the Burda, the word *ureh* (meaning “stripe” or “streak”) is glossed as *Atj. ureh*, indicating the Acehnese term for a stripe.

## Etymology
The footnote on page 62 of the source manuscript reads: “*hoewel Klinkerts Wdbk. peroes opgeeft in de zin van ,echte streep*”, kan men wellicht ook lezen: **huris, Atj. ureh, streep**.” This note explains that the Malay word *ureh* (“streak”) is rendered in the Acehnese dialect as *Atj. ureh*, providing an early attestation of the Acehnese lexical item *ureh*.
