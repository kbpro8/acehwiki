**St. Eustatius** was a Dutch Caribbean island, nicknamed “the Golden Rock” for its role as a major entrepôt for goods flowing between the Americas, Europe, and the Atlantic colonies. In the early 1780s the island supplied the rebellious American colonies and the Dutch West India Company, which strained the Dutch Republic’s neutrality during the war that began on [[20 December]] [[1780]].

## Strategic Importance in the 1780‑1784 War
When the Dutch Republic attempted to remain neutral in the conflict sparked by the American Revolution, the WIC’s activities on St. Eustatius—particularly the provision of arms to the rebels—prompted British seizures of Dutch merchant vessels. The loss of ships and cargo from St. Eustatius reduced the overall Dutch naval capacity, indirectly affecting the VOC’s ability to sustain its Asian fleet, including forces deployed against the Sultanate of [[Aceh]].
