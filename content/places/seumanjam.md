**Seumanjam** (oil palm enterprise) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Oil palm (oliepalm) enterprise near Meulaboh.

## Type

oil palm enterprise

## Description

Oil palm ([[oliepalm]]) enterprise near [[Malaboeh|Meulaboh]].

## Significance

Seumanjam is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
