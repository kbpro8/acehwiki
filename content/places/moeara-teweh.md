**Moeara Teweh** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[G. K. Dijkstra]]

Dijkstra was, as captain, civil authority and simultaneously troop commander at [[Moeara Teweh]], [[Paloppo]] and [[Sigli]], and thus experienced under Swart as provincial administrative head some six years of what he and his fellow "mindere goden" called the *schoonmaaktijd* (cleaning-up time).

### From [[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)]]

Dijkstra wrote from personal knowledge: as captain, civil authority and simultaneously troop commander at [[Moeara Teweh]], [[Paloppo]] and [[Sigli]], he had experienced some six years of what he and his fellow "mindere goden" called the *schoonmaaktijd* under Swart as provincial administrative head. In [[Pidië]] he had only to follow the road indicated by tested predecessors, but even there the beacons had in some respects to be moved in governing "this self-assured and difficult people." It became hard work; virtually everyone did it with zeal and cheerfulness, because the chief himself led the way. For at least half the time they were absent, and on returning to the station the accumulated office work and the ordinary business of the service took up a large part of the day. For their wives those were years of self-sacrifice, loneliness and worry.
