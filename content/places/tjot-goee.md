**Tjot Goeë** — written in Dutch variously as *Tjot Goeë*, *Tjot Goee*, for the neighbouring hill, *Tjot Goeë Tjoet*, and *Tjot Goë* — was a [[Dutch]] post and key communication point in the [[Sagi der XXV Moekims|sagi of the XXV Moekims]], situated on a hill approximately 30 metres high, serving as the communication post between [[Lampeneroet]] and [[Biloel]]. As described in Kruisheer's *ATJEH '96*, Tjot Goeë was favourably placed to influence the lower part of the [[IX Moekims]] (the mukims of Oleh Soesoe and [[Lamkoenjit]]) and the great *pasar* of Lamkoenjit, and was a repeated meeting point and reporting station for the various Dutch columns throughout the first semester of [[1896]]. The post was the site of an 8 c.M. Brons gun (replaced by a 7 c.M. A. Veld on [[12 April]] [[1896]]), and the [[benteng|benteng]] Paja Krah of the *imam* of Lamkoenjit stood on the south-east side of the Tjot Goeë hill. A semi-permanent *versterking* was built there by the Dutch in [[June]] [[1896]].

## Description and Location

Tjot Goeë lay in the [[Sagi der XXV Moekims|sagi of the XXV Moekims]] on a hill approximately 30 metres high, on the road between [[Lampeneroet]] and [[Biloel]]. The post commanded the great *pasar* of [[Lamkoenjit]] and the lower mukims of the [[IX Moekims]]. The garrison in [[1895]] consisted of 2 officers, 52 infantry, 10 artillery, and 1 European hospital attendant. The post was armed with an 8 c.M. Brons gun (replaced by a 7 c.M. A. Veld on [[12 April]] [[1896]]). The neighbouring hill of Tjot Goee Tjoet (also Tjot Goeë Tjoet) was a separate feature. The *benteng* Paja Krah of the *imam* of [[Lamkoenjit]] stood on the south-east side of the Tjot Goeë hill. A semi-permanent *versterking* was built there by the Dutch in [[June]] [[1896]] as part of the reorganised Dutch line.

## Historical Background

Tjot Goeë had been constructed as part of the [[buitenlinie]] to serve as the communication post between [[Lampeneroet]] (on the inner line) and [[Biloel]] (on the outer line), and to dominate the great *pasar* of [[Lamkoenjit]]. The post was the meeting-point and reporting station for the various Dutch columns operating in the [[Sagi der XXV Moekims|XXV Moekims]] and the [[IX Moekims]]. On [[9 May]] [[1895]] the post had been the base from which [[Majoor J. F. T. van Bloemen Waanders|Majoor Van Bloemen Waanders]]'s column (4 officers, 132 bayonets, 2 sections *bergartillerie*, engineer detachment and ambulance) supported the *legioen* of [[Teuku Umar|T. Djohan]] in clearing the area. The [[14 April 1894 Commission|14 April 1894 commission]] had also been associated with the post.

## Role in the 1896 Operations

Tjot Goeë was the principal Dutch communication and command post in the southern theatre throughout the first semester of [[1896]]. On [[8 April]] [[1896]] the relief column under [[Kolonel der Infanterie|Kolonel]] [[J. W. Stemfoort|Stemfoort]] reported by telephone to the [[Regeeringscommissaris]] at Tjot Goeë at 11:07 AM; the post served as the forward command post for the relief of [[Biloel]] and [[Lamkoenjit]]. On [[12 April]] [[1896]] the 8 c.M. Brons gun was replaced by a 7 c.M. A. Veld. On [[13 April]] [[1896]] an supply attempt on Tjot Rang from Tjot Goeë failed. On [[30 March]] [[1896]] three companies of the 3e Bataljon were sent to Tjot Goeë by steam tram at 2:15 PM and were reinforced by 2 sections of the 4e Bergbatterij at 5 PM. After the evacuation of the [[buitenlinie]] in [[April]] [[1896]] Tjot Goeë remained in Dutch hands as the forward post of the [[Geconcenteerde Linie|Geconcenteerde Linie (Concentrated Line)]]. In [[June]] [[1896]] a semi-permanent *versterking* was built at Tjot Goeë, reflecting the post's continued strategic importance. The [[benteng]] Paja Krah of the *imam* of [[Lamkoenjit]] on the south-east side of the Tjot Goeë hill was a target of Dutch operations.

## Significance

Tjot Goeë was the principal Dutch command-and-communication post in the southern theatre of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] in [[1896]], and the only post of the [[buitenlinie]] in the [[Sagi der XXV Moekims|XXV Moekims]] to remain in Dutch hands after the evacuation of [[12 April]] [[1896]]. Its retention and fortification as a semi-permanent *versterking* in [[June]] [[1896]] reflected the Dutch policy of consolidating a forward defensive line from which to conduct the [[Bombardment of Lampisang|bombardment of Lampisang]] and the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day offensive]] against [[Teuku Umar]].
