**Pantei Perak** was a riverside locality and military hospital encampment situated along the banks of the Aceh River near Kotta Radja in the [[Aceh Sultanate]], modern Banda Aceh, Indonesia.

## Hospital Relocation Under General Pel

Following the occupation of [[Kraton of Aceh]] in January [[1874]] during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]], the primary field hospital inside the royal palace became overcrowded, triggering severe cholera epidemics ([[Field Hospital and Cholera Epidemics in Aceh]]).

In April [[1874]], when General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]] assumed command, he ordered the [[Medical Service in Aceh]] to construct elevated wooden hospital barracks and recovery pavilions at Pantei Perak. The airy, well-drained riverside location significantly reduced mortality among European and indigenous troops throughout [[1875]] and [[1876]].
