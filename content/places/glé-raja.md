**Glé Raja** was a mountain bounding the valley of the [[Acheh River|Atjeh-rivier]] in [[Groot-Atjeh]], paired by [[G. B. Hooijer]] with the [[Goudberg]] in his account of the thunderstorms and drainage of the [[XXII Moekims]].

## In Hooijer's description

Hooijer wrote that when the thunder echoed from the [[Goudberg]] to the Glé Raja and tropical rains fell on the motionless trees, the surplus water of the middle valley — gradually raised by the sediment of earlier floods — drained towards the foot of the hills and mountains and thence by creeks and streams to the lower course of the river or to the sea coast. The passage forms part of his contrast between the drier, healthier [[XXII Moekims]] and the malarial marshes of the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]].
