**Padang Tidji** is a village in the Aceh region noted as the hometown of the Acehnese scholar‑translator [[Teungkn Tjēh Kob al. T. Panté Tjeureumèn]], who produced an Acehnese version of the Burda poem and a mawlid‑bewerking [[Teungkn Tjēh Kob al. T. Panté Tjeureumèn]] [[Atjéhse bewerking van de Burda]] [[Mawlid-bewerking]]. The source identifies him as “Teungkn Tjēh Kob al. T. Panté Tjeureumèn uit Padang Tidji” [[Padang Tidji]].
