**Padang Tidji** was the settlement in the rich Moekim VII of [[Pidië|Pidie]] where [[Panglima Polém|Panglima Polém]] established himself after withdrawing across the watershed from [[Groot-Atjeh|Greater Atjeh]]. At Padang Tidji, Polém maintained easy contact with the [[Toekoe Moehamat Dawot|sultan at Keumala]] and other enemies of the Dutch, both from Pidie and from Atjeh. The moekim was the planned target of the [[Pidië-expedition (1898)|Pidië expedition]] of [[1898]], which aimed to advance from two directions — [[Sigli]] and [[Seulimeum|Seulimeum]] — to strike at Polém's position.
