---
title: Meunasah Dua
tags: ["place", "aceh-proper"]
---

A location in [[Acheh]]. Referenced in the sadati dialogue: 'if that will not do, little brother sadati, let us go down to Meunasah Dua. I am not very well known at Meunasah Dua brother; Teungku Cheh Deuruih (a teacher established there) is still but a young man.'
