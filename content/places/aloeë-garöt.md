**Aloeë Garöt** was the *aloeë* at which [[H. Behrens|Behrens]] fell upon [[Tgk. di Mata Ië]] in [[1913]] so suddenly that the Teungkoe's turban and rosary remained caught in the bushes.

In [[1913]] viel Behrens hem, bij de *aloeë* ("brook") Garöt, so swiftly upon him that his turban and rosary remained caught in the bushes, like Absalom's hair; he was wounded in the leg in this encounter. So his invulnerability had apparently come to an end; nevertheless he maintained himself for four years more. The leg wound made possible the identification of his corpse near [[Samarkilang]] on [[10 July]] [[1917]].
