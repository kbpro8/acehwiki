**Oostkust (East Coast of Aceh)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Coastal region; mentioned with Noordkust as having had recent separatism-related difficulties among atjehsche hoofden, resolved peacefully.

## Description

Coastal region; mentioned with [[Noordkust]] as having had recent separatism-related difficulties among [[Atjeh (Aceh)|atjehsche]] hoofden, resolved peacefully.

## Significance

Oostkust (East Coast of Aceh) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
