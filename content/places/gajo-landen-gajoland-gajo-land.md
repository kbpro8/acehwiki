**Gajo-landen (Gajoland, Gajo-land)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Region to which Panglima Polém fled, then to Peutòë.

## Description

Region to which [[Panglima Polém]] fled, then to [[Peutòë]]. [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] di [[Mata Ië]]'s influence extended there. [[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]] fled there after [[Batèë Ilië]] (February [[1901]]). [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]]'s had supported the war party with rice for the [[Prang sabi|prang sabil]]; Gajosche chiefs gave all possible help to the [[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]]. The [[163-day Gajo-tocht under Van Daalen]] was a great success. [[Gajo-landen|Gajo-Landen]] and [[Alas|Alasland]] mentioned separately. 23 [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]]'s among [[Sabang]] asylum patients.

## Significance

Gajo-landen (Gajoland, Gajo-land) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
