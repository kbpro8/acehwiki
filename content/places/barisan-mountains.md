**Barisan (mountains)** (mountain range) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Heavy mountain massif running along Sumatra.

## Type

mountain range

## Description

Heavy mountain massif running along Sumatra. Outliers reach the sea at [[Lho'Nga]] on the [[Westkust]], leaving barely room for passage. Held to the right on the road to [[Sigli]].

## Significance

Barisan (mountains) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
