**Setee** was an Atjehnees kampong west of [[Kota Radja|K. Radja]] between [[Soerian]] and the western arc of the Dutch perimeter, repeatedly listed among the objectives of the [[Battle of Soerian (26 July 1874)|battle of 26 July 1874]] and finally conquered in April [[1875]] in the closing operations that completed the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]]. It is mentioned together with Lamteboe, Gitjiel and Lohong as one of the kampongs west of K. Radja whose conquest "cost fresh bloody sacrifices."

## Operations in 1874

[[Generaal Pel|Pel]]'s orders for the [[Battle of Soerian (26 July 1874)|battle of 26 July 1874]] named Setee among the kampongs to be conquered: Soerian, Poengej Blang Tjoet, [[Poe Oe]] and Setee. The aim was to gain a forward line protecting the Marassa area and the K. Radja–[[Oeleh Leh]] communication, bring [[Bital]] and Lamtermin under observation, and threaten the flank and rear of [[Toekoe Nanta]] of the [[VI Moekims]]. On the day itself the Dutch failed to take Soerian outright — though they killed some 70 of the enemy in a surrounded *benteng* — but in the days following the Atjehneezen abandoned all their western positions and the magnificent *sawah* (irrigated rice fields) stretching to the edge of Bital and [[Ketapang Doewa]] fell into Dutch hands. Setee was, however, evidently reoccupied by the enemy, for it appears again as a target of the [[Conquest of Mandarsa Poeti (28 January 1875)|conquest of Mandarsa Poeti]] of [[28 January]] [[1875]], when one of the converging columns moved on Setee to cover the right flank of the main attack on the stone *gebedenhuis*.

## The final conquest

Setee was finally conquered in April [[1875]] in the same series of operations that took the fortified kampong Lohong — "an extremely important point" — by surprise and brought the whole sawah south of [[Longbatta]] into Dutch hands. Westward, the kampongs Setee, Lamteboe and Gitjiel were conquered and occupied "at cost of fresh bloody sacrifices." With Setee in Dutch hands, the continuous line around K. Radja could be completed. By early May [[1875]] the south side of the *stelling-Pel* ran from Oedjoeng Plungan through [[Soerian]], [[Poe Oe]], Poengej Blang Tjoet, Lamteboe, Setee, [[Lamara Olejlo]], Lohong, Longbatta Z. to [[Longbatta-Mesigit]] — a line whose completion allowed operations to be suspended or postponed, and on the strength of which [[Generaal Pel|Pel]] was granted three months' leave on [[12 June]] [[1875]] to recover his shattered health.
