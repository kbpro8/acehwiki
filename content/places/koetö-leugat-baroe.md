**Koetö Leugat Baroe** (fortified kampong) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Fortified kampong in the Gajo/Alas area; once it fell to Van Daalen in 1904, armed resistance in Alaslanden was broken and most Alasser chiefs came to submit.

## Type

fortified [[Achehnese geographical terms|kampong]]

## Description

Fortified [[Achehnese geographical terms|kampong]] in the [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]]/[[Alas]] area; once it fell to [[Gouverneur Van Daalen|Van Daalen]] in [[1904]], armed [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] in [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]] was broken and most Alasser chiefs came to submit.

## Significance

Koetö Leugat Baroe is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
