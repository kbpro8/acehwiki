**Glé Geuroctèë** (viewpoint) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Famous viewpoint on Westkust, 68 km from Koetaradja.

## Type

viewpoint

## Description

Famous viewpoint on [[Westkust]], 68 km from [[Koetaradja]]. Road at ~160 m above sea, terraced view of the [[Indische Oceaan|Indian Ocean]]. Coastline so steep below that surf cannot be seen or heard from there.

## Significance

Glé Geuroctèë is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
