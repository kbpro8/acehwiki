**Iram (mythical city)** is a legendary city of towering pillars mentioned in the Qur’an and Islamic tradition, famously associated with the arrogant King [[Shaddad]]. The Malay translation of the *Burda* references Iram as a symbol of worldly grandeur that is ultimately doomed by divine judgment.

## Qur’anic and Classical Sources

Iram is alluded to in the Qur’an (e.g., [[Qur’an 89:5‑6]]) and elaborated in early Arabic literature as a city of great wealth and architectural marvels, later destroyed for its inhabitants’ impiety.

## Acehnese Literary Context

In the Acehnese Malay version of the *Burda*, Iram serves as a rhetorical contrast to the spiritual purity of the Prophet [[Muhammad]]. Its inclusion reflects the diffusion of pan‑Islamic mythic motifs into Acehnese devotional texts.
