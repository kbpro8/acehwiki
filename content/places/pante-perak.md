**Pante Perak** (also written Pantej Perak; modern Pantai Perak, "silver beach") is the grass plain to the north of [[Koeta Radja|Koeta Radja]] on which both the principal hospital of the Dutch garrison and a temporary troop camp were established in the early years of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. The hospital at Pante Perak was the place of work of [[Dominee Thenu|Dominee Thenu]], the soldiers' minister who comforted and lifted up the wounded, the sick, and the sorrowful. The troop camp, built in [[1875]] by the Staf der Genie at [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] under the direction of [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede-Luitenant C. J. Snijders]], relieved the pressure of quarters in the [[Kraton of Aceh|Kraton]] itself and in [[May 1875|May]] [[1875]] received the [[Bataillon Mineurs en Sappeurs|Compagnie Mineurs en Sappeurs]], "hetgeen in vergelijking met het tot dusver in gebruik zijnde logies in den Kraton een grooten vooruitgang beteekende."

## The Hospital and Dominee Thenu

One saw the silent, modest figure of this proclaimer of God's Word also in the hospital of Pante Perak. The hospital of Pante Perak is the book's marker of Thenu's work as a soul-shepherd: the soldiers' minister who was seen in the hospital, where he comforted and lifted up the wounded, the sick, and the sorrowful. The hospital at Pante Perak thus served, in the early phase of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]], both as the principal medical institution of the Dutch garrison at [[Koeta Radja|Koeta Radja]] and as the pastoral field of the soldiers' minister.

## The Troop Camp of 1875

After the close of his field engineer service with the [[Lamara Olejlo fortification (15 February 1875)|Lamara Olejlo fortification of 15 February]] [[1875]], [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] was attached to the Staf der Genie at [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]]. Among the peacetime works listed by the biographer [[D. van den Berg|Van den Berg]] is "het bouwen van een tijdelijk troepenkampement in de grasvlakte van Pantej Perak (ten N. van Kota Radja)." The camp was built to relieve the pressure of quarters in the [[Kraton of Aceh|Kraton]] itself, which had served as the principal Dutch billet since the [[Capture of the Kraton (24 January 1874)|capture of the Kraton]] of [[24 January]] [[24 January]] [[1874]]. In [[May 1875|May]] [[1875]] Snijders' own [[Bataillon Mineurs en Sappeurs|Compagnie Mineurs en Sappeurs]] moved into the new camp, a marked improvement on the previous Kraton quarters.

## Significance

Pante Perak is significant in *[[Oost-Indisch Journael|Oost-Indisch Journael]]* as the place of the hospital in which [[Dominee Thenu|Thenu]] was seen; it is significant in the [[1944]] biography of [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] as the site of the temporary troop camp that, in [[May 1875|May]] [[1875]], relieved the pressure of quarters in the [[Kraton of Aceh|Kraton]] and gave the [[Bataillon Mineurs en Sappeurs|Compagnie Mineurs en Sappeurs]] a proper base of operations. The construction of the camp is one of the small but cumulative improvements by which the Dutch position at [[Koeta Radja|Koeta Radja]] was transformed, in the course of [[1874]]–[[1875]], from a besieged foothold in the captured Kraton into a permanent garrison with proper troop accommodation, an ice factory for the Medical Service, a cattle-pen for 400 head outside the town, a narrow-gauge railway to [[Oleh-leh|Olehleh]], and a drained and embanked perimeter. Pante Perak — the "silver beach" — also gives its name to the principal beach of modern [[Banda Aceh|Banda Aceh]].
