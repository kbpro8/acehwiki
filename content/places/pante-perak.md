**Pante Perak** is the place of the hospital in [[Atjeh|Aceh]] — the hospital in which, in *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* by Merac ([[1947]]), one saw the silent, modest figure of this proclaimer of God's Word — that is, the hospital in which [[Dominee Thenu|Dominee Thenu]] was seen by the wounded, the sick, and the sorrowful whom he comforted and lifted up with his earnest word.

## The hospital and Thenu

One saw the silent, modest figure of this proclaimer of God's Word also in the hospital of Pante Perak. The hospital of Pante Perak is the book's marker of Thenu's work as a soul-shepherd: the soldiers' minister who was seen in the hospital, where he comforted and lifted up the wounded, the sick, and the sorrowful.

## Significance

Pante Perak is significant in *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* as the place of the hospital in which [[Dominee Thenu|Thenu]] was seen. The book's argument — that the soldiers' minister was seen in the hospital of Pante Perak, where he comforted and lifted up the wounded, the sick, and the sorrowful — is the book's marker of Thenu's work as a soul-shepherd. Pante Perak is therefore the book's marker of the hospital where the soldiers' minister was seen.
