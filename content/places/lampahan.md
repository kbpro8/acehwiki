**Lampahan** — also corrected to *Lampahan* from the OCR's *Lamsanan* — is a place on the [[Gajo-weg|Gajo-weg]] between [[Blang Rakal|Blang Rakal]] and [[Takengon|Takengon]], at kilometre 84, where the traveller can refresh himself with a cool or warm drink at the coffee-house of a Chinese who slowly but surely grows rich through his coffee-house and his trade with the coming and going men and women. There one finds the carts and the unyoked draught-animals of the Atjehnese who help to maintain the transport between [[Bireuen|Bireuen]] and the Gajo-land. A return trip between Bireuen and Takengon — the 105 kilometres — they do in no less than fifteen days; they travel by night and by day they stay at the stopping-places. *'Tijd is hier geen geld'* — *Time is no money here.*
