The **Kerkhof te Peutjoet** was the military cemetery at [[Peutjoet]] near [[Koeta Radja]] in which many of the dead of the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* and of the wider [[Atjeh-oorlog|Atjeh war]] were buried, and before which the [[Uitreiking van het vaandel aan het Korps Maréchaussée (2 April 1930)|colour was to be presented]] to the corps on [[2 April]] [[1930]].

## Description in the jubilee number

"Over geheel Atjeh liggen de graven dier dapperen verspreid" — over the whole of Atjeh the graves of those brave men lie scattered — but many rested at the Kerkhof te Peutjoet near Koeta Radja, "wier namen in den muur zijn gebeiteld als een stille, doch sprekende hulde aan hun nagedachtenis" — whose names are chiselled into the wall as a silent but eloquent tribute to their memory.

The [[1930]] jubilee number described the cemetery in elevated terms: "de doodenakker van Peutjoet is een epos, waarvan de grootsche handeling is de vestiging en bevestiging van het Nederlandsch Gezag, een stuk gewijde grond, hetwelk niet dan met ontroering kan worden betreden" — the field of the dead at Peutjoet is an epic whose grand action is the establishment and confirmation of Netherlands authority, a piece of consecrated ground which cannot be trodden but with emotion. "Daar ligt de geschiedenis van den Atjeh-oorlog beschreven, de historie eener lange worsteling, waarin de Maréchaussée de meest-eervolle plaats heeft ingenomen."

## The busts

Busts of four officers of the corps adorned the cemetery "als zoovele symbolen van moed, beleid en trouw" — as so many symbols of courage, conduct and fidelity, the words engraved on the knight's cross of the *[[Militaire Willemsorde|Militaire Willems-Orde]]*: [[W. B. J. A. Scheepens|Scheepens]], [[F. Darlang|Darlang]], [[H. M. Vis|Vis]] and [[Webb]].

## The vaandel ceremony

The presentation of the colour to the corps was to take place "straks voor het Kerkhof te Peutjoet" — shortly, before the cemetery at Peutjoet — and the editorial concluded that on that occasion "past ons een eeresaluut aan die dapperen" — a salute of honour to those brave men befitted the corps.

## Casualties commemorated

In the period [[1890]]–[[1930]] approximately 950 members of the Korps Maréchaussée fell on the field of battle and about 250 were wounded. Most of the deaths, the jubilee account records, occurred during the *[[Patrouille (Maréchaussée)|patrouilles]]*.
