**Wikkie creek** was a waterway in the interior of [[Acheh]] that featured in the Dutch December expedition. On [[25 December]] Dutch troops attempted a landing using a “tent‑boat” under the command of [[Captain Van Oyen]]. The landing was poorly coordinated, leading to confusion and a failed assault on rebel positions.

## Military Incident
The incident is described in the [[Journal of Haringman]] as a “unforgivable mistake” caused by officers acting against orders.
