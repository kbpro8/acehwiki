**Dalem** (palace/fortified residence of the Sultan) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Sultan's palace/fortified residence at Koetaradja.

## Type

palace/fortified residence of [[Sultan of Aceh|the Sultan]]

## Description

[[sultan|Sultan]]'s palace/fortified residence at [[Koetaradja]]. Source of the [[Tjakra Donja]] bronze bell. Court fled the Dalem twice to [[Keumala]] ([[1878]] and [[1898]]). After conquest, replaced by the Gouvernementswoning ([[Gouvernementswoning (Koetaradja)|Governor's residence]]).

## Significance

Dalem is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
