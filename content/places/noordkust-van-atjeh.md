**Noordkust van Atjeh** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Afdeeling of North Coast of Aceh; includes Pidië, Bireuën, Sigli, etc.

## Description

[[afdeeling|Afdeeling]] of [[North Coast]] of Aceh; includes [[Pidië]], [[Bireuën]], [[Sigli]], etc. Major rice, [[Kapok Fiber|kapok]], salt producer. Cattle census: 70,418 runderen / 25,566 karbouwen.

## Significance

Noordkust van Atjeh is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
