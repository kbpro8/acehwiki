**Lam-Tobah** — written in Dutch as *Lam-Tobah* — was the place to which [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb]] and Panglima Nja Makam retreated after the [[23 June]] [[1896]] Dutch attack on [[Kroeng-Kali]]. As described in Kruisheer's *ATJEH '96*, Lam-Tobah was the destination of the Achehnese retreat after the [[Battle of Kroeng-Kali (23 June 1896)|battle of Kroeng-Kali]] in which the compagnie of [[P. Linck|Kapitein Linck]] stormed the old fort of Kroeng-Kali and 2nd Lt. [[Heyligers]] was first inside. The kampong is mentioned only briefly in the present volume as the refuge of the defeated Achehnese leaders.

## Description and Location

Lam-Tobah lay in the [[XIII Moekims Toengkoeb]] of [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb]] to the south or south-east of [[Kroeng-Kali]]. The kampong was the destination of the Achehnese retreat after the [[23 June]] [[1896]] Dutch attack on Kroeng-Kali, and served as a refuge for the defeated Achehnese leaders [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb]] and Panglima Nja Makam. The kampong was situated in the country between [[Kroeng-Kali]] and the mountain wall at whose edge [[Habib Lengkong]]'s fort stood, and was presumably accessible by the routes that led from Kroeng-Kali into the mountainous interior of the [[XIII Moekims Toengkoeb]]. The kampong's specific location and fortifications are not detailed in *ATJEH '96*.

## Historical Background

Lam-Tobah had presumably been part of the territory of the *hoeloebalang* [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb]] throughout the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. The kampong's role as a refuge for the defeated Achehnese leaders after the [[23 June]] [[1896]] Dutch attack on Kroeng-Kali indicates that it lay outside the immediate sone of Dutch operations and was accessible to the retreating Achehnese. The kampong had presumably been used as a refuge on earlier occasions as well, given the persistence of Achehnese resistance in the [[XIII Moekims Toengkoeb]] despite repeated Dutch expeditions.

## Role in the 1896 Operations

Lam-Tobah is mentioned in *ATJEH '96* only in connection with the [[23 June]] [[1896]] Dutch attack on [[Kroeng-Kali]]. After the compagnie of [[P. Linck|Kapitein Linck]] stormed the old *benteng* of Kroeng-Kali — a square redoubt of 15 metres side, walls 1.5 metres high, with a small swivel cannon — and 2nd Lt. [[Heyligers]] was first inside, the *benteng* was destroyed. [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb]] and Panglima Nja Makam retreated to Lam-Tobah. The kampong thereby served as the immediate refuge of the principal Achehnese leaders of the [[XIII Moekims Toengkoeb]] after the loss of their principal *benteng*.

## Significance

Lam-Tobah was the immediate refuge of the defeated Achehnese leaders [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb]] and Panglima Nja Makam after the [[23 June]] [[1896]] Dutch attack on [[Kroeng-Kali]], and thus the temporary destination of the eastern Achehnese resistance after the loss of its principal *benteng*. The kampong's mention in *ATJEH '96* is brief, but its role as a refuge indicates the persistence of Achehnese resistance in the [[XIII Moekims Toengkoeb]] despite the repeated Dutch expeditions against Kroeng-Kali.
