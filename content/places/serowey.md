**Seroeway** (also referenced as **Oud‑Seroeway**) was a riverine landing site on the **Tamiang** river used by Dutch forces during the **February‑April [[1893]]** expeditions. The column arrived there on **[[13 February]] [[1893]]**, crossed the river the following morning, and used the location as a staging point for further inland advances.

## Strategic Importance

Its position allowed the Dutch to establish a foothold on the opposite bank, facilitating the movement of infantry, marines, and river‑borne artillery.

## Military Activity

During the second expedition, the **landing division** returned to **Seroeway** on **[[30 March]] [[1893]]** after breaking through river blockades with the steamship **[[Koerier (ship)]]**.
