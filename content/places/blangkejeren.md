**Blangkejeren** was/is a principal valley settlement and administrative hub of [[Gayo Lèuëos]] in the central highlands of Aceh (modern Gayo Lues Regency).

## Historical Context and Role

Captured during the [[1904]] expedition, Blangkejeren was designated in [[1910]] as the official garrison post and headquarters for the 1st Division of the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]].
