**Lamara Olejlo** (also written Lamara Oleilo) was a kampong to the south of [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] whose fortified northern edge was occupied by Dutch troops in a surprise attack in the night of [[14 February|14]]/[[15 February]] [[15 February]] [[1875]] and was thereafter fortified and held by the Dutch as part of the southward extension of the Dutch perimeter in the middle phase of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. The kampong, together with the neighbouring kampongs of Gitjiel Olejlo and Lohong, had been one of the strongpoints from which the [[Achehnese|Atjehers]] continued to harass the [[Mandarsah Poetih|Mandarsah Poetih]]. The fortification of the captured benting on [[15 February]] [[15 February]] [[1875]] — see [[Lamara Olejlo fortification (15 February 1875)|Lamara Olejlo fortification]] — was the last field engineer operation of [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede-Luitenant C. J. Snijders]] in Atjeh.

## Context

After the [[Missigit Longbatta operation (31 December 1874 – 13 January 1875)|Missigit Longbatta operation]] and the [[Capture of Pungej Blang Tjoet (23 January 1875)|capture of Pungej Blang Tjoet]] on [[23 January]] [[23 January]] and of [[Mandarsah Poetih|Mandarsah Poetih]] on [[28 January]] [[28 January]] [[1875]], the southern line of Dutch outworks was repeatedly harassed from the fortified and partly gun-armed edges of the kampongs Gitjiel Olejlo, [[Lamara Olejlo|Lamara Olejlo]], and Lohong. A reconnaissance in the night of [[14 February|14]]/[[15 February]] [[15 February]] [[1875]] led to the surprise occupation of some fortifications on the northern edge of [[Lamara Olejlo|Lamara Olejlo]].

## The Fortification of 15 February 1875

[[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] received orders at midday on [[15 February]] [[15 February]] at [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] and marched with part of the company and a detachment of 100 dwangarbeiders, with a quantity of tools and sandbags, to put the captured benting in a state of defence. The full operation is described on the [[Lamara Olejlo fortification (15 February 1875)|operation page]]. With the completion of the work Snijders' task with the field column ended.
