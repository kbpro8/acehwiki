**Lamara Olejlo** (also written Lamara Oleilo) was a kampong to the south of [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] whose fortified northern edge was occupied by Dutch troops in a surprise attack in the night of [[14 February|14]]/[[15 February]] [[15 February]] [[1875]] and was thereafter fortified and held by the Dutch as part of the southward extension of the Dutch perimeter in the middle phase of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. The kampong, together with the neighbouring kampongs of Gitjiel Olejlo and Lohong, had been one of the strongpoints from which the [[Achehnese|Atjehers]] continued to harass the [[Mandarsah Poetih|Mandarsah Poetih]]. The fortification of the captured benting on [[15 February]] [[15 February]] [[1875]] — see [[Lamara Olejlo fortification (15 February 1875)|Lamara Olejlo fortification]] — was the last field engineer operation of [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede-Luitenant C. J. Snijders]] in Atjeh.

## Context

After the [[Missigit Longbatta operation (31 December 1874 – 13 January 1875)|Missigit Longbatta operation]] and the [[Capture of Pungej Blang Tjoet (23 January 1875)|capture of Pungej Blang Tjoet]] on [[23 January]] [[23 January]] and of [[Mandarsah Poetih|Mandarsah Poetih]] on [[28 January]] [[28 January]] [[1875]], the southern line of Dutch outworks was repeatedly harassed from the fortified and partly gun-armed edges of the kampongs Gitjiel Olejlo, [[Lamara Olejlo|Lamara Olejlo]], and Lohong. A reconnaissance in the night of [[14 February|14]]/[[15 February]] [[15 February]] [[1875]] led to the surprise occupation of some fortifications on the northern edge of [[Lamara Olejlo|Lamara Olejlo]].

## The Fortification of 15 February 1875

[[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] received orders at midday on [[15 February]] [[15 February]] at [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] and marched with part of the company and a detachment of 100 dwangarbeiders, with a quantity of tools and sandbags, to put the captured benting in a state of defence. The full operation is described on the [[Lamara Olejlo fortification (15 February 1875)|operation page]]. With the completion of the work Snijders' task with the field column ended.

## Account from *Een Vaderlandsch Belang* by H. Borel (1878)

**Lamara Olejlo** was an Atjehnees kampong about 600 paces south of [[Mandarsa Poeti]] seized by surprise on the night of [[14 February|14]]–[[15 February]] [[1875]] in the [[Surprise of Lamara Olejlo (14–15 February 1875)|surprise of Lamara Olejlo]] by [[Kapitein von Ende|kapitein von Ende]], the commandant of the new Mandarsa Poeti post. The capture yielded three or four enemy *benteng* (fortifications), several cannon and *lilla*'s, and permanently extended the southern arc of the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]] at exactly the point where it had been weakest.

## The surprise

[[Kapitein von Ende|Von Ende]] had noticed that the enemy left the fortification at the northern *rand* of Lamara each evening and returned in the morning. He verified this personally by creeping across the *sawah* (irrigated rice field) on a dark night with a few men. Without asking permission, von Ende crossed the sawah in the night of [[14 February|14]]–[[15 February]] [[1875]] with part of the Mandarsa Poeti garrison and occupied the abandoned fortification. He at once reported to [[Generaal Pel|Pel]] and asked for reinforcements. Between 8 and 4 a.m. the Atjehneezen arrived as usual — to considerable surprise they met with Beaumont salvos. Von Ende beat off their attempt to retake the works and held with a handful until reinforcements under [[Kolonel van Dentekom|overste van Dentekom]] arrived, then [[Majoor Romswinckel|majoor Romswinckel]]. Fighting continued all morning as the enemy streamed in from all sides. The whole Lamara Olejlo stelling remained permanently in Dutch hands.

## The captured guns and Pel's judgement

Three or four enemy *benteng*, several cannon and *lilla*'s fell into Dutch hands, including a 4-pounder that had fired on Mandarsa Poeti — it had no carriage but was ingeniously suspended on ropes. Von Ende received from Pel both formal praise for his courage and a reprimand for his unauthorised action; "he had fully deserved both; achieved success made everything good." The cost was one dead, about 12 severely wounded, and two officers wounded after the fight was already over. Lamara Olejlo then became a permanent post of the southern arc of the *stelling-Pel*, linking [[Setee]] to Lohong and [[Longbatta]] Z. on the south side of the line. Its capture was, Borel notes, the kind of aggressive initiative that [[Jan van Swieten|van Swieten]]'s passive *afwachtende houding* had forbidden; that it succeeded so completely was the strongest possible vindication of [[Generaal Pel|Pel]]'s more aggressive *aanvallende tactiek*.
