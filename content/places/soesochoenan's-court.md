**Soesochoenan's court** (Javanese principality court) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. The Susuhunan (of Solo, Surakarta) bought one of the female army elephants from Tangsé.

## Type

Javanese principality court

## Description

The Susuhunan (of [[Surakarta|Solo]], [[Surakarta]]) bought one of the female army elephants from [[Tangsé]].

## Significance

Soesochoenan's court is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
