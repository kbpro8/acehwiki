**Tadoe** was the district near [[Meulaboh]] in which the great resistance leaders began to show signs of accommodation in [[1909]].

After [[T. Moeda Mat Sa'it]] fell on the upper [[Tripa]] in March [[1909]], the submission in [[Seunagan]], Tadoe and Tripa of all his *pangs* and band members followed. In October [[1910]] a notorious band-head from Tadoe came in submission with sixty-nine men and six blunderbusses — "they were no longer well supplied with rifles." In March [[1914]] [[T. Radja Tampo']] levied tribute on inhabitants of Tadoe and Tripa.
