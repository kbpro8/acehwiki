**Panteh-Perak** was a Dutch post in [[Groot-Atjeh]] where the military hospital stood to which, during August–September [[1879]], the sick of the [[Lamtoba column (1879)|Lamtoba column]] were evacuated by sea from [[Lampanas]] and [[Oleh-leh]], as recorded in [[W. Cool]] and [[G. B. Hooijer]]'s *[[Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis (Cool & Hooijer, 1889)|Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis]]* ([[1889]]).

## The evacuations of 1879

By [[10 August]] [[1879]], five per cent of the rank and file of the column at [[Lamtoba]] had already been sent down to Lampanas, and thence, by the steamship [[Graaf van Bijland (stoomschip)|Graaf van Bijland]], evacuated to the hospital at Panteh-Perak. The stream continued through the quarantine month: in the final reckoning of the campaign 346 men stood evacuated, of whom 119 European and 62 native soldiers, 54 coolies and 109 convict labourers, besides 39 dead. From the last division, landed at Oleh-leh on [[3 September]], several men remained behind sick at Panteh-Perak when the artillery marched on to the posts of [[Toengkoep]] and [[Lambaroe]].

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

[[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] adds: in the night of [[5 June|5]]-[[6 June]] [[1885]] the sentry behind the Dutch kampement (encampment) at Panteh Perak, in the immediate neighbourhood of [[Kuta Raja|Kota Radja]], was attacked, severely wounded and robbed of his rifle (see [[Guerilla raids inside the concentration line (1885)]]).
