**Peereboom plantation** was a sugar plantation in the interior of [[Acheh]] that was set ablaze during the Dutch expedition of December. On [[24 December]] a patrol reported that the sugar mill and the cooking house were burning; later, two sick enslaved women testified that rebels had ignited the fire three days earlier.

## Aftermath
The destruction of Peereboom illustrated the rebels’ willingness to destroy economic assets to hinder Dutch operations. The plantation remained in ruins for the remainder of the campaign.
