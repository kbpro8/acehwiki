**Bangkallan** (modern Bangkalan Regency) was a regency and native principality located on the western coast of the island of Madura, East Java, Indonesia, which served as a primary recruitment center for indigenous auxiliary troops of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] during the Aceh War.

## Military Recruitment for Aceh

Under colonial treaty obligations with the [[Dutch East Indies Government]], the rulers of Bangkallan organized auxiliary infantry units known as the [[Barissans of Bangkallan]]. 

In [[1873]], [[1874]], and [[1875]], thousands of Madurese soldiers from Bangkallan were transported to Aceh, serving as storm troops and flank guards under General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]] during the [[Attack on Mibouw]] and the [[March through the XXII Moekims]] in [[1876]].
