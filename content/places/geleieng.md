**Gléiëng** was the location in [[Groot-Atjeh|Greater Atjeh]] where [[Panglima Polém|Panglima Polém]] positioned himself after the loss of his *stelling* at [[Glé Jeuëng|Glé Jeuëng]] in May [[1897]]. The Dutch planned to establish posts at Gléiëng and [[Selimoen|Selimoen]], and [[Panglima Polém|Polém]] declared he did not believe the Company would actually do so. When assured that it would, and reminded of the fate of [[T. Baid|T. Baid]] and [[T. Tjoet Lamtengah|T. Tjoet Lamtengah]], Polém reportedly answered he would think about it "when the time came." The Dutch press also referred to Gléiëng as "Glieng" — the advanced position in the [[XXII Moekims|XXII Moekim]] where Van Heutsz was stationed and where surprises could be expected at any moment.
