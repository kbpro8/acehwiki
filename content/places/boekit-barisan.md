**Boekit Barisan** was the mountain range of [[Sumatra]] whose passes [[Gotfried Coenraad Ernst van Daalen|Van Daalen]] crossed with two hundred *[[Corps of Military Constabulary of Atjeh and Dependencies|marechaussées]]* on his march into the [[Gajòlanden|Gajò]] and [[Alaslanden|Alas]] interior.

Its limestone outliers formed the inhospitable complex behind the plain of the [[IV Moekims Lhong]], in which a band could hold out only in a few caves — the terrain that made the suppression of the [[Resistance in Daja and Lhong (1914)|Lhong resistance]] of [[1914]] "no difficult opposition."
