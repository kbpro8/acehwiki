**The 22 Moekims** — *Twenty-two Moekims*, written in Dutch *22 Moekims* — was the inland *Sagi* (third administrative division) of [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]], one of the three *Sagi's* of the sultanate together with the [[25 Moekims|25]] and [[26 Moekims|26 Moekims]] and the [[Sultan's gebied|Sultan's own territory]]. The [[Conquest of the 22 Moekims (March-June 1879)|Dutch conquest of the 22 Moekims]] under [[General van der Heijden|Van der Heijden]] from [[March 1879|March]] to half [[June 1879|June]] [[1879]] was the principal military success of Van der Heijden's governorship and brought the entire valley of Groot-Atjeh under Dutch control for the first and only time before the final pacification under [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] after [[1898]].

## Geography

The 22 Moekims was the inland *Sagi* of [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] — the only one of the three Sagi's that lay inland rather than on the sea. The two seaward Sagi's (the [[25 Moekims|25]] and [[26 Moekims|26 Moekims]]) lay on the coast; the 22 Moekims lay inland. The Dutch had mainly occupied the 25 Moekims and the [[Sultan's gebied|Sultan's territory]]. All of Groot-Atjeh was to be brought under.

## The Conquest (March – June 1879)

The [[Conquest of the 22 Moekims (March-June 1879)|conquest of the 22 Moekims]] began in [[March 1879|March]] [[1879]] and was completed by half [[June 1879|June]] [[1879]]. The enemy, fighting as he withdrew, fought with great courage but was everywhere defeated and driven out. After his *kampongs* and possessions had been destroyed, he fled to the mountains. Along the [[Atjeh River|Atjeh-rivier]] a line of posts was occupied.

## The Loss under Civil Government (1881–1884)

The conquest of the 22 Moekims would prove transient. Under the civil government of [[Pruijs van der Hoeven|Pruijs van der Hoeven]] (1881–1882) and [[Laging Tobias|Laging Tobias]] (1883–1884), the endless patrols that Van der Heijden had deemed necessary to maintain the peace were virtually abolished. In [[June 1878|June]] [[1878]] — even before the conquest of the 22 Moekims — [[Habib Abdul Rachman El-Zahir|Habib Abdul Rachman]] had invaded the Dutch *stelling* and the enemy had pressed into the 22 and 26 Moekims. Under civil government, "steeds stoutmoediger drongen sterke vijandige benden de 22 en 26 Moekims binnen en op ons gebied door" (ever bolder, strong enemy bands pressed into the 22 and 26 Moekims and onto Dutch territory), even into the immediate vicinity of [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] and [[Oleh-leh|Oleh-leh]]. The [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration system]] of [[1884]] contracted the Dutch *stelling* back to the perimeter of [[1876]], abandoning the 22 Moekims to the enemy.

## Significance

The 22 Moekims is the inland *Sagi* of [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] and the principal inland theatre of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. Its [[Conquest of the 22 Moekims (March-June 1879)|conquest by Van der Heijden]] in [[March 1879|March]]–[[June 1879|June]] [[1879]] was the principal military success of Van der Heijden's governorship; its loss under civil government was the principal disaster of the [[Civil Government Period (1881-1884)|civil government period]]; its abandonment by the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration system]] of [[1884]] contracted the Dutch *stelling* back to the perimeter of [[1876]]. The final pacification under Van Heutsz after [[1898]] would re-establish Dutch control over the 22 Moekims.
