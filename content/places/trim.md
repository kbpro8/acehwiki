The town in [[Hadramaut]] where the great saint [[Sayyid Abdallah Al-Haddad]] is buried. The Kisah Abdolah Hadat by [[Cheh Marahaban]] describes the 'rich blessings given forth by him while yet alive and even after his death from his grave at Trim ([[Hadramaut]]).'
