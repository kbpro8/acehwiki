**Pakan Badak** was a kampong and important market-place in the [[VI Moekims|VI Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]], occupied by the Dutch on [[31 December]] [[31 December]] [[1875]] at the end of the first phase of the conquest of the VI Moekims, and used as the base for the operations against the [[IV Moekims|IV Moekims]] in [[January 1876|January 1876]]. The market was a principal centre of trade in Great Acheh, with two large plank buildings on piles, and its capture gave the Dutch an important point for the projection of their power into the IV Moekims.

## The Capture, 31 December 1875

On [[26 December]] [[26 December]] [[1875]] at five in the morning three columns left the Dutch hoofd-stelling for the conquest of the [[VI Moekims|VI Moekims]] south-west of [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]]. The enemy's fortifications at [[Mibouw|Mibouw]], [[Ketapan Doewa|Ketapan Doewa]] and [[Djempit|Djempit]] were taken and Pakan Badak was occupied. "In six days the strong enemy line opposite our posts south of Kota Radja had been broken, turned and abandoned by the enemy. We were wholly master of the VI Moekims and, by the occupation of Pakan Badak, in possession of an important point that was to serve as a base for operations against the IV Moekims."

On [[31 December]] [[31 December]] [1875] the troops reached the last place, which was an important market-place. "Everything bore witness at Pakan Badak to prosperity and to much trade. On the rather large pasar stood, in parallel direction, two plank buildings, each about 35 metres long, on piles, one metre above the ground. Both had a continuous voorgalerij and were internally divided into compartments of 5 to 6 metres in the square; in nearly all these rooms, usually serving as lodgings for the merchants, one found empty cupboards and chests of European make, solid and provided with excellent locks. Besides these two buildings there were many others, more or less solid, all evidently arranged for trade; household goods, especially pots, pans and plates, one found there in great quantity."

## The Base for the IV Moekims, January 1876

From Pakan Badak the operations in the [[IV Moekims|IV Moekims]] were to take place. As the principal defenders of the VI Moekims had fled thither, such as [[Toekoe Nanta|Toekoe Nanta]] and [[Toekoe Tjihik Lamnga|Toekoe Tjihik Lamnga]], a strong resistance was to be expected, even though the head of the IV Moekims, [[Toekoe Lampassej|Toekoe Lampassej]], was on the Dutch side. On [[15 January]] [[15 January]] [[1876]] the advance into the IV Moekims began over the [[pas van Blang Kala|pas of Blang Kala]]. On [[25 January]] [[25 January]] [1876] the troops returned to Pakan Badak; in ten days, from [[28 January]] [[28 January]] to [[7 February]] [[7 February]], the [[IX Moekims|IX Moekims]] was also reduced.

## The Attack of June 1878

By the spring of [[1878]] the [[Habib Abdoerrahman|Habib]] was once more on the offensive. As soon as [[K. van der Heyden|Van der Heyden]] had departed for [[Gedong|Gedong]], news came of the Habib's intention to make a great attack with 2,000 men in the IV Moekims on the posts at [[Kroëng Raba|Kroëng Raba]], [[Boekit Seboen|Boekit Seboen]] and Pakan Badak.
