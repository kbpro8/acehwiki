**Bantam (Banten)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Comparison; if similar incidents happened in Bantam or Zuid-Borneo it would not be a big issue, but in Aceh it was different.

## Description

Comparison; if similar incidents happened in [[Banten|Bantam]] or [[Zuid-Borneo]] it would not be a big issue, but in Aceh it was different.

## Significance

Bantam (Banten) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
