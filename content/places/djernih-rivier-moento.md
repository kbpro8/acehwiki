**Djernih rivier Moento** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Original natuurmonument designated 1921; merged 1936 into Rafflesia natuurmonument Serbödjadi.

## Description

Original [[Natuurmonument|natuurmonument]] designated [[1921]]; merged [[1936]] into Rafflesia [[Natuurmonument|natuurmonument]] [[Serbödjadi]].

## Significance

Djernih rivier Moento is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
