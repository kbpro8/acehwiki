**The III Moekims** — the "Three Mukims" — were one of the smaller mukim-clusters of the immediate Sultan's territory in [[Groot-Atjeh]], and the territory over which [[Habib Abdurrahman Zahir|Habib Abdoe'r Rachman]] — the principal religious-political figure of the early Dutch war period — was given the title of *Hoofd van Tjot Poetee in de III Moekims* (Head of Tjot Poetee in the III Moekims) as one of the rewards for his service to the Sultan. The III Moekims are named in the [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] in the chapter on the *Inwendige Toestand* as one of the constituent mukim-clusters of the Sultan's immediate territory.

## The Sultan's immediate territory

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that the immediate territory of the Sultan — distinct from the three great *sagis* — included the [[Kraton of Aceh|Kraton]] and [[Kota Radja]], the [[Taman (Kota Goenoengan)|Taman]], [[Marassa]], [[Longbatta|Longbattah]], the III Moekims, the [[IV Moekims]], the [[VI Moekims]] and the [[IX Moekims]]. The III Moekims thus formed part of the directly-ruled core of the sultanate — the mukim-cluster over which the Sultan exercised direct temporal authority.

## The Habib's reward

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that, after the conflict between [[Habib Abdurrahman Zahir|Habib Abdoe'r Rachman]] and [[Toekoe Moeda Baid]] — in which the Habib, at the head of a force of two thousand men from the [[XXVI Moekims]], had marched on [[Toekoe Moeda Baid|Baid]]'s *kampong* of [[Tjot Bada]] to compel the surrender of the young Sultan [[Toeankoe Machmoed]] — the Habib was rewarded for his services with the title of *Hoofd van den Godsdienst* (Head of Religion), *Hoofd van Tjot Poetee in de III Moekims* (Head of Tjot Poetee in the III Moekims) and *Chef van de Groote Moskee* (Chief of the Great Mosque). The title of *Hoofd van Tjot Poetee in de III Moekims* was thus one of the three principal rewards given to the Habib for his service to the Sultan — and it placed the III Moekims under the Habib's temporal authority, in addition to his religious authority.

## Significance

The III Moekims' significance in the documentary record of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] is as one of the constituent mukim-clusters of the Sultan's immediate territory, and as the territory over which [[Habib Abdurrahman Zahir|Habib Abdoe'r Rachman]] was given the title of *Hoofd van Tjot Poetee* — one of the three principal rewards for his service to the Sultan. The title was the principal temporal office held by the Habib, and it placed the III Moekims under his direct authority — together with the religious authority that he exercised as *Hoofd van den Godsdienst* and *Chef van de Groote Moskee*.
