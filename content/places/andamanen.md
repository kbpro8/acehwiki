**Andamanen** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Island group north of Sumatra, mentioned as part of the great folding-mountain arc that includes the Boekit Barisan.

## Description

Island group north of Sumatra, mentioned as part of the great folding-mountain arc that includes the [[Boekit Barisan]].

## Significance

Andamanen is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
