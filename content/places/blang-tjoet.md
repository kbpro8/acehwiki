**Blang Tjoet** was an Atjehnees kampong between [[Pendetti]] and [[Longbatta-Mesigit]] on the [[Atjeh-rivier|left bank of the Atjeh-rivier]], conquered and garrisoned on [[18 January]] [[1875]] in the [[Conquest of Blang Tjoet (18 January 1875)|conquest of Blang Tjoet]] by [[Kolonel Ruempol|overste Ruempol]]. The new post, built on an abandoned Atjehnees *benteng* about 400–500 paces from the Mesigit, made transport between the Mesigit and Pendetti practically safe and was the scene of sharp fighting on [[7 January]] [[1875]] when a convoy was heavily attacked in the dense brush near the kampong.

## The conquest

After the [[Battle of Longbatta (31 December 1874)|Longbatta operation of 31 December 1874]] the area around Blang Tjoet was the scene of recurring clashes. On [[4 January]] [[1875]] [[Generaal Pel|Pel]] with two columns reconnoitred and cleared the terrain south of [[Pendetti]] and the kampongs of [[Lampoe Idjoe]], [[Lampoe Oek]], [[Longbatta]] and Blang Tjoet; no resistance was offered and the houses were burned. On [[7 January]] [[1875]] a convoy was heavily attacked in the morning; [[Majoor Romswinckel|majoor Romswinckel]] was sent with a column to aid. Sharp fighting followed, with several Dutch dead and wounded; the enemy could not be dislodged from positions in the dense brush near Blang Tjoet. The decisive blow came on [[18 January]] [[1875]], when a column of 150 bayonets and an artillery section under [[Kolonel Ruempol|overste Ruempol]], with a reserve infantry company, marched out. The enemy was permanently driven from Blang Tjoet.

## The new post

At a suitable point near the river bank, about 400–500 paces from the Longbatta-Mesigit, work immediately began on a fortification, using an abandoned Atjehnees *benteng*. After a few days of clearing, the enemy could be prevented from crossing the river by day, and transport between the Mesigit and Pendetti became practically safe. On [[23 January]] [[1875]] a new post was built at Poengej Blang Tjoet between [[Poe Oe]] and [[Kota Podjoet]] to better secure the western side of K. Radja and prevent the enemy penetrating the forward line toward Lampasej. With the completion of the continuous line around [[Kota Radja|K. Radja]] in May [[1875]], Blang Tjoet became one of the eastern-side posts of the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]], linking the Mesigit to [[Kota Alam]], [[Lemboe]] O. en N.O., [[Lampriet]], [[Langkroek]] O., [[Lamara]] Z.O. en N.O., [[Tiban]] and [[Moesapi]] along the east side of the line.
