**Paja Reubèë** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Place of fortifications captured by Van Heutsz's forces.

## Description

Place of fortifications captured by [[Generaal J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]'s forces.

## Significance

Paja Reubèë is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
