**Bradeuen** — pronounced *Bradun* — is the kloof through which the road breaks from the region of [[Lam Pisang|Lam Pisang]] into the 4 *moekims* that belong to the *onderafdeeling* [[Lho' Nga|Lho' Nga]]. It is the natural gate from the valley of [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] to the Westkust.
