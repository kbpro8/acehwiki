**Beradin** was a mountain pass in the interior of [[Acheh]] used by Dutch forces during the 1875–1876 campaign. The right‑hand column received orders to move through Beradin toward [[Koth]] before entering the [[Glitaroem]] pass.

As described in [[A. Kruisheer|Kruisheer]]'s *ATJEH '96*, Beradin was also a valley and gorge between [[Tjot Tjako]] and Beradin in the [[IV en VI Moekims|IV and VI Moekims]] theatre, and a major scene of action from [[23 May]] to [[25 May]] [[1896]] and again on [[4 June]] [[1896]]. The 9e Bataljon covered the Dutch retreat through the [[Kloof van Beradin|valley of Beradin]] on [[25 May]] [[1896]], the eastern ridge of the valley was the objective of the [[23 May]] [[1896]] *aanval* (attack), and the [[Kloof van Beradin|valley of Beradin]] was the site of the [[4 June]] [[1896]] maréchaussée engagement in which [[T. Hoesin Lhongbatta|Toekoe Hoesin Long Bata]] was killed. The [[Kloof van Beradin|valley of Beradin]] was also reached by the maréchaussée via [[Glé Taroempas]] on [[22 June]] [[1896]].

## Tactical Significance (1875–1876 campaign)

The pass allowed the Dutch to flank enemy positions and advance into the IX Moekims region.

## Description and Location (ATJEH '96 account)

Beradin and its valley lay in the [[IV en VI Moekims|IV and VI Moekims]] theatre between the mountain of [[Tjot Tjako]] on the south and the kampong of Beradin on the north. The [[Kloof van Beradin|valley of Beradin]] (also *Vallei van Beradin*) was a gorge running east-west through the mountainous country that separated the [[IV en VI Moekims|IV and VI Moekims]] from the inland *gebergte* (mountain range). The eastern ridge of the valley was the objective of the [[23 May]] [[1896]] *aanval*, and was attacked by the 5e compagnie of the 12e Bataljon. The fortification at the south-east corner of Beradin was an artillery position. The [[Kloof van Beradin|valley of Beradin]] was bounded on the south by the slopes of [[Tjot Tjako]] (with its *heilig graf* — holy grave) and [[Glé Genting]] (with its own *heilig graf* and *wachtposten* — sentry posts), and on the north by the kampong of Beradin itself. The [[Glé Taroempas]] mountain pass gave access to the [[Kloof van Beradin|valley of Beradin]] from the east.

## Historical Background (ATJEH '96 account)

Beradin had been part of [[Teuku Umar]]'s defensive *stelling* across the [[IV en VI Moekims|IV and VI Moekims]] throughout the period [[1893]]–[[1896]]. The [[Kloof van Beradin|valley of Beradin]] formed the eastern boundary of Umar's *stelling*, with the eastern ridge being the objective of the principal Dutch *aanval* on [[23 May]] [[1896]]. The fortification at the south-east corner of Beradin was an artillery position of Umar's *stelling*. The [[Glé Taroempas]] mountain pass gave access to the [[Kloof van Beradin|valley of Beradin]] from the east, and was the route by which the maréchaussée entered the valley on [[22 June]] [[1896]].

## Role in the 1896 Operations (ATJEH '96 account)

The [[Kloof van Beradin|valley of Beradin]] was a major scene of action throughout the first semester of [[1896]]. On [[23 May]] [[1896]] the eastern ridge of the valley was the objective of the principal Dutch *aanval* of the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day Dutch offensive]]: the 5e compagnie of the 12e Bataljon fought through the [[Kloof van Beradin|valley of Beradin]] toward the eastern ridge, supported by the *bergcolonne* (mountain column) on [[Tjot Tjako]] and the 9e Bataljon against [[Lam-Asan]]. On [[25 May]] [[1896]] the 9e Bataljon covered the Dutch retreat through the [[Kloof van Beradin|valley of Beradin]] — the closing action of the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day offensive]]. On [[4 June]] [[1896]] the [[Operations in IV and VI Moekims (4–5 June 1896)|two-colonne operation into the IV and VI Moekims]] included a maréchaussée engagement in the [[Kloof van Beradin|valley of Beradin]] in which [[T. Hoesin Lhongbatta|Toekoe Hoesin Long Bata]] was killed — a notable Dutch operational success. On [[22 June]] [[1896]] a maréchaussée column under [[J. W. C. Vuijkk|Luitenant Vuijkk]] crossed [[Glé Taroempas]] and descended into the [[Kloof van Beradin|valley of Beradin]] from the east, reaching the valley in the course of the operations that culminated in the [[Ladoeng Landing and Glé Broeë Attack (22–23 June 1896)|Ladoeng landing and Glé Broeë attack]]. The *versterking bij den Z. O. hoek van Beradin* (fortification at the south-east corner of Beradin) was an Achehnese artillery position attacked in the course of these operations.

## Significance (ATJEH '96 account)

The [[Kloof van Beradin|valley of Beradin]] was the principal geographic obstacle of the [[IV en VI Moekims|IV and VI Moekims]] theatre and the scene of the most-sustained combat of the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day Dutch offensive]]. Its eastern *heuvelrug* (ridge) was the objective of the principal Dutch *aanval* on [[23 May]] [[1896]], and the 9e Bataljon's covering of the Dutch retreat through the valley on [[25 May]] [[1896]] was the closing action of the offensive. The death of [[T. Hoesin Lhongbatta|Toekoe Hoesin Long Bata]] in the [[Kloof van Beradin|valley of Beradin]] on [[4 June]] [[1896]] removed one of the most-dangerous Achehnese war-leaders of the period.
