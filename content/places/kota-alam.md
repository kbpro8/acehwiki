**Kota Alam** was the kampong at the bend in the [[Atjeh-rivier]] east of [[Lemboe]] occupied by the Dutch on [[13 November]] [[1874]] in the [[Conquest of Kota Alam (13 November 1874)|conquest of Kota Alam]] by [[Kolonel Ruempol|overste Ruempol]]. Armed with two 12 c.M. *voorlaadkanonnen*, the post's purpose was to enfilade the river upstream, shell the enemy stelling of [[Longbatta]], and strike into the densely populated [[III Moekims]] on the right bank opposite.

## The strategic position

The bend in the Atjeh-rivier at Kota Alam gave the post command of the river in two directions — downstream toward [[Lemboe]] and the [[Pedir-dijk|Pedir-dijk]] posts, and upstream toward [[Longbatta]] and the [[Pendetti]] works. From Kota Alam's guns the Dutch could enfilade the river upstream and shell the enemy stelling of Longbatta, which had been the objective of the disastrous [[Battle of Longbatta (31 December 1874)|Longbatta operation of 31 December 1874]]. The densely populated [[III Moekims]] on the right bank opposite — described in Borel's account as a *sterk bevolkte kampong* east of the bivouak at [[Penajoeng]] — could also be reached. Kota Alam was therefore the natural eastern anchor of the right-bank post chain of the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]], complementing [[Kota Radja Bedil|K. R. Bedil]] at the river-mouth and [[Lemboe]] at the bend.

## The conquest and post

On [[13 November]] [[1874]] [[Kolonel Ruempol|overste Ruempol]] with a half-battalion and an artillery section attacked the south-eastern part called Kota Alam, where the Atjeh-rivier bends. Although the enemy had had a week to prepare — the [[Conquest of Lemboe (7 November 1874)|conquest of Lemboe]] had been on [[7 November]] [[1874]] — he offered only weak resistance. Kota Alam itself cost little; but during the fortification work the Dutch were fired on from all sides. The cost was 20+ dead and wounded. By evening the post was defensible, and a company was left as garrison. The next day, [[14 November]] [[1874]], a column again pushed the enemy back from Kota Alam; this time little resistance. The post was armed with two 12 c.M. *voorlaadkanonnen* — the same obsolete muzzle-loaders that Borel criticises throughout the book for the unreliable *tijdbuis* (time-fuse) shells that failed to burst on Atjeh's wet, covered terrain. With [[Blang Tjoet]] conquered on [[18 January]] [[1875]], [[Mandarsa Poeti]] occupied on [[28 January]] [[1875]], and the eastern arc of the stelling-Pel gradually closed through [[Lampriet]], [[Langkroek]] O., [[Lamara]] Z.O. en N.O., [[Tiban]] and [[Moesapi]], Kota Alam became the river-bank anchor of the eastern side of the line.
