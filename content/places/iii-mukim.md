**III Mukim** was an Acehnese territorial federation led during the campaign by [[Teuku Potjoet Ali]].

## Historical setting

After his death on [[30 January]] [[1874]], rival claims involved his relatives and [[Nja Hassim]]. Villages named in the source included Tibang, Djilengki, Tintalo, Langkroet, Lambaroe, Kwala Daja, Pinang, Langgoegoeb, and Kajoe Adang.

## Modern context

The source situates [[III Mukim]] in the historical landscape of [[Aceh]]; historical spellings follow the [[1879]] Dutch text.
