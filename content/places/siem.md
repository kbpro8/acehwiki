**Siem** was a *kampong* (village) in the northern part of the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]], burnt together with [[Kroeng Kali]] in [[1880]] after two days of fighting, and the site of the mosque of [[Masdjid Siem]].

## The action of 1880

Hooijer recorded that the column which in [[1880]] had to penetrate the northern part of the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] met strong resistance there, and could approach and set fire to the *kampongs* (villages) of Siem and [[Kroeng Kali]] only after two days of fighting. When the population that had fled returned after some time, the head appointed over it by the Dutch was murdered by [[Toekoe Nja Hassan]].

## The mosque

The stone mosque of [[Masdjid Siem]] stood on the old road from [[Lamjong]] through [[Toengkoeb]] to [[Kroeng Kali]] and was held as a fortified position by the *[[Moslemin]]* until stormed by [[Overste Sievers]] in [[October]] [[1893]].
