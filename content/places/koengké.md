**Koengké** (military bivak) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Military bivak at ~1000 m altitude; small pasanggrahan with bedding for cold nights.

## Type

military [[Bivak|bivak]]

## Description

Military [[Bivak|bivak]] at ~[[1000]] m altitude; small [[Pasanggrahan|pasanggrahan]] with bedding for cold nights.

## Significance

Koengké is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
