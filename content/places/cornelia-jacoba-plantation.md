**Cornelia Jacoba plantation** was a sugar plantation in the interior of [[Acheh]] that became a focal point of Dutch operations during the December 19–25 expedition. The detachment led by [[Captain‑lieutenant Smit]] marched overland to the plantation, while the bark “[[St Eustatius]]” later anchored there to maintain a post and secure communications.

## Events
On [[23 December]] the plantation was reported to have been abandoned by rebels, though later a patrol found the buildings still standing. The plantation was also the site where a silver candlestick bearing the name of [[Ramringt]] was discovered.
