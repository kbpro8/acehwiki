---
title: Kuala Gigieng
tags: ["place", "north-east-coast", "river"]
---

A coastal location. Sadati dialogue: 'If that please thee not, little grain of an ear of padi, I will take you to Kuala Gigieng. I will not live at Kuala Gigieng, brothers; were I to die (in that place), there is none that would look after my dead body. I have no brothers there.'
