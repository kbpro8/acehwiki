**Bataklanden** (region) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Region south of Aceh.

## Type

region

## Description

Region south of Aceh. First heathen [[Alas|Alassers]] came from Bataklanden. Bataks also used as slaves (less valued than [[Nias]]). Batak physical features seen in [[Alas|Alassers]] (broad, indented nose). Batak influence in [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]] reduces strictness of incest [[Pantang|taboo]]. Part of [[Gouverneur Van Daalen|Van Daalen]]'s [[1904]] [[March|march]].

## Significance

Bataklanden is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
