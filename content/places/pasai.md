**Pasai** was/is a historic maritime coastal kingdom and district in northern Aceh (modern North Aceh Regency).

## Historical Context and Role

Renowned historically as one of the earliest Islamic sultanates in Southeast Asia, Pasai was a hotbed of anti-colonial resistance until its subjugation alongside [[Peusangan]] in [[1899]] by Dutch expeditionary forces under [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]].
