**Troemon** — also *Trumon* — is a region on the [[Westkust (of Atjeh)|Westkust of Atjeh]], the region whose *zelfbestuurder* (native self-administrator) reported the rogue elephant to the visiting kolonel at [[Bakongan]] in [[1933]], and the region whose *Troemonse moerassen* — with [[Ië Itam]] (Zwart Water, Black Water) and [[Ië Merah]] (Rood Water, Red Water) — were notorious for their *gemeen stinkende, troebele inktwater*. In *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* Troemon is the setting of the [[Troemon tragedy 1926|Troemon tragedy of 1926]] and of the *woudloper* anecdotes of [[Thijs de Fretes]].

## The zelfbestuurder

"De *zelfbestuurder van Troemon* had het over een *solitair*, die gevaarlijk was voor de bevolking en schade aanrichtte." The *zelfbestuurder* — the native self-administrator — of Troemon reported the rogue elephant to the visiting kolonel at [[Bakongan]] in [[1933]]. The *zelfbestuurder* was the native official through whom the Dutch colonial administration governed the inheemse bevolking of Troemon.

## The Troemonse moerassen

The *Troemonse moerassen* — with [[Ië Itam]] (Zwart Water, Black Water) and [[Ië Merah]] (Rood Water, Red Water) — were notorious for their *gemeen stinkende, troebele inktwater* (foul-smelling, cloudy ink-water). The water was used both for washing and for cooking rijst and thee — and it was brak. The *Troemonse moerassen* were the setting of the *woudloper* work of [[Thijs de Fretes]], whose plain-spoken Dutch about the *stinkende rotwater* was comprehensible only to those who had spent days in the moerassen.

## The Troemon tragedy 1926

The [[Troemon tragedy 1926|Troemon tragedy of 1926]] is mentioned in the book in connection with the *Westkust verzet 1925-1927|Westkust verzet]] of [[1925]]-[[1927]]. The tragedy was part of the cycle of unrest on the Westkust of Aceh in the inter-war period.

## Significance

Troemon is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as the region on the Westkust of Aceh whose *zelfbestuurder* reported the rogue elephant, and whose *Troemonse moerassen* were the setting of the *woudloper* work of [[Thijs de Fretes]]. The book's argument is that Troemon — with its *zelfbestuurder*, its *moerassen*, and its *stinkende rotwater* — was the kind of place that made the *woudloper* tradition of the old [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Marechaussee]] what it was: the men who knew the *moerassen* and their *stinkende rotwater* as well as they knew the *rimboe*.
