**Lampaleuh** was the site of a Dutch *[[Blokhuis|blokhuis]]* in the [[XXII Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]] near the post of [[Lam Soet]], whose completion made the neighbourhood quiet and safe.

## In Hooijer's description

Hooijer recorded that at [[Lam Soet]] the *[[Mitrailleur|mitrailleur]]* mounted in the hut behind the commandant's summer-house *zelden of nooit behoefde gebruikt te worden, want na het voltooien van het blokhuis te Lampaleuh was het rustig en veilig in de buurt* — seldom or never had to be used, for after the completion of the *blokhuis* at Lampaleuh it was quiet and safe in the neighbourhood. Most of the rifle-shots fired there were aimed at iguanas in the [[Acheh River|Atjeh-rivier]].
