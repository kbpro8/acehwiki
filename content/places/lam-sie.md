**Lam Sie** (place (seat of Sagihoofd)) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Place where the old Sagihoofd Teukoe Panglima Polem resides (since May 1904).

## Type

place (seat of Sagihoofd)

## Description

Place where the old Sagihoofd [[Teukoe Panglima Polem]] resides (since May [[1904]]Village near[[Seulimeum]] where [[Panglima Polém]] lives at time of writing.

## Significance

Lam Sie is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
