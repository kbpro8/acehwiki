**Generaal-Köhler-boom** was a towering, nearly leafless banyan (banyan) tree situated at the southern end of a ruined eastern outer wall of [[Atjeh's Groote Missigit]].

## Historical Event
The tree gained historical infamy because it marked the exact location where Major General [[J.H.R. Köhler]] was standing—approximately four paces to its west—when he was fatally shot by an Acehnese marksman during the [[Second Conquest of the Great Missigit]] on [[14 April]] [[1873]].
