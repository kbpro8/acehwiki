**Boekit-Karang** was a Dutch military post in the eastern section of the geconcentreerde stelling (concentrated position) in [[Groot-Atjeh]]. The post was established on [[22 November]] [[1884]] as part of the construction of the new defensive line.

## Location

Boekit-Karang was situated in the eastern section of the geconcentreerde stelling, between [[Tjot-Iri]] and [[Roempit]].

## Establishment

Following the completion of the road from [[Oleh-Karang]] to Boekit-Karang, the post was established and occupied on [[22 November]] [[1884]]. No significant opposition was encountered during the establishment.

## Fortification (1885)

Boekit-Karang was:

**Type:** A post with 50-man Infantry garrison
**Armament:** 2 × 12cm breech-loading guns

## Infrastructure Connections

The post was connected to:
- The ceintuurtram (girdle tramway) system
- [[Roempit]] and [[Tjot-Iri]]
- Routes to the eastern areas

## Significance

Boekit-Karang's establishment completed the eastern section of the geconcentreerde stelling, closing the gap between [[Lamjong]] and [[Lampermej]].

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

[[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] names Boekit Karang one of the eighteen semi-permanently built posts of the line of [[1885]] (see [[Postenlinie]] and [[Completion of the concentrated position (March 1885)]]).
