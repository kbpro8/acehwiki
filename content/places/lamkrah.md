**Lamkrah** is a region in [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjèh], described in the [[De Atjeh-Oorlog (Kruijt, 1896)|Kruijt brochure of 1896]] as a "*broeinest van fanatisme*" (hotbed of fanaticism), where the Dutch had never been master, and where Dutch troops had recently suffered very serious losses. The Lamkrah region was the planned objective of the combined Dutch-Atjèhse operation of [[29 March]] [[29 March]] [[1896]] — the operation whose discovery, at the eleventh hour, exposed [[Teuku Umar|Toekoe Oema]]'s plan to throw himself on the exposed [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] and [[Oleh-leh|Oleh-leh]] when the Dutch troops had marched out in mass outside the line, and so precipitated the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (March 1896)|defection of 29 March 1896]].

## The Hotbed of Fanaticism

The Lamkrah region — a "*broeinest van fanatisme*" (hotbed of fanaticism), where the Dutch had never been master, where Dutch troops had recently suffered very serious losses — was one of the principal Atjèhse strongholds in the immediate vicinity of the Dutch [[Geconcentreerde Linie|Geconcentreerde Linie]]. The region's resistance to the Dutch was sustained, and the Dutch had been unable to extend their authority over it.

## The 1896 Plan

Under [[Deijkerhoff|Deijkerhoff]]'s governorship, in [[1895]], the Dutch proposed to make permanent and equip the hitherto temporary posts outside the line. And indeed Deijkerhoff managed to get it through at [[Batavia|Batavia]. But first, with the help of [[Teuku Umar|Toekoe Oema]], the Lamkrah region — a hotbed of fanaticism, where the Dutch had never been master, where Dutch troops had recently suffered very serious losses — had to be cleared of enemies. There Toekoe Oema seemed to feel little inclination, whether he did not dare, or he could no longer get enough money out and considered the suitable moment now come to throw off the mask for the umpteenth time. Yet he finally promised to march. He received at the end of [[March 1896|March]] the necessary funds, a number of breech-loading rifles, cartridges, rations, and so on. The combined operation of Dutch troops and the Atjèhse auxiliaries was to take place on [[29 March]] [[29 March]] [[1896]].

## The Discovery

Toekoe Oema's plan — when the Dutch troops had marched out in mass outside the line, to throw himself on the exposed [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] and [[Oleh-leh|Oleh-leh]] — was discovered in time, at the eleventh hour. It cost effort to convince [[Deijkerhoff|Deijkerhoff]. The marching out of the troops was countermanded, and so a catastrophe was prevented, in which the surprise attack on [[Lombok|Lombok]] in [[August 1894|August]] [[1894]] would have been child's play. Finding himself discovered, Toekoe Oema withdrew with his bands into the mountains and fortified himself there mightily; most of the paid friends (?) outside the line joined him. The Dutch posts lying outside the line were surrounded and cut off.

## Significance

Lamkrah is the Groot-Atjèh region that was the planned objective of the combined Dutch-Atjèhse operation of [[29 March]] [[29 March]] [[1896]] — the operation whose discovery exposed Toekoe Oema's plan and precipitated the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (March 1896)|defection of 29 March 1896]]. The Lamkrah region — a hotbed of fanaticism, where the Dutch had never been master — was one of the principal Atjèhse strongholds in the immediate vicinity of the Dutch Geconcentreerde Linie, and the Dutch's attempt to clear it with Toekoe Oema's help was the immediate occasion of the catastrophe of March 1896.
