In Hikayat Sulutan Boseutaman (XXXV): 'the principal royal personage is called Yahya, his minister Meuntroe Apeulaih, and his country Samteurani.' Also: 'a dependency called Dameuchah' — possibly derived from Damascus.

## Source
*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
