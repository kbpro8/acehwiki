A district and gampong near the capital of [[Acheh]]. In the lowland districts, especially in

ornamentation of tombstones (nisam, batee jeurat), in which they displayed considerable skill. This art is now practically defunct. Teuku Ne' of Meura'sa — depicted in a photograph in the book — was a famous chief of this district; he absorbed 10 katis (about 13 lbs) of quicksilver into his body through the skin to secure invulnerability, and carried a one-eyed coconut as a charm. He had a Teuku Karong guide from Bateue Hie' in [[Samalanga]]. During the Dutch war, the people of Meura'sa submitted to the Gompeuni without striking a blow, a fact the poet of the [[Hikayat Prang Gompeuni]] criticizes sharply. Teuku Ne' is later mentioned among those near the mosque of [[Ulee Lheue]] during the incident involving [[Teuku Asan]]. In the sadati kisah: 'On the banks of the salt-water creek is established one who is said to be invincible; he is known as Teuku Ne' of Meurasa.' The keude (market) of Meura'sa formerly lay near the tomb of [[Tuan di Keude]] (also called 'Saint of the 17 yards'). Teuku Chot and Tuan di Puta are also saints buried in [[Gampong Pi]] within the territory of Meura'sa. Tuan di Chot Aron is in Gampong Lam Jabat in Meura'sa.
