**Tangsé** is a valley of central [[Atjeh|Aceh]] — paired with [[Geumpang]] — that formed the principal theatre of the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]] of [[1910]]-[[1911]] against the *[[Teungkoe Sjech Saman di Tiri|Tiriō Teungkoes]]*. The bergland ten Oosten of Tangsé, against the hellingen of [[Goenoeng Alimon]] and [[Peuët Sagoë]], held the schuilplaatsen of the Tiriō family; the *hoofden van Tangsé* included [[Teukoe Dagang]], whose own bende survived the destruction of the Tiriō in [[1911]] and was hunted by luitenant [[Schmidt]] for over a year afterwards. The valley is the geographical centre of the colonne Schmidt chapter of *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]*.

## The theatre of the colonne Schmidt

"De valleien van Tangsé en [[Geumpang]] tegen de hellingen van de [[Goenoeng Alimon]] en de [[Peuët Sagoë]] op" formed the eastern side of the Tiriō theatre. "Ten Westen in het stroomgebied van de [[Kroeëng Seukoeleh]]" formed the western side. Paden from Tangsé and Geumpang naar de schuilplaats had ladangs with wachtposten. The schuilplaatsen of the Tiriō family — and of [[Teukoe Dagang]] — lay in the bergland between these two valleys.

## The begrafenis of Tgk. Tjhi Ma'at

When the corpse of [[Tgk. Tjhi Ma'at]] — killed on [[3 December]] [[1911]] by the korporaal [[Nussy]] — was carried out, "de bevolking van Tangsé liep uit naar de begrafenis." The bandages and cloths in which the lijk was gewikkeld "werden in honderden stukjes gescheurd en meegenomen als heilige reliquiën." Tangsé is therefore the place at which the last leader of the Tiriō bende was buried as a saint of the resistance.

## The posten on the paden

"Posten stonden op paden naar de [[Noordkust]], vanwaar ook wel werd gefourageerd." Tangsé was connected by paden to the Noordkust, along which fourageurs came to supply the bende; the posten on these paden were the colonne's means of cutting off the bende's supplies. The geography of the valley — open at the paden, impassable in the bergland — was what made the colonne's method of small patrouilles and omsingelingen the appropriate tactic.

## Nussy's return from the passer

"Nussy op een dag van de *[[passer]]* in Tangsé met wat pakjes in zijn handen" — was returning from the market in Tangsé with packages in his hands — when the news of his M.W.O. decoration reached him. "„Nussy dapat ridder klas tiga!" Nussy heeft het ridder derde klas gekregen." The hardened dardanel "heeft het uitgehuild van ontroering." Tangsé is therefore also the place at which the colonne's most decorated soldier received his decoration.

## Significance

Tangsé is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as the geographical centre of the colonne Schmidt chapter. It is the place at which the last organised Acehnese armed resistance was destroyed, at which the last leader of the Tiriō bende was buried as a saint, and at which the colonne's most decorated soldier received his decoration. The valley therefore ties together the military, religious and personal dimensions of the book's account of the end of the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]].
