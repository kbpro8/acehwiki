**Seulawaih Inong** — *the woman* — is the smaller of the two mountains called *Seulawaih* which rise in the middle of the valley of [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]]. The greater is the [[Seulawaih Agam|Seulawaih Agam]] — *the man* — also called the *Goudberg* or *Gold Mountain*.
