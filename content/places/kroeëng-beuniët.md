**Kroeëng Beuniët** was the river whose strongly fortified and apparently impregnable gorge barred the way to [[Tangsé]] in [[1898]].

[[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]'s answer was "the famous turning movement to the left through the impassable terrain around the gorge — through downpours, primeval forest and ravines… towards the unreachable unknown. A gamble, a specimen of daring and unshakeable self-confidence, which succeeded: Tangsé was reached and occupied."
