**Atjeh (Aceh)** (region/residency/sultanate) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Main subject of the book.

## Type

region/residency/[[sultanate]]

## Description

Main subject of the book. Region at northern tip of Sumatra. Independent [[sultanate]] until [[1873]]-[[1903]], then Dutch colonial residency. Comprises afdeelingen (divisions) including [[Noordkust]], [[Westkust]], [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]]-land, etc. Has 'slechten naam' (bad reputation) in the [[Dutch East Indies]]The northern region of Sumatra; the subject of the book. Includes both the historic[[Sultanate of Aceh]] and the colonial [[gewest|Gewest]] Atjeh. Population is concentrated on the North and East Coasts. Predominantly Muslim since ~6 centuries before Dutch contact.

## Significance

Atjeh (Aceh) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
