**Meureubo** (rubber enterprise) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. River in Westkust; bounds 1901 coal investigation area (between Meureubo and Seunagan).

## Type

rubber enterprise

## Description

River in [[Westkust]]; bounds [[1901]] coal investigation area (between Meureubo and [[Seunagan]]).

## Significance

Meureubo is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
