**Tjaleuë** (crossroads) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Crossroads where the author and a controleur intercepted the third underhoofden-trek to Koeta Radja (the 'operette van Tjaleuë').

## Type

crossroads

## Description

Crossroads where the author and a [[Controleur|controleur]] intercepted the third underhoofden-trek to [[Koeta-Radja|Koeta Radja]] (the 'operette van Tjaleuë'). A [[volksschooltje]] nearby housed a [[Brigade (Maréchaussée)|brigade]] under a kapitein.

## Significance

Tjaleuë is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
