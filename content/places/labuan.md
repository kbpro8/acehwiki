**Labuan** is an island off the north‑west coast of **[[Borneo]]** that was seized by the **[[British Empire]]** in [[1846]]. Its capture provided a strategic naval base that supported British operations in the region, including the activities of **[[James Brooke]]** in **[[Serawak]]** and the suppression of piracy that affected the **[[Sultanate of Aceh]]**’s maritime trade. Labuan’s status as a Crown colony underscored the growing British presence opposite Dutch‑controlled Aceh.

## Strategic Importance

- Served as a co‑ordination point for British anti‑piracy patrols.
- Enabled rapid deployment of forces that could challenge Acehnese influence on Borneo’s western coast.
