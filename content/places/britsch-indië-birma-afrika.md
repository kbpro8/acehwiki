**Britsch-Indië, Birma, Afrika** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Other places where the amok phenomenon occurs.

## Description

Other places where the amok phenomenon occurs.

## Significance

Britsch-Indië, Birma, Afrika is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
