**Siroen** was a Dutch military post in the southern section of the geconcentreerde stelling (concentrated position) in [[Groot-Atjeh]]. The post was established on [[14 November]] [[1884]].

## Location

Siroen was situated in the southern section of the geconcentreerde stelling, between [[Lampermej]] and [[Lambaroe]].

## Establishment

Siroen was established on [[14 November]] [[1884]], following the completion of a usable path from [[Lambaroe]]. Workers were occasionally troubled by enemy fire, but no casualties were suffered.

## Fortification (1885)

Siroen was:

**Type:** A post with 50-man Infantry garrison
**Armament:** 2 × 12cm breech-loading guns

## Significance

Siroen helped complete the southern section of the perimeter, providing coverage of routes between [[Lampermej]] and [[Lambaroe]].

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

[[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] names Siroen (also spelled Seroen in the official journal) one of the eighteen posts of the line of [[1885]], and adds: it was the only fortification of the new [[Postenlinie|line]] that the projected [[Ceintuur-trambaan|belt tramway]] would not pass along; at the clearing of the ground between [[Lambaroe]] and Siroen on [[6 April]] [[1885]] the Dutch had two dead and ten wounded; and on [[9 June]] [[1885]], at the building of a dyke near Siroen, luitenant [[Luitenant Veerman|Veerman]] and two workers were wounded (see [[Guerilla raids inside the concentration line (1885)]]).
