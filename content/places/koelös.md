**Koelös** (valley) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Valley with a crystal-clear mountain stream; the operatieterrein (operating area) of 'Ranno'.

## Type

valley

## Description

Valley with a crystal-clear mountain stream; the operatieterrein (operating area) of 'Ranno'.

## Significance

Koelös is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
