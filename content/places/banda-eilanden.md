**Banda-eilanden** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Island group mentioned as the eastern terminus of the great folding-mountain arc.

## Description

Island group mentioned as the eastern terminus of the great folding-mountain arc.

## Significance

Banda-eilanden is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
