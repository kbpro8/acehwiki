**Oring** is a village near [[Takengon|Takengon]] where Broersma met a wedding procession undertaking the purification-bath prescribed before marriage. The bride and bridegroom looked comical, their heads adorned with high, many-coloured paper decorations. The bride was literally hung with silver Spanish mats and wore dozens of silver arm-rings — *'zoo rijk en zoo mooi en zoo verlegen in den onvrijen tooi'* — *so rich and so beautiful and so shy in the unaccustomed array.* Several older women were with them, and there was music of drums and percussion and a single gong of clear tone. The sun played along on the open road, and the wind of the morning tousled the hair of well a hundred children who followed the pair, laughing, shouting, with their triangles and tambourines.
