**Hongkong** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Chinese market for Aceh boombast; closed due to China-Japan conflict.

## Description

Chinese market for Aceh boombast; closed due to [[China]]-[[Japan]] conflict.

## Significance

Hongkong is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
