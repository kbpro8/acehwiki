**Lam-Goet** — written in Dutch as also *Lam Goet* — was a kampong in the [[V Moekims Montassik]] of [[T. Rajoet]] and the residence of the district head [[T. Hoesin Lhongbatta|Toekoe Hoesin Lehong Bata]]. As described in Kruisheer's *ATJEH '96*, Lam-Goet was the object of a chastisement on [[14 April]] [[1896]] in which the fortification was stormed and an iron 9 c.M. cannon was destroyed with *dynamite*. The *versterkingen* of Lam-Goet were subsequently brought back into a state of defence. Lam-Goet was attacked again on [[17 June]] [[1896]] by the compagnie of [[P. Linck|Kapitein Linck]] of the 12e Bataljon, in the course of the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|tuchtiging of the V Moekims Montassik of 16–17 June 1896]], and was occupied without resistance and burned. The kampong is situated on the road from Lam-Goet to [[Tjot Rang]].

**Lamgoe** was a place in [[Groot-Atjeh]] occupied by the [[12e Bataljon Infanterie|12e Bataljon]] on [[24 May]] [[1896]], the second day of the [[Three-day battle in the Beradin pass (23-25 May 1896)|three-day battle in the Beradin pass]], together with [[Lamoedjong]] after the advance from [[Lamtengah]] on [[Lamroekan]].

## Description and Location

Lam-Goet lay in the [[V Moekims Montassik]] between the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] on the east and the [[III Moekims Kerkoen]] on the north. The kampong was the residence of the district head [[T. Hoesin Lhongbatta|Toekoe Hoesin Lehong Bata]] — also written Toekoe Hoesin Long Bata — one of the most active Achehnese war-leaders of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. The kampong had a fortification armed with an iron 9 c.M. cannon, which was destroyed with *dynamite* in the [[Tuchtiging van Lam Goet (14 April 1896)|tuchtiging of 14 April 1896]]. The kampong was situated on the road from Lam-Goet to [[Tjot Rang]], and was connected by road to the [[blokhuis|blokhuis]] of [[Lamgoet]] in the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] (which was the [[blokhuis]] residence of [[T. Hoesin Lhongbatta|T. Hoesin Long Bata]]). The kampong was bounded on the south by the [[Kroeng Lingkar]] and on the east by the [[III Moekims Daroe]].

## Historical Background

Lam-Goet had been the residence and power-base of [[T. Hoesin Lhongbatta|Toekoe Hoesin Lehong Bata]] throughout the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. The *bendehoofd* was one of the most-active Achehnese war-leaders, drawing Pedireezen mercenaries from the [[Westkust]] and conducting raids against the [[buitenpost|buitenposten]] of the [[buitenlinie]]. The kampong had been fortified with a *versterking* armed with an iron 9 c.M. cannon, and was a constant target of Dutch operations. After the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (29 March 1896)|defection of 29 March 1896]] [[T. Hoesin Lhongbatta|T. Hoesin Long Bata]] aligned himself openly with Umar and his Pedireezen became a major element of the hostile forces in the [[V Moekims Montassik]].

## Role in the 1896 Operations

Lam-Goet was the object of two Dutch chastisements in [[1896]]. On [[14 April]] [[1896]] the [[Tuchtiging van Lam Goet (14 April 1896)|tuchtiging of Lam-Goet]] was conducted: the fortification was stormed by a Dutch column, the iron 9 c.M. cannon was destroyed with *dynamite*, and the *versterkingen* were overrun. The *versterkingen* of Lam-Goet were subsequently brought back into a state of defence by [[T. Hoesin Lhongbatta|T. Hoesin Long Bata]]. On [[17 June]] [[1896]] the compagnie of [[P. Linck|Kapitein Linck]] of the 12e Bataljon attacked Lam-Goet in the course of the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|tuchtiging of the V Moekims Montassik of 16–17 June 1896]]; the kampong was occupied without resistance and burned. On [[5 June]] [[1896]] [[T. Hoesin Lhongbatta|T. Hoesin Long Bata]] was killed in the [[Kloof van Beradin|valley of Beradin]] by a maréchaussée patrol, removing the principal Achehnese war-leader of the [[V Moekims Montassik]]. On [[20 June]] [[1896]] the area around Lam-Goet was patrolled by Dutch troops in the course of the [[Machtsvertoon (20 June 1896)|four-column show-of-force]]. The kampong is mentioned in [[Teuku Umar]]'s letters as one of the kampongs he ordered to be defended with breech-loaders.

## Significance

Lam-Goet was the power-base of [[T. Hoesin Lhongbatta|Toekoe Hoesin Lehong Bata]], one of the most active Achehnese war-leaders of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]], and the principal strongpoint of the [[V Moekims Montassik]]. Its repeated *tuchtiging* in [[1896]] and the death of its *bendehoofd* on [[5 June]] [[1896]] broke the back of organised Achehnese resistance in this region. The kampong's fortification with an iron 9 c.M. cannon — destroyed with *dynamite* — was a notable feature of its defences.
