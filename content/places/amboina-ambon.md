**Amboina (Ambon)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Place of exile for the ex-Pretendent-Sultan under Governor Van Daalen.

## Description

Place of exile for the ex-[[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]] under Governor [[Gouverneur Van Daalen|Van Daalen]].

## Significance

Amboina (Ambon) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
