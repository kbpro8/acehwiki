A gampong where religious instruction is still given (at the time of writing) by an old Teungku who takes his name from the gampong. [[Aneu' Galong]] A village (gampong) in [[Acheh]] Proper. In the [[Hikayat Prang Gompeuni]], [[Teungku Tiro]] directed his steps to [[Aneu' Galong]] and [[Indrapuri]] during his tour of admonition. 'From [[Seubun]] the ulama directed his steps to [[Aneu' Galong]] and [[Indrapuri]]; in every place along his route he gave the chiefs instructions for the raising of sabil-contributions.' After [[Teungku Tiro]]'s seven days of seclusion and mortification (tapa) at [[Lam Panaih]], he returned from the XXII Mukims to the lowland districts, and the ulama then returned from [[Lam Panaih]] to [[Aneu' Galong]]. Mat Amin and about a hundred followers perished in [[1896]] at the [[Surprise of Aneu' Galong]] by the Dutch troops. [[Indrapuri]] A village in the XXII Mukims area of [[Acheh]]. [[Teungku Tiro]] directed his steps to [[Indrapuri]] as part of his tour of admonition to chiefs and people during the war against the Dutch. The name also appears in the Hikayat Indrapatra (literary work), which is a free imitation of a Malay romance and features a prince named Indrapatra on wandering adventures through the world.
