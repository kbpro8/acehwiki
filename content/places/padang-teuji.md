A place associated with the VII Mukims. In the [[Hikayat Pochut Muhamat]]: 'In Padang Teuji (Tiji) he ([[Pochut Muhamat]]) remains as long as is necessary for regulating the affairs of the VII Mukims and winning over the people to his cause.'
