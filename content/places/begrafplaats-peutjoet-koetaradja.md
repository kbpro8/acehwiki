**Begrafplaats Peutjoet (Koetaradja)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Cemetery in Koetaradja; on background the great monument for Generaal Pel.

## Description

Cemetery in [[Koetaradja]]; on background the great monument for [[Generaal Pel]].

## Significance

Begrafplaats Peutjoet (Koetaradja) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
