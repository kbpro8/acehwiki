title: Chot Paleue
aliases: ["gampong"]

The home gampong of [[Nya' Ahmat]] alias Uri bin Mahmut, author of the Nasihat ureueng muprang (Admonition to those engaged in the war). He is described as being 'of the gampong Chot Paleue' and composed his work in August [[1894]].
