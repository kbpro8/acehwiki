**Blang Kedjerën** — modern *Blang Kejeren* — is the chief place of the *onderafdeeling* [[Gajoe Loeös|Gajoe Loeös]] in the *afdeeling* [[Gajo- en Alaslanden|Gajo- en Alaslanden]]. It lies in the south-western part of the Gajo country, and Broersma considered that it would in time become the centre of administration of the *afdeeling*. A captain with twelve brigades (to be reduced to nine, of which three at [[Kota Tjane|Kota Tjane]]) lay at Blang Kedjerën at the time of Broersma's visit. The road-plan for the *afdeeling* foresees a road from [[Takengon|Takengon]] in south-western direction over a distance of about 20 km; thereafter a path leads through the *Intem-Intem* mountains, which must now be maintained and examined to see whether it can be developed into a road. The estimated distance is 130 km; on both sides of the mountains there is a strip of some 30 km that is uninhabited.
