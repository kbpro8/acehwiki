**Lhoksukon** was/is a inland administrative town located in northern Aceh (modern North Aceh Regency).

## Historical Context and Role

In [[1910]], Lhoksukon became the headquarters station for the 5th Division of the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]], overseeing anti-guerrilla operations across the eastern coastal plain.

## Account from *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*

Gloomy town between [[Lho'Seumawé]] and [[Sigli]]; site of fierce struggle bloodily suppressed by [[Christoffel]] in [[1910]]. Characteristic Aceh aanslagen occurred here; [[C. Schmid|kapitein C. Schmid]] murdered [[10 July]] [[1933]]. Has rijstpellerij of [[Brinkman]], [[Pira Estate]] (rubber) and [[Lho'Soekon Estate]] (vezel) nearby. Approached via paja (swamp) dike with crab-infested road.

*Source: J. Jongejans, Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu (Baarn: Hollandia Drukkerij N.V., c. 1938), pp. 86, 87.*
