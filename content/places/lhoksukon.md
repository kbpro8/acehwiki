**Lhoksukon** was/is a inland administrative town located in northern Aceh (modern North Aceh Regency).

## Historical Context and Role

In [[1910]], Lhoksukon became the headquarters station for the 5th Division of the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]], overseeing anti-guerrilla operations across the eastern coastal plain.
