**Lambaroe** was a settlement north of [[Kajoe‑leh]] that Dutch troops reached after occupying Kajoe‑leh on [[5 February]] [[1876]]. From Lambaroe the column proceeded to the [[Atjeh River]] and later to [[Pajar‑Ajer]].
