**Tjot Plieëng (Tjot Plicëng)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Place associated with Teungkoe di Tjot Plieëng (the saintly oelama).

## Description

Place associated with [[Teungkoe di Tjot Plieëng]] (the saintly [[Oelama|oelama]]).

## Significance

Tjot Plieëng (Tjot Plicëng) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
