**Linggö** was the [[Dörötstreek|Dösön valley]] of the [[Meergebied]], "formerly so turbulent", the scene of a *[[klewang]]* attack in January [[1911]] and of the [[Onrust in Linggö (1916)|unrest of 1916]].

In [[1916]] a female *[[teungkoe]]* regarded by the population as very holy brought a dozen villagers to withdraw into the mountains, where they joined [[Penghoeloe Möngkör]]; their attack on a marching bivouac failed, the band submitted, the population itself laid the Penghoeloe low in September, and the female saint was taken prisoner in the [[Gajò Loeös]].
