---
title: Leupueng
tags: ["place", "aceh-proper"]
---

A coastal location in [[Acheh]]. '[[Teuku Uma]], who had again spent a considerable time on the West Coast, now arrived at Leupueng, but none of his followers knew of the scheme which he was now fostering. To the amazement of all, he unexpectedly tendered his submission to the Gompeuni.'
