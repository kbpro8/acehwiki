**Simpang-kanan** — the *Right Branch* — is one of the two rivers which together form the [[Tamiang-rivier|Tamiang river]] in the *onderafdeeling* [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] of the *afdeeling* [[Oostkust van Atjeh|Oostkust van Atjeh]], the other being the [[Simpang Kiri|Simpang-kiri]]. The *stroomgebied* of the two rivers is the geographic core of Tamiang.
