A mukim (district) of [[Acheh]] Proper, famous in the war against the Dutch. The brave [[Imeum of Lueng Bata]] and his followers were among the foremost resisters. During the first Dutch expedition, the [[Imeum of Lueng Bata]] and [[Teuku Lam Nga]] fought with heroic valour. After the conquest of the XXVI Mukims by the Dutch and their advance through the XXII Mukims, the [[Imeum of Lueng Bata]] finally thrust his sword into its sheath and withdrew from public life. Teuku Usen of Lueng Bata (brother of the imeum) was among the principal panglimas who took up arms under [[Teuku Asan]]. The Mukim Lueng Bata was among those overcome by the Dutch after a period of comparative inactivity. The tomb of Teungku Meuntroe — the saint who specialises in punishing perjurers — is situated in the open country at Lueng Bata.
