**Landdsroon plantation** was a sugar plantation in the interior of [[Acheh]] that was observed by Dutch forces on [[25 December]] to be in ashes. The plantation, together with [[Beresteyn plantation]], had been set on fire by rebels shortly before the Dutch ships arrived.

## Context
The burning of Landdsroon was part of a series of rebel attacks on Dutch‑controlled plantations during the December campaign, aimed at denying the Dutch any usable base of operations.
