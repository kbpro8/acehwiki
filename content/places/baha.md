**Baha** — written in Dutch as *Baha* — was a kampong and fortification in the [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lamkrak complex]] and the site of combat on [[9 June]] [[1896]] during the 1e colonne's advance in the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|tuchtiging of the Lamkrak Moekims]]. As described in Kruisheer's *ATJEH '96*, Baha was attacked by the 3e compagnie (luit. [[S. v. Hulstijn|S. v. Hulstijn]]) of the 3e Bataljon advancing with maréchaussée; the right peloton (luit. [[M. Campioni|M. Campioni]]) faced the *benting* "Baha"; after salvoes the *benting* was stormed, and the Koeta Baha garrison of 5 Atjehers fled into the hands of the maréchaussée. A newly built koeta with mosque at Baha was a target of the [[9 June]] [[1896]] attack.

## Description and Location

Baha lay in the [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lamkrak complex]] in the country "streek tusschen Biloel en Ana Galoeng en benoorden het gebergte gelegen" (between [[Biloel]] and [[Ana' Galong]], north of the mountains). The kampong had a fortification — the "benting Baha" — that was the principal Achehnese defensive position. A newly built koeta with mosque at Baha was a target of the [[9 June]] [[1896]] attack, indicating that the Achehnese had recently fortified the kampong with a combined koeta-mosque complex. The Koeta Baha was the inner fort of the kampong, with a garrison of 5 Atjehers at the time of the [[9 June]] [[1896]] attack. The kampong was bounded on the north by the [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lam-Lheuë]] mukim (with its *Kling* fortification of Mon Selassa / Mon Seulassa) and on the south by the Ateuh moekim. The Lam-Lheuë bivak and the *Binasah Baroe* / *Binasa Baroe* place near Lam-Lheuë bivak were nearby.

## Historical Background

Baha had been part of the hostile [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lamkrak complex]] throughout the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. The kampong had been a base for hostile *benden* operating against the [[buitenpost|buitenposten]] of the [[buitenlinie]] — particularly [[Biloel]] and [[Lamkoenjit]]. The *benting* Baha was a recently constructed Achehnese fortification, and the newly built koeta with mosque at Baha indicated the recent Achehnese investment in the kampong's defences. The [[1878]] campaign of [[Generaal van der Heiden|Generaal van der Heiden]] had passed through the Baha country, and the [[1893]] *legioen* of [[Teuku Umar|T. Djohan]] had operated in the area.

## Role in the 1896 Operations

Baha was the site of combat on [[9 June]] [[1896]] during the 1e colonne's advance in the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|tuchtiging of the Lamkrak Moekims]]. The 3e compagnie of the 3e Bataljon under luit. [[S. v. Hulstijn|S. v. Hulstijn]] advanced with maréchaussée against Baha; the right peloton under luit. [[M. Campioni|M. Campioni]] faced the *benting* "Baha". After salvoes the *benting* was stormed, and the Koeta Baha garrison of 5 Atjehers fled into the hands of the maréchaussée. The newly built koeta with mosque at Baha was a target of the [[9 June]] [[1896]] attack, and was presumably destroyed in the course of the tuchtiging. The fall of Baha, together with the fall of [[Ateuh Raja]] and [[Lam-Djamé|Lam-Krak / Lam-Djamé]] on the same day, broke the back of organised Achehnese defence in the [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lamkrak complex]]. On [[10 June]] [[1896]] the Dutch operations continued with the destruction of remaining *versterkingen* in the Lamkrak.

## Significance

Baha was one of the three principal Achehnese strongpoints in the [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lamkrak complex]] attacked on [[9 June]] [[1896]] — together with [[Ateuh Raja]] and [[Lam-Djamé|Lam-Krak / Lam-Djamé]] — and its fall was one of the decisive acts of the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|tuchtiging of 9–10 June 1896]]. The *nieuw opgerichte koeta met missigit* at Baha demonstrated the recent Achehnese investment in the kampong's defences, and its capture was a notable Dutch operational success. The flight of the Koeta Baha garrison of 5 Atjehers into the hands of the maréchaussée was a minor but symbolic episode of the tuchtiging.
