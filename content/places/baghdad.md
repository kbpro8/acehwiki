Baghdad appears both as a real place and as a name used in the romances. In the [[Hikayat Nabi Usoh]] (Yusuf), the merchant 'lands at Baghdad (or Bitay Mukadih = [[Jerusalem]]). Here the king is converted to the true faith by Usoh.' In the [[Hikayat Putroe Baren]], the story is set in '[[Boreudat]] (Baghdad).' In the [[Hikayat Pha Suasa]], 'Raja Ahmat, the king of Baghdad ([[Boreudat]])' is the father of the twins Silver-thigh and Golden-thigh. In the Hikayat Kamarodaman, 'in place of the land of Abanus we here have Baghdad.' The romances thus use Baghdad interchangeably with Biblical [[Jerusalem]] and as a general 'royal city.'
