**Lamsoet** — written in Dutch as also *Lam-Soet*, *Lam Seet*, *Lam Soet* — was a [[Dutch]] outpost in the [[III Moekims Kerkoen]], situated on the right bank of the [[Atjehrivier]] and serving as the river crossing between [[Lambaroe]] and [[Ana' Galong]]. As described in Kruisheer's *ATJEH '96*, Lamsoet was garrisoned in [[1895]] by 2 officers, 50 men, 4 *artilleristen*, and 1 European hospital attendant, and was the location of one of the machine guns of the *buitenlinie* — one on a field-carriage and one on a breastwork-carriage; the field-carriage *mitrailleur*'s chest with barrels was lost during the evacuation of [[17 April]] [[1896]]. Lamsoet was lifted on [[17 April]] [[1896]], was retaken without opposition by the 12e Bataljon on [[2 May]] [[1896]] and burned, and was later the site at which [[Habib Samalanga]] nested with 50–60 men.

## Description and Location

Lamsoet lay in the [[III Moekims Kerkoen]] on the right bank of the [[Atjehrivier]], and served as the river crossing between [[Lambaroe]] and [[Ana' Galong]]. The post was situated among the kampongs along the right bank of the Atjeh river that had been, from [[1878]] to [[1885]], a favourite residence of marauding bands. The garrison in [[1895]] consisted of 2 officers, 50 men, 4 *artilleristen*, and 1 European hospital attendant. The post was armed with two *mitrailleurs* — one on a field-carriage and one on a breastwork-carriage — and the field-carriage *mitrailleur*'s chest with barrels was lost during the evacuation of [[17 April]] [[1896]]. The post was linked by telephone to the forward area and served as the headquarters of the *liniecommandant* in the [[17 April]] [[1896]] operations.

## Historical Background

Lamsoet had been constructed as part of the [[buitenlinie]] to control the river crossing on the [[Atjehrivier]] between [[Lambaroe]] and [[Ana' Galong]] and to dominate the kampongs along the right bank of the river that had been a favourite residence of marauding bands from [[1878]] to [[1885]]. The post was one of the eastern anchors of the [[buitenlinie]] in the [[Sagi der XXII Moekims|XXII Moekims]] / [[III Moekims Kerkoen]] sector. The post's two *mitrailleurs* were a notable feature of its armament, reflecting the Dutch reliance on early machine guns in the outer line.

## Role in the 1896 Operations

Lamsoet was one of the [[buitenpost|buitenposten]] lifted by the Dutch in [[April]] [[1896]]. On [[30 March]] [[1896]] the column of [[Majoor G. P. M. van der Noordaa|Van der Noordaa]] reached Lamsoet at 5:15 PM after the relief of the [[blokhuis|blokhuizen]] of [[Aloer Oe]] and [[Toei Selimbing]], and the wounded were re-bandaged there on [[31 March]] [[1896]] at 2 PM. On [[17 April]] [[1896]] Lamsoet was lifted by the [[Battle of Senelop (17 April 1896)|first colonne of Van Heutsz]], which used Lamsoet as its base of operations and as the headquarters of the *liniecommandant*; during the evacuation the field-carriage *mitrailleur*'s chest with barrels was lost. On [[2 May]] [[1896]] the [[Diversie to Montassik (2 May 1896)|diversionary operation to Montassik]] retalso the old Dutch post of Lamsoet without opposition by the 12e Bataljon, which arrived via Belang-West and Bralon, and the post was burned. Thereafter [[Habib Samalanga]] nested at Lamsoet with 50–60 men, making it a centre of Achehnese resistance in the [[III Moekims Kerkoen]] until the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|tuchtiging of 16–17 June 1896]] in which Lamsoet and its *versterkingen* were burned on [[17 June]] [[1896]].

## Significance

Lamsoet was the principal [[Dutch]] *buitenpost* on the [[Atjehrivier]] and the eastern anchor of the [[buitenlinie]] in the [[III Moekims Kerkoen]]. Its loss of the field-carriage *mitrailleur*'s chest with barrels during the evacuation of [[17 April]] [[1896]] was a notable material reverse, since it implied the loss of advanced weaponry to the enemy. The post's subsequent reoccupation by [[Habib Samalanga]] demonstrated that even the evacuated *buitenposten* could be turned into Achehnese strongpoints, requiring the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|tuchtiging of 16–17 June 1896]] to clear.
