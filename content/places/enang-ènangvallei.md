**Enang-ènangvallei** (valley) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Valley on the Gajoweg requiring enormous ground-moving works; a small 80 m iron bridge lies deep below between high mountain walls.

## Type

valley

## Description

Valley on the [[Gajoweg (Gayo Road)|Gajoweg]] requiring enormous ground-moving works; a small 80 m iron bridge lies deep below between high mountain walls.

## Significance

Enang-ènangvallei is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
