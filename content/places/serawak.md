**Serawak** (historically **[[Sarawak]]**) was a coastal region on the north‑west of **[[Borneo]]** that became the personal domain of **[[James Brooke]]** in [[1841]]. The establishment of the Brooke dynasty introduced a British‑aligned polity that contested the influence of the **[[Sultanate of Aceh]]**, which claimed overlordship over parts of western Borneo. The British acquisition of **[[Labuan]]** in [[1846]] further reinforced Serawak’s strategic position in the region.

## Interaction with Aceh

- The Brooke administration’s anti‑piracy campaigns intersected with Acehnese trade routes, leading to occasional clashes.
- British naval support from **[[Labuan]]** enabled Serawak to project power toward areas traditionally under Acehnese suzerainty.
