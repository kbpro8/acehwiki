**Gajo-land (Gayo Land)** (interior highland region) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Interior mountainous region of Aceh.

## Type

interior highland region

## Description

Interior mountainous region of Aceh. In the old days reachable only via bosch- and bergpaden ('[[vrouwenwegen]]' per [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]]'s '[[Snouck Hurgronje's Het Gajoland en zijne bewoners (1903)|Het Gajoland en zijne bewoners]]'). Hoofdplaats: [[Takengon]] (on a lake). [[Gajoweg (Gayo Road)|Gajoweg]] (103 km from [[Bireuën]] to [[Takengon]]) completed [[1913]]. Site of [[Gouvernements Hars- en Terpentijnbedrijf]], coffee and tea enterprises. Inhabited by the Gayo people (mentioned as distinct from Acehnese but their affairs also covered).

## Significance

Gajo-land (Gayo Land) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
