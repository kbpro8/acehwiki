**Pasé** is a region on the north coast of [[Aceh|Atjeh]], in the vicinity of [[Keureutoe|Keureutoë]] and [[Lho Soekon]]. Together with those two landschappen, Pasé stood *aan den rand van een algemeenen opstand* during the [[1907]]-[[1908]] resistance under [[Pang Nanggroë]] and the Paja Bakong oelama's.

## Role in the resistance

The rivers of Pasé, Keureutoë, and Djambo Ajé were the domain of [[Tengkoe di Paja Bakong (Mata Ië)|Tgk. di Mata Ië]], the *Heer der bronnen*. His brother, [[Tengkoe Sjech di Paja Bakong|Tgk. Seupot Mata]], was the blind oelama whose *abawar* (kris with golden handvat) was consecrated in the mystic school of Sjech Abdoel Kadeuë, the *heilige van het ijzer*.

When the *Colonne Matjan* under [[Christoffel]] began its *schoonmaak* in the vlakte of Lho Soekon, Keureutoë, and Pasé, the hardnekkigen — and those with extra on their conscience — verdwenen naar het gebergte, where the benden of the oelama's and of [[Pang Nanggroë]] them opvingen.

## Account from *Verkenningen in Atjeh* (1923)

**Pasé** — also written *Pasai* — is a river and an ancient kingdom of northern Sumatra, neighbouring the kingdom of [[Samoedra|Samoedra]] and now situated in the *onderafdeeling* [[Lho Seumawe|Lho' Seumawé]] of the *afdeeling* [[Noordkust van Atjeh|Noordkust van Atjeh]]. The river forms the northern boundary of the *onderafdeeling* [[Lho Soekon|Lho Soekon]]: *'De rivier Pasé overstekend, betreedt men Gendong en Blang Me, oude landschappen nabij zee, behoorend tot een gebied, dat nu de onderafdeeling Lho' Seumawé heet, maar waar vroeger, 10 eeuwen her, een aanzienlijk rijk was, Samoedra, en daaraan grenzend het rijk Pasé.'*

### The Ancient Kingdom

The kingdom of Pasé, like that of Samoedra, is one of the historical predecessors of the [[Aceh|Atjehnese]] sultanate. In the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries Samoedra and Pasé came under Atjeh, and the princes became subject to the sultan. The visible reminders of the old kingdom lie on the other side of the Pasé-river, in the *onderafdeeling* Lho' Seumawé.

### The Pepper-Planters

The *landschap* [[Kroeëng Pasè|Kroeëng Pasè]] (right bank of the Pasé-river), usually indicated as the *gebied van Hakim Kroeëng*, lies in the western part of the *onderafdeeling* Lho Soekon. It is not large, the population not numerous, but there is irrigation, and if all the grounds were planted, there would be a surplus that one could export. It is known that petroleum-springs occur in the basin of the river Ngom. The eastern coast of Atjeh was in older times populated by pepper-planters from [[Pidie|Pidie]], [[Samalanga|Samalanga]], [[Pensangan|Pensangan]] and Pasé, who settled temporarily and many of whom formerly remained for good — for example, in the *landschap* [[Peureula (Perlak)|Perlak]].

### Significance

Pasé is one of the two great historical kingdoms of the northern Sumatran coast — Samoedra and Pasé — and its incorporation into Atjeh in the sixteenth century is the political event that opened the eastern coast to the Atjehnese sultanate. The river remains the principal geographical feature of the *onderafdeeling* Lho' Seumawé, and the *landschap* Kroeëng Pasè preserves the name.
