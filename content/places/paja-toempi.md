**Paja Toempi** is the small estate of the [[Familie Veenhuizen|Veenhuizen family]] — a father with three sons — at kilometre 98 of the [[Gajo-weg|Gajo-weg]], between [[Lampahan|Lampahan]] and [[Takengon|Takengon]], on a *paja* of twenty-five *bouw*. The site lies at the foot of a mountain-slope, down which the washed-down soil comes as a welcome addition to the garden; but water is largely lacking, which is unfavourable for market-gardening. The family began in [[1921]] with the cultivation of potatoes alongside various vegetables. Disease appeared in the potatoes, but the Dutch seed-potatoes, imported with difficulty, held up well. The market for the produce is in Atjeh itself: the family brings its fruit and vegetables by lorry to [[Bireuen|Bireuen]] — a journey of five to six hours — and there loads them into the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]]. The family expects soon to receive three hundred *bouw* of forest-ground at kilometres 95 and 96 of the Gajo-weg, and intends to plant coffee there.
