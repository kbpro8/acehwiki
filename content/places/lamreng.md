**Lamreng** was a Dutch military post in the [[XXV Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]] — one of the two principal posts (the other being [[Lambaroe]]) at the south-eastern corner of the Dutch *geconcentreerde linie* in the late *concentratie* period. The post was the seat of the *linie-commandant* (line-commandant) for the Lamreng sector, and it was one of the principal bases for the operations against the Atjehnese fortification of [[Kaloet]] — a fortification that lay approximately 800 metres from the *spoorbaan* (railway line) between Lambaroe and Lamreng.

## The strategic position

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] records that Lamreng — together with [[Lambaroe]] — formed the south-eastern corner of the Dutch *geconcentreerde linie*, and that the two posts were connected by the *ceintuurbahn* (belt-tramway) of the [[Atjeh-tram]] system. The album's narrative of the [[De Strijd om Kaloet (1892)|Strijd om Kaloet]] records that the *Kroeng Kali|Kroeng Kali]] — the river that ran some two hundred metres west of Kaloet — flowed *recht noordwaarts* (straight northwards) towards Lamreng, and that the bed of this river was the principal route by which the Dutch patrols approached Kaloet.

## The patrols of August 1892

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that, on the night of [[22 August|22-23 August]] [[1892]], Lieutenant [[Brant]] — of the *maréchaussée* — lay in ambush with a detachment of *maréchaussées* in the dry bed of the *Kroeng Kali* opposite Kaloet, with the intention of catching the Atjehnese garrison as they came in the morning. The Atjehnese, however, *versliepen zich* (overslept), so that the Dutch patrol had to return to Lamreng at 7 a.m. without having caught anyone. The patrol returned along the footpath that the Atjehnese band had earlier followed, and the album's narrative of the patrol is one of the principal Dutch accounts of night-patrolling in the late *concentratie* period.

## The reconnaissance of 23 August 1892

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that, *directly na terugkomst te Lamreng van de patrouille Brunt* (immediately after the return to Lamreng of the Brunt patrol), a reconnaissance column — under Captain [[Jhr. Graafland]] — set out from Lamreng up the [[Kroeng Tandjong]], with Lieutenant [[Brant]] as guide. The reconnaissance destroyed the Atjehnese fortifications in the *Kroeng Tandjong* and approached to within 50 metres of Kaloet's *vóórwal* (front wall) before withdrawing. The reconnaissance — the [[Reconnaissance of Kaloet (23 August 1892)|reconnaissance of 23 August 1892]] — was the first Dutch operation against Kaloet.

## The 1 October 1892 assault

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that, on [[1 October]] [[1892]], a strong column from [[Lambaroe]] under a captain and three lieutenants — supported by 75 men from [[Lampermej]] and 50 men of the 14th Battalion — assaulted Kaloet from the north-east, while a detachment from Lamreng attempted to approach the west side of the *benting*. The assault — the [[Battle of Kaloet (1 October 1892)|battle of Kaloet (1 October 1892)]] — was repulsed with 7 killed and more than 30 wounded, including Lieutenant [[Hoolboom]] (mortally wounded) and Lieutenant [[Drijber]].

## The 1893 capture of Kaloet

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that, on [[4 August]] [[1893]], [[Toekoe Oemar]] — supported by the 4th Mountain Battery from the Kraton and approximately 1000 men — invested Kaloet, while *het geschut van Lamreng, maar vooral van Lambaroe, hield de omliggende vijandelijke kampongs voortdurend onder vuur* (the artillery of Lamreng, but especially of Lambaroe, kept the surrounding enemy kampongs continually under fire). The investment was followed by the [[Capture of Kaloet by Toekoe Oemar (August 1893)|bloodless capture of Kaloet]] on the morning of [[5 August]] [[5 August]] [[1893]], when Toekoe Oemar found the *benting* abandoned.

## Significance

Lamreng's significance in the documentary record of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] is as one of the two principal posts at the south-eastern corner of the Dutch *geconcentreerde linie* — the post whose *linie-commandant* commanded the sector opposite the Atjehnese fortification of [[Kaloet]]. The post's role in the [[De Strijd om Kaloet (1892)|Strijd om Kaloet]] of [[1892]] and [[1893]] — as the base for the night-patrols of Lieutenant [[Brant]], the reconnaissance of Captain [[Jhr. Graafland|Graafland]], the assault of [[1 October]] [[1892]] and the artillery support of [[Toekoe Oemar]]'s [[Capture of Kaloet by Toekoe Oemar (August 1893)|capture of Kaloet]] of [[August]] [[1893]] — places it as one of the principal operational centres of the late *concentratie* period.
