**Beresteyn plantation** was a sugar plantation in the interior of [[Acheh]] that was found in ashes by Dutch forces on [[25 December]]. During the operation a member of the “Corantijnse overlopers” (Corinthian border detachment) from the Surinamese frontier was captured there.

## Significance
The capture of the Surinamese border detachment highlighted the broader regional dimensions of the conflict, linking rebel activity in Aceh with the neighboring Dutch colonies.
