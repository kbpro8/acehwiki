**Gouverneurswoning te Koetaradja** was the Governor's residence at [[Koeta Radja]], seat of the Civil and Military Governor of [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]].

It was there that [[H. N. A. Swart]] put a good lieutenant's house nearby at [[Liefrinck]]'s disposal during his inspections, and there that on [[2 September]] [[1918]], in a solemn gathering, Swart handed over the government to his successor [[Van Sluys]] and delivered his [[Swart's farewell address (2 September 1918)|farewell address]]. A photograph in [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]]'s book is captioned "The Governor's Residence at Koetaradja (Atjeh)."
