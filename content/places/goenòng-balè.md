**Goenòng Balè** is a subject named in *[[Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[G. F. P. Gosenson]]

Gosenson was returning with a few *marechaussée* from an elephant hunt undertaken at the request of the inhabitants of [[Goenòng Balè]] when he was attacked by some young Acehnese of [[Seunagan]]. He "was severely wounded in the action by a sabre stroke straight across the face, by which he remained disfigured for life, and by a cut across the thigh; he and his constabulary men, though all wounded, laid the three attackers low."

### From [[Tgk. Poeti']]

On [[29 December]] [[1917]] the bivouac commander of Djeuram, *1ste luitenant* [[G. F. P. Gosenson]], returning with a few *[[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|marechaussée]]* from an elephant hunt undertaken at the request of the inhabitants of [[Goenòng Balè]], was attacked by some young Acehnese of Seunagan. He was severely wounded by a *[[klewang]]* blow across the face, disfiguring him for life, and by a cut across the thigh; he and his men, all wounded, laid the three attackers low.

### From [[Djeuram]]

On [[29 December]] [[1917]] Gosenson, returning with a few *[[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|marechaussée]]* from an elephant hunt undertaken at the request of the inhabitants of [[Goenòng Balè]], was gravely wounded in the [[Aanslag op Gosenson te Djeuram (29 December 1917)|attack]] organised by [[Tgk. Poeti']]. [[Teukoe Agam]], whom [[H. J. Schmidt]] brought to [[Daja]] in [[1914]], had formerly been head of his intelligence service at Djeuram. In [[1926]] [[Teukoe Keumangan]] acknowledged to [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]] that T. Radja Tampo' had been present on the *pasar* at Djeuram.
