**Rantau Pandjang** — modern *Rantu Panjang* — is a place in the *landschap* of [[Peureula (Perlak)|Peureula (Perlak)]] in the *onderafdeeling* [[Langsa|Langsa]] of the *afdeeling* [[Oostkust van Atjeh|Oostkust van Atjeh]], situated on the coast north-west of the chief place Peureula. It is the site of the [[Petroleum-bronnen van Perlak|petroleum-spring]] discovered by officers of the man-of-war *Timor* in May [[1875]], led thither by Atjehnese chiefs, and of the later oil-terrains of the [[Perlak Petroleum-Mij|Perlak Petroleum-Mij]] and the famous [[Zuid-Perlak Petroleum-Mij|Zuid-Perlak Petroleum-Mij]]. The [[Bataafsche Petroleum-Mij|Bataafsche Petroleum-Mij]], which exploits the Perlak Petroleum-Mij, does not process the oil on the spot but pumps it through a 120 km pipe-line to [[Pangkalan Brandan|Pangkalan Brandan]]. Only the gases are compressed at Rantau Pandjang into oil, which is likewise pumped away. There is a substantial settlement there with about thirty Europeans.

## Account from *Vijftig jaren economische staatkunde in Atjeh* ([[1923]])

**Rantau Pandjang** was the "oliestad" (oil city) which gradually arose in the interior of [[Perlak]] in [[Acheh|Atjeh]] as the Bataafsche Petroleum Maatschappij's exploitation centre — a brilliantly equipped enterprise surrounded by oil wells, pumping its crude oil through a kilometres-long pipeline to [[Pangkalan Brandan]] (see [[Bataafsche Petroleum Maatschappij|Petroleum industry in Atjeh]]).

### The oil city

Langhout writes: "Daar, in het binnenland is gaandeweg een oliestad, Rantau Pandjang, ontstaan" ("There, in the interior, an oil city, Rantau Pandjang, gradually arose"). The oil was won there and pumped to Pangkalan Brandan, the industrial city of the [[Langkat]] terrains, by a kilometres-long pipeline. Rantau Pandjang had a brilliantly equipped enterprise, surrounded by oil wells; beside a number of European workmen, a very great number of native and Chinese workers found an ample existence in the Bataafsche's enterprise. In December [[1922]], beside the "121197 ton" of crude oil pumped from the tract of [[Peureula]] (as printed), 598 tons of crude oil were pumped from the oil field at [[Aloer Mas]] to Rantau Pandjang, the exploitation figures showing over the last years a steady increase.
