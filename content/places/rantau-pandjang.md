**Rantau Pandjang** — modern *Rantu Panjang* — is a place in the *landschap* of [[Peureula (Perlak)|Peureula (Perlak)]] in the *onderafdeeling* [[Langsa|Langsa]] of the *afdeeling* [[Oostkust van Atjeh|Oostkust van Atjeh]], situated on the coast north-west of the chief place Peureula. It is the site of the [[Petroleum-bronnen van Perlak|petroleum-spring]] discovered by officers of the man-of-war *Timor* in May [[1875]], led thither by Atjehnese chiefs, and of the later oil-terrains of the [[Perlak Petroleum-Mij|Perlak Petroleum-Mij]] and the famous [[Zuid-Perlak Petroleum-Mij|Zuid-Perlak Petroleum-Mij]]. The [[Bataafsche Petroleum-Mij|Bataafsche Petroleum-Mij]], which exploits the Perlak Petroleum-Mij, does not process the oil on the spot but pumps it through a 120 km pipe-line to [[Pangkalan Brandan|Pangkalan Brandan]]. Only the gases are compressed at Rantau Pandjang into oil, which is likewise pumped away. There is a substantial settlement there with about thirty Europeans.

## The 1875 Discovery

In [[May]] [[1875]] officers of the warship *Timor*, led by Atjehnese chiefs, found a petroleum spring enclosed by a wooden tank with a lid. Raw oil collected twice a day from the tank; water was skimmed off with buckets and banana-leaf scoops, and the oil carried back to Rantau Pandjang for sale at almost six dollars the *picol*. The head's monthly income from this trade amounted to around 200 dollars, besides three other sources. This discovery directly triggered the Dutch government's interest in the petroleum resources of [[Perlak]] and ultimately led to the establishment of the [[Perlak Petroleum-Mij]] and [[Zuid-Perlak Petroleum-Mij]].

## The Oil City

By the early [[1920s]] Rantau Pandjang had become the *oliestad* (oil city) of the interior of [[Perlak]]. [[J. Langhout|Langhout]] wrote in [[1923]]: *"Daar, in het binnenland is gaandeweg een oliestad, Rantau Pandjang, ontstaan"* ("There, in the interior, an oil city, Rantau Pandjang, gradually arose"). The Bataafsche Petroleum Maatschappij's exploitation centre was brilliantly equipped, surrounded by oil wells, and pumped its crude oil through a kilometres-long pipeline to [[Pangkalan Brandan]], the industrial city of the [[Langkat]] terrains. Beside a number of European workmen, a very great number of native and Chinese workers found an ample existence in the enterprise. In December [[1922]], alongside 121,197 tons of crude oil pumped from the [[Peureula]] tract, 598 tons of crude oil were pumped from the oil field at [[Aloer Mas]] to Rantau Pandjang — the exploitation figures showing a steady increase over the preceding years.
