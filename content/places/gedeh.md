**Gedeh** (also read *Gedah*) was a place inside the Dutch [[Geconcentreerde Stelling|concentrated position]] at [[Acheh|Atjeh]] in [[1885]], with a *winkelstraat* (shop-street) frequented by patrols from the main guard of [[Kuta Raja|Kota Radja]]. On [[14 August]] [[1885]] a reconnaissance patrol of the main guard was fired upon in the shop-street of Gedeh, two men were killed, and the enemy took two rifles with him (see [[Guerilla raids inside the concentration line (1885)]]). (Distinct from [[Gedeh-Breuëh]].)
