---
title: Runeng
tags: ["place", "north-east-coast", "west-coast"]
---

The stronghold of the holy Teungku di Meuke', whose conflict with the pro-Dutch chiefs of [[Meulaboh]] is celebrated in the Hikayat Teungku di Meuke'. 'The poet celebrates the conflict waged in [[1893]] and [[1894]] by the chiefs of [[Meulaboh]], friendly to the Dutch, against the party of resistance, whose chief stronghold was Runeng and who were led by the holy Teungku di Meuke'.'
