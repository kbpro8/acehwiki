**Aloeë Soea** was the *aloeë* (brook) at which the Ambonese tracker sergeant [[Dambohpulu]] found the traces of the foragers of [[Tgk. di Barat]]'s band in February [[1912]].

Deze vond aan de Aloeë (beekje) Soea *bekas* (sporen) van de fourageurs der bende van den Teungkoe, en met uiterste moeite kon de brigade deze goed verborgen en deels weggewerkte sporen volgen. The trail so found led, after several days, to the surprise of [[22 February]] [[1912]] in which the Teungkoe and his wife were killed by [[H. Behrens|Behrens]]'s brigades.
