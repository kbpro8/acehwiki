**Lam No** was the place in the [[Daja]] country where the Civil Authority [[W. van Kregten]] and two soldiers were murdered on [[20 May]] [[1914]].

The band of twelve men, armed with *[[rentjong]]* and *[[klewang]]* and drawn from the *[[landschap]]pen* Lam No, [[Panté Tjeureumèn]], [[Djeunamprong]] and [[Oenga]], fled to the mountains with the soldiers' weapons and was pursued by several brigades. The plan to withdraw had already been formed when the extremely careless night quarters on the *keudé* at Lam No gave them "a fine chance to make a name and at the same time to provide themselves with weapons." [[Tgk. Machmoed van Lam No]], a former *teungkoe-meunasah* at odds with his *[[oelèëbalang]]* over his share of the *[[zakat]]*, was one of the four principal perpetrators.
