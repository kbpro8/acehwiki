**Simpang-kiri** — the *Left Branch* — is one of the two rivers which together form the [[Tamiang-rivier|Tamiang river]] in the *onderafdeeling* [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] of the *afdeeling* [[Oostkust van Atjeh|Oostkust van Atjeh]], the other being the [[Simpang Kanan|Simpang-kanan]]. The *stroomgebied* of the two rivers is the geographic core of Tamiang, and the great rubber-estate [[Simpang-kiri (estate)|Simpang-kiri]] of a Belgian company lies on its banks.

River and place in the [[onderafdeeling]] [[Singkel]]; location of the place [[Roendeng]], the [[Singkel]] onderafdelingshoofdplaats accessible by motorboat. One of the four districts of [[Singkel]].
