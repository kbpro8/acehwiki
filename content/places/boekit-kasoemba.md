**The Boekit Kasoemba** was the height in [[Groot-Atjeh]] that marked the inland end of the main position of [[Teuku Umar|Toekoe Oemar]] before the [[Three-day battle in the Beradin pass (23-25 May 1896)|three-day battle in the Beradin pass]] of [[23 May|23]]–[[25 May]] [[1896]].

[[P. H. R. Beuming]] records that the enemy's main position *zich van de Boekit Kasoemba tot de kust uitstrekte* — extended from the Boekit Kasoemba to the coast — and that against it the Dutch were to operate with several columns, in order at once to command a broad strip of terrain and, by a converging movement of the wings coupled with a forward pressure of the centre, to drive the enemy in a south-westerly direction.

On the first day of the battle the Dutch artillery brought a fortification lying on the Boekit Kasoemba under fire while the [[6e Bataljon Infanterie|6e Bataljon]] took position south of [[Ketapan Doea]] and the column of Overste [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] began the ascent of the [[Glé Poetih]].
