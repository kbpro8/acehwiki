**Gombong** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[H. N. A. Swart]]

Swart, who besides his myopic astigmatism was afflicted with "an unusual interest in education", kept a large and warm place in his memory for the school. When a boy asked him one day whether he could become an officer — "Hm, well, are you really clever enough for that?" said Swart; "Yes indeed, but can you not help me?" — the lieutenants conferred and the result was a *cursus* for boys eligible for more extended education. Swart's verdict: "A really good institution; I have always regretted that it was abolished and that the boys were then distributed among the orphanages" ("A really good institution; I always regretted that it was abolished and the boys then distributed among the orphanages"). Throughout his long service he repeatedly and with great pleasure met old and successful *pupillen*, often as non-commissioned officers and often with the [[Militaire Willemsorde|M.W.O.]] or an honourable mention; a colonel who headed the [[Topografische Dienst]] and a well-known *buiten-werker* had been his pupils at [[Gombong]].

### From [[Java]]

[[H. N. A. Swart]] remained obstinately on Java and in the *vredesgarnizoenen* from [[1890]] until his captaincy in [[1898]]; he served at [[Magelang]], [[Gombong]], [[Semarang]] and [[Batavia]]. [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]'s solution of the teacher problem for *[[Volksonderwijs in Atjeh|volksonderwijs]]* took the Javanese *desa* as its model; at the census of [[1930]] Java's literacy rate was 5.5 per cent against Atjeh's 1.1. Banishment to Java was the penalty [[T. Béntara Titeuë]] demanded in [[1913]] and which Dutch law could not impose for slight wounding.

### From [[Korps Pupillen te Gombong]]

**Korps Pupillen te Gombong** was the institution at [[Gombong]] in Central [[Java]] for the children of soldiers killed in action or pensioned, at which [[H. N. A. Swart]] served as instructor from [[20 August]] [[1891]].
