**Kroeng Woyla (river)** (river) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. River near Meulaboh.

## Type

river

## Description

River near [[Malaboeh|Meulaboh]]. At this [[Kroeng|kroeng]] (river) lies one end of the [[Transversaalweg (Noord-Westkust)|transversal road]] that is to connect the Noord- and [[Westkust]] of Aceh.

## Significance

Kroeng Woyla (river) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
