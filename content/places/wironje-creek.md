**Wironje creek** was a river in the interior of [[Acheh]] that featured prominently in the Dutch December expedition. Dutch vessels anchored there on [[11 am]] and the bark “[[St Eustatius]]” took up a post at the plantation “[[Cornelia Jacoba]]” to secure communications and prevent rebel re‑entry.

## Operations
Patrols from the bark “[[Zeven Provinciën]]” explored the creek and the nearby plantation “[[Prosperiteit]]”. The creek’s mouth was also the point where the Dutch attempted to block rebel retreat routes.
