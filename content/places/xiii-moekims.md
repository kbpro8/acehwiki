**The XIII Moekims** — the "Thirteen Mukims" — were a mukim-cluster of [[Groot-Atjeh]] whose *[[Uleebalang|oeloebalang]]* — [[Toekoe Tjoet Toengkoep]] — was, in the [[1890s]], one of the three chiefs of the *driemanschap* (three-man directorate) charged by the Dutch with the purification of the [[XXVI Moekims]] of *Moslemin* bands. The XIII Moekims are named in the [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] in the chapter on *De Inwendige Toestand* as the territory of the *oeloebalang* whose *vreemde houding* (strange attitude) led to his *tijdelijke* (temporary) banishment to [[Melaboe]].

## The driemanschap of the XXVI Moekims

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that, when the Dutch attempted to extend the system of using Atjehnese auxiliaries against the *Moslemin* to the [[XXVI Moekims]] — a terrain in which [[Toekoe Oemar]] had *weinig invloed* (little influence) — the leadership of the operation was entrusted to a *driemanschap* of friendly *hoofden*: [[Toekoe Noq van Marassa|Toekoe Neq van Marassa]], [[Toekoe Nju Banta]] (the *panglima* of the XXVI Moekims) and [[Toekoe Tjoet Toengkoep]] (the *oeloebalang* of the XIII Moekims). The XIII Moekims thus held a position of strategic importance in the late *concentratie* period, and their *oeloebalang* was one of the three chiefs entrusted with the principal Atjehnese auxiliary operation of the late [[1880s]] and early [[1890s]].

## The failure of the driemanschap

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that the *driemanschap* in the XXVI Moekims *liep heel wat minder gunstig af, dan in de XXV Moekims het geval was geweest* (went much less favourably than had been the case in the XXV Moekims). The failure was, in the album's reading, due in part to the *vreemde houding* (strange attitude) of [[Toekoe Tjoet Toengkoep]] — the *oeloebalang* of the XIII Moekims — who was *tijdelijk naar Melaboe moest verbannen worden* (had to be temporarily banished to Melaboe). The failure of the *driemanschap* in the XXVI Moekims — and the consequent Dutch military intervention with a full battalion and some artillery — was one of the costliest engagements of the late *concentratie* period, with 1 officer and 3 subordinates killed and 3 officers and 32 subordinates wounded.

## Significance

The XIII Moekims' significance in the documentary record of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] is as the territory of [[Toekoe Tjoet Toengkoep]] — the *oeloebalang* whose *vreemde houding* and consequent banishment to [[Melaboe]] were among the principal causes of the failure of the *driemanschap* in the XXVI Moekims. The XIII Moekims thus held a position of strategic importance in the late *concentratie* period, and their *oeloebalang*'s banishment was one of the principal documented instances of the Dutch use of internal banishment as a disciplinary instrument against unreliable *oeloebalang* allies.
