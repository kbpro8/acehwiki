**Arnhem** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[H. N. A. Swart]]

He was made corporal on [[16 March]] and sergeant on [[30 October]]. Being a few days too young to sit the examination for the *Hoofdcursus* within the year, he was obliged to remain two years on the course at [[Arnhem]]; after six years in all he passed out through the *Hoofdcursus* as an officer with a middling examination number. He re-engaged for a year on [[16 October]] [[1886]] and on [[21 November]] [[1886]] was appointed *2de luitenant* in the infantry of the army in the Netherlands Indies by royal decree no. 18 of [[24 November]] [[1886]].
