**Leupoeëng (Leupoeëngsche)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Landschap on the Westkust from which Teukoe Oemar descended and which he invaded Groot-Atjeh from in 1882-1884; he was appointed oelèëbalang of Leupoeëng in January 1896.

## Description

[[landschap|Landschap]] on the [[Westkust]] from which [[Teukoe Oemar]] descended and which he invaded [[Groot-Atjeh]] from in [[1882]]-[[1884]]; he was appointed [[oelèëbalang]] of [[Leupoeëng]] in [[January]] [[1896]].

## Significance

Leupoeëng (Leupoeëngsche) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
