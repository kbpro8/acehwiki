**Lamjoeng** was the bivouac site between [[Tongah]] and Silang on the far side of the [[Kroeng Tjoet]] where [[Generaal Pel|generaal Pel]] died suddenly in the night of [[24 February]] [[1876]] of a *slagaderbreuk* (ruptured artery), interrupting his great [[Pel's offensive against the VI, IV and IX Moekims (1875–1876)|winter offensive]] and the secret afsluitingslinie he had planned from the [[Kroeng Raba]] mouth to the [[Kroeng Raya]] mouth. Borel calls the loss "eene ware nationale ramp" — a true national disaster.

## The bivouac

On [[23 February]] [[1876]] Pel's colonne, marching northward from [[Pango]] via Lamkapang, Loeëng Iek, Poeklat and Oeleh Karang to the abandoned kampong Pinang and on through the Langgoegoeb, reached [[Tongah]] on the *linker-oever* of the Kroeng Tjoet. The colonne crossed to the far side of the river, and the bivouac was opgeslagen at Lamjoeng between Tongah and Silang. The march had been a continuous forward movement against an enemy who could no longer stand in the field; each enemy stelling had been found abandoned as the Dutch approached. Lamjoeng was therefore, in tactical terms, an unopposed bivouac — a temporary halt on the way to the [[Kroeng Raya]] mouth and the completion of the afsluitingslinie. [[Luitenant-kolonel Engel|Lt.-kol. Engel]] had arrived at Lamjoeng with the 9de bataljon to versterk the ageerende troepen.

## Pel's death

In the night of [[24 February]] [[1876]] Pel suddenly overleed at the gevolgen of a *slagaderbreuk* (ruptured artery). Borel's elegy is among the most quoted passages of the book: "Het vaderland verloor in hem een zijder trouwste, ijverigste en meest bekwame dienaren; dat verlies was in de gegeven omstandigheden bijna onherstelbaar en kon eene ware nationale ramp genoemd worden." Pel's plan to fully isolate Atjeh from the sea — the great linie from the mouth of the Kroeng Raba on the west coast to the mouth of the Kroeng Raya on the north coast — remained geheim. He had not communicated it even to his *chef van den staf*, and nothing about it was found in his archive after his death. [[Luitenant-kolonel Engel|Lt.-kol. Engel]] assumed tijdelijk oppergezag as oudste hoofdofficier, immediately took the required measures, and continued the march via Tjade to [[Kwala Gigieng]], bereikt on [[26 February]] [[1876]]. Posts were opgericht at Kwala Gigieng and on the island of [[Kota Pohama]] in the lagune, sealing the mouth of the Kroeng Tjoet. But the larger afsluitingslinie was never completed: [[Generaal Wiggers van Kerchem|Wiggers van Kerchem]] on [[10 March]] [[1876]] adopted the *afwachtende houding* and the stelling [[Kroeng Raba|K. Raba]]–[[Kwala Gigieng]] remained "nagenoeg onveranderd" through Borel's writing in [[1878]].
