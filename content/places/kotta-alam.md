**Kotta Alam** was a fortified settlement and earthen redoubt (*benteng*) of the [[Aceh Sultanate]] positioned along the Aceh River between coastal defenses at [[Oleh-leh]] and royal capital at [[Kraton of Aceh]] in modern Banda Aceh, Indonesia.

## Siege and Capture in 1874

Guarding the northern river approach to the sultan's palace, Kotta Alam was besieged by General [[J. van Swieten]] in January [[1874]] during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]]. In the [[Capture of Kotta Alam]], Dutch siege batteries breached the earthworks, and infantry battalions stormed the interior, forcing the Acehnese garrison to retreat toward [[Penajoeng]].

## Guerrilla Skirmishes in 1875

Following its capture, Kotta Alam became a forward Dutch garrison post. Throughout [[1875]], Acehnese guerrilla forces frequently launched raids and ambushed Dutch transport convoys between Kotta Alam and [[Oleh Karang]], a pattern of resistance that prompted General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]] to launch [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]] in December [[1875]].
