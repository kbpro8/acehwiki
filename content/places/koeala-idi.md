**Koeala Idi** (oil boring site) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Site of BPM sea-boring (zeeboring).

## Type

oil boring site

## Description

Site of BPM sea-boring (zeeboring).

## Significance

Koeala Idi is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
