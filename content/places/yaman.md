In [[Hikayat Putroe Baren]] (XXIV): 'The Banta goes forth to wrest from its four guardian jens a silver tree which she wants to use in building a palace. While this palace is being erected, the king of Yaman comes to carry off the beautiful princess. He is however driven back by the Banta who pursues him to Yaman and converts the people of that country to Islam.'
