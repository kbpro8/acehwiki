A gampong where the renowned teacher [[Abduggamad]] (a Malay) resided and taught. [[Teungku di Lam Nyong]] studied here under this Malay teacher. After the death of Muhamat Amin (Teungku Lam Bhu') and his successor [[Abduggamad]], a period of inactivity in learning followed until [[Cheh Marahaban]]'s appearance. In the weaving descriptions, 'ija Lam Bhu'' is listed as one of the cloth patterns named after the place where the pattern originated or is best designed. [[Pochut Muhamat]], after his victory over Jeumaloy, married a lady of royal lineage at Gampong Lham Bhu'. The widow of the learned Malay teacher was Teungku Lam Bhu', who composed in prose the treatise Sipheuet dua ploh on the twenty attributes of God, for the benefit of her disciples.

Gampong Lham Bhu' was until the coming of the Dutch an extensive and flourishing village included in the [[Banda Acheh]]. [[Pochut Muhamat]] married a lady of royal lineage here after his victory. Also name of the cloth pattern 'ija Lam Bhu''.
