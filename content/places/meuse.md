In [[Hikayat Diwa Sangsareh]]: 'Prince Diwa Sangsareh was the son of the king of Meuse, Useuman Sareh, and was born at the same instant as Aminolah, the son of the wazir of that country.' At the end: 'the two brave wayfarers are happily wedded and return to Meuse, where Sangsareh now mounts the throne of his forefathers.' Meuse also appears in the [[Hikayat Pha Suasa]] as one of the kingdoms of Syria, Turkey, [[Egypt]], etc.
