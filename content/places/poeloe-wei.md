**Poeloe Wei** (modern *Pulau We*, also *Pulo Wai/Way*; also associated with [[sabang|Sabang]]) is an island off the northern tip of [[aceh|Aceh]]. During the [[aceh-war|Aceh War]] its chief, Toekoe Nja Daoed, was recognised by the Dutch as the local head; in [[1886]] he was attacked by a raiding band from the XXVI Moekims.
