**Lampagger** was the kampong in the [[VI Moekims|VI Moekims]] sector whose *opperhoofd* (chief) submitted to the Dutch on [[10 January]] [[1876]] in the first phase of [[Generaal Pel|Pel]]'s [[Pel's offensive against the VI, IV and IX Moekims (1875–1876)|great winter offensive]] and at which a Dutch post was opgericht. From Lampagger Pel's colonne marched on [[15 January]] [[1876]] through the [[Blang Kalla]] kloof southward into the [[IV Moekims]], reaching Loenga on the right bank of the [[Kroeng Raba]] mouth.

## The submission of 10 January 1876

The submission of the *opperhoofd* of Lampagger on [[10 January]] [[1876]] was one of the early political fruits of Pel's winter offensive. The offensive had been launched on [[26 December]] [[1875]] under Pel's personal leiding, with three colonnes in *verbond* moving against the [[VI Moekims|VI]], [[IV Moekims|IV]] and [[IX Moekims|IX Moekims]]. [[Majoor Vetter|majoor Vetter]] bezette Mibouw ten zuiden van [[Kota Radja|K. Radja]]; [[Majoor F. J. E. Mekern|majoor F. J. E. Mekern]] took stelling between Mibouw and the [[Kroeng Daroe]] but was *doodelijk gewond*; [[Luitenant-kolonel Engel|lt.-kol. Engel]] advanced along the right bank of the Kroeng Daroe. [[Ketapang Doewa]] fell on [[27 December]] [[1875]], [[Djempit]] and Pakan Badak on [[28 December]] [[1875]], [[Lamtengah]] on [[8 January]] [[1876]]. The submission of the Lampagger *opperhoofd* on [[10 January]] [[1876]] was therefore part of a broader Dutch advance through the VI Moekims, in which enemy resistance collapsed more rapidly than the Dutch had expected.

## The post and the march

A Dutch post was opgericht at Lampagger as the forward base for the second phase of the offensive. From Lampagger the colonne marched on [[15 January]] [[1876]] through the [[Blang Kalla|kloof of Blang Kalla]] southward over *bergachtig terrein* (mountainous terrain) into the IV Moekims, reaching Loenga on the right bank of the Kroeng Raba mouth, which would become the *westelijke grens* (western limit) of the new afsluitingslinie. The hoofden at Loenga submitted. The colonne returned via the *noordoostelijke weg*, left a post at Boekit Seboen on [[24 January]] [[1876]], and reached Pakan Badak; it then made a *tocht* through the eastern part of the IV Moekims. The Lampagger post was therefore the operational hinge of the second phase of the offensive, exactly as the Ketapang Doewa and Lamtengah posts had been of the first phase.
