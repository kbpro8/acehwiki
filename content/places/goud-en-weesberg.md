**Goud- en Weesberg** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Twin volcanoes west of the tertiair heuvelland near Koetaradja.

## Description

Twin volcanoes west of the tertiair heuvelland near [[Koetaradja]]. Acehnese names: Silawaih-Agam (man) and Silawaih-Inong (woman). The [[Agam|agam]](higher) only recognizable as volcano by solfataras. The tertiair heuvelland broadens south of them.[[Attescheiding|attescheiding]]' (tertiary hill land) separating [[Pidië]] and [[Groot-Atjeh]] plains.

## Significance

Goud- en Weesberg is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
