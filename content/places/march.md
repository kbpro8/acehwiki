**March** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Hendrikus Colijn]]

Colijn, "the future adviser for the Outer Provinces," visited Atjeh again shortly before [[March]] [[1914]], by then already a former minister; his judgement of the situation then agreed entirely with Swart's own, as expressed in the [[Sumatra Post]] interview, and with that of the minister of Colonies who had proposed replacing the military authority in Atjeh wholly by the civil.

## Account in Hooijer's *De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh* ([[1897]])

**[[March]]** is a calendar month used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention. In the material drawn from *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]* it is associated with the storm of [[Senelop]] on [[12 March]] [[1894]]; the investment of [[Tjot Goeë]] from [[29 March]] [[1895]]; the patrol of [[Van Blokland|Kapitein Blokland]] on [[7 March]] [[1896]]; and the [[Defection of Teukoe Oemar (1896)|defection of Teukoe Oemar]] on [[29 March]] [[1896]].
