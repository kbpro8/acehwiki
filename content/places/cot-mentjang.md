**Cot Mentjang** was/is a tactical military post and hill station located in the rugged foothills of [[Groot-Atjeh]].

## Historical Context and Role

In [[1898]], Cot Mentjang served as a co-station for the 3rd Division of the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]], monitoring guerrilla movement between the valley and [[Glé Broet]].
