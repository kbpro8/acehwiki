[[Teungku Lam Paloh]] 'was a Javanese of Yogya, who married and had a family in [[Acheh]], and without much claim to learning came to be regarded as a saint by a certain coterie.' This man 'borrowed his name from the gampong (within the 'linie') where he had his abode.'
