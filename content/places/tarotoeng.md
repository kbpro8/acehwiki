**Tarotoeng** was a point of arrival for the **[[Gajo Expedition]]** of [[1904]], the final major military campaign of the **[[Aceh War]]**. The expedition, commanded by **[[Gotfried Coenraad Ernst van Daalen|Van Daalen]]**, reached Tarotoeng on **[[18 June]] [[1904]]** after 163 days of travel through the Gajo and Alas highlands.

## The Expedition

Van Daalen's force departed in **[[February]] [[1904]]** with approximately 200 *marechaussees*. The expedition traversed the Gajo highlands, a territory larger than the Netherlands, crossing high mountain passes and fighting against fortified *bentengs*.

On **[[18 June]] [[1904]]**, Van Daalen's force reached Tarotoeng in the **[[Tapanoeli]]** residency, completing its mission.

## Significance

Tarotoeng marks the conclusion of the Aceh War's final campaign. The highland regions of Aceh were brought under Dutch control, and organized Acehnese resistance came to an end.
