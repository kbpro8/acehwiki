**XXVI Mukims** was a coastal federal district (*Sagi*) of Great Aceh located along the eastern maritime border.

## History

The XXVI Mukims bordered coastal trade routes and was integrated into the Dutch military perimeter during the establishment of the [[Concentrated Line]] and subsequent campaigns by [[Teuku Umar]] in [[1893]]–[[1896]].
