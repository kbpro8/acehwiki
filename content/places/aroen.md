**Aroen** was one of the places named on the gravestones of the [[Peutjoet|Petjoet cemetery]] at [[Koeta Radja]] as a field of honour on which Dutch soldiers had fallen. [[P. H. R. Beuming]] records the inscriptions he read there: *Aan onze dierbare kameraden; aan onzen vriend en krijgsmakker, gevallen op het veld van eer te [[Lambaroe]], [[Kroeng Raba]], [[Kajoe Leh]], Aroen* — to our dear comrades; to our friend and comrade in arms, fallen on the field of honour at Lambaroe, Kroeng Raba, Kajoe Leh, Aroen.
