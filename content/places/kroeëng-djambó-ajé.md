**Kroeëng Djambó-Ajé** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Major river in northern Aceh.

## Description

Major river in northern Aceh. Its large delta on the [[Pase / Pasei|east coast]]. Upper basin (with side river [[Kroeëng Bidin]]) lies in tertiair gronden.

## Significance

Kroeëng Djambó-Ajé is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
