---
title: Lam Nyong
tags: ["place", "aceh-proper"]
---

A gampong (village) within Great [[Acheh]], famous as the seat of the renowned religious teacher [[Teungku di Lam Nyong]] (proper name Nya' Him, short for Ibrahim). He attracted hundreds of followers and drew students from all over [[Acheh]], including almost all the teachers of the North and East Coasts. He himself had studied at [[Lam Ba'et]] (in the VI Mukims) and at [[Lam Bhu']]'. He succeeded his father and grandfather in teaching at Lam Nyong. In the sadati dialogue: 'Grammar (is taught) at Lam Nyong, the learning of the law at Lam Pucho'.'
