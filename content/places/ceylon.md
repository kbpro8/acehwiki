Ceylon appears in both historical and fictional contexts. The Hikayat Diwi Sangsareh features the celestial princess Hadi'oy Jami of the land of [[Train]], and 'the hero succeeds in meeting the original... his celestial princess... comes down to him in Silan (Ceylon).' In the [[Hikayat Gumba Meuih]], 'Genggong Intan, who marries prince Kaharolah of Silan (Ceylon).' The 'utoih (tradesman) of Silan' appears in the Kisah Hizweuen (animal fables).
