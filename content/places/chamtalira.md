'In times of old Sultan Ali held sway in the kingdom of Chamtalira, by which the Achehnese mean the same that is called [[Sumatra]] in the writings of Marco Polo and Ibn Batutah.' Chamtalira is the setting of the Hikayat Eseukanda Ali and the Hikayat Nun [[Parisi]]. The holy Abdurra'uf speaks of 'the Malay language of [[Sumatra]]' using this term. [[Indra Siluka]] (in [[Keureutoe]]) In the Hikayat Eseukanda Ali: 'They purchased land from Ahli, king of [[Keureutoe]] and built a sumptuous residence which was called [[Indra Siluka]].' This is where Ali Juhari was established as a sugarcane planter and where he hoisted the cloth of Ra'na Jamin as a flag.
