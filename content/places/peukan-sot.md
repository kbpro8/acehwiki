**Peukan Sòt** was the moekim (district) near [[Sigli]] in [[Pidië|Pidie]], within the territory of [[Teukoe Béntara Pineung|uléëbalang Teukoe Béntara Pineung]], that together with [[Peukan Baroe|Peukan Baroe]] formed the stronghold of [[Habib Oesén|Habib Oesén]] and his hostile fortifications (*bentengs*). The area was the site of the [[Conquest of Peukan Baroe-Peukan Sòt (August 1897)|conquest of Peukan Baroe-Peukan Sòt]] during the [[Sigli expedition (August 1897)|Sigli expedition]] of August [[1897]].

**Pokan Soth** was one of the two *[[Moekim|moekims]]* of the [[Pedir]] country from which, according to the Minister of Colonies [[J. T. Cremer|J. T. Cremer]], the [[Sigli expedition (August 1897)|Segli expedition]] of August [[1897]] was undertaken to expel the hostile bands, the other being [[Pakan Baroe]].

## In the debate of [[1897]]

Cremer explained to the [[Tweede Kamer]] on [[16 November]] [[1897]] that the expedition had taken place "niet om [[Segli]] zelf te nemen, maar wel om de vijandelijke benden uit de moekims Pakan Baroe en Pokan Soth te verjagen." — not to take Segli itself, but to drive the hostile bands out of the moekims Pakan Baroe and Pokan Soth. The princes on that coast were not hostile to the Dutch but were not strong enough to drive out the [[Delama-benden|Delama-bands]] that had streamed together in the [[Tanawaqt]] or in the [[VII Moekims]].

After the expulsion the chief of the district took the land under his management, and it was unnecessary to garrison the place; daily supervision over the cleared district was maintained from the fixed post of [[Segli]], to which the population was gradually returning. Cremer added: "Zoo is het ook met Pedir." — So it is also with Pedir.
