**Peukan Sòt** was the moekim (district) near [[Sigli]] in [[Pidië|Pidie]], within the territory of [[Teukoe Béntara Pineung|uléëbalang Teukoe Béntara Pineung]], that together with [[Peukan Baroe|Peukan Baroe]] formed the stronghold of [[Habib Oesén|Habib Oesén]] and his hostile fortifications (*bentengs*). The area was the site of the [[Conquest of Peukan Baroe-Peukan Sòt (August 1897)|conquest of Peukan Baroe-Peukan Sòt]] during the [[Sigli expedition (August 1897)|Sigli expedition]] of August [[1897]].
