**Lampasi** was a position in [[Groot-Atjeh]] occupied by the [[6e Bataljon Infanterie|6e Bataljon]] on the first day of the [[Three-day battle in the Beradin pass (23-25 May 1896)|three-day battle in the Beradin pass]], [[23 May]] [[1896]]. Under the plan of attack the troops posted at [[Blang]] were to take the *kampongs* lying before that post and then take position at Lampasi; [[P. H. R. Beuming]] records that while the guns thundered from the [[Glé Poetih]], *had het 6e Bataljon een stelling bezet bij Lampasai (Zuid)*.
