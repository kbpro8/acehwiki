**Meurandéh Paja** was the village east of [[Lho' Soekon]] in whose *[[meunasah]]* the patrol of sergeant [[Vollaers]] was destroyed on [[26 January]] [[1905]].

The patrol, seventeen strong, "had neglected all security measures and was bivouacking carelessly"; sixteen men were killed and only one native fusilier escaped. It was for this attack that [[H. N. A. Swart]], then governing at [[Lho' Seumawè]], considered it necessary to have [[T. Tjhi' Toenòng]] arrested; Swart was then transferred, [[Gotfried Coenraad Ernst van Daalen|Van Daalen]] became Governor and converted the sentence of banishment into death, "although many were convinced that T. Tjhi' was innocent and a victim of the eternal, mutually entangled Acehnese intrigues."
