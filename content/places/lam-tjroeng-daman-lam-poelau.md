**Lam-Tjroeng / Daman / Lam-Poelau** — written in Dutch as also *Lam-Tiroeng*, *Lam Tjroeng*, also *Lam-Poclau*, and *Daman* — were three kampongs with fortifications in the [[IV en VI Moekims|IV Moekims]] of [[Teuku Umar]], attacked together on [[25 May]] [[1896]] by the 3e compagnie of the 6e Bataljon in the course of the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day Dutch offensive]]. As described in Kruisheer's *ATJEH '96*, the Lam-Tjroeng *versterking* was seized by 2e luit. [[J. C. Lamster|Lamster]] (who was first inside), the Daman *versterking* was stormed by 1e luit. [[J. Keetell|Keetell]], and the entire *stelling* Daman–Lam-Tjroeng–Lam-Poelau was cleared by 11:45 VM. The *versterking* Daman was a hill-fort near Lam-Tjroeng stormed by Janssen's compagnie on [[25 May]] [[1896]].

## Description and Location

Lam-Tjroeng, Daman, and Lam-Poelau lay in the [[IV en VI Moekims|IV Moekims]] of [[Teuku Umar]] between the hill of [[Boekit Seboen|Bt. Seboen]] on the north and the kampong of [[Lam-Pisang]] on the south-east. The three kampongs formed a continuous defensive *stelling* that anchored the western end of Umar's right wing. Lam-Tjroeng had a *versterking* on the a hill with a *versterking* south of Lam-Poelau, seized by 2e luit. [[J. C. Lamster|Lamster]] on [[25 May]] [[1896]] (Lamster was first inside). Daman had a hill-fort stormed by 1e luit. [[J. Keetell|Keetell]] and by Janssen's compagnie on [[25 May]] [[1896]]. Lam-Poelau was the site of the earlier Dutch *versterking* on [[Boekit Seboen|Bt. Seboen]], and was the kampong from which the Daman bezetting began its retreat on [[23 May]] [[1896]]. The entire *stelling* Daman–Lam-Tjroeng–Lam-Poelau was cleared by 11:45 VM on [[25 May]] [[1896]]. The three kampongs were bounded on the west by the coastal *landschap* of [[Lepong]] and on the east by the [[Kloof van Beradin|valley of Beradin]].

## Historical Background

Lam-Tjroeng, Daman, and Lam-Poelau had been part of [[Teuku Umar]]'s territory throughout his period as [[Panglima Prang Besar]] ([[1893]]–[[1896]]). The kampong of Lam-Poelau had formerly been the site of a Dutch *versterking* on [[Boekit Seboen|Bt. Seboen]], which had been abandoned and then restored by Umar. The three kampongs were integrated into Umar's elaborate defensive *stelling* across the [[IV en VI Moekims|IV en VI Moekims]] as the western anchor of his right wing, with the *versterkingen* of Lam-Tjroeng, Daman, and Lam-Poelau forming a continuous line. The *heuvelbenteng* Daman was a particularly strong position, with bamboo-thorn palisades and a small swivel cannon.

## Role in the 1896 Operations

Lam-Tjroeng, Daman, and Lam-Poelau were attacked together on [[25 May]] [[1896]] by the 3e compagnie of the 6e Bataljon under [[A. B. B. Jansen|kapitein Jansen]] in the final phase of the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day Dutch offensive]]. On [[23 May]] [[1896]] the bezetting of Daman had begun its retreat from Lam-Poelau, and the 5e compagnie of the 12e Bataljon had fought through the [[Kloof van Beradin|valley of Beradin]] toward the eastern ridge. On [[24 May]] [[1896]] the 5e Bataljon had captured [[Lam-Pisang]] and Umar's dwelling. On [[25 May]] [[1896]] the 3e compagnie of the 6e Bataljon stormed the *stelling* Daman–Lam-Tjroeng–Lam-Poelau: 2e luit. [[J. C. Lamster|Lamster]] was first inside the Lam-Tjroeng *versterking*, 1e luit. [[J. Keetell|Keetell]] stormed the Daman *versterking*, and Janssen's compagnie talso the *heuvelbenteng* Daman. The entire *stelling* was cleared by 11:45 VM. The capture of the three kampongs completed the destruction of [[Teuku Umar]]'s defensive *stelling* across the [[IV en VI Moekims|IV and VI Moekims]]. On [[4 June]] [[1896]] the [[Operations in IV and VI Moekims (4–5 June 1896)|two-colonne operation]] re-entered the area and occupied Lam-Poelau again, encountering the [[Kloof van Beradin|valley of Beradin]] in the course of which [[T. Hoesin Lhongbatta|Toekoe Hoesin Long Bata]] was killed.

## Significance

The capture of the *stelling* Daman–Lam-Tjroeng–Lam-Poelau on [[25 May]] [[1896]] was the final act of the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day Dutch offensive]] and the effective destruction of [[Teuku Umar]]'s defensive *stelling* across the [[IV en VI Moekims|IV and VI Moekims]]. The three kampongs had formed the western anchor of Umar's right wing, and their fall opened the coastal *landschap* of [[Lepong]] to the [[Marine Bombardment of Lepong (25 May, 4–5, 8–9 June 1896)|marine bombardments]] that began the same day. The actions of 2e luit. [[J. C. Lamster|Lamster]], 1e luit. [[J. Keetell|Keetell]], and kapitein [[A. B. B. Jansen|Jansen]] in the storming of the three *versterkingen* were among the most-celebrated acts of individual heroism of the offensive.
