**XX Moekims** was a traditional Acehnese administrative region and federation of twenty mukims in the northern coastal and valley territory of the [[Aceh Sultanate]], modern Aceh Province, Indonesia.

## Leadership and Diplomacy in 1874

The district was headed by prominent indigenous rulers including [[Teuku Nya Abas]] and [[Teuku Nek Purba]]. Following the [[Capture of the Aceh Kraton]] on [[24 January]] [[1874]], as recorded in *Atjeh gedurende de tweede expeditie en later* ([[1876]]), [[Teuku Nya Abas]] and [[Teuku Nek Purba]] opened diplomatic communication with Commander-in-Chief General [[J. van Swieten]], offering their submission to the [[Dutch East Indies Government]].

These overtures, facilitated by [[Teuku Nek of Meuraxa]], provided the documentary foundation for the [[General van Swieten War Telegrams]] of late January [[1874]], which predicted general pacification across northern Sumatra.
