**Peladjoe (Peladoe)** is a side‑branch of the [[Soensang River]] near [[Palembang]] that was fortified by enemy forces during the [[1821]] Dutch expedition. The Dutch fleet found the river and its tributary blocked by large piles and defended by four batteries, while the island of [[Gombora]] was also fortified (page 94).

## Defensive Works

The obstruction of the Peladjoe exemplified the tactical challenges faced by Dutch forces attempting to penetrate the interior of the Aceh‑influenced region.
