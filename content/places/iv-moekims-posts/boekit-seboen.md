**Boekit-Seboen** was a Dutch military post in the [[IV Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]]. The post was evacuated on [[12 February]] [[1885]] as part of the concentration strategy.

## Location

Boekit-Seboen was situated in the western [[IV Moekims]] region, which was characterized by steep terrain on both sides of the kloof van Beradin (Beradin ravine).

## Pre-Concentration Status

Boekit-Seboen was one of the posts established during the conquest expeditions of [[Generaal Pel]]. The post was connected to [[Boekit-Lampaja]] and [[Kroeng-Raba]] by a transport line, but the terrain prevented effective lateral expansion or enclosure of territory.

## Evacuation

Boekit-Seboen was evacuated on [[12 February]] [[1885]] along with [[Boekit-Lampaja]]. Casualties during the evacuation: 3 wounded (all lower soldiers).

## Significance

The evacuation of Boekit-Seboen acknowledged that the steep terrain of the [[IV Moekims]] made sustained control impractical. The post had been isolated from the main position, and the concentration strategy prioritized defensible terrain.
