**Boekit-Lampaja** was a Dutch military post in the [[IV Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]]. The post was evacuated on [[12 February]] [[1885]] as part of the concentration strategy.

## Location

Boekit-Lampaja was situated in the western [[IV Moekims]] region, positioned on the line from [[Pakan-Badak]] through [[Boekit-Seboen]] toward [[Kroeng-Raba]].

## Pre-Concentration Status

Boekit-Lampaja was one of the posts established during the conquest expeditions of [[Generaal Pel]]. The post was connected to the transport line through the steep [[IV Moekims]] terrain, but the kloof van Beradin limited tactical options.

## Evacuation

Boekit-Lampaja was evacuated on [[12 February]] [[1885]] along with [[Boekit-Seboen]]. Casualties during the evacuation: 3 wounded (all lower soldiers).

## Significance

The evacuation of Boekit-Lampaja was part of the systematic withdrawal from the difficult terrain of the [[IV Moekims]]. The steep ravines made defense impractical.
