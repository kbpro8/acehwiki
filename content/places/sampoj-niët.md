**Sampoj Niët** is a subject named in *[[Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[T. Tjhi' Toenòng]]

- In June [[1902]] a surprise attack on a detachment under [[Steijn Parvé]]: eight dead and wounded.
- In August [[1902]] an ambush against a transport at [[Meunasah Djeuro]]: dead and three wounded, five rifles lost.
- In November [[1903]] the operation against the *2e luitenant* [[De Kok]] with forty-five men, who was to row up the river from the *keudé* [[Sampoj Niët]] in two *prauws* with Acehnese rowers, but who had spoken of his plans beforehand. At night a few shots suddenly fell; in the pitch dark the rowers capsized the *prauws* and swam to the bank; all the soldiers went into the water; the officer and twenty-eight men drowned and forty-two rifles were lost.

### From [[1903]]

On [[2 October]] **[[1903]]** [[Christiaan Snouck Hurgronje]] wrote to Governor-General [[Rooseboom]] the letter warning against the appointment of [[Gotfried Coenraad Ernst van Daalen|Van Daalen]] to the Atjeh governorship — "as it were a Mene Tekel". On [[12 October]] [[1903]] [[H. N. A. Swart]] was charged with the government of [[Lho' Seumawè]] and on [[24 October]] extraordinarily promoted *Majoor*. In November [[1903]] [[T. Tjhi' Toenòng]]'s band destroyed the party of *2e luitenant* [[De Kok]] on the river at [[Sampoj Niët]], drowning the officer and twenty-eight men.
