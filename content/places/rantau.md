**Rantau** (oil terrain) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Boorterrein (oil field) in Tamiang-blok (BPM); one of most productive in Nederlandsch-Indië with 13 oil zones and 54 productive wells; first boring at 290 m yielded a spuiter (gusher); daily production >1,700 tons.

## Type

oil terrain

## Description

Boorterrein (oil field) in [[Tamiang]]-blok (BPM); one of most productive in [[Nederlandsch-Indië]] with 13 oil zones and 54 productive wells; first boring at 290 m yielded a spuiter (gusher); daily production >1,700 tons.

## Significance

Rantau is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
