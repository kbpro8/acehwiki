**Aroe Islands** (today the **Arú Archipelago**) lie off the coast of [[Atjeh]] in the eastern part of the Dutch East Indies. In [[1893]] the Dutch launched a punitive expedition—referred to in the corps’ records as the **Tuchtiging der Aroe‑cilanden**—to suppress a local uprising that threatened Dutch shipping and the security of the nearby Atjeh coast.

The operation involved a naval squadron of the [[Dutch Marine Corps]] and resulted in the destruction of several fortified villages and the imposition of a Dutch‑appointed local administration.
