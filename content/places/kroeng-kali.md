**Kroeng Kali** — *Krueng Kali* — is a river in the [[XXVI Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]] that flows *recht noordwaarts* (straight northward) some two hundred metres west of the Atjehnese fortification of [[Kaloet]]. The river is named in the [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] as the site of the night-patrol of Lieutenant [[Brant]] on the night of [[22 August|22-23 August]] [[1892]] — the patrol that established the topographical knowledge that would inform the subsequent [[Reconnaissance of Kaloet (23 August 1892)|reconnaissance of 23 August 1892]] — and as the site of the *excursie* (reconnaissance) of [[4 May]] [[1896]] on which Lieutenant [[C. G. A. C. Krohne|Krohne]] was mortally wounded.

## The 1892 night-patrol

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] records that the Kroeng Kali — *die een paar honderd meter westwaarts langs Kaloet, en recht noordwaarts stroomt* (which flows a couple of hundred metres westward along Kaloet, and straight northward) — was the site of the night-patrol of Lieutenant [[Brant]] of the *maréchaussée* on the night of [[22 August|22-23 August]] [[1892]]. The patrol — a *detachement maréchaussées* — lay in ambush in the dry bed of the river — *in eene der versterkingen in de bedding van het riviertje de Kroeng Kali* (in one of the fortifications in the bed of the riverlet the Kroeng Kali) — with the intention of catching the Atjehnese garrison of Kaloet as they came in the morning. The river was *dikwijls … droog; zoo ook toen* (often dry; so also then), which allowed the patrol to occupy its bed. The Atjehnese garrison, however, *versliepen zich* (overslept), and the patrol had to return to [[Lamreng]] at 7 a.m. without having caught anyone.

## The 1896 reconnaissance

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album]] records that the Kroeng Kali was also the site of the *excursie* (reconnaissance) of [[4 May]] [[1896]] on which Lieutenant [[C. G. A. C. Krohne|Krohne]] was mortally wounded. The album's roll of the fallen gives Krohne's date of death as [[4 May]] [[1896]] and records that he was *gewond op 4 Mei bij eene excursie naar Kroeng Kali (XXVI Moekims)* — *wounded on 4 May during an excursion to Kroeng Kali (XXVI Moekims)* — and that he *denzelfden dag tengevolge van deze verwonding overleed* (died the same day in consequence of this wound). The reconnaissance was undertaken in the period between the [[Relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit (April 1896)|relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit]] of [[9 April]] [[9 April]] [[1896]] and the [[Storming of Lampisang (May 1896)|storming of Lampisang]] of [[23 May]] [[23 May]] [[1896]].

## Significance

The Kroeng Kali's significance in the documentary record of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] is as the site of two of the principal documented engagements of the late *concentratie* period — the night-patrol of Lieutenant [[Brant]] of [[22 August|22-23 August]] [[1892]], and the reconnaissance of [[4 May]] [[1896]] on which Lieutenant [[C. G. A. C. Krohne|Krohne]] was mortally wounded. The river's *droog* (dry) bed was the principal route by which the Dutch patrols approached the [[Kaloet]] fortification in the [[1892]] operations, and its situation in the [[XXVI Moekims]] — the northernmost of the three *sagis* of [[Groot-Atjeh]] — places it as one of the principal operational centres of the late *concentratie* period.
