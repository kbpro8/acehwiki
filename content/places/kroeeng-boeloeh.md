**Kroeeng Boeloeh** is the river from which the *landschappen* [[Tjoenda|Tjoenda]] and [[Nisam|Nisam]] in the *onderafdeeling* [[Lho Seumawe|Lho' Seumawé]] draw their water. In the Kroeeng Boeloeh one finds, 12 km west of Lho' Seumawé, a stone dam which is from time to time endangered by the *bandjirs*. The population is lukewarm towards such works and does little or nothing on its side.
