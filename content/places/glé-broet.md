**Glé Broet** was a station of the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* in [[Atjeh]], one of the two posts — with [[Tjot Mantjang]] — at which the 3rd *[[Divisie (Maréchaussée)|Divisie]]* of the corps was quartered in [[1898]].

## The 1898 localisation

The [[1930]] jubilee number records the disposition of the corps in [[1898]] as follows: Staff at [[Koeta Radja]]; 1st Division at [[Seulimeum|Selimoen]]; 2nd Division at [[Indrapoeri]]; 3rd Division at [[Tjot Mantjang]] and Glé Broet; 4th Division at [[Tjot Goet]]; and 5th Division at [[Lam Baroe]] and [[Lho Nga|Lho'-Nga]].

The name is formed with the Acehnese topographic element *glé*, denoting a hill or mountain.

## Later dispositions

By [[1910]] the corps had been redistributed to [[Blang Kedjeren]], [[Meulaboh]], [[Takengeun|Takèngan]], [[Lam Meulo]] and [[Lho Soekon]], and Glé Broet no longer appears among the divisional stations.
