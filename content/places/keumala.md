The seat of the "court" maintained by the young pretender-Sultan Muhammad Dawot Shah after the fall of Kutaraja to the Dutch, where ulamas including Teungku Tiro paid ceremonial visits, and where the boy-sultan was known for his patronage of cricket-fighting contests.

**Keumala** was an interior settlement in [[Great Acheh|Acheh]] where the young Sultan [[Muhammad Dawot Shah]] held court during the [[Aceh War]] period, when the Dutch had driven the sultanate from [[Banda Acheh]] and the sultan was in the interior. Sayyid families from [[Mecca]] settled in [[Pidie]] and made occasional journeys to Keumala, where the young sultan treated them with the highest honour.

The location of Keumala in the interior highlands placed it beyond easy Dutch reach during the initial phases of the war, making it a symbolic refuge for Acehnese sovereignty during the period of resistance.
