**Lamno** (military post (Westkust)) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Military post on Westkust, between which and Tjalang lie 3 ponten (ferries).

## Type

military post ([[Westkust]])

## Description

Military post on [[Westkust]], between which and [[Tjalang]] lie 3 ponten (ferries).

## Significance

Lamno is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
