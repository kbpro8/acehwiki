**Kroeng Gloempang** — written in Dutch as also *Lam Gloempang*; the *versterking* of *Tjot Gloempang* in the middle of the large sawah of Lam-Krak is a related but distinct feature — was a [[Dutch]] outpost on the border of the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] and the [[Sagi der XXII Moekims|XXII Moekims]], constructed specifically to protect the [[III Moekims Daroe]] (and especially the [[III Moekims Ateuh|III Moekims Ateuh]]) against incursions from [[V Moekims Montassik|Montassik]]. As described in Kruisheer's *ATJEH '96*, Kroeng Gloempang was garrisoned in [[1895]] by 3 officers, 60 infantry, 10 *artilleristen*, and 1 European hospital attendant, was subjected to a shelling and arson attempt on [[13 April]] [[1896]], was successfully re-supplied on [[16 April]] [[1896]], was lifted on [[20 April]] [[1896]], and was the objective of the Noordercolonne in the [[Three-Column Operation to Free Tjot Rang and Kroeng Gloempang (21 April 1896)|three-column operation of 21 April 1896]]. The Tjot Gloempang *versterking* in the middle of the large sawah of Lam-Krak — recognisable by a tree of the same name — was a target of the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|tuchtiging of 9–10 June 1896]].

## Description and Location

Kroeng Gloempang lay on the border of the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] and the [[Sagi der XXII Moekims|XXII Moekims]], and was positioned specifically to protect the [[III Moekims Daroe]] (and especially the [[III Moekims Ateuh]]) against incursions from [[V Moekims Montassik|Montassik]]. The garrison in [[1895]] consisted of 3 officers, 60 infantry, 10 *artilleristen*, and 1 European hospital attendant. The post was linked by road to [[Tjot Rang]] and [[Toengkoeb]] in the eastern *buitenlinie*. The *Tjot Gloempang* a separate but related feature stood in the middle of the large sawah of [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lam-Krak]], recognisable by a tree of the same name, and was a target of the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|tuchtiging of 9–10 June 1896]]. The post was a crossroads and a meeting-point for various Dutch columns.

## Historical Background

Kroeng Gloempang had been constructed as part of the [[buitenlinie]] to protect the [[III Moekims Daroe]] (and especially the [[III Moekims Ateuh]]) against incursions from [[V Moekims Montassik|Montassik]], and to dominate the *kruispunt* of the eastern roads. The post was the eastern anchor of the *buitenlinie* between the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] and the [[Sagi der XXII Moekims|XXII Moekims]]. After [[1893]] the post served as a base for operations against the [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lamkrak]] country. The *Tjot Gloempang* *versterking* in the middle of the large sawah of [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lam-Krak]] was a separate Achehnese fortification that had long defied Dutch authority.

## Role in the 1896 Operations

Kroeng Gloempang was one of the eastern [[buitenpost|buitenposten]] besieged in the spring of [[1896]]. On [[13 April]] [[1896]] the post was subjected to a shelling and arson attempt by hostile *benden*. On [[14 April]] [[1896]] the [[Sergeant Carli|deserter Sergeant Carli]] sent a letter from the enemy side to Kroeng Gloempang on behalf of [[Toeankoe Brahim|Toekoe Brahim der V Moekims Montassik]], demanding the post be handed over in exchange for free retreat and retention of weapons. On [[16 April]] [[1896]] Kroeng Gloempang was successfully re-supplied; [[Tjot Rang]] was not. On [[20 April]] [[1896]] Kroeng Gloempang, [[Tjot Rang]], and [[Toengkoeb]] were lifted by order of the [[Regeeringscommissaris]] [[Luitenant-Generaal Vetter|Vetter]]. On [[21 April]] [[1896]] a [[Three-Column Operation to Free Tjot Rang and Kroeng Gloempang (21 April 1896)|three-column operation]] moved to free the beleaguered garrisons; the Noordercolonne from [[Tjot Iri]] was the column assigned to Kroeng Gloempang, advancing through [[Lam Gloempang]] (where heavy fighting occurred), [[Tjap Tanah]], [[Na Hatan]], and [[Lam Hasan]]. On [[9 June]]–[[10 June]] [[1896]] the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|tuchtiging of the Lamkrak Moekims]] targeted the Tjot Gloempang *versterking* in the middle of the large sawah of Lam-Krak. On [[17 June]] [[1896]] Kroeng Gloempang served as a meeting-point for the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|tuchtiging of the V Moekims Montassik]].

## Significance

Kroeng Gloempang was the principal [[Dutch]] *buitenpost* on the border between the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] and the [[Sagi der XXII Moekims|XXII Moekims]], and the eastern anchor of the *buitenlinie* in this sector. Its successful *approviandeering* on [[16 April]] [[1896]] — in contrast to the failed attempt at [[Tjot Rang]] — was a small operational success in an otherwise disastrous month. The Tjot Gloempang *versterking* in the Lam-Krak sawah was a persistent Achehnese strongpoint that was finally targeted by the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|tuchtiging of 9–10 June 1896]].
