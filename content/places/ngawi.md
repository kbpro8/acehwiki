**Ngawi** was a town in eastern [[Java]] that served as a destination for Dutch forces retreating from Japanese pressure in [[1942]]. The *Zuid‑colonne* planned to march towards Ngawi after securing bridges at [[Tjepoe]] and [[Kertosono]].

## Operational Role
A detachment led by *Lieutenant‑colonel J. G. M. Nass* was instructed to maintain contact with light troops at [[Tjaroeban]] and to assist in keeping the road to Ngawi open.

## Significance
Ngawi’s location on the main east‑west axis made it a strategic fallback point for Dutch troops attempting to regroup after the loss of Java’s coastal defenses.
