**Aneuk Galung** was/is a fortified Acehnese village and military earthwork stronghold located in the valley of [[Groot-Atjeh]].

## Historical Context and Role

On [[29 June]] [[1896]], Aneuk Galung was the site of the first major full-force battle involving the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]. In the [[Battle of Aneuk Galung]], 224 Marechaussee troops under Captain [[Jonkheer G. J. W. C. Graafland]] breached the fortified palisades and captured the entire complex in fifteen minutes, demonstrating the lethal effectiveness of aggressive light infantry tactics.
