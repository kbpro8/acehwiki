**Lambesi** was a landing-place on the coast connected with the [[Tenom|Tenom]] complex of the Atjehsche resistance, where on [[3 July]] [[1884]] the treason of [[Toekoe Oemar (Tenom)|Toekoe Oemar]] destroyed the unsuspecting crew of a Dutch war sloop: seven men were killed, and all their weapons were taken (see [[Lambesi treachery (3 July 1884)]]).
