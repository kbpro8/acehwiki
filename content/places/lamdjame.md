**Lamdjame** (also printed *Lamdjamoe*) was one of the eighteen semi-permanently built posts of the Dutch [[Geconcentreerde Stelling|concentrated line]] established around [[Kuta Raja|Kota Radja]] and [[Oleh-leh]] on [[1 March]] [[1885]]. The line had been shortened southwestward from [[Kroeng Raba]] to Lamdjame, and the new post was connected — by tramway branches from [[Ketapang Doewa]] and by the projected [[Ceintuur-trambaan|belt tramway]] round the line — with the other fortifications of the ring. On [[25 September]] [[1885]] the post was fired upon by the Atjehnese, with loss of one Dutch soldier (see [[Guerilla raids inside the concentration line (1885)]]). Verstege includes it in the official list of posts of the new line of [[1885]], as handed down in the Koloniaal Verslag.
