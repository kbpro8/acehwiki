**Grisseh** was a district in the north‑eastern part of the Dutch East Indies, referenced in the source as the area where Dutch marines under *Lieutenant‑colonel J. G. M. Nass* operated in [[March]] [[1942]]. The region experienced “onlusten” (unrest) among the indigenous population, which Dutch forces sought to suppress.

## Military Activity
The *Marinebataljon* conducted patrols, dispersed unrest, and carried out executions of local leaders to restore order. Lieutenant‑colonel Nass also led reconnaissance missions westward from Grisseh.

## Significance
Grisseh’s mention illustrates the spread of Dutch military operations beyond central Java, affecting peripheral regions that were part of the broader Aceh‑related colonial sphere.
