---
title: Pante Paku
tags: ["place", "north-east-coast"]
---

A coastal location. Sadati dialogue: 'If that will not do, little brother teungku, I will take thee to Pante Paku. I will not go to Pante Paku, for I cannot twist rope of coconut fibre (a pun on the word paku).'

## Source
*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
