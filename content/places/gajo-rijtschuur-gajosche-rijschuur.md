**Gajo Rijtschuur / Gajosche Rijschuur** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Gajo rice barn; photo caption (page 396).

## Description

[[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]] rice barn; photo caption (page 396).

## Significance

Gajo Rijtschuur / Gajosche Rijschuur is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
