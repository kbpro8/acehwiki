**Blang Kedjerèn** (isolated town) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Small friendly flower-filled town smelling of pines; one of the most remote posts in Aceh.

## Type

isolated town

## Description

Small friendly flower-filled town smelling of pines; one of the most remote posts in Aceh. Reached officially via [[Medan]]-[[Kaban Djahé]]-[[Koeta Tjané]] + 36 km auto-road, then by foot/horse. A kapitein commands the detachment and is also civil bestuur. Has the 'Oude Poes' [[De Koepel (Soosje)|soosje]] with guest book. ~70 km from [[Koeta Tjané]] = 3 day marches; bivaks at [[Meloewak]] (600 m) and [[Koengké]] ([[1000]] m).

## Significance

Blang Kedjerèn is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
