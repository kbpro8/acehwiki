**Blangkedjerèn** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Place in the Gajo Loeës; terminus of an unpaved road (15 km from Blangkedjerèn).

## Description

Place in the [[Gajo-Loeüs|Gajo Loeës]]; terminus of an unpaved road (15 km from Blangkedjerèn). To be connected to the road from [[Koeta Tjané|Koeta Tjane]] via 70 km of new construction.

## Significance

Blangkedjerèn is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
