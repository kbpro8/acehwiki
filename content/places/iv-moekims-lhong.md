**IV Moekims Lhong** was the *[[moekim]]* of [[Lhong]] in the sub-division [[Lho' Nga]], the plain about three hours' walk broad backed by the limestone outliers of the [[Boekit Barisan]].

In that limestone range a band could hold out only in a couple of caves, "whose location was very soon entirely known to the patrol commander, while he moreover had them watched by spies." [[Teukoe Mamat]] was a relative of the *[[oelèëbalang]]* of the IV Moekims Lhong, with whom he was on bad terms, and a personal enemy of the district head of the *moekim* Lhong — the basis of the intrigue behind the [[Resistance in Daja and Lhong (1914)|resistance of 1914]].
