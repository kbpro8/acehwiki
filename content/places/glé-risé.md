**Glé Risé** was the height in [[Atjeh]] whose strong and stubbornly defended enemy positions were taken on [[21 November]] [[1899]].

Captain [[H. N. A. Swart]] led his company "with judgement and with great intrepidity" in the attack; he received a blood-blister on the belly from a thrown stone. The action formed part of the citation for his promotion to Knight 3rd Class of the [[Militaire Willemsorde]] by royal decree of [[13 July]] [[1900]] no. 29.
