**Lamdjamoe** was a Dutch military post in the southern section of the geconcentreerde stelling (concentrated position) in [[Groot-Atjeh]]. The post was established on [[5 February]] [[1885]].

## Location

Lamdjamoe was situated in the southern section of the geconcentreerde stelling, between [[Blang]] and [[Lamtih]].

## Establishment

Lamdjamoe was established and occupied on [[5 February]] [[1885]].

## Fortification (1885)

Lamdjamoe was:

**Type:** A post with 150-man Infantry garrison
**Armament:** 2 × 12cm breech-loading guns and 1 Coehoorn mortar

## Infrastructure Connections

The post was connected to:
- The ceintuurtram (girdle tramway) system (junction point)
- [[Blang]] and [[Lamtih]]
- [[Lamdjamoe]] to [[Pakan-Badak]] route (now abandoned)

## Significance

Lamdjamoe served as a junction point on the ceintuurtram and provided coverage of the southern sector. The post was one of the more substantial fortifications with a larger garrison.
