**Sinabang** was the settlement on the southernmost bay of the east side of the island [[Simaloer]] (Simeulue) which gave the bay its name, and which became the industrial centre of the timber exploitation of the [[N.V. Vereenigde Javasche Houthandel Maatschappijen]], with sawmill, stockyards, worker housing, hospital and waterworks; it was reached by government telegraph in [[1914]] and served by the [[Koninklijke Paketvaart Maatschappij|K.P.M.]]

## The industrial settlement

At Sinabang the Dutch timber company concentrated its Simaloer enterprise: the sawmill and the stockpiles of the concern were established there, and hence the *stamlijn* railway was built crossing the island in its breadth. For the housing of the European employees and of the more than 1,000 imported Javanese work-people, dwellings, sheds and kitchens arose; the company executed extensive sanitation works — raising the low bayland with earth dug out by an *evacateur*, levelling the whole terrain with spacious sewerage and cement gutters along all roads, and building a water conduit with *prise d'eau* formed by a dam between two mountain offshoots (a reservoir of 3½ hectares), which puta end to the formerly frequent dysentery. To the enterprise belonged a hospital, a hotel, an ice-factory, a sociëteit-building and a bioscoop; both work- and dwelling-establishment were wholly electrically lit. The increasing post traffic made the foundation of a post- and telegraph-office at Sinabang necessary, and in [[1914]] a telegraphic connection from Sinabang over [[Tapa Toean]] to [[Singkel]] came into exploitation; the [[Koninklijke Paketvaart Maatschappij]] maintained an agency at Sinabang.
