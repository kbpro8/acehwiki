**Rotterdamsche Diergaarde** (zoo (in Rotterdam, Netherlands)) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Zoo in Rotterdam to which one of the two female army elephants from Tangsé was donated.

## Type

zoo (in [[Rotterdam]], [[Leyden (Leiden)|Netherlands]])

## Description

Zoo in [[Rotterdam]] to which one of the two female army elephants from [[Tangsé]] was donated.

## Significance

Rotterdamsche Diergaarde is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
