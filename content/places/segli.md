**Segli** was a coast-state on the north coast of [[Acheh|Atjeh]], to which the Dutch establishment at [[Pedir|Pedir]] was moved in [[February 1877|February 1877]] after the original post at Pedir proved less favourably situated for the supervision of trade. The post at Segli was attacked by a great force under [[Toekoe di Tiroe|Toekoe di Tiroe]] in [[May 1878|May 1878]], leading to an expedition by the governor [[K. van der Heyden|Van der Heyden]] to Gighen and the upper lands of Pedir. Under the [[Establishment of Civil Government in Aceh (6 April 1881)|administrative reorganisation of March 1881]], Segli became one of the three controleurs of the assistant-residency of the [[Noord- en Oostkust|Noord- en Oostkust]], alongside [[Edi|Edi]] and [[Samalanga|Samalanga]].

## The Move from Pedir, February 1877

When in [[1876]] the Dutch set up a fortification at Pedir, they met all kinds of opposition and difficulties from the population, among whom [[Toekoe di Tiroe|Toekoe di Tiroe]] proved particularly hostile. After the arrival of military reinforcements in [[June 1876|June 1876]] the resistance gave way, and by the end of that month the post was in a sufficient state. "It later proved, however, to be less favourably situated for the supervision of trade, as continually great quantities of rice were imported, which seemed to find their way to the enemy." On the north coast it was therefore decided in [[February 1877|February 1877]] to move the establishment from Pedir to Segli, and to that end a column in [[February 1877|February]] marched along the coast from [[Kroëng Raja|Kroëng Rajah]] to Segli.

## The Attack of May 1878

In [[May 1878|May 1878]] the governor [[K. van der Heyden|Van der Heyden]] led an expedition to [[Gighen|Gighen]] and the upper lands of Pedir, where [[Toekoe di Tiroe|Toekoe di Tiroe]] remained hostile and the post at Segli had been attacked by a great force. The actual object of the expedition — the kampong [[Garouw|Garouw]], the headquarters of Toekoe di Tiroe — was not reached, but the taking of [[Telok Kadjoe|Telok Kadjoe]], which had been mistaken for Garouw, had the consequence that the radja of Gighen offered his submission and promised the surrender of Toekoe di Tiroe.

## The Administrative Reorganisation, March 1881

Under the [[Establishment of Civil Government in Aceh (6 April 1881)|administrative reorganisation of March 1881]], Segli became one of the three controleurs of the assistant-residency of the [[Noord- en Oostkust|Noord- en Oostkust]] (with [[Telok Semawe|Telok Semawé]] as chief place), alongside [[Edi|Edi]] and [[Samalanga|Samalanga]].
