Paradise appears in multiple religious legends. In the [[Hikayat Asay Pade]] (origin of rice): 'When Adam and Hawa (Eve) were driven from paradise, and after they had wandered apart over the earth and met once more near the [[Mountain of Rahmat]], Jebra'i (Gabriel) gave Adam lessons in agriculture and brought him the necessary seeds from paradise.' Gabriel also brought a cap made of a leaf from one of the trees of paradise in the Hikayat Nabi Meuchuko. In the [[Hikayat Putroe Peureukison]]: 'A golden dove from Paradise comes to teach her the creed of Islam.' In the Hikayat Tujoh Kisah (Seven Stories): Paradise (Jannah) is described in Kisah 7.
