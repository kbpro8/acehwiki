**The Glé Aron** was the mountain range rising behind the lagoon on the far bank of the *kali* at [[Kroeng Raba]] on the west coast of [[Groot-Atjeh]], described by [[P. H. R. Beuming]] in the sketch *Een vreeselijke ontmoeting* in *[[Schetsen uit den strijd op Groot-Atjeh (1911)|Schetsen uit den strijd op Groot-Atjeh]]*.

Beyond the wilderness of bamboo, lianas and thorny aloes on the far bank stretched a broad lagoon of standing water, *en meer op den achtergrond verheft zich het majestueuse Glé Aron-gebergte* — and further in the background rises the majestic Glé Aron range. The landscape, Beuming writes, offered a splendid panorama: on the mountain slopes were extensive primeval forests, and the slope fell away slowly towards the sea, leaving only a narrow coastal strip between the [[Glé Loeda pass]] and the [[Kroeng Raba]].

*Onbeschrijfelijk is de indruk, welke dit natuurtafereel achterlaat. En toch...... in de schaduw van dat vriendelijk groen, vond reeds menig braaf krijger zijn somber graf* — indescribable is the impression this scene of nature leaves; and yet, in the shadow of that friendly green, many a brave soldier had already found his sombre grave.
