'The Wahhabite movement, which set Arabia in a tumult on the threshold of the nineteenth century, fought with spiritual and temporal weapons against the prevailing worship of man (saints and prophets), but it was subdued by Mohammad Ali... Only in those countries such as certain areas of Central Arabia where Wahhabism still prevails, do the Bedawins observe the five seasons of prayer.' Wahhabism is described as 'an insignificant sect' despite increases of its adherents in British [[India]].
