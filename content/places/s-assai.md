**S. Assai** (Sungei Assai, modern *Sungai Assai*) is a river of [[Acheh|Atjeh]] whose quartz-veins yielded, in the [[1922]] survey of [[P. J. J. Jansen|Jansen]], [[B. B. Lindberg|Lindberg]] and [[H. Wolvekamp|Wolvekamp]], the highest-grade gold assay of any Atjeh primary-gold occurrence recorded in the colonial geological literature: **11 gr Au/ton**. The S. Assai locality is recorded in [[Van Bemmelen's The Geology of Indonesia (1949)|Van Bemmelen's *Geology of Indonesia*]] ([[1949]]) in the *Summary of the Explorations in Sumatra* section of the gold-and-silver chapter, and the upper-Assai region is recommended by Van Bemmelen "for a more detailed investigation".

## The 1922 Jansen-Lindberg-Wolvekamp assay

Van Bemmelen's record of the S. Assai assay reads:

> "From 35 samples, taken from quartz-veins, only two gave a good assay: one from the river basin of the S. Assai, one from the S. Meranging (resp. 11 and 10.8 gr Au/ton). The upper-Assai region is recommended for a more detailed investigation."

The 11 gr Au/ton assay is the highest of the two positive assays from the 35 quartz-vein samples taken by Jansen, Lindberg and Wolvekamp in the Atjeh interior in [[1922]]. The fact that only 2 of the 35 samples yielded positive assays — and that those 2 assayed at near-identical grades (11 and 10.8 gr Au/ton) — suggests that the Atjeh primary-gold potential is concentrated in a small number of structurally-controlled quartz-vein systems rather than being disseminated across the slate-formation as a whole. The upper-Assai region's recommendation "for a more detailed investigation" is the only positive prospecting recommendation for an Atjeh primary-gold occurrence in the colonial geological literature.

## Significance

S. Assai is significant for the encyclopedia of [[Acheh]] history because it is the site of the highest-grade gold assay of any Atjeh primary-gold occurrence recorded in the colonial geological literature — 11 gr Au/ton from a quartz-vein in the slate-formation — and accordingly the most attractive primary-gold prospect recorded in the colonial geological literature on Atjeh. The upper-Assai region's recommendation "for a more detailed investigation" is the only positive prospecting recommendation for an Atjeh primary-gold occurrence in the colonial geological literature, and it accordingly marks the S. Assai as the colonial geological service's preferred target for any future primary-gold exploration in Atjeh.
