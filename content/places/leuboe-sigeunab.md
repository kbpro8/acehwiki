**Leuboe Sigeunab** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Penghoeloe Koeala]]

**Penghoeloe Koeala** was een zekeren Penghoeloe Koeala, een [[Gajòs|Gajò]] uit [[Leuboe Sigeunab]], die ook alweer stood in an odour of sanctity, who set on foot the religious movement at [[Serbödjadi]] in December [[1917]].

### From [[Serbödjadi]]

The movement, set on foot by [[Penghoeloe Koeala]], a [[Gajòs|Gajò]] of [[Leuboe Sigeunab]], cost the *gezaghebber* of [[Lököp]] his life in an encounter at the *[[gampong]]* [[Semboeang]] and ended with four dead, among them the guardian of a holy grave, and the Penghoeloe's capture.

### From [[Religious movement in Serbödjadi (December 1917)]]

The **religious movement at [[Serbödjadi]]** of December [[1917]] was a brief outbreak set on foot by a certain [[Penghoeloe Koeala]], a [[Gajòs|Gajò]] from [[Leuboe Sigeunab]] who likewise "stood in an odour of sanctity" — stood in an odour of sanctity — and which cost the life of the *gezaghebber* of [[Lököp]] before it was suppressed.
