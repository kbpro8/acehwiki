**Hollandia Drukkerij N.V. Baarn** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Printer/publisher of the book (in title page).

## Description

Printer/publisher of the book (in title page).

## Significance

Hollandia Drukkerij N.V. Baarn is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
