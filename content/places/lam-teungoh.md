A place in the XXII Mukims district. During the first Dutch expedition against [[Acheh]], the Sultan fled from [[The Dalam]], first to [[Lueng Bata]] and afterwards to Lam Teungoh (XXII Mukims), where he surrendered the reins of power with tears to [[Panglima Polem]].
