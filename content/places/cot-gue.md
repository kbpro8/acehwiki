**Cot Guè** was/is a military outpost and hill station situated in [[Groot-Atjeh]].

## Historical Context and Role

In [[1898]], Cot Guè was designated the head station for the 4th Division of the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]], providing mobile coverage over the western sector of the valley.
