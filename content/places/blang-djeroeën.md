**Blang Djeroeën** is a place in the *landschap* of [[Keureuto|Keureuto]] in the *onderafdeeling* [[Lho Soekon|Lho Soekon]], the residence of the *oeléebalang* [[Teukoe Bentara Keureuto|Teukoe Bentara Keureuto]].
