**Batoe Mengoera** was the mountain chain on which the front of the Dutch forward post of [[Biloel]] in [[Groot-Atjeh]] looked out, named by [[P. H. R. Beuming]] in his account of the post in *[[Schetsen uit den strijd op Groot-Atjeh (1911)|Schetsen uit den strijd op Groot-Atjeh]]*: *het front gaf uitzicht op een bergketen, genaamd: Batoe Mengoera*.

The post lay east of the hill [[Boekit Terin]] and west of it bordered the extensive territory of [[Lamkrak]]; it was from the broken country before this front, and from a concealed trench in the burnt-off *[[Alang-alang|alang-alang]]* field behind a bamboo thicket some fifty metres from the Dutch ambush position, that [[The sharpshooter of Biloel|the sharpshooter of Biloel]] harassed the sentries in [[1895]] and [[1896]].
