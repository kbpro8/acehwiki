**Edi** was a coastal settlement on the eastern shore of [[Atjeh]] that became a focal point of Dutch military activity in the late nineteenth century. In [[1890]] the [[Dutch Marine Corps]] launched an expedition to Edi as part of a broader effort to secure the east‑coast trade routes and to suppress local insurgents.

The expedition established a small fort and a supply depot, which served as a staging point for further operations into the interior of Atjeh.
