**Daja (Westkust)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Place on the Westkust from which Teukoe Oemar brought his whole fighting force to support the resistance at Pedir in August 1898.

## Description

Place on the [[Westkust]] from which [[Teukoe Oemar]] brought his whole fighting force to support the [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] at [[Pedir]] in [[August]] [[1898]].

## Significance

Daja (Westkust) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
