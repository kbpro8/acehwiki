**Alu Talu** is an iron-ore locality on the [[Atjeh's Westkust|West coast of Atjeh]], 78 km NW of [[Meulaboh]], recorded by [[P. J. J. Jansen|Jansen]], [[B. B. Lindberg|Lindberg]] and [[H. Wolvekamp|Wolvekamp]] in [[1922]] and reproduced in [[Van Bemmelen's The Geology of Indonesia (1949)|Van Bemmelen's *Geology of Indonesia*]] ([[1949]]). The Alu Talu locality is No. 5 in Van Bemmelen's Fig. 41 key, and is recorded together with the [[Kr. Rigaih]] locality (No. 4, 88 km NW of Meulaboh) under a single entry. For the geological description see the entry on [[Kr. Rigaih]].

## Significance

Alu Talu is significant for the encyclopedia of [[Acheh]] history because it is one of the four West-Atjeh iron-ore localities recorded in the colonial geological literature, and the second north-westernmost of the four (after [[Kr. Rigaih]] at 88 km NW of Meulaboh). The locality's contact-metamorphic origin, with the diagnostic presence of garnet, places it within the same geological model that applies to all the documented Atjeh iron-ore occurrences, and accordingly within the same mineral province that extends, in the colonial geological literature, from the northern tip of Atjeh to the southern tip of Benkulen.
