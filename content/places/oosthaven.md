**Oosthaven** — Dutch for "East Harbour" — is a place in the Lampong districts on the southern tip of [[Sumatra|Sumatra]], named in the Dutch pamphlet *[[Beschouwingen over Atjeh en onzen oorlog met dat rijk|Beschouwingen over Atjeh en onzen oorlog met dat rijk]]* by [[Een Ooggetuige|Een Ooggetuige]] ([[1880s]]) in the course of the author's discussion of the sea approaches to [[Acheh|Atjeh]] and of the wider setting of the Atjehnese theatre. The place is named alongside [[Kroeë|Kroeë]] as one of the points on the Lampong coast discussed in connection with the [[Sunda Strait|Sunda Strait]] and the sea approaches to the Atjehnese coast.

## The pamphlet's reference

The author of the *[[Beschouwingen over Atjeh en onzen oorlog met dat rijk|Beschouwingen]]* names Oosthaven in the context of a discussion of the Lampong districts and of the [[Sunda Strait|Sunda Strait]] as part of the wider setting of the Atjehnese theatre. The reference is made in the course of the author's argument on the sea approaches to Atjeh, in which the Lampong coast and the Sunda Strait are discussed as elements of the wider geography of the archipelago within which the Atjehnese coast and the [[Strait of Halakka|Strait of Halakka]] held their strategic and commercial importance.

## Significance

Oosthaven is significant within the AcehWiki corpus as a place of the Lampong districts named in the contemporary Dutch pamphlet literature of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh War]] in connection with the sea approaches to Atjeh. The place's mention in the *[[Beschouwingen over Atjeh en onzen oorlog met dat rijk|Beschouwingen]]* is a document of the wider geography within which the Dutch pamphleteers of the Atjeh War framed the Atjehnese theatre.
