**Goenoeng Löser** (mountain) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Highest peak of Aceh; surrounding enormous complex designated as 'Goenoeng Löser' wild reserve, together with adjacent Kloeët reserve.

## Type

mountain

## Description

Highest peak of Aceh; surrounding enormous complex designated as 'Goenoeng Löser' wild reserve, together with adjacent [[Kloeët]] reserve. Wildlife: elephants, rhinoceroses, tapirs, orangutans.

## Significance

Goenoeng Löser is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
