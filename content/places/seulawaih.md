---

The sadati dialogue mentions the 'mountain of Seulawaih' (the gold mountain) as a place of banishment. 'Dear little brother, I am going to banish thee to the mountain of Seulawaih.' Also: 'In the mountain range of [[Lam Weueng]] is the peak of the Seulawaih, in Lampanaih is a lilla (small cannon) with a bell.' The Panglima of the XXII Mukims went to ask for a concession in the mountain district of Seulawaih for the collection of sulphur during the civil war between Jeumaloy and [[Pochut Muhamat]].
