**Jakarta (Batavia)**, formerly known as **Batavia**, is the capital of Indonesia and a major repository of Arabic manuscripts in the archipelago. The Jakarta collection includes numerous kasidas (e.g., *Burda*, *Bānat Sudād*, works of [[Al‑Ushi]], [[Al‑Ghazzālī]]) and dates ranging from the 12th century to the 18th century.

## Manuscript Highlights
Key items include the *Burda* (no. 4, bl. 81‑90), the *Bānat Sudād* (no. 851), and commentaries such as *al‑Minal al‑Makkiyya* (no. 848). These manuscripts demonstrate the sustained interest in Arabic devotional poetry among Acehnese and broader Indonesian scholars.
