**Boer ni Geureudong** — also called simply *Geureudong* (pronounced *Gredong*) — is a volcano of the [[Gajo-land|Gajo country]] rising to 2.900 metres, south of the [[Gajo-weg|Gajo-weg]] between [[Lamsahan|Lamsahan]] and [[Takengon|Takengon]]. It is, with its neighbour [[Boer ni Telong|Boer ni Telong]] (2.600 metres), one of the two great volcanoes visible from the Gajo-weg in the approach to the highland basin.
