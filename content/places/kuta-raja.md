**Kuta Raja** was/is a historical capital of the Sultanate of Aceh and the administrative center of Dutch colonial governance in northern Sumatra (modern Banda Aceh).

## Historical Context and Role

Serving as the principal military headquarters for the [[Koninklijk Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger]] (KNIL) and the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]], Kuta Raja contained the central military staff, hospital facilities, administrative offices, and the military cemetery at [[Kerkhof Peutjoet]]. The headquarters of the Marechaussee staff remained stationed in Kuta Raja throughout the colonial era.

## Account from *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*

Capital of Aceh, formerly seat of the [[sultanate|Sultanate]] ([[Dalem]] palace). Site of the Grote Moskee ([[Meuseugit Raya|Meusigit Raja]]), [[Atjeh Museum|Atjeh-Museum]], [[Legermuseum]], [[Kerkhof Peutjoet|Peutjoet cemetery]], Gouvernementswoning (on site of old [[Dalem]]). Bust of [[Generaal J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]], [[Pastoor Verbraak]], dominée [[Dominee Thenu|Thenu]]. Headquarters of military [[gewest]]. Has bookery with standard works on Aceh.[[Meuseugit Raya|Meusigit Raja]] (Royal Mosque), whose first stone was laid in [[1879]] and completed in [[1881]]. Genuine Acehnese [[Koepiah der Atjehers|koepiah]] caps are manufactured here.

*Source: J. Jongejans, Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu (Baarn: Hollandia Drukkerij N.V., c. 1938), pp. 17, 19, 21, 24, 25, 26, 48, 49, 156, 166, 187.*
