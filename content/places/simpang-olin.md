**Simpang-Olin** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Landschap on the east coast of Aceh; rice harvest in 1937 was uitgesproken slecht (very poor).

## Description

[[landschap|Landschap]] on the [[East Coast of Aceh|east coast of Aceh]]; rice harvest in [[1937]] was uitgesproken slecht (very poor).

## Significance

Simpang-Olin is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
