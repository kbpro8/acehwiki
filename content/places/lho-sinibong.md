In the Hikayat [[Malem Diwa]], this is described as the domain of Raja Angkasa, and the location of the princess Meureundam Diwi: 'Putroe Meureundain Diwi in Lho' Sinibong on the river of [[Jambo Aye]].' The hero [[Malem Diwa]] descends from the sky, traverses various places on the East Coast of [[Acheh]], and finally arrives at Lho' Sinibong 'the domain of Raja Angkasa. The whole kingdom has been laid waste and its inhabitants devoured by the geureuda (griffin); the beautiful princess Meureundam Diwi alone, hidden in a beam of timber by her unhappy father, awaited the coming of her deliverer.' Also mentioned in the sadati kisah: 'At Chot Sinibong on the shore of Peulari, there is the gampong of the mother of Meureundam Diwi.'
