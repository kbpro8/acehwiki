Two small islands in the bay of [[Tapa' Tuan]]. They are 'believed to have been originally connected but to have been rent in twain by the dragon in his flight' (as the gigantic saint Teungku Lho [[Tapa' Tuan]] pursued the dragon). A legendary explanation for the geographic feature.
