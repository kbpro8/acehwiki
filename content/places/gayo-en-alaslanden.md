**Gayo- en Alaslanden** — *Gajo- en Alaslanden* in the spelling of the *[[Zelfbestuursverordeningen Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]]*, *Gajo LoeBus*, *Gajo LoeDoes*, and *Gajo Loelus* in OCR variants, *Gayo Alas* in modern Indonesian — was an *afdeeling* (administrative division) of the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] in the interior highlands of [[Atjeh|Aceh]], comprising the homelands of the Gayo (Gajo) and Alas peoples and the seat of the *zelfbesturende landschappen* (self-governing territories) of the *onderafdeelingen* (sub-divisions) [[Takingeun]] (the Gayo highlands, with the *gebied van Rädjö Boekét* / territory of Rädjö Boekét) and [[Alaslanden]] (the Alas valley, with the *gebieden* / territories of Bambel, Poeloe Nas, Kedjoerön Batoe Mboelan, and Kedjoerön Bambel). The Inhoud (table of contents) of the 1930 compilation places the *afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden* as the originating seat of three regulations — Regs. 4c (the Takingeun *Pantjang Alas* uitvoeringsbesluit / implementing decision), 21 (the Serbeudjadi Rafflesia-*natuurmonument* designation), and 22 (the Takingeun *alang-alang en dennenbosschen-verordening* / cogon-grass and pine-forest regulation) — and as the recipient of parallel *regelingen* (arrangements) in the comparative tables of nearly every other regulation in the book.

## Geography and the Gayo and Alas peoples


The *afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden* — the Gayo and Alas lands — occupies the highland interior of [[Atjeh|Aceh]], running south from the north-coast *onderafdeelingen* (Bireuën, Lhokseumawe, Lhoksoekon) toward the Batak lands of north Sumatra. The Gayo people (in Gayo *urang Gayo*) inhabit the northern half of the highlands, around Laut Tawar lake and the principal settlement of [[Takingeun]] (Takengon); the Alas people (in Alas *orang Alas*) inhabit the southern half, the valley of the Alas river — the *Tanah Alas* (Land of the Alas) of the Malay signatory clauses. The two peoples share a common Muslim-Batak highland culture but are linguistically and ethnically distinct: Gayo is a Central-Austronesian language with unique features; Alas is closer to the Batak languages of south Sumatra.

The Inhoud entry for Reg. No. 22 confirms the *afdeeling* scope: *"TELLFESTUURRSTEEEREEEENN van 20 Mel 1924 houdende regelen om- trent het branden van alang-alang op woeste en bebouwde granden benevens het kappen en besehndigen van dennenhosseen in de landschappen der onderafdeeling Takingeun. (Afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden)"*. The Inhoud entry for Reg. No. 4a likewise confirms: *"CALFFESTTUUSYTHREEDENNN van 27 Jull 1925 tot vaststelling van een regeling op de heffing en Inning van de zg. ,,Pantjang Alas" in de landschappen der afdeeling Pidie. (Afdeeling Gajo en Aluslanden)"* — i.e. the *Pantjang Alas* regeling is seated in the afdeeling Pidië but extended by parallel *regelingen* to the *Afdeeling Gajo en Aluslanden*. The Inhoud cross-references consistently pair the *Afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden* with the originating afdeelingen of Pidië, Noordkust, Oostkust, and Westkust.

## The onderafdeelingen of Takingeun and Alaslanden


The *afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden* comprises two principal *onderafdeelingen*: the *onderafdeeling Takingeun* (the Gayo highlands, including the *gebied van Rädjö Boekét*) to the north, and the *onderafdeeling Alaslanden* (the Alas valley, including the *gebieden* of Bambel, Poeloe Nas, Kedjoerön Batoe Mboelan, and Kedjoerön Bambel) to the south. The Takingeun signatory clauses locate the *onderafdeeling* within the afdeeling: *"De Zelfbestuurders der Onderaf- deeling Takingeum gelegen in de ofdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden Gou- vernement Atjeh en Onderhoorig- heden"* — *the Zelfbestuurders (Self-Governing Rulers) of the onderafdeeling Takingeum, situated in the afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden, Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden*. The Alaslanden signatory clauses locate the *onderafdeeling* similarly: *"Bambel dan Poeloe Nas dari Afdeeling tanah Alaslanden"* — *Bambel and Poeloe Nas of the Afdeeling tanah Alaslanden*.

The Page 119 footnote clarifies that the *afdeeling Alaslanden* of 1914 is coterminous with the *onderafdeeling Alaslanden* of 1930 — i.e. the formerly free-standing *afdeeling Alaslanden* was subordinated to the broader *afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden* between 1914 and 1930. The *onderafdeeling Serbeudjadi* — the seat of the Rafflesia-*natuurmonument* (natural monument) regulation of [[1 January]] [[1921]] (Reg. No. 21) — is a third *onderafdeeling* of the *afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden*, located in the eastern Gayo lands and ruled by the *Kedjoeroen Abog* (Panta Ali Aman Lage) as *Zelfbestuurder*.

## Regulations seated in the afdeeling


Three regulations in the 1930 compilation are seated directly in the *afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden*:

- **Reg. No. 4c** — *Zelfbestuursbesluit van 1 October 1926, houdende vaststelling van uitvoeringsvoorschriften en modellen betrekking hebbende op de regeling van de heffing en inning van de z.g. Pantjang Alas in de landschappen der onderafdeeling Takingeun* (Self-Government Decision of 1 October 1926, establishing implementing provisions and forms relating to the regulation of the levy and collection of the so-called Pantjang Alas in the territories of the sub-division Takingeun), issued by the *Zelibestuur dari negeri-negeri didalam onderafdoeling Takingeuu*, approved by the Gouverneur on [[31 December]] [[1926]] — the most-detailed operational supplement in the *Pantjang Alas* regime, providing for the *vergunning* (permit) system administered by the *Oeleebalang* (territorial chief) *in overleg* (in consultation) with the *Plaatselijk Besturend Ambtenaar* (Local Administrative Official).

- **Reg. No. 21** — *Zelfbestuursbesluit van 1 Januari 1921 houdende aanwijzing van terreinen als natuurmonumenten in het landschap Serbeudjadi* (Self-Government Decision of 1 January 1921 designating grounds as natural monuments in the territory of Serbeudjadi), issued by the *Kami Oelesbalang dari landschap Serbendjadi, Kedjoeroen Aboq, Banta Ali Aman Lage, dari onderafdeeling Serbeudjadi, afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden* — the only *natuurmonument* designation in the compilation, designating thirteen named Rafflesia-sites at *Aloer Doeren Tarotoeng*, *Aloer Kloewat*, and *Aloer Itam*.

- **Reg. No. 22** — *Zelfbestuursverordening van 20 Mei 1924 houdende regelen omtrent het branden van alang-alang op woeste en bebouwde gronden benevens het kappen en beschadigen van dennenbosschen in de landschappen der onderafdeeling Takingeun (Afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden)* (Self-Government Regulation of 20 May 1924 establishing rules on the burning of cogon-grass on waste and cultivated grounds as well as the felling and damaging of pine-forests in the territories of the sub-division Takingeun), issued by the *Zelibestuur dari negeri-negeri didalam onderafdoeling Takingeuu* — the only *dennenbosschen* (pine-forest) regulation in the compilation, with its dual subject (cogon-grass burning + pine-forest felling) and its mutual-aid fire-fighting duty on *omwonenden* (residents).

The Page 144 comparative table for Reg. No. 22 extends parallel *regelingen* to Serbeudjadi (29-5-1924, *inwerking* / entry into force 1-5-1925), Gajo Loelus (28-5-1924, *inwerking* 6-6-1924), and Alaslanden (6-6-1924) — i.e. the *afdeeling*'s three principal *onderafdeelingen* each received a parallel regeling within weeks of the originating Takingeun regulation. The Page 29 comparative table for Reg. No. 4a extends parallel *Pantjang Alas* *regelingen* to Takingeun (1-10-1926), Serbeudjadi (1-10-1926), Gajo Loelus (1-9-1926), and Alaslanden (1-11-1925) — again all three *onderafdeelingen* of the *afdeeling*.

## Parallel regelingen extended to the afdeeling


Beyond the three Gajo- en Alaslanden-seated regulations, the *afdeeling* receives parallel *regelingen* in the comparative tables of nearly every other regulation in the compilation. The Page 72 comparative table extends the Peureula' *inkomstenbelasting* (income tax, Reg. No. 9a) to *Gajo LoeBus* — *"Gajo LoeBus"* with *dagteekening* (date) *onbekend* (unknown), *inwerking* 1-1-1921. The Page 109 comparative table extends the Bireuën *heerendienst*-regeling (corvée-service arrangement, Reg. No. 14) to *Gajo LoeBus* (11 Feb 1929) and *Alaslanden* (7 Dec 1928 / 17 Oct 1928), all with *inwerking* [[1 January]] [[1929]]. The Page 113 comparative table extends the Teunom-Panga *schoolgeld*-regeling (school-fee arrangement, Reg. No. 15) to *Gajo LoeBus* (Juli-Dec 1917) and *Alaslanden* (Juli-Dec 1917). The Page 123 comparative table extends the Alaslanden cattle-transport regulation (Reg. No. 16) to *Gajo LoeBus* (24 Oct 1914) — i.e. across the entire *afdeeling*. The Page 129 comparative table extends the Lhoksoekon *klappercultuur*-*verordening* (coconut-cultivation regulation, Reg. No. 18a) to *Gajo LoeBus* (11-1916) and *Alaslanden* (12-1916). The Page 133 comparative table extends the Lhoksoekon *mantri*-instructie (petty-official instruction, Reg. No. 18b) to *Gajo LoeBus* (1 Sept 1916) and *Alaslanden* (21 Nov 1916). By 1930, the *afdeeling* was thus both an originating seat (Regs. 4c, 21, 22) and a recipient (Regs. 9a, 14, 15, 16, 18a, 18b) of the Gouvernement-wide regulatory mesh.

## Significance


The *afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden*'s significance in the Aceh Wiki History record rests on four things. First, it is the only interior highland *afdeeling* of the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] — distinct from the four coastal afdeelingen (Pidie, Noordkust, Oostkust, Westkust) that comprise the rest of the Gouvernement — and therefore the natural laboratory for regulations whose subject-matter is peculiar to the highlands: the *dennenbosschen* (pine forests, *Pinus merkusii*) protected by Reg. No. 22, the Rafflesia-*natuurmonumenten* designated by Reg. No. 21, and the highland-Gayo cattle-breeding stock protected by the Alaslanden Regs. 5 and 16. Second, it is the originating seat of the only *natuurmonument* designation in the compilation — Reg. No. 21 of [[1 January]] [[1921]], the Serbeudjadi Rafflesia-*natuurmonument* designation made under the *ordonnantie van 18 Maart 1916 Staatsblad van Nederlandsch-Indië No. 278* (ordinance of 18 March 1916, Netherlands Indies Statute Book No. 278), the only regulation in the book designating *natuurmonumenten*. Third, it is the originating seat of the most-detailed operational supplement in the *Pantjang Alas* regime — Reg. No. 4c of [[1 October]] [[1926]], the Takingeun *uitvoeringsbesluit* (implementing decision) with its four model-forms (A, B, C, D) and its *vergunning*-system administered by the *Oeleebalang* *in overleg* with the *Plaatselijk Besturend Ambtenaar*. Fourth, it is the demonstration — through the Page 119 footnote documenting the 1914-to-1930 reorganisation by which the formerly free-standing *afdeeling Alaslanden* was subordinated as an *onderafdeeling* within the broader *afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden* — of the colonial administrative-territorial reorganisation of the Atjeh highlands in the 1910s–1920s. The *afdeeling Gajo- en Alaslanden* is, in this sense, the empirical demonstration of the highland regulatory regime and the *natuurmonument*-designation motive's only appearance in the Atjeh regulatory record.

## Account from *Verkenningen in Atjeh* (1923)

**Gajo- en Alaslanden** is an *afdeeling* of the *Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden*, comprising the highland regions of the [[Gajo-land|Gajo country]] and the [[Alas-landen|Alas lands]] in the interior of northern Sumatra. The *afdeeling* was formed in [[1905]] in the aftermath of [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]]'s expeditions of [[1901]] and [[1904]], was re-allotted in [[1908]] among other administrative areas, but was restored in [[1922]] with chief place [[Takengon|Takengon]], comprising the *onderafdeelingen* [[Takengon|Takéngon]], [[Gajoe Loeös|Gajoe Loeös]], [[Serbödjadi|Serbödjadi]] and [[Alaslanden|Alaslanden]]. The administrative work is still hampered by the lack of communications. This *afdeeling* is perhaps the largest in all Sumatra, and the *assistent-resident* needs a month to visit all the *onderafdeelingen*.
