**Pangkalan Soesoe** (modern **Pangkalan Susu**) was a town in the [[Atjeh's Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh Dependencies]] that served, in the [[1913]] extension approvals of the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]], as the junction between the projected [[Koeala Simpang]]–[[Tamiang]]–[[Besitang]] line and the [[Aroebai|Arube Bay]] line via [[Telok Taboeban]]. In the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, Pangkalan Soesoe also figures in the proposed connection to [[Pangkalan Brandan]] and the [[Deli-Spoorweg-Maatschappij|Deli railway]] network — a connection that would, if built, link the Atjeh-tram to the [[Deli-tram|Deli-tramway]] at [[Pangkalan Brandan]] rather than at [[Tandjoeng Slamat]].

## Account from *De Buitenbezittingen* (1917) — Pangkalan Soesoe

The Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account of the [[1913]] extension approvals places [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]] at the central junction of the projected southern extensions of the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]]: "the doortrekking was approved of the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]] network from [[Koeala Simpang]] through [[Tamiang]] to [[Besitang]], and from Pangkalan Soesoe to [[Telok Taboeban]] (on [[Aroebai|Arube Bay]], with a good roadstead), while the connecting line between [[Besitang]] and Pangkalan Soesoe is also already in preparation."

The Bureau's later account of the [[Cultuurraad]]'s deliberations on the proposed connections between the Atjeh-tram and the [[Deli-tram|Deli-tramway]] network also identifies Pangkalan Soesoe as the proposed junction for a direct connection with [[Pangkalan Brandan]] — a connection preferred by the [[Cultuurraad]] to the originally proposed direct [[Besitang]]–[[Tandjoeng Slamat]] junction. The Bureau notes that this preference was contested: "against [the Pangkalan Brandan connection] it is objected that thereby the direct connection [[Besitang]]–[[Tandjoeng Slamat]] would be postponed to a still more distant future, which connection yet — in the opinion of many — will in later times prove necessary. Therefore there is now under consideration a direct connecting line from [[Pangkalan Brandan]] with [[Telok Taboehan]] [Telok Taboeban], there joining the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-net]]."

## Significance

[[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]] is, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, the central junction of the [[1913]] extension projects of the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]] — the point at which the [[Koeala Simpang]]–[[Tamiang]]–[[Besitang]] line, the [[Besitang]]–Pangkalan Soesoe connecting line, and the [[Telok Taboeban|Telok Taboeban]]–Pangkalan Soesoe line to [[Aroebai|Arube Bay]] all converge. Its strategic position made it the principal inland node of the [[Aroe-baai-plannen|Aroe-baai-plannen]].
