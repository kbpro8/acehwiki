**Lampermé** was a place in [[Groot-Atjeh]] between which and [[Tjot Iri]] the steam tram was regularly fired on during the crisis of the spring of [[1896]]. [[P. H. R. Beuming]] records the detail among the signs that *de strijd was weer in vollen gang* — the struggle was again in full swing — in the weeks after the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (29 March 1896)|defection of Teuku Umar]]: shots rang continually from the surrounding *kampongs*, in the distance the guns of the forward posts thundered, and nightly ambushes were laid in the terrain between the road to [[Ketapan Doea]] and [[Oleh-leh]], the strip being lit by the electric searchlights of the warships.
