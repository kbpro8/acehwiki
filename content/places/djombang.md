**Djombang** (modern Jombang) was a town in eastern [[Java]] that served as a staging point for the *Zuid‑colonne* of the *Koninklijk Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger* in [[March]] [[1942]]. The *Marinebataljon* arrived there on [[4 March]] [[1942]] and was tasked with preparing for further operations towards [[Tjepoe]] and [[Ngawi]].

## Military Use
The town hosted the *Marinebataljon*’s headquarters, a school used as barracks, and a hotel for staff. It also became the rendezvous point for a detachment led by *Lieutenant‑colonel J. G. M. Nass* heading to [[Ngandjoek]].

## Significance
Djombang’s logistical facilities were essential for Dutch troop movements during the rapid Japanese advance, highlighting the importance of interior towns in wartime strategy.
