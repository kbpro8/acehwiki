Syria appears in the [[Hikayat Nubuet]]: 'A certain woman named Fatimah Chami (from Sham = Syria) learns that the spirit of prophecy has descended on Abdallah.' In the [[Hikayat Soydina Usen]]: Muhammad was 'king at [[Medina]]; the infidel Yadib (Ach. pronunciation of Yazid) in [[Meuse]] ([[Egypt]]).' The sadati nasib mentions sending a letter 'to the land of Sham (Syria), with a command that all Dutchmen shall become Moslems.' In the [[Hikayat Pha Suasa]], war is waged by 'the kings of Cham (= Syria), Rom (Turkey), [[Meuse]] ([[Egypt]]) etc. against a certain pagan Raja Akeuram.'
