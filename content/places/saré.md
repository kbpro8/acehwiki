**Saré** (pass (highest point on Koetaradja-Sigli road)) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. At K.M.

## Type

pass (highest point on [[Koetaradja]]-[[Sigli]] road)

## Description

At [[K.M.]] 71, the highest point at 475 m, before the descent into [[Sigli]].

## Significance

Saré is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
