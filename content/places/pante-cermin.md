**Pante Cermin** was a strategic coastal landing beach located near the mouth of the Aceh River in Great Aceh.

## Historical Significance

Pante Cermin served as the primary beachhead for Dutch military invasions during the nineteenth century. On [[8 April]] [[1873]], Major-General [[Johan Harmen Rudolf Köhler]] landed the entire force of the [[First Aceh Expedition]] at Pante Cermin under naval artillery cover during the [[Landing at Pante Cermin]].

During the [[Second Aceh Expedition]], landing operations under General [[Jan van Swieten]] took place nearby at [[Pedro Point]] and Tjankoel Loee on [[9 December]] [[1873]]. Pante Cermin was fortified with stockades (*kota*) by Acehnese forces prior to its capture by colonial troops.
