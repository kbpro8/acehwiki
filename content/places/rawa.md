**Rawa** is a province — perhaps the **Rawas** country of interior [[Sumatra]] — from which settlers or strangers of mixed descent came to the [[West Coast (of Acheh)|West Coast]] of Acheh, giving rise to the contemptuous Achehnese nickname *aneu' Rawa* ("people from the province of Rawa") applied to the people of mixed descent on the West Coast. In *The Achehnese* Vol. I, Snouck Hurgronje invokes Rawa in connection with the nicknames by which the Achehnese distinguished the various foreign elements of their population.

## The Aneu' Rawa

The author explains that another contemptuous appellation of the West Coast people, alongside the Malay and Kling *ele* and the *ancu' jamee* ("descendants of strangers or guests"), is *aneu' Rawa* — "people from the province of Rawa" — to which latter nickname the epithet "tailed" (*meuikòt*) is also added. The "tailed" or "tail-less" strangers, so the Achehnese imagination ran, contributed their quota to the composition of the Achehnese race, just as the multitude of Klings in [[Great Acheh]] and on the [[East Coast]] brought more half-caste progeny into the world than now commands recognition as such.

## The West-Coast Populations

The *aneu' Rawa* thus belong to that complex of mixed populations — Kling, Arab, Chinese, and now Rawa — which settled along the [[West Coast (of Acheh)|West Coast]] of Acheh and which were regarded by the Achehnese of the interior as strangers and as less than pure Achehnese. The very use of the epithet "tailed" — a stock device of popular derision in the Indian Archipelago — marks the contempt in which these mixed populations were held by the Achehnese of the metropolitan district. The Rawa element, though less prominent than the Kling or the Chinese, was nonetheless sufficiently marked to give its name to a whole class of mixed-descent West-Coasters.

## Rawa and the Achehnese Race

The mention of Rawa is one of several notices in *The Achehnese* of the way in which the Achehnese race has been formed by the successive influx of foreign elements. The Klings, the Arabs, the Chinese, the Menangkabau, the Europeans, and the people of Rawa have each contributed to the composition of the population of [[Acheh]]; and the epithet *aneu' Rawa* preserves the memory of one such contribution, in the contemptuous form in which it survives in the speech of the Achehnese of the interior.
