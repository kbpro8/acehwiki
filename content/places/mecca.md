The holiest city of Islam and a major destination for Acehnese pilgrims and scholars, who often spent years studying there before returning to teach in Acheh. Figures such as Abdurra'uf of Singkel, Cheh Marahaban, and Raniri all studied or sojourned at Mecca, which together with Medina formed the intellectual hub from which much of Acheh's religious learning derived.
