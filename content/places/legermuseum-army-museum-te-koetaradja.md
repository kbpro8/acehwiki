**Legermuseum (Army Museum) te Koetaradja** (museum) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Army Museum at Koetaradja, under excellent leadership of Majoor Doup.

## Type

museum

## Description

[[Legermuseum te Koetaradja|Army Museum at Koetaradja]], under excellent leadership of [[Majoor Doup]]. Grew in short time since founding into a documentary of enormous value.

## Significance

Legermuseum (Army Museum) te Koetaradja is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
