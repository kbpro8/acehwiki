**Kali Solo** (Solo River) was a major river on [[Java]] that featured in Dutch defensive plans during the Japanese invasion of [[1942]]. The Dutch intended to destroy a bridge over the river at [[Tjepoe]] to hinder Japanese movement.

## Strategic Importance
Control of the bridge at Tjepoe was crucial for both Japanese advance and Dutch defensive operations. The *Marinebataljon* received orders to march towards the bridge and, if necessary, demolish it.

## Significance
The river’s geography shaped tactical decisions during the 1942 campaign, influencing the movement of troops across central Java.
