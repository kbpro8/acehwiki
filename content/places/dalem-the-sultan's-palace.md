**Dalem (the Sultan's palace)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Sultan's palace (in Kota Radja).

## Description

[[sultan|Sultan]]'s palace (in [[Koetaradja|Kota Radja]]). Its inneming (capture) sent [[Sultan of Aceh|the Sultan]] fleeing; he soon died. After fall of [[Dalem]] a new [[sultan|Sultan]] had to be proclaimed - the youth Toeankoe Moehamad Dawot.

## Significance

Dalem (the Sultan's palace) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
