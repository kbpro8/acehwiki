**Prapat** (place) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Beautiful bovenlanden place of Medan/Sibolga.

## Type

place

## Description

Beautiful bovenlanden place of [[Medan]]/[[Sibolga]].

## Significance

Prapat is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
