**Sibreh** — also known as *Sibreh (Ba' Seping)* — was a kampong in the [[III Moekims Lamkrak]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]], which was burned by the Dutch on [[29 June]] [[1896]] in the [[Tuchtiging of Sibreh and Rahat (29 June 1896)|tuchtiging which followed the Surprise of Ana' Galoeng]]. The kampong — estimated by Dutch intelligence to have been garrisoned by 12 men, with its *Passar* (market) garrisoned by 30 men, at the time of the surprise attack — was the principal civilian settlement in the immediate vicinity of the *benteng* (fort) of [[Ana' Galong]], and its burning was the closing act of the [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|surprise of Ana' Galoeng]] of [[29 June]] [[1896]]. The kampong was the home of [[Toekoe Haschim of Sibreh|Toekoe Haschim]], the *mukimhoofd* (parish-head) of Sibreh, who figures in the intelligence records of the second semester of [[1896]].

## The Tuchtiging of 29 June [[1896]]

The kampong of Sibreh was burned by the 1e colonne under [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] on [[29 June]] [[1896]], as part of the *tuchtiging* (punitive expedition) explicitly ordered in the operational plan of the [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|surprise of Ana' Galoeng]]: *het verbranden van de kampongs Rahat en Sibreh der VII Moekims Baid, en het vernietigen der eigendommen aldaar*. The burning was the closing act of the surprise attack, and was followed by the withdrawal of the three colonnes to the *geconcenteerd linie* via Ana' Galoeng, Lambarih, and Lambaroe. The tuchtiging of Sibreh and Rahat was the standard Dutch punitive response to Acehnese resistance, applied to the kampongs of the III Moekims Lamkrak in the immediate vicinity of the *benteng* (fort) which the Acehnese had considered the *hart van Groot-Atjeh*.

## The Dutch Intelligence on Sibreh

The Dutch intelligence on Sibreh, as recorded by Kruisheer, was as follows: *Ana' Galoeng was volgens berichten door ±50 man bezet, Passar Sibreh door 30-en Sibreh (Ba' Seping) door 12 man. In Ba' Tjerlaq, Blang Tjoct, Bockloet, Lambarih en Belang zouden zich geen vijanden ophouden.* The estimated enemy strength at Passar Sibreh was 30 men; at Sibreh (Ba' Seping) — Sibreh proper, distinguished by its alternative name *Ba' Seping* — only 12 men. The tuchtiging was thus directed primarily against the civilian infrastructure of the III Moekims Lamkrak, with the military garrisons of the kampongs already dispersed or dissolved by the storming of the *benteng* (fort).

## Significance

Sibreh is significant in the documentary record of *ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel* as one of the two kampongs (with [[Rahat]]) burned by the Dutch on [[29 June]] [[1896]] in the [[Tuchtiging of Sibreh and Rahat (29 June 1896)|tuchtiging which followed the Surprise of Ana' Galoeng]]. The kampong — the home of [[Toekoe Haschim of Sibreh|Toekoe Haschim]], the *mukimhoofd* (parish-head) of Sibreh — was the principal civilian settlement in the immediate vicinity of the *benteng* (fort) of Ana' Galoeng, and its burning was the visible demonstration, to the surrounding population, of the consequence of harbouring or supporting the Acehnese resistance at the *benteng* (fort). The kampong would be reoccupied by the Acehnese and would again be the object of Dutch operations in the second semester of [[1896]] — the recurring pattern of tuchtiging and reoccupation which characterised the slow, attritional warfare of the second semester.

## In the eye survey of Groot-Atjeh ([[1937]])

Sibreh appears in Dr. [[C. E. Gomperts]]'s [[Oogheelkundig kampongonderzoek in Groot-Atjeh (Maart-December 1937)|eye survey of 1937]] among the parts of [[Groot-Atjeh]] "waar de Westersche invloed meer is doorgedrongen" and where traffic was busier — with [[Oeleë Lheuë|Oeloe-Lheuë]], [[Seulimeum]], [[Indrapoeri]], [[Lho Nga|Lho-Nga]] and [[Kroeng Raja|Kroeëng-Raja]].

Gomperts used these districts as a measure of the frequency of venereal infection among the population: the annual reports of the *Gouvernements-Indisch arts* for [[1936]] and [[1937]] recorded only eight and nine gonorrhoeal infections in total, from which he concluded that the share of gonorrhoeal infection in the causes of [[Blindheid in Groot-Atjeh|blindness]] in the surveyed area "zeer gering moet zijn."
