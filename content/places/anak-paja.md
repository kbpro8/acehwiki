**Anak Paja** was a place in [[Groot-Atjeh]] with a Dutch postholder, who was treacherously murdered there with eight *pradjoerits* on [[25 June]] [[1883]] — four days after the Governor-General's telegram of [[21 June]] [[1883]] that Groot-Atjeh was almost wholly cleansed of enemies (see [[Murder of the postholder at Anak Paja (25 June 1883)]]).
