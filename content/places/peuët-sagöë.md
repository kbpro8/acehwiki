**Peuët-Sagöë** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Volcano on the border of the afdeeling Noord- en Westkust van Atjeh; only solfataras and hot springs remain of its former volcanic activity.

## Description

Volcano on the border of the [[afdeeling]] Noord- en [[Westkust van Atjeh]]; only solfataras and hot springs remain of its former volcanic activity.

## Significance

Peuët-Sagöë is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
