**Keumangan** is a great *landschap* in the eastern part of the [[Pidië|vallei of Pidië]], whose *oeléebalang* at the time of Broersma's visit was [[Teukoe Oemar Keumangan|Teukoe Oemar]], *'van een oud en aanzienlijk geslacht'* — *of an old and distinguished lineage* — whose features betray his Bengali descent. The two great chiefs of Pidië were, of old, those of [[Pidië (landschap)|Pidië]] and of Keumangan; between them the predominance in the ancient region lay, and both wished to seize it. Teukoe Oemar lives at [[Beureunoen|Beureunoen]] on the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]] in a large dwelling, before which spreads a spacious yard with grass and trees, accessible through an iron gate between high stone pillars. Within the yard he has built a new house in the Atjehnese style, entirely decorated with carving and with the mouldings picked out in colours — a building which has, at least for a few years, revived the art of wood-carving in this region.
