**Koetaradja** was the historic name of the capital of the Aceh Sultanate, located at the mouth of the Aceh River on Sumatra’s northern coast. In the early twentieth century it served as a printing centre for Acehnese religious texts, notably the [[Nalam Mo'lôt]] and a local translation of the [[Akidat al-awāmm]], both produced around 1925 [[Nalam Mo'lôt]] [[Akidat al-awāmm]] [[1925]]. The same year saw the issuance of the [[Madjmit rasa'il]] collection in Koetaradja [[Madjmit rasa'il]] [[1925]].

## Early twentieth‑century printing
According to the source, the works “Nalam Mo'lôt en de door Kreemer aan hem toegeschreven Atjêhse vertaling van de “Akidat al‑awāmm”, beide in cen omstreeks 1925 te Koetaradja nitgegeven Madjmit rasa'il” indicate a small‑scale publishing scene in the city [[Nalam Mo'lôt]] [[Akidat al-awāmm]] [[Madjmit rasa'il]] [[1925]] [[Koetaradja]]. These publications were likely intended for use in pesantren and surau, reflecting the continued vitality of Acehnese Islamic scholarship after the Dutch conquest (1873‑1904).
