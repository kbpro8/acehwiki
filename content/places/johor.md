A sultanate on the Malay Peninsula conquered by [[Sultan Iskandar Muda]] in [[1613]] as part of his campaign to extend Acehnese control over the Strait of Malacca, alongside his later conquest of Pahang in [[1618]] and his [[1628]] expedition against [[Malacca]].
