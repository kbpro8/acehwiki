**Pidië (Pedir)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. District/region; a haard (hearth) of resistance since beginning of Aceh war.

## Description

District/region; a haard (hearth) of [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] since beginning of Aceh war. Many religious schools there; influential oelamas originated there. [[Pedir Expedition|Pedir-expeditie]] of [[August]] [[1898]]. [[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]] fled to [[Pidië]] to marry off his son. [[Gampong]] Tirò (origin of the [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] Tirò lineage) is in [[Pidië]]. [[Pidië]]-vallei home of [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] leader [[Teukoe Bin Peukan]].

## Significance

Pidië (Pedir) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
