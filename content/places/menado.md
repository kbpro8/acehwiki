**Menado** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[H. N. A. Swart]]

On [[9 July]] [[1906]], under article 43 of the *Reglement op het beleid der Regeering in Nederlandsch-Indië*, he was temporarily charged with effect from [[3 September]] [[1906]] with the civil and military authority in the [[Gouvernement Celebes en Onderhoorigheden]], at 1,500 guilders a month, with the title of *Civiel en Militair Gouverneur van Celebes en Onderhoorigheden* and simultaneously Military Commander of [[Menado]] and [[Timor]] en Onderhoorigheden. There he oversaw the [[Pacification of the Province of Celebes (1906–1908)|pacification of Celebes]] and, as the closing piece of his eastern service, the [[Onderneming tegen Soembawa (1908)|undertaking against Bima and Soembawa]] in [[1908]] — an operation carried out with a handful of *marechaussée* and described by du Croo as a classic example of rapid and conclusive pacification. He was promoted Commander of the *Militaire Willemsorde* ("Military William Order") by royal decree of [[2 September]] [[1909]] no. 41 for the Soembawa operations of [[16 February]] to [[9 May]] [[1908]], and appointed Knight in the [[Order of the Netherlands Lion]] by royal decree of May [[1909]] no. 7.

### From [[Celebes]]

He held the post at 1,500 guilders a month with the title *Civiel en Militair Gouverneur van Celebes en Onderhoorigheden*, simultaneously Military Commander of [[Menado]] and [[Timor]] en Onderhoorigheden. [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] defended his subsequent appointment to Atjeh on that ground: "Governor Swart, who made himself highly deserving in the field of administration both in the South and East Division of [[Borneo]] and in Celebes." Those who knew only Atjeh considered the work in Borneo and Celebes easier and not comparable; Swart denied it, holding that "it was all the application of the same insights — though under different circumstances." He revisited Celebes with his wife in [[1928]].

### From [[1906]]

In **[[1906]]** the [[Pacification of the South and East Division of Borneo (1905–1906)|pacification of the South and East Division of Borneo]] was completed; on [[9 July]] [[1906]] [[H. N. A. Swart]] was temporarily charged, with effect from [[3 September]] [[1906]], with the civil and military authority in the [[Gouvernement Celebes en Onderhoorigheden]] and the military command of [[Menado]] and [[Timor]].
