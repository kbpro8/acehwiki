**Pantej Karang** (also *Panteh Karang*) was an Atjehnese strong place in the [[XXII Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]] at which, on [[6 May]] and [[7 May]] [[1879]], the Atjehnese inflicted great losses on the troops of generaal [[Karel van der Heijden|van der Heyden]] before they were themselves driven out, in the continuation of the [[Campaign against the XXII Moekims (1879)|campaign of 1879]]; afterwards it was kept as a Dutch post until, at the execution of the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration]], it was numbered among the outer fortifications returned to the field. The post was one of the fortifications maintained by the Dutch outside the new line that were returned to the field at the execution of the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration]]: it was evacuated between [[5 January|5]] and [[14 January]] [[1885]], nearly a month and a half before the clearing of the so-called [[1000-meter strook|free strip]] was finished (see [[Completion of the concentrated position (March 1885)]]).
