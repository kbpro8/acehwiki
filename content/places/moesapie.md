**Moesapie** (Cape Moesapie) was a maritime coastal point and geographical landmark located near [[Koerong Raba Bay]] along the northwestern coast of the [[Aceh Sultanate]], in modern Aceh Province, Indonesia.

## Landing Marker in 1873

During the amphibious landings of the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] in December [[1873]], Cape Moesapie marked the outer geographical boundary of the anchorages utilized by General [[J. van Swieten]]'s invasion fleet. Dutch naval vessels stationed between Moesapie and [[Koerong Raba Bay]] covered the debarkation of the [[Marine Corps in Aceh]] during the [[Attack on Koerong Raba]].

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

[[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] names Kota Moesapie the northeastern corner of the position of April [[1875]]: the chain from [[Kota Alam]] passed from Lemboe Oost and Lemboe Noord along [[Lampriet]], Langkroek-Oost, Lamara Zuid-Oost, Lamara Noord and [[Tiban]] to Moesapie, while the base of the triangle so formed was the coastal strip from [[Oleh-leh]] to Kota Moesapie (see [[Verstege's folding map of Groot-Atjeh (1886)]]).
