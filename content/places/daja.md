**Daja** was the *[[landschap]]* on the [[Westkust van Atjeh|west coast of Atjeh]], formerly [[H. N. A. Swart]]'s own patrol district, in which the band of [[Nja' Ali]] operated in [[1914]].

In [[1902]] Swart, as *gezaghebber* in [[Lho' Kroeët]] and Daja, was forbidden by [[Gotfried Coenraad Ernst van Daalen|Van Daalen]] to travel without military escort, a prohibition [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|van Heutsz]] overruled. In [[1914]] the murder of [[W. van Kregten]] at [[Lam No]] on [[20 May]] set off the [[Resistance in Daja and Lhong (1914)|resistance in Daja and Lhong]]; the united bands of Nja' Ali and [[Nja' Asém]] were destroyed by lieutenant [[C. A. Snell|Snell]] at the holy grave near the mouth of the [[Daja-rivier]] on [[5 August]] [[1914]]. The flare-up of resistance in the Daja districts was one of the three heavy blows [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]] names as having tested Swart's composure.

## Account from *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*

Kingdom on the [[Westkust]] near Atjeh. Conquered by [[Sultan Ali Moegajat Sjah]] first (to cover his rear), before his conquests of the Noord- and [[Atjeh's Oostkust (East Coast of Aceh)|Oostkust]].

*Source: J. Jongejans, Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu (Baarn: Hollandia Drukkerij N.V., c. 1938), pp. 26.*
