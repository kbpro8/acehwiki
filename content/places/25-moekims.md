**The 25 Moekims** — *Twenty-five Moekims*, written in Dutch *25 Moekims* — was one of the two seaward *Sagi's* (administrative divisions) of [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]], together with the [[26 Moekims|26 Moekims]] and the inland [[22 Moekims|22 Moekims]] and the [[Sultan's gebied|Sultan's own territory]]. The 25 Moekims, together with the Sultan's territory, was the principal Dutch-occupied portion of Groot-Atjeh from the [[Tweede Atjeh-expeditie (1873-1874)|second Dutch expedition]] of [[January 1874|January]] [[1874]] onwards, and remained in Dutch hands throughout the war.

## Geography

The 25 Moekims was one of the two seaward *Sagi's* of [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] — together with the [[26 Moekims|26 Moekims]]. The 25 and 26 Moekims lay on the sea; the [[22 Moekims|22 Moekims]] lay inland. The Dutch had mainly occupied the 25 Moekims and the [[Sultan's gebied|Sultan's territory]]. All of Groot-Atjeh was to be brought under.

## The Dutch Occupation

The 25 Moekims, together with the Sultan's territory, was the principal Dutch-occupied portion of Groot-Atjeh from the [[Tweede Atjeh-expeditie (1873-1874)|second Dutch expedition]] of [[January 1874|January]] [[1874]] onwards. The [[Kraton of Aceh|kraton]] of [[Kota Radja|Koeta Radja]] — the nucleus of the Dutch *stelling* — lay in the Sultan's territory; the port of [[Oleh-leh|Oleh-leh]] lay in the 25 Moekims; the [[Atjeh River|Atjeh-rivier]] ran through both. The 25 Moekims remained in Dutch hands throughout the war, even during the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration system]] of [[1884]] that contracted the Dutch *stelling* back to the perimeter of [[1876]].

## Significance

The 25 Moekims is one of the two seaward *Sagi's* of [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] and the principal Dutch-occupied portion of Groot-Atjeh throughout the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. Its occupation from the [[Tweede Atjeh-expeditie (1873-1874)|second Dutch expedition]] of [[January 1874|January]] [[1874]] onwards, and its retention throughout the war — even during the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration system]] of [[1884]] — together make it the principal geographic base of the Dutch military presence in Groot-Atjeh. The port of [[Oleh-leh|Oleh-leh]], the [[Kraton of Aceh|kraton]] of [[Kota Radja|Koeta Radja]], and the [[Atjeh River|Atjeh-rivier]] all lay within or adjacent to the 25 Moekims.
