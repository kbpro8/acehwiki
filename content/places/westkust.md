**Westkust** — the West Coast of [[Atjeh|Aceh]], in [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]'s spelling also *de Westkust* — was the heartland of the most protracted and fanatieke guerrilla resistance in the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. Comprising the landschappen of [[Lhong]], [[Leupoeëng|Leupoeëng]], [[Meulaboh]], [[Seunagan]], [[Djeuram]], [[Bakongan]], [[Troemon]], [[Kloeët]], and the Boven-Wojla federation of the Kawaj XII, the Westkust was *bosch en bergen* — *ruig land* eigende zich het meest for the *plotselinge aanvallen uit hinderlagen*. In Van Heutsz's [[1893]] tract *De onderwerping van Atjeh*, the Westkust is also the coastal region of the [[Atjeh|Atjehnese]] [[Onderhoorigheden]] comprising the statelets of [[Loong]], [[Melaboeh]], [[Telok-Kroet]], [[Troemon]] and the smaller statelets of the *Zuidelijke nederzettingen* (Kloewang, Tampat Toean, Rigas, Tenom, Senagan, Tamsih, Gempang). The Westkust served — with the [[Noordkust]] and the [[Oostkust]] — as one of the three coastal divisions on which the war's outcome turned.

## Military character

The *vechtersbazen* of the Westkust *grepen elke kans aan*, and *deden hun voordeel* with *elke fout* of ours. The *traditioneele* land of the *klewangaanvallen* was [[Seunagan]], where the Atjeher always *aanviel* with *vollen inzet* of his *persoon*. The *peudeueng* — the Westkust curved *klewang*, a *Turksche sabel* — was the characteristic weapon. The Westkust's *ruige land* made the *strijdwijze* different from the Noord- en Oostkust, where *vlak land* noopte to the *strijd* on *grooten afstand*.

## Key figures and episodes

The Westkust was the territory of [[Teukoe Oemar]] (*Heer der westelijke zee*), of [[Tjoet Nja Din]] and [[Tjoet Gambang]], of [[Tengkoe Poetih]] (grandson of the *heiligen* [[Habib Seunagan]]), of [[Tjoet Ali]] who led the [[Westkust resistance (1925-1927)|1925-1927 resistance]], and of [[Potjoet Barin]], *oeléëbalang* of [[Toengkob]] in the Boven-Wojla.

The [[Lhong punitive expedition (1897)|Lhong punitive expedition]] of April [[1897]] — with its f 30,000 fine, confiscation of *vee*, and seizure of women's gold and silver jewellery — was one of the *beschamendste* episodes on the Westkust. The 1925-1927 resistance under Tjoet Ali, ending in his death at [[Kandang]] on [[25 May]] [[1927]], was the last major eruption.

## Account from *De onderwerping van Atjeh* (Van Heutsz, 1893)

In Van Heutsz's [[1893]] tract *De onderwerping van Atjeh*, the Westkust is the coastal region of the Atjehnese [[Onderhoorigheden]] that comprised the statelets of [[Loong]] (the *Boegineesche* settlement whose *Radja* was descended from the Bugis *Daeng Mancoer Boegis* of Wadjo), [[Melaboeh]] (the seat of the *assistent-resident ter Westkust*), [[Telok-Kroet]] (the Westkust call-port opposite the island of [[Poeloe Raja]]), [[Troemon]] (the southern extremity of the entire Atjehnese coast and the southern limit of the [[1884]] *rigoureuse blokkade*), and the smaller statelets of the *Zuidelijke nederzettingen* (Kloewang, Tampat Toean, Rigas, Tenom, Senagan, Tamsih, Gempang). The Westkust served — with the [[Noordkust]] and the [[Oostkust]] — as one of the three coastal divisions on which the war's outcome turned; the Westkust's *assistent-resident* at Melaboeh was the institutional seat from which the *scheepvaartregeling* on the Westkust was administered, and the Westkust's pepper, copra and resins were, in Van Heutsz's diagnosis, the fiscal stake that gave the closure-and-*scheepvaartregeling* system its leverage over the *hoofden* of the Westkust.

### Composition: the statelets of the Westkust

The Westkust fell, in Van Heutsz's classification, into a ring of statelets whose *hoeloebalangs* (territorial chiefs) gave the region its constitutional shape. The northernmost was [[Loong]] — the *Boegineesche* settlement whose *Radja* was descended from the Bugis *Daeng Mancoer Boegis* of Wadjo (Celebes), who had been shipwrecked on [[Poeloe Bras]] and brought to Loong by Loongsche fishermen. South of Loong lay [[Melaboeh]] — the seat of the *assistent-resident ter Westkust (standplaats Melaboeh)*, the institutional seat from which the Westkust-side of the *scheepvaartregeling* was administered. Further south lay [[Telok-Kroet]] — the Westkust call-port opposite the island of [[Poeloe Raja]], where the *scheepvaartregeling* Van Heutsz prescribed in [[1893]] would establish an *etablisement*. At the southern extremity lay [[Troemon]] — the southern limit of the [[1884]] *rigoureuse blokkade* *van Tamiang op de Oostkust tot Troemon op de Westkust*.

The *Zuidelijke nederzettingen* — the smaller statelets of the southern Westkust — included, in Van Heutsz's catalogue, [[Kloewang|Kloe-wang]] (whose *radja* was, since [[1880]], the *panglima sagi XXV* Tjoet Abas), [[Tampat Toean]] (the southern Westkust call-port of Van Heutsz's [[1893]] prescription), [[Rigas]] (the *hoeloebalang*-seat of the Potjoet Toewong family), [[Tenom]], [[Senagan]] and [[Tamsih]] (the route-nodes of the Westkust path from Kemala to the Gleh Loedah pass), and [[Gempang]] (the Westkust route-node).

### The 1877 tuchtiging of Loong and the assistant-resident ter Westkust

The first Dutch military operation on the Westkust was the [[1877]] *tuchtiging* (chastisement) of Loong — the *Boegineesche* statelet whose *Radja* had resisted Dutch authority. Van Heutsz records the operation: *Achtereenvolgens werd nu in 1877 Loong op de Westkust getuchtigd, tot tweemaal opgetreden te Melaboeh en daar een post gevestigd, Langsar en Mojopahit onderworpen, nogmaals eene expeditie naar Simpang Olim gezonden, en hevig gestreden en een post gevestigd te Samalanga* (Successively in 1877 Loong on the Westkust was punished, [Dutch forces] proceeded twice to Melaboeh and established a post there, Langsar and Mojopahit were subdued, once again an expedition was sent to Simpang Olim, and heavily fought and a post was established at Samalanga). The Melaboeh post — established *tot tweemaal* (twice) — was the institutional form by which the *assistent-resident ter Westkust (standplaats Melaboeh)* was constituted. The post was abolished under the [[1884]] [[Concentratiestelsel|Concentratie]], and its functions transferred to the *militaire commandant* at Melaboeh. The post was re-established under the [[Van Teijn]] governorship, and the *assistent-resident ter Westkust* was, in the [[1891]] reconnaissance of [[Telok-Kroet]] and [[Poeloe Raja]], the *ambtenaar* who accompanied the chef van den staf.

### The 1884 Concentratie and the Westkust's closure

The [[Concentratiestelsel|Concentratie]] of [[1884]] prescribed, as its second operational component, the *rigoureuse sluiting van het geheele kustgebied, d. w. z. van Tamiang op de Oostkust tot Troemon op de Westkust* — the rigorous closure of the entire coastal region from Tamiang on the East Coast to Troemon on the West Coast. The Westkust was thus, in the prescription, the southern half of the *rigoureuse blokkade*'s scope — the coast from Melaboeh and Telok-Kroet south to Troemon that was to be closed to all foreign trade. The [[Demmeni]] *besluit* of [[13 October]] [[1884]] — the *soort scheepvaartregeling* Demmeni introduced in lieu of the full closure — established call-ports at [[Edi]], [[Segli]] and [[Olehleh|Oleh-leh]] for the *Noord- en Oostkust*, but no Westkust call-port. The Westkust was thus, in Demmeni's hybrid scheme, the closed coast *par excellence* — the coast with no open call-port through which foreign trade could be routed.

### The Van Teijn closure and the Westkust

The Westkust was, in the [[Van Teijn]] governorship of [[1886]]–[[1891]], the southern half of the progressive closure of the *Noord- en Westkust*. The closure, begun in January [[1888]] and extended in stages, was *in 't begin van 1890 eindelijk was uitgestrekt over de geheele Noord- en Westkust* — at the beginning of [[1890]] finally extended over the entire North- and West Coast. The Westkust's *licenties* at Telok-Kroet were, in Van Heutsz's diagnosis, the *moeilijkheid* of the closure — the *bijzondere omstandigheid* through which the closed Westkust continued to draw *levensmiddelen*. Van Heutsz records the difficulty: *niettegenstaande zij niet zoo afdoende was, als die in 1884 door de Regeering gewenscht, omdat de Oostkust open bleef en door bijzondere omstandigheden, ook voor Telok Semawé en Kerti op de Noord- en voor Telok Kroet op de Westkust een beperkte invoer van levensmiddelen door middel van licenties was toegestaan, trof zij, in weerwil hiervan, toch nog boven verwachting doel* (notwithstanding that it was not so effective as that wished by the Regeering in 1884, because the Oostkust remained open and through special circumstances, also for Telok Semawé and Kerti on the Noordkust and for Telok Kroet on the Westkust, a limited import of foodstuffs by means of licences was permitted, it struck, despite this, beyond expectation).

### The 1893 prescription: the Westkust's open ports

In Van Heutsz's own [[1893]] prescription, the Westkust retained three open call-ports — [[Telok-Kroet]], [[Melaboeh]] and [[Tampat Toean]] — through which the Westkust's trade was to be routed, with the entire coast between these ports closed to all foreign trade. The Westkust's pepper, copra and resins were, in Van Heutsz's diagnosis, the fiscal stake that gave the closure-and-*scheepvaartregeling* system its leverage over the *hoofden* of the Westkust. Van Heutsz records the export: *Ook van de andere artikelen van uitvoer, die allengs aan de markt zullen komen, zooals b. v. in zoo'n klapperland copra, verder gom en harssoorten, die thans alle via Tamsih, Gempang en Senagan of Kroeng Sabil de Westkust uitgaan en van meer andere artikelen* (Also of the other articles of export, which will gradually come to market, such as for example in such a coconut-land copra, further gum and resin kinds, which now all go out via Tamsih, Gempang and Senagan or Kroeng Sabil from the Westkust, and many other articles). The Westkust's export-route through Tamsih, Gempang, Senagan and Kroeng Sabil was, in Van Heutsz's prescription, the route to be re-routed through the Westkust's three open call-ports under the *scheepvaartregeling* Van Heutsz would himself implement as Governor of Atjeh from [[1898]] onward.

### The Westkust and the Gleh Loedah pass

The Westkust was, in Van Heutsz's diagnosis, the southern approach to the Gleh Loedah pass — the pass through which the Westkust route from [[Kemala]] entered the IV Moekims Kroeng Raba of Groot-Atjeh. Van Heutsz records the route, in his critique of [[Scherer|Resident Scherer]]'s claim that Groot-Atjeh could be sealed from the Westkust by closing the Gleh Loedah pass: Scherer argued that *men nog over den Barisan naar de Westkust kunnen trekken en dan zoo door den Gleh Loedah pas de IV Moekims Kroeng Raba in Groot-Atjeh bereiken* (one could still cross the Barisan to the Westkust and then so through the Gleh Loedah pass reach the IV Moekims Kroeng Raba in Groot-Atjeh), but that *die weg is lang en nagenoeg onbegaanbaar* (that road is long and practically impassable). Van Heutsz's response — *Ook deze weg naar Groot-Atjeh is te volgen en ook niet zoo bijzonder moeilijk, want men kan hem b. v. geheel te paard afleggen. Hij loopt van Kemala over Tamsih en Gempang naar Kroeng Sabil en verder langs de Westkust naar den Gleh Loedah pas enz.* (This road too to Groot-Atjeh is practicable and also not particularly difficult, for one can traverse it entirely on horseback. It runs from Kemala over Tamsih and Gempang to Kroeng Sabil and further along the Westkust to the Gleh Loedah pass etc.) — fixed the Westkust as the southern approach to the Gleh Loedah pass whose closure was, in Van Heutsz's diagnosis, the operational complement of the [[Kroeng Raja]] seal on the north-east.
