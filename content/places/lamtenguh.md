**Lamtenguh** was an Atjehnese *kampong* of the [[XXVI Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]] — the *kampong* of [[Toekoe Tjoet Machmoed van Lamtenguh]], the *[[Uleebalang|oeloebalang]]* who was the half-brother of [[Toekoe Moeda Baid]] of the [[VII Moekims]]. The *kampong* is named in the [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] in the chapter on the [[1896]] crisis as the territory of the *oeloebalang* to whom was entrusted the safety of the terrain between the Dutch temporary post at [[Anagaloeng]] and the *blokhuis* of [[Blang Tjoet]].

## The 1896 context

The [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] records that, in the early months of [[1896]], as the Dutch temporary outposts at [[Anagaloeng]], [[Senelop]], [[Biloel]] and [[Lamkoenjit]] were being besieged by the forces of [[Toekoe Oemar]] following his [[Toekoe Oemar's defection (1896)|defection from the Dutch alliance]], the security of the lines of communication between the outposts became a question of first importance. The Dutch had constructed a chain of *blokhuizen* (blockhouses) between Anagaloeng and the *geconcentreerde linie*, and the safety of the terrain between these *blokhuizen* was entrusted to allied Atjehnese *oeloebalang*s rather than to Dutch garrisons. Toekoe Tjoet Machmoed, as *oeloebalang* of Lamtenguh, was given responsibility for the sector between Anagaloeng and the *blokhuis* of [[Blang Tjoet]].

## Significance

Lamtenguh's significance in the documentary record of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] is as the *kampong* of [[Toekoe Tjoet Machmoed van Lamtenguh|Toekoe Tjoet Machmoed]] — the *oeloebalang* whose appointment to the Anagaloeng sector was a consequence of the unreliability of his half-brother [[Toekoe Moeda Baid]]. The *kampong*'s appearance in the [[Atjeh-Album (Eigen Haard, 1896)|Atjeh-Album of 1896]] is brief, but it identifies the kinship network of the *oeloebalang* class of the [[XXVI Moekims]] and the [[VII Moekims]] that the Dutch sought to manage in the late *concentratie* period.
