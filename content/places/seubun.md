A location in [[Acheh]]. '[[Teungku Tiro]] now continued his journey to Seubun. Here the poet gives an ironical description of a kanduri or religious feast organized by the ulama on a grand scale, which was unfortunately disturbed by an onset of the Dutch troops. The assembled guests found it hard, even with the bullets whistling about their ears, to tear themselves away from the dainty feast of buffalo-meat just done to a turn, with all the accompanying good cheer.'
