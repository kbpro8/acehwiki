**Gedah** was one of the two quarters of [[Koeta Radja]] lying outside the *[[Kraton of Aceh|Kraton]]* — the other being [[Nesoeh]] — which, according to [[P. H. R. Beuming]], were strongly fortified in the spring of [[1896]] during the crisis following the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (29 March 1896)|defection of Teuku Umar]]: *Nesoeh en Gedah, twee buiten den Kraton gelegen wijken van Kota Radja, werden geducht versterkt*.

Beuming's account dwells chiefly on the works at Nesoeh, which lay turned towards the most hostile front; Gedah is named as the second of the two quarters requiring protection in the weeks when all available troops were needed to hold the *[[Geconcentreerde linie|geconcentreerde linie]]* and to secure Koeta Radja and [[Oleh-leh]], and none could be spared to relieve the enclosed forward posts.
