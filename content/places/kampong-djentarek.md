**Kampong Djentarek** was a fortified settlement in the interior of [[Acheh]] that was assaulted by Dutch marine forces during the 1868‑1869 campaigns. The village’s defenses included high stone walls and densely built houses, which forced the attackers to advance “under a rain of large stones” before finally breaching the fortifications.

## 1868 Assault

During the attack on [[24 October]] [[1868]], the marine landing division, under the command of officers such as [[Lieutenant P. Spanjaard]] and [[Lieutenant D. A. van Vuuren]], engaged the defenders of Kampong Djentarek. The Dutch troops employed artillery and infantry assaults to overcome the “blocked passages” and eventually secured the village.

## Significance

The capture of Kampong Djentarek demonstrated the Dutch military’s capacity to overcome heavily fortified Acehnese villages, contributing to the broader colonial pacification of the Aceh coast.
