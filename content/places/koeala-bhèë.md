**Koeala Bhèë** was the bivouac in the [[Wòjla]] district of [[Meulaboh]] whose energetic commander made Wòjla a model *[[landschap]]*, and near which the *[[klewang]]* attack of [[25 April]] [[1918]] occurred.

Het *landschap* ("territory under a native chief") Wòjla behoorde tot het gebied van den *bivakcommandant* ("bivouac commander") te Koeala Bhèë, een energiek officier, die er een eer in stelde alle bestuursbevelen zoo goed mogelijk te doen uitvoeren. Under his direction the roads were exceptionally well maintained, the *[[gampong]]s* excelled in order and cleanliness, and tax collection and school attendance left nothing to be desired — "only the vaccination still left something to be desired." [[Teungkoe Moet]] refused to appear when summoned to Koeala Bhèë and was killed resisting arrest; on [[25 April]] [[1918]] the commander, bivouacking carelessly a day's march away, lost one killed and seven wounded in a *klewang* attack.
