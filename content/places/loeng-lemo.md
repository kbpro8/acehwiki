**Loëng Lemo** was a river in [[Acheh]] that the Dutch right‑hand column had to cross on [[30 January]] [[1876]]. A bridge was constructed for the artillery to cross, while the enemy continued to fire on the advancing troops.

### Engineering Effort
The bridge was built under fire, demonstrating the logistical capabilities of the expeditionary force.
