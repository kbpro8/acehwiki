**Pira Estate** (rubber estate) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Rubber enterprise in vicinity of Lho'Soekon.

## Type

rubber estate

## Description

Rubber enterprise in vicinity of [[Lho'Soekon]].

## Significance

Pira Estate is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
