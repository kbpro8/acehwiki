---
title: Pidie
tags: [place]
---

A district on the north coast of Acheh, historically one of the most important centers of Islamic learning and military strength in the sultanate. Pidie produced many of Acheh's most celebrated religious teachers, including Teungku Teureubue and his disciple Teungku Gade, and supplied troops and panglimas (war leaders) during both the naval campaigns of Sultan Eseukanda Muda and the later holy war against the Dutch. The chiefs of Pidie's IX Mukims bore the hereditary title Bentara Keumangan.
