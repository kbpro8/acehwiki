---
title: Gampong Meulayu
tags: ["place", "aceh-proper"]
---

A Malay village in [[Acheh]]. After Jeumaloy fled from [[Gampong Jawa]], he fled to [[Lam Barueh]], then to Gampong Meulayu, then to Krueng Raba and Krueng Kala.
