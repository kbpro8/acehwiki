**Wikkie‑kreek** was a riverine area in the Dutch colony where a major **rebellion** involving **[[Atta (rebel leader)|Atta]]**, **[[Quacco]]**, **[[Baubé]]**, and **[[Fortuyn]]** took place during the **[[1760s]]**. The Dutch established a fortified headquarters nearby and conducted multiple military operations to suppress the insurgency.

## Military Significance
The creek served as a strategic corridor for both rebel movements and Dutch troop deployments, including the **[[Kolonel De Salve|Colonel De Salve]]** expedition.
