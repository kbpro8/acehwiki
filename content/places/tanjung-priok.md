**Tanjung Priok** was a major harbor project on [[Java]], constructed between [[1871]] and [[1883]] to replace the inadequate port facilities at [[Batavia]]. The project was a significant achievement of Governor-General [[J.W. van Lansberge]]'s administration, reflecting the growing importance of steamship traffic following the opening of the [[Suez Canal]] in [[1869]].

## Background

Prior to the construction of Tanjung Priok, Batavia's harbor had become increasingly inadequate:
- Steamships had to anchor far from shore due to silting.
- The reede transport (lighterage) was inefficient and expensive.
- The opening of the Suez Canal in [[1869]] had dramatically increased shipping traffic.

Prince [[Hendrik of the Netherlands]] initiated the project in [[1871]], and plans were drawn up for the new harbor.

## Planning and Design

The project faced opposition from the Batavian commercial community, who preferred the existing location for the harbor. To resolve the dispute, a commission in the Netherlands (comprising [[P. Caland]], [[J.A.A. Waldorp]], and [[A.R. Blommendal]]) was appointed to advise on the site. They recommended [[Tanjung Priok]].

[[J.A. de Gelder]] and [[L.G. Bouricius]] were sent from the Netherlands to lead the construction, which was to be carried out entirely by the government due to the lack of qualified contractors.

## Construction

Construction began in the early [[1870s]]:

1. **Technique**: The harbor was built on a foundation of pure sea sand, which was ideal for construction and improved health conditions.

2. **Labor**: Inland workers were trained as supervisors, machinists, and telegraphers, replacing unreliable European personnel.

3. **Cost**: Despite initial fears, the project stayed within budget, with De Gelder promising completion within three years and costs "well below the estimate."

4. **Management**: The project was managed by the government due to the lack of private contractors. Alting Mees noted that "the only contractors who could be considered were not giving sufficient guarantees for proper execution."

## Challenges

1. **Commercial opposition**: The Batavian Chamber of Commerce petitioned against the project, leading to debates in the [[States-General]].

2. **Financial constraints**: The high cost (estimated at 2.5 million guilders for the supplementary credit) was a concern, especially given the financial strain of the [[Aceh War]].

3. **Technical issues**: There were disputes about the quality of materials, leading Van Bosse to complain that "the engineers are increasingly taking the keys to the treasury."

## Completion

The harbor was completed in [[1883]], a significant achievement in colonial infrastructure. Van Lansberge noted that "within three years, he (De Gelder) expects the first ship to steam into the harbor."

The project demonstrated the government's ability to undertake large-scale public works and set a precedent for other projects, including the state railways.

## Legacy

Tanjung Priok remains the main port of Jakarta to this day. Its construction was a milestone in the modernization of [[Java]], facilitating trade and communication with the [[Netherlands]] and the wider world.
