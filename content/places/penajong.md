**Penajong** was the Dutch entrenched camp occupied after the march to the [[Aceh River]] during the [[Second Dutch Expedition to Aceh]].

## Historical setting

It served as a base for the advance on the [[Baiturrahman Grand Mosque|Missigit]] and [[Kraton of Aceh]], was attacked by Acehnese forces, and later received chiefs and cultivators seeking permission to harvest rice. It lay in the Kota Radja area, now [[Banda Aceh]].

## Modern context

The source situates [[Penajong]] in the historical landscape of [[Aceh]]; historical spellings follow the [[1879]] Dutch text.
