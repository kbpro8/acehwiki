**Lageuën** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Place on the Westkust to which Teukoe Oemar fled; this flight is known in Aceh history as 'Oemar's doodentocht'.

## Description

Place on the [[Westkust]] to which [[Teukoe Oemar]] fled; this flight is known in Aceh history as 'Oemar's doodentocht'.

## Significance

Lageuën is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
