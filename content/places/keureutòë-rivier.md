**Keureutòë-rivier** is a subject named in *[[Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Tgk. di Barat]]

In the night of [[23 March|23]]–[[24 March]] [[1911]] a patrol of one brigade of *marechaussée* under the bivouac commander at [[Leuhong]], captain [[Van der Vlerk]], was stalked in a marching bivouac in the forest by the [[Keureutòë-rivier]] and attacked with cold steel by some ill-disposed persons; a native *marechaussée* was killed and captain Van der Vlerk and two Acehnese guides lightly wounded, while a model carbine was lost. It was proved that the attack had been carried out by old, devoted members of Tgk. di Barat's band. All available patrols went out to trace and pursue the Teungkoe.

### From [[Keureutòë]]

[[1911]] brought "the great improvement," the monthly reports habitually opening "the political situation remained favourable," though [[Tgk. di Barat]] returned from the Gajòland to the upper Keureutòë in June and a night attack on the patrol of captain [[Van der Vlerk]] on the [[Keureutòë-rivier]] on [[23 March|23]]–[[24 March]] was traced to his old adherents. With his death at [[H. Behrens|Behrens]]'s hands on [[22 February]] [[1912]] "the situation in the whole North Coast division became stable and ranged from favourable to very favourable; the suffering there was over."

### From [[Kroeëng Peutòë]]

Mosselman had marched out from [[Lho' Soekon]] on [[22 October]] [[1910]] and picked up fresh tracks in a side-stream of the Kroeëng Peutòë on the third day. The pursuit ran over waterfalls, boulders and slippery stones; towards four in the afternoon of [[25 October]] he saw smoke above the trees and charged with seven men into a band certainly sixty strong. The corpse of the old blind Teungkoe, defended to the death by two young men, was recognised by the golden weapon, the famous *bawar*. In March [[1911]] a patrol under captain [[Van der Vlerk]] was attacked in a marching bivouac on the [[Keureutòë-rivier]] by old adherents of [[Tgk. di Barat]].
