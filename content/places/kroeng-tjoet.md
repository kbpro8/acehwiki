The **Kroeng Tjoet** (Krueng Cut) is the river on the north coast of [[Groot-Atjeh]] flowing into the lagune of Gigieng, which served throughout the war as the major contraband supply channel giving the Atjehneezen of the [[XXVI Moekims]] access to arms, buskruit and levensmiddelen imported from Penang and Singapore. Its closure was a principal strategic object of the Dutch from [[1874]] onward, but the channel was not finally closed until [[1876]].

## The contraband channel

The Atjehnees resistance east of the [[Kraton of Atjeh|Kraton]] depended on a steady flow of imported arms and ammunition through the north-coast lagune of Gigieng, whose mouth at [[Kwala Gigieng]] was sheltered from the seaward blockade by the configuration of the coast. Boats could enter the lagune from the sea, pass up the Kroeng Tjoet, and discharge their cargoes at points within easy reach of the [[XXVI Moekims]] and the eastern approaches to the Kraton. The Dutch post at [[Moesapi]] — armed with two 12 c.M. *voorlaadkanonnen* — was supposed to cover the lagune and the Kroeng Tjoet, but its disadvantageous position meant it could not stop boats entering the river. The Kroeng Tjoet was therefore a constant preoccupation of [[Generaal Pel|generaal Pel]]'s planning; his great afsluitingslinie from the mouth of the [[Kroeng Raba]] on the west coast to the mouth of the [[Kroeng Raya]] on the north coast was designed precisely to isolate Atjeh from such supply channels.

## Closure in 1876

The Kroeng Tjoet was finally closed by the Dutch in [[1876]] in the wake of [[Generaal Pel|Pel]]'s great [[Pel's offensive against the VI, IV and IX Moekims (1875–1876)|winter offensive of 1875–1876]]. On [[23 February]] [[1876]] Pel's march northward from [[Pango]] reached [[Tongah]] on the *linker-oever* (left bank) of the Kroeng Tjoet; the bivouac was pitched at [[Lamjoeng]] between Tongah and Silang on the far side of the river. Pel died at Lamjoeng in the night of [[24 February]] [[1876]] of a *slagaderbreuk* (ruptured artery). [[Luitenant-kolonel Engel|Lt.-kol. Engel]], who had arrived at Lamjoeng with the 9de bataljon to reinforce the ageerende troepen, assumed tijdelijk oppergezag as oudste hoofdofficier and continued the march via Tjade to the [[Kwala Gigieng|Kwala Gigieng]], which was reached on [[26 February]] [[1876]]. Posts were established at Kwala Gigieng and on the island of [[Kota Pohama]] in the lagune, completely sealing the gemeenschap between sea and mouth of the Kroeng Tjoet.
