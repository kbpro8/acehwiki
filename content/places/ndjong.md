**Ndjong** is a *landschap* in the *onderafdeeling* [[Sigli|Sigli]] of the *afdeeling* [[Pidië|Pidië]], coming from [[Meureudoe|Meureudoe]]. It is for the most part mountain- and hill-land, with a plain only in the coastal strip. The lagoon along which the road leads — not the tram-line, which bends westward — stretches over a great length and offers a pleasant view over the open sea. Only further west lies the good land of Ndjong with its many *sawahs*. The *landschapshoofd* is renowned for his wealth, which shows that his revenues are significant, thanks to the treasure of the soil which is the fertility here. Without irrigation-works one could not manage here, necessary as they are, if only to limit the *bandjirs*. For the benefit of rice-cultivation the population, led by its chiefs, lays out simple works; others arose through the labour of the *B.O.W.* Behind the *landschap* rises [[Seulawaih Agam|Atjeh's Goudberg]] in a light mist — the *Seulawaih Agam*, that is, the man, and the smaller [[Seulawaih Inong|Seulawaih Inong]], that is, the woman; for in everything of Nature the Atjehnese sees or imagines the presence and the distinction of the horse.
