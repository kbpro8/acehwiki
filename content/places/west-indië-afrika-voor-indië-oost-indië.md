**West-Indië, Afrika, Voor-Indië, Oost-Indië** (colonial possessions) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Dutch colonial possessions lost to England during the Napoleonic era (late 18th/early 19th century).

## Type

colonial possessions

## Description

Dutch colonial possessions lost to [[Engeland (England)|England]] during the Napoleonic era (late 18th/early 19th century).

## Significance

West-Indië, Afrika, Voor-Indië, Oost-Indië is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
