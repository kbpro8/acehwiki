Territories on the East Coast of [[Sumatra]]. 'The Achehnese adherents of the Naqshibandiyyah or Qadiriyyah are of no account as compared with those of West [[Java]] or of Deli and Langkat.' Thus these are compared as much more active centres of orthodox mystical tariqahs.
