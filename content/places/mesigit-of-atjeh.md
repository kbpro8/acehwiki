The **Mesigit of Atjeh** was the fortified *voorwerk* (outwork) — built around a mosque — guarding the northern approach to the [[Kraton of Atjeh|Kraton]] and forming, with the [[Atjeh-rivier]] on the south and the *bamboe-doeri* (thorn-bamboo) wall on the other faces, the principal landward defence of the sultan's palace. It was carried by Dutch assault on [[6 January]] [[1874]] in the [[Storming of the Mesigit (6 January 1874)|storming of the Mesigit]] at the cost of 220 Dutch dead and wounded, and afterwards converted into a kanonbatterij from which the [[Bombardment of the Kraton (January 1874)|nineteen-day bombardment]] of the Kraton was conducted. Borel considered it a meesterlijk work and a standing reproach to Dutch reconnaissance.

## The fortification

The Mesigit as Borel describes it after its capture was an earthen borstwering some 180 metres long, fronted by bamboo-doeri, provided with *dwarswallen* (traverse walls) and *schietkokers* (loopholed firing chambers), and surrounded by a dry gracht filled with bamboo-doeri. The work lay concealed in the boschrand "als een tijger die op zijn prooi loert" ("like a tiger stalking its prey"), at no more than 1,200 paces from the Dutch bivouak at [[Penajoeng]]. Borel credits its design to no formal engineer but to the Atjehnees' natural instinct for field-works — the same instinct that produced the [[Lampoe Oek]] stelling and the [[Longbatta-Mesigit]] stone mosque later in the war. The *Beknopte beschrijving van den Kraton van Groot Atjeh* had said nothing of these preparations, and Dutch reconnaissance was negligible.

## The storming and after

[[Jan van Swieten|Van Swieten]] ordered the assault on [[6 January]] [[1874]] *tegen alle Indische traditiën in, zonder noemenswaardige voorbereiding, alleen in front* — against all Indian traditions, without preparation, only in front, where of course the work was strongest. Battalion after battalion was repulsed by "moorddadig vuur." [[Kolonel de Roy van Zuydewijn|De Roy van Zuydewijn]] finally gained footing on the borstwering while [[Generaal Pel|luitenant-kolonel Pel]] flanked the work and entered the Mesigit unopposed. Dutch losses were 220 dood en gekwetsten; enemy losses "vermoedelijk slechts onbeduidend." Once taken, a kanonbatterij was built in its eastern face, *mortieren van 20 c.M.* were brought up, and from [[6 January|6]] to [[23 January]] the batteries bombarded the Kraton.
