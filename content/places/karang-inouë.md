**Karang Inouë** (oil-palm enterprise) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Japanese oil-palm enterprise located inland beyond the BPM terrain at Peureula.

## Type

oil-palm enterprise

## Description

Japanese oil-palm enterprise located inland beyond the BPM terrain at [[Peureula]].

## Significance

Karang Inouë is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
