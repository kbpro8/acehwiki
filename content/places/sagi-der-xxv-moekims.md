**Sagi der XXV Moekims** — written in Dutch as *Sagi der XXV Moekims* and meaning "Federation of the Twenty-Five Mukims" — was one of the three traditional [[sagi]] (federations of [[moekim|mukims]]) into which [[Groot-Atjeh]] was divided, comprising twenty-five mukims situated in the southern and inland portion of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] theatre around [[Biloel]], [[Lamkoenjit]], and [[Tjot Goeë]]. As described in Kruisheer's *ATJEH '96*, the Sagi der XXV Moekims contained the principal Dutch [[buitenpost|buitenposten]] of the western portion of the [[buitenlinie]], and was the scene of the sieges and evacuations of [[April]] [[1896]] following the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (29 March 1896)|defection of Teuku Umar]]. The sagi is mentioned in Vetter's proclamation of [[26 April]] [[1896]] and in the final Dutch assessments of the war as one of the regions "onder Nederlandsch gezag" under Dutch protection and enjoying relative prosperity compared to the hostile [[Sagi der XXII Moekims]] and [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims]].

## Description and Location

The Sagi der XXV Moekims lay in the southern part of [[Groot-Atjeh]], extending from the immediate hinterland of [[Koeta Radja]] toward the foothills of the *gebergte*. It bordered the [[Sagi der XXII Moekims]] on the west and the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims]] on the east, and was the sagi in which lay the Dutch posts of [[Biloel]] (on the road along the foot of the mountains in S.O. direction toward the XXII Moekims border), [[Lamkoenjit]] (near the road Tjot Goeë–Biloel), [[Tjot Goeë]] (on a hill approximately 30 metres high, serving as the communication post between [[Lampeneroet]] and Biloel), and the sawah plain of [[Blang Bintang]]–[[Ketapan Doea]]. The sagi contained a series of [[blokhuis|blokhuizen]] — including Lehang, Gröt, Lam Tadock, Maneh, Mon Mantjang, Blang Kirai, and Mon Pandjang — that formed the western chain of the [[buitenlinie]].

## Historical Background

The Sagi der XXV Moekims had been a principal theatre of operations since [[1873]]. The Dutch posts of Biloel, Lamkoenjit, and Tjot Goeë had been established to protect the [[IX Moekims]] against incursions from the [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lamkrak]], Kroeng Mak, Loetoe, and Lam Lheue of the [[VII Moekims Baid]], and to dominate the great market of [[Lamkoenjit]]. After [[1890]] these posts were linked in the [[buitenlinie]] enclosing the sagi and isolating the hostile mukims of the [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lamkrak]]. Following [[Teuku Umar]]'s appointment as [[Panglima Prang Besar]] on [[5 September]] [[1893]] his native *legioen* operated within this sagi against the Lamkrak.

## Role in the 1896 Operations

The Sagi der XXV Moekims bore the brunt of the immediate crisis following [[Teuku Umar]]'s [[Defection of Teuku Umar (29 March 1896)|defection of 29 March 1896]]. From [[29 March]] to [[8 April]] [[1896]] the posts of [[Biloel]] and [[Lamkoenjit]] were besieged; they were relieved on [[8 April]] [[1896]] by a column under [[Kolonel der Infanterie|Kolonel]] [[J. W. Stemfoort|Stemfoort]] and then evacuated and burned on [[12 April]] [[1896]]. On [[17 April]] [[1896]] the post of [[Ana' Galong]] (technically in the [[Sagi der XXII Moekims]] but operationally part of the same chain) was lifted by the second colonne under Stemfoort. Thereafter the sagi was held only at the inner line of [[Geconcentreerde Linie|Geconcentreerde Linie (Concentrated Line)]] posts — [[Lam-Djamoe]], [[Lamtih]], [[Ketapan Doea]], [[Lampeneroet]] — from which the [[Bombardment of Lampisang|bombardment of Lampisang]] was conducted from [[27 April]] to [[23 May]] [[1896]], and from which the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day offensive of 23–25 May 1896]] was launched.

## Significance

In the final assessments of [[1896]] the Sagi der XXV Moekims was singled out, together with the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims]], as one of the prosperous regions under Dutch protection, in implicit contrast to the [[Sagi der XXII Moekims]] of [[Panglima Polim]] which remained hostile. The sagi's pacification permitted the Dutch to convert [[Lam-Djamoe]], [[Lamtih]], and [[Ketapan Doea]] into semi-permanent versterkingen and to use them as the principal bases for the conquest of the [[IV en VI Moekims|IV and VI Moekims]] of [[Teuku Umar]].
