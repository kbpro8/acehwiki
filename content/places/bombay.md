The Fathul Kareem Press at Bombay published numerous popular works cited by Snouck: 'Among the popular Urdu literature in the catalogues of the Fathul Kareem Press at Bombay we find... versions of the story of Tamim Angari in Urdu and Afghan,' and similar references for the stories of Shah Bahram, Abu Nawas, and the Amir Hamzah romance.

## Source
*The Achehnese*, Vol. II, by Dr. C. Snouck Hurgronje, translated by A.W.S. O'Sullivan, indexed by R.J. Wilkinson. Leyden: E.J. Brill, 1906.
