---
title: Lam Panaih
tags: ["place", "aceh-proper"]
---

A gampong (village) in [[Acheh]]. [[Teungku Tiro]] came first to Lam Panaih, 'his following being composed chiefly of men from [[Pidie]].' This ulama gave a great impetus to the holy war. 'Arriving at Lam Panaih he went through seven days of seclusion and mortification (tapa).' He then returned from Lam Panaih to [[Aneu' Galong]]. A little cannon (lilla) with a bell is mentioned in connection with Lampanaih (Lam Panaih) in the sadati kisah: 'In the mountain range of [[Lam Weueng]] is the peak of the [[Seulawaih]], in Lampanaih is a lilla (small cannon) with a bell.'
