**Glé Jeuëng** was one of the residences of [[Panglima Polém|the sagihoofd of the XXII Moekim]] and a strategic position on the route between [[Indrapoeri|Indrapoeri]] and [[Seulimeum|Seulimeum]] in [[Groot-Atjeh|Greater Atjeh]]. The [[Conquest of Glé Jeuëng (May 1897)|conquest of Glé Jeuëng]] on [[16 May]] [[1897]] — described as a "verwoed gevecht" (furious battle) that cost heavy casualties — was a significant milestone in the consolidation of Dutch control over the [[XXII Moekims|XXII Moekim]].

## After the conquest

After its conquest, [[Glé Jeuëng|Glé Jeuëng]] became one of the forward posts occupied outside the old [[Geconcentreerde linie|geconcentreerde linie]], along with [[Kroeëng Raja|Kroeëng Raja]], [[Leupoeëng|Leupoeëng]], [[Seulimeum|Seulimeum]], and [[Reuëngreuëng|Reuëngreuëng]]. These posts achieved complete mastery over [[Groot-Atjeh|Greater Atjeh]]: disarmed and registered, with active patrols and surveillance, the interior was no longer peacefully inhabitable for enemies.
