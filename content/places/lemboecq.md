**Lemboecq** was a kampong to the north-east of [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] whose fortified positions were captured by Dutch troops on [[7 November]] [[7 November]] [[1874]] and incorporated into the Dutch defensive perimeter in the middle phase of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. Together with the neighbouring kampongs of Lemboe, Berauw, and the Rivierbenting, Lemboecq was one of the chain of fortified villages that [[J. L. J. H. Pel|Kolonel PEL]] took and held in the first week of [[November 1874|November]] [[1874]] as the eastern outwork of the Dutch position at [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]]. The capture of Lemboecq was the immediate prelude to the [[Benting Kota Alam operation (13 November 1874)|Benting Kota Alam operation of 13 November]] [[1874]]: it was at Lemboecq that the reconnaissance of the southwest part of the kampong was made for which [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede-Luitenant C. J. Snijders]] was later cited in his [[Militaire Willemsorde|Militaire Willemsorde]] recommendation.

## The Capture of 7 November 1874

On [[7 November]] [[7 November]] [[1874]] Dutch troops "hadden zich meester gemaakt van eenige krachtig versterkte vijandelijke stellingen in de kampong Lemboecq (ten N.O. van Kota Radja) en aldaar nieuwe versterkingen Lemboe. Berauw en de Rivierbenting ingericht en bezet." The capture of Lemboecq was the first step in the extension of the Dutch perimeter north-east of [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] in the autumn of [[1874]]. The original intention had been to follow up the capture by also occupying [[Kuta Alam|Kota Alam]] further east, but for want of troops this was postponed — a postponement that was reversed on [[11 November]] [[11 November]] when intelligence indicated that the [[Achehnese|Atjehers]] were preparing to occupy [[Kuta Alam|Kota Alam]] themselves, leading directly to the [[Benting Kota Alam operation (13 November 1874)|Benting Kota Alam operation of 13 November]].

## The Willemsorde Citation

The recommendation for [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]]'s [[Militaire Willemsorde|Ridderkruis der Militaire Willemsorde]] 4th class, made by *Koninklijk Besluit of 12 November 1875 No. 18|Koninklijk Besluit* of [[12 November]] [[12 November]] [[1875]] No. 18, cites — under the heading "a. [[13 November]] [[13 November]] [[1874]]" — "Verkenning zuidwestelijk gedeelte van Lemboecq en oprichting eener versterking bij Kota Alam: onder het vuur des vijands het tot stand brengen der sterkte Kota Alam hoofdzakelijk met vrije koelies te helpen op touw zetten en de daaraan verbonden werkzaamheden met beleid en kalmte te hebben geleid." The reconnaissance of the south-western part of Lemboecq was thus part of the same operation as the [[Benting Kota Alam operation (13 November 1874)|Benting Kota Alam]] construction, and was the action for which Snijders received the [[Militaire Willemsorde|Ridderkruis]].
