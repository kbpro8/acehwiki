**Pasé (Pase)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. District through which Panglima Polém was pushed; Pasé-expeditie under Van Heutsz personally.

## Description

District through which [[Panglima Polém]] was pushed; [[Pasai|Pasé]]-expeditie under [[Generaal J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] personally. Under [[H.E. Swart|Governor Swart]] on the eve of general revolt in [[Keureutòë]] and [[Pasai|Pasé]] areas.

## Significance

Pasé (Pase) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
