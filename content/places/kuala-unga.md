A location near the West Coast associated with [[Lam Beusoe]]. Sadati dialogue: 'go as panglima to the kuta (fortress) of Chutli... I will not establish myself in Chutli; it is too close to the shore, on the border of the estuary. If that please thee not, little brother, little heart, I will set thee at Babah Awe (above Kuala Unga). I will not establish myself at Babah Awe, for I fear to die there with not one to care for me.'
