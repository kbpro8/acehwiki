**Ateuh Raja** — written in Dutch as also *Ateuh*, *Ateuh-Tjoct*, *masdjid Ateuh*, *Ateuh-Lam Lo-West*, and collectively *Groot Ateuh* for the broader Ateuh area — was a kampong in the Ateuh moekim of the [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lamkrak complex]] and the site of major combat on [[9 June]] [[1896]] in the course of the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|tuchtiging of the Lamkrak Moekims]]. As described in Kruisheer's *ATJEH '96*, Ateuh Raja was attacked by the 9e Bataljon under [[J. F. Th. Veeren|luit.-kol. Veeren]] in one continuous attack: the 1e compagnie (kapitein [[H. T. de Moulin|H. T. de Moulin]]) hit the breastwork behind the Kroeng Ateuh, and the attack was led by 2de luit. [[W. A. Engelbrecht|W. A. Engelbrecht]]. The European fusilier v. Dongen was first on the *borstwering* but received a fatal chest shot from the last Atjeher. Ateuh Raja, with Ateuh Tjoet and Groot Ateuh, formed the Ateuh sub-cluster of the broader [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lamkrak complex]].

## Description and Location

Ateuh Raja lay in the Ateuh moekim of the [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lamkrak complex]] — the "streek tusschen Biloel en Ana Galoeng en benoorden het gebergte gelegen" (the region between [[Biloel]] and [[Ana' Galong]], north of the mountains). The Ateuh moekim included the sub-kampongs of Ateuh Tjoet, Ateuh Raja, and Groot Ateuh (collectively, the "Ateuh" of the [[Lamkrak Moekims|7-mukim complex of Lam-Krak]]). The Kroeng Ateuh (Ateuh river) ran through the kampong, and a breastwork behind the Kroeng Ateuh was the principal Achehnese defensive position. The mosque of Ateuh, also *Mesigit Ateuh* was occupied at 7:45 AM on [[8 April]] [[1896]] in the course of [[Kolonel der Infanterie|Kolonel]] [[J. W. Stemfoort|Stemfoort]]'s relief of [[Biloel]] and [[Lamkoenjit]]. The kampong was bounded on the north by the [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lam-Lheuë]] mukim (with its *Kling* fortification of Mon Selassa / Mon Seulassa) and on the south by the [[Sagi der XXV Moekims|XXV Moekims]] and the [[IX Moekims]].

## Historical Background

Ateuh Raja had been part of the hostile [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lamkrak complex]] throughout the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. The kampong had been a base for hostile *benden* operating against the [[buitenpost|buitenposten]] of the [[buitenlinie]] — particularly [[Biloel]] and [[Lamkoenjit]]. The [[Masdjid Ateuh|mosque of Ateuh]] was a fortified *masdjid* that served as a strongpoint. The kampong had been the object of Dutch artillery fire in [[April]] [[1896]] — two kampongs named Ateuh were the objective of artillery fire on [[8 April]] [[1896]], and the Manch kampong was mentioned alongside Ateuh-Tjoct. The [[1878]] campaign of [[Generaal van der Heiden|Generaal van der Heiden]] had passed through the Ateuh country, and the [[1893]] *legioen* of [[Teuku Umar|T. Djohan]] had operated in the area.

## Role in the 1896 Operations

Ateuh Raja was the site of major combat on [[9 June]] [[1896]] in the course of the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|tuchtiging of the Lamkrak Moekims]]. The 1e colonne of the tuchtiging under [[J. F. Th. Veeren|luit.-kol. Veeren]] of the 9e Bataljon advanced from [[Biloel]] against Ateuh Raja in one continuous attack. The 1e compagnie (kapitein [[H. T. de Moulin|H. T. de Moulin]]) hit the breastwork behind the Kroeng Ateuh, and the attack was led by 2de luit. [[W. A. Engelbrecht|W. A. Engelbrecht]]. The European fusilier v. Dongen was first on the *borstwering* but received a fatal chest shot from the last Atjeher — one of the most-celebrated incidents of individual heroism of the tuchtiging. Earlier, on [[8 April]] [[1896]], the mosque of Ateuh was occupied at 7:45 AM by the 9e Bataljon in the course of [[Kolonel der Infanterie|Kolonel]] [[J. W. Stemfoort|Stemfoort]]'s relief of [[Biloel]] and [[Lamkoenjit]]. The *Manch* kampong was mentioned alongside Ateuh-Tjoct as an artillery target on [[8 April]] [[1896]]. On [[10 April]] [[1896]] enemy shooters in the east *rand* of Lege were driven out, and on [[12 April]] [[1896]] the W. *rand* of Oleh-Soesoeh was occupied. The [[borstwering]] behind the Kroeng Ateuh was the principal Achehnese defensive position at Ateuh Raja on [[9 June]] [[1896]].

## Significance

Ateuh Raja was the principal Achehnese strongpoint in the Ateuh moekim of the [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lamkrak complex]] and the scene of one of the most-celebrated incidents of the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|tuchtiging of 9–10 June 1896]] — the fatal wounding of European fusilier v. Dongen by "the last Atjeher" as he was first on the *borstwering*. The fall of Ateuh Raja, together with the fall of [[Baha]] and [[Lam-Djamé|Lam-Krak / Lam-Djamé]] on the same day, broke the back of organised Achehnese defence in the [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lamkrak complex]].

## Account in Hooijer's *De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh* ([[1897]])

**Ateuh** was a place in the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]] on the inner path running from [[Tjot Rang]] to [[Tjot Iri]], mentioned by [[G. B. Hooijer]] in his account of the difficulty of movement in the marshy terrain around Tjot Rang.

## In Hooijer's description

Explaining how hard it was for Dutch troops to fight in terrain where no roads had been laid, Hooijer noted that even *het binnenpad, waarop de patrouille zich voortbeweegt en dat van Tjot Rang over Ateuh naar Tjot Iri voert* — the inner path along which the patrol moves, running from Tjot Rang over Ateuh to Tjot Iri — had to be reckoned as something particularly easy.
