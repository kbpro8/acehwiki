**Vesting Kroeeng Raja** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Fortress Kroeeng Raja.

## Description

Fortress [[Kroeëng|Kroeeng]] Raja. Photo caption (page 361): 'Vesting Kroeeng Raja (middenstuk)'. Photo caption (page 376): 'Vesting Kroeeng Raja (een overdekte waterput).'

## Significance

Vesting Kroeeng Raja is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
