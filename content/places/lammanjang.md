**Lammanjang** was a *[[Kampong|kampong]]* of [[Groot-Atjeh]] taken by the [[12e Bataljon Infanterie|12e]] and [[14e Bataljon Infanterie|14e Bataljon]] on the first day of the [[Three-day battle in the Beradin pass (23-25 May 1896)|three-day battle in the Beradin pass]], [[23 May]] [[1896]]. [[P. H. R. Beuming]] records that on the enemy's left wing the fighting was most stubborn and that *ten koste van zeer veel verliezen werden achtereenvolgens genomen [[Lamtengah]], Lammanjang en [[Lam-isi]]* — at the cost of very many losses Lamtengah, Lammanjang and Lam-isi were taken in succession.
