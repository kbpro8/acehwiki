**Lambaro** was/is a important trade junction and market town located in the central plain of [[Groot-Atjeh]].

## Historical Context and Role

In [[1898]], Lambaro co-hosted the 5th Division of the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]], protecting central commercial routes connecting [[Kuta Raja]] with the interior.
