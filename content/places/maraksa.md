**Maraksa** was a coastal locality reconnoitred by Dutch forces in the later [[1874]] operations covered by [[Jan van Swieten]].

## Historical setting

Its reconnaissance was connected with the lighthouse at [[Pulau Bras]], naval action in the [[VI Mukim]], and attempts to build political relations through maritime commanders.

## Modern context

The source situates [[Maraksa]] in the historical landscape of [[Aceh]]; historical spellings follow the [[1879]] Dutch text.
