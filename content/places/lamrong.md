**Lamrong** was a Dutch military post in the southern section of the geconcentreerde stelling (concentrated position) in [[Groot-Atjeh]]. The post was established on [[25 February]] [[1885]].

## Location

Lamrong was situated in the southern section of the geconcentreerde stelling, between [[Lambaroe]] and [[Lampeneroet]].

## Establishment

Lamrong was one of the last posts established during the concentration period, occupied on [[25 February]] [[1885]].

## Fortification (1885)

Lamrong was:

**Type:** A post with 75-man Infantry garrison
**Armament:** 2 × 12cm breech-loading guns

## Infrastructure Connections

The post was connected to:
- The ceintuurtram (girdle tramway) system
- [[Lambaroe]] and [[Lampeneroet]]
- Routes to the southern areas

## Significance

Lamrong helped complete the southern section of the perimeter, providing additional coverage between [[Lambaroe]] and the more southern posts.
