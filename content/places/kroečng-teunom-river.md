**Kroečng Teunom (river)** (river) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. River formed by the confluence of the Kroečng Tangsé and Kroečng Geumpang, flowing out on the Westkust.

## Type

river

## Description

River formed by the confluence of the [[kroečng|Kroečng]] [[Tangsé]] and [[kroečng|Kroečng]] [[Geumpang]], flowing out on the [[Westkust]].

## Significance

Kroečng Teunom (river) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
