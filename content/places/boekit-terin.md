**The Boekit Terin** was a hill in [[Groot-Atjeh]] west of which lay the Dutch forward post of [[Biloel]], named by [[P. H. R. Beuming]] in his description of the post's situation in *[[Schetsen uit den strijd op Groot-Atjeh (1911)|Schetsen uit den strijd op Groot-Atjeh]]*.

Beuming places Biloel *in de nabijheid der posten [[Lamkoenjit]] en [[Tjot Goé]], ten oosten van den heuvel, genaamd: de Boekit Terin* — in the neighbourhood of the posts Lamkoenjit and Tjot Goé, east of the hill called the Boekit Terin. To the west the post bordered the extensive territory of [[Lamkrak]], and its front looked out on the mountain chain called [[Batoe Mengoera]].

The word *boekit* is glossed elsewhere in the book as *een kleine hoogte of heuvel* — a small height or hill.
