**Idische (Idi)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. District on the Eastkust; also agitated in under Van Heutsz's own leadership.

## Description

District on the Eastkust; also agitated in under [[Generaal J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]'s own leadership. [[Pang Nanggròë]]'s [[Overval|overval]] on a marchbivak in the Idische.

## Significance

Idische (Idi) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
