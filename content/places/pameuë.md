**Pameuë** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Place to which the Pretendent-Sultan fled after the Gajolanden; although attacked from three sides, he escaped and went to Pidië.

## Description

Place to which the [[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]] fled after the [[Gajolanden (Gayo Lands)|Gajolanden]]; although attacked from three sides, he escaped and went to [[Pidië]].

## Significance

Pameuë is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
