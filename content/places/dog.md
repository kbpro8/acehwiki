**Dog** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[C. H. R. Heins]]

A Chinese mason was severely wounded in the same attack; the murderers fled to the mountains with two of the overseer's sporting guns and ammunition. That same morning the *controleur* of Lho' Nga left overland for Lhong with two groups, while [[H. N. A. Swart]] himself went by the *s.s. [[Dog]]* in the afternoon, and two further groups went to [[Leupoeëng]] to prevent the affair spreading. The murder was for Swart "a very sharp and painful disappointment indeed — after the attack at [[Daja]], now murder and band-formation in quiet [[Groot Atjeh]] as well."

### From [[Resistance in Daja and Lhong (1914)]]

That became apparent the following month, and again in the quiet directly administered territory of the *afdeeling* Groot Atjeh. The *maandverslag* reported: "On the morning of 13 July the overseer of Public Works, [[C. H. R. Heins]], was murdered at [[Lhong]] (sub-division [[Lho' Nga]]) by seven persons of that territory, while a Chinese mason was severely wounded. After the murder the killers fled into the mountains with two sporting guns belonging to the overseer and with ammunition. That same morning the *controleur* of Lho' Nga left overland for Lhong with two groups, while the reporting officer went there in the afternoon with a group aboard the *s.s. [[Dog]]*. On the same day two further groups left for [[Leupoeëng]] to prevent what had happened at Lhong from spreading there."
