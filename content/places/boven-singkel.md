**Boven-Singkel** (Upper Singkel; in present-day [[Aceh Singkil Regency|Aceh Singkil Regency]]) is the upstream region of the Singkel river in southern [[Acheh|Atjeh]], mentioned in [[J. Kreemer|Kreemer]]'s [[Kreemer's De Karbouw ([[1956]])|*De Karbouw* ([[1956]])]] (p. 220) as the home of the [[marga Ramin|marga Ramin]], whose eponymous ancestor [[Gedong (Atjeher of Pasei)|Gedong]] — an Atjeher of [[Pasei|Pasei]] — gave rise, by his shipwreck on the way to [[Belawan]] and his encounters with an eel and a buffalo, to the *marga*'s double food-prohibition against eel and buffalo-meat. The legend is recorded by [[W. K. H. Ypes|Ypes]] in his *Bijdrage tot de kennis van stamverwantschap op Sumatra* (p. 57 nt. 1) and is reproduced in *De Karbouw* as one of the Sumatran *pantang*-origin legends explaining the prohibition of buffalo-meat for particular genealogical groups.

## In De Karbouw
The Boven-Singkel passage in *De Karbouw* (p. 220) — the only passage in the book that names the region — reads in full:

> *"Van de [[marga Ramin|marga Ramin]] in Boven-Singkel bestaat de volgende legende. Een Atjeher van [[Pasei|Pasei]], [[Gedong (Atjeher of Pasei)|Gedong]] geheten, die de stamvader heet te zijn van genoemde marga, leed op weg naar [[Belawan|Belawan]] schipbreuk en werd door een paling gevangen, welke hem echter naar land bracht, toen hij de eed had gezworen, nooit paling te zullen eten. Op het strand wachtte hem evenwel een buffel op (of dit een albino-dier was, wordt niet vermeld), welke hem dreigde, weder in zee te zullen stoten, doch hem ongedeerd liet landen, toen hij gezworen had, ook geen buffelvlees te zullen nuttigen. Men zegt, dat de leden van de [[marga Ramin|marga Ramin]] nu nog geen paling of karbouwenvlees mogen eten."*
The passage fixes Boven-Singkel as the home of the [[marga Ramin|marga Ramin]], a patrilineal kinship group (*marga*) whose eponymous ancestor was an Atjeher from [[Pasei|Pasei]] — a provenance that ties the Singkil-area genealogical tradition to the North-Acehnese Pasai cultural sphere. The double prohibition (eel and buffalo-meat) contracted by [[Gedong (Atjeher of Pasei)|Gedong]] during his maritime crisis is presented by *De Karbouw* as one of the Sumatran *pantang*-origin narratives alongside the [[Pantang of the albino buffalo in Atjeh|Atjeh pantang legend]] of the white buffalo licking the corpse-fluid of a fallen child (the legend explaining the prohibition for [[kawōm Dja Sandang|*kawōm* Dja Sandang]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]], the [[Gajoland|Gajoland]] groups of [[Kedjoeroen Boekit]] and [[Petiambang]], the [[Alasland|Alas]] *mergō*'s, and the Malays of [[Atjeh's Westkust]]).

## Historical-geographical context
Boven-Singkel — the upstream country of the Singkel river — lies in the southern part of what is now [[Aceh Singkil Regency|Aceh Singkil Regency]], in the border zone between the [[Acheh|Atjeh]] cultural sphere proper and the Batak country to the south-east. The population of the Singkel area includes Singkil speakers (an Austronesian language closely related to the languages of the adjacent south-Atjeh and north-Tapanuli coasts), and the area has long been a zone of cultural contact between Atjehnese, Batak and Malay influences. The [[marga Ramin|marga Ramin]] documented by [[W. K. H. Ypes|Ypes]] is one of the patrilineal *marga* of this border zone; the legend of [[Gedong (Atjeher of Pasei)|Gedong]] of [[Pasei|Pasei]] gives it an explicitly Atjehnese-Pasai ancestral provenance.


## Significance
Boven-Singkel's significance for the encyclopedia of [[Acheh]] history is as the home of the [[marga Ramin|marga Ramin]], whose ancestral legend ties the Singkil-area genealogical tradition to the [[Pasei|Pasai]] cultural sphere of northern Atjeh, and whose double food-prohibition (against eel and buffalo-meat) is one of the documented Sumatran instances of the wider Atjeh-north-Sumatran pattern in which buffalo-meat avoidance is legitimated by an ancestral encounter. The legend is also significant as a document of the cultural contact between the [[Acheh|Atjeh]] and Batak spheres in the southern Atjeh border zone.
