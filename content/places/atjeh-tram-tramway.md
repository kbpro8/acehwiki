**Atjeh-tram (tramway)** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Tramway line repeatedly attacked: Teungkoe di Mata Ië led aanslagen on it; Pang Nanggròë attacked tramkoelies and -personeel and destroyed the trambaan; Teungkoe di Tjot Tjitjém's band also damaged it.

## Description

Tramway line repeatedly attacked: [[teungkoe|Teungkoe]] di [[Mata Ië]] led aanslagen on it; [[Pang Nanggròë]] attacked tramkoelies and -personeel and destroyed the trambaan; [[Teungkoe di Tjot Tjitjém]]'s band also damaged it.

## Significance

Atjeh-tram (tramway) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
