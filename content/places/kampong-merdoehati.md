**Village Merdoehati** was an Acehnese village located to the north of the rice field (rice field) plain surrounding [[Atjeh's Groote Missigit]].

## Role in the Expeditions
The village and its adjacent northern cemetery served as a crucial bivouac location and defensive flank for the Dutch forces during the [[First Conquest of the Great Missigit]] on [[10 April]] [[1873]]. The [[6e Bataljon Infanterie]] held defensive positions here to cover the Dutch left wing from Acehnese counter-attacks.
