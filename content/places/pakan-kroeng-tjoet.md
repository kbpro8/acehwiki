**Pakan Kroeng Tjoet** was a post within the *[[Geconcentreerde linie|geconcentreerde linie]]* of [[Groot-Atjeh]] from which [[Luitenant Neelmeyer]] set out in the evening of the day he learned of the defection of [[Teukoe Oemar]] in [[March]] [[1896]], in order to warn the garrisons of [[Toengkoeb]] and [[Tjot Rang]].

## Neelmeyer's departure

Hooijer recorded that, on learning of the going-over of the [[Panglima Prang Besar]], Neelmeyer travelled in the evening *in gezelschap van Toekoe Tjoet en eenige van diens volgelingen, van Pakan Kroeng Tjoet in de geconcentreerde linie naar Toengkoeb en Tjot Rang* to warn the garrisons of those posts, placing his life defenceless in the hands of an Achehnese who two days later followed Oemar's example.
