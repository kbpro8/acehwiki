**Saparoea (Saparua)** is an island in the [[Molukken]] archipelago where, on [[17 May]] [[1817]], a Dutch landing division under [[Majoor Beetjes]] attempted to suppress a local uprising. The operation ended in a disastrous defeat, with heavy casualties among Dutch forces, including marines from the frigates [[Evertsen]] and [[Nassan]] (page 92).

## Strategic Importance

The island’s position made it a focal point for Dutch attempts to control the sea lanes and to pacify the “overzeeache” (overseas Aceh) territories.
