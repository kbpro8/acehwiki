A location on or near the West Coast. When [[Teuku Uma]] was given a man-of-war by the Dutch to convey him home: 'At Lam Beusoe one of the ship's boats landed the Teuku and his followers, but as soon as he had withdrawn, his panglimas fell upon the sailors and slew them all except two who escaped to the shore.' Referenced in the sadati dialogue as 'Lambeusoe (Lambesi) under the Keujruen Kuala'; the sadati says he will not go there 'for it is at strife with [[Kuala Unga]].'
