**Harim (mythical place)** is a legendary location referenced in the Malay translation of the *Burda* as a symbol of divine bounty and spiritual reward. The poem mentions “the *Harim*” in verses praising the generosity of the Prophet [[Muhammad]] and the blessings bestowed upon the faithful.

## Literary Role

In the Acehnese rendition, *Harim* functions as a metaphorical realm of abundance, akin to the Qur’anic description of Paradise. Its mention illustrates the integration of Qur’anic imagery into Acehnese devotional poetry.

## Connection to Aceh

The invocation of *Harim* reflects the broader Acehnese engagement with Islamic eschatology, where such mythic places serve to inspire piety among local believers.
