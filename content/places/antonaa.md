**Antonaa (river settlement)** was a small settlement along the interior waterways of [[Acheh]] mentioned in the Dutch naval reports of December. The Dutch detachment that marched overland from [[New Amsterdam]] encountered hostile “negro” forces there, resulting in two enemy deaths and the capture of several boats.

## Strategic Importance
Its location near the [[Cimbia creek]] made it a focal point for Dutch patrols and for rebel activity during the expedition.
