**Serdang** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Oelèë Lheuë]]

In den vroegen morgen van [[22 September]] j.l. trokken velen per auto of extra-trein naar Oelèë Lheuë om getuige te zijn van het vertrek van den afgetreden Gouverneur Swart; the band played the *Wilhelmus*, [[Mevrouw Swart]] received flowers, and the couple went aboard the *s.s. [[Nias]]* by the sloop of H.M. *[[Serdang]]*, saluted by three cheers and the military salute.

### From [[Swart's farewell address (2 September 1918)]]

"In the early morning of 22 September last, many went by car or special train to [[Oelèë Lheuë]] to witness the departure of the retiring Governor Swart, appointed Vice-President of the [[Raad van Nederlandsch-Indië|Council of the Netherlands Indies]]. At about a quarter to eight the retiring Governor arrived by car from [[Koeta Radja]], accompanied by the acting Governor and the Provincial Military Commander. The band played the *Wilhelmus*. His Excellency and Mrs Swart passed along the various groups in the order named above and shook hands in farewell. [[Mevrouw Swart|Mrs Swart]] received flowers, after which the departing couple, accompanied by the committee of escort, took their places with the commander of H.M. *[[Serdang]]* in that vessel's sloop and went aboard the *s.s. [[Nias]]*. As the sloop pulled away a threefold cheer rang out for a good voyage and for the welfare of the former Governor, while the crew of H.M. *Serdang* rendered the military salute from that ship."

### From [[1918]]

He left Atjeh in the early morning of [[22 September]] [[1918]] from [[Oelèë Lheuë]] aboard the *s.s. [[Nias]]*, the band playing the *Wilhelmus* and the crew of H.M. *[[Serdang]]* rendering the military salute; he arrived at [[Batavia]] to take up the Vice-Presidency.
