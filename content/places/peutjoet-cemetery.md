**Kerkhof Peutjoet** was/is a historic Dutch military cemetery located in [[Kuta Raja]] (Banda Aceh), serving as the resting place for over 2,200 colonial soldiers.

## Historical Context and Role

Among those buried at Peutjoet are approximately 950 members of the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]] who fell in combat between [[1890]] and [[1930]]. The cemetery walls bear memorial tablets, and stone memorial busts honor distinguished officers including Captain [[Willem Bernard Johan Alexander Scheepens]], Captain [[Franciscus Darlang]], Lieutenant Vis, and Lieutenant Webb.
