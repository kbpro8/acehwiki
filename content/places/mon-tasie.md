---
title: "Mon Tasie'"
tags: ["place", "aceh-proper"]
---

A place in [[Acheh]]. When Habib Abdurrahman returned to Achehnese soil, 'he assumed a considerable share in the conduct of the war. Establishing himself at Mon Tasie', he undertook several expeditions from that place, and among them one to Krueng Raba.'
