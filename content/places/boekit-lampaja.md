**Boekit Lampaja** (Lampaja hill) was a Dutch fortification in [[Groot-Atjeh]] that was returned to the field at the execution of the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration]]: it was evacuated, in succession with [[Boekit Seboen]], [[Kroeng Raba]], [[Toengkoep]], [[Anak Galoeng]] with [[Boekloe]], [[Senelop]], [[Montassik]] and [[Pakan Badak]] — "al onze troepen begaven zich toen binnen de nieuwe postenlinie, en hiermede was de concentratie voltooid" ("all our troops then went inside the new line of posts, and with this the concentration was completed") — between [[12 February|12]] and [[28 February]] [[1885]], in the second withdrawal series after the evacuation of [[Djerir]] and the other northern posts (see [[Completion of the concentrated position (March 1885)]]).
