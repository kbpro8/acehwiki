**Semarang** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[H. N. A. Swart]]

On [[27 October]] [[1894]] Swart was transferred to the *Subsistentenkader* at [[Semarang]]; on [[29 March]] [[1895]] he was appointed adjutant of the 11th Battalion; on [[11 December]] [[1895]] he was honourably relieved of that post and transferred to the *Subsistentenkader* at [[Batavia]]. On [[23 January]] he was transferred to the IInd Division of the [[Departement van Oorlog]] (Head Bureau of Infantry). By decree no. 13 of [[15 September]] [[1898]] he was awarded 825 guilders for translating into Malay the *Uittreksel uit de Recrutenschool* together with the principal duties and rules of conduct of the soldier and of the group commander in action. He was promoted *Kapitein* on [[24 July]] [[1898]].

### From [[Java]]

[[H. N. A. Swart]] remained obstinately on Java and in the *vredesgarnizoenen* from [[1890]] until his captaincy in [[1898]]; he served at [[Magelang]], [[Gombong]], [[Semarang]] and [[Batavia]]. [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]'s solution of the teacher problem for *[[Volksonderwijs in Atjeh|volksonderwijs]]* took the Javanese *desa* as its model; at the census of [[1930]] Java's literacy rate was 5.5 per cent against Atjeh's 1.1. Banishment to Java was the penalty [[T. Béntara Titeuë]] demanded in [[1913]] and which Dutch law could not impose for slight wounding.

### From [[1894]]

In **[[1894]]** the abuses in [[Lombok]] made intervention unavoidable and the minister of Colonies at last acknowledged: "our sovereignty over a native state lays upon us, with regard to what happens there, a responsibility that we cannot shake off" — the first breach in the [[Non-interventiepolitiek|policy of non-intervention]]. On [[27 October]] [[1894]] [[H. N. A. Swart]] was transferred to the *Subsistentenkader* at [[Semarang]].
