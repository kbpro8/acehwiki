**Blang Mané** (place (on Gajoweg)) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Place where the Gajoweg crosses the Peusangan-rivier, then winds through low hilly land.

## Type

place (on [[Gajoweg (Gayo Road)|Gajoweg]])

## Description

Place where the [[Gajoweg (Gayo Road)|Gajoweg]] crosses the [[Peusangan (river)|Peusangan-rivier]], then winds through low hilly land.

## Significance

Blang Mané is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
