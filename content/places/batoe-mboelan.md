**Batoe Mboelan** (settlement site) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. 'White stone' — site of the first Alasser settlement in the Alaslanden.

## Type

settlement site

## Description

'White stone' — site of the first Alasser settlement in the [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]]. The legendary white stone, said to have fallen from heaven, was said to howl to warn of approaching danger. Stone is gone; the [[September]] [[1936]] earthquake came unannounced.

## Significance

Batoe Mboelan is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
