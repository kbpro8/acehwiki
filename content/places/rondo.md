**Rondo** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Island included in the onderafdeeling Sabang together with Poelau Weh.

## Description

Island included in the [[onderafdeeling]] [[Sabang]] together with [[Poelau Weh]].

## Significance

Rondo is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
