**Tandjoeng Slamat** (modern **Tanjung Slamat**) was a town on the [[Deli-tram|Deli-tramway]] network in the [[Oostkust van Sumatra|East Coast of Sumatra]] residency that, in the [[1913]] debates over the extension of the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]], was proposed as the direct junction between the Atjeh-tram (extended via [[Besitang]]) and the [[Deli-Spoorweg-Maatschappij|Deli-Spoorweg-Maatschappij]]'s Deli-tram network. The direct [[Besitang]]–Tandjoeng Slamat connection was, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, the most ambitious of the proposed Atjeh-tram extensions — and the one most vigorously contested by the [[Deli-Spoorweg-Maatschappij|Deli-Spoorweg-Maatschappij]] and the [[Pangkalan Brandan]] commercial establishment.

## Account from *De Buitenbezittingen* (1917) — Tandjoeng Slamat

The Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account of the proposed [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]] extensions presents [[Tandjoeng Slamat|Tandjoeng Slamat]] as the projected direct junction with the [[Deli-tram|Deli-tramway]]: "a plan for further extension of the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]] network, which was vigorously defended by various authorities, was the line via [[Besitang]] directly to Tandjoeng Slamat on the [[Deli-tram|Deli-tramway]] network, by which the new concession-terrains in the lower [[Langkat|Langkatsche]] ([[Oostkust van Sumatra|O. K. Sumatra]]) would obtain a shorter and less expensive connection with the [[Aroebai|Aroebai]], and an afvoerlijn [export line] would arise that could compete with the [[Deli-Spoorweg-Maatschappij|Deli-net]]."

The Bureau notes that this direct [[Besitang]]–Tandjoeng Slamat connection was deferred — partly because the lower Langkat concession-terrains proved less suitable for European cultivation than expected, and partly because of "the vigorous resistance of the [[Pangkalan Brandan|Pangkalan Brandan]] trade and of the [[Deli-Spoorweg-Maatschappij|Deli-Spoorweg-Maatschappij]]" to the project. In place of the direct connection, a connection from [[Pangkalan Brandan]] to the Atjeh-tram was substituted — a substitution that, the Bureau notes, left open the question of the eventual direct [[Besitang]]–Tandjoeng Slamat junction: "against [the [[Pangkalan Brandan|Pangkalan Brandan]] connection] it is objected that thereby the direct connection [[Besitang]]–Tandjoeng Slamat would be postponed to a still more distant future, which connection yet — in the opinion of many — will in later times prove necessary."

## Significance

[[Tandjoeng Slamat|Tandjoeng Slamat]] is, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, the projected direct junction of the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]] with the [[Deli-tram|Deli-tramway]] — the place at which, in the most ambitious version of the [[1913]] extension plans, the two Sumatran tram networks would have been joined. The deferral of the [[Besitang]]–Tandjoeng Slamat connection marks a strategic defeat for the project to integrate the [[Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh and Dependencies]] residency with the [[Oostkust van Sumatra|East Coast of Sumatra]] residency into a single transport network.
