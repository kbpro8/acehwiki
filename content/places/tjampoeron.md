**Tjampoeron** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Meergebied]]

Consequently the troops took blows. On [[24 December]] [[1909]] a group of infantry returning from [[Samarkilang]] was surprised in its marching bivouac at [[Tjampoeron]]; the European sergeant and brigade commander, six men and four convicts were killed, and seven fusiliers and four convicts wounded. "Around these captured carbines there formed the dangerous Telong band, which kept the Lake District in turmoil for nearly two years."

### From [[Samarkilang]]

Als onze jagers het hem ál te lastig maakten, week hij voor een tijdje uit naar het toen nog vrijwel onbekende gebied van Samarkilang en het Oostelijke Gajoland, [[H. C. Zentgraaff]] wrote. On [[24 December]] [[1909]] a group of infantry returning from Samarkilang was surprised at [[Tjampoeron]], the captured carbines forming the [[Telongbende]]. In [[1911]] *ladangs* and hiding-places of [[Tgk. di Barat]] were cleared up in the Samarkilang country. On [[10 July]] [[1917]] a patrol under the native sergeant [[Toegimin]] found in the forest near Samarkilang an insignificant *ladang* with two houses; two unknown Acehnese were shot, and Acehnese of Samarkilang seeing the body threw themselves to the ground in reverence, crying "Teungkoe, be not angry — it was not our fault." From the Teungkoe's last followers formed the [[Samarkilang-bende]], whose head was captured only in [[1937]].

### From [[The resistance in Atjeh 1908–1912]]

Consequently the Dutch troops took blows. On [[24 December]] [[1909]] a group of infantry returning from [[Samarkilang]] was surprised in its marching bivouac at [[Tjampoeron]]; the European sergeant and brigade commander, six men and four convicts were killed, while seven fusiliers and four convicts were wounded. Around the captured carbines formed the dangerous [[Telongbende]], which kept the Meergebied in commotion for nearly two years.
