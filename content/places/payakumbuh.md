**Payakumbuh** (Dutch: *Pajakombo*) is a town in the [[Padangsche Bovenlanden|Padang Highlands]] of [[Padangsche Bovenlanden|Sumatra's West Coast]] where, in November [[1886]], Governor [[demmeni|Demmeni]] of Atjeh died aged fifty-five shortly after arriving on sick leave, worn out by incessant work and the Atjeh climate.
