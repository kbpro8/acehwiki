**XXII Mukims** (Dua Puluh Dua Mukim) was one of the three traditional federal districts (*Sagi*) of Great Aceh, encompassing the upper interior valley of the Aceh River.

## Role in the War

Ruled by the hereditary *Panglima Sagi* [[Panglima Polim]], the XXII Mukims served as the primary manpower reservoir and strategic stronghold for Acehnese resistance forces. Its mountain warriors (*orang tunong*) spearheaded defenses at the [[Mesjid Raya Aceh]] and [[Kraton of Aceh]].

Major colonial campaigns were launched into the XXII Mukims, culminating in the [[Battle of Selimeum]] in [[July]] [[1879]].
