**Babah Kroeëng Bidin** is a subject named in *[[Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Tgk. di Mata Ië]]

The monthly report for October [[1911]] recorded a scene from the aftermath of the raid on his *ladangs*: "a patrol from the bivouac [[Babah Kroeëng Bidin]] found, in the neighbourhood of the raided dry fields of Tgk. di Mata Ië, a woman with two children who had been wandering there since the raid without having seen any of her own people again and without being able to find the fields. In a wretched condition, living on forest fruits, she had spent about a month and a half there. She was sent to Lho' Seumawè for care."
