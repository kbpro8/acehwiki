**Cambodja** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Mentioned as the western starting point of the great folding-mountain arc.

## Description

Mentioned as the western starting point of the great folding-mountain arc.

## Significance

Cambodja is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
