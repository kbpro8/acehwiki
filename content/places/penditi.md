**Penditi** was a place close by [[Kuta Raja|Kota Radja]] in [[Groot-Atjeh]], known from the history of the [[Aceh War]]. It lay on the route of [[Jacobus Pel|generaal Pel]]'s victorious campaign of December [[1875]] – February [[1876]] through the [[XXV Moekims]], for in his [[Pel's dagorder of 8 February 1876|day order of 8 February 1876]] Pel said to his soldiers: "Den 7en dezer zijt ge met mij in triomf onze hoofdversterking weder binnengerukt over [[Missigit Longbatta|Longbatta]] en Penditi" ("On the 7th of this month you re-entered our chief fortification with me in triumph over Longbatta and Penditi"). At the closing of the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentrated line]] in February [[1885]] the watch at Penditi, close by Kota Radja, was surprised in the middle of [[25 February]] [[1885]] "in the middle of the day" by apparently friendly Atjehers: the Dutch had nine men wounded (three mortally), and the enemy carried off two rifles — one of the first proofs that inside the new position there would be no safety either (see [[Guerilla raids inside the concentration line (1885)]]).
