**Lam Temot-rivier** ("Lam Temot river") was a river crossed by the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]] on a bridge — *Brug over de Lam Temot-rivier (Atjeh-tram)* — illustrated in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's volume *De Buitenbezittingen 1904 tot 1914* as one of the two principal engineering works of the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]] network pictured in the chapter on the Atjeh-tram. The other pictured engineering work is the *berglijn* (mountain line) section of the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]] near the [[Aloer-Pantja-viaduct|Aloer-Pantja viaduct]].

## Account from *De Buitenbezittingen* (1917) — Lam Temot-rivier

The Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account of the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]] includes two illustrative plates of principal engineering works of the line: "Brug over de Lam Temot-rivier ([[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]])" and "Gedeelte der berglijn van de [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]], nabij het [[Aloer-Pantja-viaduct|Aloer-Pantja-viaduct]]." The Lam Temot-rivier bridge is, together with the [[Aloer-Pantja-viaduct|Aloer-Pantja viaduct]] and the berglijn section near it, one of the three principal engineering landmarks of the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]] identified by the Bureau's illustrations.

The bridge over the Lam Temot-rivier belongs to the post-[[1897]] long-line phase of the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]] — the phase that followed the Dutch extension of authority into the [[Atjeh's Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh Dependencies]] after the [[Toekoe Oemar's verraad (1896)|betrayal of Toekoe Oemar in 1896]]. The berglijn section near the [[Aloer-Pantja-viaduct|Aloer-Pantja viaduct]] belongs to the [[1904]]–[[1908]] *berglijntje* from [[Keude Breue]] to [[Seulimeum]] — the connection between the second tramway of the [[Atjeh's Onderhoorigheden|Dependencies]] and the old tramway of [[Groot-Atjeh|Great Atjeh]].

## Significance

[[Lam Temot-rivier|Lam Temot-rivier]] is, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, one of the principal engineering landmarks of the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]] — the river whose crossing by the Atjeh-tram is identified by the Bureau's illustrations as one of the two principal engineering works of the entire network. The bridge over the Lam Temot-rivier is thus part of the material infrastructure of the post-[[1896]] [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacification of Atjeh]].
