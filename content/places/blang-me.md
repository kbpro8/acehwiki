**Blang Mé** — also written *Blang Me* — is a place in the *onderafdeeling* [[Lho Seumawe|Lho' Seumawé]] of the *afdeeling* [[Noordkust van Atjeh|Noordkust van Atjeh]], near the sea, remarkable for its royal graves. Here and there lie the graves of old sultans and princes, of which those at Blang Mé are the best known. Some of them are monumental graves for which marble has been used. A marble tombstone, standing at the head of the grave, bears the mention of names and facts in old script of mysterious characters. Remote in the water-land, this burial-place yet offers a royal rest under the high trees and remains a place full of dedication for the pious of the land.

[[Gendong]] and Blang Mé are old landscapes near the sea belonging to the territory now forming the *onderafdeeling* [[Lho Seumawe|Lho' Seumawé]].

## Account from Atjeh als land voor handel en bedrijf (1925)

**Blang Me** was landscape near [[Lho Seumawe]] belonging old kingdom [[Samoedra]], famous for graves old sultans and princes.

### Description

Here there graves old sultans princes of which in [[Blang Me]] most known. Some praalgraves marble used marble tombstone standing at head grave bearing mention names facts Arabic inscriptions. Remote in waterland offers royal rest under high trees sacred place pious land.

[[Gendong]] and [[Blang Me]] old landscapes near sea belonging territory now underafdeeling [[Lho Seumawe]].
