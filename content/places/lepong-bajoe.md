**Lepong-Bajoe** — written also Lepong Bayoe and Lepong-Baijoe — was an Achehnese kampong lying behind and beside the kampong [[Lam-Lepong]], on the sawah before [[Selimoen]] in the valley of the [[Acheh River|Atjeh-rivier]], spared by the Dutch on [[14 August]] [[1879]] because no resistance was made from it, as recorded in [[W. Cool]] and [[G. B. Hooijer]]'s *[[Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis (Cool & Hooijer, 1889)|Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis]]* ([[1889]]).

## The affair of 14 August and the bivak

When the enemy was seen to withdraw in the direction of Lepong-Bajoe after the storming of Lam-Lepong, the artillery of the [[Lamtoba column (1879)|Gerlach column]] came into battery against it and fired fourteen shells and mortar-throws, of which six — presumably wetted at the debarkation — did not burst. The troops then advanced over the partly still soft sawahs and the narrow *galangans* without further opposition. Since it was half past two in the afternoon, the troops had marched all day under a burning sun, and the guides reckoned the distance left to [[Selimoen]] at three hours, the plan of reaching Selimoen that day was given up; the bivak was laid in the conquered quarter of the kampong, on a fairly open and not too difficult ground at the opposite side. Water for drinking and cooking had to be fetched, apart from one well in the kampong, from a rivulet fifteen to twenty minutes distant in the depth. At seven in the evening the column's rockets were answered from the direction of [[Majoor Lubeck|Lubeck]]'s force, very near, towards Selimoen.

## Aftermath

On the return march of [[18 August]] it was remarked that some inhabitants of Lepong-Baijoe had already returned to their houses; the populace came out of the kampongs peaceably everywhere, cattle already grazed again on the great sawah — a disposition which the authors expressly ascribed to the two-sided lesson: "het verbranden van Lam Lepong, waaruit wij hevig beschoten waren, en het sparen van Lepong-Baijoe, dat zich niet verzette" — the burning of Lam-Lepong, from which the column had been heavily fired upon, and the sparing of Lepong-Bajoe, which had not resisted.
