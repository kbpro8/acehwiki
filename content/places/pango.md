**Pango** was an Atjehnees kampong in the [[III Moekims]] opposite the Dutch post at [[Blang Tjoet]] across the [[Atjeh-rivier]], conquered by the Dutch on [[13 February]] [[1876]] in [[Generaal Pel|Pel]]'s three-colonne operation against the III Moekims and afterwards designated the *uiterste steunpunt* (outermost strongpoint) of the new linie toward [[Kwala Gigieng]]. Its conquest was, however, overshadowed by the [[Atoeh catastrophe (13 February 1876)|Atoeh catastrophe]] of the same day.

## The conquest of 13 February 1876

[[Generaal Pel|Pel]] granted the troepen only a couple of days' rest at [[Kota Radja|K. Radja]] after the first part of his plan had been executed — the entire *Sagie* of the [[XXV Moekims]] was now in Dutch hands — before advancing in *oostelijke richting* with three colonnes against the III Moekims on [[13 February]] [[1876]]. The *rechter-colonne* (the 13de bataljon + part of the Barissans + one section artillery under [[Majoor B. E. Mekern|majoor B. E. Mekern]]) marched *stroomopwaarts* along the left bank of the Atjeh-rivier. The *midden-colonne* (the 12de bataljon + the remainder of the Barissans + two sections artillery under [[Majoor Jeltes|majoor Jeltes]]) marched from [[Kota Alam]] along the right bank against Pango and the enemy-held [[Longbatta-Mesigit]]. The *linker-colonne* (the *rechter halve bataljon* + a section artillery under [[Majoor Diepenheim|majoor Diepenheim]]) was to cover the flank. On [[13 February]] [[1876]] Pango was conquered and a post opgericht.

## The uiterste steunpunt

The same day saw the [[Atoeh catastrophe (13 February 1876)|Atoeh catastrophe]]: [[Jan van Swieten (Kapitein)|kapitein van Swieten]]'s reinforcement detachment was massacred on the drassige *sawah* near [[Lamsajoen]]. Despite the catastrophe Pel pressed on. Meroe, Lampermej and [[Tjap Octoe]] were bemachtigd after *hevigen tegenstand* on [[14 February|14]]–[[15 February]] [[1876]]. At Tjap Octoe the troepen stuitten on a *sterke benteng* with artillery, *krachtig verdedigd*, only taken "met veel moeite en opoffering." The losses were enough to make Pel decide not to push further eastward but to designate Pango as the *uiterste steunpunt* of the new linie toward Kwala Gigieng — judged *voldoende*. From Pango Pel marched northward on [[22 February]] [[1876]] via Lamkapang, Loeëng Iek, Poeklat and Oeleh Karang to the kampong Pinang — found abandoned, "in den rug genomen" — and on to the [[Kroeng Tjoet]] and his death at [[Lamjoeng]] on [[24 February]] [[1876]].
