**Singkel (administrative area)** (administrative district) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Administrative area to which the Banjak-eilanden (Banyak Islands) belong administratively.

## Type

administrative district

## Description

Administrative area to which the [[Banjak-eilanden]] ([[Banjak-eilanden|Banyak Islands]]) belong administratively.

## Significance

Singkel (administrative area) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
