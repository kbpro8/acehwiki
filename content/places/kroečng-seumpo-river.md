**Kroečng Seumpo (river)** (river) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. River at K.M.

## Type

river

## Description

River at [[K.M.]] 18 on the [[Gajoweg (Gayo Road)|Gajoweg]]. Site of the chief of construction during the road-building.

## Significance

Kroečng Seumpo (river) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
