**Lamroekan** was a place in [[Groot-Atjeh]] on which the [[12e Bataljon Infanterie|12e Bataljon]] advanced from [[Lamtengah]] on [[24 May]] [[1896]], the second day of the [[Three-day battle in the Beradin pass (23-25 May 1896)|three-day battle in the Beradin pass]], subsequently occupying [[Lamoedjong]] and [[Lam-Goet|Lamgoe]].
