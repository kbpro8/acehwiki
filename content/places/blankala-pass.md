**Blankala Pass** was a rugged, heavily forested mountain pass and tactical defile crossing the coastal mountain range between Kotta Radja and the inland valley of the [[IV Moekims]] in modern Aceh Province, Indonesia.

## Strategic Crossing in December 1875

The pass served as a natural defensive wall protecting interior Acehnese agricultural settlements from coastal European invasion. On [[26 December]] [[1875]], following the [[Attack on Mibouw]], General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]] ordered column commander [[Lieutenant Colonel Meijer]] to force the pass during [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]].

In the [[Crossing of Blankala Pass]], troops of the [[3rd Infantry Battalion]], [[8th Infantry Battalion]], and [[Marine Corps in Aceh]] hauled artillery across the defile under sniper fire, successfully seizing the crest and descending to occupy the [[IV Moekims]].
