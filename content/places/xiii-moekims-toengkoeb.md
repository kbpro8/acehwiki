**XIII Moekims Toengkoeb** — written in Dutch as *XIII Moekims Toengkoeb* and meaning "Thirteen Mukims of Toengkoeb" — was a region of [[Groot-Atjeh]] comprising thirteen [[moekim|mukims]] situated in the east of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] theatre, of which the territorial chief was [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb]]. As described in Kruisheer's *ATJEH '96*, the XIII Moekims Toengkoeb contained the kampong of [[Toengkoeb]] (the seat of its chief), the kampong and river of [[Kroeng-Kali]], and the fortification of Batoe west of Toengkoeb. The region was the theatre of the [[Demonstration in the XIII Moekims Toengkoeb (30 April 1896)|expedition of 30 April 1896]] and the [[Second Expedition to XIII Moekims Toengkoeb (4 May 1896)|second expedition of 4 May 1896]], and was crossed by the maréchaussée march through [[Glé Taroempas]] on [[22 June]] [[1896]]. The Achehnese *stelling* east of [[Kroeng-Kali]] partly belonged to this region.

## Description and Location

The XIII Moekims Toengkoeb lay to the east of the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] and adjoined the [[V Moekims Montassik]] on the south. The kampong of [[Toengkoeb]] was the residence of the head of the XIII Moekims and the site of a Dutch temporary post (evacuated on [[21 April]] [[1896]] and handed over to [[Toekoe Nja Banta]]). The kampong of [[Kroeng-Kali]] contained the house of the *hoeloebalang* [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb]] and a series of fortifications, and was the site of the principal Achehnese *stelling* east of Kroeng-Kali that partly belonged to this mukim. The *benteng* of Batoe lay approximately 400 metres west of the crossroads near Toengkoeb. The region was bounded on the east by the [[Oostergebergte]] (eastern mountain range) at whose edge [[Habib Lengkong]]'s fort stood, and was crossed by the mountain pass of [[Glé Taroempas]] that gave access to the [[Kloof van Beradin|valley of Beradin]].

## Historical Background

The XIII Moekims Toengkoeb had been under the authority of [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb]] throughout the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. After the [[Dutch]] occupation of [[Toengkoeb]] in the [[buitenlinie]] period the religious-warriors of [[Teungkoe di Tiro|Teungkoe di Tiroe]] had used the XIII Moekims as a base from which to threaten the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]]. The [[benteng|bentengs]] Perampoean and Batoe near Toengkoeb had been built to defend the approaches to the region. After the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (29 March 1896)|defection of 29 March 1896]] [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb]] aligned himself with Umar and the XIII Moekims became a base for hostile *benden* operating against the [[buitenpost|buitenposten]] of the eastern *linie*. The Achehnese *stelling* east of [[Kroeng-Kali]] had been built up over years and was a complex of old and new *versterkingen* and *loopgraven*.

## Role in the 1896 Operations

The XIII Moekims Toengkoeb were the object of two major Dutch expeditions in [[1896]]. On [[30 April]] [[1896]] a [[Demonstration in the XIII Moekims Toengkoeb (30 April 1896)|column]] from [[Boekit Karang]] under [[Majoor J. F. Breijer|Majoor Breijer]] moved against the *benteng* of Batoe near Toengkoeb, supported by a night attack from [[Pakan Kroeng Tjoet]]. On [[4 May]] [[1896]] a [[Second Expedition to XIII Moekims Toengkoeb (4 May 1896)|second expedition]] under [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] advanced against the *stelling* east of [[Kroeng-Kali]], where 1e luit. Krohne was mortally wounded at the four-way intersection and the cavalerie conducted a charge. On [[28 April]]–[[29 April]] [[1896]] the [[Night Surprise of Benting Batoe (28–29 April 1896)|night surprise of Benting Batoe]] was conducted within this region by a column from [[Pakan Kroeng Tjoet]] under Captain Zelle's engineers. On [[17 June]] [[1896]] the *benteng* of [[Kroeng-Kali]] (a square redoubt of 15 metres side, walls 1.5 metres high, with a *lilla*) was stormed by compagnie Linck of 12e Bataljon, with 2nd Lt. Heyligers first inside; the *benteng* was destroyed. On [[20 June]] [[1896]] the [[benteng|bentengs]] Perampoean and Batoe near Toengkoeb were demolished by the [[Genie]] under Captain Zelle. On [[22 June]] [[1896]] a maréchaussée column under [[J. W. C. Vuijkk|Luitenant Vuijkk]] crossed [[Glé Taroempas]] and descended into the [[Kloof van Beradin|valley of Beradin]] from the east. On [[23 June]] [[1896]] [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb]] and Panglima Nja Makam were attacked at [[Kroeng-Kali]] and retreated to [[Lam-Tobah]].

## Significance

The XIII Moekims Toengkoeb were the principal eastern bastion of Achehnese resistance after the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (29 March 1896)|defection of 29 March 1896]]. The repeated Dutch expeditions against [[Kroeng-Kali]] and the *benteng* of Batoe demonstrated that the Dutch could not leave this region unmolested, since it threatened the eastern flank of their operations against Umar and provided a refuge for retreating Achehnese *benden*. The demolition of the [[benteng|bentengs]] Perampoean and Batoe on [[20 June]] [[1896]] and the storming of the *benteng* of [[Kroeng-Kali]] on [[23 June]] [[1896]] broke the back of organised Achehnese defence in this mukim.

## Account in Hooijer's *De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh* ([[1897]])

The **XIII Moekims Toengkoeb** were the thirteen *[[Mukim|moekims]]* of [[Toengkoeb]] within the [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]], the territory of the *[[Oeleebalang|oeleebalang]]* [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb]], brother-in-law of [[Toekoe Nja Banta]].

## In the campaign of 1893

When the [[Civiel en Militair Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Governor of Atjeh and Dependencies]] authorised [[Toekoe Nja Banta]] on [[1 September]] [[1893]] to open the struggle against the *[[Moslemin]]* east of the Dutch line, Hooijer recorded that his father-in-law [[T. Nek van Marassa|Toekoe Neq van Marassa]] and his brother-in-law *Toekoe Tjoet, oeleebalang der XIII Moekims Toengkoeb*, were to assist him.

The district was the scene of some of the most damaging failures of the alliance. Toekoe Tjoet's wavering conduct led the Dutch to occupy one of his *bentengs* at [[Toengkoeb]] on [[27 September]] [[1893]]; his banishment to [[Malaboeh]] emptied the *kampongs* (villages), since the population would not remain without him; and the removal of his wives to [[Lamreng]] by [[Toekoe Nja Banta]] caused a general dispersal that let the *Moslemin* re-occupy the ground and cut communications between [[Kroeng Kali]] and [[Toengkoeb]].
