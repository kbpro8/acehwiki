A gampong or location within [[Acheh]]. The chief of Eunjong bears the title of La'seumana. In the sadati kisah: 'The La'seumana (the Chief of Eunjong) is a fatherless child; he rules the XXII Mukims.' In the sadati dialogue: 'let us go and dwell at Eunjong in the house of the La'seumana. I will not dwell at Eunjong, the gampong there is full of holes (and thus muddy) and there are too many bangka-trees.' Ateue' (the pottery gampongs) A group of gampongs near the capital formerly noted for pottery. 'In the group of gampongs called Ateue', formerly noted for its pottery, the manufacture is still carried on by a number of women, but their products are being gradually driven out of the market by foreign goods.' The art displayed in this native pottery possesses no great merit, though formerly flourishing potteries produced pots, pans, plates, lamps, etc. for household use.

A gampong in [[Acheh]] whose chief bears the title of La'seumana. Already described above. In the [[Hikayat Pochut Muhamat]], the Achehnese embassy to [[Riouw]] and [[Singapore]] included a member who was Panglima Tibang of Eunjong.
