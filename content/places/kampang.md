**Kampang** is a place on the coast of [[Acheh|Atjeh]] named in the Dutch pamphlet *[[Beschouwingen over Atjeh en onzen oorlog met dat rijk|Beschouwingen over Atjeh en onzen oorlog met dat rijk]]* by [[Een Ooggetuige|Een Ooggetuige]] ([[1880s]]) in the course of the author's discussion of the Atjehnese coast, the pepper trade, and the sea approaches to the Atjehnese interior. The place is named in the damaged text of the pamphlet alongside other coastal places, and is discussed in connection with the pepper country of the Atjehnese coast.

## The pamphlet's reference

The author of the *[[Beschouwingen over Atjeh en onzen oorlog met dat rijk|Beschouwingen]]* names Kampang in the context of a discussion of the coastal places of Atjeh at which the pepper trade was carried on, and at which the author held that coastal lights (*kustlichten*) and better facilities were needed for the safety of world shipping. The place is one of several named in the pamphlet — alongside [[Panton Laboe|Panton Laboe]] and others — as points on the Atjehnese coast whose development was, in the author's view, central to the settlement of the country and to the development of its trade.

## Significance

Kampang is significant within the AcehWiki corpus as a coastal place of Atjeh named in the contemporary Dutch pamphlet literature of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh War]]. The place's mention in the *[[Beschouwingen over Atjeh en onzen oorlog met dat rijk|Beschouwingen]]* is a document of the geography of the Atjehnese coast as it was known to Dutch observers in the [[1880s]].

*Note on the source text.* The surviving copy of the pamphlet was digitised and processed through OCR (Nemotron-OCR v2). The OCR output is badly damaged throughout. The place-name Kampang is legible in the text, but the surrounding context is partially illegible. The article above reports only what can be read with confidence from the legible passages.
