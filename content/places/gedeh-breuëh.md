**Gedeh-Breuëh** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Railway section between Seulimeum and the Noordkust; the missing bergspoor between Seulimeum and Gedeh-Breuëh was started in 1903 and delivered early 1908.

## Description

Railway section between [[Seulimeum]] and the [[Noordkust]]; the missing bergspoor between [[Seulimeum]] and Gedeh-Breuëh was started in [[1903]] and delivered early [[1908]].

## Significance

Gedeh-Breuëh is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
