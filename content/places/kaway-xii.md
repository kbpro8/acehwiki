**Kaway XII** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Federation of 12 small landschappen between Pidië plain and the strand-landschappen of Westkust; historic gold-winning area.

## Description

Federation of 12 small landschappen between [[Pidië]] plain and the strand-landschappen of [[Westkust]]; historic gold-winning area.

## Significance

Kaway XII is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
