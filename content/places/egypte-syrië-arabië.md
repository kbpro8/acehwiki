**Egypte, Syrië, Arabië** (regions (source of golden age scholars)) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Regions from which scholars and artists came to settle in Atjeh during the golden age (1550-1650), producing translations, encyclopedic works, and fiqh books.

## Type

regions (source of golden age scholars)

## Description

Regions from which scholars and artists came to settle in Atjeh during the golden age ([[1550]]-[[1650]]), producing translations, encyclopedic works, and fiqh books.

## Significance

Egypte, Syrië, Arabië is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
