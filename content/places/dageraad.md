**Dageraad** was a detention facility near the Dutch headquarters where captured **rebels** were held. On **[[26 January]]**, a group of **53 rebels** were sentenced to death, **15** were burned, **16** were beheaded, and **22** were hanged. The camp also housed **800** prisoners at one point.

## Function
The camp served as the central location for judicial processing of insurgents during the **[[1760s]]** campaign.
