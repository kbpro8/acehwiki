**Lawé Oenan** (town) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Reached on the third day's march from Blang Kedjerën toward Alaslanden; cars take traveler onward to Koeta Tjané.

## Type

town

## Description

Reached on the third day's [[March|march]] from [[Blang Kedjerën]] toward [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]]; cars take traveler onward to [[Koeta Tjané]].

## Significance

Lawé Oenan is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
