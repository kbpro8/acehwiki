---
title: Lam Gut
tags: ["place", "aceh-proper"]
---

A gampong from which the learned teacher [[Teungku di Lam Gut]] (proper name Jalaluddin) took his title. He was a pupil of the Malay [[Abduggamad]] and became not only a popular teacher but also kali of the XXVI Mukims. His son inherited his title and dignity but transferred his duties to his son-in-law [[Cheh Marahaban]]. The grandson of the old [[Teungku di Lam Gut]] survives as kali in name only but is consulted by none. In a footnote, Snouck mentions that [[Mohammad Zain bin Jalaluddin]] from [[Acheh]] wrote a Malay treatise on Mohammedan marriage law lithographed at [[Constantinople]] in A.H. 1304, and likely connects with the [[Teungku di Lam Gut]] who in A.H. 1242 wrote the Tambih rapilin (a book of admonition). The cloth pattern 'ija Lam Gugob' also mentions nearby Lam Gugob.
