**Djoelo** (region) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Region (with Tamiang) whose inhabitants are stereotyped as skilled with poison.

## Type

region

## Description

Region (with [[Tamiang]]) whose inhabitants are stereotyped as skilled with poison.

## Significance

Djoelo is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
