**Lam Baró** — modern *Lam Baro* — is a halt on the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]] line near [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]]. Cattle from [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]], the centre of which is [[Seulimeum|Seulimeum]], goes by tram to [[Sabang|Sabang]] and to [[Medan|Medan]]. For Medan the cattle are usually loaded at Lam Baró, and the tram then brings them to [[Besitang|Besitang]]. From there the cattle are led by road to Medan, because a longer train-journey would be harmful to the animals.
