---
title: Lam Bada
tags: ["place", "aceh-proper"]

In the [[Hikayat Prang Gompeuni]], [[Teuku Asan]] fixed his head-quarters in the neighbourhood of Lam Bada, 'the place of his birth,' after gathering some panglimas and a small force.
