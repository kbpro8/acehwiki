**Lho-Soekon / Lho' Soekon** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Place on the Northkust; bivak Lho' Soekon bombarded by Pang Nanggròë.

## Description

Place on the Northkust; [[Bivak|bivak]] [[Lho'Soekon|Lho' Soekon]] bombarded by [[Pang Nanggròë]]. Place of origin of [[Tgk. di Barat|Teungkoe di Barat]] and [[Pang Nanggròë]] (Lho' Soekonsche). One of two places (with [[Sigli]]) with most [[Atjeh-moorden|atjeh-moorden]]. [[1933]] [[Atjeh-moord|atjeh-moord]] on Captain [[Schmidt]] here.

## Significance

Lho-Soekon / Lho' Soekon is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
