**Peuniti** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Koeta Radja]]

The town also housed the [[Atjehclub]], in whose reading room hung the portraits of all the Atjeh generals, and the [[Oranjehotel]], whose manager [[Hense]], a former *marechaussée* sub-officer, was murdered on [[12 February]] [[1919]]. In the former military encampment at [[Peuniti]] a native trade school was established.

### From [[Swart's farewell address (2 September 1918)]]

The same correspondent pointed to the care for education, the great success of the *Hollandsch-Inlandsche* schools and the native trade school in the former military encampment at [[Peuniti]], and concluded: "All these are things of which a former generation could not even form a conception. It is the Spring, in which the swelling buds burst open with a force of growth no longer to be restrained; the awakening to new life of a portion of mankind that slept for centuries, gathering the latent strength which comes irresistibly to expression more and more. In the musty offices one does not see that — and that is the great danger."

### From [[Volksonderwijs in Atjeh]]

An agricultural school was opened at [[Pedir]] on [[12 February]] [[1914]]: "although the population does not yet fully grasp the significance of an agricultural school, it understands enough to see that an attempt is being made here to raise the level of agriculture." The Atjeh correspondent of the *[[Nieuwe Courant]]* also noted in [[1918]] the great success of the *Hollandsch-Inlandsche* schools and the native trade school in the former military encampment at [[Peuniti]]. The younger generation of chiefs was trained at [[Bandoeng]] or [[Fort de Kock]].
