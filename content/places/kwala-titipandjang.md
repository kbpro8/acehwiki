**Kwala Titipandjang** was the site of the new Dutch fort built on [[17 February]] [[1875]] opposite the kampong Tiban, in order to better cover the banks of the Kroeng Broek and prevent the line being broken on the [[Moesapi]] side. Its construction was part of [[Generaal Pel|kolonel Pel]]'s effort to close the eastern arc of the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]] after the conquests of [[Mandarsa Poeti]] (28 January 1875), [[Lamara Olejlo]] (14–15 February 1875), and [[Lampriet]] (4 February 1875) had extended the southern and middle arcs.

## The strategic problem

The completion of the continuous line around [[Kota Radja|K. Radja]] in May [[1875]] required closing the eastern arc as well as the southern and western arcs. The eastern arc ran from [[Blang Tjoet]] through [[Kota Alam]], [[Lemboe]] O. en N.O., [[Lampriet]], [[Langkroek]] O., [[Lamara]] Z.O. en N.O., [[Tiban]] to [[Moesapi]]. The weak point of this line was the *Kroeng Broek* — a stream or watercourse along which the enemy could move unseen and break the line on the Moesapi side. To prevent this, the new fort at Kwala Titipandjang was built opposite the kampong Tiban, whose guns could enfilade the Kroeng Broek's banks and whose garrison could sally against any attempt to *verschansen* (entrench) along the stream.

## Construction and function

The new fort at Kwala Titipandjang was built on [[17 February]] [[1875]] as a permanent post, with the typical Dutch Atjeh-war layout: earthen parapet, *schanskorven* (gabions), bamboo-doeri entanglements, and a small garrison of infantry with an artillery section. From the fort the Dutch could observe the kampong Tiban across the Kroeng Broek and prevent the enemy from crossing. The construction was, Borel notes, the natural complement of the [[Kota Radja Bedil|K. R. Bedil]] fort at the downstream end of the [[Pedir-dijk]] and of [[Moesapi]] at the north-eastern corner of the stelling. With Kwala Titipandjang in place, the eastern arc of the stelling-Pel was effectively closed; the enemy could no longer break the line on the Moesapi side. The new post at [[Kwala Gigieng]] and the island post of [[Kota Pohama]] in the Gigieng lagune, established in February [[1876]] by [[Luitenant-kolonel Engel|Engel]], were the seaward extensions of the same defensive logic.
