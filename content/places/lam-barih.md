**Lam Barih** — also written *Lambarih* — was a *kampong* (village) and Dutch temporary post on the existing road from [[Lambaroe]] to [[Anagaloeng]] in the [[XXII Moekims]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]], taken by [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]] after seven days of fighting in [[November]] [[1893]] and occupied by Dutch troops on [[30 November]] [[1893]] at his repeated insistence.

## Capture and occupation

When [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]] was set the new task of attacking that part of the [[XXII Moekims]] which fringed the Dutch concentrated position on the south-east and bordered the now cleared [[Sagi der XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]], the resistance he met far exceeded that in the XXVI Moekims. Only after seven days of fighting and considerable losses could he make himself master of Lam Barih, *op een kwartiertje afstand van de geconcentreerde linie* — a quarter of an hour's distance from the *[[Geconcentreerde linie|geconcentreerde linie]]*. At his repeated urging a temporary post was occupied there by the Dutch on [[30 November]] [[1893]], on the existing road from [[Lambaroe]] to [[Anagaloeng]].

## Description

The position lay at the edge of the *kampong* chain through which the [[Acheh River|Atjeh-rivier]] rolls its yellow waters. High watch-houses permitted a view over the wide rice-fields of [[Lamkrak]], at which was directed the 12 cm. gun visible on the left of the plate; luxuriant trees shaded the officers' and soldiers' quarters raised of bamboo and *atap* (palm-thatch). Several of those trees were felled to make the fire beneath the enormous cooking-kettles, thereby saving, to the soldiers' benefit, the money that would otherwise have been booked to the mess account for firewood.

The traffic along the road, just visible at the right of the plate, gave variety to life at the post near [[Lambaroe]] and prevented the garrison from feeling so lonely and forsaken as elsewhere: *men leefde er als op een soort van boerderij* — they lived there as on a sort of farm.

## 1895–1896

Forty men were taken from Lam Barih and [[Lam Soet]] by the *liniecommandant* (commander of the line) on [[7 March]] [[1896]] as he hurried from [[Senelop]] to the relief of [[Van Blokland|Kapitein Blokland]]'s mauled patrol at [[Anagaloeng]]. Hooijer also recorded that [[Toekoe Moesa Anaq Batee]] attacked the *[[Passer|passer]]*-goers between Lambarih and [[Anagaloeng]] and penetrated the [[IX Moekims]]. The post was relieved and lifted on [[18 April]] [[1896]] by the columns that simultaneously relieved [[Anagaloeng]] and [[Senelop]], after the surrounding population had ranged itself on the side of [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Oemar]], who had gone over to the *[[Moslemin]]*, and after the *[[Blokhuis|blokhuizen]]* in the neighbourhood had been burnt by the Dutch.
