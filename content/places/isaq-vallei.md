**Isaq-vallei** (valley) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Valley between Takengon and Blang Kedjerën along the paardenpad; has large Gouvernements Hars- en Terpentijabedrijf complexes in exploitation.

## Type

valley

## Description

Valley between [[Takengon]] and [[Blang Kedjerën]] along the paardenpad; has large [[Gouvernements Hars- en Terpentijabedrijf]] complexes in exploitation.

## Significance

Isaq-vallei is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
