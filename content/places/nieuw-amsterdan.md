**Nieuw‑Amsterdan** (now **Paramaribo**) was the capital of the Dutch colony where **[[Kolonel De Salve|Colonel De Salve]]** initially operated his expedition from. The Dutch fleet anchored there before establishing a fortified headquarters on the **Wikkie‑kreek**.

## Colonial Context
The city functioned as the administrative centre for the Dutch West India Company’s operations in the region during the **[[1760s]]**.
