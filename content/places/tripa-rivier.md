**Tripa-rivier** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. River whose upper basin forms the vlakte (plain) of Gajo-Loeüs.

## Description

River whose upper basin forms the vlakte (plain) of [[Gajo-Loeüs]].

## Significance

[[Tripa (river)|Tripa-rivier]] is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
