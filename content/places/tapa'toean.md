**Tapa'Toean** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Onderafdeeling on Westkust; nilam oil factory started 1919 with government help; kapok, kruidnagel, foelie, nootmuskaat gardens imported from Sumatra's Westkust by Malay colonists; one of three weefschooltjes located here; salt-coconut copra coöperation.

## Description

[[onderafdeeling|Onderafdeeling]] on [[Westkust]]; [[Nilam|nilam]] oil factory started [[1919]] with government help; [[Kapok Fiber|kapok]], [[Cloves (Aceh)|kruidnagel]], [[Mace (Foelie)|foelie]], [[Nutmeg (Aceh)|nootmuskaat]] gardens imported from [[Sumatra's Westkust]] by Malay colonists; one of three weefschooltjes located here; salt-coconut [[Copra|copra]]coöperation.[[Westkust]] that received a military post (first commander Captain H. [[Hendrikus Colijn|Colijn]]) during the hunt for [[Teukoe Oemar]].

## Significance

Tapa'Toean is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
