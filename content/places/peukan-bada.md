---
title: Peukan Bada
tags: ["place", "aceh-proper"]
---

A coastal location in [[Acheh]]. The Dutch succeeded in defeating and slaying [[Teuku Lam Nga]] near Peukan Bada. [[Teuku Uma]] also returned from the West Coast and 'began to give trouble to the Gompeuni at Peukan Bada.'
