In the [[Hikayat Gumba Meuih]]: 'Gumbo' Meuih (Goldenhead) is the daughter of king Hamsoykasa, who rules in the country of Gulitan Sagob (in [[Sumatra]], according to the Achehnese).' This fictional country is the setting for the entire romance. [[Atrah]] (= territory of Shah Kubat, in [[Hikayat Gumba Meuih]]) In the [[Hikayat Gumba Meuih]]: Mira' Diwangga (Goldenhead's son) marries a princess from the kingdom of [[Atrah]] (the territory of Shah Kubat) named Cheureupu Intan (Diamond Sandal). [[Irandamin]] (= Iran Zemin, in [[Hikayat Cham Nadiman]]) In the [[Hikayat Cham Nadiman]]: 'Prince Cham Nadiman, son of Meunua Jho (from the Malay benua [[Johor]], the country of [[Johor]]), king of [[Irandamin]] (i.e. Iran zemin).' This is a fictional kingdom whose name is drawn from the Persian 'Iran zemin' (land of Iran). The story ends: 'finally they all go away to [[Irandamin]], the country of the hero's birth.'
