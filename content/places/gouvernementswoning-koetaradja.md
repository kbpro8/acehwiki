**Gouvernementswoning (Koetaradja)** (building) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Styleless and pretentious building on the site of the old Dalem (Sultan's palace) at Koetaradja.

## Type

building

## Description

Styleless and pretentious building on the site of the old [[Dalem]] ([[sultan|Sultan]]'s palace) at [[Koetaradja]]. Only acceptable as a piece of history.

## Significance

Gouvernementswoning (Koetaradja) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
