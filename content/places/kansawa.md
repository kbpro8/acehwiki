**Kansawa** refers to a lake described in Acehnese texts as containing the “semen of *Zarathushtra*”, a reference to Iranian eschatology. The lake is mentioned on page [[91]] of the *Burda* translation.

## Description
According to the source, the lake’s waters were believed to be the source of the future savior *Saoshyant* in Zoroastrian tradition, a motif that was re‑interpreted in Acehnese Islamic literature.

## Cultural Context
The inclusion of *Kansawa* illustrates the syncretic reception of pre‑Islamic Persian ideas within Acehnese scholarly circles.
