**Vredespark** (Peace park) was one of the two parks laid out at [[Koeta-Radja|Koeta Radja]] in the years of the definitive peace, bearing with its counterpart, the *Oorlogspark* (War park), *zeer eigenaardig respectievelijk* (very characteristically) its name. In November [[1913]] [[W. Moens|Moens]], comparing the city with the fortified place he had known in 1893–1894, recorded that the old walls — including the wall of the old [[Kraton Atjeh|kraton]] — had disappeared, that broad roads had been made, and that the two parks had been laid out; *waarvan vooral het laatste werkelijk smaakvol is aangelegd* — of which especially the last, the Vredespark, was really tastefully arranged, and was accordingly much visited, European nannies with perambulators walking there. A modern hotel stood near the Vredespark. The pair of parks, War and Peace, marked in the plan of the city the transition from the *[[Geconcentreerde linie|concentrated stronghold]]* of the war years to the open capital of the pacified province.
