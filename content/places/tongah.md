**Tongah** was the kampong on the *linker-oever* (left bank) of the [[Kroeng Tjoet]] reached by [[Generaal Pel|Pel]]'s colonne on [[23 February]] [[1876]] on the march northward from [[Pango]] toward the [[Kroeng Raya]] mouth and the sea. The bivouac was pitched at [[Lamjoeng]] between Tongah and Silang on the far side of the river, where Pel died in the night of [[24 February]] [[1876]].

## The march northward

On [[22 February]] [[1876]] Pel marched from Pango northward via Lamkapang, Loeëng Iek, Poeklat and Oeleh Karang to the kampong Pinang, which was found abandoned, "in den rug genomen" — taken in the rear — as were all the earlier enemy versterkingen there. The troepen bivouaked that night at the Mesigit te Kajoe Adang. On [[23 February]] [[1876]] the Langgoegoeb was doorgetrokken and Tongah on the *linker-oever* of the Kroeng Tjoet was bereikt. From Tongah the colonne crossed to the far side of the river, where the bivouac was pitched at Lamjoeng between Tongah and Silang. The march had been a continuous forward movement against an enemy who, after the [[Pel's offensive against the VI, IV and IX Moekims (1875–1876)|winter offensive]] had broken his main line, could no longer stand in the field. Each enemy stelling was found abandoned as the Dutch approached.

## Pel's death at Lamjoeng

At the bivouac at Lamjoeng, on the far side of the Kroeng Tjoet between Tongah and Silang, Pel in the night of [[24 February]] [[1876]] suddenly overleed at the gevolgen of a *slagaderbreuk* (ruptured artery). Borel's elegy is one of the most quoted passages of the book: "Het vaderland verloor in hem een zijner trouwste, ijverigste en meest bekwame dienaren; dat verlies was in de gegeven omstandigheden bijna onherstelbaar en kon eene ware nationale ramp genoemd worden" — the fatherland lost in him one of its most loyal, zealous and most able servants; that loss was, in the circumstances, almost irreparable and could be called a true national disaster. Pel's plan remained geheim — he had not communicated it even to his *chef van den staf* — and nothing about it was found in his archive. [[Luitenant-kolonel Engel|Lt.-kol. Engel]], who had arrived at Lamjoeng with the 9de bataljon to versterk the ageerende troepen, assumed tijdelijk oppergezag as oudste hoofdofficier and continued the march via Tjade to [[Kwala Gigieng]], bereikt on [[26 February]] [[1876]].
