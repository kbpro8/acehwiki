**Lammeulo** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Place where on 24 December 1902 the Pretendent-Sultan's favorite Potjoet di Tjot Moerong and his son Toeankoe Ibrahim were arrested.

## Description

Place where on [[24 December]] [[1902]] the [[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]]'s favorite [[Potjoet]] di Tjot Moerong and his son [[Toeankoe Ibrahim]] were arrested.

## Significance

Lammeulo is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
