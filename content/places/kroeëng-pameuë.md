**Kroeëng Pameuë** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. River basin in the tertiair gronden of Aceh.

## Description

River basin in the tertiair gronden of Aceh.

## Significance

Kroeëng Pameuë is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
