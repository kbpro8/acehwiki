**Lamngani** (also spelled *Lamgani*) was an Acehnese fortified position (*benting*) in the XXII Moekims at which future Governor [[demmeni|Demmeni]], then a lieutenant-colonel commanding a column during [[General van der Heijden|General van der Heyden]]'s offensive in [[1879]], personally distinguished himself during its storming. Brooshooft cites this action as one of the feats that earned Demmeni promotion and eventually the governorship of Atjeh.
