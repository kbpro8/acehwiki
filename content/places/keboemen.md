**Keboemen** was a *regentschap* (regency) of [[Java]] in which [[Oey Djoen Hoat]] investigated the occurrence of *[[Xerophthalmie|xerophthalmie]]*, and whose figures Dr. [[C. E. Gomperts]] set beside his own from [[Groot-Atjeh]] as a comparison strongly in Java's favour.

## The comparison

Oey Djoen Hoat found in the *Regentschap* Keboemen 88 cases of xerophthalmia among 4,532 boys examined and 20 among 3,595 girls — about 2 per cent and ½ per cent of the children respectively.

Gomperts remarked that these values "steken zeer gunstig af bij onze statistiek" — compare very favourably with our statistics. His own survey of [[1937]] found 459 cases among 11,400 children in Groot-Atjeh, about 4 per cent, rising to 7½ per cent among boys aged one to two.

The comparison belongs with Gomperts's wider observation, made in connection with the age and sex distribution of severe cases reported by [[Sie Boen Lian]], that "de verhoudingen schijnen dus in Groot-Atjeh anders te zijn dan op Java" — the proportions therefore appear to be different in Groot-Atjeh than on Java.
