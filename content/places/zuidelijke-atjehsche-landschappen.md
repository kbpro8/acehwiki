**Zuidelijke Atjehsche Landschappen** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Group of native polities in southern Aceh where verzet flared up in 1926-1927, discouraging N.V.

## Description

Group of native polities in southern Aceh where verzet flared up in [[1926]]-[[1927]], discouraging N.V. [[Handelsvereeniging Amsterdam]] from developing lands in [[Malaboeh|Meulaboh]] and [[Singkel]].[[onderafdeeling|Onderafdeeling]] on [[Westkust]]; [[Kapok Fiber|kapok]] centre; contains [[landschap|Landschap]] [[Kloeët]] with unique semi-wild [[karbouw]]herds.[[onderafdeeling]] formed during the [[1925]]-[[1927]] troubles by combining the southern part of [[Tapa'Toean]] with the [[landschap]] [[Troemon]] (formerly under [[Singkel]]). Still under a [[kapitein-fungeerend-controleur]].

## Significance

Zuidelijke Atjehsche Landschappen is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
