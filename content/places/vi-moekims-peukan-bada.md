The **VI Moekims Peukan Bada** was the federation of six *[[Moekim|moekims]]* around [[Peukan Bada]] in [[Groot-Atjeh]], cited by [[D. W. N. de Boer]] for a distinctive local term in the law of security over land.

## The local term for mortgage

De Boer recorded that the *Inlandsche hypotheek* (indigenous mortgage) was encountered in judicial practice both in [[Sigli]] and in Groot-Atjeh, though scarcely any longer under the old proper Atjehnese names — *beunda* in Sigli, *boengkaj* in Groot-Atjeh — being designated instead by the *malajo-hollandisme* (Malay-Dutch hybrid word) *[[Borok|borok]]* (from Dutch *borg*, surety), with the verb *memperborok*; or, in Groot-Atjeh and specifically in the VI moekims Peukan Bada, by the Malayised Atjehnese form *memperboengkajkan*.

## Significance

The survival of *memperboengkajkan* in this one district illustrates the uneven displacement of Atjehnese legal vocabulary by Malay and Malayo-Dutch hybrids in the early twentieth century — a process De Boer treated as evidence of the recency of the institutions concerned.
