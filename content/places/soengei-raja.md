**Soengei Raja** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. BPM oil concession in onderafdeeling Langsa; borings suspended for ~6 years.

## Description

BPM oil concession in [[onderafdeeling]] [[Langsar|Langsa]]; borings suspended for ~6 years.

## Significance

Soengei Raja is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
