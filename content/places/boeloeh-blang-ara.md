**Boeloeh Blang Ara** is the estate of the [[Handelsvereeniging Amsterdam|Handelsvereeniging Amsterdam]] (H.V.A.) in the *onderafdeeling* [[Lho Seumawe|Lho' Seumawé]] of the *afdeeling* [[Noordkust van Atjeh|Noordkust van Atjeh]], opened in [[1920]] and given up as failed in [[1922]] after the H.V.A. had spent eight tons of gold on it. The estate lies a short distance west of Lho' Seumawé on a narrow, bumpy road, and is one of the great disappointments of the post-war planting-period in Atjeh. The H.V.A. had planted 240 *bouw* of coffee and 330 *bouw* of rubber, and had built all the buildings properly. The failure is, in Broersma's judgement, ultimately the H.V.A.'s own fault: the soil was full of large stones and the rainfall was below 2.000 mm a year — facts that should have been known before the planting was begun. After the H.V.A.'s retreat the land was given back to the *landschap*, and new applicants had already come to look, attracted especially by the presence of the gardens and buildings. The land-grant was, as Broersma learned, already re-issued.
