**Kajoe‑leh** was a kampong in the interior of [[Acheh]] that was occupied by Dutch forces on [[5 February]] [[1876]]. The settlement lay amid extensive sawahs and served as a forward base for further advances toward the [[Atjeh River]].

**Kajoe Leh** was one of the places named on the gravestones of the [[Peutjoet|Petjoet cemetery]] at [[Koeta Radja]] as a field of honour on which Dutch soldiers had fallen, recorded by [[P. H. R. Beuming]] alongside [[Lambaroe]], [[Kroeng Raba]] and [[Aroen]] in the inscriptions he read there.
