**Rau** (region (Menangkabau)) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Region in Menangkabau from which Radja Dewa and his people came to Alaslanden, bringing Islam.

## Type

region ([[Menangkabau]])

## Description

Region in [[Menangkabau]] from which [[Radja Dewa]] and his people came to [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]], bringing [[Islam]].

## Significance

Rau is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
