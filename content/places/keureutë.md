**Keureutë** (region) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Region whose people are said to have 32 ways of deceiving people (more than Peusangan's 12).

## Type

region

## Description

Region whose people are said to have 32 ways of deceiving people (more than [[Peusangan]]'s 12).

## Significance

Keureutë is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
