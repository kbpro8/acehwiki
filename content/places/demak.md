The town in [[Java]] from which Teungku [[Lam Gut|Lam Guha]] ('the saint of the cave') came. He was 'a Javanese from Demak, so called because he secluded himself in a cave for many years. His tomb which lies behind the mosque at [[Ulee Lheue]], is now visited by many pilgrims.'
