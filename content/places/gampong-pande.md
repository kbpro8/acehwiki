A village near [[Kutaraja]]. It appears in the historical index as a subdivision of the capital area.

## Expanded account from *The Achehnese*, Vol. I — Pande

**Pande** was, before the Dutch war, one of the principal *gampongs* (villages) of [[Banda Acheh|the capital of Acheh]] and a constituent element of the commercial nucleus that gave its tone to the whole country. In *The Achehnese* Vol. I, Snouck Hurgronje enumerates Pande among the chief *gampongs* of the old capital.

### The Old Capital

The author describes how the [[Dalam (Sultan's Court)|Dalam]], or residence of the Sultan — which the Dutch incorrectly termed *Kraton*, and which is also known as [[Kuta Raja]] or "the king's fort" — formed, before the war, the nucleus of a number of fine and prosperous *gampongs*. The centre of these, with its mosque and market-place, was called [[Banda Acheh]] — that is, the capital or trading-mart of Acheh — and gave the tone to the whole country in matters of custom, dress, and manners. The most important of these *gampongs* were [[Gampong Jawa]], Pande, [[Peunayong]], [[Lam Bhu]], [[Lu'eng Bata]], [[Lam Seupeu'eng]], [[Ateung]], [[Batoh]] and [[Meura'sa]].

### The Banda and the Dusun

The inhabitants of these and the neighbouring villages, together with their language and customs, were distinguished by the epithet *banda* — that is, "town-bred" or "civilized" — and the people of other districts who conformed as much as possible to the manners of the capital enjoyed the same title. In contrast with these, all others who spoke in their own local dialects and were unacquainted with the manners of the town were called *duson*. As a *banda* village, Pande thus belonged to that privileged circle of communities which set the standard of Achehnese urbanity, and which formed, as it were, the metropolitan quarter of the kingdom.

### The Name

The name *Pande* — literally "craftsman" or "artisan" in Achehnese and Malay — suggests that the village may originally have been a settlement of skilled craftsmen attached to the Sultan's court, supplying the capital with the smiths, carpenters, goldsmiths and other artisans whose trades were indispensable to its life. Such artisan-quarters, known by analogous names, are a common feature of the chief towns of the archipelago, and the Pande of [[Banda Acheh]] may be reckoned among them.
