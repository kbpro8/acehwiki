**Piada** is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Boorterrein in Djambo Ajé blok; new boring with ~35 ton/day capacity.

## Description

Boorterrein in [[Djambo Ajé]] blok; new boring with ~35 ton/day capacity.

## Significance

Piada is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
