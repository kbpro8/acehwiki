**Lamsahan** is a bivouak on the [[Gajo-weg|Gajo-weg]] at kilometre 73, shortly before one reaches the first full view of the great mountain. The government has put up a few sheds there as a temporary bivouak for patrols, which go out to collect arrears of tax, or to ensure that a little cleaning is done in the *kampongs*, or to track the few evildoers who hide in the forests of the highland and the mountain-slopes, helped by a well-disposed population of *kampongs* here and there, who admire and fear these independent freebooters.
