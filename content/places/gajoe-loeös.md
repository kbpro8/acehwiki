**Gajoe Loeös** — *Greater Gajo* — is an *onderafdeeling* of the *afdeeling* [[Gajo- en Alaslanden|Gajo- en Alaslanden]], with its chief place at [[Blang Kedjerën|Blang Kedjerën]]. It is one of the four *onderafdeelingen* of the reconstituted *afdeeling* of [[1922]]. Broersma considered that Blang Kedjerën would in time become the centre of administration of the whole *afdeeling*.
