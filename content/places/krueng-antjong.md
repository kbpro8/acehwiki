**The Krueng Antjong** (also *Kr. Antjong*, modern *Krueng Antong* or *Sungai Antong*) is a river of the [[Atjeh's Westkust|West coast of Atjeh]] whose coal seams are recorded in [[J. Zwierzycki|Zwierzycki]]'s [[1922]] inventory of Sumatran coal localities (No. 17) and reproduced in [[Van Bemmelen's The Geology of Indonesia (1949)|Van Bemmelen's *Geology of Indonesia*]] ([[1949]]). The Krueng Antjong locality is one of the four Neogene-coal localities of the West-Atjeh coast.

## The 1922 Zwierzycki observation

Zwierzycki's [[1922]] entry for the Krueng Antjong locality, as reproduced by Van Bemmelen, records "3 seams of 2–5 ft". The locality is cited in the standard form "ZWIERZYCKI, 1922, p. 161" and is assigned to the Neogene series of Atjeh coals. The three seams, ranging from 2 to 5 ft in thickness, indicate a coal-bearing sequence of moderate aggregate thickness, less substantial than the five seams of 2–9 ft at the [[Krueng Raja]] (No. 16) but more substantial than the "very thin seams" of the Paleogene localities.

## Place in the Atjeh coal record

For the wider context of the West-Atjeh coal localities, see the entries on [[Krueng Raja]] and [[Krueng Kluet]]. The Krueng Antjong locality is one of the four West-Atjeh Neogene-coal localities in Zwierzycki's consolidated inventory, alongside the Krueng Raja (No. 16), East of Peureu Moue (No. 18) and Near Simpane Peust (No. 19).
