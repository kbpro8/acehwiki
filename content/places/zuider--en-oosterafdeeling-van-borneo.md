**South and East Division of Borneo** was the South and East Division of [[Borneo]], of which [[H. N. A. Swart]] was Civil and Military Resident from [[3 March]] [[1905]] and where he was promoted *Luitenant-kolonel* on [[24 October]] [[1905]].

He held the post at 1,250 guilders a month with the title *Civiel en Militair Resident der South and East Division of Borneo*. His conduct there and in [[Celebes]] in the years [[1905]]–[[1908]] formed the citation for his promotion to Commander of the [[Militaire Willemsorde]]. The [[South and East Division of Borneo]] was among the first territories opened under the *[[Korte Verklaring]]*, with [[Djambi]], [[Korintji]] and [[Midden Sumatra]].
