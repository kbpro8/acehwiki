**South and East Division of Borneo** was the South and East Division of [[Borneo]], of which [[H. N. A. Swart]] was Civil and Military Resident from [[3 March]] [[1905]] and where he was promoted *Luitenant-kolonel* on [[24 October]] [[1905]].

He held the post at 1,250 guilders a month with the title *Civiel en Militair Resident der South and East Division of Borneo*. His conduct there and in [[Celebes]] in the years [[1905]]–[[1908]] formed the citation for his promotion to Commander of the [[Militaire Willemsorde]]. The [[South and East Division of Borneo]] was among the first territories opened under the *[[Korte Verklaring]]*, with [[Djambi]], [[Korintji]] and [[Midden Sumatra]].

## Maréchaussée brigades sent to Borneo ([[1905]])

the [[1930]] jubilee number of *[[Het Nieuws van den Dag voor Nederlandsch-Indië]]* cites the Southern and Eastern Division of Borneo as the leading instance of the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* rendering extraordinary services outside [[Atjeh]]: "De Maréchaussée bewees ook buiten Atjeh haar buitengewone diensten, zooals in de Z. en O. Afdeeling van Borneo, waarheen in 1905 drie brigades werden gedirigeerd, wien 't mocht gelukken den pretendent-sultan onschadelijk te maken" — the Maréchaussée proved its extraordinary services outside Atjeh as well, as in the Southern and Eastern Division of Borneo, whither three *[[Brigade (Maréchaussée)|brigades]]* were directed in 1905, to whom it fell to succeed in rendering the *[[Pretendent-sultan|pretendent-sultan]]* harmless.

The despatch illustrates the transferability of the methods the corps had developed in Atjeh — small, mobile, lightly armed brigades operating on local intelligence — to other theatres of colonial warfare.
