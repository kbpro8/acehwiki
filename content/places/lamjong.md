**Lamjong** was a post within the *[[Geconcentreerde linie|geconcentreerde linie]]* of [[Groot-Atjeh]], the nearest to [[Toengkoeb]], from which a medical officer had to be fetched when one was needed at that outpost, and the starting point of the old road running through [[Toengkoeb]], [[Masdjid Siem|Messigit Siem]] and [[Kroeng Kali]] to the foot of the hill country bounding the [[Acheh River|Atjeh valley]] on the east.

## Medical support for Toengkoeb

Hooijer recorded that the garrison of [[Toengkoeb]] numbered only two officers and fifty men and that no *officier van gezondheid* (medical officer) was present; when one was needed *werd hij van den meest nabijgelegen post in de geconcentreerde linie — van Lamjong — gehaald*.

## The old road

The road formerly laid through the *kampong* (village) chain — of which [[Kroeng Kali]] formed the north-eastern and [[Toengkoeb]] the opposite extremity — ran from Lamjong in the concentrated line by way of Toengkoeb and [[Masdjid Siem|Messigit Siem]] to [[Kroeng Kali]] and thence to the foot of the bare hill country east of the valley.
