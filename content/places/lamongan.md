**Lamongan** was a regency in the eastern part of [[Java]] that became a focal point of Dutch military activity during the Japanese invasion in [[1942]]. Dutch marines under *Overste Roelofsen* were ordered to assist the *assistant‑resident* of Lamongan, dispersing local unrest and conducting reconnaissance westward.

## Military Operations
On [[4 March]] [[1942]] a motorised section of the *Marinebataljon* arrived in Lamongan, patrolled the area, and executed a detachment to suppress “onrust” (unrest) among the indigenous population. The operation also involved the execution of a “tiental helhamels” (ten local leaders) to restore order.

## Significance
Lamongan’s strategic location made it a key point in the Dutch defensive network on Java, influencing the broader campaign against Japanese forces.
