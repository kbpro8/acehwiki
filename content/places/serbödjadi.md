**Serbödjadi** is an *onderafdeeling* of the *afdeeling* [[Gajo- en Alaslanden|Gajo- en Alaslanden]], with its chief place at [[Lökop|Lökop]]. It is one of the four *onderafdeelingen* of the reconstituted *afdeeling* of [[1922]].
