**Meurendoe** — also written *Meureundue*, *Meureudoe* — was an under-afdeeling (sub-division) of the [[Noordkust van Atjeh|Noordkust van Atjeh]] in the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]], adjacent to the [[Pidie|Pidie]] valley. Meurendoe is documented in the historical record principally by [[J. Kreemer|J. Kreemer]] in his [[Kreemer's De rijstcultuur in het gewest Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden (1918)|*De rijstcultuur in het gewest Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden* (1918)]] and by [[J. H. Heyl|J. H. Heyl]] in his 1916 article in the *[[Deli Courant|Deli Courant]]* (No. 183), as one of the rice-producing sub-divisions of the Noordkust whose surplus could, in principle, be exported to the [[Sumatra's Oostkust|Sumatra's East Coast]] plantation belt.

## The Rice Cultivation of Meurendoe

Kreemer's regional survey of the [[rijstbouw in Atjeh|Atjeh rice economy]] records that in the under-afdeeling Meurendoe the rice cultivation is carried on both on irrigated and on unirrigated fields, to a combined area of about 3,000 hectares. The irrigated fields of Meurendoe include, Kreemer notes, "alle sawah's in het gelijknamige landschap Meureudoe" — all the sawahs in the same-named *landschap* (self-governing native state) of Meureudoe.

The yield of the Meurendoe rice-fields is, according to [[J. H. Heyl|Heyl]]'s calculation in the *Deli Courant* of [[8 August]] [[1916]], about 30 *pikoel* of rice per hectare — between the 8 *pikoel* per hectare of the rain-dependent sawahs of [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] and the 36 *pikoel* per hectare of the irrigated Pidie valley. The Meurendoe yield is thus respectable, and reflects the partial irrigation of the sub-division.

## The Rice Surplus of Meurendoe

Meurendoe is one of the sub-divisions of the Noordkust that, in Kreemer's regional survey, produce a surplus of rice over local consumption. Kreemer notes that "in de onderafdeelingen Sigli, Meureudoe en Bireuen is de productie als regel grooter dan de consumptie" — in the sub-divisions Sigli, Meureudoe and Bireuen the production is, as a rule, greater than the consumption. Meurendoe is thus one of the rice-surplus sub-divisions of the Noordkust, and one of the candidates for the [[Rijstvraagstuk (1916)|Rijstvraagstuk]] — the colonial policy debate on whether Atjeh could become the rice-storehouse of Sumatra's East Coast.

[[J. H. Heyl|Heyl]]'s 1916 calculation included Meurendoe in the list of sub-divisions that could supply rice to Deli, with a cultivated sawah area of about 3,000 hectares, a total production of about 90,000 *pikoel*, a local consumption of about 14,000 *pikoel*, and a surplus for export of about 76,000 *pikoel*.

## Significance

Meurendoe is significant for the encyclopedia of Aceh history as a documented sub-division of the Noordkust van Atjeh, as one of the rice-surplus sub-divisions of the Noordkust, and as one of the candidates for the [[Rijstvraagstuk (1916)|Rijstvraagstuk of 1916]] — the colonial policy debate on the economic integration of Atjeh with the Sumatra East Coast plantation belt. The Meurendoe rice-economy — partly irrigated, partly rain-dependent, with a yield of 30 *pikoel* per hectare — exemplifies the partial-irrigation pattern of the Noordkust, between the fully-irrigated Pidie valley and the wholly rain-dependent sawahs of Groot-Atjeh.
