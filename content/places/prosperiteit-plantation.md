**Prosperiteit plantation** was a sugar plantation in the interior of [[Acheh]] that was reported as recently abandoned by rebels during the Dutch December expedition. A “negro” patrol later found the buildings still standing and recovered a silver candlestick engraved with the name of the preacher [[Ramringt]].

## Military Significance
The plantation’s location near the [[Wironje creek]] made it a target for Dutch patrols seeking to suppress rebel activity and to secure the riverine supply line.
