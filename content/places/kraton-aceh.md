**Kraton (Aceh)** was the royal palace complex of the [[Aceh Sultanate]] situated in the interior of northern Sumatra. In the 1873 Dutch expedition, the Kraton area served as the objective of the first marine landing on [[5 April]] [[1873]]. Dutch forces established a beach bivouac near the Kraton after encountering only light resistance, but subsequent attempts to advance inland met with heavy fire from Acehnese defenders.

The Kraton’s proximity to the **Mesigit** made it a strategic target for both sides. Control of the palace would have symbolised the subjugation of the Aceh Sultanate.

## Historical context
The Kraton had long been the seat of the Acehnese sultanate’s political and religious authority. Its architecture blended Malay, Islamic, and indigenous Acehnese elements.
