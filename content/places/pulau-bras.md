**Pulau Bras** (Dutch: *Poeloe Bras*) is an island off the north-western tip of [[aceh|Aceh]], near [[Poeloe Nasi|Poeloe Nassie]] and [[Poeloe Wei|Poeloe Wai]]. During the [[aceh-war|Aceh War]] it was used on occasion as a staging point. On [[23-december|23 December]] [[1885]] a party from the warship *Palembang* doing target practice on the island was surprised by an armed band from the [[vi-moekims|VI Moekims]]; one man was killed before the raiders withdrew.

## Lighthouse in 1874

[[Jan van Swieten]] recorded the construction of a lighthouse on [[Pulau Bras]] during the later [[1874]] phase of the [[Aceh War]], alongside reconnaissance of [[Maraksa]] and naval operations involving the [[VI Mukim]].
