**Wimbia‑kreek** was a waterway where a group of **Negers** who had previously fled with **[[Atta (rebel leader)|Atta]]** settled before returning to the plantation **[[JJhannaa]]** to seek Christian employment. The creek is mentioned in the context of the **[[1760s]]** Dutch anti‑rebellion campaign.

## Role in the Conflict
Its proximity to rebel activity made it a focal point for Dutch attempts to control slave movements and plantation labor.
