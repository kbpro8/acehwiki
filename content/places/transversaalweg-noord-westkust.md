**Transversaalweg (Noord-Westkust)** (road) is a location in or related to [[Aceh]] described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*. Road to connect North and West coasts of Aceh.

## Type

road

## Description

Road to connect North and West coasts of Aceh. From [[Malaboeh|Meulaboh]] goes 60 km inland, then a gap of ~47 km (with a usable trace but needing engineering works). About 15 km before [[Geumpang]] connects to the already-completed section and the [[asweg]] (asphalt road). Eventually intended to be a valuable factor for lasting peace and order.

## Significance

Transversaalweg (Noord-Westkust) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the geography and historical landscape of [[Aceh]].
