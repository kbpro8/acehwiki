**A. Pentuwa Gade** is an iron-ore locality on the [[Atjeh's Westkust|West coast of Atjeh]], 68 km SE of [[Meulaboh]], recorded by [[P. J. J. Jansen|Jansen]], [[B. B. Lindberg|Lindberg]] and [[H. Wolvekamp|Wolvekamp]] in [[1922]] and reproduced in [[Van Bemmelen's The Geology of Indonesia (1949)|Van Bemmelen's *Geology of Indonesia*]] ([[1949]]). The A. Pentuwa Gade locality is No. 8 in Van Bemmelen's Fig. 41 key, and is the south-easternmost of the West-Atjeh iron-ore occurrences recorded by the 1922 survey.

## The 1922 Jansen-Lindberg-Wolvekamp observation

The 1922 report of Jansen, Lindberg and Wolvekamp, as reproduced by Van Bemmelen, records simply:

> "Locality: 68 km SE of Meulaboh. No particulars available."

The absence of detailed geological description for the A. Pentuwa Gade locality — in contrast to the more substantial descriptions of the [[Kr. Geuenteuet]], [[Gle Mon Ampeuet]], [[Kr. Ligan (Babah Loh)|Kr. Ligan (Babah Loh)]], [[Kr. Rigaih]] and [[Kwala Boe Tjot Plaj|Kwala Boe and Tjot Plaj]] localities — indicates that the Jansen-Lindberg-Wolvekamp survey did not have the time or the resources to conduct a detailed examination of every Atjeh iron-ore occurrence, and that the A. Pentuwa Gade locality was recorded only as a point on the map without further particulars. The locality's inclusion in Van Bemmelen's Fig. 41 key, however, establishes it as one of the eight canonical Atjeh iron-ore localities of the colonial geological literature.

## Significance

A. Pentuwa Gade is significant for the encyclopedia of [[Acheh]] history because it is one of the eight canonical Atjeh iron-ore localities of the colonial geological literature, and the south-easternmost of the West-Atjeh iron-ore occurrences recorded by the [[1922]] Jansen-Lindberg-Wolvekamp survey. The locality's inclusion in Van Bemmelen's Fig. 41 key — despite the absence of detailed geological description — establishes it as part of the documented colonial-era iron-ore record of Atjeh, and its south-easterly position (68 km SE of Meulaboh, in contrast to the NW-of-Meulaboh positions of the other West-Atjeh iron-ore localities) marks it as the southern anchor of the West-Atjeh iron-ore zone.
