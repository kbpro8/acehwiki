**Lam-Krak / Lam-Djamé** — written in Dutch as also *Lam-Krak Moekim*, *Lam-Djamé* for the kampong in the northern *rand* of Lam-Krak, *Lam-Djamee* — was the kampong and mukim that gave its name to the broader [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lamkrak complex]], the site of two redoutes near the northern *rand* attacked on [[9 June]] [[1896]] by von Schauroth's compagnie, and the location of the [[Masdjid Lam-Krak|fortified mosque of Lam-Krak]] (a listed target of the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|tuchtiging of 9–10 June 1896]]). As described in Kruisheer's *ATJEH '96*, both *bentings* at Lam-Krak / Lam-Djamé were taken by Blankenstein and Kuipers.

## Description and Location

Lam-Krak / Lam-Djamé lay in the northern *rand* of the [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lamkrak complex]] — the "streek tusschen Biloel en Ana Galoeng en benoorden het gebergte gelegen" (the region between [[Biloel]] and [[Ana' Galong]], north of the mountains). The kampong of Lam-Djamé was situated in the northern *rand* of the Lam-Krak moekim, and was the site of two redoutes (*bentings*) that were attacked on [[9 June]] [[1896]]. The [[Masdjid Lam-Krak|fortified mosque of Lam-Krak]] — also called *Missigit Lam-Krak* — was a listed target of the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|tuchtiging of 9–10 June 1896]] and was fired upon by the marine on [[8 June]]–[[9 June]] [[1896]]. The kampong was bounded on the north by the [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lam-Lheuë]] mukim (with its *Kling* fortification of Mon Selassa / Mon Seulassa) and on the south by the Ateuh moekim. The graveyard of Lam-Krak was a site to be located by maréchaussée brigades, and the fortified guardhouse by the holy grave of Teungkoe Lam-Bira was in the Moekim Lam-Krak.

## Historical Background

Lam-Krak / Lam-Djamé had been the central kampong and mukim of the broader [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lamkrak complex]] throughout the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. The kampong had been a base for hostile *benden* operating against the [[buitenpost|buitenposten]] of the [[buitenlinie]] — particularly [[Biloel]] and [[Lamkoenjit]]. The [[Masdjid Lam-Krak|fortified mosque of Lam-Krak]] was a particularly strong Achehnese position, and was a listed target of the [[1893]] *legioen* of [[Teuku Umar|T. Djohan]]. The [[1878]] campaign of [[Generaal van der Heiden|Generaal van der Heiden]] had passed through the Lam-Krak country, and the [[1893]] *legioen* of [[Teuku Umar|T. Djohan]] had operated in the area. The *Koeta Binasa Baroe* *versterking* in the kampong-grand of Lam-Krak was one of the listed Achehnese fortifications of the mukim.

## Role in the 1896 Operations

Lam-Krak / Lam-Djamé was the scene of major combat on [[9 June]] [[1896]] in the course of the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|tuchtiging of the Lamkrak Moekims]]. Two redoutes (*bentings*) near the northern *rand* of Lam-Krak were attacked by the compagnie of von Schauroth; both *bentings* were taken by Blankenstein and Kuipers. The [[Masdjid Lam-Krak|fortified mosque of Lam-Krak]] was a listed target of the tuchtiging and was presumably destroyed in the course of the operation. The *Missigit Lam-Krak* was fired upon by the marine on [[8 June]]–[[9 June]] [[1896]]. On [[10 June]] [[1896]] the Dutch operations continued with the destruction of remaining *versterkingen* in the Lamkrak. The graveyard of Lam-Krak was a site to be located by maréchaussée brigades in the course of the tuchtiging. The fall of Lam-Krak / Lam-Djamé, together with the fall of [[Ateuh Raja]] and [[Baha]] on the same day, broke the back of organised Achehnese defence in the [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lamkrak complex]].

## Significance

Lam-Krak / Lam-Djamé was the central kampong and mukim of the broader [[Lamkrak Moekims|Lamkrak complex]] and the symbolic heart of the hostile southern mukims. The fall of its two redoutes on [[9 June]] [[1896]] and the destruction of its [[Masdjid Lam-Krak|fortified mosque]] were the decisive acts of the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|tuchtiging of 9–10 June 1896]] and broke the back of organised Achehnese defence in the Lamkrak.
