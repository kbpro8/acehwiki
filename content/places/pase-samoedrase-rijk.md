**The Pasé Samoedrase Rijk** — the Samudra Pasai Kingdom — was the precolonial North-Sumatran Islamic kingdom whose Sultans, six or seven centuries ago, spread Islam by fire and sword in North Sumatra and who, in war, used elephants, and elephant-hunting was a princely entertainment. In *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* by Merac ([[1947]]), the Pasé Samoedrase Rijk is the book's — and the colonial memory's — marker of the precolonial Islamic kingdom that preceded the Acehnese empire and that had used elephants in war.

## The elephants in war

Six or seven centuries ago, when the Sultans of the Pasé Samoedrase Rijk spread Islam by fire and sword in North Sumatra, elephants were used in war and elephant-hunting was a princely entertainment. The Pasé Samoedrase Rijk — the Samudra Pasai Kingdom — is the book's marker of the precolonial Islamic use of elephants in war: the Sultans who spread Islam by fire and sword in North Sumatra and who used elephants in war.

## The sequence: Pasé Samoedrase Rijk, Acehnese empire

The book's sequence is: the Pasé Samoedrase Rijk (the Samudra Pasai Kingdom, six or seven centuries ago) — the Acehnese empire (in the period of glory of [[Sultan Iskandar Muda|Sultan Iskandar Muda]], when the Sultans had a thousand elephants as riding- and pack-animals and also as fighting-animals) — the waning of the old splendour of the Sultan's court — the disappearance of the elephants. The Pasé Samoedrase Rijk is therefore the book's — and the colonial memory's — marker of the precolonial Islamic kingdom that preceded the Acehnese empire and that had first used elephants in war in North Sumatra.

## Significance

The Pasé Samoedrase Rijk is significant in *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* as the precolonial North-Sumatran Islamic kingdom whose Sultans first used elephants in war in North Sumatra. The book's argument — that the Pasé Samoedrase Rijk was the precolonial Islamic kingdom that preceded the Acehnese empire and that had used elephants in war — is the book's — and the colonial memory's — marker of the precolonial use of elephants in North Sumatra. The Pasé Samoedrase Rijk is therefore the book's marker of the precolonial Islamic kingdom: the Sultans who spread Islam by fire and sword in North Sumatra and who used elephants in war.
