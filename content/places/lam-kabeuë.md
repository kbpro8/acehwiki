**Lam-Kabeuë** was a valley *[[Moekim|moekim]]* of [[Groot-Atjeh]] covered by [[Oogheelkundig kampongonderzoek in Groot-Atjeh (Maart-December 1937)|eye survey of 1937]], and one of the three *moekims* in which the Acehnese population knew *[[Xerophthalmie|xerophthalmie]]* under the name *penjikit sisë*.

## Food supply and xerophthalmia

Lam-Kabeuë was among the valley *moekims* — with [[Indrapoeri]] and [[Seulimeum]] — in which the rice harvest of [[1936]]–[[1937]] was less favourable, the long drought having caused the *[[Paditeelt|paddi]]* planting to fail. Dr. [[C. E. Gomperts]] found that the percentage of xerophthalmia cases in these *moekims* was "vrijwel gelijk" to that in [[Lam Teuba|Moekim Lam-Teuba]], where there was a rice surplus — one of the three comparisons on which he rested his conclusion that food supply alone could not explain the distribution of the disease.

## Acehnese nomenclature

In Lam-Kabeuë, as in [[VI Moekims Lhong|Lhong]] and [[Lho Nga|Lho-Nga]], xerophthalmia was known under the name *penjikit sisë*; in the remainder of the surveyed area the condition had no such name.
