**asweg** (English: "asphalt road") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Asphalt road.

## Translation

asphalt road

## Description

Asphalt road. The asweg connects to the transversaal road about 15 km before [[Geumpang]]. Also used as term for the main [[Noordkust]] road.

## Significance

The term *asweg* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
