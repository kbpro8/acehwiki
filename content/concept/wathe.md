**Wathé** (Atjehnese; Malay *hasil*) was the general term in [[Aceh|Atjeh]] for the revenues due to a territorial chief — import and export duties, produce dues, and other *adat* incomes — and the principal object of the *[[Oeleebalang|oeléëbalang]]*'s administrative attention.

## Character of the revenue

[[D. W. N. de Boer]] described the *zelfbestuurder* (self-governing ruler) as a ruler whose attention was absorbed, besides by weightier political business, above all by "nauwgezette contrôle op de richtige binnenvloeiing van alle hem toekomende *wathé*'s" — scrupulous supervision of the correct inflow of all *wathé* due to him: import and export duties and other *adat* incomes. He referred for this to *[[De Atjehers (Snouck Hurgronje)|De Atjehers]]*, Vol. I, p. 122, and to [[C. van Vollenhoven|Van Vollenhoven]]'s *[[Adatrecht (Van Vollenhoven)|Adatrecht]]*, p. 164.

## Effect on land policy

The chief's fiscal interest shaped his attitude to settlement. Free immigration was desirable to him with a view to future growth of *wathé*; he had no need to screen and bind individual settlers, provided they contributed to the prosperity of the *[[Landschap (Atjehnese)|landschap]]* and to the income of its head. Clearance was accordingly made as easy as possible, and agricultural credit was granted against a customary percentage of *hasil*, given willingly because — as De Boer remarked — it was one of the best investments available: "In Atjeh gaat het alleen om economische perspectieven, geenszins om idealistische."

The prospect of future *wathé* also explains why the right of avail continued to assert itself against other spheres of avail wherever pepper, *[[Pinang|pinang]]* or other plantations were laid out in frontier districts; most boundary disputes between the *zelfbesturen* (self-governing territories) arose from this.

## Evidentiary use

In the *[[Zelfbesturende landschappen|self-governing territories]]*, the continuing collection of *wathé* — together with the measuring-out under the *[[Haq radja taléë|haq radja taléë]]* and the former *[[Boeët oemòng|boeët oemòng]]* and *[[Adat glé|adat glé]]* levies — was one of the means by which the *radja* or his representative knew whether ground applied for still had an owner.

## Specific wathé of the pepper economy

Under the pepper tariff the term appears in three specific forms: *[[Wathé pangkaj|wathé pangkaj]]*, *wathé peutoeha* (or *wathé parang*), and *[[Wathé oeléëbalang|wathé oeléëbalang]]*.
