**Arabic language in Aceh** refers to the historical use and study of Classical Arabic within the Sultanate of [[Acheh]] for religious, legal, and literary purposes. The presence of Arabic terms in the 16th‑century Malay translation of the **Burda of al‑Busiri** (e.g., *shaitan*, *sunnat*) demonstrates the bilingual competence of Acehnese scholars who incorporated Arabic theological vocabulary into Malay texts (pages 52‑58).

### Educational Institutions
- **Madrasahs**: Taught Qur’an recitation, Arabic grammar (*nahwu*), and rhetoric, enabling the translation of works like the *Burda*.
- **Sufi lodges** (*zawiyahs*): Emphasised Arabic *dhikr* and the study of prophetic traditions (*hadith*).

### Literary Impact
Arabic served as the source language for many Acehnese devotional poems and legal codes. The translation of the *Burda* illustrates how Arabic literary forms were adapted into Malay *syair* structures, enriching Acehnese literary tradition.
