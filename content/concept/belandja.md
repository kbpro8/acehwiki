**Belandja** (Malay *belanja*, expenditure, maintenance money) was the subsistence advance made in [[Aceh|Atjeh]] to a cultivator or immigrant settler, covering food, clothing and other daily necessities until the crop came in.

## In the thiplah-tano contract

Under the *[[Thiplah tano|thiplah-tano]]* contract imposed on non-indigenous cultivators, the settler received as *[[Pangkaj|pangkaj]]* a buffalo, the necessary *[[Bibit|bibit]]* and a *belandja* of twenty guilders as an advance for provisions; half of the *belandja* had to be repaid in instalments.

## In aboen meu'oeë

Under the *[[Aboen meu'oeë|aboen meu'oeë]]* form of *[[Mawaih|mawaih]]*, the giver defrayed in addition to everything else the most necessary *belandja* of the *mawaih*-taker: clothing, food and smoking materials, the cultivator retaining only one-fifth of the harvest.
