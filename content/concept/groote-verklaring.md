**The Groote Verklaring** — *the Great Declaration* — was the older political declaration in eighteen articles by which the colonial government had required the *[[Oeléebalang|oeléebalangs]]* of the Atjehnese *landschappen* to acknowledge the suzerainty of the Dutch government, before the government in [[1899]] replaced it with the shorter *[[Korte Verklaring (1899)|Korte Verklaring]]* in three articles. The replacement was made because the *Groote Verklaring* was found too detailed and too restrictive for the practical purposes of the post-pacification government.
