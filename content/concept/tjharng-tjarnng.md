**tjharng / tjarnng** (English: "capable, well-developed (man)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Term used by Kreemer for the ideal man — developed in body and mind, able to handle all life's circumstances well and easily.

## Translation

capable, well-developed (man)

## Description

Term used by [[J. Kreemer|Kreemer]] for the ideal man — developed in body and mind, able to handle all life's circumstances well and easily.

## Significance

The term *tjharng / tjarnng* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
