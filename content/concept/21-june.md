## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this date in [[1883]] [[Rutgers van Rozenburg]] brought the worrying state of Atjeh into the Kamer and the Governor-General telegraphed that Groot-Atjeh was nearly cleansed of enemies — four days before the [[Murder of the postholder at Anak Paja (25 June 1883)|Anak Paja murders]].
