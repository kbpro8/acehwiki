**Atjeh-roman** (English: "Aceh novel (fictional romance)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Fictional/romanticized stories about Atjeh, of which Jongejans says 'we have enough of them'.

## Translation

Aceh novel (fictional romance)

## Description

Fictional/romanticized stories about Atjeh, of which [[Resident Jongejans|Jongejans]] says 'we have enough of them'. He promises not to write one, but to tell purely, simply, and truthfully.

## Significance

The term *Atjeh-roman* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
