**The Eervolle Vermelding** — the Honorable Mention — was a Dutch colonial military decoration, worn with pride in the *[[negorij]]* or *[[kampong]]* of the Ambonese and Menadonese soldiers. The decoration was also known as the *[[Kroontje (Eervolle Vermelding)|Kroontje]]* — the "Little Crown" — and was, in the colonial army's decoration economy, the recognition that came before the [[Militaire Willemsorde|M.W.O.]] and the [[Medaille voor Moed en Trouw]].

## The Kroontje

"Dragers van de [[Medaille voor Moed en Trouw]] en van het *Kroontje* (de Eervolle Vermelding)" — the *Kroontje* is the Eervolle Vermelding. The "Little Crown" — the small crown emblem worn on the ribbon of a decoration — was the visible sign of the Honorable Mention. The *Kroontje* was therefore both the decoration itself (the Honorable Mention) and its visible emblem (the little crown on the ribbon).

## The decoration economy

The Eervolle Vermelding was, in the colonial army's decoration economy, the recognition that came before the [[Militaire Willemsorde|M.W.O.]] and the [[Medaille voor Moed en Trouw]]. The book's example is [[Salawono]], who at the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Marechaussee]] korps had "een *Eervolle Vermelding* en de *[[Medaille voor Moed en Trouw]]* verdiend" — an Honorable Mention and the Medal for Courage and Loyalty — before earning his M.W.O. 4e klas at the [[Storming of Limboer (22 June 1903)|storming of Limboer]] and his M.W.O. 3e klas on [[Celebes]]. The Eervolle Vermelding was therefore the first step in the decoration career of the colonial soldaat.

## The trots of the negorij

"Ridders [[Militaire Willemsorde|M.W.O.]], dragers van de [[Medaille voor Moed en Trouw]] en van het *Kroontje* (de Eervolle Vermelding), waren de trots van hun *[[kampong]]* of *[[negorij]]*." The Eervolle Vermelding — like the M.W.O. and the Medaille voor Moed en Trouw — was the pride of the soldier's home community. The *negorij* tracked the soldier's wedervaren through the gehele Archipel, and his decoration was worn at the *[[Koninginnedag]]* rondgang.

## Significance

The Eervolle Vermelding is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as the first step in the decoration career of the colonial soldaat. The book's argument is that the Eervolle Vermelding — like the [[Militaire Willemsorde|M.W.O.]] and the [[Medaille voor Moed en Trouw]] — was the pride of the soldier's home community, and that the *negorij*'s tracking of the soldier's wedervaren was the mechanism by which the decoration economy of the colonial army was tied to the home community. The Eervolle Vermelding is therefore the book's marker of the first step in the decoration career that culminated, for [[Salawono]], in the M.W.O. 3e klas and the *slippendragers* at the [[Funeral of Dominee Thenu (11 May 1937)|funeral of Dominee Thenu]].
