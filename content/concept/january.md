**[[January]]** is a calendar month used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention. In the material drawn from *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]* it is associated with the [[Evacuation of Kroeng Kali (January 1894)|evacuation of Kroeng Kali]] in [[January]] [[1894]].
