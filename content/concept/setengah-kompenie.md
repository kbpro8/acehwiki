**setengah Kompenie** (English: "Half-Company (half-military)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Local nickname for the Gouvernements Marine vaartuig (e.g., the 'Wega'), reflecting its semi-military character.

## Translation

Half-Company (half-military)

## Description

Local nickname for the [[Gouvernements Marine]] vaartuig (e.g., the 'Wega'), reflecting its semi-military character.

## Significance

The term *setengah Kompenie* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
