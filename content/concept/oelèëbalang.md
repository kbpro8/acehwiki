**Oelèëbalang** was the title of the hereditary territorial chiefs of [[Aceh|Atjèh]], described by Kreemer as "the real lords of the land" whose authority predated the Sultans. They were made Gouvernementsambtenaren (government officials) by Stbl. [[1881]] N°. 79, and served as sole speaking judges and members of *moesapat*'s (indigenous courts).

## Nature and authority

The Oelèëbalang's were the hereditary territorial chiefs whose authority predated the Sultanate. They held sovereignty rights over land and trade within their *oelèëbalangschap* (domain). Addressed as *teukoe*, *teukoe pö*, *teukoe ampön*, or *radja*, they were the real power in the Atjèhnese countryside, with the Sultan's authority often more nominal than real outside the capital.

## Made Gouvernementsambtenaren (1881)

By Stbl. [[1881]] N°. 79, the Oelèëbalang's were made Gouvernementsambtenaren (government officials), formalising their position within the Dutch colonial administrative structure. They acted as sole speaking judge in their own *oelèëbalangschap* and as members of the *[[moesapat|moesapat]]*'s (indigenous courts) established in [[1879]].

## Structure: banta and rakan

The Oelèëbalang was assisted by a **banta** — his principal helper or right-hand, usually one of his younger brothers or close blood relatives. Below the banta were various **rakan** (assistants). The Oelèëbalang also had relations with the *imeum*'s (moekim-heads) and, in the Onderhoorigheden, with *toeha peuët* (councils of four elders), *toeha lapan* (councils of eight), and *peutoeha*'s (lower heads).

## Wasé oelèëbalang

The Oelèëbalang's sovereignty right over pepper and pinang was called **[[wasé oelèëbalang|wasé oelèëbalang]]** — originally a fixed amount ($2/pikul pepper, $1–1½/bahra pinang in Groot-Atjèh). This was reorganised by governor [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] in [[1906]] to 10% of value (causing pepper decline), then by governor [[Governor Swart|Swart]] in [[1910]] to fixed amounts.

## Oelèëbalang Pöteu

A special rank was **Oelèëbalang Pöteu** — "Oelèëbalang of our Lord (the Sultan)" — a rank equal to nearly independent landschapshoofden, with secular authority but without land. Borne by hovelingen (courtiers) as a favour: Teukoe Boeka Seurama (payong-drager), Teukoe Rama Seutia (chief of lijfwacht), Teukoe Keudjroeën Geundrang, and Teukoe Keureukön (originally *Katib oel-moelk* = "schrijver des Koninkrijks").
