**Dienst van den schijn** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Broersma's criticism of the Swart era: too much outward impression created that Aceh was thoroughly pacified and economically promising.

## Description

[[Broersma]]'s criticism of the [[Gouverneur Swart|Swart]] era: too much outward impression created that Aceh was thoroughly pacified and economically promising.

## Significance

The term *Dienst van den schijn* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
