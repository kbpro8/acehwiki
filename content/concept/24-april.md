**[[24 April]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[Oriënteerend oogheelkundig onderzoek bij de Atjehsche bevolking van Groot-Atjeh gedurende 1937|Gomperts's report of 1940]]* it marks the following item.

## Events on this date

- The abstract in the *Nederlandsch Tijdschrift voor Geneeskunde* of [[24 April]] [[1937]] of the paper by [[Schneider en Weiland|Schneider and H. Weiland]] in the *Klinische Wochenschrift* on the urinary excretion of vitamin A in severe infectious disease — cited by Dr. [[C. E. Gomperts]] among the *Geraadpleegde Literatuur* of his survey of [[Groot-Atjeh]] ([[1937]])
