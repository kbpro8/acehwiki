**Djandji Atjeh** (English: "Acehnese promise (not meant to be kept)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. An Acehnese expression for a promise that one has no intention of keeping; illustrates Acehnese mistrust.

## Translation

Acehnese promise (not meant to be kept)

## Description

An Acehnese expression for a promise that one has no intention of keeping; illustrates Acehnese mistrust.

## Significance

The term *Djandji Atjeh* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
