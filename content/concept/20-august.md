**[[20 August]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]* it appears in the articles listed below.

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this date in [[1879]] [[Toekoe Moeda Daoed]] and [[Toekoe Tjoet Hadjet]] swore their submission to generaal [[Karel van der Heijden|van der Heyden]], who the same day wrote his letter partly quoted in the pamphlet ('de krijgsverrichtingen op groote schaal zijn afgeloopen') — see [[Van der Heijden's pacification of Groot-Atjeh]]; also the closing date of [[H. G. Boumeester|Boumeester]]'s shot-free inspection.
