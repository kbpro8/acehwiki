A **bandjir** (modern spelling *banjir*) is a sudden flood of a river in the [[Acheh|Atjeh]] plain — typically the [[Acheh River|Atjeh River]] or its tributaries such as the [[Kroeng Daroe|Kroeng Daroe]] — caused by heavy monsoon rain in the interior, that periodically threatened the Dutch positions and communications around [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] during the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. The bandjir of [[4 December]] [[4 December]] [[1874]] that nearly cost [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede-Luitenant C. J. Snijders]] his life — see [[Bandjir at Penajoeng (4 December 1874)|bandjir at Penajoeng]] — is the most vividly recorded instance, but the biographer [[D. van den Berg|Van den Berg]] also records the embankment of the [[Kroeng Daroe|Kroeng Daroe]] and other drainage works undertaken by the Staf der Genie at [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] in [[1875]] to make the [[Kraton of Aceh|Kraton]] free of bandjir.

## The Bandjir of 4 December 1874

In the first days of [[December 1874|December]] [[1874]] a bandjir threatened the two light trestle bridges that had been thrown across the [[Acheh River|Atjeh River]] at Pakan Atjeh and at [[Penajoeng|Penajoeng]]. By preventing driftwood, tree-trunks, and creepers from collecting against the trestles, the Pakan Atjeh bridge was saved. The [[Penajoeng|Penajoeng]] bridge was in greater danger, and on [[4 December]] [[4 December]] [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] was sent there. The whole country was under water; unable to swim, he took three native mineurs who could. Wading to chest-height over the unpathed ground they reached [[Penajoeng|Penajoeng]] in half an hour — only to see the surviving section of the bridge torn away by the flood. On the return march Snijders was seized by a cross-current at a point where he stood armpit-deep, and would have drowned had not his companions held out a stick by which he regained his footing.

## The Bandjir-Control Works of 1875

After the close of his field engineer service with the [[Lamara Olejlo fortification (15 February 1875)|Lamara Olejlo fortification of 15 February]] [[1875]], [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] was attached to the Staf der Genie at [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] for what were essentially peacetime duties. Among the works listed by [[D. van den Berg|Van den Berg]] are "appoleinngweekanneedd tot het bandjirvrijmaken van den Kraton (o.a. door bedijking van de Kroeng Daroe ter plaatse)" — drainage works to make the [[Kraton of Aceh|Kraton]] free of bandjir, including by the embankment of the [[Kroeng Daroe|Kroeng Daroe]] at the relevant place.
