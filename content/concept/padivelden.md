**padivelden** (English: "paddy fields (with setting grain)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Rice fields where the ears (aren) are already setting - one of the phases of rice cultivation visible on a trip through Atjeh.

## Translation

paddy fields (with setting grain)

## Description

Rice fields where the ears (aren) are already setting - one of the phases of rice cultivation visible on a trip through Atjeh.

## Significance

The term *padivelden* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
