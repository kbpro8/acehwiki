**Koerier** was a Dutch steamship (*Hr.Ms.*) specially equipped for the **Tamiang** river expedition of **[[March]] [[1893]]**. It carried four rapid‑fire 3.7 cm cannons and reinforced decks to navigate the most hazardous sections of the river.

## Operational Role

On **[[30 March]] [[1893]]**, **Koerier** led the flotilla that broke two river blockades, sustaining five Dutch casualties, including **Lieutenant ter zee 2c klasse Van Dijk** and two marines.

## Significance

The ship’s heavy armament and reinforced hull exemplify Dutch adaptations to riverine combat in the **Aceh War**.
