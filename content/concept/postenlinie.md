**The Postenlinie** ("line of forts") was the celebrated defensive perimeter of fortified posts surrounding [[Kota Radja]] in [[Groot-Atjeh|Great Atjeh]], built in the [[1870s]] and [[1880s]] as the defensive ring of the Dutch military position in [[Acheh|Atjeh]]. In the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, the postenlinie defined the geography of the Dutch presence in Atjeh from the [[1873]] outbreak of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-war]] until the [[Toekoe Oemar's verraad (1896)|betrayal of Toekoe Oemar in 1896]] rendered the defensive strategy obsolete. The [[1885]] [[Ceintuur-trambaan|ceintuur-trambaan (belt-tramway)]] was built along the postenlinie to allow rapid movement of troops between the forts; after the [[1896]] abandonment of the postenlinie, the belt-tramway was "gradually cleared" and the long-line strategy of [[1897]] onward replaced it.

## Account from *De Buitenbezittingen* (1917) — Postenlinie

The Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account of the [[Postenlinie|postenlinie]] appears in the chapter on the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]]. The Bureau's narrative identifies the postenlinie as the strategic frame within which the [[1885]] [[Ceintuur-trambaan|ceintuur-trambaan]] was built: "in [[1885]] military necessity led to the construction of a ceintuur-trambaan along the famous postenlinie, which was connected by three radial lines (*straalbanen*) to [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]], and thereby to the old tramway to the roadstead."

The Bureau's narrative of the [[1896]] abandonment of the postenlinie is explicit about its strategic consequences: "with the abandonment of the postenlinie after the [[Toekoe Oemar's verraad (1896)|betrayal of Toekoe Oemar]] in [[1896]], the utility of the [[Ceintuur-trambaan|ceintuurbaan]] lapsed, which was since gradually cleared. In its place came the long tramway, which since [[1897]] has followed step by step our extension of authority in [[Atjeh's Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh's Onderhoorigheden]]."

The Bureau's framing of the postenlinie — together with the [[Ceintuur-trambaan|ceintuur-trambaan]] built along it in [[1885]] — places it as the principal material embodiment of the defensive phase of the Dutch presence in Atjeh: the phase that ran from the [[1873]] outbreak of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-war]] to the [[1896]] strategic reorientation. The postenlinie was the physical expression of the doctrine that the Dutch position in Atjeh could be secured by a perimeter of fortified posts around [[Kota Radja]] connected by rail; its abandonment after [[1896]] marks the doctrinal shift to the offensive, long-line strategy of the [[1897]]-onward phase.

## Significance

The [[Postenlinie|postenlinie]] is, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, the principal material embodiment of the defensive phase of the Dutch presence in [[Acheh|Atjeh]] — the phase of the [[1873]]–[[1896]] period in which the Dutch position was confined to a fortified perimeter around [[Kota Radja]]. Its abandonment after the [[Toekoe Oemar's verraad (1896)|betrayal of Toekoe Oemar in 1896]] marks the strategic reorientation that opened the long Atjeh campaign of conquest and [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacification]] — the campaign that the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]]'s long line of [[1897]] onward would serve as the principal logistical instrument.

## Account from *De Atjeh-Oorlog* by J.A. Kruijt (1896)

In [[J. A. Kruijt|Kruijt]]'s analysis, the *Postenlinie* — the Dutch system of fortifications and posts around [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] in [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] — was progressively expanded from the *stelling* bequeathed by [[Jan van Swieten|Van Swieten]]'s second expedition of [[1873]]–[[1874]] to an elaborate ring of thirty-eight fortifications by [[1875]], and contracted by the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration system]] of [[1884]] to a smaller perimeter that persisted through the end of the war.

The *Postenlinie* was expanded by [[J. L. J. H. Pel|Pel]] (1874–1876), [[Wiggers van Kerchem|Wiggers van Kerchem]] and [[Diemont|Diemont]] (1876–1877), and [[General van der Heijden|Van der Heijden]] (1877–1881). Under Van der Heijden the *stelling* was further extended to cover the [[22 Moekims|22]] and [[26 Moekims|26 Moekims]] after their conquest in [[1879]]; by [[1881]] it demanded a garrison of approximately 10,000 men. Kruijt's judgement was severe: "*dat zich steeds uitbreiden onzer stelling na Van Swieten, door Pel en Van der Heijden, dat verbrokkelen onzer macht in Atjeh, dat verslinden van alle middelen op dat ééne punt*" (that continuous expansion of our *stelling* after Van Swieten, by Pel and Van der Heijden, that fragmentation of our power in Atjeh, that devouring of all means on that one point) — he had always condemned.

The [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration system]] of [[Demmeni|Demmeni]] (1884–1886) contracted the *Postenlinie* to a smaller perimeter around [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] — approximately the same as that taken by Pel in [[1876]]. It was a continuous line forming a kind of isosceles triangle, with the post [[Lambaroe|Lambaroe]] as the apex and the sea coast from [[Oleh-leh|Oleh-leh]] to [[Kota Pohama|Kota Pohama]] as the base. A 1,000-metre wide, felled, open strip surrounded it; a *[[Ceintuur-trambaan|ceintuursporbaan]]* (belt railway) and telephone connected the posts. The concentrated *Postenlinie* was completed by [[July 1886|July]] [[1886]].

In the period of the concentration, the *Postenlinie* was the principal site of the daily guerrilla warfare between Dutch patrols and Atjehese *[[Muslemin|Muslemin]]*. The 1,000-metre open strip was soon largely overgrown; the Muslemin made the line unsafe, damaged the railway and bridges, destroyed the telephone line, fired on the trains, and made raids inward. Under [[Deijkerhoff|Deijkerhoff]]'s governorship (1892–1896), the arming of [[Teuku Umar|Toekoe Oemar]] as *[[Panglima Prang Besar|Panglima Prang Besar]]* created what was in effect a "second line" of temporary posts outside the original *Postenlinie*, occupied by Toekoe Oemar's auxiliaries. Its dissolution at the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (March 1896)|defection of 29 March 1896]] was the immediate cause of the crisis that brought [[Vetter|Vetter]] as *Regeeringscommissaris*.
