**volksschooltje** (English: "village school") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Small village school in the towns along the road.

## Translation

village school

## Description

Small village school in the towns along the road. School buildings smell 'onfrissche luchtjes' (unfresh smells) - hygiene is something the Atjehner still needs to be taught. Hygiene education will only show visible results in a next generation.

## Significance

The term *volksschooltje* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
