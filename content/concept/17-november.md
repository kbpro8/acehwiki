**[[17 November]]** is the date of the second and concluding sitting of the [[Atjeh debate in the Tweede Kamer (16-17 November 1897)|Atjeh debate]] in the [[Tweede Kamer]] in [[1897]], and of the division in which [[Van Kol's motion of 16 November 1897|Van Kol's motion]] on the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] was rejected by seventy-two votes to three.

## The sitting of [[17 November]] [[1897]]

The Minister of Colonies [[J. T. Cremer|Cremer]] opened by placing on record the [[Verklaringen van de kustvorsten van Atjeh|declarations of the sixty-two coastal princes]] of [[Acheh|Atjeh]], an observation he had omitted the previous afternoon and which he judged indispensable to a right understanding of the Dutch position.

[[H. van Kol]] replied with the speech printed under the headings *Nog eens de zeeroof*, *Onze volkenrechtelijke stelling*, *Ons prestige in Indië*, *Waarom niet uit Java?*, *Atjeh onneembaar*, *De belanghebbenden* and *De conclusie*, setting out in detail the [[Atrocities in the Aceh War as alleged in the Tweede Kamer (1897)|atrocities]], the doctrine of the [[Kring van belang|kring van belang]], the prophecy of [[Toekoe Baid]], and the list of commercial beneficiaries of the war.

[[Abraham Kuyper|Kuyper]] answered [[Van der Zwaag]], [[Pieter Jelles Troelstra|Troelstra]] and Van Kol, invoking the impoverishment that the loss of the colonies would bring, the Russian legend of the two heavenly beings, and the answer of the Christus concerning the penny and the image of Caesar. [[Ketelaar]] rose to withdraw the support he had given the motion. [[Pieter Jelles Troelstra|Troelstra]] replied to the minister's jest about Russian and Swiss professors and was twice called to order by the [[Voorzitter der Tweede Kamer|President]]. Cremer answered briefly on the question of cruelties, quoting [[Mr. J. H. Bergsma|Bergsma]]'s statement of [[22 September]] [[1896]].

The general deliberation and the deliberation on the motion of order were then closed, and the motion was put and rejected by seventy-two votes to three, the three being [[H. van Kol|Van Kol]], [[Pieter Jelles Troelstra|Troelstra]] and [[Van der Zwaag]].
