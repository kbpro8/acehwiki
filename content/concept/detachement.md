**detachement** (English: "military detachment") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Military garrison.

## Translation

military detachment

## Description

Military garrison. Example: [[Samalanga]], a detachement under a kapitein (captain).

## Significance

The term *detachement* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
