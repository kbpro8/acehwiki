**adat / adatwoning** (English: "customary law/tradition / traditional house") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Adat: customary law/tradition.

## Translation

customary law/tradition / traditional house

## Description

[[Adat]]: customary law/tradition. Adatwoning: traditional house of the [[zelfbestuurder]] (self-administrator). Some old [[Adat|adat]] houses still decorated with beautiful wood carving inside and out.

## Significance

The term *adat / adatwoning* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
