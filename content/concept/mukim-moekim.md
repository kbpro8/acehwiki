**mukim / moekim** (English: "Mukim (Friday-prayer congregation / territorial unit)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Originally a 'Vrijdagskring' (Friday-prayer circle) requiring at least 40 male free adult Muslims (moekims) for a valid Friday service in a meuseugit.

## Translation

[[Mukim]] (Friday-prayer congregation / territorial unit)

## Description

Originally a '[[Vrijdagskring]]' (Friday-prayer circle) requiring at least 40 male free adult Muslims (moekims) for a valid [[Friday service (Jum'at)|Friday service]] in a [[meuseugit]]. Evolved into a territorial unit comprising several gampongs, headed by an [[imeum]]. Self-standing mukims exist alongside oelèëbalangschappen; some owe their independence to wakeuh-land declarations by [[Sultan of Aceh|the Sultan]].

## Significance

The term *mukim / moekim* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
