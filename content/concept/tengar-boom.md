**Tengar boom** (English: "Tengar tree") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Tree whose bark is important for tanning fish nets.

## Translation

Tengar tree

## Description

Tree whose bark is important for tanning fish nets.

## Significance

The term *Tengar boom* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
