The **Heerengracht** was the name given by the garrison of [[Kroeng Gloempang]] in [[Groot-Atjeh]] to the long passage between the barracks at the end of which the officers were quartered — a joke on the principal patrician canal of Amsterdam.

## The inscription

[[G. B. Hooijer]] read the soldiers' facetiousness from the plate of the interior of the post: *het Heerengracht aan het einde van den langen gang tusschen de barakken, duidde aan, dat daar de officieren gelegerd waren, waaronder wij rechts Kapitein van der Wedden en links luitenant Schadée onderkennen kunnen* — the *Heerengracht* at the end of the long passage between the barracks indicated that the officers were quartered there, among whom [[Kapitein Van der Wedden|Van der Wedden]] could be recognised on the right and [[Kapitein Schadée|Schadée]] on the left.

A neighbouring inscription, *Japan 5 gulden een flesch portwijn*, referred to the dwelling of some Japanese women established at [[Oleh-leh]]; and above the entrance of the post was posted *Welkom in de restauratie van volkspijs*.
