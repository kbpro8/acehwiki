**Atjeh-moorden** (English: "Aceh murders") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Chapter IX title in the book (page 13): 'Atjeh-moorden' - about the phenomenon of murders (likely amok/religious killings) in Aceh.

## Translation

Aceh murders

## Description

Chapter IX title in the book (page 13): 'Atjeh-moorden' - about the phenomenon of murders (likely amok/religious killings) in Aceh. Content not in pages 1-70.

## Significance

The term *Atjeh-moorden* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
