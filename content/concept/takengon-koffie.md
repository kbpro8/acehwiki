**Takengon-koffie** (English: "Takengon coffee (Arabica)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Superior, well-paid Arabica coffee grown in the Takengonsche at higher altitudes; sold as dessert coffee in Europe and to enhance aroma of ground coffee mélanges.

## Translation

[[Takengon]] coffee (Arabica)

## Description

Superior, well-paid [[Arabica Coffee (Aceh)|Arabica coffee]] grown in the Takengonsche at higher altitudes; sold as dessert coffee in [[Europe]] and to enhance aroma of ground coffee mélanges.

## Significance

The term *Takengon-koffie* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
