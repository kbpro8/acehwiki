The **administrative division of Atjeh of [[1908]]** was the reorganisation of [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] carried out by [[H. N. A. Swart]] on assuming the governorship, and the framework within which the progressive substitution of civil for military administrators took place between [[1909]] and [[1918]].

## The division of [[1908]]

Five *afdeelingen* were formed, each under an Assistant Resident:

- [[Groot Atjeh]];
- the [[Noordkust van Atjeh|Noordkust]];
- the [[Oostkust van Atjeh|Oostkust]];
- the [[Westkust van Atjeh|Westkust]];
- [[Bambél]], comprising the [[Gajò Loeös]], the [[Alaslanden]] and [[Singkel]].

In *afdeelingen* where it already proved possible, a *controleur* conducted the administration — among others at [[Idi]] and [[Singkel]]. [[Sabang]] came under a "burgerlijken" civil authority.

## The change of [[1909]]

The following year already brought a change; the following sub-divisions could then be placed under civil administrators: the *[[sagi]]* of the [[XXII Moekims]], [[Tamiang]], [[Serbödjadi]], [[Poelò Raja]], [[Tapa' Toean]] and the separately administered territory of the [[VII Moekims Pidië]] — "that is to say, [[Groot Atjeh]] had by then reached the point where civil administration could be introduced there everywhere."

## The change of [[1910]]

In [[1910]] the *afdeeling* [[Bambél]] was abolished; [[Singkel]] was joined to the Westkust, while the [[Gajò Loeös]] and the [[Alaslanden]] became independent *afdeelingen*, each under an officer-*gezaghebber*.

## Progressive de-militarisation

The substitution continued through the governorship. In July [[1911]] the *controleur* [[G. J. van Dongen]] took over the administration of the sub-division [[Pidië]] from the civil authority, the *marechaussée* captain [[W. A. Herold]]; by [[1912]] the garrison of Pidië could be reduced by two companies. [[Lho' Seumawè]] had likewise been under a *controleur* for a considerable time.

[[I. Feenstra]] summarised the whole process at Swart's departure: "Slowly but surely the corps of administrative officials of Atjeh has been reshaped. The lieutenants and captains who served as *controleurs* returned to the ranks and were replaced by civil officials; the majors who acted as heads of divisions gave way to assistant residents."

## Completion

The process was completed only long after Swart. On [[7 March]] [[1936]] Governor [[Van Aken]], handing over to Resident [[J. Jongejans]], declared that he considered the sub-divisions [[Alaslanden]], [[Lam Meulò]], [[Meulaboh]] and [[Tapa' Toean]] — the last still governed by officers — "made entirely ripe … for civil administration," and that only considerations of economy had restrained him from proposing it. [[Maurice Henri du Croo|Du Croo]] called that "the keystone of the pacification — eighteen years after Swart's retirement."
