**Hars** (English: "Rosin") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Solid resin remaining after distilling balsem; packed in 100 KG kisten; goes mainly to Java for batikkerijen, zeepfabrieken, papierfabriek Padalarang.

## Translation

Rosin

## Description

Solid [[Resin (Aceh)|resin]] remaining after distilling [[Balsem|balsem]]; packed in 100 KG kisten; goes mainly to [[Madiun and Surabaya|Java]] for batikkerijen, zeepfabrieken, papierfabriek [[Padalarang]].

## Significance

The term *Hars* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
