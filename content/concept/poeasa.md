**poeasa** (English: "fasting (Ramadan)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Annual fasting prescription; fairly strictly observed.

## Translation

fasting (Ramadan)

## Description

Annual fasting prescription; fairly strictly observed. On days before and at the end of the fasting period, beef is universally consumed; ~15-day supply conserved with vinegar, salt and spices.

## Significance

The term *poeasa* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
