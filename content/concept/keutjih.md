**Keutjih** (*Keuntjih*) was traditionally the local Acehnese title for a village headman (*keuchik*), responsible for village administration and civil affairs under an [[Uleebelang]].

## Marechaussee Jargon Usage

In the internal terminology of the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]], the term *Keuntjih* was used informally to denote squad-level Corporals (*korporaal*).
