**Atjeh-filuren** (English: "Aceh filleting/murders (chapter heading only)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Chapter VIII title in the book (page 13): 'Atjeh-filuren' - presumably about the violent robberies/attacks characteristic of Aceh in certain periods.

## Translation

Aceh filleting/murders (chapter heading only)

## Description

Chapter VIII title in the book (page 13): 'Atjeh-filuren' - presumably about the violent robberies/attacks characteristic of Aceh in certain periods. Content not in pages 1-70.

## Significance

The term *Atjeh-filuren* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
