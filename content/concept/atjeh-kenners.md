**Atjeh-kenners** (English: "Aceh experts") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. People who know Aceh well; self-proclaimed experts who, per Jongejans, often spread the most fantastic tales about Aceh, contributing to its bad reputation.

## Translation

Aceh experts

## Description

People who know Aceh well; self-proclaimed experts who, per [[Resident Jongejans|Jongejans]], often spread the most fantastic tales about Aceh, contributing to its bad reputation. [[Resident Jongejans|Jongejans]] distances himself from the long-time 'Atjeh-kenner' category and the '[[adoration mutuelle]]' among European cliques.

## Significance

The term *Atjeh-kenners* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
