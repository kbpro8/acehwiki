**The Kroeëng Meureubo** is a Westkust van Atjeh river whose *brongebied* lies in the *groote Westelijke lengtedal* and which breaks through the Westelijke Barisan to reach the Indian Ocean north of the [[Kroeëng Wojla|Kroeëng Wojla]] delta. In the [[De Buitenbezittingen, Deel II, Aflevering 1|Encyclopaedisch Bureau's Sumatra survey]] (1917), the Kroeëng Meureubo is one of the two principal rivers (with the [[Kroeëng Seunagan|Kroeëng Seunagan]]) that drain the *groote Westelijke lengtedal* to the Westkust between the [[Kroeëng Wojla|Kroeëng Wojla]] to the south and the [[Kroeëng Teunom|Kroeëng Teunom]] to the north.

## Course

The Bureau's Hoofdstuk I describes the Kroeëng Meureubo as one of the two rivers (with the Kroeëng Seunagan) "wier brongebieden gelegen zijn in het groote Westelijke lengtedal. Zij doorbreken beide dus de Westelijke Barisanketen." The Kroeëng Meureubo thus belongs to the same hydrographical category as the [[Kroeëng Wojla|Kroeëng Wojla]], the [[Kroeëng Teunom|Kroeëng Teunom]] and the [[Lao Alas|Lao Alas]]: a river whose *brongebied* lies in the *groote Westelijke lengtedal* and whose lower course breaks through the Westelijke Barisan to the Indian Ocean.

## Significance

The Kroeëng Meureubo is, in the Bureau's Sumatra survey, one of the two principal rivers (with the Kroeëng Seunagan) that drain the *groote Westelijke lengtedal* between the Kroeëng Wojla and the Kroeëng Teunom. The Bureau's emphasis on the Kroeëng Meureubo's *doorbreking* of the Westelijke Barisan frames the river as the hydrographical artery of the central Westkust — the river whose *brongebied* sustained the Dutch *bivak* at [[Meulaboh|Meulaboh]] and whose lower course defined the strategic *kustvlakte* of the central Westkust.

In the [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|staff history's]] Atjeh chapter, the Kroeëng Meureubo's *kustvlakte* — at [[Meulaboh|Meulaboh]] — is the site of the principal Dutch Westkust *bivak*, and the southern destination of the [[1ste Garnizoensbataljon|1ste Garnizoensbataljon]]'s Meulaboh detachment.
