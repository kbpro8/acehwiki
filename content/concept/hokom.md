**hokom** (English: "Holy Law (Islamic law)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Domain represented by the teungkoe meunasah in the gampong, in contrast to the adat represented by the keutjhi'.

## Translation

Holy Law (Islamic law)

## Description

Domain represented by the [[teungkoe meunasah]] in the [[Gampong|gampong]], in contrast to the [[Adat|adat]] represented by the [[keutjhi']].

## Significance

The term *hokom* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
