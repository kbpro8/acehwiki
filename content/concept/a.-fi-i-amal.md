**A. fi 'I-a'mãl** was an Acehnese‑Malay lexical item recorded on page 93 of the *Malay translation of the Burda*, glossed as “tanam, ‑an ‘amal saleh, de tuin der goede werken” (the garden of good deeds). The phrase combines the Arabic **‘amal** (عمل) “deed” with a local suffix, denoting a cultivated space of charitable activity.

## Cultural Significance

The term was used in moral exhortations encouraging the performance of benevolent acts, likening them to planting a garden.
