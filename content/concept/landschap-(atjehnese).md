**The Landschap** — the Atjehnese *landschap* or *oeléebalangschap* — was the principal territorial and political unit of pre-colonial and early colonial Atjeh, governed by an *[[Oeléebalang|oeléebalang]]* with his own *zelfbestuur* (self-government) under the suzerainty of the Dutch government. The *landschap* is the unit of account for the pepper-trade, the petroleum-cijns, the *hacil*, the *pangkai*, and the *Korte Verklaring (1899)|Korte Verklaring* of [[1899]]. Each *landschap* has its own *landschapskas* (treasury) and its own *begrooting* (budget); the *begrooting* of the associated *landschappen* of the *afdeeling* [[Pidië|Pidië]] for [[1923]] goes over an amount of more than f 457.103. Among the expenditures the salaries take a wide place: there are *oeléebalangs* who receive 100, 200 guilders monthly, the highest-paid receiving 500 guilders; an army of *oelama's* and *imeums* receives 10 and 15 guilders monthly.

## The *Landschap* and the Pepper-Planters
When pepper-planters from elsewhere settled in a *landschap*, new *oudtten* arose to authority-bearing chiefs. A fragmentation of authority could occasionally be convenient for the Dutch administration, but as a rule it caused difficulty and delay in the application of measures. In [[Lho' Seumawé|Lho' Seumawé]] the fragmentation went so far that there came to be too many chiefs and petty chiefs. The *landschap* Peureula (Perlak), by contrast, is an old Atjehnese settlement and the pepper-cultivation is of old date there.
