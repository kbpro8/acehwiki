**Voetrot** (English: "Foot rot (pepper disease)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Most destructive disease of pepper plants; destroyed whole complexes.

## Translation

Foot rot (pepper disease)

## Description

Most destructive disease of pepper plants; destroyed whole complexes. [[Banka]] variëteit tried—initial success then killed off within weeks. [[Lampongers|Lampong]] variëteit more resistant but discovered too late.

## Significance

The term *Voetrot* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
