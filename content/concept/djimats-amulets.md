## Description

Amulets given by revered oelamas (e.g. [[Teungkoe di Tjot Plieëng]]), bearing their seal; found on many fallen Acehnese.

## Significance

The term *Djimats (amulets)* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
