**repressief optreden** (English: "repressive military action") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Dutch military posture of responding to resistance with repression.

## Translation

repressive military action

## Description

Dutch military posture of responding to [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] with repression. Characteristic of the period up to and including [[Gouverneur Swart|Swart]]'s early governorship ([[1908]]-[[1918]]), then transitioning to preventive action.

## Significance

The term *repressief optreden* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
