**Poeloe Bras** — modern *Pulau Bras* / *Pulau Breueh*, the largest of the Banyak islands off the [[Westkust van Atjeh|Westkust van Atjeh]] north of [[Singkel|Singkel]] — was the site of the Dutch-built *vuurtoren* (lighthouse) described in the [[De Kleine Oosterling|school-textbook]] narrative of the late 19th century *opbouw* of Atjeh, and the principal Dutch navigational aid on the Westkust of Atjeh. In the [[De Kleine Oosterling|Wijma school reader]], Poeloe Bras is the staff history's principal instance of the Dutch colonial state's *opbouw* of the Westkust's maritime infrastructure, and the principal Dutch *vuurtoren* of the Atjeh coast.

## The vuurtoren

The [[De Kleine Oosterling|Wijma school reader]] records the construction of the vuurtoren at Poeloe Bras as part of the school reader's account of the *opbouw* of Atjeh after the [[Capture of the Kraton (24 January 1874)|capture of the kraton of 24 January 1874]]: "Reeds thans is er in Atjeh vrijwat veranderd. Wij hebben op het eiland Poeloe Bras een vuurtoren gebouwd, en daaraan hebben reeds vele schepen met hun bemanning het behoud te danken." The vuurtoren at Poeloe Bras was thus a Dutch-built installation on the island, and its construction is the school reader's principal instance of the Dutch *opbouw* of the Westkust's maritime infrastructure.

The school reader's emphasis on the *behoud* (preservation/salvation) of *vele schepen met hun bemanning* — many ships with their crews — frames the Poeloe Bras vuurtoren as the staff history's principal instance of the Dutch colonial state's *zorg* (care) for the maritime safety of the Atjeh coast, set against the *fanatiek* resistance of the Atjeh-oorlog.
