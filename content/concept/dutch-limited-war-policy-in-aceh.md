**Dutch limited-war policy in Aceh** was the political and military approach advocated by [[Jan van Swieten]] for [[Aceh]]: obtain security and acknowledged Dutch supremacy without immediate direct administration or indiscriminate destruction.

## Use in the source

Van Swieten attributed the original approach to discussions with [[I. D. Fransen van de Putte]] and [[James Loudon]]. He held that Acehnese self-defence should not be punished by burning settlements or devastating fields, and that the Dutch should let non-belligerent people distinguish themselves from war leaders. The argument is a primary-source position, not an objective description of Dutch policy.

## Significance

Understanding this term is necessary for reading the political relationships recorded during the [[Aceh War]].
