**Dosia** is the Acehnese word for “sin” or “transgression”. It is listed on [[81]] with the gloss “zonde”. The term appears in moral verses of the *Burda* of al‑Bu‑s‑iri, where it is contrasted with *berkat* (blessing) and *taqwa* (piety).

Examples from the source include “dosia, zondig (A. ‘as1)” and “bagai‑… allerlei zonden (A. al‑sisyān)”.

## Moral usage

In Acehnese Islamic teaching, *dosia* denotes actions that separate the believer from divine favor, and the *Burda* frequently exhorts readers to avoid *dosia* and seek *berkat*.
