**K.M.** (English: "kilometer (marker)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Kilometer markers along the Gajoweg; e.g., K.M.

## Translation

kilometer (marker)

## Description

Kilometer markers along the [[Gajoweg (Gayo Road)|Gajoweg]]; e.g., K.M. 36 (border [[Bireuën]]-[[Takengon]]), K.M. 46 ([[Blang Rakal]]), K.M. 75 ([[Lampahan]]), K.M. 95 (highest point [[1485]] m), K.M. 100 (woudgrens).

## Significance

The term *K.M.* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
