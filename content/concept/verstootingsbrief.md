**verstootingsbrief** (English: "divorce / repudiation letter") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Letter a husband sends to the village authorities announcing he has repudiated his wife; the wife need not be present.

## Translation

divorce / [[Talaq (Ach. taleuë)|repudiation]] letter

## Description

Letter a husband sends to the village authorities announcing he has repudiated his wife; the wife need not be present.

## Significance

The term *verstootingsbrief* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
