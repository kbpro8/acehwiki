**afdeeling** (English: "administrative division (residency sub-unit)[[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Major administrative division of a Dutch colonial residency.

## Translation

administrative division (residency sub-unit)

## Description

Major administrative division of a Dutch colonial residency. Aceh ([[Residentie Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Residentie Atjeh]]) contains afdeelingen such as Atjeh's [[Noordkust]], [[Oostkust van Atjeh]], [[Westkust van Atjeh]]Major administrative division of the colonial[[gewest]] Atjeh, e.g., Afdeeling Noord- en [[Westkust van Atjeh]], Afdeeling [[Westkust van Atjeh]]Colonial administrative division; Atjeh has four:[[Groot-Atjeh]], [[Noordkust]], [[Atjeh's Oostkust (East Coast of Aceh)|Oostkust]], [[Westkust]].

## Significance

The term *afdeeling* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
