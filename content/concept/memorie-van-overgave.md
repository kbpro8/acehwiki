**Memorie van Overgave** was the memorandum of transfer drawn up by a departing Netherlands Indies administrator for his successor.

The *Memorie van Overgave* of [[H. N. A. Swart]]'s first civil successor contained the sentence that [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]] set out to refute: "everything in Atjeh that walks about on two legs hates the European." Du Croo's fifth summary proposition held that this was "in its generality a gross exaggeration and untrue — everything depends on the person."
