**Geconcentreerde linie / Concentratie** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Dutch defensive line policy of 1886-1893 (7 years) limiting Dutch control to a perimeter around Koetaradja; its failure was demonstrated and the policy was abandoned.

## Description

Dutch defensive line policy of [[1886]]-[[1893]] (7 years) limiting Dutch control to a perimeter around [[Koetaradja]]; its failure was demonstrated and the policy was abandoned. The [[Korps Marechaussée]] was founded ([[2 April]] [[1890]]) to do police work within this stelling.

## Significance

The term *Geconcentreerde linie / Concentratie* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
