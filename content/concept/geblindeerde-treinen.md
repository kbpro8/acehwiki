**geblindeerde treinen** (English: "Armored trains") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Used to test newly laid sections of the Atjeh-tram through hostile territory.

## Translation

Armored trains

## Description

Used to test newly laid sections of the [[Atjeh-tram]] through hostile territory.

## Significance

The term *geblindeerde treinen* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
