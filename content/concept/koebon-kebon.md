**koebon / kebon** (English: "plantation / estate (small)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Plantation — the estates founded on Atjeh's tertiaire gronden are called 'Kebons' and are less profitable than those on Sumatra's Oostkust.

## Translation

plantation / estate (small)

## Description

Plantation — the estates founded on Atjeh's tertiaire gronden are called 'Kebons' and are less profitable than those on [[Sumatra's East Coast|Sumatra's Oostkust]].

## Significance

The term *koebon / kebon* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
