**Moenkar en Nakir** (English: "Munkar and Nakir, angels of the grave") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Two fearsome angels who visit the dead in the grave immediately after burial to interrogate them on the true faith; they pre-torment those who cannot answer.

## Translation

[[Munkar and Nakir]], [[Munkar and Nakir|angels of the grave]]

## Description

Two fearsome angels who visit the dead in the grave immediately after burial to interrogate them on the true faith; they pre-torment those who cannot answer. Only those who died in the faith can answer.

## Significance

The term *Moenkar en Nakir* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
