**Oud-atjehsch recht / staatsrecht** (English: "Old Acehnese law / state law") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Ancient Acehnese legal tradition, including the bride-price (bruidsschat) institution.

## Translation

Old Acehnese law / state law

## Description

Ancient Acehnese legal tradition, including the bride-price ([[bruidsschat]]) institution. To reconcile the institution of a king with existing legal customs, the Atjehnese conceived the land/realm/nation as a 'bride' married to a 'bridegroom' (the king) who had to pay a '[[bruidsschat]]' (bride wealth). Had real legal consequences and persisted until the Dutch conquest.

## Significance

The term *Oud-atjehsch recht / staatsrecht* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
