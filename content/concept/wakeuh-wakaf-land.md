**wakeuh / wakaf-land** (English: "Religious endowment land") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Land withdrawn from ordinary traffic and dedicated to a purpose permitted by Islamic law.

## Translation

Religious endowment land

## Description

Land withdrawn from ordinary traffic and dedicated to a purpose permitted by Islamic law. Many self-standing mukims owe their independence to a [[sultan|Sultan]]'s wakeuh-declaration of their territory (likely for political reasons).

## Significance

The term *wakeuh / wakaf-land* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
