**Alstonia scholaris** is a tropical tree known locally in Aceh as *pulahi* or “kaju gabus”. The species is described on page [[90]] of the *Burda* translation, noting its striking red blossoms.

## Botanical Description
A fast‑growing evergreen, *Alstonia scholaris* produces fragrant white flowers; the text mentions a “fraai rood van kleur, doch on‑welriekend” (beautiful red colour, but not fragrant).

## Cultural Use
The wood was traditionally used for construction and boat‑building in Aceh, while the tree’s medicinal bark featured in local remedies.
