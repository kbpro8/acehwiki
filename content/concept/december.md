**[[December]]** is a calendar month used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention. In the material drawn from *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]* it is associated with the [[Capture of Anagaloeng by Toekoe Djohan (20–21 December 1893)|capture of Anagaloeng]] on [[20 December]]–[[21 December]] [[1893]].
