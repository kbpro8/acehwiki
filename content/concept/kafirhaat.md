**kafirhaat** (English: "hatred of infidels") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Acehnese hatred of the infidel (i.e., the Dutch), said to be deep-rooted; much written about by experts, especially Snouck Hurgronje.

## Translation

hatred of infidels

## Description

Acehnese hatred of the infidel (i.e., the Dutch), said to be deep-rooted; much written about by experts, especially [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]].

## Significance

The term *kafirhaat* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
