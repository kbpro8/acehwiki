The **Eeresabel** — the sabre of honour — was a Netherlands-Indies military distinction conferred for gallantry, and one of the three classes of award recorded in the *[[Overzicht van Onderscheidingen toegekend aan het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden (1890-1930)|register of honours]]* of the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* printed in the [[1930]] jubilee number, alongside the *[[Militaire Willemsorde|Militaire Willems-Orde]]* and the *[[Orde van Oranje-Nassau]]*.

## Recipients in the Maréchaussée register

The register records the Eeresabel as conferred upon the following members of the corps:

- [[1897]] — Kapt. Mar. [[W. B. J. A. Scheepens]]
- [[1898]] — 1ste Luits. Mar. [[K. J. C. Rijnen]] and [[F. Darlang]]
- [[1899]] — 1ste Luit. Mar. [[H. Houtzagers]]
- [[1900]] — Eur. Sergt. Mar. [[H. M. H. den Rooijen]], Alg. Stb. No. 27367
- [[1902]] — Onderluit. der Mar. [[A. H. E. von und zu Egloffstein]], Alg. Stb. No. 43741
- [[1903]] — 1ste Luit. Mar. [[Vastenou|J. H. Ch. Vastenou]]
- [[1904]] — 1ste Luit. Mar. [[Campioni|M. J. B. H. Campioni]]
- [[1905]] — 1ste Luit. Mar. [[J. E. W. van Gesseler Verschuir]]
- [[1905]] — 1ste Luits. [[J. W. Ebbink]] and [[H. T. Aukes]]
- [[1906]] — 1ste Luit. Mar. [[G. F. B. Watrin]]
- [[1908]] — 1ste Luit. Mar. [[J. van Braam Morris]]
- [[1911]] — 1ste Luit. Mar. [[H. J. Schmidt]]
- [[1912]] — Kapt. Mar. [[H. Behrens]], since promoted major

## Significance

The Eeresabel occupied a position below the Militaire Willems-Orde in the hierarchy of Netherlands-Indies military distinctions, and in several cases in the Maréchaussée register the same officer received first the sabre and later the knight's cross — as with [[F. Darlang]], who received the Eeresabel in [[1898]] and the Militaire Willems-Orde 4th class in [[1904]], and [[H. J. Schmidt]], Eeresabel in [[1911]] and knight 4th class in [[1909]] and 3rd class in [[1913]].
