**Atjeh-Trammemeje** (English: "The Atjeh Tram (local nickname)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Local nickname for the Atjeh-Staatsspoor, evoking old-style travel.

## Translation

The [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh Tram]] (local nickname)

## Description

Local nickname for the Atjeh-Staatsspoor, evoking old-style travel.

## Significance

The term *Atjeh-Trammemeje* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
