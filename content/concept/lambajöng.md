**Lambajöng** (English: "Dark purple color in ikat") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Characteristic dark purple color of antique Acehnese kain (textile), typically interwoven with silver thread.

## Translation

Dark purple color in [[Ikatten|ikat]]

## Description

Characteristic dark purple color of antique Acehnese kain (textile), typically interwoven with silver thread.

## Significance

The term *Lambajöng* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
