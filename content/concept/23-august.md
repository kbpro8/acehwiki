**[[23 August]]** is the date on which, in [[1933]], the ophthalmologist [[J. Tijssen|Dr. J. Tijssen]] came again to [[Koeta Radja]] and travelled thence to [[Bakongan]], beginning the tour of the [[Atjeh's Westkust|West Coast of Atjeh]] recorded in *[[Een strijd in Atjeh (1934)]]*.

The account reads: "Den 23en Augustus van dit jaar kwam Tijssen opnieuw in Kota Radja en reisde van daar door naar Bakongan, land, waarvan men zich den naam herinnert door het verzet van [[1925]] en '26." — On the 23rd of August of this year Tijssen came again to Kota Radja and travelled thence to Bakongan, a land whose name is remembered for the resistance of 1925 and '26.

At Koeta Radja he took counsel with the governor [[A. Ph. van Aken|Van Aken]], who wished him to go this time to the West Coast. The journey that followed is described at [[Tijssen's journey to the West Coast of Atjeh and Simaloer (1933)]]; it produced two hundred and forty-seven treatments for eye disease, twenty-four cataract operations, and the series of the [[Seven juvenile cataracts of Atjeh's Westkust|seven juvenile cataracts]] between [[Meulaboh]] and [[Singkel]].
