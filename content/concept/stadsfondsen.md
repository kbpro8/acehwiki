**Stadsfondsen** (English: "Municipal funds / councils") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Bodies managing most of the great markets at onderafdeelingsplaatsen; comparable to small municipal councils.

## Translation

Municipal funds / councils

## Description

Bodies managing most of the great markets at onderafdeelingsplaatsen; comparable to small municipal councils.

## Significance

The term *Stadsfondsen* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
