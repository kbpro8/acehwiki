**Prophet Muhammad** (c. 570 – 632 CE) is the founder of Islam and the central figure of the religion, revered throughout the Muslim world, including the Sultanate of [[Acheh]]. The 16th‑century Malay translation of the **Burda of al‑Busiri** (see [[Malay translation of the Burda (16th century)]]) portrays him as “the lord of both worlds, master of jinn and humanity” (page 54) and emphasizes his role as the ultimate intercessor against *shaitan* (page 53).

### Significance in Aceh
- **Spiritual Model**: Acehnese *ulama* and Sufi orders invoked the Prophet’s virtues in sermons and *dhikr* gatherings.
- **Literary Inspiration**: The *Burda*’s verses praising the Prophet were memorised in Acehnese *madrasahs* and recited during *maulid* festivals.
- **Political Legitimacy**: Acehnese sultans claimed descent from the Prophet’s spiritual lineage to bolster their authority.
