**peureumadani** (English: "carpet / tapestry") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Carpet laid over mats during festivities.

## Translation

carpet / tapestry

## Description

Carpet laid over mats during festivities.

## Significance

The term *peureumadani* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
