**Assistent-Resident** (English: "Assistant Resident (Dutch colonial rank)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Colonial administrative rank; the Assistent-Resident at Takengon had to drive ~750 km via Langsa–Medan–Kaban Djahé to visit the remote parts (Gajo Loeös, Alaslanden, Serbödjadi) of his ressort before the 1934 administrative redivision.

## Translation

Assistant Resident (Dutch colonial rank)

## Description

Colonial administrative rank; the Assistent-Resident at [[Takengon]] had to drive ~750 km via [[Langsar|Langsa]]–[[Medan]]–[[Kaban Djahé]] to visit the remote parts ([[Gajo Loeös]], [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]], [[Serbödjadi]]) of his [[ressort]] before the [[1934]] administrative redivision.

## Significance

The term *Assistent-Resident* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
