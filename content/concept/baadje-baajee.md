**baadje / baajee** (English: "jacket") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Men's jacket; barely a necessary garment for men.

## Translation

jacket

## Description

Men's jacket; barely a necessary garment for men. Bovenlanders (highlanders) often go bare-chested with the jacket over the shoulder. Most common: wide white or black jacket with short sleeves; silk (yellow preferred) for festive wear.

## Significance

The term *baadje / baajee* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
