**Boschreserve** (English: "Forest reserve") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Forest reserved for hydrological reasons or exploitation regulation; sufficient reserves established except in Groot-Atjeh (too late) and large blang terrains of Takengonsche/Blangkedjerensche.

## Translation

Forest reserve

## Description

Forest reserved for hydrological reasons or exploitation regulation; sufficient reserves established except in [[Groot-Atjeh]] (too late) and large [[Blang|blang]] terrains of Takengonsche/Blangkedjerensche.

## Significance

The term *Boschreserve* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
