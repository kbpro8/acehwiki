**Remilling bedrijf** (English: "Rubber reprocessing factory") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Government-licensed factories that reprocess wet bevolkingsproduct (slabs) into more preservation-resistant dry product (sheets); Aceh has two at Langsa (capacities 500 & 200 ton/year).

## Translation

Rubber reprocessing factory

## Description

Government-licensed factories that reprocess wet bevolkingsproduct ([[Slabs|slabs]]) into more preservation-resistant dry product ([[Sheets|sheets]]); Aceh has two at [[Langsar|Langsa]] (capacities 500 & 200 ton/year).

## Significance

The term *Remilling bedrijf* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
