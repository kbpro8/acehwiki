**The Depèq** was the dry wind of the [[Gajo-land|Gajo country]] in the months April–October, which sometimes rose to storm-force. The *depèq* is the principal meteorological feature of the Gajo highlands, and the principal obstacle to market-gardening at [[Takengon|Takengon]] and the surrounding country in those months. The Gajos must be able to protect their gardens against the *depèq*, otherwise the young plants are damaged. The *depèq* sometimes grows so strong that the praws of fishermen on [[Laoet Tawar|Laut Tawar]] must quickly take shelter.
