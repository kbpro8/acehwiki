**sawah / ladang** (English: "irrigated rice field / dry (swidden) rice field") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Two types of rice cultivation in Aceh; the Atjeher can work systematically and persistently on both when necessary.

## Translation

[[Sawah|irrigated rice field]] / dry (swidden) rice field

## Description

Two types of rice cultivation in Aceh; the [[Atjeh (Aceh)|Atjeher]] can work systematically and persistently on both when necessary.

## Significance

The term *sawah / ladang* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
