**Jinn** are supernatural beings in Islamic theology, mentioned in the Malay translation of the *Burda* as “the djinn’s cries” that accompany the birth of the prophetic messenger. The poem portrays them as part of the cosmic audience that witnesses the Prophet’s miracles.

## Role in the *Burda*
In verses 66–68 of the Malay text, the *jinn* are described as shouting to one another when the “gods’ messenger” is born, highlighting the universal recognition of the Prophet’s sanctity.

## Belief in Aceh
Acehnese *ulama* incorporated belief in *jinn* into legal rulings and exorcism practices. The *Burda*’s reference reinforced the notion that even unseen beings acknowledge the Prophet’s authority.
