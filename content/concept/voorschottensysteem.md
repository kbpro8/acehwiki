**voorschottensysteem** (English: "Advance (loan) system") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. System of providing advances à fonds perdu for house-building materials etc.

## Translation

Advance (loan) system

## Description

System of providing advances à fonds perdu for house-building materials etc. to new colonists; later abandoned.

## Significance

The term *voorschottensysteem* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
