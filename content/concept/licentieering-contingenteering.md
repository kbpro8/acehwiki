**Licentieering / Contingenteering** (English: "Licensing and quota system") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Government import controls to prevent guilder depreciation from inflating cost of living; imports remained nearly constant as a result.

## Translation

Licensing and quota system

## Description

Government import controls to prevent guilder depreciation from inflating cost of living; imports remained nearly constant as a result.

## Significance

The term *Licentieering / Contingenteering* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
