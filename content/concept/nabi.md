**nabi** is the Acehnese rendering of the Arabic *nabi* “prophet”. The term appears repeatedly in the *Burda* translation (pages [[88]]‑[[89]]), indicating its centrality in Acehnese Islamic literature.

## Definition
Refers to the messengers sent by God, especially the Prophet Muhammad, but also includes earlier prophets recognized in the Qur’an.

## Usage in Acehnese Texts
The glossary lists *nabi* with multiple citations (e.g., 35, 38, 43, 72, 94, 110, 146, 160), showing its frequent occurrence in verses, prayers, and didactic poems.

## Significance
The prominence of *nabi* reflects the deep reverence for prophetic authority in Acehnese religious culture, influencing law, education, and governance.
