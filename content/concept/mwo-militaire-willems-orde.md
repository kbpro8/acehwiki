**M.W.O. (Militaire Willems-Orde)** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Highest Dutch military honour; 43 Knights M.W.O.

## Description

Highest Dutch military honour; 43 Knights M.W.O. awarded to [[Marechaussée|marechaussée]] men in 40 years. [[J. C. Lamster|Lamster]] earned the knighthood 4th class in [[1898]] as 1st lieutenant.

## Significance

The term *M.W.O. (Militaire Willems-Orde)* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
