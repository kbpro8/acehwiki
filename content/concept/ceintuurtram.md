**De Ceintuurtram** (the Girdle Tramway) was the railway system connecting the Dutch military posts within the geconcentreerde stelling (concentrated position) in [[Groot-Atjeh]]. Operational by [[1885]], the system provided rapid movement of troops, supplies, and materials throughout the defensive perimeter.

## Description

The ceintuurtram consisted of:

### Ceintuurbaan (Girdle Line)

The main circular line connecting all posts from [[Kota-Pohama]] in the west through [[Lamjong]], [[Boekit-Karang]], [[Roempit]], [[Tjot-Iri]], [[Lampermej]], [[Lambaroe]], [[Lamrong]], [[Lampeneroet]], [[Ketapan-Doea]], [[Blang]], and [[Lamdjamoe]] to [[Lamtih]] in the south.

### Straallijnen (Radial Lines)

Two lines connecting to [[Kota-Radja]]:
- Line to [[Lambaroe]]
- Line to [[Ketapan-Doea]]

### Technical Specifications

- Gauge: 75cm
- Locomotive tractive power: approximately 100 tons
- Single track with necessary switches, turntables, and other accessories
- Earthworks wide enough to allow a crushed stone road alongside the track for other transport

## Purpose

The tramway served multiple military purposes:

### Rapid Reinforcement

Troops could be quickly moved from any post to any other post to reinforce threatened sections.

### Supply

Materials and supplies for construction and maintenance could be efficiently transported.

### Tactical Mobility

The system allowed the Dutch to concentrate force at any threatened point while maintaining distributed presence throughout the line.

## Development

### Initial Construction

Construction began immediately following the concentration decision of [[19 August]] [[1884]]. The line from [[Kota-Radja]] toward [[Ketapan-Doea]] was the priority to support construction of the southern posts.

### Progress by March 1885

By [[1 March]] [[1885]]:
- Both radial lines were completed
- The ceintuurbaan from [[Lamdjamoe]] to the river at [[Lampermej]] was completed
- The bridge over the [[Atjeh-rivier]] was nearly finished
- The earthworks to [[Lamjong]] were entirely ready

### Remaining Work

Work continued on:
- The southern section of the ceintuurbaan
- The tram line from [[Lambaroe]] to [[Lamjong]]
- Various radial roads

## Significance

The ceintuurtram represented significant investment in the infrastructure of the concentrated position. By providing reliable internal communications, the system addressed one of the key vulnerabilities of the previous scattered positions—the inability to quickly reinforce threatened points.
