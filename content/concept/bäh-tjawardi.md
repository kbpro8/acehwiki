**Bäh tjawardi** (English: "Cloisonné (inlaid enamel)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Acehnese technique of inlaying with differently colored enamels, a kind of cloisonné; also not mastered by Chinese smiths.

## Translation

Cloisonné (inlaid enamel)

## Description

Acehnese technique of inlaying with differently colored enamels, a kind of cloisonné; also not mastered by Chinese smiths.

## Significance

The term *Bäh tjawardi* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
