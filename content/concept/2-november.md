**[[2 November]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]* it marks the following events in the history of [[Groot-Atjeh]].

## Events on this date

- [[Assembly of the auxiliary troops at Masdjid Siem (2 November 1893)]] — about 2,400 Achehnese auxiliaries assembled under [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]] ([[1893]])

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

[[J. J. W. E. Verstege|Verstege]] mentions the treaty of this date in [[1871]] with England as the legal basis minister [[Willem Maurits de Brauw|de Brauw]] ([[27 January]] [[1886]]) claimed for his statement about the Dutch undivided sovereignty over Sumatra including Atjeh.
