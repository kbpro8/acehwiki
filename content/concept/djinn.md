**Djinn** is the Acehnese transcription of the Arabic *jinn*, referring to supernatural beings made of smokeless fire. It appears on [[82]] with the gloss “geest”. In the *Burda* of al‑Bu‑s‑iri, *djinn* are mentioned in verses that discuss the unseen world (*ghaib*) and the limits of human knowledge.

The term is used in folklore and religious discourse, often to caution against reliance on unseen forces.

## Theological context

Acehnese Islamic scholars treat *djinn* as part of the *ghaib* (unseen), acknowledging their existence while emphasizing that only Allah has power over them.
