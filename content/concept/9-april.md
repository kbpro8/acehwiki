**[[9 April]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]* it marks the following events in the history of [[Groot-Atjeh]].

## Events on this date

- [[Tjot Goeë]] relieved after eleven days of investment ([[1895]])
- [[Siege and relief of Biloel (March–April 1896)]] — a relief force reached [[Biloel]] and withdrew again on news that [[Teukoe Oemar]] was approaching ([[1896]])
