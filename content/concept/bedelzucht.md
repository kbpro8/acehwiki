**Bedelzucht** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Practice under Governor Swart of buying off malcontents with large sums; created a 'bedelzucht' still noticeable in the present day.

## Description

Practice under [[H.E. Swart|Governor Swart]] of buying off malcontents with large sums; created a 'bedelzucht' still noticeable in the present day. Current administration head cannot meet the demand, though large sums are still paid in toelagen, onderstanden, studiefondsen.

## Significance

The term *Bedelzucht* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
