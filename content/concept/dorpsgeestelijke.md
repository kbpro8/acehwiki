**dorpsgeestelijke** (English: "village religious official") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Village religious official involved in marriage ceremonies, naming of children, etc.

## Translation

[[Lébé|village religious official]]

## Description

[[Lébé|Village religious official]] involved in marriage ceremonies, naming of children, etc. Performs the Islamic marriage contract; receives notice of divorce.

## Significance

The term *dorpsgeestelijke* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
