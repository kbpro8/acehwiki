**rëdjö tjhi'** (English: "Oldest prince (in Gajo Loeës)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Head of the elder branch of the stam in the onderafdeeling Gajo Loeës; highest authority in the blah.

## Translation

Oldest prince (in [[Gajo-Loeüs|Gajo Loeës]])

## Description

Head of the elder branch of the stam in the [[onderafdeeling]] [[Gajo-Loeüs|Gajo Loeës]]; highest authority in the [[Blah|blah]]. Each rëdjö tjhi' is assisted by an [[imöm]] and a [[Wakil|wakil]].

## Significance

The term *rëdjö tjhi'* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
