**The Banta** was the deputy of an *[[Oeléebalang|oeléebalang]]* in an Atjehnese *landschap*, typically the eldest of the brothers or relatives of the chief, who acted as regent during the *oeléebalang*'s absence. The position of the *banta* in an Atjehnese *landschap* is, says [[Dr. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] (Deel I, p. 96), to be compared with that of the *patih* in a Javanese *regentschap*. When [[Teukoe Tjhi Mohamad Thajeb|Teukoe Tjhi Mohamad Thajeb]] was a member of the [[Volksraad|Volksraad]] in [[1918]]–[[1919]], the *oeléebalangschap* of [[Peureula (Perlak)|Peureula]] was held by his *banta*.

## Account from Atjeh als land voor handel en bedrijf (1925)

**Banta** was position in Atjeh landscape, comparable per Dr. [[Snouck Hurgronje]] (deel I p.96) with *patih* in principality or regency on Java, eldest of brothers or relatives observing administration when oeléëbalang away.

### Examples from Broersma

In [[Peureula]] 1910 administration observed by *banta* while [[Teukoe Tjhi Moeda Peusangan]] visited Java; main cause travel to Bandoeng son school, visits blind institute girls school hat plaiteries, stay Batavia sister married Atjeh head exiled Ternate.

[[1918]]-[[1919]] banta observed while [[Teukoe Tjhi Mohamad Thajeb]] member [[Volksraad]].
