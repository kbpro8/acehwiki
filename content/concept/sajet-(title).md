**Sajet** — the Atjehnese form of the Arabic *sayyid* (also *sajjid*) — is the title borne by those who claim descent from the Prophet Muhammad. As [[Dr. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] notes, the *sajets* *'hadden godsdienstigen adel en grooten invloed'* — *possessed religious nobility and great influence.* In Atjeh, the older *Teungkoe* of those who claim *sajet* ancestry is replaced by *[[Habib|habib]]* — *beloved* — as in the name of [[Teungkoe habib Moehamat bin Sajet Oesoeih|Teungkoe habib Moehamat bin Sajet Oesoeih]] of [[Peudawa Rajeu|Peudawa Rajeu]].
