**The Kroeëng Wojla** — also called the **Inong Wojla** (*Inong* = "mother" in Acehnese) — is the principal river of the southern [[Westkust van Atjeh|Westkust van Atjeh]], formed by the confluence of its two *bronrivieren* the *Reuengeuët* and the *Meugeurintjeng*, and reaching the Indian Ocean north of [[Meulaboh|Meulaboh]] after breaking through the Westelijke Barisan. In the [[De Buitenbezittingen, Deel II, Aflevering 1|Encyclopaedisch Bureau's Sumatra survey]] (1917), the Kroeëng Wojla is the southernmost of the Westkust rivers that flow through the *groote Westelijke lengtedal* and break through the Westelijke Barisan to the Indian Ocean.

## Course

The Bureau's Hoofdstuk I describes the Kroeëng Wojla as the outflow of the *groote Westelijke lengtedal* of South-Atjeh. The two *bronrivieren* — the *Reuengeuët* and the *Meugeurintjeng* — drain the upper *Woi ni Tripö* and the upper *Seunagan*; the *Woi ni Tripö* itself, "na de doorbreking der beide ketenen, daalt snel af naar de Westkust, om ten noorden van Soesoh den Indischen Oceaan te bereiken." The Kroeëng Wojla's *delta*, north of Soesoh, marks the beginning of the *vrij breede kustvlakte* that "zich voortzet tot voorbij de Wojla delta om dan snel tegen de steile kusten van Poeloe Raja te versmallen."

After its delta, the Kroeëng Wojla reaches the Indian Ocean north of [[Meulaboh|Meulaboh]]. "Ten Noorden daarvan bereikt de Wojla den Indischen Oceaan." The Kroeëng Wojla is thus the principal hydrographical artery of the southern Westkust, draining the *groote Westelijke lengtedal* of South-Atjeh through its break-through of the Westelijke Barisan.

## Significance

The Kroeëng Wojla is, in the Bureau's Sumatra survey, the southernmost of the Westkust rivers that drain the *groote Westelijke lengtedal* of Atjeh. The Bureau's emphasis on the river's *delta* as the geographical beginning of the *vrij breede kustvlakte* of the southern Westkust frames the Kroeëng Wojla as the geographical foundation of the Westkust's principal *kustvlakte* — the *kustvlakte* that sustained the Dutch *bivaks* at [[Meulaboh|Meulaboh]], [[Tapatoean|Tapatoean]] and [[Tjalang|Tjalang]], and that formed the principal theatre of the [[Evacuation of women and children from the Westkust of Atjeh (March 1942)|Westkust evacuation of March 1942]].

The Kroeëng Wojla's alternative name *Inong Wojla* — "*Inong* = moeder" — is the Bureau's principal evidence of the Acehnese onomastic tradition of female-personified rivers, a tradition that links the Kroeëng Wojla to the wider Acehnese cultural landscape of the Westkust.
