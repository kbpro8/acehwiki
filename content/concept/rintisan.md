A **rintisan** (Malay, a cut path or cleared line) was the boundary path by which a clearer of waste land in [[Aceh|Atjeh]] joined the stakes planted at the corners of his terrain, thereby making his occupation visible.

## Function in the law of occupation

[[D. W. N. de Boer]] described occupation as being announced in the first instance by planting ordinary stakes at the corners of the ground and connecting these to one another by a *rintisan*; and in a further stage by complete fencing, *[[Pageue|pageue]]*. The cutting of the *rintisan* was thus the elementary act of appropriation, prior to enclosure and prior to the acquisition of the settled *[[Milik|milik]]*.
