**Batakpaard** (English: "Batak horse") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Horse breed from Batak region; competes successfully against the smaller, shorter Gajo-paard.

## Translation

Batak horse

## Description

Horse breed from Batak region; competes successfully against the smaller, shorter [[Gajo-paard]].

## Significance

The term *Batakpaard* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
