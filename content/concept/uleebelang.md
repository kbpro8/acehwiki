**Uleebelang** (*Oëleuëbalang*) was traditionally the title of hereditary feudal district rulers and noble military chiefs in the Sultanate of Aceh who governed hereditary territories (*negeri* or *sagoë*).

## Marechaussee Jargon Usage

Within the internal jargon and camp culture of the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]], the term *Oëleuëbalang* was adopted by soldiers to designate detachment and company captains (*kapitein-divisiecommandant*). This linguistic adoption reflected how Marechaussee units borrowed Acehnese structural terminology to build an internal military hierarchy suited to local conditions.
