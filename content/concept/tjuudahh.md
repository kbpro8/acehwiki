**tjuudahh** (English: "outwardly and inwardly excellent (woman)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Term for the feminine ideal — outwardly and inwardly excellent.

## Translation

outwardly and inwardly excellent (woman)

## Description

Term for the feminine ideal — outwardly and inwardly excellent.

## Significance

The term *tjuudahh* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
