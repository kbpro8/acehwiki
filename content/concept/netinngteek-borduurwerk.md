**Netinngteek-borduurwerk** (English: "Gajo machine-embroidery (formerly hand-embroidery)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Gajo needlework/embroidery; formerly done by hand with arabesques, meanders and other figures in red, white, green and yellow on a black ground.

## Translation

[[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]] machine-embroidery (formerly hand-embroidery)

## Description

[[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]] needlework/embroidery; formerly done by hand with arabesques, meanders and other figures in red, white, green and yellow on a black ground. Now done on trap-machines in a loods on the [[pasar]] at [[Takengon]]. Products: cushions, tea-cosies, kleedjes, and the short jackets (baadjes) of [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]] women.

## Significance

The term *Netinngteek-borduurwerk* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
