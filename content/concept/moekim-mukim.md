**Moekim / Mukim** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Acehnese administrative-religious unit.

## Description

Acehnese administrative-religious unit. [[Sagi]] confederations of mukims: [[XXII Moekims]] ([[Panglima Polém]]'s [[Sagi|sagi]]), [[XXVI Moekims]].

## Significance

The term *Moekim / Mukim* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
