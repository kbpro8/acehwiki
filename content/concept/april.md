**[[April]]** is a calendar month used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention. In the material drawn from *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]* it is associated with the relief of [[Tjot Goeë]] on [[9 April]] [[1895]] and the lifting of [[Lam Koenjit]] and [[Biloel]] four days later; the night assault on [[Lam Koenjit]] of [[4 April]]–[[5 April]] [[1896]]; the abortive relief of [[Biloel]] on [[9 April]] [[1896]]; the [[Relief of Tjot Rang (16 April 1896)|relief of Tjot Rang]] on [[16 April]] [[1896]]; and the [[Relief and lifting of Anagaloeng, Lambarih and Senelop (18 April 1896)|relief and lifting of Anagaloeng, Lambarih and Senelop]] on [[18 April]] [[1896]].
