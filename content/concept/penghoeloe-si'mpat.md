**penghoeloe si'mpat** (English: "Penghoeloe si'mpat (territorial chief with secular power)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Chief of a marga in the Alaslanden, holding secular power over part of the realm; serves as one of four advisors to the keudjroeën.

## Translation

Penghoeloe si'mpat (territorial chief with secular power)

## Description

Chief of a [[marga]] in the [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]], holding secular power over part of the realm; serves as one of four advisors to the [[keudjroeën]].

## Significance

The term *penghoeloe si'mpat* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
