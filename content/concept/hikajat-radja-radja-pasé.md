**Hikajat radja-radja Pasé** (English: "Tale of the Kings of Pasai (Acehnese historical text)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Acehnese/Malay historical text edited by Dulaurier.

## Translation

Tale of the Kings of [[Pasai]] (Acehnese historical text)

## Description

Acehnese/Malay historical text edited by [[E. Dulaurier|Dulaurier]]. Contains a legend in loose connection suggesting the [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gayo]] already bore the name '[[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gayo]]' before their conversion to [[Islam]].

## Significance

The term *Hikajat radja-radja Pasé* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
