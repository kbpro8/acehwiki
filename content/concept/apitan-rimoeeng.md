**Apitan rimoeeng** — *the tiger-captain* — was the Atjehnese nickname of the *marechaussée* officer [[Christoffel|Christoffel]], who broke the post-pacification resistance in the hill-country of [[Keureuto|Keureuto]] about [[1908]] and was so called *'omdat zijn greep bloedig was'* — *because his grip was bloody.* The title *apitan rimoeeng* places Christoffel in the same series as the *'tijger van Atjeh'* [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] himself, and is the principal nickname of the post-pacification *marechaussée*-captains.
