**vertoogen** (English: "representations/diplomatic notes") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Diplomatic representations made by the Dutch for 2 years (1871-1873) after the Sumatra Treaty, trying to establish better relations with Atjeh before resorting to military intervention.

## Translation

representations/diplomatic notes

## Description

Diplomatic representations made by the Dutch for 2 years ([[1871]]-[[1873]]) after the Sumatra Treaty, trying to establish better relations with Atjeh before resorting to military intervention.

## Significance

The term *vertoogen* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
