The **Concentrated Line** (*Geconcentreerde Linie*) was a defensive military strategy introduced by Governor [[Henry Demmeni]] in [[1884]] to reduce colonial expenditures in Aceh.

## Strategy and Infrastructure

The strategy abandoned outer military posts, retreating Dutch forces into a compact ring of 16 heavily fortified redoutes around [[Kraton of Aceh|Kota Raja]].

The posts were interconnected by an armed steam tramway network (*ceintuurbaan*), telephone lines, and tactical roads, fronted by a 1,000-meter cleared zone (*1000 M. strook*) to prevent surprise attacks. The system remained the core Dutch defensive layout until the [[Treason of Teuku Umar]] in [[1896]].
