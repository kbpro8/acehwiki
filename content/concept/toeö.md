**toeö** (English: "Adat expert (in Gajo)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Adat-kenner in Gayo society; in Gajo Loeës reduced to settling minor disputes and acting as intermediary between rëdjö's and blah members.

## Translation

[[Adat]] expert (in [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]])

## Description

[[Adat]]-kenner in [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gayo]] society; in [[Gajo-Loeüs|Gajo Loeës]] reduced to settling minor disputes and acting as intermediary between rëdjö's and [[Blah|blah]] members.

## Significance

The term *toeö* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
