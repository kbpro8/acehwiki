**Poekoel 8 Atjeh** (English: "Acehnese 8 o'clock (i.e., 'around some time')") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Acehnese expression equivalent to the Dutch 'around an hour or so' — illustrates Acehnese lack of punctuality.

## Translation

Acehnese 8 o'clock (i.e., 'around some time')

## Description

Acehnese expression equivalent to the Dutch 'around an hour or so' — illustrates Acehnese lack of punctuality. Anecdote told of an old landschapshoofd who arrived hours late to [[Koninginnedag]] with the excuse 'I have no clock' (despite three broken clocks in his voorgalerij).

## Significance

The term *Poekoel 8 Atjeh* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
