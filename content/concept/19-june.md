**[[19 June]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]* it appears in the articles listed below.

- [[1908]]
- [[Christiaan Snouck Hurgronje]]
- [[H. N. A. Swart]]

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this date in [[1879]] the robber-people of [[Ladoeng]] and [[Kroeng Rajal]] on Atjeh's North Coast was punished without Dutch loss (see [[Punishment of Ladoeng and Kroeng Rajal (19 June 1879)]]).
