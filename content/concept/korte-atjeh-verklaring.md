**korte Atjeh-verklaring** (English: "Short Atjeh declaration") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Three-article contract of 1898 designed by Van Heutsz and Snouck Hurgronje: (1) recognition that the territory is part of Nederlandsch-Indië and loyalty to the Queen of the Netherlands; (2) promise not to enter into political relations with foreign powers, treating Netherlands' enemies as enemies and friends as friends; (3) promise to comply with and uphold all regulations issued by the Governor of Atjeh and Onderhoorigheden concerning the landschap.

## Translation

Short Atjeh declaration

## Description

Three-article contract of [[1898]] designed by [[Generaal J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] and [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]]: (1) recognition that the territory is part of [[Nederlandsch-Indië]] and loyalty to the Queen of [[Netherlands|the Netherlands]]; (2) promise not to enter into political relations with foreign powers, treating [[Leyden (Leiden)|Netherlands]]' enemies as enemies and friends as friends; (3) promise to comply with and uphold all regulations issued by the Governor of Atjeh and [[Onderhoorigheden]] concerning the [[landschap]]. Later adopted as the '[[Uniform model korte verklaring|uniform model korte verklaring]]' for most of [[Nederlandsch-Indië]].

## Significance

The term *korte Atjeh-verklaring* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
