**Alang** (English: "Imperata cylindrica (alang-alang grass)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Tough grass covering large plains that should be forest; especially in Groot-Atjeh where reservation came too late; result of repeated burning preventing young-forest formation.

## Translation

Imperata cylindrica (alang-alang grass)

## Description

Tough grass covering large plains that should be forest; especially in [[Groot-Atjeh]] where reservation came too late; result of repeated burning preventing young-forest formation.

## Significance

The term *Alang* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
