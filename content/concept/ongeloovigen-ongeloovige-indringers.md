**ongeloovigen / ongeloovige indringers** (English: "unbelievers / unbelieving invaders") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. How Atjehners framed the Portuguese and later the Dutch - the simplest interpretation of Islamic religious propaganda.

## Translation

unbelievers / unbelieving invaders

## Description

How Atjehners framed [[Portuguese Traders|the Portuguese]] and later the Dutch - the simplest interpretation of Islamic religious propaganda. Used to mobilize '[[levées en masse]]' on a religious basis.

## Significance

The term *ongeloovigen / ongeloovige indringers* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
