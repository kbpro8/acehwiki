**Petocha peuët** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Acehnese phrase meaning 'the four'; Pang Nanggròë was one of the 'petocha peuët' of Teungoh.

## Description

Acehnese phrase meaning 'the four'; [[Pang Nanggròë]] was one of the 'petocha peuët' of Teungoh.

## Significance

The term *Petocha peuët* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
