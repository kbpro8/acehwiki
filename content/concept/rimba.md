**Rimba** was the Malay and Atjehnese term for jungle or heavy secondary forest, and in Atjehnese land law it marked the point beyond which abandoned cultivation ceased to be recognisable as such.

## Legal relevance

In the *[[Zelfbesturende landschappen|zelfbesturende landschappen]]*, [[D. W. N. de Boer]] recorded, a stricter procedure applied where an application concerned *[[Sawah|sawah]]* ground "die nog niet *rimba* geworden is" — which had not yet become jungle — and which, lying between worked *sawah*'s or directly adjoining them, evidently belonged to someone and had been abandoned only recently. In such a case the applicant received only a three-year *bruikleen* (loan for use) under *[[Haq padjoh asoé|padjoh asoè]]*, not full possession. Conversely, land that had reverted to *rimba* was treated as waste and could be issued outright after the *[[Three-month warning term for re-clearance in the Atjehnese self-governing territories|three-month warning term]]*.
