**[[October]]** is a calendar month used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention. In the material drawn from *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]* it is associated with the appearance of Dutch soldiers before [[Kroeng Kali]] on [[6 October]] [[1893]]; the [[Relief of Kroeng Kali by the 3rd battalion (October 1893)|relief of Kroeng Kali]] and the storm of [[Masdjid Siem]] by [[Overste Sievers]]; and the transfer of Masdjid Siem to [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]] on [[21 October]] [[1893]].
