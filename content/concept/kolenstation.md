**Kolenstation** (English: "Coal station") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Coaling facility for naval and merchant ships at Sabang; original purpose of the harbor.

## Translation

Coal station

## Description

Coaling facility for naval and merchant ships at [[Sabang]]; original purpose of the harbor.

## Significance

The term *Kolenstation* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
