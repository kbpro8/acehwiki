The **thiploeëh-tha** (Atjehnese *thiploeëh*, "ten," and *tha*, Malay *satu*, "one" — hence "one of the ten") was the Atjehnese tithe levied on gardens (*lampoih*) outside the *[[Gampong|gampong]]* boundaries, forming the third head of the *[[Adat glé|adat glé]]*.

## Character and distribution

[[D. W. N. de Boer]] identified it with the "tienden van het gewas" — tithes of the crop — found elsewhere in the Archipelago as well, citing [[Bali]] among other places. It was thus not a peculiarly Atjehnese institution but the local form of a widespread tenth-part levy on garden produce.

## Abolition

Like the other sovereign levies of the *[[Oeleebalang|oeléëbalangs]]*, the *thiploeëh-tha* was abolished by article 15 sub I of [[Staatsblad 1919 No. 822]], the chiefs being compensated by a fixed *[[Civiele lijst|civiele lijst]]*.
