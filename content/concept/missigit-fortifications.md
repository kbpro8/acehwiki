**Missigit Fortifications** refers to the extensive military engineering and architectural defenses utilized by the Acehnese around [[Atjeh's Groote Missigit]].

## Evolution
Originally, the mosque was defended by its 2.5-meter high hard stone (stone) walls and the natural bastions formed by accumulated earth. Following the [[Second Conquest of the Great Missigit]] in April [[1873]], the Acehnese drastically improved the site's defenses. By December [[1873]], they had constructed a massive earthen envelope (aarden enveloppe) with a fireline extending over 700 meters, protecting the north and west sides. These earthworks were designed to withstand heavy artillery and effectively delayed the Dutch advance during the [[Third Conquest of the Great Missigit]] on [[6 January]] [[1874]].
