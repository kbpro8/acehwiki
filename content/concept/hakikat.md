**Hakikat** is the Acehnese word for “essence” or “reality”, derived from the Arabic *ḥaqīqah*. It appears on [[82]] with the gloss “wezen, essentie (A. masnd)”. In the *Burda* translation, *hakikat* is invoked in philosophical verses that contemplate the nature of being and divine truth.

The term is central to Acehnese Sufi thought, where seekers aim to perceive the *hakikat* of existence.
