**[[22 May]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]* it appears in the articles listed below.

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this date in [[1881]] governor [[A. Pruys van der Hoeven|Pruijs van der Hoeven]] forbade the patrols the kampongs, bound them to fixed roads and forbade arrests outside the 500-metre ring (see [[Normale bestuursinrichting (Atjeh, 1881-1884)]]); on this date in [[1883]] the Kamer heard of Governor-General [[F. s'Jacob|s'Jacob]]'s intended visit to Atjeh.
