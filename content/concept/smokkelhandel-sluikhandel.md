**smokkelhandel / sluikhandel** (English: "smuggling") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Smuggling at Sabang's free port.

## Translation

smuggling

## Description

Smuggling at [[Sabang]]'s free port. In [[1930]]-[[1934]], unheard-of sums were involved. Now less profitable, so less prevalent. Effective suppression requires fast motorboats and a seaplane.

## Significance

The term *smokkelhandel / sluikhandel* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
