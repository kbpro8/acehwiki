**moepakat** (English: "Consultation / deliberation") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Atjeh practice of consultation; deeply valued.

## Translation

Consultation / [[Mupakat|deliberation]]

## Description

Atjeh practice of consultation; deeply valued. Decisions in the [[Gampong|gampong]] are taken after moepakat with a college of elders, or with all adult male inhabitants for important decisions.

## Significance

The term *moepakat* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
