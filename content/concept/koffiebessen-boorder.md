**koffiebessen-boorder** (English: "coffee berry borer (insect pest)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. A boorder (borer insect) that uses coffee berries as broodplaats (brood-place) for its larvae; the coffee bean is eaten out from within.

## Translation

coffee berry borer (insect pest)

## Description

A boorder (borer insect) that uses coffee berries as broodplaats (brood-place) for its larvae; the coffee bean is eaten out from within. Severely affected [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]]. Less severe above [[1000]] m altitude.

## Significance

The term *koffiebessen-boorder* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
