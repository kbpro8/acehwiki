**The Wadoek** was the dam or reservoir of an Atjehnese *landschap*, by which the water of a river was collected for the irrigation of the *[[Sawah|sawahs]]*. The *wadoek* is the principal piece of irrigation-infrastructure of the Atjehnese rice-cultivation. In [[Peureula (Perlak)|Peureula]] a small irrigation-work has recently been undertaken, of which one serves to improve a *wadoek* laid out in [[1906]] — that is, then was begun with a dyke. Now 250 hectares can be irrigated. In [[Peusangan|Peusangan]] about a third of the soil is irrigable — that is, from the swamp — and for that there are a few *wadoeks*.
