**olifanten (army elephants)** (English: "army elephants") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Army elephants used for military transport between Tangsé and Geumpang until recent years.

## Translation

army elephants

## Description

Army elephants used for military transport between [[Tangsé]] and [[Geumpang]] until recent years. Three stationed at [[Tangsé]]. Loaded by lying down, covered with heavy deck, chained down, soldiers walked over them with spiked shoes to 'stow' loads. Male '[[Agam]]' was put down after becoming recalcitrant. Females: one donated to [[Rotterdamsche Diergaarde]], one sold to the Soesochoenan.

## Significance

The term *olifanten (army elephants)* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
