**[[10 June]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]* it appears in the articles listed below.

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this with the preceding day in [[1879]] the victory at [[Gleieng]] was completed and the fleeing enemy was driven out of his hiding corners (Verstege after Kielstra, D. III).
