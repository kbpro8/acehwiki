**soekoe** (English: "Soekoe (matrilineal clan in Menangkabau system)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Clan division in the matriarchal Menangkabau system, originally used in the maleische staatjes on the Westkust.

## Translation

Soekoe (matrilineal clan in [[Menangkabau]] system)

## Description

Clan division in the matriarchal [[Menangkabau]] system, originally used in the maleische staatjes on the [[Westkust]]. The Dutch contributed to a shift to the patriarchal [[Atjeh (Aceh)|Atjehsche]] form by replacing the multi-headed with a single-headed bestuur.

## Significance

The term *soekoe* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
