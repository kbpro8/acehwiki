**Tjab thikoereueng** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Pretendent-Sultan's ninefold seal; he misused his purely traditional authority and the seal solely to obtain money for his extravagances.

## Description

[[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]]'s ninefold seal; he misused his purely traditional authority and the seal solely to obtain money for his extravagances.

## Significance

The term *Tjab thikoereueng* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
