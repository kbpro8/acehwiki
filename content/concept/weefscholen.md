**Weefscholen** (English: "Weaving schools") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Schools at Oelèë Lheuë and Samalanga opened on the initiative of local oelèëbalangs to promote old Atjehsch weaving and silk culture.

## Translation

Weaving schools

## Description

Schools at [[Oelèë Lheuë]] and [[Samalanga]] opened on the initiative of local oelèëbalangs to promote old Atjehsch weaving and silk culture.

## Significance

The term *Weefscholen* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
