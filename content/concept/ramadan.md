**Ramadan** is the ninth month of the Islamic lunar calendar, observed by Muslims worldwide as a period of fasting and heightened devotion. The source records that the Ottoman custodians of the Prophet’s cloak performed a special ceremony on the 15th of [[Ramadan]] during which the *Burda* poem’s water‑soaked cloth was distributed to members of the Acehnese royal household. This ritual linked the holy month with the dissemination of the poem in the Sultanate of [[Acheh]].
