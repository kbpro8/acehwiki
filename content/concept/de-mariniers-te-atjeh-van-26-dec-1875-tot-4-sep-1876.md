**De mariniers te Atjeh van 26 December 1875 tot 4 September 1876** is a Dutch‑language monograph authored by R. J. J. P. Cambier that chronicles the Dutch marine operations in [[Atjeh (Aceh)]] during the period [[1875]]–[[1876]]. The work provides day‑by‑day narratives, orders of battle, and illustrations of uniforms (see page 471).  

## Content Overview  

- **Introduction** – Context of the broader [[Aceh War]] and the strategic importance of [[Groot Atjeh]].  
- **Chronology** – Detailed entries from the landing on [[26 December 1875]] through the final engagement on [[4 September 1876]].  
- **Appendices** – Maps (including the *Kaart van Groot Atjeh*), uniform plates, and casualty tables.  

## Historical Value  

The monograph is a primary source for scholars studying Dutch amphibious tactics, colonial logistics, and Acehnese resistance. It is frequently cited in later works such as the *Atjeh Marineblad* (1890‑1891).
