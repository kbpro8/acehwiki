**Landschapsgerecht** was the territorial court of a *[[landschap]]*, in which the *landschapsrechter* sat.

It was during a sitting of the *Landschapsgerecht* at [[Sigli]] on [[10 October]] [[1913]] that the *landschapsrechter* [[T. Béntara Titeuë]] attacked and fatally wounded the Civil Authority of [[Pidië]], overste [[W. B. J. A. Scheepens]], who presided over the *[[Moesapat]]* in that capacity.
