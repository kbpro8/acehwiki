**familie-gedachte** (English: "family concept/kinship principle") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Ancient Atjehnese societal organization based on kinship.

## Translation

family concept/kinship principle

## Description

Ancient Atjehnese societal organization based on kinship. In the pre-Hindu era, people lived in scattered hamlets and villages of members of a single 'family'; everything important happened in the 'family circle' and each circle was its own little realm. This is the foundation that [[Sultan Iskandar Moeda]] later reputedly organized into the '[[Nanggroe / Familie-stelsel (institution)|nanggroe]]' system.

## Significance

The term *familie-gedachte* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
