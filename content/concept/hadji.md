**hadji** (English: "pilgrim to Mecca") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Older people who properly fulfill religious duties and hadjis frequently wear small white caps.

## Translation

pilgrim to [[Mecca]]

## Description

Older people who properly fulfill religious duties and hadjis frequently wear small white caps.

## Significance

The term *hadji* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
