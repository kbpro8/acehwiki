**arbeiders-kolonisatie** (English: "Worker colonization") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Form of colonization attempted by cultuurlichamen to provide a future supply of trained labor after the abolition of the poenale sanctie.

## Translation

Worker colonization

## Description

Form of colonization attempted by [[Cultuurlichamen|cultuurlichamen]] to provide a future supply of trained labor after the abolition of the [[Poenale Sanctie|poenale sanctie]].

## Significance

The term *arbeiders-kolonisatie* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
