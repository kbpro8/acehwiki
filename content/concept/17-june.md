**[[17 June]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]* it appears in the articles listed below.

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this date in [[1885]] the patrol of 25 men from [[Tjot-Goe]] to the line fell into an ambush behind the tramway (see [[Guerilla raids inside the concentration line (1885)]]); see also the decision year [[1884]] at [[Concentration decision (16-17 June 1884)]].
