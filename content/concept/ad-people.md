**‘Ad (people of ‘Ad)** refers to an ancient Arabian tribe mentioned in the Qur’an as a powerful nation that was destroyed for rejecting the prophet [[Hūd]]. The Malay translation of the *Burda* cites the *‘Ad* as an example of divine retribution against obstinate peoples.

## Qur’anic Account

The Qur’an recounts the story of the *‘Ad* in several passages (e.g., [[Qur’an 26:123‑140]]) highlighting their arrogance and ultimate downfall.

## Role in Acehnese Religious Narrative

By invoking the *‘Ad* in the *Burda* translation, Acehnese poets draw a parallel between past communities that denied divine guidance and contemporary believers, reinforcing the moral imperative to remain faithful to the Prophet [[Muhammad]].
