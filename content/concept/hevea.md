**hevea** (English: "rubber tree (Hevea brasiliensis)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Rubber tree; cultivation boomed from 1910 onward, attracted by British-colony profits.

## Translation

rubber tree (Hevea brasiliensis)

## Description

Rubber tree; cultivation boomed from [[1910]] onward, attracted by British-colony profits. Total area 34,080 H.A. as of end [[1937]]; export 11,800,000 K.G. in [[1937]]. Worst hit by economic depression.

## Significance

The term *hevea* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
