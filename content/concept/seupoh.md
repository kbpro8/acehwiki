**Seupoh** (English: "Gold filigree/soesa work") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Acehnese gold/silver smithing technique using soesa (gold wire/thread); Chinese smiths cannot master it.

## Translation

Gold filigree/soesa work

## Description

Acehnese gold/silver smithing technique using soesa (gold wire/thread); Chinese smiths cannot master it.

## Significance

The term *Seupoh* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
