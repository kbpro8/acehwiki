**Adat Pangkal** was the traditional financial return or interest fee paid to a capital sponsor (*peutoeha pangkaj*) for advancing setup capital to establish a pepper plantation (*seunebok lada*).

## Customary Principles

The *pangkaj* capital advance consisted of rice, tools, clothing, and cash ($20–25 per 1,000 vines). In return, the sponsor secured exclusive purchasing rights over the crop and a fixed fee per pikul exported (*Adat Pangkal*). The advance attached to the land as a preferred debt.
