**indische jongens** (English: "Indian boys (South Asian workers)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. South Asian workers among the opzichters (supervisors) at the Atjeh-tram workshop at Sigli.

## Translation

Indian boys (South Asian workers)

## Description

South Asian workers among the opzichters (supervisors) at the [[Atjeh-tram]] workshop at [[Sigli]].

## Significance

The term *indische jongens* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
