**Dam ai** (also rendered *damai*) is the Acehnese word for “peace”. It appears on [[81]] with the gloss “vrede (A. salm)”. In the *Burda* translation the term is used in verses that call for reconciliation and the cessation of conflict, e.g., “dengan men‑damai antaranja, onder aanbod van vrede”.

The concept is central to Acehnese customary law (*adat*) where *dam ai* resolves disputes.
