**Actie-tijden** (English: "Action/war years") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. The years of military action during the Aceh War; Penang served as refuge and finance base; cattle left to themselves; klewang-aanvallen occurred.

## Translation

Action/war years

## Description

The years of military action during the Aceh War; [[Penang]] served as refuge and finance base; cattle left to themselves; [[Klewang|klewang]]-aanvallen occurred.

## Significance

The term *Actie-tijden* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
