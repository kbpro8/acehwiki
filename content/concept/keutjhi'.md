**keutjhi'** (English: "Gampong head (village chief)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Head of a gampong in Groot-Atjeh; the 'father' of the village.

## Translation

[[Gampong]] head (village chief)

## Description

Head of a [[Gampong|gampong]] in [[Groot-Atjeh]]; the 'father' of the village. Consults elders before decisions; important decisions involve all adult male inhabitants in [[moepakat]].

## Significance

The term *keutjhi'* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
