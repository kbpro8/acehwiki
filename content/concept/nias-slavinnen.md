**Nias-slavinnen** (English: "Nias slave women") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Slave women from Nias, considered more attractive than Acehnese women; as concubines of their Acehnese masters they contributed to the development of the Acehnese 'race'.

## Translation

[[Nias]] slave women

## Description

Slave women from [[Nias]], considered more attractive than Acehnese women; as concubines of their Acehnese masters they contributed to the development of the Acehnese 'race'.

## Significance

The term *Nias-slavinnen* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
