**[[29 March]]** is the date of the defection of the cunning adventurer [[Teukoe Oemar]] on [[29 March]] [[1896]] — the critical days that followed the Graafland era and preceded the glorious period of Van Heutsz. In *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* by Merac ([[1947]]), [[29 March]] is the book's marker of the defection of Teukoe Oemar: the crisis that opened the way to the glorious period of Van Heutsz.

## The defection of Teukoe Oemar

"But after the defection of the cunning adventurer Teukoe Oemar on 29 March 1896, critical days began." After the defection of the cunning adventurer Teukoe Oemar on [[29 March]] [[1896]], critical days began. The [[29 March]] [[1896]] defection — the turning of the Acehnese chief who had been allied with the colonial army — is the book's marker of the critical days between the Graafland era and the glorious period of Van Heutsz.

## Significance

[[29 March]] is significant in *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* as the date of the defection of [[Teukoe Oemar]] on [[29 March]] [[1896]]. The book presents this defection as the crisis that opened the way to the glorious period of Van Heutsz, marking the pivotal transition between two phases of the Aceh War. The date [[29 March]] thus serves as the book's marker for Teukoe Oemar's betrayal and its consequences for colonial strategy.
