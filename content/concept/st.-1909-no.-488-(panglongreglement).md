**St. 1909 No. 488** — the *Staatsblad* (State Gazette) of the Netherlands-Indies for the year [[1909]], number 488 — was the colonial government's *panglongreglement* (panglong-regulation), by which the Chinese *panglong* labour-form of the wood-cutting industry of the eastern coast of Sumatra was regulated. The regulation was given for *panglongs* on [[Bengkalis|Bengkalis]] and the other islands near Siak, also in Siak itself, but on the islands, in the forests, supervision was difficult. The labour-form at [[Bajan|Bajan]] has traits of the *panglong*-enterprise but is, under direct European supervision, a modified form.
