**Toeangkoe** was the term used within the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* for the *divisiecommandant* — the commander of a *[[Divisie (Maréchaussée)|divisie]]* — in the corps's internal hierarchy of Acehnese titles recorded by [[W. J. Mosselman]].

## Place in the hierarchy

One *Toeangkoe* or division commander had under him three *[[Oeleebalang|Oeleuhbalangs]]* (officers); each *Oeleuhbalang* four *[[Maréchaussée-vocabulaire|Toekoes]]* (European non-commissioned officers); and each *Toekoe* one *[[Panglima|Panglima]]*, one *[[Keuchi|Keutjih]]* and seventeen *[[Kawan (Maréchaussée)|Kawans]]*. See *[[Maréchaussée-vocabulaire]]*.

## The Ouwe

Mosselman also refers to the division commander by the familiar name *onze Ouwe* — our Old Man: "dan vierde onze Ouwe, onze Divisiecommandant den teugel" — then our Old Man, our division commander, gave us the rein — describing the licence allowed the men when they were off duty and spending the few *centen* they had saved.

Under the arrangements of [[1899]], and expressly on the [[Tocht naar de Gajo-Loëös (1904)|expedition to the Gajo-Loëös]] of [[1904]], division commanders were also charged with the civil administration as *civiel-gezaghebber*.
