**Meu'oeë** was the Atjehnese term for the working of *[[Sawah|sawah]]*'s — the ploughing and preparation of the rice field — and specifically for the first working of the ground of a new season.

## Legal significance

The period between the harvest (*[[Keumeukoh|keumeukoh]]*) and the *meu'oeë* of the following season was the only time of year in which a pledge of land under *[[Gala|gala]]* could be redeemed by *[[Teuboih|teuboih]]*.

## In the terminology of sharecropping

The verb underlies the name of the most one-sided of the Atjehnese share-cropping figures, *[[Aboen meu'oeë|aboen meu'oeë]]* — the putting out to contract of the working of the *sawah* — in which the giver supplied ground, cattle, seed, planting wage and subsistence, and the cultivator received one-fifth of the harvest.
