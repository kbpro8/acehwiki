**Moslimin (as Acehnese used it)** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Beureunoen 'decided to become moslimin (- kwaadwillige)' - i.e.

## Description

[[Beureunoen]] 'decided to become moslimin (- kwaadwillige)' - i.e. to join the [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]]/outlaw life. A specific Acehnese-Dutch usage where 'moslimin' = resister/outlaw.

## Significance

The term *Moslimin (as Acehnese used it)* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
