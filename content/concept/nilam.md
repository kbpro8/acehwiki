**Nilam** was the Sumatran name for the patchouli plant — *[[Pogostemon Cablin (patchouli plant)|Pogostemon Cablin Benth.]]* — and for the essential oil distilled from its leaves. On Java the same plant is called *[[Dilem|dilem]]*. On Sumatra the chief centres of cultivation are on the [[Westkust van Atjeh|Westkust van Atjeh]] at [[Tjalang|Tjalang]], [[Meulaboh|Meulaboh]] and [[Tapa Toean|Tapa Toean]], and on the Oostkust at [[Langsa|Langsa]]. Soil and climate in Atjeh appear naturally more favourable to the plant than on Java, where the oil, though of respectable constants, has *'lang niet zoo intensief'* an odour. The Atjeh nilam-oil is the subject of [[Ir. W. Spoon|Ir. W. Spoon]]'s *[[Atjeh Patchouli-Olie (1932)|Atjeh Patchouli-Olie]]*.
