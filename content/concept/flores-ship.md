**Flores** was a Dutch steamship (*Hr.Ms.* “*Hollandse Rijksmagazijn‑schip*”) employed by the **Royal Netherlands Navy** during the **Tamiang** expedition of **[[February]] [[1893]]**. The vessel, together with **[[Soembawa (ship)]]** and **[[Sindoro (ship)]]**, formed part of the flotilla commanded by **[[W. Allirol]]** that cleared river obstructions.

## Operational Use

The ship carried a **eel‑flotilla** of armed boats that engaged enemy fire while dismantling blockades of rotan, rope and bamboo on the **Tamiang** river.

## Significance

**Flores** demonstrates the logistical flexibility of the Dutch navy in riverine operations during the **Aceh War**.
