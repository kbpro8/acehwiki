**The Atjehsche peper- en pinanghasil** ("Atjeh pepper and betel-nut yield") was the principal miscellaneous revenue of the [[Landschapskassen|landschapskassen (territorial treasuries)]] of the [[Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh and Dependencies]] residency — the most important such revenue of any Buitenbezittingen gewest. In the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, the Atjehsche peper- en pinanghasil yielded f 125,000 (pepper) and f 55,000 (pinang) to the landschapskas in [[1913]] — figures that the Bureau singles out as "very important" among the miscellaneous revenues of the Buitenbezittingen landschapskassen.

## Account from *De Buitenbezittingen* (1917) — Atjehsche peper- en pinanghasil

The Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account of the miscellaneous revenues of the Buitenbezittingen landschapskassen notes that "[[1914|many other revenues]] contribute in one or more landschappen, in more or less important measure, to the revenues. Very important are, among others, the peper- en pinanghasil in Atjeh (in [[1913|1913]]: f 125,000 and f 55,000), the licences for the bird-hunt in [[New Guinea|New Guinea]] ([[1913|1913]]: f 65,000), the slaughter-tax levied in most landschappen ([[1913|1913]]: f 18,000), the revenues from the issue of land for house-building, small-scale agriculture and horticulture (so-called erfpacht voor huizenbouw en grondhuur; [[1913|1913]]: f 65,000)."

The Bureau's framing places the Atjehsche peper- en pinanghasil at the head of the list of "very important" miscellaneous revenues — ahead of the New Guinea bird-hunt licences, the slaughter-tax, and the erfpacht revenues. The pepper and pinang yields of Atjeh reflect the long-established commercial agriculture of the Atjehnese coast, which the Dutch had been taxing since the early years of the occupation and which continued, even after the [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacification]], to be the principal non-concession revenue of the residency.

The Atjehsche peper- en pinanghasil also appears, indirectly, in the Bureau's account of the [[Aroe-baai-plannen|Aroe-baai-plannen]] of [[1913]]. The Bureau notes that the defenders of the Aroe-baai-plannen hoped, by the laying of the [[Besitang]]–[[Pangkalan Soesoe]]–[[Telok Taboeban|Telok Taboeban]] connecting link, to divert "the export of the [[Acheh|Atjehnese]] products (pepper, rubber, etc.) — which increases steadily with the reclamation of the [[Tamiang|Tamiangsche moerassen]] — to be led via [[Aroebai|Arube Bay]]." The pepper that yielded f 125,000 to the landschapskas in [[1913]] was thus the same pepper that the Aroe-baai-plannen were designed to divert through [[Aroebai|Arube Bay]] rather than through [[Belawan]] or [[Sabang]].

## Significance

[[Atjehsche peper- en pinanghasil|The Atjehsche peper- en pinanghasil]] is, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, the principal miscellaneous revenue of the [[Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh and Dependencies]] residency — the most important such revenue of any Buitenbezittingen gewest. Its prominence in the Bureau's list of "very important" miscellaneous revenues reflects the long-established commercial agriculture of the Atjehnese coast; its connection to the [[Aroe-baai-plannen|Aroe-baai-plannen of 1913]] reflects the central place of the pepper and rubber trade in the project to develop [[Aroebai|Arube Bay]] as a deep-water harbour.
