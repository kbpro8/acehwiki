**Orde van Oranje-Nassau** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Dutch civil/military order; Panglima Polém wears the Knight's Cross; 7 Oranje-Nassau's awarded to marechaussée men in 40 years.

## Description

Dutch civil/military order; [[Panglima Polém]] wears the Knight's Cross; 7 [[Oranje-Nassau (Orde van Oranje Nassau)|Oranje-Nassau]]'s awarded to [[Marechaussée|marechaussée]] men in 40 years.

## Significance

The term *Orde van Oranje-Nassau* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
