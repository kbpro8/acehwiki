**Islamic devotional literature** encompasses works composed to inspire love, reverence, and emulation of the Prophet Muhammad and the pious ancestors. The *Burda* of Al‑Busiri is a paradigmatic example of this genre, celebrated for its poetic praise (madīḥ) of the Prophet and its widespread use in liturgical recitation, memorization, and commentary. The Malay translation of the *Burda* found in the OCR manuscript demonstrates how such Arabic devotional texts were transmitted into the Malay‑speaking world, where they were read in suraus, studied in pesantren, and copied by scribes. The manuscript’s extensive footnotes—providing Malay glosses, Arabic etymologies, and editorial corrections—illustrate the scholarly engagement with devotional texts in the early modern Malay intellectual milieu.
