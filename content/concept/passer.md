A **passer** was the periodic market of an Achehnese *kampong* (village), and in the Dutch outpost system of [[Groot-Atjeh]] an index of the political disposition of the surrounding population.

## The passer at Biloel

The clearest instance in *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]* is the *passer* under the great tree at [[Biloel]]. On the morning of [[29 March]] [[1896]], when [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]] was to have assembled a thousand warriors and a thousand coolies there for the action against [[Lamkrak]], *verscheen er des morgens niemand op de passer onder den grooten boom, zelfs de politie-*oppasser* (orderly) niet* — nobody at all appeared at the *passer* under the great tree, not even the police *oppasser*. It was the first sign of the defection.

## Other passers

The Dutch temporary post at [[Tjot Rang]] was quartered in an old Achehnese fortification on a height *bij de passer te Tjot Rang*. [[Toekoe Moesa Anaq Batee]] attacked the *passer*-goers travelling between [[Lam Barih|Lambarih]] and [[Anagaloeng]] in [[1896]].
