**radja moeda** (English: "Deputy ruler (in Alaslanden)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Deputy of the keudjroeën in each of the two Alasvallei realms.

## Translation

Deputy ruler (in [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]])

## Description

Deputy of the [[keudjroeën]] in each of the two [[Alaslanden|Alasvallei]] realms.

## Significance

The term *radja moeda* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
