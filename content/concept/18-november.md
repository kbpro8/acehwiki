**[[18 November]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]* it appears in the articles listed below.

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

Date in [[1879]] of Governor-General [[Johan Willem van Lansberge|Van Lansberge]]'s banquet speech, and in [[1881]] (the same month) of minister [[W. van Goltstein|van Goltstein]]'s reference to the false philanthropy that nearly brought [[Karel van der Heijden|Van der Heyden]] on the accused's bench.
