**parang** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[T. Béntara Titeuë]]

In October [[1913]] the *oelèëbalang*'s son, [[Teukoe Béntara Titeuë|the young Teukoe]], walking through the *[[gampong]]* with a few followers, grew thirsty and ordered one of them to climb a coconut palm on an Acehnese's yard and pick a few coconuts. The owner, angered that no permission had been asked, refused; the young Teukoe grew angry in turn, holding that the picking was the *oelèëbalang*'s right; the owner threatened him with his *[[parang]]*; the Teukoe held to his order; and the owner brought the *parang* down on his skull, causing a bleeding head wound. The perpetrator was arrested and brought to Sigli for trial before the *Moesapat* ("customary-law court").

### From [[Idi]]

The attack of [[11 June]] [[1890]] on the enemy positions on the left bank of the [[Idi-rivier]] was the action in which *2e luitenant* [[H. N. A. Swart]] was first into the summit fortification and was wounded; the column returned to Idi at 4.30 p.m. and occupied the bivouacs. In March [[1913]] the *controleur* of Idi and an accompanying official were attacked with a *[[parang]]* and both wounded, one of the [[Atjeh-moord|Atjeh murders]]. Idi was among the sub-divisions where a *controleur* already conducted the administration under the [[Administrative Division of Atjeh (1908)|division of 1908]].

### From [[II Moekims Titeuë]]

The affair began when the *oelèëbalang*'s son, walking through the *[[gampong]]*, ordered a follower to pick coconuts from a tree on another man's yard and was struck over the skull with a *[[parang]]* by the owner. The monthly report closed: "Peace and order in the territory of [[Titeuë]] were not disturbed for a moment by what had occurred."
