**The Korte Verklaring (1899)** — *the Short Declaration of 1899* — was the short political declaration in three articles by which the colonial government in [[1899]] replaced the older *[[Groote Verklaring|Groote Verklaring]]* in eighteen articles, and which the *[[Oeléebalang|oeléebalangs]]* of the Atjehnese *landschappen* were required to sign as the formal token of their submission to the Dutch government. By signing the *Korte Verklaring*, the *oeléebalang* acknowledged the suzerainty of the Dutch government and agreed to govern his *landschap* in accordance with the government's instructions.

## Use in Atjeh
[[Teungkoe habib Moehamat bin Sajet Oesoeih|Teungkoe habib Moehamat bin Sajet Oesoeih]] of [[Peudawa Rajeu|Peudawa Rajeu]] signed the *Korte Verklaring* in [[1899]]. [[Sri Tengkoe Abdoel Hamid Orangkaja Sri Maharadja Mangkoe Boemi|Sri Tengkoe Abdoel Hamid Orangkaja Sri Maharadja Mangkoe Boemi]] of [[Lho Seumawe|Lho' Seumawé]] signed it in [[1904]]. The *Korte Verklaring* is the principal political compact between the *oeléebalangs* and the Dutch government in the post-pacification period, and the formal basis of the colonial administration of the Atjehnese *landschappen*.
