**penghoeloe Tjiq** (English: "lineage descended from Radja Dewa in Alaslanden") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Influential geslacht (lineage) in Alaslanden descended from Radja Dewa, the Menangkabau immigrant who brought Islam to the Alassers.

## Translation

lineage descended from [[Radja Dewa]] in [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]]

## Description

Influential geslacht (lineage) in [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]] descended from [[Radja Dewa]], the [[Menangkabau]] immigrant who brought [[Islam]] to the [[Alas|Alassers]].

## Significance

The term *penghoeloe Tjiq* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
