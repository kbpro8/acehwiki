**Militaire Willemsorde** (Military William Order) was the highest Dutch chivalric order for military merit, instituted in **[[1815]]**. During the **Aceh War**, several officers—including **[[P. J. graaf van Hogendorp]]**, **[[N. A. vau Blom]]**, and **[[D. van Peppel]]**—were appointed to the 4th class for distinguished conduct in riverine operations such as the **Tamiang** expeditions of **[[1893]]**.

## Criteria

Awarded for bravery, leadership, and successful command in combat, the order was often cited in Dutch naval yearbooks.
