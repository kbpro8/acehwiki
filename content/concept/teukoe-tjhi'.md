**Teukoe Tjhi'** (English: "Title of the zelfbestuurder of Keureutòë") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Title of the zelfbestuurder of the oldest core of the landschap Keureutòë in Lho' Soekon; could not pronounce judgment without the presence of the peutoeha peuët.

## Translation

Title of the [[zelfbestuurder]] of [[Keureutòë]]

## Description

Title of the [[zelfbestuurder]] of the oldest core of the [[landschap]] [[Keureutòë]] in [[Lho'Soekon|Lho' Soekon]]; could not pronounce judgment without the presence of the [[peutoeha peuët]].

## Significance

The term *Teukoe Tjhi'* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
