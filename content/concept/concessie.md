**Concessie** (English: "Concession") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Mining concession; oil concessions in Langsa onderafdeeling issued to BPM (3) and earlier Perlak companies; no new concessions issued after 1928.

## Translation

Concession

## Description

Mining concession; oil concessions in [[Langsar|Langsa]] [[onderafdeeling]] issued to BPM (3) and earlier [[Perlak]] companies; no new concessions issued after [[1928]].

## Significance

The term *Concessie* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
