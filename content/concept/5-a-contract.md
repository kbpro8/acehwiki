**5 A contract** (English: "Type of oil exploration contract") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Type of contract signed from 1928 between the government and BPM/NKPM (replacing new concessions).

## Translation

Type of oil exploration contract

## Description

Type of contract signed from [[1928]] between the government and BPM/[[N.K.P.M.|NKPM]] (replacing new concessions). BPM received 5 blocks: Oost-Atjeh, [[Tamiang]], Noord-Atjeh, [[Djambo Ajé]], Atjeh-complex. [[N.K.P.M.|NKPM]] received 3: [[Pasai|Pasé]], [[Rajeu]], [[Langsar|Langsa]].

## Significance

The term *5 A contract* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
