The **peutoeha parang**, also called **peutoeha djaga**, was the overseer employed by a *[[Peutoeha pangkaj|peutoeha pangkaj]]* in the Atjehnese pepper economy, through whom planters were enrolled and advances disbursed.

## Functions

An aspirant planter might report either directly to the capitalist contractor or to one of his overseers, and be enrolled orally as an *[[Aneu' seuneubô|aneu' seuneubô]]*. The advance — *padi*, clothing, tools and cash — was then handed over through the *peutoeha djaga*.

## Remuneration

The overseer's honorarium was originally included in the *[[Wathé pangkaj|wathé pangkaj]]* of one-tenth of the harvest. Under the fixed tariff later introduced by the administration, in consequence of disputes arising among others between *peutoeha pangkaj* and *peutoeha parang*, the *aneu' seuneubô* rendered fifty cents per *picol* as *wathé peutoeha*, also called *wathé parang*, in addition to one guilder per *picol* as *wathé pangkaj*.
