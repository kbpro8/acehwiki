**marga** (English: "Marga (territorial unit in Alaslanden)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Territorial division created in 1912 in the Alaslanden, four per realm, each under a penghoeloe si'mpat.

## Translation

Marga (territorial unit in [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]])

## Description

Territorial division created in [[1912]] in the [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]], four per realm, each under a [[penghoeloe si'mpat]]. Replaced a flawed earlier division.

## Significance

The term *marga* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
