**Trassi** (English: "Shrimp paste") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Indispensable condiment for the oriental rice meal; 1937 export 7.5 ton.

## Translation

Shrimp paste

## Description

Indispensable condiment for the oriental rice meal; [[1937]] export 7.5 ton.

## Significance

The term *Trassi* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
