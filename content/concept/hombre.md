**Hombre** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Van Militair naar Civiel Bestuur]]

"And there lies the catch." Virtually all soldiers governing in Atjeh and there "vastgegroeid" judged the proposition wrong: in their view Atjeh could not for the foreseeable future be governed by *[[Binnenlandsch Bestuur|B.B.]]* officials. Du Croo set out their case in the imagery of the card game *[[Hombre]]* — "spadille, manille, basta, ponto — de 4 hoogste troeven in het vroeger in Atjeh algemeen gespeelde Hombre-kaartspel."
