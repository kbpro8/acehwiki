**Koninginnedag** — "Queen's Day" — was the annual celebration of the Queen's birthday in the [[Nederlands-Indië|Dutch East Indies]], the occasion on which every *[[negorij]]* on [[Ambon]] had its *fluitorkest* (flute orchestra) make a *rondgang* (tour) along the Resident and the Militaire Commandant. In *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* Koninginnedag is the book's set-piece of the colonial loyalty of the Ambonese *negorij*: the day on which the *negorij*'s pride in its soldiers — its *Ridders [[Militaire Willemsorde|M.W.O.]]*, its *[[Medaille voor Moed en Trouw]]* dragers, its *[[Kroontje (Eervolle Vermelding)|Kroontje]]* holders — was made visible.

## The rondgang on Ambon

"On [[Ambon]] had every *[[negorij]]* its *fluitorkest* and on the morning of Koninginnedag these orchestras made *rondgang* along the Resident and the Militaire Commandant." The *rondgang* — the tour — of the *fluitorkest* along the Resident and the Militaire Commandant was the Ambonese *negorij*'s annual display of loyalty to the Queen. The *rondgang* was therefore both a political ceremony (the display of loyalty) and a social ceremony (the display of the *negorij*'s pride in its soldiers).

## The tangsi celebration

On posts and in the *[[tangsi]]* Koninginnedag was celebrated with morning parade, sports, and evening music and dance. The Koninginnedag celebration was one of the *feesten* of the *tangsi* — alongside the *[[Tahoen Baroe|Nieuwjaar]]* (the grootste, vrolijkste feest), the *[[Poeusa]]* (the end of the fasting month), the *[[schietfeest / prijsschieten|schietfeest]]* (the annual shooting festival), and the *[[compagniesfeest / bataljonsfeest|compagniesfeest]]*.

## The colonne Schmidt Koninginnedag

"Op Koninginnedag ging men op stap, op [[2 September]] vond Nussy een voetafdruk." On Koninginnedag the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]] went "op stap" — on the move — to look for sporen of a rund that *[[Tgk. Tjhi Majét]]* had to slacht for his *[[kandoeri]]* (feestmaaltijd at the eind of the *[[vastenmaand]]*). On [[2 September]] Nussy found the *prent* of a runderhoef — the sporen were carefully bedekt with bladeren. The Koninginnedag was therefore, in the colonne Schmidt, the occasion of the patrol that led to the destruction of the Tiriō bende.

## Significance

Koninginnedag is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as the annual celebration of the Queen's birthday and the occasion of the Ambonese *negorij*'s display of loyalty. The book's argument is that Koninginnedag was both a political ceremony (the display of loyalty to the Queen) and a social ceremony (the display of the *negorij*'s pride in its soldiers). The Koninginnedag of the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]] — the patrol that led to the destruction of the Tiriō bende — is the book's small marker of the irony of the colonial loyalty: the day of loyalty to the Queen was, in the colonne Schmidt, the day of the patrol that destroyed the last Acehnese resistance.
