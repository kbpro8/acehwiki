**Klewang-aanval** (English: "Sword (klewang) attack") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Sword attacks by Acehnese against Dutch troops during action years—testimony to skill of Acehnese smiths in forging blanke wapenen.

## Translation

Sword ([[Klewang|klewang]]) attack

## Description

Sword attacks by Acehnese against Dutch troops during action years—testimony to skill of Acehnese smiths in forging blanke wapenen.

## Significance

The term *Klewang-aanval* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
