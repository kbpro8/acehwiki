**Teungkoe** — also written *Teukoe* (pronounced *tekoe*) or *Tengkoe* — is an Atjehnese honorific which is, in Broersma's account, *'eigenlijk wel hetzelfde als toeankoe'* — *really much the same as toeankoe* — and a designation of high official dignity. The fuller form *[[Toeankoe (title)|toeankoe]]* is borne particularly by the sultan and his relatives; the shorter form *teungkoe/teukoe/tengkoe* is borne by chiefs and dignitaries, often in combination with a further title, as in *Teukoe Tjhi Perlak* (where *Tjhi* is the *keutihi* title), *Teungkoe habib Moehamat bin Sajet Oesoeih* (where *habib* indicates Arabic descent), or *Teukoe moeda Oesoch* (where *moeda* means *young*). The title is the principal non-sultanic honorific of Atjehnese chiefs.
