**saudörö's** (English: "Brothers (in Gajo)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Stemgerechtigden (voting members) of the blah, who together with the rëdjö, toeö, and imöm form the blah — effectively a republican government with patriarchal traits.

## Translation

Brothers (in [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]])

## Description

Stemgerechtigden (voting members) of the [[Blah|blah]], who together with the rëdjö, [[toeö]], and [[imöm]] form the [[Blah|blah]] — effectively a republican government with patriarchal traits.

## Significance

The term *saudörö's* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
