**Atjeh-opstellen** (Aceh Essays) is a series of writings by [[Keuchenius|W. H. J. Keuchenius]], published in April [[1896]], which analyzed the situation in [[Aceh]] and the military campaign led by [[J. B. van Heutsz]]. The work is significant as it represented a contemporary, public endorsement of Van Heutsz's policies and is cited by Keuchenius to establish his own credibility and engagement with Acehnese affairs.

## Publication and content

Keuchenius published his Atjeh-opstellen in April [[1896]], coinciding with the transfer of Lieutenant-Colonel J. B. van Heutsz to Aceh. In these writings, Keuchenius identified a "light-point" emerging on the Aceh horizon. He argued that after numerous failed attempts, the only remaining option was the submission of Aceh through "[[Kracht van wapenen|kracht van wapenen]]" (force of arms) and that Van Heutsz was the man designated for this task. The works appear to have been a public commentary on Dutch colonial policy in Aceh, arguing for a decisive, military solution.

## Significance

The Atjeh-opstellen serve as a primary source for understanding the climate of opinion among colonial supporters in the early 20th century. Keuchenius uses them to establish his authority and his long-standing interest in Acehnese affairs, which he then parleys into a platform for advocating broader economic reforms in his [[1905]] letter to Governor-General Van Heutsz. They are a clear example of how the resolution of the Aceh War was used to legitimize other colonial policies and personalities.
