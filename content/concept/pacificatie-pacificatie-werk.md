**pacificatie / pacificatie-werk** (English: "pacification / pacification work") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Dutch policy of consolidating control: regulating administration, organizing judiciary, introducing public education, supporting war-damage recovery, restoring landshoofden to old position.

## Translation

pacification / pacification work

## Description

Dutch policy of consolidating control: regulating administration, organizing judiciary, introducing public education, supporting war-damage recovery, restoring landshoofden to old position. Began under [[Gouverneur Van Daalen|Van Daalen]] ([[1905]]-[[1908]]), continued under [[Gouverneur Swart|Swart]] ([[1908]]-[[1918]]).

## Significance

The term *pacificatie / pacificatie-werk* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
