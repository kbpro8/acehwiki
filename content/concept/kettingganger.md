A **kettingganger** — literally a "chain-walker" — was a convict sentenced to labour in irons, employed in large numbers on Dutch military works and transport in [[Groot-Atjeh]] alongside hired *koelies* (porters).

## Under Van der Heijden

[[G. B. Hooijer]] recorded that the great encampment raised by the columns of [[General van der Heijden]] at [[Toengkoeb]] contained, besides two battalions of infantry, a detachment of cavalry, three sections of artillery, sappers and survey personnel, *een stoet van duizend dwangarbeiders en koelies* — a train of a thousand convict labourers and coolies. At the bivouacs on both banks of the [[Acheh River|Atjeh-rivier]] near [[Anagaloeng]], *honderden en nogmaals honderden koelies en kettinggangers brachten op hun matjes de nachten door onder afdakjes, waaronder zij nauwelijks konden staan* — hundreds upon hundreds of coolies and convicts passed the nights on their mats beneath lean-tos under which they could scarcely stand.

## The lamplighter of Tjot Rang

During the investment of [[Tjot Rang]] in [[April]] [[1896]], Hooijer recorded that *de brutale kettingganger, tevens lantaarnopsteker*, tried under the enemy's fire to catch some fish from the surrounding pools as a delicacy for the officers, while the garrison looked out from one day to the next for relief.

## Coolies in transport

Hired Achehnese coolies formed part of the fortnightly *[[Vivres|vivres]]* transports, carrying *[[Tandoe|tandoes]]* and provisions, and a thousand coolies were to have been assembled by [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]] at [[Biloel]] on [[29 March]] [[1896]] for the action against [[Lamkrak]].
