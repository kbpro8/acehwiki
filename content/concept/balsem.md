**Balsem** (English: "Balsam / pine resin") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Resin tapped from Pinus Merkusii via gutter-wound in sapwood; refreshed every 3 days; collected in cups, distilled to terpentijn (turpentine) + hars (rosin).

## Translation

Balsam / pine [[Resin (Aceh)|resin]]

## Description

[[Resin (Aceh)|Resin]] tapped from [[Pinus merkusii|Pinus Merkusii]] via gutter-wound in sapwood; refreshed every 3 days; collected in cups, distilled to [[Terpentijn|terpentijn]] (turpentine) + [[Hars|hars]] (rosin).

## Significance

The term *Balsem* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
