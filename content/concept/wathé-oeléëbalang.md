**Wathé oeléëbalang** was the export duty of one guilder per *picol* payable to the self-governing ruler on the export of pepper from a *[[Landschap (Atjehnese)|landschap]]* of [[Aceh|Atjeh]], under the fixed tariff introduced by the administration for the pepper economy.

## Position in the tariff

The *[[Aneu' seuneubô|aneu' seuneubô]]* had to render one guilder per *picol* as *[[Wathé pangkaj|wathé pangkaj]]* and fifty cents per *picol* as *wathé peutoeha*, "onverminderd het als *wathé oeléëbalang* aan den zelfbestuurder nog verschuldigde bedrag van nogmaals f 1.— per picol bij den uitvoer van het product" — without prejudice to the further one guilder per *picol* due to the self-governing ruler on export.

## Effect on the interest of the ruler

Where the *[[Oeleebalang|oeléëbalang]]* was himself the *pangkaj*-giver, he therefore took two guilders per *picol*. This, [[D. W. N. de Boer]] noted, was why the wealthiest among the self-governing rulers were so strongly committed to pepper cultivation. Under the free-planter system the ruler's calculation was similar: for a single outlay of twenty guilders per thousand vines he obtained a quarter of the production of each new plantation, plus that much more *[[Wathe|wathé]]* on export and that many more percentages of the income tax.
