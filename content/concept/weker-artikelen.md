**Weker-artikelen** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Critical articles in the Avonpost titled 'Hoe beschaafd Nederland in de twintigste eeuw vrede en orde schept op Atjeh' that prompted the High Government to invite the landvoogd personally to investigate Aceh, leading to the dismissal of Lt.-Gen.

## Description

Critical articles in the Avonpost titled 'Hoe beschaafd [[Netherlands|Nederland]] in de twintigste eeuw vrede en orde schept op Atjeh' that prompted the High Government to invite the landvoogd personally to investigate Aceh, leading to the dismissal of Lt.-Gen. [[Gouverneur Van Daalen|Van Daalen]] and the exile of the ex-[[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]] to [[Amboina (Z.M. stoomschip)|Amboina]].

## Significance

The term *Weker-artikelen* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
