**volkscredietbank** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[J. C. Lamster]]

Lamster described the treatment of the returning *uitgewekenen*, the caution of not troubling the newly submitted with novelties, the small interest-free advances from the *[[volkscredietbank]]*, the extension of the [[Atjehtram]] to [[Deli]], the preparation of the [[Gajòweg]] for motor traffic, the replacement of muddy paths by gravelled *kampoeng* paths planted with shade trees, and the endless work of administrators and patrols in improving the neglected and unhygienic *kampoengs*.

### From [[Het Pacificeerende Bestuurswerk 1908-1918]]

"They were, however, wise enough not to trouble the newly submitted at once with all manner of novelties. Measures were also taken so that, with the help of family, of friends, or if need be of a modest interest-free advance from a [[volkscredietbank|people's credit bank]], they could begin their new life on a reasonable footing."
