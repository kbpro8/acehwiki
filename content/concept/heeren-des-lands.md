**heeren des lands** (English: "Lords of the land") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Epithet for the oelèëbalangs of Groot-Atjeh, who consider themselves more prominent than their counterparts in the onderhoorigheden because no political contract was signed with them, only with those of the onderhoorigheden.

## Translation

Lords of the land

## Description

Epithet for the oelèëbalangs of [[Groot-Atjeh]], who consider themselves more prominent than their counterparts in the [[Onderhoorigheden|onderhoorigheden]] because no political contract was signed with them, only with those of the [[Onderhoorigheden|onderhoorigheden]].

## Significance

The term *heeren des lands* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
