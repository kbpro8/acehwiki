**schakel-officieren** (English: "Liaison officers") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Military officers stationed at semi-permanent or temporary bivaks in unreliable regions, tasked with patrolling the surrounding area and forming a link between the onderafdeelingschef and the local chiefs/population.

## Translation

Liaison officers

## Description

Military officers stationed at semi-permanent or temporary bivaks in unreliable regions, tasked with patrolling the surrounding area and forming a link between the onderafdeelingschef and the local chiefs/population. Still functioning at [[Blang Pidië]], [[Djeuram]], [[Koeala Bhée]], [[Geunteuët]], and [[Lököp]].

## Significance

The term *schakel-officieren* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
