**Allah (God)** is the Arabic term for the singular deity of Islam, central to the theological framework of Acehnese Islam. In the Malay translation of the *Burda*, *Allah* appears repeatedly in verses praising divine mercy and seeking forgiveness.

## Theological Role

*Allah* is described in the Qur’an as the sole creator and sustainer of the universe. The poem invokes *Allah* to affirm the Prophet [[Muhammad]]’s status as the most faithful of all creations.

## Presence in Acehnese Religious Texts

The use of *Allah* in Acehnese poetry reflects the integration of Arabic religious terminology into the Malay language, underscoring the community’s adherence to orthodox Islamic belief.
