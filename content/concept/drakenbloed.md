**Drakenbloed** (English: "Dragon's blood") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Reddish powder dye from rotan-vrucht (rattan fruit); 1937 yield 3,850 KG.

## Translation

Dragon's blood

## Description

Reddish powder dye from [[Rattan (Aceh)|rotan]]-vrucht ([[Rattan (Aceh)|rattan]] fruit); [[1937]] yield 3,850 KG.

## Significance

The term *Drakenbloed* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
