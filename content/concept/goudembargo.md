**Goudembargo** (English: "Gold embargo") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Government measure that coincided with significant world-market price rises, dealing import trade a sensitive blow but without serious consequences.

## Translation

Gold embargo

## Description

Government measure that coincided with significant world-market price rises, dealing import trade a sensitive blow but without serious consequences.

## Significance

The term *Goudembargo* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
