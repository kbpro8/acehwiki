**kinderen van vader keutjhi en moeder teungkoe** (English: "'Children of father keutjhi and mother teungkoe'") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Atjehsch metaphor describing the gampongbevolking as children of the village chief (father) and the village religious leader (mother), illustrating the dual source of village authority.

## Translation

'Children of father keutjhi and mother [[teungkoe]]'

## Description

Atjehsch metaphor describing the gampongbevolking as children of the village chief (father) and the village religious leader (mother), illustrating the dual source of village authority.

## Significance

The term *kinderen van vader keutjhi en moeder teungkoe* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
