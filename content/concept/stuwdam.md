**Stuwdam** (English: "Dam / weir") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Primitive irrigation dams; mostly not resistant to heavy bandjirs (floods); water tapped exclusively from small rivers which carry little water in dry season.

## Translation

Dam / weir

## Description

Primitive irrigation dams; mostly not resistant to heavy bandjirs (floods); water tapped exclusively from small rivers which carry little water in dry season.

## Significance

The term *Stuwdam* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
