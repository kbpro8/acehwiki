**adoration mutuelle** (English: "mutual admiration") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Mutual admiration clique mentality found among so many groups of Europeans in the Dutch East Indies, especially in Atjeh, to the detriment of land and people.

## Translation

mutual admiration

## Description

Mutual admiration clique mentality found among so many groups of Europeans in the [[Dutch East Indies]], especially in Atjeh, to the detriment of land and people. [[Resident Jongejans|Jongejans]] claims to be free of it.

## Significance

The term *adoration mutuelle* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
