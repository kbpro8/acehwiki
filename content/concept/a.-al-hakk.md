**A. al-hakk** was an Acehnese‑Malay rendering of the Arabic phrase **al‑ḥaqq** (الحق) “the truth”, glossed in the Burda glossary as “nubureatnja, de waarheid van zijn profeetschap” (the truth of his prophethood). The entry appears on page 92 of the *Malay translation of the Burda* and illustrates how Acehnese scholars expressed doctrinal affirmations of the Prophet’s authenticity.

## Context

The term is used in devotional passages that emphasize the veracity of the Prophet Muhammad’s message, reinforcing Acehnese theological orthodoxy.
