**erfelijk individueel bezitsrecht** (English: "Hereditary individual possession right") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Form of land tenure granted to colonists who complete the development of their allocated plots within the stipulated period (usually three years); compared to the system on Java.

## Translation

Hereditary individual possession right

## Description

Form of land tenure granted to colonists who complete the development of their allocated plots within the stipulated period (usually three years); compared to the system on [[Madiun and Surabaya|Java]].

## Significance

The term *erfelijk individueel bezitsrecht* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
