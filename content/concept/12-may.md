**[[12 May]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[Het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh (Jubileum-nummer 1930)|Het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh]]* it marks the following events.

## Events on this date

- The departure of [[W. H. Loef]] for the Indies as a soldier, [[12 May]] [[1899]] ([[1899]])

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

Hospital return of the Atjeh garrison quoted in the pamphlet: on the King's feast-day of this date in [[1874]], 308 ambulance patients and 223 quarter-sick at [[Kuta Raja|Kota Radja]] (see [[Bezettingskorps in Atjeh]]).
