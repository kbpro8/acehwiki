**ratéb** (English: "ritual dance/play with self-wounding") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Performed to embellish family celebrations.

## Translation

ritual dance/play with self-wounding

## Description

Performed to embellish family celebrations. Players in two rows; only tambourines and human voice. Develops into an 'invulnerability' performance in which players use iron awls and other sharp weapons, sometimes putting on glowing chains, burning fuse in mouth/throat, pot of glowing coals on head, cutting tongue, sitting on thorns. Includes tricks.

## Significance

The term *ratéb* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
