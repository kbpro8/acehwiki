**[[8 December]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]* it appears in the articles listed below.

## Articles referring to this date

- [[1908]]
- [[The resistance in Atjeh 1908–1912]]
- [[Pidië]]

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this date in [[1885]] [[I. D. Fransen van de Putte|Fransen van de Putte]], in the Eerste Kamer, declared the concentration excellently successful, recommended passive waiting for 'a generation or two', and urged the government not to give way to the theoretical offensive demands, which he caricatured (see [[Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk in zake het Atjeh-vraagstuk (Verstege, 1886)]]).
