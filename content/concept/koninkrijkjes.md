**koninkrijkjes** (English: "small kingdoms") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. The numerous small polities under various princes and petty princes with varying power and influence that existed in pre-Islamic Atjeh.

## Translation

small kingdoms

## Description

The numerous small polities under various princes and petty princes with varying power and influence that existed in pre-Islamic Atjeh. [[Marco Polo]] (c. [[1290]]) mentioned six of them.

## Significance

The term *koninkrijkjes* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
