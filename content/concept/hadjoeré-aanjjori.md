**hadjoeré / aanjjori** (English: "'scoundrels' / 'thieves' (for pradjoerits)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Acehnese derogatory names for pradjoerits (armed police) — wordplay from the actie-tijd illustrating Acehnese humor and contempt for the police.

## Translation

'scoundrels' / 'thieves' (for [[Pradjoerits|pradjoerits]])

## Description

Acehnese derogatory names for [[Pradjoerits|pradjoerits]] ([[Gewapende Politie|armed police]]) — wordplay from the [[actie-tijd]] illustrating Acehnese humor and contempt for the police.

## Significance

The term *hadjoeré / aanjjori* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
