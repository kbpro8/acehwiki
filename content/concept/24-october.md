**[[24 October]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]* it appears in the articles listed below.

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this date in [[1885]] the enemy blocked the tramway between [[Oleh-leh]] and [[Kuta Raja|Kota Radja]] with iron wire and beams; in [[1872]] this was the date of minister [[I. D. Fransen van de Putte|Fransen van de Putte]]'s letter advising no war with Atjeh (see [[1872]]).
