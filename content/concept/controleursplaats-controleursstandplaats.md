**controleursplaats / controleursstandplaats** (English: "controller's seat") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Town where a controleur (controller, BB official) is stationed.

## Translation

controller's seat

## Description

Town where a [[Controleur|controleur]] (controller, BB official) is stationed. Examples: [[Lho'Nga]] (until [[1932]]), [[Tjalang]], [[Bireuën]], [[Meureudu|Meureudoe]].

## Significance

The term *controleursplaats / controleursstandplaats* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
