**April 1873** is the month of the historic storming of the mosque of [[Koeta-Radja|Koeta Radja]] by Dutch troops — the [[Eerste Atjeh-expeditie (1873)|Eerste Atjeh-expeditie]] — when "the building burned down and later was completely demolished." In *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* by Merac ([[1947]]), April 1873 is the book's marker of the opening of the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]].

## The storming

"At the historic storming of this mosque by the Dutch troops in April 1873, the building burned down and later was completely demolished." At the historic storming of this mosque by Dutch troops in April 1873, the building burned down and later was completely demolished. The April 1873 storming is the book's marker of the opening of the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]]: the colonial army's storming of the [[Meusigit Raja|mosque]] that the Acehnese population had built on the site of the first mosque of [[Sultan Iskandar Muda]].

## Significance

April 1873 is significant in *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* as the month of the historic storming of the mosque of Koeta Radja. The book presents the burning and demolition of the mosque as the act that ignited the Aceh War — a foundational event in the colonial history of Aceh. The month April 1873 thus serves as the book's marker for the beginning of the conflict.
