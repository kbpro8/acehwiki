**angkaphuwelijk** (English: "matriarchal marriage form (Gajo)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Marriage form in Gajo — a matriarchal element inserted into a patriarchal system; the man follows the woman, is incorporated into the wife's lineage, and the married man and his offspring come under the authority of the father-in-law (not the mother-in-law).

## Translation

matriarchal marriage form ([[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]])

## Description

Marriage form in [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]] — a matriarchal element inserted into a patriarchal system; the man follows the woman, is incorporated into the wife's lineage, and the married man and his offspring come under the authority of the father-in-law (not the mother-in-law). The institution remains patriarchal but with a matriarchal insertion.

## Significance

The term *angkaphuwelijk* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
