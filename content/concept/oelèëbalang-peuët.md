**oelèëbalang peuët** (English: "Four-oelèëbalang") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Sub-chief title encountered in the zelfbestuursgebied.

## Translation

Four-[[oelèëbalang]]

## Description

Sub-chief title encountered in the zelfbestuursgebied.

## Significance

The term *oelèëbalang peuët* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
