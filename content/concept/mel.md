**Mèl** — Acehnese: *to submit*, *to surrender* — is the act of submission to the *Kompeuni*'s authority, the alternative to [[Sjahid|sjahid]]. *In die harde dagen voor den Atjeher maar twee mogelijkheden: mèl of sjahid. Deze twee woorden hebben het lot van tienduizenden Atjehers bepaald, en ook thans nog, nu de oorlog lang achter ons licht, hoort men ze voortdurend in den mond der ouderen als ze spreken over het lot der mannen uit vroegere jaren. Men zegt: Pang Agam mèl in Lho Soekon, en Teukoe X werd sjahid aan de Wojla. Mèl of sjahid, entweder oder. Hij heeft zich gemeld: mèl, dus onderworpen, of is sjahid geworden: gevallen in den strijd tegen de ongeloovigen*.

## The ceremony of mèl

The submission ceremony — *op goede Atjehsche manier, dus heel duidelijk* — was as follows, as [[Gosenson]] required of the forty candidates at [[Troemon]] in [[1926]]: *ieder ventje dat zich wil onderwerpen, moet inleveren: klewang en rentjong of toembak (lans); dit laatste was facultatief. Er zat zooveel ijzer onder de bevolking dat het best met wat minder kon. Verder moesten ze zich onderwerpen op goede Atjehsche manier, dus heel duidelijk. Den volgenden middag stonden ze alle veertig buiten het bivak, hun wapens gewikkeld in witte doeken, en de wacht liet ze één voor één binnen; het is altijd mogelijk dat die menschen zich op het laatste moment nog bedenken. Het ging echter heel goed; ze kwamen in volgorde binnen, gaven hun wapens over, knielden, brachten den kniekus en daarna den voetkus, en zeiden: "Tjee ampon dèësa oelon" (bede om vergiffenis), waarmee nu wel heel klaar vaststond wie voortaan in dit land de baas zou zijn*. The *kniekus* and the *voetkus* — the [[Sembah|sembah]] of submission — were the visible sign.

## The mèl of T. Tjhi Toenong

[[T. Tjhi Toenong]] — the *toenong* of [[Keureutoe|Keureutoë]] — *meldde zich met zijn volgelingen te Lho Seumawé* on [[5 October]] [[1903]] — *toen Panglima Polim zich in de tweede helft van 1903 had onderworpen wist T. Tjhi Toenong dat ook voor hem het uur was gekomen*. *Hij onderwierp zich* — but the *mèl* was deceptive; T. Tjhi Toenong, *die in een dozijn gevechten niet de kroon van den sjahid had geschonnen*, was arrested within the year, condemned for his role in the [[Meurandeh Paja massacre (1905)|Meurandeh Paja overval]], and [[Execution of T. Tjhi Toenong (1905)|fusilladed]] at the zeestrand of [[Lho Seumawé]] on [[5 March]] [[1905]].

## The mèl of T. Radja Sabi

[[T. Radja Sabi|T. Radja Sabi]] — the *adelaarsjong* of the *toenong* line — *meldde zich* at [[Lho Soekon]] on [[13 March]] [[1919]]. *Saja tida tahan lagi — ik kan het niet meer dragen! Nu daalde hij uit de bergen en de wouden naar de vlakte, en nabij kampong Aloë Mira, in de buurt van menschen, maakte hij zich eene schuilplaats in de struiken. Des nachts ging hij de kampong in om voedsel te zoeken. Niemand scheen hem meer te kennen; hij was als een steen, in de zee geworpen en — vergeten*. *Hij had genoeg van het bosch; hij verlangde naar de vlakte*. After years of *boschleven* with the Tiro-oelama's, the *mèl* of T. Radja Sabi ended the *adelaarsjong*'s long flight.

## The mèl of the others

*Habib Abdoerrachman el Zahir* *onderwierp zich* on [[13 October]] [[1878]] *op deze voorwaarde: dat hij zich te Mekka zou vestigen met een jaargeld, ten laste der koloniale begrooting, van niet minder dan 10.000 dollar*. *Panglima Polim* *zich in de tweede helft van 1903 had onderworpen*. *Pang Agam mèl in Lho Soekon*. The *mèl* was a public act, witnessed by the *oeléëbalang* and the *keuchi'*; it ended the *prang sabil* of the individual and brought him under the *adat* of the Gouvernement. *Een lafaard omdat hij mèl had verkozen boven sjahid* — so the women of the resistance judged the *mèl*-submitter; *alle anderen zijn gesneuveld uit haar hart en haar leven gezet, en haar kind had geen vader meer*.

## The Dutch policy framework of *Onderwerping* — Van Heutsz's 1893 tract

The Acehnese act of *mèl* — the personal submission of an individual warrior or chief to Dutch authority — is distinct from, but historically intertwined with, the Dutch colonial policy framework of ***Onderwerping*** — *subjugation* or *submission* as a political object. The two terms share the same Dutch/Malay root (*onderwerpen* / *menundukkan*, "to submit"), but they denote different things: *mèl* is the act of an individual, *Onderwerping* is the policy of a state. The intersection of the two — the Dutch policy of producing *mèl* across the entire Acehnese population — is the analytical core of [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]'s [[De onderwerping van Atjeh (Van Heutsz, 1893)|1893 tract]] *De onderwerping van Atjeh*, in which Van Heutsz diagnosed why two decades of war had failed to produce the *volkomen onderwerping* (complete subjugation) of the *rijk* (realm).

### The *onderwerping* as the title and analytical core of the 1893 tract

The *onderwerping* is the title and the analytical core of Van Heutsz's 1893 tract. The book's title-page declares the subject: "*De onderwerping van Atjeh DOOR J. B. VAN HEUTSZ, Majoor der Infanterie gewezen Chef van den Staf in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden*" — The subjugation of Atjeh, by J. B. Van Heutsz, Major of Infantry, formerly Chief of Staff in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden. The opening sentence fixes the diagnostic frame:

> "Het zal nu weldra 20 jaren zijn, dat Nederland den oorlog met Atjeh begon en nog altijd kan men aan het telkens veranderen van stelsel duidelijk zien, dat de Regeering den juisten weg nog niet weet aan te geven, die tot de volkomen onderwerping van dat rijk leiden moet".

— it will soon be twenty years since the Netherlands began the war with Atjeh, and one can still see clearly from the constant changing of systems that the Regeering does not yet know how to indicate the right path that must lead to the complete subjugation of that realm. The *onderwerping* is, in this opening, the predicate the war was fought to produce and the predicate the policy oscillations had failed to achieve.

### The *axiom of submission*

The diagnostic is anchored in Van Heutsz's *axiom of submission*: "*Een vrijheidlievend en krijgshaftig volk onderwerpt zich niet anders dan gedwongen*" — a freedom-loving and warlike people does not submit otherwise than under compulsion. The axiom appears in the 1893 tract in the chapter on the post-[[1877]] *verzoeningspolitiek* — the policy of conciliation G.-G. [[Van Lansberge|van Lansberge]] had prescribed in March [[1877]] — and is the analytical hinge on which Van Heutsz's diagnosis turns: the *verzoeningspolitiek* could not produce the *onderwerping* because the Atjehnese could not be conciliated into submission; only *dwang* — compulsion — could produce the *onderwerping*, and the *dwang* required the operational instruments — the closure of the coast, the [[Scheepvaartregeling|scheepvaartregeling]], the assumption of the [[Hatsil|hatsil]] and the [[Opium pacht|opium-pacht]] — the policy oscillations had withdrawn. The *mèl*-submitter, in this axiom, submits not because he is conciliated but because he is compelled; the *onderwerping* of the *rijk* is the aggregate of individual *mèl*s produced by the operational *dwang*.

### The successive policy *stelsels* tested by the *onderwerping*

The *onderwerping* is, in Van Heutsz's narrative, the test against which the successive policy *stelsels* are measured. The first *stelsel* was the [[Van Swieten]] *geconcentreerde stelling Kotja-Radja* of [[1874]] — the concentrated defensive perimeter around [[Kota Radja]] garrisoned by 2500 men, under which the *Onderhoorigheden* were to be conciliated under *art. 5* of Van Swieten's [[Van Swieten's Instructie (22 April 1874)|Instructie]]. The second was the [[Pel (governor)|Pel]] coastal-line plan of [[30 April]] [[1875]] — the dispersed forty-seven-post coastal occupation that replaced the concentrated stelling. The third was the [[Diemont]] *verzoeningspolitiek*-cum-*gewapend optreden* of November [[1876]]. The fourth was the [[Van der Heijden]] conquest of the Sagi's of [[1878]]–[[1879]] — the campaigns against the [[XXII Moekims]] and [[XXVI Moekims]] that, in Van Heutsz's reading, briefly achieved the *onderwerping* of Groot-Atjeh: "*In September 1879, nog geen 14 maanden na het begin van zijne zegerijke veldtochten, had Generaal v. d. Heijden aan alle verzet een einde gemaakt*". The fifth was the *Civiel Gouverneur* regime of [[1881]]–[[1883]] whose "*ellendige zwakke stelsel*" followed the Van der Heijden dismissal. The sixth was the [[Laging Tobias]] *scheepvaartregeling* of May [[1883]] — the one measure Van Heutsz singled out as "*alles wat men kon verlangen*". The seventh was the [[Concentratiestelsel|Concentratie]] of [[19 August]] [[1884]] — the withdrawal to the *geconcentreerde stelling* combined with the *rigoureuse blokkade*. The eighth was the [[Demmeni]] *partieele blokkade*-cum-*soort scheepvaartregeling* of [[13 October]] [[1884]] — the *tweeslachtig ding* Van Heutsz denounces as the worst of both worlds. The ninth was the [[Van Teijn]] closure of the Noord- en Westkust of [[1888]]–[[1890]] — the operational model the 1893 tract would generalise. Each *stelsel* is, in Van Heutsz's diagnosis, measured by the test of *onderwerping*: did it, or did it not, advance the *volkomen onderwerping* of the *rijk*?

### The *18 artikelen* and the consolidation of *onderwerping*

The *onderwerping* was, in the operational order Van Heutsz prescribed, to be consolidated by the *18 artikelen* signed and sworn by each submitting statelet. The *18 artikelen* — the *beëdigde 18 artikelen* — were the surrender declarations the coastal chiefs had signed on their submission to Dutch authority: "*de 18 artikelen door de hoofden van de verschillende staten bij hunne onderwerping aan ons gezag beredigd en bezworen*". The articles bound the chiefs to recognise Dutch sovereignty, to abstain from hostilities, and to submit to such fiscal and administrative arrangements as the Dutch should prescribe. The text of the *18 artikelen* is reproduced in Van Heutsz's 1893 tract as *Bijlage II*. Van Heutsz's prescription was that the *18 artikelen* should be backed by *zware boeten* — heavy fines — for non-compliance: "*Voor [deze] moeten op het niet trouw nakomen van de verplichtingen opgelegd in de artikelen 2, 11, 13, 14 enz. van de geteekende verklaring zeer zware boeten gelegd worden, bij niet gewillige voldoening in te houden van de opbrengst der uitgevoerde producten*". The *boeten* were, in turn, to be the "*bijzondere inkomsten*" of the *scheepvaartregeling* — the graduated monthly fines by which the *onderwerping* of the chiefs was to be sustained.

### The *onderwerping* consolidated by the assumption of *hatsil* and *opium* revenues

The *onderwerping* was, in Van Heutsz's prescription, to be consolidated not only by the *18 artikelen* and the *boeten* but also by the assumption of the *hatsil* and the *opium-pacht*. The substitution was the constitutional complement of the *scheepvaartregeling*: the *cajoleering*-policy of paying hostile chiefs *traktement* — about ƒ 8000 a month, of which part flowed onward to [[Kemala]] and to the priest [[Tengkoe di Tirou]] — was to be abandoned in favour of a fiscal arrangement in which the chiefs received a *billijke schadeloosstelling* and a *tiënste* of the revenues collected, in exchange for the rights they ceded. The *hatsil* — the customary revenue share formerly paid to the sultan — was to be assumed by the Dutch, optionally slightly increased; the *opium-pacht* — anchored in the [[1890]] offer of a European firm to farm the opium-pacht at ƒ 250,000 per month for the first year, rising to ƒ 400,000 — was to be introduced as the principal "*vaste inkomsten*" of the *scheepvaartregeling*. The *onderwerping* and the *scheepvaartregeling* were, in this sense, the two sides of the same operational order: the *onderwerping* was the political predicate the *scheepvaartregeling* was designed to produce, and the *scheepvaartregeling* was the operational instrument by which the *onderwerping* was to be consolidated.

### Significance of the *Onderwerping* framework

The *Onderwerping*'s significance in the [[Aceh Wiki History]] record rests on three things: it was the political object of the [[Atjeh-oorlog (1873-1904)|Atjeh-oorlog]] — the *volkomen onderwerping* of the *rijk* whose achievement the tract's opening sentence declares the war's purpose — and the analytical core of Van Heutsz's 1893 diagnosis; it was the test against which the successive policy *stelsels* were measured — the test the *verzoeningspolitiek* of [[1877]] and the *Civiel Gouverneur* regime of [[1881]]–[[1883]] had failed, the test the Van der Heijden conquests of [[1878]]–[[1879]] and the Van Teijn closure of [[1888]]–[[1890]] had briefly passed, and the test the *Concentratie* of [[1884]] and the *partieele blokkade* of Demmeni had betrayed; and it was the political predicate the *scheepvaartregeling* was designed to produce and the *18 artikelen* were designed to consolidate — the predicate whose operational backing was the closure of the coast, the *scheepvaartregeling*, the *hatsil*, and the *opium-pacht*, and whose absence the *cajoleering*-policy of *traktement*-payments to hostile chiefs had masked. The *onderwerping* is the Dutch colonial-policy counterpart of the Acehnese *mèl* — the state-policy whose object was the production of the individual acts of *mèl* that, in their aggregate, would constitute the *volkomen onderwerping* of the *rijk*.

## Significance

The *mèl* was the visible end of the resistance. *Deze twee woorden hebben het lot van tienduizenden Atjehers bepaald* — *mèl* or *sjahid*. Every *mèl* was a *kniekus* and a *voetkus*, a wrapping of the weapons in white cloth, a *Tjee ampon dèësa oelon*; every *mèl* was a transfer of loyalty from the *toenong* to the *baroh*, from the *oelama* to the *oeléëbalang*, from the *prang sabil* to the *adat* of the Gouvernement.
