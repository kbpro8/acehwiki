The **mantri-ziekenverpleger** was the indigenous nursing assistant of the Netherlands-Indies medical service, one of whom was attached to Dr. [[C. E. Gomperts]] throughout his [[Oogheelkundig kampongonderzoek in Groot-Atjeh (Maart-December 1937)|eye survey of 1937]] in [[Groot-Atjeh]].

## In the survey

Gomperts recorded under the heading *Personeel*: "Aan verslaggever was een mantri-ziekenverpleger toegevoegd, die vele- en goede diensten bij dit onderzoek heeft bewezen" — a *mantri-ziekenverpleger* was attached to the reporter, who rendered many and good services in this survey. His salary for the ten months of the survey, ƒ200, appears as a separate item in the statement of expenditure alongside the medicines, the travelling costs of the *Officier van Gezondheid* and the ophthalmological equipment.

The *mantri* appears in the first of the two photographs printed with the report, captioned "Oogheelkundige uitrusting met mantri-ziekenverpleger" — ophthalmological equipment with the *mantri-ziekenverpleger*.

## The interpreter

Besides the *mantri*, Gomperts's own chauffeur — an Atjehnese — served as "een zeer verdienstelijke tolk" (a very deserving interpreter) and was also helpful with the distribution of medicines. The five-seater car carrying the whole equipment still had room for four persons including the driver.
