**[[16 November]]** is the date of the first sitting of the [[Atjeh debate in the Tweede Kamer (16-17 November 1897)|Atjeh debate]] in the [[Tweede Kamer]] in [[1897]], held during the general deliberations on the [[Indische begrooting voor 1898|budget of Nederlandsch-Indië for 1898]].

## The sitting of [[16 November]] [[1897]]

The sitting is headed in the pamphlet *[[Atjeh in de Tweede Kamer (1897)]]* as *Vergadering van Dinsdag 16 Nov. [[1897]]*. On that day [[H. van Kol]], deputy for Enschedé, delivered the long historical indictment of Dutch policy in [[Acheh|Atjeh]] since [[1824]] and tabled [[Van Kol's motion of 16 November 1897|his motion]] for a commission of enquiry.

There followed on the same day the speeches of [[Pijnacker Hordijk]]; of [[J. B. Verhey|Verhey]] on the capacity of the Indies army and the [[Beri-beri epidemic in the Atjeh garrison|beri-beri mortality]]; of [[Abraham Kuyper|Kuyper]], printed under the heading *Een Christen aan het woord*; of [[Van der Zwaag]]; of [[Bahlmann]] on the two founding errors of the war; of [[Van Karnebeek]]; of [[Pyttersen]] with the precedent of [[Bonjol|Bondjoe]]; of [[De Visser]]; of [[Van Vlijmen]]; and of [[Pieter Jelles Troelstra|Troelstra]] on the [[Economic interests alleged behind the Atjeh-oorlog (1897)|economic interests]] behind the war. The Minister of Colonies [[J. T. Cremer|Cremer]] replied at length on the same day.

The debate was resumed and concluded on [[17 November]] [[1897]].

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this date in [[1882]] the Kamer-debate on the worrying state of Atjeh produced the statements of minister [[Willem Maurits de Brauw|de Brauw]] (see [[1882]]) and the warning of [[Rutgers van Rozenburg]] about the blood yet to be shed.
