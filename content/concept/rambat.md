**rambat** (English: "raised floor section of the Acehnese house") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Connection between voor- and achtergalerij, with a raised floor and one or two steps.

## Translation

raised floor section of the Acehnese house

## Description

Connection between voor- and achtergalerij, with a raised floor and one or two steps. Only entered by housemates and intimate friends.

## Significance

The term *rambat* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
