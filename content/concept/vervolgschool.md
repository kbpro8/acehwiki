**Vervolgschool** (English: "Continuation school") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Two-year course serving as 4th–5th class of volksschool, located in all larger places.

## Translation

Continuation school

## Description

Two-year course serving as 4th–5th class of [[Volksschool|volksschool]], located in all larger places. [[Sabang]]'s vervolgschool has Nederlandsch as a subject (no [[Hollandsch-Inlandsche school]] there). Two vervolgscholen have a 6th-class [[Landbouwklas|landbouwklas]]. [[Koeta-Radja|Koeta Radja]]'s vervolgschool has a handelsklas. 40 in number, ~100 teachers, ~3,200 pupils.

## Significance

The term *Vervolgschool* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
