**salam** refers to a *laurel* tree, identified in the source as “Benua Pohon (A. Dhit Salam)”, a place mentioned in Arabic poetry situated between *Mekka* and *Medina* (page [[90]]).

## Botanical Identification
The term likely denotes *Salix* or a related *Lauraceae* species, symbolically associated with victory and peace in Islamic poetry.

## Literary Role
In Acehnese literary tradition, *salam* appears in poetic allusions to the sacred journey between the holy cities, embodying a spiritual waypoint.
