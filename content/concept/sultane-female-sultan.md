**Sultane (female sultan)** (English: "female Sultan/Sultana") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Female ruler of Atjeh.

## Translation

female [[sultan|Sultan]]/Sultana

## Description

Female ruler of Atjeh. After [[Sultan Iskandar Moeda]]'s death, a successor's brief reign was followed by a Sultana - and in the 17th century three more Sultanas ruled in succession. This was contradictory to the [[bruidsschat]] concept (a woman cannot be married to a woman), evidence of the post-[[Sultan Iskandar Moeda|Iskandar]] crisis.

## Significance

The term *Sultane (female sultan)* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
