**Generaal Swart** is a Dutch-language military biography co-authored by [[Maurice Henri du Croo]] and [[Schmidt (Kolonel)|Kolonel Schmidt]]. The book concerns the life and career of a [[K.N.I.L.]] general named Swart — likely [[H. N. A. Swart]] (Hendrik Nikolaas Albert Swart), who served in the [[Aceh War]] as a young officer and later rose to high command. Du Croo's collaboration with Schmidt on this volume, together with *[[Marechaussée in Atjeh (book)|Marechaussée in Atjeh]]*, marked his contribution to the historical literature of the [[Dutch East Indies]] army and the colonial memory of the [[Aceh War]].

## Subject

The subject of the biography — [[H. N. A. Swart|General Swart]] — was the Aceh-connoisseur general whose ten-year administration consolidated order and peace in Aceh and brought the [[Aceh War]] to an end. In the colonial memory preserved by *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* (Merac, [[1947]]), Swart is the figure under whose administration "order and peace were consolidated in that province" — the phase that "brought the Aceh War to an end." The biography by du Croo and Schmidt thus belongs to the same commemorative literature that produced Merac's portrait of the general, though approached from the perspective of fellow [[K.N.I.L.]] officers rather than from the palaver-frame of the colonial sociëteit.

## Authors

- [[Maurice Henri du Croo]] served in Atjeh from [[1925]] and was decorated Knight of the [[Orde van Oranje-Nassau]] with Swords in [[1927]] for his service in the ongoing [[Aceh War]].
- [[Schmidt (Kolonel)|Kolonel Schmidt]] was a fellow [[K.N.I.L.]] officer who also collaborated with du Croo on *[[Marechaussée in Atjeh (book)|Marechaussée in Atjeh]]*.

## Significance

*Generaal Swart* is significant as a period document that consolidates the colonial memory of the [[H. N. A. Swart|Swart]] administration as the closing phase of the [[Aceh War]]. Together with *[[Marechaussée in Atjeh (book)|Marechaussée in Atjeh]]*, it forms the literary legacy of [[Maurice Henri du Croo]]'s Atjeh service. The biography is also one of the few book-length treatments of the [[H. N. A. Swart|Swart]] administration by a fellow officer, and is therefore a primary source for the institutional memory of the [[K.N.I.L.]] in the interwar period.
