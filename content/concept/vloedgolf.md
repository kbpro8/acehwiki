**vloedgolf** (English: "tidal wave / flood wave") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Tidal/flood wave that, combined with an aardbeving (earthquake), destroyed Oelèë Lheuë in August 1936.

## Translation

tidal wave / flood wave

## Description

Tidal/flood wave that, combined with an [[aardbeving]] (earthquake), destroyed [[Oelèë Lheuë]] in [[August]] [[1936]].

## Significance

The term *vloedgolf* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
