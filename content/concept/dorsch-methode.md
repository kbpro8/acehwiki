**Dorsch-methode** (English: "Threshing method") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Primitive Atjehse threshing method: trampling sheaves with bare feet while leaning on two sticks.

## Translation

Threshing method

## Description

Primitive Atjehse threshing method: trampling sheaves with bare feet while leaning on two sticks. Requires easily-detaching grain without sharp protrusions, excluding several superior [[Madiun and Surabaya|Java]] rice varieties.

## Significance

The term *Dorsch-methode* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
