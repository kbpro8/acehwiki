**Proeftuin** (English: "Experiment garden") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Trial gardens set up by Landbouwvoorlichtingsdienst in various places (e.g., Tangsé-vallei for robusta coffee) to demonstrate improvements.

## Translation

Experiment garden

## Description

Trial gardens set up by Landbouwvoorlichtingsdienst in various places (e.g., [[Tangsé-vallei]] for [[Robusta Coffee (Aceh)|robusta coffee]]) to demonstrate improvements.

## Significance

The term *Proeftuin* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
