**Patchouly / Nilam** (English: "Patchouli (Pogostemon cablin)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Aromatic plant exclusively of Westkust afdeeling; leaves yield a heavy-sweet essential oil used in perfumery.

## Translation

Patchouli (Pogostemon cablin)

## Description

Aromatic plant exclusively of [[Westkust]] [[afdeeling]]; leaves yield a heavy-sweet essential oil used in perfumery. Grown on hill slopes (rooftbouw system); affected by 'lepra-ziekte' (knob formation on leaves). [[1937]] export: 1,120 ton [[Nilam|nilam]]-blad + 22,000 KG oil.

## Significance

The term *Patchouly / Nilam* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
