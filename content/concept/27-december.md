**[[27 December]]** is the date of the solemn transfer of the [[Meusigit Raja]] to the Acehnese people on [[27 December]] [[1881]] — in the name of the Dutch government — by the Governor of Atjeh and Dependencies, [[A. Pruys van der Hoeven|A. Pruys van der Hoeven]]. In *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* by Merac ([[1947]]), [[27 December]] is the book's marker of the solemn transfer of the Meusigit Raja.

## The solemn transfer

"The mosque, whose first stone was laid on 9 October 1879 in the presence of Governor-General Van Lansberge, all the civil and military authorities, and many Acehnese chiefs and clergy, was completed in 1881 and on 27 December of that year was solemnly transferred, in the name of the Dutch government, by the Governor of Atjeh and Dependencies, Pruys van der Hoeven, to the Acehnese people." The mosque — completed in [[1881]] — was on [[27 December]] [[1881]] solemnly transferred to the Acehnese people by the Governor of Atjeh and Dependencies, Pruys van der Hoeven, in the name of the Dutch government. The [[27 December]] solemn transfer is the book's marker of the colonial state's formal handover of the Meusigit Raja to the Acehnese people.

## Significance

[[27 December]] is significant in *[[Oost-Indisch Journael]]* as the date of the solemn transfer of the [[Meusigit Raja]] on [[27 December]] [[1881]]. The book emphasizes that this ceremony was conducted in the name of the Dutch government by the Governor of Atjeh and Dependencies, Pruys van der Hoeven — marking the colonial state's formal return of the mosque to the Acehnese people. The date [[27 December]] thus serves as the book's marker for this pivotal act of colonial religious politics.
