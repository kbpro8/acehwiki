**wegenfonds** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[The resistance in Atjeh 1908–1912]]

The new course brought, in accordance with Swart's views, immediate redress where possible: the unlawfully withheld incomes were paid out, as were the securities wrongly deposited in the *[[wegenfonds]]* (road fund); the *[[oelèëbalang]]s* were supported and gradually returned to their *gampongs*; rice and seed-*padi* were supplied at the administration's expense, as were advances on a generous scale for the purchase of plough cattle.

### From [[Oelèëbalang]]

The *oelèëbalangs* were supported and gradually returned to their *[[gampong]]s*; the withheld incomes and the securities wrongly deposited in the *[[wegenfonds]]* were repaid; rice, seed-*padi* and advances for the purchase of plough cattle were supplied.

### From [[Boetenstelsel]]

The general policy of the new course was that "the unlawfully withheld incomes were paid out, as were the security deposits wrongly paid into the *[[wegenfonds]]* (road fund); the territorial chiefs were supported and gradually returned to their *[[gampong]]s*."
