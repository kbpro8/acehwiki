**[[16 April]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]* it marks the following events in the history of [[Groot-Atjeh]].

## Events on this date

- [[Relief of Tjot Rang (16 April 1896)]] — two columns of 2,700 men relieved [[Tjot Rang]] ([[1896]])

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this date in [[1874]] the Dutch suffered the [[Battle of Lampoe Oek (16 April 1874)|Lampoe Oek defeat]] of 9 dead and 86 wounded (of whom ten officers), about 700 paces from their own walls; the same date in the [[1874]] secret sessions heard [[Van Lijnden van Sandenburg]]'s 'onvergeeflijke fout' and [[Godefroi]]'s and the acclamation's first speeches (see [[Stieltjes]]).
