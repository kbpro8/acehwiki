A **controleur** was a Netherlands Indies civil administrator of the *Binnenlandsch Bestuur*, and in [[Groot-Atjeh]] during the alliance period the office was frequently filled by army officers acting in that capacity — *waarnemende controleurs* — in the districts cleared outside the *[[Geconcentreerde linie|geconcentreerde linie]]*.

## Luitenant Neelmeyer

The one named acting *controleur* in *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]* is [[Luitenant Neelmeyer]], described as *den waarnemenden controleur in zijn gebied* — the acting *controleur* in the territory of [[T. Tjoet Toengkoeb]] — and as an officer who had a particular knack of dealing with that difficult chief. It was in his capacity as *controleur* that he knew where the Achehnese auxiliary bands were to assemble on the morning after the defection of [[Teukoe Oemar]], and therefore rode by night to warn the garrisons of [[Toengkoeb]] and [[Tjot Rang]].

## The projected regular administration

Among the changes intended for [[1896]], which Hooijer suggested might have been the motive of Oemar's defection, was the conversion of the temporary posts into fixed settlements with all their apparatus: clearance of terrain, bridges, roads, dykes and tramways, vigorous police supervision by the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Korps Marechaussée]], and *een geregeld bestuur door onze waarnemende controleurs* — a regular administration by the Dutch acting *controleurs*.
