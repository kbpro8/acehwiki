**postendokter** (English: "district doctor") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Doctor who regularly visits the small polikliniekjes (clinics) of the little towns along the road from Sigli.

## Translation

district doctor

## Description

Doctor who regularly visits the small polikliniekjes (clinics) of the little towns along the road from [[Sigli]].

## Significance

The term *postendokter* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
