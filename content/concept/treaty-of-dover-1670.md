**Treaty of Dover (1670)** was the secret agreement concluded on **[[3 December]] [[1670]]** between **[[England|England]]** and **[[France|France]]**, in which **[[Charles II of England]]** received a subsidy of two million livres from **[[Louis XIV]]** in exchange for supporting French hostilities against the **[[United Provinces (Dutch Republic)]]**. The treaty’s clandestine nature undermined Dutch security and contributed to the **[[Rampjaar|Disaster Year]]** [[1672]], affecting the **[[Dutch East India Company|VOC]]**’s ability to protect its trade with the **[[Sultanate of Aceh]]**.

## Clause on Religion
One clause promised the conversion of the English king to Catholicism, a point that heightened domestic opposition in England and intensified diplomatic pressure on the Republic.
