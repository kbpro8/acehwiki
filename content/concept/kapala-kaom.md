**Kapala Kaom** was the informal clan leader among West Coast Acehnese settler communities (*nangrooe ranto*) in districts like [[Meulaboh]].

## Social Role

Unlike territorial lords (*uleebalang*), a *Kapala Kaom* held non-hereditary authority based on personal prestige and lineage, providing guidance and managing pepper credit networks for his clan members.
