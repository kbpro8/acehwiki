**Ikan tongkol** (English: "Tuna species") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Tonijnsoort (tuna species); preferred fresh fish of Acehnese.

## Translation

Tuna species

## Description

Tonijnsoort (tuna species); preferred fresh fish of Acehnese.

## Significance

The term *Ikan tongkol* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
