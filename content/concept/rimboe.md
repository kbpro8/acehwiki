**rimboe** (English: "jungle/primeval forest") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Jungle.

## Translation

jungle/primeval forest

## Description

Jungle. 'Rimboe-romantiek' (jungle romance) of the army elephants at [[Tangsé]]. 'Onbetreden oerwoud' (untrodden primeval forest) practically does not exist in Atjeh - all areas of any size have been patrolled multiple times by military.

## Significance

The term *rimboe* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
