**[[November]]** is a calendar month used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention. In the material drawn from *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]* it is associated with the [[Assembly of the auxiliary troops at Masdjid Siem (2 November 1893)|assembly of 2,400 auxiliaries]] on [[2 November]] [[1893]]; the [[Evacuation of the bentengs at Hoengtjala (12 November 1893)|evacuation and sack of Hoengtjala]] on [[12 November]] [[1893]]; and the [[Capture of Lam Barih and occupation of the post (November 1893)|occupation of the post at Lam Barih]] on [[30 November]] [[1893]].
