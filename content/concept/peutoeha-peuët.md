**peutoeha peuët** (English: "Four peutoeha") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Four-member council in Keureutòë with the right to be present at judicial verdicts by the Teukoe Tjhi'; traditionally consulted in important matters.

## Translation

Four [[Peutoeha|peutoeha]]

## Description

Four-member council in [[Keureutòë]] with the right to be present at judicial verdicts by the [[Teukoe Tjhi']]; traditionally consulted in important matters.

## Significance

The term *peutoeha peuët* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
