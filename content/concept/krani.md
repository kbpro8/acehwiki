**krani** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Attack on Gosenson at Djeuram (29 December 1917)]]

The investigation, conducted by [[H. J. Schmidt]], brought to light that the notorious [[Tgk. Poeti']], through the intervention of a certain *[[krani]]* (clerk) [[Hamit]], had organised the attack with the intention of causing resistance in Seunagan to break out again.
