**Landschap / landschap** (English: "native polity / landship") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Native self-governing polity (e.g., Landschap Peusangan, Simpang-Olin, Peureula', Zuidelijke Atjehsche Landschappen) — the pre-colonial political units under their own rulers (vorsten).

## Translation

native polity / landship

## Description

Native self-governing polity (e.g., [[Landschap Peusangan]], [[Simpang-Olin]], [[Peureula]]', [[Zuidelijke Atjehsche Landschappen]]) — the pre-colonial political units under their own rulers (vorsten).

## Significance

The term *Landschap / landschap* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
