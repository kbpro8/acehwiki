**figh-boeken (fiqh books)** (English: "Islamic jurisprudence books") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Encyclopedic fiqh books dating from Atjeh's golden age (1550-1650), produced by scholars from Egypt, Syrië, Arabië who settled in Atjeh.

## Translation

Islamic jurisprudence books

## Description

Encyclopedic fiqh books dating from Atjeh's golden age ([[1550]]-[[1650]]), produced by scholars from [[Egypt]], Syrië, Arabië who settled in Atjeh. (Dutch spelling: figh-boeken; modern: fiqh.)

## Significance

The term *figh-boeken (fiqh books)* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
