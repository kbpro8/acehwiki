The **[[Kwala|kwala]]** (Acehnese/Malay *kuala*) was the Atjehnese term for a river-mouth — the point at which a river or waterway entered the sea, and the natural point of access from the sea to the interior. The *kwalas* of [[Groot-Atjeh]] — [[Kwala Atjeh]] at the mouth of the [[Atjeh-rivier]], [[Kwala Gigieng]] at the mouth of the [[Kroeng Raya|Gigieng]] lagune, [[Kwala Tjangkoel]] at the entrance to the lagune — are the geographic features that define the strategic geography of Borel's narrative.

## The Term and Its Geographic Significance

The kwala was, in Borel's account, the Atjehnese/Malay term for a river-mouth — the point at which a *kroeng* (river) or waterway entered the sea. The kwala was the natural point of access from the sea to the interior: the place where boats could enter the river, where trade could be conducted, and where military forces could be landed. The kwala was thus both a commercial and a military feature: the Dutch [[Strategische driehoek (Atjeh)|strategische driehoek]] Kwala Atjeh–Kota Radja–Oeleh Leh took its name from the kwala at the mouth of the Atjeh-rivier; the projected afsluitingslinie from the [[Kroeng Raba]] to the [[Kroeng Raya]] was intended to close the kwalas of the west and north coasts to Atjehnese trade and contraband.

## The Kwalas of Groot-Atjeh

The kwalas of Groot-Atjeh are the geographic features that define the strategic geography of Borel's narrative. [[Kwala Atjeh]] at the mouth of the Atjeh-rivier was one corner of the strategische driehoek — the point at which the river communication from the sea reached the Dutch vestiging. [[Kwala Gigieng]] at the mouth of the Gigieng lagune on the north coast was the eastern end of the projected afsluitingslinie; it was reached by [[Luitenant-kolonel Engel|lt.-kol. Engel]]'s colonne on [[26 February]] [[1876]], and a post was established there to seal the gemeenschap between sea and the mouth of the [[Kroeng Tjoet]]. [[Kwala Tjangkoel]] was the waterway into the lagune; ships passing it were under [[Toekoe Nanta|Toekoe Nanta]]'s artillery at [[Lampanti]], [[Lamti]] and [[Sabang]] until those were reduced. The [[Kroeng Raba|Kroeng Raba]]-baai on the west coast was the projected western end of the afsluitingslinie; the landing of the [[Padangsche brigade|Padangsche brigade]] there — proposed by [[Generaal Verspijck|generaal Verspijck]] and rejected by [[Jan van Swieten|van Swieten]] out of "vrees" — was, in Borel's account, the missed opportunity of the second expedition. The kwalas of the vassal coastal states — [[Pasangan]], [[Simpang Olim]], [[Edi]], [[Gedoeng]] — were the points through which the Atjehneezen received hulpbenden, wapens and levensmiddelen from their allies.

## The Closure of the Kwalas

The closure of the kwalas of Groot-Atjeh was, in Borel's account, the strategic objective of [[Pel's plan for the Atjeh line|Pel's plan]]: the afsluitingslinie from the Kroeng Raba on the west coast to the Kroeng Raya on the north coast would isolate Atjeh from the sea and reduce it to a binnenstaat. The plan was approved by [[Gouverneur-Generaal van Lansberge|gouverneur-generaal van Lansberge]] in the second half of [[1875]], but was interrupted by Pel's [[Death of Generaal Pel (24 February 1876)|sudden death]] on [[24 February]] [[1876]] and never completed. The closure of the Kroeng Tjoet mouth — achieved by the establishment of posts at Kwala Gigieng and on the eiland [[Kota Pohama]] in the lagune in February [[1876]] — was the most significant single step; the Kroeng Tjoet had been the major contraband supply channel giving access to the [[XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]]. The sea-expedition to the mouth of the Kroeng Raya, later undertaken under [[Generaal Diemont|generaal Diemont]]'s administration by [[Karel van der Heijden|kolonel van der Heijden]], led to "geene bevredigende uitkomst" and did not link up with the Tongah–Kwala Gigieng line. The kwalas thus remained, in Borel's account, the unfinished business of the Atjeh-oorlog: the strategic objective that Pel's death had interrupted and that the *afwachtende houding* of his successors had not resumed.
