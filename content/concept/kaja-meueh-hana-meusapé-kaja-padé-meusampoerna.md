**kaja meueh hana meusapé, kaja padé meusampoerna** (English: "'rich in gold is not sufficient; rich in rice is perfect'") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Acehnese saying emphasizing the primacy of rice wealth over gold wealth — quoted as typifying the population's economic orientation.

## Translation

'rich in gold is not sufficient; rich in rice is perfect'

## Description

Acehnese saying emphasizing the primacy of rice wealth over gold wealth — quoted as typifying the population's economic orientation.

## Significance

The term *kaja meueh hana meusapé, kaja padé meusampoerna* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
