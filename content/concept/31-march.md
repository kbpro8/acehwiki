**[[31 March]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]* it appears in the articles listed below.

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

Closing date of the loss period of the ground-clearing at the [[1000-meter strook|free strip]] in 1885: from 12 to 31 March alone 8 killed and 25 wounded (see [[Completion of the concentrated position (March 1885)]]).
