**kribbendam** (English: "crib dam") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Type of dam built of crib-logs, exemplified by the kribbendam in the Peusangan-rivier.

## Translation

crib dam

## Description

Type of dam built of crib-logs, exemplified by the kribbendam in the [[Peusangan (river)|Peusangan-rivier]].

## Significance

The term *kribbendam* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
