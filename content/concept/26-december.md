**[[26 December]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]* it appears in the articles listed below.

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this date in [[1875]] [[Jacobus Pel|Pel]] marched with his troops out of the western gates of [[Kuta Raja|Kota Radja]] 'tot het fnuiken der macht van de Atjehers' — as he recounted in his day order of [[8 February]] [[1876]].
