**Attescheiding** (English: "Tertiary hill-land separation") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Local term for the tertiary hill and mountain land between the twin volcanoes Goud- en Weesberg and the central mountain land, separating the plains of Pidië and Groot-Atjeh.

## Translation

Tertiary hill-land separation

## Description

Local term for the tertiary hill and mountain land between the twin volcanoes [[Goud- en Weesberg]] and the central mountain land, separating the plains of [[Pidië]] and [[Groot-Atjeh]].

## Significance

The term *Attescheiding* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
