**Stormladders** were crucial pieces of military equipment utilized by the Dutch forces to breach the high stone walls of [[Atjeh's Groote Missigit]].

## Tactical Use
Carried by conscripted laborers (forced laborers) under heavy fire, these heavy wooden ladders were placed against the mosque walls during the [[First Conquest of the Great Missigit]] and [[Second Conquest of the Great Missigit]]. They were the site of the war's most brutal hand-to-hand combat, as soldiers like Captain [[J.P. van Lier]], Captain [[J.H. Huijer]], and Gunner [[C.H. Kool van Heerens]] used them to assault the Acehnese defenders who fiercely guarded the top of the walls with lances and klewangs.
