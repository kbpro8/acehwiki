**Bruidschat** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Bride price paid by groom's family; for Amat Leupon's marriage to Intjah one tahil (~f 40.-), for which Amat's mother had to sell a cow.

## Description

Bride price paid by groom's family; for [[Amat Leupon]]'s marriage to [[Intjah]] one [[Tayl|tahil]] (~f 40.-), for which [[Banta Ahmat|Amat]]'s mother had to sell a cow.

## Significance

The term *Bruidschat* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
