**[[26 April]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link.

## Events on this date

- [[Besluit van den Civielen en Militairen Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden 26 April 1911 No. 2199]] — the decision by which *[[Verjaring in Atjehsch adatrecht|verjaring]]* (prescription) was introduced into Atjehnese *[[Adatrecht|adatrecht]]*, fixing periods of fifteen and ten years for written and unwritten inheritance claims respectively ([[1911]])

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this date in [[1874]] [[Jan van Swieten|van Swieten]] left Atjeh with the so-called chief force; the same evening the enemy moved in force against the inherited position of [[Jacobus Pel|Kolonel Pel]], punishing Marassa the next day (see [[Departure of Jan van Swieten from Atjeh (April 1874)]]).
