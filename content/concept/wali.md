The **wali** was the guardian under Islamic law who, in [[Aceh|Atjeh]], acted for a woman in matters of marriage and property. In the context of Atjehnese land law, the *wali* appears as the person who — whether the father himself or another guardian — made over the *[[Atra peunoelang|atra peunoelang]]* to a married daughter or ward on the occasion of the birth of her first child, the gift usually consisting of a rice field and a head of plough cattle.
