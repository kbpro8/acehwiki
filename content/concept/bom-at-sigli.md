**bom (at Sigli)** (English: "loading buoy/platform") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Loading platform at Sigli where large prauwen load/unload the cargo of KPM ships lying at roadstead.

## Translation

loading buoy/platform

## Description

Loading platform at [[Sigli]] where large prauwen load/unload the cargo of KPM ships lying at roadstead. Always busy and noisy.

## Significance

The term *bom (at Sigli)* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
