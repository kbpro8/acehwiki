**Christelijk militair internaat** (English: "Christian military boarding school") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Founded Koetaradja 1929 for children of Christian soldiers (Ambonese, Menadonese, Timorese) stationed outside Koetaradja.

## Translation

Christian military boarding school

## Description

Founded [[Koetaradja]] [[1929]] for children of Christian soldiers (Ambonese, Menadonese, Timorese) stationed outside [[Koetaradja]]. Accompanied by an Amboneesche School.

## Significance

The term *Christelijk militair internaat* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
