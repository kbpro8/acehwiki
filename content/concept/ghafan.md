**Ghafan** (from Arabic *kafan*, the shroud) was the term used in [[Aceh|Atjeh]] for the funeral expenses of a deceased person, defrayed from his estate before the residue was transferred to the *[[Baitul mal|bait ul-mâl]]*.

## Meaning and extension

[[D. W. N. de Boer]] glossed the word as "eigenlijk = lijkkleed" — properly, the shroud — but noted that it was used *pars pro toto*, that is for all the funeral costs. The amount was that "hem naar rang en stand competeerende" — appropriate to the deceased's rank and station.

## Place in the settlement of heirless estates

Where an *[[Oeleebalang|oeléëbalang]]* laid *ex officio* seizure on an unadministered estate for the *bait ul-mâl*, the *ghafan* was first met out of the proceeds of the goods left by the deceased, the *[[Eeang male|eeang male]]*; only what remained was booked over to the mosque fund.
