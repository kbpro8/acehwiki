**A. al-hara** was an Acehnese‑Malay lexical item listed on page 92 of the *Malay translation of the Burda*, glossed as “birahi jang volmaakte liefde” (perfect love, akin to the love that makes one die for the beloved). The term stems from the Arabic **al‑ḥarāʾ** (الحراء) meaning “desire” or “passion”. In Acehnese devotional poetry the word conveyed an ecstatic, self‑sacrificial love for the Divine.

## Literary Usage

The term occurs in verses describing the lover’s yearning for God, echoing Sufi motifs of annihilation in love.
