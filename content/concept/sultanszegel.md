**The Sultanszegel** — the Sultan's seal of appointment, the *tjap sikoereuëng* — is the royal instrument by which the Sultan of [[Aceh|Atjeh]] conferred the dignity of [[Uleebalang|oeléëbalang]] on a vassal chief. The example reproduced in Zentgraff's *Atjeh* is an *aanstellingsbrief* of *wijlen Sultan Ibrahim Mansoer* to the *oeléëbalang* of Olé Glé, *ongeveer eene eeuw oud* as of [[1938]]. The seal bears the *tjap Sikoereuëng*, the nine circles that are the visual signature of the Sultan's authority.

## The tjap Sikoereuëng

The *tjap sikoereuëng* — literally the *seal of nine*, the *negen cirkels* — is the recurring visual device of the Acehnese Sultanate's regalia: nine concentric circles, each ringed with Arabic lettering, stamped into the appointment brief. *Een aanstellingsbrief van wijlen Sultan Ibrahim Mansoer aan den oeléëbalang van Olé Glé. Het stuk draagt den "tjap Sikoereuëng", met de negen cirkels, en is ongeveer eene eeuw oud*. The same seal figures in other appointments of the late Sultanate: when [[T. Tjhi Toenong]]'s wife, Tjoet Nja Din's kin, received her husband's commission from the Sultan as oeléëbalang of [[Keureutoe]], the commission was *het stuk met de "tjap sikoereuëng", de negen cirkels*. The seal was the visible token of the *Sultanszegel* — without it, no appointment was complete.

## The parallel amulet

The *Sultanszegel* has its religious parallel in the *zegelamulet* of [[Tengkoe Sjech Saman di Tiro]] — a sealed paper of invulnerability, *een heel bijzonder stuk*, that passed from the great Tiro-oelama to [[Tengkoe Tjot Plieëng]] and was lost, with his *rantej boei*, when the latter was killed by *luitenant* Terwogt's patrol in [[June]] [[1904]]. *De zegelamulet was een heel bijzonder stuk, en in vroegere jaren het eigendom geweest van den nog meer vermaarden Tengkoe Sech Saman di Tiro, het hoofd van de groote familie der Tiro-oelama's*. Where the Sultan's seal conferred political authority, the Tiro-oelama's amulet conferred spiritual protection; the two together — the *tjap Sikoereuëng* and the *zegelamulet* — defined the dual frame of Acehnese legitimacy, the Sultanate and the *oelama*.

## Significance

The Sultanszegel is, in Zentgraff's documentation, the visible remnant of the old Sultanate — *ongeveer eene eeuw oud* — preserved into the colonial era as the title-deed of the *oeléëbalang* families. The T. Tjhi family of [[Keureutoe]] held one; the *oeléëbalang* of Olé Glé held another. The parallel with the *zegelamulet* of Tgk. Sjech Saman di Tiro, passed down to Tgk. Tjot Plieëng and lost in [[1904]], is exact: in both cases a sealed paper, in both cases a *tjap*, in both cases an authority that outlived the man who conferred it.
