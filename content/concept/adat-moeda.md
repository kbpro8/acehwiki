**Adat moeda** ("young *adat*"), also called **adat baroe** ("new *adat*"), was the name given in certain districts of [[Aceh|Atjeh]] — among them the *landschap* (territory) [[Sawang]] in the *onderafdeeling* (subdivision) [[Lho' Seumawe|Lho' Seumaweh]] — to the fine imposed for evasion of the *[[Beschikkingsrecht in Atjeh|beschikkingsrecht]]*, consisting in the confiscation of ten per cent of the harvest, or of the proceeds in the event of sale or other alienation of the land concerned.

## Relation to the older penalty

In former times, [[D. W. N. de Boer]] recorded, evasion of the right of avail was punished by outright *verbeurdverklaring* — confiscation of whatever had been cleared without permission. The *adat moeda* was a later and milder system of fines that developed alongside that penalty.

## Evidence of recent origin

De Boer treated the institution as demonstrably recent. Both its Malay name and the absence even of a proper Atjehnese term for it pointed, in his judgement, to "de nieuwigheid van dit instituut" — the newness of the institution.

## Restriction to gardens

The fine was said to be applied only in respect of gardens. De Boer explained this as a practical consequence: it was as good as impossible to lay out a new *[[Sawah|sawah]]* clandestinely, whereas the laying out of a garden in the forest or in the mountains could long remain unnoticed, so that in practice evasion of the right of avail would be attempted only with gardens.
