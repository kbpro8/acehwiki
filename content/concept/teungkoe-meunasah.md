**teungkoe meunasah** (English: "Full title of the village religious leader") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Full title of the village religious leader in Atjeh.

## Translation

Full title of the village religious leader

## Description

Full title of the village religious leader in Atjeh. Hereditary, but the holder must be a religious, pious Muslim, preferably literate. If no suitable male heir exists in the [[teungkoe]] lineage, the dignity passes to a male relative in the sideline or even to an outsider. Together with the [[keutjhi']] he is the joint decision-maker in the [[Gampong|gampong]], representing the [[hokom]] (holy law) while the [[keutjhi']] represents the [[Adat|adat]].

## Significance

The term *teungkoe meunasah* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
