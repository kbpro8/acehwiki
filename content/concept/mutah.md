**mutah** is the Acehnese word for “pearl”. It appears in the lexical list on page [[88]] of the *Burda* translation, linked to the Arabic *durr*.

## Definition
In Acehnese maritime culture, *mutah* denotes the precious gem harvested from the seas surrounding Aceh, symbolizing both material wealth and spiritual purity.

## Cultural Context
Pearls were prized in the Acehnese court and featured in diplomatic gifts to foreign powers, such as the Dutch East India Company.

## Significance
The entry reflects the blending of Arabic lexical influence with local economic terminology.
