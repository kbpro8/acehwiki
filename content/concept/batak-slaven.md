**Batak-slaven** (English: "Batak slaves") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Batak slaves were little valued and Batak slave women rarely occurred, unlike Nias slave women.

## Translation

Batak slaves

## Description

Batak slaves were little valued and Batak slave women rarely occurred, unlike [[Nias]] slave women.

## Significance

The term *Batak-slaven* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
