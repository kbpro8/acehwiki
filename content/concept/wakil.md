The **wakil** (Arabic-Malay *wakīl*, "representative, deputy") was the deputy or representative of an Atjehnese *[[Oeleebalang|oeléëbalang]]*, acting for him in the administration of rights in land.

## Functions in the law of land

[[D. W. N. de Boer]] recorded three functions. Notice of an intention to clear waste land had to be given in advance to the *oeléëbalang* "of diens plaatsvervanger" — or his deputy. Payment of the *[[Haq radja taléë|haq radja taléë]]* secured the occupant a *registratie-in-memorie* (informal entry in the chief’s register) with the *radja* or his *wakil*. And in the *[[Zelfbesturende landschappen|self-governing territories]]* it was as a rule known to the *radja* or his representative — through the measuring-out of ground, the former *[[Boeët oemòng|boeët oemòng]]* and *[[Adat glé|adat glé]]* levies, and the continuing *[[Wathe|wathé]]* collection — whether land applied for still had an owner.

## Limits of assistance in Groot-Atjeh

In [[Groot-Atjeh]], where no active *[[Beschikkingsrecht in Atjeh|beschikkingsrecht]]* existed, no assistance in land disputes could as a rule be expected from the *radja* or his *wakil* by persons other than his relatives or those otherwise attached to him.
