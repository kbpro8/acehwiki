**Hausse** (English: "Boom period") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Economic boom; e.g., the hausse years of pinang trade; hausse period in last quarter 1936 after guilder devaluation.

## Translation

Boom period

## Description

Economic boom; e.g., the hausse years of [[Pinangnut (Betel Nut)|pinang]] trade; hausse period in last quarter [[1936]] after [[Guilder devaluation|guilder devaluation]].

## Significance

The term *Hausse* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
