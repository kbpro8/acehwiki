**Hacil** (also *hacil radja*, *wasé oelèëbalang*, *hacil tanah*) was traditional lordly right or ground rent paid by pepper planters to [[Uleebalang|oeléëbalang]] and later divided between landscape treasury and capital provider.

## Details

For use ground paid *hacil radja*, lordly right; got again oelèëbalang. Adat pepper culture brought those payments and more e.g., for leader head garden *peutoeha*. So much deducted *wasé pangkai*, *wasé oeléëbalang*, *wasé peutoeha* and more *wasé*.

After pacification Netherlands bestuur thought rights could be determined and at export via customs collected, while when "landscape" established as self-government area *wasé oelèëbalang* ground right landscape treasury belonged. Broersma notes healer weakened patient because attacked life source oeléëbalang.

[[1915]] government new regulation customs not retained. *Hacil tanah* or *wasé oelèëbalang* divided 50% landscape 50% *pangkaigiver* and *peutoeha* who from leader pepper planters became head municipality *kepala kampong* together.

Practice now: *hacil* for oelèëbalang old lordly right 1 gulden per *picol*; capital provider f 2.50 per picol of which f 0.50 to peutoeha; plus freight.

In [[Langsa]] mention heads provided *pangkai* and per admin regulation interest and also landscape income. After pepper failure tax collection decreased.

In [[Peureula]] landscape encouraging sawah ordering for pepper revival motor plows etc.

In begroting landscapes afdeeling [[Pidie]] [[1923]] *hacil* for pepper 9500, pinang 12,500 etc.
