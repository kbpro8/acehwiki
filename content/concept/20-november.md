**[[20 November]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[Het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh (Jubileum-nummer 1930)|Het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh]]* it marks the following event.

## Events on this date

- The Dinner at Court (*Diner ten Hove*) attended by [[G. F. B. Watrin]] during his European leave, at which he had the privilege of sitting at table on the left of [[Wilhelmina of the Netherlands|Queen Wilhelmina]] — "het was de mooiste tijd van mijn loopbaan, bekroond met een Diner ten Hove op 20 November 1913" ([[1913]])
