**levées en masse** (English: "mass levies / mass mobilization") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Mass religiously-based mobilization for war, used by Atjeh against 'ungeloovige indringers'.

## Translation

mass levies / mass mobilization

## Description

Mass religiously-based mobilization for war, used by Atjeh against 'ungeloovige indringers'. The Dutch would later experience the impact of this at first hand.

## Significance

The term *levées en masse* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
