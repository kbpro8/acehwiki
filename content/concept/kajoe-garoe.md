**Kajoe garoe** — Malay ***kayu garu***, English **agarwood**, **eaglewood**, or **aloeswood** — is the fragrant heartwood of the *Aquilaria* tree, prized from antiquity as incense and perfume-base, that was one of the principal trade-goods of the [[Acheh|Atjehnese]] sphere and the gift accompanying the [[Batu|king of Batu]]'s appeal to [[Pero de Faria|Pero de Faria]] of [[Malacca|Malacca]] in [[1539]]. It is, with [[kapoer baroer|kapoer baroer]] and [[minjak tanah|minjak tanah]], one of the three trade-goods whose names underlie the *bedekte taal* register of Malay-Javanese historical geography.

## The 1539 embassy

[[Mendez Pinto|Pinto]], at the opening of his account of the [[Atjeh-Aroe War (1539)|Atjeh-Aru war]], records that the embassy of the king of [[Batu|Batu]] to [[Pero de Faria|Pero de Faria]] in [[1539]] — offering to recognise the King of Portugal as suzerain in exchange for protection against [[Acheh|Atjeh]] — was accompanied by a rich gift of [[kapoer baroer|kamfer]] and [[kajoe garoe|kajoe garoe]] (eaglewood). The gift was, in the political economy of the early-sixteenth-century Sumatran west coast, the standard token of submission: the king of Batu offered the two most valuable products of his country in exchange for Portuguese aid against the Atjehnese sultan's expansion.

## Decoding and the *Xarnauz* record

The same *kajoe garoe*, [[P. de Roo de la Faille|De Roo de la Faille]] notes, underlies the *Xarnauz* record of the *Roteiro* of [[Vasco da Gama]] — the "land of Christians and the king is Christian" whose king "has 400 elephants of war. In the land is much benzoin and there is aloës won". The "Christians" De Roo de la Faille decodes as *keris-tijang* "kris-bearers"; the elephants as the [[Astina|elephant-land]] of the Sumatran east coast; the benzoin as *loban djawi* ("Javanese incense"); and the aloës as the *kajoe garoe* of the [[Acheh|Atjehnese]] sphere. The three trade-goods — *kajoe garoe*, benzoin, and war-elephants — together fix the *Xarnauz* of the *Roteiro* as the [[Aroe|Aru]]-[[Taroema|Taroema]]-[[Astina|Astina]] complex at [[Aroe-baai|the bay of Aroe]].

## Significance

For AcehWiki, *kajoe garoe* is one of the three trade-goods whose names underlie the *bedekte taal* register of Malay-Javanese historical geography — the eaglewood of the [[Acheh|Atjehnese]] sphere that accompanied the [[Batu|king of Batu]]'s [[1539]] embassy to [[Malacca|Malacca]], and that the *Roteiro* of [[Vasco da Gama]] records as one of the products of the [[Aroe|Aru]]-[[Astina|Astina]] complex. Its role in the political economy of the early-sixteenth-century Sumatran coast also fixes it as a standard token of submission in the [[Atjeh-Aroe War (1539)|Atjeh-Aru war]] period.
