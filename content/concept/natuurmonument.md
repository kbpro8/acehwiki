**Natuurmonument** (English: "Nature monument") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Designated nature protected area; first in Aceh 1921 (Aroe Koembai, Djernih rivier Moento), merged 1936 as 'Rafflesia natuurmonument Serbödjadi'.

## Translation

Nature monument

## Description

Designated nature protected area; first in Aceh [[1921]] ([[Aroe Koembai]], [[Djernih rivier Moento]]), merged [[1936]] as 'Rafflesia natuurmonument [[Serbödjadi]]'. Also proposed '[[Silaboehan]]' moerasbosch monument.

## Significance

The term *Natuurmonument* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
