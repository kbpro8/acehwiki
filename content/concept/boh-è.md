**Boh è** — literally "the fruits of the manure" — was the name given in [[Aceh|Atjeh]] to the first harvest of a pepper garden laid out by a free planter, which fell wholly to the planter himself.

## Context

Under the second form of the *[[Contract of the Atjehnese pepper planter|pepper planter's contract]]*, in which the planter applied directly and independently to the *[[Oeleebalang|oeléëbalang]]* for ground and a *[[Pangkaj|pangkaj]]* of twenty guilders per thousand vines, neither the *pangkaj* nor the cost of tools and clothing supplied had to be repaid, and the entire proceeds of the first harvest fell to the planter as *boh è*. Only thereafter was the garden divided in two (*mawaih tano* or *mawaih plah tano*), the *pangkaj*-giver as a rule leaving his half in the planter's hands on *mawaih asoë* terms — so that after four years the planter enjoyed in total three-quarters of the return of the whole plantation.
