**oelèébalang** (English: "zelfbestuurder / native self-ruler") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Acehnese zelfbestuurder (self-ruling chief under Dutch indirect rule).

## Translation

[[zelfbestuurder]] / native self-ruler

## Description

Acehnese [[zelfbestuurder]] (self-ruling chief under Dutch indirect rule). Acts as judge; divorce cases can be brought to him, or to the [[Moesapat|moesapat]] (native court) if he is negligent.

## Significance

The term *oelèébalang* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
