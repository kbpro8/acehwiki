**The Kroeëng Keureutë** is a *Noordkust* van Atjeh river that drains the [[Laut Tawar|Laut Tawar]] lake and the *hoogvlakte* of [[Takengon|Takengon]] and [[Ketol|Ketol]] to the *Noordkust* of Atjeh. In the [[De Buitenbezittingen, Deel II, Aflevering 1|Encyclopaedisch Bureau's Sumatra survey]] (1917), the Kroeëng Keureutë is one of the two principal exceptions (with the [[Peusanganrivier|Peusanganrivier]]) to the *te korten loop* of the [[Diamantpunt|Diamantpunt]]-area *Noordkust* rivers, and the principal hydrographical connection between the central-Atjeh *hoogvlakte* and the *Noordkust* *kustvlakte*.

## Hydrography

The Bureau's Hoofdstuk I notes that the Kroeëng Keureutë — together with the Peusanganrivier — is one of the two *Uitzonderingen* to the *te korten loop* of the *Noordkust* rivers: "Uitzonderingen hierop zijn: de Peusanganrivier, [die] de afvloeiing der Laoet Tawar naar de Noordkust [overneemt] … voorts de Kroeëng Keureutë." The Kroeëng Keureutë is thus the second principal hydrographical exception to the *te korten loop* of the *Noordkust* rivers — the river whose *stroomgebied* extends inland to the *hoogvlakten* of Takengon and Ketol.

## Significance

The Kroeëng Keureutë is, in the Bureau's Sumatra survey, one of the two principal exceptions (with the Peusanganrivier) to the *te korten loop* of the *Noordkust* rivers, and the principal hydrographical connection between the central-Atjeh *hoogvlakte* and the *Noordkust* *kustvlakte*. The Bureau's emphasis on the Kroeëng Keureutë's *stroomgebied* — extending inland to the *hoogvlakten* of Takengon and Ketol — frames the Kroeëng Keureutë as the geographical foundation of the Dutch colonial state's central-Atjeh *bivak* at Takengon, and as the strategic hydrographical artery of the central-Atjeh *hoogvlakte*'s connection to the *Noordkust*.
