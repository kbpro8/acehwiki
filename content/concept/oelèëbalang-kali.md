**Oelèëbalang-kali** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Unsalaried advisors in the landschapsgerecht; receive part of civil court costs as reward.

## Description

Unsalaried advisors in the [[Landschapsgerecht|landschapsgerecht]]; receive part of civil court costs as reward. Their advice in criminal cases is unusable (based on Mohammedan law, e.g. hand-amputation for theft).

## Significance

The term *Oelèëbalang-kali* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
