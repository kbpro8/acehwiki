**Vovo** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Term used by Amat's mother; Amat was not regarded by her as 'vovo' (proper).

## Description

Term used by [[Banta Ahmat|Amat]]'s mother; [[Banta Ahmat|Amat]] was not regarded by her as 'vovo' (proper).

## Significance

The term *Vovo* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
