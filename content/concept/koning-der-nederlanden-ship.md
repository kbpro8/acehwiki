**Koning der Nederlanden** was a Dutch steamship (*Hr.Ms.*) that participated in the **Tamiang** river operations of **[[1893]]**, carrying marine troops and supporting riverine assaults. **[[C. Faassen]]** commanded the vessel during a joint operation with **[[Koerier (ship)]]** in **[[December]] [[1893]]**.

## Operational Use

The ship’s crew included **20 marines** from the **Koning der Nederlanden** under the command of a lieutenant of the infantry.
