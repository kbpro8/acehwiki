**besnijdenis** (English: "circumcision") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. For boys, after elementary Quran schooling, around age 6 or later (in Gajo sometimes earlier).

## Translation

circumcision

## Description

For boys, after elementary [[Quran]] schooling, around age 6 or later (in [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]] sometimes earlier). For girls, around age 2 or in [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]] even months old, performed by [[Bidan|midwife]]. In Aceh often without festivities; in [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]]/[[Alas]] with festivities like a wedding. Girls' circumcision in Aceh is very secretive.

## Significance

The term *besnijdenis* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
