**Rentjong (rencong)** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Traditional Acehnese dagger used in close combat.

## Description

Traditional Acehnese dagger used in close combat. [[Tjoct Nja Din]] attempted to stab her betrayer with a rentjong. [[Tgk. di Barat|Teungkoe di Barat]], hit in right arm, drew his rentjong with his left hand and passed his [[Karabijn (M. 95)|carbine]] to his wife.

## Significance

The term *Rentjong (rencong)* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
