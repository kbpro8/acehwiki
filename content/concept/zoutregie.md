**Zoutregie** (English: "Salt monopoly") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Government salt monopoly; Aceh falls outside the zoutregiegebied (salt monopoly area), so traditional salt-winning continues.

## Translation

Salt monopoly

## Description

Government salt monopoly; Aceh falls outside the zoutregiegebied (salt monopoly area), so traditional salt-winning continues. Introduction would force population to pay 150-200% more.

## Significance

The term *Zoutregie* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
