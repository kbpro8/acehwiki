**aardbeving** (English: "earthquake") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Earthquake that, combined with a vloedgolf, destroyed Oelèë Lheuë in August 1936.

## Translation

earthquake

## Description

Earthquake that, combined with a [[vloedgolf]], destroyed [[Oelèë Lheuë]] in [[August]] [[1936]].

## Significance

The term *aardbeving* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
