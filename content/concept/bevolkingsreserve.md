**bevolkingsreserve** (English: "Population reserve (lands)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Lands still available in sufficient measure in Atjeh on which Javanese contract laborers could be settled as colonists starting in 1930.

## Translation

Population reserve (lands)

## Description

Lands still available in sufficient measure in Atjeh on which Javanese contract laborers could be settled as colonists starting in [[1930]].

## Significance

The term *bevolkingsreserve* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
