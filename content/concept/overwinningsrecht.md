**Overwinningsrecht** (English: "Right of conquest") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Legal basis by which the Netherlands acquired Atjeh after the capture of the Kraton and death of the Sultan in 1874.

## Translation

Right of conquest

## Description

Legal basis by which [[Netherlands|the Netherlands]] acquired Atjeh after the [[Capture of the Kraton|capture of the Kraton]] and death of [[Sultan of Aceh|the Sultan]] in [[1874]].

## Significance

The term *Overwinningsrecht* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
