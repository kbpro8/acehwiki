**koepiah / toengkat (legend)** (English: "cap / staff (of the sea saint)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. The petrified cap (koepiah, kopiah) and staff (toengkat, tungkat) of the Tapa Toean sea saint, lying as two rocks in the sea bay at Tapa Toean.

## Translation

cap / staff (of the sea saint)

## Description

The petrified cap ([[Koepiah der Atjehers|koepiah]], kopiah) and staff (toengkat, tungkat) of the [[Tapa Toean]] sea saint, lying as two rocks in the sea bay at [[Tapa Toean]]. Fishermen avoid them but accidental contact is a good omen.

## Significance

The term *koepiah / toengkat (legend)* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
