**[[25 September]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]* it appears in the articles listed below.

## Articles referring to this date

- [[Pang Nanggròë]]

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this date in [[1884]] [[Kota Pohama]] and [[Roempit]] were (among the first) new posts occupied for the new line (see [[Completion of the concentrated position (March 1885)]] title and [[Postenlinie]]); on this date in [[1885]] [[Lamdjame]] was fired on with loss of one man (see [[Guerilla raids inside the concentration line (1885)]]).
