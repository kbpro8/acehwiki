**Roembiapalm** (English: "Nipa/atap palm") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Palm whose leaves are strung together for atap (roof thatching).

## Translation

Nipa/[[Atap|atap]] palm

## Description

Palm whose leaves are strung together for [[Atap|atap]] (roof thatching).

## Significance

The term *Roembiapalm* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
