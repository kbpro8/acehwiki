**[[11 August]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]* it appears in the articles listed below.

## Articles referring to this date

- [[1908]]
- [[Heerendienst]]
- [[Het Pacificeerende Bestuurswerk 1908-1918]]

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this date in [[1885]] the night watch of six men returning from [[Lamara]] to [[Ketapang Doewa]] was attacked: six wounded, three of whom died the same day (see [[Guerilla raids inside the concentration line (1885)]]); from the Kielstra notes cited by Verstege, on this date in [[1879]] more than 67 Atjehsche prisoners were made in a Dutch surprise.
