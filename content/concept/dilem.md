**Dilem** was the Javanese name for the patchouli plant — *[[Pogostemon Cablin (patchouli plant)|Pogostemon Cablin Benth.]]* — and for the essential oil distilled from its leaves. On Sumatra the same plant is called *[[Nilam|nilam]]*. The Java *dilem*-oil is exported mainly to Japan, the Netherlands, France and Germany, and has its own direct trade-channels, not via Singapore. The quality of the Java *dilem*-oil differs somewhat from that of the oil from Atjeh-leaf: the scent is *'lang niet zoo intensief'*; the differences are presumably connected with the growing-conditions — soil and climate appear in Atjeh naturally more favourable to the plant than on Java.
