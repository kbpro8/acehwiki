**imöm** (English: "Islamic authority (in Gajo)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Religious authority in the Gajo blah, alongside the rëdjö and toeö.

## Translation

Islamic authority (in [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]])

## Description

Religious authority in the [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]] [[Blah|blah]], alongside the rëdjö and [[toeö]].

## Significance

The term *imöm* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
