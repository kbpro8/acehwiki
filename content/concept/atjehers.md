**The Atjehers** (Dutch: *Atjehers*; English: Acehnese people) are the inhabitants of [[North Sumatra]], specifically of the territory of [[Acheh]]. The *Nederlandsch Indië Platen Atlas* ([[1926]]) classifies them within the Malay racial group (*Maleische ras*) of the western half of the Indonesian Archipelago, alongside the Javanese, Sundanese, Madurese, Balinese, Makassarese, and Buginese.

## Account from *Nederlandsch Indië Platen Atlas* (1926) — The Atjehers

The atlas provides two distinct pieces of information about the Atjehers:

### Origins

On the question of their origins, the atlas states: "Wat de herkomst van deze bewoners van Noord-Sumatra aangaat, ontbreekt het ons aan betrouwbare gegevens. Vast staat wel, dat vreemdelingen uit Hindostan, Arabie, Java enz. in deze bevolking zijn opgelost." (As regards the origins of these inhabitants of North Sumatra, reliable data are lacking to us. What is certain, however, is that foreigners from Hindustan, Arabia, Java, etc. have been absorbed into this population.)

### Dress

The atlas describes their distinctive clothing: "De Atjeh-sche kleederdracht onderscheidt zich door de wijde broek, zoowel door mannen als door vrouwen gedragen. Het bovenlijf is bedekt met een baadje, terwijl de vrouwen nog een kleed om de schouders slaan." (The Atjeh-style dress is distinguished by the wide trousers, worn by both men and women. The upper body is covered with a jacket, while the women additionally drape a cloth over their shoulders.)

### Aristocratic Culture

The atlas also depicts the "zitplaats van een vorstelijken Atjeher" (seat of a noble Atjeher), consisting of fine cloths and cushions, and a group of Atjehers gathered before a mosque, indicating the centrality of Islam in their communal life.

## Classification

The atlas places the Atjehers in the Malay racial group (*Maleische ras*) which predominates in the western half of the Archipelago. It notes, however, that "deze Maleische volken zijn echter niet van onvermengde afkomst; Hindoes, Chineezen en andere Aziaten hebben zich er in opgelost" (these Malay peoples are, however, not of unmixed origin; Hindus, Chinese, and other Asians have been absorbed into them). For the Atjehers specifically, the mixture is described as including peoples from Hindustan (India), Arabia, and Java — consistent with Acheh's historical role as an Islamic trading sultanate with extensive international contacts.
