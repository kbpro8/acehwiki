**Pacificatie** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Dutch post-war pacification policy (in Swart's era and after) - replacing military coercion with civil administration, road-building, dispute resolution, and economic development.

## Description

Dutch post-war pacification policy (in [[Gouverneur Swart|Swart]]'s era and after) - replacing military coercion with [[Binnenlandsch Bestuur (B.B.)|civil administration]], road-building, dispute resolution, and economic development. 'Naarmate de militaire dwang kon worden uitgeschakeld, kwamen de zegeningen van het pacificatie-werk tot hun recht' ([[J. Kreemer|Kreemer]]).

## Significance

The term *Pacificatie* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
