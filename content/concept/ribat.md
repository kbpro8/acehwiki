**Ribāt (monastic house)** refers to a fortified religious establishment used by Sufi scholars and warriors. The source records that the wezier [[Tādj al‑Dīn b. Hinna]] commissioned a personal ribāt in [[Cairo]] to house his collection of Islamic relics. This institution exemplifies the type of religious infrastructure that linked the Sultanate of [[Acheh]] with the broader Islamic world, facilitating the flow of devotional texts such as the *Burda* poem.
