**The reuntjong** — also *rencong*, the Acehnese kris or dagger — was the traditional bladed weapon of the Acehnese, and the symbolic weapon of the Acehnese resistance in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]*. The *reuntjong* is paired in the book with the *[[klewang]]* of the [[Manisee (Manisé)|Manisee]]: the two blades meet in the moment of *[[Peutoeha Gam Masén]]*'s death, when Gam Masén "trok zijn *reuntjong*, de Atjehse kris" and threw himself on the two voorste Marechaussees — the *[[dardanellen|dardanellen]]* of the luitenant — before being shot by [[Nussy]] and worked over with the *klewang* by [[Enoch]].

## The reuntjong and the resistance

The *reuntjong* was the symbolic weapon of the Acehnese resistance. When *[[Peutoeha Gam Masén]]* was omsingeld and summoned to surrender, "hij trok zijn *reuntjong*, de Atjehse kris" — he drew his *reuntjong*, the Acehnese kris. The drawing of the *reuntjong* was the refusal of submission: the Acehnese hoofd who drew his *reuntjong* was the hoofd who would not come in. The *reuntjong* was therefore both a weapon and a sign: the sign of the resistance that would not submit.

## The reuntjong and the klewang

The *reuntjong* of the Acehnese resister is paired in the book with the *[[klewang]]* of the [[Manisee (Manisé)|Manisee]]. The two blades meet in the moment of *[[Peutoeha Gam Masén]]*'s death: Gam Masén drew his *reuntjong*, threw himself on the *[[dardanellen|dardanellen]]*, and was killed by Nussy's *[[karabijn (M. 95)|karabijn]]* and Enoch's *klewang*. The pairing is the book's small symbolic register of the war: the *klewang* of the colonial soldier against the *reuntjong* of the Acehnese resister.

## Significance

The *reuntjong* is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as the symbolic weapon of the Acehnese resistance. The book's argument is that the *reuntjong* — the traditional bladed weapon of the Acehnese — was both a weapon and a sign: the sign of the resistance that would not submit. The pairing of the *reuntjong* with the *[[klewang]]* is the book's small symbolic register of the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]]: the two blades that met in the moment of Gam Masén's death are the two blades that met, in the book's economy, throughout the forty-five-year war.
