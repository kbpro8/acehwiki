**Ongole fok-vee** (English: "Ongole breeding cattle") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Indian Ongole cattle breed imported for cross-breeding to improve size of Acehnese cattle stock (degeneration visible).

## Translation

Ongole breeding cattle

## Description

Indian Ongole cattle breed imported for cross-breeding to improve size of Acehnese cattle stock (degeneration visible).

## Significance

The term *Ongole fok-vee* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
