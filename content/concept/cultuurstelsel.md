**The Cultuurstelsel** — the Cultivation System — was the Dutch colonial forced-crop system imposed on the indigenous population of [[Java]] from [[1830]] (formally [[1831]]) to c. [[1870]] under which a portion of village land and labour was set aside for the cultivation of export crops (sugar, coffee, indigo) for government account. The system is, in the [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] retrospective, the principal source of the [[Batig Slot|batig slot]] — the surplus extracted from the [[Nederlandsch-Indië|Nederlandsch-Indische]] archipelago — and accordingly the principal object of the [[Ereschuld|ereschuld]] doctrine of [[C. Th. van Deventer|Van Deventer]].

## The 700-million figure

The total surplus extracted from the [[Nederlandsch-Indië|Indies]] during the [[Cultuurstelsel|cultuurstelsel]] period — from [[1831]] to [[1877]] inclusive — was, in the round figure given by minister [[De Waal|De Waal]] and preserved by [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]], "rondom 700 millioen" — about 700 million guilders. The figure is the canonical *ereschuld* reference-point. The "van [[1831]] af" formula — "from 1831 onward" — is preserved by [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] with the gloss: "Dus sedert de invoering van het cultuurstelsel" — that is, since the introduction of the cultivation system.

## The dismantling

The [[Cultuurstelsel|cultuurstelsel]] was progressively dismantled in the [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]]-predecessor period. The *Suikerwet* of [[1891]], the *Koffiewet* of [[1891]] and the *Indische Mijnwet* of [[1899]] (see [[Mijnwet]]) are the principal legislative acts of the dismantling; the abolition of the *bijdrage* of [[Nederlandsch-Indië]] to the finances of the realm by the [[Indische Comptabiliteitswet amendment (26 June 1903)|Comptabiliteitswet amendment of 26 June 1903]] is the formal closure of the [[Cultuurstelsel|cultuurstelsel]]-[[Batig Slot|batig slot]] era.

## Significance for Atjeh

The [[Cultuurstelsel|cultuurstelsel]] bears on Atjeh indirectly. The surplus it generated — the [[Batig Slot|batig slot]] — funded the Dutch colonial state that undertook the [[Atjeh-oorlog ([[1873]]-[[1904]])|Atjeh-oorlog]] of [[1873]]-[[1904]]; the [[Ereschuld|ereschuld]] doctrine that the surplus provoked was the principal liberal self-criticism of that colonial state; and the [[Ethische Politiek|ethical-policy]] proclamation of [[Troonrede of 1901|1901]] was, in part, the political response to that self-criticism.
