**The Schaftorde** — the *food tariff* — was the system of regulated food provisioning for the Dutch forces in [[Atjeh]], established by *Extract-besluit* for the year [[1893]] and modified by further *Extract-besluiten* of [[23 September]] [[1895]] and [[22 Augustus]] [[1896]]. The *schaftorde* — which set the daily rations and the monetary allowances for European, Inlander (indigenous), and Officer personnel — is the subject of *Bijlagen IV, V, IX–XII, and XV* of [[A. Kruisheer|Kruisheer]]'s *[[ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel]]*, and the comparative overview of the *voedingswijze* (diet) on war footing in [[1896]] is the subject of *Bijlage XII*. The *schaftorde* (food tariff (schaftorde)) is the documentary record of the Dutch military logistics of the second semester of [[1896]] — the system by which the Dutch forces in Atjeh were fed and provisioned during the operations of the second semester.

## The Extract-besluiten

The *schaftorde* (food tariff (schaftorde)) was established and modified by a series of *Extract-besluiten* (extract decisions) of the *Opperbestuur* (Supreme Government). *Bijlage IV* — the *Extract-besluit regelende invoerende de schaftordes voor het jaar [[1893]]* (p. 173) — established the *schaftorde* (food tariff (schaftorde)) for [[1893]]. *Bijlage V* — the *Extract-besluit ddo. 23 Sept. '95 idem de voeding betreffend* (p. 177) — modified the *schaftorde* (food tariff (schaftorde)) with respect to the *voeding* (diet) as of [[23 September]] [[1895]]. *Bijlage VIII* — the *Extract-besluit ddo. 22 Augustus '96 betreffende de toelagen en den bijslag* (p. 181) — modified the *toelagen en den bijslag* (allowances and supplements) as of [[22 Augustus]] [[1896]]. These three *Extract-besluiten* — together with the *Schaftorde volgens tarief No. 20 voor Europeanen* (Bijlage IX, p. 183), the *Schaftorde voor Inlanders* (Bijlage X, p. 187), the *Schaftorde voor Officieren* (Bijlage XI, p. 191), and the *Schaftorde van 21 December [[1896]]* (Bijlage XV, p. 208) — constitute the documentary record of the *schaftorde* (food tariff (schaftorde)) system in [[1896]].

## The Voedingswijze on War Footing

The *Vergelijkend overzicht van de voedingswijze volgens gewone schaftorde en op oorlogsvoet in [[1896]]* (Bijlage XII, p. 195) — the comparative overview of the *voedingswijze* (diet) according to the ordinary *schaftorde* (food tariff (schaftorde)) and on war footing in [[1896]] — is the documentary record of the modification of the *schaftorde* (food tariff (schaftorde)) for the conditions of active military operations. The *oorlogsvoet* (war footing) *schaftorde* — which provided enhanced rations and allowances for personnel engaged in active operations — was the system under which the Dutch forces in Atjeh were provisioned during the operations of the second semester of [[1896]], including the [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|surprise of Ana' Galoeng]] of [[29 June]] [[1896]], the [[Excursie to the IV Moekims (Kroeng-Raba) (7 July 1896)|excursie of 7 July 1896]], and the various *tuchtigingen* and reconnaissances of July–November [[1896]].

## Significance

The *schaftorde* (food tariff (schaftorde)) is significant in the documentary record of *ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel* as the system of regulated food provisioning for the Dutch forces in Atjeh. The *schaftorde* — established by *Extract-besluit* for [[1893]] and modified by further *Extract-besluiten* of [[23 September]] [[1895]] and [[22 Augustus]] [[1896]] — is the documentary record of the Dutch military logistics of the second semester of [[1896]], and the *Vergelijkend overzicht van de voedingswijze volgens gewone schaftorde en op oorlogsvoet in [[1896]]* (Bijlage XII) provides the comparative framework within which the *oorlogsvoet* (war footing) *schaftorde* (food tariff (schaftorde)) of the second semester may be assessed.
