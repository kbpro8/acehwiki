**Oepah poela** (Atjehnese, the "planting wage") was the wage paid for the planting-out of rice in [[Aceh|Atjeh]], and one of the variable elements by which the several forms of *[[Mawaih|mawaih]]* were distinguished from one another.

## In the mawaih forms

Under *mawaih bahagi lhée* the rice-field holder supplied the ground, plough cattle and *[[Bibit|bibit]]*, and contributed half of the *oepah poela*, the cultivator retaining one-third of the product. Sometimes the *mawaih*-giver contributed two-thirds of the planting wage because he could otherwise find no one to work his fields — as in the *landschap* (territory) [[Aree|Aréë]] in the *onderafdeeling* (subdivision) [[Sigli]], a land of traders who had to offer better terms to draw workers from other *landschappen* (territories). Under *[[Aboen meu'oeë|aboen meu'oeë]]* the giver bore the whole of the *oepah poela* alone, and the cultivator received only one-fifth of the harvest.
