**[[19 August]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[Het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh (Jubileum-nummer 1930)|Het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh]]* it marks the following events.

## Events on this date

- The decision to adopt the *[[Concentratiestelsel|concentratie]]* — the concentration of Dutch forces within a defensive line in [[Groot-Atjeh]] — taken on [[19 August]] [[1884]], after which “kleine benden slopen de linie binnen, vielen onze convooien aan en vernielden kunstwerken” and which formed the immediate background to the raising of the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* in April [[1890]] ([[1884]])
