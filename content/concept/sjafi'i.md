**Sjafi'i** (English: "Shafi'i (school of Islamic jurisprudence)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Islamic legal school that requires forty male worshippers for a valid Friday service; the school generally followed in the East Indies.

## Translation

Shafi'i (school of Islamic jurisprudence)

## Description

Islamic legal school that requires forty male worshippers for a valid [[Friday service (Jum'at)|Friday service]]; the school generally followed in the East Indies.

## Significance

The term *Sjafi'i* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
