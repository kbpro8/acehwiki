The **Eerekruis voor belangrijke krijgsverrichtingen** (Honour Cross for Important Military Operations), popularly known as the *Expeditiekruis* (Expedition Cross), was a Dutch campaign decoration instituted for award to military and civilian personnel who had taken part in important military operations — chiefly the colonial expeditions of the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, including the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. The cross was worn with clasps (*gespen*) inscribed with the name of the expedition; the clasp "Atjeh 1873–1876" was awarded for service in the early phase of the Atjeh war. Together with the [[Atjeh-medaille|Atjeh-medaille]], the Expeditiekruis with clasp was the standard campaign medal of the early Atjeh expeditions. [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Generaal Snijders]] was granted the right to wear the Expeditiekruis with clasp "Atjeh 1873–1876" by grant of [[17 March]] [[17 March]] [[1877]], in respect of his Atjeh service of [[1874]]–[[1875]].

## Form and Wear

The Eerekruis was a cross worn on the chest with one or more clasps inscribed with the name and date of the expedition. The clasp "Atjeh 1873–1876" was the principal Atjeh clasp, covering the period of the [[First Aceh Expedition (1873)|Eerste Atjeh-expeditie]] of [[April 1873|April]] [[1873]], the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)|Tweede Atjeh-expeditie]] of [[December 1873|December]] [[1873]]–[[January 1874|January]] [[1874]], and the prolonged mopping-up operations of [[1874]]–[[1876]]. The cross was awarded together with the [[Atjeh-medaille|Atjeh-medaille]] for the same service, but was a distinct decoration.

## Snijders' Award

[[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] was granted the right to wear the Eerekruis with clasp "Atjeh 1873–1876" by grant of [[17 March]] [[17 March]] [[1877]], "ter zake van de krijgsverrichtingen tegen het rijk van Atjeh" — together with the [[Atjeh-medaille|Atjeh-medaille]]. He had earlier been appointed Ridder 4e klasse der [[Militaire Willemsorde|Militaire Willemsorde]] by [[Koninklijk Besluit of 12 November 1875 No. 18|KB of 12 November 1875 No. 18]] for the same service; the three decorations together constituted the full campaign award for his Atjeh service.

## Holders

The biographer [[D. van den Berg|Van den Berg]] records that Snijders and Kapitein-Luitenant ter Zee J. Boom (who died in [[1942]] at the age of 95) were "vermoedelijk wel tot de allerlaatste dragers" of both the Expeditiekruis with clasp and the [[Atjeh-medaille|Atjeh-medaille]].
