**Wakeuëh** (also *wakab*; Gayo: *wökös*; Alas: *wakap*) was an Atjèhnese term with two distinct meanings: (1) a pious foundation (*waqf* in Islamic law) — something given for a specific person or for general benefit, taken out of commerce; and (2) "free-lordly" villages or regions (*nanggröë wakeuëh*) outside ordinary administrative authority, comparable to Javanese *përdikan*.

## The pious foundation

In its first sense, *wakeuëh* was the Atjèhnese form of Islamic *waqf* — a pious endowment. Something given for a specific person or for general benefit was "taken out of commerce" (removed from ordinary trade and ownership). This was the standard Islamic legal concept.

## Nanggröë wakeuëh (free-lordly territories)

In its second sense — which only shared the name with Islamic *waqf* — *nanggröë wakeuëh* were "free-lordly" villages or regions outside the ordinary administrative authority, comparable to Javanese *përdikan*-stichtingen. These were:
- Recognised by the Sultan or Oelèëbalang (or sometimes self-established)
- Inhabited by *oereuëng bibeuëh* — "privileged persons" of the (or by the) ruler, with special privileges
- Gradually abolished under Dutch administration

## Examples of wakeuëh-territories

Notable *wakeuëh*-territories included:
- **Tirö** (in [[Pidië|Pidië]]) — a centre of Mohammedan study, generally recognised as *wakeuëh*. Surrounding chiefs left maintenance of order to the oelama's, recognised Tirö as a refuge for those involved in internal conflicts, and abstained from hostilities near its meunasah.
- **Peusangan** — the gampöng of the oelama-family Teungkoe di Awé Geutah was long *wakeuëh*-territory.
- **Mön Keulajoe** (Gloempang Doea) — *wakeuëh* territory for the benefit of an extensive habib-family.
- **Rëdëloeng** — a Gayo kampoeng in the Meergebied, *wökös* because the principal ancestor of its people was a saint whose grave is located near the place.
- **Loeëng Bata, Pagar Ajé, Lam Sajoen** — *wakeuëh*-districts in the sagi of the XXV Moekim's.
- **III Moekim's Keureukön** — *wakeuëh*-district in the sagi of the XXII Moekim's.
- **Lho' Goelöng, Tjadé' Mön Batèë, Koeala Gigiëng** — (no longer existing) *wakeuëh*-districts in the sagi of the XXVI Moekim's.

## Tanoh radja

The **tanoh radja** ("royal land") was a related but distinct concept — a strip of land seven large fathoms wide (*deupa meunara*) on both sides of the Atjèh river, formerly reserved for the Sultans. Not really *wakeuëh*, though sometimes called so.
