**klapperculuur** (English: "coconut cultivation") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. First groot-landbouwindustrie introduced in Aceh.

## Translation

coconut cultivation

## Description

First groot-landbouwindustrie introduced in Aceh. European coconut cultivation is now defunct; total planted area ~[[1000]] H.A., almost all in Chinese and native hands, cultivated no differently from the indigenous population's practice.

## Significance

The term *klapperculuur* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
