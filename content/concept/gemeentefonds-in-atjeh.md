**The Gemeentefonds in Atjeh** — *the municipal fund in Atjeh* — was the colonial government's form of municipal government for the chief places of the *Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden*, instituted at [[Langsa|Langsa]], [[Idi|Idi]], [[Lho Seumawe|Lho' Seumawé]], [[Sigli|Sigli]] and [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] (with the bathing-place at [[Oelèë Lheue|Oelèë Lheuë]]). The *gemeentefonds* is, in Broersma's account, a useful institution and it works easily. The head of the local government, here the *controleur*, is chairman of the committee of four members which manages the fund. It looks after the interests of local traffic, health, and street-lighting. The proceeds are payments for street-lighting, the yield of rent on the market-sheds. It maintains a slaughterhouse, a tennis-court, a cinema. Some municipal funds maintain a bathing-place at sea. There are people who would rather live under the management of an Atjehnese municipal fund than under the care of a town-council on Java.
