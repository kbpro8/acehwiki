**suikerriet-molen / mangel** (English: "sugar cane press (floating, water-powered)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Floating sugar cane press seen on river trips to Singkel: a firmly anchored raft with attached waterwheel (scheprad) powering a simple mangel (roller press).

## Translation

sugar cane press (floating, water-powered)

## Description

Floating sugar cane press seen on river trips to [[Singkel]]: a firmly anchored raft with attached waterwheel (scheprad) powering a simple mangel (roller press). Water turns the wheel which turns counter-rotating rollers. Petrol cans catch the sap; pulp comes out the other side. Often operated by children. Used on all pasars in Atjeh.

## Significance

The term *suikerriet-molen / mangel* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
