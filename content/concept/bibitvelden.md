**bibitvelden** (English: "rice seedling beds") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Enclosed pieces of sowing ground (zaaigoed) for rice seedlings - one of the phases of rice cultivation visible on a trip through Atjeh.

## Translation

rice seedling beds

## Description

Enclosed pieces of sowing ground (zaaigoed) for rice seedlings - one of the phases of rice cultivation visible on a trip through Atjeh.

## Significance

The term *bibitvelden* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
