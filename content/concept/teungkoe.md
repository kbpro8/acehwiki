Village religious leader (Atjehsch)") is a term or concept described in[[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Village religious official responsible for ritual slaughter (slacht).

## Translation

religious official / village religious teacher

## Description

[[Lébé|Village religious official]]responsible for ritual slaughter (slacht). Must wash thoroughly before slaughtering; his wife must not be pregnant nor he ill. May not clench teeth nor wipe knife too quickly during slaughter, lest meat be tough or unpalatable. Also leads funeral prayers.[[Gampong|gampong]]; the 'mother' of the village (with the [[keutjhi']]Title prefixed to names of religious scholars/teachers; e.g.Teungkoe Tirò, Teungkoe di [[Mata Ië]].

## Significance

The term *teungkoe* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
