**Berkat** is the Acehnese noun meaning “blessing” or “divine favor”. It is listed on several pages of the source (e.g., [[80]], [[81]]) with the simple gloss “(A.)”. In the *Burda* of al‑Bu‑s‑iri the term is used to denote the benevolent gifts granted by Allah, often contrasted with *dosia* (sin).

Typical usage includes phrases such as “berkat Allah” (“the blessing of God”) and “memeri karmii, hij schonk gunst” (the verse on granting *berkat*).

## Religious context

*Berkat* is a central concept in Acehnese Islam, appearing in prayers (*do'a*), sermons, and poetry to express gratitude and hope for divine assistance.
