**sisalplant** (English: "sisal (Agave species)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Agave fiber plant grown at the Lho' Soekon vezel-onderneming.

## Translation

[[Sisal (Aceh)|sisal]] (Agave species)

## Description

Agave fiber plant grown at the [[Lho'Soekon|Lho' Soekon]] vezel-onderneming. Thick fleshy leaves harvested when sufficiently long. Seeds germinate on the bloom stalk before dropping, so plant material can be gathered around the mother plant.

## Significance

The term *sisalplant* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
