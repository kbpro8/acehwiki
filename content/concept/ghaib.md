**Ghaib** is the Acehnese term for “the unseen” or “hidden realities”, derived from the Arabic *ghāʾib*. It appears on [[82]] with the gloss “kerdja‑, verborgenheden”. In the *Burda* of al‑Bu‑s‑iri, *ghaib* is discussed alongside *djinn* and *hakikat* to denote aspects of reality beyond human perception.

## Theological context

In Acehnese Islamic belief, *ghaib* encompasses angels, jinn, and divine decree, all of which are acknowledged in the Qur’an and Hadith.
