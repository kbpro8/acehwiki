**Rawshaniyya** was a Sufi sect founded by [[Bayazid]] in the 16th century. The sect’s literature and its scholars, such as [[Akhond Derwizeh]], produced commentaries on the creed poem *[[Bad' al-Amali]]*.

## Connection to Aceh

The widespread scholarly attention to the creed poem, including Rawshaniyya commentaries, reflects the broader Islamic intellectual currents that reached Aceh via Malay translations.
