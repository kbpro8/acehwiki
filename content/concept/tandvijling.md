**tandvijling** (English: "tooth filing") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. For both sexes in Aceh (women after marriage, youths after tooth replacement at age 12-15).

## Translation

[[Koh gigoe|tooth filing]]

## Description

For both sexes in Aceh (women after marriage, youths after tooth replacement at age 12-15). In Aceh usually only upper teeth filed halfway. [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]]: upper teeth sawn with grass-knife-made saw, then ground flat — for girls sometimes to the gum. [[Alas]]: both upper and lower teeth filed.

## Significance

The term *tandvijling* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
