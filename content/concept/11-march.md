**[[11 March]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]* it appears in the articles listed below.

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this date in [[1881]] the Governor-General [[Johan Willem van Lansberge|Van Lansberge]] signed the Kabinetsbrief introducing the [[Normale bestuursinrichting (Atjeh, 1881-1884)|normale bestuursinrichting]] in Atjeh — in Verstege's reading, the political mistake from which the later [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration]] necessarily followed.
