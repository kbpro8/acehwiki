**Hollandsch-Inlandsche school** (English: "Dutch-Native school (HIS)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Seven-year Western-style primary school comparable to Dutch lagere school.

## Translation

Dutch-Native school (HIS)

## Description

Seven-year Western-style primary school comparable to Dutch lagere school. 8 in Atjeh, 16 teachers, ~1,500 pupils. Subjects: Dutch, arithmetic, geography, history, nature, drawing, gymnastics, plus Maleisch. Teachers mostly inheemsch; head teacher usually European. 7 particular (private) HIS maintained by [[Mohammadijah]] and [[Taman-Siswa]] alongside government schools.

## Significance

The term *Hollandsch-Inlandsche school* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
