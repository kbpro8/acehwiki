**Demonstratieproeven** (English: "Demonstration trials") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Field demonstrations used by Landbouwvoorlichtingsdienst to convince skeptical Acehnese of recommended measures; key to gaining population's trust.

## Translation

Demonstration trials

## Description

Field demonstrations used by Landbouwvoorlichtingsdienst to convince skeptical Acehnese of recommended measures; key to gaining population's trust.

## Significance

The term *Demonstratieproeven* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
