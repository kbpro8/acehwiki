**Acehnese Pepper Varieties** refers to the three primary cultivars of black pepper (*Piper nigrum*) distinguished by Acehnese planters (*aneu seuneubo*).

## Primary Cultivars

1. **Lada Koelit**: Characterized by thick leaves, hard stems, and large berries; forms tall vines but yields irregularly.
2. **Lada Minjeu** (or *Lada Selassih*): Features thin light-green leaves and abundant small berries; highly favored for commercial yield.
3. **Lada Tjangde**: Small-leaved, slow-growing cultivar with dense berry clusters.

Planters categorized fruit development into eight distinct maturation stages, harvesting berries at full ripeness (*poetih geutiee*) for white pepper or slightly unripe for black pepper.
