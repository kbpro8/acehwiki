**Blok (block / stocks)** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Means to which the population resorted to protect themselves against insane outbursts.

## Description

Means to which the population resorted to protect themselves against insane outbursts. Van Loon found people chained in 'block' for over 10 years, completely grown crooked, unable to walk. Author saw similar in [[Borneo (Z.M. stoomschip)|Borneo]] (one man wrapped from neck to feet in thick [[Rattan (Aceh)|rotan]]).

## Significance

The term *Blok (block / stocks)* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
