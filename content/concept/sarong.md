***Sarong*** was the wrapped garment worn about the loins throughout the archipelago and in [[Acheh|Atjeh]].

[[P. H. R. Beuming]] records it in his description of [[The sharpshooter of Biloel|the sharpshooter of Biloel]], the Acehnese marksman killed at dawn before the post of [[Biloel]]. He was *een lange, stevig gebouwde kerel met baardeloos gelaat. Zijn kleeding bestond slechts uit een lange zwarte pantalon, en om de lendenen droeg hij een korte sarong. Het hoofd was gedekt met een Atjehsche fez* — a tall, strongly built fellow with a beardless face, dressed only in long black trousers, with a short *sarong* about the loins and an Acehnese fez on his head. About his naked upper body hung a cartridge pouch of the Dutch troops' pattern, and in his right hand he carried a [[Beaumont rifle|Beaumont]] rifle.
