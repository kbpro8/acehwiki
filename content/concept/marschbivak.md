**marschbivak** (English: "march bivak") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Smaller temporary military post.

## Translation

[[March|march]] [[Bivak|bivak]]

## Description

Smaller temporary military post. Example: [[Geunteuët]] on the [[Westkust]].

## Significance

The term *marschbivak* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
