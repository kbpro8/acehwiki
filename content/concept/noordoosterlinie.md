**The Noordoosterlinie** (Dutch for "North-Eastern Line") was the north-eastern of the four lines of the [[Concentratie-linie|concentratie-linie]] around [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] in [[Acheh|Atjeh]], including the [[missigit Lambada|missigit Lambada]] which was the object of a Dutch échec on [[12 October]] [[12 October]] [[1876]]. After the [[Van Lansberge's visit to Aceh (March-April 1877)|inspection by Van Lansberge]] in [[March 1877|March 1877]] the line was reinforced, and on [[25 January]] [[25 January]] [[1877]] its extension to [[Kwala Loë|Kwala Loë]] and [[Kroëng Raja|Kroëng Raja]] was undertaken under the personal direction of the commander-in-chief.

## The Missigit Lambada

The most important action on the Noordoosterlinie was the storming of the [[missigit Lambada|missigit Lambada]] on [[12 October]] [[12 October]] [[1876]], which was repulsed. The échec was a setback for the Dutch, who were already in [[1876]] reducing their offensive operations in expectation of the Governor-General's visit in [[April 1877|April 1877]].

## The Closure of the Coast, January 1877

In [[January 1877|January 1877]] the troops resumed operations for the completion of [[J. L. J. H. Pel|General Pel]]'s program: the occupation of the coast from [[Kwala Gighen|Kwala Gighen]] to [[Kroëng Raja|Kroëng Raja]], by which the enemy still maintained communication with the sea. On [[25 January]] [[25 January]] [1877] the operations began under the commander-in-chief's personal direction with a simultaneous landing at [[Kwala Loë|Kwala Loë]] and [[Kroëng Raja|Kroëng Raja]]. The latter place was not deemed suitable for a settlement; the troops returned along the beach to Ladoeng and thence over the mountains to [[Lambaroe Angan|Lambaroe Angan]] to join the other column, which had landed at Kwala Loë under Colonel [[K. van der Heyden|Van der Heyden]]. After the junction, the strong [[Lamnga|Lamnga]] was occupied, abandoned by the enemy. On [[30 January]] [[30 January]] [1877] the [[missigit Lambada|missigit Lambada]], from which the troops had been repulsed in October, was stormed. "With this the objective was in essence attained, and although no post was set up at Kroëng Raja, the occupation of the coast from Kwala Gighen to Kwala Loë had in fact cut the enemy off from the sea."

## The Operations of September 1876

In [[September 1876|September 1876]] after the arrival of new reinforcements, the Dutch troops again took more offensive action. The object of the operations was to better secure the Noordoosterlinie, which was continually threatened by the enemy's attacks, and to obtain a better boundary line by the occupation of the [[Kroëng Tjoet|lagune Kroëng Tjoet]]. By diversions in the [[Zuidoosterlinie|Zuidoosterlinie]] the enemy's attention was diverted, and on [[4 September]] [[4 September]] operations began in the [[XXVI Moekims|XXVI Moekims]].
