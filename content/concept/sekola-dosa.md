**sekola dosa** (English: "'sin school' (for volksschool / sekola desa)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Acehnese wordplay renaming 'sekola desa' (village school, i.e., volksschool) as 'sekola dosa' ('sin school').

## Translation

'sin school' (for [[Volksschool|volksschool]] / sekola desa)

## Description

Acehnese wordplay renaming 'sekola desa' (village school, i.e., [[Volksschool|volksschool]]) as 'sekola dosa' ('sin school'). Originated in the [[actie-tijd]], indicating low appreciation of Western education at the time.

## Significance

The term *sekola dosa* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
