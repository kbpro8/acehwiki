**sirihdoek** (English: "betel cloth pouch") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Cloth in which men carry their sirih (betel-chewing) supplies.

## Translation

betel cloth pouch

## Description

Cloth in which men carry their sirih (betel-chewing) supplies. Slung over the shoulder. Presented to guests and acquaintances like a European cigar case.

## Significance

The term *sirihdoek* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
