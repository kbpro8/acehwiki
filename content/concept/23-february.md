**[[23 February]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[Het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh (Jubileum-nummer 1930)|Het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh]]* it marks the following events.

## Events on this date

- The award to sergeant and *brigade-commandant* [[W. H. Loef]] of the knight’s cross of the *[[Militaire Willemsorde|Militaire Willems-Orde]]* 3rd class, [[23 February]] [[1917]] ([[1917]])
