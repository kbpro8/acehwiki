**bileuë** (English: "Mosque caller (muezzin)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Mosque officer who calls the faithful to prayer with a loud voice; part of the meuseugit personnel.

## Translation

Mosque caller (muezzin)

## Description

Mosque officer who calls the faithful to prayer with a loud voice; part of the [[meuseugit]] personnel. No role in civil governance.

## Significance

The term *bileuë* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
