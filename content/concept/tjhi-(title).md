**The Tjhi** — also written *tjhi*, short for *keutihi* (pronounced *ketjhi*) — was an Atjehnese title borne by the *[[Oeléebalang|oeléebalangs]]*, especially those who from of old had been the military commanders of their *landschappen* and were now the administrators and judges over their people. The title is added to the personal name, as in *Teukoe Tjhi Perlak*, *Teukoe Tjhi Peusangan*, *Teukoe Tjhi Mohamad Thajeb*. The word *keutihi* means *oud* — *old* — and the title thus designates the seniority of the bearer.
