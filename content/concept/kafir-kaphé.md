**Kafir / Kaphé** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Acehnese term for non-Muslim (especially the Dutch).

## Description

Acehnese term for non-Muslim (especially the Dutch). '[[kafirhaat|Kafirhaat]]' (hatred of the infidel) was fuelled by [[Oelama|oelama]] fanaticism. [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]]: '[[Kafir|kafir]]-hatred, like any other passion, does not exclusively dominate a people; among a people like the Acehnese, various causes have made this hatred a habit, which however can be subdued by overmight and unlearned through prolonged intercourse.'

## Significance

The term *Kafir / Kaphé* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
