The **[[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]]** was the defensive line around [[Kota Radja|K. Radja]] designed by [[Generaal Pel|generaal Pel]] and completed in May [[1875]] — a nearly *gelijkbenige driehoek* (isosceles triangle) whose *basis* was the sea-strand from [[Moesapi|K. Moesapi]] to [[Oeleh Leh]], and whose *tophoek* (apex) was [[Longbatta-Mesigit|Longbatta Mesigit]] in the south-east, some 6 000 metres into enemy territory perpendicular to the strand. The line comprised 36 posts and covered approximately 2 500 hectaren — about three times the area of Amsterdam. Borel credits Pel with the design but insists that it came "een jaar te laat": the same stelling could have been occupied at low cost in the spring of [[1874]] if [[Jan van Swieten|van Swieten]] had not applied the *afwachtende houding* (wait-and-see posture) after the [[Fall of the Kraton (24 January 1874)|fall of the Kraton]].

## Geometry and Extent

The stelling-Pel was a nearly isosceles triangle. The *basis* ran along the sea-strand from K. Moesapi at the north-eastern corner to Oeleh Leh at the south-western corner — the line of communication between the Dutch vestiging and the sea. The *tophoek* was at Longbatta-Mesigit in the south-east, some 6 000 metres into enemy territory perpendicular to the strand. The south side ran from [[Oedjoeng Planggan]] via [[Soerian]], [[Poe Oe]], [[Poengej Blang Tjoet]], [[Lamteboe]], [[Setee]] Z., [[Lamara Olejlo]], [[Lohong]], [[Longbatta]] Z. to Longbatta Mesigit. The east side ran from Blang Tjoet via [[Kota Alam]], [[Lemboe Oost|Lemboe O. en N.O.]], [[Lampriet]], [[Langkroek]] O., [[Lamara]] Z.O. en N.O., [[Tiban]] to Moesapi. The total enclosed area was approximately 2 500 hectaren — about three times the area of Amsterdam. The line comprised 36 posts, each garrisoned by a detachment of infanterie and (where appropriate) artillerie, and connected by roads, telegraph and (where feasible) the field railway between K. Radja and Oeleh Leh.

## Construction and Completion

The stelling-Pel was the work of two years of exhausting effort by the [[Bezettingskorps in Atjeh|bezettingskorps]] under Pel. The conquest of [[Blang Tjoet (18 January 1875)|Blang Tjoet]] on [[18 January]] [[1875]] and of [[Conquest of Mandarsa Poeti (28 January 1875)|Mandarsa Poeti]] on [[28 January]] [[1875]], the [[Surprise of Lamara Olejlo (14–15 February 1875)|surprise of Lamara Olejlo]] on the night of [[14 February|14]]–[[15 February]] [[1875]], the conquest of [[Lohong]], [[Setee]], [[Lamteboe]] and [[Gitjiel]] in April [[1875]] — all were stages in the construction. The [[Completion of the defensive ring around Kota Radja (May 1875)|completion of the continuous line]] in early May [[1875]] gave the Dutch an unbroken line from Blang Tjoet on the left bank of the [[Atjeh-rivier]] westward over Longbatta Mesigit, Lohong, Lamara, Gitjiel, Setee, Lamteboe, Poengej Blang Tjoet, Poe Oe, Soerian and Blang Oe to the sea-strand at Oeleh Leh. K. Radja now formed the "doelmatige centraal stelling" it should have been from the beginning. The [[Strategische driehoek (Atjeh)|strategische driehoek]] Kwala Atjeh–Kota Radja–Oeleh Leh — which van Swieten's defenders had attributed to van Swieten — was, Borel clarifies, Pel's work, not van Swieten's.

## Critique: A Year Too Late

Borel's central critique of the stelling-Pel is that it came "een jaar te laat" — a year too late. The same stelling could have been occupied at low cost in the spring of [[1874]] if van Swieten had not "stak het zwaard voor goed in de scheede" after the [[Expedition to Ketapang Doewa (15 February 1874)|expedition to Ketapang Doewa]] of [[15 February]] [[1874]]. The [[Capture of Kota Goenoengan (12 January 1874)|capture of Kota Goenoengan]] on [[12 January]] [[1874]] had shown that the Atjehnese hoofdmacht had already withdrawn from the western face of the Kraton; the omtrekkende beweging had not been pursued. The result was that every post of the stelling-Pel had to be conquered at high cost in [[1874]] and [[1875]]: Lemboe, taken on [[25 December|25]]–[[26 December]] [[1873]] and immediately abandoned, had to be [[Conquest of Lemboe (7 November 1874)|reconquered]] on [[7 November]] [[1874]]; Soerian, briefly in Dutch hands in February [[1874]], had to be [[Battle of Soerian (26 July 1874)|re-fought]] on [[26 July]] [[1874]]; Lampoe Oek, the scene of the [[Battle of Lampoe Oek (16 April 1874)|échec of 16 April 1874]], was only [[Destruction of the Lampoe Oek fortification (18 September 1874)|destroyed by artillery]] on [[18 September]] [[1874]]. Borel also criticises the stelling-Pel for containing too many redundant posts — a feature of the *posten-systeem* that he indicts throughout the book.
