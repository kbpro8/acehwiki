**Ambachtsleergang** (English: "Vocational craft school") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Public vocational school at Sigli (at the Atjeh-spoor workshops), originally for metaalbewerking (metalworking), later extended to houtbewerking (woodworking).

## Translation

Vocational craft school

## Description

Public [[Sigli ambachtsleergang|vocational school at Sigli]] (at the Atjeh-[[Bekas|spoor]] workshops), originally for metaalbewerking (metalworking), later extended to houtbewerking (woodworking).

## Significance

The term *Ambachtsleergang* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
