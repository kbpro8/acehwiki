**Map of Groot Atjeh** is a Dutch cartographic work dated circa [[1873]] that depicts the interior of [[Atjeh (Aceh)]], including river systems, topography, and the locations of Dutch bivouacs during the 1875‑1876 campaigns. The map is reproduced in the *De geschiedenis van het Nederlandsche Korps Mariniers* (page 471) and was used by the [[Korps Mariniers]] for operational planning.  

## Features  

- **Scale and Legend** – Shows major rivers such as the [[Peusangan River]] and the routes of marine columns.  
- **Bivouac Sites** – Marks the temporary camps referred to as “*Bivaks op Groot Atjeh*”.  
- **Strategic Points** – Highlights fortified towns like [[Banda Aceh]] and interior strongholds.  

## Historical Context  

The map reflects Dutch attempts to chart the difficult terrain of Aceh for military purposes during the early phase of the [[Aceh War]].
