**Nanggroe** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Term for a state (used for the Acehnese realm).

## Description

Term for a state (used for the Acehnese realm).

## Significance

The term *Nanggroe* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
