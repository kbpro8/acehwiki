**Sundried copra** (English: "Sun-dried copra") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Sun-dried coconut kernel; superior quality; propagated by Landbouwvoorlichtingsdienst to replace smoked copra.

## Translation

Sun-dried [[Copra|copra]]

## Description

Sun-dried [[Copra|coconut kernel]]; superior quality; propagated by Landbouwvoorlichtingsdienst to replace [[Smoked copra|smoked copra]].

## Significance

The term *Sundried copra* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
