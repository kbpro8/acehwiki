The **gantang** was the Atjehnese measure of capacity equal to one-eighth of a *[[Naleh|naleh]]* and to two *[[Areh|areh]]*; eighty *gantang* made one *[[Goentja|goentja]]*. It formed part of the scale by which the size of a rice field in [[Aceh|Atjeh]] was expressed, the area being reckoned by the quantity of *[[Bibit|bibit]]* required to plant it.
