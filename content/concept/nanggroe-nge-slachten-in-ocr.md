**nanggroe (nge-slachten in OCR)** (English: "family-based state system") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Institution that Sultan Iskandar Moeda reputedly organized/introduced in Atjeh; literally the 'family system'.

## Translation

family-based state system

## Description

Institution that [[Sultan Iskandar Moeda]] reputedly organized/introduced in Atjeh; literally the '[[Nanggroe / Familie-stelsel (institution)|family system]]'. Represents the kinship-based political organization of Atjeh. (Note: OCR garbles the term as 'nge-slachten' on page 26.)

## Significance

The term *nanggroe (nge-slachten in OCR)* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
