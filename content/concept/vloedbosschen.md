**Vloedbosschen** (English: "Tidal/mangrove forests") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Tidal swamp forests in Oostkust onderafdeelingen (Idi, Langsa, Tamiang); exploited for roundwood poles, firewood, charcoal, bakau bark (tannin), tengar bark (net tanning).

## Translation

Tidal/mangrove forests

## Description

Tidal swamp forests in [[Atjeh's Oostkust (East Coast of Aceh)|Oostkust]] onderafdeelingen (Idi, [[Langsar|Langsa]], [[Tamiang]]); exploited for roundwood poles, firewood, charcoal, bakau bark (tannin), tengar bark (net tanning).

## Significance

The term *Vloedbosschen* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
