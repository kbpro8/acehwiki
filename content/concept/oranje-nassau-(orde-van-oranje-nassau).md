**The Oranje-Nassau** — the Orde van Oranje Nassau, the Order of Orange-Nassau — was a Dutch royal order of chivalry, awarded to both military and civilian recipients for service to the Dutch state. In *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* the Oranje-Nassau is mentioned as a decoration awarded to [[Schmidt]] (before his [[Militaire Willemsorde|M.W.O.]]) and to [[Dominee Thenu]] in [[1901]] — the latter an exceptional honour, since the *Inlandse godsdienstleraar* officially ranked only for a *medaille*.

## Schmidt's Oranje-Nassau

"De luitenant [[Schmidt]] had zijn sporen op Atjeh reeds behoorlijk verdiend. Eerst had hij de Oranje-Nassau gekregen en later de M.W.O." Schmidt received the Oranje-Nassau before his M.W.O. — making him an unusually decorated luitenant. The Oranje-Nassau was therefore a recognition of Schmidt's service before the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]] that would earn him the M.W.O.

## Thenu's Oranje-Nassau (1901)

[[Dominee Thenu]] was made *Ridder in de Orde van Oranje Nassau* in [[1901]], at age 33 — "reeds in [[1901]], toen hij 33 jaar was, Ridder werd in de Orde van Oranje Nassau" — exceptional, since the *Inlandse godsdienstleraar* officially ranked only for a *medaille*. The decoration was [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]'s doing, and signalled that in [[Atjeh|Aceh]] the formal hierarchy of ranks and honours would be bent to recognise genuine geestelijk gezag.

## Significance

The Oranje-Nassau is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as a Dutch royal order awarded to both [[Schmidt]] (as a military decoration, before his M.W.O.) and [[Dominee Thenu]] (as an exceptional honour for an *Inlands godsdienstleraar*). The book's argument is that the Oranje-Nassau was the decoration that recognised service before the M.W.O. — and that, in Thenu's case, the decoration was an exceptional bending of the rulebook to recognise the geestelijk gezag of the *Soldaten-Dominee*.

## The Orde van Oranje-Nassau in the Korps Maréchaussée ([[1890]]-[[1930]])

The *[[Overzicht van Onderscheidingen toegekend aan het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden (1890-1930)|Overzicht van Onderscheidingen]]* printed in the [[1930]] jubilee number of *[[Het Nieuws van den Dag voor Nederlandsch-Indië]]* records the following appointments in the Orde van Oranje-Nassau within the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]*:

- [[1903]] — knight: *Kapt. Mar.* [[J. la Gordt Dillié]]
- [[1906]] — knight: *1ste Luit. Mar.* [[Schmidt|H. J. Schmidt]]
- [[1910]] — Officer: *Kapt. Mar. Jhr.* [[Boreel|J. J. Boreel]]
- [[1914]] — Officer: *Majoor v/h Korps Mar.* [[Th. J. Veltman]]
- [[1914]] — knight: *Kapt. Mar.* [[W. Muurling]]
- [[1925]] — Officer: *Kapt. Mar.* [[Kaniess|P. W. F. Kaniess]]
- [[1928]] — knight: *Kapt. Mar.* [[J. C. J. Bongers]], since replaced with the arm of the Infantry
- [[1928]] — knight: *1ste Luit. Mar.* [[Kolonel Meijer|H. Meijer]]

The order also appears in the later careers of members of the corps. The sergeant [[Heynen|J. C. Heijnen]] received the knight's cross of the Orde van Oranje-Nassau on his pensioning as *adjunct-hoofdcommissaris* of police; and [[W. V. Rhemrev]] was appointed Officer in the order with the swords for his suppression of the [[Communistische opstand ter Sumatra's Westkust (1926-1927)|communist rising]] on [[Westkust|Sumatra's Westkust]] in [[1926]].
