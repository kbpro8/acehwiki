The **[[Achterlaadkanonnen van 12 c.M.|achterlaadkanonnen van 12 c.M.]]** (12 cm breech-loading rifled guns) were the modern steel artillery pieces trialled at Tjikarang on Java in October [[1873]] and sent with the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873–1874)|second expedition]] against [[Atjeh (Borel account)|Atjeh]] — the first breech-loading rifled guns ever used on an Indische expedition. Borel, himself a kapitein der artillerie, consistently praises the achterlaadkanonnen and indicts [[Jan van Swieten|generaal van Swieten]] for recalling them to Java in February [[1874]]. The guns' *schokbuis* (impact fuze) shells burst reliably on contact — in contrast to the *tijdbuis* (time fuze) shells of the [[Voorlaadkanonnen in Atjeh|voorlaadkanonnen]] that failed on Atjeh's wet, bedekte terrain.

## The First Use on an Indische Expedition

The despatch of the achterlaadkanonnen van 12 c.M. to Atjeh in [[1873]] was, in Borel's account, a notable innovation: breech-loading rifled steel guns had never before been sent on an Indische expedition. The guns were trialled at Tjikarang on Java in October [[1873]] and judged to have "uitstekende diensten bewezen." They formed part of the materieel of the two companies of [[Vesting-artillerie in Atjeh|vesting-artillerie]] sent with the second expedition, together with mortieren van 20 c.M. and the older [[Voorlaadkanonnen in Atjeh|voorlaadkanonnen van 12 c.M.]] The achterlaadkanonnen were the principal piece with which the [[Bombardment of the Kraton (January 1874)|nineteen-day bombardment]] of the [[Kraton of Atjeh|Kraton]] from [[6 January]] to [[23 January]] [[1874]] was conducted. Their *schokbuis* shells burst reliably on contact with the earthen borstwering of the Kraton, in contrast to the *tijdbuis* shells of the voorlaadkanonnen, half of which failed to explode. Borel credits the artillerie alone with driving the enemy out of the Kraton.

## The Recall to Java

After the [[Fall of the Kraton (24 January 1874)|fall of the Kraton]] on [[24 January]] [[1874]] and the [[Van Swieten declares the Aceh War ended (1874)|declaration]] that the offensive was over, van Swieten "stak het zwaard voor goed in de scheede." Among the measures of force reduction that followed the [[Expedition to Ketapang Doewa (15 February 1874)|expedition to Ketapang Doewa]] of [[15 February]] [[1874]] was the recall to Java of one of the two companies of vesting-artillerie, with much of the materieel — including the achterlaadkanonnen van 12 c.M., "het beste geschut dat hij bezat." "Wat den opperbevelhebber bewoog zich zoo ontijdig en onnoodig te ontdoen van het beste geschut dat hij bezat, valt moeilijk te beslissen," Borel writes. The recall was, in Borel's judgement, an act of strategic vandalism: it stripped the [[Bezettingskorps in Atjeh|bezettingskorps]] of the one piece of modern materieel that could reliably engage the Atjehnese stellingen.

## Pel's Request for Return and the Vindication

[[Generaal Pel|kolonel Pel]] — who inherited the bezettingskorps on [[26 April]] [[1874]] — "dacht er evenwel geheel anders over" than van Swieten on the question of artillery. As early as [[28 April]] [[1874]] he ordered the vesting-artillerie to open fire from the voorlaad-kanonnen van 12 c.M. on all kampongs within range — daily, at varying hours. Half of the 12 c.M. puntgranaten failed to explode, exposing the inadequacy of the obsolete materieel. The bombardment led directly to the request to return the achterlaadgeschut from Java. With the returned achterlaadkanonnen the vesting-artillerie could at last engage the enemy stellingen effectively. The [[Destruction of the Lampoe Oek fortification (18 September 1874)|destruction of the Lampoe Oek fortification]] on [[18 September]] [[1874]] — "What had not been obtained earlier through fierce struggle costing heavy sacrifices was now achieved by a few cannon shots" — was the work of the 12 c.M. achterlaadkanon. The destruction of Mandarsa Poeti on [[5 October]] [[1874]], the silencing of the enemy lilla's after the first enemy shelling of K. Radja on [[24 August]] [[1874]], the bombardment of [[Pendetti]] on [[15 May]] [[1874]] and the destruction of the enemy benteng south of K. Radja on [[29 August]] [[1874]] were all the work of the achterlaadkanonnen. Borel's proposal for the [[Indische Brigade (proposed)|Indische Brigade]] in [[1878]] prescribed that the brigade be armed with steel achterlaadkanonnen with schokbuis — the modern materieel whose absence Borel had indicted throughout the book.
