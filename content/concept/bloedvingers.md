The **bloedvingers** (“blood-fingers”) were the distinguishing facings on the collar of the uniform of the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* in [[Atjeh]] — a trimming on both corners of the collar which, with a flat curl on the sleeves, marked the *maréchaussée* off from other soldiers of the Netherlands-Indies army.

## Description

The [[1930]] jubilee number records that in their clothing the *maréchaussées* were distinguished from other soldiers "door een uitmonstering, bestaande uit een belegsel op beide hoeken van den kraag, z.g. bloedvingers, en een platte krul op de mouwen" — by a facing consisting of a trimming on both corners of the collar, the so-called *bloedvingers*, and a flat curl on the sleeves. The facings were of gold for the officers and of orange cloth for the lower ranks.

## Context

The distinctive uniform accompanied a distinctive status. Placement with the Maréchaussée "is een onderscheiding geweest en is dat gebleven" — was and remained a distinction — and the corps was exempt from garrison duties and ran its own *menage* (mess). Members received extra pay: captains on the scale of the General Staff, lieutenants and *onderluitenants* on that of the Engineers, with a separate scale of pay created for non-commissioned officers and men.

## Additional detail from the jubilee number

Unlike regular KNIL infantry units that wore standard line uniforms, Marechaussee personnel wore specialized field uniforms designed for jungle agility:
- **Collar Patches**: Bright red or orange cloth facing on both front corners of the collar, nicknamed *bloedvingers*.
- **Sleeve Scrolls**: Flat ornamental sleeve scroll decorations (*platte krul*), rendered in gold lace for officers and orange wool cloth for enlisted ranks.
- **Attila**: A dark blue tunic (*atilla*) paired with white trousers for festive dress occasions in post garrison life.

The distinctive insignia made Marechaussee soldiers immediately recognizable throughout Aceh, instilling strong corps pride (*korpsgeest*) among the troops.
