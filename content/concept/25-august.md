**[[25 August]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]* it appears in the articles listed below.

## Articles referring to this date

- [[Gajòlanden]]
- [[The resistance in Atjeh 1908–1912]]

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this date in [[1885]] the blockhouse near the [[Wachthuis aan de Lingkar|Kali Lengkar]] was fired upon: three wounded (see [[Guerilla raids inside the concentration line (1885)]]).
