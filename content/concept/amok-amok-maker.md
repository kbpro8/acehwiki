**Amok (amok-maker)** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. A psychosis occurring in the Indian Archipelago, Malakka, British India, Burma, Africa.

## Description

A psychosis occurring in the [[East Indian Archipelago|Indian Archipelago]], [[Malakka]], [[Hindostan|British India]], [[Birma|Burma]], Africa. The perpetrator suddenly, outside himself, with drawn weapon, races through the busiest streets, also entering houses, stabbing all in his way. Makes no distinction among victims. Caused by intense emotions (marital infidelity, grief, sense of injustice, gambling losses, life-weariness, shame, feverish states). The amok-maker is insane, has lost all self-control. ~25% of the listed [[Atjeh-moord|atjeh-moord]] statistics are actually amok cases.

## Significance

The term *Amok (amok-maker)* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
