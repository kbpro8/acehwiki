**Mawaih Plah Tanoh** was a traditional Acehnese crop-sharing land tenure agreement where a financial investor and a planter shared pepper garden ownership and crop yields 50/50.

## Related Agreements

Variants included *Meudoewa laba* (a two-year profit-sharing management contract) and *Mawaih boh* (a 50/50 yield-sharing harvesting agreement).
