**Keude** (*kêdé*, *kedeh*) was market street with Chinese tokos in towns of [[Atjeh]], center daily trade.

## Examples per Broersma

[[Peureula]] small municipality centre: in keude market street Chinese tokos 2nd class native school etc. Outside poor dwellings population not belonging land.

[[Idi]] keude market street entirely occupied by small shops Chinese and temple.

[[Simpang Olim]] oeléëbalang when not traveling found near meuseugit where also market keude.

[[Meureudoe]] market Meureudoe brisk busy keude daily few hundred guilders turnover but not makes population prosperous subject wrong practices traders.

General: coming place immediately at station keude commercial street shops Chinese whole day busy around Atjehers busy Chinese earlier years followed military bivouaks.
