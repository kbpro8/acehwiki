**Mandoers** — *overseers* in Dutch colonial military vocabulary — were the native overseers employed by the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army|K.N.I.L.]] to supervise the [[Dwangarbeiders|forced labourers]] (*dwangarbeiders*) in the field operations of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]], organised in small numbers (typically one or two to a detachment of 15–25 *dwangarbeiders*) and attached to the various Dutch columns. The *mandoers* are the principal native supervisory personnel of the [[1896]] campaign in *[[ATJEH '96 (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96]]* (1913).

## Role and organisation

The *mandoers* were employed by the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army|K.N.I.L.]] to supervise the [[Dwangarbeiders|forced labourers]] in the field operations of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. They were organised in small numbers — typically one or two to a detachment of 15–25 *dwangarbeiders* — and attached to the various Dutch columns. Each company of infantry, section of artillery, detachment of engineers, and platoon of cavalry was allocated one *mandour* for its 15 *dwangarbeiders*; each battery of artillery was allocated one *mandour* for its 12 *dwangarbeiders*; each ambulance was allocated one *mandour* for its 15 *dwangarbeiders*. The *mandoers* were fed according to the same *schaftorde* (ration scale) as the *dwangarbeiders*.

## The 1896 campaign

The *mandoers* were the principal native supervisory personnel of the [[1896]] campaign. The *1e Deel* of *[[ATJEH '96 (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96]]* records the *mandoers*' allocation to the various Dutch columns of the year, including the [[Relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit (8 April 1896)|relief column of Biloel and Lamkoenjit]] on [[8 April]] [[1896]] (where the column's train was allocated 8 *mandoers* and 166 *dwangarbeiders*) and the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|great tuchtiging of the IV and VI Moekims of 23–25 May 1896]] (where the column was allocated 4 *mandoers* and 215 *dwangarbeiders* under the *gewestelijke intendant*). The *Tweede Deel* records the *mandoers*' allocation to the [[Excursie to Lepong and Lohong (10–14 August 1896)|expedition to Lepong of August 1896]] — for which the *Nota betreffende de verpleging van troepen en dwangarbeiders bij den tocht naar Lepong* (Bijlage XIV of the *Tweede Deel*) set out the detailed arrangements for the feeding and care of the *mandoers* and *dwangarbeiders*.

## Significance

The *mandoers* are the principal native supervisory personnel of the [[1896]] campaign in *[[ATJEH '96 (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96]]* and the principal native supervisors of the [[Dwangarbeiders|forced labourers]]. The *mandoers*' role in the supervision of the *dwangarbeiders* was essential to the conduct of the Dutch field operations, and their care and feeding were the subject of the *Nota betreffende de verpleging van troepen en dwangarbeiders bij den tocht naar Lepong* (Bijlage XIV of the *Tweede Deel* of *ATJEH '96*).
