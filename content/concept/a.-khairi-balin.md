**A. khairi ba'lin** was an Acehnese‑Malay term recorded on page 92 of the *Malay translation of the Burda*, glossed as “suami, jang pengasih, liefhebbend echtgenoot” (loving, affectionate husband). The phrase combines the Arabic **khayr** (good) with a local suffix, reflecting Acehnese ideals of marital affection.

## Cultural Relevance

The term appears in verses praising the virtues of a benevolent husband, illustrating gendered moral expectations in Acehnese society.
