**[[17 October]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]* it appears in the articles listed below.
