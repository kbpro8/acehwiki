**Levertraan** — cod-liver oil, *Oleum jecoris aselli* — was the principal therapeutic and preventive agent against *[[Xerophthalmie|xerophthalmie]]* and *[[Keratomalacie|keratomalacie]]* in the Netherlands Indies, and the single largest item of medicine distributed during Dr. [[C. E. Gomperts]]'s [[Oogheelkundig kampongonderzoek in Groot-Atjeh (Maart-December 1937)|eye survey]] of [[Groot-Atjeh]] in [[1937]].

## Distribution during the survey

The statement of expenditure for the survey from March to December [[1937]] records 404.1 kilogrammes of *Oleum jecoris aselli* distributed, valued at ƒ202.05 — the largest single item of medicine by value after the malaria after-treatment tablets and the santonin-calomel tablets.

## In treatment

Gomperts recorded that when circumstances changed favourably "door levertraan-toediening en verstrekken van vitamine-A rijke voeding" — by the administration of cod-liver oil and the supply of vitamin-A-rich food — the corneal changes of xerophthalmia often disappeared, whereas if circumstances remained unfavourable the corneal changes appeared as in keratomalacia. This observation was the ground of his argument, against Professor [[Mulock Houwer]], that the lesser corneal changes were a preliminary stage of keratomalacia rather than a distinct condition.

In a seventeen-year-old youth with *hemeralopie* (night blindness) and Bitôt's spots, both subjective and objective signs disappeared promptly "na een vitamine-A-rijke voeding en therapie," allowing the diagnosis to be made without determining the blood vitamin A level.

Cod-liver oil was also the therapy on which Gomperts and [[J. Tijssen|Tijssen]] agreed without reservation: against Tijssen's ninth proposition on xerophthalmia, "Therapie: *levertraan*," Gomperts recorded simply "Ook de meening van schrijver."

## As a preventive measure

The second of Gomperts's five recommended measures for combating xerophthalmia and preventing blindness through keratomalacia was the distribution of cod-liver oil to the population "met royale hand" — with a liberal hand.

## In the mission's work

The same remedy had earlier been singled out for praise by Tijssen in the work of the Protestant *[[Zending|zending]]*, which by the treatment of *[[Avitaminose in Atjeh|avitaminose]]* with cod-liver oil had, in his words, prevented early blindness in hundreds of children.
