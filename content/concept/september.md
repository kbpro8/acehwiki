**[[September]]** is a calendar month used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention. In the material drawn from *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]* it is associated with the [[Governor's authorisation to Toekoe Nja Banta (1 September 1893)|Governor's authorisation to Toekoe Nja Banta]] of [[1 September]] [[1893]]; the conferment on [[Teukoe Oemar]] of the dignity of [[Panglima Prang Besar]]; the capture of [[Lamgoet]] on [[4 September]] [[1893]]; and the [[Dutch occupation of a benteng at Toengkoeb (27 September 1893)|Dutch occupation of a benteng at Toengkoeb]] on [[27 September]] [[1893]].
