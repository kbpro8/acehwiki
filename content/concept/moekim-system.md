**The Moekim system** was the sub-district administrative structure of the [[Groot-Atjeh|Acehnese realm]], in which groups of kampongs were organized into *moekim* (also spelled *mukim*) — a unit that combined territorial, religious, and military functions. Each *moekim* was headed by an *imam* who served as both religious leader and civil administrator. The *moekim* system formed the intermediate level between the kampong and the *sagie* (the great confederation), and was one of the defining features of Acehnese political organization. During the [[Aceh War]], the *moekim* divisions — particularly the [[XXII Moekims]], the [[XXV Moekims]], the [[XXVI Moekims]], the [[III Moekims]], and the [[VI Moekims]] — served as the primary units of military recruitment and political alignment.

## Structure

The Acehnese political hierarchy was organized as follows:

1. **Sultan** — the nominal sovereign, whose authority was in practice limited by the *sagie* chiefs.
2. **Sagie** — the great confederation, of which there were three in [[Groot-Atjeh]]: the [[XXVI Moekims]] (closest to the capital), the [[XXV Moekims]] (western district), and the [[XXII Moekims]] (eastern/southern district).
3. **Moekim** — the sub-district, grouping several kampongs under an *imam*.
4. **Kampong** — the village, headed by a *toengkoe* or *teungku*.

Each *sagie* was headed by a *panglima* — the panglima of the XXVI Moekims, the panglima of the XXV Moekims, and the panglima of the XXII Moekims. The panglima was the most powerful political and military figure in his sagie, and his authority often exceeded that of the Sultan.

## The Major Moekim Groupings

The *moekim* groupings most frequently encountered in the historical sources of the Aceh War include:

- **[[XXVI Moekims]]**: The sagie closest to the capital at [[Kotta Radja]], whose panglima exercised significant influence over the court.
- **[[XXV Moekims]]**: The western sagie, whose panglima was [[Toekoe Nek Radja Moeda Setia|Toekoe Nek]] of [[Maraksa]], who submitted to the Dutch early in the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)|Second Expedition]].
- **[[XXII Moekims]]**: The eastern/southern sagie, whose panglima was [[Panglima Polim]], the most formidable opponent of the Dutch and the leading figure of the resistance.
- **[[III Moekims]]**: A smaller grouping whose submission was anticipated as a consequence of the Dutch occupation of surrounding areas.
- **[[VI Moekims]]**: A coastal district on the west coast, subject to the [[Bombardment of VI Moekims|naval bombardment]] during the Second Expedition.

## Military Significance

During the Aceh War, the *moekim* system was of critical military significance. [[Th. E. R. Kraaijenhoff|Controleur Kraaijenhoff]] estimated that the sagie of the XXII Moekims alone could field 30,000 able-bodied men, and the total Acehnese fighting strength at up to 50,000. The *moekim* was the unit through which fighters were recruited, organized, and deployed.

The *moekim* system also determined the pattern of submission. When a panglima or a group of *moekim* chiefs submitted to the Dutch, the entire sagie could be affected. Conversely, when a sagie remained hostile, all its component *moekim* fought together.
