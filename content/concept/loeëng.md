**loeëng** is a subject named in *[[Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Pidië]]

Swart considered the improvement of the *sawah* culture in Pidië especially important, since rice from the [[Noordkust van Atjeh|north coast]] had a good and easy outlet to [[Sumatra's Oostkust]], where there was always a shortage. He sought and found a collaborator to help him improve the existing water conduits, the *[[loeëng]]s*. An agricultural school was opened at [[Pedir]] on [[12 February]], of which the population did not yet fully grasp the significance but understood enough to see that an attempt was being made to raise agriculture.

### From [[Het Pacificeerende Bestuurswerk 1908-1918]]

"I have always tried," Swart related, "to obtain very good workers in Atjeh for the matters in which I took an interest. Workers who felt for their task. Above all the irrigated rice cultivation in [[Pidië]] had to be improved. On the [[Noordkust van Atjeh|North Coast]] there was a good and easy outlet for the rice to [[Sumatra's Oostkust]], where there was always a rice shortage. So I sought and found a collaborator who could help me with improvements to the existing watercourses, the *[[loeëng]]s*. What had been done in that respect up to then, and what the Acehnese did himself, was insufficient. Moreover, waterworks in the great rivers were needed with a view to irrigation."

### From [[Pacificatie van Atjeh]]

The pacification in its constructive aspect comprised the [[Het Pacificeerende Bestuurswerk 1908-1918|pacifying administrative work]]: the repayment of unlawfully withheld incomes and securities, the support of the *[[oelèëbalang]]s* and their return to their *gampongs*, the issue of rice and seed-*padi*, generous advances for the purchase of plough cattle, the improvement of *sawah* irrigation and of the *[[loeëng]]s*, the encouragement of pepper, oil-palm and rubber cultivation, the building of roads and of the [[Gajòweg]], the extension of the [[Atjehtram]], the founding of *[[volksonderwijs]]* including girls' schools and the [[Normaalcursus]] at [[Koeta Radja]], and the care of public health.
