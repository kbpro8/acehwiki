**A. al-siddte** was an Acehnese‑Malay rendering of the Arabic **al‑ṣiddiq** (الصِّدّق) meaning “the sincere”, glossed on page 93 of the *Malay translation of the Burda* as “de oprechte; bijnaam van Abu Baker”. The term was used to denote the quality of sincere devotion, especially in reference to the first Caliph [[Abu Bakr]].

## Contextual Note

The entry reflects the high esteem accorded to Abu Bakr in Acehnese Sufi tradition.
