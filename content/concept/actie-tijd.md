**actie-tijd** (English: "the (Aceh) war / action period") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. The 37-year Aceh War period (c.

## Translation

the (Aceh) war / action period

## Description

The 37-year Aceh War period (c. [[1873]]–c. [[1910]]) during which the Dutch military campaigned in Aceh. Source of much wordplay, jokes, mutual admiration of heroism, and the construction of roads and tramways by the [[Indisch Leger]].

## Significance

The term *actie-tijd* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
