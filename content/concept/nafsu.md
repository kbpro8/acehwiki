**nafsu** denotes “desire” or “worldly passion” in Acehnese, derived from the Arabic *nafs* “self, soul”. It appears on page [[88]] of the *Burda* translation, cited in several contexts (18, 24, 144, 136, 159).

## Definition
In Acehnese moral instruction, *nafsu* represents the inner inclinations that can lead to sin if unchecked, a concept central to Sufi‑influenced teachings.

## Contextual Use
The term is paired with admonitions to control *nafsu* through *taqwa* (piety) and is contrasted with *taqwa* in later sections of the glossary.

## Significance
Its inclusion highlights the transmission of Arabic spiritual psychology into Acehnese ethical discourse.
