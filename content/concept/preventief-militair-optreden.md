**preventief militair optreden** (English: "preventive military action") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Dutch military posture of preventing resistance before it develops, replacing the repressive approach during Swart's governorship (1908-1918).

## Translation

preventive military action

## Description

Dutch military posture of preventing [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]] before it develops, replacing the repressive approach during [[Gouverneur Swart|Swart]]'s governorship ([[1908]]-[[1918]]).

## Significance

The term *preventief militair optreden* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
