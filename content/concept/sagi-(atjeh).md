**The Sagi** — plural *Sagi's* — was the principal administrative division of [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] under the [[Sultanate of Aceh|Sultanate of Atjeh]], the three Sagi's of the [[25 Moekims|25]], [[26 Moekims|26]], and [[22 Moekims|22 Moekims]] together with the [[Sultan's gebied|Sultan's own territory]] constituting the fourfold division of Groot-Atjeh. The two seaward Sagi's (the 25 and 26 Moekims) lay on the coast; the inland Sagi (the 22 Moekims) lay inland. The Dutch conquest of all three Sagi's under [[General van der Heijden|Van der Heijden]] in [[1879]] brought the entire valley of Groot-Atjeh under Dutch control for the first and only time before the final pacification under [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] after [[1898]].

## The Fourfold Division

[[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] consists of three — or rather four — divisions: the [[Sultan's gebied|Sultan's own territory]] and the three *Sagi's* of the [[25 Moekims|25]], [[26 Moekims|26]], and [[22 Moekims|22 Moekims]]. The first two lay on the sea; the last inland. The Dutch had mainly occupied the 25 Moekims and the Sultan's territory. All of Groot-Atjeh was to be brought under.

## The Dutch Conquest (1879)

The Dutch conquest of the three Sagi's was the principal military success of [[General van der Heijden|Van der Heijden]]'s governorship. The [[Conquest of the 22 Moekims (March-June 1879)|conquest of the 22 Moekims]] began in [[March 1879|March]] [[1879]] and was completed by half [[June 1879|June]] [[1879]]. The [[Conquest of the 26 Moekims (1-4 July 1879)|conquest of the 26 Moekims]] began on [[1 July]] [[1 July]] [[1879]] and was completed with full success in four days. Then there was still an expedition to [[Selimoen|Selimoen]], far in the interior. Remote chiefs submitted. All of Groot-Atjeh was Dutch. The irreconcilable principal chiefs withdrew to [[Kemala|Kemala]], the hinterland of [[Pedir|Pedir]].

## The Loss and Re-Conquest

The conquest of the three Sagi's would, however, prove transient. Under the civil government of [[Pruijs van der Hoeven|Pruijs van der Hoeven]] and [[Laging Tobias|Laging Tobias]] (1881–1884), the endless patrols that Van der Heijden had deemed necessary to maintain the peace were virtually abolished, the enemy reorganised, and the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration system]] of [[1884]] contracted the Dutch *stelling* back to the perimeter of [[1876]]. The three Sagi's — except the 25 Moekims and the Sultan's territory, which remained in Dutch hands — were progressively lost to the enemy.

## Significance

The Sagi is the principal administrative division of [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] under the [[Sultanate of Aceh|Sultanate of Atjeh]]. The three Sagi's of the [[25 Moekims|25]], [[26 Moekims|26]], and [[22 Moekims|22 Moekims]], together with the [[Sultan's gebied|Sultan's own territory]], constitute the fourfold division of Groot-Atjeh. The Dutch conquest of all three Sagi's under Van der Heijden in [[1879]] was the high-water mark of the Dutch military position in Groot-Atjeh before [[1898]]; the loss of the 22 and 26 Moekims under civil government was the principal disaster of the [[Civil Government Period (1881-1884)|civil government period]]; the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration system]] of [[1884]] contracted the Dutch *stelling* back to the perimeter of [[1876]]. The final pacification under Van Heutsz after [[1898]] would re-establish Dutch control over all three Sagi's.
