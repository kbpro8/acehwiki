**The Atjehsch Museum** — the *Atjeh Museum* — was a museum of Atjehnese artistic and utility industry, housed in an Atjehnese building at the esplanade of [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]]. The museum is, in Broersma's account, *'een bezoek overwaard zoowel om de zorgvuldige ordening die het bezoek leerzaam maakt als om de verzameling zelf'* — *worth a visit, both for the careful ordering that makes the visit instructive and for the collection itself.* The smith-craft, the weaving, all artistic industry and utility industry as well as the products of the land are represented there. It is to be regretted that the museum, now well-supplied, will perhaps soon have to miss a good part of its wealth, when an owner of many objects takes back his collection.
