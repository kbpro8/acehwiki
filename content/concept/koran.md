**Koran** is the holy scripture of Islam, composed in Classical Arabic. The source cites the Koran on several pages (e.g., [[81]], [[82]], [[83]]) when explaining lexical items derived from Qur’anic terminology such as *djinn*, *ghaib*, and *sura al‑Fīl*. The *Burda* of al‑Bu‑s‑iri frequently quotes the Koran to give theological weight to its verses.

## Influence on Acehnese literature

Acehnese scholars translated and commented on the Koran from the sixteenth century onward, integrating its verses into local poetry, law, and education.
