**The Tegal** was the Javanese term for a rain-fed rice-field — that is, a rice-field dependent on rainfall rather than on irrigation. In Atjeh, the great open rice-field of an Atjehnese *landschap* is called the *[[Blang|blang]]*, but on Java it would be called a *tegal*. Broersma uses the Javanese term to gloss the Atjehnese for his Javanese reader: *'op Java zou men het noemen tegal, dus van regen afhankelijk rijstveld.'* There are in all Atjeh very many *tegals*, but one gladly wishes to know of true *sawahs*. The Atjehnese *tegal* is, however, in the wandeling (colloquially) called *sawah* — a usage Broersma deplores.
