**geblindeerde trein** (English: "armored train") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Armored train used on the Atjeh-tram line when the railway was attacked during the Aceh war.

## Translation

armored train

## Description

Armored train used on the [[Atjeh-tram]] line when the railway was attacked during the Aceh war.

## Significance

The term *geblindeerde trein* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
