**The Oeléebalang tjoet** — also written *oelébalang tjoet* — was the sub-chief of a *moekim* or other subdivision of an Atjehnese *landschap*, subordinate to the *[[Oeléebalang|oeléebalang]]*. The word *tjoet* means *small*; the *oeléebalang tjoet* is thus the *small oeléebalang* or sub-chief. The institution is the lower level of the Atjehnese territorial chieftaincy, and the principal intermediary between the *oeléebalang* and the *kepala kampong* (village-head). [[Mohamad Ali Alamsjah|Mohamad Ali Alamsjah]] was *oeléebalang tjoet* of [[Djeumpa|Djeumpa]], a sub-district within the *landschap* of [[Peusangan|Peusangan]], under his brother the *oeléebalang* [[Mohamad Djohan Alamsjah|Mohamad Djohan Alamsjah]]. The Atjesch internaat at [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]], founded in [[1922]], was in Broersma's time still a prominent institution: an *oeléebalang tjoet* could have his children admitted, but they did not yet come.
