**Vrijhaven** (English: "Free port") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Status granted to Sabang on 4 April 1896, opening it to world shipping traffic.

## Translation

Free port

## Description

Status granted to [[Sabang]] on [[4 April]] [[1896]], opening it to world shipping traffic.

## Significance

The term *Vrijhaven* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
