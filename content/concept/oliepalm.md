**oliepalm** (English: "oil palm (Elaeis guineensis)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Oil palm cultivation began in 1911 with Belgian/French capital.

## Translation

oil palm ([[Palm Oil (Aceh)|Elaeis guineensis]])

## Description

Oil palm cultivation began in [[1911]] with Belgian/French capital. The variety used (from [[West-Afrika|West Africa]]) produces more in Ned. Indië than in its homeland. Yields [[Palm Oil (Aceh)|palmolie]] (from fruit flesh) and palmpitten (palm kernels), both of commercial value. 11,325 H.A. planted by end [[1937]]; 8,838,500 K.G. [[Palm Oil (Aceh)|palm oil]] and 1,636,500 K.G. palm kernels exported in [[1937]].

## Significance

The term *oliepalm* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
