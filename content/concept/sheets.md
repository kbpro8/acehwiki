**Sheets** (English: "Dry rubber") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Droge (dry) rubber; higher price than slabs; propagated by Landbouwvoorlichtingsdienst for kapitaalkrachtiger tuinbezitters.

## Translation

Dry rubber

## Description

Droge (dry) rubber; higher price than [[Slabs|slabs]]; propagated by Landbouwvoorlichtingsdienst for kapitaalkrachtiger tuinbezitters. In [[1937]] max f 81/100KG, min f 33.

## Significance

The term *Sheets* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
