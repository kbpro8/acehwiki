**kali-moesapat** (English: "Religious court advisor") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Government-appointed kali attached to each moesapat (indigenous court).

## Translation

Religious court advisor

## Description

Government-appointed [[kali]] attached to each [[Moesapat|moesapat]] (indigenous court).

## Significance

The term *kali-moesapat* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
