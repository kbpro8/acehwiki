**T. Djohan Pahlawan** — *Teuku Johan the Champion* — was the personal name conferred by the Dutch governor [[Deijkerhoff|Deijkerhoff]] on the Westkust chieftain [[Teuku Umar|Teukoe Oema]] on [[30 September]] [[30 September]] [[1893]], together with the title *[[Panglima Prang Besar|Panglima prang besar]]* (Great War Commander), as the formal mark of his official pardon and his appointment as the Dutch's principal Atjèhse auxiliary in the [[Pahlawan-klucht|Pahlawan-klucht]] of [[1893]]–[[1896]]. The name — *Djoehan* (Johan) *Pahlawan* (Champion) — placed Teukoe Oema in the position of a Dutch-sponsored champion, with the personal name and the title that the Dutch used in their official correspondence and in their reports of his operations.

## The 30 September 1893 Conferment

On [[25 August]] [[25 August]] [[1893]] the governor Deijkerhoff and Teukoe Oema met; and in [[September 1893|September]] [[1893]] the decision came from Buitenzorg; and on [[30 September]] [[30 September]] [[1893]] the governor Deijkerhoff officially informed Teukoe Oema: that he was granted unconditional and complete forgiveness, that he would bear the title *Panglima prang besar Teukoe Djoehan Pahlawan*, and that he might maintain, at government expense, a fixed legion of 250 men.

## The Name

The name *Teukoe Djoehan Pahlawan* — "*Teuku Johan the Champion*" — was the personal name conferred on Teukoe Oema as the formal mark of his pardon. The name combined *Teuku* (the Atjèhse aristocratic title), *Djoehan* (Johan, presumably from the Arabic *Yohannā* or the Malay *Johan*, meaning "champion" or "victor"), and *Pahlawan* (from the Persian *pahlavān*, meaning "champion" or "hero"). The doubled sense of "champion" — *Djoehan* and *Pahlawan* together — emphasised the Dutch representation of Teukoe Oema as their champion in the war against the Atjèhse resistance.

## The Pahlawan-klucht

The conferment of the name was the formal beginning of the [[Pahlawan-klucht|Pahlawan-klucht]] — the "*Pahlawan farce*", as [[J. A. Kruijt|Kruijt]] would later satirically call it — the Dutch policy of arming, paying, and styling Teukoe Oema as the Dutch's principal Atjèhse auxiliary. The name *Pahlawan* — "Champion" — gave the policy its satirical name. The Pahlawan-klucht culminated, two and a half years later, in the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (March 1896)|defection of 29 March 1896]], when Teukoe Oema, taking with him the Dutch rifles, ammunition, and rix-dollars, withdrew into the mountains with his bands.

## Significance

The name *T. Djohan Pahlawan* is the formal mark of the Dutch policy of arming [[Teuku Umar|Teukoe Oema]] as the principal Atjèhse auxiliary in the [[Pahlawan-klucht|Pahlawan-klucht]] of [[1893]]–[[1896]]. The conferment of the name on [[30 September]] [[30 September]] [[1893]] — together with the title *Panglima prang besar* and the permission to maintain a fixed legion of 250 men at government expense — gave the policy its name, and was the formal beginning of the policy that would culminate in the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (March 1896)|defection of 29 March 1896]].
