**adat / adatrecht** (English: "customary law") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Customary rules of life that govern daily life in Aceh (as elsewhere in the archipelago).

## Translation

customary law

## Description

Customary rules of life that govern daily life in Aceh (as elsewhere in [[East Indian Archipelago|the archipelago]]). Constantly evolving; now influenced also by European law and religious regulation. The [[Adat|adat]] dominates marriage ceremonies — no marriage is valid unless all [[Adat|adat]] ceremonies are fulfilled. Includes [[Adat|adat]]-gebruiken, [[Adat|adat]]-voorschriften, [[Adat|adat]]-plechtigheden.

## Significance

The term *adat / adatrecht* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
