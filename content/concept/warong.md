**Warong** (English: "Small shop/stall") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Small shops/warongs around pasarloodsen; mostly Chinese-operated; sell mixed goods (manufacturen, specerijen, petroleum, klapperolie, etc.).

## Translation

Small shop/stall

## Description

Small shops/warongs around pasarloodsen; mostly Chinese-operated; sell mixed goods (manufacturen, specerijen, petroleum, klapperolie, etc.).

## Significance

The term *Warong* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
