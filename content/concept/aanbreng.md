**aanbreng** (English: "tribute / submission offering") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Submission offering that the Dutch hoped the Sultan would bring for his entire kingdom - this did not materialize after the Second Aceh Expedition.

## Translation

tribute / submission offering

## Description

Submission offering that the Dutch hoped [[Sultan of Aceh|the Sultan]] would bring for his entire kingdom - this did not materialize after the [[Second Aceh Expedition]].

## Significance

The term *aanbreng* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
