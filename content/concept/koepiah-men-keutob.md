**Koepiah men-keutob** (English: "Specific head covering of Acehnese chiefs") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Specific head covering of Acehnese chiefs; woven from differently colored cloth cut into 1 cm squares then rolled and built up.

## Translation

Specific head covering of Acehnese chiefs

## Description

Specific head covering of Acehnese chiefs; woven from differently colored cloth cut into 1 cm squares then rolled and built up. Increasingly falling into disuse. Also koepiahs woven from arenpalm fiber, mainly worn by old men.

## Significance

The term *Koepiah men-keutob* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
