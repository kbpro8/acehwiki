**karakter van den strijd** (English: "character of the struggle") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Initial Dutch plan (1873): take the capital with a large force and compel the Sultan to sign a treaty.

## Translation

character of the struggle

## Description

Initial Dutch plan ([[1873]]): take the capital with a large force and compel [[Sultan of Aceh|the Sultan]] to sign a treaty. Failed. Then: Atjehners responded with persistent guerrilla [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]], sometimes by whole population, sometimes by organized bands under religious and secular leaders. [[Oelama|ulama]] gained great power.

## Significance

The term *karakter van den strijd* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
