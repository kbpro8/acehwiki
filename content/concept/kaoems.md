**kaoems** (English: "familiegroepen (kinship groups) in Atjeh") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Family groups in Aceh, now disappeared.

## Translation

familiegroepen (kinship groups) in Atjeh

## Description

Family groups in Aceh, now disappeared. Their disappearance has weakened the incest [[Pantang|taboo]] within one's own kin group in Atjeh; in [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]] the [[Pantang|taboo]] is still strictly observed.

## Significance

The term *kaoems* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
