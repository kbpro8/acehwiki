**transversaalweg** (English: "transversal road (cross-road)[[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. 210 km road project from Sigli to Meulaboh connecting the Noordkust to the Westkust.

## Translation

[[Transversaalweg (Noord-Westkust)|transversal road]]Transverse road

## Description

210 km road project from [[Sigli]] to [[Malaboeh|Meulaboh]] connecting the [[Noordkust]] to the [[Westkust]]. By [[1930]] about 100 km built from [[Sigli]] and ~70 km (OCR garbled) from [[Malaboeh|Meulaboh]]; further construction suspended in [[1930]] due to high cost. Missing link through uninhabited mountainous terrain estimated at c. 1 million guilders.

## Significance

The term *transversaalweg* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
