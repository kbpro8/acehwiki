**Kapiteinsposten** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Remaining military posts in Aceh; the author wants the few remaining kapiteinsposten replaced by civil administration, leading to the great question of troop reduction.

## Description

Remaining military posts in Aceh; the author wants the few remaining kapiteinsposten replaced by [[Binnenlandsch Bestuur (B.B.)|civil administration]], leading to the great question of troop reduction.

## Significance

The term *Kapiteinsposten* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
