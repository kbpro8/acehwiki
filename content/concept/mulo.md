**M.U.L.O.** (English: "More Uitgebreid Lager Onderwijs (Expanded Lower Education / junior secondary)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Junior secondary school for all landaarden at Koeta Radja; many inheemsche including Atjehsch pupils.

## Translation

More Uitgebreid Lager Onderwijs (Expanded Lower Education / junior secondary)

## Description

Junior secondary school for all landaarden at [[Koeta-Radja|Koeta Radja]]; many inheemsche including Atjehsch pupils.

## Significance

The term *M.U.L.O.* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
