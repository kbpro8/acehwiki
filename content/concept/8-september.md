**[[8 September]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[Het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh (Jubileum-nummer 1930)|Het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh]]* it marks the following events.

## Events on this date

- The birth of [[W. J. Mosselman]], [[8 September]] [[1884]] ([[1884]])
- The promotion of [[W. H. Loef]] to sergeant, [[8 September]] [[1903]] ([[1903]])
- The transfer of [[W. J. Mosselman]] to the *Grenadiers en Jagers*, [[8 September]] [[1907]] ([[1907]])
