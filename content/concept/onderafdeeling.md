**onderafdeeling** (English: "subdivision of an afdeeling[[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Sub-division of an afdeeling; e.g.

## Translation

subdivision of an [[afdeeling]]Sub-division

## Description

Sub-division of an [[afdeeling]]; e.g. [[Tapa'Toean]], [[Malaboeh|Meulaboh]], [[Singkel]], [[Simeuloeë]] ([[Westkust]]); [[Takengon]], [[Gajo-Loeös]] (interior); [[Lho'Seumawé]], [[Bireuën]], [[Sigli]] ([[Noordkust]]); [[Tamiang]], [[Langsar|Langsa]], Idi, [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]] ([[Atjeh's Oostkust (East Coast of Aceh)|Oostkust]]).

## Significance

The term *onderafdeeling* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
