**chandoeri** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Pang Nanggròë]]

Another, attempted south of [[Matang Raja]], was to saw through in the same way all the beams and joists of a large uninhabited house, to have sleeping mats laid and cosy little lamps hung and an Acehnese feast set out, then to send word that a *[[chandoeri]]* of resistance men would be held in that house that evening, lure the troops there, stage a flight on their arrival, count on their not leaving the feast untouched, and then cut the *rotans* so that the house came down on the revellers, accompanied by a brisk *klewang* attack.

## Account from *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*

Joint meal held on the 3rd, 5th, 7th, 10th, 30th, 40th, 44th, or 100th day after death, the anniversary of death, and at the placement of gravestones.

*Source: J. Jongejans, Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu (Baarn: Hollandia Drukkerij N.V., c. 1938), pp. 176.*
