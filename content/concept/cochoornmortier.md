The **cochoornmortier** — the Coehoorn mortar, a light mortar of Dutch design — was among the ordnance mounted in the bastions of the Dutch post of [[Lam Koenjit]] in [[Groot-Atjeh]].

## Emplacement at Lam Koenjit

[[G. B. Hooijer]] recorded that on entering the gate of the outwork of [[Lam Koenjit]], above which the name of the post was written, one had to turn left to enter the *[[Benteng|benteng]]* itself, *waarbinnen, in de bastions, twee 7½ c.m. veldstukken en een cochoornmortier waren opgesteld* — within which, in the bastions, two 7½ cm. field-pieces and a Coehoorn mortar were mounted.

In the night of [[4 April]] to [[5 April]] [[1896]] one of these bastions was stormed by the enemy, and, Hooijer wrote, had the gun there not been loaded with case-shot the attack might have proved fatal.
