**Vrije koelies** (free coolies — hired labourers) were the civilian labour force hired by the Dutch army in [[Acheh|Atjeh]] to perform engineer and construction work under military cover, as distinct from the [[Dwangarbeiders|dwangarbeiders]] (forced labourers) used for similar work. The vrije koelies were hired on contract to work, not to fight; their tendency to flee under fire — and the consequent need for engineer officers to hold them to the work, "met de sabel in de vuist" — is a recurring feature of the engineer operations of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] recorded in the [[1944]] biography of [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Generaal Snijders]] by [[D. van den Berg|Van den Berg]].

## Use in Atjeh

The vrije koelies were used in substantial numbers in the engineer operations of [[1874]]–[[1875]]. At the [[Benting Kota Alam operation (13 November 1874)|Benting Kota Alam operation of 13 November]] [[1874]] [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] commanded a section of [[Bataillon Mineurs en Sappeurs|Mineurs en Sappeurs]] in the vanguard, with 180 vrije koelies following under a light infantry screen with tools and materials. The coolies were hired "om te werken, doch niet om te vechten"; when the Atjehese fire erupted, "de vrije koelies, die gehuurd waren om te werken, doch niet om te vechten, en van wie in den loop van den middag één werd doodgeschoten en een andere gewond, toonden af en toe neiging om voor het hevige geweervuur op de vlucht te slaan, hetgeen SNIJDERS echter met de sabel in de vuist wist te verhinderen." At the [[Missigit Longbatta operation (31 December 1874 – 13 January 1875)|Missigit Longbatta operation]] of [[1 January|1]]–[[13 January]] [[13 January]] [[1875]] Snijders had about 100 vrije koelies in addition to a detachment of mineurs, of whom three were killed and twelve wounded during the work.

## Distinction from Dwangarbeiders

The biographer [[D. van den Berg|Van den Berg]] distinguishes the vrije koelies from the [[Dwangarbeiders|dwangarbeiders]] (forced labourers) used in the later [[Lamara Olejlo fortification (15 February 1875)|Lamara Olejlo fortification of 15 February]] [[1875]], where Snijders was given "een afdeeling van 100 dwangarbeiders" instead of vrije koelies. The distinction is preserved in the wording of the biographer and reflects a real difference in the labour regime of the two types of work. The vrije koelies were hired hands who could in principle leave; the dwangarbeiders were conscript or corvée labour who could not.

## Snijders' Working-Party Command

By the end of [[1874]] [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]], in his peacetime Staf der Genie work at [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]], "beschikte [...] over zijne mineurs, over een groot aantal einde 1874 900 vrije koelies en soms bovendien nog over soldaten en dwangarbeiders" — that is, he commanded some 900 vrije koelies in addition to his mineurs, soldiers, and dwangarbeiders. The figure gives a sense of the scale of the peacetime engineer works at [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] in the last months of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]'s middle phase.
