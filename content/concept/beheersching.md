**Beheersching** — *domination* — was the policy of Dutch domination of the [[XXV Moekims]] laid down by [[Jhr. C. H. A. van der Wijck|Governor-General Van der Wijck]] in his telegram of [[18 July]] [[1896]], which supplemented the *pacificatie* (pacification) framework of the [[Notulen der conferentie van 18 Juni 1896|conference of 18 June 1896]]. The *beheersching* (domination programme) programme — *eene nadere uiteenzetting zijner bedoelingen omtrent de verdere actie* (a further exposition of his intentions regarding the further action) — was the policy within which [[J. W. Stemfoort|Stemfoort]]'s second tenure as *wd. Civiel en Militair Gouverneur* was conducted, and governed the Dutch forward movement of the second semester of [[1896]].

## The Telegram of 18 July [[1896]]

The telegram of [[18 July]] [[1896]] laid down the *beheersching* (domination programme) programme in the following terms: *De XXV Moekims moeten in haar geheel door ons beheerscht worden, wij moeten de woelgeesten en de hen volgende benden onschadelijk maken, minstens verdrijven, vervolgens moet er naar gestreefd worden, de medegesleepte kampongbevolking en hare traditioneele hoofden te doen terugkeeren; aan die hoofden mogen geen wapens worden verstrekt, en vuurwapens zelfs slechts tot zeer gering aantal in hun bezit gelaten worden, maar hun worde dan ook alleen de eisch gesteld om tegen dieven en roovers enz. op te treden, terwijl wij de woelgeesten en benden á faire moeten nemen en langzamerhand van hoofden en bevolking gedaan moeten krijgen, daarin geholpen te worden, door geheime inlichtingen en aanwijzingen hunnerzijds.* The *beheersching* (domination programme) programme thus comprised five elements: (1) the *beheersching* (domination programme) of the XXV Moekims in their entirety by the Dutch; (2) the *onschadelijk maken, minstens verdrijven* (rendering harmless, or at least driving out) of the *woelgeesten* (troublemakers) and the bands following them; (3) the *terugkeeren* (return) of the *medegesleepte kampongbevolking en hare traditioneele hoofden* (dragged-along kampong population and their traditional chiefs); (4) the prohibition on furnishing weapons to those chiefs, and the restriction of firearms in their possession to very small numbers; and (5) the demand that those chiefs act against thieves and robbers, while the Dutch *á faire* (take on) the *woelgeesten* (troublemakers (woelgeesten)) and bands, and gradually *gedaan moeten krijgen* (dispose of) them with the help of the chiefs and population.

## The Authorisation of Bezetting

The telegram further specified that *omenent het bezetten dezer moekims, kan niet beslist worden, voordat eerst door leger-bestuur mij verzekerd is, dat voortdurende aanvulling van troepen en dwangarbeiders steeds mogelijk zullen zijn* — the occupation of these mukims could not be decided until the army administration had assured him that continuous replenishment of troops and forced labourers would always be possible. The authorisation of *bezetting* (occupation) — which modified the *geen postenstelsel* policy of the conference of [[18 June]] [[1896]] — was the principal policy shift of the second semester of [[1896]], and was the legal basis for the [[Occupation of Kroeng-Raba (Lok-Nga) (15 July 1896)|occupation of Kroeng-Raba (Lok-Nga)]] of [[15 July]] [[1896]], the [[Construction of Blang Bintang Camp (7 September 1896)|construction of the Blang Bintang camp]] of [[7 September]] [[1896]], and the [[Temporary Occupation of Indrapoeri (28 October 1896)|temporary occupation of Indrapoeri]] of [[28 October]] [[1896]].

## Significance

The *beheersching* (domination programme) programme of [[18 July]] [[1896]] is significant in the documentary record of *ATJEH '96 — Tweede Deel* as the policy framework within which the Dutch forward movement of the second semester of [[1896]] was conducted. The *beheersching* (domination programme) programme — which supplemented but did not replace the *pacificatie* (pacification) framework of the conference of [[18 June]] [[1896]] — was the most explicit statement of Dutch colonial intentions in Atjeh in the second semester of [[1896]], and the authorisation of *bezetting* which it contained was the legal basis for the three permanent Dutch forward positions established in the second semester. The *beheersching* (domination programme) programme would be *ingetrokken* (withdrawn) by the *Instruction for Civiel en Militair Gouverneur Van Vliet* (Bijlage III) of November [[1896]], which consolidated all prior policy documents into a single instruction.
