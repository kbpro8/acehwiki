**The tramweg aanleg in Atjeh** — tramway construction in Atjeh — was the Dutch military infrastructure project authorised by the wet of [[26 May]] [[1897]] that constructed a tram line from [[Koetaradja|Koetaradja]] through [[Indrapoeri|Indrapoeri]] to [[Seulimeum|Seulimeum]], transforming the logistics of the [[Atjeh-oorlog (1873-1904)|Atjeh War]] and freeing troops from the burden of covering transport convoys for more direct action against the enemy.

## Authorisation and construction

By the wet (law) of [[26 May]] [[1897]], funds were authorised for tram construction in [[Groot-Atjeh|Greater Atjeh]]. The section [[Koetaradja|Koetaradja]]-[[Lambaré|Lambare]] of the old *ceintuurbaan* (circumference line) was extended, and by October [[1897]] had already reached [[Indrapoeri|Indrapoeri]]; a year later, the line was complete to [[Seulimeum|Seulimeum]].

## Military significance

The tramway transformed the Dutch operational posture. Troops that had previously been required to cover transport convoys — consuming manpower and restricting offensive operations — became available for more direct action against the enemy. The construction of the road from [[Indrapoeri|Indrapoeri]] to [[Seulimeum|Seulimeum]] (17 km long and 5 m wide), combined with the tramway, enabled the rapid movement of men and materiel through the interior of [[Groot-Atjeh|Greater Atjeh]], supporting the consolidation of Dutch positions at [[Glé Jeuëng|Glé Jeuëng]], [[Seulimeum|Seulimeum]], and the series of posts that achieved complete mastery over the [[XXII Moekims|XXII Moekim]].
