Native state (zelfbesturend landschap)") is a term or concept described in[[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Administrative unit governed by a zelfbestuurder, e.g., Peusangan.

## Translation

'landscape'/native state under self-rulelandschap)

## Description

Administrative unit governed by a [[zelfbestuurder]], e.g., [[Peusangan]]. Landschapshoofden (regional chiefs) were cooperators in the failed [[Sigli]]rice mill cooperative. Landschappen are part of afdeelingen.[[zelfbestuurder]] (self-ruler) under Dutch indirect rule. E.g. [[Kaway XVI]] is a landschap. The head is the landschapshoofd (called [[Maharadja]] at [[Lho'Seumawé]]Self-governing territory within the colonial administrative structure; partially finance Landbouwvoorlichtingsdienst and veearts personnel.

## Significance

The term *landschap* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
