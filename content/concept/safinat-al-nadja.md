**Safinat al-nadja'** (“The Ship of Salvation”) is a Malay devotional text authored by [[Salim b. Samir]]. It was printed in [[Singapore]] in [[1295]]/[[1878]] and achieved lasting popularity in the Indonesian archipelago, including Aceh.

## Content and Influence

The work combines Quranic exegesis, moral instruction, and Sufi themes. Its widespread circulation created a literary environment that facilitated the translation of other Arabic works, such as the *[[Bad' al-Amali]]*.
