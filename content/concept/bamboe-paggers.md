**bamboe-paggers** (English: "bamboo fences (sharpened stakes)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Notorious Atjehnese bamboo fences: a double hedge of sharply pointed bamboo stakes, oriented so an intruder anywhere faces the threat of these vicious spikes.

## Translation

bamboo fences (sharpened stakes)

## Description

Notorious Atjehnese bamboo fences: a double hedge of sharply pointed bamboo stakes, oriented so an intruder anywhere faces the threat of these vicious spikes. Above the entrance gate, a covered platform on high poles serves as a watchtower.

## Significance

The term *bamboe-paggers* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
