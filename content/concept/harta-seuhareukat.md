**Harta seuhareukat** (Atjehnese *atra thihareukat*; the Malay-Minangkabau form *harta seharkat*) was the property in [[Aceh|Atjeh]] acquired through the joint labour of husband and wife during marriage, and subject on divorce to division in fixed proportions varying by district.

## Land newly cleared during marriage

Ground made fruitful by man and wife in new clearance during the marriage was regarded as belonging to both together, although the husband had the management of it; the produce of such ground served the common maintenance of the couple. Property so acquired by the joint labour of man and wife bore, [[D. W. N. de Boer]] wrote, the same name in Atjeh as in [[Minangkabau]]: *harta seharkat*, Atjehnese *atra thihareukat*.

## Division on divorce

In the event of divorce the *harta seharkat* was divided as follows:

- in the *onderafdeeling* (subdivision) [[Sigli]] — two-thirds to the husband, one-third to the wife;
- in the *onderafdeeling* [[Lho' Seumawe|Lho' Seumawe]] and in [[Groot-Atjeh]] — one half to each.

## Significance

The institution, together with the *[[Atra peunoelang|atra peunoelang]]*, formed the customary counterweight in Atjeh to the small share allotted to a wife or widow under Mohammedan inheritance law, and its regional variation illustrates the district-by-district diversity of the *[[Adat|adat]]* within a single *gewest* (region).
