**rëdjö moedö** (English: "Younger prince (in Gajo Loeës)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Head of the younger branch of the stam in the onderafdeeling Gajo Loeës; assisted by an imöm and a wakil.

## Translation

Younger prince (in [[Gajo-Loeüs|Gajo Loeës]])

## Description

Head of the younger branch of the stam in the [[onderafdeeling]] [[Gajo-Loeüs|Gajo Loeës]]; assisted by an [[imöm]] and a [[Wakil|wakil]].

## Significance

The term *rëdjö moedö* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
