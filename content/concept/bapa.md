**Bapa** is the Acehnese word for “father” or “paternal figure”. It appears on [[80]] of the source, glossed as “a, dengan jang menjajan, door een zorgzaam vader (A. bikhairi abin)”. The term conveys both biological fatherhood and the moral quality of caring responsibility.

In the *Burda* translation, *bapa* is invoked in moral exhortations that liken divine guidance to a caring father.

## Cultural significance

The concept of *bapa* underpins Acehnese kinship terminology and appears in customary law (adat) where the *bapa* holds authority over family affairs.
