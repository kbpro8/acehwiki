**onderhoofden-actie** (English: "under-chiefs' movement") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. A movement in various landschappen where under-chiefs (onderhoofden) act against their head; exists in more or less concealed form across landschappen.

## Translation

under-chiefs' movement

## Description

A movement in various landschappen where under-chiefs (onderhoofden) act against their head; exists in more or less concealed form across landschappen. Flared up acutely in one [[landschap]], leading to the 'operette van [[Tjaleuë]]'.

## Significance

The term *onderhoofden-actie* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
