**Smoked copra** (English: "Smoke-dried copra") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Copra dried over fires (above-vuur); quality significantly below European criterion; original bevolkingsproduct.

## Translation

Smoke-dried [[Copra|copra]]

## Description

[[Copra]] dried over fires (above-vuur); quality significantly below European criterion; original bevolkingsproduct.

## Significance

The term *Smoked copra* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
