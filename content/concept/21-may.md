**[[21 May]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]* it marks the following events in the history of [[Groot-Atjeh]].

## Events on this date

- [[Establishment of the post at Biloel (20–21 May 1894)]] — the post of [[Biloel]] completed around the old [[Messigit]] ([[1894]])
