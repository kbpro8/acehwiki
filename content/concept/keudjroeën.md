**keudjroeën** (English: "Keudjroeën (title granted by Sultan of Atjeh)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Title granted by the Sultan of Atjeh to some rëdjö's in the Gajo lands when the sultanate was still supreme.

## Translation

Keudjroeën (title granted by [[Sultan of Atjeh (d. 1874)|Sultan of Atjeh]])

## Description

Title granted by [[Sultan of Aceh|the Sultan]] of Atjeh to some rëdjö's in the [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]] lands when the [[sultanate]] was still supreme. Conferred greater prestige on the family but did not extend their authority beyond their own [[Blah|blah]]. Also the title of the ruler of the two realms in the [[Alaslanden|Alasvallei]] after the abdication of [[Radja Keumala]].

## Significance

The term *keudjroeën* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
