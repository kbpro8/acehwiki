**souvereiniteitsverklaring** (English: "Declaration of sovereignty / submission") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Document signed by oelèëbalangs of the onderhoorigheden acknowledging Dutch authority and hoisting the Dutch flag.

## Translation

Declaration of [[Suzerainty question|sovereignty]] / submission

## Description

Document signed by oelèëbalangs of the [[Onderhoorigheden|onderhoorigheden]] acknowledging Dutch authority and hoisting the Dutch flag. Initially 6 articles; later expanded to 15 then 18 articles; replaced by the 3-article '[[korte Atjeh-verklaring]]' in [[1898]].

## Significance

The term *souvereiniteitsverklaring* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
