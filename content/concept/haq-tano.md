**Haq tano** ("right of the ground"; *tano*, ground) — also called **haq lampoih** ("right of the garden"; *lampoih*, garden) — was the share of produce which, in [[Aceh|Atjeh]], a new occupant holding a garden or *[[Ladang|ladang]]* under the *[[Haq toeleuèng|haq toeleuèng]]* had to deliver to the proven original possessor.

## Rates

The share amounted to one half of the yield if the ground in question lay within the *[[Gampong|gampong]]* boundaries, and one third of the product if it lay outside the *gampong* — that is, in the forest or in the mountains. As a counter-obligation the original possessor, the successful plaintiff, had to supply the *[[Bibit|bibit]]*. A defeated occupant unwilling to accept these terms forfeited all right to the ground.
