**Anderson's Mission to the East Coast of Sumatra** — formally *Mission to the East Coast of Sumatra in 1825* — is the British colonial report by [[Anderson|Anderson]] that records the principal east-coast topography of [[Sumatra]] in the early nineteenth century, and which [[P. de Roo de la Faille|De Roo de la Faille]] draws on for the *bedekte taal* identification of [[Boeboen]] with [[Sjahroe'n Noewi|the padoeka Boeboennja]] of the [[Sadjarah Malajoe]], for the [[Kota Djawa|Javanese fortification at Deli]], and for the *principally Achenese* population of [[Tamijang]] (i.e. [[Tamijang|Tamiang]]). It is the principal British colonial source for the east-coast Sumatran theatre in the immediate pre-[[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] period.

## Topographical record

[[Anderson|Anderson]]'s record of the [[Aroe-baai|bay of Aroe]] — "from [[Tamijang|Timian]] to [[Boeboen|Kwala Bubon]] the land forms a deep bay, not easily perceived from a distance, in consequence of two islands that front it" (the islands being [[Pulo Breueh|Pulo Tampasalee]] and [[Poelo Kampi|Pulo Sampa-tuah]], i.e. Tampilis and Kampei) — is, [[P. de Roo de la Faille|De Roo de la Faille]] notes, "a clear description of the bay of Aroe with the *teroesan* between Tampilis and Kampei, and the kampong [[Boeboen|Boeboen]] at the river-mouth of [[Tandjoengpoera]]". The same report records the *principally Achenese* population of [[Tamijang|Timian]] (i.e. [[Tamijang|Tamiang]]) — the east-coast polity whose Atjehnese population fixes it as part of the [[Aceh Sultanate|Atjehnese sultanate]]'s sphere in the early nineteenth century.

## The *Kota Djawa* record

[[Anderson|Anderson]]'s record (p. 293) of "remains of a Javanese fortification" along the road from [[Medan|Medan]] to [[Belawan|Belawan]], at [[Deli|Deli-Toewa]], is the principal British colonial attestation of the [[Kota Djawa|Kota Djawa]] — the [[Djawaka|Djawaka]] fortification whose dedication to [[Puteri Idjo|Putri Hijau]] ties it to the [[Acheh|Atjehnese]] sultanate's design on the [[Deli]]-[[Asahan]]-[[Aroe]] cultural zone.

## Significance

For AcehWiki, Anderson's *Mission* is the principal British colonial source for the east-coast Sumatran theatre in the immediate pre-[[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] period — the source on which [[P. de Roo de la Faille|De Roo de la Faille]] draws for the *bedekte taal* identification of [[Boeboen]] with [[Sjahroe'n Noewi|Sjahroe'n Noewi's padoeka Boeboennja]], for the [[Kota Djawa|Kota Djawa]] at [[Deli]], and for the Atjehnese population of [[Tamijang|Tamiang]]. Its record of the [[Aroe-baai|bay of Aroe]] also fixes the east-coast topography against which the [[Atjeh-Aroe War (1539)|Atjehnese conquest of Aroe]] of [[1539]] is measured.
