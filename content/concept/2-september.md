## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

End-date named for the Selimoen column of 27 July-2 September [[1879]], by which [[Karel van der Heijden|van der Heyden]] caused [[Toekoe Moeda Daoed]] to be fetched from his remote highland seat (see [[Selimoen Expedition (August 1879)]]).
