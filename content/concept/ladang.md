**The Ladang** was the swidden-field of an Atjehnese *landschap* — a piece of forest opened so far as necessary to obtain one rice-harvest. This reckless rice-cultivation, in Sumatra still wanted and usual, has been restricted in [[Peureula (Perlak)|Peureula]] by the rule that the laying out of a *ladang* is permitted only in the old pepper-gardens, which the *ladang*-makers must first clean. That is an excellent measure, by which the abandoned and ruined pepper-gardens do not degenerate into wilderness. In the highlands of [[Gajo-land|Gajo-land]] *ladang*-cultivation on the mountain-slopes is also practised; it runs off in four months. In [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] there are *ladangs* in the hill-country, though few.
