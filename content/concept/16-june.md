**[[16 June]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]* it appears in the articles listed below.

## Articles referring to this date

- [[1908]]
- [[Christiaan Snouck Hurgronje]]
- [[H. N. A. Swart]]

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this date, with the next day, in [[1884]] the secret sessions decided upon the [[Concentration decision (16-17 June 1884)|concentration in Groot-Atjeh]]; and on this date the Kamer voted 1⅔ million guilders for the [[Blockade of the Atjeh coast (1884-1885)|blockade of the Atjeh coast]].
