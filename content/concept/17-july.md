## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this date in [[1883]] a number of men of different social positions and turn of mind addressed the King by petition against the intended concentration of the Atjeh garrisons — the address which, like the protests of [[1882]], was never answered (see [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)]] and [[Rutgers van Rozenburg]]).
