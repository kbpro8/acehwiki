**Gajo-paard** (English: "Gayo horse") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Horse breed from Gajoland; small and short in build; cannot compete with Batakpaard.

## Translation

[[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gayo]] horse

## Description

Horse breed from [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajoland]]; small and short in build; cannot compete with [[Batakpaard]].

## Significance

The term *Gajo-paard* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
