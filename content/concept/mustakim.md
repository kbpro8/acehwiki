**mustakim** means “right” or “straight” in Acehnese, signifying moral correctness. The term is listed on page [[88]] of the *Burda* translation, paired with the Arabic *mustakim*.

## Definition
Used in religious instruction to denote the path of righteousness, *mustakim* appears in sermons, legal codes, and poetry.

## Contrast
Often juxtaposed with *mushiil* (“unclear”) and *mutah* (“pearl”), forming a moral spectrum in Acehnese theological literature.

## Significance
The term underscores the direct adoption of Qur’anic vocabulary into Acehnese moral discourse.
