**bruidsschat** (English: "bridewealth / bride price") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Payment by the groom to the bride's father, fixed in the presence of witnesses according to the bride's possessions.

## Translation

bridewealth / bride price

## Description

Payment by the groom to the bride's father, fixed in the presence of witnesses according to the bride's possessions. Bemiddelaar's fee = 8% of bruidsschat. Engagement gift = a gold ornament worth half the bruidsschat. Paid on the day after the wedding in [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]]. Settlement of bruidsschat is part of the Islamic marriage contract.

## Significance

The term *bruidsschat* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
