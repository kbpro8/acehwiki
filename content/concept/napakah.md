**napakah** (English: "husband's duty to provide wife's livelihood") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Husband's obligation to provide for his wife's livelihood once the bruidsschat is paid and she belongs to him.

## Translation

husband's duty to provide wife's livelihood

## Description

Husband's obligation to provide for his wife's livelihood once the [[bruidsschat]] is paid and she belongs to him.

## Significance

The term *napakah* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
