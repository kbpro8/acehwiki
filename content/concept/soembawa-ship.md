**Soembawa** was a Dutch steamship (*Hr.Ms.*) that participated in the **Royal Netherlands Navy**’s riverine operations on the **Tamiang** river during the **February [[1893]]** expedition. It operated alongside **[[Flores (ship)]]**, **[[Sindoro (ship)]]**, and under the command of **[[W. Allirol]]**.

## Role

The vessel contributed to the removal of enemy‑constructed barriers, enabling the infantry column to proceed upriver.
