**[[May]]** is a calendar month used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention. In the material drawn from *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]* it is associated with the [[Establishment of the post at Biloel (20–21 May 1894)|establishment of the post at Biloel]] in the night of [[20 May]]–[[21 May]] [[1894]] and the transfer of the garrison of [[Boekit Daroe]].
