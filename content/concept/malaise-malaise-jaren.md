**malaise / malaise-jaren** (English: "depression years (Great Depression)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Economic depression years that severely restricted expansion of cultures in Aceh; budgets cut, erfpachtperceelen returned, ondernemingen closed.

## Translation

depression years (Great Depression)

## Description

Economic depression years that severely restricted expansion of cultures in Aceh; budgets cut, erfpachtperceelen returned, ondernemingen closed.

## Significance

The term *malaise / malaise-jaren* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
