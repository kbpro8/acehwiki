**[[18 February]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[Het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh (Jubileum-nummer 1930)|Het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh]]* it marks the following events.

## Events on this date

- The award to *brigade-commandant* [[J. C. Heijnen]] of the knight’s cross of the *[[Militaire Willemsorde|Militaire Willems-Orde]]* 4th class, [[18 February]] [[1902]]; he received the 3rd class on [[31 July]] of the same year ([[1902]])
