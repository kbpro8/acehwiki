**keumpenie** (English: "the Company (Dutch colonial power)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Acehnese/Malay term for the Dutch colonial power (from 'compagnie', the VOC).

## Translation

the Company (Dutch colonial power)

## Description

Acehnese/Malay term for the Dutch colonial power (from 'compagnie', the VOC). Used by [[Teukoe Panglima Polem]]'s context: refrained from action against 'the keumpenie'.

## Significance

The term *keumpenie* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
