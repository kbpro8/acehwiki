The **[[Stormvrijheid|stormvrijheid]]** ("storm-freedom," cleared field of fire) was the cleared zone around a Dutch post or bivouak from which the [[Bamboe-doeri versperringen|bamboo-doeri]] thickets, the boschrand and other cover had been removed, so that an Atjehnese *charge* with the [[Klewang|klewang]] could be broken by *salvoes* of [[Beaumont-geweer|Beaumont-geweer]] fire before the warriors could close. The achievement of *stormvrijheid* was, in Borel's account, one of the principal labours of the bezettingskorps — the *kappen* (cutting) that was one of the two principal means (*kappen en branden*) of overcoming Atjeh's resistance — and the failure to achieve it was the cause of many of the Dutch casualties of the war.

## The Concept

The *stormvrijheid* was, in Borel's account, the cleared zone around a Dutch post or bivouak from which the bamboo-doeri thickets, the boschrand and other cover had been removed. The purpose was to deny the Atjehneezen the cover from which they could launch a *charge* with the klewang, and to give the Dutch *salvoes* of Beaumont-geweer fire a clear field in which to break the charge before the warriors could close. The *stormvrijheid* was, in effect, the Dutch counter-measure against the Atjehnese tactic of the *charge* — the "blanke klewang in de vuist" with which warriors sprang from the boschrand. Without *stormvrijheid*, a Dutch post or bivouak was at the mercy of a determined Atjehnese *charge*; with *stormvrijheid*, the *charge* could be broken by fire. The achievement of *stormvrijheid* required the *kappen* (cutting) of the bamboo-doeri thickets and the boschrand around the post — a laborious and dangerous work, performed by woodcutters under enemy fire, that exposed the Dutch to the very attacks it was intended to prevent.

## The Cost of Failure

The failure to achieve *stormvrijheid* was, in Borel's account, the cause of many of the Dutch casualties of the war. The [[Acehnese attack on Penajoeng bivouak (13 January 1874)|attack on Penajoeng bivouak]] of [[13 January]] [[1874]] — when a "bende woestelingen" penetrated the legerplaats from the north side near the veekraal at 2 PM on klaarlichten dag — was, Borel notes, the consequence of placing the bivouak against the boschrand without proper clearing. The [[Battle outside the ooster-face (13 October 1874)|battle outside the ooster-face]] of [[13 October]] [[1874]] — when a strong column under [[Kapitein van der Straate|kapitein van der Straate]] tried to penetrate the wilderness before sunrise, and the woodcutters who began at 6 AM were attacked from all sides — was a typical example of the cost of attempting to clear the boschrand under enemy fire. The [[Night attack on Longbatta-Mesigit (22 November 1875)|night attack on Longbatta-Mesigit]] of [[22 November]] [[1875]] — when a band of ~50 Atjehneezen climbed the low borstwering and "met de blanke klewang" hacked among sleeping manschappen — was, Borel suspected, "hoogst waarschijnlijk" the fault of schildwachten who had neglected their post, but it was also the consequence of the *stormvrijheid* that had not been fully achieved around the post.

## The Achievement

The achievement of *stormvrijheid* was, in Borel's account, one of the principal labours of the bezettingskorps. The *kappen* of the bamboo-doeri thickets and the boschrand around each post was a daily labour, performed by woodcutters under enemy fire. The 150 *klapperboomen* felled on [[18 September]] [[1874]] to clear the field of the new ooster-batterij — costing 9 wounded including 1 officer — was a typical example. The *stormvrijheid* was, however, never fully achieved around all the posts of the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]]: the boschrand grew back, the bamboo-doeri thickets regenerated, and the Atjehneezen continually reoccupied the cleared zones. The *stormvrijheid* was thus, in Borel's account, a continuous labour rather than a fixed achievement — a feature of the *kappen en branden* that Borel identifies as the two principal means of overcoming Atjeh's resistance, and a tactical complement to the *mobiele colonnes* that he advocated. The lesson Borel draws is that the *stormvrijheid* could only be achieved by sustained and costly labour, and that the *posten-systeem* — which dispersed the force among many small posts, each requiring its own *stormvrijheid* — was inherently vulnerable in Atjeh.
