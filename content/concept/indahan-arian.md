**Indahan arian** was the Toba-Batak institution which [[D. W. N. de Boer]] equated with the Atjehnese *[[Atra peunoelang|atra peunoelang]]* — the property made over by a father or other guardian to his married daughter on the occasion of the birth of her first child, when the couple set up an independent household.

## Comparative use

The equation belongs to the systematic comparison between [[Aceh|Atjeh]] and the [[Toba-Bataklanden|Toba-Batak lands]] running through De Boer's study, which also matched the Atjehnese *[[Peungkleh|peungkleh]]* with the Batak *mandjaë* and the Atjehnese *[[Djinamee (Atjehnese marriage gift)|djinaméë]]* with the Batak *sinamot*.
