**Terpentijn** (English: "Turpentine") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Volatile oil distilled from balsem along with water; purified and packed in drums or petroleumblikken; goes mainly to Europe.

## Translation

Turpentine

## Description

Volatile oil distilled from [[Balsem|balsem]] along with water; purified and packed in drums or petroleumblikken; goes mainly to [[Europe]].

## Significance

The term *Terpentijn* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
