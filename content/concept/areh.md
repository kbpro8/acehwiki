The **areh** was the smallest of the Atjehnese measures of capacity recorded by [[D. W. N. de Boer]], equal to one-half of a *[[Gantang|gantang]]*; one hundred and sixty *areh* made one *[[Goentja|goentja]]*. Like the larger units it served to express, by way of the quantity of *[[Bibit|bibit]]* needed, the size of a rice field.
