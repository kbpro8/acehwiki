**cijns / canon** (English: "lease fee / ground rent") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Annual fee paid for a concession or erfpacht.

## Translation

lease fee / ground rent

## Description

Annual fee paid for a concession or [[erfpacht]]. Concession clause: half cijns after 7th year if operating at loss. Canon paid for [[erfpacht]]; failure to pay caused rights to lapse.

## Significance

The term *cijns / canon* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
