**kapitein-fungeerend-controleur** (English: "Acting-controller captain") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Title held by infantry captains serving as military civil administrators of onderafdeelingen; currently at Meulaboh, Zuidelijke Atjehsche landschappen, and Gajo Loeës.

## Translation

Acting-controller captain

## Description

Title held by infantry captains serving as military civil administrators of onderafdeelingen; currently at [[Malaboeh|Meulaboh]], [[Zuidelijke Atjehsche Landschappen|Zuidelijke Atjehsche landschappen]], and [[Gajo-Loeüs|Gajo Loeës]].

## Significance

The term *kapitein-fungeerend-controleur* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
