**Seunebok Lada** (historically spelled *seuneubo lada*) was the traditional Acehnese collective agricultural plantation system organized for large-scale pepper cultivation (*Piper nigrum*).

## Organization and Structure

A *seunebok* was established when a capital investor ([[Adat Pangkal|Peutoeha Pangkaj]]) teamed up with a practical agricultural manager ([[Adat Peutoeha|Peutoeha Djaga]]) to recruit landless laborers (*aneu seuneubo*).

The community cleared virgin forest, constructed drainage ditches (*loeeng*), planted *dadap* (*Erythrina*) shade trees, and set out pepper vines. Over time, many *seunebok* developed into permanent villages (*gampong*), with the *peutoeha* or his descendants serving as village head.
