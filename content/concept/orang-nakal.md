**Orang nakal** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Term used by oelèëbalang Teukoe Oemar of Keumangan for those incarcerated; he wondered why they longed for the fine prisons.

## Description

Term used by [[oelèëbalang]] [[Teukoe Oemar]] of [[Keumangan]] for those incarcerated; he wondered why they longed for the fine prisons.

## Significance

The term *Orang nakal* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
