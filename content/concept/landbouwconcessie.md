**landbouwconcessie** (English: "agricultural concession") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Agricultural concession of land, issued by the Gouvernement (before 1920).

## Translation

agricultural concession

## Description

Agricultural concession of land, issued by [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Gouvernement|the Gouvernement]] (before [[1920]]). Had attractive clause: after the 7th concession year, in case of operating loss, only half the cijns was due. No new ones issued after 1 Feb [[1920]] — all new ondernemingen on erfpachtperceelen.

## Significance

The term *landbouwconcessie* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
