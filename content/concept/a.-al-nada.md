**A. al-nada** was an Acehnese‑Malay term recorded in the Burda glossary (page 92) meaning “anugeraha jang – volmaakte gunst” (perfect generosity, magnanimity). It derives from the Arabic **al‑naḍā** (النَضَاء) meaning “generosity” or “bounty”. In Acehnese moral literature the word signified the ideal of self‑sacrificial kindness.

## Application

The term appears in passages praising the benevolence of saints and sultans, linking moral virtue to divine favor.
