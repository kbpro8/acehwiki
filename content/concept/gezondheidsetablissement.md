**A gezondheidsetablissement** — a health establishment or sanatorium — was a colonial convalescent station, and by [[1911]] one had been established at [[Kroeng Raba]] on the west coast of [[Groot-Atjeh]].

[[P. H. R. Beuming]] notes the fact in his description of the [[Kroeng Raba Bay|Kroeng Raba bay]], which he calls first among the picturesque landscapes of Groot-Atjeh: *Omgeven door een rotsachtig hoog gebergte, steeds onder het bereik van den frisschen zeewind, is het klimaat er overheerlijk. Tegenwoordig bevindt zich dan ook aldaar een gezondheidsetablissement* — surrounded by a rocky high mountain range and constantly within reach of the fresh sea wind, the climate there is delightful; at present, accordingly, a health establishment is found there.

The remark measures the distance between the Kroeng Raba of the [[1896]] garrison — where the *[[Benting|benting]]* [[Lok-Nga|Lho-Nga]] lay lonely at the river mouth, no warship venturing to approach the dangerous coast — and the pacified district of [[1911]].
