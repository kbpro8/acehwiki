**Datoek besar** (English: "Great Datoek (district head)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Title of the district head in the four districts of Singkel (Beneden-Singkel, Simpang kanan, Simpang kiri, Poelau Banjak).

## Translation

Great [[Datoek]] (district head)

## Description

Title of the district head in the four districts of [[Singkel]] ([[Singkel|Beneden-Singkel]], [[Simpang Kanan|Simpang kanan]], [[Simpang Kiri|Simpang kiri]], Poelau Banjak). Hereditary.

## Significance

The term *Datoek besar* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
