**[[18 April]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]* it marks the following events in the history of [[Groot-Atjeh]].

## Events on this date

- [[Relief and lifting of Anagaloeng, Lambarih and Senelop (18 April 1896)]] — the eastern posts relieved, lifted and burnt ([[1896]])

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this date in [[1874]] minister Gericke van Herwijnen closed the secret Kamerzitting with 'In plaats van den aangeboden olijftak verkozen zij het zwaard', and the member [[Stieltjes]] pronounced his greatest grief: the [[First Aceh Expedition (5-29 April 1873) Kreemer's account|expedition]] begun while the army was unreplenished; see also [[Gericke|Gericke van Herwijnen]].
