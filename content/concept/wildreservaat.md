**Wildreservaat** (English: "Wildlife reserve") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Wildlife reserve; Goenoeng Löser and Kloeët reserves protect elephants, rhinoceroses, tapirs, orangutans.

## Translation

Wildlife reserve

## Description

Wildlife reserve; [[Goenoeng Löser]] and [[Kloeët]] reserves protect elephants, rhinoceroses, tapirs, orangutans.

## Significance

The term *Wildreservaat* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
