**Slabs** (English: "Wet rubber") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Nat (wet) rubber; lower price than sheets.

## Translation

Wet rubber

## Description

Nat (wet) rubber; lower price than [[Sheets|sheets]].

## Significance

The term *Slabs* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
