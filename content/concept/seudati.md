**seudati** (English: "Acehnese competitive singing performance") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Two competing groups sing to each other at festive occasions (weddings, circumcisions).

## Translation

Acehnese competitive singing performance

## Description

Two competing groups sing to each other at festive occasions (weddings, circumcisions). Each group has a leader, well-dressed boys, lead singers, followers, helpers, sometimes a master of ceremonies. Begins seated in the posture for ritual prayer, then becomes a contest of pantuns and mutual taunts, ending with both groups reciting simultaneously trying to outshout each other.

## Significance

The term *seudati* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
