The **Tasman** was the vessel on board which members of the deputation travelling from [[Java]] to [[Koeta Radja]] for the [[Uitreiking van het vaandel aan het Korps Maréchaussée (2 April 1930)|presentation of the colour]] to the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* made the passage in [[1930]].

## The beer on board

The ship is named in the [[1930]] jubilee number in an incidental but characteristic passage. During the interview with Colonel [[L. A. Snell]], the colonel was telephoned by an old comrade who was to make the journey to Koeta Radja with him, and the interviewer heard him say: "Zorg er voor, dat er aan boord van de Tasman een voldoende voorraad ijskoud bier aanwezig is, want een goed Maréchaussée heeft een gezonden dorst!" — see to it that there is a sufficient supply of ice-cold beer on board the Tasman, for a good Maréchaussée has a healthy thirst.

The interviewer offered the exchange as "een trekje van de levensblijheid van den man, die zoo vaak den dood is tegemoet getreden" — a trait of the cheerfulness of the man who had so often gone to meet death.
