**[[25 June]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]* it appears in the articles listed below.

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this date in [[1883]] the Dutch [[Murder of the postholder at Anak Paja (25 June 1883)|postholder of Anak Paja]] was treacherously murdered with eight pradjoerits — four days after the telegram that Groot-Atjeh was almost cleansed of enemies.
