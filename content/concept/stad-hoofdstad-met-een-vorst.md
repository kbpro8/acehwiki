**stad / hoofdstad met een vorst** (English: "city / capital with a prince") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. New form of societal organization that came with Hindu contact: a 'city' centered on a vorst's fortified residence, with market and trade quarter, drawing surrounding population.

## Translation

city / capital with a prince

## Description

New form of societal organization that came with Hindu contact: a 'city' centered on a vorst's fortified residence, with market and trade quarter, drawing surrounding population. The first transition to a different organization and social structure.

## Significance

The term *stad / hoofdstad met een vorst* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
