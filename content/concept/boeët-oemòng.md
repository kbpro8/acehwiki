The **boeët oemòng** was the Atjehnese land rent (*landrente*) levied within the *[[Gampong|gampong]]* boundaries on wet rice fields (*[[Oemong|oemòng]]*), assessed on the yield at the rate of one *[[Naleh|naleh]]* of *padi* per *naleh* of *[[Bibit|bidjeh]]* (seed rice) — that is, according to [[D. W. N. de Boer]], approximately two to three per cent.

## Position in the Atjehnese tax system

The *boeët oemòng* was one of the taxes levied by the *[[Oeleebalang|oeléëbalangs]]* in the period when they were still sovereigns in their own territories. De Boer distinguished two categories: within the *gampong* boundaries, the *boeët oemòng* on wet rice fields; and outside the *gampong*, the *[[Adat glé|adat glé]]*, comprising an identical levy on wet rice fields, the *[[Padi lintjah|padi lintjah]]* or *padi tadjoë* on dry rice fields (*[[Ladang|ladang]]*), and the *[[Thiploeëh-tha|thiploeëh-tha]]* or tithe on gardens.

The existence of these revenues explains the *oeléëbalang*'s reluctance to burden clearance with heavy initial charges: he preferred to defer the *[[Haq radja taléë|haq radja taléë]]* until after the first harvest rather than risk the abandonment of a clearance and the loss of the annual emoluments that permanent settlement would bring.

## Abolition

All these levies — which, De Boer noted, left much room for arbitrariness — were brought to an end by the *zelfbestuursordonnantie* (self-government ordinance) [[Staatsblad 1919 No. 822]], article 15 sub I, the *oeléëbalang* receiving by way of recompense a fixed *[[Civiele lijst|civiele lijst]]* (civil list) calculated separately for each of them.

## Evidentiary use

Even after abolition the memory of the *boeët oemòng* and *adat glé* assessments retained practical importance: in the *[[Zelfbesturende landschappen|self-governing territories]]* it was largely through these former levies, together with the measuring-out under the *haq radja taléë* and the continuing collection of *[[Wathe|wathé]]*, that the *radja* or his deputy knew whether land applied for by a clearer still had an owner.
