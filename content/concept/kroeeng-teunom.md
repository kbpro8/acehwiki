**The Kroeëng Teunom** is the Westkust van Atjeh river formed by the confluence of the [[Geumpang|Geumpang]] and [[Tangsé|Tangsé]] rivers — both of which belong to the *groote Westelijke lengtedal* of Sumatra — and the last Westkust river to break through the first or Westelijke Barisan coulisse. In the [[De Buitenbezittingen, Deel II, Aflevering 1|Encyclopaedisch Bureau's Sumatra survey]] (1917), the Kroeëng Teunom is the hydrographical link between the *binnenland* *bivaks* of the [[4de (IVde) Divisie Marechaussee|IVde Divisie Maréchaussee]] at Geumpang and Tangsé and the Westkust *kustvlakte*.

## Course

The Bureau's Hoofdstuk I describes the Kroeëng Teunom as "welke ontstaat uit de Geumpang en Tangsé rivieren, welker beide dalen weer behoren tot het groote Westelijke lengtedal." The Kroeëng Teunom is thus the outflow of the *groote Westelijke lengtedal* through the Westelijke Barisan coulisse — the *laatste rivier ter Westkust, die de eerste of Westelijke Barisan coulisse doorbreekt.* North of the Kroeëng Teunom's breakthrough, the Westelijke lengtedal's outflow is carried by the [[Atjehrivier|Atjehrivier]] to the sea at [[Oelèë Lheuë|Oelèë Lheuë]].

## Significance

The Kroeëng Teunom is, in the Bureau's Sumatra survey, the last Westkust river to break through the Westelijke Barisan coulisse, and the hydrographical link between the *binnenland* *bivaks* of the IVde Divisie Maréchaussee and the Westkust *kustvlakte*. The Bureau's emphasis on the Kroeëng Teunom's *doorbreking* of the Westelijke Barisan — and on its composition from the Geumpang and Tangsé rivers — frames the Kroeëng Teunom as the geographical foundation of the Dutch colonial state's central-Atjeh guerrilla plan: the river whose *binnenland* tributaries (the Geumpang and Tangsé) sustained the IVde Divisie's *bivaks*, and whose *doorbreking* of the Westelijke Barisan defined the strategic choke-point of the planned central-Atjeh guerrilla against a Japanese landing at Kotaradja.

In the [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|staff history's]] Atjeh chapter, the Kroeëng Teunom's *binnenland* tributaries — the Geumpang and Tangsé — are the site of Lt [[L. C. G. F. Onvlee|Onvlee]]'s [[safeguarding of the vaandel of the Korps Maréchaussee|safeguarding of the vaandel of the Korps Maréchaussee]] and of the [[Poort stelling (Tangse)|Poort stelling]] capitulation of [[18 March]] [[18 March 1942|18 March 1942]].
