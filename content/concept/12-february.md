## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this date in [[1874]] the opperbevelhebber [[Jan van Swieten|van Swieten]], against his own journal's confession of losses (4 February), passed over to the [[Annexation proclamation of Atjeh (1874)|annexation of Groot-Atjeh]] ('the skin of the bear sold before the bear was shot'); on this date in [[1885]] the series of evacuations of the posts [[Boekit Seboen]] to [[Pakan Badak]] began in the completion of the concentration (see [[Completion of the concentrated position (March 1885)]]).
