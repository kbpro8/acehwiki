**[[26 January]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]* it appears in the articles listed below.

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

Bijlage A, B, C and the lecture itself of [[Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk in zake het Atjeh-vraagstuk (Verstege, 1886)|Verstege's pamphlet]] were delivered at the [[Kiesvereeniging Burgerpligt|Burgerpligt]] meeting at Amsterdam on this date in [[1886]], introduced by its president [[R. van de Werk]] with the words that the Atjeh war, then thirteen years old, had cost 'so many human lives and millions upon millions of money'; see also [[J. J. W. E. Verstege]], [[M. T. H. Perelaer]], [[C. Bosscher]].
