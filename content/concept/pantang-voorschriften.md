**pantang-voorschriften** (English: "taboo prohibitions (during pregnancy)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Numerous prohibitions imposed on a pregnant woman and her husband to protect the unborn child.

## Translation

[[Pantang|taboo]] prohibitions (during pregnancy)

## Description

Numerous prohibitions imposed on a pregnant woman and her husband to protect the unborn child. E.g., the woman may not tie anything around her neck (lest the child be born with the umbilical cord around its neck), nor repair torn clothing (would obstruct delivery), nor cut cloth or split [[Pinangnut (Betel Nut)|pinang]] nuts (would cause harelip), nor bathe in the evening (would hinder delivery). The husband may not kill an animal, twist rope, or hammer in a nail/spike during the pregnancy.

## Significance

The term *pantang-voorschriften* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
