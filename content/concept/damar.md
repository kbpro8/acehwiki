**Damar** (English: "Damar (resin)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Resin collected from forests; 1937 yield 292,050 KG (Huissoort = ordinary type).

## Translation

Damar ([[Resin (Aceh)|resin]])

## Description

[[Resin (Aceh)|Resin]] collected from forests; [[1937]] yield 292,050 KG (Huissoort = ordinary type).

## Significance

The term *Damar* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
