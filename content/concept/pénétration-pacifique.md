**Pénétration pacifique** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Marshal Lyautey's quoted doctrine: 'Wegenaanleg is de ware pénétration pacifique' - road construction is the true peaceful penetration.

## Description

Marshal Lyautey's quoted doctrine: 'Wegenaanleg is de ware pénétration pacifique' - road construction is the true peaceful penetration. Where the road begins, hostilities cease; the population's interests are so served that they have too much to lose to cause serious difficulties.

## Significance

The term *Pénétration pacifique* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
