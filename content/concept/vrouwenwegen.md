**vrouwenwegen** (English: "women's paths (old Gayo footpaths)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Old footpaths to Gajo-land, so difficult that the Bireuën-Meureudu connection was called 'de weg van een vrouw, die water gaat scheppen' (the way of a woman going to draw water).

## Translation

women's paths (old [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gayo]] footpaths)

## Description

Old footpaths to [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]]-land, so difficult that the [[Bireuën]]-[[Meureudu]] connection was called 'de weg van een vrouw, die water gaat scheppen' (the way of a woman going to draw water). [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]]'s '[[Snouck Hurgronje's Het Gajoland en zijne bewoners (1903)|Het Gajoland en zijne bewoners]]' enumerates dozens of these women's paths. Replaced by the [[1913]] [[Gajoweg (Gayo Road)|Gajoweg]].

## Significance

The term *vrouwenwegen* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
