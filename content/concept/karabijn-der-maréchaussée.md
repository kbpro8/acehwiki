The **karabijn der Maréchaussée** was the carbine with which the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* in [[Atjeh]] was armed — first the *Beaumont-karabijn* and later the *Mannlicher* — and which, with the *[[Klewang|klewang]]*, constituted the corps's characteristically light armament.

## The two patterns

The [[1930]] jubilee number records that the *maréchaussées* "waren bewapend met een karabijn, eerst de Beaumont-karabijn en later de Mannlicher, en het specifieke wapen van den Atjeher, een klewang, waarmede hij thans zelf werd bestreden" — were armed with a carbine, first the Beaumont carbine and later the Mannlicher, and with the specific weapon of the Atjehnese, a klewang, with which he was now himself fought.

The *[[Beaumont-geweer|Beaumont]]* was the older Netherlands service pattern; the *Mannlicher* the magazine rifle that succeeded it, the carbine version of which was carried by the corps.

## Tactical importance

The light armament was one of the reasons for the corps's mobility. The jubilee account observes that the *maréchaussées* "verplaatsten zich in hun gehardheid en ook door hun lichte bewapening gemakkelijk" — moved easily by reason of their hardiness and also of their light armament — which fitted them for the *[[Patrouille (Maréchaussée)|patrouille]]* work of pursuing the resistance into the interior.

An instance of the carbine's use under extreme conditions is recorded from the [[Gevecht waarin kapitein Paris sneuvelde (Westkust van Atjeh, 1925-1927)|Westkust engagement]] in which Captain [[J. Paris]] fell: the *maréchaussée*-soldier [[Leleng]], having lost the use of his left hand through a wound, took part in the firing into the *[[Alang-alang|alang-alang]]* by placing his carbine between his knees and discharging it with his right hand.
