**restrictie-bepalingen** (English: "restriction regulations (quota)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Production-quota regulations imposed on rubber (1934), tea, and other cultures during the depression, limiting output to support prices.

## Translation

restriction regulations (quota)

## Description

Production-quota regulations imposed on rubber ([[1934]]), tea, and other cultures during the depression, limiting output to support prices.

## Significance

The term *restrictie-bepalingen* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
