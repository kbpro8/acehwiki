**repa / repana / dab** (English: "tambourines (small, large, and thin-wood types)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Repa = small tambourine played with one hand.

## Translation

tambourines (small, large, and thin-wood types)

## Description

Repa = small tambourine played with one hand. Repana = large tambourine played with both hands. Dab = small thin-wood tambourine. Acehnese also use copper slagbekkens (imported).

## Significance

The term *repa / repana / dab* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
