**Hok gantjing** was the Acehnese term — given in parentheses as *(Hok gantjing)* in [[W. Cool]] and [[G. B. Hooijer]]'s *[[Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis (Cool & Hooijer, 1889)|Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis]]* ([[1889]]) — for the *waarborgsom*, the surety-money imposed by the Dutch Militaire Bevelhebber upon a submitted district of [[Groot-Atjeh]] in [[1879]] in lieu of punitive destruction: the payment of a bond for every house, as the guarantee of the district's good behaviour.

## The rate of 1879

The institution is described in the narrative of the [[Occupation of the Lamtoba valley (July–August 1879)|occupation of the Lamtoba valley]]. In the first days of the occupation the column-commandant had announced that the Militaire Bevelhebber, highly angered at the reception given to the chiefs of the [[XXVI Moekims]], had ordered all the kampongs of the valley to be burnt. In view, however, of the peaceful submission of the population, word came in the following days from the Militaire Bevelhebber that the originally fixed punishment was abandoned, and that in its place was prescribed the payment of a surety-money — *de betaling eener waarborgsom (Hok gantjing)* — fixed at ten dollars for every wooden house and three dollars for every bamboo house. The same scale was prescribed, in the [[March order for the Selimoen expedition (11 August 1879)|march order of 11 August 1879]], for the district of [[Selimoen]] upon its submission — together with the taking of five hostages.

## Enforcement

In the Lamtoba valley the bond proved beyond the chiefs' means: at the conference held by Overste [[Majoor Gerlach|Gerlach]] with all the heads of the valley on [[21 August]] [[1879]], they declared themselves unable to pay the fixed sureties, the more so since the population had not yet wholly returned to the kampongs. The alternative laid down was the taking of hostages: the [[Imam of Lamtoba|Imam]] and the [[Panglima Banten|Rijksbestierder]], with half of the wakils and kampong-heads, had to follow the column to [[Kota Radja]] — the decision from which the whole hostage affair of the [[Evacuation of the Lamtoba valley (21–23 August 1879)|evacuation]] unfolded, including the flight and voluntary return of Panglima Banten.

## Significance

The hok gantjing belongs to the system by which Generaal [[General van der Heijden|van der Heijden]]'s government converted the conquest of [[1879]] into a civil order: graded money-bonds, hostage-taking in default, and the sparing of kampongs that answered — the counterpart of the *[[Tuchtiging|tuchtiging]]* by fire applied to [[Kepaja Doewa]] and [[Lam-Lepong]].
