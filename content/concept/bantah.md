**Bantah** is the Acehnese noun meaning “resistance” or “opposition”. It is listed on [[80]] of the source, glossed as “per an verzet (A. djodal)”. The term is employed in classical Acehnese texts to describe the act of defying an authority or opposing a claim, often in a moral or religious sense.

In the *Burda* of al‑Bu‑s‑iri, *bantah* appears in verses that exhort believers to resist sin and falsehood, e.g., “bantah terhadap kebatilan” (“resistance against falsehood”).

## Semantic field

*Bantah* is related to other moral concepts such as *bela* (defence) and *bela‑diri* (self‑defence). It also contrasts with *taat* (obedience).
