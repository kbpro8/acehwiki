**A. tadjim** was an Acehnese‑Malay term recorded on page 92 of the *Malay translation of the Burda*, glossed as “tiada akan ber‑ , zal geen geluid geven” (to be silent, to give no sound). The word derives from the Arabic **tadjīm** (تَدْجِيم) meaning “silencing”. In Acehnese devotional contexts it signified the virtue of humility and restraint.

## Usage

The term appears in admonitory verses urging believers to refrain from idle talk.
