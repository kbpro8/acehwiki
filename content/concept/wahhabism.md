**Wahhabism** is a reformist Islamic movement founded by [[Ibn Taimiyya]] (d. 1328) that emphasizes a strict monotheism and rejects practices it deems *shirk* (associating partners with God). The source discusses Wahhabi criticism of the *Burda* poem, arguing that certain verses (e.g., 147‑149, 153‑155) exceed permissible veneration of the Prophet. In the 19th century, Wahhabi ideas spread to the Sultanate of [[Acheh]], influencing local debates over the permissibility of the *Burda* as an amulet.
