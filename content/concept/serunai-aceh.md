**Serunai** (also *seruné*, *serune*, *sarunai*; Dutch: *obo-achtig instrument* — "oboe-like instrument") is a **double-reed conical-bore aerophone** of the **shawm family**, indigenous to **[[Acheh]]** (Atjēh), northern Sumatra. It is the **principal melodic instrument** for outdoor, processional, and ceremonial music in Aceh, and one of the **"typisch Mohammedaansche speeltuigen"** ("typically Mohammedan instruments") identified by **[[Jaap Kunst]]** for Atjēh in *[[Muziek en Dans in de Buitengewesten]]* ([[1946]], p. 10), alongside the *[[Gambus (Aceh)|gambus]]*, *[[Rabana (Aceh)|rabana]]*, *[[Marwas]]*, and *[[Bangei]]*.

## Organology

- **Hornbostel-Sachs classification**: 422.112 (conical bore, double reed, with fingerholes)
- **Body**: conical wooden tube, typically **jackfruit** (*nangka* / *Artocarpus heterophyllus*), **rosewood** (*sonokeling* / *Dalbergia latifolia*), **ebony** (*kayu hitam* / *Diospyros*), or **teak** (*jati* / *Tectona grandis*); length 30–45 cm
- **Bell**: flared wooden or metal (brass, copper) bell (*terompet*), often detachable
- **Reed**: **double reed** (*pelipit* / *daun*) made from **palm leaf** (*daun enau* / *Arenga pinnata*), **bamboo skin** (*kulit bambu*), or **metal** (brass, silver); tied to a **metal staple** (*stapel* / *kancing*) inserted into the pirouette
- **Pirouette** (*pelipit* / *tempat pelipit*): metal or wooden disc against which the player's lips rest, enabling circular breathing
- **Fingerholes**: **7 fingerholes** (6 front, 1 thumbhole) + 1–2 vent holes near bell
- **Tuning**: approximately **diatonic / pelog-like** scale; keynote around **D4–G4** (variable)
- **Playing technique**: **circular breathing** (*napas berputar*) essential for continuous sound; high pressure, loud, penetrating timbre
- **Range**: approximately **1.5 octaves** (with overblowing)
- **Ornamentation**: rapid tonguing (*ceplos*), grace notes (*gesek*), vibrato (*getar*), glissando (*geser*)

## Historical and Regional Context

The *serunai* belongs to the **widespread shawm family** that spread across the **Islamic world** from **Central Asia** (*surnay*, *zurna*, *sorna*, *suona*) to **North Africa** (*ghaita*, *rhaita*), **Anatolia** (*zurna*), the **Balkans** (*zurla*), **South Asia** (*shehnai*, *nadaswaram*), and **Southeast Asia** (*serunai*, *serune*, *sarunai*, *saronai*).

The **Acehnese *serunai*** is most closely related to the **Minangkabau *serunai*** and **Malay *serunai***, sharing the **palm-leaf double reed** and **detachable metal bell** — features distinguishing it from the Javanese *saronen* (which often uses a *pirouette* without a detachable bell) and the Indian *shehnai* (which has a wider bore and more holes).

## Role in Acehnese Music

The *serunai* is **indispensable** for:

### 1. [[Seudati]] (Men's Seated Dance-Song)
- **Lead melodic instrument** — plays the *lagu* (melody) while *[[Rabana (Aceh)|rabana]]* and *[[Marwas]]* provide rhythm
- Repertoire: *Seudati Agam* (men's), *Seudati Inong* (women's — *serunai* sometimes replaced by *[[Suling (Aceh)|suling]]*)
- Performance: outdoor, competitive, processional

### 2. [[Sedati (dance)|Sedati]] (Seated Dance Around a Boy)
- Described by **Kunst (1946, p. 10)**: *"lenige, zich om een kleinen jongen centreerende, door een groot aantal jongelieden tegelijk uitgevoerde sedati-dansspel"*
- *Serunai* leads the ensemble (*serunai*, *rabana*, *marwas*, *geudoemba*)

### 3. [[Saman]] (Dance of a Thousand Hands)
- *Serunai* plays **melodic introduction** (*buka*) and **interludes** between *tak* cycles
- In **Gayo Lands** (*[[Gayo Lands]]*), *serunai* sometimes replaced by *[[Suling (Gayo)|suling]]* for indoor performances

### 4. [[Meuseukat]] (Women's Seated Dance-Song)
- *Serunai* provides melodic counterpoint to vocal lines
- Softer playing style, often with *suling* doubling

### 5. [[Zikir]] / [[Dzikir]] / [[Hadrah]] (Sufi Liturgical Processions)
- *Serunai* plays *qasidah* melodies during *maulid* processions
- Circular breathing allows unbroken sound for *dhikr* cycles

### 6. [[Pekan]] / [[Kanduri|Kenduri]] / [[Maulid]] (Feasts & Celebrations)
- Processional music from *meunasah* (prayer hall) to host's house
- *Serunai* + *rabana* + *marwas* = core processional trio

### 7. [[Pencak Silat]] (Martial Arts) Accompaniment
- *Serunai* drives the tempo for *silat* demonstrations
- Rhythmic melodic patterns (*gending*) match *jurus* (movement sequences)

## Construction (Traditional Acehnese)

| Component | Material | Notes |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| Body | *Nangka* (jackfruit), *Sonokeling* (rosewood), *Kayu hitam* (ebony), *Jati* (teak) | Turned on lathe or hand-carved; conical bore reamed |
| Bell | Brass, copper, *nangka* wood | Detachable; flared for projection |
| Reed | *Daun enau* (sugar palm leaf), *kulit bambu* (bamboo skin), brass | Tied to staple with *tali ijuk* (sugar palm fiber) |
| Staple | Brass, copper, silver | Conical, fits into pirouette |
| Pirouette | Brass, wood, bone | Lip rest for circular breathing |
| Fingerholes | Burned/drilled | 6 front + 1 thumb + 1–2 vents |
| Binding | *Tali ijuk*, rattan | Reinforces joints, decorative |
