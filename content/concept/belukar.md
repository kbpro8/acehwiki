**Belukar** denotes “shrub vegetation” or “undergrowth” in Acehnese. The source lists it on [[80]] with the gloss “struikgewas (A. adjam en adjām)”. The term is used in geographical descriptions and metaphors, often to signify a dense, tangled environment.

In the *Burda* translation, *belukar* appears in verses contrasting the tangled undergrowth of the world with the clarity of divine guidance.
