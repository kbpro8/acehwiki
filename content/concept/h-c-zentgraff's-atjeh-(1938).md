***Atjeh*** — H.C. Zentgraff's *Atjeh*, printed by Drukkerij "De Unie" at [[Batavia]] and dated on the title page *8 APR. 1938* — is the source-book of the present wiki. The author, [[H.C. Zentgraff|Zentgraff]], wrote it as a partial corrective to the run of Atjeh-war literature in which *heldenrollen* were reserved for the Dutch and the Acehnese reduced to *sloebers, vuilbuiken, althans gemeene verraders* — men too easily dismissed as traitors. He had, *dank zij de door het Legerbestuur welwillend verleende vergunning*, consulted the military archives, and he had *Informaties ingewonnen bij velen die in den krijg eene rol speelden*; the result is a book that deliberately grants the Acehnese enemy *à tout seigneur tout honneur*.

## Structure

The book is divided into twelve chapters, of which the page-citations of the present wiki follow the original pagination: *Inleiding*; *Van schande en vernedering*; *De Vrouwen*; *Het Adelaarsjong*; *Vergeelde brieven*; *Verhalen van de Blang*; *Van vreemde dingen*; *Hoe Tjoet Ali "Sjahid" werd*; *Van den Sjahid*; *Van sterven en begraven*; *De onbekende "Beer"*; and *Het Korps Maréchaussée*. The *Inleiding* sets the moral frame; the *Vergeelde brieven* chapter prints the [[Tiro-oelama's ultimatum letter (1885)|ultimatum letter of 1885]] and the [[Brief van 18 Radjab 1325|brief van 18 Radjab 1325]]; *Het Adelaarsjong* follows the wanderings of [[T. Radja Sabi]]; *Het Korps Maréchaussée* closes the book with the history of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|corps]] and the anticipation of the fiftieth-jubilee *Kandoeri Rajeu* of [[April]] [[1940]].

## Sources and method

Zentgraff had, by his own account, *in de militaire archieven zeer belangrijk materiaal raadplegen* — the Legerbestuur's permission giving him access to official reports otherwise closed: among them the pencil-notes of *Bivak Tamoen* of [[22 April]]–[[27 April]] [[1897]] from [[G.C.E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] to his wife, the Hoedt *dagboek* of the [[Gajoetocht (1904)|Gajoetocht]], the Mosselman patrouille-rapport on the death of [[Tjoet Meuthia]], and the Behrens report on the death of [[Tengkoe di Barat]]. He layered these with oral information from *velen die in den krijg eene rol speelden* and with the pamphlets and *hikajat prang* that circulated among the Acehnese themselves — the [[Tadkirat arrakidin|Tadkirat arrakidin]] of [[Tengkoe Koetakarang]] among them.

## The second bundel

Zentgraff projected a *tweede bundel* — a second volume — to follow this one; it never appeared. The *Atjeh* of [[1938]] therefore remains his single book on the Atjeh-oorlog, and the present wiki's source-section, on every article, cites it accordingly: *Atjeh*, door H.C. Zentgraff. Gedrukt bij Drukkerij "De Unie", [[1938]], Batavia.

## Significance

The book appeared eight years after the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Korps Maréchaussée]]'s *Kandoeri Masoesé* jubilee of [[2 April]] [[1930]] and two years before the projected fiftieth-jubilee of April 1940 — *als straks, in April 1940, het Korps Maréchaussée eene halve eeuw bestaat*. The second jubilee never came off; the Japanese invasion of March 1942 swept away the colonial world the book documented. *Atjeh* is therefore a valedictory as much as a history: the record of an institution and a war that were already passing from living memory into the archives Zentgraff had been allowed to read.
