**Snouck Hurgronje phonetic schrijfwijze** (English: "Snouck Hurgronje phonetic writing system") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. System devised by Professor Snouck Hurgronje for writing Atjehsch in Latin characters phonetically.

## Translation

[[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] phonetic writing system

## Description

System devised by Professor [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] for writing Atjehsch in Latin characters phonetically. The [[1931]] introduction of Atjehsch as a school language was intended to spread this writing system and possibly revive Atjehsch as a literature language.

## Significance

The term *Snouck Hurgronje phonetic schrijfwijze* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
