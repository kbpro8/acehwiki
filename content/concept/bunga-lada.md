**Bunga Lada** (historically spelled *boengong lada*) was a speculative agricultural futures contract where pepper planters sold unharvested future pepper crops to traders at heavily discounted fixed prices.

## Legal Status

During price collapses, planters who failed to deliver contracted volumes accumulated severe debts. Colonial authorities declared *Bunga Lada* contracts illegal (*haram*) and unenforceable in court.
