**[[23 April]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[Het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh (Jubileum-nummer 1930)|Het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh]]* it marks the following event.

## Events on this date

- The birth of [[Dersjant|L. Dersjant]], later colonel of Infantry and knight of the *[[Militaire Willemsorde|Militaire Willems-Orde]]* 4th class, who after completing the *[[Koninklijke Militaire Academie]]* was appointed second lieutenant of Infantry in [[1900]] and placed with the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* in February [[1903]] ([[1877]])
