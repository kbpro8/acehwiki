**Volksschool** (English: "People's (village primary) school") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Most elementary form of schooling for the masses: reading and writing in Latin and Arabic characters, simple arithmetic.

## Translation

People's (village primary) school

## Description

Most elementary form of schooling for the masses: reading and writing in Latin and Arabic characters, simple arithmetic. By the time of writing: 328 volksscholen, ~600 teachers, ~36,000 pupils. School fee 2.5–50 cents/month, mostly 5–10 cents. Three or four learning years. 201 of the 328 schools use Atjehsch alongside Maleisch. Alphabet percentage in [[1930]] census: 1.1% (vs 5.5% on [[Madiun and Surabaya|Java]]).

## Significance

The term *Volksschool* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
