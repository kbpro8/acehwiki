**kazematten (at Sabang)** (English: "casemates / old fortifications") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Old casemates at Sabang.

## Translation

casemates / old fortifications

## Description

Old casemates at [[Sabang]]. The drive to them passes the psychiatric hospital. From the walls, the view over the bays is wide and grand. Also: from the bays one understands that effective anti-smuggling is impossible without fast motorboats and a seaplane for the customs.

## Significance

The term *kazematten (at Sabang)* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
