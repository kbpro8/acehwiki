**Pasar / Keudé** (English: "Market") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Market; Acehnese word 'keudé' refers both to the whole complex and to individual shops.

## Translation

Market

## Description

Market; Acehnese word 'keudé' refers both to the whole complex and to individual shops. Markets have weekly market day; pasarloodsen for meat/fish/vegetables; warongs for general goods. Some landscape pasars privately owned by [[zelfbestuurder]].

## Significance

The term *Pasar / Keudé* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
