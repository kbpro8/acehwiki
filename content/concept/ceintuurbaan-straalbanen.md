**ceintuurbaan / straalbanen** (English: "Belt railway / radial railways") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Circular and radial railway lines around Koeta Radja, built in 1885 and dismantled in 1896 when the concentratiestelsel was abandoned.

## Translation

Belt railway / radial railways

## Description

Circular and radial railway lines around [[Koeta-Radja|Koeta Radja]], built in [[1885]] and dismantled in [[1896]] when the [[Concentration system (1884–1896) — Kreemer's account|concentratiestelsel]] was abandoned.

## Significance

The term *ceintuurbaan / straalbanen* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
