**[[5 January]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link.

## Events on this date

- Publication of the instalment of *[[Het recht op den grond in Atjeh (De Boer)|Het recht op den grond in Atjeh]]* containing page 65, whose running head bears the notation "1932 Januari 5" ([[1932]])

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

Opening date of the first evacuation series of the concentration in [[1885]] — [[Djerir]], Indrapoerie, [[Gle Kambing]], [[Long Lemoh]], [[Tjot Basetoel]], [[Samagani]], [[Lepong Ara]], [[Pantej Karang]] (see [[Completion of the concentrated position (March 1885)]]).
