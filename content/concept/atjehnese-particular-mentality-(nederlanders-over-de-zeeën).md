**The Atjehnese particular mentality** — the *bijzondere mentaliteit* of [[Acheh|Atjeh]] — is, in the source's narrative, the reason given by the [[Netherlands|Dutch]] for sparing [[Acheh|Atjeh]] from occupation during the [[Second politionele actie (18 December 1948)|second *politionele actie* of 18 December 1948]]. The phrase is the source's only explicit characterisation of Atjehnese particularism in the revolutionary period.

## The 1948 Atjeh exemption

The source records: "alle grote plaatsen op Java en Sumatra, behalve in Atjeh, dat men om zijn bijzondere mentaliteit voorlopig ontszag, werden bezet" (all the major places on Java and Sumatra, except in Atjeh, which was for the time being spared on account of its particular mentality, were occupied). The [[1948]] Atjeh exemption is the source's most significant Atjeh-related observation for the Indonesian revolution period. The phrase "zijn bijzondere mentaliteit" (its particular mentality) is the source's only explicit characterisation of Atjehnese particularism.

## The historical depth

The [[1948]] characterisation — the "bijzondere mentaliteit" of [[Acheh|Atjeh]] — is, in the source's wider narrative, the accumulation of three centuries of Dutch-Atjehnese interaction: the sixteenth-century rise of the great sultanate, the early-seventeenth-century elephant-fight at the court of [[Sultan Iskandar Muda|Iskandar Muda]], the [[Anglo-Dutch Treaty of 1824|1824 undertaking]]'s impossible double commitment, the [[Atjehoorlog|Atjehoorlog]] of [[1873]]-[[1904]] with the [[1896]] [[Teungku Um|Teungku Um]] defection and the [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] *pacificatie*, and the [[Korte verklaring|korte verklaring]]'s 261 submitted chiefs. The [[1948]] Dutch decision to spare Atjeh was, on this reading, the institutional acknowledgment that the "bijzondere mentaliteit" built up over three centuries could not be set aside by a single military action.

## Significance

The [[Atjehnese particular mentality (Nederlanders over de Zeeën)|Atjehnese particular mentality]] is, in the source's narrative, the reason given by the Dutch for sparing Atjeh from occupation during the [[Second politionele actie (18 December 1948)|second *politionele actie* of 18 December 1948]]. The phrase is the source's only explicit characterisation of Atjehnese particularism in the revolutionary period — and the institutional acknowledgment of the historical depth of the Dutch-Atjehnese relationship.
