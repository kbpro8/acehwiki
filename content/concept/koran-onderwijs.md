**Koran-onderwijs** (English: "Koranic education") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Elementary Islamic education provided by a godsdienstonderwijzer in every gampong to children starting at age 6–7 (often later).

## Translation

Koranic education

## Description

Elementary Islamic education provided by a godsdienstonderwijzer in every [[Gampong|gampong]] to children starting at age 6–7 (often later). Teaches Arabic script, then recitation of the Koran, basic faith doctrine, and the [[Five daily prayers (seumayangs)|five daily prayers]]. Continues until age ~14 when the pupil has recited the entire Koran once or twice. [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]]: produces only that 'adepts like parrots without understanding can recite what was revealed to the Prophet 13 centuries ago in [[Mecca]]'.

## Significance

The term *Koran-onderwijs* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
