The **Atjeh-medaille** (Atjeh medal) was a separately instituted bronze medal, struck from captured [[Achehnese|Atjehese]] cannon, awarded by the Dutch government to all military and civilian personnel who had served with the troops in [[Acheh|Atjeh]] in [[1873]] and [[1874]] or as civilian officials in those years. The medal was popularly — though wrongly — called the *Kraton-medaille* (Kraton medal). Together with the [[Eerekruis voor belangrijke krijgsverrichtingen|Eerekruis voor belangrijke krijgsverrichtingen]] (the so-called Expeditiekruis) with the clasp "Atjeh 1873–1876", the Atjeh-medaille was the principal campaign medal of the early [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Generaal Snijders]] was awarded the Atjeh-medaille and the Expeditiekruis with clasp by right of his service in [[1874]]–[[1875]]; the biographer [[D. van den Berg|Van den Berg]] records that Snijders, with Kapitein-Luitenant ter Zee J. Boom (who died in [[1942]] at the age of 95), was probably among the very last surviving holders of these honours.

## Institution and Form

The Atjeh-medaille was a separately instituted decoration — distinct from the [[Militaire Willemsorde|Militaire Willemsorde]] and the [[Eerekruis voor belangrijke krijgsverrichtingen|Eerekruis]] — and was struck in bronze from captured Atjehese cannon. The award criteria were broadly inclusive: "alle militairen en burgers, die hadden deel uitgemaakt van de, in 1873 en 1874 in Atjeh aanwezige troepen of burgerlijke staatsambtenaren."

## The Popular Name "Kraton-medaille"

The medal was popularly called the *Kraton-medaille* — a name the biographer explicitly corrects: "de Atjeh-medaille (in den volksmond ten onrechte Kraton-medaille genoemd)." The popular name derives from the [[Capture of the Kraton (24 January 1874)|capture of the Kraton]] of [[24 January]] [[24 January]] [[1874]], the central event of the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)|second Aceh expedition]] and the occasion for which the medal was popularly supposed to have been instituted. The official name remained Atjeh-medaille.

## Holders

The medal was awarded to all military and civilian personnel of the [[1873]]–[[1874]] Atjeh expeditions. [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] received the right to wear the medal by grant of [[17 March]] [[17 March]] [[1877]], together with the [[Eerekruis voor belangrijke krijgsverrichtingen|Eerekruis]] with clasp "Atjeh 1873–1876". The biographer notes that Snijders and Kapitein-Luitenant ter Zee J. Boom — who died in [[1942]] at the age of 95 — were "vermoedelijk wel tot de allerlaatste dragers" of both decorations.
