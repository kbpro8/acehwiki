**Hukum** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Islamic religious law (alongside adat).

## Description

Islamic religious law (alongside [[Adat|adat]]).

## Significance

The term *Hukum* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
