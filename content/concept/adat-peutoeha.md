**Adat Peutoeha** was the customary management commission paid to a pepper garden supervisor (*peutoeha djaga* or *peutoeha seuneubo*) for managing a plantation complex (*seunebok lada*).

## Structure

The *peutoeha* received no fixed salary, depending instead on *Adat Peutoeha* levied as a fixed sum per pikul of harvested pepper (averaging 0.40–0.50 guilders per pikul).
