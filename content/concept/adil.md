**adil** was the Acehnese and Arabic term for just or equitable, the quality [[H. N. A. Swart]] regarded as decisive for an administrator's authority.

Swart's second principle of administration was that he demanded and himself provided proper administration of justice, giving decisions as just as possible "with regard to the [[adat]]*" and always with the *[[oelama]]* behind the green table. "Die rechtspraak bracht mede dat de Atjehers den *toean* als voorzitter van den *[[Moesapat]]* *adil* ("just") (rechtvaardig) oordeelden. En wie werkelijk als adil te boek staat, kan als bestuurder veel — zoo niet alles — bereiken." [[Maurice Henri du Croo|Du Croo]]'s seventh summary proposition held that the qualities the Acehnese valued in Swart above all "were his honesty and his justice, qualities which he also demanded of his subordinates."
