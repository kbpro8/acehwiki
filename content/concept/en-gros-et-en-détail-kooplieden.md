**En gros et en détail kooplieden** (English: "Wholesale and retail merchants") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Acehnese wholesale-importers also act as middlemen for European textiles and as retailers—true 'en gros et en détail' merchants.

## Translation

Wholesale and retail merchants

## Description

Acehnese wholesale-importers also act as middlemen for European textiles and as retailers—true 'en gros et en détail' merchants.

## Significance

The term *En gros et en détail kooplieden* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
