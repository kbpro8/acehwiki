**devaluatie** (English: "devaluation") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Currency devaluation that improved prospects for rubber estates.

## Translation

devaluation

## Description

Currency devaluation that improved prospects for rubber estates.

## Significance

The term *devaluatie* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
