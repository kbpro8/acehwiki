**[[29 June]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[Het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh (Jubileum-nummer 1930)|Het Korps Maréchaussée in Atjeh]]* it marks the following events.

## Events on this date

- The [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|action at Anagaloeng]], the first engagement in which the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* took part in its entire composition — three officers and 221 non-commissioned officers, corporals and men — and in which it captured the *versterking* in a quarter of an hour ([[1896]])
