**[[16 May]]** is the date on which, according to the report in *[[De Kroniek]]* of that day cited by [[H. van Kol]] in the [[Tweede Kamer]], the Acehnese chief [[Toekoe Tjoet Machmoed Lam Tenzah]] was killed with his wife, his children and seven followers as they slept, by volleys fired through the thin walls of their dwelling.

The event of [[16 May]] [[1897]] is narrated at [[Killing of Toekoe Tjoet Machmoed Lam Tenzah (16 May 1897)]]. Van Kol adduced it in the [[Atjeh debate in the Tweede Kamer (16-17 November 1897)|Atjeh debate]] of [[16 November]] [[1897]] as evidence of the demoralisation produced by the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]], adding that he would keep silence about further abominable facts at his disposal, having enough in these for his purpose.
