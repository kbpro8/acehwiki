**Wasiët** (Arabic *waṣiyya*) was the testament or testamentary disposition in [[Aceh|Atjeh]]. Its uncertainty in the circumstances of the pilgrimage was, according to [[D. W. N. de Boer]], one of the reasons for the frequency of division of estates during the lifetime of the testator.

## Context

A man about to depart for [[Mekka]] preferred to settle his affairs beforehand in *[[Atra boeloeng|boeloeng]]*, since he feared that through sudden death or other circumstances he might not have the opportunity to draw up a proper *wasiët*, and did not wish to run the risk that a disposition made on the journey or in the Holy Land would fail to be transmitted, or would not be carried out according to his wish.
