**Landbouwklas** (English: "Agricultural class") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Sixth-class attached to vervolgscholen, offering practical and theoretical instruction in agriculture, horticulture, livestock, fishery, volkscredietwezen, etc.

## Translation

Agricultural class

## Description

Sixth-class attached to vervolgscholen, offering practical and theoretical instruction in agriculture, horticulture, livestock, fishery, volkscredietwezen, etc. First opened in [[1935]]; full success.

## Significance

The term *Landbouwklas* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
