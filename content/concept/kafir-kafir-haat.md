**kafir / kafir-haat** (English: "unbeliever / hatred of unbelievers") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Term for non-Muslims (especially Christians/Dutch).

## Translation

unbeliever / hatred of unbelievers

## Description

Term for non-Muslims (especially Christians/Dutch). [[Kafir]]-haat = hatred of unbelievers, long cultivated in regions like [[Keumala]] even after the war. Religious framing of the Aceh War as jihad against 'ongeloovige indringers' (unbelieving invaders).

## Significance

The term *kafir / kafir-haat* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
