**Kroeëng** is the [[Atjeh|Atjehsch]] (Acehnese) word for *river*, glossed by the [[De Buitenbezittingen, Deel II, Aflevering 1|Encyclopaedisch Bureau's Sumatra survey]] (1917) in its hydrographical chapter as one of four cognate regional terms for "river" in northern Sumatra: "*Kroeëng* (Atjehsch.) = *Woi* (Gajo'sch.) = *Lae* (Dairisch) = *Lao* (Karo'sch.) = rivier." The *Kroeëng* toponym is the principal hydrographical marker of the Atjeh region in Dutch colonial writing, and the principal linguistic evidence of the Atjehnese onomastic landscape of the Westkust and the *binnenland*.

## The toponymic pattern

The Bureau's Hoofdstuk I glosses the *Kroeëng* toponym in a footnote to its description of the *groote Westelijke lengtedal* of Sumatra. The gloss is given as part of the explanation of the toponymy of the northern Sumatran rivers — the *Kroeëng Wojla*, *Kroeëng Teunom*, *Kroeëng Seunagan*, *Kroeëng Meureubo*, *Kroeëng Keureutë*, and so on — that drain the Westelijke Barisanketenen of Atjeh to the Indian Ocean. The *Kroeëng* toponym is thus the principal hydrographical marker of the Westkust van Atjeh and of the *binnenland* of Groot-Atjeh.

The cognate regional terms — *Woi* (Gajo), *Lae* (Dairisch), *Lao* (Karo'sch) — mark the linguistic frontier of the Atjehnese *Kroeëng* toponym: *Woi* characterises the Gajo and Alas rivers of the central-Atjeh hoogvlakte (e.g. the [[Lao Alas|Woi ni Agoesein]]), while *Lae* and *Lao* characterise the Batak rivers of the south (e.g. the [[Lae Renoen|Lae Renoen]]). The *Kroeëng* toponym thus marks the northern limit of the Gajo–Batak hydrographical frontier, and the southern limit of the Atjehnese onomastic landscape.

## Significance

The *Kroeëng* toponym is, in the Bureau's Sumatra survey, the principal hydrographical marker of the Atjeh region in Dutch colonial writing. The Bureau's gloss of the cognate regional terms — *Kroeëng* = *Woi* = *Lae* = *Lao* = *rivier* — is the staff history's principal linguistic evidence of the Atjehnese onomastic landscape, and the principal evidence of the demographic frontier between the Atjehnese, Gajo, and Batak populations of northern Sumatra. The *Kroeëng* toponym is thus both a hydrographical and a linguistic marker: it identifies a river as Atjehnese, and it locates that river within the demographic frontier of the Atjehnese *gewest*.
