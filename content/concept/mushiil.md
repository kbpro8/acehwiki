**mushiil** designates something “ambiguous” or “unclear” in Acehnese. The term is listed on page [[88]] of the *Burda* translation, paired with the Arabic *shubah* (“doubtful”).

## Definition
The word conveys the notion of uncertainty, often employed in theological debates to describe verses with multiple possible interpretations.

## Example
In the glossary, *mushiil* is contrasted with *mustakim* (“straight, correct”), highlighting a moral dichotomy in Acehnese religious discourse.

## Significance
The term illustrates how Arabic epistemological categories were incorporated into Acehnese scholastic vocabulary.
