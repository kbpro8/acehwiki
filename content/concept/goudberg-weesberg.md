**Goudberg / Weesberg** (English: "Gold Mountain / Orphan Mountain") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Two named mountains near Sigli.

## Translation

Gold Mountain / [[Weesberg|Orphan Mountain]]

## Description

Two named mountains near [[Sigli]]. The watershed between them is a barren [[Blang|blang]]. The road encircles [[Goudberg]] partly, then descends along [[Weesberg]] slopes to the coastal plain.

## Significance

The term *Goudberg / Weesberg* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
