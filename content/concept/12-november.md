**[[12 November]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]* it marks the following events in the history of [[Groot-Atjeh]].

## Events on this date

- [[Evacuation of the bentengs at Hoengtjala (12 November 1893)]] — the enemy abandoned [[Hoengtjala]], which was then sacked ([[1893]])
