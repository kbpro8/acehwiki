**erfpacht** (English: "leasehold (long-term land lease)[[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Long-term lease of land from the state for agricultural exploitation.

## Translation

leasehold (long-term land lease)

## Description

Long-term lease of land from the state for agricultural exploitation. After 1 Feb [[1920]], issuance delegated to the [[Zelfbestuur]]Leasehold system; early European coconut estates on erfpachtperceelen around[[Koeta-Radja|Koeta Radja]] and [[Poelau Weh]], later [[Westkust]].

## Significance

The term *erfpacht* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
