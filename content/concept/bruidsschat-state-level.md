**bruidsschat (state-level)** (English: "bride wealth (state-level)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Conceptual bride wealth paid by a new Sultan as 'bridegroom' to 'marry' the realm/nation (conceived as the 'bride').

## Translation

bride wealth (state-level)

## Description

Conceptual bride wealth paid by a new [[sultan|Sultan]] as 'bridegroom' to 'marry' the realm/nation (conceived as the 'bride'). Reconciled the kingly institution with the Atjehnese legal custom of bride-price. Persisted until the Dutch conquest.

## Significance

The term *bruidsschat (state-level)* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
