**The Atjehsche producten** ("Atjehnese products") were the export products of the [[Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh and Dependencies]] residency — principally pepper and rubber — whose steadily increasing production, especially with the reclamation of the [[Tamiang|Tamiangsche moerassen (Tamiang swamps)]], was the economic rationale of the [[Aroe-baai-plannen|Aroe-baai-plannen of 1913]] to develop [[Aroebai|Arube Bay]] as a deep-water harbour. In the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, the Atjehsche producten were the trade that the Aroe-baai-plannen were designed to divert from [[Belawan]] and [[Sabang]] to [[Aroebai|Arube Bay]] — and, in the most ambitious version of the plans, to develop into a deep-water export trade that would compete with [[Singapore]].

## Account from *De Buitenbezittingen* (1917) — Atjehsche producten

The Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account of the [[Aroe-baai-plannen|Aroe-baai-plannen]] of [[1913]] explicitly identifies the Atjehsche producten as the trade that the plans were designed to divert. The Bureau writes: "the defenders of the [[Aroe-baai-plannen|Aroe-baai-plannen]], who see in this natural harbour [of [[Aroebai|Arube Bay]]] a competitor of [[Belawan]] and even of [[Singapore]] and [[Sabang]], hope, by the laying of this connecting link, to see the first step taken toward the realisation of those future plans, by the export of the Atjehsche producten (pepper, rubber, etc.) — which increases steadily with the reclamation of the [[Tamiang|Tamiangsche moerassen]] — to be led via [[Aroebai|Arube Bay]]."

The Bureau's account of the [[Landschapskassen|landschapskassen]] likewise identifies pepper as one of the principal miscellaneous revenues of the Atjeh landschapskas: "[[1914|Very important]] are, among others, the peper- en pinanghasil in Atjeh (in [[1913|1913]]: f 125,000 and f 55,000)." The pepper that yielded f 125,000 to the landschapskas in [[1913]] was thus the same pepper that the Aroe-baai-plannen were designed to divert through [[Aroebai|Arube Bay]]. The Bureau's account of the rubber cultivation in the Buitenbezittingen — which expanded rapidly in the [[1910s]] in response to the "rubberboom" of [[1909]] — likewise identifies Atjeh as one of the principal sites of rubber cultivation, especially with the reclamation of the Tamiang swamps.

The Atjehsche producten thus occupy a double place in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account: as the principal miscellaneous revenue of the Atjeh landschapskas (pepper, pinang), and as the export trade that the [[Aroe-baai-plannen|Aroe-baai-plannen]] of [[1913]] were designed to divert from [[Belawan]] and [[Sabang]] to [[Aroebai|Arube Bay]]. The two roles are connected: the same pepper and rubber that yielded f 125,000 and f 55,000 to the landschapskas were the pepper and rubber whose steadily increasing production — especially with the reclamation of the Tamiang swamps — was the economic rationale of the Aroe-baai-plannen.

## Significance

[[Atjehsche producten|The Atjehsche producten]] are, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, the principal export trade of the [[Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh and Dependencies]] residency — the trade whose steadily increasing production was the economic rationale of the [[Aroe-baai-plannen|Aroe-baai-plannen of 1913]]. The double role of the Atjehsche producten — as the principal miscellaneous revenue of the Atjeh landschapskas, and as the export trade that the Aroe-baai-plannen were designed to divert through [[Aroebai|Arube Bay]] — places them at the intersection of the financial and the infrastructural projects of the [[1904]]–[[1914]] decade in Atjeh.
