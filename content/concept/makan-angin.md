**makan angin** (English: "eating the wind (evening stroll)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Traditional evening outing/stroll.

## Translation

eating the wind (evening stroll)

## Description

Traditional evening outing/stroll. Recently practiced on moonlit evenings on the new 'platje' (platform) at [[Oelèë Lheuë]].

## Significance

The term *makan angin* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
