**optieperceel** (English: "option plot") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Plots on which preferential rights (voorkeurs-rechten) are established in connection with ongoing explorations.

## Translation

option plot

## Description

Plots on which preferential rights (voorkeurs-rechten) are established in connection with ongoing explorations. 13 perceelen totaling 287,000 H.A. as of end [[1937]].

## Significance

The term *optieperceel* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
