**[[20-february|20 February]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. It appears in articles drawn from Mr. P. Brooshooft's *Geschiedenis van den Atjeh-oorlog 1873–1886* (Utrecht: F. B. van Ditmar, 1886) and from other Aceh War sources.
