**kraamroostering** (English: "post-partum roasting") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Practice of lying the new mother on a rustbank with the oven stoked beneath/next to her for 1–2 weeks to dry her out and return her body to its former state.

## Translation

post-partum roasting

## Description

Practice of lying the new mother on a rustbank with the oven stoked beneath/next to her for 1–2 weeks to dry her out and return her body to its former state. Sometimes sulfur, old rags, horn pieces, onions and other foul-smelling things are thrown on the fire to keep spirits/kraamduivelin away. The mother is also seated on warm ash covered with cloths as a variant. Done with considerable suffering.

## Significance

The term *kraamroostering* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
