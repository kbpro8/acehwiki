***Tjekkoh*** was the tree lizard whose call punctuated the tropical night in [[Acheh|Atjeh]], glossed by [[P. H. R. Beuming]] as *een boomhagedis, die duidelijk roept: "tjekkoh"* — a tree lizard that calls distinctly *tjekkoh*.

Beuming names it in his account of the night ambush before [[Biloel]]: as the patrol lay behind the *[[Sawah|sawah]]* dyke waiting for dawn, *slechts het eentonige geluid der krekels en het geroep van den tjekkoh verbrak de stilte van den tropischen nacht* — only the monotonous sound of the crickets and the call of the *tjekkoh* broke the stillness of the tropical night. The great marsh mosquitoes attacked the men violently, but the Indies soldier regarded these plagues as a necessary evil.
