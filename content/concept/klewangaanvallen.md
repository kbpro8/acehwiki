**klewangaanvallen** (English: "Klewang attacks (sword attacks)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Sword attacks by Acehnese fighters on railway repair parties; repairs often had to be done under fire at risk of such attacks.

## Translation

[[Klewang]] attacks (sword attacks)

## Description

Sword attacks by Acehnese fighters on railway repair parties; repairs often had to be done under fire at risk of such attacks.

## Significance

The term *klewangaanvallen* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
