**Oelèë Lheuë** — modern *Ulee Lheue* — is the harbour at the mouth of the [[Atjehrivier|Atjehrivier]], immediately west of [[Koeta Radja|Koeta Radja]], at which the Atjehrivier reaches the Indian Ocean after its course through the *vruchtbare vlakte van Groot-Atjeh*. In the [[De Buitenbezittingen, Deel II, Aflevering 1|Encyclopaedisch Bureau's Sumatra survey]] (1917), Oelèë Lheuë is the hydrographical outflow of the Atjehrivier and the principal haven of the colonial capital of the *gewest* Atjeh.

## Hydrography


The Bureau's Hoofdstuk I describes the outflow of the Atjehrivier at Oelèë Lheuë as follows: "Het Noordelijkste deel van het Westelijke lengtedal behoort geheel tot het stroomgebied van de Atjehrivier, welke tusschen de beide Westelijke Barisanketenen, door de vruchtbare vlakte van Groot-Atjeh, haar weg vindt naar zee om langs Koeta Radja bij Oelèë Lheuë den Indischen Oceaan te bereiken." Oelèë Lheuë is thus the *terminus* of the Atjehrivier's course through the Groot-Atjeh *kustvlakte*.

## Significance


Oelèë Lheuë is, in the Bureau's Sumatra survey, the hydrographical outflow of the Atjehrivier and the principal haven of the colonial capital. The Bureau's emphasis on the haven's situation at the mouth of the Atjehrivier — and on its proximity to Koeta Radja — frames Oelèë Lheuë as the principal Dutch haven of the Atjeh coast, and as the principal logistical connection between the colonial capital and the Indian Ocean shipping routes.

In the [[De Kleine Oosterling|school-textbook]] account of the [[Building of Oleh-leh harbor|building of the haven of Oleh-leh]] (the Dutch name for Oelèë Lheuë) and the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh spoorweg]] from Oleh-leh to [[Kotta-Radja|Kotta-Radja]], Oelèë Lheuë is the principal Dutch-built haven of the Atjeh coast, and the principal logistical connection between the colonial capital and the Indian Ocean shipping routes.

## Account from *Verkenningen in Atjeh* (1923)

**Oelèë Lheue** — vulgarly *Oeleleh*, modern *Ulee Lheue* — is the harbour-place (*havenplaatsje*) of [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]], situated on the western side of the *afdeeling* [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]], and the southern terminus of the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]] line, which runs from Oelèë Lheuë to [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]]. The first railway traffic in Atjeh ran between Kota Radja and Oelèë Lheue.

### The Harbour


Oelèë Lheuë receives monthly on the roadstead about fifteen ships of the [[Kon. Paketvaart|Kon. Paketvaart]], which unload into the praows of a Chinese entrepreneur or into those of the [[Atjeh Transport-Mij|Atjeh Transport-Mij]], which is a subsidiary of the [[Sabang-Mij|Sabang-Mij]]. That company has besides a small steamer which calls at the trading-pier at Oeleleh to bring or fetch goods and passengers. The principal export figures are, in 1918 and 1921: pepper 316.660 and 371.270 kg; pinang-nuts 529.050 and 244.744 kg; coprah 77.376 and 375.361 kg.

### Decline


Oelèë Lheuë has however not progressed in importance, for formerly it was actually the only harbour for Atjeh, while afterwards, after the administrative settlements on other coastal places, shipping also directed itself there. There is now only one government steamer; formerly there were more.

### The Bathing-Place


The *gemeentefonds* of Kota Radja manages a bathing-place at Oelèë Lheuë. The properly arranged swimming-place must be an attraction for the townsmen, for a dance-floor lies beside it — *'een uitspanning te land en te water waar men zelís te land niet op een droogje behoeft te zitten.'*

### Significance


Oelèë Lheue is the original harbour of Atjeh and the southern terminus of the Atjeh-tram. Its decline in importance, as the colonial government opened other harbours along the coast, is the index of the dispersal of trade that followed the pacification.
