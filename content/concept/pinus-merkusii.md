**Pinus merkusii** (English: "Merkus pine (Sumatran pine)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Pine species occurring on blang-terreinen in sufficient quantity to initiate large-scale resin and turpentine extraction.

## Translation

Merkus pine (Sumatran pine)

## Description

Pine species occurring on [[Blang|blang]]-terreinen in sufficient quantity to initiate large-scale [[Resin (Aceh)|resin]]and turpentine extraction. Tolerates poor soil and drought, surviving where loofhout cannot regenerate due to repeated burning.[[Balsem|balsem]] ([[Resin (Aceh)|resin]]) for [[Resin (Aceh)|resin]]/terpentine industry; wood usable for housing and crates.

## Significance

The term *Pinus merkusii* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
