**kali** (English: "Religious judge (now advisor)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Originally religious judges in Atjeh; now function as Islamic scholars attached to the judiciary, advising the judge.

## Translation

Religious judge (now advisor)

## Description

Originally religious judges in Atjeh; now function as Islamic scholars attached to the judiciary, advising the judge. Originally heard family- and inheritance-law cases. Appointed by the [[oelèëbalang]], the kali became a tool of the chief and gradually shifted from judge to judicial advisor. Currently a [[kali-moesapat]] is government-appointed for each [[Moesapat|moesapat]].

## Significance

The term *kali* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
