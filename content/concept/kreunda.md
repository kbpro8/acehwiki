**kreunda** (English: "coffin / bier") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Acehnese coffin: plank construction with rotan or coconut-leaf-stalk bottom, covered with a loose lid.

## Translation

coffin / bier

## Description

Acehnese coffin: plank construction with [[Rattan (Aceh)|rotan]] or coconut-leaf-stalk bottom, covered with a loose lid. Carried by two men with the lid on their heads. Bound with white-cotton bands; silk cloths draped over. In the grave, head placed to the north, face turned toward [[Mecca]].

## Significance

The term *kreunda* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
