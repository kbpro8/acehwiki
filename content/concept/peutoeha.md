**The Peutoeha** was the eldest of the pepper-planters in an Atjehnese *landschap*, the representative of the *[[Oeléebalang|oeléebalang]]* in pepper-affairs, and in the course of the years the leader of the pepper-planters who had grown into the *kepala kampong*, the village-head. The *peutoeha* receives, in Broersma's account, f 0.50 of the f 2.50 per *picol* that the *pangkaigever* receives. The institution is the principal lower-level intermediary of the Atjehnese pepper-trade, and the principal means by which the *oeléebalang*'s authority reached the individual pepper-planter.
