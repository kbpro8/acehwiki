**teureuban** (English: "turban (used here as corpse head-cloth)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Large cloth placed under the head of a deceased man as part of the shrouding.

## Translation

turban (used here as [[Bangkai (Acehnese term)|corpse]] head-cloth)

## Description

Large cloth placed under the head of a deceased man as part of the shrouding.

## Significance

The term *teureuban* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
