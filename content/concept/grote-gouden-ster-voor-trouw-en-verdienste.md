**Grote Gouden Ster voor trouw en verdienste** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Decoration worn by Panglima Polém after his submission.

## Description

Decoration worn by [[Panglima Polém]] after his submission.

## Significance

The term *Grote Gouden Ster voor trouw en verdienste* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
