**[[12 March]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]* it marks the following events in the history of [[Groot-Atjeh]].

## Events on this date

- [[Storm of Senelop (12 March 1894)]] — [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]] took [[Senelop]] from the [[Sister of Habib Samalanga|sister of Habib Samalanga]] ([[1894]])
