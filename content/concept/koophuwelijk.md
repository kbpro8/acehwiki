**koophuwelijk** (English: "purchase marriage (Gajo)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Gajo marriage by purchase; had several variants including schaaphuwelijk (abduction marriage), vluchthuwelijk (elopement marriage — girl seeks out the man in his dwelling), and loonhuwelijk (labor marriage — man buys his wife wholly or partly through labor instead of money).

## Translation

purchase marriage ([[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]])

## Description

[[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]] marriage by purchase; had several variants including schaaphuwelijk (abduction marriage), vluchthuwelijk (elopement marriage — girl seeks out the man in his dwelling), and loonhuwelijk (labor marriage — man buys his wife wholly or partly through labor instead of money).

## Significance

The term *koophuwelijk* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
