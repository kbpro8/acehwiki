**robusta / arabica / liberica (coffee)** (English: "coffee varieties") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Robusta in Alaslanden devastated by koffiebessen-boorder (coffee berry borer).

## Translation

coffee varieties

## Description

Robusta in [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]] devastated by [[koffiebessen-boorder]] (coffee berry borer). Arabica ('[[Takengon-koffie]]') grown in the Takengonsche above [[1000]] m; berry borer less severe at altitude. Total [[2400]] H.A. planted; 589,500 K.G. exported [[1937]].

## Significance

The term *robusta / arabica / liberica (coffee)* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
