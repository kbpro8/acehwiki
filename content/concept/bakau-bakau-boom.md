**Bakau-bakau boom** (English: "Mangrove tree (bakau)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Mangrove tree; bark contains very good tannin (looistof).

## Translation

Mangrove tree (bakau)

## Description

Mangrove tree; bark contains very good tannin (looistof).

## Significance

The term *Bakau-bakau boom* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
