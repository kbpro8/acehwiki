**Galar** was a title or adult name in Acehnese usage as explained in a note by [[Jan van Swieten]].

## Use in the source

The source stated that a *galar* could be hereditary with a dignity or replace a childhood name in adulthood. It gave the example of [[Nja Abas]], called *Galar Sia Oelama*, and explained the layered names of the panglima of the [[XXII Mukim]].

## Significance

Understanding this term is necessary for reading the political relationships recorded during the [[Aceh War]].
