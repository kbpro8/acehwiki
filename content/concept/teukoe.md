**Teukoe** was the Acehnese title of a secular chief or nobleman, abbreviated *T.*.

The title is borne by the great secular figures of du Croo's narrative: [[Teukoe Oemar]], [[T. Tjhi' Toenòng]], [[T. Radja Sabi]], [[T. Dagang]], [[T. Radja Tampo']], [[Teukoe Mamat]], [[T. Béntara Titeuë]], [[T. Bén Blang Pidië]] and [[T. Tjhi' Moehammad Djohan Alamsjah]]. [[H. N. A. Swart]] habitually addressed a chief with the words "And, Teukoe, is all well?" — the peculiarity of the little word "en" that so puzzled the chiefs of [[Tangsé]] that they asked [[H. J. Schmidt]] whether the Governor was angry.
