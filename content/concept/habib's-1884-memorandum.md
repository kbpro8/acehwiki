**Habib Abdoerrachman's 1884 memorandum** — the *memorie* of 3 Moharram 1302, that is October [[1884]], sent from [[Mekka]] by [[Habib Abdoerrachman el Zahir|the Habib]] to the Dutch Indies government — is the proposal by which the banished Arab, drawing his colonial pension in Arabia, offered himself as the future *Regent* of Atjeh. The seal at the foot of the document reads: *Rijksbestierder van het Gouvernement ATJEH* — State-Administrator of the Government of ATJEH.

## The context: the Habib in Mekka

The Habib had submitted on [[13 October]] [[1878]] on the condition that he settle in Mekka with a yearly pension of ten thousand dollars — *een vorstelijk jaargeld* — and had been transported to Djeddah on [[24 November]] [[1878]] aboard *Hr. Ms. "Curaçao"*. *Deze goede "rijksbestierder" van Atjeh hij had overigens in Mekka nog veelvuldig contact met Atjehers die ter bedevaart gingen en tevens hun licht bij hem opstaken*. From Mekka he still watched Atjeh's affairs; the memorandum of October 1884 was the result. *Curiositeitshalve laat ik dat stuk hier afdrukken*, Zentgraff writes.

## The six points

The Habib's schema opens: *Ten opzichte van de Atjehsche zaken zou ik de volgende punten willen voorstellen* — and proceeds to lay out his plan. He proposed himself as *Regent* of Atjeh, with the title of *Radja* or some similar dignity; he was to cooperate with a *college* of chiefs, scholars, and notables, who would assist him in the government of the country; and the schema as a whole was, he claimed, a plan whose execution *tot de onderwerping van Atjeh zou leiden*. *Hij zag zichzelf ulteraard als de aanstaande "regent" van Atjeh. Men kan die verwachting nu een weinig dwaas vinden, en nogal arrogant, doch men moet haar zien in het licht van dien tijd, opkomend in de ziel van een man wiens vrijwillig heengaan van Atjeh zóó belangrijk werd geacht, dat de Kompeuni hem een voor Arabië vorstelijk jaargeld betaalde*.

## The seal

The Habib signs the memorandum: *(Get.) HABIB ABDOERRACHMAN 3 Moharram 1302. (October 1884) EL ZAHIR*. Beneath the signature stands the seal — *Hieronder een zegel, waarin: "Rijksbestierder van het Gouvernement ATJEH"*. The title was his own: a self-conferred dignity that none in Atjeh had yet granted and that the Dutch government, for its part, did not take up. The schema was not acted upon; it remained, in Zentgraff's word, a *curiositeit*.

## Significance

The 1884 memorandum is the last political document of the Habib's career. He had been, in the late 1870s, *een zóó gevaarlijk tegenstander* that the government had been willing to pay him ten thousand dollars a year to remove him from Atjeh; in Mekka, six years later, his memorandum shows him still imagining a return as *Regent*. The proposal coincides, almost exactly, with the rise of [[Tengkoe Sjech Saman di Tiro]] — *nà de periode van den Habib zorgden de Tiro-oelama's hiervoor* — and the religious leadership of the *prang sabil* passed from the Habib to the Tiro family, where it would stay for thirty years.
