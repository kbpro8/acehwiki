**Willemsorde** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Dutch military honour; of currently serving officers Colonel Gosenson alone wears it.

## Description

Dutch military honour; of currently serving officers Colonel Gosenson alone wears it.

## Significance

The term *Willemsorde* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
