**ponten (rakits)** (English: "ferries (rafts)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Pontoon ferries/rafts used on the Westkust road.

## Translation

ferries (rafts)

## Description

Pontoon ferries/rafts used on the [[Westkust]] road. In dry season with low water, the chauffeur must skillfully maneuver from the high banks onto the ferry. In rainy season, the fast-flowing rivers and dead wood make crossing difficult. Being replaced by iron bridges.

## Significance

The term *ponten (rakits)* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
