**Wasé oelèëbalang** was the sovereignty right of the [[Oelèëbalang|Oelèëbalang]]'s over pepper and pinang — originally a fixed amount ($2/pikul pepper, $1–1½/bahra pinang in Groot-Atjèh). Reorganised by governor [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] in [[1906]] to 10% of value (causing pepper decline), then by governor [[Governor Swart|Swart]] in [[1910]] to fixed amounts, the *wasé oelèëbalang* was mostly absorbed into *landschapskas* salaries.

## Original form

Originally, the *wasé oelèëbalang* was a fixed amount per pikul (approximately 61.8 kg) of pepper and per *bahra* (3 pikul) of pinang exported from the Oelèëbalang's domain:
- $2 per pikul pepper
- $1–1½ per bahra pinang

This was part of the broader system of *wasé* (from Arabic *haṣīl*, "produce / yield / tax") — the chiefs' various traditional revenues and levies.

## Van Daalen's reform (1906)

Governor [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]]'s besluit of [[12 December]] [[1906]] N°. 112 reformed the *wasé oelèëbalang* from fixed amounts to **10% of value**. This percentage-based levy was far more burdensome than the previous fixed amounts and caused the pepper decline as planters found the tax uneconomic.

## Swart's reform (1910)

Governor [[Governor Swart|Swart]]'s besluit of [[13 January]] [[1910]] N°. 1 returned the *wasé oelèëbalang* to fixed amounts, halting the pepper decline. A further reform in [[1915]] (Bt. [[13 September]] N°. 435) removed *bestuursbemoeienis* (administrative interference) from private-law pepper contracts entirely.

## Absorption into landschapskassen

By Kreemer's time, the *wasé oelèëbalang* had been mostly absorbed into the *[[landschapskassen|landschapskas]]* (regional treasury) salaries — the Oelèëbalang's received fixed monthly salaries from the landschapskas rather than collecting the *wasé* directly.
