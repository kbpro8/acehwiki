**The Atjesch internaat** — the *Atjehnese boarding-school* — was a boarding-school for school-going Atjehnese children under European supervision, founded at [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]] in [[1922]]. The institution is one of the principal educational establishments of the post-pacification period, and the type-case of the colonial government's attempt to educate the sons of *oeléebalangs* in European habits.

## The Institution
The *Atjesch internaat* is a boarding-school for school-going Atjehnese children under European supervision, where one tries, while maintaining simplicity, to satisfy all requirements. The sleeping-halls are roomy; the food, after being fixed according to medical advice, is carefully prepared. The boarding-fee is sixty guilders a month, but a child can be admitted for less if the parents cannot pay the usual amount. Remarkable is that all the housed children are still from prominent families. An *[[Oeléebalang tjoet|oeléebalang tjoet]]* could have his children admitted, but they do not yet come. It is therefore so far a prominent institution.

## Significance
The Atjesch internaat is the principal educational initiative of the post-pacification period for the sons of the Atjehnese aristocracy, and the type-case of the colonial government's attempt to educate them in European habits while preserving their Atjehnese identity. The institution is also the type-case of the social exclusivity of the early civilian period: only the prominent families come, and the *oeléebalang tjoets* do not yet send their sons.
