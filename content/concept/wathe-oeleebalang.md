**Wathe Oeleebalang** (also known as *Hacil Oeleebalang*) was the customary land right tax and export duty paid to district chieftains (*uleebalang*) on pepper exported from their territories.

## Legal Structure and Reforms

Historically, *Wathe Oeleebalang* was a fixed fee per unit of export cargo. A portion was historically forwarded to the monarch as *Wathe Radja*.

In [[1906]], Governor [[Gotfried Coenraad Ernst van Daalen]] promulgated [[Decree 112/K of 1906]], reforming *Wathe Oeleebalang* into a 10% ad valorem export tax. In [[1910]], Governor [[H.E. Swart]] replaced it with a flat duty of 1.50 guilders per pikul.
