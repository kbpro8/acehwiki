**The Sirat al-mustaqim** — Arabic *Ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm*, "The Straight Path" — is a Malay-language handbook of Islamic religious duties (*fiqh*) begun by [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|Nūruddīn ar-Rānīrī]] in [[1634]] and completed in [[1644]], and one of the principal works of the Atjehnese court of Sultan Iskandar Thani. The work is repeatedly attested in [[Over Maleise Literatuur (1947)|Hooykaas's survey]] of Malay literature as a key vehicle for the diffusion of the *fiqh* of the Shāfiʿite school into the Malay-Indonesian world from its Atjehnese point of composition.

## Composition and Date

The composition of the *Sirat al-mustaqim* was begun by [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|ar-Rānīrī]] in [[1634]] — three years before his formal arrival in Atjeh in [[1637]] — and was completed only ten years later, in [[1644]]. The earlier date of [[1634]] was established by [[H. Djajadiningrat|Djajadiningrat]] in *TBG* 69, pp. 109-111; it corrects the earlier assumption of [[P. Voorhoeve|Voorhoeve]] (*BKI* 107, p. 357) that the *Sirāṭ* had been composed before ar-Rānīrī's arrival in Atjeh. The work was completed at the command of Sultan Iskandar Thani in March [[1638]] — the same year in which ar-Rānīrī also composed the *[[Bustanu's-Salatin|Bustān as-Salāṭīn]]* and the *Bad' khalq as-samawat wal-arwah* — and forms part of ar-Rānīrī's project of providing the Achehnese faithful with the literary apparatus of a Shāfiʿite sultanate.

## Diffusion: The Kedah Episode

[[C. Hooykaas|Hooykaas]] records one of the key episodes in the early diffusion of the *Sirat al-mustaqim* in his chapter on historiography. In his account of the *Hikajat Marong Mahawangsa* (the Kedah chronicle), Hooykaas notes that, when the king of Kedah converted to Islam under the influence of *Sjaich 'Abdoellāh Jamani* and was given the throne-name Sultan Moezaffar Sjah, "Toen men dit in Atjéh vernam, liet de Moslim geworden vorst aldaar Sjaich Noeroddin diens boek Sirāṭ al-moestakīm naar Kēdah brengen ter onderrichting in de Wet" (When this was heard in Atjeh, the Muslim ruler there had Sjaich Noeroddin's book *Sirāṭ al-mustaqīm* brought to Kedah for instruction in the Law). The episode illustrates the role of the *Sirat al-mustaqim* — composed in Atjeh by a Gujarati-born scholar — as the principal vehicle by which the *fiqh* of the Shāfiʿite school was transmitted from Atjeh to the Malay Peninsula in the early seventeenth century.

## Place in the Atjehnese Literary Tradition

The *Sirat al-mustaqim* belongs to the cluster of ar-Rānīrī's Atjehnese compositions — the *[[Bustanu's-Salatin|Bustān as-Salāṭīn]]* of [[1638]], the *Bad' khalq as-samawat wal-arwah* of [[1638]], the *Tibjān fi ma'rifat al-adyān*, and the *Sirat al-mustaqim* itself — that together constitute the literary apparatus of ar-Rānīrī's orthodox reform in Atjeh. The composition of the *Sirāṭ* in [[1638]] — at the very moment of ar-Rānīrī's public disputation against the *Wujudiyyah* mystics — marks the beginning of his project to provide the Achehnese faithful with the literary apparatus of a Shāfiʿite sultanate.

## Significance

For AcehWiki, the *Sirat al-mustaqim* is significant as the principal Malay-language *fiqh* handbook produced at the Atjehnese court in the seventeenth century. Its composition by [[Nur al-Din al-Raniri|ar-Rānīrī]] in [[1634]]-[[1644]] — at the command of Sultan Iskandar Thani — places it at the heart of the orthodox reform of the Atjehnese court in the 1630s and 1640s, and its diffusion to Kedah in the early seventeenth century (recorded by Hooykaas) illustrates the role of the *Sirāṭ* as the principal vehicle by which the Shāfiʿite *fiqh* of the Atjehnese court was transmitted to the Malay Peninsula.
