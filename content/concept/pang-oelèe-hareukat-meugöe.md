**pang oelèe hareukat meugöe** (English: "'the pangulee (headman) is the master of agriculture' / 'agriculture is the master of all livelihoods'") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Acehnese saying quoted in nearly every economic description of Aceh; indicates that the population seeks its main livelihood in agriculture, especially rice cultivation.

## Translation

'the pangulee (headman) is the master of agriculture' / 'agriculture is the master of all livelihoods'

## Description

Acehnese saying quoted in nearly every economic description of Aceh; indicates that the population seeks its main livelihood in agriculture, especially rice cultivation.

## Significance

The term *pang oelèe hareukat meugöe* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
