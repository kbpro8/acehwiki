**Kawan** is the Malay word for companion, friend, or comrade.

## Marechaussee Jargon Usage

In the informal internal hierarchy of the [[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]], *Kawan* was used to designate the rank-and-file private Marechaussee soldiers (*manschappen*). It emphasized intense mutual loyalty and comrade-in-arms bonds (*kameradentrouw*) forged during grueling long-distance jungle patrols.
