**eige beheer** (English: "Own management (direct rule)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Initial administrative choice for Groot-Atjeh after 1874, taking it into direct Dutch management pending a decision between direct bestuur or a vazalstaat under a Sultan's heir.

## Translation

Own management ([[Rechtstreeks bestuurd gebied|direct rule]])

## Description

Initial administrative choice for [[Groot-Atjeh]] after [[1874]], taking it into direct Dutch management pending a decision between direct bestuur or a vazalstaat under a [[sultan|Sultan]]'s heir. [[Rechtstreeks bestuurd gebied|Direct rule]] was [[Corea|chosen]].

## Significance

The term *eige beheer* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
