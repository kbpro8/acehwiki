**The Atjehsche vlag** — the Atjehnese flag — is the [[Acheh|Atjehnese]] standard whose flying, in [[1862]], from the *benteng* of an [[Atjehsche avonturier at Aroe-baai (1862)|Atjehscher avonturier]] on the hill of [[Poelo Kampi|Kampi]] in [[Aroe-baai|the bay of Aroe]], is the only recorded instance of an Atjehnese military assertion on the east-coast Sumatran theatre in the immediate pre-[[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] period. The episode, recorded by [[Van Rijn van Alkemade|Van Rijn]] in [[1888]], is the principal token of the survival of an Atjehnese memory of the [[Atjeh-Aroe War (1539)|Atjehnese conquest of Aroe]] of [[1539]] more than three centuries after the event.

## The 1862 episode

In [[1862]] — eleven years before the [[Eerste Atjeh-Expeditie (1873)|first Dutch expedition against Atjeh]] of [[1873]] — an unnamed Atjehnese adventurer threw up a *benteng* on the hill of [[Poelo Kampi|Kampi]] in [[Aroe-baai|the bay of Aroe]] "and here defiantly let the Atjehsche flag fly". The episode is recorded in the [[Van Rijn van Alkemade|Van Rijn]] report of [[1888]], which notes that the impression of the hill — "as if one has a fortress with breastwork before one" — recalled the Tanjore inscription's "ancient Malayur situated on a high hill".

## Significance

For AcehWiki, the Atjehsche vlag is the principal symbolic token of the survival of an Atjehnese memory of the [[Atjeh-Aroe War (1539)|Atjehnese conquest of Aroe]] of [[1539]] into the nineteenth century. The choice of [[Poelo Kampi|Kampi]] — the [[Malajoer|sacred islet]] of the older Hindu-Buddhist geography, the [[Noesa-n-ta-ra|Noesa-n-ta-ra]] of the [[Pararaton]] — for the Atjehnese flag is, in [[P. de Roo de la Faille|De Roo de la Faille]]'s reading, no accident: it marks the same place that the [[Atjehsche vloot|Atjehnese fleet]] of [[Alleredin Mahomet]] had taken in [[1539]].
