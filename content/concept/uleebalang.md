**Uleebalang** was an Acehnese territorial chiefship, rendered *oeloebalang* in the [[1879]] Dutch source.

## Use in the source

[[Jan van Swieten]] described [[Teuku Langgoegoeb]] as uleebalang of the [[III Mukim]] and alleged that his military appointee, [[Teuku Potjoet Ali]], killed him and assumed effective authority. The succession conflict after Potjoet Ali’s death shows the office’s political importance.

## Significance

Understanding this term is necessary for reading the political relationships recorded during the [[Aceh War]].
