**rëdjö / penghoeloe** (English: "Clan head (in Gajo)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Hereditary head of a blah; successor chosen by married men of the clan from among the deceased's sons, with preference for the youngest son (if he bears the required luck signs).

## Translation

Clan head (in [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]])

## Description

Hereditary head of a [[Blah|blah]]; successor [[Corea|chosen]] by married men of the clan from among the deceased's sons, with preference for the youngest son (if he bears the required luck signs).

## Significance

The term *rëdjö / penghoeloe* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
