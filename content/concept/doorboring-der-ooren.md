**doorboring der ooren** (English: "ear piercing") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Ear-lobe piercing in Aceh around age 6-7; Alas at 3-4.

## Translation

ear piercing

## Description

Ear-lobe piercing in Aceh around age 6-7; [[Alas]] at 3-4. [[Alas]] girls get two holes per ear (lobe + rim). Disappearing in lowlands and coast. Large round ear-discs (gold, horn, wood, or ivory) worn in the wide holes by girls/women.

## Significance

The term *doorboring der ooren* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
