## Account in Hooijer's *De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh* ([[1897]])

**[[20 May]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. ## Events on this date

- [[Establishment of the post at Biloel (20–21 May 1894)]] — the garrison of [[Boekit Daroe]] transferred to [[Biloel]] and the road over the hills of the [[IX Moekims]] laid out ([[1894]])

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this date in [[1875]] the meeting at the residence of the Lieutenant-Governor of [[Penang]] between the Dutch agent [[Lavino]] and [[Habib Abdoerrachman el Zahir]] produced the sultanate negotiation that the Indian government put aside (see [[Herstel van het sultanaat]]); on this date in [[1874]] the sick and wounded at [[Kuta Raja|Kota Radja]] made a seventh of the garrison (see [[Bezettingskorps in Atjeh]]).
