**concentratie-stelsel / concentratieline** (English: "Concentration system / line") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Defensive strategy of holding a concentrated line in Groot-Atjeh, in place from 1884 to 1893.

## Translation

[[Concentratie-Stelsel|Concentration system]] / line

## Description

Defensive strategy of holding a [[Concentrated Line|concentrated line]] in [[Groot-Atjeh]], in place from [[1884]] to [[1893]]. When abandoned, the [[Koeta-Radja|Koeta Radja]] [[Ceintuur-trambaan|ceintuurbaan]] and straalbanen of the [[Atjeh-tram]] were also dismantled ([[1896]]).

## Significance

The term *concentratie-stelsel / concentratieline* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
