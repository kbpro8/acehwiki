**[[4 July]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]* it marks the following events in the history of [[Groot-Atjeh]].

## Events on this date

- [[Establishment of the post at Lam Koenjit (4 July 1894)]] — the intermediate post erected against the attacks of [[T. Rajoet]] ([[1894]])
