**Pogostemon Cablin Benth.** — the patchouli plant — is the labiate plant from whose leaves the patchouli-oil is distilled. On Sumatra the plant is called *[[Nilam|nilam]]*; on Java *[[Dilem|dilem]]*. On Sumatra the chief centres of cultivation are on the [[Westkust van Atjeh|Westkust van Atjeh]] at [[Tjalang|Tjalang]], [[Meulaboh|Meulaboh]] and [[Tapa Toean|Tapa Toean]], and on the Oostkust at [[Langsa|Langsa]]. Soil and climate in Atjeh appear naturally more favourable to the plant than on Java, where the oil, though of respectable constants, has *'lang niet zoo intensief'* an odour.
