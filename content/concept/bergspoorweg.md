**bergspoorweg** (English: "Mountain railway") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Heavy mountain railway required between Seulimeum and Padang-Tidji to cross the watershed; the missing Seulimeum–Gedeh-Breuëh section was started in 1903 and delivered early 1908.

## Translation

Mountain railway

## Description

Heavy mountain railway required between [[Seulimeum]] and [[Padang]]-Tidji to cross the watershed; the missing [[Seulimeum]]–[[Gedeh-Breuëh]] section was started in [[1903]] and delivered early [[1908]].

## Significance

The term *bergspoorweg* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
