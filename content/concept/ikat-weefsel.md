**Ikat-weefsel** (English: "Tie-dye textile") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Old Acehnese silk ikat textile; somber tone, very supple; once thriving home industry, now practically gone (1930).

## Translation

Tie-dye textile

## Description

Old Acehnese silk [[Ikatten|ikat]] textile; somber tone, very supple; once thriving home industry, now practically gone ([[1930]]).

## Significance

The term *Ikat-weefsel* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
