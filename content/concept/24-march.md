**[[24 March]]** is a calendar date used in the Aceh Wiki History date-linking convention, by which every full date is split into a day-month link and a year link. In the material drawn from *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|Generaal Swart, Pacificator van Atjeh]]* it appears in the articles listed below.

## Articles referring to this date

- [[Keureutòë]]
- [[Tgk. di Barat]]

## Account from Verstege's *Een beroep op het Nederlandsche volk* (1886)

On this date in [[1879]] the [[Capture of Indrapuri (24 March 1879)|strong missigit of Indrapoerie]] was taken; on the night of this date in [[1885]] the workers at the clearing of the new line were fired upon: three wounded, among them luitenant [[Luitenant Colenbrander (1885)|Colenbrander]] (see [[Guerilla raids inside the concentration line (1885)]]).
