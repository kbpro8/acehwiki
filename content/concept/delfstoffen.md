**Delfstoffen** (English: "Minerals / useful minerals") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Useful minerals in Aceh: petroleum (Peureula' historic; major production at Rantau); coal (Meureubo-Seunagan, Tapa'Toean, Simaloer—not exploited); gold (Kaway XII, Gajolanden); iron, copper, pyrite, zincblende, loodglans (not yet economic deposits).

## Translation

Minerals / useful minerals

## Description

Useful minerals in Aceh: petroleum ([[Peureula]]' historic; major production at [[Rantau]]); coal ([[Meureubo]]-[[Seunagan]], [[Tapa'Toean]], [[Simaloer]]—not exploited); gold ([[Kaway XII]], [[Gajolanden (Gayo Lands)|Gajolanden]]); iron, copper, pyrite, zincblende, loodglans (not yet economic deposits).

## Significance

The term *Delfstoffen* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
