**The Wasé** — also called *hacil* — was the *heerlijk recht* (lordly right) paid to the *[[Oeléebalang|oeléebalang]]* on each *picol* of pepper produced in his *landschap*. The *wasé oeléebalang* was a ground-right, and when *'het landschap'* was instituted as a self-governing unit, the *wasé oeléebalang* belonged to the *landschapskas*. The *hacil* is now, in Broersma's account, a guilder per *picol*; the *pangkaigever* — usually the *oeléebalang* or an *[[Oeléebalang tjoet|oeléebalang tjoet]]* — receives separately f 2.50 per *picol*, of which he gives the *[[Peutoeha|peutoeha]]* f 0.50. The *hacil* is the principal revenue of the *oeléebalang* from the pepper-trade, and the principal means by which the *landschap* participates in the pepper-economy.

## The 1915 Regulation
In [[1915]] the colonial government came with a new regulation, by which the customs-office no longer had any concern. The *hacil tanah* or *wasé oeléebalang* was to be divided fifty-fifty between the *landschap* and the *pangkaigever* and the *peutoeha*. That regulation too has not encouraged the *pangkaigever* — *'ook die regeling heeft den verstrekker van kapitaal niet aangemoedigd.'*
