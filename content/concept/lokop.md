**Lokop** was a Dutch *bivak* of the [[1ste Garnizoensbataljon|1ste Garnizoensbataljon]] — later of the [[5de (Vde) Divisie Marechaussee|Vde Divisie Maréchaussee]] — in the upper *oostkust* hinterland of [[Atjeh|Atjeh]], in the *afdeling* [[Oostkust van Atjeh|Oostkust van Atjeh]] between [[Lho Seumawé|Lho Seumawé]] and the central-Atjeh hoogvlakte. In the [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|Dutch staff history's]] account (Deel VI, Hoofdstuk V), Lokop is the *oostkust* *bivak* whose *defensietaak* — the beveiliging of the vernielingswerken of the *olieterreinen* — placed it in the operational chain of the Dutch central-Atjeh defence in March [[1942]].

## Pre-war garrison

As of [[1 January]] [[1940]], Lokop held four brigades of the 1ste Garnizoensbataljon. In the [[1941]] reorganisations the Lokop brigades were given a *defensietaak*: "de beveiliging der vernielingswerkzaamheden van de olieboor-terreinen in de omgeving van deze plaatsen" — Lokop, [[Lho Seumawé|Lho Seumawé]] and [[Koeala Simpang|Koeala Simpang]]. The Lokop brigades were not equipped with automatic weapons.

## March 1942 reorganisation and transfer to Blangkedjeren

In February [[1942]] the Lokop and Lho Seumawé *Infanterie* detachments, "die eveneens met een defensietaak waren belast, zouden naar hun bivaks terugkeren" — that is, the *Infanterie* brigades were restored to Lokop and Lho Seumawé after a temporary reduction. The Maréchaussee brigades that had relieved them at Koeala Simpang "kregen eveneens een defensietaak."

After the Japanese landings of [[12 March]] [[12 March 1942|12 March]], "het detachement Lokop voerde eerst door het terrein naar de weg, die van de kust naar deze plaats afboog, marcheerde en daarna in de richting van Blangkedjeren." The Lokop brigades were eventually "naar Blangkedjeren verplaatst," where they formed part of the consolidated Dutch force under Lt-Col [[Blogg|Blogg]] and Captain [[F. J. van Nues|Van Nues]] at the [[Sumatra capitulation (28 March 1942)|Blangkedjeren capitulation of 28 March 1942]].

## Significance

Lokop is, in the staff history's Atjeh chapter, the *oostkust* *bivak* whose *defensietaak* — the beveiliging of the *olieterreinen* — placed it in the operational chain of the Dutch central-Atjeh defence. The staff history's emphasis on the Lokop brigades' transfer to Blangkedjeren in March [[1942]] frames Lokop as the staff history's principal narrative instance of the Dutch *oostkust* *defensietaak*'s absorption into the central-Atjeh theatre — the *bivak* whose original mission (oil-installation defence) was superseded by the broader Dutch consolidation at Blangkedjeren.
