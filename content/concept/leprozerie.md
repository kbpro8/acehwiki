**Leprozerie** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Leper asylums.

## Description

Leper asylums. In Aceh the costs are borne by the [[Landschapskasen|landschapskasen]]. Isolation not strict enough to prevent contact with the outside world. Some lepers attempted [[Atjeh-moord|atjeh-moord]] (with knives tied to finger-stumps).

## Significance

The term *Leprozerie* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
