**Rasakhout** (English: "Rasa wood (termite-resistant)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Wood from Simaloer highly resistant against paalworm (shipworm); historically major timber export from Simaloer.

## Translation

Rasa wood (termite-resistant)

## Description

Wood from [[Simaloer]] highly resistant against paalworm (shipworm); historically major timber export from [[Simaloer]].

## Significance

The term *Rasakhout* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
