**gewest** (English: "region / colonial administrative unit") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Colonial administrative region — Atjeh was a gewest.

## Translation

region / colonial administrative unit

## Description

Colonial administrative region — Atjeh was a gewest. Headed by a Head of Gewestelijk Bestuur.

## Significance

The term *gewest* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
