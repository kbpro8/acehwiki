**Péngoeloe Si-émpat** — "the four *péngoeloe*s" — was the council of four *soekoe* (clan) chiefs that, alongside the *Kédjoerön* and the *Radja Moeda*, constituted the advisory and quasi-legislative body of the *landschappen* of the [[Alaslanden|Alaslanden]]: [[Batoe Mboelan|Batoe Mboelan]] and [[Bambél|Bambél]]. *De Buitenbezittingen* treats the *Péngoeloe Si-émpat* as the Alas counterpart of the Gayo *tjèq*'s and of the Tamiang *Datoeq Ampat Soekoe* — a council of four that originated as a *patriarch-raadsheer* (patriarchal counsellor) body of *soekoe* chiefs and that gradually, over the course of the nineteenth century, acquired a territorial dimension.

## Origins and constitutional position

The *Péngoeloe Si-émpat*, *De Buitenbezittingen* records, originated as the four senior *Péngoeloe's soekoe* of the *landschap* — the heads of the four principal clan-organisations of the Alas country — who served as the *patriarch-raadsheer* of the *Kédjoerön*. The *Radja Tjèq of Alas|Radja Tjèq* — the [[Sultans of Atjeh|Sultan of Atjeh]]-recognised overlord of the Alas valley — reserved for himself the fourth seat in each *landschap*'s council, so that three of the four seats were held by *Péngoeloe Si-émpat* in the narrow sense, while the fourth was held by the *Radja Tjèq* himself or by his deputy.

## Territorialisation

In the course of the nineteenth century, the *Péngoeloe Si-émpat* — originally the heads of *soekoe*'s whose members were scattered across the *landschap* — gradually acquired territorial authority over the *soekoe* members resident in particular areas. *De Buitenbezittingen* records that "hunner oorspronkelijke positie als overige Péngoeloe's soekoe en patriarch-raadsheer hadden ze eerst weten om te zetten in eene, welke overeenkwam met die der tjèqs van de Gajo Loeës" — their original position as the remaining *Péngoeloe's soekoe* and patriarchal counsellors, they first managed to transform into one that corresponded to that of the *tjèqs* of the Gayo Loeës. Each *Péngoeloe Si-émpat* thus came to gather under himself a number of *Péngoeloes soekoe*, and the *tjèq*-authority — originally personal and genealogical — became "meer en meer territoriaal in de door die soekoes bewoonde nederzettingen" (increasingly territorial in the settlements inhabited by those *soekoe*s). At the time of the Dutch arrival, the handbook records, there were four *Péngoeloe Si-émpat* in Batoe Mboelan and three in Bambél, each with a "hoewel zeer verbrokkeld, toch bepaald aan te wijzen territoriaal gebied" — a, although very fragmented, yet definitely identifiable territorial area.

## The 1914 reorganisation

The Dutch reorganisation of the Alaslanden, carried out in consultation with the *kerapatan* (great council) of the two *landschappen*, regularised the territorial positions of the *Péngoeloe Si-émpat*. The *landschap* of [[Poeloe Nas|Poeloe Nas]] (formerly Batoe Mboelan) was divided into four *mérga*'s under the *Péngoeloe si-émpat* as *bezoldigde hoofden* (salaried chiefs): *Mérga Klis* under *Imom Balei*; *Mérga Képala Dèsa* under *Batin*; *Mérga Kling Kérétan*; and *Mérga Tjébéro* under *Béntjirim*. The *landschap* of Bambél was similarly divided: *Mérga 'ngKran* under *'ngKran*; *Mérga Béroeh* under *Biaq Moeli*; *Mérga Sékédang* under *'nDjaar*; and *Mérga Sélian* under the *Radja Tjèq Batoe Mboelan*. The *Kédjoerön* retained direct administration of his residential kampong.
