**Groot-Atjehsche grootspraak** (English: "Groot-Aceh boastfulness") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Equivalent to Dutch 'Haagsche bluf' (Hague bluster) — characteristic defect of Groot-Aceh people.

## Translation

Groot-Aceh boastfulness

## Description

Equivalent to Dutch 'Haagsche bluf' (Hague bluster) — characteristic defect of Groot-Aceh people.

## Significance

The term *Groot-Atjehsche grootspraak* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
