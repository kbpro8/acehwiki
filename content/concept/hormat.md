**hormat** was the Malay and Acehnese term for respect, deference and the outward marks of it.

The question of *hormat* divided [[H. N. A. Swart]] from [[Gotfried Coenraad Ernst van Daalen|Van Daalen]] at their first meeting on [[Poelò Raja]] in [[1902]], when Van Daalen judged that an *[[oelèëbalang]]* who had paid his respects in the customary manner had shown insufficient respect. [[Christiaan Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] condemned Van Daalen's "ridiculous demands for marks of respect." Swart nevertheless required outward propriety: at a halt in [[Pidië]] he refused to receive a chief who was improperly dressed and deficient in *hormat*, to the astonishment of [[Liefrinck]] — "and I do not receive the chiefs in that way."
