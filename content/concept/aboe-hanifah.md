**Aboe Hanifah** (English: "Abu Hanifah (Hanafi school of Islamic jurisprudence)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Islamic legal school whose interpretation the Atjehers invoke to hold valid Friday services with fewer than the forty worshippers required by the Sjafi'i school.

## Translation

[[Imeum Hanapi|Abu Hanifah]] (Hanafi school of Islamic jurisprudence)

## Description

Islamic legal school whose interpretation the [[Atjehers]] invoke to hold valid Friday services with fewer than the forty worshippers required by the [[Sjafi'i]] school.

## Significance

The term *Aboe Hanifah* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
