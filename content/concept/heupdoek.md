**heupdoek** (English: "hip cloth") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Once nearly indispensable clothing item, considered a sign that the wearer professed Islam.

## Translation

hip cloth

## Description

Once nearly indispensable clothing item, considered a sign that the wearer professed [[Islam]]. Now declining as European dress displaces it. Usually black cloth with ends sewn together; silk for festive wear.

## Significance

The term *heupdoek* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
