**The Korpslied van de Marechaussee** — the corps song of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen]] — was the more formal song of the corps (as distinct from the soldiers' own *[[Marechausseelied]]*), narrating the *daden* (deeds) of the corps vanaf de *oprichting* (from its founding). Its *refrein* — "*Hai marsosé, hai marsosé… memboeroeh moesoeh, memboeroeh moesoeh th' tjapé-é, / naik-toeroen goenoeng, / masoek-kaloear rimba, / memtjari bekas dani anak marsosé!*" (Hunting the enemy relentlessly, mountain up and down, in and out of the bush, seeking traces of the marechaussee-son!) — was led by a *voorzanger* who sang new couplets, after which all fell in on the *refrein*.

## The refrein

The *refrein* of the *korpslied* — "*Hai marsosé, hai marsosé… memboeroeh moesoeh, memboeroeh moesoeh th' tjapé-é, / naik-toeroen goenoeng, / masoek-kaloear rimba, / memtjari bekas dani anak marsosé!*" — narrated the work of the corps: hunting the enemy relentlessly (*memboeroeh moesoeh, memboeroeh moesoeh th' tjapé-é*), mountain up and down (*naik-toeroen goenoeng*), in and out of the bush (*masoek-kaloear rimba*), seeking traces of the marechaussee-son (*memtjari bekas dani anak marsosé*). The *refrein* was therefore a compressed narrative of the colonne method: the *bekas* (spoor), the *rimboe* (jungle), the *goenoeng* (mountain), the *moesoeh* (enemy).

## The voorzanger and the couplets

A *voorzanger* — the lead singer — sang new couplets, after which all fell in on the *refrein*. The *voorzanger* was a recognised role at feestjes; [[Tanamal]] — the *Marechaussee 1e klas* at [[Bakongan]] — was *voorzanger* at the post's feestjes. Sometimes a 13-14 year old *[[anak-kolong]]* would lead the *voorzanger* role — a child of the *[[tangsi]]* who had grown up with the song. The *korpslied* was therefore a living composition: new couplets were added as the corps's *daden* accumulated.

## The distinction from the Marechausseelied

The *korpslied* is to be distinguished from the more informal *[[Marechausseelied]]* — the soldiers' own song. The *Marechausseelied* — with its *refrein* "*Manisee-Manisee! 't Is terlaloe Manisee!*" — was the soldiers' affectionate self-identification; the *korpslied* — with its *refrein* "*Hai marsosé, hai marsosé…*" — was the corps's own narrative of its *daden*. Both were composed in their first couplets by [[Dominee Thenu]], but the *korpslied* was the more formal, the more historical, and the more narratively ambitious of the two.

## Significance

The *korpslied* is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as the corps's own narrative of its *daden*. The book's argument is that the *korpslied* — composed by the *Soldaten-Dominee* and grown by the soldiers — was the cultural production of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Marechaussee]]'s collective memory: the *daden* of the corps vanaf de *oprichting* were narrated in the *korpslied*, and the *refrein* was the corps's compressed narrative of its own method. The *korpslied* is therefore the book's marker of the corps's historical self-consciousness: the corps knew what it had done, and it sang what it knew.
