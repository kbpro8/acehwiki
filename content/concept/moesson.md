**moesson** (English: "monsoon") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Climatic seasons that differ across regions of Aceh and determine whether farmers plant late-, mid-, or early-ripening rice varieties.

## Translation

monsoon

## Description

Climatic seasons that differ across regions of Aceh and determine whether farmers plant late-, mid-, or early-ripening rice varieties.

## Significance

The term *moesson* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
