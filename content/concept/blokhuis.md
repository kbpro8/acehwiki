*blockhouse* — Dutch for "blockhouse" — was the standard [[Dutch]] colonial fortification in the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]: a small fortified wooden or earthen structure, typically surrounded by a bamboo-thorn fence, garrisoned by a platoon or company of infantry, and often armed with a machine gun and a 7 cm L.A. mountain gun. The blokhuis was the building-block of the [[buitenlinie|buitellinie]] — the outer line of Dutch posts around [[Koeta Radja]] — and the principal target of Achehnese attacks in the spring of [[1896]]. Kruisheer's *ATJEH '96* names dozens of blokhuizen: besieged [[28 March]]–[[29 March]] [[1896]]; the blokhuizen at [[Blang Kirai]], [[Lamboekoeng]], [[Bak Tjerlak]], [[Blang Tjoet]], [[Oedjoeng Boekloet]]; the blokhuis at [[Toei Selimbing]] and at [[Pagani]].

## Etymology and Meaning

The word *blokhuis* is a Dutch compound: block, log + house. The literal sense is "blockhouse" — a small fortified structure of logs or planks. In the Dutch colonial context the term came to denote a particular type of small field fortification, usually a single wooden building capable of housing 15–20 men, surrounded by a bamboo-thorn fence and often reinforced by an earthen breastwork. The blokhuis was smaller and more temporary than the Achehnese earthen fortification and more exposed than the general fortification. The plural is *blokhuizen*; the spelling *blokhus* in Kruisheer's text is an OCR variant.

## Historical Context

In the first semester of [[1896]] the blokhuis was the most exposed element of the Dutch position in [[Groot-Atjeh]]. The [[Siege of blokhuis Aloer Oe (28–29 March 1896)|siege of blokhuis Aloer Oe]] of [[28 March]]–[[29 March]] [[1896]] — in which the garrison held out for two days against Achehnese attack, the *mitrailleur* twice made unserviceable and twice repaired by *Sgt. son* of the artillery — was the first major test of the blokhuis after Umar's defection. The blokhuizen at [[Biloel]], [[Lamkoenjit]], [[Ana' Galong|Anak Galoeng]], [[Lambarih|Lam Barih]], [[Lamsoet|Lam Soet]] and [[Senelop]] were similarly exposed, and the [[Evacuation of Biloel and Lamkoenjit (12 April 1896)|evacuation of Biloel and Lamkoenjit]] of [[12 April]] [[1896]] and the [[Evacuation of Kroeng-Gloempang, Tjot-Rang, Toengkoeb (20 April 1896)|evacuation of Kroeng-Gloempang, Tjot-Rang, Toengkoeb]] of [[20 April]] [[1896]] marked the systematic abandonment of the buitenlinie's blokhuizen. The [[Battle of Senelop (17 April 1896)|battle of Senelop]] of [[17 April]] [[1896]] — in which a Dutch column under [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] was driven back from the relief of blokhuis Lamsoet with the loss of a *mitrailleur* and a 7 cm L.A. *kanon* — confirmed that the blokhuizen could no longer be supported.

## Examples in the Source

Kruisheer's principal blokhuis references include: *blokhuis Aloer Oe* — besieged [[28 March]]–[[29 March]] [[1896]], *mitrailleur* twice made unserviceable and twice repaired by *Sgt. son*. The blokhuizen at [[Blang Kirai]], [[Lamboekoeng]], [[Bak Tjerlak]], [[Blang Tjoet]], [[Oedjoeng Boekloet]] — named as fortified posts in the buitenlinie. The blokhuis at [[Toei Selimbing]] — referenced in the post-war period (page 296). The blokhuis at [[Pagani]] — used as a terrain reference point on the coast. The blokhuis at [[Lamara]] — site of an ambush on [[25 April]] [[1896]] in which *Sgt. de Kok* was wounded. The blokhuis at [[Biloel]] and [[Lamkoenjit]] — evacuated on [[12 April]] [[1896]]. The blokhuis at [[Senelop]] and [[Lamsoet]] — unsuccessfully relieved on [[17 April]] [[1896]]. The enemy's use of fire-arrows against blokhuis Aloer Oe is recorded.

## Significance

The blokhuis is the material expression of the Dutch "forward" policy of projecting presence into the Achehnese moekims through small exposed posts. Its vulnerability — demonstrated at [[Aloer Oe]], [[Senelop]] and [[Lamsoet]] — and its high cost in garrison troops made it the principal casualty of [[Teuku Umar]]'s defection. The systematic evacuation of April [[1896]] marked the abandonment of the buitenlinie as a strategic concept and the adoption of the more concentrated geconcentreerde linie as the Dutch defensive perimeter. The blokhuis as an institution would persist — Van Heutsz's later *pacificatie* policy still relied on small fortified posts — but its role would be subordinated to the mobile column. The blokhuis's centrality to the spring [[1896]] crisis makes it one of the defining institutions of the period covered by Kruisheer.

## Account in Hooijer's *De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh* ([[1897]])

A **blokhuis** was a small blockhouse of the type used in [[Groot-Atjeh]] to link the Dutch temporary posts outside the *[[Geconcentreerde linie|geconcentreerde linie]]* between [[1893]] and [[1896]]; twenty-three of them formed the connecting chain, and they were garrisoned not by Dutch troops but by the *[[Legioen van T. Djohan (T. Oemar's Legion)|legioen]]* of [[Teukoe Oemar|Toekoe Djohan]] or by friendly Achehnese.

## The system

[[G. B. Hooijer]] recorded that at Oemar's request the Dutch occupied temporary posts outside their line and spread their military power over a wider area, and that *de 23 blokhuizen, die deze posten verbonden, waren door zijn legioen of door bevriende Atjehers bezet*.

## Unreliability

The arrangement was fragile. Near [[Anagaloeng]], one *blokhuis* garrisoned by the people of [[Toekoe Moeda Baid]] and under his supervision fell or was played into the hands of the *[[Moslemin]]* a few hours after a Dutch officer's inspection had found everything in order. On the morning of [[29 March]] [[1896]] the *blokhuizen* held by friendly Achehnese near [[Biloel]] were found abandoned — one of the first signs of the defection of [[Teukoe Oemar]]. In [[April]] [[1896]] the Dutch themselves burnt the *blokhuizen* near [[Senelop]] and [[Lam Barih|Lambarih]] before lifting those posts.

## Effect where reliable

At [[Lam Soet]], the completion of the *blokhuis* at [[Lampaleuh]] made the neighbourhood quiet and safe, so that the *[[Mitrailleur|mitrailleur]]* mounted there seldom or never had to be used.
