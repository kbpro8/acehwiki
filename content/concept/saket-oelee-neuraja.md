The **sakét oelèe neuraja** (in [[J. Kreemer|Kreemer]]'s spelling; variant *s.o. neurajeuq*; literally "illness at the onset of maturity" — *ziekte bij het begin van de wasdom*) is the Atjehnese concept of a critical maturation-crisis through which, according to the Atjeher, every buffalo (like all other animals and humans) must pass before it is adult. The crisis shows no specific disease-symptoms but is recognised, in buffaloes, by "the falling-out of the nest-hairs" (*het uitvallen der nestharen*); if the animal does not pass this crisis, all sorts of illnesses can ensue. The concept is recorded in [[J. Kreemer|Kreemer]]'s [[Kreemer's De Karbouw ([[1956]])|*De Karbouw* ([[1956]])]] (p. 172) — citing [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]]'s [[Snouck Hurgronje's De Atjehers ([[1893]]-94)|*De Atjehers*]] (vol. II, p. 52) and [[Kreemer's Atjeh ([[1922]]-23)|Kreemer's *Atjeh*]] (vol. II, p. 624).

## The concept
The sakét oelèe neuraja passage in *De Karbouw* (p. 172) reads:

> *"Volgens de Atjeher moet iedere karbouw, evenals alle andere dieren en mensen, vóórdat hij volwassen is, een soort krisis doormaken, die *sakét oelèe neuraja* (of *s.o. neurajeuq*, d.i. 'ziekte bij het begin van de wasdom') wordt genoemd, maar geen bepaalde ziekte-symptomen vertoont. Bij buffels heet dat kritieke moment 'het uitvallen der nestharen.' Doorstaat het dier die krisis niet, dan kunnen allerlei ziekten daarvan het gevolg zijn."*
The structural elements are:
1. **Universality**: every buffalo — like all other animals and humans — must pass through the crisis before adulthood.
2. **Name**: *sakét oelèe neuraja* (or *s.o. neurajeuq*), literally "illness at the onset of maturity" (*ziekte bij het begin van de wasdom*).
3. **Symptomatology**: no specific disease-symptoms; the crisis is recognised, in buffaloes, by "the falling-out of the nest-hairs" (*het uitvallen der nestharen*).
4. **Consequence of failure**: if the animal does not pass this crisis, all sorts of illnesses can ensue.

## Significance
The *sakét oelèe neuraja* is significant for the encyclopedia of [[Acheh]] history as a documented instance of the Atjehnese folk-veterinary concept of a critical maturation-crisis — a concept that frames the buffalo's transition to adulthood as a ritual-magical passage rather than as a merely physiological one. The concept's universality (every buffalo, like all other animals and humans, must pass through it) and its symptom-free character (recognised only by the falling-out of the nest-hairs) document a folk-veterinary taxonomy in which the buffalo's life-cycle was integrated with a wider conceptual scheme of maturation-crisis applying to all animate beings. The concept is documented by [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] in [[Snouck Hurgronje's De Atjehers ([[1893]]-94)|*De Atjehers*]] (vol. II, p. 52) and by [[J. Kreemer|Kreemer]] in [[Kreemer's Atjeh ([[1922]]-23)|*Atjeh*]] (vol. II, p. 624), and is re-cited in [[Kreemer's De Karbouw ([[1956]])|*De Karbouw*]] (p. 172) as one of the principal documents of the Atjehnese folk-veterinary conceptualisation of the buffalo's life-cycle.
