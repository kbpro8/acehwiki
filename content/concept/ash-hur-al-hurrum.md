**ash‑hur al‑hurrum** is an Arabic term meaning “the forbidden months” (the months during which warfare was traditionally prohibited). In the *Maleise tekst van de Burda*, the phrase appears unchanged, reflecting its status as a direct borrowing that resisted Malay paraphrase.

## Usage in the Text

The term is listed among the Arabic words that were taken over verbatim because they were difficult to translate, alongside [[cibbän]] and [[rakham]].

## Significance

Its inclusion illustrates the penetration of Islamic legal concepts into Acehnese Malay vocabulary, highlighting the role of Arabic religious terminology in shaping moral discourse.
