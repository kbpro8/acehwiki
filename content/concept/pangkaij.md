**Pangkaij** (English: "Acehnese advance-payment system") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Atjehsch voorschotten-systeem (advance/credit system) for trade; has heavier burdens attached than the Chinese voorschotten-system; used on Noord- and Oostkust.

## Translation

Acehnese advance-payment system

## Description

Atjehsch voorschotten-systeem (advance/credit system) for trade; has heavier burdens attached than the Chinese voorschotten-system; used on Noord- and [[Atjeh's Oostkust (East Coast of Aceh)|Oostkust]].

## Significance

The term *Pangkaij* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
