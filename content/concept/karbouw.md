## Translation

carabao / water buffalo

## Description

Carabao/water buffalo; dominant in [[Westkust]] (marshlands); [[1937]] total 97,958 in Aceh; wild herds exist (formed during [[Actie-tijden|actie-tijden]]); [[Simaloer]] has degenerated smaller race without [[Tayl|tail]]-tuft. Used as ploegvee (plow animal).

## Significance

The term *karbouw* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
