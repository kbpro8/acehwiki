**amarah** is the Acehnese term for “anger”, derived from the Arabic *‘amārah* “to incite”. It appears on page [[88]] in the glossary, linked to Qur’an XII: III555 (verse reference in the source).

## Definition
Used in moral treatises to caution against uncontrolled *amarah*, which can lead to *sin* and *disorder*.

## Example
The entry cites a Qur’anic verse to illustrate the theological grounding of the concept.

## Significance
The term demonstrates how Quranic moral vocabulary was localized in Acehnese preaching.
