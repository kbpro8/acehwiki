**Sjahid (vs. atjeh-moord)** is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Belief that by murdering a European the perpetrator ascends directly to heaven; central to the atjeh-moord motivation.

## Description

Belief that by murdering a European the perpetrator ascends directly to heaven; central to the [[Atjeh-moord|atjeh-moord]] motivation.

## Significance

The term *Sjahid (vs. atjeh-moord)* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
