**Kafir** (English: "Infidel (Dutch colonizer)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. Term used by Acehnese for the hated Dutch colonizer; agricultural advice initially rejected as interference by the 'gehate kafir'.

## Translation

Infidel (Dutch colonizer)

## Description

Term used by Acehnese for the hated Dutch colonizer; agricultural advice initially rejected as interference by the 'gehate kafir'.

## Significance

The term *Kafir* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
