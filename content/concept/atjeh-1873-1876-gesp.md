The **Atjeh 1873–1876 gesp** (Atjeh 1873–1876 clasp) was the bar or clasp worn on the ribbon of the [[Eerekruis voor belangrijke krijgsverrichtingen|Eerekruis voor belangrijke krijgsverrichtingen]] (the so-called Expeditiekruis) to denote service in the Atjeh expeditions of [[1873]]–[[1876]]. The clasp was the principal Atjeh clasp, covering the period of the [[First Aceh Expedition (1873)|Eerste Atjeh-expeditie]] of [[April 1873|April]] [[1873]], the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)|Tweede Atjeh-expeditie]] of [[December 1873|December]] [[1873]]–[[January 1874|January]] [[1874]], and the prolonged mopping-up operations of [[1874]]–[[1876]]. [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Generaal Snijders]] was granted the right to wear the clasp by grant of [[17 March]] [[17 March]] [[1877]] in respect of his Atjeh service of [[1874]]–[[1875]].
