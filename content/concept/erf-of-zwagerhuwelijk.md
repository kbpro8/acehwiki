**erf- of zwagerhuwelijk** (English: "levirate marriage (Gajo)") is a term or concept described in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in relation to [[Aceh]]. In Gajo, by levirate marriage, the wife passes to a brother or other male relative on the husband's death.

## Translation

levirate marriage ([[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]])

## Description

In [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]], by levirate marriage, the wife passes to a brother or other male relative on the husband's death.

## Significance

The term *erf- of zwagerhuwelijk* is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the cultural, administrative, or institutional vocabulary of [[Aceh]].
