**Sama mendjadi satoe**, a Acehnese association, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Later Acehnese association (after Sarikat Atjeh) founded with governor's encouragement; now also defunct.

## Type

Acehnese association

## Description

Later Acehnese association (after [[Founding of Sarikat Atjeh and 'Sama mendjadi satoe'|Sarikat Atjeh]]) founded with governor's encouragement; now also defunct.

## Significance

[[Founding of Sarikat Atjeh and 'Sama mendjadi satoe'|Sama mendjadi satoe]] is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
