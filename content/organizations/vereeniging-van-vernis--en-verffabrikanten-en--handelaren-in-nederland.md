**The Vereeniging van Vernis- en Verffabrikanten en -Handelaren in Nederland** — the Association of Varnish and Paint Manufacturers and Traders in the Netherlands, abbreviated *V.V.V.F.* — was the Dutch trade association that, in [[1930]], represented the 28 principal varnish and paint manufacturers and traders of the Netherlands. The association is named in the [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet of 1930]] as the partner of the [[Afdeeling Handelsmuseum]] in the [[1930]] market-reception survey of Atjeh-turpentine, and as the addressee of the [[Circulaire of 5 May 1930 to the V.V.V.F.|circular letter of 5 May 1930]] by which sample *H.M. no. 1607* was distributed to its members.

## The 1924 consultation

The [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet]] records that the *V.V.V.F.* had first been consulted on the quality of Atjeh-turpentine in [[1924]], when the [[Afdeeling Handelsmuseum]] *met het Bestuur Uwer Vereeniging van gedachten wisselde over de kwaliteit van de Sumatraansche terpentijnolie, afkomstig van Pinus Merkusii* — *exchanged thoughts with the Board of your Association on the quality of the Sumatran turpentine oil, derived from Pinus Merkusii*. The *V.V.V.F.* had then *de reuk van die terpentijnolie te zeer afwijkende geacht van dien van zuivere terpentijnolie uit Frankrijk of Noord-Amerika* — *considered the odour of that turpentine oil too different from that of pure turpentine oil from France or North America*. The 1924 consultation had thus produced a negative evaluation of the trial Atjeh-turpentine — an evaluation that the [[1930]] pamphlet sought to revisit.

## The 1930 survey

The [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet]] records that, in [[1930]], the Afdeeling Handelsmuseum again approached the *V.V.V.F.* — this time with the proposal that the association *onder hare (28) leden een enquête zou houden* (would hold a survey among its (28) members). The Afdeeling Handelsmuseum supplied the necessary turpentine samples — *voorzien van een toelichtende circulaire* (provided with an explanatory circular) — and the *V.V.V.F.* distributed them among its members. Eleven members *verstrekten een beoordeeling van het hun toegezonden monster* (provided an evaluation of the sample sent to them), and these eleven evaluations — *gerangschikt naar de ledenlijst van de V.V.V.F.* — are preserved in the pamphlet as the principal documentary record of the European market reception of factory-scale Atjeh-turpentine.

## The eleven evaluations

The [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet]] records the eleven evaluations in detail — they are the principal documentary record of the European market reception of factory-scale Atjeh-turpentine. The evaluations are numbered I to XI in the pamphlet, and they are arranged *gerangschikt naar de ledenlijst van de V.V.V.F.* (arranged according to the membership list of the V.V.V.F.). The general verdict is summarised by Spoor in the closing paragraph of the pamphlet: *Het pleit geheel gewonnen heeft de Atjeh-terpentijn nog niet, maar vele beoordeelaars laten niet onduidelijk doorschemeren, dat het tenslotte ook een prijzenkwestie zou zijn, of zij het Indische product in hun bedrijf kunnen toepassen* — *The Atjeh-turpentine has not yet fully won the case, but many evaluators let it be understood not unclearly that, in the end, it would also be a question of price whether they could apply the Indian product in their business*.

## The 28 members

The [[Circulaire of 5 May 1930 to the V.V.V.F.|Circulaire of 5 May 1930]] records that the *V.V.V.F.* had 28 members in [[1930]]. The eleven evaluations preserved in the pamphlet thus represent approximately 39% of the membership — a substantial response rate for a voluntary survey, and one that the Afdeeling Handelsmuseum judged sufficient to establish the general verdict on the market reception of the factory-scale product.

## Significance

The V.V.V.F.'s significance in the historiography of colonial Aceh is as the Dutch trade association that represented the principal European market for Atjeh-turpentine, and as the partner of the [[Afdeeling Handelsmuseum]] in the [[1930]] market-reception survey. The eleven evaluations preserved in the [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet of 1930]] are the principal documentary record of the European market reception of factory-scale Atjeh-turpentine, and the V.V.V.F.'s 1924 negative evaluation of the trial product was the principal obstacle that the [[1930]] evaluation sought to overcome.
