**Soerabaya** was a steam warship of the [[Marine (Dutch Navy)|Royal Netherlands Navy]] that served as the ship of the naval commander — Kolonel Jhr. [[Van Rensselaar Bowier|van Rensselaar Bowier]] — in the sea-borne expedition that carried the [[Lamtoba column (1879)|Lamtoba column]] from [[Oleh-leh]] and landed it at [[Lampanas]] on [[27 July]] [[1879]], as recorded in [[W. Cool]] and [[G. B. Hooijer]]'s *[[Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis (Cool & Hooijer, 1889)|Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis]]* ([[1889]]).

## The passage of 26 July 1879

At the embarkation, the Z. M. Stoomschip Soerabaya received the staff of the expedition, the artillery, the staff and three companies of the [[9e Bataljon Infanterie]], and the personnel of the recording party. Generaal [[General van der Heijden|van der Heijden]] himself was present on board, where the Kolonel held the command; in his spacious cabin the Colonel offered the General and the staff the *rijsttafel* — a European calf, fattened on board, slaughtered for the occasion — while in the longroom the ship's officers entertained the land force at their own table. Long after the General had left the ship, and after all the boats had steamed away from the roadstead of Oleh-leh at four o'clock, toasts to the land- and sea-service were still being drunk on board; at half past eight in the evening, in bright moonlight, she anchored with the squadron on the roadstead of Lampanas.

## The landing

On the morning of [[27 July]] [[1879]] the Soerabaya was the conduct of the whole landing: about her gathered the sloops of the several ships, and on her afterdeck under the awning stood the column-commandant with his officers and the Kolonel Bowier, watching the landing point. Two Achehnese *praus* with their dark-brown sails, a silver streak behind them, cut across the water towards her where she lay. It was from her that the trains of boats towed by the steam barkasses were swung to the strand, and upon her deck the last greetings were exchanged when, the landing accomplished, the column marched for the interior.
