**Japanese concern (in Alaslanden coffee)**, a Foreign capital plantation concern, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Coffee cultivation in the Alaslanden is largely in the hands of a Japanese concern.

## Type

Foreign capital plantation concern

## Description

Coffee cultivation in the [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]] is largely in the hands of a Japanese concern.

## Significance

Japanese concern (in Alaslanden coffee) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
