**Indië Weerbaar** (“Defensible Indies”) was the movement for the military self-defence of the Netherlands Indies for which the former Maréchaussée officer [[W. V. Rhemrev]] acted as organiser, travelling to the Netherlands with a deputation in [[1917]].

## Rhemrev's part

The [[1930]] jubilee number of *[[Het Nieuws van den Dag voor Nederlandsch-Indië]]* records in its portrait notice of Rhemrev that "in 1917 ging de heer Rhemrev als organisator van de beweging *Indië Weerbaar* met een deputatie naar Holland" — in 1917 Mr. Rhemrev went to Holland with a deputation as organiser of the *Indië Weerbaar* movement.

The notice supplies no further particulars of the movement's programme, but places the episode in Rhemrev's career between his service with the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* in [[Atjeh]] and the [[Gajolanden|Gajo]] and [[Alaslanden|Alas]] lands and his later civil activity as co-founder of the *[[Vereeniging Nederlandsch-Indische Jaarbeurs]]* and promoter of the *[[Technische Hoogeschool te Bandoeng]]*.
