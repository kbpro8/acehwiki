**N.V. 'Vrijehaven en Kolenstation Sabang'**, a Harbor company, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Company to which the interests of de Lange & Co.

## Type

Harbor company

## Description

Company to which the interests of [[de Lange & Co.]] in [[Sabang]] were transferred. Operated the free port, coal station, dry docks, workshops, ice factory, water supply, hospital, and hotel at [[Sabang]].

## Significance

N.V. 'Vrijehaven en Kolenstation Sabang' is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
