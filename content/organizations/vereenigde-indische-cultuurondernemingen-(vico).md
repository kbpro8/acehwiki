**The Vereenigde Indische Cultuurondernemingen** — the VICO, the United Indies Cultivation Enterprises — was a Dutch planting-company active in the *onderafdeeling* [[Langsa|Langsa]] of the *afdeeling* [[Oostkust van Atjeh|Oostkust van Atjeh]], founded with predominantly Belgian capital. The VICO holds the estates [[Langsa (estate)|Langsa]] (to be distinguished from the government rubber-estate) and [[Kemoening|Kemoening]] in Langsa — the two rubber-lands are under one management and have a factory together. The VICO also holds the estate [[Damar Sipoet|Damar Sipoet]] with a thousand *bouw* of rubber. In Langsa the VICO also held the land-grant at the Gajo-weg, partly together with a Japanese company, before the land was given back to the *landschap* in [[1922]].
