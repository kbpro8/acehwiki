**Marine Corps in Aceh** was the amphibious infantry and naval landing contingent of the Royal Netherlands Navy that served alongside the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] during coastal invasions and interior campaigns against the [[Aceh Sultanate]] between [[1873]] and [[1876]].

## Role in the First and Second Expeditions

During the [[First Aceh Expedition]] in April [[1873]], marine detachments covered the debarkation at [[Meuraxa]] on [[8 April]] and fought in the storming of coastal redoubts and the [[Battle of Baiturrahman Mosque]]. During the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] in December [[1873]], marines secured the beachhead at [[Koerong Raba Bay]] under General [[J. van Swieten]].

## Mountain Fighting Under Lieutenant Colonel Meijer

During [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]] in December [[1875]], the [[Marine Corps in Aceh]] formed part of the elite column commanded by [[Lieutenant Colonel Meijer]]. Marines fought alongside the [[3rd Infantry Battalion]] during the [[Crossing of Blankala Pass]], capturing mountain defiles and descending into the [[IV Moekims]].
