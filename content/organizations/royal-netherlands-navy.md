**Royal Netherlands Navy** (Dutch: *Koninklijke Marine*) was the maritime arm of the Kingdom of the Netherlands, responsible for protecting Dutch overseas interests, including the Dutch East Indies.

## Role in Aceh

During the 1870s the navy provided the essential sea‑power for operations against the Sultanate of [[Aceh]]. Captains such as [[J.J. Korndörffer]] reported on voyages from the Netherlands via [[Batavia]] to the Aceh coast, and naval vessels supplied artillery support for marine and army units during the [[Aceh Expedition 1875–1876]].

## Fleet Composition

The fleet employed steam‑powered warships, gunboats, and transport vessels capable of navigating the shallow waters of the Aceh coastline and the Strait of Malacca.
