**The Regeeringscommissaris** — also referred to as *Government Commissioner* or, in full, *Regeeringscommissaris voor Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden* — was the emergency civil-military office created by the Dutch government and dispatched from the Netherlands to [[Aceh War|Atjeh]] after [[Teuku Umar]]'s [[Defection of Teuku Umar (29 March 1896)|defection of 29 March 1896]], superseding the [[Civiel en Militair Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Civiel en Militair Gouverneur]] and concentrating both civil and military authority in the hands of a senior Lieutenant-General directly answerable to the Governor-General and the Minister of Colonies. Held by Luitenant-Generaal J. A. Vetter from [[7 April]] to [[28 June]] [[1896]], the office was abolished on [[28 June]] [[1896]] when Vetter departed per the stoomschip *Coen* to Batavia. As Regeeringscommissaris, Vetter issued the [[Proclamation of the Regeeringscommissaris (12 April 1896) (Bijlage III/V)|first proclamation of 12 April 1896]] announcing the purpose of his arrival, ordered the [[Evacuation of Biloel and Lamkoenjit (12 April 1896)|evacuation of Biloel and Lamkoenjit]], decided to lift the *buitenlinie* posts, issued the [[Proclamation of Lt. Gen. Vetter to the heads and people of the 3 sagi of Groot-Atjeh (27 April 1896) (Bijlage VII)|second proclamation of 27 April 1896]] announcing operations against T. Oemar Melaboch, and ordered the [[Disarmament of Buitenlinie Chiefs (mid-June 1896)|disarmament of buitenlinie chiefs in mid-June 1896]].

## Background and Formation

The office of Regeeringscommissaris voor Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden was an emergency mechanism of the Dutch colonial government, used to send a senior official — typically a senior general — directly from the Netherlands or from the seat of government in Batavia to a troubled territory, with full authority to override the local administration. The decision to dispatch a Regeeringscommissaris to Atjeh was made by the Governor-General [[Jhr. C. H. A. van der Wijck]] in late March [[1896]], in response to the collapse of the Dutch allied policy embodied in the *legioen* of [[Teuku Umar]] and the imminent danger to the *geconcentreerde Linie (Concentrated Line)|geconcentreerde linie* itself. The choice fell on Luitenant-Generaal J. A. Vetter, Army Commander, who departed Batavia with Kolonel J. W. Stemfoort and Major of the General Staff J. F. Breijer aboard the *Carpentier* and *Bantam*, carrying the [[9e Bataljon Infanterie|9e Bataljon]] and the news that Generaal-Majoor C. Deykerhoff was being honorably relieved of his functions as Civiel en Militair Gouverneur.

## Role in the Atjeh War

The Regeeringscommissaris period ([[7 April]]–[[28 June]] [[1896]]) was the most consequential phase of the spring [[1896]] campaign. On [[7 April]] [[1896]] Vetter arrived; on [[8 April]] [[1896]] he issued secret operational instructions (*geheim schrijven*) for the [[Relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit (8 April 1896)|relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit]] under Kolonel Stemfoort. On [[12 April]] [[1896]] he issued his [[Proclamation of the Regeeringscommissaris (12 April 1896) (Bijlage III/V)|first proclamation]] (Bijlage III/V) to the heads and population of Atjeh, announcing the purpose of his arrival. The same day he ordered the [[Evacuation of Biloel and Lamkoenjit (12 April 1896)|evacuation of Biloel and Lamkoenjit]]. He decided to lift the *buitenlinie* posts, with the [[Evacuation of Anak-Galoeng, Lam-Barih, Lam-Soet, Senelop (17 April 1896)|17 April evacuation of Anak-Galoeng, Lam-Barih, Lam-Soet, and Senelop]] and the [[Evacuation of Kroeng-Gloempang, Tjot-Rang, Toengkoeb (20 April 1896)|20 April evacuation of Kroeng-Gloempang, Tjot-Rang, and Toengkoeb]]. On [[26 April]] [[1896]] he issued the [[Government Resolution of 26 April 1896 No. 159/11 (Bijlage VI)|Besluit]] (Bijlage VI) dismissing T. Djohan Pahalawan from government service. On [[27 April]] [[1896]] he issued his [[Proclamation of Lt. Gen. Vetter to the heads and people of the 3 sagi of Groot-Atjeh (27 April 1896) (Bijlage VII)|second proclamation]] (Bijlage VII) to the heads and people of the three sagi of [[Groot-Atjeh (Great Aceh)|Groot-Atjeh]], announcing operations against T. Oemar Melaboch. He supervised the [[Bombardment of Lampisang (27 April – 23 May 1896)|bombardment of Lampisang from 27 April to 23 May]], the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day offensive of 23–25 May]] against Umar's stelling at [[Lam-Pisang]], the [[Razing of Lam-Asan (30 May – 2 June 1896)|razing of Lam-Asan of 30 May–2 June]], the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|Lam-Krak tuchtiging of 9–10 June]], and the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|V Moekims Montassik tuchtiging of 16–17 June]]. In mid-June [[1896]] he ordered the [[Disarmament of Buitenlinie Chiefs (mid-June 1896)|disarmament of buitenlinie chiefs]]. On [[26 May]] [[1896]] he issued a *dagorder* expressing satisfaction at the success against Umar's "*sterke, geoefende, goed versterkte en goed bewapende bende*" (strong, well-trained, well-fortified, and well-armed band). On [[27 June]] [[1896]] he held his [[Vetter's Farewell Parade (27 June 1896)|farewell parade]]; on [[28 June]] [[1896]] he departed per stoomschip *Coen* to Batavia, and the office was abolished.

## Composition and Strength

The office of Regeeringscommissaris voor Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden was held by Luitenant-Generaal J. A. Vetter (*Commandant van het Leger*), from [[7 April]] to [[28 June]] [[1896]]. He was assisted by Major of the General Staff J. F. Breijer (later promoted Luitenant-kolonel der infanterie) as his adjudant. Civil affairs during the Regeeringscommissaris period were temporarily exercised by Resident K. F. H. van Langen, military command by Kolonel J. W. Stemfoort — both under Vetter's orders. The office's office is recorded as a separate organization in the spring [[1896]] administrative structure.

## Significance

The Regeeringscommissaris period was the most decisive phase of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] in the spring of [[1896]]. By superseding the discredited [[Civiel en Militair Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Civiel en Militair Gouverneur]] and concentrating authority in the hands of the Army Commander himself, the Dutch government broke the administrative inertia that had followed Umar's defection and authorized the wholesale counter-offensive — evacuation of the buitenlinie, bombardment of Lampisang, three-day offensive, Lam-Krak tuchtiging, V Moekims Montassik tuchtiging — that defined the spring campaign. Vetter's two proclamations (Bijlage III/V of [[12 April]] [[1896]] and Bijlage VII of [[27 April]] [[1896]]) and his [[Government Resolution of 26 April 1896 No. 159/11 (Bijlage VI)|Besluit of 26 April 1896]] (Bijlage VI) dismissing T. Djohan Pahalawan formalized the rupture with the pre-defection Dutch policy. His [[Departure of Vetter and Restoration of Civil and Military Governor (28 June 1896)|departure on 28 June 1896]] marked the end of the Regeeringscommissaris period and the restoration — under Stemfoort — of the unified Civiel en Militair Gouverneur office.
