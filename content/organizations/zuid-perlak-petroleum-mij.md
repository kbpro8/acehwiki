**The Zuid-Perlak Petroleum-Mij** — the *South-Perlak Petroleum Maatschappij* — was the *befaamde* — *famous* — oil-company whose terrains lie at [[Rantau Pandjang|Rantau Pandjang]] in the *landschap* of [[Peureula (Perlak)|Peureula (Perlak)]] in the *onderafdeeling* [[Langsa|Langsa]] of the *afdeeling* [[Oostkust van Atjeh|Oostkust van Atjeh]], alongside the terrains of the [[Perlak Petroleum-Mij|Perlak Petroleum-Mij]] exploited by the [[Bataafsche Petroleum-Mij|Bataafsche Petroleum-Mij]]. Broersma mentions the Zuid-Perlak Petroleum-Mij in passing as the *befaamde* company whose terrains lie at Rantau Pandjang, without giving further details of its operation.

## Account from *Vijftig jaren economische staatkunde in Atjeh* ([[1923]])

Author [[J. Langhout|Joh. Langhout]] records the Zuid Perlak Maatschappij as the holder of the foremost of the province's remaining mineral concessions when — under Governor [[H. N. A. Swart|Swart]], whose government explored for petroleum in [[Peusangan River|Peusangan]] without exploitation following — the province's mineral concessions were not extended, "hetwelk te betreuren viel" ("which was to be deplored"). The Zuid Perlak concession was exploited "by de Koninklijke" — the [[Koninklijke Petroleum Maatschappij]] — and some years before [[1923]] the Zuid Perlak Maatschappij transferred its works to the [[Bataafsche Petroleum Maatschappij]], which already exploited other Perlak terrains. The Zuid-Perlak enterprise stands in Langhout's chapter as part of the petroleum complex which he reckoned "one of the foremost factors of the pacification of this part of the coast tract" (see [[Bataafsche Petroleum Maatschappij]]).
