**The Zuid-Perlak Petroleum-Mij** — the *South-Perlak Petroleum Maatschappij* — was the *befaamde* — *famous* — oil-company whose terrains lie at [[Rantau Pandjang|Rantau Pandjang]] in the *landschap* of [[Peureula (Perlak)|Peureula (Perlak)]] in the *onderafdeeling* [[Langsa|Langsa]] of the *afdeeling* [[Oostkust van Atjeh|Oostkust van Atjeh]], alongside the terrains of the [[Perlak Petroleum-Mij|Perlak Petroleum-Mij]] exploited by the [[Bataafsche Petroleum-Mij|Bataafsche Petroleum-Mij]]. Broersma mentions the Zuid-Perlak Petroleum-Mij in passing as the *befaamde* company whose terrains lie at Rantau Pandjang, without giving further details of its operation.
