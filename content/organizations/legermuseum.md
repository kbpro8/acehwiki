**Legermuseum**, a museum, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Army museum that preserves pieces of the original 'Kölee boom' from in front of the Meuseugit Raja at Koeta Radja.

## Type

museum

## Description

Army museum that preserves pieces of the original 'Kölee boom' from in front of the [[Meuseugit Raja]] at [[Koeta-Radja|Koeta Radja]].

## Significance

Legermuseum is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
