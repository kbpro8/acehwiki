**Hof van Politie en Justitie** was the colonial judicial body responsible for legal proceedings against rebels. Governor **[[Governor Van Hoogenheim|Van Hoogenheim]]** noted on **[[26 January]]** that the court was **involledig** (incomplete) and sought the appointment of impartial judges from the Netherlands.

## Role in the Campaign
The court oversaw the sentencing of rebels at **[[Dageraad]]**, including death sentences and other punishments.
