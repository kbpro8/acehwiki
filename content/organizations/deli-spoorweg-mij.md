**Deli-Spoorweg Mij.**, a Private railway company, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Took over transport from Besitang southward to Medan and beyond.

## Type

Private railway company

## Description

Took over transport from [[Besitang]] southward to [[Medan]] and beyond. Connection at [[Besitang]] with the Atjeh-Staatsspoor completed in [[1919]].

## Significance

Deli-Spoorweg Mij. is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
