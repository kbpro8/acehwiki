**Krankzinnigengesticht (Sabang)**, a psychiatric hospital, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Psychiatric hospital near the road to the kazematten at Sabang.

## Type

psychiatric hospital

## Description

Psychiatric hospital near the road to the kazematten at [[Sabang]]. Houses ~[[1300]] patients. Worktherapy (werktherapie) is applied with favorable results. Staff: 2 doctors, 4 European and 34 native nurses/verplegers, plus 150 oppasseressen/oppassers (attendants).

## Significance

Krankzinnigengesticht (Sabang) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
