**Sabangmaatschappij**, a Company, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Began construction of a landing field near Sabang in 1928; vast labor and high costs involved.

## Type

Company

## Description

Began construction of a landing field near [[Sabang]] in [[1928]]; vast labor and high costs involved.

## Significance

Sabangmaatschappij is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
