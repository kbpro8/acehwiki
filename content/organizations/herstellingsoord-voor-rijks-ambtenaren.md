**The Herstellingsoord voor Rijks-Ambtenaren** — the convalescent home for state civil servants — was the projected institution of the *Vereeniging tot Ondersteuning van Burgerlijke Ambtenaren en Beambten in 's Rijks dienst* (Association for the Support of Civil Officials and Employees in State Service) for whose founding capital [[P. H. R. Beuming]] published *[[Schetsen uit den strijd op Groot-Atjeh (1911)|Schetsen uit den strijd op Groot-Atjeh]]* in December [[1911]].

## The appeal

The title-page of the book states that it was *uitgegeven ten bate van het te stichten Herstellingsoord voor Rijks-Ambtenaren* — published for the benefit of the convalescent home for state civil servants that was to be founded — and the price was set as low as possible, at f 0.10 a copy, for that reason. Beuming stated that the net proceeds would be handed to the board of the philanthropic association *als bijdrage tot het stichtingskapitaal voor het Herstellingsoord*.

In his preface he described himself as *gedreven door een onweerstaanbaar verlangen om naar bescheiden krachten mede te werken aan de verwezenlijking van een ideaal* — driven by an irresistible desire to co-operate according to his modest powers in the realisation of an ideal — namely the founding of the home, and set beside it his second object, that of making more general the public interest in and sympathy for the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Netherlands Indies Army]].

He connected the two purposes explicitly: *Waar geregeld velen uit het leger in burgerlijken Staatsdienst treden, hoop ik, dat ook onze militairen met mijn streven kunnen sympathiseeren* — since many regularly pass from the army into civil state service, he hoped that soldiers too could sympathise with his effort, and that the sketches would find a good reception both inside and outside the barracks.

## Significance for the Aceh literature

The *Herstellingsoord* is the occasion of the book and therefore of the preservation of Beuming's Atjeh material. His narratives of [[The sharpshooter of Biloel|the sharpshooter of Biloel]], the [[Three-day battle in the Beradin pass (23-25 May 1896)|three-day battle in the Beradin pass]], the [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|surprise of Anak Galoeng]], the [[Patrol to the Goeah Teroes on the Glé Betoes (25 September 1897)|patrol to the Goeah Teroes]] and his portrait of [[Pastoor Verbraak]] were all written and printed as a charitable fund-raising exercise for a Dutch civil-service benevolent society.
