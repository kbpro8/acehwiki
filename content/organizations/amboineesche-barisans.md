**Amboineesche Barisans** were indigenous troops from Ambon serving within the Royal Netherlands East Indies Army (KNIL) during the Aceh War.

## Role in the Conflict
These troops were highly valued for their combat effectiveness and loyalty. They fought alongside European forces in the fierce close-quarters engagements at [[Atjeh's Groote Missigit]]. During the [[Second Conquest of the Great Missigit]], the tragic death of an Amboinese fuselier prompted Lieutenant [[J.G. Blanken]] and Captain [[J.H. Huijer]] to vault the enemy walls to recover his body.
