**Coolies**, a labor force, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Manual laborers at the Atjeh-tram workshop (more than half Atjehers), on the Gajoweg construction (who murdered Kapitein Heinzenknecht), and elsewhere.

## Type

labor force

## Description

Manual laborers at the [[Atjeh-tram]] workshop (more than half [[Atjehers]]), on the [[Gajoweg (Gayo Road)|Gajoweg]] construction (who murdered [[Kapitein Heinzenknecht]]), and elsewhere.

## Significance

Coolies is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
