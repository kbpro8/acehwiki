**The Nieuwe Rotterdamsche Courant** was the leading Dutch liberal daily of [[Rotterdam]], which appears in both of the books here treated: as the paper whose report of the resignations of [[J. T. Cremer|J. T. Cremer]] gave [[Pieter Jelles Troelstra|Troelstra]] his charge of [[1897]], and as the paper for which [[C. Graadt van Roggen]] wrote the three letters on [[Acheh|Atjeh]] reprinted in [[1934]].

## In the Atjeh debate of [[1897]]

[[Pieter Jelles Troelstra|Troelstra]] told the chamber on [[16 November]] [[1897]]: "hebben wij niet terstond na de benoeming van dezen Minister in de Nieuwe Rotterdamsche Courant kunnen lezen, dat deze Minister niet alleen was afgetreden als voorzitter van het bestuur van de [[Koninklijke Paketvaart-Maatschappij|Koninklijke]], maar bovendien als voorzitter van commissarissen der [[Deli Maatschappij]], als commissaris van de [[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij|spoorwegmaatschappij]], als commissaris van de [[Senembah Maatschappij|Senembah]] en van de Medan-maatschappij?" From this he inferred that the minister must be particularly well informed about the connection between Dutch capital on the [[Oostkust van Sumatra|East Coast of Sumatra]] and the conquest of Atjeh.

On [[17 November]] Troelstra returned to the paper in another connection, observing that the minister had treated [[Van Kol's motion of 16 November 1897|the motion]] in a manner praised by the *Nieuwe Rotterdamsche Courant* "met die geringschatting... die zij volgens dat blad verdient," — with that disparagement... which, according to that paper, it deserves, and attributing to that disparagement the weakness of the minister's arguments.

## The Graadt van Roggen letters of [[1933]]

The pamphlet *[[Een strijd in Atjeh (1934)]]* reprints three letters written for the *Nieuwe Rotterdamsche Courant* by [[C. Graadt van Roggen]], who after a short period of journalism in the Indies was returning to his former function, "een redacteurschap bij de Nieuwe Rotterdamsche Courant." The foreword to the pamphlet was written by [[H. Wanting]], described on the title-page as "Redacteur \u201eNieuwe Rotterdamsche Courant\u201d." The letters record the conversations aboard the *[[Baloeran]]* with [[J. Tijssen|Dr. J. Tijssen]] on his eye-surgery campaigns in the *[[Kampong|kampongs]]* of Atjeh.
