**The Tamiang Rubber-Syndicate** was a planting-syndicate formed in [[1910]] in the *onderafdeeling* [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] of the *afdeeling* [[Oostkust van Atjeh|Oostkust van Atjeh]], alongside the [[Tamiang Landbouw-syndicaat|Tamiang Landbouw-syndicaat]] of the same year. Both syndicates were part of the first wave of rubber-planting in Tamiang, which began slowly about [[1910]] and accelerated about [[1915]].
