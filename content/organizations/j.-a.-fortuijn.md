**J. A. Fortuijn** was the [[Amsterdam]] publisher and bookseller, of N. Leliestraat 69, who issued in [[1897]] the pamphlet *[[Atjeh in de Tweede Kamer (1897)]]* on behalf of the [[Sociaaldemokratische Arbeiderspartij|Sociaaldemokratische Arbeiderspartij in Nederland]], and who was the party's principal publisher in the years of its first parliamentary representation.

## The Atjeh pamphlet

The title-page of the pamphlet bears the imprint "J. A. Fortuijn, Amsterdam. [[1897]]," with the printing attributed to Masereeuw of [[Rotterdam]] and the price fixed at ten cents. The work reprints the verbatim record of the [[Atjeh debate in the Tweede Kamer (16-17 November 1897)|Atjeh debate]] of [[16 November|16]]–[[17 November]] [[1897]], including the full text of [[Van Kol's motion of 16 November 1897|Van Kol's motion]] and the division list of seventy-two against three.

## Other publications

The last leaf of the pamphlet advertises the party's list, obtainable from the treasurer of the S.D.A.P. at Kwakkeldijk 6, [[Utrecht]], from the administration of *[[De Sociaaldemokraat]]* at Zwaanshals 127, Rotterdam, from Fortuijn at Amsterdam, and through all booksellers. Among the titles are [[Pieter Jelles Troelstra|Troelstra]]'s *Wat willen de Sociaaldemokraten? Antwoord op vijf vragen van Pastoor Arriens* (fifth edition, five cents) and *De S.D.A.P., haar ontstaan, doel en streven* (forty pages); *Neemt deel aan den strijd!* by W. P. G. Helsdingen; a pamphlet by W. H. Vliegen; *Hoe de bezitters den Staat exploiteeren* and *Rijk en arm, of maatschappelijke tegenstellingen* by Rienzi; *Onze aarde zij een paradijs* by Rienzi; *De arbeidskerk* by Trevor; *Kiesrecht en sociaaldemokratie* by Troelstra; *De kerkvaders en de eigendom*; *Tot aanval en verdediging* by W. Liebknecht; *De puinhoopen* by Volney; *De Volksalmanak Vooruit*; *Het kapitalistisch tijdvak* by Leo; and a portrait of Wilhelm Liebknecht.

The same leaf carries the subscription notices for *[[De Sociaaldemokraat]]*, the organ of the party, appearing twice weekly under the editorship of Mr. P. J. Troelstra and W. H. Vliegen, and for *[[De Nieuwe Tijd]]*, both described as published by J. A. Fortuijn, N. Leliestraat 69, Amsterdam.
