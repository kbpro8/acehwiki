**Hoogere Krijgsschool** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[H. N. A. Swart]]

Du Croo drew attention to two peculiarities of this episode. The first was that six weeks of Atjeh service "dat is alles!" — that was the whole of it — for a man who would become one of the Indies' foremost *Buiten-mannen* (Outer-Provinces men); until his promotion to captain in the transitional year [[1898]] Swart obstinately remained on [[Java]] and in the *vredesgarnizoenen* (peace garrisons). The second was that in those young years the pull towards the *buitengewesten* was simply absent: Swart preferred to lay "een draagvlak" for later years, to learn everything he could within the military framework, with the [[Hoogere Krijgsschool]] in the background.
