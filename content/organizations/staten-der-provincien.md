**Staten der Provinciën** was the collective assembly of the seven provinces of the **[[United Provinces (Dutch Republic)]]** that, from **[[1651]]**, assumed supreme authority over the militia and the right to pardon military officials. This centralisation contributed to administrative “knoeierij” and the weakening of the **[[Dutch East India Company|VOC]]**’s grip on the **[[Sultanate of Aceh]]** during the **[[Rampjaar|Disaster Year]]** [[1672]].

## Legislative Powers
The **Staten der Provinciën** could grant clemency to officers, a prerogative that was abused, leading to a decline in military discipline and the neglect of fortifications in Dutch overseas holdings, including Aceh’s strategic ports.
