**S.O.K.**, a oil company, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Oil concern at Pangkalan Brandan, terminus of the pipeline from the BPM oil terrain at Rantau Pandjang (Peureula).

## Type

oil company

## Description

Oil concern at [[Pangkalan Brandan]], terminus of the pipeline from the BPM oil terrain at [[Rantau Pandjang|Rantau Pandjang (Peureula)]].

## Significance

S.O.K. is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
