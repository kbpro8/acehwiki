**Bataafsche Petroleum Maatschappij (B.P.M.)**, a Petroleum company, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Built large fuel tanks at Sabang for oil-fired ships and motor vessels.

## Type

Petroleum company

## Description

Built large fuel tanks at [[Sabang]] for oil-fired ships and motor vessels. Constructed (with [[Peudawa]] [[Zelfbestuur|zelfbestuur]]) the [[1934]] landing field in Idi for aerial mapping of its [[Peureula]]' oil fields.

## Significance

Bataafsche Petroleum Maatschappij (B.P.M.) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
