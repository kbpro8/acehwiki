**Legermuseum te Koetaradja**, a museum, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Army Museum at Koetaradja under excellent leadership of Majoor Doup.

## Type

museum

## Description

Army Museum at Koetaradja under excellent leadership of [[Majoor Doup]]. Grew quickly since founding into a documentary of enormous value. Source for [[Resident Jongejans|Jongejans]]' military section.

## Significance

Legermuseum te Koetaradja is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
