**Atjehsche vereenigingen van rubberplanters (Kaway XVI)**, a agricultural cooperative, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Rubber-planters' associations in the landschap Kaway XVI (Westkust van Atjeh), founded on the initiative of zelfbestuurder Teukoe Tjhi' Mohamad Ali Akbar, who kept them under his leadership.

## Type

agricultural cooperative

## Description

Rubber-planters' associations in the [[landschap]] [[Kaway XVI]] ([[Westkust van Atjeh]]), founded on the initiative of [[zelfbestuurder]] [[Teukoe Tjhi' Mohamad Ali Akbar]], who kept them under his leadership. Made [[Kaway XVI]] the most important rubber producer of the entire [[afdeeling]] [[Westkust]].

## Significance

Atjehsche vereenigingen van rubberplanters (Kaway XVI) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
