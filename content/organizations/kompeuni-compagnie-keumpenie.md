**Kompeuni / Compagnie / Keumpenie**, a Dutch colonial administration / authority in Aceh (popular Acehnese terms), appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Acehnese popular term for the Dutch colonial government/authority.

## Type

Dutch colonial administration / authority in Aceh (popular Acehnese terms)

## Description

Acehnese popular term for the Dutch colonial government/authority. Used throughout the text.

## Significance

Kompeuni / Compagnie / Keumpenie is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
