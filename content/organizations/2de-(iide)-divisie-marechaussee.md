**The 2de (IIde) Divisie Marechaussee** was the Westkust division of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Korps Maréchaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen]] during [[1940]]–[[1942]], under the command of Captain [[L. H. J. Kloprogge|L. H. J. Kloprogge]] at [[Meulaboh|Meulaboh]], and the only Maréchaussee division to conduct sustained Dutch resistance during the [[Japanese invasion of Atjèh|Japanese invasion of March 1942]]. In the [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|Dutch staff history's]] account (Deel VI, Hoofdstuk V), the IIde Divisie is the staff history's principal narrative unit of the Westkust campaign — the formation that evacuated 3,000 women and children over 600 km of Westkust road, fought the [[Battle of Blang Pidië (16 March 1942)|Battle of Blang Pidië]] on [[17 March]] [[17 March 1942|17 March 1942]], and capitulated at [[Lae Boetar|Lae Boetar]] on [[29 March]] [[29 March 1942|29 March 1942]].

## Organisation as of 1 January 1940

As of [[1 January]] [[1940]], the IIde Divisie Marechaussee was grouped as follows (each brigade of Maréchaussee numbering 20 men):

- [[Djeuram|Djeuram]]: 4 brigades
- [[Lam Ie|Lam Ie]]: 4 brigades

**Total**: 8 brigades (about 160 men).

The IIde Divisie was the only Maréchaussee division on the Westkust after the [[1940]] reductions. By December [[1941]] the Westkust Maréchaussee strength was down to 11 brigades — four at [[Koeala Bee|Koeala Bee]] (Iste Divisie), three at Djeuram, and four at [[Meulaboh|Meulaboh]] (one detached to [[Tjalang|Tjalang]]).

## Command

The IIde Divisie was commanded throughout the Westkust campaign by Captain [[L. H. J. Kloprogge|L. H. J. Kloprogge]]. When, on [[10 March]] [[10 March 1942|10 March 1942]], Kloprogge marched with two brigades to relieve [[Tjalang|Tjalang]], he handed acting command of the remaining division to 1st Lt [[A. Zijlmans|A. Zijlmans]]. Lt [[H. R. van Ham|H. R. van Ham]] at [[Djeuram|Djeuram]] commanded the Djeuram detachment.

## Operations of March 1942

The IIde Divisie's operations of March [[1942]] are the staff history's principal Westkust narrative, organised around the [[Evacuation of women and children from the Westkust of Atjeh (March 1942)|evacuation of 3,000 women and children]] from the Westkust outposts to [[Lae Boetar|Lae Boetar]]. The principal engagements were:

- The [[Tjalang|Tjalang]] relief and the march on [[Lam No|Lam No]] ([[10 March|10]]–[[13 March]] [[13 March 1942|13 March]]).
- The [[Battle of Blang Pidië (16 March 1942)|Battle of Blang Pidië]] and the 15-hour ambush march of [[17 March]] [[17 March 1942|17 March]].
- The convoy march to [[Bakongan|Bakongan]] and the Singkel area ([[18 March|18]]–[[29 March]] [[29 March 1942|29 March]]).
- The [[Capitulation at Lae Boetar (29 March 1942)|capitulation at Lae Boetar]] of [[29 March]] [[29 March 1942|29 March]].

## Significance

The IIde Divisie is, in the staff history's Atjeh chapter, the principal Dutch formation of the Westkust campaign, and the only Maréchaussee division to conduct sustained resistance during the Japanese invasion. The staff history's contrast between the IIde Divisie's sustained resistance — achieved under Captain [[L. H. J. Kloprogge|Kloprogge]]'s calm and judicious leadership — and the panic that overtook the *2de Garnizoensbataljon* on the noordkust under Lt-Col [[C. J. Bekkers|Bekkers]] is the compilers' principal evidence that the structural cause of the noordkust collapse was not the Maréchaussee's fighting spirit in general, but the *2de Garnizoensbataljon*'s specific demoralisation.
