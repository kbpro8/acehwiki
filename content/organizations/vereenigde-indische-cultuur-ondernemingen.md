**Vereenigde Indische Cultuur-Ondernemingen** (*Vico*) was plantation company active in [[Langsa]] and elsewhere.

## Holdings

Has enterprise [[Langsa]] (to distinguish government enterprise) which by mostly Belgian capital founded, and [[Kemoening]]. Both rubber lands under one management together factory. Moreover [[Damar Sipoet]] thousand *baoe* rubber. Only oliepalm enterprise in underafdeeling is [[Karang Inouë]] [[1922]] 800 *baoe* plant yet belonging [[Vereenigde Ned. Oliepalm-Cultuur]] then for 24k gulden Sipef. Recently little money handed Japanese company [[Dutch Borneo Plantations Cy]]. [[Blang Simpo]] worthless rubber land returned, [[Aloer Soenasang]] unopened returned, [[Panton Meuriah]] Japanese not maintained etc.

Notes general phenomenon [[Langsa]] much money used quickly become large; when difficulty came no money preservation worse than [[Temiang]]. 12k contractors time now <2k assistants gone management mandoer alone. Situation can change as soon rubber market more favorable.
