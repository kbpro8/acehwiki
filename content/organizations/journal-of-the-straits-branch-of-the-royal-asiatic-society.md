**Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society** (J.S.B.R.ASS) was a learned periodical published in Singapore that featured articles on the languages, history, and cultures of the Malay Peninsula and archipelago. It published an edition of the [[Hikayat Raja-raja Pasai]] edited by [[J. P. Mead]].

## Publication History
The source records that Mead’s edition of the Hikayat Raja-raja Pasai appeared in J.S.B.R.ASS volume 66 (1914). The journal provided a platform for colonial administrators, missionaries, and scholars to disseminate research on Malay manuscripts and epigraphy.

## Contribution to Acehnese Historiography
By making the Hikayat accessible to an English‑reading audience, J.S.B.R.ASS facilitated early scholarly engagement with the history of [[Pasai]] and its successor states in [[Aceh]]. The journal’s articles remain cited in bibliographies of Malay literary texts and in studies of the Islamisation of Sumatra.
