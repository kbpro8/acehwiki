**N.V. Société Financière des Caoutchoucs**, a Plantation concern (mainly Belgian capital), appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Operating on East Coast of Aceh and Sumatra's Oostkust.

## Type

Plantation concern (mainly Belgian capital)

## Description

Operating on [[East Coast of Aceh]] and [[Sumatra's East Coast|Sumatra's Oostkust]]. After [[1926]]-27 disturbances, conducted explorations in [[Malaboeh|Meulaboh]] and [[Singkel]] vlakten resulting in rubber and oilpalm estates in both. Currently exploring for oilpalm lands.

## Significance

N.V. Société Financière des Caoutchoucs is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
