**3rd Infantry Battalion** was a front-line line infantry battalion of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] that played a prominent combat role during [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]] in the [[Aceh Sultanate]] in [[1875]] and [[1876]].

## Strength and Organization

According to official order-of-battle tables in *Atjeh gedurende de tweede expeditie en later* ([[1876]]), on [[26 December]] [[1875]] the battalion consisted of 18 European officers, 392 European rank-and-file, and 182 indigenous soldiers, totaling 592 men.

## Combat Operations Under Pel and Meijer

During the opening offensive of December [[1875]], the [[3rd Infantry Battalion]] operated under the command of [[Lieutenant Colonel Meijer]]. The battalion led the assault during the [[Crossing of Blankala Pass]] and descended into the [[IV Moekims]] alongside marine and artillery detachments.

On [[13 February]] [[1876]], the battalion formed the vanguard of General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]]'s column during the [[March through the XXII Moekims]], engaging Acehnese forces loyal to [[Panglima Polem]] across fortified agricultural settlements.
