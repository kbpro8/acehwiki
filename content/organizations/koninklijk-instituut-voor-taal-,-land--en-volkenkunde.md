The **Koninklijk Instituut voor Taal-, Land- en Volkenkunde** (Royal Institute of Linguistics, Geography and Ethnology) was the Netherlands learned society and library, seated at Galewstraat 159 in 's-[[The Hague|Gravenhage]], whose collections preserved the offprint of *[[Het recht op den grond in Atjeh (De Boer)|Het recht op den grond in Atjeh]]* by [[D. W. N. de Boer]], catalogued as DLP-141 and bearing the accession marks "BIBLIOTHEEK KITLV 0060 4460 60 081470 428."

## Role in the transmission of the Atjehnese land-law literature

The institute's library was the repository through which the technical literature on the *[[Adat|adat]]* of the Netherlands Indies — including the Atjehnese material of [[C. Snouck Hurgronje]], [[C. van Vollenhoven]] and De Boer — was collected, catalogued and made available for comparative study. The stamped address and shelf marks on the De Boer offprint record the passage of the study from the colonial periodical press into the scholarly apparatus of *[[Adatrecht (Van Vollenhoven)|adatrecht]]* research in the Netherlands.

## Significance

The survival of De Boer's study, a short practitioner's paper printed in a periodical, depended on the institute's practice of acquiring and binding offprints of colonial scholarship. The [[Aceh|Atjeh]]-related holdings so preserved constitute a substantial part of the surviving documentation of Atjehnese customary land law.
