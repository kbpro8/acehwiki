**The Dutch Republic** (officially the **Republic of the Seven United Netherlands**) was the sovereign state that governed the **[[Dutch East India Company]]** and its overseas possessions from the late sixteenth century until its dissolution in **[[1795]]**.  During the mid‑seventeenth century, the Republic’s navy, under commanders such as **[[Michiel de Ruyter]]**, protected the **[[Indische retourvloot]]**, which regularly called at **[[Aceh]]** for the import of pepper and other spices.

## Maritime policy and Aceh
The Republic’s commercial policy relied on a monopoly over the spice trade, with **[[Aceh]]** being a key supplier.  Naval engagements such as the **[[Battle of Schooneveld]]** and the **[[Battle of Kijkduin]]** were fought to keep the sea lanes open for the **[[Dutch East India Company]]**’s ships returning from **Aceh** and other parts of the **[[East Indies]]**.
