**G. Kolff & Co.** was a major printing and publishing firm based in [[Batavia]], described in the *Nederlandsch Indië Platen Atlas* ([[1926]]) as the largest such enterprise in the [[Netherlands India|Dutch East Indies]].

## Account from *Nederlandsch Indië Platen Atlas* (1926) — G. Kolff & Co.

The atlas was printed by G. Kolff & Co., as stated on the title page: "Druk van G. Kolff & Co." The firm is also depicted in a plate showing the printing works: "Een gedeelte der plaatdrukkerij van de Firma G. Kolff & Co. te Batavia, de grootste inrichting op dit gebied in Nederlandsch-Indië, waar o.a. dit boekwerk uitgegeven werd." (A section of the plate-printing works of the firm G. Kolff & Co. at Batavia, the largest establishment in this field in the Dutch East Indies, where, among other things, this work was published.)

The atlas also notes that the firm was connected with the Neresco cigar factory in Djokjakarta, which in the preceding year had produced 35% of the imported cigar output.

## Significance

G. Kolff & Co.'s role as the publisher of the *Nederlandsch Indië Platen Atlas* placed it at the centre of the colonial government's jubilee publishing programme. The firm's status as the largest printing and publishing house in the Indies made it the natural choice for a work intended for wide distribution both in the colony and in the Netherlands.
