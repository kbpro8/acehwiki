**Hoofd van Gewestelijk Bestuur**, a Head of Regional Administration, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Colonial official whose sanction was required for erfpacht issuance after 1920.

## Type

Head of Regional Administration

## Description

Colonial official whose sanction was required for [[erfpacht]] issuance after [[1920]].

## Significance

Hoofd van Gewestelijk Bestuur is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
