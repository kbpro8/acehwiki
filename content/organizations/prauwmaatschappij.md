**The Prauwmaatschappij** and the *prauwenveren* (prauw ferries) were the lighterage undertakings that carried troops, officials and goods between the ships lying on the Acehnese roadsteads and the shore, and which [[H. van Kol]] named in the [[Tweede Kamer]] on [[17 November]] [[1897]] among the private beneficiaries of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]].

## The charge

In the passage printed under the heading *De belanghebbenden*, Van Kol listed among those for whom the war "geene windeieren legt" the contractors and *tokohouders* who did good business in Atjeh by the victualling of troops and officials, and "de Prauwmaatschappij en stoomvaartondernemingen door den invoer van weerbare mannen en den uitvoer van zieken, gewonden en geëvacueerden" — the prauw company and the steam-shipping undertakings, through the import of able-bodied men and the export of the sick, the wounded and the evacuated.

He returned to the *prauwenveren* in the same speech with figures: they, together with the [[Koninklijke Paketvaart-Maatschappij|paketvaartmaatschappij]], took a considerable share of the profits; "deze laatsten maakten kolossale winsten door hunne dure vrachten (het vervoer van één soldaat kostte van boord naar den wal f 1.18), en konden jaarlijks 20\u201325 pct. dividend uitkeeren." — these last made colossal profits by their expensive freights (the conveyance of a single soldier from ship to shore cost f 1.18), and could pay out yearly 20 to 25 per cent dividend.

## Context

The trade existed because the roadsteads of the Acehnese coast — above all [[Oleh-leh]] — had no deep-water quays, so that every man and every ton of stores landed for the war had to pass through the lighters. The volume of that traffic is indicated by the parallel figures Van Kol quoted from *[[De Kroniek]]* of [[10 October]] [[1897]]: the outlay for the Atjeh war, counting officers' salaries, men's pay, the medical service and the transport of troops and goods, had risen from f 9,947,030 in [[1871]] to f 18,176,000 in [[1895]]; and the evacuation figures given by [[J. B. Verhey|Verhey]] — some 2,900 evacuations of *berri-berri* sufferers in [[1895]] alone — measure the return traffic in the sick.

The Minister of Colonies [[J. T. Cremer|Cremer]] did not answer the charge concerning the *prauwenveren* specifically, confining his defence to the [[Koninklijke Paketvaart-Maatschappij|Koninklijke]] and the tobacco companies.
