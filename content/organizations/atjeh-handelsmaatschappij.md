**The Atjeh Handelsmaatschappij** — the *Atjeh Trading Company* — was a Dutch trading-company active in the *Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden* in the post-pacification period, with offices at [[Langsa|Langsa]], [[Lho Seumawe|Lho' Seumawé]] and [[Kota Radja|Kota Radja]]. At Langsa it is one of the trading-houses that remained after the Factorij, the Deli-Atjeh-Mij. and the firm Kehding had closed their offices. The Atjeh Handelsmaatschappij is one of the principal Dutch intermediaries in the trade between the Atjehnese *oeléebalangs* and the European import-market; in Broersma's chapter on trade and labour he describes it as a body that has much to do with the Atjehnese traders in cottons (*katoentjes*) and manufactures, who buy on three- and four-months' credit and sometimes get an extension.
