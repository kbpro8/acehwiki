**Zeemeeuw** — the *Seagull* — was a Dutch government steamboat (*gouvernements-stoomboot*) that served throughout the expedition of the [[Lamtoba column (1879)|Lamtoba column]] of [[1879]] as the intelligence and supply vessel between [[Oleh-leh]] and the roadstead of [[Lampanas]], as recorded in [[W. Cool]] and [[G. B. Hooijer]]'s *[[Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis (Cool & Hooijer, 1889)|Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis]]* ([[1889]]).

## The reconnaissance voyage

In the last days of July [[1879]], in order to obtain the intelligence that Generaal [[General van der Heijden|van der Heijden]]'s chiefs had failed to supply, the chief of the military recording brigade — 1e Luitenant der Infanterie [[G. B. Hooijer]] — was sent with a corporal-*opnemer* and several Achehnese chiefs, among whom the celebrated [[Panglima Tibang Moehamad|Panglima Tibang]], "per gouvernements-stoomboot Zeemeeuw" to Lampanas. From the roadstead, where a warship of the navy lay, Hooijer opened his investigation; it was to the Zeemeeuw that [[Toekoe Mas]], the head of Lampanas, came aboard for the conference at which the landing point was determined and the first sketch of the Lamtoba plain was composed.

## The supply line

At the landing of [[27 July]] the little steamer followed among the great steamships of the squadron, "met loshangende witte zeilen aan den grooten mast en kleurrijke seinvlaggen in top," tracing the soft rise and fall of the sea. Thereafter she maintained the link of supply between Oleh-leh and Lampanas: when the runderpest-thinned valley of [[Lamtoba]] failed to furnish fresh meat for the troops — no slaughter cattle having marched with the column — the Zeemeeuw, at the column-commandant's request, began to bring up slaughter stock with her provision runs. She assisted likewise at the landing of the artillery horses, towing the iron pontoon raft on which they were lowered. On [[23 August]] she came upon the roadstead again, with materials from Oleh-leh for the new [[Quarantine at Lampanas (August–September 1879)|quarantine camp]] being raised at Lampanas — too late that evening to be unloaded by the exhausted coolies before dark.
