**Atjeh-tram werkplaats (Sigli workshop)**, a railway workshop, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Workshop of the Atjeh-tram at Sigli.

## Type

railway workshop

## Description

Workshop of the [[Atjeh-tram]] at [[Sigli]]. ~200 [[Coolies|coolies]] (more than half [[Atjehers]], who perform well). Headed by a werktuigkundig ingenieur with ~6 opzichters (supervisors) including some [[indische jongens]]. Maintains all rolling material in the [[gewest]]. Partly electric. Since [[1935]] trains [[Ambachtsleergang|ambachtsleergang]] graduates.

## Significance

Atjeh-tram werkplaats (Sigli workshop) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
