The **Council of Eight** (*Raad van Achten*) was an influential political and diplomatic committee formed in [[Penang]] by Acehnese noblemen and merchants following the [[First Aceh Expedition]] in [[1873]].

## Function and Impact

Comprising eight prominent Acehnese representatives residing in British [[Penang]], the Council organized arms and gunpowder procurement, managed contraband shipments past Dutch naval blockades, published news reports supporting the [[Sultanate of Aceh]], and coordinated overseas diplomatic appeals to maintain Acehnese resistance against Dutch invasion.
