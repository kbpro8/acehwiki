**Dutch marine / marine**, a Naval forces, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Blockaded the east coast of Aceh during the war; the vorsten's trade with Penang made them vulnerable to such blockade.

## Type

Naval forces

## Description

Blockaded the [[East Coast of Aceh|east coast of Aceh]] during the war; the vorsten's trade with [[Penang]] made them vulnerable to such [[Blokkade|blockade]].

## Significance

Dutch marine / marine is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
