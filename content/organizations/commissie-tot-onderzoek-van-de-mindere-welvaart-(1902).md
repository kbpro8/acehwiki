**The Commissie tot onderzoek van de mindere welvaart (1902)** — the Commission for the Investigation of the Diminished Welfare of the Indigenous Population — was the commission installed by the [[Ministerie van Koloniën]] in [[1902]] in response to misoogst and hongersnood in several parts of [[Java]]. Its final report, when it appeared in [[1914]], filled no fewer than 33 volumes — a monument of [[Ethische Politiek|ethical-policy]] documentation that [[D. Fock|Fock]] drily noted had "naar het meer dan volmaakte [gestreefd]" — aspired to more than perfection.

## The installation

The commission was installed in [[1902]], when misoogst and hongersnood in several parts of [[Java]] had made it impossible to wait for the report. The principal causes of the Indies poverty — population growth and the pressure that the [[Atjeh-oorlog ([[1873]]-[[1904]])|Atjeh-oorlog]] was exerting on the colonial budget — were already well known, even if not always openly acknowledged.

## The report

The final report of the commission, when it appeared in [[1914]], filled 33 volumes. [[D. Fock|Fock]]'s dry verdict was that "naar het meer dan volmaakte was gestreefd" — it had aspired to more than perfection. More modest, but at least as useful, in [[D. Fock|Fock]]'s judgement, were the *economisch-historische bijdragen* that the [[Ministerie van Koloniën]] published in [[1904]] under the editorship of [[C. Th. van Deventer|Van Deventer]], [[D. Fock|Fock]] himself and [[Kielstra]].

## The Atjeh connexion

The connexion between the [[Commissie tot onderzoek van de mindere welvaart (1902)|commission]] and Atjeh is twofold. First, the [[Atjeh-oorlog ([[1873]]-[[1904]])|Atjeh-oorlog]] was, in the [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] retrospective, one of the two principal causes of the Indies poverty that the commission was installed to investigate. Second, the [[Commissie tot onderzoek van de mindere welvaart (1902)|commission]]'s installation in [[1902]] is, alongside the [[Troonrede of 1901|troonrede of 1901]] and the [[Indische Comptabiliteitswet amendment (26 June 1903)|Comptabiliteitswet amendment of 26 June 1903]], one of the three foundation acts of the [[Ethische Politiek|ethical-policy]] state in which the conquest and [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacificatie]] of Atjeh was conducted.
