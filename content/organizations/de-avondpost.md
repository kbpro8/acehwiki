**De Avondpost** — an evening newspaper published in [[The Hague]] — is the journal in which the [[Avondpost "Wekeer" articles (October 1907)|"Wekeer" articles]] of October [[1907]] appeared, exposing needless cruelties committed against the Atjehnese population under [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]]'s command. The publication is the proximate cause of the [[Van Daalen's resignation (December 1907)|December 1907 resignation of Van Daalen]] and the [[Van Heutsz's inspection tour of Atjeh (December 1907)|inspection tour of Van Heutsz]] of the same month.

## The "Wekeer" articles

Under the pseudonym "Wekeer" — a *maréchaussée*-officer from Atjeh — a series of articles appeared in *[[De Avondpost]]* in October [[1907]] detailing needless cruelties allegedly committed against the Atjehnese population and tolerated, if not encouraged, by [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] in his capacity as governor and military commander. The articles are the type-specimen of the colonial-press exposé of the Atjehnese-war parliamentary record.

## The "grote klok" image

The [[De Avondpost|De Avondpost]] publication was, in the retrospect of [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]'s [[1905]] warning to [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]] — "als het aan de grote klok komt dan voor jou een ander" — if it comes to the great bell, then another for you — the "great bell" itself. The *De Avondpost* "Wekeer" articles were the public exposure that ended [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]]'s tenure as governor of Atjeh.

## Significance

[[De Avondpost|De Avondpost]] is, in the [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] retrospective, the type-specimen of the colonial-press outlet whose publications could overturn the colonial administration's preferred narrative. The [[Avondpost "Wekeer" articles (October 1907)|"Wekeer" articles]] of October [[1907]] are the proximate cause of the political-military crisis that ended [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]]'s tenure and installed the new course of the [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacificatie]] under the [[Van Heutsz's report on Atjeh (June 1908)|June 1908 report]].
