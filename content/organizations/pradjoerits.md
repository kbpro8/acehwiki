**Pradjoerits**, a armed police, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Armed police servants maintaining orde and tucht.

## Type

[[Gewapende Politie|armed police]]

## Description

[[Gewapende Politie|Armed police]] servants maintaining orde and tucht. Called 'hadjoeré' (scoundrels) or 'aanjjori' (thieves) by the Acehnese in [[actie-tijd]] wordplay.

## Significance

Pradjoerits is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
