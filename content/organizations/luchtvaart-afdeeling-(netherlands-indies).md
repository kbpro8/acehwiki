The **Luchtvaart-Afdeeling** (Aviation Department) was the air arm of the Netherlands-Indies army to which the former Maréchaussée sergeant [[W. J. Mosselman]] transferred in [[1916]], and in which he became, with [[W. Leendertz]], one of the first two Netherlands-Indies military aviators to hold the international flying licence.

## Mosselman's transfer

The [[1930]] Maréchaussée jubilee number records that Mosselman, having served with the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* from December [[1906]] to January [[1912]] and risen through the ranks to *onderluitenant*, transferred in [[1916]] to the Luchtvaart-Afdeeling. He and the former captain of Artillery W. Leendertz were the first two military aviators to obtain the *Internationaal vliegbrevet*.

On his retirement in [[1922]] Mosselman was retained for the army as reserve lieutenant for special services.
