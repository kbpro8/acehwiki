**The Atjehsche Landbouw Vereeniging** — the *Atjèh Agricultural Society* — was the Atjèhse agricultural society, projected (in the [[De Rijkdom van Atjeh (1923)|1923 brochure]] of the [[Atjeh-Instituut|Atjeh-Instituut]]) to play a "*grootsche rol*" (great role) in the development of the [[rijstbouw|rijstbouw]] as the principal future export crop of Atjèh. The Society was to be the principal Atjèhse institutional vehicle for the post-war agricultural development of Atjèh — the body that would concern itself with the improvement of the rice cultivation, the introduction of better rice varieties, and the construction and operation of the rice mills (*[[pellerijen|pellerijen]]*).

## The Projected Role

The Atjeh-Instituut brochure of [[1923]], having set out the diagnosis of the declining [[pepercultuur|pepercultuur]] and the prescription of the [[rijstbouw|rijstbouw]] as the new great-trade article of Atjèh, turned to the institutional requirements of the prescription. The foregoing consideration, the brochure writes, will certainly be endorsed by those who have occupied themselves in Atjèh itself with this question; in particular the Atjehsche Landbouw Vereeniging is reckoned on, which "*op dit gebied een zoo grootsche rol zal kunnen vervullen*" (in this field will be able to play so great a role). To what extent the help of the administration can or cannot be missed, lies not for the judgement of the Atjeh-Instituut.

## Significance

The Atjehsche Landbouw Vereeniging is, in the projection of the Atjeh-Instituut brochure of [[1923]], the principal Atjèhse institutional vehicle for the post-war agricultural development of Atjèh. The Society's projected role — "*een zoo grootsche rol*" in the development of the rijstbouw — places it at the centre of the Atjeh-Instituut's prescription for the post-war economic development of Atjèh.
