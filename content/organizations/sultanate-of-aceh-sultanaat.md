**Sultanate of Aceh (Sultanaat)**, a Monarchy, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Sultanate whose restoration was strictly excluded by the Dutch after submission of the Pretendent-Sultan.

## Type

Monarchy

## Description

[[sultanate|Sultanate]] whose restoration was strictly excluded by the Dutch after submission of the [[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]]. Problems of restoration (actueeler dan ooit) excluded from book's scope by author. Footnote: with the death of the [[Pretendent-sultan|Pretendent-Sultan]] in [[Batavia]] on 6 [[April]] 'j.l.', the question took a wholly different aspect.

## Significance

Sultanate of Aceh (Sultanaat) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
