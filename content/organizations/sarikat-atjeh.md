**Sarikat Atjeh**, a Acehnese association, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Acehnese association founded in earlier years with strong encouragement from the then-governors and a youth movement.

## Type

Acehnese association

## Description

Acehnese association founded in earlier years with strong encouragement from the then-governors and a youth movement. Now defunct; only a bundle of yellowed letters in the archive remains.

## Significance

[[Founding of Sarikat Atjeh and 'Sama mendjadi satoe'|Sarikat Atjeh]] is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
