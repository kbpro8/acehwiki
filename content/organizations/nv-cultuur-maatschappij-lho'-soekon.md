**N.V. Cultuur Maatschappij Lho' Soekon**, a Fiber plantation company, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Operates the only large vezel-onderneming (fiber estate) in Aceh, located in onderafdeeling Lho' Soekon.

## Type

Fiber plantation company

## Description

Operates the only large vezel-onderneming (fiber estate) in Aceh, located in [[onderafdeeling]] [[Lho'Soekon|Lho' Soekon]]. [[1620]] H.A. planted with [[Sisal (Aceh)|sisal]]. Has a modern factory and décauville-[[Bekas|spoor]] with mechanical traction. Directed by the [[Nederlandsche Handel-Maatschappij|Nederlandsche Handel Maatschappij]]. Originated from a successful trial planting in the [[De Alaslanden|Alaslanden]] that was abandoned due to transport difficulties. [[1937]] fiber export: 4,050,000 K.G.

## Significance

N.V. Cultuur Maatschappij Lho' Soekon is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
