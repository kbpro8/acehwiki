The **Dutch East Indies Army** (KNIL) was the colonial military force maintained by the Kingdom of the Netherlands in the East Indies, responsible for conducting the invasion campaigns against the [[Sultanate of Aceh]].

## Role in the Aceh War

The army mobilized 3,300 troops for the [[First Aceh Expedition]] in [[1873]] under Major-General [[Johan Harmen Rudolf Köhler]] and over 8,200 soldiers for the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] in [[1873]]–[[1874]] under Lieutenant-General [[Jan van Swieten]].

Despite capturing the [[Kraton of Aceh]], the army suffered high casualties from combat and cholera epidemics, leading to decades of static defensive deployment along the [[Concentrated Line]] and aggressive counter-guerrilla campaigns under commanders like [[Karel van der Heijden]] and [[Jacobus Pel]].
