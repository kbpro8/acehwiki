**Dienst van Verkeer en Waterstaat**, a public works/transport department, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Department of Transport and Public Works.

## Type

public works/transport department

## Description

Department of Transport and Public Works. With [[Topografische Dienst]] studied the [[Tripa]]-vallei road trace. Also responsible for maintaining the problematic road stretch near [[Lho'Seumawé]] built over swamp and infested with crab burrows.

## Significance

Dienst van Verkeer en Waterstaat is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
