**The 15de Bataljon Infanterie** — also written *15e Bataljon* — was a Dutch colonial field infantry battalion of the [[Oost-Indisch Leger]], present in [[Aceh War|Atjeh]] at the start of [[1896]] and best known for providing the garrison of [[Ana' Galong]] — the critical Dutch post in the [[Sagi der XXII Moekims|XXII Moekims]] described by the Acehnese as the heart of [[Groot-Atjeh (Great Aceh)|Groot-Atjeh]]. The battalion furnished one captain, three lieutenants, and 120 *bajonetten* for the Ana' Galong garrison (replacing the [[Lambaroe]] detachment on [[7 March]] [[1896]]), and its companies — led by Kapitein J. P. Linck and Kapitein P. H. Bodemeijer — contributed to the *dekkingstroepen* of the [[Razing of Lam-Asan (30 May – 2 June 1896)|razing of Lam-Asan of 30 May–2 June 1896]] and to the mixed battalion under Maj. K. W. Steinmetz in the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|tuchtiging of V Moekims Montassik of 16–17 June 1896]].

## Background and Formation

As a *veldbataljon* of the colonial army, the 15de Bataljon combined European and indigenous companies armed with Beaumont rifles. In the early months of [[1896]] it furnished the garrison of [[Ana' Galong]] — one captain, three lieutenants, 120 *bajonetten*, one medical officer, one hospital attendant, an ambulance, and 16 artillerymen — replacing the [[Lambaroe]] detachment on [[7 March]] [[1896]]. The post, former main residence of [[Teungkoe di Tiro (Tengkoe di Tiroe)|Tengkoe di Tiroe]] who died there, had been conquered by the *legioen* of [[Teuku Umar]] on [[30 November]] [[1893]] and was continually shot at by the enemy; in early [[1896]] the entrance was under such fire that a privy on the north side could not be used. The 15de Bataljon's garrison there endured the [[31 March]] [[1896]] bombardment by grenade and gunfire from [[Sibreh]] at 12:45 PM. Captain H. F. T. van Blokland was post commander of [[Ana' Galong]] and the patrol leader of [[Patrol of Captain Van Blokland (7 March 1896)|the famous patrol of 7 March 1896]].

## Role in the Atjeh War

From [[30 May]] to [[2 June]] [[1896]] the 15de Bataljon contributed companies to the *dekkingstroepen* of the [[Razing of Lam-Asan (30 May – 2 June 1896)|razing of Lam-Asan]] — a major operation that destroyed the kampong and its *versterkingen* using 2 sectien [[Genietroepen (Engineers)|genietroepen]], 800 *dwangarbeiders*, and 400 Chinese *koelies* under the protection of multiple battalions (12de, 15de, 6e, 9e). The 15de Bataljon's companies, alongside the [[12e Bataljon Infanterie|12de Bataljon]], provided the protective cordon around the demolition parties. On [[16 June]] [[1896]] a mixed battalion under Maj. K. W. Steinmetz — drawing companies from the 15de Bataljon — formed part of the Reserve-Colonne under Luit.-kol. G. F. Soeters in the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|tuchtiging of V Moekims Montassik and XIII Moekims Toengkoeb]]. The battalion's Kapitein J. P. Linck and Kapitein P. H. Bodemeijer commanded the companies during these operations. (Kapitein J. P. Linck of the 15de Bataljon is not to be confused with Kapitein P. Linck of the [[5e Bataljon Infanterie|5e Bataljon]] who stormed the [[Kroeng-Kali]] *benteng* on [[17 June]] [[1896]].)

## Composition and Strength

The 15de Bataljon's strength on [[1 January]] [[1896]] — recorded in the [[Bijlage I (A) — Staat van troepensterkte op 1 Januari 1896|Staat van troepensterkte op 1 Januari 1896]] (Bijlage A) — formed part of the overall Atjeh garrison. The battalion's Ana' Galong garrison comprised 1 captain, 3 lieutenants, and 120 *bajonetten*, plus a medical officer, a hospital attendant, an ambulance, and 16 artillerymen. Company commanders during the spring included Kapitein J. P. Linck and Kapitein P. H. Bodemeijer. The [[Bijlage VIII — Staat van troepensterkte op 1 Juni 1896|strength return of 1 June 1896]] records the battalion in the overall Atjeh garrison with the other Dutch battalions.

## Significance

The 15de Bataljon's chief significance in the spring [[1896]] campaign lies in its garrisoning of [[Ana' Galong]] — the most exposed Dutch post in the [[Sagi der XXII Moekims|XXII Moekims]], considered by the Acehnese as the heart of [[Groot-Atjeh (Great Aceh)|Groot-Atjeh]]. The post had been conquered by [[Teuku Umar]]'s *legioen* on [[30 November]] [[1893]] and was, by early [[1896]], under near-continuous fire. The 15de Bataljon's role in the [[Razing of Lam-Asan (30 May – 2 June 1896)|razing of Lam-Asan]] and the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|V Moekims Montassik tuchtiging]] exemplifies the operational employment of garrison battalions as *dekkingstroepen* for engineer-led demolition operations.
