**The 4de (IVde) Divisie Marechaussee** was the [[Tangse|Tangse]]–[[Geumpang|Geumpang]] division of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Korps Maréchaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen]] during [[1940]]–[[1942]], the principal Dutch formation of the central-northern Atjeh plateau guerrilla theatre. In the [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|Dutch staff history's]] account (Deel VI, Hoofdstuk V), the IVde Divisie is the staff history's principal narrative unit of the planned central-northern Atjeh guerrilla — the formation whose brigades were to operate from the Tangse–Geumpang plateau against the Japanese flank and rear in the event of a Japanese landing at Kotaradja.

## Organisation as of 1 January 1940

As of [[1 January]] [[1940]], the IVde Divisie Marechaussee was grouped as follows (each brigade of Maréchaussee numbering 20 men):

- [[Tangsé|Tangsé]]: 3 brigades
- [[Geumpang|Geumpang]]: 3 brigades

**Total**: 6 brigades (about 120 men).

## Operations 1940–1942

The IVde Divisie's Tangsé detachment was commanded from [[1 December]] [[1 December 1941|1 December 1941]] by Lt [[L. C. G. F. Onvlee|Onvlee]] — the officer entrusted with the [[safeguarding of the vaandel of the Korps Maréchaussee|safeguarding of the vaandel of the Korps Maréchaussee]]. When the [[F-beweging|F. beweging]] rising broke out in February [[1942]], the IVde Divisie's brigades were among the nearest Maréchaussee brigades ordered by Colonel [[G. F. V. Gosenson|Gosenson]] on [[23 February]] [[23 February 1942|23 February]] to march to [[Seulimeum|Seulimeum]] for the suppression of the rising — the deployment that would form the initial element of the [[Seulimeum-colonne|Seulimeum-colonne]].

The IVde Divisie's Tangsé and Geumpang positions were also the designated refuge of the *2de hoofdgroep* under Lt-Col [[C. J. Bekkers|Bekkers]] after the Japanese landings of [[12 March]] [[12 March 1942|12 March]] — the plateau on which the *2de Garnizoensbataljon* was to conduct the planned guerrilla in cooperation with the IVde Divisie brigades. The staff history records the failure of this plan: the [[Poort stelling (Tangse)|collapse of the Poort stelling]] on [[13 March]] [[13 March 1942|13 March]] and the [[Poort stelling (Tangse)|capitulation of Tangse on 18 March 1942]] ended the planned central-northern guerrilla before it had begun.

## Significance

The IVde Divisie is, in the staff history's Atjeh chapter, the principal narrative unit of the planned central-northern Atjeh guerrilla — the formation whose existence was the basis of the Dutch operational plan for a sustained guerrilla in the Tangse–Geumpang plateau after a Japanese landing. The staff history's emphasis on the IVde Divisie's progressive commitment to the Seulimeum-colonne in February [[1942]] — and on the subsequent collapse of the Tangse stelling in March [[1942]] — frames the IVde Divisie as the staff history's principal instance of the structural failure of the Dutch guerrilla plan: the formation whose existence was to have enabled the guerrilla was itself dissolved before the guerrilla could be launched.
