**Gouvernements Hars- en Terpentijabedrijf**, a government enterprise, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Government enterprise for resin and turpentine exploitation.

## Type

government enterprise

## Description

Government enterprise for [[Resin (Aceh)|resin]] and turpentine exploitation. Has large complexes in the [[Isaq-vallei]] on the paardenpad between [[Takengon]] and [[Blang Kedjerën]]. Also has considerable interests in the dennenbosschen near [[Takengon]] ([[Pinus merkusii]]).

## Significance

Gouvernements Hars- en Terpentijabedrijf is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
