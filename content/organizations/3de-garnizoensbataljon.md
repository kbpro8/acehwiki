The **3de Garnizoensbataljon** was the third Dutch colonial garrison battalion in [[Atjeh|Atjeh]], responsible for the eastern and northern Atjeh coast (*Oostkust* and *Noordkust*) districts. As of [[1 January 1940]], the 3de Garnizoensbataljon held 34 brigades in total, distributed across multiple detachments in the Sigli–Lho Seumawé corridor and the eastern Atjeh coast. It was reduced through the two-phase pre-invasion drawdown of [[1940]] and [[1941]], and formally dissolved following the [[15 February 1941]] reduction that stripped it of its remaining troop detachments.

## Strength and deployment as of 1 January 1940

The [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|staff history]] records the 3de Garnizoensbataljon's total strength as of [[1 January 1940]] at 34 brigades, distributed across detachments including the hoofdkwartier (headquarters) and war detachments at multiple Oostkust and Noordkust posts. The detachment structure included posts at [[Sigli|Sigli]], [[Lho Seumawé|Lho Seumawé]], [[Lhok Seumawé|Lhok Seumawé]], [[Peusangan|Peusangan]], [[Idi|Idi]], [[Langsa|Langsa]], and other eastern coast stations.

## The first reduction phase (1940)

The first phase of the pre-invasion drawdown, carried out through [[1940]], reduced the 3de Garnizoensbataljon by the dissolution of multiple war detachments. The staff history records the specific posts dissolved and the redistribution of their brigades to other formations and to the column formed for deployment outside Atjeh. After the first-phase reductions, the 3de Garnizoensbataljon retained its headquarters and a reduced number of detachments.

## The 15 February 1941 reduction and dissolution

On [[15 February 1941]], as part of the second and final phase of the pre-invasion drawdown, the 3de Garnizoensbataljon was reduced to its [[Sigli|Sigli]] (8 brigades) and [[Lho Seumawé|Lho Seumawé]] (4 brigades) detachments only. With these final reductions, the 3de Garnizoensbataljon no longer had any troops remaining at its headquarters and was formally dissolved. The 3de Garnizoensbataljon's dissolution on [[15 February 1941]] was one of several formal dissolutions effected by that day's reduction order.

The brigades released by the dissolution of the 3de Garnizoensbataljon's headquarters and war detachments were redistributed: a column of 15 brigades was formed for assignment elsewhere outside Atjeh; the remaining brigades were absorbed into the [[2de Garnizoensbataljon|2de Garnizoensbataljon]] at Kotaradja (reinforced with 8 brigades from the dissolved Sabang war detachment) and the [[1ste Garnizoensbataljon|1ste Garnizoensbataljon]] (whose staff was moved to [[Medan|Medan]]).

## Significance

The 3de Garnizoensbataljon is significant in the [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|staff history's]] account as the garrison battalion responsible for the eastern and northern Atjeh coast, and as one of the principal formations reduced and dissolved in the two-phase pre-invasion drawdown of [[1940]]–[[1941]]. Its dissolution on [[15 February 1941]] was the culminating event of the Dutch pre-invasion troop reduction in Atjeh, and the formal close of the 3de Garnizoensbataljon's presence as a distinct military formation in the eastern Atjeh garrison.
