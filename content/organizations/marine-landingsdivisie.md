**Marine-landingsdivisie** was the naval infantry unit utilized by the Dutch military during the [[First Aceh Expedition]].

## Operations
Under the command of Captain-Lieutenant [[J.W. Binkes]], this division formed the main body of the assault force during the [[First Conquest of the Great Missigit]] on [[10 April]] [[1873]]. They provided critical support and executed a disciplined withdrawal during the [[Retreat from the Missigit]]. They suffered casualties, including the death of medical officer [[C. Noordewier]].
