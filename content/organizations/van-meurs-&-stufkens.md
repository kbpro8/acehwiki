**Van Meurs & Stufkens** was a Dutch publishing house based in [[Rotterdam|Rotterdam]] that was active in the late nineteenth century and issued, among other works, the anonymous pamphlet *[[Beschouwingen over Atjeh en onzen oorlog met dat rijk|Beschouwingen over Atjeh en onzen oorlog met dat rijk]]* — *Reflections on Aceh and our war with that kingdom* — by [[Een Ooggetuige|Een Ooggetuige]] in the [[1880s]]. The house is one of several Rotterdam publishers whose output included Dutch colonial pamphlet literature on the [[Aceh War|Atjeh War]].

## Publishing activity

Van Meurs & Stufkens issued the *[[Beschouwingen over Atjeh en onzen oorlog met dat rijk|Beschouwingen over Atjeh en onzen oorlog met dat rijk]]* with a folded map — the [[Kaart van het Oorlogstooneel|*Kaart van het Oorlogstooneel, de Vaarwaters en vereischte Kustlichten voor den Wereldhandel*]] — in [[Rotterdam|Rotterdam]] in the [[1880s]]. The colophon of the surviving copy records the place of publication as Rotterdam and the publisher as Van Meurs & Stufkens. The publication of a pamphlet on the Atjeh War with an accompanying map of the theatre of war places the house within the body of Dutch publishers — including the [[Nieuwe Rotterdamsche Courant|Nieuwe Rotterdamsche Courant]] press and other Rotterdam and [[The Hague|The Hague]] houses — that supplied the Dutch reading public with pamphlet literature on the colonial war.

## Significance

For the AcehWiki corpus, the significance of Van Meurs & Stufkens lies in the house's publication of the *[[Beschouwingen over Atjeh en onzen oorlog met dat rijk|Beschouwingen over Atjeh en onzen oorlog met dat rijk]]*, which is one of the contemporary Dutch pamphlet sources on the [[Aceh War|Atjeh War]]. The house's Rotterdam location is consistent with the strong interest of the Rotterdam merchant community in the colonial trade — including the pepper trade of [[Acheh|Atjeh]] — and with the role of Rotterdam publishers in circulating pamphlet literature on colonial questions to that mercantile readership.
