**The 3de (IIIde) Divisie Marechaussee** was the [[Takengon|Takengon]]-oriented division of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Korps Maréchaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen]] during [[1940]]–[[1942]], commanded in March [[1942]] by Captain [[F. Wetzels|F. Wetzels]], and the principal Dutch formation of the central-Atjeh theatre of the [[Japanese invasion of Atjèh|Japanese invasion of March 1942]]. In the [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|Dutch staff history's]] account (Deel VI, Hoofdstuk V), the IIIde Divisie is the staff history's principal narrative unit of the [[Bireuën|Bireuën]]–[[Takengon|Takengon]] three-position defence — the formation that fought the [[First stelling south of Bireuën (15 March 1942)|first stelling south of Bireuën]] under Lt [[W. van Pesch|Van Pesch]] and conducted the only successful Dutch guerrilla action of the central-Atjeh campaign.

## Organisation as of 1 January 1940

As of [[1 January]] [[1940]], the IIIde Divisie Marechaussee was grouped as follows (each brigade of Maréchaussee numbering 20 men):

- [[Takengon|Takengon]]: 9 brigades
- [[Lam Meulo|Lam Meulo]]: 6 brigades

**Total**: 15 brigades (about 300 men).

## Operations of March 1942

In [[Major W. F. Palmer van den Broek|Palmer van den Broek]]'s three-position defence of the [[Bireuën|Bireuën]]–[[Takengon|Takengon]] road, the IIIde Divisie supplied the troops of both the first stelling (three brigades under Lt [[W. van Pesch|Van Pesch]], with four machine guns and two pieces of light field artillery) and the second stelling (five brigades under Captain [[F. Wetzels|Wetzels]] as IIIde divisie commander). The IIIde Divisie's troops at the first stelling conducted the staff history's only Dutch tactical success of the central-Atjeh campaign — the engagement of [[15 March]] [[15 March 1942|15 March 1942]], in which the Japanese suffered approximately 40 killed for no Dutch loss — and the staff history's only successful Dutch guerrilla of the central-Atjeh campaign (Lt Van Pesch and sergeant [[Haveman|Haveman]]'s two-week ambush campaign of [[16 March]] [[16 March 1942|16 March]]–[[1 April]] [[1 April 1942|1 April]]).

## Significance

The IIIde Divisie is, in the staff history's Atjeh chapter, the staff history's principal narrative unit of the central-Atjeh campaign, and the formation whose troops conducted the only Dutch tactical success and the only successful Dutch guerrilla of the central-Atjeh theatre. The staff history's emphasis on the IIIde Divisie's exemplary function — set against the panic that overtook the *2de Garnizoensbataljon* on the noordkust — frames the IIIde Divisie's troops as the staff history's principal evidence that the central-Atjeh Maréchaussee, when competently led, was still capable of effective resistance in March [[1942]].
