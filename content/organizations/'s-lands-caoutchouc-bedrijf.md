**'s Lands Caoutchouc Bedrijf**, a government enterprise, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Government rubber enterprise near Langsa, alongside other rubber-tuinen, koffie- and oliepalmondernemingen.

## Type

government enterprise

## Description

Government rubber enterprise near [[Langsar|Langsa]], alongside other rubber-tuinen, koffie- and oliepalmondernemingen.

## Significance

's Lands Caoutchouc Bedrijf is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
