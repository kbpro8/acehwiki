**De Erven F. Bohn** was the Haarlem publishing house that issued *[[De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh (Hooijer, 1897)|De verlaten posten in Groot-Atjeh]]* by [[G. B. Hooijer]], jointly with [[G. Kolff & Co.]] of Batavia.

## The imprint

The title-page of the work reads *DE VERLATEN POSTEN IN GROOT-ATJEH, DOOR G. B. HOOIJER, Gep. Luit.-Kolonel O. I. Leger. Met 24 platen. HAARLEM DE ERVEN F. BOHN. BATAVIA. G. KOLFF & Co.* The joint Haarlem–Batavia imprint was the standard arrangement for colonial works intended for readers in both the Netherlands and the Netherlands Indies.

The copy from which the text is known bears the stamp of the *Koninklijk Instituut voor de Tropen, Centrale Boekerij, Amsterdam*.
