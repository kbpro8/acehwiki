**The Resident voor Atjehsche zaken en de scheepvaartregeling** — also referred to as the *Resident for Acehnese affairs* or, more loosely, the *Resident van Atjeh* — was the senior Dutch colonial civil-administrative office at [[Koeta Radja]] charged with the coordination of Atjeh-related affairs and the regulation of shipping in the waters of [[Aceh War|Atjeh]]. Held by K. F. H. van Langen through the spring of [[1896]], the office became the temporary seat of civil authority in Atjeh after the dismissal of Gen.-Maj. C. Deykerhoff on [[7 April]] [[1896]], when Van Langen was temporarily charged with civil affairs under the [[Regeeringscommissaris (Government Commissioner)|Regeeringscommissaris]] authority of Lt.-Gen. J. A. Vetter. Van Langen was relieved of these temporary functions on [[28 June]] [[1896]] when Kolonel J. W. Stemfoort was appointed (provisionally) as Civiel en Militair Gouvernur.

## Background and Formation

The office of Resident voor Atjehsche zaken en de scheepvaartregeling combined two distinct portfolios: (1) the coordination of all Acehnese affairs — the political, diplomatic, and administrative relations between the Dutch colonial government and the various *adathoofden*, *oeloebalang*, *panglima sagi*, and religious leaders (*oelama*, *teungkoe*) of [[Groot-Atjeh (Great Aceh)|Groot-Atjeh]] and the outlying territories of the Atjeh sultanate — and (2) the *scheepvaartregeling*, the regulation of shipping in the waters of Atjeh, including the *reede* of [[Oleh-leh]] and [[Sabang-baai / Poeloe Weh|Sabang]], the coastal trade, and the packet service of the [[Koninklijke Paketvaart-Maatschappij (KPM)|KPM]]. The Resident ranked below the [[Civiel en Militair Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Civiel en Militair Gouverneur]] in the formal hierarchy but worked closely with the military commandant and the *assistent-resident* in the day-to-day administration. On [[1 January]] [[1896]] the office was held by K. F. H. van Langen, who resided at [[Koeta Radja]].

## Role in the Atjeh War

On [[7 April]] [[1896]] — with the arrival of [[J. A. Vetter (Luitenant-Generaal)|Lt.-Gen. Vetter]] as [[Regeeringscommissaris (Government Commissioner)|Regeeringscommissaris]] and the dismissal of Gen.-Maj. C. Deykerhoff as Civiel en Militair Gouverneur — the office of Resident voor Atjehsche zaken en de scheepvaartregeling became the temporary seat of civil authority in Atjeh: Resident K. F. H. van Langen was "*belast met de civiele zaken*" (charged with civil affairs), under the orders of the Regeeringscommissaris, while the military command fell to Kolonel J. W. Stemfoort. Van Langen was a recipient — alongside the Governor, the C. P. J. van Vliet, and the *assistent-resident* at Koeta Radja — of [[Teuku Umar]]'s [[Second Letter of Teuku Umar (Bijlage IV)|second letter of 12 April 1896]] (Bijlage IV), which was dated from Lampisang, 29 Sawal 1313, and which demanded ƒ150,000 monthly to pay his warriors, promised to fight the enemies of the *Kompeni* but to exclude *oelebalangs* from military affairs, requested that his son succeed him as Panglima Prang, mentioned the *benting te Ana' Galong* as a place to receive *vivres*, and refused to appear before the Governor until "het land van Selimoen" was acquired. Van Langen's temporary civil-affairs role continued through the [[Departure of Vetter and Restoration of Civil and Military Governor (28 June 1896)|28 June 1896 departure of Vetter]], at which point he was "*eervol ontheven van zijne tijdelijke functiēn van Civiel Bestuurder van het gewest*" (honorably relieved of his temporary functions as Civil Administrator of the territory), and Kolonel Stemfoort was appointed (provisionally) as Civiel en Militair Gouvernur.

## Composition and Strength

The office of Resident voor Atjehsche zaken en de scheepvaartregeling was held throughout the spring [[1896]] period by K. F. H. van Langen. As a civil-administrative rather than military office, it had no fixed troop strength, but its incumbent was supported by the *assistent-resident* at Koeta Radja and by the various *controleurs* of the [[Controleur 2e klasse bij het Binnenlandsch Bestuur|Binnenlandsch Bestuur]] stationed across the Atjeh territories.

## Significance

The office of Resident voor Atjehsche zaken en de scheepvaartregeling acquired its spring [[1896]] significance through the temporary elevation of its incumbent, K. F. H. van Langen, to the seat of civil authority in Atjeh during the [[Regeeringscommissaris (Government Commissioner)|Regeeringscommissaris]] period ([[7 April]]–[[28 June]] [[1896]]). Van Langen's receipt of [[Teuku Umar]]'s [[Second Letter of Teuku Umar (Bijlage IV)|second letter of 12 April 1896]] — alongside the Governor, the *overste van den staf*, and the *assistent-resident* — illustrates the way in which the Resident's office served as a formal channel of communication with the Atjeh chiefs. Van Langen's relief on [[28 June]] [[1896]] marked the formal end of the Regeeringscommissaris period and the restoration of the unified [[Civiel en Militair Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Civiel en Militair Gouverneur]] office under Stemfoort.
