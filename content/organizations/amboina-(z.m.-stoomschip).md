**Amboina** was a steam warship of the [[Marine (Dutch Navy)|Royal Netherlands Navy]] stationed on the roadstead of [[Lampanas]] during the stay of the [[Lamtoba column (1879)|Lamtoba column]] ashore in July–September [[1879]], as recorded in [[W. Cool]] and [[G. B. Hooijer]]'s *[[Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis (Cool & Hooijer, 1889)|Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis]]* ([[1889]]).

## The Lampanas station

During the five weeks in which the column operated in the interior — from the landing of [[27 July]] [[1879]] through the occupation of [[Lamtoba]], the [[Selimoen Expedition (August 1879)|march to Selimoen]], and the return to the [[Quarantine at Lampanas (August–September 1879)|quarantine camp]] at Lampanas — the Amboina and the [[Merapi (Z.M. stoomschip)|Merapi]] kept the sea-watch on the roadstead of the little coastal village. On [[4 September]] [[1879]], when the last division of the troops had been put aboard the transport [[Graaf van Bijland (stoomschip)|Graaf van Bijland]] for [[Oleh-leh]], "stoomde ... de oorlogsschepen Merapi en Amboina, die tijdens het verblijf der troepen op de reede van Lampanas, aldaar gestationeerd waren geweest, naar hun station voor Pedir terug": the two warships steamed back to their station before [[Pedir]], and the daily movement that had so long ruled the roadstead and the coast of Lampanas vanished for good.
