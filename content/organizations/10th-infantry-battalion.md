**10th Infantry Battalion** was a regular infantry battalion of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] deployed in northern Sumatra during the Aceh War between [[1873]] and [[1876]].

## Personnel Strength

According to official returns published in *Atjeh gedurende de tweede expeditie en later* ([[1876]]), on [[26 December]] [[1875]] the battalion consisted of 18 European officers, 212 European soldiers, and 363 indigenous soldiers, totaling 593 men.

## Garrison and Defensive Duties

Throughout [[1875]] and [[1876]], the [[10th Infantry Battalion]] was assigned primarily to garrison duties around [[Kraton of Aceh]], [[Penajoeng]], and [[Oleh-leh]], guarding supply lines and protecting the [[Medical Service in Aceh]] from Acehnese infiltration.
