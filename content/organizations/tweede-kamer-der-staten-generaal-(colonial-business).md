**The Tweede Kamer der Staten-Generaal** — the Lower House of the Dutch Parliament — is, in the [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] retrospective on the [[1891]]-[[1918]] period, the parliamentary forum in which the [[Atjeh-oorlog ([[1873]]-[[1904]])|Atjeh-oorlog]], the [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacificatie]], the [[Ethische Politiek|ethical-policy]] proclamation and the Atjeh oil contracts were debated. The colonial business of the [[Tweede Kamer]] is the principal subject of [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]]'s *Oost-Indië in het Nederlandse Parlement 1891-1918*, the source of the present Aceh Wiki History articles.

## The colonial business

The colonial business of the [[Tweede Kamer]] comprised principally the annual *begrotingsdebat* (budget debate) for the [[Ministerie van Koloniën|Ministry of Colonies]], the legislative treatment of *wetsontwerpen* (draft laws) affecting the Indies — the [[Indische Comptabiliteitswet amendment (26 June 1903)|Comptabiliteitswet amendment]] of [[26 June]] [[1903]], the [[Mijnwet|Mijnwet]] of [[1899]] and its [[Mijnwet art. 5a (1910)|Article 5a amendment]] of [[1910]], the *adat*-law revisions — and the interpellations on specific episodes such as the [[Van Daalen's Gajo-Alas-Batak expedition (1904)|Van Daalen expedition]] of [[1904]] and the [[Avondpost "Wekeer" articles (October 1907)|"Wekeer" affair]] of October [[1907]].

## The chambermembers

The [[Tweede Kamer]] of the [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] period comprised both colonial specialists and generalists. The colonial specialists — [[H. B. van Kol|Van Kol]] (s.d.), [[C. Th. van Deventer|Van Deventer]] (v.d.), [[J. T. Cremer|Cremer]] (lib.), [[H. C. Thomson|Thomson]] (lib.), [[D. Fock|Fock]] (lib.), [[J. K. J. de Stuers|De Stuers]] (catholic) — were the principal speakers on Indies affairs. The generalists — [[Abraham Kuyper|Kuyper]] (a.r.), [[Lohman (De Savornin Lohman)|Lohman]] (c.h.), [[Troelstra|Troelstra]] (s.d.), [[De Stuers|De Stuers]] — intervened on matters of principle (the [[Ethische Politiek|ethical-policy]] proclamation, the *zending* question, the *doel heiligt de middelen* defence of the [[Van Daalen's Gajo-Alas-Batak expedition (1904)|Van Daalen expedition]]).

## The Vermeulen verdict

[[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]]'s retrospective on the [[Tweede Kamer]]'s colonial business is mixed. The chambermembers "hadden wel eens het gevoel, dat al deze expedities, excursies en verkenningstochten van [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]], [[G. C. E. van Daalen|Van Daalen]], [[Swart (colonel, Atjeh)|Swart]] en die andere generaals achteraf goedgepraat moesten worden en dat daarvoor gretig geput werd uit het arsenaal van leuzen, motieven en edele bedoelingen, dat in de loop der jaren door de ministers en de kamerleden zelf was opgestapeld" — sometimes had the feeling that all these expeditions, excursions and reconnaissance tours of Van Heutsz, Van Daalen, Swart and those other generals had to be justified after the fact, and that for this purpose eager use was made of the arsenal of slogans, motives and noble intentions that the ministers and the chambermembers themselves had piled up over the years.

The Indische debate, [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] concluded, "heeft iets van een schimmenspel van ideeën en beginselen. Maar heel zelden krijgen de kamerleden die niet in Indië zijn geweest, een flits te zien van het leven in Indië zelf" — has something of a shadow-play of ideas and principles. But very rarely do the chambermembers who have not been in the Indies catch so much as a flash of life in the Indies itself.

## The ambtelijke adviezen complaint

A persistent complaint of the chambermembers was that the minister of Colonies "zich er achter verschool" — hid behind — the *ambtelijke adviezen* (official advices) from the governor-general, the residents, the directors, the [[Raad van Indië]], the Hooggerechtshof, the generals, the staatscommissies. The complaint is, in the [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] retrospective, the procedural expression of the colonial-parliamentary dilemma: that the chambermembers, lacking first-hand knowledge of the Indies, were dependent on the very official communications whose candour they suspected.

## Significance

The [[Tweede Kamer der Staten-Generaal (colonial business)|Tweede Kamer]] is the parliamentary forum in which the [[Atjeh-oorlog ([[1873]]-[[1904]])|Atjeh-oorlog]], the [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacificatie]] and the [[Ethische Politiek|ethical-policy]] state were politically contested. The [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] section of the [[1955]] *Schets* is the principal narrative source for the colonial business of the [[Tweede Kamer]] in the [[1891]]-[[1918]] period.
