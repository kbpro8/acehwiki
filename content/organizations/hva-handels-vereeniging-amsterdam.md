**H.V.A. (Handels-Vereeniging Amsterdam)**, a Plantation enterprise, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Active on Westkust and Noordkust; with Société Financière fairly active but limited to a few undertakings.

## Type

Plantation enterprise

## Description

Active on [[Westkust]] and [[Noordkust]]; with [[Société Financière]] fairly active but limited to a few undertakings.

## Significance

H.V.A. (Handels-Vereeniging Amsterdam) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
