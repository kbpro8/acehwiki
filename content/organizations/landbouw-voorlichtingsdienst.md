**Landbouw-Voorlichtingsdienst**, a Government agricultural extension service, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Conducted bodem-onderzoeken (soil investigations) in Aceh.

## Type

Government agricultural extension service

## Description

Conducted bodem-onderzoeken (soil investigations) in Aceh.

## Significance

Landbouw-Voorlichtingsdienst is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
