**8th Infantry Battalion** was an active combat infantry battalion of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] that served in the Aceh War during the campaigns of [[1873]] to [[1876]], operating under the command of [[Overste Engel]] during [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]].

## Strength and Deployment

As recorded on [[26 December]] [[1875]], the battalion comprised 18 European officers, 291 European rank-and-file, and 182 indigenous soldiers, totaling 491 men assigned to the Left Division in Aceh.

## Combat in the IV Moekims

During late December [[1875]] and January [[1876]], one company of the [[8th Infantry Battalion]] joined [[Lieutenant Colonel Meijer]]'s column during the [[Crossing of Blankala Pass]] into the [[IV Moekims]]. By [[30 January]] [[1876]], the entire battalion under [[Overste Engel]] was deployed in mobile field columns around [[Pekan Badak]], engaging in continuous skirmishing against Acehnese defenders.
