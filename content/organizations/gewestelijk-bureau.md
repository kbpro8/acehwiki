**Gewestelijk Bureau** is a subject named in *[[General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh (1943)|General Swart, Pacifier of Atjeh]]* ([[1943]]) by [[Maurice Henri du Croo|M. H. du Croo]], in connection with the history of [[Atjeh]] and the governorship of [[H. N. A. Swart]].

## Account in du Croo’s Generaal Swart ([[1943]])

### From [[Koeta Radja]]

At Koeta Radja stood the *[[Gewestelijk Bureau]]*, to which officers could be attached for form's sake while operating freely elsewhere, as [[Scheurleer]] was in [[1911]] while collecting information about [[Pang Bintang]]. It was to Koeta Radja that [[H. J. Schmidt]] came in June [[1908]] to plead against his posting to [[Padang Tidji]], and there that Swart consulted the *[[Register van Bendehoofden]]*.
