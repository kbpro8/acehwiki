**The Landsdrukkerij Weltevreden** — the State Printing Office at Weltevreden, the southern district of [[Batavia]] that had become, by the late nineteenth century, the administrative seat of the Dutch East-Indies colonial government — was the official printer of the colonial state and the publisher of the [[1930]] compilation *[[Zelfbestuursverordeningen Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]]* (Self-government Regulations of Atjeh and Dependencies), issued as *Serie A No. 9* (Series A No. 9) of the *Mededeelingen van de Afdeeling Bestuurszaken der Buitengewesten van het Departement van Binnenlandsch Bestuur* (Communications of the Bureau of Administrative Affairs of the Outer Territories of the Department of the Interior). The title page of the 1930 compilation names the *Landsdrukkerij* (State Printing Office) as its sole publisher, with the imprint *WELTEVREDEN 1930* — the State Printing Office is the institutional venue at which the colonial *Departement van Binnenlandsch Bestuur* (Department of the Interior)'s *Afdeeling Bestuurszaken der Buitengewesten* (Bureau of Administrative Affairs of the Outer Territories) committed its third collected bundle of *zelfbestuursverordeningen* (self-government regulations) to print.

## The State Printing Office at Weltevreden

The *Landsdrukkerij* (State Printing Office) — literally, the "Land's Printing-Office" — was the official government printer of the Dutch East Indies, the institutional descendant of the colonial-era *Landsdrukkerij* that had, since the early nineteenth century, printed the *Javasche Courant* (Java Gazette), the *Staatsblad van Nederlandsch-Indië* (Netherlands-Indies Statute Book), the *Gouvernementsbesluiten* (Government Decrees), and the whole body of official regulations, ordinances, and administrative publications by which the colonial state published its acts. By the late nineteenth century, the *Landsdrukkerij* had been relocated from the old city of Batavia to the new administrative district of Weltevreden — the planned governmental quarter around the *Koningsplein* (King's Square, today's Medan Merdeka) where the *Gouvernement* (the Government House), the *Departementen* (the Departments), and the official residencies had been gathered. The *Landsdrukkerij* at Weltevreden was, in the early twentieth century, the printing-house of the colonial state: it produced the *Staatsblad van Nederlandsch-Indië*, the *Gouvernementsbesluit* series, the departmental *Mededeelingen* (Communications) and *Verzamelingen* (Collections) of regulations, and the official compilations of colonial-administrative acts of which the [[1930]] *Zelfbestuursverordeningen Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden* is a representative example.

## Publication of the Zelfbestuursverordeningen compilation (1930)

The [[1930]] compilation *Zelfbestuursverordeningen Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden* — the third collected bundle (*derden bundel*) of *zelfbestuursverordeningen* (self-government regulations) issued by the *Afdeeling Bestuurszaken der Buitengewesten* (Bureau of Administrative Affairs of the Outer Territories), as the *Voorwoord* (Preface) of the compilation itself notes — was published by the *Landsdrukkerij* at Weltevreden as *Serie A No. 9* (Series A No. 9) of the *Mededeelingen van de Afdeeling Bestuurszaken der Buitengewesten van het Departement van Binnenlandsch Bestuur* (Communications of the Bureau of Administrative Affairs of the Outer Territories of the Department of the Interior). The title page of the compilation gives the imprint in the standard form: *WELTEVREDEN 1930* — and the *Voorwoord* (Preface), signed by the *Afdeeling Bestuurszaken der Buitengewesten*, records that the publication of the bundle "beteekent een nieuwe stap in de richting, aangegeven in het voorwoord van nummer 5 dezer Serie van de Mededeelingen" — means a new step in the direction indicated in the foreword to number 5 of this Series of the *Mededeelingen* (Communications). The *Landsdrukkerij* was, in this institutional arrangement, the printer-of-record for the colonial state's *zelfbestuur* (self-government) policy: the venue at which the regulations issued by the *zelfbestuurders* (self-governors) of the Atjeh *landschappen* (polities) — under the supervisory approval of the *Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden* (Governor of Atjeh and Dependencies) and the *Gouverneur-Generaal* of Nederlandsch-Indië (Governor-General of Netherlands-India) — were collected, printed, and circulated for the use of the colonial-administrative readership in the Indies and the metropole.

## Significance

The *Landsdrukkerij Weltevreden*'s significance in the [[Aceh Wiki History]] record is bibliographic and institutional. The State Printing Office is the publisher of the second of the two principal source-books of the [[Aceh Wiki History]] project — the [[1930]] compilation of *Zelfbestuursverordeningen Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden* (Self-government Regulations of Atjeh and Dependencies) that, together with [[Joannes Benedictus van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]]'s [[1893]] tract *[[De onderwerping van Atjeh (Van Heutsz, 1893)|De onderwerping van Atjeh]]*, supplies the principal primary-source corpus for the project's coverage of the Dutch colonial administration of Atjeh. The *Landsdrukkerij*'s imprint on the 1930 compilation is the institutional marker by which the compilation is identified as an official publication of the colonial state — not a private or commercial imprint but the formal act of publication of the *Departement van Binnenlandsch Bestuur*'s *Afdeeling Bestuurszaken der Buitengewesten* (Bureau of Administrative Affairs of the Outer Territories of the Department of the Interior) — and the venue at which the colonial state's *zelfbestuur* (self-government) policy in Atjeh was, in the closing years of the Dutch East Indies, given its definitive printed form.
