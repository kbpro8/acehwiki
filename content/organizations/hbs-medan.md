**H.B.S. Medan**, a secondary school, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Hogere Burgerschool (Higher Civic School) at Medan where Aceh-based parents send their children — close enough to Langsa (160 km vs 600 km from Koeta Radja) to be 'next door' by outer-district standards.

## Type

secondary school

## Description

Hogere Burgerschool (Higher Civic School) at [[Medan]] where Aceh-based parents send their children — close enough to [[Langsar|Langsa]] (160 km vs 600 km from [[Koeta-Radja|Koeta Radja]]) to be 'next door' by outer-district standards.

## Significance

H.B.S. Medan is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
