**Scheepsbouwmaatschappij 'de Schelde'**, a shipbuilding company (in Holland), appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Dutch shipyard where the Sabangbaai ship was built in 1903 before crossing to NEI under its own power.

## Type

shipbuilding company (in [[Leyden (Leiden)|Holland]])

## Description

Dutch shipyard where the Sabangbaai ship was built in [[1903]] before crossing to NEI under its own power.

## Significance

Scheepsbouwmaatschappij 'de Schelde' is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
