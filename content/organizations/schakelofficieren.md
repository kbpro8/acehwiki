**Schakelofficieren**, a Liaison officers (military-civil), appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Officers serving alongside the fungeerend-controleur (military and civil combined).

## Type

Liaison officers (military-civil)

## Description

Officers serving alongside the fungeerend-[[Controleur|controleur]] (military and civil combined).

## Significance

Schakelofficieren is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
