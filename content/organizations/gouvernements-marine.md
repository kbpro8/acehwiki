**Gouvernements Marine**, a Government Navy, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Operated the Gouvernementsvaartuig 'Wega' stationed at Oelèë Lheuë for monthly runs to Westkust posts.

## Type

Government Navy

## Description

Operated the Gouvernementsvaartuig 'Wega' stationed at [[Oelèë Lheuë]] for monthly runs to [[Westkust]] posts. Vessels nicknamed by locals '[[setengah Kompenie]]' (half-military). Played a major role in the [[Bakongan-actie]] [[1925]]-[[1927]], ferrying goods and casualties between [[Oelèë Lheuë]] and the action area.

## Significance

Gouvernements Marine is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
