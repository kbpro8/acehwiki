**Koninklijke Marine**, a Royal Netherlands Navy, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Recipient of coal deliveries at Sabang before its opening as a free port in 1896.

## Type

Royal [[Leyden (Leiden)|Netherlands]] Navy

## Description

Recipient of coal deliveries at [[Sabang]] before its opening as a free port in [[1896]].

## Significance

Koninklijke Marine is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
