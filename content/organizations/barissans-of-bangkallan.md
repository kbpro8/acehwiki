**Barissans of Bangkallan** was an indigenous auxiliary military organization recruited from the regency of Bangkallan on Madura, which served alongside regular battalions of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] during campaigns against the [[Aceh Sultanate]] between [[1873]] and [[1876]].

## Organization and Strength

As documented in official returns on [[26 December]] [[1875]], two full battalions of barissans were deployed in Aceh, comprising 40 European and indigenous officers and over 1,600 Madurese soldiers. A specialized reinforcement contingent from Bangkallan numbered 21 officers and 497 soldiers.

## Role in Pel's Offensive Campaigns

During [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]], the [[Barissans of Bangkallan]] served as vanguard and flank guards. On [[26 December]] [[1875]], two barissan battalions led the assault during the [[Attack on Mibouw]]. 

On [[13 February]] [[1876]], the Bangkallan auxiliary troops marched alongside the [[3rd Infantry Battalion]], [[12th Infantry Battalion]], and [[13th Infantry Battalion]] during the [[March through the XXII Moekims]], engaging forces led by [[Panglima Polem]].
