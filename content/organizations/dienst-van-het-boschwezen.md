**The Dienst van het Boschwezen** — the Forestry Service of the [[Nederlandsch-Indië]] government — was the colonial forestry service charged with the management and exploitation of the government forests of the Netherlands Indies. The Dienst van het Boschwezen is named in the [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet of 1930]] as the colonial service on whose behalf the [[Baleq]] exploitation trial was undertaken from [[14 September]] [[1924]], and as the service that supplied the standing [[Pinus Merkusii]] forests that formed the raw material of the [[Baleq]] enterprise.

## The 1924 exploitation trial

The [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet]] records that, by the [[Besluit van 27 June 1924 on Atjeh-turpentine exploitation|Besluit of 27 June 1924]], the Regeering placed funds at the disposal of *'s Lands Caoutchouchedrijf* for an *exploitatie-proef* (exploitation trial) *uit te voeren door 's Lands Caoutchouchedrijf voor rekening van den Dienst van het Boschwezen* — *to be carried out by 's Lands Caoutchouchedrijf on behalf of the Dienst van het Boschwezen*. The trial was begun on [[14 September]] [[1924]] in the *Semnenboschcomplexen* of [[Paja Djorok]] and [[Blangtekoer]] — both near the road from [[Takengon]] to [[Bireuen]] in Atjeh. The *Semnenboschcomplexen* were thus forests of the Dienst van het Boschwezen, and the trial was undertaken on the Dienst's behalf.

## The explorations

The [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet]] records that the *Semnenboschcomplexen* of Paja Djorok and Blangtekoer had been identified by the Dienst van het Boschwezen in the course of its *exploratie van de dennenbosschen* — its exploration of the pine forests. The exploration was undertaken by the Dienst's foresters, who *hier en daar balsem ingezameld* (here and there collected balsam) — that is, collected the raw resinous exudate of the tapped pines — which was then *in het laboratorium van de Afdeeling Nijverheid te Buitenzorg* (in the laboratory of the Industry Department at Buitenzorg) *gesplitst werd in hars en terpentijn* (split into resin and turpentine). The product of the trial was thus not factory-scale turpentine but *roept-monster* — a "shouted sample" — that is, a trial sample rather than a regular product.

## The Proefstation voor het Boschwezen

The [[Atjeh-terpentijn (Spoor, 1930)|Atjeh-terpentijn pamphlet]] records that the *Mededeeling no. 19 van het Proefstation voor het Boschwezen* — the publication of the [[Proefstation voor het Boschwezen]] — was the source of the observation that the odour of the first Atjeh-turpentine samples of [[1924]] had been *afwijkend van die van de goede Amerikaansche en Fransche soorten* (different from that of the good American and French sorts). The *Proefstation voor het Boschwezen* was the research station associated with the Dienst van het Boschwezen, and its *Mededeeling* no. 19 was the principal published source on the silviculture of [[Pinus Merkusii]] in the [[Gajo-landen|Gajo-Landen]] — including the [[C. Brandts Buys|Brandts Buys]] monograph on the *quarre* tapping method.

## Significance

The Dienst van het Boschwezen's significance in the historiography of colonial Aceh is as the colonial forestry service that identified and managed the [[Pinus Merkusii]] forests of the [[Gajo-landen|Gajo-Landen]]-[[Atjeh]] border region, and as the service on whose behalf the [[Baleq]] exploitation trial was undertaken from [[14 September]] [[1924]]. The Dienst's explorations of the *dennenbosschen* — the pine forests — of Atjeh were the principal source of the standing timber that supplied the [[Baleq]] enterprise, and its research station — the [[Proefstation voor het Boschwezen]] — was the principal source of the silvicultural knowledge that underpinned the *quarre* tapping method.
