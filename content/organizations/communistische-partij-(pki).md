**The Communistische Partij (PKI)** — the *Partai Komunis Indonesia*, the Communist Party of Indonesia — was the Indonesian communist party that conducted the [[Communist uprisings of 1926|communist uprisings of 1926]] on [[Java]] and that, in the late-colonial period, was active on [[Ambon]] — where the colonial army kept watch that the *gevaarlijke communist Patty* (the book's misspelling/abbreviation of *Partai Komunis*) would not "zijn hoofd af zou slaan" of the former sergeant [[Nussy]], who had become *mantri-politie* on Ambon under the civil bestuur of his former commander, the Assistent-Resident [[Schmidt]].

## The 1926 uprisings

The PKI conducted the [[Communist uprisings of 1926|communist uprisings of 1926]] on Java. The uprisings, together with the *nasmeulend verzet* (smouldering resistance) that flared up on the [[Westkust (of Atjeh)|Westkust of Atjeh]] at the same time, forced a partial restoration of the [[K.N.I.L.]]'s strength after the cutbacks of the post-First-World-War period.

## The Soekarno connection

The book notes that between [[1906]] and [[1927]] "de communisten, met [[Soekarno]] (u kent hem wel!) en zijn handlangers, hun slag wilden slaan" — the communists, with Soekarno and his henchmen, tried to make their strike on Java. The connection between the PKI uprisings of [[1926]] and Soekarno is the book's framing of the communist threat as continuous with the later Indonesian nationalist movement.

## The threat to Nussy on Ambon

The PKI was active on [[Ambon]] in the late-colonial period. The colonial army kept watch that the *gevaarlijke communist Patty* would not "zijn hoofd af zou slaan" of [[Nussy]] — would not "behead" Nussy — because "*Nussy* was altijd soldaat gebleven, *soldaat sadja*" — Nussy had always remained a soldier, nothing but a soldier. The PKI's threat to Nussy is the book's small register of the political danger to the old colonial army's Ambonese soldaten in the late-colonial period: the soldaten who had served the Dutch were, in the late-colonial period, suspected by both the Republican nationalists and the communists.

## Significance

The Communistische Partij (PKI) is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as the Indonesian communist party that conducted the [[Communist uprisings of 1926|communist uprisings of 1926]] and that, in the late-colonial period, was active on Ambon. The book's argument is that the PKI's threat to [[Nussy]] — the old colonial army's Ambonese soldaten — was the political danger of the late-colonial period: the soldaten who had served the Dutch were, in the late-colonial period, suspected by both the Republican nationalists and the communists.
