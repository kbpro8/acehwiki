**Oeléëbalang institution**, a Traditional territorial nobility, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. 

## Type

Traditional territorial nobility

## Significance

Oeléëbalang institution is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
