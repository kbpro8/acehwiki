**The Bond van Vrije Liberalen** was a Dutch political association that hosted a pivotal meeting in [[Amsterdam]] on [[13 January]] [[1917]] at which former [[Governor-General]] [[J. B. van Heutsz]] delivered a major speech on the defence of [[Netherlands India|Netherlands Indies]], making the organisation a significant arena in the colonial defence debate.

## The Meeting of 13 January 1917

On [[13 January]] [[1917]], the Bond van Vrije Liberalen convened a public meeting in [[Amsterdam]] addressed by [[J. B. van Heutsz]]. The former Governor-General, leveraging his reputation as the conqueror of [[Aceh]], spoke on the urgent necessity of reforming the defence system of [[Netherlands India|Netherlands Indies]]. His speech drew upon the defence proposals advanced by his son-in-law, [[Lieutenant-Colonel J. Van der Weyden]], and articulated the case for a substantial reorganisation of colonial military forces in light of the conditions created by the [[First World War]].

## Knobel's Proposal

At the meeting, Heer Knobel rose to propose that [[J. B. van Heutsz]] be appointed *buitengewoon Regeerings-Commissaris* with unlimited powers to carry out the necessary reforms to the defence system. Knobel described Van Heutsz as "den man bij uitnemendheid" — the man par excellence — for such a task. This proposal received "levendig applaus en langdurige toejuichingen" (lively applause and prolonged cheers) from those assembled.

## The Board's Restraint

Despite the enthusiasm of the audience, the *Bestuur* (board) of the Bond van Vrije Liberalen prudently declined to include Knobel's motion regarding the appointment of Van Heutsz as *Regeerings-Commissaris* in any formal resolution of the meeting. This decision reflected the board's awareness that such a proposal, while popular among the association's members, raised delicate constitutional and political questions about the proper relationship between private political associations and the authority of the [[Ministry of Colonies]].

## Vattier Kraane's Address

[[C. C. Vattier Kraane]] also spoke at the meeting, addressing the relationship between economic development in [[Netherlands India|Netherlands Indies]] and the requirements of colonial defence. His contribution broadened the discussion beyond purely military considerations to encompass the economic foundations upon which any sustainable defence system would need to rest.

## Press Coverage

The meeting was reported in both *De Nieuwe Courant* and *De Telegraaf* on [[13 January]] and [[14 January]] [[1917]], ensuring that Van Heutsz's arguments and the reception they received reached a national readership. The press coverage amplified the political impact of the gathering and contributed to the mounting public pressure on the government to address colonial defence reform.

## Significance

The Bond van Vrije Liberalen meeting of [[13 January]] [[1917]] was a key moment in the defence debate of that year. It demonstrated how the authority of [[Aceh]] veterans — above all [[J. B. van Heutsz]] — was deployed to shape the terms of [[Netherlands India|Netherlands Indies]] defence policy discussion in the metropole. By providing a platform for Van Heutsz's speech and for Knobel's dramatic proposal, the Bond helped to transform what had been a specialist military question into a matter of broad political concern. The meeting complemented the activities of the [[Vereeniging ter Beoefening van de Krijgswetenschap]] and the [[Indische Krijgsvereeniging]] in pressuring the government toward decisive action.
