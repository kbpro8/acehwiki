**Perlak Petroleum-Maatschappij** was oil company drilling terrains near [[Peureula]] later exploited by [[Bataafsche Petroleum Maatschappij]].

## Operations

Near [[Rantau Pandjang]] north-west [[Peureula]] found May [[1875]] officers [[Timor]] petroleum source led By Atjeh heads. Yield 60 gantang earlier 40 gantang i.e., over picol almost 6 dollar. Head monthly income 200 dollars besides three sources.

Now drilling terrains of Perlak Petroleum-Mij which Bataafsche Petroleum-Maatschappij exploits, and terrains of famous [[Zuid-Perlak Petroleum-Mij]]. Bataafsche processes oil not at place but pumps through pipeline 120 km to [[Pangkalan Brandan]]. Only gases at [[Rantau Pandjang]] compressed to oil likewise pumped.

Settlement with about 30 Europeans. Oil cijns made oeléëbalang rich; after [[1912]] petroleum accijns in landscape treasury and oeléëbalang fixed income.
