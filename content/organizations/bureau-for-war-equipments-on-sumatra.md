**Bureau for War Equipments on Sumatra** was a specialized administrative and logistical military bureau established in June [[1873]] by the [[Dutch East Indies Government]] in [[Batavia]] to organize intelligence, transport, and munitions for the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] against the [[Aceh Sultanate]].

## Purpose and Organization

Created immediately after the defeat of the [[First Aceh Expedition]], the bureau was administratively separated from the Department of War. As noted by General [[G. G. Booms]] in [[1875]], the personnel of the bureau were assigned directly to the headquarters staff of Commander-in-Chief General [[J. van Swieten]], operating as a temporary, de facto [[General Staff of the KNIL]].

## Achievements in Campaign Preparation

Throughout late [[1873]], the bureau synthesized scattered geographical reports, interrogated informants, and organized the [[Transport and Coolie System in Aceh]]. Its preparatory work enabled General [[J. van Swieten]] to land over 13,000 troops at [[Koerong Raba Bay]], capture [[Meuraxa]], and successfully occupy [[Kraton of Aceh]] in January [[1874]].
