**3e Bataljon Infanterie** was a core infantry unit of the Dutch military that played a central role in the [[First Aceh Expedition]] in [[1873]].

## Combat History
Commanded by officers such as Major [[F.P. Cavaljé]] and Captain [[J.P. van Lier]], the battalion led the vanguard during the [[First Conquest of the Great Missigit]] on [[10 April]] [[1873]]. The battalion was heavily engaged in ladder assaults and close-quarters combat against the Acehnese defenders. It was again fully deployed as the primary assault force during the [[Second Conquest of the Great Missigit]] on [[14 April]] [[1873]].
