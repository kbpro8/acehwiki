**The Benkoelen** — *Hr. Ms. Benkoelen*, a Dutch warship of the [[Koninklijke Marine|Koninklijke Marine] — was the vessel that, in [[1884]], conveyed the Atjèhse chieftain [[Teuku Umar|Teukoe Oema]] from [[Meulaboh|Meulaboh]] to [[Rigaih|Rigaih]] and thence to [[Lambeusong|Lambeusong]] on the [[Westkust|Westkust]] of [[Atjeh|Atjèh], where Toekoe Oema — after being brought ashore by the armed sloop of the *Benkoelen* — massacred the sloop's crew. The massacre of the sloop of the *Benkoelen* was one of the two principal acts of Toekoe Oema's pre-submission career (the other being the [[June 1886|June]] [[1886]] capture of the *[[Hok Canton|Hok Canton]]*), and was one of the warning signs that the Dutch would later disregard when they armed him as their principal Atjèhse auxiliary in the [[Pahlawan-klucht|Pahlawan-klucht]].

## The 1884 Conveyance

In [[1884]] — before his submission to the Dutch — Toekoe Oema was conveyed by the warship *Benkoelen* from Meulaboh to Rigaih and thence to Lambeusong. The conveyance — by a Dutch warship, with Toekoe Oema and his companions aboard — was, in form, an act of Dutch transportation of an Atjèhse chieftain. The Dutch, in this period of the [[Concentratie-stelsel (1884-1896)|concentration system]] under [[Demmeni|Demmeni]], were not yet at war with Toekoe Oema; the conveyance was, presumably, a Dutch-facilitated movement of the chieftain along the Westkust.

## The Lambeusong Massacre

At Lambeusong, after the *Benkoelen* had brought Toekoe Oema and his companions ashore, Toekoe Oema massacred the armed sloop of the *Benkoelen* that had brought them. The massacre — the killing of the crew of a Dutch warship's sloop by the chieftain whom the warship had just conveyed — was one of the most brazen acts of Atjèhse aggression against the Dutch in the period of the concentration system. The Dutch, however, did not retaliate; and Toekoe Oema, in his later account — when he again feigned good disposition and offered submission — dismissed the massacre as the deed of his youth, done in thoughtlessness.

## The Precedent

The massacre of the sloop of the *Benkoelen* at Lambeusong was the precedent for Toekoe Oema's [[June 1886|June]] [[1886]] capture of the *Hok Canton* at the roadstead of Rigaih — the second of the two principal acts of Toekoe Oema's pre-submission career. Both acts were, in Toekoe Oema's later account, the deeds of his youth, done in thoughtlessness; but they were the deeds that the Dutch would later disregard when they armed him as their principal Atjèhse auxiliary — and that the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (March 1896)|defection of 29 March 1896]] would expose as the warning signs that the Dutch had refused to recognise.

## Significance

The *Benkoelen* is the Dutch warship whose [[1884]] conveyance of Toekoe Oema from Meulaboh to Rigaih and thence to Lambeusong — and the subsequent massacre of the *Benkoelen*'s armed sloop at Lambeusong by Toekoe Oema — constitutes one of the two principal acts of Toekoe Oema's pre-submission career. The massacre — the killing of the crew of a Dutch warship's sloop by the chieftain whom the warship had just conveyed — was one of the most brazen acts of Atjèhse aggression against the Dutch in the period of the concentration system, and one of the principal warning signs that the Dutch would later disregard when they armed Toekoe Oema as their principal Atjèhse auxiliary.
