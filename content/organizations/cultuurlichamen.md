**Cultuurlichamen**, a Agricultural enterprise bodies, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Agricultural enterprise bodies that have attempted to set up 'arbeiders-kolonisatie' (worker colonization) in preparation for the abolition of the poenale sanctie.

## Type

Agricultural enterprise bodies

## Description

Agricultural enterprise bodies that have attempted to set up '[[arbeiders-kolonisatie]]' (worker colonization) in preparation for the abolition of the [[Poenale Sanctie|poenale sanctie]]. With the exception of a few capital-strong bodies, these attempts have not shown great results.

## Significance

Cultuurlichamen is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
