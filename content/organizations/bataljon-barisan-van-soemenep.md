**The bataljon barisan van Soemenēp** (barisan battalion of Soemenēp, in eastern [[Madura]]; the same Madurese auxiliaries were also called, after the adjoining residency, the barisan of Pamekassan) was a battalion of the *[[Barisans|barisan]]* — the native auxiliary infantry of the Netherlands Indies Army — serving in [[Atjeh]] during the period December [[1875]] – September [[1876]].

## Service in 1876

[[Kolonel Meijer|H. F. Meijer]], in *[[Atjeh van 26 December 1875 tot 4 September 1876]]* ([[1883]]), records its companies in every combined operation of the consolidation year — in which the Soemenēp and Pamekassan battalions stood nearly permanently attached to the European field battalions:

- One company, commanded by Captain [[M. A. Thieme]], took part in the [[Storming of Lambaroe (7 March 1876)|relief column of 6–7 March 1876]] with the [[8e Bataljon|8th Infantry Battalion]], formed part of Major [[Majoor Diepenheim|Diepenheim]]'s attacking column, and covered the transport of the wounded from the field to the hospital at Kota Radja on [[7 March]].
- A company of the battalion barisan of Pamekassan marched, with the [[12e Bataljon|12th Infantry Battalion]], on 22 March along the southern line to screen the building of [[Night attack on Atoë Noord (22-23 March 1876)|Atoë Noord]], and on [[9 April]] into the [[VI Moekims]] with Major [[Majoor Jeltes|Jeltes]] after the [[Koewala Pantjoer patrol ambush (7 April 1876)|Pantjoer patrol ambush]].
- Three companies of the barisan of Soemenēp formed the bulk of the 2nd column, under Lieutenant-Colonel [[Luitenant-kolonel Engel|Engel]], in the [[Biloel Zuid operation (13-15 May 1876)|Biloel Zuid operation]] of 13–15 May.
- In May a company of barisan was sent to strengthen the post of [[Boekit Seboen]] when reports spoke of a thousand ill-disposed Atjehers near it.

## Battleworthiness

Meijer's judgment of the barisan as field troops in Atjehnese warfare was sober: with the [[1e Bataljon Barisan van Bangkallang|Bangkallang battalion]] it belonged to *"the least drilled and disciplined of all the infantry"* on the war's terrain, deserving praise when it held as at the [[Ambush between Kota Alam and Oleh Karang (28 July 1876)|stand of 28 July]] and the butt of his criticism where its young men — as in the [[Gli Taroem night ambush (8-9 August 1876)|Gli Taroem night]] — could no longer be kept silent by their officers.
