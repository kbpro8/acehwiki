**Lampehan-bendetje** was the small band still wandering in [[Bireuën]] at the beginning of [[1913]], whose head reported himself in February of that year.

In Februari meldde zich in Bireuën het hoofd van het daar nog zwervende Lampehan-bendetje and Bireuën too was clean. Its submission belongs to the sequence of eliminations of [[1913]] which cleared district after district in the phase [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]] called "the needles in the haystack."
