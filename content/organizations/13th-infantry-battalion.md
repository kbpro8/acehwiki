**13th Infantry Battalion** was an active combat infantry battalion of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] deployed as reinforcements to the Aceh operational theater during [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]] in [[1875]] and [[1876]].

## Composition

Arriving in late [[1875]], the battalion numbered 18 European officers, 185 European soldiers, and 377 indigenous soldiers, totaling 580 men.

## Operations in the Interior

By [[30 January]] [[1876]], the battalion was operating in mobile field columns around [[Pekan Badak]]. On [[13 February]] [[1876]], the [[13th Infantry Battalion]] formed part of the primary offensive strike force during the [[March through the XXII Moekims]], battling Acehnese defenders under [[Panglima Polem]] across rice paddies and bentengs.
