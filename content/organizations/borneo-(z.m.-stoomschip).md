**Borneo** was a steam warship of the [[Marine (Dutch Navy)|Royal Netherlands Navy]] that formed one of the four steamers of the squadron which carried the [[Lamtoba column (1879)|Lamtoba column]] from [[Oleh-leh]] and landed it at [[Lampanas]] on [[27 July]] [[1879]], as recorded in [[W. Cool]] and [[G. B. Hooijer]]'s *[[Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis (Cool & Hooijer, 1889)|Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis]]* ([[1889]]).

## Service of the expedition

At the embarkation of [[26 July]] [[1879]], the Z. M. Stoomschip Borneo received the detachment of Mineurs with the remaining European company of the [[9e Bataljon Infanterie]] — the baggage and personeel of the expedition being so distributed among the four steamships: the [[Merapi (Z.M. stoomschip)|Merapi]] with two European companies of the [[6e Bataljon Infanterie]] and the ambulance; the [[Soerabaya (Z.M. stoomschip)|Soerabaya]] with the staff, the artillery, three companies of the 9e Bataljon and the recording personnel; the Borneo as described; and the [[Graaf van Bijland (stoomschip)|Graaf van Bijland]] with the staff, the two native companies of the 6e Bataljon, the Intendance and the unattached coolies. Together with the squadron she swung at anchor on the calm sea of the Lampanas roadstead through the golden morning of [[27 July]], and discharged her troops into the boat-trains towed to the strand by the steam barkasses.
