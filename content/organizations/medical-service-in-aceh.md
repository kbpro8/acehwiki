**Medical Service in Aceh** was the military healthcare, hospital, and sanitary corps of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] deployed in the [[Aceh Sultanate]] to combat combat casualties and tropical diseases between [[1873]] and [[1876]].

## Strength and Organization in 1875

On [[26 December]] [[1875]], the medical service in Aceh totaled 120 personnel, comprising 24 European military physicians and surgeons and 105 indigenous medical assistants and hospital orderlies.

## Crisis in the Kraton Hospital and Reforms

During the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] in early [[1874]], the service faced a catastrophic medical crisis when the primary hospital inside [[Kraton of Aceh]] became overcrowded, triggering severe cholera outbreaks ([[Field Hospital and Cholera Epidemics in Aceh]]). 

Under General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]] in [[1874]] and [[1875]], the [[Medical Service in Aceh]] reorganized its sanitary infrastructure, erecting elevated wooden hospital pavilions at [[Pantei Perak]] along the Aceh River and at [[Oleh-leh]], significantly reducing disease mortality during [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]].
