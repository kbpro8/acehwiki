**Zelfbestuur (self-administration)**, a colonial administrative system, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. System in which a landschap (region) is governed by a native zelfbestuurder (self-administrator) under Dutch suzerainty.

## Type

colonial administrative system

## Description

System in which a [[landschap]] (region) is governed by a native [[zelfbestuurder]] (self-administrator) under Dutch [[Suzerainty question|suzerainty]]. Example: [[Teukoe Hadji Tjhi' Mohamad Djohan Alamsjah]] of [[Peusangan]]. Zelfbestuurders were 'ingesteld' (engaged) in recent ordonnance on cattle control.

## Significance

Zelfbestuur (self-administration) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
