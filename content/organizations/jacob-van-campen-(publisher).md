**Jacob van Campen** was the Amsterdam *drukkerij* (printing house) and publisher that in [[1914]] issued the booklet *[[Reisindrukken van Atjeh (Moens, 1914)|Reisindrukken van Atjeh]]* by the retired naval lieutenant [[W. Moens]] — *Reisindrukken van Atjeh, door W. Moens* (Amsterdam, Jacob van Campen, 1914). The feuilleton had originally appeared in the *[[Algemeen Handelsblad]]* and was reprinted in brochure form at the request of several readers of that newspaper, with the intention, as the *uitgeefster* (publisher, feminine) wrote in the foreword, of making a truth that must gladden understood in wider circles: *Er is vrede op Atjeh, de Atjeh-oorlog is voorbij. Er heerscht rechtszekerheid en welvaart en orde* — There is peace in Atjeh, the Atjeh war is over. There reign legal security and prosperity and order. A copy of the publication reached the university library of Groningen, in whose collection it was preserved.
