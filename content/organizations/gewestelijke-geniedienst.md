**Gewestelijke Geniedienst**, a Government regional engineering service, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. 

## Type

Government regional engineering service

## Significance

Gewestelijke Geniedienst is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
