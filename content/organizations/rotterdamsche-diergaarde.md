**Rotterdamsche Diergaarde**, a zoo, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Rotterdam Zoo in the Netherlands.

## Type

zoo

## Description

Rotterdam Zoo in [[Netherlands|the Netherlands]]. Received one of the female army elephants from [[Tangsé]] as a gift.

## Significance

Rotterdamsche Diergaarde is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
