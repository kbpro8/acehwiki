**Merapi** was a steam warship of the [[Marine (Dutch Navy)|Royal Netherlands Navy]] that took part, under the command of Kapitein-Luitenant [[Kapitein-Luitenant Greve|Greve]], in the sea-borne expedition that landed the [[Lamtoba column (1879)|Lamtoba column]] at [[Lampanas]] on [[27 July]] [[1879]], kept the naval station there during the troops' stay, and returned to the station before [[Pedir]] on [[4 September]] [[1879]], as recorded in [[W. Cool]] and [[G. B. Hooijer]]'s *[[Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis (Cool & Hooijer, 1889)|Eene schoone bladzijde uit Atjeh's geschiedenis]]* ([[1889]]).

## The transport of 26–27 July 1879

At the embarkation of [[26 July]] [[1879]], the Z. M. Stoomschip Merapi received two European companies of the [[6e Bataljon Infanterie]] and the ambulance. On the passage to the north coast she lay with the squadron's other steamships — the [[Soerabaya (Z.M. stoomschip)|Soerabaya]], [[Borneo (Z.M. stoomschip)|Borneo]] and [[Graaf van Bijland (stoomschip)|Graaf van Bijland]] — on the roadstead of Lampanas, and landed her troops in the debarkation of the next morning.

## The station at Lampanas

While the column operated in the interior, the Merapi and the [[Amboina (Z.M. stoomschip)|Amboina]] remained stationed on the roadstead of Lampanas. Her commander, Greve, as the senior naval officer present, regulated with the column-commandant the three-division embarkation by which the transport Graaf van Bijland repatriated the whole column between [[2 September|2]] and [[4 September]] [[1879]]; when the last division had gone out at ten o'clock on the last morning, the Merapi and the Amboina steamed back to their station before Pedir.
