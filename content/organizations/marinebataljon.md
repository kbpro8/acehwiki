**Marinebataljon** was a Dutch naval infantry battalion of the *Koninklijk Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger* that operated in the Dutch East Indies during World War II. In [[March]] [[1942]] the battalion, under the command of *Overste Roelofsen*, conducted patrols and counter‑insurgency actions in the region north‑east of [[Grisseh]] and around [[Lamongan]].

## Composition and Command
- Commander: *Overste Roelofsen* (colonel of artillery, later *Kolonel Van Dijk* for the Zuid‑colonne)
- Sub‑units: motorised sections on motor‑bikes with side‑cars, a detachment led by *Lieutenant‑colonel J. G. M. Nass*, and later a small radio‑car unit.
- Assigned to the *Zuid‑colonne* alongside the *Legioen Mangko Negoro* and artillery companies.

## Operations
The battalion was ordered to form at [[Djombang]] on [[4 March]] [[1942]] and to advance towards [[Tjepoe]] to destroy a bridge over the [[Kali Solo]]. It also participated in reconnaissance missions westward and escorted civilian families to safety.

## Significance
The *Marinebataljon* exemplified the Dutch effort to maintain order and resist Japanese advances in the East Indies, including the Aceh peninsula.
