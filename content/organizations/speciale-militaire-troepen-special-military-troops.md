**Speciale militaire troepen (special military troops)**, a military unit, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Special military troops that, even today, are still considered necessary in some regions for control/contrôle - descendant of the Atjeh-war military units.

## Type

military unit

## Description

Special military troops that, even today, are still considered necessary in some regions for control/contrôle - descendant of the Atjeh-war military units. (Likely refers to the [[Marechaussée|marechaussee]].)

## Significance

Speciale militaire troepen (special military troops) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
