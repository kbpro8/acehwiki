The **Atjeh Museum** at [[Koeta Radja]] was the provincial museum founded in the pavilion which [[Acheh|Atjeh]] had built for the colonial exhibition at [[Semarang]] ([[1914]]), re-erected in the Atjehnese capital after the exhibition and opened by Governor [[H. N. A. Swart]] in the second half of [[1915]].

## From Semarang to Koeta Radja

The annual-market exhibitions held in Atjeh — the first under Governor [[Gotfried Coenraad Ernst van Daalen|G. C. E. van Daalen]] (April [[1908]]), the second in [[1911]], which succeeded so well that it was decided to hold such an exhibition once every three years — were interrupted before [[1914]] because of the colonial exhibition to be held that year at Semarang, and in [[1917]] the world war was still in full course. The invitation to participate in the Semarang exhibition was followed: the province appeared with a thoroughly well-kept exposition in a separate pavilion, which drew much attention. "Dit paviljoen in Atjeh teruggebracht, werd te Koeta-Radja weer opgericht tot een Atjehmuseum, dat in de tweede helft van 1915 door den gouverneur werd geopend" — "This pavilion, brought back to Atjeh, was re-erected at Koeta Radja as an Atjeh museum, which was opened by the Governor in the second half of 1915". The book closes its photo sequence with a plate of the "Atjeh-museum te Koeta-Radja" (together with the plate "Papier-a of Poelo Weh").

## Account from *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu*

Museum at [[Koetaradja]] in Acehnese architectural style, holding collections on land and people of Aceh (currently being reorganized). Houses the [[Tjakra Donja]] bell.

*Source: J. Jongejans, Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu (Baarn: Hollandia Drukkerij N.V., c. 1938), pp. 19, 48.*
