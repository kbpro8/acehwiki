**Gouvernement Sumatra**, a Provincial government of Sumatra, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Established as a consequence of the 'Bestuurshervorming' in the buitengewesten.

## Type

Provincial government of Sumatra

## Description

Established as a consequence of the 'Bestuurshervorming' in the [[Buitengewesten|buitengewesten]]. As a result, the [[Residentie Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|gewest Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] is no longer led by a Gouverneur but by a Resident, with a gewestelijk Secretaris, an [[Assistent-Resident]] ter beschikking (also administrator of the landschapkassen), and a [[Controleur]] ter beschikking.

## Significance

Gouvernement Sumatra is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
