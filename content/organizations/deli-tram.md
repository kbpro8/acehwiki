**The Deli-tram** ("Deli tramway") was the tramway network of the [[Deli-Spoorweg-Maatschappij|Deli-Spoorweg-Maatschappij]] on the [[Oostkust van Sumatra|East Coast of Sumatra]] — distinct from the Deli-spoorweg (the 92 K.M. main railway line from [[Belawan-Deli|Belawan-Deli]] via [[Medan]] to [[Deli-Toea|Deli Tua]]) — that, by [[1913]], extended 170 K.M. southward to [[Tebing Tinggi|Tebing Tinggi]] and northward to [[Pangkalan Brandan|Pangkalan Brandan]]. In the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, the Deli-tram was the principal tramway network with which the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]]'s southern extension was projected to connect — either at [[Tandjoeng Slamat]] (the direct connection, deferred in [[1913]]) or at [[Pangkalan Brandan]] (the substitute connection, also under consideration in [[1913]]–[[1914]]).

## Account from *De Buitenbezittingen* (1917) — Deli-tram

The Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account of the [[Deli-Spoorweg-Maatschappij|Deli-Spoorweg-Maatschappij]]'s network notes that the original [[1888]] spoorweg — from the anchorage at [[Belawan-Deli|Belawan-Deli]] via [[Medan]] to [[Deli-Toea|Deli Tua]], with a zijlijntje to [[Timbang-Langkat]] ([[Bindjai|Binjai]]) — was extended in the [[1902]]–[[1907]] phase with tramwegen (tramways): "the existing spoorwegstelsel was extended in all directions with tramwegen, which now reach southward to [[Tebing Tinggi|Tebing Tinggi]] and northward to [[Pangkalan Brandan|Pangkalan Brandan]], the small export place south of the [[Aroebai|Aroe-baai]], with its shallow roadstead."

The Bureau's account of the [[Aroe-baai-plannen|Aroe-baai-plannen]] of [[1913]] then identifies the Deli-tram network as the principal tramway network with which the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]]'s southern extension was projected to connect. The Bureau notes that "a plan for further extension of the [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]] network, which was vigorously defended by various authorities, was the line via [[Besitang]] directly to [[Tandjoeng Slamat|Tandjoeng Slamat]] on the Deli-tram network" — a direct connection that would have linked the two networks at [[Tandjoeng Slamat|Tandjoeng Slamat]]. The deferral of this direct connection — and the substitution of a connection from [[Pangkalan Brandan]] to the Atjeh-tram — is the principal commercial conflict of the [[1913]] extension plans.

The Bureau's statistical tables for the Deli-tram show that the network's route length grew from 115 K.M. in [[1904]] to 170 K.M. in [[1913]], with the aanlegkapitaal rising from f 5,857,000 in [[1904]] to f 10,915,000 in [[1913]]. The Deli-tram's growth is, in the Bureau's framing, the principal private-sector counterpart to the Gouvernement's [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]] — the two networks together forming the principal railway infrastructure of northern Sumatra.

## Significance

[[Deli-tram|The Deli-tram]] is, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, the principal tramway network of the [[Oostkust van Sumatra|East Coast of Sumatra]] and the principal private-sector counterpart to the Gouvernement's [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tramway]]. The Deli-tram's connection with the Atjeh-tram — either at [[Tandjoeng Slamat]] (deferred) or at [[Pangkalan Brandan]] (under consideration) — is the principal infrastructural project of the [[1913]] extension plans, and the principal locus of commercial conflict between the Atjeh residency and the established trading interests of the East Coast.
