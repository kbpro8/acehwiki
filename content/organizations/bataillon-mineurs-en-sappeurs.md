The **Bataillon Mineurs en Sappeurs** (Battalion of Miners and Sappers) was the combatant engineer battalion of the Dutch army from its organisation in the early nineteenth century until its reorganisation as the *Korps Genietroepen* in [[1881]]. The battalion was garrisoned at [[Nijmegen|Nijmegen]] until [[1873]] and thereafter at [[Utrecht|Utrecht]]; it supplied the sapper companies — the *Compagnie Mineurs en Sappeurs* — for the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. The battalion is the unit with which [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Generaal C. J. Snijders]] served his only extended troop duty in the engineers, both as a cadet in the [[1870]] mobilisation and as a Tweede Luitenant in Atjeh in [[1874]]–[[1875]].

## Organisation

The battalion was the combatant element of the [[Korps Ingenieurs, Mineurs en Sappeurs|Korps Ingenieurs, Mineurs en Sappeurs]]. Its officers served alternatingly with the battalion and with the *Korps Ingenieurs* (the staff element); the typical lieutenant of the engineers served three to three-and-a-half years with the battalion in the lieutenants' rank. [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] was a marked exception: he served, "gerekend over een reeks van eenige tientallen jaren, de eenige, die in dezen rang, althans hier te lande, nimmer bij den troep had gediend" before his short battalion command of [[1886]]–[[1887]].

## The 1870 Mobilisation

In the [[1870]] mobilisation at the outbreak of the [[Franco-Prussian War|Franco-Prussian War]], the [[Koninklijke Militaire Academie|KMA]] cadets on leave were called up and posted to various troop units. The cadet [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]], then a cadet-korporaal, was posted with 14 cadets of the Netherlands and Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger to the Staf of the Bataillon Mineurs en Sappeurs at [[Utrecht|Utrecht]] on [[10 August]] [[10 August]] [[1870]]. He was assigned to the 2e Compagnie Mineurs en Sappeurs of the Utrechtsche Linie; the detachment, however, kept him only at fatigue and corvée duties — a disappointment. After the Battle of Sedan the demobilisation came on [[11 September]] [[11 September]] [[1870]].

## Service in the Aceh War

A *Compagnie Mineurs en Sappeurs* of the Bataillon was attached to the field army at [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] during the middle phase of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. In [[November 1874|November]] [[1874]] the company was commanded by [[Van den Blijck|Eerste-Luitenant Van den Blijck]] (infantry) with [[Bartelds|Eerste-Luitenant Bartelds]] (infantry) as second-in-command, both infantry officers on temporary detachment for want of trained engineer officers, under the supervising engineer officer [[W. J. C. Leers|Majoor der Genie W. J. C. Leers]]. [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede-Luitenant C. J. Snijders]] was detached to the company from the metropolitan army by [[Koninklijk Besluit of 10 June 1873 No. 33|KB of 10 June 1873 No. 33]], arriving at [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] on [[8 November]] [[8 November]] [[1874]].

The company's service in Atjeh included the [[Benting Kota Alam operation (13 November 1874)|Benting Kota Alam operation of 13 November]] [[1874]], the [[Missigit Longbatta operation (31 December 1874 – 13 January 1875)|Missigit Longbatta operation of 31 December 1874 – 13 January 1875]], the [[Capture of Pungej Blang Tjoet (23 January 1875)|capture of Pungej Blang Tjoet on 23 January 1875]], the [[Capture of Mandarsah Poetih (28 January 1875)|capture of Mandarsah Poetih on 28 January 1875]], and the [[Lamara Olejlo fortification (15 February 1875)|Lamara Olejlo fortification of 15 February 1875]]. For these services Snijders was decorated with the [[Militaire Willemsorde|Ridderkruis der Militaire Willemsorde]] 4th class by [[Koninklijk Besluit of 12 November 1875 No. 18|KB of 12 November 1875 No. 18]].

## Reorganisation of 1881

By [[1881]] the Bataillon was reorganised as the *Korps Genietroepen* (Engineer Troops Corps), with the addition of spoorweg- and telegraaftroepen (railway and telegraph troops). The Staf der Genie element continued as the *Korps Ingenieurs* (Engineers Corps).
