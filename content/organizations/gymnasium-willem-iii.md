The **Gymnasium Willem III** at [[Weltevreden]] was the secondary school in the Netherlands Indies at which the Maréchaussée officer [[W. V. Rhemrev]] received his education before entering the *[[Koninklijke Militaire Academie]]* at [[Breda]].

## Rhemrev's schooling

The [[1930]] Maréchaussée jubilee number opens its portrait notice of Rhemrev: "De heer Rhemrev ontving zijn opleiding aan het voormalig Gymnasium Willem III te Weltevreden, en aan de Kon. Militaire Academie te Breda. In 1902 werd hij officier" — Mr. Rhemrev received his education at the former Gymnasium Willem III at Weltevreden and at the Royal Military Academy at Breda; in [[1902]] he became an officer.

The notice describes the school as *voormalig* (former), indicating that it no longer existed under that name at the time of writing.
