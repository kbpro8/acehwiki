**Intendance and Supply Service in Aceh** was the military commissariat and logistical supply branch of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] responsible for provisioning, arming, and feeding troops in the [[Aceh Sultanate]] between [[1873]] and [[1876]].

## Personnel and Operational Burden

According to official tables from [[26 December]] [[1875]], the Intendance and Quartermaster service in Aceh comprised 29 European officers and 43 indigenous personnel. This small administrative corps managed the complex [[Transport and Coolie System in Aceh]] and supervised thousands of forced laborers ([[Forced Labor in Aceh Military Campaigns]]).

## Criticisms and Performance

As reported from [[Pekan Badak]] on [[30 December]] [[1875]] and [[2 January]] [[1876]], the intendance struggled against severe environmental obstacles during [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]]. Although commentators praised General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]] for establishing well-stocked magazines at [[Sinangri]], the Intendance and Supply Service was hampered by a shortage of carriers, which slowed the advance of infantry columns.
