**Nanggroe / Familie-stelsel (institution)**, a traditional administrative institution, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Reputed to have been organized/introduced in Atjeh by Sultan Iskandar Moeda.

## Type

traditional administrative institution

## Description

Reputed to have been organized/introduced in Atjeh by [[Sultan Iskandar Moeda]]. Means 'family system' - the ancient kinship-based organization of Atjehnese society. (Note: OCR garbles the term as 'nge-slachten' on page 26.)

## Significance

Nanggroe / Familie-stelsel (institution) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
