**Moesapats (inlandsche rechtbanken)**, a Native courts, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Native courts whose case files provide material for atjeh-moord research.

## Type

Native courts

## Description

Native courts whose case files provide material for [[Atjeh-moord|atjeh-moord]] research. Resident must have sufficient forces to 'bijhouden' (keep up) the [[Moesapat|moesapat]] work - in practice officials are so overburdened that [[Moesapat|moesapat]] work is treated as a necessary side job.

## Significance

Moesapats (inlandsche rechtbanken) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
