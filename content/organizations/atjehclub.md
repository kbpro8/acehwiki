**Atjehclub** was the club at [[Koeta Radja]] in whose reading room hung the portraits of all the Atjeh generals.

[[I. Feenstra]], writing at [[H. N. A. Swart]]'s departure in September [[1918]], "stood there as we all did, in the reading room of the Atjeh Club, beneath the portraits of all the Atjeh generals," and reflected: "These oldest generals of Atjeh may well look grim; as guardians of Netherlands sovereignty on a strip of sand between the sea and the unknown interior."
