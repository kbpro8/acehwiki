**M. Nijhoff** — Martinus Nijhoff — was the Dutch publishing house of [['s-Gravenhage|'s-Gravenhage]] (The Hague) that served as the publisher of the [[Koninklijke Vereeniging Koloniaal Instituut|Koninklijke Vereeniging Koloniaal Instituut]], and thus as the publisher of the *[[Bijdragen tot de Taal, Land en Volkenkunde van Nederlandsch Indie|Bijdragen tot de Taal-, Land- en Volkenkunde van Nederlandsch-Indië]]* (the *B.K.I.*), of the annual *Literatuur-Overzicht*, and of the separate offprints of vernacular-text editions issued from the *B.K.I.* — including the [[H. T. Damsté|Damsté]] edition of the [[Hikajat Eitia Toedjoh|Hikajat Eitia Toedjoh]] ([[1939]]) and the [[Luc. van der Linden|van der Linden]] commemoration of the [[Bij het derde eeuwfeest van de martelaren van Atjeh (1138-1938)|third centenary of the Atjeh martyrs]]. As the Institute's publisher, Nijhoff was the principal Dutch metropolitan venue for the publication of Atjehsch and other vernacular text editions, and for the bibliographic record of the [[1939]] Atjeh scholarship.

## Role as publisher of the Koninklijk Instituut

Nijhoff's role as the publisher of the Koninklijk Instituut is recorded on the title pages of the Institute's publications throughout the late-colonial period. The *[[Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939 (Van Meurs, 1940)|Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939]]* by [[H. van Meurs|H. van Meurs]], published in [[1940]], carries the imprint "'s-Gravenhage — M. Nijhoff — 1940," and the separate offprint of the [[Hikajat Eitia Toedjoh|Hikajat Eitia Toedjoh]] (Tekst A of Damsté's edition of the Seven Sleepers legend) was issued by Nijhoff in [[1939]] as an offprint from deel 98 of the *B.K.I.* The choice of Nijhoff as the Institute's publisher placed the Institute's publications within the mainstream of Dutch academic publishing, and it ensured the wide distribution of the Institute's work through the standard channels of academic exchange.

## The Atjeh-related publications of Nijhoff

The Atjeh-related publications of Nijhoff, in its capacity as the Institute's publisher, include:

- The *[[Bijdragen tot de Taal, Land en Volkenkunde van Nederlandsch Indie|B.K.I.]]*, of which deel 98 ([[1939]]) contains the [[H. T. Damsté|Damsté]] edition of the [[De legende van de heilige zeven slapers in het Atjehsch (Damsté, 1939)|De legende van de heilige zeven slapers in het Atjehsch]] (No. 560 of the [[1939]] survey).
- The separate offprint of the [[Hikajat Eitia Toedjoh|Hikajat Eitia Toedjoh]] (Tekst A), issued by Nijhoff in [[1939]] (No. 570 of the [[1939]] survey).
- The *[[Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939 (Van Meurs, 1940)|Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939]]* by [[H. van Meurs|H. van Meurs]], published in [[1940]].

These publications together make Nijhoff the publisher of record for the principal [[1939]] Atjehsch text edition and for the principal bibliographic record of the [[1939]] Atjeh scholarship.

## Significance

For AcehWiki, M. Nijhoff is significant as the publisher of the Koninklijk Instituut's Atjeh-related publications — the *B.K.I.*, the separate offprints of vernacular-text editions, and the annual *Literatuur-Overzicht*. The house's imprint on the [[Hikajat Eitia Toedjoh|Hikajat Eitia Toedjoh]] and on the [[Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939 (Van Meurs, 1940)|Literatuur-Overzicht over het jaar 1939]] places it at the intersection of Atjehsch philology and colonial bibliography, and it makes Nijhoff the publisher of record for the principal [[1939]] Atjeh scholarship. The corpus's entries on these publications accordingly cite the Nijhoff imprint as the publication detail of record.
