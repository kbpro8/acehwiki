The **Vereeniging "Ons Leger"** (Society "Our Army") was a Dutch society founded in the late nineteenth century to promote public interest in, and support for, the Dutch armed forces. The Vereeniging was the principal public vehicle for Snijders' post-war agitation on behalf of the national defence, and the body in whose name he undertook his [[Snijders' 1920 visit to Atjeh|first Indies journey in 1920]]. The Vereeniging also published the illustrated monthly magazine *Ons Leger*, in which many of Snijders' post-war articles and the memorials of his death appeared.

## Snijders' Chairmanship

After his resignation as [[Opperbevelhebber van Land- en Zeemacht|Opperbevelhebber]] in [[November 1918|November]] [[1918]], Snijders became chairman of the Vereeniging "Ons Leger," in which capacity he undertook his [[Snijders' 1920 visit to Atjeh|first Indies journey in 1920]] — to represent the Vereeniging at the Algemene Vergadering of "Indië Weerbaar" at [[Bandoeng|Bandoeng]] on [[24 May]] [[24 May]] [[1920]]. He was accompanied by Kapitein van den Generalen Staf E. H. A. van den Akker as secretary. After his return he resumed his agitation for the defensie-vraagstuk; in [[April 1921|April]] [[1921]] the *Proeve eener oplossing van het legervraagstuk* was published, the work of a commission of six under his chairmanship. The commission advocated amendment and improvement of the pending draft dienstplicht wet (conscription law) and the corresponding organisatie-wet, and the taking in hand of the question of economic weerbaarheid (economic defence).

## The Magazine *Ons Leger*

The Vereeniging's monthly magazine *Ons Leger* carried many of Snijders' post-war articles, and after his death on [[26 May]] [[26 May]] [[1939]] the October [[1939]] issue carried the memorial article by Kolonel b.d. H. Walaardt Sacre, "Generaal Snijders en de Nederlandsche luchtvaart," which is one of the principal sources for his post-war aviation work.
