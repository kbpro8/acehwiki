The **VI Moekim's of Groot-Atjeh** (the Six *Mukim*s of Greater Aceh) were a federation of six *mukim* — the Islamic administrative unit (from Arabic *muqīm*, literally "settled" or "resident") used in [[Acheh|Atjeh]] to organise the territorial-administrative structure of the sultanate — that formed one of the principal territorial federations of the [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] heartland around [[Banda Acheh|Koetaradja]]. The VI Moekim's are mentioned in [[J. Kreemer|Kreemer]]'s [[Kreemer's De Karbouw ([[1956]])|*De Karbouw* ([[1956]])]] (p. 246) as the inhabitants who, in the period documented by [[Christiaan Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]], held the [[Rabò annual seven-day feast|annual seven-day beach feast at Rabò]] — the seventh day of which culminated in the ritual sacrifice of a buffalo that was first stabbed and then led bleeding over all the rice-fields.

## The *mukim* as an Atjehnese administrative unit
The *mukim* (in Atjehnese orthography *mukìm* or *moekim*) is an Islamic administrative unit specific to the [[Acheh|Atjeh]] sultanate, denoting a territorial parish organised around a *meunasah* (the village prayer-house and religious school) and headed, in the colonial-period account of *De Karbouw*, by a *teungkoe meunasah* (the village religious official, who also served as the ritual slaughterer). The *mukim* system was the basis of the Atjehnese state's territorial organisation: a federation of *mukim*s (the VI, the [[XXII Moekim's|XXII]], the [[XXV Moekim's|XXV]], the [[XXVI Moekim's|XXVI]], etc.) constituted the larger territorial units that, taken together, formed the [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] heartland of the sultanate. The VI Moekim's — the smallest of the great *mukim*-federations — were one of these.


## In De Karbau
The VI Moekim's of Groot-Atjeh appear in *De Karbouw* (p. 246) as the inhabitants who held the [[Rabò annual seven-day feast|annual seven-day beach feast at Rabò]]:

> *"Op het nabij [[Poelo Breuëh|Poelo Breuëh]] (Poelau Bras) gelegen eilandje [[Rabò|Rabò]] in Groot-Atjeh plegen de bewoners der VI Moekim's elk jaar aan het strand een zevendaags feest te geven."*
The passage fixes the VI Moekim's as a defined territorial-administrative unit capable of collective ritual action — the holding of an annual feast on the islet of [[Rabò|Rabò]] near [[Poelo Breuëh|Poelo Breuëh]]. The fact that the feast was held on an islet off the [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] coast, rather than on the mainland territory of the VI Moekim's themselves, suggests that the islet had a specific ritual significance for the federation — perhaps as a peripheral, liminal site appropriate to the apotropaic and carnivalesque character of the rite.

## Significance
The VI Moekim's of Groot-Atjeh are significant for the encyclopedia of [[Acheh]] history as one of the principal *mukim*-federations of the [[Groot-Atjeh|Groot-Atjeh]] heartland, and as the documented performers of the [[Rabò annual seven-day feast|annual seven-day buffalo-sacrifice feast at Rabò]]. The federation exemplifies the *mukim*-based territorial organisation of the [[Acheh|Atjeh]] sultanate, in which the territorial units of the state were constituted as federations of Islamic parishes (the individual *mukim*s, each organised around a *meunasah* and headed by a *teungkoe meunasah*). The holding of the Rabò feast by the VI Moekim's is a documented instance of collective ritual action at the level of the *mukim*-federation — a fact that ties the territorial-administrative structure of the sultanate to the ritual-agricultural calendar of its buffalo-sacrifice complex.
