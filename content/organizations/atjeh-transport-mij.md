**The Atjeh Transport-Mij** — the *Atjeh Transport Maatschappij* — was a subsidiary of the [[Sabang-Mij|Sabang-Mij]], operating the praow-service at the harbour of [[Oelèë Lheue|Oelèë Lheuë]]. The ships of the [[Kon. Paketvaart|Kon. Paketvaart]] unload into the praows of a Chinese entrepreneur or into those of the Atjeh Transport-Mij. The company has besides a small steamer which calls at the trading-pier at Oeleleh to bring or fetch goods and passengers.
