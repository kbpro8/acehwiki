**The Bureau van Financieele aangelegenheden** ("Bureau of Financial Affairs") was a Dutch colonial bureau established by Gouvernementsbesluit (G. B.) of [[30 January]] [[1911]] No. 3 to provide centralised leadership and oversight of the [[Landschapskassen|landschapskassen (territorial treasuries)]] of the [[Zelfbesturen|self-governing territories]] of the [[Buitenbezittingen|Outer Possessions]], including the [[Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh and Dependencies]] residency. In the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, the Bureau van Financieele aangelegenheden was the principal institutional innovation of the [[1910]] centralisation of the landschapskassen — the "mijlpaal" (milestone) year that the Bureau identifies as the beginning of regular, complete, and uniform data on the territorial treasuries.

## Account from *De Buitenbezittingen* (1917) — Bureau van Financieele aangelegenheden

The Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account of the [[Bureau van Financieele aangelegenheden|Bureau van Financieele aangelegenheden]] appears in the chapter on the [[Landschapskassen|landschapskassen]]. The Bureau notes that "[[1910]] forms a mijlpaal (milestone) on the way of that development. Before that year the establishment and management of the landschapskassen was rather à l'improviste, without pursuing uniformity in the various regulations; since that year the management and use of the monies have been subjected to general rules and direction, and oversight has been more centralised."

The institutional instrument of this centralisation was the [[Bureau van Financieele aangelegenheden|Bureau van Financieele aangelegenheden]], "established by G. B. of [[30 January]] [[1911]] No. 3," from which "the central leadership (since [[1913]] also including actual oversight of the technical management) of the [[Landschapskassen|landschapskassen]] proceeds." The Bureau van Financieele aangelegenheden was thus the institutional embodiment of the [[1910]] mijlpaal — the bureau through which the central Gouvernement exercised its oversight of the territorial treasuries of the Buitenbezittingen, including the eighteen kassen of the [[Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh and Dependencies]] residency.

## Significance

The [[Bureau van Financieele aangelegenheden|Bureau van Financieele aangelegenheden]] is, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, the principal institutional instrument of the post-[[1910]] centralisation of the [[Landschapskassen|landschapskassen]] of the Buitenbezittingen — including the eighteen kassen of the [[Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh and Dependencies]] residency. Its establishment in [[1911]] marks the formal translation of the [[1910]] mijlpaal of centralised oversight into a permanent institutional structure, and is one of the principal administrative consolidations of the post-[[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacification]] order in Atjeh.
