The **Vereeniging Nederlandsch-Indische Jaarbeurs** (Netherlands-Indies Trade Fair Association) was the body of which the former Maréchaussée officer [[W. V. Rhemrev]] was a co-founder, and through whose agency he helped to raise the funds for the establishment of the *[[Technische Hoogeschool te Bandoeng]]*.

## Rhemrev's role

The [[1930]] Maréchaussée jubilee number records that "de heer Rhemrev was mede-oprichter van de Vereeniging Ned. Indische Jaarbeurs, en door zijn tusschenkomst werd door Handel en Industrie in Nederland vier millioen bijeengebracht voor de oprichting van de Technische Hoogeschool te Bandoeng" — Mr. Rhemrev was co-founder of the Netherlands-Indies Trade Fair Association, and through his intervention four million was brought together by Trade and Industry in the Netherlands for the establishment of the Technical College at [[Bandung|Bandoeng]].

The episode belongs to Rhemrev's civil career, following his service with the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]* and his work as organiser of the *[[Indië Weerbaar]]* movement in [[1917]].
