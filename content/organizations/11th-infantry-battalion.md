**11th Infantry Battalion** was a combat infantry battalion of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] that served in the Aceh theater during the occupation of [[Kraton of Aceh]] and throughout [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]] in [[1875]] and [[1876]].

## Order of Battle in 1875

In December [[1875]], the battalion mustered 18 European officers, 212 European soldiers, and 363 indigenous soldiers, totaling 593 men assigned to the Left Division in Aceh.

## Role in Coastal Defense

The battalion manned defensive redoubts between [[Meuraxa]] and [[Kotta Alam]], repelling Acehnese skirmishers and securing the Aceh River communication line under General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]].
