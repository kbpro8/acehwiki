**The 10e Bataljon Infanterie** was a battalion of the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Netherlands Indies Army]] embarked at [[Semarang]] on [[26 April]] [[1896]] as part of the reinforcement sent to [[Acheh|Atjeh]] for the operations against [[Teuku Umar|Toekoe Oemar]].

[[P. H. R. Beuming]] records that on that day the 10e Bataljon and three companies of the [[5e Bataljon Infanterie|5e Bataljon]] embarked at Semarang, and on the following day the 4th company of that corps, the 2nd battery of mountain artillery, one platoon of cavalry and two sections of engineer troops — *een flinke versterking was men dus wachtende*, a substantial reinforcement was therefore expected. These were the troops whose arrival allowed the [[Three-day battle in the Beradin pass (23-25 May 1896)|three-day battle in the Beradin pass]] to be undertaken in May.
