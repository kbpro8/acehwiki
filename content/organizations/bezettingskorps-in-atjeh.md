The **[[Bezettingskorps in Atjeh|bezettingskorps in Atjeh]]** was the reduced occupation corps that remained in [[Atjeh (Borel account)|Atjeh]] after the [[Departure of Jan van Swieten from Atjeh (April 1874)|departure of the hoofdmacht]] under [[Jan van Swieten|generaal van Swieten]] on [[26 April]] [[1874]]. Placed under the command of [[Generaal Pel|generaal Pel]] — at first as kolonel, from May 1874 also as civiel en militair bevelhebber — it was, in Borel's account, far below the strength the position required, and was left in a "zeer gebrekkige stelling" that it took Pel two years to reconstruct. The bezettingskorps bore the brunt of the [[Crisis at Kota Radja (May–June 1874)|crisis of May–June 1874]], the [[Cholera catastrophe at Kota Radja (February 1875)|cholera catastrophe of February 1875]] and the [[Battle of Longbatta (31 December 1874)|Longbatta disaster of 31 December 1874]], and was repeatedly reinforced by individual bataljons from Java as casualties and disease took their toll.

## Composition and Strength

The bezettingskorps that Pel inherited in April [[1874]] consisted of the troops van Swieten had judged sufficient to hold the captured [[Kraton of Atjeh|Kraton]] and the new Dutch hoofdplaats of [[Kota Radja|K. Radja]] after his [[Van Swieten's afscheidsrede (1874)|afscheidsrede]] of [[23 April]] [[1874]] declaring the war ended. Its strength was augmented by the half bataljon that, "à quelque chose malheur est bon," was kept in Atjeh as an unintended consequence of the [[Battle of Lampoe Oek (16 April 1874)|échec at Lampoe Oek]] of [[16 April]] [[1874]]. Throughout the period described by Borel the bezettingskorps's strength was far below what the stelling required. By the end of May [[1874]] the Dutch macht had "ten gevolge van het overgroot aantal zieken bijna niets meer beteekende"; some companies could field fewer than 30 men of an original 125. Borel insists that 500 kloeke Atjehneezen willing to sacrifice themselves would have sufficed "om den Kraton, en met dezen onze geheele vestiging ter kuste van Atjeh, ten val te brengen." The reinforcements that began to arrive with the *Baron Sloet van de Beele* on [[11 June]] [[1874]] allowed Pel to clear the ambulances and resume the offensive, but the bezettingskorps remained chronically understrength. The 5de bataljon, ~500 strong when it arrived in December [[1874]], could field barely 100 usable soldiers by [[15 February]] [[1875]]; the 10de bataljon bore the brunt of the Longbatta disaster on [[31 December]] [[1874]]. On paper the Dutch troepensterkte in Atjeh in the first half of [[1875]] was between 5 000 and 6 000; the actual field strength was a fraction of that.

## The Burden of the Inherited Position

The bezettingskorps inherited from van Swieten not merely a numerical weakness but a strategic one. Van Swieten had recalled one of the two companies of [[Vesting-artillerie in Atjeh|vesting-artillerie]] and the [[Achterlaadkanonnen van 12 c.M.|achterlaadkanonnen van 12 c.M.]] to Java in February [[1874]]; he had permitted [[Padi-snijden on the Langkroek sawah (1874)|padi-snijden]] on the [[Langkroek]] [[Sawah|sawah]]; he had ordered no [[Zeehoofd|zeehoofd]] built at [[Oeleh Leh]]; and he had given Pel the verbal instruction to keep "zooveel mogelijk vechten weinig, eene zuiver afwachtende houding bewaren." The bezettingskorps thus found itself besieged in a half-finished fortress: the [[Desecration of Dutch graves at Lampoeloe (1874)|desecration of Dutch graves at Lampoeloe]] on [[26 April]] [[1874]] and the enemy attack on Marassa the same night were the "eerste wrange vrucht" of the inherited system. Pel's reversal — the daily bombardment of kampongs from [[28 April]] [[1874]], the establishment of the Oeleh Leh post in early May, the [[Battle of Lampoeloe (20 June 1874)|battle of Lampoeloe]] of [[20 June]] [[1874]] — was the work of the bezettingskorps acting with what means it had. Throughout, Pel repeatedly urged Batavia for reinforcements; Borel credits him with restoring the position from "allerdonkerendst" state.

## Reinforcements and the Coming of the Stelling-Pel

The bezettingskorps was sustained through 1874 and 1875 by a stream of reinforcements: the 6de bataljon (two companies with staff) arrived at K. Radja on [[1 September]] [[1874]] from Padang, "with music at the head"; the [[Compagnie mariniers in Atjeh|compagnie mariniers]] arrived late October [[1874]]; the 5de and 10de bataljons, ~500 strong each, arrived in December [[1874]] for the Longbatta operations; the [[Bataljon Barissans van Madura|Barissans bataljon]] arrived on [[10 December]] [[1875]] and the 9de bataljon on [[21 December]] [[1875]]. With these in hand, Pel was able to launch the great [[Pel's offensive against the VI, IV and IX Moekims (1875–1876)|offensive of December 1875 – February 1876]] that conquered the [[VI Moekims|VI]], [[IV Moekims|IV]] and [[IX Moekims|IX Moekims]] and reached the [[Kroeng Tjoet]]. After Pel's [[Death of Generaal Pel (24 February 1876)|sudden death]] on [[24 February]] [[1876]] the bezettingskorps passed to [[Generaal Wiggers van Kerchem|generaal Wiggers van Kerchem]] and reverted to the *afwachtende houding*; it remained in the stelling-Pel, "nagenoeg onveranderd," until the [[Enemy breakthrough in the IV, VI and IX Moekims (June 1878)|enemy breakthrough of June 1878]] exposed the danger of stripping it of mobile forces.
