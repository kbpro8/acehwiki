The **Pepper Syndicate** (*Pepersyndicaat*) was a commercial joint venture established by the *Atjehsche Handel Maatschappij* and the *Nederlandsche Handel-Maatschappij* (NHM) to monopolize the pepper trade in Aceh.

## Operations and Failure

The syndicate provided large advance capital loans to local chiefs (*uleebalang*) and garden supervisors (*peutoeha seuneubo*) in exchange for exclusive pepper purchasing rights.

However, due to speculative price collapses, secret side-sales (*boengong lada*) by planters, double export tax assessments in [[Banda Aceh]], and the instability of the war environment, the syndicate suffered heavy financial losses totaling tens of thousands of guilders and ultimately collapsed.
