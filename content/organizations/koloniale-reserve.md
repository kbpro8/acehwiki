**The Koloniale Reserve** was a corps of the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Netherlands Indies Army]], recruited and depot-based at [[Nijmegen]], which took part in the [[Three-day battle in the Beradin pass (23-25 May 1896)|three-day battle in the Beradin pass]] of [[23 May|23]]–[[25 May]] [[1896]] as part of the main force assembled at [[Ketapan Doea]].

## At Lamtengah, [[23 May]] [[1896]]

[[P. H. R. Beuming]] records the corps in action on the first day of the battle, on the enemy's left wing, where the [[12e Bataljon Infanterie|12e]] and [[14e Bataljon Infanterie|14e Bataljon]] were engaged in a violent firefight and took [[Lamtengah]], [[Lammanjang]] and [[Lam-isi]] in succession at the cost of very many losses.

*De koloniale reserve, die hier in actie kwam, hield zich kranig* — the colonial reserve, which came into action here, bore itself gallantly. At Lamtengah especially it had a hard time. Its commander, *de sympathieke kapitein* [[Drijber]], was wounded in arm and leg but refused to be carried to the ambulance and, borne in a *[[Tandoe|tandoe]]*, continued to command in the midst of his men. This rare proof of courage in the beloved commander, Beuming writes, so transported the men *dat ze zich met een donderend hoerah op den vijand wierpen. Ze waren gewoonweg onweerstaanbaar, die Nijmeegsche jongens* — that they threw themselves on the enemy with a thundering hurrah; they were simply irresistible, those Nijmegen lads.

The rifle fire from the *knodsen* — the barrack term for the 6.5 mm repeating rifles — brought death and destruction into the enemy ranks, and after a furious hand-to-hand fight the *[[Benting|benting]]* was taken, in which a great number of [[Beaumont rifle|Beaumont rifles]] issued in their time to [[Teuku Umar|Toekoe Oemar]] were recovered.
