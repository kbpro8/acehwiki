## Type

Government immigration/colonization commission

## Description

Government commission to which the following colonization complexes were submitted: [[Langsar|Langsa]] (2 complexes: 5,100 ha and 10,000–20,000 ha), [[Groot-Atjeh]] (1 complex of c. 5,000 ha), and [[Westkust]] (5 complexes of 18,000, 20,000, 7,500, 15,000, and 122,000 ha).

## Significance

Controle-commissie voor Immigratie en Kolonisatie is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
