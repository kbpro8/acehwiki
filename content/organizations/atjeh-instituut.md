**The Atjèh Instituut** — founded in [[1914]] with the approval of the Dutch Crown (*Koninklijk besluit van 31 Juli 1914 N°. 61*) — was the scholarly association that commissioned [[J. Kreemer|J. Kreemer]]'s encyclopaedic survey *Atjèh* ([[1922]]–[[1923]]), the work that is the principal source of this wiki's Kreemer entries. Its purpose, as recorded in its statutes and quoted by Kreemer in his preface, was "om, in samenwerking met het [[Koloniaal Instituut|Koloniaal Instituut]], zoo volledig mogelijke gegevens te verzamelen betreffende de bevolking van Atjèh en Onderhoorigheden en deze gegevens beschikbaar te stellen ten behoeve van het welzijn en van de stoffelijke en de geestelijke ontwikkeling van die bevolking en van onze betrekkingen met haar" — "to collect, in collaboration with the Koloniaal Instituut, the most complete possible data concerning the population of Atjèh and Onderhoorigheden, and to make these data available for the welfare and for the material and spiritual development of that population and of our relations with it."

## Foundation and directorium

The Atjèh Instituut was founded in [[1914]], its statutes approved by *Koninklijk besluit van 31 Juli 1914 N°. 61* and published as a supplement to the *Nederlandsche Staatscourant* of [[18 September]] [[1914]] N°. 219. The founding directorium, as recorded by Kreemer, consisted of:
- **Prof. Dr. [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|C. Snouck Hurgronje]]** — chairman (voorzitter), serving until [[1920]].
- **C. J. Hasselman** — treasurer (penningmeester).
- **F. A. Liefrinck** — member.
- **Dr. C. W. Janssen** — member.
- **Prof. J. C. van Eerde** — secretary (secretaris).

The address of the association was: *de Afdeeling Volkenkunde van het Koloniaal Instituut te Amsterdam* — the Ethnology Department of the Koloniaal Instituut in Amsterdam.

## The commission of Kreemer's *Atjèh*

The Instituut's "naaste plan" — immediate plan — was "de samenstelling van een samenvattend boek over het gewest Atjèh en Onderhoorigheden, een beeld gevende van den actueelen toestand van land en volk in uitgebreiden zin" — "the composition of a summarising book about the residency of Atjèh and Onderhoorigheden, giving a picture of the actual condition of land and people in the broad sense." Kreemer was appointed *archivaris van het Atjèh Instituut* with effect from [[1 August]] [[1914]], and was charged with the composition of the work. He departed for Atjèh on New Year's Day [[1916]], arriving on [[18 February]] [[1916]] and remaining until [[23 October]] [[1917]].

## Financial support

The Instituut's work was supported financially by the *Departement van Koloniën*, the *Koloniaal Instituut*, the *Koninklijk Nederlandsch Aardrijkskundig Genootschap*, and the *Maatschappij ter bevordering van het natuurkundig onderzoek der Nederlandsche Koloniën*. The publisher was *N.V. Boekhandel en Drukkerij voorheen E.J. Brill* of Leiden.

## Significance

The Atjèh Instituut is, in Kreemer's narrative, the institutional context out of which the principal twentieth-century encyclopaedic survey of Atjèh emerged. Its foundation in [[1914]] — coinciding with the close of the [[Aceh War|Atjèh-oorlog]] and the beginning of the [[Civil Government period|civil-government period]] — marks the moment at which Dutch colonial scholarship on Atjèh shifted from the military-administrative accounts of [[E. B. Kielstra|Kielstra]] and [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]] to the comprehensive ethnographic and geographical survey that Kreemer's work represents.
