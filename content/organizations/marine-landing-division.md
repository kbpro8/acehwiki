**Marine Landing Division** was the combined amphibious force assembled by the Dutch for the 1873 Aceh expedition. It comprised two marine companies (approximately 120 men each) equipped with Beaumont and Snider rifles, a battery of landing artillery, mortars, an ambulance, and a train of supplies. The division was commanded by [[Captain-terr zee Binkes]] and operated under the overall authority of [[General-Major Köhler]].

The division’s first operation was the landing on [[5 April]] [[1873]] at the **Kraton** area, followed by a series of engagements at the **Mesigit**, the beach bivouac, and subsequent inland forays. The division was later incorporated into the broader colonial forces stationed at [[Padang]] and [[Fort de Kock]].

## Composition
- **Infantry**: Two marine companies (≈240 men total).
- **Artillery**: One half‑battery of landing guns and several mortars.
- **Support**: Ambulance and logistical train.
- **Naval support**: Warships **Antwerpen**, **Marnix**, **Soerabaja**, **Sumatra**, and auxiliary vessels **Bonbeek**, **Koning der Nederlanden**.
