**The Genietroepen** — also referred to as *Engineers* or *Genie* — was the combat engineer arm of the [[Oost-Indisch Leger]] in [[Aceh War|Atjeh]] during the spring of [[1896]], responsible for the construction and destruction of fortifications, the bridging of rivers, the demolition of enemy *bentengs* and *versterkingen*, and the building of campements and defense works at [[Koeta Radja]]. Detachments were commanded variously by Kapitein A. J. T. Zelle (colonne Lamtih), 1e Luit. A. S. Ruzette (Bergcolonne), 1e Luit. E. J. de Rochemont (colonne Veeren), 1e Luit. C. J. de Bruyn, and (in the planned March column) Luitenants N. Plantenga and A. S. Ruzette. The genietroepen's most dramatic actions of the spring were the destruction of [[Teuku Umar]]'s house at [[Lam-Pisang]] with dynamite, tar, petroleum, the demolition of the [[Kroeng-Kali]] *benteng*, and the destruction of the *bentengs* Perampoean and Batoe near [[Toengkoeb]].

## Background and Formation

As the combat engineer arm of the colonial army, the genietroepen comprised European and indigenous *minderen* trained in demolition, bridging, fortification, and field engineering. In the early months of [[1896]] the genietroepen were already responsible for the construction of fortifications, bridges, palissadeering, campements, and defense works at [[Koeta Radja]] — including the new [[Ana' Galong]] palissadeering and bastions. On [[30 March]] [[1896]] the genietroepen worked to improve the defense of Koeta Radja: barbed wire standing fence around [[Nesoch]]; two gun stands on the south side of the campement; ~14 covered infantry positions; banketten and covered infantry positions along the enceinte of kampong Baroe and kampong Gedah; raised guardhouses made more storm-proof; 3 cm armor plates placed on different banketten; and a palissadeering placed behind the fence between the laboratory and the watch on the road to [[Oleh-leh]].

## Role in the Atjeh War

On [[8 April]] [[1896]] a detachment of genietroepen under 1e Luit. A. S. Ruzette — 1 officer, 40 European and native men — formed part of [[J. W. Stemfoort (Kolonel der Infanterie)|Kolonel Stemfoort]]'s relief column for [[Biloel]] and [[Lamkoenjit]], with orders to repair the first two bridges south of [[Tjot Goeë]]. On [[2 May]] [[1896]] a detachment of genie under Luit. Ruzette formed part of the [[Diversie to Montassik / Battle of Senelop (2 May 1896)|diversie to Montassik]], restoring the *brug* over the Kroeng Lingkar at 4:30 AM with three brigades of [[Korps Marechaussée|marechaussée]]. On [[4 May]] [[1896]] a detachment under Luit. de Rochemont formed part of the [[Second Expedition to XIII Moekims Toengkoeb (4 May 1896)|second expedition to XIII Moekims Toengkoeb]]. On [[23 May]] [[1896]] a detachment under 1e Luit. A. S. Ruzette formed part of the Bergcolonne in the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day offensive]], and on [[24 May]] [[1896]] a genietroepen detachement of 43 minderen under Kapitein W. A. Zelle destroyed [[Teuku Umar]]'s house at [[Lam-Pisang]] with dynamite, tar, petroleum. On [[29 May]] [[1896]] a sectie genietroepen under luit.-adj. E. J. de Rochemont formed part of Luit.-kol. C. J. Laceulle's colonne in the [[Machtsvertoon in III Moekims Daroe (29 May 1896)|machtsvertoon in III Moekims Daroe]]. From [[30 May]] to [[2 June]] [[1896]] two sectien genietroepen led the demolition work in the [[Razing of Lam-Asan (30 May – 2 June 1896)|razing of Lam-Asan]], supported by 800 *dwangarbeiders* and 400 Chinese *koelies*. On [[16 June]]–[[17 June]] [[1896]] the genietroepen destroyed the [[Kroeng-Kali]] *benteng* and the *bentengs* Perampoean and Batoe near [[Toengkoeb]] in the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|tuchtiging of V Moekims Montassik]].

## Composition and Strength

The genietroepen were commanded by various officers through the spring: Kapitein A. J. T. Zelle (colonne Lamtih); 1e Luit. A. S. Ruzette (Bergcolonne and various operations from [[8 April]] through [[23 May]] [[1896]]); 1e Luit. E. J. de Rochemont (colonne Veeren and the [[Machtsvertoon in III Moekims Daroe (29 May 1896)|29 May machtsvertoon]]); 1e Luit. C. J. de Bruyn; and (in the planned March column) Luitenants N. Plantenga and A. S. Ruzette. On [[8 April]] [[1896]] the detachment under Luit. Ruzette numbered 1 officer and 40 European and native men. On [[24 May]] [[1896]] the detachement under Kapitein W. A. Zelle numbered 43 *minderen*. The genietroepen employed *dynamiet, teer, petroleum*, and the standard engineering tools of palissade-construction, bridging, and demolition.

## Significance

The genietroepen were indispensable to the Dutch conduct of the spring [[1896]] campaign. Without their bridging of the Kroeng Lingkar at 4:30 AM on [[2 May]] [[1896]], the [[Diversie to Montassik / Battle of Senelop (2 May 1896)|diversie to Montassik]] could not have begun; without their demolition of [[Teuku Umar]]'s house at [[Lam-Pisang]] with *dynamiet, teer, petroleum* on [[24 May]] [[1896]], the symbolic heart of Umar's stelling would have remained standing; without their razings of [[Lam-Asan]], [[Kroeng-Kali]], and the *bentengs* near [[Toengkoeb]], the Dutch punitive operations of May–June [[1896]] would have left the enemy's fortified base intact for re-occupation. The genietroepen's employment of 800 *dwangarbeiders* and 400 Chinese *koelies* in the [[Razing of Lam-Asan (30 May – 2 June 1896)|razing of Lam-Asan]] illustrates the way engineer-led demolition operations mobilized coerced labor on an industrial scale.
