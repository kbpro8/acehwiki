**K.N.I.L.** (Koninklijk Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger) was the abbreviation for the Royal Netherlands East Indies Army, the principal Dutch colonial military force in the *Dutch East Indies* during the early twentieth century. The army’s structure and command were altered by the *Regeling op den Staat van Oorlog* on [[10 May]] [[1940]].

## Operational Context (1942)
During the Japanese invasion in [[1942]], the KNIL faced severe shortages of equipment and intelligence, leading to defeats such as the loss of Java. Units like the *Marinebataljon* and *Legioen Mangko Negoro* operated under its command.

## Significance
The KNIL’s actions across the archipelago, including in Aceh, shaped the course of the war in Southeast Asia and the eventual decolonisation process.
