**De Sociaaldemokraat** was the organ of the [[Sociaaldemokratische Arbeiderspartij|Sociaaldemokratische Arbeiderspartij in Nederland]], advertised on the closing leaf of *[[Atjeh in de Tweede Kamer (1897)]]*.

The notice reads: "De Sociaaldemokraat. Orgaan der Sociaaldemokratische Arbeiderspartij in Nederland. Verschijnt tweemaal per week. Redactie: Mr. P. J. Troelstra en W. H. Vliegen. Bureau van Administratie: Zwaanshals 127, Rotterdam. Prijs 60 cent per kwartaal." (*The Social Democrat. Organ of the Social-Democratic Workers' Party in the Netherlands. Appears twice a week. Editors: Mr. P. J. Troelstra and W. H. Vliegen. Administration office: Zwaanshals 127, Rotterdam.*)

The paper appeared twice weekly under the joint editorship of [[Pieter Jelles Troelstra|Troelstra]] — the deputy for Tietjerksteradeel who spoke at length in the [[Atjeh debate in the Tweede Kamer (16-17 November 1897)|Atjeh debate]] — and W. H. Vliegen. Its administration at Zwaanshals 127, [[Rotterdam]], was one of the three addresses from which the party's [[Acheh|Atjeh]] pamphlet could be ordered, the others being the party treasurer at [[Utrecht]] and the publisher [[J. A. Fortuijn]] at [[Amsterdam]].

The paper was the natural vehicle for the agitational campaign Troelstra announced in the debate: to traverse the whole of the Netherlands, its towns, villages and hamlets, to rouse the people against the injustice committed in Atjeh for twenty-four years.
