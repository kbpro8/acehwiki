**Haagsch Maandblad (magazine)**, a Magazine, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Published Majoor Lamster's article 'Atjeh vóór en na de pacificatie' in May 1928 issue, defending the civil administration against over-pessimism.

## Type

Magazine

## Description

Published [[Majoor Lamster]]'s article 'Atjeh vóór en na de [[Pacificatie|pacificatie]]' in May [[1928]] issue, defending the [[Binnenlandsch Bestuur (B.B.)|civil administration]] against over-pessimism.

## Significance

Haagsch Maandblad (magazine) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
