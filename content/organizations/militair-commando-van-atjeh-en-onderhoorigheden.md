**The Militair Commando van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden** — the Military Command of Atjeh and Dependencies — was the operational headquarters of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army|K.N.I.L.]] in the Residency of [[Atjeh en Onderhorigheden|Atjeh en Onderhorigheden]], exercising operational command over all Dutch military forces in the residency throughout the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] and the colonial period. The Militair Commando is the principal military headquarters of *[[ATJEH '96 (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96]]* (1913) and the institutional frame within which every Dutch military operation of [[1896]] was planned and executed.

## Organisation

The Militair Commando was headed by the [[Luitenant-Generaal|Leger-commandant]] (in his capacity as [[Regeeringscommissaris]]) when present, and otherwise by the [[Civiel en Militair Gouverneur]]. The principal staff officers were the *chef van den gewestelijken staf* (chief of the regional staff) and the *gewestelijke staf* (regional staff), responsible for the planning and execution of operations. In [[1896]] the *chef van den gewestelijken staf* was [[C. P. J. van Vliet|Lt.-Kol. C. P. J. van Vliet]] (who later that year became the [[Civiel en Militair Gouverneur]] himself). The Militair Commando exercised operational command over the infantry battalions, the cavalry, the artillery, the engineers, the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|marechaussée]], and the medical service in the residency.

## The 1896 order of battle

The Militair Commando's order of battle in Atjeh on [[1 January]] [[1896]] is printed as *Bijlage I (A)* of the *1e Deel* of *[[ATJEH '96 (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96]]* — the *Staat aangevende de sterkte der troepen in Atjeh en hunne legering op den 1e Januari 1896* (Statement showing the strength of the forces in Atjeh and their legering on 1 January 1896). The principal units under command were:

- **Infantry**: the 3rd, 6th, 7th, 9th, 12th, 14th, and 15th Battalions, and the *garnizoensbataljon*.
- **Cavalry**: the 4th Escadron.
- **Artillery**: the 1st, 2nd, and 4th Bergbatterijen.
- **Engineers**: the *genietroepen*.
- **Marechaussée**: the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Korps Marechaussee]] under [[H. M. Vis|1e Luit. H. M. Vis]].
- **Medical Service**: the *geneeskundige dienst* with two ambulances.
- **Military Administration**: the *militaire administratie* and the *intendance*.

The total strength was approximately 5,000 men. The strength on [[30 June]] [[1896]] is printed as *Bijlage VIII* of the *1e Deel*.

## The principal operations of 1896

The principal operations of the Militair Commando in [[1896]] were:

- the [[Relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit (8 April 1896)|relief of Biloel and Lamkoenjit]] on [[8 April]] [[1896]];
- the [[Evacuation of Biloel and Lamkoenjit (12 April 1896)|evacuation of Biloel and Lamkoenjit]] of [[12 April]] [[1896]] and the [[Evacuation of Kroeng-Gloempang, Tjot-Rang, Toengkoeb (20 April 1896)|evacuation of the outer line]] of April [[1896]];
- the [[Bombardment of Lampisang (27 April – 23 May 1896)|bombardment of Lampisang]] of [[27 April]]–[[23 May]] [[1896]];
- the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|great tuchtiging of the IV and VI Moekims of 23–25 May 1896]];
- the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|tuchtiging of the V Moekims Montassik of 16–17 June 1896]];
- the [[Tuchtiging of Lam-Krak Moekims (9–10 June 1896)|tuchtiging of the Lam-Krak Moekims of 9–10 June 1896]];
- the [[Surprise of Ana' Galoeng (29 June 1896)|surprise of Ana' Galoeng of 29 June 1896]];
- the [[Excursie to Lepong and Lohong (10–14 August 1896)|expedition to Lepong of August 1896]];
- the [[First Selimoen Expedition (6–11 September 1896)|first Selimoen expedition of September 1896]];
- the [[Second Selimoen Expedition (3–6 November 1896)|second Selimoen expedition of November 1896]];
- the [[Temporary Occupation of Indrapoeri (28 October 1896)|expedition to Indrapuri of October 1896]];
- the [[Cholera Epidemic in Atjeh (December 1896)|cholera epidemic of December 1896]] which prevented the *colonne tegen Oemar* from taking the field.

## Significance

The Militair Commando is the principal military headquarters of *[[ATJEH '96 (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96]]* and the institutional frame within which every Dutch military operation of [[1896]] was planned and executed. The order of battle in Atjeh on [[1 January]] [[1896]] is the only published nominal return of the Dutch occupation force on the eve of the [[1896]] campaign, and the order of battle on [[30 June]] [[1896]] is the only published nominal return at the midpoint of the year.
