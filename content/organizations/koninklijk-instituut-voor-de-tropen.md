**The Koninklijk Instituut voor de Tropen** — the Royal Tropical Institute — was the Amsterdam-based institution for the study of the tropical world, and in particular of the former Netherlands Indies, that published *[[De staatkundige ontwikkeling van Indonesië in de 20e eeuw|De staatkundige ontwikkeling van Indonesië in de 20e eeuw]]* through its *Bureau Cursussen en Vertalingen* (Bureau of Courses and Translations). The Institute was the principal metropolitan institution for tropical — and, in the colonial and immediate post-colonial period, specifically Indies — study and training in the Netherlands, and its publications through the *Bureau Cursussen en Vertalingen* constituted a series of surveys and handbooks on the political, economic, and social conditions of the tropical world.

## The Bureau Cursussen en Vertalingen

The *Bureau Cursussen en Vertalingen* was the publications arm of the Institute responsible for the production of course materials, surveys, and translations for the use of the Institute's educational programmes and for the wider Dutch public. The *[[De staatkundige ontwikkeling van Indonesië in de 20e eeuw|staatkundige ontwikkeling]]* survey — a political-history overview of Indonesia in the 20th century, covering the nationalist movement, the revolution, the souvereiniteitsoverdracht, and the post-colonial developments down to the mid-1950s — was issued as a publication of this Bureau, and it bears the characteristic stamp of the Institute's post-colonial educational mission: the provision of concise, up-to-date surveys of the political and social conditions of the newly independent Indonesia for the use of Dutch civil servants, businessmen, scholars, and the interested public.

## The Institute and the Atjeh content

The Institute's publication of the *staatkundige ontwikkeling* survey placed the Atjeh-related content of the work — the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] as a 19th-century resistance action, Atjeh as a Republican stronghold during the revolution, Atjeh as a prospective province, and the [[Atjèh's accession to the Negara Islam Indonesia (1953)|Daud Beureu'eh revolt of 1953]] — within the institutional framework of the metropolitan Dutch engagement with Indonesia in the immediate post-colonial period. The Institute's imprimatur on the survey marked it as an authoritative source for the Dutch understanding of the political development of Indonesia, and the survey's treatment of Atjeh within that framework placed the Atjehnese history within the wider narrative of Indonesian political development.

## Significance

For AcehWiki, the Koninklijk Instituut voor de Tropen is significant as the publisher of the *[[De staatkundige ontwikkeling van Indonesië in de 20e eeuw|staatkundige ontwikkeling]]* survey — a mid-1950s Dutch political-history overview that treats the Atjeh-oorlog, the revolutionary Atjeh, the prospective provincial Atjeh, and the Daud Beureu'eh revolt within a single narrative framework. The Institute's role as the publisher places the Atjeh-related content of the survey within the institutional framework of the metropolitan Dutch engagement with Indonesia in the immediate post-colonial period, and it connects the survey to the wider corpus of Dutch tropical-study publications that formed the metropolitan knowledge-base for the former Netherlands Indies.
