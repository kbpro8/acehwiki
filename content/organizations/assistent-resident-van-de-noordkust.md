The **Assistent-Resident van de Noordkust** was the Netherlands-Indies administrative officer at the head of the *afdeeling* (district) [[Noordkust|Noordkust van Atjeh]], and one of the two officials whose reports prompted the introduction of *[[Verjaring in Atjehsch adatrecht|verjaring]]* into Atjehnese customary law.

## Role in the prescription decree of 1911

The [[Besluit van den Civielen en Militairen Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden 26 April 1911 No. 2199|decree of 26 April 1911 No. 2199]] recited the missive of the Assistent Resident van de Noordkust of [[29 March]] [[1911]] No. 606/9, alongside that of the [[Assistent-Resident van Groot-Atjeh|Assistent Resident van Groot-Atjeh]] of [[7 March]] of the same year No. 593/9.

## The afdeeling

The *afdeeling* Noordkust van Atjeh contained the *onderafdeeling* (subdivision) [[Lho' Seumawe|Lho' Seumaweh]], within which lay the *landschap* (territory) [[Sawang]] — the district in which the *[[Adat moeda|adat moeda]]* fine, the *[[Thiplah tano|thiplah-tano]]* institution, the [[1917]] revision of the *[[Thirhōm idja|thirhōm idja]]* reserve and the pepper usages of the *Sawangsche toenong* were recorded by [[D. W. N. de Boer]].
