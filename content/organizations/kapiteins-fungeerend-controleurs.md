**Kapiteins-fungeerend controleurs**, a Military officers serving as civil administrators, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Three posts in Aceh where civil administration is in the hands of captain-fungeerend controleurs.

## Type

Military officers serving as civil administrators

## Description

Three posts in Aceh where [[Binnenlandsch Bestuur (B.B.)|civil administration]] is in the hands of captain-fungeerend controleurs. Otherwise Aceh is now fully geciviliseerd (under [[Binnenlandsch Bestuur (B.B.)|civil administration]]), alongside some [[Schakelofficieren|schakelofficieren]] (officers serving alongside fungeerend controleurs, military and civil).

## Significance

Kapiteins-fungeerend controleurs is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
