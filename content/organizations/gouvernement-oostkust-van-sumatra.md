**The Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra** (Government of the East Coast of Sumatra) was the colonial administrative unit governing the northeastern coastal plain and hinterland of Sumatra from [[1878]] to [[1942]], elevated from a Residentie to a Gouvernement under a Governor in [[1915]]. Its northern boundary with the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] was fixed by [[Staatsblad 1908 No. 604|Staatsblad [[1908]] No. 604]] along the [[Wilhelmina-gebergte|Wilhelmina-keten]] crest, following the [[1908]] transfer of [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] to [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh]] ([[Staatsblad 1908 No. 112|Staatsblad [[1908]] No. 112]]). The capital moved from [[Bengkalis|Bengkalis]] ([[1878]]–[[1887]]) to [[Medan|Medan]] ([[1887]] onward).

## Formation and evolution

### 1873–1878: Part of Residentie Riouw

The territory formed the northern part of [[Residentie Riouw en Onderhoorigheden|Residentie Riouw en Onderhoorigheden]], considered a "worthless wilderness." The [[Siak|Sultan of Siak]] nominally held suzerainty over the coastal sultanates ([[Langkat|Langkat]], [[Deli|Deli]], [[Serdang|Serdang]], [[Asahan|Asahan]], etc.) under the [[1858]] Siak Treaty.

### 1873: Separation of Siak

The [[Siak|Siak]] region (including the future cultivation zone) was separated from [[Residentie Riouw en Onderhoorigheden|Riouw]] and made a separate Residentie Siak with capital at [[Bengkalis|Bengkalis]] ([[1873]]–[[1884]]). The [[Siak|Sultan of Siak]] ceded [[Bengkalis|Bengkalis]] island in full ownership to the Netherlands in [[1874]].

### 1878: Oostkust as separate Residentie

*Staatsblad 1878* erected the Residentie Oostkust van Sumatra as an independent *gewest* under a Resident at [[Bengkalis|Bengkalis]], reflecting the tobacco boom's administrative demands.

### 1887: Capital moves to Medan

The cultivation zone's economic center of gravity shifted north; the capital moved to [[Medan|Medan]] in the heart of the [[Deli|Deli]]–[[Serdang|Serdang]]–[[Langkat|Langkat]] plantation belt.

### 1905–1915: Interior expansion

Between [[1905]]–[[1915]], all independent mountain statelets ([[Batak Karo|Karo]], [[Alaslanden|Alas]], [[Batak Pakpak|Pakpak]], [[Simeloengoen|Simeloengoen]], Karolanden) were brought under Dutch authority, extending the *gewest*'s boundaries to the eastern [[Barisan|Barisan]] crest and in the [[Batak Karo|Karo Plateau]] even beyond the watershed.

### 1915: Elevation to Gouvernement

*Staatsblad 1915* (implied) elevated the Residentie to a Gouvernement under a Governor, reflecting its economic importance (tobacco, rubber, tea, oil). H. J. Gruijssen (listed as Governor from [[1917]]) was among the first.

## Relation to Atjeh

- **Northern boundary**: Fixed by [[Staatsblad 1908 No. 604|Staatsblad [[1908]] No. 604]] along the [[Wilhelmina-gebergte|Wilhelmina-keten]] from [[Besitang|Besitang]]–[[Tamiang|Tamiang]] watershed to Liang Gergas.
- **Tamiang transfer**: [[Staatsblad 1908 No. 112|Staatsblad [[1908]] No. 112]] moved [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] (northernmost sultanate) to [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh]].
- **Transport links**: [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]] (narrow gauge) to [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]]; [[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij|Deli-tram]] (standard gauge) to [[Pangkalan Brandan|Pangkalan Brandan]]; junction planned at [[Bookit Koeboe|Bookit Koeboe]] with three-rail section to [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]].
- **Post/Telegraph**: [[Atjeh-tram|Atjeh-tram]] along [[Aroebai|Aroebaai]] completed the inter-gouvernement link; only [[Pangkalan Brandan|Pangkalan Brandan]]–[[Besitang|Besitang]] road gap remained.
- **Harbor rivalry**: [[Aroebai|Aroebaai]] ([[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh]]-backed) vs. [[Belawan|Belawan]] ([[Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra|Oostkust]]-backed) for ocean-harbor status; [[Belawan|Belawan]] won (Plan Nobel).
- **Economic integration**: [[Deli Spoorweg Maatschappij|D.S.M.]] sought to pull [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh]]/northern traffic to [[Belawan|Belawan]]; [[Bataafsche Petroleum Maatschappij|Koninklijke Petroleum Maatschappij]] oil flowed out via [[Pangkalan Soesoe|Pangkalan Soesoe]].

## Significance

The [[Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra|Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra]] is, in the Encyclopaedisch Bureau's account, the principal colonial-administrative counterpart of the [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]] in northern Sumatra — the plantation-economy *gewest* whose tobacco, rubber, tea and oil exports made it, by [[1915]], the economic centre of gravity of the Buitenbezittingen and whose northern boundary, fixed by [[Staatsblad 1908 No. 604|Staatsblad [[1908]] No. 604]] along the [[Wilhelmina-gebergte|Wilhelmina-keten]] crest, was the only precisely demarcated boundary of the *gewest*. The [[1908]] transfer of [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] to [[Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Atjeh]] marks the moment at which the [[Gouvernement Oostkust van Sumatra|Oostkust]] assumed its final [[1918]] shape — the moment at which the *gewest*'s northern limit ceased to be the [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] River and became the [[Besitang|Besitang]]–[[Wilhelmina-gebergte|Wilhelmina-keten]] watershed.
