**Hof (court) of the Pretendent-Sultan at Keumala**, a Royal court in exile, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. After fall of Dalem, the 'hof' found refuge in Keumala and served as a kernel for organised resistance.

## Type

Royal court in exile

## Description

After fall of [[Dalem]], the 'hof' found refuge in [[Keumala]] and served as a kernel for organised [[Bantah (Acehnese term)|resistance]]. The Dutch government encouraged already-submitted chiefs to visit it ([[Keumala]]-tochten).

## Significance

Hof (court) of the Pretendent-Sultan at Keumala is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
