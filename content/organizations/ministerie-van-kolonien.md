**The Ministerie van Koloniën** — the Dutch Ministry of Colonies — was the central administrative organ of the Dutch colonial state in the [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]]-period [[1891]]-[[1918]]. The ministry was headed by a minister who was, in turn, responsible to the [[Tweede Kamer der Staten-Generaal (colonial business)|Tweede Kamer]] for the conduct of colonial affairs — including the [[Atjeh-oorlog ([[1873]]-[[1904]])|Atjeh-oorlog]], the [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacificatie]] and the [[Ethische Politiek|ethical-policy]] program.

## The ministerial succession

The ministers of Colonies of the [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] period comprised, in chronological order: [[Mr L. W. C. Keuchenius|Keuchenius]] ([[1888]]-[[1893]]), [[C. H. Pijnacker Hordijk|Pijnacker Hordijk]] ([[1890]]-[[1891]]), [[Mackay]] ([[1891]]-[[1894]]), [[W. K. baron van Dedem|Van Dedem]] ([[1893]]-[[1899]]), [[C. H. A. van der Wijck|Van der Wijck]] ([[1894]]-[[1897]]), [[Mr J. H. Bergsma|Bergsma]] ([[1897]]-[[1901]]), [[J. T. Cremer|Cremer]] ([[1899]]-[[1904]]), [[W. Rooseboom|Rooseboom]] ([[1901]]-[[1902]]), [[Jhr T. A. J. van Asch van Wijck|Van Asch van Wijck]] ([[1902]]-[[1905]]), [[A. W. F. Idenburg|Idenburg]] ([[1904]]-[[1909]]), [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] ([[1905]]-[[1908]] in parallel), [[D. Fock|Fock]] ([[1908]]-[[1909]]), [[A. W. F. Idenburg|Idenburg]] ([[1909]]-[[1913]]), [[J. H. de Waal Malefijt|De Waal Malefijt]] ([[1913]]-[[1918]]) and [[Th. B. Pleyte|Pleyte]] ([[1916]]-[[1921]]).

## The advisory role of Snouck Hurgronje

The most consequential single advisor to the [[Ministerie van Koloniën|Ministry]] in the [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] period was [[C. Snouck Hurgronje|Snouck Hurgronje]]. The lectures he gave to future colonial civil servants were published in [[1911]] as *[[Nederland en de Islam ([[1911]])|Nederland en de Islam]]*, and the [[1911 Indische begrotingsdebat|1911 begrotingsdebat]] "concentreerde zich vooral om de door deze adviseur van het ministerie van Koloniën ontvouwde denkbeelden" — concentrated especially on the ideas unfolded by this advisor of the Ministry of Colonies. The Snouckian view of Islam — set out in his [[1893]]-[[1894]] *De Atjehers* and elaborated in [[1911]] — was the basis of the [[A. W. F. Idenburg|Idenburg]]-[[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] argument that the Atjehnese resistance was religiously motivated and could be broken only by a demonstration of Dutch "onwrikbare overmacht" — unshakeable superiority.

## The ambtelijke adviezen complaint

A persistent complaint of the [[Tweede Kamer der Staten-Generaal (colonial business)|Tweede Kamer]] was that the minister of Colonies "zich er achter verschool" — hid behind — the *ambtelijke adviezen* (official advices) from the governor-general, the residents, the directors, the [[Raad van Indië]], the Hooggerechtshof, the generals, the staatscommissies. The complaint is, in the [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] retrospective, the procedural expression of the colonial-parliamentary dilemma: that the chambermembers, lacking first-hand knowledge of the Indies, were dependent on the very official communications whose candour they suspected.

## The publications

The [[Ministerie van Koloniën|Ministry]] was responsible, alongside its political and administrative functions, for a series of official publications. The [[Commissie tot onderzoek van de mindere welvaart (1902)|commissie tot onderzoek van de mindere welvaart]] was installed by the [[Ministerie van Koloniën|Ministry]] in [[1902]]; the *Bijdragen tot de kennis van het inlandsch bedrijf* of [[1904]] were edited, under the [[Ministerie van Koloniën|Ministry]]'s aegis, by [[C. Th. van Deventer|Van Deventer]], [[D. Fock|Fock]] and [[Kielstra]].

## Significance

The [[Ministerie van Koloniën|Ministry of Colonies]] is the central administrative organ of the Dutch colonial state in the [[W. H. Vermeulen|Vermeulen]] period. Its ministers — [[J. T. Cremer|Cremer]], [[Jhr T. A. J. van Asch van Wijck|Van Asch van Wijck]], [[A. W. F. Idenburg|Idenburg]], [[J. B. van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]], [[D. Fock|Fock]], [[J. H. de Waal Malefijt|De Waal Malefijt]], [[Th. B. Pleyte|Pleyte]] — are the principal political figures of the Atjehnese-war and *pacificatie* period.
