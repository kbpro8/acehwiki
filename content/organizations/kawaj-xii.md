**The Kawaj XII** — the *Federation of the Twelve Kawaj* — was the federation founded in the middle of the 17th century by Pidiërs from the upper regions of [[Pidië|Pidië]] and [[Meulaboh|Meulaboh]], drawn thither by the gold, in the course of the 17th-century Atjèhse gold exploitation that followed the loss of the [[Perak|Perak]] tin trade to the Dutch in [[1645]]. The federation is one of the principal Atjèhse political institutions of the 17th century, and its founding — together with the subsequent return of its members to the lowland along the coast of Meulaboh and [[Tapa Toean|Tapa Toean]] for the [[pepercultuur|pepercultuur]] — is the principal episode in the 17th-century internal colonisation of the Westkust of Atjèh by Pidiërs.

## The Founding

When, in [[1645]], the tin trade of [[Perak|Perak]] came principally into the hands of the Dutch, the Sultan of Atjèh missed important trade advantages. By means of the gold exploitation in Atjèh itself, one thought to be able to replace the important source of income obtained through the tin trade. Drawn by the gold in the upper regions of Pidië and Meulaboh, mainly Pidiërs moved thither in the middle of the 17th century and founded there the famous federation of the Kawaj XII.

## The Decline of Gold and the Westkust Colonisation

The business of gold-seeker in the upper regions of Pidië and Meulaboh did not, however, remain profitable, so that after some time a great part of the population of those regions sought another existence and moved further west, to the lowland along the coast of Meulaboh and Tapa Toean, which was excellently suited for the pepercultuur. This population returned to their old love: instead of gold-seeker one became pepper-planter again. In this way the Westkust of Atjèh was populated by Pidiërs.

## The Prang Rawa

The Pidiëse colonists soon came into conflict with the Malays who were colonising Meulaboh from the sea. This struggle, known as the [[Prang Rawa|Prang Rawa]], took place at the very beginning of the 18th century and ended with the victory of the Pidiërs. The victory of the Pidiërs in the Prang Rawa confirmed the Pidiëse colonisation of the Westkust and the displacement of the Malay colonists.

## Significance

The Kawaj XII is one of the principal Atjèhse political institutions of the 17th century, and its founding — together with the subsequent return of its members to the lowland along the coast of Meulaboh and Tapa Toean for the pepercultuur — is the principal episode in the 17th-century internal colonisation of the Westkust of Atjèh by Pidiërs. The federation is the institutional expression of the 17th-century Atjèhse gold exploitation, and its decline — with the decline of the gold exploitation and the return to pepper — is the institutional expression of the return to the pepercultuur as the basis of Atjèh's wealth.
