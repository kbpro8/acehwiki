**Vrijwillig automobielcorps** (Volunteer Motor Corps), referred to in the source as “Vauhee”, provided motorised transport personnel—primarily Javanese drivers—for Dutch military operations in the Dutch East Indies during World War II. In [[March]] [[1942]] the corps supplied drivers to assist the *Marinebataljon* in moving troops and equipment between [[Goebeng]] and the *M.K.C.* (military camp).

## Contributions
The corps’ drivers enabled the *Marinebataljon* to overcome logistical delays caused by a shortage of motorised personnel, allowing the unit to resume its march towards [[Djombang]].

## Significance
The *Vrijwillig automobielcorps* illustrates the reliance of Dutch colonial forces on local manpower for transport and logistics, a factor that affected operations throughout the archipelago, including the Aceh region.
