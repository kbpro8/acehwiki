**Indische Jongens (Indian boys)**, a ethnic workforce, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. South Asian (Indian) workers among the opzichters (supervisors) at the Atjeh-tram workshop at Sigli.

## Type

ethnic workforce

## Description

South Asian (Indian) workers among the opzichters (supervisors) at the [[Atjeh-tram]] workshop at [[Sigli]].

## Significance

Indische Jongens (Indian boys) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
