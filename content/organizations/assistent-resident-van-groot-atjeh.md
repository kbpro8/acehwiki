The **Assistent-Resident van Groot-Atjeh** was the Netherlands-Indies administrative officer at the head of the *afdeeling* (district) [[Groot-Atjeh]], and one of the two officials on whose reports the introduction of *[[Verjaring in Atjehsch adatrecht|verjaring]]* into Atjehnese customary law was founded.

## Role in the prescription decree of 1911

The [[Besluit van den Civielen en Militairen Gouverneur van Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden 26 April 1911 No. 2199|decree of 26 April 1911 No. 2199]] recited, as its first ground, the missive of the Assistent Resident van Groot-Atjeh of [[7 March]] [[1911]] No. 593/9, together with that of the [[Assistent-Resident van de Noordkust|Assistent Resident van de Noordkust]] of [[29 March]] of the same year No. 606/9. These reports drew attention to the inconsistent treatment of old claims of debt by the *[[Moesapat|moesapat]]*'s of the *gewest* (region), prescription being applied quite arbitrarily.
