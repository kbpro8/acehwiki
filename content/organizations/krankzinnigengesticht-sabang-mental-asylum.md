**Krankzinnigengesticht Sabang (mental asylum)**, a Government mental hospital, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Founded in 1925 in old barracks (under Dr.

## Type

Government mental hospital

## Description

Founded in [[1925]] in old [[Tangsi|barracks]] (under [[Dr. van Heuvel]]). Directors: [[Dr. J. A. Latumeten]] ([[1926]]-[[1935]]), [[Dr. J. A. Lichtveld]] (from [[1935]]). Now ~[[1400]] patients including 548 [[Atjehers]], 23 [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]]'s, 2 [[Alas|Alassers]], 79 Javanen, 41 [[Chinese in Atjeh|Chineezen]], plus 652 from outside the residency. Staff of 248 including 11 Europeans. Work-therapy: weaving, [[Rattan (Aceh)|rotan]]/timber, bookbinding, agriculture (coconut). Few or no dwangmiddelen (coercive measures). Some patients walk freely in [[Sabang]].

## Significance

Krankzinnigengesticht Sabang (mental asylum) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
