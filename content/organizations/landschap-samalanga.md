**Landschap Samalanga**, a Self-governing territory, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Landschap whose zelfbestuurder provided a landing field.

## Type

Self-governing territory

## Description

[[landschap|Landschap]] whose [[zelfbestuurder]] provided a landing field. Site of a weefschool initiated by the local [[oelèëbalang]].

## Significance

Landschap Samalanga is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
