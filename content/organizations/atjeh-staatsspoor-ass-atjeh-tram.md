**Atjeh-Staatsspoor (A.S.S.) / Atjeh-tram**, a State railway, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Military-origin railway from Oelèë-Lheuë through Koeta Radja, Sigli, Lho'Seumawé, Langsa to Besitang, with branch lines to Pangkalan Soesoe and Lammeulo.

## Type

State railway

## Description

Military-origin railway from Oelèë-[[Lheue|Lheuë]] through [[Koeta-Radja|Koeta Radja]], [[Sigli]], [[Lho'Seumawé]], [[Langsar|Langsa]] to [[Besitang]], with branch lines to [[Pangkalan Soesoe]] and [[Lammeulo]]. Nicknamed by locals 'het [[Atjeh-Trammemeje]]'. A vital link in the [[Pacification of Atjeh|pacification of Atjeh]]. Annual transport roughly 100,000 tons of goods and 2 million passengers per year. Lost traffic to buses and trucks but made a profit in [[1937]] after severe budget cuts.

## Significance

Atjeh-Staatsspoor (A.S.S.) / Atjeh-tram is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
