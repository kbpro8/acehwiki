**The 4de Garnizoensbataljon** — also known as the **"Mobiele Reserve"** (Mobile Reserve) — was a garrison battalion of the [[K.N.I.L.|Netherlands Indies Army]] raised in [[Atjeh|Atjeh]] in [[1928]] in the aftermath of the [[Bakongan uprising|Bakongan uprising]] of [[1925]]–[[1927]], and dissolved in [[1940]] for reinforcement of Java, Malakka and the Oostkust van Sumatra. In the [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|Dutch staff history's]] account (Deel VI, Hoofdstuk V), the 4de Garnizoensbataljon is the only Dutch garrison battalion of Atjeh explicitly identified as a mobile reserve — a strategic reserve held back from routine garrison duty for rapid deployment against renewed Atjehnese resistance.

## Origin and purpose

The 4de Garnizoensbataljon was first established in 1928, after the great uprising at Bakongan of 1925–1927, and formed the so-called "Mobiele Reserve." The battalion was thus a direct institutional response to the [[Bakongan uprising|Bakongan uprising]], the most serious Atjehnese rising since the [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacification]]. Its mission was to maintain a mobile reserve capable of rapid deployment to any point in Atjeh where renewed resistance might break out — a mission the staff history treats as a structural recognition that the [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacification]] had not in fact pacified Atjeh, but had only suppressed open resistance.

## Organisation as of 1 January 1940

As of [[1 January]] [[1940]], the 4de Garnizoensbataljon was grouped as follows:

- **Staff + 2 companies** at [[Kotaradja|Kotaradja]] (with 16 brigades in total), of which *two brigades* were stationed at [[Sabang|Sabang]] for police duties.

**Total**: 16 brigades (each brigade of garrison infantry numbering 21 men, for a battalion strength of about 336 men).

## Dissolution of 1940

The 4de Garnizoensbataljon was the first Atjeh garrison battalion to be dissolved in the [[1940]] drawdown. In April 1940 one company of the 4de Garnizoensbataljon was directed to the Riouw Archipelago (south of Singapore), while in May of the same year the other company was sent to Java. The 4de Garnizoensbataljon — which, as noted above, constituted the "Mobiele Reserve" — was thereby removed entirely from Atjeh.

The dissolution of the 4de Garnizoensbataljon in [[1940]] — only twelve years after its formation — is presented by the staff history as the structural recognition that the Dutch pre-invasion force drawdown had abandoned the institutional memory of the [[Bakongan uprising|Bakongan uprising]] and the [[Lhong action 1933|Lhong revolt]] of [[1933]]. The staff history's emphasis on the disappearance of the mobile reserve from Atjeh before the Japanese landings frames the dissolution as a strategic error of the pre-invasion reductions: the battalion whose very purpose was to suppress renewed Atjehnese resistance was no longer available when renewed Atjehnese resistance — the [[F-beweging|F. beweging]] rising of February [[1942]] — broke out.

## Significance

The 4de Garnizoensbataljon is, in the staff history's Atjeh chapter, the institutional measure of the Dutch strategic abandonment of the mobile reserve concept in Atjeh. The staff history's emphasis on the battalion's formation in [[1928]] as a direct response to the [[Bakongan uprising|Bakongan uprising]] — and on its dissolution in [[1940]] only twelve years later — frames the battalion as a brief institutional recognition that the [[Pacificatie van Atjeh|pacification]] was reversible, and the dissolution as a strategic error that would be vindicated by the [[F-beweging|F. beweging]] rising of February [[1942]].
