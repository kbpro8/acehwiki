The **Technische Hoogeschool te Bandoeng** was the technical college at [[Bandung|Bandoeng]] for whose establishment four million guilders were raised by trade and industry in the Netherlands through the intervention of the former Maréchaussée officer [[W. V. Rhemrev]].

## The founding subscription

The [[1930]] jubilee number of *[[Het Nieuws van den Dag voor Nederlandsch-Indië]]* records, in its portrait notice of Rhemrev, that he was co-founder of the *[[Vereeniging Nederlandsch-Indische Jaarbeurs]]*, and that through his intervention "werd door Handel en Industrie in Nederland vier millioen bijeengebracht voor de oprichting van de Technische Hoogeschool te Bandoeng" — four million was brought together by Trade and Industry in the Netherlands for the establishment of the Technical College at Bandoeng.

Bandoeng was also the garrison at which Colonel [[L. A. Snell]] commanded the 2nd Regiment of Infantry at the time of the jubilee, and where his knight's cross of the *[[Militaire Willemsorde|Militaire Willems-Orde]]* had been presented to him in April [[1927]]; and the seat of the *Christelijk Lyceum* at which the retired sergeant [[W. H. Loef]] was concierge.
