**De Koepel (Soosje)**, a social club at Sabang, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Social club ('de Koepel') at Sabang, on the hill road, slightly forward-shifted from the European residences and hotel, with delicious views over the bay.

## Type

social club at [[Sabang]]

## Description

Social club ('de Koepel') at [[Sabang]], on the hill road, slightly forward-shifted from the European residences and hotel, with delicious views over the bay.

## Significance

De Koepel (Soosje) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
