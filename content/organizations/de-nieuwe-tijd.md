**De Nieuwe Tijd** was the social-democratic review advertised on the closing leaf of *[[Atjeh in de Tweede Kamer (1897)]]* alongside the party organ *[[De Sociaaldemokraat]]*, at a subscription of eighty-seven and a half cents a quarter, published by [[J. A. Fortuijn]] of N. Leliestraat 69, [[Amsterdam]].

It appears in the record of the [[Acheh|Atjeh]] question only in this bibliographical capacity, as one of the two periodicals through which the [[Sociaaldemokratische Arbeiderspartij]] carried its case against the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] beyond the [[Tweede Kamer]] to a reading public — the programme [[Pieter Jelles Troelstra|Troelstra]] announced in the debate of [[16 November|16]]–[[17 November]] [[1897]] when he undertook to rouse the whole country against the war.
