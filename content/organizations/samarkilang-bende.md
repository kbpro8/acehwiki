**Samarkilang-bende** was the band formed from the last followers of [[Tgk. di Mata Ië]] after his death near [[Samarkilang]] on [[10 July]] [[1917]], whose head was not captured until [[1937]].

His spirit long went on working; out of his last followers there formed the well-known Samarkilang band, whose leader was only taken prisoner in 1937 — twenty years later! The persistence of the band twenty years after the pacification is one of [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]]'s indications of how long the memory and prestige of the great resistance leaders survived them.
