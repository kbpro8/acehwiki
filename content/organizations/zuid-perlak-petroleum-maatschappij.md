**Zuid-Perlak Petroleum-Maatschappij** was famous oil company operating terrains near [[Peureula]] adjacent to [[Perlak Petroleum-Maatschappij]].

## Description

Broersma mentions terrains of befaamde Zuid-Perlak Petroleum-Mij alongside Perlak Petroleum-Mij which [[Bataafsche Petroleum Maatschappij]] exploits.
