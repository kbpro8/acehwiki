**Nederlandsch-Indisch Gouvernement / Regeering**, a Dutch colonial government, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. The Dutch colonial government that replaced the Sultan after 31 January 1874 proclamation.

## Type

Dutch colonial government

## Description

The Dutch colonial government that replaced [[Sultan of Aceh|the Sultan]] after [[31 January]] [[1874]] proclamation. From [[1819]]-[[1871]] bound by treaties to leave Acehnese independence intact.

## Significance

Nederlandsch-Indisch Gouvernement / Regeering is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
