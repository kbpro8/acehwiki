**The 1e Divisie Marechaussee uit Blang Kedjeren** was the division of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen]] based at [[Blang Kedjeren]] in the highlands of central [[Atjeh|Aceh]] — the division whose captain, around [[1933]], told the [[Hotel de Boer]] story at the *Soos* in [[Koeta-Radja]] after travelling through [[Medan]] with his two *[[dardanellen|dardanellen]]*. The division is the principal unit of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Marechaussee]] mentioned in the prologue of *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]*, and the unit through which Dames introduces the book's two great themes: the *plichtsbesef* (sense of duty) of the Ambonese soldier, and the boundary between the tall story and the *waarheid*.

## The captain and the dardanellen

"In het 1933 of daaromtrent jaar zat de kapitein, commandant van de 1e Divisie Marechaussee uit [[Blang Kedjeren]], op de Soos in [[Koeta-Radja]] een groot verhaal af te steken over een belevenis in [[Hotel de Boer]]." The captain of the division — travelling through Medan with his two *[[dardanellen|dardanellen]]* — sat in the lobby of [[Hotel de Boer]] in *veldtenue* (field grey-green rather than the expected white) drinking a large bottle of beer, only to discover two Marechaussees with drawn *[[klewang]]* and *[[karabijn (M. 95)|karabijn]]* in de aanslag standing behind his chair. The two Marechaussees — having followed their captain into the hotel as bodyguards — had taken position behind his chair with drawn swords and cocked carbines.

"Deze twee *dardanellen* nu waren jaren terug op de Blang gekomen en steeds daar gebleven. Eerst nu kwamen ze eens in de bewoonde wereld terug" — these two *dardanellen* had come to the Blang years before and had always stayed there. Only now did they come back to the bewoonde wereld. The episode is the book's first image of the *plichtsbesef* of the Ambonese soldier: the *dardanellen* who, in the chiquest hotel of Medan, stood behind their captain with drawn swords because that was their duty.

## The point of the anecdote

"Het verhaal van de kapitein was niet waar. Maar hij heeft het verteld en met zeer groot succes." The story is almost certainly embellished, but the *Atjehmensen|Atjehmenseni* at the Soos — who knew the hotel — "hebben het niet voor onmogelijk gehouden." The point is that the *plichtsbesef* of the Ambonese Marechaussee was such that "de grenzen van het belachelijke verre konden worden overschreden" — the boundaries of the ridiculous could be far exceeded — and that "wij dan lang niet altijd gelijk hadden" — we were not always right.

## Significance

The 1e Divisie Marechaussee uit Blang Kedjeren is significant in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* as the division through which Dames introduces the book's two great themes: the *plichtsbesef* of the Ambonese soldier, and the boundary between the tall story and the *waarheid*. The division is the unit of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Marechaussee]] at [[Blang Kedjeren]] — one of the most *afgelegen* posts of the colonial army in [[Atjeh|Aceh]] — and its captain's Hotel de Boer story is the book's methodological frame: the boundary between the tall story and the *waarheid* is the boundary the book will observe.

## Account from *Nederlands-Indië contra Japan (1959)* — 1ste Divisie Marechaussee

**The 1ste Divisie Marechaussee** was the first of the six divisions of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Korps Maréchaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen]] stationed in the *gewest* of [[Atjeh|Atjeh]] during [[1940]]–[[1942]]. In the [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|Dutch staff history's]] account (Deel VI, Hoofdstuk V), the 1ste Divisie is the Blangkedjeren- and central-Atjeh-oriented Maréchaussee division whose eight brigades at [[Blangkedjeren|Blangkedjeren]] — together with the four brigades at [[Koeala Bee|Koeala Bee]] — formed the principal Dutch force in the Alas valley theatre on the eve of the [[Japanese invasion of Atjèh|Japanese invasion of March 1942]].

## Organisation as of 1 January 1940

As of [[1 January]] [[1940]], the 1ste Divisie Marechaussee was grouped as follows (each brigade of Maréchaussee numbering 20 men):

- [[Blangkedjeren|Blangkedjeren]]: 8 brigades (Iste Divisie)
- [[Koeala Bee|Koeala Bee]]: 4 brigades (Iste Divisie)

**Total**: 12 brigades (about 240 men).

## Operations 1940–1942

The 1ste Divisie's Blangkedjeren brigades were progressively drawn upon for the pre-invasion reorganisations of [[1940]]–[[1941]]. By March [[1942]], the Iste Divisie's Blangkedjeren strength had been consolidated with troops of various other units at Blangkedjeren under Lt-Col [[Blogg|Blogg]]'s command. The 1ste Divisie's Koeala Bee brigades remained on the Westkust, where they formed part of Captain [[L. H. J. Kloprogge|Kloprogge]]'s reduced IIde divisie command after the [[1940]] reductions.

## Significance

The 1ste Divisie Marechaussee is, in the staff history's Atjeh chapter, the Blangkedjeren-oriented division whose troops formed the backbone of the Dutch defence at the [[Sumatra capitulation (28 March 1942)|Blangkedjeren capitulation of 28 March 1942]]. The staff history's emphasis on the division's progressive reduction through [[1940]]–[[1941]] is part of the compilers' broader argument that the Dutch pre-invasion force drawdown structurally disabled the Maréchaussee's capacity to mount sustained resistance.
