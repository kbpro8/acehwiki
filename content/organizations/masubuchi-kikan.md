**Masubuchi-kikan** — the M-organisatie, or Japanese military-administration organisation for [[Atjèh|Atjèh]] under [[Masubuchi|Major (later Lieutenant-General) Masubuchi]] — was the formal successor to the [[Fujiwara-kikan|Fujiwara-kikan]] that, from the establishment of Japanese authority in March [[1942]] until the [[Japanese capitulation of 15 August 1945|Japanese capitulation of 15 August 1945]], served as the institutional core of the occupation's political apparatus in the residency. Named for its head in the standard Japanese practice of naming administrative *kikan* (機関, "agency" or "organisation") after their commanders, the Masubuchi-kikan was both an instrument of the *gunseibu* (military administration) and a personal apparatus through which Masubuchi — "*bapak Atjèh*" to his followers — played, in Piekaar's words, "the game of Acehnese politics in a truly masterful manner."

## Origins in the F-organisatie

The Masubuchi-kikan's genealogy runs directly through the [[Fujiwara-kikan|Fujiwara-kikan]]. Piekaar notes that "in the Japanese Masubuchi, whom we shall get to know better in the second part of this work, this organization found for Aceh an extremely capable leader" — Masubuchi had been the organiser of the F-movement before the invasion, and after [[12 March]] [[1942]] he transformed the fifth-column network into the apparatus of occupation. As chief of police and *hishokan* (private secretary) to Resident [[lino|lino]], head of the Resident's Information Service (*Atjèh-senden-han*), and from [[16 January]] [[1943]] head of the department of general affairs, Masubuchi accumulated the offices through which the *kikan* could coordinate propaganda, intelligence, and political surveillance.

## The structure of the occupation

Under Masubuchi's direction, the F-organisatie's fifth-column network was regularised into the administrative apparatus of the occupation. The governing commissions established by [[Said Aboe Bakar|Said Aboe Bakar]] and the other F-propagandists were dissolved and replaced by a monarchical Indonesian administration under the *gun-chó* / *son-chó* system, with Masubuchi retaining personal control of the information service, the [[Atjèh Shimbun|Atjèh Shimbun]] editorship (until he relinquished it to S. Sagawa in early [[1943]]), and the police. "Of middle age, already for years before the war — e.g. as a planter on Sumatra's East Coast — in the Netherlands Indies, Masubuchi was in the Resident's staff the specialist in Indonesian affairs. He possessed an intuitive knowledge of the psyche of the Indonesian in general and of the Atjèhinese in particular." His principal institutional creations — the [[Maibkatra|Maibkatra]] of [[10 March]] [[1943]], the [[PETA|people's army]] of [[22 November]] [[1943]], the [[Atjèh Council|Atjèh Council]] of [[17 November]] [[1943]] — were the apparatus through which the *kikan* governed.

## Asia Timoer Raja and Indonesia Merdéka

The propaganda shift from the [[Asia Timoer Raja|Asia Timoer Raja]] idea (September [[1942]] – September [[1944]]) to the [[Indonesia Merdéka|Indonesia Merdéka]] idea (September [[1944]] – August [[1945]]) was conducted under Masubuchi's continued direction. "With great foresight, he knew how to keep the various groups in Acehnese society in balance, which, as the cards became increasingly unfavorable for Japan, meant by no means a simple task." The *kikan*'s grip extended through the *kempeitai* — which arrested and executed [[Teukoe Moehamad Hasan|Teukoe Moehamad Hasan]] in [[1944]] — and through the systematic absorption of the [[Poesa|Poesa]] *oelama*'s into the [[Maibkatra|Maibkatra]] and the [[Atjèh Council|Atjèh Council]]. "How great his popularity, particularly among his followers from the former F-organization, with whom he constantly maintained the bond through meetings and commemorative meals, may be evident from the telling honorary title '*bapak Atjèh*.'"

## Reactivation in 1945

On [[3 July]] [[1945]], with the Allies approaching, "Masubuchi, on 3 July 1945 released from the leadership of the economic affairs department, took upon himself the task of creating a new 5th column organization, which shortly — in the event of an Allied invasion — would have to carry out its destructive work behind the Allied lines. [[Said Aboe Bakar|Said Aboe Bakar]], [[Teukoe Panglima Pólém Moehamad Ali|Teukoe Panglima Pólém Moehamad Ali]], [[Teungkoe Moehamad Daoed Beureu-éh|Teungkoe Moehamad Daoed Beureu-éh]] and [[Teukoe Moehamad Amin|Teukoe Moehamad Amin]] took a leading part in the new organization, named by its founder 'Masubuchi-kikan.' The old F-organization would resume its task as M-organization." After the capitulation, Masubuchi was ordered arrested by the Allied commander in [[Medan|Medan]] on [[21 October]] [[1945]], but, Piekaar records, "by committing '*hara-kiri*' he managed to escape trial as a war criminal."
