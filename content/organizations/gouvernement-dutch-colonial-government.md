**Gouvernement (Dutch colonial government)**, a Colonial government, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Conducted geological investigations in Aceh.

## Type

Colonial government

## Description

Conducted geological investigations in Aceh. Reserved issuance of landbouw-concessies to itself (before [[1920]]).

## Significance

Gouvernement (Dutch colonial government) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
