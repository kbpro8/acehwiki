**Landschap Peudawa**, a Self-governing territory, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Zelfbesturend landschap in the onderafdeeling Idi; collaborated with BPM to build the 1934 landing field.

## Type

Self-governing territory

## Description

Zelfbesturend [[landschap]] in the [[onderafdeeling]] Idi; collaborated with BPM to build the [[1934]] landing field.

## Significance

Landschap Peudawa is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
