**Landschapskasen**, a Regional treasuries of the landschappen (Acehnese self-governing regions), appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Bear the costs of leprosy arrangements in Aceh.

## Type

[[Landschapskassen|Regional treasuries]] of the landschappen (Acehnese self-governing regions)

## Description

Bear the costs of leprosy arrangements in Aceh.

## Significance

Landschapskasen is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
