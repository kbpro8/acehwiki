**12th Infantry Battalion** was a line infantry battalion of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] sent as reinforcements to Aceh during [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]] in early [[1876]].

## Reinforcement Strength

Arriving in Aceh after December [[1875]], the battalion comprised 19 European officers, 209 European rank-and-file, and 382 indigenous soldiers, totaling 610 men.

## Combat in the XXII Moekims

On [[13 February]] [[1876]], the [[12th Infantry Battalion]] was deployed alongside the [[3rd Infantry Battalion]] and [[13th Infantry Battalion]] during the [[March through the XXII Moekims]]. Operating under General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]], the battalion engaged Acehnese forces loyal to [[Panglima Polem]], capturing fortified villages across the agricultural interior.
