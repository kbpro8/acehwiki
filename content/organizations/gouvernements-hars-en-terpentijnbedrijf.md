**Gouvernements Hars- en Terpentijnbedrijf**, a government enterprise (in Gajo-land), appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Government enterprise producing resin (hars) and turpentine in Gajo-land, dependent on the Gajoweg as conditio sine qua non.

## Type

government enterprise (in [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]]-land)

## Description

Government enterprise producing [[Resin (Aceh)|resin]] ([[Hars|hars]]) and turpentine in [[Gajo-land (Gayo Land)|Gajo]]-land, dependent on the [[Gajoweg (Gayo Road)|Gajoweg]] as conditio sine qua non.

## Significance

Gouvernements Hars- en Terpentijnbedrijf is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
