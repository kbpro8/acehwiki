**Javasche Bank te Koetaradja** was the branch of the Javasche Bank at [[Koeta Radja]], the first stone of whose building was laid by Governor [[H. N. A. Swart]] in [[1915]].

A photograph in [[Maurice Henri du Croo|du Croo]]'s book shows the ceremony: "Governor Swart lays the first stone of the building for the Java Bank at Koetaradja (1915)." It belongs among the visible signs of the economic normalisation of Atjeh in the second half of Swart's governorship.
