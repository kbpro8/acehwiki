**Legioen Mangko Negoro** was a colonial infantry legion attached to the *Zuid‑colonne* of the *Koninklijk Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger* during the Japanese invasion of the Dutch East Indies in [[1942]]. Two companies of the legion were part of the forces marching from [[Djombang]] towards [[Tjepoe]] and later to [[Kertosono]].

## Role in the Zuid‑colonne
The legion, together with a fort‑company and a half‑squadron of cavalry (1/2 III esk cav.), formed a motorised contingent that advanced alongside the *Marinebataljon*. Its troops were tasked with securing bridges and maintaining lines of communication.

## Significance
The *Legioen Mangko Negoro* illustrates the diversity of colonial forces mobilised by the Dutch in the defense of the archipelago, including operations affecting the Aceh region.
