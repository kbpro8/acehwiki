**Sigli ambachtsleergang**, a vocational training school, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Vocational training opened at Sigli in 1935, initially teaching only metaalbewerking (metalworking).

## Type

vocational training school

## Description

[[Sigli ambachtsleergang opened|Vocational training opened]] at [[Sigli]] in [[1935]], initially teaching only metaalbewerking (metalworking). Houtbewerking (woodworking) added at start of [[1937]]-[[1938]] course. Graduates (abiturienten) find ready work at the [[Atjeh-tram]] workshop as fully qualified workers.

## Significance

Sigli ambachtsleergang is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
