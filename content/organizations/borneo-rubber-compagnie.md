**The Borneo Rubber Compagnie** — described by Broersma as *'een Japansch lichaam'* — *a Japanese body* — also referred to as the *Dutch Borneo Plantations Cy.* — was the Japanese-owned company that acquired the older rubber-estate [[Tandjong Semantoh|Tandjong Semantoh]] in [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] from the oldest Tamiang planter, [[H. C. Reuter|H. C. Reuter]]. It is one of the early tokens of the transfer of European planting-interests in northern Sumatra to Japanese capital in the post-war period. The same Japanese-owned company also acquired the estate [[Blang Simpo|Blang Simpo]] in [[Langsa|Langsa]] as worthless rubber-land returned to the *landschap*.
