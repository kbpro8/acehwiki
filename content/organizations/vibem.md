**V.I.B.E.M.**, a wood exploitation company, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Wood exploitation company established at Sinabang (Simeuloeë) in 1917 on a large and luxurious scale.

## Type

wood exploitation company

## Description

Wood exploitation company established at [[Sinabang]] ([[Simeuloeë]]) in [[1917]] on a large and luxurious scale. Buildings and installations still stand but in a deplorable state of neglect at time of writing.

## Significance

V.I.B.E.M. is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
