**Raad van Achten (Council of Eight)**, a Acehnese diplomatic representation abroad, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Formed in Penang as representative of the Acehnese war party, consisting of eight Acehnese notables, to manage Acehnese interests abroad.

## Type

Acehnese diplomatic representation abroad

## Description

Formed in [[Penang]] as representative of the Acehnese war party, consisting of eight Acehnese notables, to manage Acehnese interests abroad. Among their orders: bring war materiel and [[Opium (Aceh)|opium]] through the [[Blokkade|blockade]] line into Aceh, fan the war mood there, and stir up revolt against [[Leyden (Leiden)|Netherlands]] throughout [[East Indian Archipelago|the archipelago]] ([[Hotz]]).

## Significance

Raad van Achten (Council of Eight) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
