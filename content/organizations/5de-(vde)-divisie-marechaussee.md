**The 5de (Vde) Divisie Marechaussee** was the [[Paja Bakong|Paja Bakong]]–[[Panton Laboe|Panton Laboe]] division of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Korps Maréchaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen]] during [[1940]]–[[1942]], and one of the two Maréchaussee divisions (together with the [[3de (IIIde) Divisie Marechaussee|IIIde Divisie]]) assigned to the central-Atjeh three-position defence of the [[Bireuën|Bireuën]]–[[Takengon|Takengon]] road in mid-March [[1942]]. In the [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|Dutch staff history's]] account (Deel VI, Hoofdstuk V), the Vde Divisie is the staff history's principal narrative unit of the third stelling at [[Bireuën|Bireuën]]–[[Takengon|Takengon]] — the stelling held under Captain [[L. A. Boucherie|Boucherie]] — and of the guerrilla conducted by the Vde Divisie's detached brigade under Lt [[C. Meulders|Meulders]].

## Organisation as of 1 January 1940

As of [[1 January]] [[1940]], the Vde Divisie Marechaussee was grouped as follows (each brigade of Maréchaussee numbering 20 men):

- [[Lho Soekoen|Lho Soekoen]]: 5 brigades
- [[Paja Bakong|Paja Bakong]]: 4 brigades
- [[Panton Laboe|Panton Laboe]]: 3 brigades

**Total**: 12 brigades (about 240 men).

## Operations of March 1942

In the [[1940]]–[[1941]] reorganisations, the 12 Maréchaussee brigades previously stationed at the outposts of Lho Soekoen (5 brigades), Paja Bakong (4 brigades) and Panton Laboe (3 brigades) were likewise assigned a defensive task, namely the protection of the demolition works at the oil installations in the vicinity of Koeala Simpang.

In [[Major W. F. Palmer van den Broek|Palmer van den Broek]]'s three-position defence of the Bireuën–Takengon road, the Vde Divisie supplied the troops of the third stelling (four brigades under Captain [[L. A. Boucherie|Boucherie]] as Vde divisie commander). The Vde Divisie's detached brigade under Lt [[C. Meulders|Meulders]] conducted the staff history's principal Dutch diversionary manoeuvre of the central-Atjeh theatre — a two-week guerrilla in the terrain north-west of the Bireuën–Takengon road, twice attacking marching Japanese with *klewang* charges and inflicting dozens of killed, before Meulders's capture and execution together with sergeant Kerrebijn.

The Vde Divisie's troops at [[Panton Laboe|Panton Laboe]] were commanded by Lt [[J. F. Westerweel|Westerweel]], who on the night of [[11 March|11]]–[[12 March]] [[12 March 1942|12 March 1942]] was the first Dutch officer to report the approach of the Japanese invasion fleet off [[Idi|Idi]].

## Significance

The Vde Divisie is, in the staff history's Atjeh chapter, the staff history's principal narrative unit of the third stelling at Bireuën–Takengon and of the Meulders guerrilla — the formation whose troops conducted the staff history's principal Dutch diversionary manoeuvre of the central-Atjeh theatre, and whose capture and execution of Lt Meulders and sergeant Kerrebijn is the staff history's clearest single instance of Japanese summary execution of Dutch Maréchaussee officers captured in the field after capitulation.
