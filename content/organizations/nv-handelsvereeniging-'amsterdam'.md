**N.V. Handelsvereeniging 'Amsterdam'**, a Cultuur-concern / trading and plantation company, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Began exploring Meulaboh and Singkel gronden in 1925 but abandoned due to the 1926-27 rebellion.

## Type

Cultuur-concern / trading and plantation company

## Description

Began exploring [[Malaboeh|Meulaboh]] and [[Singkel]] gronden in [[1925]] but abandoned due to the [[1926]]-27 rebellion. Also interested in oilpalm terrain in [[onderafdeeling]] [[Lho'Seumawé|Lho' Seumawé]]. Exploring for new oilpalm lands.

## Significance

N.V. Handelsvereeniging 'Amsterdam' is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
