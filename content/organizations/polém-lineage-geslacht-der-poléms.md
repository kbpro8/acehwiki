**Polém lineage (geslacht der Poléms)**, a Royal-rival noble lineage, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Lineage of Panglima Polém, equalled the Sultans in power and prestige.

## Type

Royal-rival noble lineage

## Description

Lineage of [[Panglima Polém]], equalled the Sultans in power and prestige. Several times besieged Sultans in their [[Dalem]], drove them out, and proclaimed new Sultans more compliant to them.

## Significance

Polém lineage (geslacht der Poléms) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
