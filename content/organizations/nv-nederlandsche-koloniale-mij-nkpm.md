**N.V. Nederlandsche Koloniale Mij. (NKPM)**, a Oil company (representing Standard Oil), appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. 

## Type

Oil company (representing [[Standard Oil]])

## Significance

N.V. Nederlandsche Koloniale Mij. (NKPM) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
