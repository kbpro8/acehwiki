**Indische legerverband** was the combined armed forces of the Dutch colonial administration in the East Indies, comprising the army, navy, and the [[Royal Netherlands Marine Corps|Dutch Marine Corps]]. It coordinated the numerous campaigns against indigenous resistance, most famously the **Aceh War** and the subsequent operations in [[Atjeh]] between [[1873]] and [[1908]].

The corps organised the deployment of marine battalions for amphibious landings, such as those at [[Kuta (Atjeh)|Kuta]] in April [[1873]] and September [[1873]], and later for expeditions to [[Edi (Atjeh)|Edi]], the [[Aroe Islands]], and [[Bali]].
