**Staten van Holland** was the provincial assembly of the **[[United Provinces (Dutch Republic)]]** that, from the mid‑17th century, exercised authority over the appointment of military commanders and the allocation of war‑time subsidies. After the death of **[[Willem II]]**, the **Staten van Holland** opposed the Crown’s attempt to install a new army chief, contributing to the decline of Dutch military readiness during the **[[Rampjaar|Disaster Year]]** [[1672]].

## Role in Colonial Administration
The **Staten van Holland** held the right to grant pardons to military officials, a practice that led to “knoeierij” (corruption) and the deterioration of the **[[Dutch East India Company|VOC]]**’s effectiveness in the **[[Sultanate of Aceh]]**. Their decisions influenced the staffing of the **VOC**’s factories on the Aceh coast.
