**Sigli coöperatieve rijstpellerij**, a cooperative rice mill (defunct), appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. A few years before writing, a rice mill on cooperative basis existed at Sigli.

## Type

cooperative rice mill (defunct)

## Description

A few years before writing, a rice mill on cooperative basis existed at [[Sigli]]. Cooperators were landschapshoofden (regional chiefs) of the [[afdeeling]]. Given the relations between those chiefs with their many nuances, observers wondered how long the business would last. The rice mill still exists; the cooperative has folded.

## Significance

Sigli coöperatieve rijstpellerij is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
