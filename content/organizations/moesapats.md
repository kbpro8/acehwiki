**Moesapats**, a Indigenous courts, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Inheemsche rechtbanken where important cases are heard.

## Type

Indigenous courts

## Description

Inheemsche rechtbanken where important cases are heard. Each [[Moesapat|moesapat]] has a [[kali-moesapat]] appointed by the government.

## Significance

Moesapats is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
