The **Economische Vereeniging te Koeta Radja** (Economic Association at [[Koeta Radja]]) was the association founded in [[1919]] by the then agent of the [[Javasche Bank te Koetaradja|Javasche Bank]] at that place, the heer [[J. Straatemeier]], aiming to advance the trading interests of its members and generally to study all possible economic questions occurring in [[Acheh|Atjeh]] and eventually to give advice about them to the Government and her officials.

## Working

From its foundation the association served the economic interests of the whole province in no small measure. Every year a report appeared, in which in a business-like, succinct manner the particulars and figures of the several cultures and trading establishments in Atjeh were communicated. Beside this, in the beginning of [[1920]] the said chairman of the association composed a voluminous report for the service of the [[Atjèh Instituut|Atjeh Instituut]] at [[Amsterdam]], in which the economic condition of the province was discussed into its details. Author [[J. Langhout|Joh. Langhout]] mentions the association in the closing sentence of his chapter on the "steunpilaren der pacificatie" — the press, banks and trading houses sustaining the pacification (see [[Nieuwsblad voor Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden]], [[Nederlandsche Handel-Maatschappij]]).
