**Firma Nass**, a trading firm / hotelier, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Well-known firm with toko's in various places in Aceh.

## Type

trading firm / hotelier

## Description

Well-known firm with toko's in various places in Aceh. Liquidated in [[1932]]; its Hotel Nass at [[Lho'Seumawé]] (well-known to old Aceh customers) disappeared with the liquidation.

## Significance

Firma Nass is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
