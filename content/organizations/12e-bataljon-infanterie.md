**The 12e Bataljon Infanterie** — also written *12de Bataljon* — was a Dutch colonial field infantry battalion of the [[Oost-Indisch Leger]], present in [[Aceh War|Atjeh]] at the start of [[1896]] and engaged in nearly every major Dutch column of the spring, from the immediate counter-measures after [[Teuku Umar]]'s [[Defection of Teuku Umar (29 March 1896)|defection of 29 March 1896]] through the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day offensive of 23–25 May 1896]] and the [[Tuchtiging der V Moekims Montassik (16–17 June 1896)|tuchtiging of V Moekims Montassik of 16–17 June 1896]]. Commanded initially by Overste G. Ilgen, then by Maj. G. F. Soeters, and in his absence (wd.) by Kapitein A. J. van Geelen, the battalion furnished troops for the buitenposten of [[Tjot Rang]] and [[Toengkoeb]] and supplied companies for the planned March column of Lt.-Col. C. P. J. van Vliet. The battalion was one of the largest in the theatre, with a [[Bijlage VIII — Staat van troepensterkte op 1 Juni 1896|1 June 1896 strength]] of 13 officers and 512 minderen.

## Background and Formation

As a *veldbataljon* of the colonial army, the 12e Bataljon combined European and indigenous companies armed with Beaumont rifles. In the early months of [[1896]] it was among the principal line-infantry units of the Atjeh garrison, providing troops for the buitenposten of [[Tjot Goeë]], [[Tjot Rang]], and [[Toengkoeb]], and figuring in Lt.-Col. C. P. J. van Vliet's planned (but cancelled) March column under Overste G. Ilgen. On [[30 March]] [[1896]] three of its companies were sent by *stoomtram* to [[Lampeneroet]] with orders to march to [[Tjot Goeë]] and await orders, two of these returning at 9 PM to [[Koeta Radja]]; during that march European fusiliers Van Gelder (No. 39084) and De Vries (No. 39291) were severely wounded by fire from [[Lam Tehen]] and [[Koeta Karang]]. On [[31 March]]–[[1 April]] [[1896]] one European company of the 12e Bataljon held the intersection of the Koeta Radja–Lampeneroet and Koeta Radja–Ketapan Doewa roads, with one section forward at the bridge over Kroeng Daroe and one at village Lohong, in the *hinderlagen* laid to protect [[Koeta Radja]].

## Role in the Atjeh War

On [[14 April]] [[1896]] the 12e Bataljon operated against [[Lam-Goet|Lam Goet]] in the [[Tuchtiging van Lam Goet (14 April 1896)|tuchtiging of Lam Goet]] under Maj. G. F. Soeters. On [[17 April]] [[1896]] it was committed in [[J. W. Stemfoort (Kolonel der Infanterie)|Stemfoort]]'s colonne at [[Battle of Senelop (17 April 1896)|Senelop]]. On [[20 April]] [[1896]] it operated again in the final phase of the [[Evacuation of Kroeng-Gloempang, Tjot-Rang, Toengkoeb (20 April 1896)|evacuation of the buitenlinie]]. On [[2 May]] [[1896]] it formed the *rechtercolonne* of the [[Diversie to Montassik / Battle of Senelop (2 May 1896)|diversie to Montassik]] under Maj. Soeters (described in the source as "Maj. Sterss" or "Maj. Sters"), with *½ escadron cavalerie* under Ritmeester Jhr. de Lannoy; the column talso [[Lamsoet]] without opposition at 6 AM and occupied [[Lam-Oe]]. On [[23 May]] [[1896]] the battalion — now 18 officieren, 240 Eur. and 205 inl. minderen — formed part of the *colonne Lamtih* under Luit.-kol. G. F. Soeters in the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day offensive]]. On [[24 May]] [[1896]] the battalion burned villages west of [[Pantjoeran]] and on [[25 May]] [[1896]] led the march to Djirat. The 3e compagnie, under Kapitein F. W. G. C. L. Steup (injured by falling into a pit at the start of the [[Lam Manjang]] march on [[23 May]], his command passing to 1e luit. Ferguson), led the attack on [[Lam Manjang]] and [[Lam Isi]]. From [[30 May]] to [[2 June]] [[1896]] the battalion formed part of the *dekkingstroepen* of the [[Razing of Lam-Asan (30 May – 2 June 1896)|razing of Lam-Asan]] under Luit.-kol. Soeters. On [[22 June]]–[[23 June]] [[1896]] the battalion marched in the 2e (land) Colonne under [[J. W. Stemfoort (Kolonel der Infanterie)|Kolonel Stemfoort]] in the [[Ladoeng Landing and Glé Broeë Attack (22–23 June 1896)|Ladoeng landing and Glé Broeë attack]].

## Composition and Strength

The 12e Bataljon's strength evolved through the spring. On [[23 May]] [[1896]] it fielded 18 officieren, 240 European and 205 indigenous *minderen* under Luit.-kol. G. F. Soeters. The [[Bijlage VIII — Staat van troepensterkte op 1 Juni 1896|strength return of 1 June 1896]] recorded 13 officers and 512 minderen, making it one of the largest Dutch battalions in the theatre. Command during the spring passed from Overste G. Ilgen to Maj. G. F. Soeters, with (wd.) Kapitein A. J. van Geelen deputizing during absences. Company commanders included Kapitein F. W. G. C. L. Steup (3e compagnie), Kapitein S. A. Drijber (5e compagnie, *koloniale reserve*), 1e luit. Ferguson (taking over the 3e compagnie on [[23 May]]), and 1e luit. Tetenburg (peloton commander who brought *munitieaanvulling* to the 12e Bataljon at Lam Manjang on [[23 May]]). Medical support was provided by the ambulance under Officier v. gez. J. F. Langenberg (12 minderen). The 5e compagnie under Kapitein S. A. Drijber conducted the [[Three-Column Operation to Free Tjot Rang and Kroeng Gloempang (21 April 1896)|evacuation of Tjot Rang on 21 April 1896]].

## Significance

The 12e Bataljon Infanterie was a workhorse of the spring [[1896]] campaign, present from the immediate counter-measures of [[30 March]] [[1896]] through the [[Ladoeng Landing and Glé Broeë Attack (22–23 June 1896)|final June operation at Glé Broeë]]. Its repeated commitment under successive commanders (Ilgen → Soeters → van Geelen) illustrates the strain that the spring [[1896]] operational tempo placed on Dutch battalion command. The capture of [[Lamsoet]] and [[Lam-Oe]] without opposition on [[2 May]] [[1896]] — in contrast to the costly storm of [[Senelop]] the same day by the [[Korps Marechaussée|marechaussée]] and the [[3e Bataljon Infanterie|3e Bataljon]] — illustrates the differential outcomes that terrain and enemy disposition produced in the same operation. The 12e Bataljon's 3e compagnie, leading the assault on [[Lam Manjang]] on [[23 May]] [[1896]], bore the brunt of the heaviest fighting on the first day of the [[Three-day Dutch offensive of 23–25 May 1896|three-day offensive]].

**12e Bataljon Infanterie** was a Dutch infantry unit that participated in the [[First Aceh Expedition]].

## Combat History
They were among the first units to conduct the [[Landing on Atjeh's Coasts]] on [[8 April]] [[1873]]. Later, they provided vital cover and rear-guard defense during the intense operations of the [[Second Conquest of the Great Missigit]] on [[14 April]] [[1873]].
