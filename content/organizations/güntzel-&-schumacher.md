**Güntzel & Schumacher** — also written *Handel-Mij. Güntzel & Schumacher* — was a Dutch trading-company active in the *Gouvernement Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden*, with an office at [[Langsa|Langsa]] and a coast-trade consisting of a tug with *tongkangs*, plying between [[Idi|Idi]] and [[Belawan|Belawan]] in connection with its licence for the exploitation of flood-forests. At Langsa it is one of the trading-houses that remained after the Factorij, the Deli-Atjeh-Mij. and the firm Kehding had closed their offices.
