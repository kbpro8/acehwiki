**De Gebroeders van Cleef** was the publishing house at [['s-Gravenhage (The Hague)|'s-Gravenhage]] (The Hague) that in [[1893]] issued the pamphlet *[[Hoe Atjeh te onderwerpen (Coblijn, 1893)|Hoe Atjeh te onderwerpen]]* by [[W. A. Coblijn]], gepensionneerd Kolonel van het Oost-Indisch Leger — the war-critical treatise in which the management of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] by politics was indicted and a detailed [[Coblijn's programme for the subjugation of Atjeh|programme for the subjugation of Atjeh]] was set out. The book was printed by Ruigrok & Co. at [[Haarlem]], and a copy, stamped as belonging to the *Bibliotheek van de 2de Kamer der Staten-Generaal* (Library of the Second Chamber of the [[Staten-Generaal]]), was preserved in the parliamentary library at The Hague.
