**The 1ste Divisie Marechaussee** was the first of the six divisions of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Korps Maréchaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen]] stationed in the *gewest* of [[Atjeh|Atjeh]] during [[1940]]–[[1942]]. In the [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|Dutch staff history's]] account (Deel VI, Hoofdstuk V), the 1ste Divisie is the Blangkedjeren- and central-Atjeh-oriented Maréchaussee division whose eight brigades at [[Blangkedjeren|Blangkedjeren]] — together with the four brigades at [[Koeala Bee|Koeala Bee]] — formed the principal Dutch force in the Alas valley theatre on the eve of the [[Japanese invasion of Atjèh|Japanese invasion of March 1942]].

The **1ste Divisie Maréchaussee** was the first divisional formation of the [[Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen|Korps Maréchaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen]] in the period of the Dutch pre-invasion garrison drawdown ([[1940]]–[[1941]]). As of [[1 January 1940]], the 1ste Divisie was garrisoned principally at [[Blangkedjeren|Blangkedjeren]] — the principal Dutch administrative post of the Gajo Alas interior — with a detachment at [[Koeala Bee|Koeala Bee]] in the south.

## Operations 1940–1942

The 1ste Divisie's Blangkedjeren brigades were progressively drawn upon for the pre-invasion reorganisations of [[1940]]–[[1941]]. By March [[1942]], the Iste Divisie's Blangkedjeren strength had been consolidated with troops of various other units at Blangkedjeren under Lt-Col [[Blogg|Blogg]]'s command. The 1ste Divisie's Koeala Bee brigades remained on the Westkust, where they formed part of Captain [[L. H. J. Kloprogge|Kloprogge]]'s reduced IIde divisie command after the [[1940]] reductions.


## Organisation as of 1 January 1940

As of [[1 January]] [[1940]], the 1ste Divisie Marechaussee was grouped as follows (each brigade of Maréchaussee numbering 20 men):

- [[Blangkedjeren|Blangkedjeren]]: 8 brigades (Iste Divisie)
- [[Koeala Bee|Koeala Bee]]: 4 brigades (Iste Divisie)

**Total**: 12 brigades (about 240 men).


## Significance

The 1ste Divisie Maréchaussee is significant in the [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|staff history's]] account as the principal Maréchaussee formation of the Gajo Alas interior, and the divisional formation whose dissolution during the pre-invasion drawdown of [[1940]]–[[1941]] reduced Dutch military presence in the southern Atjeh interior to a minimum by the time of the Japanese invasion.



The 1ste Divisie Marechaussee is, in the staff history's Atjeh chapter, the Blangkedjeren-oriented division whose troops formed the backbone of the Dutch defence at the [[Sumatra capitulation (28 March 1942)|Blangkedjeren capitulation of 28 March 1942]]. The staff history's emphasis on the division's progressive reduction through [[1940]]–[[1941]] is part of the compilers' broader argument that the Dutch pre-invasion force drawdown structurally disabled the Maréchaussee's capacity to mount sustained resistance.

The [[Nederlands-Indië contra Japan|staff history]] records the following divisional deployment for the Maréchaussee as of [[1 January 1940]]:

| Divisie | Headquarters | Brigades | Secondary posts |
|---------|-------------|---------|-----------------|
| Iste Divisie | Blangkedjeren | 8 | Koeala Bee (4 brigades) |

Total as of 1 January [[1940]]: 12 brigades.

The 1ste Divisie was the Blangkedjeren-oriented division — responsible for the Gajo Alas interior, the most remote of the Dutch Atjeh garrison's areas of responsibility. Blangkedjeren was the administrative centre of the *onderafdeeling Gajo Alas*, the southernmost of the Dutch Atjeh interior posts.


## The 1940 and 1941 reductions

The pre-invasion drawdown of [[1940]] and [[1941]] reduced the Maréchaussee through two phases. The [[Netherlands-Indië contra Japan|staff history's]] account of the first phase (the reductions of [[1940]]) records the dissolution of the Blangkedjeren-oriented detachments as part of the general reduction from 14 divisies to the smaller garrison force. The 1ste Divisie's brigades were among those dissolved in the first phase of reductions, and the divisional formation was effectively wound up during [[1940]]–[[1941]].

By March [[1942]] the Iste Divisie's residual Blangkedjeren strength had been consolidated with troops of various other units at Blangkedjeren under the command of Lt-Col [[Blogg|Blogg]]. The Divisie's Koeala Bee brigades, meanwhile, remained on the Westkust, where after the [[1940]] reductions they formed part of Captain [[L. H. J. Kloprogge|Kloprogge]]'s reduced IIde Divisie command.


## The Blangkedjeren capitulation of 28 March 1942

After the Japanese landings of [[12 March 1942]], the residual Dutch forces in the Gajo Alas interior — now under Lt-Col Blogg — surrendered at Blangkedjeren on [[28 March 1942]], the last of the four Atjeh capitulations. The Blangkedjeren capitulation closed the regular Dutch campaign in Atjeh.
