**Hollandia Drukkerij N.V. Baarn**, a Publisher/printer of the book, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Publisher of the book.

## Type

Publisher/printer of the book

## Description

Publisher of the book. Director of the publishing house requested [[Resident Jongejans|Jongejans]]to write a popular-scientific work on Atjeh with newest data and new photographic material.[[Land en volk van Atjeh vroeger en nu (Jongejans, 1939)|Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu]]'.

## Significance

Hollandia Drukkerij N.V. Baarn is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
