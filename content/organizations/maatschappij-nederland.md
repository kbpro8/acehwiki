The **Maatschappij "Nederland"** (the "Netherlands" Company) was a Dutch shipping line that, in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, operated the mail and passenger service between the [[Netherlands|Netherlands]] and [[Nederlandsch-Indië|Nederlandsch-Indië]]. The company's steamships carried the metropolitan officers detached to the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger]] for the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]], including [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede-Luitenant C. J. Snijders]], who sailed from [[Nieuwediep|Nieuwediep]] on [[10 July]] [[10 July]] [[1873]] on the steamship *Conrad* (2500 tons, still fitted with sail for use in fair winds) for [[Batavia|Batavia]], where he arrived on [[31 August]] [[31 August]] [[1873]] after seven and a half weeks. Snijders returned to Europe on the company's *Koning der Nederlanden* in [[August 1875|August]]–[[September 1875|September]] [[1875]].

## The *Conrad* Voyage of July–August 1873

The *Conrad* left [[Nieuwediep|Nieuwediep]] on [[10 July]] [[10 July]] [[1873]] carrying a reinforcement detachment of 400 men with their officers, a number of officers returning from European leave, and most of the metropolitan officers being detached to the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger]] for the [[Second Aceh Expedition (1873-1874)|second Aceh expedition]] — among them [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] and [[J. W. N. Cramer|J. W. N. Cramer]]. Prince Hendrik, brother of the King, saw the troops off, dining on board and addressing the departing officers before returning to shore. In the Bay of Biscay the *Conrad* suffered storm damage and put in at [[Gibraltar|Gibraltar]] for ten days of repair; under the guidance of [[G. P. de Neve|Kolonel G. P. de Neve]] the officers made an excursion to [[Tangier|Tangier]]. After Gibraltar the *Conrad* called only at Port Said and Aden, and reached [[Batavia|Batavia]] on [[31 August]] [[31 August]] [[1873]].

## The Return Voyage of August–September 1875

[[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] left [[Oleh-leh|Olehleh]] on [[4 August]] [[4 August]] [[1875]] on the steamship *Atjeh* of the Nederlandsch-Indische Stoomvaartmaatschappij, called at [[Penang|Penang]] and [[Singapore|Singapore]], and reached [[Batavia|Batavia]] on [[14 August]] [[14 August]]. After a few days at [[Buitenzorg|Buitenzorg]] he sailed for Europe on the *Koning der Nederlanden* of the Maatschappij "Nederland" on [[26 August]] [[26 August]], reaching [[Napels|Naples]] on [[30 September]] [[30 September]] and continuing overland to his parents' house at 's-Gravenzande on [[22 October]] [[22 October]] [[1875]].
