**N.V. Serdang Cultuur Maatschappij**, a Tea plantation company, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Only company to actually develop its erfpachtperceel for tea cultivation in the Takengonsche.

## Type

Tea plantation company

## Description

Only company to actually develop its erfpachtperceel for tea cultivation in the Takengonsche. Operates the [[Redelong (theeonderneming)|Redelong]] tea estate with 805 H.A. of tea. Built the modern tea factory completed end [[1937]].

## Significance

N.V. Serdang Cultuur Maatschappij is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
