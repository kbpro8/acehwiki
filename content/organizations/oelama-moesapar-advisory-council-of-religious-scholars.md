**Oelama-moesapar (advisory council of religious scholars)**, a Advisory council of religious scholars, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Highest religious scholars chosen as the college (the landschapsgerecht's religious advisory board), usually named oelama-moesapar.

## Type

Advisory council of religious scholars

## Description

Highest religious scholars [[Corea|chosen]] as the college (the [[Landschapsgerecht|landschapsgerecht]]'s religious advisory board), usually named [[Oelama|oelama]]-moesapar.

## Significance

Oelama-moesapar (advisory council of religious scholars) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
