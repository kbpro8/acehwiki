**De Rijzende Zon** (“The Rising Sun”) was the *bouwmaatschappij* (building company) of which the former Maréchaussée officer [[W. V. Rhemrev]] was director at the time of the [[1930]] jubilee of the *[[Korps Maréchaussée te voet in Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden|Korps Maréchaussée]]*.

## Rhemrev's directorship

The jubilee number records: "Thans is de heer Rhemrev Directeur van de bouwmaatschappij *De Rijzende Zon*, terwijl hij tevens deelneemt aan de exploitatie van de nieuwe rubber-tapmethode, onlangs gedaan door den heer [[Jules Bosch]]" — at present Mr. Rhemrev is director of the building company *De Rijzende Zon*, while he also takes part in the exploitation of the new rubber-tapping method recently devised by Mr. Jules Bosch.

The directorship followed Rhemrev's placement on the army reserve after his suppression of the [[Communistische opstand ter Sumatra's Westkust (1926-1927)|communist rising]] on [[Westkust|Sumatra's Westkust]] in [[1926]].
