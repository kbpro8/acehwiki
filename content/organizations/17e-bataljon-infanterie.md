**The 17e Bataljon Infanterie** was a battalion of the [[Nederlandsch-Indisch Leger|Netherlands Indies Army]], in garrison at [[Padang]], two companies of which were the first reinforcement to reach [[Acheh|Atjeh]] in the crisis of [[1896]].

[[P. H. R. Beuming]] records that after a time of tension — in which there were no troops available to relieve the enclosed forward posts, all available men being needed to hold the *[[Geconcentreerde linie|geconcentreerde linie]]* and to secure [[Koeta Radja]] and [[Oleh-leh]] — two companies of the 17e Bataljon arrived at Oleh-leh on [[3 April]] [[1896]] under the command of Majoor [[G. F. Soeters|Soeters]].

The men were distributed without delay over the posts [[Blang]], [[Lamdjamoe]] and [[Ketapan Doea]], *waardoor de tijdelijk daar gelegerde troepen weer beschikbaar kwamen* — by which the troops temporarily quartered there became available again.
