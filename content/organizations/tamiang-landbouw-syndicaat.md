**The Tamiang Landbouw-syndicaat** — the *Tamiang Agricultural Syndicate* — was a planting-syndicate formed in [[1910]] in the *onderafdeeling* [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] of the *afdeeling* [[Oostkust van Atjeh|Oostkust van Atjeh]]. The rubber-lands Rantau, Tamiang and Oepah, formerly of the Tamiang Landbouw-syndicaat, now belong to the [[Anglo Dutch|Anglo Dutch]].
