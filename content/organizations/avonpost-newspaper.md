**Avonpost (newspaper)**, a Newspaper, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Newspaper in which the first 'Weker-artikelen' (alarm articles) titled 'Hoe beschaafd Nederland in de twintigste eeuw vrede en orde schept op Atjeh' appeared during Van Daalen's governorate, prompting a high-level investigation.

## Type

Newspaper

## Description

Newspaper in which the first '[[Weker-artikelen]]' (alarm articles) titled 'Hoe beschaafd [[Netherlands|Nederland]] in de twintigste eeuw vrede en orde schept op Atjeh' appeared during [[Gouverneur Van Daalen|Van Daalen]]'s governorate, prompting a high-level investigation.

## Significance

Avonpost (newspaper) is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
