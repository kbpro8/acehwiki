**de Lange & Co.**, a Bataviaasche firma (trading firm), appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Founded the Sabang coal station with financial support from the Nederlandsche Handel Mij; laid the first stone for the harbor enterprise at Sabang.

## Type

Bataviaasche firma (trading firm)

## Description

Founded the [[Sabang]] coal station with financial support from the Nederlandsche Handel Mij; laid the first stone for the harbor enterprise at [[Sabang]].

## Significance

de Lange & Co. is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
