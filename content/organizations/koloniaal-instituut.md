**The Koloniaal Instituut** — the *Colonial Institute* of Amsterdam, later the *Koninklijk Instituut voor de Tropen* (Royal Tropical Institute) — was the principal Dutch scholarly institution for the study of the colonies, founded in Amsterdam in the late nineteenth century, whose *[[Boekerij van het Koloniaal Instituut|Boekerij van het Koloniaal Instituut]]* (Library of the Colonial Institute) series published, in [[1896]], J. A. Kruijt's historical brochure *[[De Atjeh-Oorlog (Kruijt, 1896)|De Atjeh-Oorlog: Kort Overzicht van Oorsprong, Verloop en Gevolg]]*. The Koloniaal Instituut thereby served as the institutional context of one of the earliest sustained Dutch historical critiques of the conduct of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]].

## The *Boekerij van het Koloniaal Instituut*

The *Boekerij van het Koloniaal Instituut* was a publication series of the Koloniaal Instituut, devoted to scholarly and polemical works on the Dutch colonial enterprise. The series was the principal Dutch publishing venue for works on the colonies that combined scholarly rigour with policy relevance. The publication of Kruijt's brochure in the series in [[1896]] placed it in the institutional context of the Koloniaal Instituut's scholarly authority.

## The Institutional Context of Kruijt's Brochure

Kruijt's brochure, published in the *Boekerij van het Koloniaal Instituut* series by [[Loman & Funke|Loman & Funke]] of 's-Gravenhage in [[1896]], was a sustained polemical indictment of the Dutch conduct of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] from [[1873]] to [[1896]]. Its publication by the Koloniaal Instituut gave it the institutional authority of the principal Dutch scholarly institution for the study of the colonies. Its tone — at once scholarly and polemical, annotated with personal judgements on the principal actors — reflects the genre of the colonial brochure of the late nineteenth century, as cultivated by the Koloniaal Instituut and its *Boekerij*.

## Significance

The Koloniaal Instituut, as the publisher of Kruijt's brochure, is the institutional context of one of the earliest sustained Dutch historical critiques of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]]. The *Boekerij van het Koloniaal Instituut* series, of which the brochure was a volume, was the principal Dutch publishing venue for works on the colonies that combined scholarly rigour with policy relevance. The Koloniaal Instituut's institutional authority, behind Kruijt's polemic, gave the brochure a weight that a non-institutional publication would not have had.
