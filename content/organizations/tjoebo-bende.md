**Tjoebo-bende** was the small band of [[Pidië]] named in the [[1913]] list of the last resistance groups that refused to submit.

It appears with [[Pang Bintang]], [[T. Dagang]] and the remnants of the [[Tirò Teungkoes|Tirò band]] under [[Tgk. Ma' Aréh di Lam Goet]] among the principal band-heads and resistance leaders of Pidië still unsubmitted at the beginning of [[1913]]. All were eliminated in the course of that year and "Central Pidië was cleared."
