The **Nederlandsch-Indische Stoomvaartmaatschappij** (Dutch Indies Steamship Company, often abbreviated NISM) was a Dutch colonial steamship company that operated coastal and inter-island services in [[Nederlandsch-Indië|Nederlandsch-Indië]] in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries. The company's steamers carried [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Tweede-Luitenant C. J. Snijders]] from [[Batavia|Batavia]] to [[Surabaya|Surabaya]] in [[September 1873|September]] [[1873]] (on his posting to the Staf der Genie at Surabaya) and from [[Oleh-leh|Olehleh]] to [[Batavia|Batavia]] in [[August 1875|August]] [[1875]] (on his departure from Atjeh), the latter voyage on the steamship *Atjeh*. The company thus served as the principal carrier between the port of [[Oleh-leh|Olehleh]] — the seaward gateway of [[Koeta Radja|Kota Radja]] — and the rest of the archipelago.

## The Voyage of September 1873

On [[20 September]] [[20 September]] [[1873]], having received his posting to the Staf der Genie at [[Surabaya|Surabaya]], [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] departed [[Batavia|Batavia]] by NISM steamer; he arrived at [[Surabaya|Surabaya]] on [[24 September]] [[24 September]] [[1873]].

## The Voyage of August 1875

On [[4 August]] [[4 August]] [[1875]] [[Cornelis Jacobus Snijders|Snijders]] left [[Oleh-leh|Olehleh]] on the steamship *Atjeh* of the Nederlandsch-Indische Stoomvaartmaatschappij. After a few days at [[Penang|Penang]] and [[Singapore|Singapore]] he reached [[Batavia|Batavia]] on [[14 August]] [[14 August]]. The choice of an NISM steamer for the departure from Atjeh — and the name of the steamer — is a small reminder of the place of Atjeh in the Dutch colonial imagination.
