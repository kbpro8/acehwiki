The **Geneeskundig Tijdschrift voor Nederlandsch-Indië** (“Medical Journal for the Netherlands Indies”) was the principal medical periodical of the Netherlands Indies, in whose Afl. 19 of Deel 80 ([[1940]]) Dr. [[C. E. Gomperts]]'s [[Oriënteerend oogheelkundig onderzoek bij de Atjehsche bevolking van Groot-Atjeh gedurende 1937|report on the Groot-Atjeh eye survey]] appeared, printed at Batavia-Centrum by G. Kolff & Co.

## The Gomperts offprint

The report occupies pages 1191 to 1238 of the volume and was received by the editors on [[25 October]] [[1939]]. The offprint was catalogued as DLP-157 in the Bibliotheek KITLV. Running footers in the text carry the journal's abbreviated title, "Gen. Tijdschr. Afl. 19."

## As a source cited within the report

Gomperts's list of consulted literature cites four separate volumes of the journal — the years [[1935]], [[1936]], [[1937]] and [[1938]] — as the vehicle of the Netherlands-Indies debate on *[[Xerophthalmie|xerophthalmie]]* in which he was intervening. The published work of Mevrouw [[Van Stockum]], Dr. [[J. H. de Haas]], [[Sie Boen Lian]], [[Oey Djoen Hoat]] and [[J. Tijssen]] on the subject appeared largely in its pages.

Alongside it Gomperts cited the *Mededeelingen van den Dienst der Volksgezondheid in Nederlandsch-Indië* (year XXVI, [[1937]], No. 3), the *[[Volkstelling 1930]]* part IV on the indigenous population of [[Sumatra]], the *Klinische Wochenschrift* of [[1937]] as abstracted in the *Nederlandsch Tijdschrift voor Geneeskunde*, and the *Handelingen van het 7e Nederlandsch-Indisch Natuurwetenschappelijk Congres*.
