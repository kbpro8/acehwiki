**The Anglo Dutch** was a planting-company in [[Tamiang|Tamiang]] which held the rubber-lands Rantau, Tamiang and Oepah, formerly of the [[Tamiang Landbouw-syndicaat|Tamiang Landbouw-syndicaat]].
