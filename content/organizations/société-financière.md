**Société Financière**, a Financial company / plantation enterprise, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. Active on Westkust and Noordkust; with H.V.A.

## Type

Financial company / plantation enterprise

## Description

Active on [[Westkust]] and [[Noordkust]]; with H.V.A. fairly active but limited to a few undertakings.

## Significance

Société Financière is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
