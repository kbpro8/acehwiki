The **Landbouwdienst** — the Agricultural Service of the Netherlands Indies — was the body whose instruction Dr. [[C. E. Gomperts]] proposed to enlist in the campaign against *[[Xerophthalmie|xerophthalmie]]* in [[Groot-Atjeh]], by stimulating the cultivation of vitamin-A-rich crops in the villages.

## The recommendation

The last of Gomperts's five recommended measures for combating xerophthalmia and preventing blindness through *[[Keratomalacie|keratomalacie]]* was that local agriculture — in particular the *erfbouw* (yard cultivation) of vitamin-A-rich crops — "zal moeten worden gestimuleerd met voorlichting van den landbouwdienst," should be stimulated with instruction from the Agricultural Service.

## The crops named

Gomperts listed the crops to be encouraged: *papaja*, *peper*, *bajem* (amaranth), *cassave singkong*, *gambas*, *gendjer*, *kangkoeng*, *katjang pandjang* (long bean), *katoek*, *ketimoen* (cucumber), *melindjo*, *oebi*, *prei* (leek), *roode kool* (red cabbage), *tales merah*, *boea-nona*, *djeroek garoet*, *mangga*, *zuurzak* (soursop) and *tomaten*.

The recommendation followed from his finding that the basic food of the Atjehnese *[[Kampong|kampong]]* population consisted of rice, and that the supplementing of this basic food with vegetables and meat was very sparse, so that the population suffered a chronic shortage of vitamin A.
