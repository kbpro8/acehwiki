**The Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen** was the elite *Marechaussee* (military police / light infantry) corps of the [[K.N.I.L.]] raised for counter-insurgency work in [[Atjeh|Aceh]] and its dependencies — the corps whose soldiers were known to themselves and to the Acehnese as *[[Manisee (Manisé)|Manisee]]* or *[[Manisee (Manisé)|Marsosé]]*, whose *[[Marechausseelied]]* and *[[Korpslied van de Marechaussee|korpslied]]* were composed by [[Dominee Thenu]], and whose *[[dardanellen|dardanellen]]* (point-men), *[[bekas]]* (spoorzoekers) and *[[bivak]]* (camp) culture are the principal subject of *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]*. The corps was the instrument by which the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]] was militarily finished — by the method of small, highly trained patrouilles operating with local intelligence against a religiously motivated resistance — and the institutional home of the book's named Ambonese soldiers: [[Nussy]], [[Thijs de Fretes]], [[Tanamal]], [[Salawono]] and the rest.

## Formation and the Manisee name

The corps was formed for the Aceh war. Its soldiers were known as *[[Manisee (Manisé)|Manisee]]* (also *Manisé*) or *[[Manisee (Manisé)|Marsosé]]* — names that resounded in the *[[Marechausseelied]]*: "*Manisee-Manisee! 't Is terlaloe Manisee!*" The *refrein* of the *Marechausseelied* — "*Manisee-Manisee! 't Is terlaloe Manisee!*" — was the soldiers' own identification. The corps was renamed *Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Celebes* when its theatre was extended to [[Celebes]] in the early 20th century; the older name *Atjeh en Onderhorigen* is the one used in *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]* for the Aceh era.

## The method: small patrouilles and the dardanellen

The corps's method was the small patrouille — a brigade or two, with *[[dardanellen|dardanellen]]* (point-men) at the front, *[[bekas]]* (spoorzoekers) reading the footprints, and a *[[bivak]]* (camp) built each night with *[[klewang]]* as the only tool. The *colonne* — a small force of forty-odd militairen with *dwangarbeiders* as dragers — was the operational unit; the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]] of [[1910]]-[[1911]] is the book's set-piece example. The method had been developed in the [[Gouverneur-Generaal Van Heutsz|Van Heutsz]] era and was brought to its perfection by officers like [[Schmidt]] and patrouillecommandanten like [[Vastenou]] and [[Dersjant]].

The *dardanellen|dardanel* — the point-man — was the key figure. *[[Nussy]]* "was dardanel geweest bij [[Vastenou]] en [[Dersjant]], vermaarde patrouillecommandanten, die schitterende resultaten hadden geboekt." The *dardanel* went ahead, alone or in pairs, and shot first; his was the most dangerous post in the corps, and the most decorated.

## The brigade hierarchy

On [[Atjeh|Aceh]] the brigade hierarchy was: the *baas* (the brigadecommandant, a European sergeant or sergeant 1e kl); the *pang* (the sergeant 2e kl, so called on Aceh); the *keutjhi* (the korporaal). The brigade was divided in three groepen accordingly. The soldiers divided themselves into *[[kongsi]]s* by *[[bangsa]]* — Ambonezen + Timorezen together; Menadonezen; Javanen + Soendanezen + Madoerezen together — for *gezamenlijk koken* and other huishoudelijke bezigheden on patrouille.

## The songs and the dominee

The corps's *[[Marechausseelied]]* and *[[Korpslied van de Marechaussee|korpslied]]* were composed by [[Dominee Thenu]]. The *Marechausseelied* — with its *refrein* "*Manisee-Manisee! 't Is terlaloe Manisee!*" and its third regel "*Hari-hari naik goenoeng*" or "*Satoe mati ganti sepoeloeh*" — was the soldiers' own identification. The *korpslied* — with its *refrein* "*Hai marsosé, hai marsosé… memboeroeh moesoeh th' tjapé-é, naik-toeroen goenoeng, masoek-kaloear rimba, memtjari bekas dani anak marsosé!*" — narrated the daden of the corps vanaf de *oprichting*.

## The decorations

The corps was the great nursery of [[Militaire Willemsorde (M.W.O.)|M.W.O.]] ridders in the colonial army. [[Salawono]] (Ridder 3e klas) earned his 4e klas at the [[Storming of Limboer (22 June 1903)|storming of Limboer]] and his 3e klas on [[Celebes]]. [[Nussy]] became Ridder 3e klas for the [[colonne Schmidt campaign against the Tiriō Teungkoes (1910-1911)|colonne Schmidt]]. [[Schmidt]] received the *[[Eresabel]]* for [[1910]] and the M.W.O. 3e klas for [[1911]]. The four retired M.W.O.-ridders who acted as *slippendragers* at the [[Funeral of Dominee Thenu (11 May 1937)|funeral of Dominee Thenu]] — [[Salawono]] (3e kl.), [[Pattiasina]], [[Kazen]] and [[Pattipuluhu]] — were all old Marechaussees.

## Significance

The Korps Marechaussee van Atjeh en Onderhorigen is the institutional hero of *[[Oom Ambon van het K.N.I.L.]]*. The book's argument is that the corps — through its method of small patrouilles, its *dardanellen|dardanel* system, its *[[bekas]]* spoorzoekers, and its songs — was the instrument by which the [[Atjeh-oorlog|Aceh War]] was actually finished. The corps is also the book's principal theatre of *[[Oom Ambon]]*: the Ambonese soldier of the Marechaussee is the figure through whom Dames makes his argument about what the colonial army was and what was lost in its dissolution.
