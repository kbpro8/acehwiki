**9th Infantry Battalion** was a line infantry battalion of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] stationed in the [[Aceh Sultanate]] during [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]] in [[1875]] and [[1876]].

## Composition and Division

In December [[1875]], the battalion was split into right and left half-battalions. Total strength comprised 18 officers, 210 European soldiers, and 363 indigenous soldiers, totaling 591 men.

## Field Operations

On [[30 January]] [[1876]], the left half-battalion of the [[9th Infantry Battalion]] operated in field columns alongside the [[8th Infantry Battalion]] and [[13th Infantry Battalion]], conducting reconnaissance and clearing Acehnese earthworks in the vicinity of [[Pekan Badak]] and [[Kraton of Aceh]] under General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]].
