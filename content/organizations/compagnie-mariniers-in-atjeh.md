The **[[Compagnie mariniers in Atjeh|compagnie mariniers in Atjeh]]** was the marine company of the [[Indische Leger|Indische leger]] despatched to [[Atjeh (Borel account)|Atjeh]] in late October [[1874]] — a company that had previously lain in garrison at Padang Pandjang for six months. Its first real fight took place on [[13 October]] [[1874]], during the [[Battle outside the ooster-face (13 October 1874)|battle outside the ooster-face]] of [[Kota Radja|K. Radja]], when a strong column under [[Kapitein van der Straate|kapitein van der Straate]] tried to penetrate the wilderness before sunrise and was repulsed. The compagnie mariniers forms one of the small but recurrent threads in Borel's account, illustrating both the chronic shortage of trained infantry and the slow professional maturation of troops learning the particular demands of Atjehnese warfare.

## Origin and Despatch

The compagnie mariniers had been raised for service in the Netherlands Indies and had lain for six months in garrison at Padang Pandjang on Sumatra's Westkust before being ordered to Atjeh. Its despatch in late October [[1874]] was part of the trickle of reinforcements that sustained the [[Bezettingskorps in Atjeh|bezettingskorps]] under [[Generaal Pel|generaal Pel]] after the [[Departure of Jan van Swieten from Atjeh (April 1874)|departure of the hoofdmacht]] under [[Jan van Swieten|generaal Swieten]] on [[26 April]] [[1874]]. The marine company arrived in Atjeh alongside a steady stream of individual bataljons — the 6de bataljon (two companies with staff) arrived at K. Radja on [[1 September]] [[1874]] from [[Padang]], "with music at the head"; the 5de and 10de bataljons, ~500 strong each, arrived in December [[1874]] for the Longbatta operations. The mariniers thus entered a theater in which the bezettingskorps was still recovering from the [[Crisis at Kota Radja (May–June 1874)|crisis of May–June 1874]], in which some companies could field fewer than 30 men of an original 125, and in which Borel judged that 500 kloeke Atjehneezen could have taken the Kraton.

## First Fight: [[13 October]] [[1874]]

The compagnie mariniers' first real fight took place on [[13 October]] [[1874]], in the [[Battle outside the ooster-face (13 October 1874)|battle outside the ooster-face]]. On [[12 October]] the Dutch had again lost ~10 dead and wounded clearing brush. On the morning of [[13 October]] a strong column under [[Kapitein van der Straate|kapitein van der Straate]] (180 men) tried to penetrate the wilderness before sunrise, hoping to find the far edge of the wood. The hope was disappointed: the wilderness extended so far the troops had to set up in its midst. At 6 AM the woodcutters began, but the Atjehneezen discovered them and attacked from all sides. "Mariniers, in their first real fight, wasted ammunition and had to give up position," Borel records. The whole covering force had to retire; by 8 AM the enemy's fire was overwhelming. [[Generaal Pel|Pel]] ordered retreat to the Kraton. Only when artillery on the zuider- and ooster-bastions opened fire were the Atjehneezen stopped. The day cost several dead and wounded and proved, in Borel's words, that the enemy was "complete master of terrain near the zuider- and ooster-facen." After this, clearing work was halted. The episode is one of the few moments at which Borel speaks slightingly of a Dutch unit's performance, and it functions in his narrative as a cautionary example of how raw troops, however brave, squandered their fire in the conditions of Atjehnese woodland fighting.

## Later Service

The compagnie mariniers remained in Atjeh through the campaigns of [[1874]]–[[1875]] and beyond. Borel mentions the marine (in the broader sense, including the ships' crews) as having provided zee-bescherming of the [[Stelling-Pel|stelling-Pel]] throughout the occupation, and as having performed the [[Submission of Pasangan (15 August 1878)|bombardment of Pasangan]] on [[15 August]] [[1878]] — the naval bombardment that brought about the submission of the vassal coastal state and that Borel cites as a model for the *aanvallende stelsel*. The compagnie mariniers' presence in Atjeh is also recorded in connection with the first enemy shelling of K. Radja on [[24 August]] [[1874]] — when enemy brought lilla's and at least one cannon onto a covered position and fired at the Dutch flag flying high over the Kraton, and Dutch fire from the 12 c.M. [[Achterlaadkanonnen van 12 c.M.|achterlaadkanonnen]] at [[Pakan Atjeh]] silenced the enemy guns — and in connection with the [[Occupation of the ooster-benteng (17 August 1874)|occupation of the ooster-benteng]] of [[17 August]] [[1874]], when [[Kapitein van Randwijk|kapitein van Randwijk]] seized the grafheuvel east of Pakan Atjeh at double-quick pace by surprise. The compagnie mariniers is thus one of the small regular units whose sustained service in Atjeh Borel implicitly credits, even when he records their earliest and least successful engagement.
