**Loman & Funke** was a Dutch publishing house of 's-Gravenhage (The Hague) that published, in [[1896]], J. A. Kruijt's historical brochure *[[De Atjeh-Oorlog (Kruijt, 1896)|De Atjeh-Oorlog: Kort Overzicht van Oorsprong, Verloop en Gevolg]]* as a volume in the *[[Boekerij van het Koloniaal Instituut|Boekerij van het Koloniaal Instituut]]* (Library of the Colonial Institute) series. The firm thereby served as the publisher of one of the earliest sustained Dutch historical critiques of the conduct of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]].

## The Publication of *De Atjeh-Oorlog* (1896)

Loman & Funke published J. A. Kruijt's *De Atjeh-Oorlog* in [[1896]], at 's-Gravenhage, as a volume in the *Boekerij van het Koloniaal Instituut* series of the [[Koloniaal Instituut|Koloniaal Instituut]] of Amsterdam. The brochure, of seventy-nine pages, was the principal Dutch-language historical critique of the conduct of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] from [[1873]] to [[1896]], written in the immediate aftermath of the [[Defection of Teuku Umar (March 1896)|defection of Teuku Umar]] of [[March]] [[1896]].

## Significance

Loman & Funke's publication of Kruijt's brochure placed it in the institutional context of the [[Koloniaal Instituut|Koloniaal Instituut]] — the principal Dutch scholarly institution for the study of the colonies — and ensured its dissemination to the Dutch colonial-policy audience at the Hague. The brochure's publication in the *Boekerij van het Koloniaal Instituut* series aligned it with the institutional critique of the Dutch conduct of the war that the Koloniaal Instituut was uniquely positioned to articulate.
