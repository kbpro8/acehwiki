**Surinamese border detachment** refers to a small unit of the Dutch colonial forces stationed on the Corantijn (Corinth) River frontier of the Surinam colony. During the Dutch December expedition in [[Acheh]], a member of this detachment was captured at [[Beresteyn plantation]] by rebel forces, as recorded in the [[Journal of Haringman]].

## Significance
The capture demonstrates the interconnectedness of Dutch colonial holdings in the Caribbean and the East Indies, and the reach of rebel activity beyond the immediate theater of war.
