**Mohammadijah**, a Islamic cultural/religious association, appears in [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]]'s *Land en Volk van Atjeh Vroeger en Nu* in connection with [[Aceh]]. One of the religious-cultural associations that maintain particular Hollandsch-Inlandsche scholen in Atjeh alongside the government schools.

## Type

Islamic cultural/religious association

## Description

One of the religious-cultural associations that maintain particular Hollandsch-Inlandsche scholen in Atjeh alongside the government schools.

## Significance

Mohammadijah is recorded by [[J. Jongejans|Jongejan]] as part of the institutional landscape of [[Aceh]].
