**6th Infantry Battalion** was a regular infantry battalion of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army]] deployed in the Aceh operational theater during the [[Second Aceh Expedition]] and throughout [[General Pel's Campaign in Aceh]] in [[1875]] and [[1876]].

## Strength and Order of Battle

In December [[1875]], the battalion was divided between the right and left military divisions in Aceh. Total battalion strength stood at 18 European officers, 212 European soldiers, and 363 indigenous soldiers, totaling 593 men.

## Role in the Attack on Mibouw

On [[26 December]] [[1875]], the right half-battalion of the [[6th Infantry Battalion]] participated in the opening combat of General [[J. L. J. H. Pel]]'s winter offensive. Fighting alongside two battalions of auxiliary troops and artillery, the unit executed the [[Attack on Mibouw]], storming Acehnese fortifications around [[Pekan Badak]] and [[Sinangri]].
