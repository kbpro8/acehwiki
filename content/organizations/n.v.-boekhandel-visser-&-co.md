**N.V. Boekhandel Visser & Co** was a Dutch colonial publishing house based in [[Weltevreden|Weltevreden]], the colonial quarter of [[Batavia|Batavia]], in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries. N.V. Boekhandel Visser & Co is the publisher of *[[ATJEH '96 (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96]]* (1913) by [[A. Kruisheer|Kapitein A. Kruisheer]] — the principal printed primary source for the operational history of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]] in [[1896]].

## Role as publisher of ATJEH '96

The title-page of both the *1e Deel* and the *Tweede Deel* of *[[ATJEH '96 (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96]]* carries the imprint *Weltevreden: N.V. Boekhandel Visser & Co, 1913*. N.V. Boekhandel Visser & Co was, in [[1913]], one of the principal colonial publishers of military and colonial literature in the Dutch East Indies, specialising in the publication of works by serving and former officers of the [[Royal Netherlands East Indies Army|K.N.I.L.]] and of the colonial civil administration. The *1e Deel* of *ATJEH '96* was issued with two maps and four portraits (of [[C. H. A. van der Wijck|Gouverneur-Generaal Jhr. C. H. A. van der Wijck]], [[C. Deykerhoff|Gen.-Maj. C. Deykerhoff]], [[J. A. Vetter|Lt.-Gen. Vetter]], and [[Teukoe Oemar|Teuku Umar]]); the *Tweede Deel* was issued with three maps and six portraits (including [[J. J. K. de Moulin|Gen.-Maj. De Moulin]], [[J. W. Stemfoort|Kolonel Stemfoort]], [[C. P. J. van Vliet|Kolonel Van Vliet]], and [[J. B. van Heutsz|Lt.-Kol. Van Heutsz]]).

## Significance

N.V. Boekhandel Visser & Co is the publisher of *[[ATJEH '96 (book by A. Kruisheer)|ATJEH '96]]* and one of the principal colonial publishers of military and colonial literature in the Dutch East Indies in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries. The imprint of the firm on the title-pages of both volumes of *ATJEH '96* makes it the publisher of record for the principal Dutch-language source on the [[1896]] campaign of the [[Aceh War|Atjeh-oorlog]].
